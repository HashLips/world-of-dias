# The Whale That Never Finished Its Shadow

## Name

The Whale That Never Finished Its Shadow

## Summary

Rim rumor of a sky-whale whose shadow stops halfway across the water—colossal beauty that refuses Forced-Final completion. Teaches wonder at unfinished scale without inventing a hunt or a taxonomy.

## Myth Text

Fishermen say a whale larger than a festival barge crosses the unfinished sky, and its shadow on the Nauw sometimes ends mid-wave as if the creature forgot the rest of itself. Listen posts on Outer Rim Distress Listen claim they hear the missing half as a low note that never resolves. Singing Fishermen treat the note as weather manners, not prey.

Some tellings blame Horizon-Null pressure. Some blame F960 becoming weather. Wise versions refuse both names and keep the whale distant. Children draw the half-shadow on pier wood and are told not to “finish” the drawing with a Forced-Final line—the myth’s soft lesson in crayon form.

Coastal comedy adds that a bottled boat once tried to catch the shadow and only caught the joke: contained voyages cannot hold unfinished sky.

Standalone folklore. Unsettled cousins stay unsettled. Soft limits hold: no ferry into the whale; no Frequency Zero answer; magnificence kept at rumor distance.
