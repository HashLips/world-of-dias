# The Scaffold That Refused a Ribbon

## Name

The Scaffold That Refused a Ribbon

## Summary

Scaffold-mile warning: workers try to cut a completion ribbon on living planks, and the boards lean away until the ribbon is put back in the pocket. Teaches unfinished persistence—do not Forced-Final a walkway that is still becoming.

## Myth Text

A foreman brings cloth and speech. The mile groans. Planks tilt just enough that the scissors miss. Apprentices learn to walk the honest middle: shore what fails, leave what still drafts. Persistence-scale rule is the sober cousin; clamp-mercy myth is the angry twin.

Standalone folklore. Soft limit: becoming is not a defect to stamp shut.
