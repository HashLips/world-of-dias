# Why the Ladle Comes Before the Stamp

## Name

Why the Ladle Comes Before the Stamp

## Summary

A Softfruit joke-myth. A proud stamp hand skips the court shelf, seals three wrong favors, and only then takes a ladle. Stew lands on the seal and the seal lands in the stew. The Row laughs for a week. The city keeps the moral: notice hunger and fairness before you publish a load.

## Myth Text

In the Veloria telling he is not a villain. He is busy, which is the local costume for harm. The shelf had tokens on it, and a child waiting, and a Host who had already looked at him once. He smiled the smile of a person who will be fair later. The stamp came down three times. Three favors, sealed hungry, slightly wrong in the way favors are wrong when the sealer has not eaten and does not intend to notice who else has not eaten. Then he reached for the ladle as a reward.

The stew did not care about sequence. It went onto the fresh seal. The seal, offended or merely wet, went back into the stew. For a week the Row told it louder than the work required, which is how Softfruit files a statute in the body. Almost-oranges were refused with extra theater. Second-ladle tokens clacked like applause. Nobody was exiled. He was fed, after, correctly, and the three favors were done again by a hand that had waited.

Cousin tellings at Flagweek say breakfast comes first for the same reason: a city that stamps before it feeds will stamp its own mood and call it law. The ladle version stays sticky and local, mint and wood and a laugh that ends before it becomes cruelty. Houses have a rule cousin written more dryly. The myth is not that rule. The myth is why the street will enforce the pause even when the rule book is in another room.

## Notes

Comedy ethic. Status **myth**. The separate ladle-before-stamp rule is the practice; this is the story that makes the practice memorable.
