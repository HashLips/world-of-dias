# The Beacon That Called the Wrong Shore

## Name

The Beacon That Called the Wrong Shore

## Summary

Rescue caution myth: a well-beacon shard sings a shore that saves one boat by misdirecting another chase. Kind wrongness can rescue—and still owes a later apology to whoever followed the lie too far.

## Myth Text

Raiders chase a rim boat. A beacon shard hums a false landing. The boat slips into Quiet Well shade. The raiders beach on stones and curse the light. Years later a fisher who trusted the same song in peacetime runs dry and learns the shard’s mercy was selective.

Mutual-rescue tellings insist you still answer distress—and you still confess when your beacon lied for someone else’s survival. The well-that-lied myth is the parent; this is the child who remembers the cost.

Standalone folklore. No ferry invents a true shore through wrong light. Frequency Zero stays unanswered. Lesson: tools of kind wrongness need later honesty, not Forced-Final pride.
