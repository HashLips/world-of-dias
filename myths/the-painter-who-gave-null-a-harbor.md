# The Painter Who Gave Null a Harbor

## Name

The Painter Who Gave Null a Harbor

## Summary

A teaching tale about an Averra painter who made F500 beautiful as a black harbor with piers—and the sailors who almost provisioned for it.

## Myth Text

The painting sold. Boots packed. Softfruit arrived with the Eight-Band Difference Chapbook and a void-notch glyph. “Anti-scenery is not a pier,” the host said. The painter kept the work labeled *wrong on purpose* thereafter, and Averra still hangs it to teach Band Confusion Corrections. Endings disagree whether the painter cried; they agree the harbor was never built.
