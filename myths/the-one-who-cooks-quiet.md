# The One Who Cooks Quiet

## Summary

Along canopy trails and in low voices at Verdant rest-fires, a story persists: somewhere in a grey mountain of western Wabet lives a dark exile who cooks matter and frequency into a soup. Taste it, and you become something you cannot unbecome. Most versions insist he does not hunt Wabet. Wabet comes to him.

## Myth Text

They say he arrived from a place that eats names, crossed Nauw while the sky briefly forgot its color, and walked west until even the birds stopped arguing. He found a hollow in the ash-colored rise and stayed.

He keeps a pot that never empties the same way twice. He lives on what he stirs. If you find the cave—and few do without a guide who already paid—he may let you taste. The gift is always yours alone. The ruin, if ruin comes, is also yours alone.

Some tellings make him kind. Some make him bored. The oldest canopy versions make him **careful**: he will not spill his darkness into the orchards, and he expects you not to drag a crowd up the mountain after you leave.

Skeptics say the story exists to scare fruit thieves off useless ridges. Returned seekers with impossible habits make the skepticism harder to keep.

## Notes

Rumor-status by design. Canonical counterparts are Vorrith Kael, Stillhollow, the Still Pot, and Giftbroth Change. This myth is how most of F432 hears the pressure without a map pin.
