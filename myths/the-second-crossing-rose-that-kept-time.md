# The Second Crossing Rose That Kept Time

## Name

The Second Crossing Rose That Kept Time

## Summary

Crossing-yard beauty tale: a rose blooms only when both lanes remember courtesy—arrival and return sharing the span without Forced-Final shoving. Teaches beauty as traffic manners made visible.

## Myth Text

When drivers rush, the rose stays bud. When the yard keeps lane discipline and lets the second crossing feel like a greeting, the bloom opens and keeps a soft hour of color. Story cousins pin human faces to the rose; the myth prefers the plant as referee.

Standalone folklore. Lesson: magnificence can be a flower that refuses chaos entertainment dressed as urgency.
