# The Horn That Waited for Morning

## Name

The Horn That Waited for Morning

## Summary

Desert beauty tale: a horn-tree will not sound for panic or Forced-Final dawn drills—only for the breeze that arrives when night has been honestly kept. Teaches patience as desert manners.

## Myth Text

Caravans blow early to “make” morning. The tree stays mute. A walker keeps night watch without inventing false light. Near true dawn, the breeze leans through the horn and the dunes answer once. Sibling energy to **Horn for the Night Breeze** story-craft; the myth keeps the tree’s refusal older than any one traveler.

Standalone folklore. Soft limits: no ferry through sound into other bands; water oath still binds.
