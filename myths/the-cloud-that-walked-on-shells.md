# The Cloud that Walked on Shells

## Summary

**The Cloud that Walked on Shells** is the soft telling of Glasswater Sky-Turtles—colossal gentleness mistaken for weather, and the rule that wonder is not a ladder.

## Myth Text

They say on certain thermal days a cloud grows toes.

It paddles the air the way koi paddle glasswater, and its shadow cools wheat without breaking it. Poets call it turtle. Surveyors call it unverified. Children call it proof the sky can be kind and still refuse to land.

A boy once tried to climb the shell with kite-string and prayer. The myth ends before the fall—on purpose. Elders stop at the moment the shadow paused, as if considering, then drifted on.

Tube heirs add: sky-raid memory watches for claws; turtle days teach the difference between threat and hugeness.
