# The First Pot That Gossiped

## Name

The First Pot That Gossiped

## Summary

Pot-signal origin joke: the first resonant pot did not carry orders—it carried neighbor news, and the mid-ring leaned closer to hear. Teaches civic tech as gossip infrastructure before House messaging.

## Myth Text

A cook sets a pot on a ledge and hums while stirring. Three streets away, another pot hums the same joke about a missing goat. Soon ledges sprout arrays. Houses try to stamp official bulletins into the pots; the pots prefer weddings, weather, and who owes fruit. **Pot Signal Customs** grow around volume manners, night quiet, and not using pots to shame the hungry.

Why-the-third-ring-leans tellers claim façades bent toward the first gossip pot. Array Court guides shrug and sell tea either way.

Standalone folklore. Lesson: resonant tech can be loveable without becoming a subway—or a sermon horn.
