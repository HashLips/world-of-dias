# The Chair Left Warm

## Summary

**The Chair Left Warm** teaches that love may keep a seat for the absent—and that trapping a ghost in endless performance is not the same gift.

## Myth Text

They say the first third chair was not moss and manners, but a stool left near a Softfruit plate after a clerk did not come home from Bleed-Sky week.

For three meals the chair stayed. Steam rose. No one claimed a vision. On the fourth meal a child sat, apologized to empty air, and ate. The table did not scold. The dead were not consulted by force.

Later tellings add afterglow—a brief name-light above the wood. Gift-listeners smile. Skeptics say it was lamp oil. Both agree on the rule the myth protects: **keep the chair warm; do not chain the echo.**
