# The Core That Watches Without Eyes

## Name

The Core That Watches Without Eyes

## Summary

Distant magnificence rumor: Veloria’s core glow notices crowds without using faces. Teaches awe without inviting a subway into opacity—keep wonder, refuse False maps of what the light wants.

## Myth Text

From Core Approach Overlook, people say the glow has no eyes and still turns when a lie is loud enough. High Steward Steps tellings claim the light leans warmer for shared festivals and colder for Forced-Final speeches. Outer-ring jokes say the core watches pot-signal gossip and finds it funnier than House minutes.

No version agrees what the watcher is. Some name Distant Companies as if rumor could become taxonomy. Some whisper Frequency Zero as absence wearing brightness. Wise tellers stop before answers. The myth’s job is to keep the core colossal and unread—beauty and threat at a distance that manners can survive.

Children are taught not to invent ferry tickets into the glow. Adults who try to sell “core maps that finally explain” get Softfruit laughter and empty pockets.

Standalone folklore. Soft limits hold: no ferry; Zero unresolved; Distant Companies unsettled. The lesson is magnificence without ownership.
