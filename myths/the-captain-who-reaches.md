# The Captain Who Reaches

## Summary

Locals say that when the Lantern in the Stone pulses hard, a faint figure like the *Glasswake*’s captain sometimes approaches **Glasswake Rock**, reaches toward the fused lantern, never greets the living, and vanishes before the lamp can be taken. Whether the figure is a ghost, an echo, or a scrap of an unfinished Fracture event is left open.

## Myth Text

On nights when the blue-white wash runs through the glass, walkers on the salt edge claim a second presence forms just beyond ordinary sight: coat or coat-memory, hand out, eyes only for the engraving and the ribs. Dogs go still. Speech feels too large, the way Listening Heat makes jokes feel too large inland. The figure never answers a name. It never looks at the living. It never frees the lantern. Before the reach completes, the shore is only rock and corrosion again.

Dunewalkers tell children the lesson without solving the ontology: you may witness a wanting that is not yours to finish. Keepers file the tale as folklore pressure around a real Prime Relic, not as a scheduled apparition you can book.

## Notes

Status is **myth** for the captain-figure. The lantern, rock, and pulse are separately canonical. Do not promote the figure to confirmed inhabitant without a deliberate plan.
