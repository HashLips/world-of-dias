# The Tea That Cooled Three Rings

## Name

The Tea That Cooled Three Rings

## Summary

Third-ring origin comfort: a teapot poured for three arguing ring-blocks cooled tempers before clearance stamps could harden the quarrel. Teaches tea as civic craft between pot-signal gossip and House paper.

## Myth Text

Clearance nearly closed a lane over a pot-signal insult. A tea house set three cups on one tray—no speeches, no winners. Steam did the work. The lane stayed open. Later tellings claim the buildings leaned in to smell the pour; engineers disagree politely over tea.

Standalone folklore. Lesson: some ring conflicts need temperature change more than new stamps.
