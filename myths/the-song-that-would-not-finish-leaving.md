# The Song That Would Not Finish Leaving

## Name

The Song That Would Not Finish Leaving

## Summary

A tavern myth about a chorus that kept returning a half-breath late because too many winters had sung it, teaching that art leaves remainder.

## Myth Text

Each spring the chorus arrived as if it had been waiting in the beams. Singers who tried to force last year’s grief into this year’s joke found the room resisting. Singers who scraped the old grief with one honest verse and then chose a kinder ending found the drift soften. The myth refuses a moral about never singing again; it asks for craft. Softfruit serves fruit during the argument. Houses sometimes stamp the story Partial and still hum it after work.
