# The Well That Lied to Save the Town

## Name

The Well That Lied to Save the Town

## Summary

Quiet Well origin tale: the well’s map lied about paths so raiders, Forced-Final clerks, and hungry shortcuts could not find the heart of the place. Kind wrongness can be shelter—if you remember it is wrong on purpose.

## Myth Text

When strangers came with clean stamps and greedy ropes, the Quiet Well rearranged its reflected paths. Roads that looked straight bent into date-palm shade. A beacon shard hummed a shore that was not the shore. The town drank; the hunters drank dust.

Later Houses called the well dishonest. Locals called it manners. Children learn the rhyme: *wrong map, warm throat*. The sibling myth **The Map That Must Stay Wrong** keeps the practice alive as rule-shaped folklore. Anyone who “corrects” the well for purity’s sake is told this older story until they put the chalk down.

Standalone folklore. Soft limits: no ferry through the well into other bands; Wrongness is shelter craft, not a Frequency Zero answer.
