# The Phase Stamp That Blinked Twice

## Name

The Phase Stamp That Blinked Twice

## Summary

Glassfold craft warning: a phase stamp used twice on the same wall-eye to “be thorough” opens a second glance the glass did not agree to. Teaches one honest stamp, then look away.

## Myth Text

A ledge worker stamps a phase window cleanly. Doubt arrives. They stamp again for courage. The wall-eye blinks twice—once for the legal glance, once for the stare. What shows in the second blink is the worker’s own unfinished fear wearing a neighbor’s face. Spacing rules and anti-stare manners are the sober cousins of this nightmare comedy.

Phase Bench apprentices practice the clap: stamp, read, power down. Anyone who jokes about “double-stamping for science” buys tea for the ledge.

Standalone folklore. Soft limit cousin to reveal manners: thoroughness is not a second window.
