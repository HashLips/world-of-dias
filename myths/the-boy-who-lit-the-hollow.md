# The Boy Who Lit the Hollow

## Name

The Boy Who Lit the Hollow

## Summary

After a Driftfall youth climbed Ashcalm Rise with a True Blue, Wabet orchard lanes filled with talk: the quiet cook had been visited, the hollow had taken light, and calm country’s bargain of ignoring the grey shoulder had cracked for a season of rumor.

## Myth Text

They say the boy went up hungry and came down emptier of stone and louder in story. Some versions claim the mountain glowed cyan for a night. Some claim Keepers would have stopped him if they had known in time. Bram’s lane swore the visit would draw more seekers; Fellgrove’s later abundance got folded into the same gossip whether it belonged there or not.

Skeptics said one orphan cannot uproar a region. Returned tellings from share-rows disagreed: enough doors closed, enough fingers pointed at Ashcalm, that “the boy who lit the hollow” became a week’s weather of talk.
