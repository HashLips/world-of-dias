# The Stamp That Promoted a Whisper

## Name

The Stamp That Promoted a Whisper

## Summary

A House teaching tale about a junior who stamped Law on a Zero whisper to end an argument, and the week of cold drawers and worse decisions that followed.

## Myth Text

The whisper was Careful-grade—taboo guess dressed as fear. The junior wanted quiet. The Law stamp made a clean sound. Softfruit refused lunch to the hallway that celebrated. Drawers went cold. A gate nearly sold a ferry fantasy with a House-looking seal. Seniors scraped the stamp’s aftermath for days and made the junior carve a new Careful handle by hand. Endings differ on forgiveness; they agree on the moral: grade is manners for a world that holds echoes.
