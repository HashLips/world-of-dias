# The Beast that Guards Both Sides

## Summary

**The Beast that Guards Both Sides** says the Long-Gate Beast is not Nauw’s monster or Sorel’s—it sits in the argument itself, hungry for anyone who pretends only one side of a gate is human.

## Myth Text

They say the first sighting was not a body but a lean: carts tilting toward whichever speech was louder.

Nauw porch tellers claim the beast eats soft promises. Sorel yard tellers claim it eats hard stamps. A third telling—quieter—says it eats the moment someone stops listening across the threshold.

Children ask what it looks like. Elders answer: like the coat you refuse to hang on the other hook.

Some nights the beast is only a story to keep feet careful. Some nights the careful feet are the only proof anyone needs.
