# The Distress Note That Paid Itself

## Name

The Distress Note That Paid Itself

## Summary

Rim rescue myth: a distress note is answered before ledgers open, and later the rescued boat returns the favor without being billed. Teaches mutual rescue over debt theater—help first, accounts second.

## Myth Text

A listen post hears a thin call. Clerks want a deposit. The listeners launch anyway. Years later the same boat answers someone else’s call and leaves no invoice. Distant Companies rumors try to own the ending; rim people keep the myth unsigned on purpose.

Sibling energy to **Distress Before Debt**. Soft limits: no ferry ticket hides inside gratitude.
