# The True Blues

## Name

The True Blues

## Summary

Across Nauw roads and Driftfall docks, people whisper of **True Blues**: Resonant Shards whose break glows a hard electric blue and whose resonance is strong enough that strangers pause. Ordinary shards are common enough. True Blues are not. Finding one on open dirt is treated as luck that asks a price later.

## Myth Text

They say the blue is not paint and not glass-trick. It is frequency showing through matter the way heat shows through thin iron. Elders claim True Blues remember paths between bands; skeptics claim they only remember greed.

A common shore version: if you pick one up on a public road, someone will already be watching. A dock version: if a crew knows you have one, sleep light. A kinder version from Wabet travelers: leave it where it lies unless the stone chooses your hand—though nobody agrees what “chooses” means.

Returned seekers sometimes produce blue fragments that do not glow. Those are called false blues, and they teach humiliation faster than geology.

## Notes

Rumor-status by design. Canonical counterpart for one named True Blue is [`artifacts/roadglow-shard.md`](../artifacts/roadglow-shard.md). Does not claim all blue stones are Resonant Shards—only that the True Blue pattern is culturally recognized as scarce.
