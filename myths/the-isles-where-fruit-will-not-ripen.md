# The Isles Where Fruit Will Not Ripen

## Summary

West-coast Wabet says the Songripe Isles refused orchard logic after the Fracture—fruit stalls green while songs ripen on schedule. Mainland traders call it inconvenience. Islanders call it mercy with a melody.

## Myth Text

One version says a storm tried to plant market-time on the islets and the sea spat the clocks back. Another says the birds voted. Soen Tide teaches the practical version: sing the calendar you have.
