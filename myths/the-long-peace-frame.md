# The Long Peace Frame

## Name

The Long Peace Frame

## Summary

An F200 folk telling that the meridian was given an inner rectangle, a frame inside the realm’s frame, so living light would have a hall to stand in. Peace, in this story, is geometry with neighbors. It is not emptiness, and it is not a creation dogma for all of Dias.

## Myth Text

Before the Soft-Rays learned procession, the bright field had brilliance and no aisle. You could be luminous and still have nowhere to be beside anyone. Aurel-kind say someone, or something, or the field itself in a moment of manners, drew a quieter rectangle down the spine. A frame inside the long frame. Breath-pulse began there: a long bright inhale, a softer exhale, and in the pause a place where a quiet rib could be offered without dimming the whole sky.

The hall in the story is the Soft-Ray Procession Hall, though the myth is older than the nickname and does not care about charts. Visitors who arrive and call it vacant sky are not struck. They are turned, gently, until the rows of light resolve into neighbors. The turning is the plot. Serenity was never the absence of persons. It was persons arranged so that one brightness does not eat the next.

Dimming stories attach as the frame’s fear, not as its moral. If the inner rectangle fails, the tellers say, serenity has nowhere to stand, and the field goes back to being only bright. That fear is why gathering manners exist: match the pulse lightly, do not harvest it, do not joke your way through a coherence gate as if the frame were a lock you were clever enough to pick. The myth elevates a cartographer’s nickname. It does not claim to have built the world.

## Notes

Cartography nickname elevated to myth. Not a creation dogma for all Dias. Luminosity as community, not as a weapon.
