# Ferry Ticket to Nowhere

## Name

Ferry Ticket to Nowhere

## Summary

Comedy myth of a boat that sells tickets between frequency bands and always docks at the same pier. The joke teaches Dias’s soft limit: there is no ferry (no subway) between bands. Wanting a door is not the same as having one.

## Myth Text

Travelers say that somewhere on the Nauw water a captain sells passage from home-sweet air to louder dramas and colder notches—F-numbers dressed as destinations. You buy a cheerful stub. You board a little boat that looks almost official. You sail. You disembark. The pier under your feet is the pier you left.

In Veloria telling, the captain is often **Ell Ferrylie**, and the pier is **Ferrylie Joke Pier**. The ticket print shows a tiny boat; after purchase comes a strike-through **no-ferry mark**, or fine print that reads: destination nowhere you do not already stand. Tourists laugh and pocket the stub. Seekers who hunger for a crossing find the same laugh dangerous.

The myth insists the boat is longing wearing comedy clothes. People who want Frequency Zero solved, or a safe ride out of F432 into another band’s weather, keep paying for the bit because the bit names the ache without pretending science can stamp a subway. Houses sometimes hate the joke for making soft limits public. Softfruit vendors like it because noticing is not a ticket.

**Orrin Veld** cites the myth whenever apprentices ask for widen instructions or band doors. He treats Ell’s pier as ethics warning, not tourism. Frederick Lumens’s orchard hunger is the serious twin of the same longing; the ticket is how coastal comedy says what a costly consultation already said: too costly to cross. After Orrin and Issa Partial publish partial spectrum honesty with no-ferry disclaimers, school boards pin fake stubs beside packets so children learn the limit in ink, not only in Row whispers.

The myth remains rumor on purpose. If it became official doctrine, someone would sell a “real” ferry next week. Standalone folklore; the Calibration Saga references it without owning every pier joke in the basin.
