# Why Flagweek Breakfast Comes First

## Name

Why Flagweek Breakfast Comes First

## Summary

Flagweek origin joke: the parade once started before anyone ate, and half the city mistook hunger for political fury. Breakfast first is civic craft—joy before chaos entertainment.

## Myth Text

Flags went up at dawn. Stomachs stayed empty. By midmorning every color looked like an insult, every float like a rival House. Someone dragged bowls onto the Flagweek Strip, forced a shared chew, and only then let the chaos begin. The fight shrank into theater. The rule stuck: breakfast before banners bite.

Softfruit and Hearth Laughter cousins claim the bowls were ladle-court cousins. Parade-goats refuse to walk until oats appear—animal proof, street theologians say.

Standalone folklore. Lesson: festival magnificence needs sugar and salt before slogans, or joy turns into stamp weather.
