# The Shelf Clearing

## Name

The Shelf Clearing

## Summary

*The Shelf Clearing* is an old Driftfall pier tale, told rarely and badly at the Salt Ledger, about an island off the south coast that was "cleared" by claim-papers long ago until it vanished. Nobody on the mainland connects it to a real place. The Aumeri remember an event they call the Clearing, and the tale may be the Long Shore's half-memory of it. That link is **unconfirmed**, and the tale's details contradict what Nessa Orvay told Ansel.

## Myth Text

*In the old salvage days, before the yards had names, a boat came home from a clear-weather run with a chart nobody had seen before: an island past the shelf, with good stone and sweet water and steps cut in its cliff.*

*Men took papers out to it. This stone is mine, said one paper. This water is mine, said another. The island people had no papers, so the island was nobody's, so the island was everybody's.*

*They took the cut stone off its walls to build the first piers. They took the young ones to haul. When the island people would not leave, the paper-men called them a hazard and came back with more boats.*

*And one year the boats went out and the island wasn't there. Just water, and a smell of wet stone, and the sea counting under the keel. Pier-boys say if you dig under the oldest pier at Driftfall you'll find cut stone with steps on it. Nobody digs.*

## Notes

- Nessa's account (chapter 9) says the survivors stayed and built the boom. They did not vanish then. The tale's "it wasn't there" may confuse the Clearing with later hiding, or may be invented.
- Do not confirm the pier stones or any Driftfall guilt as fact. Status **myth**.
