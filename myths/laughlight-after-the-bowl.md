# Laughlight after the Bowl

## Summary

**Laughlight after the Bowl** says genuine shared laughter thickens plaza seedgrass—and forced laughter grows a thin, itchy green that fools no one for long.

## Myth Text

They say the first Laughlight sprouted where a table refused a ledger and still filled bowls.

Osa’s name walks into the myth like a cousin, not a goddess: host first, grass later. Children check cracks after breakfast. If the green is soft, the laugh was real. If it scratches the ankle, someone performed joy for a stamp.

Laughter Week banners are stored cloth. Laughlight is stored weather. Both matter. Only one grows.

A Free-Captain once ordered a cheer. The grass that week was pale and mean. The yard still tells it when recruiters smile too hard.
