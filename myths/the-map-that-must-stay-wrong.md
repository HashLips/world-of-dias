# The Map That Must Stay Wrong

## Summary

Lumira tells of a surveyor who published a perfect oasis chart and returned to find the well gone—not stolen, simply no longer willing to be found. Since then, Keepers teach that some maps must stay wrong on purpose.

## Myth Text

A Basin buyer paid for truth. The dunes answered with absence. Children now draw decoys before they draw homes. Elders say the well is not hiding from kindness; it is hiding from advertisement.

## Notes

Supports Quiet Well rule without requiring literal confirmation of a vanishing oasis.
