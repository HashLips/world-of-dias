# The First Line That Failed

## Summary

Rim families whisper of a year when the first spout was claimed by a boat that was not within hail. The Answer went out anyway. The season that followed answered back with empty nets and a storm that learned names.

## Myth Text

Whether weather punished a lie or coincidence wore a story’s clothes is argued every Hush Week. Jessa Quill’s loft treats the tale as operational warning: do not call a false first “first.”
