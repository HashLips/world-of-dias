# Why the Ladle Refused the Wish

## Name

Why the Ladle Refused the Wish

## Summary

A Softfruit teaching tale: a guest asks the ladle to make a debt vanish by “resonating harder,” and learns that resonance answers craft and remainder—not wishes.

## Myth Text

A guest set a coin beside the fee bowl and whispered for the hall to erase a debt-mark overnight. The host dipped the ladle, tasted steam, and set the ladle down dry. *It resonates,* the host said. *It does not obey hunger dressed as law.* The guest pointed at a fruit-road handbill that promised wish-hum. Softfruit fed the guest lunch, burned only the handbill’s promise line, and made the guest wipe tables until the board felt kind again. In some tellings the coin stays as scrape-honest tip; in others it is returned with a Partial Echo Slash drawn in juice.

Cultures use the tale when someone treats Everything resonates as a vending machine.

## Notes

Myth status: teaching story, not a claim about a magic ladle personality.
