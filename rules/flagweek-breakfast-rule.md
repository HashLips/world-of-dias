# Flagweek breakfast rule

## Name

Flagweek breakfast rule

## Overview

Flags may change weekly. Breakfast must not become a weapon of allegiance. The morning table stays continuous even when cloth does not.

## Description

Mir Weekly enforces the rule as comic and deadly serious. Spoil the porridge for politics, and the strip’s memory outlasts your flag.

## Effect

- Lets border satire carry real stakes
- Protects children from weekly civil war at the table
- Gives Three Opinions visits a hard custom to bounce off
