# Flagweek breakfast rule

## Name

Flagweek breakfast rule

## Overview

On the **Flagweek Strip**, flags may change weekly. **Breakfast must not become a weapon of allegiance.** The morning table stays continuous even when cloth does not.

## Description

Mir Weekly enforces the custom as comic and deadly serious. Spoil the porridge for politics—refuse a child a seat because last night’s flag lost—and the strip’s memory outlasts your cloth. Allegiance is for poles and pamphlets. Care is for the bowl.

Visitors who treat breakfast as recruitment get laughed out, then frozen out. Three Opinions visits bounce off this hard custom: you may argue flags; you may not starve the argument’s children.

## Effect

- Lets border satire carry real stakes without weekly civil war at the table.
- Protects ordinary joy when symbols thrash.
- Teaches Flagweek households a soft-limit cousin: belonging politics stop at the spoon.
