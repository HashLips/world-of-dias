# Bloomline no-victory-speech rule

## Name

Bloomline no-victory-speech rule

## Overview

During a confluence hold, **no Forced-Final victory speeches**. Sit the mat; weather outranks applause.

## Description

**Who follows:** Terrace guides, Softfruit visitors, heart-deer-respecting locals.

**Who breaks:** Speakers declaring crisis solved mid-hold; clamp merchants; tourists standing on mats to sermonize.

**What happens:** Hold continues until the speaker sits; mats folded only when weather ends; clamp ban cited.

## Effect

- Gives holding rule a speech-specific tooth.
- Blocks Forced-Final mood ownership.
- Ties myth to enforcement.
