# Averra offshore courtesy rule

## Name

Averra offshore courtesy rule

## Overview

Mainland communities may answer Averra rituals with light or silence, but may not force contact at ceremonial hours. The practical signal for those windows is **The Offshore Pass**.

## Description

When a recognized pass is open, vessels hold distance. Observers may watch, interpret, light a answering lamp, or keep silent. They may not board, flare-summon, or “creep closer for one honest look.” Intrusion during closed hours is worse: it treats Quiet Cloister business as a tourist dock.

People who reject Averra myths still follow the rule—provocation nights are expensive in hulls and reputations. Sael Corvin’s isle stays curated partly because this courtesy holds on the water.

## Effect

- Preserves ritual coexistence without political merger.
- Limits provocation around high-rumor nights.
- Keeps sealed memory politics from becoming open naval theater.
