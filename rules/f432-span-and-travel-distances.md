# F432 span and travel distances

## Name

F432 span and travel distances

## Overview

**F432** is a vast frequency realm, not a small city-state. Its lived stage stretches about **4,000 km east–west** and **3,500 km north–south**. Journey talk that treats Veloria, Wabet, and Sorel as neighboring market squares is comedy or ignorance; the distances below are the working measures convoy stewards, toll marshals, and private freighter hires use when they plan bruise, fodder, and rest.

## Description

**Who follows:** Fruit-road stewards, Brindle March marshals, ox and draft-pony crews, Softfruit and shed clerks who buy against arrival windows, and private freighter hires such as **The Late Bruise**.

**Who breaks:** Speakers who shrink the realm to make a raid sound like a day trip; carts that claim “one more good day” past beast and fruit limits; flight talk that pretends a night freighter is the whole road and owes the waystations nothing.

**What happens:** Waystations and Span Toll chalk refuse the shrink story. Convoys that ignore the ~25 km good-day pace lose animals, fruit, and welcome. Freighters that skip stops without paying lend and labor are already talked about as raid under **Fruit Road and Toll Travel**.

### Realm span

| Axis | Approximate measure | How people use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| East–west | ~4,000 km | Wabet’s western orchards to the far eastern edges of Nauw weather |
| North–south | ~3,500 km | Northern Basin approaches down through Long Gate country into Sorel |

These numbers do not invent new soft-limit crossings or ferries between frequencies. They describe how far F432’s own roads and private craft must travel **inside** the band.

### Working corridor distances

| Corridor | About | Ground pace | Night freighter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Veloria City** ↔ **Wabet** (Lent Strip / orchard approaches) | ~1,400 km | ~55 days by ox wagon at ~25 km per good day | One long night flight for **The Late Bruise** |
| **Veloria City** ↔ **Sorel** (south via **Long Gate**) | ~1,100 km | Weeks of march, span, and exile-road manners | Not a scheduled freighter route in this rule |
| **Sorel** ↔ **Wabet** | ~1,300 km | Westfold / orchard approaches; not a shortcut that deletes ethics | Not a band ferry |

Exact wagon days flex with heat, bruise, and documented rest. The **~25 km per good day** figure is the braid’s teaching pace: braided waystations on the **Eastbound Fruit Road** are sited so ox wagons, **Nauw Basin Oxen**, and **Fruit-Road Draft-Ponies** can reach shade, water, and soup-tag stops without Forced-Final pushing.

### Flight vs wagon

A night freighter flight can skip waystations wagons must attend. That speed is real—**Veloria** to **Wabet** in one long night for The Late Bruise—and it does not erase reciprocity. Wagons still carry the ordinary hard loads; the plane is private hire from grass like the **Lent Strip**, not a replacement for the road. Skipping stops without restore remains raid talk, even when the hull arrives on time.

## Effect

- Sets F432’s scale so sagas and market math stay honest.
- Gives stewards a shared pace (~25 km/good day) tied to braided waystations.
- Separates freighter convenience from road ethics without inventing cross-frequency passage.
- Lets clerks convert distance into bruise windows and fodder tallies instead of clock pride alone.

## Notes

Companion culture for how fruit survives those distances: **Fruit-Road Keeping**. Movement manners: **Fruit Road and Toll Travel**, **Beast and Relay Transport**, **Transport of Dias**. Do not read these km as tickets to other bands.
