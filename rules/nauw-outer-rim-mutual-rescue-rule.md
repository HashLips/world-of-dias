# Nauw Outer Rim mutual rescue rule

## Name

Nauw Outer Rim mutual rescue rule

## Overview

Any vessel capable of safe intervention must answer distress within outer-rim range. Life support cannot wait on contracts.

## Description

Commercial priority, rival affiliation, and debt do not void rescue obligation. Salvage may be argued later. Violation marks a captain unreliable across ports—often permanently. Singing Fishermen culture treats the rule as tide ethics: the sea is hard enough without refusing hands.

## Effect

- Maintains minimum trust in high-risk waters.
- Reduces retaliatory refusal cycles between crews.
- Lets joy of safe return outrank ledger spite.

## Notes

Open question: who judges “capable of safe intervention” when a captain claims the swell is too high.
