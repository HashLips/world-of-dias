# Thin-Room Ear consent rule

## Name

Thin-Room Ear consent rule

## Overview

A **Thin-Room Ear** may listen only with known consent of the room’s keepers—or under explicit distress exception. Care must not become custody by stealth.

## Description

**Who follows:** Licensed listeners, Operator-adjacent stewards under restricted-tech manners, Softfruit refusing souvenir ears.

**Who breaks:** Spies treating ears as toys; Houses planting ears without disclosure; sellers of “harmless” ear kits for market stalls.

**What happens:** Device seizure under Restricted and Taboo Tech; nook bans; bulb-containment myths assigned as caution. Distress exceptions still exist; stealth careers do not.

## Effect

- Draws a consent line on unsettled listening devices.
- Blocks specimen temptation dressed as safety.
- Keeps Listening primer from endorsing surveillance default.
