# Hearthvale shelter-first rule

## Name

Hearthvale shelter-first rule

## Overview

In **Hearthvale**, immediate shelter and stabilization come **before** status judgment. Heat, a bowl, and a safe floor first—history and disputes after the body stops shaking.

## Description

New arrivals from Long Gate and other roads receive baseline protection and intake support before adjudication of Compact/Freeband history, debts, or rumor. Accountability still happens; it waits until people can stand.

This is a practical signature of Sorel’s second-beginning ethos. Exclusion-first factions call it naive. Rebuild kitchens call it why children survive the first week.

## Effect

- Cuts mortality and revenge-entry cycles.
- Attracts criticism from security-first models.
- Makes Hearthvale feel like refuge without erasing later hard law.
