# Resonance feats have costs

## Name

Resonance feats have costs

## Overview

**Resonance feats have costs** is the law-grade rule that deliberate use of resonance or echo always spends something—load, souring, misfile risk, social debt, sealed access, or bodily strain—even when the feat succeeds.

**Claim grade:** law (cost exists); observation for particular cost textures; taboo for “cost-free omnipotence” devices.

**Not:** free miracle; clean delete of consequence; proof that paying gold removes remainder.

## Description

### Plain definition

A **cost** is a real consequence attached to a resonant feat: the room sours, the reader tires, the seal hides needed access, the stare invites nest trouble, the scrape must stay on the page, the crowd owes manners afterward.

### Common cost classes

| Cost class | What you pay | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Load / fatigue | Body and attention | Alignment fatigue; operator load-noise |
| Sour remainder | Place or tool turns harsh | Gate argument-stain; over-listened room |
| Partiality debt | You only got a slice | Partial Echo Slash honesty |
| Seal tradeoff | Access hidden for later people | Sealed ledger cold spot |
| Stare-cost | Eyes and nests | F610 stare past glance |
| Social debt | Manners and trust | Softfruit correction; seized stall |

### Who follows

Anyone who treats craft as billable: Softfruit hosts, clerks, Ember Keepers, Veil Scribes, Operators with load discipline.

### Who breaks

Sellers of cost-free complete readings; Forced-Final speeches that erase the bill; wish-handbills.

### What happens when broken

The cost arrives anyway—often uglier—and institutions answer with bans, scrape, and refusal.

## Effect

- Makes succeed ≠ free
- Supports drama without inventing new magic systems
- Links soft limits to lived consequence

## Notes

Companion to Resonance does not grant wishes. Soft Limits of Dias lists refusals; this rule lists the bill when you act inside or against them.
