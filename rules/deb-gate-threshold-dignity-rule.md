# DEB Gate threshold dignity rule

## Name

DEB Gate threshold dignity rule

## Overview

At Veloria’s **DEB Gate**, force is lawful only when proportionate, documented, and non-humiliating to compliant entrants. Strict entry is allowed. Spectacle humiliation is not.

## Description

Ward officers may deny access and detain for cause. They must log why. Command urgency does not erase procedure. Performative degradation of lawful travelers—mock searches meant as theater, forced kneeling for entertainment, jeering lines—is punishable under gate custom and Concord-adjacent oversight.

Travelers often steady themselves with the **DEB Gate Touch** (two fingers on the frame). The dignity rule is what makes that touch a greeting rather than a flinch before cruelty.

Taught as **order without spectacle**. First-arrival chapters (including Calen’s echo at the gate) assume the rule is usually kept—and that breaks of it are memorable scandals.

## Effect

- Preserves legitimacy of a strict gate.
- Creates traceable accountability in high-tension entry events.
- Lets joy and wonder survive the threshold: people can arrive without being broken for the crowd.
