# Unsigned scrap-care rule

## Name

Unsigned scrap-care rule

## Overview

Useful scrap that refuses a signature may be **heard and acted on without forced ownership**. Do not stamp anonymous help into a House career—or into nonactionable silence—just because it will not salute.

## Description

**Who follows:** Scrap-nook listeners, Softfruit auditors of care, rim crews learning basin quiet, Kesh-adjacent refusals (no ferry paper, no panic leaflets).

**Who breaks:** Clerks who demand names before listening; Houses that convert unsigned help into recruitment; filers who bury living scrap as noise.

**What happens:** The scrap goes static; the nook empties; Glowfly pillars dull. Community trust shifts to Softfruit and unsigned tokens. Soft limits: scrap is not a band ferry.

## Effect

- Protects unsigned care as civic texture.
- Blocks both recruitment and erasure of helpful scrap.
- Gives Listening and Load Noise a rule spine.
