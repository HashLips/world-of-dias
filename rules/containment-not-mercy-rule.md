# Containment-not-mercy rule

## Name

Containment-not-mercy rule

## Overview

Bottling living scrap “to keep it safe” is **custody until proven care**. Do not invent a cage and call the cage mercy—especially near Forced-Final locks.

## Description

**Who follows:** Row mentors, Softfruit shared-table openers, scrap-nook listeners.

**Who breaks:** Specimen tourists; Houses filing bottled wonder as trophies; anyone sealing scrap without a release plan.

**What happens:** Stoppers opened under mentor watch; bulb myth assigned; clamp ban and ear-consent cited; Softfruit may demand table release.

## Effect

- Names containment temptation as a manners crime.
- Protects living wonder from specimen pride.
- Soft limits: bulb ≠ band ferry.
