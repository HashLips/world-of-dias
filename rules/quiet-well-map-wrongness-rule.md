# Quiet Well map-wrongness rule

## Name

Quiet Well map-wrongness rule

## Overview

Public maps of **Quiet Well** and sister oases must remain **strategically wrong**. Accurate water charts are treated as extraction technology—not as kindness to strangers.

## Description

Silent Dune Keep maintains decoy marks, rotated true paths, and penalties for publishing clean oasis charts. Travelers who “find the well” honestly usually do so through Keep assent, dune-walker trust, or earned silence—not through a market map that tells every caravan where to drain the water.

The myth *The Map That Must Stay Wrong* is the story face of this rule. Fruit-money surveyors and Basin clerks who demand perfect charts meet dune manners that look like lies and are care.

## Effect

- Preserves oasis longevity under caravan pressure.
- Creates conflict with Nauw/Basin accuracy culture.
- Makes finding water a moral plot, not only navigation.

## Notes

Keep cartography perforated—readers should feel the wrongness is intentional. Open question: what happens when a Resonant Shard or echo-read makes the decoys useless.
