# F200 coherence gate rule

## Name

F200 coherence gate rule

## Overview

**F200** is not entered by walking harder. Traversal requires **coherence alignment**—your resonance must match enough of the band’s high-order clarity, or the lumen realm attenuates you, drifts you, or ejects you toward less stable bands.

## Description

Directional maps alone fail. Travelers who treat F200 like a bright country beyond a hill find themselves thinned, confused, or returned without a ferry story that makes sense. Aurel Meridian cultures and prepared instruments speak of alignment practice; F432 mostly knows F200 indirectly—through bleed, rumor, and rare successful approaches.

This is a soft limit cousin to “no subway”: understanding luminosity is not a ticket.

## Effect

- Preserves F200’s structural clarity.
- Makes casual tourism impossible.
- Explains why F432 catalogs stay partial about light-band life.
