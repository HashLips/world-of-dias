# Second Crossing temporary-chalk rule

## Name

Second Crossing temporary-chalk rule

## Overview

Lane chalk at Second Crossing **must wash**—temporary courtesy marks only. Permanent paint is arrogance toward unfinished weather and kills the rose’s soft hour.

## Description

**Who follows:** Yard wardens, fruit-road marshals, Softfruit festival volunteers, kids redrawing after storms.

**Who breaks:** Drivers painting Forced-Final lanes; Houses claiming permanent right-of-way ink; anyone shoving both directions at once.

**What happens:** Paint scraped; chalk redrawn; rose stays bud until manners return; lane discipline rule is cited. Beauty remains traffic made visible.

## Effect

- Protects returning-span courtesy with a materials rule.
- Blocks permanent ownership cosplay of a living yard.
- Ties rose myth to enforceable practice.
