# Quiet Cloister silence-bead rule

## Name

Quiet Cloister silence-bead rule

## Overview

During between-repetitions practice, hold a **Silence Bead** and wait one full breath after looking before speech. Silence is craft, not a gag order for the street outside.

## Description

**Who follows:** Cloister guides, Veil Scribe visitors, Row makers after helpful-glass days.

**Who breaks:** Speakers who start Forced-Final talks mid-bead; tourists treating beads as mute punishments for others; Houses scheduling “efficiency sermons” in cloister rooms.

**What happens:** Guides end the session; bead warmers (early speakers) buy tea; Softfruit jokes pair beads with melting-smile pins—one against fake urgency, one against fake cheer.

## Effect

- Makes intentional quiet enforceable as practice manners.
- Cousin to anti-stare without claiming F610 windows.
- Keeps Quiet Places primer actionable.
