# Claim Grade Discipline

## Name

Claim Grade Discipline

## Overview

**Claim Grade Discipline** requires that serious claims about how Dias works be graded as **law**, **theory**, **observation**, or **taboo guess**—and that promotion between grades be deliberate, argued, and rare.

## Description

### Grade tests (short)

- **Law:** Would Softfruit and a Resonant House both rearrange lunch or ledgers if this were false? If yes, and cultures across bands still act as if it holds, treat as law-grade.
- **Theory:** Does it guide work while remaining revisable without rewriting the world’s identity? Theory.
- **Observation:** Was it witnessed or instrumented as remainder/event without a closed cause? Observation.
- **Taboo guess:** Would treating it as a tool, commute, or verdict break soft limits or seal ethics? Taboo guess.

### Who follows

Clerks with Claim Grade Stamp Set; Softfruit hosts refusing Forced-Final cosmology at fruit; House juniors under F432 partial knowledge protocol; gate officers demoting ferry talk.

### Who breaks

Pamphleteers; wipe-sellers; anyone who stamps law on a taboo guess to win an argument.

### What happens when broken

Policy built on poetry; cruelty built on incomplete echoes; ferry fantasies with stamps.

## Effect

Makes Cosmology of Dias’ law/theory/taboo table operable in daily institutions, with observation restored as its own grade.
