# Resonance does not grant wishes

## Name

Resonance does not grant wishes

## Overview

**Resonance does not grant wishes** is the law-grade rule that wanting a result harder does not make resonance deliver it. Resonance answers participation and craft; it is not a vending machine for desire.

**Claim grade:** law.

**Not:** “think it and the echo obeys”; paid complete-life readings; ferry tickets sold as hope.

## Description

### Plain definition

A **wish** here means asking the world to produce an outcome because you desire it, without matching craft, consent, band habit, or accepted cost. Softfruit ladles, House stamps, and road customs all refuse that bargain as ordinary practice.

### Who follows

Softfruit hosts who refuse ladle-wishes; clerks who file partials; Ember Keepers who will not miracle-reset; Veil Scribes who stop; road crews who laugh at ferry stubs and keep walking.

### Who breaks

Handbill sellers promising “one hum and your debt dissolves”; complete-reading stalls; Forced-Final mercy speeches; Zero-wipe gadget talk.

### What happens when broken

The claimed result fails, sour remainder rises, social correction follows (ladle refusal, seized handbill, House Theory stamp, No-Ferry chill)—or, rarely, a costly mystery event that still does not become a reliable wish tool.

## Effect

- Keeps Everything resonates from collapsing into fantasy omnipotence
- Makes Softfruit manners and House craft load-bearing
- Gives comedy and danger the same soft-limit teeth (Ferry Ticket to Nowhere)

## Notes

Pairs with Resonance feats have costs. Desire is allowed; selling desire as physics is not.
