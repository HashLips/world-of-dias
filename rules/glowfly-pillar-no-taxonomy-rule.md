# Glowfly pillar no-taxonomy rule

## Name

Glowfly pillar no-taxonomy rule

## Overview

Glowflies gathering at a listening pillar may signal unsigned help. **Do not stamp them into Distant Companies taxonomy** or any settled army list. Wonder stays smaller than the stare.

## Description

**Who follows:** Scrap-nook listeners, Softfruit night jokers who still work, Row ethics demos.

**Who breaks:** Panic leaflet printers; Houses naming fly patterns as proof of companies; tourists graphing flies past manners.

**What happens:** Pillars dull; unsigned scrap goes static; Softfruit and Kesh-adjacent refusals cite the rule. Mysteries of Dias keeps companies unsettled.

## Effect

- Protects listening craft from taxonomy panic.
- Reinforces Soft Limits on Distant Companies.
