# Softfruit keep-mark share rule

## Name

Softfruit keep-mark share rule

## Overview

A **Keep-Mark Cord** marks fruit held for sharing. Cutting it early to sell the keep as ordinary stock is a manners crime; the keep outranks coin for the marked meal window.

## Description

**Who follows:** Softfruit vendors, waystation cooks, fruit-road crews under ethics customs.

**Who breaks:** Vendors snipping cords for surge pricing; tourists treating keep bowls as free-for-all without greeting; Houses reclassifying keep as taxable excess mid-meal.

**What happens:** Cords will not warm for the breaker; ladle courts refuse service; Softfruit comedy names the snipper. Emergency hunger still eats—lifestyle theft of keep does not.

## Effect

- Protects share tempo with a physical mark.
- Cousin to ladle-before-stamp and waystation soup-tag.
- Aligns Softfruit with Wired Bloom proof spirit without orchard law.

Artifact: Softfruit Keep-Mark Cord.
