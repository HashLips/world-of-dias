# Distress-before-debt rescue rule

## Name

Distress-before-debt rescue rule

## Overview

When a distress note is honest, **launch before ledgers open**. Deposits may follow; they may not gate the first answer. Gratitude may return later as fish-hook favor—not interest theater.

## Description

**Who follows:** Outer Rim listen crews, Singing Fishermen cousins, Softfruit teaching Distress Before Debt, mutual-rescue stewards.

**Who breaks:** Clerks demanding deposit before launch; sellers of “rescue subscriptions”; anyone billing panic as product.

**What happens:** Listen posts refuse the breaker’s future calls for help-priority; returned gratitude hooks hang as manners trophies naming who paid back without invoice; Softfruit empties the subscription stall. Cousin to Nauw Outer Rim mutual rescue rule—this clause sharpens the debt timing.

## Effect

- Makes rim ethics enforceable beyond goodwill speeches.
- Aligns story/myth cousins with rule language.
- Soft limits: gratitude is not a ferry ticket.
