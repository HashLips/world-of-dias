# Nineteen-nineteen fairness-check rule

## Name

Nineteen-nineteen fairness-check rule

## Overview

When two sides claim **equal numbers**, Softfruit (or open-ledger auditors) may demand a **nineteen-nineteen check**: look for hidden weight before calling the ledger fair.

## Description

**Who follows:** Softfruit field interviewers, Span Toll witnesses, basin open-ledger stewards, copy-desk trainers.

**Who breaks:** Houses waving matching sums as moral victory; clerks who refuse the yellow scale prop; sellers of “balanced” sheets that ate margins.

**What happens:** Fairness cards come out; witness chips drop; packets without margin ribbons fail the check automatically. Comedy enforcement preferred; quiet recalls for Houses.

## Effect

- Makes “equal count ≠ honest weight” enforceable.
- Pairs open-ledger rule with Softfruit teaching tools.
- Blocks pretty math as Forced-Final fairness.

Artifact: Nineteen-Nineteen Fairness Cards.
