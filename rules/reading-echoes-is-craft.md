# Reading echoes is craft

## Name

Reading echoes is craft

## Overview

Hearing that something remains is not the same as **reading** it. In Dias, echo-reading is skilled, partial, risky work—never a free window into total truth.

**Claim grade:** law (craft requirement); taboo for omniscient reading-as-appliance.

## Description

### Craft, not gift absolutism

Some people have talent. Softfruit still insists talent without manners is a hazard. Houses insist talent without classification is a governance failure. Operators insist talent without load discipline is noise. All three are protecting the same soft limit: **no casual omniscience**.

### What craft includes

- Knowing when not to listen further
- Separating aftertone from story you want to be true
- Scraping corrections instead of erasing
- Filing partials as partials
- Accepting that two honest readers can disagree

### Who follows

Trained listeners, careful hosts, junior clerks with Partial Echo Slash stamps, road witnesses who know the difference between a feeling and a verdict.

### Who breaks

Market “complete reading” sellers; stalkers with instruments; anyone who treats a first rise as courtroom proof.

### What happens when broken

People get filed as finished objects. Places get over-read until they sour. Soft limits fray toward Zero-curiosity dressed as diligence.

## Effect

Keeps echo-readers like Calen meaningful as practitioners inside a law, not wizards outside it.
