# House margin-ribbon reserve rule

## Name

House margin-ribbon reserve rule

## Overview

Publish packets that touch spectrum, bands, or load claims must keep a **margin ribbon** (or equal blank run) for scrapes, ask-again notes, and no-ferry disclaimers. Clean edges that ate their honesty are noncompliant.

## Description

**Who follows:** Issa Partial students, Inkspan desks, Softfruit school-board pin crews, Houses under open-ledger pressure.

**Who breaks:** Forced-Final clerks who snip ribbons for “pretty pages”; stamp galleries that reward margin-eating grins; sellers of clean certainty sheets.

**What happens:** Boards pull the packet; scrape blades and ask-again pads become mandatory remediation; the stamp-that-ate myth gets taped to the desk. Softfruit prefers comedy enforcement; Houses prefer quiet recalls.

## Effect

- Makes Partial Publish Courtesy physically checkable.
- Pairs with scraped correction preference and no-ferry disclaimer publish.
- Blocks pretty silence as institutional virtue.
