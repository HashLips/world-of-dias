# Soft-ray watching distance rule

## Name

Soft-ray watching distance rule

## Overview

Visitors may witness Soft-Ray processions from quiet-rib distance. They may not witness them from the range where a stare tries to grab coherence and pocket the pulse. The rule protects serenity as a social fact. Spectacle harvesting is the breach, even when the harvester calls it awe.

## Description

Aurel-kind hosts, Soft-Ray watchers, and guests who can stand without turning the hall into a mirror all follow it. They match the breath-pulse lightly or they do not match it at all. They accept a Meridian Quiet Rib if offered and do not demand one as proof of belonging. Tourists who chase “catch the pulse,” and delegations who set a brighter lamp to help the light, break it. The consequence is not a blow. Wrong-tick unease sharpens your outline. The gate chills. Quiet ribs are withdrawn. The row completes as if the gap where you stood had always been margin. You are omitted, which is a severe hospitality and a fair one.

Mannered guests who look away on purpose are not congratulated. They are allowed to remain visitors. That is the prize, and it is enough.

## Effect

F200’s gatherings stay inhabitated rather than performed. Serenity is blocked from becoming either emptiness or a show. Coherence ethics get a field-scale twin that ordinary scenes can enact without a metaphysical trial.

## Notes

Hospitality, not a purity test and not a weapon. No ferry in the watching.
