# Exceptional crossing is not infrastructure

## Name

Exceptional crossing is not infrastructure

## Overview

**Exceptional crossing is not infrastructure** governs present-age claims about movement between frequency realms: real exceptional crossings do not establish a road, ferry, subway, or ticketed service.

**Claim grade:** present institutional protocol and technological constraint. The obligation to distinguish demonstrated capability from advertised capability persists; the absence of infrastructure is not an eternal law of physics.

**Not:** public transit between bands; grocery-tour expeditions; “understanding equals ticket.”

## Description

### Plain definition

**Infrastructure** means repeatable, publishable, ordinary logistics. Present band-crossing does not meet that standard. Cultures record genuine exceptional visitors alongside rare approaches, sealed practices, accidents, and legends; those evidence grades must remain distinct. They cannot honestly sell schedules on that basis.

### Who follows

No-Ferry Disclaimer Custom publishers; Softfruit hosts; House partial-knowledge clerks; Ember, Veil, Open Hand, and Operator cultures that warn instead of ticket.

### Who breaks

Ferry-stub sellers; bleed-as-door pamphlets; spa-weekend lumen posters; null sport tours.

### What happens when broken

Comedy (Ferry Ticket to Nowhere), seized handbills, No-Ferry Overstrike Chill, social freeze—or costly harm when dare culture meets real absence/coherence weather.

## Effect

- Protects soft limits without denying rare wonder
- Keeps atlas work from drawing subway lines between realms
- Supports saga crossings as expensive exceptions

## Notes

Future controlled crossings may be established through a later story's discoveries without retroactively supplying earlier characters with a route. A repeatable experiment would still not establish public transport, a safe return, or access to every band. See [Frequency Contact and Discovery Progression of Dias](../world/frequency-contact-and-discovery-progression-of-dias.md).

Companion to Frequency realm is not a country. Band gates (coherence, negation, reveal) explain *why* approach is hard; this rule forbids pretending hard approach is a pier.
