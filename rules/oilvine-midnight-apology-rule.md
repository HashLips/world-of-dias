# Oilvine midnight apology rule

## Name

Oilvine midnight apology rule

## Overview

After forcing a tool, joking a measure-mark into place, or promising clean undo you cannot give, **apologize in oil-dark with an Oilvine Apology Cloth before the next hard run**. Audience performance does not count.

## Description

**Who follows:** Row makers, Glassfold visitors under Row manners, Softfruit cooks after burnt pots (borrowed custom).

**Who breaks:** Hands that clamp Forced-Final and skip apology; performers who apologize for show at noon; anyone treating instruments as servants without later honesty.

**What happens:** The next bite hardens—spool, latch, chalk hinge. Community shame is quieter than a fine: benches remember. Myth already taught incomplete forgiveness on vault night; the rule still requires the attempt.

## Effect

- Makes craft manners enforceable as workshop custom.
- Pairs with scrape preference for paper and apology cloth for metal/glass.
- Does not guarantee latch success—honesty ≠ omnipotence.

## Notes

Myth: Oil Apology at Midnight. Artifact: Oilvine Apology Cloth.
