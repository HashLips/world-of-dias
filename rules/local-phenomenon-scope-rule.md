# Local phenomenon scope rule

## Name

Local phenomenon scope rule

## Overview

**Local phenomenon scope rule** says a named occurrence is a **local phenomenon** when it is a *pattern of happening*—with conditions, seasons, or triggers—rather than a durable place you can put a gate on.

**Claim grade:** law.

## Description

### Tests (any two usually suffice)

- **It recurs** under describable conditions (wrong-sky nights; timed reveal; silence hours).
- **It does not hold civic inventory** the way a town does (no mayor of a bleed; no permanent toll booth on a shadow mismatch).
- **It can visit many places** inside a realm (or bruise one place repeatedly) without *being* those places.
- **People argue about causes** more than they argue about property lines.

### Contrasts

| If it is… | Then classify as… |
| --- | --- |
| A walkable district with bakers and roofs | region / place |
| A whole vibrational layer of Dias | frequency realm |
| A bruise, window, drift, stillness, or weather-of-meaning | local phenomenon |
| A believed story about why the bruise exists | myth (may point at a phenomenon) |

### Who follows

Climate clerks, Watch officers, orchard watches, Cove Guides, House echo-filers who must keep “event” columns separate from “site” columns.

### Who breaks

Anyone selling tickets *to* a phenomenon as if it were a district; anyone building a permanent embassy to a one-hour sky stutter.

### What happens when broken

People pack for a journey that is actually a waiting weather. Money and fear move to the wrong shelf. Softfruit will pour tea and correct you kindly; Houses will stamp **nonactionable** and still misfile if the column was wrong.

## Effect

Protects the nesting hierarchy’s bottom rung. Lets wonder be described without relocating cities into the omen.

## Notes

Examples that pass the tests: **Distant lumen-abyss bleed**, **Bleed-Sky Weather**, **Unfinished edge drift**, **Quartermark Drift**. Place examples that fail the tests: **Veloria City**, **The Returning Span**, **Quiet Well**.
