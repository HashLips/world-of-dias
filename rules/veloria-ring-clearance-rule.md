# Veloria ring clearance rule

## Name

Veloria ring clearance rule

## Overview

**Veloria ring clearance rule** says movement between the city’s rings is permission-graded by role, timing, and system load. You do not walk toward the core because you are curious; you walk when your clearance matches your purpose—and when the city’s load can bear you.

## Description

Veloria is concentric. Outer rings hold markets, Softfruit, Calibration-adjacent craft, and visitor queues. Inner rings hold Operator floors, sealed House work, and core infrastructure. Clearance is stamped, spoken, or wafered depending on office—clerks and gate hands enforce the same idea: **purpose + timing + load**.

During stress windows (bleed weather, core surge, festival crush), clearance narrows automatically. Nonessential crossings defer. A visitor who was welcome at dawn may be turned at dusk without personal insult—the rule frames it as engineering. Critics hear class filter. Both readings can be true in the same hour.

Does not invent a subway between frequency bands. Ring clearance is F432 civic geography, not a ferry to F200 or F500.

## Effect

- Protects core infrastructure from chaotic surges.
- Makes belonging feel layered: who may stand where, when.
- Gives Operators and clerks a shared language for “not tonight.”
- Reinforces outsider stories that Veloria is opaque—even when the opacity is load care.
