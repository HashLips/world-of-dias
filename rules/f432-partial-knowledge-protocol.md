# F432 partial knowledge protocol

## Name

F432 partial knowledge protocol

## Overview

F432 institutions treat cross-frequency and cosmic claims as usable only when tagged by **confidence** and **source type**. Incomplete knowledge is carried forward—not deleted, not baptized as law.

## Description

Policy, trade, and safety decisions may reference rumors, ledger leaves, Softfruit talk, or Operator scrap. Records must separate:

1. **Witnessed fact** — checked, named observer or instrument.
2. **Copied claim** — someone else’s certainty, attributed.
3. **Symbolic interpretation** — meaning, myth, or taboo guess.

Unknowns stay marked. This is why the Supplemental Harmonic Ledger Leaf and Partial Harmonic Index can matter without pretending the catalog is finished—and why Calibration’s partial spectrum publish fits the same manners family.

Does not answer Frequency Zero. It explains how F432 stays governable while Zero and Distant Companies remain rumor-grade.

## Effect

- Keeps governance functional despite incomplete cosmic knowledge.
- Prevents rumor from becoming law without explicit elevation.
- Lets mystery stay productive instead of fogging every page.
