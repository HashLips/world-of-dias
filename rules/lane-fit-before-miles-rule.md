# Lane-fit before miles rule

## Name

Lane-fit before miles rule

## Overview

Waystations **fit boots before Forced-Final miles**. Wrong size means you wait—not that you march bleeding to keep a schedule.

## Description

**Who follows:** Waystation keepers, fruit-road drafts, Brindle walkers beside carts.

**Who breaks:** Drivers shoving unfit walkers onto chalk; sellers of one-size-pride boots; Houses punishing wait as laziness.

**What happens:** March delayed without shame; load-share straps unused until feet are honest; Softfruit cites body manners.

## Effect

- Makes travel courtesy bodily and checkable.
- Blocks schedule worship over flesh.
- Aligns with lane chalk and load-share ethics.
