# Dias nesting hierarchy

## Name

Dias nesting hierarchy

## Overview

**Dias nesting hierarchy** is the standing rule for how scales of place and occurrence fit together: **Dias → frequency realm → region (and finer places) → local phenomenon**. It keeps maps, ledgers, and stories from treating a weather bruise as a country, or a city as a frequency.

**Claim grade:** law.

## Description

### The stack (required reading order)

1. **Dias** — the world-container of known existence. Not a single flat map; the whole cosmos in which frequencies nest.
2. **Frequency realm** — a vibrational layer of Dias (for example **F432**, **F200**, **F500**). Realms share cosmic space but normally remain separate. Geography words work best *inside* a realm.
3. **Region and place** — lived geography *inside* a realm: continents, provinces, cities, roads, rooms. These inherit the realm’s character; they do not replace it.
4. **Local phenomenon** — a recurring event pattern that happens *in* places without becoming a place itself (bleed nights, reveal windows, silence hours, unfinished edge drift).

### What this rule forbids

- Calling a **local phenomenon** a **frequency realm**.
- Treating a **frequency realm** as an ordinary **country** you reach by road mileage alone.
- Collapsing **Dias** into **F432** (home band is not the whole container).
- Inventing a fifth “secret layer” that skips soft limits (no Frequency Zero as a mapped realm; no band subway).

### Who follows

Surveyors, Resonant House clerks, Softfruit story-tellers teaching children, gate officers writing incident forms, and anyone who must file *where* something happened without lying about *what kind of where*.

### Who breaks

Tour-map sellers who label “F500” as a harbor district; panic ledgers that promote a cold pocket into a new frequency; storytellers who relocate whole cities into another band because the sky looked wrong for an hour.

### What happens when broken

Misfiled danger, false ferries in the imagination, and policy that tries to govern a bruise as if it were a border. Correction culture exists: see **Stack Teaching Customs** and **Wrong Nest Corrections**.

## Effect

- Makes scale talk teachable in plain speech for travelers, clerks, and storytellers.
- Lets `parent_region` / `place_type` in the archive match how inhabitants argue about scale.
- Keeps wonder (bleed, echo, reveal) from erasing geography—and geography from pretending frequency is just scenery paint.
