# Delci handshare dream rule

## Name

Delci handshare dream rule

## Overview

It is said that if **Delci Beta** holds your hand while he is dreaming, you will see the **same dream**—walk the same descent, enter the same Passage Round, and witness whatever destination his logbook opens.

## Description

The rule does not claim that anyone can invent Delci's architecture. The Passage Round belongs to his inward life. Handshare only **joins a guest** to a journey already underway or about to begin.

Consent is practical rather than ceremonial: he offers a hand on the Good Trade rock; the guest accepts or declines. No handshare, no shared sight. Breaking the hold mid-dream is reported to drop the guest back to waking mountain air while Delci may continue alone.

## Effect

- Makes Delci's still hours a possible **side path** for companions such as Calen Brink, not only solitary rumor.
- Keeps dream authorship with Delci: the guest sees, but the **logbook signature** that opens the grand door remains his.

## Notes

Applies only to Delci's known method. Does not generalize to other dreamers in Dias unless later canon says so.
