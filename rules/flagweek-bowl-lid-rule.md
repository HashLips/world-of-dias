# Flagweek bowl-lid rule

## Name

Flagweek bowl-lid rule

## Overview

**Breakfast bowl lids stay on until the shared chew begins.** Removing lids to shout slogans early is a breakfast-rule violation with props.

**Who follows:** Strip cooks, parade marshals, Softfruit volunteers.

**Who breaks:** Slogan shouters; allegiance recruiters at porridge; melting-smile pin targets who demand Forced-Final cheer first.

**What happens:** Oatmeal diplomacy; lids reseated; breakfast rule and anti-forced-cheer both cited.
