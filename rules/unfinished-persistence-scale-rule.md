# Unfinished persistence scale rule

## Name

Unfinished persistence scale rule

## Overview

Map claims in **The Unfinished** must include a **persistence class**, not just a location. Position without persistence is insufficient navigation data.

## Description

Routes and structures are tagged fleeting, unstable, holding, steady, or enduring based on repeated pass confirmation. Draftkin pedagogy treats this scale as basic literacy—cousin to F960 iterative manifestation. Half-Made atlases that omit persistence are considered lies of confidence.

## Effect

- Enables useful movement in revising terrain.
- Prevents false certainty from single-pass maps.
- Makes “unfinished” a measurable craft word.

## Notes

Open question: who promotes a holding mark to enduring when politics wants a road to look permanent.
