# Velorian Basin open-ledger rule

## Name

Velorian Basin open-ledger rule

## Overview

Basin trade disputes resolve in **visible records** before private settlements are accepted. Hidden clauses are unenforceable in civic courts.

## Description

Parties may negotiate quietly, but final obligations must be logged in shared ledger formats available to ward mediators. This is why Basin commerce stays high-volume without constant collapse—and why Moon-Market walks still end at a book someone can point to. Cousin spirit to VEL Mark Board daylight: agreed numbers beat whispered traps.

## Effect

- Lowers fraud repeat rates.
- Pulls informal trust circles into formal process.
- Keeps market joy from becoming private predation.

## Notes

Open question: how “visible” a ledger must be when Houses prefer sealed shelves.
