# Residual seam non-window rule

## Name

Residual seam non-window rule

## Overview

A **residual calibration seam** must not be treated as an endless F610 reveal window. If the glass stays bright after polite witnessing should end, you **power down**, you do not **widen**.

## Description

F610 manners (and Veil Scribe timed windows) treat reveal as a glance with an end. A residual seam is what happens when high-precision multi-band work leaves a hairline of reveal-pressure inside F432 after the instrument should go opaque again.

The rule states, in craft and later in publish footnotes:

- Do not schedule research around a seam that will not close.
- Do not call forced widening a gift, a door, or “one more honest look.”
- Do not vault a nest by pretending the seam is a door you can lock from the outside without manners.

In **The Calibration Saga**, Orrin’s Spectrum Frame leaves the famous seam; anti-stare manners arrive late; the Caliburn nests in the persistence. The vault attempt fails partly because the team treats containment as metal while the seam is still a stare.

## Effect

- Keeps “helpful glass” from becoming policy for omniscience.
- Aligns Row craft with Dias soft limits (no subway, no Forced-Final clamp as spiritual victory).
- Gives Warden Brusk and F610 teachers a sentence to shout when apprentices aim instruments at the sky like porch lights.
