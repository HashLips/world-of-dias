# Softfruit anti-forced-cheer rule

## Name

Softfruit anti-forced-cheer rule

## Overview

**Forced-Final cheer is not joy.** Permanent smile stamps, helpful-glass grins filed quiet, and “be happy” clearance speeches earn melting-smile pins—not applause.

## Description

**Who follows:** Softfruit vendors, ladle courts, festival volunteers, Calibration apprentices after helpful-glass lectures.

**Who breaks:** Clerks selling permanent happiness; Houses demanding smile-as-loyalty; anyone filing living agreement as cheerful noise.

**What happens:** Melting-smile pins appear on stalls and desks; Softfruit comedy empties fake-cheer products; ask-again culture gets cited. Real pours still happen one glass at a time.

## Effect

- Protects joy literacy against fake belonging.
- Gives Softfruit a rule twin to ladle-before-stamp.
- Keeps helpful glass from being praised as manners.
