# Life inherits band habit

## Name

Life inherits band habit

## Overview

**Life inherits band habit** is the law-grade rule that living beings take on measurable costs and skills from the frequency they inhabit or approach (load, fatigue, softening, contagion, revision urge, timing, recovery pace, stare-cost).

**Claim grade:** law (inheritance of habit); observation for particular species tells; taboo for using lifeforms as ferry tickets.

**Not:** a travel method; permanent omniscience as upgrade; settled rumor-beast taxonomy.

## Description

Who follows: healers, Softfruit hosts, Ember Keepers, Veil Scribes, Draftkin makers, hearttide attendants, and road crews who watch visitor strain. Who breaks: recruiters who promise “one night in lumen and you’re upgraded”; sport parties that treat null approach as courage theater; stares sold as enlightenment.

What happens when broken: bodies show the receipt—fatigue, thinning continuity, crowd weather injury, stare-cost strain—while social manners answer with bans, scrape, and refusal.

Keeps Unraveled / Distant Companies from becoming confirmed life catalogs without deliberate plan.

## Effect

- Makes cross-band encounter costly and specific.
- Supports wonder in adaptation without omnipotence.
- Anchors later living-world species and habitat work without inventing new metaphysics.

## Notes

Pairs with Matter answers its band—bodies are matter that argues back.
