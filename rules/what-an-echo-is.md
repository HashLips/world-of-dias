# What an echo is

## Name

What an echo is

## Overview

An **echo** in Dias is a **residual imprint** left by resonance—emotional, structural, historical, habitual, or otherwise—that can persist in matter, place, tool, body, crowd, or craft long after the originating event has moved on.

**Claim grade:** law (definition of echo as residual imprint); observation for particular textures; taboo for omniscient reading-as-tool.

## Description

### Positive definition

An echo is remainder. It is what the world keeps when participation has happened. It may be faint as peel-sweet on a cloth or stubborn as a sealed House file. It may rise suddenly (Harmonic Echo-Rise) or sit quiet for years until a listener arrives with the wrong kind of attention.

Echoes are typically:

- **Partial** — never a perfect transcript
- **Situated** — hosted by places, objects, or practices without *being* those places
- **Disputable** — factions argue meaning harder than existence
- **Actionable or not** — some demand manners; some demand seals; some demand refusal

### Negative definition (what an echo is not)

- Not a full soul trapped in a spoon (unless a rare Echoform story claims otherwise—and even then, soft limits apply)
- Not automatic prophecy
- Not a subway ticket between frequencies
- Not proof that Frequency Zero is understood

### Who follows

Anyone who eats at Softfruit, files a ledger, stands a gate booth, or handles Resonant Artifacts. The rule is physics-of-meaning; customs are how cultures survive it.

### Who breaks

Those who treat echoes as total knowledge; those who erase instead of scrape; those who sell “complete readings” in markets; those who pretend a place with no felt echo has repealed the principle.

### What happens when broken

Misfiled danger, cruel certainty, and stories that skip soft limits. Softfruit corrects with food. Houses correct with stamps. Roads correct with consequences.

## Effect

Gives cosmology and daily craft a usable noun without turning Dias into omniscience magic. Anchors Resonant Artifacts and Echoforms as special cases of a general law, not alien systems.
