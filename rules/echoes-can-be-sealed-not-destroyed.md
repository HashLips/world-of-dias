# Echoes can be sealed not destroyed

## Name

Echoes can be sealed not destroyed

## Overview

In ordinary Dias practice, **sealing** an echo removes casual access; it does not prove the remainder was annihilated. Cultures that sell “total destruction” of echoes are treated as dangerous marketers or mystery claimants—not as standard physics.

**Claim grade:** law (sealing ≠ proven annihilation as ordinary expectation); observation for particular seal crafts.

## Description

### Working expectation

Houses seal. Softfruit refuses and airs. Operators triage. Open Hand leaves unfinished. Across disagreements, honest craftspeople admit sealed drawers can still feel cold, and “wiped” pages can still sour a desk when the wipe was a lie.

True annihilation—if it exists—belongs near Frequency Zero talk and soft-limit rarity, not beside lunch.

### Who follows

Seal clerks, courtesy-shelf archivists, Softfruit hosts who prefer scrape and air, makers who reset tools without pretending yesterday never touched the chisel.

### Who breaks

Wipe-sellers; panic officers who burn records and call the smoke purity; anyone who treats Zero as a household appliance.

### What happens when broken

False cleanliness, colder rooms, and later surprises that feel like betrayal by furniture.

## Effect

Protects the echo principle from being locally repealed by bureaucracy cosplay.
