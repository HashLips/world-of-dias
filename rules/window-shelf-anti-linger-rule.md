# Window shelf anti-linger rule

## Name

Window shelf anti-linger rule

## Overview

When the soft close sounds on a Glassfold shelf, the work ends. Staring to extend a window is a guild breach. The rule sharpens anti-stare manners into something a Timer can enforce with a packed slate and an elbow, not with a theology. Reveal is an interval. It is not property, and it is not a compulsion machine that owes you the truth about your neighbor.

## Description

Veil Scribes and the timing guild follow it. They pack before the second note so the packing is part of the minute. They welcome ask-again on a later window. Clarity addicts break it, apprentices most of all, because talent confuses duration with skill. The consequence is taught as the myth of the minute that would not stay: afterglow that nests, a map more confident than the seeing, escort down to tea, serpent rumors stripped back to a headache. The ledge does not hate the lingerer. It stops spending clarity on a face that will not blink. Companions who waited on the lower shelf do not laugh. They have seen that particular shame.

Structure that was shown remains what it was. It does not grow a moral about who is pure.

## Effect

Windows stay useful because they end. Greed is blocked from turning a courtesy into a possession. The guild keeps its precision and its right to walk an apprentice down.

## Notes

Field sharpening of anti-stare manners. Not a lie-detector law.
