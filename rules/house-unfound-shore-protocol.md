# House unfound-shore protocol

## Name

House unfound-shore protocol

## Overview

The House unfound-shore protocol is the Resonant Houses' old, rarely used procedure for a claim that an uncharted shore exists inside F432. It has been invoked perhaps four times in living memory. The Houses' first response is to **classify and contain the claim**, not to occupy the shore. It applies the F432 partial-knowledge manners to a geographic claim. It is not a licence to take land, and it is not a crossing rule, though it is often triggered by crossing language.

## Description

**Grades:**
- *unfound-shore, hazard pending:* claim received; source unread.
- *unfound-shore, withdrawn:* a shore was reported seen and then not found. Unclosable.
- (*unfound-shore, charted* exists in the manual. No present-age use is recorded.)

**Steps under *hazard pending*:**
1. Copies found by House clerks are stamped **copied claim, no source**.
2. **No position** for the shore is printed in any House or Concord document.
3. A public **hazard notice** is issued to calm markets and warn boats.
4. Petitions for watch (from the Ward) or for claim (from commercial backers) are **noted and deferred**.
5. An **envoy** goes to the holder of any original to **request** a loan under partial-knowledge manners: read, graded, returned.
6. If refused, the refusal is reported. A bloc may then seek a **hazard writ** for a survey-and-ward expedition.

**Who follows it:** House classification tables, their clerks and envoys, and Concord desks for summaries.
**Who breaks it, or bends it:** blocs that use a writ to turn a survey into a show of force; captains who treat "place a ward" as licence to shoot; road sellers who ignore the stamps.
**What happens when it bends:** as at the Stillmouth, a brief and costly clash, testimonies that will not agree, and a file that can be neither closed nor explained.

## Effect

The protocol buys calm and time, and it keeps the Houses legitimate. It cannot compel an original out of Wabet keeping, cannot control the Ward once a writ is issued, and cannot make different testimonies agree. In the Island Saga it ended at *unfound-shore, withdrawn*.

## Notes

The four pulls readers are trained to weigh are security, truth, legitimacy and exploitation. Clerks add a fifth informally: the person the claim is addressed to. The protocol gives no jurisdiction at sea to Concord marshals.
