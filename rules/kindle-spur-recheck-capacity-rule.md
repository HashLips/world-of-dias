# Kindle spur recheck capacity rule

## Name

Kindle spur recheck capacity rule

## Overview

A spur marked open at dusk must be rechecked before midnight capacity is treated as real. Glow and beds decide. Speeches do not. The rule is how F840 keeps recovery infrastructure from lying to the people most tempted to believe a tough story. Warmth is not forever, and a nook that will not say so is not hospitable.

## Description

Ember Keepers, Spur Rest Keepers, and horns that are willing to be unpopular follow it. They count bodies against heat. They let hounds vote with their feet. Fee-trap hosts break it by selling air. Push-through captains break it by arguing for beds the glow will not vouch for. What happens is plain: the glow absents even if show-fires remain, hounds leave the threshold, and the hospitality shame is public enough that a captain goes red and then, if the nook is healthy, stops arguing. People sleep elsewhere without being blamed. Morning does not build a monument to the argument.

The rule allows fees for broth and blankets, things that can be handed over. It refuses a price on breathing.

## Effect

Rest stays truthful. Continuity beats forced-final speeches about a hearth that is “done.” Travelers live who would have been spent on someone else’s vanity.

## Notes

Continuity ethic. A dark horn is compliance with the rule, not a failure of welcome.
