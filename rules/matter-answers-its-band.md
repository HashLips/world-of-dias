# Matter answers its band

## Name

Matter answers its band

## Overview

**Matter answers its band** is the law-grade rule that physical substance behaves according to its frequency layer (hold, glow, thin, revise, rhyme, tint, window, or recover). It does not behave the same in every band, and wishing does not override band habit.

**Claim grade:** law.

**Not:** universal ferry lumber; dead decoration; poetry without craft consequence.

## Description

Who follows: makers, bakers, harbor crews, Ember Keepers, Open Hand revisers, Softfruit teachers, and anyone who trusts a tool’s remainder. Who breaks: sellers of “universal timber” stamped for every realm; chart-painters who draw F500 as paint-dark soil; ferry fantasists who invent boarding materials from effect talk.

What happens when broken: craft fails first—boards warp wrong, landmarks starve, draft holds collapse, ash refuses monument pride. Social consequence follows: House Theory stamps, Softfruit corrections, seized wrong maps.

Does not destroy echo law. Matter’s answering *is* a face of Everything resonates; remainder still leaves after use.

## Effect

- Makes band difference tangible in workshops and kitchens.
- Grounds Resonant Artifacts without Earth-tech paste.
- Blocks “same physics, new wallpaper” storytelling.

## Notes

Open: full mineral catalogs per band remain incomplete by design. Soft limit: no ferry plank from this rule.
