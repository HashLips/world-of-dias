# Frequency realm is not a country

## Name

Frequency realm is not a country

## Overview

**Frequency realm is not a country** is the companion rule to nesting: a frequency realm is a **layer of resonance**, not a neighboring state you reach by adding road miles.

**Claim grade:** law.

## Description

### What a realm is

A frequency realm (F432, F200, F500, and the other indexed bands) is a vibrational state of Dias with its own expectations for matter, mood, memory, and possible craft. Inside it, countries, roads, and harbors can exist—as they do richly in F432.

### What a realm is not

- Not a province of F432.
- Not a harbor district labeled with an F-number for tourists.
- Not a subway stop.
- Not automatically a treaty partner of Veloria’s desks.

### Practical speech

Honest between-realm words lean toward **nearness of tone**, **bleed**, **omen**, **refusal**, and **exceptional passage**. Honest inside-realm words are **west**, **bridge**, **toll**, **orchard**, **gate**.

### Who follows

Gate officers, Resonant Houses, Softfruit mentors who stop ferry fantasies at the ladle, surveyors who refuse to draw F500 as an island east of Driftfall.

### Who breaks

Wrong-map sellers; panic pamphlets; anyone who treats understanding a band as a ticket there.

### What happens when broken

Imagined ferries, invented embassies, and stories that skip soft limits. Correction is cultural as much as clerical: see **Stack Teaching Customs**.

## Effect

Keeps cosmology talk and lived geography from contradicting each other. Protects No-Ferry ethics without forbidding wonder about other bands.

## Notes

Pairs with [`Dias nesting hierarchy`](dias-nesting-hierarchy.md) and [`Resonance behavior soft limits`](resonance-behavior-soft-limits.md). Indexed bands remain real; they simply are not countries.
