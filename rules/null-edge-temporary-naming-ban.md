# Null-edge temporary naming ban

## Name

Null-edge temporary naming ban

## Overview

In polite F432 rooms, names for null-edge places and for whatever is said to linger there stay temporary and contested. No rushed species map. No Frequency Zero solution stamped out of fear. A permanent name is a handle, and a handle is how rumor convinces itself it can be carried home in a jar. The ban is institutional manners. It is not a claim about physics.

## Description

Taboo-tech teachers, Softfruit caution comics, and Houses that dislike dock panic follow it. They say “something like a cairn, for now.” They hang a warning tag instead of printing a tour. They believe partial testimony without demanding the missing middle. Rumor merchants break it when they sell field guides to the Unraveled, when they confirm Horizon-Nulls over a drink, when they staff a boat because a listener story was vivid. What happens is social and doctrinal: the room refuses the guide, the status stays rumor or unknown, and anyone trying to close Zero is told, plainly, that the closure is not on offer. Tags replace tours. If you go anyway, you do not get to come back and upgrade the ban with your missing hours.

## Effect

Soft limits stay soft. Distant Companies, Horizon-Nulls, and the Unraveled are not promoted into confirmed taxonomy by panic or by charisma. Curiosity survives. Sport does not get a permit.

## Notes

Manners, not a solved abyss. Zero remains open on purpose.
