# Holding cove tone consent rule

## Name

Holding cove tone consent rule

## Overview

Deep tone-share in a holding cove requires cloth consent. Ease is an invitation. It is not a tool you may use to extract a confession, a concession, or a mood that makes your terms look like a gift. The rule is how F380 stays ethical weather instead of a lie detector with nicer water.

## Description

Tidehearts, Cove Tone Guides, and guests literate in the custom follow it. Cloths go down before deep talk. Intent is named: argument, mending, quiet, gathering. Exit to a quieter current is allowed and is not mocked. Interrogators in hearttide clothing break it, and so do speakers who push forced cheer because gratitude would grease a deal. When the rule breaks, ease collapses. The haze thins. Heavy current weather moves in, slate at the edges, reeds uninterested. Guides end the hold. People step to the bank and will not meet the extractor’s eyes, not from drama, from fatigue at having been used as an instrument.

The rule does not ban grief and does not ban hard talk. It bans the use of other people’s climate as a pry bar.

## Effect

Mood stays shareable. Negotiation can be smaller and truer. The cove is blocked from becoming a courtroom or a clinic you did not agree to enter.

Consent is the lawfeel. Not interrogation culture.
