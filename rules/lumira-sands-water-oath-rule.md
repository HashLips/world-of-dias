# Lumira Sands water oath rule

## Name

Lumira Sands water oath rule

## Overview

Mapped water points in **Lumira Sands** are shared survival infrastructure, not private leverage. Sabotage or death-trap concealment of verified potable nodes is among the sands’ worst offenses.

## Description

Guides and Keepers may control **when** routes are disclosed (map-wrongness cousins), but once a node is verified potable it cannot be sold as a trap or ruined for rivals. Enforcement is mostly reputational—and reputational penalties are existential. The Oath of Uncut Water is the myth face.

## Effect

- Sustains uneasy coexistence among rival groups.
- Separates strategic secrecy from murder-by-thirst.
- Keeps oasis ethics harder than market charts.

## Notes

Open question: who verifies “potable” when silence law and Keep assent disagree.
