# Resonance behavior soft limits

## Name

Resonance behavior soft limits

## Overview

Resonance and frequency explain wonder in Dias, but they do not grant omnipotence on demand. These soft limits keep the cosmos consistent enough for trust and broad enough for discovery.

## Description

The following limits are **working expectations**—strong enough that stories and institutions can rely on them, soft enough that rare exceptions remain meaningful mysteries rather than routine tools.

1. **Partial echoes** — Echoes can be read, misread, sealed, or refused. No craft reliably yields total knowledge of a person, place, or age.
2. **Exceptional crossing** — Moving between frequency realms is not ordinary logistics. When it occurs, cultures treat it as omen, sealed practice, accident, or legend—not commuting.
3. **No local repeal** — A zone that seems not to resonate is an event to investigate. It is not a standard switch operators flip for convenience.
4. **Realm character holds** — Tools and bleed can lean a place toward another band’s mood; they do not casually convert F432 into F200, or F840 into F500, as a completed rewrite.
5. **Zero stays unfinished** — Frequency Zero may be named, feared, filed, or refused. It is not solved as ordinary plot inventory.

Exceptions, if any, should feel costly, disputed, and rare—and should deepen mystery rather than erase the rulebook.

## Effect

- Protects Dias from “anything can happen” magic while preserving awe.
- Gives factions something to argue about: how close to a limit one may push.
- Supports the unsettled layer (bleed, null curiosity, revision hunger) without requiring instant apocalypse.
