# Memory is band-shaped

## Name

Memory is band-shaped

## Overview

**Memory is band-shaped** is the law-grade rule that recollection—personal, civic, and echo-held—changes form by frequency: stick, rhyme, thin, window, scar, or revise.

**Claim grade:** law for band-shaping; observation for kitchen and ledger tells; taboo for omnipotent archive fantasies.

**Not:** total surveillance; perfect transcripts on demand; a ferry made of remembering harder.

## Description

Who follows: clerks who scrape instead of erase, Softfruit hosts who trust table warmth, Veil Scribes with phase stamps, Ember Keepers who name cold gaps, F120 lab drift loggers. Who breaks: Forced-Final speeches that delete scar; sellers of “perfect total recall” devices; travelers who treat near-return as a rewind ferry.

What happens when broken: false certainty, social harm, and often a thinner true remainder than before—echoes seal and sour more easily when manners fail.

Personal memory and world-echo braid but do not collapse into surveillance. Resonance behavior soft limits still bind.

## Effect

- Explains why the same event feels differently kept across bands.
- Protects mystery without denying craft.
- Gives investigators band-aware evidence manners.
