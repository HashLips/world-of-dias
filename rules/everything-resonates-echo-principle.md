# Everything resonates echo principle

## Name

Everything resonates echo principle

## Overview

The foundational law of Dias is simple and total: **everything resonates; everything leaves an echo.**

**Claim grade:** law.

## Description

Resonance is not a rare talent bolted onto an otherwise ordinary world. It is how reality behaves. Matter, places, tools, bodies, crowds, and long habits can hold vibrational imprint. An **echo** is residual impression—emotional, structural, historical, or otherwise—left by that resonance.

Echoes are often **partial**. They may be disputed, misread, sealed, or ignored. The principle does not require that every echo be legible, useful, or safe.

Civilizations develop technologies, rites, professions, and conflicts from this same law: reading echoes, preserving them, classifying them, silencing them, or refusing to listen.

### Lived proof (ordinary scale)

The law is easiest to trust where it is humble. Softfruit tables keep laughter residue. Gate booths keep argument-stain. Fruit roads keep gratitude marks in dust. Dough remembers kneading mood. Tools lean toward yesterday’s motion. Ledgers go cold where seals bite down. Songs drift because prior singings remain in the beams.

These are not separate magics. They are the same sentence spoken in kitchens, booths, orchards, shops, and copy rooms: everything resonates; everything leaves an echo.

### Craft and limit

Reading remainder is craft (see Reading echoes is craft). Soft limits forbid casual omniscience and local repeal. Sealing hides access more often than it annihilates remainder (see Echoes can be sealed not destroyed). Frequency Zero remains the contested name for absence—not a household wipe switch.

Plain-speech orientation: Resonance and Echo of Dias. Vocabulary: Echo Vocabulary of Dias. Definitions: What an echo is; Why everything leaves an echo.

## Effect

- Grounds frequency realms, harmonic echoes, Prime Relics, and related phenomena under one identity.
- Allows unlimited story expansion without inventing a new metaphysics each time.
- Keeps exceptions meaningful: something that seems not to resonate is a mystery, not a default.
- Lets ordinary life teach the law before saga pressure arrives.
