# Brindle three-thank-yous rule

## Name

Brindle three-thank-yous rule

## Overview

After unclipping a load-share strap, give **three thank-you taps**. Silent dumps onto poorer carts void Brindle goodwill faster than a toll fine.

## Description

**Who follows:** Brindle drivers, Ashen teams borrowing march manners, fruit-road wagons.

**Who breaks:** Carts that dump weight without taps; Forced-Final haulers; anyone treating oxen as disposable engines.

**What happens:** Strap beads stay cold next share; line-ox handlers refuse; three-thank-yous myth recited.

## Effect

- Makes load-sharing emotionally checkable.
- Protects animal and neighbor dignity.
- Aligns myth with march law.

## Notes

Myth: The Three Thank-Yous of Brindle. Artifact: Brindle Load-Share Strap.
