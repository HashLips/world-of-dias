# Frequency as fundamental law

## Name

Frequency as fundamental law

## Overview

In Dias, **frequency** is a fundamental law of reality: different layers, beings, tools, and places can resonate differently, with consequences for what is possible there.

## Description

Unusual abilities, environments, and technologies are not treated as unexplained magic. They are framed as **resonance behavior**—sometimes well mapped, sometimes barely named.

Frequency may influence abilities, perception, communication, technology, environments, creatures, artifacts, social classes, factions, professions, belief systems, and historical events.

Designations such as **F432**, **F200**, **F500**, and **F960** are working classifications used by travelers, scholars, and institutions. Familiarity with a designation should eventually imply some expectations (stability, luminosity, null pressure, unfinished revision, and so on)—without pretending the catalog is complete.

The system must remain:

- **consistent enough** that stories can rely on rules
- **broad enough** that discoveries, contradictions, and exceptions stay possible

## Effect

- Makes frequency realms feel like physics with culture, not costume changes.
- Lets institutions fight over classification (as Resonant Houses already do) without inventing new metaphysics.
- Keeps Frequency Zero meaningful as the theorized outside or absence of the system.
