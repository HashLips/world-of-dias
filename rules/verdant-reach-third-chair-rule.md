# Verdant Reach third-chair rule

## Name

Verdant Reach third-chair rule

## Overview

Formal mediation meals in **Verdant Reach** are set with one **unassigned seat**—the third chair—for return possibility.

## Description

The empty chair represents absent stakeholders who might still come back to the table. Agreements finalized without offering this seat can be contested as procedurally incomplete. Practical, not merely sentimental: it creates recurring opportunity windows. The Third-Chair Breakfast story teaches the custom; the tall red chair in local art jokes about how large the invitation can feel.

## Effect

- Keeps disputes from hardening into permanent exclusion.
- Embeds reconciliation into ordinary breakfast craft.
- Gives household governance a visible open question.

## Notes

Artwork: Third Chair. Open question: how long the chair may stay empty before the meal is allowed to decide without the missing voice.
