# Heartlean Colorstone

## Name

Heartlean Colorstone

## Overview

The **Heartlean Colorstone** is a palm-smooth stone kept at Farstock, not sold. While it is held, it takes the color of the person holding it, a Hearttide lean — mood as weather in the hand, not a feeling you can buy and keep.

## Description

River-gray when it sits on shelf-quiet cloth. In a palm it warms and shifts: amber, chalk, workshop blue, whatever the holder is carrying that hour. The shift is visible to other people, which is why children dare each other to touch it. Set down with consent, it fades back toward gray over a few breaths, as if the color remembered it had a place. Forced cheer makes the surface look right and feel brittle, kin to a Hearttide Holding Cloth used as a pry. Stolen — taken off the cloth, off the hook, out of the keeper’s refusal — the color does not fade. It stands up.

## Cultural Meaning

Outer-ring gossip calls it a mood-stone and then argues, because no such object had a file in the city’s polite lists. Farstock never used that name. Pim’s refusal rests on a sentence he keeps on a card and does not pretend to have invented: ease you can purchase is the brittle kind. The stone is not ease. It is a lean. Owning the lean is the mistake.

## Usage

Kept in a wooden case lined with Shelf-Quiet Cloth, often with a Refusal Ribbon on the hook rather than on the stone. Visitors may be allowed to hold it over the counter. They may not take it. After the Farstock Saga it is kept again. It is not a map, not a ferry token, and not a tool for reaching F500.

## Notes

The Lean Echo is what the stolen color became. Whether that being knew a way remains unknown. The stone, back in the case, does not explain.
