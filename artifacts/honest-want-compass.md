# Honest-Want Compass

## Name

Honest-Want Compass

## Overview

An **Honest-Want Compass** swings once, toward the last thing the holder has admitted they want, and then sticks. It will not swing toward a frequency number, no matter the shaking.

## Description

Brass case, a needle that behaves until a true want is spoken or clearly felt, then turns — often toward a person, a stall, a bed, a door already known — and locks with a soft tick. Lies make it shiver and refuse. Asked for F500, F200, a ferry, or “the wonderful place,” it stays on the human want underneath, or it stays still if the holder will not admit one. It is embarrassing in a shop, which is why it lives in a drawer until someone insists.

## Cultural Meaning

A small civic cruelty against people who dress appetite up as destiny. The compass is not wise. It is literal, and literal is sometimes kinder than a guide.

## Usage

Rarely sold. Sometimes shown, over the counter, to a customer who says they want “whatever it is.” Joss Merrow was not shown it. He might have hated it, which is not proof he should have been given the stone instead.

## Notes

Not a road instrument. Cannot be corrected into a band-finder. If it ever points south by itself, treat that account as rumor until the needle is in the room.
