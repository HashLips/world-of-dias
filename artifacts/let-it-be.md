# Let It Be

## Name

Let It Be

## Overview

**Let It Be** is an F380 shore assembly set: eleven black peg-markers with bulbous spade heads sized for a V formation on tan sand cloth or real shore, used during large-sun watches when the rule is to stand and not rearrange.

## Description

Markers plant point-down. Stewards place the largest at the V tip facing inland or toward watchers as in the plate. Blue cloth bands can stand in for sea when drills are inland. Children learn that latecomers take rear small pegs. Adults who shove the V for photos replace the whole set’s tips. The name is local Dias talk for unforced presence—not an imported lyric.

## Cultural Meaning

In F380, Let It Be is permitted stillness at the water edge. Resonance favors intact Vs. Forcing speech mid-sun empties the set.

## Usage

Coastal watches, teaching sands, and quiet festivals. Pegs counted; sand cloth folded.

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Dias name only.
