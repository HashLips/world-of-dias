# Modern Times

## Name

Modern Times

## Overview

**Modern Times** is a Hollowmere wall-port kit: a dark green panel with circular aperture and extendable thin limb, a blue rest bench, and an orange-red floor ribbon marking the approach path, used for careful almost-touch transfers without slamming contact.

## Description

Limb tips are soft-capped. Benches hold the arched rest of the plate. Ribbons keep feet off the orange block weight until transfer ends. Children watch through a teaching gap. Adults who grab the limb early reset the port and walk the ribbon again. Name stays local Dias talk for the interface—not an imported story title.

## Cultural Meaning

In Hollowmere, Modern Times is patient interface. Resonance favors the almost-touch. Treating the port as a toy empties the kit.

## Usage

Loft-side rooms, quiet transfers, and apprentice drills. Caps replaced; ribbons straightened.
