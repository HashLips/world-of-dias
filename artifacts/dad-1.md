# Dad

## Name

Dad

## Overview

**Dad** is a kept Driftfall guidance kit: a blue bordered looking-orb on a soft white tether, used when a taller walker shares a glimpse with a smaller one without deciding their next step for them.

## Description

The loud piece is the bright blue circle in a thick irregular white border, meant to be held or hung so a white tether can point toward the learner without wrapping the throat. Pier hosts pair it with a simple hand-strap reminder: contact first, vision second. Red bird tokens sometimes pin nearby as witness marks. The kit is not a throne and not a title badge. Anyone taller in the moment may carry it; anyone smaller may refuse the tether. Selling it as a “father brand” emptied of teaching is bad pier craft. The artifact’s job is shared looking with an open mouth for questions.

## Cultural Meaning

Driftfall treats guidance as a handhold, not a ownership stamp.

## Usage

Pier walks, guest child tours, and apprentice first-looks. Lent with the tether unknotted.

## Notes

Guidance kit. Offer the blue look; keep the questions free.
