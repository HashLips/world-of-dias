# Echo Metaphor Chapbook

## Name

Echo Metaphor Chapbook

## Overview

A small stitched book of approved folk metaphors for resonance and echo, with warnings in the margins about metaphors that smuggle ferry fantasies or total readings.

## Description

Softfruit stock and Averra press both print versions. Pages include second taste, kind dust, sulking booth, scraped page, wet paint—each with a one-scene story and a “do not mean” line. Children color the Resonate Ring Marks; adults dog-ear the Partial Echo Slash page.

## Cultural Meaning

Shared speech reduces accidental cruelty. It also reduces wrong-map poetry being mistaken for logistics.

## Usage

Gifted to new Softfruit hosts, gate apprentices, and travelers who ask honest questions after bleed nights.

## Notes

Not scripture. If a metaphor stops helping, Softfruit revises the chapbook rather than declaring the cosmos wrong.
