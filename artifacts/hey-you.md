# Hey You

## Name

Hey You

## Overview

**Hey You** is an Averra Isle perch-cloth and call name: a segmented ground-cloth marked with a pink light-circle, laid so tall quiet bodies can stand as bird perches while a chosen listener answers a long-legged bird’s lean.

## Description

The cloth shows pink-orange-yellow segments and a painted or dyed pink disc. Studio and yard versions differ in weave; isle versions smell faintly of damp plaster and feather oil. People stand in dark clothes as living posts. Gold and white stilt-birds arrive when they will. The large purple bird of the plate is the serious caller—height nearly matching a standing listener. When it leans beak-close, the named person answers without stepping out of the circle’s stillness. Averra damp-studio apprentices practice the soft answer first with cloth birds on poles, then with living stilts when invited. A Hey You cloth folded wrong—pink disc creased through the center—is considered a muddled call.

## Cultural Meaning

On Averra Isle, “hey you” is not casual heckling; it is the bird’s selection and the person’s duty to reply without turning the navy circle into a chase. Resonance prefers consent: you may decline by bowing out before the lean, not after.

## Usage

Isle gatherings, studio warm-ups, and rare pier welcomes when stilt-birds travel with guests. Stored dry, aired after damp nights.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Artifact kept as cloth/call practice matching the plate’s circle.
