# Make It Yours

## Name

Make It Yours

## Overview

**Make It Yours** is a Lumira Sands stage-mark set: five sash tags or floor marks for stand, sprout, lunge, mid-flight, and full bird, used so a learner cannot claim finished flight without walking each dark silhouette.

## Description

Marks lay on light grey cloth under mustard banners. Wing-sprout drills use soft crescent pads strapped to the back. Shadow checks confirm feet still touch until the fourth mark. Children hop the first three only. Adults who pin the fifth bird mark on day one remove it and restart at stand. Sand wind is part of the lesson—hold the lunge until grit settles.

## Cultural Meaning

Around Lumira Sands, Make It Yours means earned metamorphosis. Resonance favors unbroken sequences. Selling “instant bird” shortcuts empties the set into fraud.

## Usage

Teaching yards, festival becoming-walks, and apprenticeship days. Sashes counted; pads aired.

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Matches five-stage flight plate.
