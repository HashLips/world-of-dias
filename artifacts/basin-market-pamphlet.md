# Basin Market Pamphlet

## Overview

A **Basin Market Pamphlet** sells weather as opportunity—bruise timing, stall rights, fair jokes—opinion-fast paper that still empties purses.

## Description

Bright scrap; dye-flower ink; cartoons of moths and toll lizards. Prices expire faster than flags on Flagweek Strip. Collectors paste them under crates as mood archaeology.

## Cultural Meaning

Market theater you can fold. Not Salt Ledger truth.

## Usage

Investigation: who wanted a crowd where; false bruise panics; comic dread that became real dread.
