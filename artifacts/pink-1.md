# Pink

## Name

Pink

## Overview

**Pink** is Averra Isle head-board craft: a salmon-pink abstracted profile on charcoal, large eye made of intersecting black arcs, beak-like taper, and teardrop marks—quiet looking kept as a hung reminder.

## Description

Isle damp studios and hearthline walls keep Pink as a painted or cut board. The salmon mass is rounded at the crown and thick through the neck; the eye is the first read—two black arcs crossing with a small pupil, large enough to slow a room. Mouth stays two short parallel curves; the face ends in a sharp soft point that locals refuse to treat as a weapon shape. A teardrop at the ear place and a sweeping black neck-line complete the usual board. People hang Pink when a studio needs quieter looking—less chatter, more honest down-left attention. Resonance is the pink/void contrast; memory is the arc eye; wonder is the beak taper that stays gentle. Boards return without scratched pupils. Soft talk that paints Pink as a mascot for loud parties is considered a misuse. The craft is a face for care, not a mask for hiding cruelty.

## Cultural Meaning

On Averra Isle, Pink means attentive quiet. The large eye is the rule.

## Usage

Studio boards, guest-hall reminders, and private readiness walls. Not a chase banner.

Named for the salmon head-craft. Pair with the artwork plate in assets.
