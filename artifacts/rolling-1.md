# Rolling

## Name

Rolling

## Overview

**Rolling** is Songripe Isles arch-stretch craft: a white elongated practice silhouette in extreme backbend with head low between pillar legs and one long arm opening four tapering fingers on navy, blue shadows kept clean.

## Description

Isle quiet rooms keep Rolling as a board and a taught posture name. The deep arch is the work; the splayed four fingers are the finish tell; the three-toothed foot mark appears in careful copies. Navy practice cloths make the white line loud. Local care forbids shoving travelers into the roll for entertainment, and forbids calling it done when the head-lift cheats the between-leg tuck. Resonance is stretch against void; memory is the upward finger fan; wonder is the hard blue shadow. Boards return uncreased. Soft talk that treats Rolling as empty showing-off misses the isle rule: the roll is honest tension, then rest.

## Cultural Meaning

On Songripe Isles, rolling means the deep arch practiced with clean shadows.

## Usage

Quiet practice rooms and readiness boards. Not festival contortion for bets.
