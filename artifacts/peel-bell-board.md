# Peel Bell Board

## Name

Peel Bell Board

## Overview

The Peel Bell Board is the slate in The Peel and Bell where names, debts, and places get written when someone is willing to say them in front of the room.

## Description

A dark stone slab in a fruitwood frame, hung at standing eye-height beside the bell. Chalk lives in a cup that also collects peel-grit. The writing is the same family as Span Toll tallies: blunt, abbreviated, hard to romanticize. Bram Keel wipes what has been paid. He does not wipe what has been located until the person who chalked it asks, and sometimes not then, if the room has already stood up.

## Cultural Meaning

Outer-ring law without a marshal's cord. If it is on the board, it is no longer a hull secret. People trust it more than a speech because chalk can be checked by a stranger who just wanted a drink.

## Usage

Lark Venn used it to mark where the Vitrin tree stands, after she would not fly the last fruit out as a private hire. The board did the publishing. The bar did the witnessing. What the wagons did afterward was no longer up to the pilot.

## Notes

Not a magical map. Chalk and witness. The cost of writing a place where carters drink is that carters can go.
