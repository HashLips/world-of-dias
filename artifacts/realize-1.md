# Realize

## Name

Realize

## Overview

**Realize** is an F200 arch-reveal board: three tan walkers on a dark brown arch before an orange-red sun, long jagged cyan ribbons falling from their feet to uncover a jagged white colossal face on dark teal.

## Description

Realm teaching walls keep Realize as a board for the moment projection becomes admission. The upper walk looks ordinary—tiny blue eye-dots, left-to-right pace, settlement blocks on the arch. The craft's lesson is the cyan fall: each step drops a bright jagged ribbon until the white fragmented face below can no longer be denied. A vertical brown line may bisect the lower telling as a measure cut. Local care forbids covering the white face to keep the walk "pretty," and forbids using the board to terrify children without the care talk. Resonance is walk to ribbon to face; memory is the orange semicircle; wonder is how much teal can hold. Boards travel between F200 halls and return with cyan unmuddied. Soft talk that treats realize as a private ego win misses the realm rule: the craft is shared noticing of what the walk drops.

## Cultural Meaning

In F200, realize means admitting the lower face your walk already projects.

## Usage

Teaching boards and readiness halls. Not a scare prop for empty cruelty.
