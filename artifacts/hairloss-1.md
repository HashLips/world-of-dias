# Hairloss

## Name

Hairloss

## Overview

**Hairloss** is a kept F840 transition token: a split-face portrait card used when someone sheds a cover and needs both weathers of the change kept visible and dignified.

## Description

The card shows cream/olive face and dual tall hat against olive-dash and burnt orange. Hosts place it during quiet change talks—after illness, after role-shed, after a loud style ends—without Earth clinical mockery. The name is local and careful. Selling it as a joke about baldness empties the craft. Frequency manners: speak to both halves; refuse one-side-only flattery.

## Cultural Meaning

F840 treats shedding as a readable season, not a punchline.

## Usage

Quiet change circles, guest care after loss of a role-mark, and apprentice transition drills. Lent with soft talk rules spoken once.
