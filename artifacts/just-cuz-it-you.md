# Just Cuz It You

## Name

Just Cuz It You

## Overview

**Just Cuz It You** is a Claimscar Yard wrap: a black cloth or leather mantle shaped to cradle a white capsule token, often marked with a blue band seal, kept for one named person rather than general checkout.

## Description

Capsules may hold a scrap of writing, a soft resonance bead, or a cookfire favor chip—always small enough to nest in the white void of the plate’s design. The mantle’s calligraphic cut is stitched so edges look swept. Blue band tape or ribbon crosses the wrap when the holder is present in the yard. Secondfire circles respect the seal; breaking it to snoop is a known disgrace. Children receive practice capsules with seeds, not secrets.

## Cultural Meaning

Around Claimscar Yard, the phrase means particular care without ranking the whole yard. Resonance sticks to honest naming. Borrowing a sealed mantle “for fun” is not fun.

## Usage

Personal tokens at cookfire circles, grief holds, and apprenticeship favors. Stored dry; seals replaced when frayed.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Matches mantle-and-capsule care.
