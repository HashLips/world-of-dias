# Pure

## Name

Pure

## Overview

**Pure** is Aelwyn Canopy bowl-pedestal craft: a tall blue four-legged stand, a wide clear bowl, one orange fish kept inside, and the teaching that floor echo-shapes may scatter without being forced into the glass.

## Description

Canopy rooms keep Pure as a quiet set. The pedestal's blue legs are long and thin so the bowl sits honestly above the sandy practice floor. The bowl stays wide-mouthed and clear; the single orange fish is the living measure—or a craft fish used in drills when a live one is not lent. Five flat orange silhouettes may be laid on the floor in mixed directions as echo-tokens, never scooped up to fake a fuller bowl. Local care says the pure hour is the one fish kept distinct, not a denial that echoes exist. Resonance is height between bowl and floor; memory is the notched orange tail; wonder is the brown wall's stillness. Sets return with glass uncracked and legs true. Soft talk that pours all echoes into the bowl for show is considered a break of canopy care.

## Cultural Meaning

Under Aelwyn Canopy, pure means one clear hold above acknowledged echoes.

## Usage

Quiet rooms, teaching sets, and seasonal path displays. Not a market trick bowl.

## Notes

Named for the single held measure. Pair with the artwork plate in assets.
