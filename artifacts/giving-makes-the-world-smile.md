# Giving Makes the World Smile

## Name

Giving Makes the World Smile

## Overview

**Giving Makes the World Smile** is a kept Veloria City gift set: bright blue ribbon strips used when a taller and smaller pair practice giving that leaves a pale light-path instead of a boast-shadow.

## Description

Ribbons are vivid blue and cut to hand-length. Hosts coach a pass from tall to small (or reverse) while watching the “shadow”: if the giver’s talk darkens the room, restart. City talk treats the pale blue path in the plate as manners metaphor made visible. Selling ribbons as smile-brand merch without the soft-exchange rule empties the craft. Dias name only.

## Cultural Meaning

Veloria City measures gifts by the path they leave.

## Usage

Guest thank-yous, children’s first gifts, and apology offerings. Lent as a ribbon bundle.

## Notes

Blue ribbon gift set. Give light; refuse boast-shadow.
