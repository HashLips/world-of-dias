# Wired Bloom Markers

## Name

Wired Bloom Markers

## Overview

**Wired Bloom Markers** are Wabet’s resonant harvest tags—star-petaled blossoms with bright centers, threaded to a cradle by thin blue lines that remember a restoration mend. Mythic everyday tech: proof you owed the land something back before you picked.

## Description

Not phones. Not app stores. Each bloom can be pressed to a channel repair, seed return, or labor exchange; the thread hums like a softfruit keep-mark or pot-signal string. Cradles travel with orchard crews; selling blooms as “magic flowers” is a fruit-road misunderstanding Elyra’s people correct patiently, then firmly.

## Cultural Meaning

Open Hand ethic made portable. Access to trees often waits on a warmed bloom. Null buyers who want the song inside the thread are refused—understanding is not a ticket, and neither is a harvest tag.

## Usage

Tagged to mends; checked before major picks; cited in reciprocity disputes; never bottled like Blue Litter (different hazard family).
