# Gate Officer Nest Phrase Strip

## Name

Gate Officer Nest Phrase Strip

## Overview

A short cloth strip pinned inside gate booths listing approved correction phrases for travelers who announce journeys into other frequencies by ordinary road.

## Description

Indigo cloth, white stitch, slightly greasy from glove thumbs. Top line always: *You are going to a place. The sky may argue. That is not the same journey.* Additional lines rotate by season and panic pamphlet fashion.

## Cultural Meaning

Keeps soft limits polite. Officers who invent crueler jokes are sent to Softfruit to relearn tone.

## Usage

Spoken aloud, never shouted. Copied into incident margins when a traveler insists F-numbers are destinations like towns.

## Notes

Does not replace actual gate law. It protects cosmology from becoming a screaming match.
