# Outlook

## Name

Outlook

## Overview

**Outlook** is a Sorel path-board craft: terracotta field, turquoise yellow-edged way, three yellow-eyed open-mouthed figures, and a taller thin white-eyed watcher at the far end—distance kept as local care.

## Description

Sorel workshops paint or cut Outlook as a teaching board hung near road forks and guest halls. The trio's yellow eye-slits and open mouths stand for shared talk that can tip into chant; the needle-thin figure's white eye stands for a different watch that must not be shouted into sameness. The turquoise path with yellow borders is the actual manners line: you may walk it, but you do not drag the far watcher into the cluster for comfort. A white beam from the tall feet and a small black bird on jagged white scrap often appear in the full plate as weather of the hour. People borrow the board when a yard is arguing about whether everyone must face the same way. Resonance is the path between mouth-chorus and lone eye; memory is how children learn the two eye colors mean two honest jobs; wonder is the huge yellow disc that warms both ends without erasing the gap. Returned boards should keep the yellow path-edges bright and the thin figure unsmudged. Soft talk that paints the tall one as enemy is considered a misuse of Outlook.

## Cultural Meaning

In Sorel, outlook means holding distance without contempt. Different eyes can share one turquoise way.

## Usage

Teaching boards and fork markers; sometimes carried in road briefings. Not used as a weapon-banner.

## Notes

Named for the kept distance. Pair with the artwork plate in assets.
