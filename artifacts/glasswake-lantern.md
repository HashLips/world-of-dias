# Glasswake Lantern

## Name

Glasswake Lantern

## Overview

The **Glasswake Lantern** is an ancient Prime Age maritime signal lantern, fused into coastal rock near Lumira Sands. Locals call it the **Lantern in the Stone**. It is not a working paraffin lamp in the ordinary sense: it has no usable fuel store and no reliable flame, yet it sometimes emits a pale blue-white pulse and, during stronger nearby resonance, shipboard sounds. It is also not confirmed proof of where the vessel *Glasswake* now rests—only a durable clue object whose strongest readings remain disputed.

## Description

Visually it reads as a heavily weathered brass or copper paraffin-style ship lamp: thick glass, protective metal ribs, a carrying handle, salt corrosion, and mineral buildup. The name **GLASSWAKE** is engraved into the housing. The lantern once belonged to the Prime Age vessel *Glasswake*, lost at sea during the Fracture in waters near the coast that later became part of Lumira Sands in Wabet.

The fusion with dark coastal rock is physical, not a jam or later mounting. Stone passes through parts of the metal housing. Mineral growth surrounds the frame. Sections of the lamp appear to have resolved into the rock as reality separated into different resonant states. The object therefore spans several ages: made and used aboard ship in the late Prime Age, transformed during the Fracture, recorded and storied in Post-Fracture local memory, and still present in the Present Age at **Glasswake Rock**.

Occasional pale blue-white pulses occur without visible flame or fuel. Stronger resonance nearby may carry fragments of shipboard sound—water on a hull, strained timber, rope, footsteps, a distant bell. Those behaviors are described under **Glasswake Pulse**. Local stories of a faint captain-figure reaching for the lantern are filed under **The Captain Who Reaches** and remain unresolved as ghost, echo, or unfinished-event fragment.

## Cultural Meaning

Under Lumira Silence Law and Silent Dune Keep practice, the site is approached quietly. The lantern is treated as a wound-marker and a listening object, not a trophy to pry free. Dunewalkers and coastal walkers use the local name Lantern in the Stone when teaching children not to shout at strange light. Scholars classify it among **Prime Relics** and **Resonant Artifacts**; Keepers care more that visitors do not advertise the rock as a spectacle well.

Some researchers report that the lantern’s strongest pulses appear to align with the *Glasswake*’s final heading. That claim is useful as a future investigation hook and is **not** confirmed here as a solved bearing to the vessel’s fate.

## Usage

Not lit as ordinary ship gear. Studied in place, sketched, listened to during resonance weather, and left seated in the stone. Removal attempts are refused by Keep courtesy and by the fusion itself: the lamp and rock are one object now. Pulse timing and sound scraps may be logged; they do not grant safe passage, wishes, or a finished map of the lost ship.
