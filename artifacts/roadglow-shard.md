# Roadglow Shard

## Name

Roadglow Shard

## Overview

The **Roadglow Shard** is a palm-sized Resonant Shard with a dark outer break and an interior that glows electric blue. Calen Brink found it on the dirt approach to Veloria. She later gifted it to **Frederick Lumens** on a Driftfall dock. In Stillhollow, Frederick traded it into **the Still Pot**; as a solid carried object it no longer exists—its matter and blue resonance were compounded into Vorrith’s life-broth and Frederick’s later **Apple Touch**.

## Description

It is not scrap rail and not a teaching sample. The stone feels denser than its size, warm at the fracture lines, and bright enough in shade to draw eyes that know what they are seeing. Echo-sensitive hands hear layered pressure rather than a single memory: road dust, distant city hum, and something older that refuses a clean label.

It is distinct from the **Basin Rail Shard**. That piece teaches ordinary market echo. Roadglow sits on the rare end of the same class—luminous blue matter that keeps frequency hard enough for strangers to recognize on sight.

## Cultural Meaning

Among shore crews and road traders, a True Blue is not a toy. To hold one openly is to invite theft, awe, or both. Campfire tellings say such shards once marked paths between bands; Present Age practice treats that as hungry folklore—and still steps carefully around the light.

After the Stillhollow compounding, Wabet rumor treats Roadglow as the light that "lit the hollow"—a spent beacon more than a recoverable prize.

## Usage

Carried. Hidden. Shown once too often. Gifted under insistence. Stolen. Reclaimed. Traded into the Still Pot.

Calen kept it through the Harmonic years as a private companion from her first approach. Frederick received it as thanks and challenge, then surrendered it for Giftbroth Change. Visual memory remains in artwork; physical shard does not.
