# Skeletal Show

## Name

Skeletal Show

## Overview

**Skeletal Show** is Calibration Row structure-globe craft: a medium-blue teaching figure marked with thick black skeletal lines, wide black hat, and a thin staff tipped with a light-blue globe bearing dark-green marks—structure shown honestly on pink ground.

## Description

Row halls keep the craft for lessons when measures must show their insides. Black ribs and limb lines stay visible in careful copies; the white almond eye and wide pointed hat are recognition. The globe's dark-green organic marks are the weather or map sample you present without claiming ownership of a whole world. Local care forbids painting over the bones to look "complete," and forbids waving the globe as a threat. Resonance is shown structure under yellow crescent weather; memory is the green puddle and long shadow; wonder is pink ground under such quiet monument. Sets return with globe marks unsmudged. Soft talk that treats the show as a haunted joke misses the Row rule: skeletal means honest frame.

## Cultural Meaning

On Calibration Row, skeletal show means presenting structure and globe marks without disguise.
