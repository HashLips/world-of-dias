# Tricky Mind

## Name

Tricky Mind

## Overview

A **Tricky Mind** is a head-shaped vase made by Verdant Reach potters and given to guests along with white star flowers. The guest arranges the bouquet; the host looks at the shadow the vase throws on the wall.

## Description

The vases are glazed a warm golden yellow, shaped as a profile head with a long neck, a narrow eye ringed in red, small red lips, and a flat open crown. The star flowers that go with them are common in the Reach's fields—white, spiky, black-centered, on stiff branching stems—and they look dramatic no matter how they are arranged. Hosts set the finished vase on a sill near a lamp, so a large plain shadow of the head falls on the wall behind it. The shadow never shows flowers clearly, only the head's outline.

## Cultural Meaning

Reach hospitality is generous but observant. The bouquet is the self a guest chooses to show; the shadow is the self that comes in regardless. Hosts never praise or criticize an arrangement, which would be rude. They simply notice whether the guest seems at ease with their shadow on the wall. A guest who turns the vase so the shadow falls out of sight is assumed to need quiet.

## Usage

Given on arrival, kept on the sill during a stay, returned empty on departure. The flowers are left at the field's edge, not thrown away.
