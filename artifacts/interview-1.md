# Interview

## Name

Interview

## Overview

**Interview** is an Ashcalm Rise consult set: a small black one-legged stool for the speaker, a long black desk bar for the recorder, and a pot of thin write-tools, used so talks stay seated, written, and free of performative bullying.

## Description

The stool’s single leg teaches balance—fidgeting shows. The desk is uncluttered; only the active tool leaves the pot. Speakers may wear bright cloth so their presence reads like the plate’s orange; recorders stay dark-clad to keep the page primary. Window outlines in Rise sheds remind both parties the talk is held in, not broadcast. Children practice with chalk before ink. Adults who slam tools mid-answer replace the pot’s whole set as apology.

## Cultural Meaning

On Ashcalm Rise, Interview means care through record, not trap. Resonance prefers notes that the speaker can hear read back. Hiding the pot to force memory-only talks is considered sharp practice.

## Usage

Load disputes, craft apprenticeships, traveler courtesy checks. Stored with stool nested under desk.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Matches stool, desk, tool pot.
