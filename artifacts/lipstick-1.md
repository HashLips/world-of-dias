# Lipstick

## Name

Lipstick

## Overview

**Lipstick** is an F610 pigment-bath set: a tall stemmed glass for dark brown resin or ink, a teal stir-rod, and charcoal-bodied sticks with bright magenta working tips, used to keep tips conditioned without losing them in the bath.

## Description

Glass stays clear enough to see immersion depth. Tips are counted against a rack before and after soaks. Stir-rod never doubles as a mouth tool. Children practice with sealed dummy tips. Adults who leave a tip drowned overnight replace the bath and apologize to the next maker. Name is Dias craft talk for the magenta tip—not a beauty-market story.

## Cultural Meaning

In F610, Lipstick is pigment discipline. Resonance favors tips that stay half-seen. Drinking the bath for dares is a known stupid echo.

## Usage

Maker benches, mark stations, and teaching tables. Glasses washed; tips racked dry after.

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Craft pigment, not Earth makeup culture.
