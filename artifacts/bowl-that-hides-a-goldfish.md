# Bowl That Hides a Goldfish

## Name

Bowl That Hides a Goldfish

## Overview

A **Bowl That Hides a Goldfish** is a teaching bowl whose bright fish is always slightly where the eye did not expect—kind wrongness practice for maps that must stay wrong to keep people safe.

## Description

Round glass; blue water; elongated gold fish that seems to move when you stamp a “correct” path. Softfruit interviewers slam it on tables next to fairness cards. Not a pet shop product; not a ferry into other bands.

## Cultural Meaning

Beauty as training for shelter lies. Find the fish; remember the map can mercifully mislead.

## Usage

Quiet Well teachers; Softfruit field kits; children’s chapbook demos.
