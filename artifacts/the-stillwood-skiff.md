# The Stillwood Skiff

## Name

The Stillwood Skiff

## Overview

The Stillwood Skiff is a wooden vessel, boat-shaped, that travels a handspan above lanes and quiet grass in rural Wabet. What holds it up is not known. It is not a frequency ferry, and Willa Plank will not pretend it is.

## Description

A narrow hull of pale, close-grained wood, ribs showing, no metal keel. It would be a river skiff if it consented to water. Instead it hangs about a handspan clear of ruts, hems, and the tops of low weeds. A pole rests in Willa's hands. She uses it to push and to steady, the way a punter would, against the ground she does not quite touch. There is no gas bag, no rotor, no singing stone on display. The wood is warm in sun and ordinary under the palm. Passengers, rarely taken, feel a slight refusal in the knees, as if a step had been declined by the air.

It does not climb. A low branch will stop it. A closed gate stops it. It will not rise to meet Sky Drifters, and Willa has never asked it to. At night it makes almost no sound except the pole's tap. Moth Kear, who had not seen a machine, did not count the skiff as one. Sella Orrow agreed with the child and still did not call the lift a kindness.

When asked what holds it, Willa says she does not know. She has said that for years. The not-knowing has not turned into a doctrine. The skiff also has not turned into a road. It carries one or two people and a basket. Fruit for market still goes by wagon.

## Cultural Meaning

Wabet neighbors treat it as a local stubbornness, like a spring that should not be on a hill and is. Outsiders want a lesson. They get a pole and a refusal. Take-without-restore does not become holy because the taker is hovering. If Willa skipped a waystation in it, the road would still call that raid. She does not skip. She nods at stewards and keeps to lanes.

## Usage

Willa's own going: messages, a basket, a look at a field. She passed Orrow House's lane on the night the lamp stayed lit. She did not join the stew. The skiff's lift remains unexplained in canon. Unknown is the status of the cause. The craft's existence on the lane is canonical.
