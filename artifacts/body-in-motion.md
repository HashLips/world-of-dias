# Body in Motion

## Name

Body in Motion

## Overview

**Body in Motion** is a kept Sorel motion-memory kit: a spiked white disc that can hold a profile-mark, a pale practice figure or cloth stand-in, and bird tokens used so remembering stays kinetic instead of freezing into a stamp.

## Description

The disc is the loud piece—white, ray-spiked, fitted to accept a black or dark profile insert facing the mover. Practice figures are spindly and light so apprentices can recline, reach, and change angle without strain theater. Bird tokens pin to a shoulder or stand on the floor as witness marks: someone (or some non-human neighbor) is watching the care, not the performance. Faint secondary discs may be chalked behind for assembly drills. Sorel hosts correct anyone still calling the kit Exile Road gear; the manners live here. The rule spoken at open is short: move while you honor, or stop claiming honor. Selling the spiked disc as a static wall trophy without the motion lesson is considered emptied craft.

## Cultural Meaning

Sorel treats stillness-as-ownership with suspicion. Memory that cannot change posture becomes a stamp that eats margin.

## Usage

Procession drills, guest rememberings, and quiet yard practice. Lent with profile inserts blank until the gathering names what face is being held.

## Notes

Motion-memory kit of Sorel. Move, then mean it.
