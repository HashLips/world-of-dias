# Saltjaw Hazard Notice

## Name

Saltjaw Hazard Notice

## Overview

The Saltjaw Hazard Notice is the Resonant Houses' public account of the loss of *The Patient Lead*, issued under the grade *unfound-shore, hazard pending*. It was carried to Sallow Strand by Severin Ault, who did not write it, and posted by House clerks, including on the Saltjaw cookhouse board. Every sentence in it is accurate. Its shape is false: it omits the plea, the margin columns, and *struck—not rock*, and it turns Ansel's MEASURE into an "impression."

## Description

A single printed House sheet. Across the top: *SALTJAW HAZARD NOTICE*, with the classification table's stamp and the grade beside it. Full text:

> *On the fifth day of a shelf survey in Wetwake of this year, the survey vessel* The Patient Lead *of Saltjaw, Driftfall, was lost at the southwest edge of the Saltjaw shelf after striking an uncharted obstruction in fog. One crew member drowned. Two were recovered alive. The surveyor, A. Kestry, is missing and presumed drowned.*
>
> *A personal letter written by the surveyor while adrift was recovered on the Lumira coast and is held at Sallow Strand. The letter describes confusion, cold, and an impression of land seen at dusk in poor visibility. No shore exists at the described bearing on any chart. Painted images circulating on the roads and claiming a "way through" are copied claims with no source and are unfounded. There is no crossing.*
>
> *Vessels are advised to keep clear of the shelf edge southwest of Saltjaw pending survey. Further copies should be brought to a House clerk.*

**What it omits or bends** (Maren's comparison, chapter 21):
- "uncharted obstruction" replaces "Not rock. Not wreck... wood and stone together";
- "impression of land" replaces MEASURE and "I have checked myself twice";
- the margin columns and "show them to Tamsy" are left out entirely;
- the plea "I am not finished" is left out, and "presumed drowned" stands in its place.

## Cultural Meaning

It calmed the markets and discouraged dare-crews, which is what it was for. To Maren, "*presumed drowned* is tidy." It and the road copies agree on one thing only: neither keeps the margins.

## Usage

Posted, and filed in Maren's notebook in the "told" column. After the expedition the Houses settle the file as *unfound-shore, withdrawn*, and no amended notice ever reconciles the testimonies.

## Notes

It is a model of truthful omission. Use it when a House document must be accurate and still mislead.
