# Gilding Bitters

## Name

Gilding Bitters

## Overview

Gilding Bitters is a peel-and-coin sip that makes colors look briefly more gold. It also makes the eyes water.

## Description

A thumb's worth in a small glass. Orange peel, a bitter herb from the basin ditches, and a sweet that arrives late. While it is on the tongue the room golds: wool looks warmer, bruises on fruit look like sunset, third-ring lamps look like gilding hour even at midnight. Tears come without sadness. The gold leaves before the tears do.

## Cultural Meaning

A toast drink when a convoy has made the basin. People who live in the outer ring know it is a trick of the cup and still let it happen, because the real gilding hour is not always on duty when the thirst is. It does not call weather. It does not repaint the city.

## Usage

Never mixed into Bell-Milk. Bram tried once. The bell-voice came out weeping gold and he lost a bench to laughter. Safe, silly, and a little proud.

## Notes

Non-ferry. Not a pigment for art forgery and not a light from another band.
