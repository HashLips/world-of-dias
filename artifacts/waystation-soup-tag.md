# Waystation Soup Tag

## Name

Waystation Soup Tag

## Overview

A **Waystation Soup Tag** clips to a bowl or pack to show you paid labor or peel for the shared pot at a fruit-road stop. Lived reciprocity, not a ferry stub.

## Description

Carrot-dyed wood; sometimes Wired Bloom thread leftovers. Handbills that promise free eternal soup without tags are wishful comedy—and still get people fed once, then taught manners.

## Cultural Meaning

Road ethics you can chew. Restoration-before-pick cousins smile at honest tags.

## Usage

Waystation cooks; caravan kids running errands; Softfruit auditors on friendly days.
