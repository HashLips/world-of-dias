# Perform

## Name

Perform

## Overview

**Perform** is Nauw Outer Rim Seas display-craft: a small black rectangular card held above an eight-point orange-yellow sunburst collar on a pink-rimmed platform, with the purple long-neck telling pose looking up in study rather than in grabby spectacle.

## Description

Rim workshops keep Perform as a teaching stand and a card set. The platform's pink rim marks the safe edge; the orange radial sits where the neck of the display rises, eight rounded points bright against chartreuse practice cloths. The black card is small on purpose—easy to lose if you flourish—so hands use three careful digits and an upward eye-line in the plate as the model. Local care forbids tossing the card into a crowd for cheap noise, and forbids calling the craft finished when the sunburst is bent. Resonance is the held black against the radial glow; memory is the upward tilt; wonder is how much chartreuse can loud a quiet study. Stands are lent between tide halls and returned with cards uncreased and points unchipped. Soft talk that turns Perform into a dare to drop the card is considered a break of seaside care. The artifact is display with manners, not a shout that erases the measure on the card.

## Cultural Meaning

On the Outer Rim Seas, to perform is to show the card cleanly over the sunburst—study visible, grip honest.

## Usage

Tide-hall teaching stands and readiness displays. Rarely used as parade toss-gear.
