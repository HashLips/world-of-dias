# T1

## Name

T1

## Overview

**T1** is the first card in the tone set used at Bloomline Holding Terrace: a red figure inside an unclosed white ring. It names the gentlest readable state in the Hearttide band—flushed, present, still self-held.

## Description

Terrace tenders keep T1 as a painted board about the size of a forearm, its red worn soft at the edges from handling. The image is simple on purpose: one head, one long neck, one sliver eye, and a white loop that breaks open where the neck passes through. Later cards in the set reportedly close the ring, double it, or let the red spill past it, but T1 is the only one shown freely to visitors. Children at the estuary play a guessing game with it, holding the card beside a friend's face and asking whether the water near their feet has warmed.

## Cultural Meaning

T1 makes a promise. If you match the card, you may enter a hold and leave it again without giving reasons. Tenders consider it rude to push someone at T1 toward a decision; the open gap belongs to them. The card turns emotional reading into consent rather than diagnosis.

## Usage

Shown at the terrace step before stabilization holds, propped at Warm Current Holding Cove on gathering days, and lent to mediators who travel inland. It is returned face-down so the next hand meets it fresh.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. The higher cards are not catalogued here and should not be invented casually.
