# Well-Beacon Shard

## Overview

A **Well-Beacon Shard** pulses or marks toward water with accuracy that Silence Law treats as violence—danger object of the chart black market.

## Description

Glass-sand fragment; wrong-bright in hush light. Keep schools smash them when found. Fruit-money buyers pay for “safety.” Wells that get advertised sometimes stop willing to be found.

## Cultural Meaning

Vesh’s wound made product. Path-only teaching’s enemy.

## Usage

Seizure plots; dare caravans; moral tests for Nauw travelers who think maps are neutral.
