# Letter to Myself at the Crossing

## Name

Letter to Myself at the Crossing

## Overview

A short letter **Frederick Lumens** wrote to his future self as a Driftfall teenager—near the age when he received **Roadglow Shard**—promising that he would one day be bold enough to attempt a frequency crossing. It is the object that turns him from orchard ownership back toward the road—and the tangible remnant left after his disappearance from F432 tellings.

## Description

Folded paper, salt-softened at the creases, kept through liner years and orchard years. The hand is young, pressed hard enough to dent the page. No formal seal. No address but the self.

### Letter text (canonical)

Frederick—

If you are reading this, you got older. Do not use that as an excuse.

I am writing from Driftfall with rope burns and a head full of campfire bands. The elders name frequencies like weather. I want more than weather. I want to stand somewhere the air has different laws and know I walked there on purpose.

Promise me this:

Be bold enough to try a crossing. Not talk. Not crates. Not safe seasons. A real attempt to leave this band and see another.

If you have gotten comfortable, remember the boy who listened at the fire. Comfort is not the destination. It is a bunk you sleep in between destinations.

I do not know how the crossing works yet. I will learn. You will learn. Do not let the learning stop at stories.

Travel the frequencies. Keep wanting the door even when your hands are full.

— Frederick
(the younger one)

## Cultural Meaning

In Frederick’s arc the letter is a private vow object and, after *No More Apples*, the public remnant of an unfinished crossing. Whether he succeeded is not answered in The Lumen Saga.

## Usage

Carried. Reread under stress. Kept when Roadglow was lost. Produced when he gifted Fellgrove. Left behind (in archive and rumor) when he vanished after returning to Stillhollow.
