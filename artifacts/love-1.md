# Love

## Name

Love

## Overview

**Love** is a Velorian Basin twin-reach set: soft black and orange body-marks or sashes, light center-meet cords for fingertip practice, and a tray for a shed blue practice-mask, used to rehearse mirrored leans without collapsing into one color.

## Description

Partners keep separate hues on purpose. Cords guide hands to the heart-gap without yanking spines. Mask trays sit left of the sand cloth so a face can be set down before the open-mouth call. Blue cloth hand-shapes stake the ground as memory markers—not toys to uproot. Children practice far apart. Adults who paint both partners the same color redo the drill until difference returns.

## Cultural Meaning

Around the Velorian Basin, Love is two reaches, not one erase. Resonance favors rooted feet and shed masks chosen freely. Forcing a mask on mid-song empties the set.

## Usage

Basin yards, festival sands, and teaching deserts. Cords coiled; hand-stakes counted.

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Matches mirrored desert pair.
