# Softfruit Second-Ladle Token

## Name

Softfruit Second-Ladle Token

## Overview

A **Softfruit Second-Ladle Token** is a sticky clay or wood chip that grants a second helping when the shelf is fair, and teaches waiting when it is not. Children trade them with a seriousness adults should envy. Adults use them to slow a rush toward the pot. Spend them like winnings and a Host will hang them back on the hook without a speech long enough to be worth repeating.

## Description

Fruit-stain patina, a carved ladle silhouette, sometimes a hole where a keep-mark cord once tried to turn the chip into jewelry and was discouraged. The token is cool in a hot hand, which is why it works on a stamp hand that itches. It clacks against its neighbors when the court door drafts, a soft inventory that is not a debt. Stacks are a tell. A stack means someone tried to win breakfast. The shelf is the token’s home. A pocket is a visit. Tokens that leave the court as souvenirs come back sticky and are not offended; the court assumes export and expects return, the way a ladle expects rinsing.

## Cultural Meaning

Ladle before stamp, in a shape a child can hold. Hunger noticed is civic. Hunger stamped first is pride, and pride is the poverty Softfruit will mock. The token is not currency, which is why it makes a bad bribe and a good delay. Three favors sealed after stew, correctly, are the token’s favorite story about itself, even though the token would never tell it. The Host would, later, lightly.

## Usage

The Second-Ladle Shelf, Table Hall jokes, the opening of a field interview once the rush is over and questions will not stand between a person and a bowl. Not a festival coin. Not a House chit. If it starts to clear debts, it has left the culture and should be put back on a hook until it remembers.

## Notes

Softfruit manners object. Fairness you can clack against wood.
