# Refusal Ribbon

## Name

Refusal Ribbon

## Overview

A **Refusal Ribbon** is a red-brown ribbon Pim ties on the hook of an object that is not for sale. If the object is pocketed anyway, the ribbon stays on the hook. Leaving is not permission.

## Description

Woven flat, the color of dried peel and old iron. It is ordinary cloth with an obstinate knot. It does not bind a thief’s hands or cry out. Its whole work is to remain where the refusal was, so the shop can show, in the morning, that a thing left against a no. People who want the ribbon to be a curse are asking it to become the brittle ease Pim will not sell.

## Cultural Meaning

The shop’s public ethics, visible and small. A ribbon is easier to argue with than a lecture, and less easy to forget than a shake of the head.

## Usage

Hooks behind the counter, especially the Heartlean Colorstone’s. Not tied to guests. Not a Hearttide Holding Cloth and not a substitute for one.

## Notes

On the morning after the theft, the ribbon was still on the hook. That is how the empty case read as a taken thing rather than a sale.
