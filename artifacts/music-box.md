# Music Box

## Name

Music Box

## Overview

**Music Box** is a craft-object or kept thing associated with **F120 (frequency realm)**, remembered because what it holds (or refuses to hold) teaches a local rule about care, display, and passage.

## Description

In plates and yard talk, **Music Box** shows as a clear graphic silhouette rather than a crowded scene: color fields and hard edges that read at a glance on parchment or market board. Around **F120 (frequency realm)**, people treat the image as a reminder that touch leaves a living echo—whether the subject is creature, craft, growth, weather, or mark. The picture does not invent a bureaucracy; it thickens a habit already in the road: slow down, look twice, decide whether the memory gets to stay. Hands that borrow it are expected to return it cleaner in meaning than in polish.

## Cultural Meaning

Around **F120 (frequency realm)**, the object is less a trophy than a teaching prop: what you display, you also keep answering for.

## Usage

Shown, lent, or parked in a known place; rarely sold without a story that can travel with it.
