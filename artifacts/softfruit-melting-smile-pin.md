# Softfruit Melting Smile Pin

## Name

Softfruit Melting Smile Pin

## Overview

A **Softfruit Melting Smile Pin** is a yellow smile badge that drips when worn as Forced-Final cheer—joy that refuses to stay stamped. Softfruit comedy with teeth: real belonging is poured, not pinned forever.

## Description

Bright yellow face on blue backing; black smile that looks melted by design. Worn at ladle courts and Row tea to mock helpful-glass grins and clerk smiles filed quiet. Children get solid pins; adults who sell “permanent happiness” get the dripping version taped to their stall.

## Cultural Meaning

Joy literacy. Melting is manners against fake cheer and nonactionable smiles.
