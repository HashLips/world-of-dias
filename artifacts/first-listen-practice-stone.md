# First-Listen Practice Stone

## Name

First-Listen Practice Stone

## Overview

A palm stone used by echo-craft teachers for first lessons: students learn to notice remainder without inventing a full story, then set the stone down before curiosity becomes trespass.

## Description

River-smooth, pocket-sized, often marked with a faint Partial Echo Slash. Some stones have hosted years of student hands and carry a mild habit echo—warmth of concentration, not secrets. Teachers rotate stones so no single rock becomes a cult object.

A proper first listen ends with three sentences maximum about what was *felt*, never a biography of a stranger imagined into the grain.

## Cultural Meaning

Anti-omniscience pedagogy. Talent is welcomed; manners are required.

## Usage

Softfruit side rooms, House junior drills (when Houses admit Softfruit methods work), private tutors who refuse market theatrics.

## Notes

If a stone begins producing rises like Harmonic Echo-Rise, stop the lesson and treat it as an event—craft, not toy.
