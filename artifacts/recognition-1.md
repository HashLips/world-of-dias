# Recognition

## Name

Recognition

## Overview

**Recognition** is an F380 hall-count board: black observer silhouette in front, rows of translucent mint figures with red outlines, and one solid dark-blue raised arm among white converging floor lines—the craft of noticing a single ask in a regulated hall.

## Description

Realm halls hang Recognition when assemblies need a picture for how a raise is seen. The mint many are not nothing; they are the glowing collective. The dark-blue arm is the interruption that local care must answer without turning the hall into a hunt. The black back-of-head in the foreground is the reminder that recognition starts with looking, not with shouting over rows. White floor lines keep the perspective honest. Resonance is one solid against many translucent; memory is the red borders; wonder is charcoal holding mint light. Boards return with the raised arm unsmudged. Soft talk that treats recognition as crowning a favorite misses the realm rule: the craft is fair noticing of the ask.

## Cultural Meaning

In F380, recognition means seeing the raised arm and still respecting the mint rows.

## Usage

Hall boards and assembly readiness. Not a weapon for silencing the many.
