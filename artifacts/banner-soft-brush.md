# Banner Soft-Brush

## Overview

A **Banner Soft-Brush** lifts Banner Moths from stalls and pots without crushing them—joy tech for fair weeks and Pip Ringroad’s serious work.

## Description

Soft bristles; short handle; often citrus-stained. Vendors hang them beside pots. Crushing a moth with a boot is bad shopkeeping; the brush is the antidote made object.

## Cultural Meaning

Market mysticism as gentleness. Children earn trust by brushing first.

## Usage

Fair care; teaching; apologizing after a near-crush.

## Notes

Joy object. Chapter starter: brushes vanish before fair week—or one brush finds a wrong-color moth.
