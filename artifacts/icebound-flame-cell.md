# Icebound Flame Cell

## Name

Icebound Flame Cell

## Overview

An **Icebound Flame Cell** is a Resonant Artifact pattern: a translucent cold cell that holds a living flame—or flame-like echo—without ordinary burning.

## Description

Cells vary in size from palm cubes to chest crates. The outer material reads as ice-glass; the interior event reads as fire, grief-heat, or war-echo depending on who listens. Sealing is the point: the heat does not spread, and the echo does not fade on schedule.

Handlers who overstay the seal are said to go limp—gold-husk tired in the stories—draped over the cell they would not abandon.

## Cultural Meaning

Operators value cells for load isolation. Houses value them for evidence that will not shout in a market. Critics call them cruelty with good filing manners.

## Usage

Contained, transported under silence protocols, rarely opened. Opening is a special-occasion act, often refused.
