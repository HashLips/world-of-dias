# The Late Bruise

## Name

The Late Bruise

## Overview

The Late Bruise is an ordinary freight plane, Lark Venn's hire, used to lift fruit from the Lent Strip in rural Wabet to Mercer Shed in Veloria. It is a machine with wings, a hull, and a timetable it often misses. It is not a Sky Drifter and not a way out of F432.

## Description

A blunt high-wing freighter, metal and rivet, painted a peel-green that has gone dull at the nose. Two engines. A cargo hull with a side door wide enough for a crate and too narrow for an ox, which is the point drivers make when they are feeling kind. The deck smells of Softfruit, oil, and the wet rope Opal Mire uses to keep loads from walking. Seats for a pilot and one other. Instruments are third-ring practical: fuel, height, a resonant compass that sulks near certain weather and is not a voice. No one in the cockpit is accompanied by a talking mind.

It lands on grass when Yarrow Bine has lent the grass. The wheels bruise a strip and stop. Rural people who have never seen a machine hear it before they see it, a rude continuous animal, and some of them cover their ears without shame. From below, at height, it can be mistaken by a child for a Drifter. It is not. It comes down. Drifters do not.

The name is Lark's. Fruit that flies still bruises; it just bruises later, in the shed, when someone finally opens the crate. She liked the honesty. Dain liked the speed.

## Cultural Meaning

Among wagon crews the plane is a private convenience with a reputation problem. Flight that pretends to be the whole road — that skips waystations, that takes and does not restore the stops — is already talked about as raid. The Late Bruise is tolerated while ordinary fruit still moves by wagon, pony, and ox, and while Lark pays the lend in marks and in labor on the grass. It becomes suspect the moment the hull's count stops matching what the market can see.

## Usage

Private freight hire for Dain Mercer's distribution, not a public service and not a replacement for the Eastbound Fruit Road. It carries labeled Softfruit and, in the saga, a cloth-wrapped breed that was not on the public slate. It does not cross frequencies. It does not dock with anything in the sky.
