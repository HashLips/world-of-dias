# Tubehold Dam Walk Sketch

## Name

Tubehold Dam Walk Sketch

## Overview

A dam-and-windmill settlement sketch for night watchers: waterlines, listening-tube posts, mill streets, and the child-tube bench elders pretend is ordinary furniture.

## Description

Graphite on mill paper, often damp at the edges. Dam gates drawn oversized (civic religion). Windmills numbered for watch rotation. Thin blue lines = waterlines with tube posts. A small goldfish doodle near the teaching bench nods to *Find the Goldfish* without claiming magic. Drifter camp margin marked outside the wall—welcome for stories, not for rewriting the water.

Sits inside Glasswater Fields on regional charts. Does not move Tubehold onto Averra or the Outer Rim.

## Cultural Meaning

Infrastructure as walkable geography: vigilance has streets.

## Usage

Night-watch training; Softfruit hosts use a cleaner copy when explaining “Glasswater honesty.”

Settlement-level atlas artifact for Volume II deepen pass.
