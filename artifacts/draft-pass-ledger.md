# Draft Pass Ledger

## Name

Draft Pass Ledger

## Overview

A **Draft Pass Ledger** records first pass, clean pass, and holding pass for places in the Unfinished. Distance here is persistence, not conquest. The ledger is how a redraw stays maintenance instead of becoming a ghost story about vandals. If it is dated, the court can bear its own changes. If it is not, every morning looks like an attack.

## Description

Graphite-smudged pages, cobalt ticks in the margin, copper strike-throughs that remain legible because erasure, in this grammar, is dated and not denied. Entries name how long a latch held, who noticed the moss move, what the outline was yesterday and what bears weight today. They do not name a finisher. “Finished” is a word the pages make look silly. The book hangs under scaffolds that have been temporary through more than one pass, and people pat the cover when they enter: I will write the change, not I am forgiven. Chalk dust gets in the binding. That is correct. A clean ledger in the Ghost Quarter is a ledger someone is lying with.

## Cultural Meaning

Open Hand survival grammar, bound. Finality worship looks like vandalism here because it scrapes the strike-throughs and offers a smooth page to a delegation. Foundation Revisers would rather be rude than smooth. Visitors who carve two persistence ticks and then eat dust-bread have started to read the ledger with their hands, which is the literacy the quarter actually wants.

## Usage

Foundation courts, scaffold arcs, the margins of atlas disputes. The atlas may argue. The ledger has already dated the door. Bring a pencil. Do not bring a ribbon.

## Notes

Alive drafting tool. Not an archive of a dead city. The city is still deciding, and the book is how the decision stays shareable.
