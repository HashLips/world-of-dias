# Mind

## Name

Mind

## Overview

**Mind** is a Wabet muzzle-calm strap and practice cue: a soft contact band for blue long-eared burden-beasts and a reminder to crouch red-marked before speaking load orders, used so mind arrives as touch, not shout.

## Description

Straps are short and soft. Handlers crouch to eye-slit height of the plate’s red figure. Shadows on grey ground teach waiting—if shade still swings hard, wait. Children practice on calm beasts only. Adults who bark orders over a lifted hoof walk the next mile leading by hand.

## Cultural Meaning

Around Wabet, Mind is load manners. Resonance favors muzzle-first contact. Treating the blue beast as furniture empties the strap.

## Usage

Road pauses, well approaches, and teaching yards. Straps washed; beasts watered.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Matches red-blue care plate.
