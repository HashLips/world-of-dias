# Eat Sleep Rave Repeat

## Name

Eat Sleep Rave Repeat

## Overview

**Eat Sleep Rave Repeat** is a kept Coral Strand cycle kit: colored body-state overlays on a pale disc used to teach shore days that must rotate eat, rest, dance, and return without grinding one state into harm.

## Description

Hosts lay a pale yellow disc cloth and place or paint four translucent figure-tokens—pink lunge, orange sit, yellow lie, blue edge-hand. Apprentices practice naming which state they are refusing. Strand festivals that skip sleep get the kit brought out as correction, not as décor. The name stays Dias shore-speech; no Earth party-brand reading is required. Selling the pink figure alone as a dance idol empties the craft.

## Cultural Meaning

Coral Strand loves rhythm and distrusts loops that eat the sleeper.

## Usage

Shore festival planning, guest stamina talks, and pier recovery mornings. Lent as a full four-state set.

## Notes

Cycle diagram kit. Rotate; do not grind.
