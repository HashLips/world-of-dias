# Outer-Ring Door Nail

## Name

Outer-Ring Door Nail

## Overview

An **Outer-Ring Door Nail** is a spare iron nail from Farstock’s old door, sold sometimes as a pocket piece that warms on cold mornings and does not open any door but the memory of one.

## Description

Black iron, square head, a little bright where thumbs worry it. Warmth is mild, the warmth of metal that has lived in a handled door, not a coal. It will not pick a lock, mark a route, or heat a room. People who want a key are disappointed on purpose.

## Cultural Meaning

Outer-ring households like objects that admit they came from a real threshold. The nail says: you found the street. It does not say: the street owes you the shop’s secrets.

## Usage

Pocket charm, conversation piece, sometimes nailed back into a drafty shutter at home. Pim will not sell the nail that is actually holding the hinge.

## Notes

Not a relic of the Fracture. Not a gate key.
