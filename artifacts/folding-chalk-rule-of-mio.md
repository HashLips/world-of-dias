# Folding Chalk Rule of Mio

## Name

Folding Chalk Rule of Mio

## Overview

**Mio Chalkrule**’s folding rule—wood and brass, chalk-stained—with affectionate crooked ticks Mio swears are more honest than factory-straight ones. It is Orrin’s daily prop while building and teaching the Spectrum Frame.

## Description

Three hinged segments. Edges soft from pocket wear. Measure-marks along one face are slightly irregular on purpose: Mio believes a hand that can love a crooked tick will notice when a “perfect” dial is lying. Orrin uses it to set Frame apertures, check Softfruit cart heights against “keep” stories, and tap arguments on the Tea Slit Bench.

Mio visits the alcove once during the Frame build to confirm the etched marks still read; accepts Orrin’s chaos as “within tolerance.” After escape, chalk dust on the rule sometimes settles in slit-shapes; Orrin brushes it off and keeps working.

## Cultural Meaning

Affectionate rigor. Proof that Calibration Saga science is handmade, not House-sealed. Apprentices borrow the idea (not the original) when learning scraped corrections.

## Usage

Aperture setting; teaching; nervous tapping. Not a Resonant Artifact of legend—just the right tool in the right pocket.
