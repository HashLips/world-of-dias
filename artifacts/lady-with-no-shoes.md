# Lady with No Shoes

## Name

Lady with No Shoes

## Overview

**Lady with No Shoes** is an F432 formal sit set: four thin tan chair-lines arranged as a minimal seat, navy floor bands for foot placement, and a red V-neck formal garment cue, used when bare pointed contact is the required greeting.

## Description

The chair is almost only suggestion—rails and back lines—so the sitter’s weight and feet do the real work. Navy bands tell where toes may rest. Shoes stay in a tray beyond the maroon room’s edge. Children practice pointed-foot stillness before long sits. Adults who smuggle thin soles are asked to stand until the bands are free again. The set smells faintly of floor oil and cloth dye.

## Cultural Meaning

In F432, Lady with No Shoes is honest contact manners. Resonance sticks to bare points on the bands. Mocking someone’s feet mid-sit is poor care.

## Usage

Formal frequency rooms, apology sits, and teaching chambers. Chair-lines checked for splinters; bands redrawn when faded.
