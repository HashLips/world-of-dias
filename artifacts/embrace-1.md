# Embrace

## Name

Embrace

## Overview

**Embrace** is a kept Aelwyn Canopy measured-hold set: six pink-sphere poles on white staffs used to teach that a welcome hold keeps even gaps and never smothers the reclining guest.

## Description

Poles are light, spheres soft-looking but firm enough to count. Hosts set six in a line or gentle arc and invite a guest to rest so contact points stay readable. Fewer than six for a joke hug is allowed; removing poles mid-hold to “get closer” is the emptied craft. Canopy talk uses the set after storms when people grab too hard from fear. Selling spheres as candy décor without spacing manners empties the lesson.

## Cultural Meaning

Aelwyn prefers embraces you can still breathe inside.

## Usage

Guest welcome drills, path-rest care, and apology holds. Lent as a six-pole set.

## Notes

Measured hold set. Even gaps; no smother.
