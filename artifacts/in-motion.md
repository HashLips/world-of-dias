# In Motion

## Name

In Motion

## Overview

**In Motion** is an Outer Rim crescent-row set: yellow crescent headpieces with a single vivid red hook reserved for the agreed front walker, plus light grey veils or translucent cloaks for those who follow in layered echo.

## Description

Crescents are light wood or shell-composite, curved to sit stable in Rim wind. The red hook tip is painted or inlaid and transferred hand-to-hand when leadership of a short crossing or dock carry changes. Followers wear grey layers that read translucent in mist—hence the plate’s fade. Mask-face paint (white slits and L-brow) is optional for formal rows. Drills teach the reset: red hook returns to the pouch if two people claim front. Salt air dulls yellow; evening wipe keeps the line bright.

## Cultural Meaning

On the Outer Rim Seas, In Motion means moving as a readable row. Resonance prefers the red hook that was given, not grabbed. Breaking line for vanity is a cold joke that earns you the back grey for a week.

## Usage

Dock carries, fog crossings, and festival processions along Rim paths. Stored nested; red hook counted separately.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Matches layered crescents and red tip.
