# Nesting Structure Comparison Plate

## Name

Nesting Structure Comparison Plate

## Overview

A teaching plate (wood, cloth, or printed board) showing the Dias stack as a readable diagram: world-container → frequency realm → region/place → local phenomenon—beside a crossed-out wrong diagram that paints bands as neighboring countries.

## Description

### Diagram content (what the plate shows)

```
Dias (world-container)
 └── Frequency realm (e.g. F432)
      └── Region / place (e.g. Nauw → Veloria)
           └── Local phenomenon (e.g. Bleed-Sky Weather visits; does not become a district)
```

Wrong diagram (struck through with No-Ferry / wrong-nest marks): F200 / F500 drawn as harbors east of Driftfall with ticket stubs.

Softfruit editions use four bowls drawn under the stack. House editions add Claim Grade: stack = law; ferry map = taboo guess.

## Cultural Meaning

Makes Nesting and Scale of Dias teachable as a picture a book can reproduce in prose or as art direction.

## Usage

Softfruit orientation, clerk drills, Averra exhibits next to beautiful wrong charts. Paired with Frequency Comparison Ledger Leaf for band-difference teaching.

## Notes

Teachable structural comparison artifact for cosmos structure—not a ferry map.
