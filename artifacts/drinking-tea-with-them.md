# Drinking Tea with Them

## Name

Drinking Tea with Them

## Overview

**Drinking Tea with Them** is a kept Velorian Basin guest set: a high red table on spindly pink legs, twin red stools, and a single white cup used when company should stay quiet and shared.

## Description

The table is small and high on purpose so elbows cannot claim territory. One white cup travels to the center; a second cup is refused unless someone genuinely needs water medicine, not status. Stools match. Basin hosts bring the set for neighbor talks, apology sips, and first meetings with non-human guests who prefer height and stillness. Filling the table with snacks to avoid silence is allowed; filling it to dominate is not. Selling the single cup as a novelty pair-breaker empties the craft.

## Cultural Meaning

Basin hospitality measures care by shared measure, not by competing cups.

## Usage

Guest sips, path-rest talks, and quiet reconciliations. Lent with the cup washed.

## Notes

Shared cup set. One cup is the courtesy.
