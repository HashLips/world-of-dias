# 3d Room

## Name

3d Room

## Overview

**3d Room** is a kept practice-interior kit on Averra Isle: yellow table, pink sphere, red marked urn, hanging green-bulbed lamp, and a floor sleeve that lets a black reach-form rise for drill. Studios use it to teach how deep a hand—or a memory—may enter a room.

## Description

The kit is small enough to reset in a damp-studio corner. The table is yellow, rectangular, thin-legged, and deliberately slightly unsteady so students feel when they lean too hard. The pink sphere rests alone on it; touching it without the lamp’s green permission is the first fail. The red urn stands opposite as the room’s witness-vessel, black inside, with a simplified figure-mark on the belly that Averra talk treats as the place’s own signature rather than a person. A floor sleeve opens to admit a soft black reach-form—clawed, three-fingered, meant to hover over the sphere and then withdraw. The pendant lamp’s lime-green bulb is the go-sign. When lit, hover is allowed. When dark, the reach must sink. Instructors draw or hang a white diagonal beam across the corner so volume can be checked by eye. The artifact is not furniture for living in; it is furniture for learning not to grab what the room is only showing.

## Cultural Meaning

Averra Isle treats depth as a manners skill. A third dimension without consent is just a prettier theft. **3d Room** keeps that rule tactile.

## Usage

Reset between lessons in Averra Damp Studios. Lent to visiting makers who need a quiet reach-drill before working near guest tables. Not sold as décor without the courtesy lesson spoken once.

## Notes

Practice kit. Claw may hover; claw may not claim.
