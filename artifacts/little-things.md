# Little Things

## Name

Little Things

## Overview

**Little Things** is an F610 arrangement set: a green trapezoid door-block with yellow slot and pink handle, a grey ring with black pendant, a red pole with six grey pebble counters, and a minimal grey chair mark, used to teach that many small pieces still serve one enterable room.

## Description

Tables are white. Learners place the yellow oval glow and magenta top-wave as weather toys, then must still open the pink handle. Pebble counts track borrowed tools. Children love the blue ribbon; adults enforce return-to-place. Losing a pebble means the pole’s lesson repeats until six return.

## Cultural Meaning

In F610, Little Things is household resonance literacy. Resonance favors full pebble counts. Throwing away 'weird' parts for neatness empties the teaching.
