# Own Train

## Name

Own Train

## Overview

**Own Train** is Brindle March shared-grip craft: white reaching hands, each palm stamped with a light-azure eight-spoke wheel, aimed at an empty center under a jagged black weather-arc—motion owned together.

## Description

March crews paint Own Train on tarp edges, cart boards, and lean-to chalk when a load or a walk must be many-handed. The azure wheel in each palm is the matched mark: eight spokes, thin ring, clear at a glance so no one can claim a private different count. Hands reach the empty middle on purpose; putting a trophy there turns the craft into a grab contest. The jagged black arc in the full plate is kept in the telling as the risk-line—weather, tear, or unfinished quarrel—that the train does not ignore. People say you are in Own Train when every palm shows the wheel and nobody closes fingers on a hidden prize. Resonance is the spoke-match across hands; memory is the empty center; wonder is the deep blue field that makes the white grips loud. Boards are lent between fruit-road crews and returned with spokes unsmudged. Soft talk that renames the craft as one boss's private convoy is considered a break of local care.

## Cultural Meaning

On Brindle March, owning the train means sharing the grip. Empty center is the honesty.

## Usage

Crew boards, tarp marks, and lean-to reminders before shared hauls. Not sold as a solo status badge.
