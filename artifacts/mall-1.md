# Mall

## Name

Mall

## Overview

**Mall** is an Elder Shelf commons kit: seven pose markers for distinct public moods, a pale reflective floor-band cloth, and three empty light-grey wall frames, used to keep a hall honest about many states at once.

## Description

Markers are flat black stands or floor prints: look-away, hunch, turn, arms-up, reach, and pair-lean. The reflective band is polished cloth or sealed stone strip—cleaned without scrubbing someone out of view. Empty frames stay empty on purpose so the room does not pretend to sell a single story. Stewards rotate which pose stands near the door. Children may copy poses; they may not mock the hunch.

## Cultural Meaning

On Elder Shelf, Mall is shared variety. Resonance favors mirrored honesty. Forcing everyone into arms-up empties the commons.

## Usage

Shelf halls, petition waits, and quiet markets of manners. Markers stored; band polished soft.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Matches greyscale commons plate.
