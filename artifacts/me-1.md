# Me

## Name

Me

## Overview

**Me** is a Cinder Nook wade-claim cord: a simple dark line worn or held while standing waist-deep in rim water with one arm raised, used to speak a solitary first self-word before shared smoke work begins.

## Description

Cord keeps a hand from thrashing if the bottom shifts. Peach-hour or blue-hour both allowed; mountains need not be named. Reflection courtesy means waiting if another black shape already ripples the pool. Children practice on shore with arm-raise only. Adults who splash into someone’s Me redo their own claim at next low light.

## Cultural Meaning

Around Cinder Nook, Me is permitted solitude at the waterline. Resonance favors one eye and one arm. Turning the rite into a group yell empties it.

## Usage

Rim pools, pre-winter checks, and quiet teachings. Cord dried; pools left unlittered.

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Matches solitary wade plate.
