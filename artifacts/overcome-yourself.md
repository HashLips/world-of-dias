# Overcome Yourself

## Name

Overcome Yourself

## Overview

**Overcome Yourself** is a Velorian Basin mirror-mitt set: a tall coral-framed glass, a yellow forehead band, and a deep-red rounded mitt tied with thin strings—used to meet your own gray reflection without bare-handed thrashing.

## Description

Basin practice rooms keep the coral frame tall enough for a standing drill. The near body stays unmarked white in the telling; the reflection is allowed to go cool gray with only slit eyes, so the work is seeing the harder self without flattery. The yellow band is tied before the bout-with-self begins. The red mitt covers the working hand or truncated practice arm and hangs its fastening strings where both you and the glass can see them—proof the strike, if any, is padded and accountable. Behind the reflected figure the glass-space is kept black, sometimes with a jagged gray peak-shape as the far weather of the drill. People do not use Overcome Yourself to shame neighbors. Local care says you overcome the wild grab in your own reflection first. Resonance is the match and mismatch across the coral edge; memory is how the dangling strings look in glass; wonder is the yellow pennant scrap that often hangs in the same room as twin readiness. Sets are lent, returned with mitt strings untangled and frame unchipped. Selling the mitt as a charm that wins roads is considered empty talk.

## Cultural Meaning

In the basin, overcoming yourself is a padded, mirrored honesty—not a public humiliation sport.

## Usage

Practice rooms and private drills; sometimes shown at youth readiness days. Rarely used as parade gear.
