# Loft Hail Chalk

## Overview

**Loft Hail Chalk** marks Storm Choir and First Line answers on loft boards—and sometimes refuses to wipe a lied hail clean.

## Description

Salt-white sticks in a net pouch; boards darkened by years of roll call. Jessa-types trust lists over speeches. Apprentices learn rope-before-stone beside the chalk dust.

## Cultural Meaning

Coordination as love; answer-empty as public wound.

## Usage

Check-in memory; teaching eight-beat answers; evidence in loft disputes.

## Notes

Slight resonance. Chapter starter: a name that won’t erase after a lied hail.
