# Golden Bath

## Name

Golden Bath

## Overview

**Golden Bath** is a kept Wabet wash kit: a golden four-legged basin, a cyan pause-orb token, and footprint marks used so restorative recline stays quiet care rather than spectacle.

## Description

The basin is bright and reflective on purpose—dirt and rush show. Cyan orb hangs or floats in teaching setups as the “still above water” mark. Three cyan footprint cloths lead guests in; they stop short so no one tracks mud into the gold. A black sickle-shaped floor token reminds: cut only what the wash requires (thread, bandage, overgrowth jokes)—never dignity. Ringwash hosts lend the kit for guest recoveries. Selling the gold tub as luxury flex empties the craft.

## Cultural Meaning

Wabet washes to restore, not to display.

## Usage

Quiet recoveries, guest aftercare, and apprentice wash manners. Lent with orb and footprint marks.

## Notes

Wash basin kit. Approach soft; bathe quiet.
