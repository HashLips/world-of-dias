# Resonance Feat Sorting Chapbook

## Name

Resonance Feat Sorting Chapbook

## Overview

A pocket book with three judgment columns—Succeed / Fail / Forbidden—plus worked Softfruit examples and pointers to soft-limit entries. Used to test a proposed resonant feat before anyone sells it.

## Description

Matte cover, dull ink, No-Ferry Mark on the inside flap. Softfruit editions smell of peel; House editions include Claim Grade reminders. Each spread states a proposed feat in one sentence, then sorts it with a short why. Sellers who add a “guaranteed wish” leaf find Softfruit burning that leaf only.

Typical worked pages: table-warmth manners (succeed); complete-life reading stall (forbidden); ferry stub (fail + taboo comedy); Ember cold-gap naming (succeed); Forced-Final overnight heal (fail/forbidden in recovery); glance-and-stop (succeed); forever-stare window (fail + cost).

## Cultural Meaning

Makes Resonance Can and Cannot of Dias teachable without poetry standing in for a verdict.

## Usage

Gifted after seized handbills, Softfruit orientation, and junior House drills. Kept beside Soft Limits of Dias on teaching shelves.

## Notes

Pair with Five-Domain Effect Chapbook (what bands change) and Eight-Band Difference Chapbook (how bands differ). This one answers: may this feat work as claimed?
