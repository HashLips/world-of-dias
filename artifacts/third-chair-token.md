# Third-Chair Token

## Overview

A **Third-Chair Token** is a small wood or clay marker Bess-style hosts give travelers who may claim the empty chair—welcome made portable.

## Description

Palm-sized; sometimes moss-stained; sometimes carved with a crude three. Not VEL. Not a Keep pass. Just a promise that a chair exists somewhere west.

## Cultural Meaning

Elder hospitality infrastructure; “third chair” idiom given an object.

## Usage

Travel courtesy; teaching children welcome; rarely forged because forging welcome is a special shame.

## Notes

Slight resonance. Chapter starter: a token that warms when the holder lies about hunger.
