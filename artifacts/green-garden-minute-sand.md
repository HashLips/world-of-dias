# Green Garden Minute Sand

## Name

Green Garden Minute Sand

## Overview

**Green Garden Minute Sand** runs a short glass for one honest garden minute—weed, water, or witness—then stops. Discipline craft against endless “improvement” stares.

## Description

Green-tinted sand in a palm glass; flips once per task. Forced-Final gardeners who flip without looking get peacocks and Softfruit commentary.

## Cultural Meaning

Timed stewardship. Cousin to anti-stare manners in living soil.

## Usage
