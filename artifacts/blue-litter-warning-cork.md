# Blue Litter Warning Cork

## Name

Blue Litter Warning Cork

## Overview

A **Blue Litter Warning Cork** seals sample jars that must not become bottles—visual ban craft against underwood hazard dressed as souvenir. Not a collectible “magic cork.”

## Description

Blue-stained cork with a slash mark; burns the nose faintly. Sold only as warning stock to wardens; tourists who cork Blue Litter for sale meet Open Hand refusal and Softfruit laughter without kindness.

## Cultural Meaning

Ban as care. Wired blooms are the allowed beauty cousin; Blue Litter is not.

## Usage

Wabet wardens; fruit-road inspectors; Row ethics demos.

## Notes

Rule: Blue Litter bottling ban.
