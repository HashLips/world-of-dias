# Resonant Shards

## Name

Resonant Shards

## Overview

A **Resonant Shard** is matter capable of carrying or preserving a frequency and its associated echoes. Shards are a major subtype under **Resonant Artifacts** and a recurring conceptual symbol of Dias.

## Description

Shards may contain memories, historical events, emotional residue, unknown signals, environmental resonance, encoded fragments, or contradictory impressions. They vary widely in age, material, origin, frequency, and legibility.

Some shards are ordinary tools in local practice. Others are historically priceless. Some remain completely unreadable to Present Age methods. A stone may hold a vanished city’s last calm; a scrap of metal may layer every hand that carried it; a shard may refuse interpretation for generations.

**True Blues** are a folk name for shards whose break glows hard electric blue and whose resonance is strong enough that strangers recognize them on sight. They are scarce relative to common echo-matter. Named example: [`artifacts/roadglow-shard.md`](roadglow-shard.md). Rumor frame: [`myths/the-true-blues.md`](../myths/the-true-blues.md).

Not every Prime Relic is a shard, and not every shard is a Prime Relic. The terms overlap when provenance and behavior justify both labels.

## Cultural Meaning

Shards appear in stories as windows into history, as contested evidence, and as quiet companions to characters who listen. The visual and conceptual motif is recorded in [`symbols/resonant-shard.md`](../symbols/resonant-shard.md).

## Usage

Carried, studied, sealed, gifted, buried, or left where found. Echo-readers such as Calen Brink may hear more from a shard than institutions prefer to file.
