# Pisces

## Name

Pisces

## Overview

**Pisces** is Driftfall twin-neck figure craft: pale-pink sinuous necks ending in identical slit-eyed crested heads, dark red side fins, and black featureless stride legs—kept to teach one body holding two attentions.

## Description

Pier workshops carve or cast Pisces as a small jointed figure and paint the full plate on loft boards. The two necks must curve opposite without snapping the join; white jagged crests stay matched; slit-eyes stay small and dark. Red petal-fins along the necks are part of the recognition, not optional glitter. Legs stay solid black and mid-stride so the lesson is motion shared under split looking. A gold mark under the forward foot appears in careful copies as the contact honesty. Local care forbids breaking one neck off to "simplify" the craft, and forbids using the figure to mock anyone who attends two duties at once. Resonance is the S-curve pair; memory is the crest match; wonder is pink flesh over black legs on olive-slate ground. Figures are lent between net-lofts and returned with crests unchipped. Soft talk that files Pisces as an Earth star-story prop is ignored; on Driftfall the name is the twin-neck walker craft.

## Cultural Meaning

At Driftfall, Pisces means split attention with one stride. Both crests count.

## Usage

Loft boards, teaching figures, and pier readiness displays. Not a trophy for catching fish alone.
