# Faith

## Name

Faith

## Overview

**Faith** is a kept F840 teaching set: a small white tile and a gray through-blade token used to show that holding a small true mark can cost, and that cost should stay visible rather than be preached away.

## Description

The white tile is palm-small, blank until a gathering writes one careful word or mark. The gray blade token is never sharpened for harm drills; it hangs or rests as a cost-sign behind the kneeler’s shoulders in staging. Frequency hosts forbid turning the set into Earth-faith pageantry. The lesson is local: keep the small true thing; do not lie that keeping is free; do not glorify the wound. Selling the tile as a lucky charm empties the craft.

## Cultural Meaning

F840 respects costly keeping and rejects both cheap belief and pain worship.

## Usage

Quiet vow renewals, apology keeps, and apprentice courage talks. Lent with blade token blunt and tile wipeable.

## Notes

Costly keep tile. Culture- and religion-agnostic.
