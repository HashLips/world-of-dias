# She Moves

## Name

She Moves

## Overview

**She Moves** is Sorel volume-dress craft: an enormous magenta floor-length gown, a wavy yellow wide brim, and a tall black cylindrical crown angled on that brim—motion kept grand while the face stays hidden from the low-angle telling.

## Description

Sorel halls keep the craft as board and sometimes as teaching garment silhouette. The dress volume is the first rule—give it floor. The yellow brim's irregular wavy edge and the precarious black crown are the second: stacked headwear that must stay balanced in the tell. Local care forbids yanking the crown off for laughs, and forbids demanding the face turn for the crowd. Resonance is magenta against gray with yellow-black stack; memory is the white back; wonder is billow without a shown step. Pieces return with brim uncrushed. Soft talk that invents a royal blood story misses Sorel's rule: she moves means the volume is allowed to travel.

## Cultural Meaning

In Sorel, She Moves means grand quiet passage in volume and stacked brim-crown.

## Usage

Hall boards and ceremonial readiness. Not a costume for forced facing.
