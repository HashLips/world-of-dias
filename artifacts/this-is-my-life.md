# This Is My Life

## Name

This Is My Life

## Overview

A **This Is My Life** case is a single piece of luggage that a traveler declares, on arrival at Driftfall Piers, to hold everything they own. Once marked with a pale triangle and a closed eye, it is exempt from dockhand inspection and salvage tallies.

## Description

Any case can be declared, but most are sky-blue, because the pier's paint store sells that color cheapest. The traveler kneels on the boards in sight of a harbor hand, sets the case down, and says the phrase. The hand paints the mark—a pale cyan triangle with a closed eye inside—on the lid. Musicians often make the declaration with their instrument still slung, since the instrument is understood to be part of the life, not baggage. After that, the case may be carried, stored, or slept on, but no one at the piers will open it without the owner's word.

## Cultural Meaning

Driftfall runs on salvage, and salvage culture can treat everything unattended as fair game. The declared case is the counterweight: a visible boundary around one person's entire life. The closed eye means the pier has agreed not to look. Breaking that agreement is one of the few things that will get a dockhand barred from every crew.

## Usage

Declared once per arrival. A declared traveler may be asked to play or work for passage, but never to open the case as payment. Old cases with layers of faded marks are proudly kept.
