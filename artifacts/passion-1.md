# Passion

## Name

Passion

## Overview

**Passion** is Veloria City portrait-craft: a blue split face under a jagged red-orange hair crown and matching lips, held in thick black calligraphic outline on void black—intensity contained as local care.

## Description

City workshops and house walls keep Passion as a painted board or inked panel used when someone needs a shape for strong feeling that must not become a street fight. The face stays cool blues—cerulean lit, navy shaded—so the heat is reserved for hair and mouth. Eyes stay narrow and unblinking in the telling; they mark attention, not accusation. The red-orange hair is jagged on purpose, a crown of motion frozen. Black lines vary thick and thin like brush accountability around jaw and neck. People hang or lend the craft before hard talks, festivals of measure, or private readiness, as a reminder that passion without a container scorches the room. Resonance is the hot/cool split; memory is the windswept points over the brow; wonder is the void black that makes both colors louder. Boards return with outlines unsmudged. Soft talk that treats Passion as permission to harm is considered a break of city care. The artifact is a face you can point to, not a person you get to invent a whole blood story for.

## Cultural Meaning

In Veloria City, passion is heat with a black edge. Cool skin under a hot crown is the honesty.

## Usage

Wall panels, readiness rooms, and private boards. Rarely used as a chase poster.
