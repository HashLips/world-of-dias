# Earth Form Far Train

## Name

Earth Form Far Train

## Overview

**Earth Form Far Train** is a kept Brindle March far-send mark: an orange drip-figure token paired with a cyan-green receiving-arc used when a linked load must seed a far place without planting a claim-flag.

## Description

Teaching boards hang the orange elongated figure above a curved cyan-lime arc so drivers and packers see distance as drip-care. The orange pool mark on the arc means arrival has stained honestly—someone received. March talk refuses to read this as owning the far ground. “Train” means the linked carts or shared sequence, not Earth rail romance. Lending the mark for conquest speeches empties the craft. Thank-yous still apply when the far end helps unload.

## Cultural Meaning

Brindle sends care farther than pride can comfortably supervise.

## Usage

Long fruit-road briefings, far gift packs, and apprentice distance drills. Lent as figure-plus-arc pair.

## Notes

Far-send mark. Drip to seed; do not stamp to own.
