# Wishful Fruit-Road Handbill

## Overview

A **Wishful Fruit-Road Handbill** promises bruise-perfect timing, open waystations, or “no toll this week”—forged or hopeful paper that still turns carts.

## Description

Cheap fiber; almost-right fruit-road marks; stamps that look VEL-clean in haze. Sometimes a child’s drawing sold as schedule. Sometimes a crew’s lie. Behavior changes either way.

## Cultural Meaning

Proof of motive even when false. Investigators collect handbills the way loft crews collect hails.

## Usage

Plot engine: convoy follows a handbill into Bleed weather; Mutual Table posts a correction song; toll marshals nail a forger with grease-thumb comparison to a real Span Toll Book.
