# Airing Cloth for Sour Rooms

## Name

Airing Cloth for Sour Rooms

## Overview

A plain vinegar-and-sun cloth used to air booths, stalls, and tables after sour aftertones—repair manners for remainder, not a magic erase.

## Description

Undyed square, often patched, smelling cleanly sharp. Softfruit pairs it with one true spoken apology when the sour came from people. Gates keep a stack for booth sulks. Markets air stalls at dusk with the same cloth logic even when they use different fabric names.

The cloth does not destroy echoes. It helps rooms accept correction the way scrape helps pages.

## Cultural Meaning

Physical humble cousin to Scrape Not Erase. Proof that living with resonance includes maintenance.

## Usage

After argument-stain, brittle cheer, or market cruelty. Not used to “clean” sealed House drawers without clearance—that is how juniors meet cold spots the hard way.

## Notes

If someone sells the cloth as Absolute Wipe, Softfruit cuts the seller off from free lunch.
