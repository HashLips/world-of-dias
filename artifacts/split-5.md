# Split

## Name

Split

## Overview

**Split** is Averra Isle formal-stripe craft: a black chest-up board with blank white eye voids, thin white face marks, one undulating crown cord, and a vertically striped garment with a solid white narrow center hang—attention split clean between pattern and void.

## Description

Damp studios hang Split when a room needs formal looking without soft flattery. Blank eyes stay pupil-less; stripes stay vertical and slightly irregular; the white center hang stays narrow and clear. The crown cord is the single soft motion allowed. Local care forbids adding pupils for comfort, and forbids using the board to intimidate guests. Resonance is black/white rhythm; memory is the cord past the shoulder; wonder is linocut starkness. Boards return unsmudged. Soft talk that treats Split as a villain mask misses Averra's rule: split is honest formal attention.

## Cultural Meaning

On Averra Isle, split means patterned garment and blank eyes held together as care.

## Usage

Studio boards and readiness walls. Not a scare face.
