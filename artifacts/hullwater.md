# Hullwater

## Name

Hullwater

## Overview

Hullwater is a clear bar drink that tastes like pear skin and makes the next sentence feel overheard two tables away.

## Description

It looks like water that has decided to be expensive. No foam. A chill. The taste is peel and a green sweetness with no pulp. After swallowing, the drinker's following sentence arrives in the room as if someone else had already repeated it. The words are not stolen. They are only early. Listeners hear them in their own ears a breath before the mouth finishes. The effect is one sentence, sometimes two if the speaker is drunk on their own news.

## Cultural Meaning

The gossip drink. People order it when they want a story to travel and then blame the cup. Kett Rind likes it because she already talks like that. Bram charges extra when the room is full, not because the drink is stronger, but because the tables are closer and the effect feels crueler.

## Usage

Do not order it if you meant to whisper a kindness. The kindness will arrive publicly and slightly too soon. It does not reveal hidden people, hidden fruit, or other bands. It only nudges a sentence into the air ahead of itself.

## Notes

Named by drinkers after hull-gossip, not by any pilot's manual. Non-ferry. Not a truth serum. Liars still lie. They simply lie where more people catch the shape.
