# Tv

## Name

Tv

## Overview

**Tv** is travelers' slang for the framed black slate on the long table at Kindle Spur Rest Nook, where a keeper chalks the next stretch of the Hearthline each evening, together with the taut wind strings strung across the nook's upper corner that hum when weather is turning.

## Description

The slate is plain, set upright in a wooden double frame at the table's end. Keepers draw only three things on it: the next peak along the route, its reflection in the lake at its foot, and the moon as it actually is that night. If the lake is frozen, the reflection is left off. If cloud is due, the moon is drawn half-erased. Across the corner above, four lines of waxed cord are strung at angles; in still air they are silent, and before wind they begin to hum. A keeper stands near them at dusk with one hand raised, listening, and updates the slate if the strings change their note.

## Cultural Meaning

F840 recovery ethics reject false comfort. The nook exists so people can find out whether they can continue, and the slate gives them honest information without a speech. Travelers look, listen to the strings, and decide for themselves. Keepers never tell anyone to go or stay.

## Usage

Redrawn every evening, wiped every morning. Travelers sometimes add a small mark at the slate's corner to show they went on; keepers leave those marks until the slate is full.
