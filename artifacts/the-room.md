# The Room

## Name

The Room

## Overview

**The Room** is how mainland Nauw refers to a particular arrangement said to be kept in Averra Isle meeting spaces: a blue-painted room containing exactly three red things—a door, a hanging lamp, and a low table—and nothing else.

## Description

The arrangement is known almost entirely from one plate and a few visitors' secondhand descriptions. Walls, floor, and ceiling are washed in blues of different depths. The door is red on its inner face. The lamp is a red bell-shaped shade on a long red cord, hung off-center. The table is low and bare, wide enough for several people to set things on while sitting on the floor around it. Visitors report that they were asked, gently but firmly, to leave red clothing at the threshold and to place anything they carried on the table before speaking.

## Cultural Meaning

Mainland interpreters argue about meaning, which is itself part of Averra's reputation. The common guess is that limiting the room to three red things keeps attention on what is brought to the table rather than on decoration. Isle courtesy prefers that outsiders not over-explain it.

## Usage

Reported in meeting and archive spaces on Averra. Mainland imitations exist in a few Veloria houses, where they are considered slightly showy.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Purpose deliberately unsettled; do not invent ceremonies.
