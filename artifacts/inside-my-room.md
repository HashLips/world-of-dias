# Inside My Room

## Name

Inside My Room

## Overview

**Inside My Room** is an Averra Isle perch-chamber set: paired white reveal-doors, a black rectangular pedestal left with its vertical stress-crack visible, and a cyan T-bar stand for one teal-marked bird or craft proxy, used when hallway red must stay outside a quiet look.

## Description

Doors are plain and bright so the opening itself is the signal. The pedestal’s jagged front line is not hidden with filler; studios teach that a stand telling the truth holds better resonance than a pretty lie. The T-bar is vivid cyan, wiped of grit before each perch. Living birds of teal-and-charcoal marking are invited, not trapped; cloth bird-proxies serve apprentices. The red outer hall remains saturated on purpose—warm traffic, damp talk—so the grey room can feel cool. Fingers that bang the doors earn a week of door-holding duty.

## Cultural Meaning

Around Averra Isle, saying a thing is inside my room means it deserves opened doors and closed mouths. Resonance favors the soft look. Dragging hallway arguments onto the pedestal is poor care.

## Usage

Damp-studio critiques, rare guest reveals, and morning perch checks. Stored with doors latched, T-bar bagged.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Matches doors, pedestal crack, cyan perch, teal bird.
