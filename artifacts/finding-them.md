# Finding Them

## Name

Finding Them

## Overview

**Finding Them** is a kept Songripe Isles call kit: a dark palm-board with a 3x3 white oval grid and teal bird tokens used to teach seeking by readable invitation rather than by chasing.

## Description

The palm-board is brown and open; nine white marks sit in a neat grid that must stay countable. Seed, salt, or chalk may fill the ovals depending on the isle. Bird tokens remind hosts what kind of neighbor the pattern calls. Isles rule: set the grid, wait, greet what arrives; do not run the hills empty-handed shouting names. Selling the grid as a lucky stamp without the wait-rule empties the craft.

## Cultural Meaning

Songripe prefers being findable over being loud.

## Usage

Guest reunions, path calls after storm, and children’s first seek drills. Lent with all nine marks intact.

## Notes

Nine-grid call hand. Invite; do not chase.
