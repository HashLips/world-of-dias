# Tick Tock

## Name

Tick Tock

## Overview

A **Tick Tock** disc is a training object used on the Glassfold Ledge: a large circle painted with fine red-and-blue stripes on a yellow-and-blue striped board, inside which a darker figure is hidden. It can only be seen by a viewer who sways gently instead of staring.

## Description

Discs are hand-ruled by Veil Scribes with a fine brush and a straightedge, and the hidden figure is made by darkening the stripes within its outline. Every disc's figure is different and kept secret from the apprentice who trains on it. The apprentice sits at arm's length, sways slowly side to side, and counts aloud—tick, tock—marking on a slate each count at which the figure surfaces. At the end, the scribe compares the slate with the disc's known rhythm. Apprentices who claim to see the figure while holding perfectly still are failed for the day.

## Cultural Meaning

F610's discipline is refusing to let unstamped certainty become policy. Tick Tock teaches the first half of that: reveals come in rhythm, and staring produces false sightings. Learning to record when you saw something, not just what, is the beginning of honest reveal work.

## Usage

Used in apprentice training at the Glassfold Phase Bench and repainted when an apprentice has memorized one. Retired discs are hung in ledge houses, where guests try them for fun.
