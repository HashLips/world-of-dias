# Reincarnation

## Name

Reincarnation

## Overview

**Reincarnation** is F840 echo-carry craft: a red keeper figure cradling a limp white form above a charcoal plane that shows fading translucent repeats—return shaped as careful holding, not as a shouted doctrine.

## Description

Realm halls keep the craft as a board and sometimes as a paired figure set. The red carrier's bowed head and large four-fingered hands are the care model; the white form stays limp and horizontal in the telling so the work is support, not parade. Echo layers on the dark plane are required in full copies—without them the craft loses its name. Local care forbids dropping the white form to watch the echoes splash, and forbids selling the set as a joke about endings. Resonance is the color split and the fade stack; memory is the hanging spindly arm in the ghosts; wonder is sage quiet above charcoal. Sets return with fingers unchipped. Soft talk that imports Earth afterlife sermons is set aside; on F840 the name is the carry-and-echo hour.

## Cultural Meaning

In F840, reincarnation means holding the limp form while admitting the fading repeats below.

## Usage

Hall boards and care drills. Not a festival drop-stunt.
