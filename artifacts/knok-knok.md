# Knock Soft

## Name

Knock Soft

## Overview

**Knock Soft** is a Mercer Shed door kit: a soft knuckle-pad, a thin listen-wedge for safe gap checks, and a cyan eye-disc hung as reminder that hard pounding is seen and stored, used before entering marked rooms.

## Description

The pad dulls sound without muting it to sneakiness. The wedge keeps fingers out of pinch. The cyan disc with tan lid-bands copies the plate’s wall-eye so children learn the watcher is local care, not a threat tale. Shed workers queue at pink-floor doors; the black yard-animal often arrives first and is not shooed if it stays quiet. Red-marked visitors beyond the pillar wait for the soft knock’s answer before stepping in.

## Cultural Meaning

Around Mercer Shed and Soft Mark manners, Knock Soft is entry care. Resonance sticks to patient queues. Kicking a door under the eye is a cold echo that follows a name.

## Usage

Shed rooms, Soft Mark adjacent stores, and teaching doors for apprentices. Pads replaced when hard.
