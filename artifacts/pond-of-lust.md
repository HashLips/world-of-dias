# Pond of Lust

## Name

Pond of Lust

## Overview

**Pond of Lust** is an Averra Isle shore-warning board: shell-pink standing figure on a terracotta band over dark teal, pink window marks above, and long pink drips showing where the boundary melts—pull named so it can be refused.

## Description

Isle studios paint Pond of Lust for damp edges and guest talks when wanting gets careless. The standing pink keeps arms close; the terracotta band holds other pink shapes already half under—knees, head-curve, reaching limb—so the board refuses a clean empty water story. Drips into teal are the honest instability: the pond is not a stable mirror you pose beside. Umber upper windows watch without becoming a bureaucracy of lust. Local care treats the name as a Dias place-rule, not as Earth gossip. Resonance is the melt-line; memory is the submerged set; wonder is how bright terracotta stays while teal waits below. Boards hang near real wet margins and return without graffiti that turns the warning into a dare. Soft talk that shoves someone toward the band for laughs is considered a break of Averra care. The craft is a named pull you can walk away from.

## Cultural Meaning

On Averra Isle, the pond's lust is attraction that drips. Standing careful is the rule.

## Usage

Shore boards, studio warnings, and guest-hall readiness. Not decorative party gloss.
