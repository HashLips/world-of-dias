# Lady in Red

## Name

Lady in Red

## Overview

**Lady in Red** is an F200 leap set: a vibrant red billowing dress, optional long gold hair-fall (wig or worn length), and a single neon-green pointed lead shoe, used for festival jumps that must stay readable against dark floors.

## Description

Dress cut allows swirl without trip. Gold hair-fall is weighted softly so it cascades left as in the plate without blinding the jumper. The green shoe marks the lead foot for spotters; its pair may be plain white or also green, but at least one neon point must show. Platform edges are taped lighter brown for depth. The name is local Dias talk for the set—not an imported lyric. After leaps, green shoes are counted back into a marked box.

In F200, Lady in Red is joyful motion with a lead-foot rule. Resonance prefers visible greens. Pulling hair-fall for laughs empties the festival care.
