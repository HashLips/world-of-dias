# Thin-Room Ear

## Overview

A **Thin-Room Ear** is a taboo listener toy—small, deniable, said to thicken absence when names stack past soft custom.

## Description

No agreed make: shell, wire, cold glass. Cells call it research. Softfruit Hall calls it sport. Ren’s seal arguments haunt its rumor chain.

## Cultural Meaning

Danger object—listening past consent. Third naming made handheld.

## Usage

Not for canonical protagonists as loot. Appears as seized evidence, bad gift, or dare.

Danger. Chapter starter: an Ear found in a child’s fair bag—or a tavern that goes quiet mid-joke.
