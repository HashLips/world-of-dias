# News

## Name

News

## Overview

**News** is the Velorian Basin rain-umbrella craft and the habit it teaches: walk bowed under a sky-blue canopy while golden-orange rain falls, and let the pale memory-double stay half a step behind without trying to shake it off.

## Description

The kept object is a simple umbrella: bright blue canopy, thin shaft, black hook handle large enough to feel in a wet grip. Basin walkers associate it with the hour when rain turns warm gold-orange and word arrives without a stamped sheet. People say you are carrying News when your own outline grows a pale pink double in the weather—memory matching your bow—and you do not spin around to argue with it. The umbrella is not sold as a charm that forces truth. It is lent or parked by doors when someone must walk through a weather of arrivals and still keep local care: head quiet, eye a slit, canopy small against a sky you do not own. Returning it cleaner means the hook is unbent, the canopy is dry enough to fold, and the carrier can say what walked behind them without turning the double into a rumor campaign. Hands that grab the umbrella only to look important in the yard miss the point; the craft is for receiving weight, not for performing a broadcast.

## Cultural Meaning

In the Velorian Basin, News is quieter than a shout and wetter than a sealed letter. The double is accepted as resonance, not as a haunting to be driven out.

## Usage

Carried in colored rain; sometimes hung by a shed peg as a sign that arrivals are being walked carefully. Rarely auctioned without the rain-story attached.

## Notes

Named for the arrival habit, not for a newspaper. Pair with the artwork plate in assets.
