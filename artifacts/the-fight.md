# The Fight

## Name

The Fight

## Overview

**The Fight** is a long teal-and-green cloth used in Lumira Sands to hold a quarrel without breaking the desert's calm. Two people who disagree lie head to head, one turned the opposite way, hands over their own faces, and the cloth is laid between them like a stream of water.

## Description

Fight cloths are dyed in two greens—a deep sea-green and a bright, almost glowing teal—so that laid across sand they ripple like a current. They are long enough to pass between two lying bodies with room at both ends. Nobody speaks while the cloth is down. Each person is expected to argue silently with their own reflection: what they wanted, what they said, what they would say next. Sands folk claim the cloth cools the air between quarrelers by a few degrees, and that you can see your own face faintly in its sheen if you look sideways.

## Cultural Meaning

In a place whose calm is a resource, a loud fight is a kind of theft. The cloth turns conflict inward first. The rule is not that disagreements must end, only that they must pass through reflection before they reach the other person. Witnesses are allowed but do not take sides.

## Usage

Kept rolled in oasis houses and at Quiet Well's edge; lent by whoever is nearest. When both parties lower their hands, a witness draws the cloth away and the speaking begins. A cloth used for a fight that ended well is hung in the shade overnight before being rolled again.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. No violence is part of the custom.
