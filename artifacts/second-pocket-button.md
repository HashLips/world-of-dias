# Second-Pocket Button

## Name

Second-Pocket Button

## Overview

A **Second-Pocket Button** sews itself back onto the garment it fell from, if you are still in the same room before dusk. It is mending, not luck you can spend on a journey.

## Description

Horn or dyed wood, two holes, a little thread already in love with it. Drop it, stay near, and by the time the light goes amber it is seated again, stitch slightly neater than the owner’s hand. Leave the room, or wait until after dusk, and it is only a button on the floor. It will not cross a street to find a coat, and it will not mend a decision.

## Cultural Meaning

A joke adults tell when they claim Farstock sells miracles. The miracle expires at the doorway. That is why Pim likes it.

## Usage

Sold in twists of paper, often with a warning said plainly. Tailors on the loop buy them and then refuse to admit it.

## Notes

Cannot retrieve stolen goods, people, or stones. The saga’s retrieval is not this button working at a larger scale.
