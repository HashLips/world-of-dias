# Girl with No Horns

## Name

Girl with No Horns

## Overview

**Girl with No Horns** is a kept F200 airing kit: a pale green hang-wire with pink clips, blue knee contact-caps, and purple crescent tokens used when a loud weight must be shed and left visible on the floor.

## Description

Hosts string the green line, clip pairs, and invite a careful hang drill—or a stand-in purple form—for equalization talk. Crescent tokens stay on the lavender cloth below as the shed pieces; hiding them mid-airing empties the craft. Frequency manners treat the title as plate-name, not as an Earth gender rule. Blue caps mark where contact is allowed. Selling crescents as jewelry without the shed-story is emptied craft.

## Cultural Meaning

F200 respects shedding that stays readable.

## Usage

Quiet airing hours, apology sheds, and apprentice weight-release drills. Lent as wire-clips-caps-crescents set.

## Notes
