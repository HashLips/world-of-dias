# Averra Offshore Courtesy Token

## Name

Averra Offshore Courtesy Token

## Overview

An **Averra Offshore Courtesy Token** is a small pale disc captains show, mostly to themselves and to each other, while they wait for the Gray Post to exist again. It is a manners print for an isle that inhales and exhales permission. No schedule is engraved, because a schedule would be a lie about Averra and a temptation to the holder. The blank face is the point.

## Description

Pale stone or glazed clay, a wave mark on one face, nothing on the other. Pocket-polished. It does not open a door, summon a pier, or impress a harbormaster who wants infrastructure to behave. Captains who rage do not lose the token. They lose the contracts of people who have watched them rage at fog. Harbors that mock the token find fewer Averra-adjacent agreements, not because of a curse, because the captains who wait well talk to each other and stop recommending the mockers. In a good hour the disc stays in the pocket even while you moor. Showing it at the bollard like a ticket is how you announce you have misunderstood.

## Cultural Meaning

Obscurity respected as ethics, not as an insult to your time. Waiting is a craft. The token is a reminder you can touch when the pier is absent and your crew wants a speech about betrayal by timber. Artists like the blank face. Old Masters rumor sometimes tries to claim the discs as signs. Wait Captains who let that claim ride have started selling a landing they do not have. Damp-studio gifts, when a landing actually happens, may include a token passed back, still blank. The blankness after the landing is the courtesy surviving success.

## Usage

Deck pockets. Quiet harbor boards beside a no-conquest habit. Not a boarding pass into Averra’s interior, not a key to Crown Ash, not a map of the Hearthline’s kilns. If you need the disc to feel powerful, you are not in the practice.

## Notes

Manners object. The isle remains obscure on purpose. The token helps you bear that without making it a hunt.
