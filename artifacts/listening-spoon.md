# Listening Spoon

## Name

Listening Spoon

## Overview

A **Listening Spoon** is a small tin spoon that hums, very quietly, with the nearest real cookpot. It does not hum with secrets, confessions, or bands.

## Description

Dented tin, short handle, a polite buzz in the molars when soup is on within a house or a stall. The hum matches boil and simmer. It goes still for whispers, ledgers, and people rehearsing lies. Held to a wall, it is only a spoon against a wall.

## Cultural Meaning

A reminder that resonance in Veloria’s outer ring is allowed to be domestic. Wonder that only serves power is not the only wonder the city keeps.

## Usage

Kitchens, travel kits, the joke of tapping it on Farstock’s counter to ask if the kettle is ready. Pim’s kettle usually is.

## Notes

Cannot be tuned to F500, F380, or a neighbor’s sealed talk. People who try look foolish, which is part of the design only by accident.
