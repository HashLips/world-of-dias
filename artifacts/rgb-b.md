# Rgb B

## Name

Rgb B

## Overview

**Rgb B** is Velorian Basin triple-blue measure craft: three elongated cobalt figures stacked on cream in crawl, recline, and kneel, white slit eyes, limbs opening into long fluid tendril-fingers—the B channel kept as its own honest set.

## Description

Basin workshops cut or paint Rgb B when a room needs the blue measure without mixing other channels into the same board. The three poses are required: top crawl with flame-like rear fingers, middle recline with mixed tapered and rounded digits, bottom kneel reaching a tentacle cluster. White eye-slits stay thin. Local care forbids painting red or green over this board to "complete" it for show, and forbids mocking the tendrils as unfinished hands. Resonance is cobalt on cream; memory is the pose stack; wonder is the multiplying tips. Boards travel between damp studios and return with edges clean. Soft talk that files the name as Earth screen jargon is ignored; in the basin Rgb B is the triple blue reach craft.

## Cultural Meaning

In the Velorian Basin, Rgb B means the blue measure held complete in three poses.

## Usage

Studio boards and readiness stacks. Not a incomplete sample swatch.
