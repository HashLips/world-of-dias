# Ox-Patience

## Name

Ox-Patience

## Overview

Ox-Patience is a thick drink that slows the drinker's blink and temper. Toll arguments order it when fists are closer than chalk.

## Description

Brown, glossy, sweet with cooked peel and a grain that sticks to the cup. It tastes like a road noon. After two swallows the blink goes long, the jaw unclenches, and hurry becomes slightly ridiculous. Thought does not stop. It loses its sharpest elbow. A person can still stand, drive oxen, and refuse a bad price. They are less likely to flip a bench while doing it.

## Cultural Meaning

Named in respect for Nauw Basin Oxen and for every driver who has waited on a marshal without becoming one. Puck Hennet drinks it when he is already patient, which Bram says is a waste and Puck says is maintenance. It is not a drug of erasure. Anger returns. It returns walking.

## Usage

Served in bowls more often than cups. Not for anyone about to fly a plane or pole a boat through a narrow cut. Lark Venn will not take it on a day she intends to lift.

## Notes

House drink. Non-ferry. Does not replace rest documented at a toll.
