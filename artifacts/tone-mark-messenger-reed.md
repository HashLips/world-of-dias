# Tone-Mark Messenger Reed

## Name

Tone-Mark Messenger Reed

## Overview

A **Tone-Mark Messenger Reed** carries a short tone-mark phrase along pot-signal cousins or Operator scrap hum—schedule pings, meet-me ticks, “ask again” nudges. Mythic messaging craft, not an app store.

## Description

Hollow reed with a sealed load bead; speaks in tones more than words. Can arrive wrong if late-mutter echo rides the line. Does not cross frequency bands. Houses hate when Softfruit uses them for lunch invites; Softfruit loves that hatred.

## Cultural Meaning

Belonging tempo made audible. Failure modes stay mythic: sealed load, wrong tick, null itch—never perfect always-on convenience.

## Usage

Held by clerks, Operators, and licensed runners; civilians borrow day-reeds for Festival lanes; powered down at night mute.

Culture anchors: Everyday Resonant Tech; restricted-tech manners still apply.
