# Running Over Nails

## Name

Running Over Nails

## Overview

**Running Over Nails** is Lumira Sands spike-passage craft: a mustard ground of thin black vertical spikes crossed by a pale-pink crouch under a magenta oval halo, with dark triangular pierce marks kept honest at contact points.

## Description

Sand practice yards build or paint the spike field as a teaching set—spikes regular, mustard base, pale yellow surround. The magenta halo is part of the craft: crossing without it is considered unfinished. Pierce triangles are not hidden in careful copies; denying contact is the named failure. Local care forbids forcing guests across for entertainment, and forbids yanking spikes out to fake an easy road. Resonance is halo above pain admitted; memory is the eye-slit calm; wonder is how orderly the black field can look. Sets return with spikes true. Soft talk that treats the name as cruelty bragging misses the sands rule: the craft is enduring passage with marks shown.

## Cultural Meaning

In Lumira Sands, running over nails means crossing a hard rule while admitting contact.

## Usage

Practice yards and readiness boards. Not a spectacle pit.
