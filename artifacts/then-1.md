# Then

## Name

Then

## Overview

A **Then** clock is a hand-sized, handless-case clock used in Hearthvale when a newcomer and a receiving household agree on the moment the newcomer's old life ended. Both set the hands together; after that, everything before that moment is called then.

## Description

Then clocks are assembled from scrap like much Hearthvale craft: a rust-red disc cut from old roof metal, a blue square of glass or enamel behind it, and a violet translucent square laid over as the face. The hands are plain white arrows on a blue hub, loose enough to turn with a fingertip. There are no numerals, because the time set is not a time of day but a time of agreement. Two people hold the clock between them, each with one hand, fingers framing rather than gripping, and move the arrows until both nod.

## Cultural Meaning

Hearthvale shelters people who left under shame or pressure. The Then clock gives arrivals control over where their past stops and gives hosts a promise not to keep reaching back past it. Asking someone about their then without being invited is considered unkind. The clock does not erase anything; it marks a border in time that both sides have chosen.

## Usage

Set once on arrival, kept on a shelf in the shared room, never reset by one person alone. If a newcomer later wants to talk about their then, they hand the clock to a friend first.
