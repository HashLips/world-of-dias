# Rim Gratitude Fish-Hook

## Name

Rim Gratitude Fish-Hook

## Overview

A **Rim Gratitude Fish-Hook** is returned after a rescue with a fish or a favor attached—help first, accounts second, gratitude made concrete without debt theater.

## Description

Simple curved hook; often painted orange on storyboards; never billed as interest. Listen posts hang returned hooks as manners trophies. Selling “rescue subscriptions” with fake hooks is pier fraud.

## Cultural Meaning

Mutual rescue literacy. Beauty of a line that pulls both ways over time.

## Usage

Distress listen crews; Singing Fishermen; Softfruit cousins teaching Distress Before Debt.
