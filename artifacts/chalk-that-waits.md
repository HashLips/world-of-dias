# Chalk That Waits

## Name

Chalk That Waits

## Overview

**Chalk That Waits** will not mark a surface until the holder has said aloud the thing they mean to write. It is a nuisance for liars and a mercy for tallies.

## Description

Short white sticks, slightly waxy, sold in Farstock beside ordinary chalk so the difference must be learned by use. Spoken truth — even an ugly truth — lets the line come. A rehearsed false reason produces dust and no mark. It does not judge the goodness of the truth, only whether the mouth and the hand are in the same story. Once the sentence is said, it writes like chalk and washes off like chalk.

## Cultural Meaning

Kin to toll manners and to Flexible Tick Pencils, though it does not bend with emotional weather. It waits on speech. Outer-ring clerks who hate it are often the reason it sells.

## Usage

Practice slates, children’s wagers, Harl Fenlow’s pocket on the night he tried to write that they were only looking. The chalk waited. He did not say the theft out loud. The stick left his fingers clean.

## Notes

Not a contract engine and not admissible as a Resonant House instrument. A person can still do harm after speaking truly. The chalk only refuses the cheap version.
