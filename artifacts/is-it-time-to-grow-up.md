# Is It Time to Grow Up

## Name

Is It Time to Grow Up

## Overview

**Is It Time to Grow Up** is a Driftfall rack-and-case set: a grey peg wall of identical red cone-caps with white trim and tip-puffs, plus a lime-green handled case of pier tools, used when a worker decides they are ready to take one cap into weather without a minder.

## Description

Caps are cloth-and-felt cones sized for head or for covering a wet resonance node on rail gear—local use varies by crew, but the wall count stays sacred. The lime case holds line-wax, soft scrapers, and spare trim rings. Morning supervisors ask the title question aloud; a yes means one cap leaves, hooks stay even. Returning a soaked cap without drying it first is the classic not-yet. Children play with spare tip-puffs only, never the numbered wall row.

## Cultural Meaning

Around Driftfall, growing up is inventory honesty plus weather readiness. Resonance sticks to the returned dry cap. Mocking the cone shape with Earth-holiday jokes is considered imported noise; pier talk keeps the caps as work marks.

## Usage

Pier sheds, apprentice first-solos, and storm prep. Caps counted nightly.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. No Earth festival reading.
