# House Margin Ribbon

## Name

House Margin Ribbon

## Overview

A **House Margin Ribbon** is tied along a packet edge to reserve scrape-space for disclaimers, no-ferry marks, and ask-again notes. Evidence craft against clean pages that ate their honesty.

## Description

Thin cloth in House colors with a blank white run for ink. Partial packets wear them proudly; Forced-Final clerks snip them and inherit the stamp-that-ate myth.

## Cultural Meaning

Margin as civic virtue. Beauty of unfinished paper.

## Usage

Issa Partial’s students; Inkspan desks; Softfruit pinning ribbons on school boards beside ferry stubs.
