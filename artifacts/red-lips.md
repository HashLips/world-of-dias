# Red Lips

## Name

Red Lips

## Overview

**Red Lips** is an F610 profile board: pale greenish-white angular face, thin closed black eye, small sharp red lips, red ribbon loops for a headpiece, and a long black neck on olive—quiet heat without a demanded gaze.

## Description

Realm halls keep Red Lips when a room needs a face that rests the eyes. The closed black slit is intentional; the red mouth stays small and sharp; the ribbon loops are the craft's bright memory marks, one often curving toward the chest in careful copies. Black neck and shoulders hold the pale head steady. Local care forbids painting the eye open "to make it friendlier," and forbids using the board as a flirt joke that erases the quiet rule. Resonance is red on pale and olive; memory is the ribbon path; wonder is how little line can still mean a whole face. Boards return with lips and loops clean. Soft talk that confuses this craft with louder passion plates misses F610's narrower care: closed eye, small red, held still.

## Cultural Meaning

In F610, red lips means quiet heat on a resting gaze.

## Usage

Quiet halls and readiness walls. Not a loud festival face.
