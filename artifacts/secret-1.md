# Secret

## Name

Secret

## Overview

**Secret** is Cinder Nook sealed-panel craft: an orange grommeted plate hung on yellow lines, red circular seal bearing a white bird, orange ribbon strands from a top slot, and a black profile kept below until the seal's care is met.

## Description

Nook kilns and quiet walls keep Secret as a hung panel. Corner grommets and yellow lines are the mount honesty; the red seal's white bird is the recognition mark; the five orange ribbons are not decoration to yank. The black profile in the full plate is the waiting listener—closed white sliver, no peel. Blue diagonal may cut behind as the hour's measure beam. Local care forbids breaking the seal for gossip, and forbids displaying an empty plate as if sealed. Resonance is bird on red on orange; memory is the ribbon drape; wonder is gray holding such formal color. Panels return with seals intact. Soft talk that treats every secret as a dare to open misses the Nook rule: sealed means held.

## Cultural Meaning

In Cinder Nook, secret means a bird-sealed panel you approach with a quiet profile.

## Usage

Quiet walls and readiness hangs. Not a pry toy.

Named for sealed display. Pair with the artwork plate in assets.
