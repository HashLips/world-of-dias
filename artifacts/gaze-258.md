# Gaze

## Name

Gaze

## Overview

**Gaze** is a kept F120 point-and-look kit: a blue profile token, a pointing hand, and a jagged black starburst mark with grey center used to teach that attention must name its object.

## Description

Instructors hang the starburst, place the pointing hand beneath, and ask apprentices to speak one concrete sentence about the grey center before offering opinions. Vague “I just feel it” answers restart the drill. Frequency hosts treat the kit as pedagogical signage, not as an Earth occult eye. Selling the starburst alone as a mystery charm empties the craft.

## Cultural Meaning

F120 distrusts unnamed staring.

## Usage

Apprentice observation drills, guest briefings, and quiet dispute openings. Lent as mark-plus-pointer set.

## Notes

Point-and-look kit. Name what you gaze.
