# Eight-Band Difference Chapbook

## Name

Eight-Band Difference Chapbook

## Overview

A stitched pocket book with one spread per established frequency: fundamental difference, one sensory tell, one wrong confusion, and a pointer to the realm entry—not a ferry schedule.

## Description

Matte covers, dull ink, maps reduced to icons so no one navigates by them. Softfruit editions smell faintly of peel; House editions include a Claim Grade reminder that catalog completeness is Theory-open. Children color the F432 spread first; juniors are tested on F200/F500 poles before quieter bands.

## Cultural Meaning

Makes Frequency as fundamental law teachable without turning Dias into a tourist brochure.

## Usage

Gifted after bleed nights, wrong-map seizures, and Softfruit orientation. Averra exhibits leave copies beside beautiful wrong charts.
