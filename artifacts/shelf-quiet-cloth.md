# Shelf-Quiet Cloth

## Name

Shelf-Quiet Cloth

## Overview

**Shelf-Quiet Cloth** is a plain square that makes a shelf feel paused: dust settles slower on it, and objects set there are less pawed by the room’s attention. It is housekeeping with a lean. It is not a Hearttide Holding Cloth.

## Description

Undyed linen, soft at the edges, no rose-amber gradient and no cove weave. Spread under an object, it lends a small stillness — not silence, not consent-magic, not a share of anyone’s mood. The Heartlean Colorstone rests on it and returns toward gray more readily. A stolen mood does not stain it brittle, because it was never asked to regulate a heart. It can line a wooden case. That is the grandest thing it does.

## Cultural Meaning

Farstock’s answer to people who arrive having heard of holding cloths and want a cheaper feeling. Pim will talk about the difference until they buy candy instead. Ease is not in the linen.

## Usage

Shelves, the stone’s case, the retrieval on the Long Gate, when the case was opened and the cloth gave the color a place to be put down. The cloth did not open a way. It received what had been standing up.

## Notes

Do not merge this entry with Hearttide Holding Cloth. Cove consent and shop keeping are relatives, not the same tool. Exit and putting-down remain different manners.
