# Pocket Load Slate

## Name

Pocket Load Slate

## Overview

A **Pocket Load Slate** is a hand-sized resonant slate for jotting load ticks, meet marks, and scrape notes away from a full Frame. Personal craft for clerks and Row makers—not a smartphone, not a band door.

## Description

Matte face; chalk or tone-stylus; can show helpful agreement if left graphing past manners. Cousin to larger watch-passage slates. Children are not sold full core terminals; apprentices earn scratched day-slates first.

## Cultural Meaning

Portable noticing. Anti-stare manners apply: power down when the glass smiles too much.

## Usage

Row pockets, Inkspan desks, Softfruit field interviews; never treated as ferry navigation.

## Notes

Soft limits locked. Holder class: licensed makers, clerks, rare civilians under mentor marks.
