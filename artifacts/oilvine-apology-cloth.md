# Oilvine Apology Cloth

## Name

Oilvine Apology Cloth

## Overview

An **Oilvine Apology Cloth** is a soft rag kept for midnight tool apologies—wipe, speak, do not perform. Craft manners object, not magic eraser.

## Description

Smells of oilvine; dark-stained; often folded under benches. Used after forced spools, rushed latches, or jokes that treated instruments like servants. Does not guarantee forgiveness; the oil-apology myth already taught incomplete mercy on vault night.

## Cultural Meaning

Honesty toward tools. Cousin to scrape blades for paper and apology cloths for metal/glass.

## Usage

Row makers, Glassfold ledge workers, Softfruit cooks after burnt pots; spoken apology still required.
