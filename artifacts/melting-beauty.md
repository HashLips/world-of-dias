# Melting Beauty

## Name

Melting Beauty

## Overview

**Melting Beauty** is a Veloria City vessel set: a red-shelled jug lined to release teal fluid under stress, a navy drip tray/table, and three yellow spark-stems for marking useful emissions, used when beauty and leak must be handled together.

## Description

Trays catch viscous teal before floor stain. Spark-stems clip to the mouth when readings still rise. Makers train not to seal a collapsing red wall just to look pretty. Children watch spark counts; they do not taste teal. Adults who chase sparks while drips run off the table redo tray duty for a week.

## Cultural Meaning

In Veloria City, Melting Beauty is care under failure of form. Resonance favors caught drips and counted sparks. Performing melt for spectacle empties the set.

## Usage

Studio stress tests, clerk caution displays, and teaching tables. Trays cleaned; stems racked.
