# Redmile Quiet

## Name

Redmile Quiet

## Overview

Redmile Quiet is a dark sip that softens the drinker's footsteps for a while. Road crews dislike it. Quiet feet near a redmile already mean trouble.

## Description

Almost black, with a scent of wet bark and cold tea. It is small on purpose. A full mug would be a prank Bram refuses to pour. After it, boot-nails stop announcing themselves. Cloth still rustles. Breath still exists. The floor simply gets less of the person. The effect is mundane-odd, a padding of sound, not a removal from sight.

## Cultural Meaning

Named for the scrub stretches where Strider reports and bell-dense spacing are already the manners. To drink your steps quiet in a bar is a joke. To drink them quiet before a night road is how people get called raid-adjacent even if they only wanted to leave without a farewell.

## Usage

Bram pours it for gamblers and for anyone who asks too sweetly. He waters the second one. Nia Toll will not sit beside a cup of it. It is not invisibility and not a ferry.

## Notes

House drink of The Peel and Bell. The name is a warning, not a license to skip a waystation.
