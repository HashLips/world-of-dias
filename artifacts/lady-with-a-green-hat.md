# Lady with a Green Hat

## Name

Lady with a Green Hat

## Overview

**Lady with a Green Hat** is an F380 listen-hat: a long bright-green tapering curve fixed on a lime attachment disc, worn during closed-eye hours when other senses lead, used so bystanders know to approach soft and from the kinder side.

## Description

Hat body is light stiff felt or grown resin-fiber, balanced so the tip arcs without neck strain. The lime disc seats snug; loose discs mean do not wear. Wearers keep eyes closed or soft-lashed as in the plate. Rooms hang a deep red cloth behind practice seats for contrast drills. Children wear short green tips first. Adults who tap the tip to “wake” someone mid-listen replace the disc as apology.

## Cultural Meaning

In F380, the green hat is consented quiet. Resonance prefers side-speech. Treating the hat as costume mockery empties its care.

## Usage

Listen hours, frequency rest niches, and teaching rooms. Hats hung on wide pegs; discs checked.
