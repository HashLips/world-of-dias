# Double Echo

## Name

Double Echo

## Overview

**Double Echo** is a kept F200 vessel kit: a pale V-glass for a red measure and a skewer of cobalt echo-spheres used when a second reading must land without emptying the first.

## Description

The glass is stemmed and wide, meant to sit only where a careful mouth or stand can keep it level. Red fill marks the agreed first measure—do not top it off to look generous. Cobalt spheres on a thin skewer are the second-reading tokens; one may be released to fall toward the eye-mark as a teaching drop. Spilling the red while chasing the blue is the fail. Frequency hosts lend the kit for dispute sips and quiet calibrations of memory. Selling spheres as candy charms empties the craft.

## Cultural Meaning

F200 treats a second echo as care only when the first measure stays honest.

## Usage

Frequency dispute readings, apprentice balance drills, and guest second-looks. Lent as glass plus skewer set.

## Notes

Double-measure vessel. Catch the blue; keep the red.
