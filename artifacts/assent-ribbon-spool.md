# Assent Ribbon Spool

## Overview

The **Assent Ribbon Spool** holds blue ribbon for Rope Tree promises—civic joy with threshold teeth.

## Description

Salt-silvered wood spool; ribbon the color of belonging. Watch houses keep spools counted. A cut ribbon found early is a story, not a craft project.

## Cultural Meaning

Corin’s first tie made supply chain. Assent is public.

## Usage

First-landing rites; teaching children; evidence in belonging trials.

## Notes

Joy object. Chapter starter: spool short; someone cut ribbons overnight.
