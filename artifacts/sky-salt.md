# Sky-Salt

## Name

Sky-Salt

## Overview

Sky-Salt is a salty foam drink that makes the drinker want to look up. It does not call Sky Drifters, and it cannot make them land.

## Description

Clear liquid under a coarse white foam, served cold. The salt is real, from a rim-coast sack Bram buys like any kitchen. The foam collapses into a bright, mineral finish. A moment later the neck insists. Chin lifts. Eyes find the window or the lamp-smoke or the empty air above the bell. If Drifters are in the sky, the drinker sees them because the head turned, not because they came.

## Cultural Meaning

Outer-ring humor about a city that always has something overhead and rarely has language for it. Drinking Sky-Salt is a way to admit you are going to stare. It is bad manners to pretend the drink summoned what was already drifting. Drifters are not vehicles and not replies.

## Usage

One glass. A second makes the neck ache and the philosophy worse. Children are given the foam on a spoon and no sermon.

## Notes

Explicitly not a hail, a hook, or a ferry. The desire to look up ends. The Drifters' nature does not get explained by a bar.
