# Looked from

## Name

Looked from

## Overview

**Looked from** is an F840 rest kit: a wavy ochre mound-cloth, a pale pink disc hung or laid behind, and a bright blue strand-sash worn from crown along the back, used for crouch hours when looking out must stay soft.

## Description

Mound-cloth cushions knees. Pink disc blocks harsh chart glare. Blue sash must hang free—pinning it is the classic mistake. Travelers learn the eye-slit quiet before speaking. Children use short sashes. Adults who step across the pink to startle get the mound for a silent count before they may talk again.

## Cultural Meaning

In F840, Looked from is protected looking. Resonance favors free blue strands. Treating the crouch as laziness empties the kit.

## Usage

Rest niches, post-route cool-downs, and teaching rooms. Cloths aired; sashes coiled.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Matches crouch rest plate.
