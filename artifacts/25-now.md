# 25 Now

## Name

25 Now

## Overview

**25 Now** is a kept register-pair used on the Nauw Outer Rim Seas: a red three-legged board and a lime-green screen that together hold one counted present. People borrow the pair when a harbor hour must be remembered without becoming a rumor mill.

## Description

The board is a short red frame on three legs, meant to stand angled toward whoever is being counted. Its face takes one dark organic mark—often a wavy stroke that reads as a living silhouette rather than a letter. The screen is a small lime-green display on a stand, angled the other way, showing a sharp geometric icon that Outer Rim talk calls an eye-bird. Between them, the habit places two split-tone stand-ins: one black-white, one white-orange, under a thin blue arch that means both readings share the same now. Floor light from board and screen should meet without fighting. If the green pool eats the yellow rays, or the board’s mark is wiped to flatter the screen, the count is already spoiled. Hands that borrow the pair return both pieces together; splitting them is how a twenty-sixth reading sneaks in.

## Cultural Meaning

The object teaches a sea-rim rule: a present moment may be held two ways at once, but not endlessly rewritten. Twenty-five honest now-counts is the local courtesy ceiling. Beyond that, memory becomes performance.

## Usage

Set up for harbor disputes, guest arrivals, and quiet weather-claims. Shown, lent, and parked near a listening pier; rarely sold without the blue-arch courtesy spoken aloud.

## Notes

Register-pair, not a trophy. Board and screen travel as one.
