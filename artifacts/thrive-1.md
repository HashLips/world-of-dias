# Thrive

## Name

Thrive

## Overview

A **Thrive** shawl is a broad mint-colored cloth patterned with flowing rust shapes, made at Warm Current Holding Cove for two people to wear together during a tone-share. Draped over both sets of shoulders, it holds them close while their Hearttide moods even out.

## Description

The shawls are woven wide and soft, mint ground with irregular rust-orange pools and a single pale cream stripe that is supposed to fall between the two wearers. People sit or lie cheek to cheek under it, usually in the cove's warmest shallows. Over a long breath or several, F380's coupling does its work: wearers report that their colors seem to bleed—one person's flush showing on the other's lips, a calmer person's stillness reaching into a restless partner's eyes. The effect fades in about a day, which keepers consider exactly right.

## Cultural Meaning

Thrive shawls make closeness into an exchange, not a rescue. Both people give a little tone and take a little. The cove's consent rule applies in full: both must ask, both must agree, and either may lift the shawl off at any moment without explanation. Sharing with someone who did not ask is treated as a serious breach.

## Usage

Kept folded on warm stones at the cove, lent for an afternoon, washed in the tributary between uses. Long-term partners sometimes weave their own and bring it back each season.
