# Span Toll Book

## Overview

A **Span Toll Book** records crossings—coin, labor-rest, stamps, and marshal initials—metered evidence with chalk weather in the margins.

## Description

Grease-thumbed binding; columns for route, payer, rest-hours owed, and “almost enough” notes. Nessa-style hands write small. Fake stamps leave ghosts the paper remembers poorly and marshals remember well.

## Cultural Meaning

Ansa’s never-a-shrug made portable. Not a novel—an argument waiting for overlap.

## Usage

Investigation: who crossed before a convoy vanished; labor-rest disputes; forged stamp hunts.
