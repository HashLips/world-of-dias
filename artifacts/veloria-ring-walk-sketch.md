# Veloria Ring Walk Sketch

## Name

Veloria Ring Walk Sketch

## Overview

A folded settlement-level sketch used by Softfruit hosts and ring clerks: four concentric bands, labeled approaches, and a short walk list—enough for a book atlas chapter without pretending the core is tourist-open.

## Description

Ink on soft paper, often grease-stained at the outer-market crease. Rings are numbered outward-in or labeled Outer / Second / Third / Core. Pins mark DEB Gate, Softfruit Table Hall, Pot Signal Lane, Ringwash Baths, Calibration Row, and Core Approach Overlook. A dashed “do not schedule” note sits on the core. Sky Drifters are drawn as open ovals above, never touching roofs.

Matches Rings of Veloria Primer and Veloria Walkable Atlas of Dias. Does not contradict F432 Map V2 regional placement of the city in the basin.

## Cultural Meaning

Proves settlement-level map depth exists for the capital—realm and regional charts alone are not enough for a walkable mental map.

## Usage

Gifted to new ring-workers; pinned in Softfruit orientation; copied (badly) by tourists who still get lost on the third ring lean.

## Notes

Settlement-level atlas artifact for Phase II map consistency. Not a ferry diagram.
