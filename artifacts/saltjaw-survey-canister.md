# Saltjaw Survey Canister

## Name

Saltjaw Survey Canister

## Overview

The Saltjaw Survey Canister is the waterproof tube Ansel Kestry carried strapped to his chest on the shelf survey to keep his field leaves dry. After the strike he wrote the Kestry Letter on its flat, sealed the letter inside, over-greased the cap, and released it into the outward run of a Long Slack. It came ashore twelve days later at Low Bell Cove on the Lumira coast, where Wick Hollen found it. It is the vessel that carried the letter. It is not itself a message, a relic, or a device.

## Description

- **Body:** a tube of tinned copper about as long as a forearm and as thick as a wrist. It came ashore dented and salt-crusted.
- **Cap:** a screw cap, **round-turned in the Saltjaw pattern**. Driftfall yards turn theirs square-edged, and Saltjaw turns them round. It is seated on a **greased leather ring**. Ansel greased the ring with his tallow twist thickly, all the way round, and screwed it down harder than any day's work needs. "Someone wanted this to stay dry for a long time," Odile said.
- **Strap ring:** a ring on one side with a scrap of strap still fastened. The rest of the strap was cut or torn when he let it go.
- **Marking:** the **Sprout Vent sign** (a core circle inside a ring of ticks) scratched into the tin near the cap. Driftfall oral charts use it, and surveyors mark their gear with it.
- **Float line:** it lies low in the water with only the cap showing.
- **Contents when found:** twelve sheets of heavy waxed survey paper. Eleven were blank. The twelfth was the folded, sealed Kestry Letter. The two wax pencils, the chalk stub and the tallow twist were not inside; Ansel kept them.

## Cultural Meaning

On the Sorel coast a survey canister means a working surveyor and paper that matters. On the Lumira coast it means the sea has brought a letter. Wick recognized it because he had worked three seasons on Driftfall Salvage Shore.

## Usage

Before the strike, carried strapped on the chest during soundings. After it, the letter's vessel. Now it is kept empty in a drawer at the Letter House, labelled in Odile's hand.

## Notes

The boom watch on Aumere saw "something shiny, like a fish" go out on the run. That is how Ansel learned his letter might have escaped.
