# Secondfire Kiln Walk Sketch

## Name

Secondfire Kiln Walk Sketch

## Overview

A kiln-town settlement sketch: courtyard fires, slate boards of names-in-progress, first-bowl approach from Long Gate road, and the quiet room where refusal is allowed dignity.

## Description

Charcoal and kiln-ash fingerprint at the corner. Teaching fire courtyard centered; workshop rings around it. Slate wall doodled with blank name lines. Laughter Week banner loft marked closed between seasons. Arrow north toward Long Gate / Hearthvale approach; arrow west toward Westfold Toll country; arrow east toward Flagweek paths. No Concord ring geometry.

Matches Hearthvale territory placement under Sorel. Compact manners, not ring wafers.

## Cultural Meaning

Second beginning as walkable space: heat, slate, table—not a wasteland backdrop.

## Usage

Given to new arrivals after first bowl; Hanna Kiln keeps the least-smudged copy.

## Notes

Settlement-level atlas artifact. Refusal corner is labeled; not a dungeon.
