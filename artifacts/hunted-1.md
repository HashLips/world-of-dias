# Hunted

## Name

Hunted

## Overview

**Hunted** is a Brindle March path-aim set: a yellow practice bow, a blue blunt-tipped arrow, and a pouch of small teal watcher tokens placed along a training track so an aim stays public and stoppable.

## Description

The bow is bright yellow recurve wood or laminated reed, sized for drill rather than war boasts. The blue arrow is weighted for show of line, not for piercing livestock. Teal tokens—carved heads or stamped shoulder-shapes—are set along the pink-beige path edge before a drill begins; a few may be hung on thin cords to “float” as the plate shows. The white crouch role and red archer role rotate. March youth learn to hold the draw until a yield word comes, then lower. Adults who skip watcher tokens and shoot private are fined in labor at the thank-you lean, not in blood. The set smells of bow oil and ditch dust.

## Cultural Meaning

On Brindle March, Hunted is rehearsal for restraint under eyes. Resonance prefers the arrow that returns to the quiver after a clean yield. The teal row means: you are not alone with your aim.

## Usage

Path drills, festival accuracy games with blunt tips, and rare dispute cooling when two parties agree to a watched aim-and-yield instead of a brawl. Stored unstrung.
