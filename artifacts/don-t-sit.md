# Don't Sit

## Name

Don't Sit

## Overview

**Don't Sit** is a kept Averra Isle sit-refusal marker: a small yellow chair placed behind a pink platform stripe so guests practice waiting until the room’s permission is spoken.

## Description

The yellow chair is ordinary and tempting. The pink platform is the only surface you may touch while waiting—hands optional, seat forbidden. Some studios hang a black arch-token above as a grin-reminder; others rely on the plate alone. Hosts use it before hard talks and critique hours. Sitting early is corrected once with humor, then firmly. The artifact pairs with Chair Room’s three-seat courtesy: this one teaches the pause before those seats fill. Selling the yellow chair as cute décor without the refusal rule empties the craft.

## Cultural Meaning

Averra likes welcome and distrusts premature settling.

## Usage

Pre-talk lobbies, damp-studio waits, and guest hall pauses. Lent with the platform cloth.

## Notes

Sit-refusal marker. Chair empty until spoken leave.
