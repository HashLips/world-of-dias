# Feeding Them

## Name

Feeding Them

## Overview

**Feeding Them** is a kept Velorian Basin share-seed kit: a pale pour-hand stand-in, golden seed measure, and red-crest bird tokens used to teach feeding smaller neighbors without turning the act into status theater.

## Description

The kit is small—seed pouch, four bird tokens with red crests, and a kneeling-care reminder card. Basin hosts bring it when guests need a manners drill before market or path. Pour low, pour measured, leave enough for the stand. Cyan ribbon marks on the card mean willingness is visible; the single red eye-mark means watch what you drop. Selling the kit as charity branding empties it. “Them” stays open: birds first in the plate, other small neighbors by local extension.

## Cultural Meaning

Basin care prefers quiet full bellies over loud generosity.

## Usage

Path rests, guest apologies, and children’s first share drills. Lent with seeds topable and tokens complete.

## Notes

Share-seed kit. Feed; do not perform.
