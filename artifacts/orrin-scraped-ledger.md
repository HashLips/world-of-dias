# Orrin Scraped Ledger

## Name

Orrin Scraped Ledger

## Overview

Orrin Veld’s pocket ledger where corrections are **scraped, never erased**. It is the portable face of Row’s scraped-correction preference—oil-stained pages that remember every wrong certainty.

## Description

Small enough for an apron pocket. Softfruit peel bookmark. Columns for Frame runs, Softfruit interviews, anonymous scrap summaries, and personal vows (“count without lying”). Early pages file Frederick Lumens as *orchard man who wants a door*. Middle pages track amber help and cyan drift. Late pages leave blank lines where widen hunger almost won.

When a digit is wrong, Orrin scrapes and rewrites beside the scar. Readers of the ledger (Tam, sometimes Sera) can see the history of almost-agreement. House juniors who glimpse it call it messy; Operators who glimpse it call it a liability; Auntie calls it proof he has not slept.

## Cultural Meaning

Method as character. The saga’s science is not clean revelation—it is revision you can still feel under your thumb.

## Usage

Daily field notes; source excerpts for the Issa Partial Packet; teaching prop for apprentices learning scrape manners.
