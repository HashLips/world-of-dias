# The Soft Mark

## Name

The Soft Mark

## Overview

The Soft Mark is a basin blimp, a slow gas craft with a peel painted on its bag, hired to hang over markets and sheds when someone with VEL marks wants the sky to wear a sign. It is a vehicle. Sky Drifters are not.

## Description

A long envelope of doped cloth, dun until the paint, then a ridiculous bright peel with a bite taken out. A small gondola underneath for Cress Adel and one ballast hand. Engines are modest and sulky. The craft moves when the wind is argued with, not when it is ignored. Cables, sand, a valve wheel polished by anxious palms. From the ground it blocks a patch of gilding light and looks, to outer-ring eyes, like a cloud that took a job.

It cannot land in a lent field without a crew, a calm, and a willingness to be hated by the grass. Mostly it does not land near the fruit at all. It loiters. The sign is the work.

Inside the gondola the technology is present-day for the third ring: gauges, a speaking tube, a heater that smells like hot dust. None of it thinks. None of it opens another band.

## Cultural Meaning

Basin people treat blimps as boastful weather. Children wave. Wagon drivers do not yield to a shadow. A blimp over an auction says the host paid for attention he hopes not to need. Cress finds this vulgar and cashes the hire anyway.

## Usage

Private aerial notice and, rarely, a lift of light parcels that cannot bruise. Not fruit-road logistics. Not a Drifter. Not a ferry. During the Vitrin show it hung over Mercer Shed and made the cloth below look smaller than the sign above, which was not the comfort Dain wanted once the day turned.

## Notes

Linked from Transport of Dias. Lift is gas and ballast, explained as far as a blimp is ever explained, and boring on purpose so it cannot be confused with the Stillwood Skiff.
