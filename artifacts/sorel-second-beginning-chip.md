# Sorel Second-Beginning Chip

## Name

Sorel Second-Beginning Chip

## Overview

A **Sorel Second-Beginning Chip** is presented when someone claims the right to remake a craft, name, or yard role after exile weather—without pretending the past never happened. Dignity craft, not amnesia.

## Description

Slate-colored chip; scratched once per beginning; kept visible. Houses that demand clean erase instead of scrape inherit Softfruit scorn.

## Cultural Meaning

Second beginning as honesty. Sibling to kiln laughter tokens and scraped ledgers.

## Usage

Sorel yard stewards; Long Gate courtesy cousins; Hearthvale mentors hosting remakers.
