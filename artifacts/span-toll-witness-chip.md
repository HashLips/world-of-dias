# Span Toll Witness Chip

## Name

Span Toll Witness Chip

## Overview

A **Span Toll Witness Chip** is dropped into the toll book tray when a talk-token dispute needs a third party’s mark. Evidence craft against pretty numbers that still tip unfair.

## Description

Bone or kiln clay; scratched with the witness’s tick. Not for sale as “immunity chips.” Cousin energy to fairness cards when sums match and weight does not.

## Cultural Meaning

Open ledger manners at a choke point. Unsigned chips are scrap; signed chips are care.

## Usage

Span Toll clerks; Softfruit auditors; Brindle drivers calling foul on load dumps.
