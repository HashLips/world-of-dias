# Little Romance

## Name

Little Romance

## Overview

**Little Romance** is an F500 lean set: a soft pink ground cloth, optional practice straps for balance, and a removable sharp triangular vane that clips to hip or back, used to rehearse close leans without grabbing the oddity.

## Description

Partners mark foot points on the pink cloth. The vane teaches spatial manners—point means clearance. Children practice far apart first. Adults who yank a vane mid-lean mend it and sit out the next round. Name stays local Dias talk for small shared care.

In F500, Little Romance is consented closeness with room for strange gear. Resonance favors soft pink ground and free triangles. Staging leans for mockery empties the set.
