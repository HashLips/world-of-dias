# Festival Sky-Whale Lantern

## Overview

**Festival Sky-Whale Lanterns** are civic light-constructs flown or hung in whale-festival seasons—joy objects that make the sky feel briefly companionable.

## Description

Paper, lightwood, and careful flame or glow-cell cousins; shaped like gentle keels of light. Not living Sky Drifters; not Outer Rim whales. Children know the difference and still gasp.

## Cultural Meaning

Wonder without Operator clearance. Softfruit evenings sometimes pause for a lantern drift.

## Usage

Festival display; teaching scale and gentleness; rare runaway lantern as chapter hook.

## Notes

Joy/civic. Chapter starter: a lantern drifts off-route toward the core glow—or toward Bleed-Sky.
