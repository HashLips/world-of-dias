# No-Ferry Stub Press

## Name

No-Ferry Stub Press

## Overview

A **No-Ferry Stub Press** is a small brass press that prints cheerful band-destination stubs and immediately overstrikes them with a **No-Ferry Mark**. Comedy tool that teaches the soft limit in ink.

## Description

Sits on Ferrylie Joke Pier counters and school boards after partial publish. Tourists keep the stubs; seekers who want a subway hate the second strike. The press never opens a door—only names the ache.

## Cultural Meaning

Soft-limit literacy as souvenir craft. Houses sometimes try to ban it for “undermining seriousness”; Softfruit sells tea beside it.

## Usage

Pier joke sales; classroom demos; Row ethics shelves next to fake tickets.
