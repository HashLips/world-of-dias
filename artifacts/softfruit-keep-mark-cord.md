# Softfruit Keep-Mark Cord

## Name

Softfruit Keep-Mark Cord

## Overview

A **Softfruit Keep-Mark Cord** is a short resonant string tied to a bowl, crate, or ladle to mark fruit held for sharing rather than sale. Humble everyday tech: the keep outranks the coin for the length of a meal.

## Description

Braided peel-fiber with a warm bead that hums faintly when the bowl is greeted aloud. Not a lock. Not a ferry. Vendors knot it before Flagweek breakfast and after restoration picks; cutting it early is considered stamp manners dressed as hunger.

## Cultural Meaning

Ladle-before-stamp made portable. Cousin energy to Wired Bloom Markers without orchard law attached.

## Usage

Tied by Softfruit vendors and fruit-road crews; checked at ladle courts; cited in petty disputes before House clerks are invited.

## Notes

Holders: Softfruit table keepers, waystation cooks. Place: Softfruit Ladle Court / Table Hall.
