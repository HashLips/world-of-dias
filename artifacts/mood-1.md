# Mood

## Name

Mood

## Overview

**Mood** is an Ashcalm Rise neck-ring set: bright orange solid coils stacked on a long neck (or neck-sleeve), usually taught as seven for steady work, worn with a flat black cap, used so mood is countable before a talk begins.

## Description

Rings click soft. Caps sit brow-level as in the plate. Speakers count rings before load disputes. Children wear two practice coils. Adults who yank rings mid-argument replace the whole stack and sit navy-quiet until invited back.

## Cultural Meaning

On Ashcalm Rise, Mood is visible patience. Resonance favors spoken counts. Using rings to rank people permanently empties the set into cruelty.

## Usage

Load talks, Rise sheds, and teaching rows. Rings cleaned; caps brushed.
