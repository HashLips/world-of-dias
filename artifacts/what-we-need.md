# What We Need

## Name

What We Need

## Overview

**What We Need** is a round drop-hole set low in a leaning tower along the Anonymous Drop Wall in Veloria. Residents say that when something urgent is left in it, a long dark reach carries it across to the neighboring roof faster than any courier could.

## Description

The hole is ordinary to look at: a round opening in green-painted brick, wider than most drops on the wall, dark inside. People leave folded notes in it naming what they need—medicine, a tool, a load curve, a meal for someone who cannot ask—along with anything they can offer. Most of the time, ordinary couriers collect from it. But rooftop residents tell of nights when a black arm, long and clawed, slides out of the hole and passes a parcel across the gap to the next roof, where someone finds it in the morning. A small watcher is always said to be sitting on the tower's edge when it happens.

## Cultural Meaning

The Drop Wall runs on unsigned care, and What We Need is its most extreme version: help so anonymous that nobody is sure what delivers it. Residents do not try to catch the reach or peer into the hole. Asking the rooftop watcher who sent something is considered a breach of the wall's whole purpose.

## Usage

Used for urgent needs only; trivial requests are said to sit in the hole for weeks. Notes are never signed.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Nature of the reach deliberately unsettled.
