# Aumere Turning Boom

## Name

Aumere Turning Boom. The Aumeri call it "the boom." The Hazard Notice calls it "an uncharted obstruction." The Ward report calls it "a ring of roped timbers, man-made."

## Overview

The Aumere Turning Boom is a floating ring of great roped timbers and stone weights laid by the Aumeri around the quiet water of the Stillmouth after the Clearing. It moves with the Counting Current and helps hold the quiet. It turns aside drifting things, and it gives the islanders a controlled door. It is the "something heavy and long under the water, wood and stone together" that broke *The Patient Lead*. When the island withdrew, the boom was left behind.

## Description

- **Timbers:** long, dark, pitched and seasoned. They lie end to end so low in the water that only their backs show, "like a string of sleeping seals."
- **Joints:** lashed with heavy rope. From some joints ropes run taut down into the water.
- **Stone weights:** rounded blocks of pale stone, half-awash between timbers, each bound in a **rope cradle**, to make the timbers ride low. After a Hushlean storm parted the south side and took three cradles, Tavi Corran rebuilt them with **heavier stones**. A boat carried over a joint in fog on a hard pull now came down on stone.
- **Swing-gap door:** at one place two timbers are joined by a long loop of rope instead of lashings, so they can swing apart. Narrow boats go through on a rest, timed to the count.
- **Motion:** a slow pulse runs along it with the current. Each timber lifts and settles and knocks its neighbour: *tok*.
- **After the withdrawal:** unbroken, floating around empty water in white broken sea, with one grey-pennanted Ward bolt standing in a timber.

## Cultural Meaning

To the Aumeri it was the first answer to the Clearing, and endless work: there is a mending rota on a slate by the harbour. To Tavi, guilt. To the Ward, the obstruction that justified a ward. To Driftfall, timber worth salvaging, until Vexra Coil said it is "a grave marker and a fence around a door nobody can open."

## Usage

Before: kept the quiet, turned drift, and let islanders in and out by the swing-gap. Now: an unofficial ward. The white water around it tells boats where not to go.

## Notes

How the boom contributes to holding the quiet is not explained. Do not make it a machine for the withdrawal.
