# Bar

## Name

Bar

## Overview

**Bar** is a kept listening-ledge set on Averra Isle: a white wedge shelf, a green guest cup, and a row of blue smile-tokens used to teach how to sit in cheerful company without surrendering your own measure.

## Description

The ledge is a pale wedge that can clamp to a red-cloth hall table or stand alone on a yellow floor mat. The green cup is small and rectangular in the plate’s telling—easy to hold, hard to hide behind. Smile-tokens—nine blue peg-bodies with white slit eyes and wide grins—are optional teaching props for apprentices who need to feel what a chorus does to a quiet guest. Hosts place them behind the seat when rehearsing welcome; they remove them when a real guest arrives if the guest prefers fewer faces. The artifact’s rule is simple: the cup belongs to the sitter; the chorus does not get to drink the story. Averra halls that keep **Bar** permanently usually paint or cloth the wall red so the plate’s warning stays familiar. Selling the smile-tokens as party décor without the listening rule is considered empty craft.

## Cultural Meaning

Averra Isle likes warm rooms and distrusts unanimous delight. **Bar** keeps a seat for the person who still needs to think.

## Usage

Guest welcome drills, damp-studio decompression, and pier-side quiet cups. Lent with the green cup washed and returned with it.

## Notes

Listening ledge. Green cup is the measure.
