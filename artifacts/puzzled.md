# Puzzled

## Name

Puzzled

## Overview

**Puzzled** is Claimscar Yard flag craft: a thin white pole and a vibrant unmarked red cloth planted at a teal split, used when a yard must hold a mark without forcing a finished claim-story.

## Description

Yard hands raise Puzzled when a cookfire circle or secondfire ring needs a visible pause. The cloth stays blank on purpose—no stamp, no joke crest—so the red wave means "not settled yet" rather than "ours forever." The white pole is thin and straight; the long shadow in the plate is kept in tellings as the low-light honesty of the hour. Local care forbids painting a hasty emblem just to end awkward silence, and forbids using the flag to claim a neighbor's tools. Resonance is blank red against teal weather; memory is the empty field of cloth; wonder is how loud a solitary wave can be. Flags are lent between yard rings and returned uncrested. Soft talk that mocks people for staying puzzled misses the craft: unfinished meaning is sometimes the correct local rule.

## Cultural Meaning

In Claimscar Yard, Puzzled means a mark that refuses a fake finish.

## Usage

Yard pauses, circle markers, and temporary road questions. Not a conquest banner.

Named for held not-knowing. Pair with the artwork plate in assets.
