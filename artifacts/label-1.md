# Label

## Name

Label

## Overview

**Label** is an F500 harness-and-anchor set: orange-red mid-body harness with riser lines and hoof tethers ending on small black ground squares, plus a prepared green soft-patch for landing, used to move lean creatures or similar loads without unlabeled free ends.

## Description

Harness pads protect ribs; lines stay bright for count. Anchor squares stake or weight to charcoal ground. The green patch—moss cloth or dyed pad—waits under the hang so a lower is never onto bare hardpan. Handlers call out line names before lift. Children practice on weighted yellow dummies first. A missing anchor square stops the job.

## Cultural Meaning

In F500, Label means visible responsibility for a hang. Resonance favors short, soft landings. Decorating a living creature with lines for spectacle empties the set into cruelty.

## Usage

Careful transports, study holds with time limits, and training yards. Lines coiled; patches dried.
