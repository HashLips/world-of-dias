# Shoes

## Name

Shoes

## Overview

**Shoes** is Velorian Basin swarm-pattern craft: dense overlapping angular forms in cyan, magenta, yellow, and royal blue on slate, each with beak-dot, slender pointed leg, sharp tail, and three-line crest, all aimed one way.

## Description

Basin workshops print and paint Shoes when a hall needs a picture of many matched movers. The four colors are the channel set; the three crest lines are the count you do not smudge; the shared aim is the local care. Drop shadows stay thin so layering reads. Local care forbids randomizing directions to fake energy, and forbids selling single cutouts as if the swarm did not matter. Resonance is bright flock on slate; memory is the beak black-dots; wonder is how the name holds without becoming Earth shoe talk. Boards return with crests clear. Soft talk that treats Shoes as footwear catalog misses the basin rule: the craft is the swarm's matched angle.

## Cultural Meaning

In the Velorian Basin, Shoes means many sharp forms moving as one aim.

## Usage

Hall textiles, boards, and readiness patterns. Not a single vanity cutout.
