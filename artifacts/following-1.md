# Following

## Name

Following

## Overview

**Following** is a kept Hollowmere teaching cloth: a cyan field printed with black many and white almond eyes, used to drill processions that follow with gaze and seam-space instead of blind shove.

## Description

Hosts hang or lay the cloth before a gather. Apprentices practice naming a leader without erasing their own eye-marks. Peach seam lines on the print are the breath-gaps that must survive in a real crowd. Net-loft and hall keepers correct anyone who treats following as permission to crush. Selling the cloth as spooky décor empties the craft.

## Cultural Meaning

Hollowmere likes company that can still see.

## Usage

Procession drills, guest festival briefings, and children’s line games. Lent and returned uncreased when possible.

## Notes

Gaze-crowd cloth. Follow with eyes; keep seams.
