# Hearttide Holding Cloth

## Name

Hearttide Holding Cloth

## Overview

A **Hearttide Holding Cloth** is a soft square Tidehearts lay at a holding cove’s rim to signal that tone-share has consent behind it. Mood weather made into fabric. Until the cloth is down, deep talk is only weather happening to you. After it is down, the weather is something people agreed to hold together, and they may still leave.

## Description

Woven rose-amber and smoke-teal, gradients rather than crests, so no house can own the feeling. Threads seem to lean with whatever the cove is carrying, a motion you might be imagining and that Guides treat as real enough to watch. Dry-stored cloths go flat and dull, honest about being unused. Cove-used cloths hold a faint warmth and a direction. Forced cheer stains the weave brittle: a harsh hand-feel, colors that look right and behave wrong, reeds nearby unimpressed. A cloth used as a pry bar, laid to extract a confession, cools in the fist. Guides end the hold rather than “fix” the cloth with a brighter dye.

## Cultural Meaning

Consent before deep share. The cloth is not mind reading and not a truth serum you can sit on. Softfruit guests recognize the kinship with a ladle: a tool that notices before it publishes. Merchants who want good negotiation air may borrow the custom and may not buy the result. Ease that can be purchased is the brittle kind.

## Usage

Holding coves, estuary talks, the rim of a basin where shoulders are about to drop. Rare gifts to Softfruit tables are metaphors, not franchises: a cloth on a bench means the same pause, not a claim that the court has become the Bloomline.

## Notes

Climate-coupling manners. Not a diagnostic device. Exit remains part of correct use.
