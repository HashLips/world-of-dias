# Suit Up

## Name

Suit Up

## Overview

**Suit Up** is a kept craft-object or staged thing associated with **F120 (frequency realm)**—remembered because what it holds, shows, or refuses to hold teaches a local rule about care and passage. It is wonder you can set on a table without turning it into a sermon.

## Description

In the plate, **Suit Up** arrives as a clean silhouette and color field: readable at a glance, stubborn about details. Hands that borrow it are expected to return it cleaner in meaning than in polish. Markets may display it; yards may hide it; roads may joke about it. The shared seriousness underneath the joke is resonance—touch leaves a living echo, and somebody has to decide whether that echo stays.

## Cultural Meaning

Around **F120 (frequency realm)**, the object is less a trophy than a teaching prop. Display is a promise to keep answering questions about where it has been.

## Usage

Shown, lent, parked in a known place, or carried between jobs. Rarely sold without a story that can travel with it.
