# The Reedpol

## Name

The Reedpol

## Overview

The Reedpol is a wooden pole-skiff on a Wabet river bend the Eastbound Fruit Road refuses to straighten. Toma Reed carries side-loads of fruit across water the wagons would rather not ford.

## Description

A real boat. It sits in the river, leaks a little at the scarf-joint when the season swells, and smells of reed, tar, and banana leaf used as a pad. One poler, a few crates, a dog that is not canonically named because Toma says names make dogs proud. The hull is dark with water. The pole is ash, worn white at the grip. Current does most of the thinking. Toma does the rest with her shoulders.

No lift but buoyancy. No engine. Rural people recognize it at once, which is a relief on a road where other craft have started to look like arguments. Children who fear the freight plane will still accept a ride if the crate is theirs to watch.

## Cultural Meaning

Water is a way around a rude bend, not a way around the tolls of the world. Toma pays her landing courtesies and does not sell the crossing as freedom. Fruit that rides the Reedpol still has to meet a wagon or a shed. Skipping the waystation on the far bank to "make up time" is the same raid-talk as skipping it with a cart.

## Usage

Short river hops beside the fruit road during the Vitrin days and before them. Ordinary logistics. While The Late Bruise was in the air with a secret, the Reedpol was in the water with Softfruit that still needed a door.

## Notes

Linked from Transport of Dias. One of the saga's real water craft. Not a ferry between frequencies.
