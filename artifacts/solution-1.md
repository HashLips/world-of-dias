# Solution

## Name

Solution

## Overview

**Solution** is Nauw Outer Rim Seas hammer-set craft: a black claw hammer stood upright with a bright red floppy bow on the handle, six bright blue nails at the base—work answered with a pause mark, not with blind striking.

## Description

Tide halls keep the set for teaching when a fix must stay visible. The red bow is required in careful copies as the pause of respect; removing it to "look serious" is considered unfinished care. The six blue nails and their white light-streaks in the plate are the count still on the floor—solution that admits what remains to be driven. Local care forbids using the bowed hammer as a trophy to threaten, and forbids hiding the nails after a job for false cleanliness. Resonance is ceremonial red on work black; memory is the six; wonder is mint quiet behind the off-white disc. Sets return with ribbon uncut. Soft talk that treats solution as instant erase misses the seas rule: bow first, then drive what still shows.

## Cultural Meaning

On the Outer Rim Seas, solution means a ribboned pause around honest nails.

## Usage

Hall teaching sets and readiness displays. Not a weapon bow.
