# Walking

## Name

Walking

## Overview

**Walking** shoes are the soft, heel-less slip-ons sold to travelers at the start of Saltwhisper Road. They come in complementary pairs—one walker in mint with white soles, the other in coral with green soles—so partners on the dune route can be matched at a glance.

## Description

The shoes are felted uppers stitched to thin flexible soles, light enough to fold into a pocket. They are made without heels so that no step strikes the salt crust hard; Keepers say a heel strike carries farther over the dunes than a shout. A pair of travelers buys one set: two shoes in mint, two in coral, and they decide between them who wears which. Some pairs swap one shoe each, so each walker has one of each color, which Keepers find either charming or confusing. Worn shoes are traded back at the road's far end for a small refund.

## Cultural Meaning

Saltwhisper is a negotiated permission, not a right, and walking soft is part of the bargain. The colors make companionship visible: a walker in mint should be near a walker in coral. A lone walker in mismatched shoes is gently asked where their partner went. The shoes teach the desert's calm as a way of moving.

## Usage

Required on the restricted stretches, recommended everywhere else on the Sands. Children's sizes come in the same two colors.
