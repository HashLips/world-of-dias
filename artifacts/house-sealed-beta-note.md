# House Sealed Beta Note

## Overview

A **House Sealed Beta Note** is filtered research or hazard language on a slip—high seal, low street, sometimes kinder silence and sometimes panic control.

## Description

Thin stock; hazard stripe; redaction bands; Operator excerpts pasted under oath marks. Street copies are almost always forged or wishful. Real notes smell like Calibration mint and clerk fear.

## Cultural Meaning

Evidence that can save a core or erase a witness. Ethics live in [`Sealed Knowledge Ethics`](../cultures/sealed-knowledge-ethics.md).

## Usage

Investigation: what was known before Bleed week; schism fuel; Softfruit arguments over “nonactionable.”
