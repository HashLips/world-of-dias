# Observation Intake Slate

## Name

Observation Intake Slate

## Overview

A pocket or desk slate for capturing observation-grade claims: what was sensed, where, when, and by whom—before any theory paragraph is allowed ink.

## Description

Matte face, scratched grid, header line for Partial / Observation stamps. Operators and House juniors both use cousins of this form. Softfruit keeps a paper version near the bowl for guests who arrive shaking after wrong-sky.

The slate’s discipline is negative space: a blank “cause” box that must stay blank until a committee or a calm morning fills Theory—never Careful disguised as Law.

## Cultural Meaning

Material manners for epistemology. Prevents fear from skipping grades.

Bleed nights; gate stain reports; first listens that must not become biographies.
