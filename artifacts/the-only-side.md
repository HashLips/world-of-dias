# The Only Side

## Name

The Only Side

## Overview

**The Only Side** is the small white profile card some Veloria desks prefer to any frontal likeness. A petitioner's side silhouette is cut or inked in black on white and pinned to their chest or file; any front-facing portrait offered alongside it is crossed out in green.

## Description

The cards are palm-sized, stiff, and plain. A clerk sits the petitioner sideways to a lamp, traces the shadow of head and neck, fills it in black, and leaves one white slit for the eye. The front-facing portraits people often bring—painted, flattered, decorated with whatever they think a clerk wants to see—are marked with a broad green X and returned. The joke at the Clerk Stamp Gallery is that one woman once arrived with a live blue bird nesting in her hair for the portrait, and even that got crossed out.

## Cultural Meaning

The habit rests on a simple belief: a front face can be performed, a side face mostly cannot. Desks that keep it say the profile carries a truer echo, less shaped by the moment of being looked at. Desks that refuse it call it vanity in reverse. The gallery keeps both arguments on its shelves.

## Usage

Made at desks that keep the custom, pinned to files and sometimes to coats during a hearing. Old cards are kept in drawers, not destroyed, and clerks claim they can recognize regulars by silhouette alone.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. A desk habit, not a city decree.
