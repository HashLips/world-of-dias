# Feeding Time

## Name

Feeding Time

## Overview

**Feeding Time** is a kept F500 hour kit: a dark rail token, a small fruit-tree stand-in with yellow crescents, and a white approach-beast mark used to teach fallen-first feeding behind a visible boundary.

## Description

Hosts set a low rail and place one crescent on the platform before any hanging fruit may be touched. The white creature token approaches; apprentices practice stretch-then-pause. Frequency talk treats the yellow crescents as share-fruit of the hour, not as Earth banana claims. Closing the rail early after crown-stripping is the correction. Selling the kit as cute animal décor empties the craft.

## Cultural Meaning

F500 feeds on schedule and leaves the hanging count readable.

## Usage

Apprentice hunger manners, guest orchard hours, and quiet night feeds. Lent with at least one fallen crescent mark.

## Notes

Rail-and-fruit kit. Fallen first.
