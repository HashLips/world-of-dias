# Kite

## Name

Kite

## Overview

**Kite** is an Ashcalm Rise five-line float harness: yellow-orange cords meeting in a handler grip and spreading to head, hand, and foot anchors on a buoyant red-marked load, used when something—or someone—must fly without spinning free.

## Description

Cords are bright for countability against white sky or pale ground. Anchors clip soft so skin and cloth are not cut. Handlers train the strained lean of the plate before real weight. Red cloth sleeves or paint mark the float so watchers see which body is aloft. Children fly small red dummies first. A snapped line mid-air is grounds for grounding the whole set until replaced.

## Cultural Meaning

On Ashcalm Rise, Kite is shared lift with countable care. Resonance favors five equal lines. Cutting a line as a joke empties the craft into hazard.

## Usage

Festival floats, rescue lifts over short gaps, and training yards. Lines coiled dry; anchors oiled.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Matches five-tether kite labor.
