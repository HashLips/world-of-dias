# Softfruit Resonance Bowl

## Name

Softfruit Resonance Bowl

## Overview

A wide, plain bowl kept near Softfruit ladles to teach answering and remainder: it warms after honest shared talk and stays oddly cool after performed cheer.

## Description

Unglazed inner ring, outer cloth sleeve stitched with a Resonate Ring Mark. No jewels. No House crest. The bowl is ugly on purpose so guests do not treat it as a relic auction piece. Hosts fill it with water or weak tea; the lesson is not the liquid—it is how the room changes around the liquid after lunch.

Apprentices learn to set palms near the rim without grabbing. Warmth after laughter is common. Brittle “rightness” after Forced-Final smiles is the warning texture Softfruit trusts more than speeches.

## Cultural Meaning

Kitchen proof of the echo principle. Softfruit’s answer to people who say resonance is only for Houses and saga heroes.

## Usage

Orientation for new guests; repair after gate argument-stain; quiet demonstration that reading echoes begins with manners, not instruments.

## Notes

Not a ferry key. Not a total reader. If someone tries to sell “bowl verdicts,” Softfruit hides the bowl and serves fruit.
