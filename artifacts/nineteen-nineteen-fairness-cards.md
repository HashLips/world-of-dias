# Nineteen-Nineteen Fairness Cards

## Name

Nineteen-Nineteen Fairness Cards

## Overview

**Nineteen-Nineteen Fairness Cards** are a teaching deck and yellow balance prop: both pans hold cards that sum to nineteen, yet the scale still tips. Used to show that equal numbers are not the same as honest weight.

## Description

Two fans of four cards each; bright yellow scale arm that leans left anyway. Softfruit interviewers slam it on tables when Houses claim “the counts match.” Clerks hate it; children love the trick; Operators sometimes borrow it for load-noise ethics talks.

## Cultural Meaning

Evidence manners: look for hidden weight—margin eaten, helpful glass, unsigned scrap—before calling a ledger fair.

## Usage

Field interviews, copy-desk training, Flagweek breakfast arguments that need a prop before stamps.
