# Backstab

## Name

Backstab

## Overview

**Backstab** is a kept warning-box on Averra Isle: a small open terracotta chest that studios place on a bench when scissors, needles, and “helpful” edits are in play, so cuts stay announced and never land from behind.

## Description

The box is humble—reddish-brown, open-topped, sized for a table corner. Inside it, Averra Damp Studios keep teaching tokens: a blue bird-neck stand-in for the forward-looking witness, a white scissors hand for the edit that must be spoken aloud, and a black hand-bird form with a needle-rod for the temptation to work unseen. Instructors open the box at the start of a critique and close only the lid-flaps, never the lesson: if a tool leaves the box without a spoken cut-line, the session stops. Apprentices learn to name the trim before the blades move. Guests who joke that the name is about tavern knives are corrected once, kindly, and then firmly. The artifact’s job is to keep craft sharp and people uncut. Selling the box as a novelty without the announce-rule is treated as bad studio craft.

## Cultural Meaning

Averra Isle folds care into critique. A cut you will not say to a face is a cut you should not make.

## Usage

Bench warning during edit sessions, guest critiques, and collaborative pierces of cloth or parchment. Lent between damp studios; returned with tokens complete.

## Notes

Warning box. Tools stay visible. Cuts stay spoken.
