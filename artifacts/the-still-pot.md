# The Still Pot

## Name

The Still Pot

## Overview

The Still Pot is Vorrith Kael’s working vessel: a resonant container in which materials, atoms, and frequencies are compounded into a thick living broth. The brew sustains Vorrith. A single taste by another being can trigger **Giftbroth Change**—a unique, unnatural capacity that may bless or ruin the drinker.

## Description

Accounts disagree on color. Some describe a bulbous yellow vessel on a small pale table; others remember only a white bowl holding golden liquid with geometric chunks that refuse to dissolve. Both may be true at different stages of the mix. What remains constant is the **stirring**: Vorrith treats compounding as continuous life-maintenance, not a festival recipe.

The broth is not a potion sold by dose. It is Vorrith’s **life-source**. He fabricates and remakes it because his exile body burns wrong in F432 without a private frequency soup to stand on. The pot’s resonance is therefore intimate—closer to a heart device than to a market relic.

Classed under **Resonant Artifacts** by those who insist on taxonomy: it retains, transforms, and reveals echoes through mixture rather than through a single sealed memory.

## Cultural Meaning

To seekers, the Still Pot is a lottery of selfhood.  
To cautious Wabet locals, it is proof that not every powerful object should be shared.  
To Keepers who hear the rumor, it sits in uneasy tension with reciprocity harvest ethics: the pot takes many inputs into one mouthful that may never give the land anything back.

## Usage

- Vorrith consumes the broth to remain coherent in Stillhollow.
- A visitor who tastes it undergoes Giftbroth Change—one unique unnatural capacity, unpredictable before the swallow.
- Vorrith does not custom-order powers. He does not refund tastes. He may warn. He may also simply watch.

## Notes

The substance is sometimes called **giftbroth**, **hollow broth**, or **the quiet soup** in rumor-speech. Keep the artifact name **The Still Pot** as the canonical vessel entry.
