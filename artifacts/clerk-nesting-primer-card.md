# Clerk Nesting Primer Card

## Name

Clerk Nesting Primer Card

## Overview

A pocket card issued to copy-room juniors and gate apprentices: one side the Scale Ladder Glyph, the other three sentences that must be spoken correctly before a seal is practiced.

## Description

Stiff fiber the color of old tea, corners rounded by thumbs. Front: five rungs in dry ink. Back: *Dias contains frequencies. Frequencies contain places. Places host events.* A thin Nest Mark watermark shows under slant light. Cards are deliberately ugly so no one frames them as art and forgets to use them.

## Cultural Meaning

Proof that orientation is a craft, not a vibe. Softfruit laughs at the ugliness and still keeps a stack by the ladle for travelers who ask honest questions.

## Usage

Morning drills; correcting a wrong incident form; lending to a gallery when a wrong map is exhibited so viewers have a handheld antidote.

## Notes

Does not grant clearance. Does not open bands. Teaches speech that keeps soft limits intact.
