# The Offshore Pass

## Name

The Offshore Pass

## Overview

**The Offshore Pass** is the named courtesy window—and the small token or chalk mark that signals it—when mainland vessels may answer Averra rites with light or silence but must not force contact. It is the practical object behind the Averra offshore courtesy rule.

## Description

Sometimes a painted board hung at a mainland lookout; sometimes a stamped wafer carried by Concord-adjacent skippers; sometimes only a shared clock phrase (*pass is open / pass is closed*). During a recognized pass, boats hold distance. Interpretive claims are allowed. Boarding, signal flares meant to summon the isle, and “just one closer look” are not.

## Cultural Meaning

Preserves coexistence without political merger. People who reject Averra myths still follow the Pass because provocation nights are expensive. Quiet Cloister business never treats the Pass as a ticket into Sael’s shelves.

## Usage

Displayed or spoken at ceremonial hours; logged in harbor gossip; cited when someone breaks courtesy and the bay remembers.
