# Issa Partial Packet

## Name

Issa Partial Packet

## Overview

The **Issa Partial Packet** is the partial spectrum publish set **Issa Partial** helped release with Orrin: law, theory, and taboo guess separated; Softfruit folk data beside Frame graphs; no-ferry marks on every section that might be misread as a door.

## Description

A tied bundle (and later school copies): residual graph citations, scraped-ledger excerpts, Softfruit keep-time columns from Sera’s notes, plain-language warnings about residual seams, and a short refusal of widen instructions. Margins carry struck boats. F960 appears only as disputed etiquette, not as a ferry geography.

**What it does for the reader of Dias:** this is the saga’s science payoff object—you can leave the Calibration Saga knowing what Orrin proved (background bands measurable from F432) and what he refused to sell (subway, endless seam, Frequency Zero answered).

Pellun double-files some copies: still “nonactionable” in one drawer, **partial courtesy shelf** in another—institutional embarrassment with a public upside.

## Cultural Meaning

Care manuals, not hero pamphlets. Issa treats disclaimers as kindness. Softfruit Corner applauds when Sera reads sections aloud at a discount.

## Usage

Basin release; school curriculum; Row teaching after escape. Not a House seal of approval.
