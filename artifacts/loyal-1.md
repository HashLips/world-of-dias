# Loyal

## Name

Loyal

## Overview

**Loyal** is a Songripe Isles vow set: a thick yellow diagonal chest-band and a small inner-eye token (almond disc) worn or shown when a person keeps outward watch and inward honesty at once.

## Description

Bands wrap shoulder to ribs as in the plate. Inner-eye discs hang under a soft flap or sit on a cord—revealed during vow checks. Cyan cloth backdrops are used in teaching so the red of urgency stays on the speaker, not the room. Children wear short practice bands. Adults who hide the disc while claiming the yellow redo the vow at next tide-song hour.

## Cultural Meaning

Around Songripe Isles, Loyal is watch plus honesty. Resonance favors revealed inner eyes. Using the band to bully silence empties it.

## Usage

Tide-song duty swears, isle errands, and teaching circles. Bands washed; discs counted.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Matches red profile loyalty plate.
