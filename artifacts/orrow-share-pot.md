# Orrow Share-Pot

## Name

Orrow Share-Pot

## Overview

The Orrow Share-Pot is the pot Sella Orrow uses for the portion of a meal set aside for the work that kept the field. It is a kitchen thing with a road idea in it: take, then restore.

## Description

Iron, black at the rim, sized for a household and a spare bowl. The handle is wrapped in cloth so a hurried hand can lift it. Inside, on ordinary nights: stew of Hearthvale onion, share-apple, herbs, sometimes a bone. One bowl is ladled before the household eats and left for Hob, for a neighbor who mended a stake, or for the next person who put labor back into the lent grass. The custom is local and plain. It is not a spell with a diagram.

## Cultural Meaning

Kin to fruit-road reciprocity without claiming to be the road's law. A pot that restores is how a small holding stays on speaking terms with a field it uses. Putting a hidden, costly fruit into that pot in order to vanish the cook is a taking. The saga's backfire sat on that fact. Canon does not claim the iron knows jurisprudence. It claims the night went worse for the hand that broke the share than for the woman who tasted her own stew.

## Usage

Dain Mercer slipped the kept Vitrin into the pot to dose Sella. The unseen he already wore thickened on him. Her edges faded. She stayed in the room, angry and visible, and threw out what she would not serve. The pot was scrubbed. It is still a pot.

## Notes

Do not upgrade this into a frequency device or a reliable weapon. The worse invisibility happened. The mechanism of the mix remains unknown. The farmer was not poisoned and not erased.
