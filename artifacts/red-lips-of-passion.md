# Red Lips of Passion

## Name

Red Lips of Passion

## Overview

**Red Lips of Passion** is an F500 marked-bust board: orange shoulders and face on pale pink, black intersecting cross-lines instead of eyes, small bright red lips, red wavy head-cover and maroon bun—heat kept quiet and countable.

## Description

Realm rooms hang the board when intensity needs a face that refuses spectacle. The black cross stands where a gaze would be—presence without hunting eyes. The red lips stay small and closed; the maroon bun sits neat on the red cover. Orange body-color is the warm field; pale pink behind keeps the calm. Local care forbids enlarging the mouth into a leer for jokes, and forbids treating the cross as a curse mark. Resonance is small red on orange; memory is the cross geometry; wonder is how still the bust can feel. Boards return with lips unsmudged. Soft talk that reads the name as permission for harm misses the realm rule: passion is the held mouth and the honest mark.

## Cultural Meaning

In F500, red lips of passion means contained heat with a clear face-mark.

## Usage

Readiness boards and quiet halls. Not a chase poster.
