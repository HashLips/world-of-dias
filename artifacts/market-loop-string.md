# Market-Loop String

## Name

Market-Loop String

## Overview

A **Market-Loop String** is a knotted cord the length of the walk from Veloria’s market loop to Farstock’s door, one street and no farther. It helps a person find a shop they have already reached. It does not point onward.

## Description

Waxed linen, knots at the bakery breath, the gutter, the hesitation-step. Hold it and walk; when the cord goes slack in the hand at the right corner, you are on the street. Used backward, toward the loop, it only proves you know the way you came. It has no interest in Sorel, the Long Gate, or any band.

## Cultural Meaning

A joke against people who want maps to elsewhere. The shop’s wonder begins with being able to return to a door, not with escaping the ring.

## Usage

Sold cheaply to visitors who got lost between the loop and the lane and are ashamed of it. Children use them as skipping ropes until the knots argue.

## Notes

If someone claims a longer string leads out of F432, they are selling a different object.
