# Softfruit Manners Chapbook

## Overview

A **Softfruit Manners Chapbook** teaches share plates, soft address, and third-chair kindness—child evidence of how Veloria wants to be, not how stamps always are.

## Description

Small bound pages; fruit stains; three-script cards simplified into pictures. Axiomorph geometry jokes in the margins. Adults pretend they don’t still check the page on hard days.

## Cultural Meaning

Reliable for manners; unreliable for House politics. Joy of learning made pocket-sized.

## Usage

Investigation: what a lost child was taught; contrast with a forged “manners” sheet that shames the stamped; Softfruit lunch as proof of culture.
