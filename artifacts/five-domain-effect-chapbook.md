# Five-Domain Effect Chapbook

## Name

Five-Domain Effect Chapbook

## Overview

A stitched pocket book with five spreads—matter, life, memory, perception, environments—each carrying one Softfruit kitchen/gate tell, one cross-band contrast, one wrong confusion, and a pointer to the domain primer. Not a travel schedule.

## Description

Matte covers, dull ink, no ferry maps. Softfruit editions smell faintly of peel and dough; House editions add a Claim Grade reminder that particular band effects may be Observation while the five-face cut is law-grade teaching. Children color the matter spread first (dough and stone); juniors are tested on memory vs perception before they are allowed to argue environments.

Where the Eight-Band Difference Chapbook asks “how do bands differ?”, this chapbook asks “what does a band *do* to stuff, bodies, remembering, sensing, and place?” Sellers who staple a “how to visit” leaf into the back find Softfruit burning that leaf only—and keeping the five spreads.

## Cultural Meaning

Makes Frequency Effects of Dias teachable in markets and junior rooms without collapsing five faces into wallpaper vibes.

## Usage

Gifted after bleed nights, wrong-effect talk, and Softfruit orientation beside Nest Mark lessons. Averra exhibits leave copies near beautiful wrong charts that treat null as a painted harbor.

## Notes

Read with Five-Domain Effect Teaching. Pair with Eight-Band Difference Chapbook; do not merge them into one confused tourist pamphlet.
