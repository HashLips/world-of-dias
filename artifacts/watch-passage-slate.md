# Watch Passage Slate

## Overview

A **Watch Passage Slate** posts safe-passage windows, quiet hours, and reef limits—slow public truth with few beds behind it.

## Description

Weatherproof slate; chalk or grease pencil; Yara-style handwriting without apology. Porch guests read it before arguing for beds.

## Cultural Meaning

Assent culture made schedule. Not rumor; not House seal—island logistics.

## Usage

Ferry timing; belonging trials evidence; teaching intentional arrival.

## Notes

Communication artifact. Chapter starter: slate erased overnight; ferry still comes.
