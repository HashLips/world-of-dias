# Bread-Smoke Vial

## Name

Bread-Smoke Vial

## Overview

A **Bread-Smoke Vial** is a stoppered thumb of glass that, when opened, smells of that morning’s outer-ring bread. The scent is a memory of a street, not a courage you can hoard.

## Description

Thick glass, waxed cork, a faint warm brown film that never quite becomes a liquid. Opened, it is crust and hearth and the particular sweetness of the loop’s early ovens. The smell lasts a few breaths and then is gone until the next day, when it is that day’s bread, not a preserved perfect morning. It does not show visions. It does not make a hard road feel like a kitchen.

## Cultural Meaning

Homesickness with a stopper. Travelers like it until they learn it will not become yesterday on command. The limit is the point.

## Usage

Pockets, windowsills, gifts for people leaving the ring who are not yet ready to admit they will miss the ovens. Pim restocks from the bakery he can walk to, which keeps the object honest.

## Notes

Not an emotional drug and not a Hearttide tool. Sniffing it on the Long Gate would smell like bread and then like wind.
