# Mir Weekly Leaf

## Overview

A **Mir Weekly Leaf** is the strip’s public newsprint—opinion weather that can outrun loft truth and still sell breakfast jokes.

## Description

Cheap fiber; ink that smears in haze; headlines that flip with flags. Mir’s voice is person and paper both. Leaves pile under toll benches and Claimscar tarps.

## Cultural Meaning

Comic dread made portable. Breakfast-holds sometimes survive bad headlines; sometimes not.

## Usage

Public mood; plot engine when a leaf lies early and a hail arrives late; teaching certainty grades.

## Notes

Communication artifact. Chapter starter: a Leaf names the wrong Free-Captain as dead.
