# Concept

## Name

Concept

## Overview

**Concept** is a kept Outer Rim teaching form: a kneeling stump-stand and a white perched figure used when a hard idea must be balanced after a cut without erasing the cost.

## Description

The kit is two pieces that only work together. The lower stand is a pale kneeling torso-shape ending at a flat waist plane that may be marked with a red drip-thread so the scar stays readable. The upper piece is a white deer-headed perch with long lifting legs, set carefully on the stump for drills. Students learn to steady the perch without wiping the red mark. Outer Rim hosts bring it to harbor disputes where a plan wants to float free of what it severed. Selling the white figure alone as a cute animal toy is emptied craft. The object teaches balance with memory, not violence for show.

## Cultural Meaning

Nauw rim talk treats a concept that hides its cut as a lie that will tip later.

## Usage

Harbor planning circles, quiet apology drills, and guest teaching when cost must stay visible. Lent as a pair only.

## Notes

Balance form. Scar stays; perch steadies.
