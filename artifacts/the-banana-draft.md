# The Banana Draft

## Name

The Banana Draft

## Overview

The Banana Draft is a slow market barge on the Glasswater and basin cuts, captained in practice by Sarn Vell. It moves fruit the last wet miles into Nauw distribution after the road has already done the hard easting.

## Description

A flat, beamy wooden barge with a stub mast it rarely bothers to dress and a sweep-oar for the narrows. The hold is lined so Market Banana and Softfruit do not take the water's cold all at once. Paint was yellow once. It is now the color of a peel left on a step. Ropes are thick. The deck has a kettle and a argument. Sarn knows every sulk in the current between Glasswater Fields and the basin quays.

It is slower than a gossip and slower than The Late Bruise. It is how a crate becomes a stall without asking a plane to learn doors, porters, and the smell of a city morning. Third-ring quays have a winch. The barge itself is older manners: wood, water, a person who can be fined.

## Cultural Meaning

Banana in the name is reverence and advertising at once. Nauw will forgive a slow boat that arrives with the supreme fruit unsplit. It will not forgive a boatman who dumps a waystation's duty because the air looked faster. Sarn likes the plane as a spectacle and not as a colleague.

## Usage

Ordinary distribution. On the days the hull-gossip started, the barge was still taking the fruit that looked like fruit. After the bar named the orchard, Sarn kept the same route. Wonder did not redraw the canal.

## Notes

Linked from Transport of Dias. Real water craft, distinct from The Reedpol: barge, not skiff; Nauw distribution, not a Wabet bend.
