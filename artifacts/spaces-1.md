# Spaces

## Name

Spaces

## Overview

**Spaces** is Driftfall listen-hourglass craft: blue glass between yellow-orange caps, chunky brown terrain-matter pouring, watched by two facing white profiles whose yellow ear-lines meet at the base—duration held as shared listening.

## Description

Net-lofts keep Spaces for talks that need time visible without fine-sand hurry. The brown interior is chunky on purpose—terrain, not dust—so the pour has weight you can see. Twin white profiles and black almond eyes are the care model: face the glass, do not turn away mid-fall. Yellow lines from ears to base are required in full boards as the listening triangle. Local care forbids shaking to force the pour, and forbids using one profile alone as if the space did not need two. Resonance is blue glass on deep red; memory is the brown mountain-chunks; wonder is the ear-line meet. Boards return with caps true. Soft talk that treats spaces as empty gaps misses Driftfall's rule: spaces are what two listeners hold around falling matter.

## Cultural Meaning

At Driftfall, spaces means shared listening around a visible pour.

## Usage

Loft boards and readiness talks. Not a shake toy.
