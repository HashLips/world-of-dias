# It's Growing

## Name

It's Growing

## Overview

**It's Growing** is an Averra Isle growth collar: a layered upward crescent (dark brown base, mustard band, bright yellow edge) worn or mounted under a white face-plate with optional dual pink head-blocks, used to mark a project or person enlarging under calm orange-eyed attention.

## Description

The crescent thickens by adding yellow edge-wraps as milestones pass—never by leaping to full width in one night. Pink blocks clip left and right; unequal color is allowed and often preferred as honest asymmetry. Orange eye-paints or lens-dots stay on the white disc during reviews. Studios hang a wall version beside works-in-progress. When the yellow edge reaches the agreed measure, the collar is oiled and stored until the next growth cycle. Snapping a crescent early to “finish” is a known studio shame.

## Cultural Meaning

On Averra Isle, It's Growing means measured enlarge with calm lids. Resonance favors uneven pink that still faces forward. Rushing the yellow edge empties the name.

## Usage

Apprentice reviews, damp-studio project walls, and rare isle festivals of unfinished craft. Stored flat.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Matches crescent and dual pink growth marks.
