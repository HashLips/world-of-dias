# Tips

## Name

Tips

## Overview

**Tips** at The Sketchy Gallery are fruits left on chairs after an artist talk, as thanks for the speaker. The custom replaced coin tips in a district where money tends to carry questions.

## Description

Talks at the gallery are held in a gray side room with rows of rounded red chairs on thin blue legs. When a talk ends, listeners who enjoyed it leave a fruit on their seat—an apple, a fig, a citrus, or, as the joke goes, the biggest banana they could find at the Basin stalls that morning. Nobody hands fruit to the speaker directly. The speaker waits until the room is empty and then walks the rows collecting. A talk that ends with no fruit on any chair is quietly understood to have been a bad one, and nobody needs to say so.

## Cultural Meaning

The third ring sits near the inner core, where Operators and Houses watch currency closely. Fruit is honest: it spoils, it cannot be laundered, and its value is obvious. Tips in fruit say "this was nourishing" without creating a debt. The last listener who stays seated, blocking the collection, is a gallery fixture—tolerated, teased, and sometimes offered a share.

## Usage

Practiced after every public talk. Speakers often carry the fruit straight to Softfruit tables to share, which completes the loop.
