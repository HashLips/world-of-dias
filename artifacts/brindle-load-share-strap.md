# Brindle Load-Share Strap

## Name

Brindle Load-Share Strap

## Overview

A **Brindle Load-Share Strap** lets neighboring carts clip weight across a march line so no single ox or mule takes a Forced-Final haul. Lived travel craft under the load-sharing rule.

## Description

Wide leather with resonant beads that warm when the share is honest. Three thank-you taps are custom after unclipping. Using the strap to dump debt onto a poorer cart voids Brindle goodwill faster than a toll fine.

## Cultural Meaning

Reciprocity you can buckle. Beauty of a line that leans together.

## Usage

Brindle drivers, Ashen relay teams borrowing march manners, fruit-road wagons at Second Crossing.
