# Veld Blackened Spool

## Name

Veld Blackened Spool

## Overview

The **Veld Blackened Spool** is the Spectrum Frame’s corner filament spool scorched on vault night—when the Caliburn thinned and power surged along the lattice instead of staying latched.

## Description

Once a clean side spool feeding calibration filament into the Frame’s apertures. After the failed vault: char along one cheek, fused filament teeth, a smell like hot wax and load-fan dust. Orrin leaves it on the alcove shelf beside the sprung latch. In *Still Measuring*, an empty or ruined spool vacancy is part of the incomplete Frame’s teaching silhouette—students see what “one more widen, then lock it” costs in craft materials.

Cause in-world: not random lamp fire. Contact between bruise-violet thinning and live filament under forced containment—Echoform escape as electrical manners the Row does not have a full manual for.

## Cultural Meaning

Proof object. Less poetic than wrong ticks; harder to file as weather. Softfruit kids are not allowed to touch it; Silk Scrap is told no, firmly, once.

## Usage

Shelf warning; not rewound for teaching models (schools use clean dummy spools). Cited when explaining why the original Frame stays incomplete.
