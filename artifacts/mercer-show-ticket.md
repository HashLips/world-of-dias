# Mercer Show Ticket

## Name

Mercer Show Ticket

## Overview

A Mercer Show Ticket is the card Dain Mercer handed out at The Peel and Bell, promising a show of something no one in Nauw had seen.

## Description

Thick paper, cream, printed in a third-ring shop and slightly too clean for the bar. One side: Mercer Shed, a morning hour, the words *a fruit*. No picture. The other side blank so Bram could not be accused of selling a second meaning. Ink smells new. The corners soften fast in a peel-damp pocket.

## Cultural Meaning

In a city of ring-fair tokens, a blankish ticket still means a door. This one meant a room and a cloth. It was not a ferry stub and carried no band name. People tried to resell them before dawn. The bar would not broker the trade. The shed took the cards at the door and did not take responsibility for what the fruit did after the first bite.

## Usage

Admission to the Vitrin showing and the auction that followed. Eight fruits were sold inside. The ticket itself was not the purchase of a fruit. Hess Calder and Ilen Voss still had to bid in VEL marks until the numbers hurt.

## Notes

A paper object. Not The Rail That Sold the Same Platform and not a passage document.
