# Hollowmere Cove Walk Sketch

## Name

Hollowmere Cove Walk Sketch

## Overview

A salt-stained settlement sketch used by loft apprentices: cove tiers from brine barrels to public net-lofts, with check-in hooks marked and storm-choir paths dashed.

## Description

Ink on scrap sailcloth or net-paper. Lower tier: drying lines, brine barrels, guest hammock posts. Mid tier: family sheds, rope-height gossip rails. High tier: Hollowmere Net-Loft Stage and lantern hooks. A thin coast arrow points toward Rim-Rest and Cinder Nook; whale lanes are left blank on purpose (ethics, not ignorance). Fruit-barge greeting pier marked with a banana doodle someone always redraws.

Matches Hollowmere prose and Outer Rim placement on Map of Nauw / F432 Map V2. Not a Veloria ring plate.

## Cultural Meaning

Proves cove-town settlement depth: stacked height, not concentric rings.

## Usage

Pinned in loft apprenticeship; gifted after First Line Answer seasons; copied badly by basin tourists who still miss the check-in call.

## Notes

Settlement-level atlas artifact. Local ferry marks ≠ band ferry.
