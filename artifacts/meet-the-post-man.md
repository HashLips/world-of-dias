# Meet the Post Man

## Name

Meet the Post Man

## Overview

**Meet the Post Man** is a Bloomline Estuary courier greet kit: long olive floppy cap with bright blue bead tip, optional gritted-teeth face mark or mask insert, and a red yellow-spotted tunic cue, used so packet handoffs begin with a hold-still face both parties can read.

## Description

Cap bead must be visible from the door. Teeth-mark is a flat insert or paint—smile without chatter. Dual-eye stickers remind couriers to check route and person. Spotted tunics weather estuary damp. Children play post with empty packets. Adults who shove packets past the bead without pause walk the last mile again as apology.

## Cultural Meaning

Along the Bloomline Estuary, Meet the Post Man is door manners for news. Resonance favors settled beads. Mocking the gritted mouth empties the kit into rudeness.

## Usage

Ferry posts, estuary doors, and training counters. Caps dried; beads checked.
