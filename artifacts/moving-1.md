# Moving

## Name

Moving

## Overview

**Moving** is a Brindle March travel set: a long thin shoulder staff and a rounded knotted aft sack, used so a walker keeps burden readable and behind while taking the long stride of the plate.

## Description

Sacks knot closed; staffs balance forward grip. March children carry soft practice sacks. Adults who swing bundles into thank-you lean queues restaff and rewalk. Blue road flags mark open stretches matching the plate’s field.

## Cultural Meaning

On Brindle March, Moving is burden with courtesy. Resonance favors aft knots. Using the staff to prod people aside empties the set.

## Usage

Fruit-road walks, lean approaches, and teaching strides. Sacks patched; staffs checked.
