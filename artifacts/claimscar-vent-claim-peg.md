# Claimscar Vent-Claim Peg

## Name

Claimscar Vent-Claim Peg

## Overview

A **Claimscar Vent-Claim Peg** is a painted stake or hull-nail crews drive near Sprout Vent runoff to announce a claim window. Ugly paperwork of Driftfall’s dirt and rope. It is not honor pageantry. A peg is how a yard avoids discovering, at the worst hour, that two crews thought the same mouth of water was a private story. Breakfast can be shared. Pegs are not shared by the steam of a kettle. They are shared, if at all, by being looked at in the light.

## Description

Scarred wood, crew colors, a point that has met more rock than ceremony. A second paint layer, still wet at secondfire, is the detail the yard fears and laughs at: the double claim, refreshed in the dark, expecting daylight to be polite. Pegs argued at the ring get hotter than morning stew allows. Dogs do not care about the colors and care a great deal about whether the argument delays food. A peg flipped by a runner’s hand is a reveal. The shine of wet paint does the talking. Afterward the peg is not a trophy. It goes back in the ground as a single honest mark, or it does not go back, and the runoff has one less lie and one more grudge that breakfast will have to host again.

## Cultural Meaning

The vent-claim rule made sharp enough to trip on. Sharing a bowl does not share a peg, and the myth of the breakfast that ended a double claim exists so people remember the difference. Garen Scar’s crews treat pegs as weather they can plan around. Visitors who want them to be heraldry are handed a splinter and a clearer idea of the yard.

## Usage

Yard edges, runoff lines, salvage piles where a claim might hide under a tarp. Not in the Cookfire Circle’s center. The center is for stew. The edge is for stakes. Confusing the two is how paint wars start before the kettle has gone around.

## Notes

Frontier artifact. Rivalry craft, not a banner of realm-war.
