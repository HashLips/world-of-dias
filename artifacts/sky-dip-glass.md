# Sky-Dip Glass

## Name

Sky-Dip Glass

## Overview

A **Sky-Dip Glass** is a palm lens that makes Veloria’s Sky Drifters look one shade nearer, and no nearer than that.

## Description

A thick round of slightly green glass in a horn rim, comfortable in a hand that is used to coins. Through it the pale clusters above the rings gain a hint of edge, as if the sky had leaned down the width of a courtesy. They do not descend. The glass will not focus on ledgers, faces, or the Long Gate’s far end. Pointed at a festival sky-whale lantern, it only shows paper and light, which helps children keep the categories straight.

## Cultural Meaning

Wonder as a practice of looking up without demanding a landing. Farstock sells the look, not the sky.

## Usage

Roof hours, fair weeks, the walk home when the rings feel too close and the sky is the only wide thing. Useless as a spyglass and proud of it.

## Notes

Does not reveal frequency bands. A person who sees F500 in it is seeing their own story, not the glass.
