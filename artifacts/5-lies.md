# 5 Lies

## Name

5 Lies

## Overview

**5 Lies** is a kept gather-mark set on Ashcalm Rise: five white pillar-figures and a pouch of thin black face-bands used when a circle needs to name its soft untruths out loud and then stop.

## Description

The set is usually five portable white stand-ins—elongated, simple, tall enough to read as pillars rather than dolls—plus black ribbon-bands for faces. Circles place three tall marks as the count-holders and two shorter ones for learners looking up. Each speaker who admits a soft lie takes a band across the face for the length of the naming; the single eye-mark on a banded head means “I am being heard, not performing.” The object does not punish. It meters. After five named softenings, the bands come off and the circle either repairs what the softenings were covering or agrees the day can continue. Selling the set as costume without the stop-rule is considered a Rise insult. The dark teal cloth often laid under the pillars is part of the kit: an empty field so scenery cannot dilute the count.

## Cultural Meaning

Ashcalm Rise treats a soft lie admitted as care, and a soft lie hoarded as harm. Five is the local ceiling for the first kind in one sitting.

## Usage

Brought to rise-yard gathers, family weather-talks, and guest apologies. Lent more often than sold. Returned with bands clean and unknotted.

## Notes

Gather-mark set. Five, then stop.
