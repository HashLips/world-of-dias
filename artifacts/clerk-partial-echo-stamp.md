# Clerk Partial Echo Stamp

## Name

Clerk Partial Echo Stamp

## Overview

An ink stamp carrying the Partial Echo Slash, used to brand a page, wafer note, or margin as incomplete remainder rather than closed fact.

## Description

Hardwood handle, replaceable pad, ink that smells faintly of vinegar and dust. Juniors who forget to stamp partials buy kettle rounds. Seniors who stamp partial to avoid responsibility are corrected harder than juniors—cowardice is not craft.

## Cultural Meaning

Paper manners for a world that holds echoes. The stamp is how institutions obey soft limits in public.

## Usage

Copy rooms, gate incident forms, courtesy shelves, anywhere a complete-sounding sentence would lie.

## Notes

Does not seal. Sealing is another rite. Partial and sealed can stack; they must not be confused.
