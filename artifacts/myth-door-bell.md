# Myth-Door Bell

## Name

Myth-Door Bell

## Overview

The **Myth-Door Bell** is a thumb-sized bell on Farstock’s inner jamb. It rings, once, when someone who still half-believes the shop is real steps onto the street. It does not summon the shop, and it does not ring for scorn.

## Description

Brass gone dull at the lip, hung where a draft could be blamed. The tone is small, more click than chime. Adults who have decided the door is a children’s story can walk the lane in silence. Children who are only daring each other sometimes get a ring and sometimes do not; the bell is picky about performed belief. It never rings a path, a name, or a warning about what is inside.

## Cultural Meaning

Outer-ring humor at the expense of people who want magic to beg. The bell notices attention. It does not reward it with a bargain.

## Usage

Left on the jamb. Pim will not sell it. Visitors hear it and pretend they did not, which is its own outer-ring manners.

## Notes

Rill Ostmark heard it on her first approach and called it a draft. The bell did not argue.
