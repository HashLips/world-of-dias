# Ideas Are Made

## Name

Ideas Are Made

## Overview

**Ideas Are Made** names a Coral Strand set of cultivated blue idea-stems: tall slender plants with flared bell tips used as thinking perches, kept in fives so a maker can try more than one posture before speaking an idea into a room.

## Description

Stems are vivid blue, gently curved, leafed with small points, base-tufted and shadow-long in evening light. The bell tips are wide enough for a crouched or arched body. Strand workshops grow them in olive plots facing open red-weather skies when the season allows; indoor copies use dyed reed stems with cupped tops for rehearsal. Sitters wear dark clothes and keep a single eye-slit focus. Poses are not dance for show—they are pressure tests. A stem snapped for impatience is replaced with apology water at the root.

## Cultural Meaning

On Coral Strand, saying ideas are made means they are grown and perched, not snatched. Resonance prefers the idea that survived more than one pose. Mocking a fetal curl on a tip is considered cutting a stem early.

## Usage

Workshop mornings, strand teaching yards, and quiet festivals of unfinished thoughts. Cut stems are composted, never sold as trophies.

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Artifact/flora craft matching the five blue bells.
