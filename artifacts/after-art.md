# After Art

## Name

After Art

## Overview

**After Art** is a kept after-looking set in Driftfall: a tall-backed orange chair, a blue corner cloth, and a hanging frame used to revisit a finished plate before anyone names what it “means.”

## Description

The chair is the loud piece—bright orange, backrest absurdly tall, seat low enough that a sitter must hunch into honesty rather than lounge into opinion. Driftfall makers place it in a cool blue corner with a darker floor cloth so the room feels like a small pier-side study rather than a gallery. The frame on the wall holds the finished work or a translucent copy of it; the set’s rule is that you sit and look before you speak praise, sale, or critique. A single dark eye-mark, sometimes pinned to the chair’s crest, reminds sitters that one careful witness is enough. Guests who try to stand and lecture from the chair are asked to sit again. The artifact travels as a manners tool between Driftfall Piers studios and quiet guest rooms; it is not a throne.

## Cultural Meaning

Driftfall treats the hour after making as sacred to care, not to branding. **After Art** keeps that hour physical.

## Usage

Set after a plate is hung. Used for revisit sessions, pier apprenticeships, and guest thanks that need looking before words. Lent with the stop-rule: sit first, speak second.

## Notes

After-looking set. Tall orange chair is the teacher.
