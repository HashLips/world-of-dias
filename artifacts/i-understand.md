# I Understand

## Name

I Understand

## Overview

**I Understand** is a Calibration Row assent set: a plain black high-backed chair paired with a small red chest-token (cloth heart or painted pip) worn or held to the sternum when a person has finished listening and needs the room to stop pushing for more speech.

## Description

The chair is unornamented—straight back, thin legs, meant to be sat in, not displayed. The red token is bright on purpose so others can see assent without demanding a speech. People sit white-clothed or unadorned so the mark stays readable. The mustard-wall rooms of Row alcoves often host this after a difficult measure or a long visitor explanation. Children learn not to poke the red mark for fun. Adults who keep talking after the token appears are corrected with a soft hand toward the door, not a shout.

## Cultural Meaning

On Calibration Row, I Understand is manners after information: the center is shown, the argument ends, care continues later if needed. Resonance sticks to honest pauses. Fake tokens worn to escape chores are a known small disgrace.

## Usage

Alcoves, quiet desks, and rare family rooms when a hard truth has landed. Chair stays; tokens travel in a pocket pouch.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. No invented saga machinery—only the local pause tool.
