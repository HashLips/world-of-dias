# Quillmend Softfruit Field Notes

## Name

Quillmend Softfruit Field Notes

## Overview

**Quillmend Softfruit Field Notes** are Sera Quillmend’s peel-stained notebook correlating fruit keep-times with sky moods—Orrin Veld’s favorite folk dataset, and proof that F432 was measuring itself before anyone built a Frame.

## Description

Waxed cover sticky with citrus. Columns for keep, joke density, second-dawn talk, slit-quiet mornings, and hearttide lean (laughter density, gift tempo, how quickly strangers become “colleague”). Peel bookmarks. Peel-sweet herb sprigs pressed flat. Clerks have offered marks for the book; Sera names refusal prices.

Orrin’s scraped ledger cites these notes more often than House summaries. When the Frame later shows F380 gradients, Sera’s hearttide columns are the human-readable twin—not a realm jump, a climate of feeling. The partial spectrum packet’s Softfruit section owes them phrasing.
