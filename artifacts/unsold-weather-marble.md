# Unsold Weather Marble

## Name

Unsold Weather Marble

## Overview

An **Unsold Weather Marble** is a glass sphere that clouds when basin rain is about an hour out. It forecasts water. It does not forecast a heart.

## Description

Clear glass with a faint internal veil. Sun keeps it bright. Before rain the veil thickens to milk. It does not swirl with anger, grief, or wanting, even if the person holding it is a storm. Pim calls it unsold in the name because people keep trying to buy it as a mood and he keeps explaining weather until they tire.

## Cultural Meaning

Outer-ring pride in tools that refuse to be oracles. A marble that only knows rain is a civic kindness: you bring the washing in.

## Usage

Windowsills, stall counters, children’s pockets on fair days. Useless underground and uninterested in frequency weather of the null-band kind.

## Notes

Not a Heartlean Colorstone. If it takes a person’s color, it is broken or it is a different marble.
