# Great Time

## Name

Great Time

## Overview

**Great Time** is a kept Veloria City icon set: a white smiling vessel mark and four tally sticks used when a celebration must stay countable instead of dissolving into untracked hype.

## Description

Hosts set the white smile-form and add one tally line per agreed good hour or guest thanks. Erasing tallies to make the smile “purer” is emptied craft. City clerks and guest halls keep pocket versions. The form’s flat base means it sits on a table as object, not as floating slogan. Dias name only.

## Cultural Meaning

Veloria City likes great times that still add up.

## Usage

Festival closings, guest thank-you counts, and Flagweek quieter cousins. Lent with blank tally sticks.

## Notes

Tally smile icon. Smile; keep the count.
