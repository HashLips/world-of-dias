# Chair Room

## Name

Chair Room

## Overview

**Chair Room** is a kept waiting-trio on Averra Isle: three crimson tall-backed chairs set on a pale floor cloth so hard talks begin only when ask, answer, and witness each have a seat.

## Description

The chairs match on purpose—same crimson, same exaggerated back height—so status theater struggles to claim the tallest. Hosts angle the side chairs inward and leave the center forward for the person who asked for the room. Shadows in the plate are teaching noise: light should be hard enough to show who fidgets. The set travels between Averra Damp Studios and guest halls. Sitting before the third chair is placed is bad manners. Selling one chair alone as décor empties the craft. The object is furniture for readiness, not for display ownership.

## Cultural Meaning

Averra treats empty tall chairs as care: the conversation has a shape before egos fill it.

## Usage

Hard guest talks, studio critiques that need a witness, and pier mediation. Lent as a trio only.

## Notes

Waiting trio. Three seats or none.
