# Shapes

## Name

Shapes

## Overview

**Shapes** is Bloomline Estuary measure-set craft: mint-green floating sphere, thick red ribbon with pink edge stroke, sharp yellow open V, and cyan diagonal on dark reddish-brown—several measures kept distinct in one field.

## Description

Estuary studios paint Shapes when a room must hold more than one rule without collapsing them. The mint sphere floats with a soft shadow; the red ribbon curves with its lighter edge; the yellow V opens large; the cyan cut stays straight. Local care forbids merging colors to "simplify," and forbids treating the set as a finished road map. Resonance is cool against warm on dark; memory is the four-part count; wonder is how much motion a ribbon can keep beside rigid lines. Boards travel between estuary halls and return with edges clean. Soft talk that calls the craft empty decoration misses the estuary rule: shapes are measures you keep separate on purpose.

## Cultural Meaning

Along the Bloomline, shapes means plural measures held without smear.

## Usage

Studio boards and readiness fields. Not a muddy blend exercise.
