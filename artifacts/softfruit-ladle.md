# Softfruit Ladle

## Overview

The **Softfruit Ladle** is the hall’s metering spoon—everyday tech with a slight habit of sticking when a truce is false.

## Description

Wood darkened by citrus and banana mash; notch marks for ladle-fee vs fruit-share. Regulars swear it pours thin for banana-boasts and generous for honest green-crate days. Clerks call superstition; Softfruit staff call manners.

## Cultural Meaning

Lumen of Softfruit’s habit made civic. Multi-kind meals begin with the ladle’s arc.

## Usage

Hall service; teaching children ladle-before-stamp; settling small quarrels before Pot Signal debts.

## Notes

Slight resonance—not a Prime Relic. Chapter starter: the ladle refuses a liar’s bowl.
