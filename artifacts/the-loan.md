# The Loan

## Name

The Loan

## Overview

**The Loan** is the small red flask that Pot Signal Lane uses as the sign of a favor extended. When someone in the lane lends you time, cover, coin, or help, they may hand you an empty red flask on an open palm, and you are expected to receive it palm under palm.

## Description

The flasks are cheap red glass, bell-shaped, the size of a thumb, usually stoppered and empty. What matters is how they change hands. The lender stands still, often in a doorway, flask resting on a flat palm. The borrower slides their own palm beneath and lifts it away. A flask snatched by the neck is said to carry the debt badly: lane stories tell of borrowers who sniffed the empty glass hoping for something more, felt dizzy with obligation, and found themselves owing twice. Returned flasks are set in a flower pot's saucer as a public sign that a favor has been repaid.

## Cultural Meaning

In a district that runs on favors, manners are the only contract. The flask makes the terms visible without paper: slow hands mean an honest loan, quick hands mean trouble. The lane does not shame lenders for offering; it watches how people take.

## Usage

Handed over at the moment a favor is granted, carried until repaid, returned to a pot saucer. Children practice the palm-under-palm motion with pebbles. A flask that has passed through many honest hands is kept on a windowsill as a mark of a trustworthy house.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. The flask holds no substance in proper lane use.
