# Life Kind Sorting Card

## Name

Life Kind Sorting Card

## Overview

A four-corner Softfruit card: Collar / Awe / Table / Open—used to teach Life Kinds of Dias without a lecture voice.

## Description

Stiff paper, often fruit-stained. Corners labeled **Collar** (ordinary animal), **Awe** (extraordinary creature—wonder or hazard mark on the back), **Table** (people), **Open** (ambiguous / refuse forced label). Tiny ink examples: dog, butterfly, spoon, blank sky oval (Drifter). Reverse lists the four sorting tests in short clerk hand.

## Cultural Meaning

Proves the taxonomy is teachable to children and travelers—Volume III’s first question as manners, not academic jargon.

## Usage

Orientation at Softfruit Table Hall; trail packs for Wabet walkers; sometimes pinned beside Animals / Creatures primers in House libraries (with different tone).

## Notes

Not a hunting license. Open corner stays open for Sky Drifters and honest rumor.
