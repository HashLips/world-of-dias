# Ask-Again Stamp Pad

## Name

Ask-Again Stamp Pad

## Overview

An **Ask-Again Stamp Pad** prints a small measure-slit mark that means: re-run, re-ask, do not bury this as noise. It is the polite opposite of a smiling **nonactionable** grave.

## Description

Ink smells faintly of oilvine. The stamp face is a narrow vertical slit with a second tick beside it. Used when glass agrees too helpfully, when visitor wrong-tick unease shows, or when a ledge reading refuses to stay dull.

## Cultural Meaning

Institutional manners after Calibration caution tales. Asking again is craft, not cowardice.

## Usage

Clerk Stamp Gallery training desks; Row alcoves; Softfruit corner jokes when someone stamps too fast.
