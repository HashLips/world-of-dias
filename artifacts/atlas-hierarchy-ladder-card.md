# Atlas Hierarchy Ladder Card

## Name

Atlas Hierarchy Ladder Card

## Overview

A stiff pocket card listing the geography ladder—realm → region/territory → settlement → landmark/route → room—with three worked F432 chains and one other-band chain on the back. Used before journey planning and map filing.

## Description

Matte stock, dull ink, Nest Mark in the corner. Front shows the rung names and typical `place_type` words. Back shows:

1. Veloria chain (through Velorian Basin)  
2. Quiet Well chain (through Lumira Sands)  
3. Claimscar chain (through Driftfall)  
4. Returning Span chain (F120—not a Nauw add-on)

No ferry stubs. Softfruit editions smell faintly of peel; House editions add “immediate parent only.”

## Cultural Meaning

Makes Geographical Hierarchy of Dias hand-portable so clerks stop skipping parents.

## Usage

Gifted in Softfruit orientation and clerk nesting drills. Kept in surveyor pockets with the Nesting Structure Comparison Plate drawing reduced to icons.

## Notes

Sibling to Clerk Nesting Primer Card (scale of meaning) and Scale Ladder Glyph (symbol).
