# Gilding Candy Twist

## Name

Gilding Candy Twist

## Overview

A **Gilding Candy Twist** is hard candy in basin-gold paper. It is sweet. It melts. It is not a mood, a memory you can keep, or a light you can follow.

## Description

Thumb-sized, amber when the paper is off, flavored with honey and a little peel. Eaten at gilding hour it tastes like the hour only because the hour is already in the air. Eaten in the dark it tastes like candy. The paper is pretty and useless as a map. There is no second effect, no hearttide lean, no courage.

## Cultural Meaning

Adults who fear Farstock imagine every gold thing is a lure. The candy exists so a child can leave the shop with gold that is allowed to be candy.

## Usage

Sold by the handful. Sticky pockets. Fair weeks. The click-bird is the saga’s gift; the candy is what the shop was already willing to sell to anyone.

## Notes

Do not promote it into an alchemical sweet. Children already know.
