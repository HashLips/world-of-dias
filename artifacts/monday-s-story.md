# Tide-Berth Rest

**Tide-Berth Rest** is an Outer Rim berth set: olive mat and dark cover, a red night-glow square with soft oval face, and paired red slippers, used so sleepers can reach light without scattering footwear or waking the whole rack line.

Glow squares sit left of the mat as in the plate—warm, not harsh. Slippers park upper-left of the berth footprint. Four-finger reach is taught as soft. Children get dimmer glows. Adults who leave one slipper in the aisle walk dawn cleanup. File remains monday-s-story; name is Tide-Berth Rest.
