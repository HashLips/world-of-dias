# Next Lvl

## Name

Next Lvl

## Overview

**Next Lvl** is a Songripe Isles stepped register: a segmented run of red-faced, blue-sided pillars joined by white rib and vertebra forms, watched by a white slit-eyed head and finished by a pink-eyed block with orange fins.

## Description

Isle workshops keep Next Lvl as a teaching craft and a working measure. The body is a horizontal march of tall thin pillars—red to the front, blue on the sides—so a glance can count stages without a written ladder. White bone-like joins and curved rib arcs stitch the pillars into one resonant spine. At the left, a white head with a single black eye-slit sits tucked in the structure, reminder that the register is not mute lumber. A mint-green orb with a yellow bar often hangs in the telling as the soft light of the measuring hour. The right-hand terminus is a heavier block carrying a pale pink almond eye with a blue pupil, plus jagged orange fins that isle hands say are for steadying the next step, not for flying off. People do not treat Next Lvl as a vanity trophy. Local care says you stand before it and name the measure you are actually on; lying about the count is worse than standing still. Resonance lives in the ribbed joins; memory lives in the watching head and the pink eye; wonder is how the charcoal ground makes every color count. The object is shown, lent for teaching, and returned with the fins unchipped and the eye clean.

## Cultural Meaning

On Songripe Isles, Next Lvl is honest counting made visible. Rank talk without the register is considered empty weather.

## Usage

Workshop teaching prop; sometimes carried in festival measures as a silent count. Not sold as a toy that unlocks doors by itself.
