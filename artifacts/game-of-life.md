# Game of Life

## Name

Game of Life

## Overview

**Game of Life** is a kept Cinder Nook sample-stand kit: a red three-legged stand, a pink protecting circle, and a leaf token used so living study stays a careful game with watchers and a bowed caretaker, not a trophy hunt.

## Description

The red tripod is light and stable; the pink circle seats a real or token leaf with veins readable. Two watcher marks and one bowed-care mark remind who may speak and who tends. Ochre mass and loop marks in the plate are optional teaching overlays for “what hangs above the sample.” Nook hosts forbid winning language that ends the leaf. Selling the pink circle as a cute coaster empties the craft. Dias name only.

## Cultural Meaning

Cinder Nook studies life by protecting the piece in play.

## Usage

Ash-soft study hours, guest ecology talks, and children’s careful-looking games. Lent as stand-plus-circle set.

## Notes

Sample-stand kit. Protect the leaf; refuse the trophy.
