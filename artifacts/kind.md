# Kind

## Name

Kind

## Overview

**Kind** is a Hollowmere sit-mat and posture name: a mustard-toned ground cloth placed where a person sits arms-open so lean blue companion-animals may approach, used when room must be made without words.

## Description

The mat is plain, wide enough for one sitter and three small four-leggers. Households keep a deep-red trapezoid panel or screen behind the seat as in the plate—warm backstop, not a cage. Blue companions of Hollowmere yards (lean deer-hound echoforms in local talk) learn the mat means no sudden boots. Children practice open arms until a blue sits to the left hand. Adults who call Kind while kicking space clear are corrected at the next loft meeting.

## Cultural Meaning

In Hollowmere, Kind is body-made welcome. Resonance sticks to honest open arms. Using the mat as bait for capture empties it into cruelty.

## Usage

Yard evenings, post-loft cool-downs, and quiet teachings. Mat brushed; panel wiped.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Matches open-arm sit and blue companions.
