# Knowledge

## Name

Knowledge

## Overview

**Knowledge** is an F120 measure-stance mat: a mustard cloth marked for foot and hand points of a red X-pose, with optional white eye-paints, used before teaching so the body shows open measure and the mouth stays quiet until checks finish.

## Description

Foot marks sit wide; hand marks sit out and slightly down. Learners paint or sleeve red for drills matching the plate. White eye-dots or thin tapes keep the awake-slit reminder. The mat’s top-left may carry a tiny house-mark as collection stamp. Sessions begin in X, breathe, then speak. Collapsing the X to lecture early is a known correction with a return to silence.

## Cultural Meaning

In F120, Knowledge is stance before spill. Resonance favors open measure. Using the mat as a humiliation pose empties it.

## Usage

Teaching yards, frequency drills, and apprentice openings. Mat brushed; paints washed off after.
