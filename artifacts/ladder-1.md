# Ladder

## Name

Ladder

## Overview

**Ladder** is a Driftfall pier climb ladder: bright red two-rail stock with many rungs, kept so a black “shadow duty” spotter stands offset as the plate’s twin, used for loft, hull, and shed climbs where lean angle and rung honesty matter.

## Description

Red paint is maintenance signal—chips mean inspect wood. Spotters take the shadow position left-behind the climber’s path. Floor under the base is kept light-marked like the sky-blue strip so trip clutter shows. Children learn rung-count songs. A ladder left unshadowed on a busy pier may be pulled from service until a spotter is named.

## Cultural Meaning

Around Driftfall, Ladder is climb with admitted risk. Resonance favors counted rungs. Hiding damage under fresh red empties trust.

## Usage

Piers, sheds, festival hangs. Stored dry; inspected after salt storms.

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Matches red ladder and black twin shadow.
