# Untold Tail

## Name

Untold Tail

## Overview

An **Untold Tail** is any catch at Rim-Rest that tangles more than one household's night-line. By custom it belongs to none of them: it is released, and it is not talked about in the morning.

## Description

Rim-Rest households each drop a single night-line from their raft edge at dusk, weighted, with one hook. Most paint a pale eye on the line's float so neighbors can tell whose is whose in lamplight. Lines hang at different depths by family habit. When something swims through the cluster and fouls several lines at once—a ribbon of weed, a long-tailed fish, or something nobody can name—the first household to wake frees the hooks, lets the catch go, and says nothing. Children are taught to untangle without pulling, from the lowest hook up.

## Cultural Meaning

In a place where gossip moves faster than boats, the untold tail is a deliberate silence. A catch that crossed many lines would start arguments about who hooked it first, so the bay removes the prize and the story together. The rule protects neighborliness more than fish.

## Usage

Observed every night the cluster is on the water. Anyone who boasts about an untold tail is expected to cook for the households whose lines it crossed.
