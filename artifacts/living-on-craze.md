# Living on Craze

## Name

Living on Craze

## Overview

**Living on Craze** is an F840 runner harness: a red-white striped lifebuoy-style mid-belt for large runner-birds, a bright blue ankle return-line, and tall black curved hats marking pilot and passenger, used on dotted chart routes when speed must stay recoverable.

## Description

Buoy belts pad the bird’s midsection. Ankle lines clip to route posts marked with red up-triangles inside circles matching the plate’s field. Hats distinguish who may pull the line. Fish-courier jobs keep a blue soft-fish dummy for drills. Children watch from ground; they do not ride. Adults who cut the ankle line for thrills mend every post on the route as apology labor.

## Cultural Meaning

In F840, Living on Craze is permitted speed with a return rope. Resonance favors landed fish and intact lines. Untethered races for show empty the harness into hazard.

## Usage

Chart routes, courier drills, and festival runs with soft landings. Belts inspected; lines coiled.
