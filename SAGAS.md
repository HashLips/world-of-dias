# Sagas of Dias

Sagas are optional narrative journeys through the archive.

They follow inhabitants (or linked story threads) across places and choices so readers feel how Dias works—while still leaving cosmic mysteries open for exploration in the lore folders.

You do not need a saga to understand the repo. Use them when you want a guided story path with emotional payoff.

World identity behind all sagas: [`CORE-PILLARS.md`](CORE-PILLARS.md)

---

## Available sagas

### The Harmonic Saga

Primary narrative thread of Dias. Follows **Calen Brink** learning to read harmonic echoes across F432 and beyond.

- Path: [`HARMONIC-SAGA-PATH.md`](HARMONIC-SAGA-PATH.md)
- Spine: [`world/the-harmonic-saga.md`](world/the-harmonic-saga.md)

Includes three movements (19 chapters) plus optional Driftfall side journeys listed on that path (including Delci Beta and the Passage Round).

## Adding a saga

When a new saga journey is ready:

1. Give it a reading-path file (or a clear section on an existing path file).
2. Add one short entry to **this** list.
3. Do **not** rewrite [`GUIDED-PATH.md`](GUIDED-PATH.md) steps 1–5.
