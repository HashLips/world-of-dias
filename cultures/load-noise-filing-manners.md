# Load-Noise Filing Manners

## Name

Load-Noise Filing Manners

## Overview

**Load-Noise Filing Manners** are how Operators and House juniors label civic wonder as **noise** without saying wonder aloud—so the core can keep standing and outer rings do not panic.

## Beliefs and Values

Keep load legible. Carry what you cannot publish. Prefer anonymous scrap, deferred escort, nonactionable stamps, and “enhanced monitoring” (fear with a schedule). The Calibration Saga shows the cost: shapes filed as nothing can nest. After the Caliburn escapes, cover stories compete; Quiet Unbinding stays ambient coincidence, not a named army.

## Practices and Traditions

- Left-pocket drops at the Anonymous Drop Wall (Kesh and kin).
- Tokens without names.
- Pellun-style nonactionable until the stamp hesitates.
- Vela’s napkin shapes treated as off-shift doodles until they match an Echoform.

## Lore

Rhymes with Harmonic core-load scenes without copying Calen’s gift plot. Operator labor in *The Load the Core Carries* is the serious twin; Calibration is what happens when noise grows a slit face.
