# Calareth Bay Kitchen Foodways

## Overview

**Calareth Bay Kitchen Foodways** is sanctuary eating: few pots, careful reef catch, mainland gift-crates, and meals that never excuse moving stone.

## Beliefs and Values

A bed is a privilege; a meal is assent practiced with salt. Closing reefs (limits before damage) applies to appetite. Boisterous joy belongs on Coral Strand—not in Quiet approaches.

## Practices and Traditions

### Staples

- Bay kitchen herbs; careful small catch; moss-green dock simple breads.
- Guest-hall porridge and salt soap appetite (travelers often eat lighter).
- Green Months: Verdant Reach herb gift-crates “for the bay kitchens.”

### Luxury

- Coral Strand feast days (tide-pool teaching ends in shared joy food).
- Still Months cool-hour work meals—mending with tea, not spectacle.
- Rare mainland fruit treated as guest gift, not rank theater (Nauw banana-boast lands poorly).

### Crisis / pressure

- Ferry-wait rations when windows close.
- Ribbon disputes: kitchens do not feed belonging-revoked guests as if nothing happened—porch food maybe; hall beds no.
- Tide-Loud Weeks: reef-speaking weather limits harvest; Watch slates guide safe appetite windows.

### Kindness practice

Feed intentional arrivals who accept ribbon rules; teach children reef limits as care.

### Hard edge

No feast that celebrates broken assent; Watch can refuse kitchen privilege with belonging.

## Lore

Calareth meals taste like restraint that still loves you.
