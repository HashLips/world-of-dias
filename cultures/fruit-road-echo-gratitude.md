# Fruit-Road Echo Gratitude

## Name

Fruit-Road Echo Gratitude

## Overview

**Fruit-Road Echo Gratitude** is the road custom of leaving thanks in a way the route can hold—water shared, a peel buried, a joke told clean—so later walkers meet kindness as faint aftertone rather than as obligation paperwork.

## Beliefs and Values

Roads remember use. Brutal haste leaves sour dust. Shared water leaves kind dust. You do not own the echo you leave; you only choose whether it helps the next pair of boots.

## Practices and Traditions

Caravans teach apprentices to notice where the road “answers” with ease after a good camp and resists after a cruel one. Gratitude is practical: a mark on the world’s habit, not a prayer for miracles. No one serious claims gratitude opens frequency doors.

## Lore

Pairs with Road Dust Gratitude Mark as the phenomenon travelers argue about at fires. Softfruit recognizes the same law at tables; fruit-road crews insist the law also walks.

## Notes

Does not invent band ferries. Gratitude is F432 place manners under resonance law.
