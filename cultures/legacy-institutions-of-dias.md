# Legacy Institutions of Dias

## Overview

**Legacy Institutions of Dias** are how names and duties outlive bodies—ledgers, ribbons, hearths, unfinished works—without needing a single inheritance church.

## Beliefs and Values

A good legacy feeds the next table. A bad one traps the next generation in someone else’s unfinished quarrel.

## Practices and Traditions

### Institution kit

| Institution | What passes | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Salt Ledger / Compact witness** | Overlap testimony; yard standing | Ink-first erasure of living witnesses |
| **Assent ribbons / Watch rolls** | Belonging promises; retired ribbon etiquette | Tourism of grief; ribbon as brand |
| **Hearth / Ember continuity** | Warmth duty; cold-gap honesty | Sealing hearths against need |
| **Open Hand revision bands** | Process knowledge; visible seams | Forced-final clamps that erase undo |
| **Fruit-road / Softfruit manners** | Share habits; third-chair practice | Coin replacing care language |
| **Tube inheritance (Glasswater)** | Night duty; denied reflections | Children left alone with wrong-season tubes |
| **Mir / civic memory** | Public mood archive | Leaf as only history |

### Kindness practice

Leave one usable instruction with every inheritance—not only a name.

### Hard edge

Weaponizing a dead person’s stamp, ribbon, or kiln slate against their kin.
