# The Quiet Unbinding

## Overview

**The Quiet Unbinding** is the lightly named slow-dark throughline for stage prep: a pattern of pressures that loosen bonds—seals, maps, belonging, undo, horizon—without a completed reveal or named final army.

## Beliefs and Values

Enough unease for heroes later; not enough to cancel Softfruit lunch. Keep perforated. Do not promote to confirmed cosmology here.

## Practices and Traditions

### Authoring rule

Show as coincidence of pressures, a sealed shelf, a joke that goes quiet—not a villain monologue explaining the Unbinding. Sagas may name agents later. Secrets that rhyme with this throughline stay distributed—see [`Secrets of Dias`](../world/secrets-of-dias.md)—not dumped here.
