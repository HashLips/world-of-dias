# F432 Information Channels

## Overview

**F432 Information Channels** maps how word travels—from gangplank-fast to House-slow—without pretending one pipe owns truth.

## Beliefs and Values

Every channel has a honesty weather. Gangplank gossip is fast and wet. House seals are dry and late. Good citizens learn which pipe fits which need.
