# Row Tea-Cylinder Custom

## Name

Row Tea-Cylinder Custom

## Overview

**Row Tea-Cylinder Custom** drinks tea from graduated cylinders so quantities stay honest during argument. Lines do not flatter; cups do.

## Beliefs and Values

If you cannot agree on a graph, you can still agree on a milliliter mark. Argument pauses require honest refill. Mint is optional comfort, not a spell.

## Practices and Traditions

Orrin’s signature prop—tea in a cylinder on the alcove bench and at the Tea Slit Bench. Cylinder mint steeped after bitter fights. Auntie Mosscoil still brings ordinary cups and is ignored affectionately. The custom predates the Spectrum Frame and outlives the vault; students inherit it with incomplete Frame models.

## Lore

Everyday face of Measurement Saga culture: honesty in small tools before honesty in publish packets.
