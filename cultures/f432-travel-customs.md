# F432 Travel Customs

## Overview

**F432 Travel Customs** is the shared manners of going—what you pay, what you restore, how you greet a waystation, and when movement itself becomes insult.

## Beliefs and Values

Arrival without reciprocity is raid with luggage. Speed is not virtue on hush roads. A journey that cannot name its next water, toll, or hail is not brave—it is unfinished planning.

## Practices and Traditions

### Before you leave

- Name destination **ring / cove / yard / assent**—not only “west.”
- Pack regional soft words (below water; kettle peace; First Line).
- Know whether you travel **festival-dense** or **danger-sparse** (see Festival vs Danger Movement).

### Payment forms that count as “paid”

| Form | Where accepted | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VEL Marks | Most supervised tolls & markets | Dominant shared coin |
| Labor / documented rest | Span Toll, some waystations | Reciprocity made schedule |
| Wire / wood / news | Claimscar kettles | Movement into belonging |
| Ribbon + assent | Calareth | Not coin-first |
| Soft guide-debt | Lumira with Keep | Path only; not publish |
| Pot favor | Veloria underbelly | Parallel to VEL when trust holds |

### Waystation manners

- Restore what you take (Fruit Road Reciprocity).
- Softfruit / Shared Table seat before hard bargain when quarreling on arrival.
- Do not Concord-rank-drop in Sorel yards.
- Do not demand accurate well maps as “safety paperwork” in Wabet.

### Insults of movement

- Skipping hail on Rim water.
- Solo redmile swagger after dusk reports.
- Painting claims with tourist paint.
- Ferrying into Calareth without ribbon intent.

## Kindness practice

Teach a new traveler one payment form and one soft word before the first toll.

## Hard edge

Refuse convoy welcome to those who take without restoring; turn back loud desert traffic.
