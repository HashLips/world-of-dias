# F840 Hearth-Corridor Architecture

## Overview

**F840 Hearth-Corridor Architecture** builds survival as a warm network: shelter stations, ember veins, and corridors you follow for heat rather than for scenery.

## Beliefs and Values

A good structure **keeps warmth honest**. Beauty is secondary to whether a cold-gap crossing can be timed. Continuity of hearth-chain is civic duty.

## Practices and Traditions

### Forms

- **Core Hearth Spine:** reliable warm route with shelter nodes.
- **Kindle Spurs:** secondary branches with variable heat.
- **Cold Gaps:** intervals that demand gear, timing, or guides.
- Materials read as ash-brick, ember-glass, heat-sink stone; interiors glow orange-gold at nodes.

### Sacred / domestic / defensive

- **Sacred:** oldest continuous hearths; warmth-that-stayed shrines.
- **Domestic:** shelter bunks along the spine.
- **Defensive:** sealing a cold gap against pursuit—or against letting cold in.

## Lore

Exile stories from F432 sometimes borrow hearth-corridor metaphors for Secondfire kilns—warmth as remaking—without claiming the same physics.
