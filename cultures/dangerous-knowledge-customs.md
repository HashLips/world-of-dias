# Dangerous Knowledge Customs

## Overview

**Dangerous Knowledge Customs** governs study that can wound rooms, wells, memories, or selves—F500 talk, Zero talk, perfect water maps, unconsented seals—without pretending ignorance is always virtue.

## Beliefs and Values

Some knowledge is a tool; some is a weather system. Holding it requires customs, not swagger. Soft words exist so warning remains possible. Sealed study can be care or hoarding—stances disagree.

## Practices and Traditions

### Hazard clusters

| Knowledge | Why dangerous | Custom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Horizon-Null / Nullmouth detail** | Thins rooms; erasure curiosity | Warn once, argue twice, leave; prefer House cold terms for official work |
| **Frequency Zero forcing** | Sacred refusal / hazard filing | Do not tool-ize; Houses may file Zero-talk as hazard language |
| **Perfect public well maps** | Advertise water → wound | Keep maps wrong on purpose; path-only teaching |
| **Unconsented memory seals** | Steal echo-story | Taboo craft; social freeze |
| **Core Operator floor detail** | Panic / load risk | Ring clearance; “above my ring” |
| **Bleed timing as weapon** | Stack bruise exploitation | Do not teach as sport |

### How initiates are taught danger

1. Soft pairs first (taboo + substitute).  
2. Witness of aftermath (a quiet tavern; a lost well story).  
3. Clearance or Keep assent before instruments.  
4. Right to refuse sealed curiosity sold as status.

### Stance friction

- **File and stabilize:** classify first; release later.  
- **Witness and share:** some sealing feels like abandonment.  
- **Make and leave open:** refuse forced-final knowledge that erases undo.  
- **Silence or exile:** move the wound rather than rewrite the file.

## Kindness practice

Warn without humiliating; give soft words with the taboo.

## Hard edge

Sport naming; selling Null toys; publishing wells; Zero as party trick.
