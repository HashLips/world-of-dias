# F960 Scaffold Architecture

## Overview

**F960 Scaffold Architecture** treats buildings as elegant iterations: frames that complete and uncomplete, revision seams left visible, persistence without pretending finality.

## Beliefs and Values

A finished facade that denies its drafts is dishonest. Draftkin and Open Hand makers prefer **truthful scaffolding**—structure you can revise without shame.

## Practices and Traditions

### Forms

- Sketch-line beams; chalk-geometry walls; proportions that refine across seasons.
- Galleries and halls that change circulation when a “revision” settles.
- Nest and workshop architecture that looks like crumpled concept models made sturdy.
- Avoid horror deformation; emphasize deliberate iteration.

### Sacred / domestic / defensive

- **Sacred:** halls of last-line-never-drawn contemplation.
- **Domestic:** revisable dwellings with stable accountability addresses.
- **Defensive:** frames that refuse hostile permanence—hard to occupy what won’t stay still.

## Lore

Visiting Nauw architects either fall in love or get headaches. Both reactions are common.
