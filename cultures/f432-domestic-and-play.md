# F432 Domestic and Play

## Overview

**F432 Domestic and Play** gathers household objects, greetings, bedtime fears, and children’s games—the texture of staying another day.

## Beliefs and Values

Homes teach belonging before Houses do. Games teach soft-step and check-in before sermons. Bedtime fears should be nameable so they do not become Null sport.

### Bedtime fears (child-scale)

- Redmile stilts on lonely roads (bells help).  
- Pale rope if you shout (soft steps help).  
- Missing a hail (answer practice helps).  
- Stamp that sends you south (kind clerks help—sometimes).  
- Thin-room talk (leave before third naming).  
- Wrong-color moth on the sill (usually just a moth—usually).
