# F432 Plural Tender Economies

## Overview

**F432 Plural Tender Economies** names the stack of valid payment beside and beneath VEL—so gifts and marks can share one scene without contradiction.

## Beliefs and Values

One tender to rule all trade would flatten Dias. VEL makes strangers interoperable; local tenders make neighbors real. Cheating a kindness tender is often worse manners than shorting a coin.

## Practices and Traditions

### Tender stack

| Tender | Where it clears | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **VEL Marks** | Supervised markets, tolls, most stalls | Counterfeit stamps; ring-mouth pricing |
| **Barter goods** | Roads, yards, hush camps | Unequal bruise value; raid branding |
| **Labor / documented rest** | Span Toll, waystations | Fake rest papers |
| **Pot favors** | Veloria Pot Signal Lane | Broken trust; collector crackdowns |
| **Ladle fee / fruit share** | Softfruit Table Hall | Banana-boast without share |
| **Wire / wood / news** | Claimscar kettle | Kettle theft; empty news |
| **Ribbon assent** | Calareth | Belonging revoked |
| **Path-only guide debt** | Lumira | Publishing the path |
| **Catch share / First Line** | Outer Rim | Lied hail; greedy catch |
| **Good Trade fairness** | Majestic Good Trade | Still-hour broken by hustle |

### How they braid

A traveler may pay Span Toll in labor, buy Basin tea in VEL, settle a second-ring debt in pot favor, and offer Softfruit fruit-share the same day. Clerks prefer VEL-clean; streets prefer whatever keeps someone fed.

### Kindness practice

Accept a valid local tender before shaming someone for short VEL.

### Hard edge

Forging VEL; selling pot favors as threats; treating ribbon or kettle peace as purchasable exemptions from ethics.
