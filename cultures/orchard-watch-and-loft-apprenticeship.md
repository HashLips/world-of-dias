# Orchard Watch and Loft Apprenticeship

## Overview

**Orchard, Watch, and Loft Apprenticeship** gathers the living outdoor schools of Wabet fruit roads, Calareth/Lumira restraint, and Outer Rim song coordination.

## Beliefs and Values

Rope, ribbon, and soft-step are literacy. A child who can only recite House codes but cannot answer a hail is unfinished for Rim life—and vice versa.

## Practices and Traditions

### Orchard / fruit-road

- Soft-Bear ledge etiquette; unclaimed breakfast honesty.  
- Share-knife watching before touching Lucky forms.  
- Wagon-wave codes; bruise-timing as math you feel.  
- Reciprocity audits at waystations (Open Hand Way teaching).

### Watch / Keep / isle

- Ribbon meaning walked, not only posted.  
- Reef limits; Elder Shelf day-pass patience.  
- Soft-step sand trays; decoy dunes before true wells.  
- Yara Moss–style slate walks without softening rules.

### Loft / Tide

- Rope-before-stone.  
- Eight-beat First Line practice.  
- Storm Choir listening; lied hail ethics.  
- Whale-path stanzas before tonnage arguments.

### Kindness practice

Teach check-in / soft-step / ribbon before giving power tools (tillers, maps, knives).

### Hard edge

Using apprentices as unpaid claim paint; sending kids alone on redmiles or noon slips as “toughening.”
