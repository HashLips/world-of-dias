# F432 Band Speech Customs

## Name

F432 Band Speech Customs

## Overview

**F432 Band Speech Customs** are how home-band people talk about other frequencies in markets, galleries, and Softfruit rooms—metaphor first, designation second, ferry never.

## Beliefs and Values

- Numbers are tools; feelings often arrived earlier.
- Poles (light / null) are named carefully; quieter bands deserve equal care, not gossip toys.
- Speaking a band as a destination is bad manners unless you are discussing exceptional omen or sealed craft—and even then, Softfruit watches your face.

## Practices and Traditions

Street talk prefers images: second dawn, cold pocket, same road not same day, wet paint, brief true face, warmth that stayed. Ledger talk prefers F-numbers with confidence tags. Mixing the two without warning is how pamphlets are born.

Children learn home square before void notch. Adults who skip poles and jump to reveal gossip get Band Confusion Corrections with fruit.

## Lore

Overlaps Frequency Speech Metaphors and Folk Resonance Metaphors. Houses translate folk images into classification language; Softfruit translates classification back into lunch.

## Notes

Does not add ferries. Keeps Established Frequency Realms speakable in ordinary F432 life.
