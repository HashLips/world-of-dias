# Sealed Knowledge Ethics

## Overview

**Sealed Knowledge Ethics** argues when hiding is care, when it is power, and when “nonactionable” is a kindness or a knife.

## Beliefs and Values

Seals can protect wells, cores, and children. Seals can also erase witnesses and grow Null dares in the dark. Stance clusters disagree; streets feel the weather either way.

## Practices and Traditions

### Seal types

| Seal | Care reading | Power reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wrong maps** | Mercy for water | Keep monopoly on path |
| **Operator filter** | Load safety | Core contempt for rings |
| **House hazard file** | Panic control | Narrative ownership |
| **Ribbon / assent limits** | Belonging protection | Tourism gatekeeping |
| **Open Hand refusal to seal undo** | Stewardship | “Chaos” to hardliners |
| **Ren-style null seals** | Containment | Invitation to Cells |

### Ethics questions (use in scenes)

1. Who is protected if this stays sealed?  
2. Who is abandoned?  
3. Is there a soft-word warning path?  
4. Does the seal erase undo or only delay release?

## Kindness practice

Pair every seal with a usable warning in a public channel when harm is imminent (Bleed-Sky shutters, not Null anatomy).

## Hard edge

Sealing to win a schism; sealing children’s Softfruit quarrels as “hazard.”
