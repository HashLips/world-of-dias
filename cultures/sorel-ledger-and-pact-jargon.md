# Sorel Ledger and Pact Jargon

## Overview

**Sorel Ledger and Pact Jargon** is kettle, claim, and slate speech—words for debts, masks, paint, and second beginnings.

## Beliefs and Values

Ink is optional; memory is not. A pact you cannot say plainly will not hold under vent haze. Concord language used as rank is a fight invitation.

## Practices and Traditions

### Kettle and yard

| Phrase | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Wire-bowl** / **news-bowl** | Stew earned by contribution |
| **Kettle peace** | Truce at cookfire; claims paused |
| **Paint-true** | Claim mark within Vent-Claim Rule |
| **Double-paint** | Two crews same sprout—crisis |
| **Tarp edge** | Where outsiders stand until trusted |
| **Beach-calm** | Shore hang-about that hides inland grit |

### Salt Ledger talk

At the Ledger, “recording” often means **overlapping witness in speech**, not writing. “Put it in salt” = say it where crews can hear without choosing a side yet.

### Sample dialogue

- “Wire-bowl or tarp edge—pick one.”
- “Paint-true till dusk. Double-paint and we stop being funny.”
- “Keep Concord boots off my kettle peace.”
- “Second-slate if you want remaking; don’t perform refusal at Laughter Week.”

## Kindness practice

Offer news-bowl language to new exiles before paint lectures.

## Hard edge

Exile-within for kettle theft and claim lies; cold shoulder for Concord-rank dropping.
