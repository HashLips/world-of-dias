# Myth Cycles of Fracture and Kin

## Overview

**Myth Cycles of Fracture and Kin** groups story-families that encode cosmic wound and local kinship—without treating every tale as confirmed cosmology.

## Beliefs and Values

Myths are how a loveable world remembers pressure. They comfort by giving shape; they frighten by refusing tidy endings. Canon status in `myths/` files still governs what is rumor vs established.

### How cycles are used

- Children get soft versions first (bells, fruit thanks).  
- Initiates get harder edges (Null naming limits; exile moral weight).  
- Houses may flatten myths into hazard codes; streets keep the poetry.

### Kindness practice

Tell the version that helps the listener survive the week—without lying that dread is fake.

### Hard edge

Using Fracture myths to justify cruelty; selling “confirmed cosmology” from a tavern cycle.
