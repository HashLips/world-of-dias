# Echoes of the Dead

## Overview

**Echoes of the Dead** is the living argument that after-death resonance can comfort a table—or hollow a room into horror—depending on stance, consent, and seal ethics.

## Beliefs and Values

Gift-listeners hear a parent’s laugh in wind and call it care. Resource-extractors try to bottle it. Warning-speakers fear Bleed-shaped grief. Illusion-skeptics say memory is enough. All four can sit at one funeral and quarrel kindly—or not.

## Practices and Traditions

### Comfort habits

| Habit | Feel | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Afterglow name-light (brief) | Warm recognition | Demanding a repeat as proof |
| Kept chair / third chair for absent | Belonging continues | Trapping the living in performance |
| Song that names once | Shared release | Endless naming as spectacle |
| Unfinished work left visible | Honest inheritance | Forcing heirs to finish against will |

### Horror habits

| Habit | Feel | Who pushes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forced playback of death-echo | Violation | Null curiosity; bad Operators |
| Thin-room listening on wake nights | Extraction | Taboo device sport |
| Claiming Unraveled “isn’t death” as comfort | Gaslight dread | Hardliners / Cells |
| Selling afterglow as product | Market mysticism gone cruel | Black-marketeers |

### Kindness practice

Ask consent before sharing a death-echo story outside kin.

### Hard edge

Turning comfort-echo into a sealed House asset without the family’s yes.
