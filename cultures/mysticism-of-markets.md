# Mysticism of Markets

## Overview

**Mysticism of Markets** is belief that never needs a temple: prices, pots, moths, and fruit rank as omen and meaning—atheism-of-echoes that still prays with a ladle.

## Beliefs and Values

Some swear echoes are filing noise and still won’t crush a Banner Moth. Some treat VEL-clean settlement as moral weather. Markets comfort with predictability and frighten with stamp-heavy seasons.

A Resonant House clerk and a fruit mystic can share Softfruit Hall: one files, one thanks, both tip the ladle.

### Kindness practice

Defend kindness markets as sacred-adjacent infrastructure.

### Hard edge

Selling omens as guaranteed cosmology; crushing moths; weaponizing rank against the hungry.
