# Beast and Relay Transport

## Overview

**Beast and Relay Transport** integrates living engines already seeded in the fauna—ponies, oxen, llamas, goats, relays—into how journeys actually move.

## Beliefs and Values

A beast is not a cart with blood. Mistreatment on fruit roads loses waystation welcome. Relay duty (F840 cousins; F432 metaphors) teaches that continuity of care is logistics.

## Practices and Traditions

### Common F432 draft & ride

| Animal | Typical work | Care note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fruit-Road Draft-Ponies** | Eastbound wagons | Bruise-timing rests; water before boast |
| **Nauw Ring-Ox / Basin Oxen** | Heavy Basin / ring freight | Gate-small patience; fodder tallies |
| **Openhand Draft-Goats** | Way / maker hill paths | Stubborn wisdom; rope ethics |
| **Mountain Trade Llamas** | Height & Good Trade approaches | Pack honesty; thin air manners |
| **Hearttide River Horses** | Emotional-loud river work | Don’t fight secrets nearby |
| **Ashen Relay Ponies** (F840 / talk) | Warmth-corridor metaphor & rare presence | Relay rotation before ceremony |

### Relay as method

- Swap tired beasts at waystations (Fruit Road).
- Ember-style watch rotations as human relay tech.
- Raft-ring “relay” of news along gangplanks (information transport).

### Kindness practice

Name the animal in journey talk; rest on bruise and heat, not only clock.

### Hard edge

Waystations refuse chronic overdrivers; Rim shames those who treat whale-kin as draft.
