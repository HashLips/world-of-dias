# Echo interpretation stances

## Name

Echo interpretation stances

## Overview

Across Dias, organizations and cultures agree that resonance leaves traces—and disagree fiercely about what those traces **mean** and what should be done with them.

## Beliefs and Values

Common axes of disagreement:

- what echoes are (record, hazard, sacred voice, market noise, unfinished draft of reality)
- whether memories should be preserved or sealed
- whether frequencies should be manipulated
- who may hold powerful Resonant Artifacts
- which technologies should exist
- how resonance should be studied
- whether some frequencies are too dangerous to approach
- whether the past should be listened to at all
- what **Frequency Zero** names (if it should be named)

## Practices and Traditions

| Stance cluster | Tendency | Living exemplars in canon |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **File and stabilize** | Classify first; release later; panic is a governance failure | Resonant Houses; Veloria Ring Protocol habits |
| **Load discipline** | Keep systems legible; treat some echo-rise as nonactionable noise unless core load fails | Valorian Operators; core harmonic classification rule |
| **Make and leave open** | Creation, unfinishedness, and revision as ethical practice; resist final sealing | The Open Hand of Making; F960-adjacent making culture |
| **Witness and share** | Basin-first or shore-first testimony; listening as civic hygiene | Attitudes seen around Calen’s allies, H-11 leanings, Driftfall witness habits |
| **Silence or exile** | Remove inconvenient resonance from public life; move people rather than rewrite the filing | Pressures Calen meets under House stress and Sorel exile roads |

These are **stances**, not exclusive membership cards. Individuals cross them. Houses schism over them (H-11 vs H-19).

## Lore

This matrix does not invent new factions. It organizes how existing groups already behave so future stories can align characters with recognizable worldviews—or show them breaking from one.
