# Null-Distance Courtesy

## Name

Null-Distance Courtesy

## Overview

**Null-Distance Courtesy** is F432’s public manners around talk of F500. Warn. Do not invite. Tag. Do not tour. Leave Frequency Zero open. The courtesy is a soft limit practiced in docks, Rows, and classrooms so curiosity does not dress itself up as a party and walk toward an edge that does not owe anyone a story with an ending.

It is etiquette under dread. It is not a map.

## Beliefs and Values

Curiosity is allowed. Sport approaches are not. A person may ask what the margin is said to do to memory. A person may not hire a boat, a guide, or a joke-crew to “go see the cairn” as if null were a viewpoint. Survivor partiality deserves belief without a demand for complete maps. Partial is not failure of nerve. Partial is what the edge does to testimony.

Names for the Unraveled, Horizon-Nulls, and distant companies stay unconfirmed in polite rooms. Polite, here, means unwilling to promote a rumor into a taxonomy because the tavern was bored. Temporary names are a mercy. Permanent names are handles, and handles are how people convince themselves they can carry the margin home.

## Practices and Traditions

Hang a null-proximity warning tag beside the no-ferry jokes, so the joke and the warning share a wall and neither gets to pretend it is the whole culture. Refuse abyss party plans in the same tone you would refuse a fire in a library: not hysteria, refusal. Teach the identity-slip story as caution, a rain that asks your name twice, and stop before the teaching becomes a ghost tour. When a witness returns missing hours, you do not title the thing they saw in order to comfort yourself.

Softfruit collects the comedy edge, the sticker, the exaggerated tag. Houses file the serious edge. Both are the courtesy if they refuse the tour.

## Lore

Dock and Row share the ethic even when they share little else. Ignore it and the consequence is social first and worse after: you become the person whose plan other people will not staff, and if you go anyway the stories that return are the kind the courtesy was invented to keep from spreading as invitation. Zero stays unsolved. That is not a gap in the culture. That is the culture’s point.

## Notes

Culture of soft limits. Not confirmed abyss taxonomy. No ferry, no sport, no closed answer.
