# F200 Filament Architecture

## Overview

**F200 Filament Architecture** describes structures that read as lenses, standing waves, and intentional geometry rather than cut stone—architecture of light-flow for energy-kind life.

## Beliefs and Values

A “building” is a **coherence volume**: a place where pattern holds long enough to gather. Shadow in the F432 sense is rare; brightness modulation is wall, door, and greeting.

## Practices and Traditions

### What you “see”

- Vertical or diagonal **filament ribs** along Aurel Meridian’s bright spine.
- Haloed volumes that suggest rooms without casting familiar shade.
- Lattice gatherings where Aurel-kind socialize as nodes in air.
- Path = current; boundary = interference fringe.

### Limits for F432 visitors

Carbon metaphors fail. Doors may be **permission in the field**, not hinges. Touching a corona without invitation is as rude as kicking a Veloria gate.

### Sacred / domestic / defensive

- **Sacred:** meridian nodes of high coherence.
- **Domestic:** resting lattices; soft corona hearths.
- **Defensive:** dissonance fields that turn strangers aside without violence.

## Lore

F432 sailors call it “buildings made of held breath.” Aurel-kind find that phrase almost accurate.
