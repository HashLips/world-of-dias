# Joy of Learning Practices

## Overview

**Joy of Learning Practices** protects the loveable middle of education—galleries that keep afternoons, songs that teach coordination, craft mistakes that become style—so learning is not only fear management.

## Beliefs and Values

A world that only teaches danger becomes grimdark. Dias wants children who can clap once for Mega Butterfly luck, count Banner Moth fair-snow, and turn a crooked weld into a signature seam.

## Practices and Traditions

### Mistakes → style

- Draftkin revision seams as intelligence.  
- Kiln glaze “errors” kept as house marks.  
- Loft stanzas that wobble then become local favorites.  
- Pot codes that reseed after collectors smash them—literacy as joyous resistance.

### Kindness practice

Praise a useful mistake publicly once; teach the fix privately if needed.

### Hard edge

Turning someone’s learning wound into fair entertainment; forcing gallery uncanny on the unwilling.
