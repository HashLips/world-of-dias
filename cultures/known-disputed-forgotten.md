# Known Disputed Forgotten

## Overview

**Known, Disputed, Forgotten** is how Dias grades the past so authors do not accidentally harden rumor into lab fact.

## Beliefs and Values

Filing everything as known creates panic or tyranny. Forgetting on purpose can be mercy (wrong maps) or crime (erased witnesses). Dispute is often the honest middle.

## Practices and Traditions

### Grades

| Grade | Meaning | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Known** | Lived agreement across many communities | Frequencies exist after Fracture; F432 trades in VEL; Long Gate crosses to Hearthvale; Silence Law binds Lumira travel |
| **Disputed** | Strong stories, competing frames | Fracture cause; Distant Companies war; Lucky Fruit mechanism; how many Houses truly bind realm policy; Calen-scale testimony vs sealed codes |
| **Forgotten** | Lost, sealed, or refused | Full Prime Realm daily life; true Operator floor maps for public; some well paths; names thinned by Nullmouth rumor; Zero-as-tool attempts |

### Who grades

Houses prefer known via classification. Streets keep disputed alive as myth. Keeps sometimes choose forgotten-as-mercy. Open Hand leaves revision seams so forgotten can be recovered without false finality.

### Kindness practice

Label your certainty when teaching children history.

### Hard edge

Selling disputed as known for power; forcing forgotten that should have been witnessed.
