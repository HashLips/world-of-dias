# Softfruit Ladle Queue Manners

## Name

Softfruit Ladle Queue Manners

## Overview

**Softfruit Ladle Queue Manners** keep a sticky court fair. Tokens, second helpings, the theatrical refusal of almost-oranges, and a delay before any stamp, all without cruelty and without letting fairness become a scold. Hunger is noticed before a load is published. That order is the whole politics of the shelf.

Joy here has standards. Sweetness without noticing is just sugar on a hierarchy.

## Beliefs and Values

Children should learn the custom by watching hooks, not by being lectured into gratitude. Pride is the only poverty Softfruit enjoys mocking: the person who would rather win breakfast than share it. A second helping is a civic tool, not a prize and not a currency. Spend it like currency and you have misunderstood the wood you are standing under.

Stamps are not evil. Stamps that land before mouths do are how a street becomes a House’s bad mood. The manners hold the pause.

## Practices and Traditions

Shelf fairness checks before the Pot Signal queue is allowed to feel important. Almost-oranges refused with theater, so the standard stays funny enough to remember. Ladles rinsed with mint, a small clean smell that tells the next person the tool was loved and not hoarded. Field-interview edges only after the rush, when Sera or someone like her can ask questions without standing between a child and a bowl. Hosts rotate. The apron is sticky on purpose and owned by no one in particular.

Peel-boat races under the shelf are part of the manners. Play is how the court keeps seriousness from hiring a uniform.

## Lore

Chapbooks and field notes agree on the sequence even when they disagree on the jokes. The myth of why the ladle comes before the stamp is told to children who know the ending and still want the impatient person to get a second bowl after waiting like a neighbor. Ignore the queue and you can eat if you are loud. You eat as a story the court tells for the wrong morning. The second helping arrives late, cool, and correct. Nobody is exiled. Everybody has been noticed.

## Notes

Street culture of the Softfruit ways. Do not franchise the court into a brand that forgets the hooks.
