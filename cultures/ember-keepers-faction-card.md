# Ember Keepers Faction Card

## Overview

Faction card for **Ember Keepers**—infrastructural care on F840’s Hearthline; F432 meets them mostly as metaphor cousins (Secondfire) and rare contact.

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Purpose** | Maintain warmth, shelter, relay continuity after crisis |
| **Method** | Heat nodes; cold-gap honesty; watch rotations; earned trust records |
| **Public face** | Quiet resilience; “warmth that stayed” |
| **Private fear** | Cold-gap lies; hearth sealed against need; spectacle overtaking maintenance |
| **Echoes** | Continuity traces in ash—care as listening, not extraction |
