# Life Kind Teaching

## Name

Life Kind Teaching

## Overview

**Life Kind Teaching** is Softfruit and trail-host practice for sorting living neighbors: animal, extraordinary creature, people, or “we do not know yet”—so wonder does not become cruelty and mystery does not become fake certainty.

## Beliefs and Values

A strange outline is not permission to hunt a person. A beloved Soft-Bear is not a citizen. A Sky Drifter is not a pet you failed to catch. Honesty about ambiguity is manners.

## Practices and Traditions

Hosts use four bowls or four card corners:

| Bowl | Kind | Wrong claim corrected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collar | Ordinary animal | “All weird life is monster” |
| Awe / teeth | Extraordinary creature | “It’s just a big dog” when Redmile bells apply |
| Table | People | “Stylized neighbor = creature class” |
| Open / empty | Ambiguous | “Force a species name for Sky Drifters” |

Children sort tokens (dog collar, butterfly wing, bowl spoon, blank chalk). Adults argue edge cases (Human-Eyed Whales, Soft-Bears) without pretending the argument is already won.

## Lore

Companion to Life Kinds of Dias and Life Kind Sorting Card. Band Confusion Corrections handles frequency mix-ups; this custom handles life-kind mix-ups.

## Notes

Never used to authorize harm against peoples. Rumor creatures stay useful as trail ethics even when taxonomy is thin.
