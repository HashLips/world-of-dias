# F610 Anti-Stare Manners

## Name

F610 Anti-Stare Manners

## Overview

**F610 Anti-Stare Manners** refuse to treat residual seams as endless reveal windows. Timed truth is manners: glance, close, power down. A seam that will not dull is a **stare**—rude to the city and dangerous to the watcher.

## Beliefs and Values

Reveal is not omniscience. Forced-Final “done” is not moral victory over unfinished quiet. Widening a seam “just to be honest” is taboo guess dressed as virtue. Veil Scribe timed windows are the cousin discipline.

## Practices and Traditions

When glass stays bright past polite length: stop the run, shutter if Bleed Weather asks, do not schedule research around the leftover crack. Warden Brusk shouts the porch-light version. In the Calibration Saga, these manners arrive **late**—after the Spectrum Frame has already left a residual seam and the Caliburn has nested.

## Lore

The saga is the cautionary tale of stare manners delayed. Publish footnotes later teach the rule students wish Orrin had posted on the alcove door.
