# Sorel Exile and Yard Movement

## Overview

**Sorel Exile and Yard Movement** is how people enter second beginnings—and how Freeband yards, Flagweek weather, and Good Trade quiet reshape the road after.

## Beliefs and Values

Long Gate is moral infrastructure, not only wood and stone. Freedom of movement in Driftfall is real and expensive. Breakfast can hold while flags change; claims cannot.

## Practices and Traditions

### Long Gate (Nauw→Hearthvale)

- Main supervised exile/arrival spine into northern Sorel.
- Shame/defiance braid in mouths and boots.
- Watch-Hounds and northern judgment weather on approaches.
- Myth weight: [`The Long Gate Argument`](../myths/the-long-gate-argument.md).

### Exile roads (unsupervised)

- Wolves, underprovision, first-shelter hunger.
- Compact belonging earned after arrival—not assumed at the bridge.

### Flagweek Strip

- Allegiance weather; breakfast-holds rule.
- Movement through comic dread and cloth snap.

### Claimscar / Driftfall yards

- Paint-true timing; kettle peace vs double-paint.
- Beach hang-about vs inland grit paths to Sprout Vent.
- Provisional settlements that reconfigure—architecture as movement.

### Westfold & Good Trade

- Westfold: proper fee-door toward Wabet calm.
- Good Trade mountain: still-hour approaches; sitting rock as destination without walls.

### Sample hard-week beat

Long Gate crossing → Hearthvale first bowl → Strip breakfast → Claimscar news-bowl → optional vent-window watch → Good Trade still hour if earned.

## Kindness practice

One bowl and a name before asking which crew a newcomer will owe; hold breakfast across flag flips.

## Hard edge

Exile-within for claim lies; cold shoulder for Concord-rank movement theater; wolf-road romanticism that gets people killed.
