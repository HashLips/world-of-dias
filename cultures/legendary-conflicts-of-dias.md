# Legendary Conflicts of Dias

## Overview

**Legendary Conflicts of Dias** are remembered fights—encoded in myth, idiom, and toast—not necessarily repeatable as Present Age campaigns.

## Beliefs and Values

Legend teaches cost. Treating legend as marching orders is how Null sport and crusades start.

## Practices and Traditions

Festivals and tables continue—legend is spice, not diet.
