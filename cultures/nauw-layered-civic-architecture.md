# Nauw Layered Civic Architecture

## Overview

**Nauw Layered Civic Architecture** is the building dialect of trade-rings, gates that measure you, and coast towns that stack work by height. From a distance Nauw reads as ambition in bands: stone markets outside, brighter tech inward, alien power at rare cores.

## Beliefs and Values

A building should **advertise use** without lying about class. Outer-ring stone is not “backward”—it is the handshake with travelers. Core hypertech is not “better life”—it is load-bearing mystery most citizens never enter. Good Nauw building lets you know which ring (or cove tier) you are in by underfoot feel alone.

## Practices and Traditions

### Veloria rings (canonical silhouette)

| Band | Underfoot / wall feel | Typical buildings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outer ring | Cobble, timber beams, lime plaster, guild doors | Market sheds, Softfruit-style halls, flower-pot signal streets, medieval-scale inns |
| Second ring | Brick, glass, ordinary plumbing | Housing blocks, [Ringwash Baths](../regions/ringwash-baths.md), clerk desks, quiet courts |
| Third ring | Steel, composite, lit signage | Workshops, [Sketchy Gallery](../regions/the-sketchy-gallery.md), robotics sheds, Calibration-adjacent labs |
| Central core | Materials that refuse ordinary names; sealed approaches | Operator chambers; realm power plant—seen as glow more than facade |

Streets **step** rather than sprawl: you feel the grade change when you cross a ring seam. Pot-signal flower pots and hanging signs are civic furniture, not decoration only.

### Gates and thresholds

The [DEB Gate](../regions/deb-gate-of-veloria.md) teaches the national habit: entry is **architecture as judgment**—dual forms, monumental color fields, traveler small against the instrument. Smaller town gates echo the idea at human scale (toll arches, painted lintels, Span Toll posts).

### Coastal Nauw (Hollowmere, Rim-Rest)

- **Hollowmere:** cliff-tier building—brine barrels low, family sheds mid, public **net-lofts** high. Lantern hooks make the cliff read as a breathing stringline at night. Hammocks over inns.
- **Rim-Rest:** raft-and-pier clusters; homes are part hull, part borrowed shore; gangplanks as corridors; rope as railing and law.

### Sacred / domestic / defensive in practice

- **Sacred:** gate oath space; Operator core (closed); loft stages for First Line Answers.
- **Domestic:** baths, shared fruit halls, ring apartments, pier lofts.
- **Defensive:** measurement and ring protocol more than curtain walls; bells and dense caravan spacing on redmile approaches.

## Lore

Nauw’s skyline argument is always the same: does the core serve the rings, or do the rings feed the core? Architecture keeps both answers visible in one circle.
