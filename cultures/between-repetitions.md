# Between Repetitions

## Name

Between Repetitions

## Overview

**Between Repetitions** is Driftfall’s name for serial, patterned image-making—and for the exhibition frame that treats a week of labor as many small panels that become one gift. It is how Solven Wetch’s grid practice is understood in public without turning it into House spectacle.

## Beliefs and Values

Repetition is attention, not boredom. A week handed back to the person who lived it is kinder than a portrait that explains them. Pattern lets viewers find themselves in the third row before they find their face.

## Practices and Traditions

- Capture many small moments (lino, chalk, scrap) across days.
- Arrange them in a grid or calendar rhythm.
- Offer the finished week back—often at The Salt Ledger—without forcing thanks.
- Exhibition talk uses the phrase *Between Repetitions* for shows that honor interval over climax.

## Lore

Anchors *Workday* and *The Week He Handed It Back*. Real-world exhibition language and in-world Driftfall habit share the name on purpose: patterned witness is the point.
