# Driftfall Vent-Crew Subculture

## Overview

**Driftfall Vent-Crew Subculture** is the dirt-and-rope life of Freeband crews who stake Sprout Vent runoff, salvage rights, and ugly inland work beach calm prefers not to advertise.

## Beliefs and Values

Breakfast may be shared; claims may not. Freedom without contribution is tourism. Vent windows are weather wars—timing is character.

## Practices and Traditions

- Paint marks with expiry habits under Vent-Claim Rule.
- Cookfire Circle mornings: wire/wood/news buys stew.
- Jokes that run mean and affectionate; dogs as professional witnesses.
- Rivalry that stops at kettle rim until someone paints twice.

## Kindness practice

Feed a new exile one bowl before asking which crew they will owe.

## Hard edge

Enforce double-paint consequences; exile-within-exile for kettle thieves and claim liars.
