# Ember Keeper Hearth Tech

## Overview

**Ember Keeper Hearth Tech** is warmth as infrastructure: shelter nodes, ember relays, cold-gap timing gear, and the duty rotation that keeps a fire from becoming a myth of abandonment.

## Beliefs and Values

Beauty is secondary to whether a traveler can cross a cold gap alive. Continuity of hearth-chain is civic craft. Every child learning relay duty before ceremonial language is tech education.

## Practices and Traditions

### Devices & methods

| Piece | Function |
| --- | --- |
| **Hearth-spine stations** | Reliable warm nodes along Ashen Hearthline |
| **Kindle spur markers** | Variable heat branches—read before commit |
| **Cold-gap kits** | Timing, gear, guide protocols |
| **Ember-glass / ash-brick fittings** | Hold and meter heat |
| **Watch rotations** | Human tech: schedule as device |

### F432 cousins (metaphor + kiln)

Secondfire kilns borrow hearth language for remaking selves—without claiming F840 physics. “Keeping kiln fire” as reconstruction over revenge is cultural tech adjacent to Ember craft.

### Kindness practice

Add kindling; teach relay; publish cold-gap honesty.

### Hard edge

Sealing a hearth against refugees for spite; lying about spur stability.

## Lore

The warmth that stayed is a tech story as much as a myth: rotation, not miracle fuel.
