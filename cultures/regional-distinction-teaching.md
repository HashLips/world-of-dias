# Regional Distinction Teaching

## Name

Regional Distinction Teaching

## Overview

**Regional Distinction Teaching** is Softfruit and road-host practice for keeping Nauw, Wabet, and Sorel from collapsing into one “generic F432 countryside.”

## Beliefs and Values

Same home band, three hearts. Calm is not markets. Exile remaking is not Softfall. Fruit roads connect; they do not erase difference.

## Practices and Traditions

| Wrong claim | Correction |
| --- | --- |
| “Sorel is just dirty Nauw.” | Second beginning; salvage law; Freebands—not Concord rings |
| “Wabet is empty parkland.” | Chosen calm with Keepers, Silence Law, fruit duty east |
| “Nauw is all of F432.” | Home-band capital region—not the west or the south |
| “Long Gate leads to F500.” | Leads to Hearthvale in Sorel |

Hosts use three bowls or three map corners: west calm, north trade, south remake.

## Lore

Companion to F432 Major Regions of Dias and Atlas Hierarchy Teaching. Band Confusion Corrections handles frequency mix-ups; this custom handles region mix-ups inside F432.

## Notes

Map pins for the three regions stay distinct on F432 Map V2 and the dashboard atlas.
