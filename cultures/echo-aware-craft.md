# Echo-Aware Craft

## Overview

**Echo-Aware Craft** is the maker stance behind Dias technology: tools are built knowing that materials, gestures, and use leave traces—and that some traces should be kept, some filtered, some refused.

## Beliefs and Values

A tool that pretends to be mute is lying or unfinished. Craft is judged by **what echoes it invites**. Open Hand makers leave revision seams; Operators keep load legible; Ember Keepers keep warmth continuous; House sealers argue about what the public may hear.

## Practices and Traditions

### Craft clusters (living)

| Cluster | Priority | Typical makers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Make and leave open** | Revision, stewardship, clean undo | Open Hand; F960 Draftkin workshops |
| **Load discipline** | Legibility, nonactionable noise control | Valorian Operators; core techs |
| **File and stabilize** | Classification before release | Resonant Houses; clerks with instruments |
| **Witness and share** | Testimony tools; shore memory | Loft chalk; Salt Ledger speech-tech |
| **Warmth continuity** | Heat that stays; relay duty | Ember Keepers; Secondfire kilns (metaphor cousins) |

### Materials with manners

- Wood and rope that have been sung over (Rim).
- Clay and slate that accept second names (Hearthvale).
- Alloys and unknowns that refuse ordinary naming (Veloria core).
- Filament and lens-fields (F200)—permission as hinge.
- Ash-brick and ember-glass (F840).
- Scaffold frames that revise (F960).

### What “good work” looks like

- Everyday tech that still feels wondrous (a pot that signals without paper).
- Restricted tech that admits its clearance.
- Refusal to build taboo listeners “just to see.”

## Kindness practice

Teach apprentices what a tool remembers before teaching what it can cut.

## Hard edge

Call out craft that erases accountability (sealed harm with no revision band, null toys for sport).
