# Wabet Orchard-Kid Subculture

## Overview

**Wabet Orchard-Kid Subculture** is the soft-boot world of children who grow up between unripe orchards, Softfall paths, and eastbound fruit wagons.

## Beliefs and Values

Fruit is living reciprocity, not only cargo. Unclaimed breakfast is a right of the hungry, not a loophole for the greedy. Loud games near Quiet approaches are grown-up failures taught early.

## Practices and Traditions

- Counting Lucky Fruit rumors without touching perfect forms until an adult names the share.
- Trail games: soft-step races, choir-frog hush contests, wagon-wave codes to Nauw haulers.
- Gift of a single shared fruit to Soft-Bears and to strangers who ask kindly.
- Mocking Nauw banana boasts as comedy—then packing bananas anyway with care.

## Kindness practice

Leave the unclaimed breakfast portion honest; teach younger kids the Soft-Bear fruit ledge.

## Hard edge

Call out orchard theft dressed as “play”; refuse to guide map-curious strangers toward water.

## Notes

Feeds Nauw’s fruit culture without belonging to Concord manners.
