# Lumira Silence Law

## Name

Lumira Silence Law

## Overview

Lumira Silence Law is the desert culture of speaking softly, publishing little, and treating accurate public maps of water as a moral failure.

## Beliefs and Values

The land provides for those who respect its silence. Noise is not only volume—it is advertisement. A well spoken of too clearly becomes a wound.

## Practices and Traditions

- False marks and true paths taught as one craft
- Night camps keep speech below water-sound
- Caravans without Keep assent receive labor-tax or turnback
- Children learn decoy dunes before true oases

## Lore

Iskar Lüm’s Silent Dune Keep is the living enforcement. Quiet Well is the emblematic protected node.

## Notes

Distinct from Averra’s sealed mystery and from Wabet Open Hand orchard ethics.
