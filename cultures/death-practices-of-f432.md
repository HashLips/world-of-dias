# Death Practices of F432

## Overview

**Death Practices of F432** maps burial, ash, sea, song, and silence by region—shapes of grief so loss does not become only spectacle or only void.

## Beliefs and Values

A body deserves a practice the living can finish. Different shores disagree on method; they agree that unfinished farewell breeds rumor-hunger.

## Practices and Traditions

### Practice table

| Region / people | Primary holding | What the living do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nauw / Veloria** | Soft burial or city crypt lanes; fruit left for the absent | Quiet street day; Softfruit plate held empty one meal |
| **Outer Rim / Hollowmere** | Sea return when the body can be found; loft song when it cannot | Nets unmended one tide; name in hail once, then work resumes |
| **Wabet** | Quiet timber graves; soft processions | No shouted names across hush; unclaimed breakfast continues |
| **Lumira** | Silence-first; ash or sealed dune return | Pale rope / silence law manners; no sport naming |
| **Calareth** | Island assent burial; ribbon retired not burned | Few beds stay reserved one night for kin; slate notes quiet hours |
| **Sorel / Claimscar** | Ash or scrap-cairn; Compact witness if disputed | Second-beginning meal after, not costume remake as wake |
| **Hearthvale / Secondfire** | Kiln-adjacent ash; slate name erased gently | Small remaking table; Laughter Week may wait a season |
| **Glasswater** | Dam-side ash scatter or tube-watch night | Tube heirs keep one silent drift for the dead |
| **F840 contact metaphor** | Hearth continuity over tomb spectacle | Warmth that stayed; cold-gap honesty about who is gone |

### Kindness practice

Invite mourners to one concrete next meal. Keep fruit/share open even when names are hard.

### Hard edge

Staging a death for Flagweek entertainment; forcing open-casket echo “proof” on unwilling kin.
