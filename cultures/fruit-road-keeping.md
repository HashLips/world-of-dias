# Fruit-Road Keeping

## Name

Fruit-Road Keeping

## Overview

**Fruit-Road Keeping** is the traveling craft of making fruit stay good across F432’s long corridors. Hard fruit and peel-kept loads travel the wagon fruit road and are sold or traded along waystations as they go. Soft or high-value fruit—and urgent loads—go by plane into **Veloria City** (for example Late Bruise fruit runs from the **Lent Strip**). Keeping is bruise-timing, shade, cool crates, and honest rest—not a magic fridge, and not a recipe for **Vitrin**.

## Beliefs and Values

Fruit that arrives looking perfect after a raid pace is suspicious. Fruit that arrives honest after shade, peel-keep, and waystation rest is welcome. Flight bruises later; it does not forgive theft of stops. Softfruit keep-marks still outrank coin for a marked meal window; road keeping outranks clock pride for the length of a corridor.

Abundance without restoration is still raid. Keeping methods that skip the braid’s pace to force green-crate theater lose waystation welcome the same way take-without-restore carts do.

## Practices and Traditions

### What rides which road

| Load | Usual path | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hard fruit, peel-kept staples, ordinary share crates | Wagon fruit road (Eastbound braid; ox and **Fruit-Road Draft-Ponies**) | Survives ~25 km good days; can sell/trade at waystations as the convoy moves |
| Softfruit, high-value, urgent labeled crates | Private freighter into Veloria (e.g. **The Late Bruise**) | Shortens heat hours; bruise still comes—later, in the shed |
| Mixed convoy | Hard loads on wagons; soft picks lifted when hire and lend allow | Plane is convenience, not the whole road |

Wagon crews sell and trade peel-kept loads at braided stops as they go: soup-carrot yards, shade stalls, Softfruit-adjacent stalls that greet a keep cord before they price. The road is a market that walks. Freighter hulls that pretend otherwise are counted against the shed, not against the braid’s manners.

### Established keeping methods

**Peel-keep.** Loads wrapped or lined with peel-fiber and peel craft already on the road: peel-fiber kin to Softfruit Keep-Mark Cord materials, cart-shade frames where **Eastbound Peel-Vine** climbs, and sprigs of **Peel-Sweet Herb** tucked to keep buyer moods honest when crates open. Peel-keep is packing and scent discipline—rind against rind, fiber against bruise—not an unseen glass breed and not a taught mix. It does not invent Vitrin’s method.

**Cool crates.** Thick-walled share and staple crates that hold morning cool through a good day when stacked with air gaps and kept off sun-facing boards. Green-crate anxiety in **Fruit Trade Economy** is partly this craft failing in heat weeks. Cool crates are wood, wet-rope, and shade—not resonant ice, not a House cold-room that erases distance.

**Shade tarps.** Canvas and vine-trained frames over wagon beds. Stewards raise them before noon heat, not after the first soft fruit weeps. Shade stalls at Span Toll and waystations continue the same manners when carts pause.

**Waystation rest that refreshes loads.** Documented rest is logistics for beasts **and** for fruit. Opening lids in shade, re-stacking for airflow, wetting rope, checking peel-keep wraps, and greeting Softfruit keep cords are part of the stop—not idle theater. Soup-tag and reciprocity peg rules still apply: a cart that takes water and shade without restore does not get to call its fruit “well kept.”

### Bruise-timing as the spine

Stewards (Tomas Share type) time rest to heat and bruise, not to clocks alone. **F432 span and travel distances** give the corridor lengths; Fruit-Road Keeping says what those lengths do to flesh. A ~1,400 km Veloria–Wabet wagon run (~55 days at ~25 km/good day) is a keeping syllabus. A night freighter run is a different syllabus: less heat hours, later bruise, same duty to lend grass and not raid the stops you skipped.

### Kindness practice

Teach new hands to open a crate in shade before they boast; mark soft picks for flight or early sale; leave peel-sweet and airflow for the next crew’s noon.

### Hard edge

Refuse welcome to carts that call Forced-Final pace “keeping”; name freighter loads whose hull count and market count diverge; never publish a Vitrin mix as road craft.

## Lore

Sits beside **Fruit Road Reciprocity** and **Fruit-Road Ethics** as the practical cousin: not only *share and restore*, but *how the fruit survives long enough to be shared*. **The Late Bruise** is named for bruise deferred, not bruise denied. Softfruit keep-mark share rule protects meal windows; Fruit-Road Keeping protects the corridor that feeds those windows.

## Notes

Do not invent a magic fridge. Do not invent Vitrin’s mix. Do not treat freighter skip-stops as a new soft-limit ferry. Distance measures live in **F432 span and travel distances**.
