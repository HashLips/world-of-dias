# Outer Rim Song-Calendar Speech

## Overview

**Outer Rim Song-Calendar Speech** is how loft and deck keep time, route, and ethics in chant—joy as coordination, memory as seamanship.

## Beliefs and Values

A song that cannot be answered is incomplete. A calendar that cannot be sung will not survive fog. Whale talk must stay contestable—reverence and hunger share docks.

## Practices and Traditions

### Core song-forms

| Form | Function | Texture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First Line Answer** | Eight-beat call answered craft by craft; first good catch not taken alone | Joy + audit |
| **Storm Choir** | Check-in roll; missing voice is public matter | Fear + care |
| **Hush Weeks** | Acoustic discipline; thin markets; tea-and-repair talk | Soft economy |
| **Bright Inshore swell-songs** | Market open; raft-rings loud with news | Festival |
| **Whale-path stanzas** | Route recall tied to migration respect | Ethic argument bait |
| **Fruit-barge night songs** | Welcome + inspection of inland guests | Hospitality with teeth |

### Spoken loft slang

| Phrase | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Answer empty** | Failed check-in; social stain |
| **Lied hail** | Claimed presence/safety falsely—worse than missed fish |
| **Eye-back** | Whale looked back; compact talk required |
| **Rope-before-stone** | Child raised right for raft life |
| **Loft-true** | Memory Jessa-types trust; list over speech |
| **Gangplank fast** | News already everywhere |

### Calendar feel in speech

People date events by song-season: “before Hush,” “two Storm Choirs after the barge,” “the Bright Inshore she lied hail.” Inland “Flagweek” metaphors land poorly; sailors translate to loft-time.

### Sample dialogue

- “First Line’s up—don’t answer empty on my watch.”
- “Say eye-back if you saw it; don’t hide behind tonnage talk.”
- “We’ll mend in Hush. Bright Inshore’s for shouting prices, not apologies.”

## Kindness practice

Teach apprentices the check-in before the boast-song.

## Hard edge

Public naming of lied hails; refuse crews that skip whale-path stanzas when paths are active.
