# Reliable and Unreliable Records

## Overview

**Reliable and Unreliable Records** teaches which papers and songs investigators should trust tonight—and which still move people when false.

## Beliefs and Values

Ink is not virtue. Overlap testimony can beat a clean stamp. A wrong map that saves water is kinder than a true chart that sells a well. Forgeries that shape behavior are still evidence—of someone’s motive.

## Practices and Traditions

### Reliability grades (working)

| Grade | Examples | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **High (slow)** | Watch slates; toll books with marshal marks; Salt Ledger overlap talk | Logistics; disputes |
| **Medium** | Song calendars; loft hail chalk; Softfruit manners chapbooks | Weather; teaching |
| **Opinion-fast** | Mir leaves; market pamphlets | Mood; never sole proof |
| **Mercy-wrong** | Quiet Well charts; path-only notes | Survival; do not “correct” in public |
| **Wishful / forged** | Fruit-road handbills; fake VEL stamps; fake ribbons | Plot engines; seize with care |
| **Sealed** | House beta notes; Operator load excerpts | Hazard; consent and ethics apply |

### Kindness practice

Say your certainty grade when you hand someone a sheet.

### Hard edge

Forging a breakfast-hold notice; forging Quiet Well true paths for coin.
