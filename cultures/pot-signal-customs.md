# Pot Signal Customs

## Name

Pot Signal Customs

## Overview

Pot Signal Customs are the second-ring Veloria folk codes that turn flower pots into messages: debt, sanctuary, warning, and rendezvous without official paper.

## Beliefs and Values

Not everyone can enter the core. Everyone still needs a weather system. Windowsills are infrastructure.

## Practices and Traditions

- Bloom direction, color, and step-placement as grammar
- Children learn basic signals before market math
- Collectors try to criminalize; lanes reseed codes
- Favors settle faster than VEL when trust holds

## Lore

Feeds underbelly sagas that never require Operator floors or DEB Gate oaths.
