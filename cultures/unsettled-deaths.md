# Unsettled Deaths

## Overview

**Unsettled Deaths** are endings that refuse ordinary rites—Unraveled rumor, missing time, revision wounds—where grief has no clean body and love must invent a shape anyway.

## Beliefs and Values

Ordinary funerals still matter most. Unsettled cases must not become the default story of Dias. When they appear, communities need soft words and hard boundaries—not sport.

## Practices and Traditions

### Unsettled types

| Type | What fails | Holding practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Unraveled / F500 talk** | Body, name, or intention “comes apart” without ordinary death | Soft words only; no tavern anatomy; Mutual Table for the living left behind |
| **Missing time** | Hours/days gone; person returns wrong-aged or not at all | Window-talk honesty; no permanent verdict from one blank |
| **Revision wounds (F960-adjacent)** | Injury that looks briefly redrawn; death mid-undo | Leave seam visible; refuse Forced-Final as mercy theater |
| **Almost-same loss (F120)** | Mourning a death that “already happened” differently | One naming; refuse recurrence as receipt |
| **Bleed-shadow absence** | Wrong-color dawn takes a check-in | Shutter + song once; House labels slow |

### Kindness practice

Hold a chair and a meal even when there is no body. Do not demand Unraveled “proof” from grieving kin.

### Hard edge

Null Cells recruiting with “death isn’t real there”; Houses sealing missing-time witnesses as hazard files forever.
