# Open Hand and Ember Teaching

## Overview

**Open Hand and Ember Teaching** is how makers and warmth-keepers educate: revision without shame, relay before ceremony, kiln heat as craft and metaphor.

## Beliefs and Values

Teach the next hand. Warmth continuity is a lesson plan. Remaking is offered, not cosplayed onto the unwilling.

## Practices and Traditions

### Open Hand pedagogy

- Visible revision bands as textbooks.  
- Clean undo before sharper cuts.  
- Attribution drills; commons stewardship.  
- F432 Way transplants: soup-carrot travel classes, relocate-don’t-smash etiquette.

### Ember / hearth pedagogy

- Relay duty before ceremonial language.  
- Cold-gap honesty; spur stability truth.  
- Watch rotations as human tech curriculum.

### Secondfire kiln teaching

- Hanna Kiln–style fires: plates, tools, selves.  
- Slate names-in-progress.  
- Laughter Week as pressure release—not denial.  
- Right to refuse remaking taught beside the invitation.

### Kindness practice

Offer undo and second bowls; never force slate talk as payment for bread.

### Hard edge

Sealed “mastery” that hides harm; selling second beginning as paid costume; lying about cold gaps.
