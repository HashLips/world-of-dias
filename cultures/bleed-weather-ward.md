# Bleed Weather Ward

## Overview

**Bleed Weather Ward** is a pressure-niche faction: people who organize around Bleed-Sky and related stack bruises—containment, warning, and sometimes overreach.

## Beliefs and Values

Bleed nights are not only omen; they are logistics. Windows should shut; children should get soft words. Fear of being late can become hunger to control the bruise.

## Practices and Traditions

### Faction card

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Purpose** | Reduce harm during bleed weather and related events |
| **Method** | Warning nets; shutter drills; petitions to Houses; street watch rotations |
| **Public face** | Civic care; “we close the windows before the jokes start” |
| **Private fear** | Being too late; being used as panic theater; Null cells treating bruises as invitations |
| **Echoes** | Warning first; resist resource extraction of bleed events |

### Mixed morality

Good Wards teach soft pairs and Shared Table calm. Bad Wards hoard timing intel, inflate fear for funding, or drift toward containment cults (see Unsettled Faith Edges).

### Kindness practice

Warn once clearly; help shutters; refuse Null sport on bleed nights.

### Hard edge

Selling bruise timing; treating every odd moth as casus belli.
