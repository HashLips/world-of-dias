# F380 Hearttide Interview

## Name

F380 Hearttide Interview

## Overview

**F380 Hearttide Interview** is a measurement manners practice: before trusting a mood graph, ask a gathering how the place *felt*. Hearttide is treated as falsifiable climate—laughter, gifts, colleague-speed—not poetry alone and not a realm you board.

## Beliefs and Values

Communal affect can lean a place. Manners refuse treating lean as realm conversion, and refuse forcing hearttide with tools as if it were a ferry. Interviewers note laughter density, gift tempo, and whether strangers become “colleague” after one good question.

## Practices and Traditions

Softfruit carts are natural interview stations. Sera Quillmend’s notebook columns make answers comparable across mornings. Orrin used that cart talk as proxy data when the Spectrum Frame showed F380-like gradients without converting F432 into F380. The practice exists without the saga; the saga proves it can sit beside instruments in a publish packet.

## Lore

Tied to the F380 hearttide realm character. Does not invent a new band. Schools later chalk “felt / kept / joked” beside Frame traces so students see twin evidence types.
