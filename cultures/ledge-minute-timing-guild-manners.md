# Ledge Minute Timing Guild Manners

## Name

Ledge Minute Timing Guild Manners

## Overview

**Ledge Minute Timing Guild Manners** are the field etiquette of people who work inside reveal windows without owning them. Veil Scribes and Window Shelf Timers share the manners. The window is a timed courtesy of the Glassfold Ledge. Guild hands are guests of that courtesy, even when they hang the chime.

Precision is the culture. Greed for a longer minute is the embarrassment.

## Beliefs and Values

Intervals are tools. A tool you will not put down has become a theft, and the theft is from the soft close, the second note that lets the ledge put its coat back on. Structure may reveal itself. Souls do not owe confession to glass. A layout is not a conscience, and a helpful minute is not proof that the person beside you is pure.

Linger is ruder than ignorance. Ignorance can ask again. Linger spends the next window in advance and then blames the ledge for going opaque.

## Practices and Traditions

Chime discipline: one clean note to open, one softer note to close, and no ornamental third note for people who like rituals more than information. Dual overlays, base terrain and reveal-phase, packed before the close so the packing is part of the minute and not a panic after it. Ask-again rather than stare-again. Apprentices are walked down if they fall in love with a particular window. The myth of the minute that would not stay is told on the guild first, a schoolroom shame with a useful ending: the apprentice who kept looking walked down with a map more confident and less true than the one they climbed with.

Anti-stare manners sit beside the guild talk, not beneath it. The guild does not get a special license to linger because it owns slates.

## Lore

Visitors thank Timers for ending miracles on time, once they have seen a smeared copy. Clerks who try to stamp a revealed layout into a permanent claim find the ink going stupid. The consequence of breaking the manners is local and humiliating: headache, bad map, a shelf that spends its next clarity on other faces. No puzzle reward waits for the person who refuses to blink.

## Notes

Precision culture. Not a priesthood of hidden truth, not a lie detector with glass.
