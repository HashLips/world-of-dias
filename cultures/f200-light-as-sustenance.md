# F200 Light-as-Sustenance

## Overview

**F200 Light-as-Sustenance** describes how energy-kind “eat”: coherence, luminous current, and pattern preservation rather than carbon kitchens—yet F432 visitors still need metaphors and packed fruit.

## Beliefs and Values

A meal is a **held field**. Dissonance is a kind of hunger. Offering F432 bread without asking whether light-feeding is preferred can be kindness or confusion—ask.

## Practices and Traditions

### Staples (Aurel-kind)

- Resting in high-coherence meridian nodes.
- Lattice gathering as social feeding.
- Brightness modulation cycles that locals compare to breathing meals.

### Luxury

- Rare node alignments where many coronae share one calm fringe.
- Pattern-play with else-green like **Coherence Clover** (children count panes—not a Nauw salad).

### Crisis

- Dissonance hunger: wrong note in the shared field; appetite for silence and repair.
- Visitors from F432 who treat light-feeding as performance spirituality get gently corrected—or left in their packed rations.

### Cross-frequency etiquette

F432 guests should bring their own carbon food unless invited into a coherence rest. Do not assume Aurel-kind want banana rank explained at length.

### Kindness practice

Ask name and feeding need before frequency lecture.

### Hard edge

Do not harvest luminous “souvenirs” from meridian structure; that is not foraging, it is vandalism of person-adjacent space.
