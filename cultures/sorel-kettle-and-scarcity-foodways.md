# Sorel Kettle and Scarcity Foodways

## Overview

**Sorel Kettle and Scarcity Foodways** is salvage cooking: stew that makes a yard a people, kiln bread that remakes a day, and crisis pots that still refuse to become weapons.

## Beliefs and Values

Breakfast may be shared when claims cannot. A thin pot honestly named beats a rich pot bought with lied paint. Laughter Week feasts are defiance becoming home—not denial of hunger.

## Practices and Traditions

### Staples

- **Hearthvale stew-onion** pots; scrap broth; kiln bread; salvage greens.
- Driftfall shore fish and rope-crab when beach-calm allows.
- Flagweek breakfast tables that outlast allegiance flips.
- Ember-cat hearth warmth as “cooking weather” in Hearthvale dusk.

### Luxury

- Hearth Laughter Week feast plates.
- Good Trade still-hour shared travel food (quiet, good).
- Rare Nauw fruit gifts that arrive without Concord sermons attached.

### Crisis / pressure

- Vent-haze weeks: thin pots, grit in everything, appetite argument.
- Exile-road strips: dried whatever, shame/defiance braid in the mouth.
- Double-paint nights: kettle peace enforced harder—or broken into hunger fights.
- Refusal of second beginning: some exiles will not eat remaking metaphors with their bread; feed them anyway without forcing slate talk.

### Kettle grammar (food = speech)

Wire-bowl / news-bowl / tarp-edge standing. Stew is contribution literacy.

### Kindness practice

One bowl before paint lecture; feed refusal without mocking it at Laughter Week.

### Hard edge

Kettle thieves earn exile-within; using breakfast as allegiance weapon on Flagweek Strip violates breakfast-holds.

## Lore

Sorel’s loveable world taste is smoke, onion, and a joke that survives scarcity.
