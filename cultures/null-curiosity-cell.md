# Null Curiosity Cell

## Overview

**Null Curiosity Cell** names small, non-dominant groups that chase absence—Horizon/Nullmouth talk as sport or “research.” Present as pressure, not street majority.

## Beliefs and Values

They claim bravery or scholarship. Streets claim they thin rooms. Houses claim hazard. All three can be true in one night.

## Practices and Traditions

### Authoring rule

One cell, one bad tool, one thinned tavern—not a continent converted. Majority soft customs still rule Softfruit Hall.

### Kindness practice (opposition)

Soft words; leave before third naming; support Ward warnings without panic theater.

### Hard edge

Social freeze; taboo tech seizure; Keep/House hazard language when earned.
