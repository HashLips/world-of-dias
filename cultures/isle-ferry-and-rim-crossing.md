# Isle Ferry and Rim Crossing

## Overview

**Isle Ferry and Rim Crossing** covers Calareth’s infrequent intentional ferries and Outer Rim movement by loft, raft-ring, barge, and whale-path season.

## Beliefs and Values

Calareth traffic is **intentional**, not casual tourism. Rim water movement is coordination song before it is sightseeing. Missing a hail is a logistics failure and a moral stain.

## Practices and Traditions

### Calareth / Hallow Bay

- Ferries infrequent; windows posted on Watch slates.
- Arrival: porch until ribbon; then Bay-Ribbon Guest Hall beds.
- Dories moss-green; Rope Tree as contract architecture.
- Tide-Loud Weeks and Still Months change safe passage windows.

### Outer Rim water movement

| Mode | Feel | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raft-rings (Rim-Rest) | Seasonal sleep on water | Storm check-in as serious as loft |
| Cove tiers (Hollowmere) | Hammocks over inns | First Line / Storm Choir answer |
| Fruit barges | Festival + inspection | Inland guests judged like line-thieves risk |
| Whale-path seasons | Ethic argument weather | Compact talk after eye-back |

### Sample journey beat

Hollowmere loft hail → barge to Basin → (rare) ferry slate to Hallow Bay → ribbon → guest hall quiet week.

### Kindness practice

Teach check-in before giving a new hand a tiller; walk ribbon rules without apology.

### Hard edge

Refuse habitual answer-empty crews; revoke Calareth belonging for ribbon-break movers of stone/story.
