# F432 Shared Table

## Overview

**F432 Shared Table** names the ordinary civic habit of eating, bargaining, and arguing across race lines without treating difference as spectacle. It is culture practice, not a signed treaty.

## Beliefs and Values

The good table assumes: every compact race is a neighbor first. Prejudice exists—especially where speech modes, body shapes, or swarm law confuse clerks—but friendship and trade outnumber horror stories in lived F432.

Wabet adds silence etiquette. Nauw adds loud negotiation. Sorel adds salvage fairness: who fixed the road eats first when the kettle’s thin.

## Practices and Traditions

### Who cooks

- **Humans** and **Kharad** dominate many street kitchens and kiln-bakeries; grit and heat favor dense hands.
- **Choirglass** host tone-meals where food is paced to soft harmonics—guests learn to chew between pulses.
- **Palewind** rarely “cook” alone; they season by drifting spice-motes through shared steam, then settle to watch others eat.
- **Lineborn** prefer flatbreads, leaf wraps, and foods that read well in profile on a sketch-mat.
- **Axiomorph** hosts may arrange plates by geometry; refusing the pattern is rude, mocking it is worse.
- **Veyrin** kitchens often showcase one cook’s signature trait (perfect salt-memory, knife-balance, heat-sense)—useful, not magical catering.

### Who sails

- Outer Rim: **Humans**, **Choirglass** song-crews, and **Palewind** veil-scouts share decks; compact talk after whale sightings can split a crew for a night and mend by morning.
- River and bay: mixed oars are normal; **Axiomorph** navigators are prized for route math, mistrusted when they won’t small-talk.

### Who keeps silence

- Lumira and Quiet Well: Silence Law binds all kinds equally—**Kharad** footfalls and **Palewind** whisper-drift are both taught as soft craft.
- Canopy trails: soft voices for Soft-Bears and for neighbors; shouting is a character judgment.

### Markets and law

- Census tags from the [Major Races Compact](f432-major-races-compact.md) ease stalls and contracts.
- Translation desks in Veloria hire **Choirglass** and bilingual **Humans** side by side.
- Prejudice flares when clerks treat Palewind partitions as “many people” for tax, or Lineborn as “not fully there” for witness seats. Good houses correct both.

### Friendship that sticks

- Shared fruit on Wabet trails.
- Bell-trains on Nauw redmiles that refuse to leave a rear cart—any race—alone.
- Sorel repair circles where the one who brings wire eats, whatever their outline.

## Lore

Visitors from F200 or F960 are welcome at many tables and exoticized at others. Shared Table culture says: ask their name before asking their frequency.
