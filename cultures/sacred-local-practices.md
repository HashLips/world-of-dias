# Sacred Local Practices

## Overview

**Sacred Local Practices** are the lived holy ordinary—ribbons, silence, fruit thanks, hearth continuity, gate touches, loft answers—more chapel than cathedral.

## Beliefs and Values

Sacred is what a community will not treat as joke when pressed. Practices comfort belonging and frighten those who break them.

### Atheism-of-echoes coexistence

Many practice fruit thanks or ribbon law while denying echoes are “spirits.” The practice still binds. Mystics and skeptics can share Softfruit Hall.

### Kindness practice

Teach the comfort meaning before the punishment meaning.

### Hard edge

Weaponizing sacred practice to exile without hearing; performing rites as tourism.
