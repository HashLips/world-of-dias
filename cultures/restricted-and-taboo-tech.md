# Restricted and Taboo Tech

## Overview

**Restricted and Taboo Tech** draws the line between tools you need permission to touch and tools you should not build—or should not admit you built.

## Beliefs and Values

Restriction can be care (keep load legible; protect wells; seal icebound flame). Restriction can also be power hoarding. Taboo exists because some devices wound rooms, memories, or waters faster than laws can catch up.

## Practices and Traditions

### Restricted (clearance / Keep / House)

| Class | Why restricted | Exemplars / feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Core instruments** | Realm power stability | Operator floors; multi-realm converters |
| **Sealed pattern cells** | What they keep is the danger | Icebound Flame Cell pattern |
| **Harmonic filters / classifiers** | Misfile → exile or panic | House codes; ring clearance tools |
| **Accurate water instruments** | Publishing wells as violence | Banned or Keep-bound in Lumira |
| **Cross-band survey kits** | Partial knowledge becomes policy | Ledger fragments misuse |

### Taboo (social freeze or worse)

| Class | Why taboo | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Null-adjacent listeners** | Thin rooms; erasure curiosity | Sport naming + devices = double sin |
| **Unconsented memory seals** | Steal a person’s echo story | House and street both hate when caught |
| **Forced-final tools** | Erase revision / refuse clean undo | Open Hand hard opposition |
| **Belonging-break gadgets** | Cut ribbons / forge assent | Calareth revokes hard |
| **Well-publishing beacons** | Advertise water | Silence Law enemies |

### Soft institutional language

Houses say **stabilized**, **nonactionable**, **sealed**. Streets hear **hidden**, **ignored**, **locked**. Both can be right; neither is automatically virtuous.

## Kindness practice

Explain *why* a tool is restricted when refusing a hand—especially to exiles and apprentices.

## Hard edge

Punish taboo craft used as sport; refuse restricted clearance as fashion accessory.
