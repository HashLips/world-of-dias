# F432 Civic Time

## Overview

**F432 Civic Time** is how people in the home band say *when* without pretending Dias shares one universal clock. It is market rhythm, fruit weather, gate duty, festival cloth, and soft era markers—not a Fracture-dated planetary calendar.

Deep antiquity stays perforated on purpose. For eras and saga anchors, see [`Known Eras and Markers`](../world/known-eras-and-markers.md).

## Beliefs and Values

- **Lived time beats cosmic time** for daily life. A child learns Flagweek before they learn ledger-wave gossip.
- **Seasons are social.** Fruit arrival, butterfly migration, laughter week, and echo-rise talk rearrange work as much as rain does.
- **Clocks serve tables and gates**, not the reverse. A meal delayed for gilding hour is not laziness in the Basin; a gate closed for dignity is not mere bureaucracy at Deb Gate.
- **Other frequencies have time-feels, not full F432 calendars.** Borrowing their metaphors is poetry; governing by them is hubris.

## Practices and Traditions

### Ordinary civic rhythm (shared seed)

| Beat | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| **Market stretch** | The busy middle of a trading day; stalls open, VEL counted aloud, fruit ranked before argument |
| **Gate turn** | Shift change at bridges and city rings; Watch-Geese and hounds reset; travelers bunch |
| **Gilding lean** | Late-day pause in the Velorian Basin when light turns kind—work softens without a formal holiday |
| **Lamp row** | Evening market and ledger hours; Nightledger Owls and night-bats become the timekeepers |
| **Still mile** | On certain roads (Brindle March especially), carts hold or creep when hush deepens—time measured in shared breath |

People still count days and weeks. They just decorate the count with **named seasons** more than numbered months.

### Shared festival calendar seed (F432-wide recognition)

These are widely *recognized* across Nauw, Wabet, and Sorel even when not identically kept:

| Season / festival | Tone | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First Fruit Arrival** | Joy, gratitude, rivalry | When Wabet shipments thicken Nauw markets; Fruit Reverence peaks |
| **Wingroad / Butterfly Window** | Awe + caution | Mega Butterfly migration Averra → Glasswater; open routes warned |
| **Ring-Fair Lights** | Civic spectacle | Veloria fairs; Banner Moths; Festival Sky-Whales as constructed wonder |
| **Flagweek** | Chaotic celebration (Sorel-famous, Nauw-known) | Flags change; breakfast must not become a weapon ([`Flagweek breakfast rule`](../rules/flagweek-breakfast-rule.md)) |
| **Hearth Laughter Week** | Soft dignity, play | Hearthvale claim that a second life needs room to shed weight aloud |
| **Echo-rise talk season** | Unease under ordinary work | Not a holiday—an observational season when harmonic reports thicken |
| **Bleed-sky nights** | Quiet fear, closed windows | Rare; moths of wrong color; not scheduled, only named after |

Exact start days drift by weather, harvest, and local vote. That drift is considered healthy.

### Regional variants

**Nauw — Orchard-and-Ring time**

- Anchors: fruit markets, Basin gilding hour, Deb Gate turns, ring-fair calendars.
- “When” phrases: *after first banana glut*, *before wingroad*, *in echo-rise week*, *at gilding*.
- Pressure: civic clocks want regularity; fruit and migration refuse it.

**Wabet — Quiet-and-Song time**

- Anchors: Silence Law travel windows in Lumira (move when the land allows), canopy path seasons, Songripe **song-calendars** that replace harvest calendars on the isles.
- “When” phrases: *after the dunes accept steps*, *in tide-song measure*, *when the Rope Tree ribbons renew*.
- Pressure: mainland traders call song-time inconvenient; islanders call clock-time rude.

**Sorel — Second-Beginning time**

- Anchors: Flagweek cloth changes, Hearth Laughter Week, rebuild “roof-raising” days, exile arrival seasons at the Long Gate.
- “When” phrases: *since my second beginning*, *between flags*, *after the yard stopped arguing*.
- Pressure: freedom from northern calendars is a point of pride; shared F432 festival names still sneak in through trade and kin.

### Frequency-local time feels (not full calendars)

| Band | Time feel | F432 borrowing |

| **F120** | Near-return; Quartermark Drift offsets | “Same appointment, different ending” stories |
| **F610** | Reveal windows; phase stamps | Investigators speak in *windows*, not hours |
| **F840** | Recovery seasons; hearth continuity | “After the warmth held” as a life chapter mark |
| **F200 / F500** | Rarely scheduled; noticed as bleed | Wrong-sky nights, not feast days |
| **F380** | Mood-weather duration | “While the estuary was kind / narrow” |
| **F960** | Revision weather; edges that move | Makers say *this draft of the week* |

Do not invent parallel twelve-month systems for each band unless a future section truly needs them. Feel first; bureaucracy later.

## Lore

Civic time is one reason F432 feels loveable: the year keeps offering **tables, songs, and shared pauses**. It is also why the unsettled layer stings—echo-rise and bleed-sky arrive on top of ordinary gladness without asking permission.

Institutions may stamp ledgers with tighter counts. Streets still answer *when* with festivals and fruit.
