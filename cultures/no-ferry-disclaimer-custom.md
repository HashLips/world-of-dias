# No-Ferry Disclaimer Custom

## Name

No-Ferry Disclaimer Custom

## Overview

**No-Ferry Disclaimer Custom** stamps spectrum papers with struck-through boat marks and plain language: **understanding is not a ticket**. There is no safe subway between frequencies.

## Beliefs and Values

Joke piers become religions when loneliness pays. Soft limits must be taught in public ink, not only whispered on the Row. Ell Ferrylie’s comedy tickets are cited as ethics warnings, not as enemy propaganda.

## Practices and Traditions

Issa’s packet margins. School chalk beside F200/F500 diagrams. Apron patches on the Row when apprentices argue for “one widen.” Same custom spoken by Orrin years earlier to Frederick Lumens without the stamp yet invented.
