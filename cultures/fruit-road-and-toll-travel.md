# Fruit Road and Toll Travel

## Overview

**Fruit Road and Toll Travel** is the spine logistics of Wabet→Nauw produce and the March discipline that meters carts into Basin markets—plus the Westfold fee-door between Hearthvale and Wabet calm.

## Beliefs and Values

Bruising schedules beat clock pride. Tolls make law visible. A perfect fruit is not an excuse to break welcome. Redmiles after dusk are not romantic.

## Practices and Traditions

### Eastbound Fruit Road

- Braided waystations, wagon rights, sharing-knife customs.
- Stewards time rest to heat and bruise (Tomas Share type).
- Junction arguments: Lucky Fruit as blessing, commodity, or omen.
- Reciprocity: take sustains convoy; leave continues waystations.

### Span Toll (Brindle March)

- Coin, labor, or documented rest—never a shrug.
- Chalk tallies, shade stalls, marshal patience (*Bull on the Hill* jokes).
- False VEL stamps and refused reciprocal rest are saga fuel.

### Westfold Toll (Hearthvale↔Wabet)

- Supervised “proper” road into calm country.
- Marks, papers, patience; Keepers watch raid energy; Hearthvale watches short coin.
- Failure to pay births softer stories—and quieter illegal water paths.

### Redmile / scrub approaches

- After dusk Strider reports: **bell-dense** spacing ([The Bells Against Redmile](../myths/the-bells-against-redmile.md)).
- Softlane Hare ahead = kindness omen, not immunity.

### Sample journey beat

Dawn load at Verdant edge → waystation soup-carrot → Span Toll chalk argument → Basin mist and Softfruit ladle → optional barge toward Hollowmere.

## Kindness practice

Document rest for those short on VEL but rich in labor; teach bruise-timing to new hands.

## Hard edge

Freeze welcome for take-without-restore convoys; marshal delay for stamp fraud.
