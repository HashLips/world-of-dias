# Nauw Fruit-Table Foodways

## Overview

**Nauw Fruit-Table Foodways** is how the crowded heart eats: fruit above other classes, banana supreme, tables that argue rank and still feed strangers.

## Beliefs and Values

You may eat meat, grain, and fish daily and still treat fruit as the moral apex of the meal. Wasting fruit is worse manners than wasting stew. Lucky Fruit is wonder under knife etiquette—not a casino chip.

## Practices and Traditions

### Staples

- **Market Banana** and other Wabet→Nauw fruit as daily apex (imported dependence openly joked about as **Wabet-sweet**).
- Basin grains, rivercress, pocket-parsley, mist-herb teas.
- Glasswater fish and field produce; Outer Rim salt-catch when barges run.
- Yard eggs, milk-goat cheese, grin-pig feast days (animal food respected, fruit still ranked higher).

### Luxury

- **Lucky Fruit** shares with sharing-knife rite.
- **Sun-Sweet Sample-Melon** cut larger on Sun-Sweet Afternoon.
- Ring-fair sweets dyed with marigold; Softfruit evening bowls with mixed citrus.

### Crisis / pressure

- **Green-crate** weeks when shipments lag—people eat “full” without feeling ranked.
- Redmile **bell-dense** travel food: dense bread, dried fruit strips, less fresh banana theater.
- Exile roads south: fruit gifts become farewell weight.

### Meal shapes

| Meal | Feel |
| --- | --- |
| Softfruit ladle lunch | Shared Table peace; pay fruit or fee |
| Ring-fair night | Color, moth-dust, banana-boast risks |
| Basin gilding-hour tea | Mist-herb cups; pause as food |
| Gate-side quick eat | Gate-small appetite; compliance before pleasure |

### Kindness practice

Offer fruit share before haggling tone hardens.

### Hard edge

Freeze out Lucky-touch thieves and chronic rank-blind mockery; shame waste at public tables.

## Lore

Nauw’s food drama is never only calories—it is belonging ranked in peels and stems.
