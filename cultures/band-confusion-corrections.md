# Band Confusion Corrections

## Name

Band Confusion Corrections

## Overview

**Band Confusion Corrections** are the social scripts used when someone mixes up established frequencies—or paints one as a harbor district.

## Beliefs and Values

Correct the difference, keep the wonder. Do not solve Zero to win. Do not invent a ferry to sound brave.

Overlaps Wrong Nest Corrections (scale) but aims at **band identity**. Chapbooks and Softfruit tags help; Houses stamp Theory when someone sells a closed model of a quiet band.
