# Fruit Road Reciprocity

## Name

Fruit Road Reciprocity

## Overview

Fruit Road Reciprocity is the traveling culture of Wabet-to-Nauw produce routes: take what sustains the convoy, leave what continues the waystations, and never let a perfect fruit become an excuse to break welcome.

## Beliefs and Values

Abundance without restoration is raid with better branding. Sharing-knives are etiquette and audit.

## Practices and Traditions

- Public cutting tables at Open Hand Way
- Labor or seed-return logged against crate tallies
- Disputes heard before fists
- Perfect-form Lucky Fruit handled as omen and commodity both—never as free theft

## Lore

Anchors Tomas Share’s teaching and distinguishes Wabet Open Hand from F960 Open Hand of Making.
