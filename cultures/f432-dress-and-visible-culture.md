# F432 Dress and Visible Culture

## Overview

**F432 Dress and Visible Culture** is how people look on purpose—labor weather, region manners, and kind-body tells—so scenes and plates stay local.

## Beliefs and Values

Clothes signal care and work, not costume cosplay. Showing a mark you have not earned is insult. Hiding a needed mark (ribbon, claim paint honesty, revision band) is also insult.

## Practices and Traditions

### Kindness practice

Dress for the neighbor’s comfort in Shared Table rooms—pulse wait, soft-step, kettle peace.

### Hard edge

Costume appropriation of Watch ribbons, Keep hush marks, or Compact paint as party wear.
