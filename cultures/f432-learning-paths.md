# F432 Learning Paths

## Overview

**F432 Learning Paths** maps what children learn first versus what initiates learn later—by region and vocation—so growth feels local, not generic fantasy school.

## Beliefs and Values

First lessons should keep you alive and kind. Later lessons may keep ledgers honest or cores stable. Skipping soft words to chase sealed study is a classic way to become dangerous or merely ridiculous.

### Hearsay school

Gangplank gossip, Softfruit lunch clusters, Salt Ledger witness, Flagweek three-opinion weather—all teach faster than classrooms and lie more often. Good mentors translate hearsay into checkable practice.
