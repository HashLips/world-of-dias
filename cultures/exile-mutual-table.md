# Exile Mutual Table

## Overview

**Exile Mutual Table** is Sorel mutual-aid organizing that tries to feed and shelter across crew lines—kettle peace scaled into a network without becoming a new Concord.

## Beliefs and Values

One bowl before paint. Second beginnings without costume. Aid that captures debts into crew ownership is just racket.

## Practices and Traditions

### Faction card

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Purpose** | Keep new exiles alive long enough to choose belonging |
| **Method** | Shared tables; route tips; Secondfire referrals; anti-fraud watch on “aid” rackets |
| **Public face** | Breakfast that holds; news-bowls; quiet slate help |
| **Private fear** | Capture by Free-Captains; Concord co-option; burnout in haze weeks |
| **Echoes** | Witness-and-share of road trauma; refuse filing that re-exiles |

### Relation to Freebands

Overlaps membership; argues with yards that would monetize desperation. Carrow-era Compact politics may bless or side-eye depending on season.

### Kindness practice

Anonymous first bowls; no forced slate talk for bread.

### Hard edge

Expose fake aid that double-paints newcomers.
