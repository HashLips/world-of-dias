# Sorel Salvage Architecture

## Overview

**Sorel Salvage Architecture** builds with whatever survived the last move—hull scars, kiln brick, tarp, bottle-glass—and treats permanence as a rumor you earn slowly.

## Beliefs and Values

A Sorel building should **admit its history**. Fresh paint that hides salvage is suspicious; many coats that leave the name as texture are honest ([Salt Ledger](../regions/the-salt-ledger.md)). Breakfast can be shared under one roof-rule; claims cannot. Freedom shows in how fast a yard can reconfigure without asking Veloria.

## Practices and Traditions

### Driftfall yards and shore

- Tarps over scarred hull ribs; cookfire stone rings; painted claim marks on uprights.
- Beach hang-about architecture: low benches, shade cloth, no urgency in the framing.
- Vent-facing lean-tos inland—built to be abandoned when sprout windows turn ugly.
- [Claimscar Cookfire Circle](../regions/claimscar-cookfire-circle.md) as civic hearth: kettle peace, paint-mark war.

### Hearthvale / Secondfire rebuild

- Kiln courtyards with slate boards of names-in-progress.
- Brick that remembers ash; warm workshops that smell of clay and laughter-week storage.
- Teaching fires as sacred-domestic hybrid: craft and remaking metaphor share one chimney.

### Strip and toll edges

- Flagweek Strip: temporary poles, changeable banners, breakfast tables that outlast allegiance.
- Toll posts and blackbridge watch sheds—functional, ugly, reliable.

### Good Trade height

- Sitting rock and still-hour clearings as architecture without walls: the mountain’s “building” is the pause.

### Sacred / domestic / defensive

- **Sacred:** kiln second-beginning fires; Good Trade still hours; vent omen watches.
- **Domestic:** tarp lofts, cookfires, kiln-courtyard beds.
- **Defensive:** claim paint timing, crew marks, vent watches—not city walls.

## Lore

Sorel’s skyline is smoke and flags before towers. If you see a perfect new facade with no salvage story, locals assume either Nauw money or a lie.
