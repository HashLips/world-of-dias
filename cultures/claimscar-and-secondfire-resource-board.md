# Claimscar and Secondfire Resource Board

## Overview

**Claimscar and Secondfire Resource Board** lists what a hard week in Sorel actually runs on—kettle fuel and remaking supplies, not Concord catalogs.

## Beliefs and Values

Name resources honestly. “We have onion and wire” is better leadership than “we have destiny.”

## Practices and Traditions

### Claimscar Yard (frontier)

| Resource | How it moves | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stew / kettle heat | Cookfire Circle | Wire, wood, or news buys bowl |
| Claim paint / marks | Crew stores | Vent-Claim Rule timing |
| Salvage hull / tarp | Yard stacks | Building = dinner roof |
| Scrap greens / shore fish | Forage + trade | Beach-calm vs inland grit |
| Dogs / watch labor | Crew bond | Professional patience at kettle |
| Vent-window intel | Gossip + scars | Worth more than coin some nights |
| Nauw fruit (rare) | Gifts / theft / trade | Politics attached |

### Secondfire (rebuild town)

| Resource | How it moves | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kiln heat / clay / slate | Teaching fires | Remaking tools and selves |
| Stew-onion / kiln bread | Courtyard kitchens | Staple of second beginning |
| Names-in-progress boards | Public craft | Food + identity adjacent |
| Supply routes (Alwen-type) | Quiet inspections | Continuity without sermons |
| Laughter Week stores | Seasonal banners / feast gear | Joy as infrastructure |
| Exile refusal space | Social, not menu | Feed without forcing remaking talk |

### Shared scarcity rules

- Thin pot: say so.
- Double-paint nights: kettle peace first or nobody eats well.
- Do not spend Laughter Week feast as mockery of refusal stories.

## Kindness practice

Publish a morning board: what’s in the pot, what’s needed, what’s not for sale.

## Hard edge

Hoarding wire during kettle shortage; selling “second beginning” as paid costume.
