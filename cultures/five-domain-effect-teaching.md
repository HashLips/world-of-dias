# Five-Domain Effect Teaching

## Name

Five-Domain Effect Teaching

## Overview

**Five-Domain Effect Teaching** is the Softfruit-and-junior custom for explaining how a frequency shapes five lived faces—matter, life, memory, perception, and environments—without turning the lesson into a ferry brochure.

## Beliefs and Values

One law, five faces. Difference must be felt in kitchens and gates before it is stamped Theory. Understanding a band’s effects is not a ticket there. Frequency Zero stays unfinished even when students beg for a sixth empty bowl.

## Practices and Traditions

Hosts set five bowls or five chapbook spreads and refuse to let a student mash them into “vibes.”

| Domain | First Softfruit tell | Correction if misfiled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Matter | Kneaded Dough Memory; harbor stone that keeps a job | Not “magic dirt paint” |
| Life | Ordinary resonance load in the shoulders of a busy hall | Not “upgrade weekend in lumen” |
| Memory | Table-warmth recall; scrape not erase | Not total-recall devices |
| Perception | Mixed-weather sense; glance manners | Not permanent stare |
| Environments | Civic mixed climate; bleed as guest weather | Not “bruise = F500 district” |

Teaching order follows Frequency Effects of Dias: home band first, bright/null poles next, quieter ledgers last. Band Confusion Corrections runs beside this custom when someone names a face wrong.

## Lore

Stack Teaching Customs covers nest bowls (where things sit). Five-Domain Effect Teaching covers what a nest *does* to substance, bodies, recollection, sense, and place-weather. Juniors who pass both can host a table without inventing subways. Houses may Theory-stamp models afterward; Softfruit keeps the first hour sensory and kind.
