# Fruit-Road Ethics

## Name

Fruit-Road Ethics

## Overview

**Fruit-Road Ethics** is the plain-speech name convoy hands and market theaters use for right conduct on the Eastbound Fruit Road: share, restore, refuse perfect-fruit excuses, and treat wishful handbills as comedy or crime—not as law.

## Beliefs and Values

Abundance without restoration is a raid with better branding. A forged “no toll this week” notice does not become true because oranges are sweet. Reciprocity is the spine; ethics is the street word for it.

## Practices and Traditions

- Sharing-knife etiquette at waystations.
- Calling out wishful handbills before carts rearrange around a lie.
- Yard skits and market theater that cool disputes before chalk hardens.
- Teaching newcomers: Fruit Road Reciprocity is the longer name; Fruit-Road Ethics is what you shout when someone pockets a waystation pot.
