# Entertainment of F432

## Overview

**Entertainment of F432** is how rings keep evenings—chaos that still holds breakfast, galleries that move with you, markets that stage truth-as-joke, song that meters weather, cricket silence that means a house is thinking.

## Beliefs and Values

Entertainment is not escape from Dias pressure; it is practice for living with it. Comic dread, soft applause, and shared silence all count.
