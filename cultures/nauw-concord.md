# Nauw Concord

## Overview

**Nauw Concord** is Nauw’s structured civic authority—High Steward seat, trade continuity, and the moral weight of who gets sent south.

## Beliefs and Values

Order lets difference share tables. Order also creates exile roads. Concord claims necessary fiction of fairness; critics hear fruit-rank comfort deciding belonging.
