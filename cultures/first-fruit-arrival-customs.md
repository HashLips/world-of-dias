# First Fruit Arrival Customs

## Overview

**First Fruit Arrival Customs** name the Nauw market joy-weather when Wabet crates thicken—rivalry and gratitude sharing one table.

## Beliefs and Values

Abundance is a neighbor act. The first glut tests manners harder than scarcity: waste and banana-boast become public character.

## Practices and Traditions

| Beat | Custom |
| --- | --- |
| **Crate open** | Rank spoken before price; green crates stay quiet |
| **Share** | First banana glut offers go to children, Softfruit ladles, and tired road crews |
| **Rivalry** | Stall jokes allowed; mockery of fruit rank is not |
| **Lucky Fruit hush** | Perfect-form finds are whispered, not auction-shouted as proof |
| **Close** | Leftover rank shared or dried—dumping glut is hard edge shame |

## Lore

Tied to Fruit Reverence and Civic Time’s orchard-and-ring calendar. Road myth cousins: Big Times Walked the Fruit Road.

## Kindness practice

Offer glut share before coin gloat.

## Hard edge

Treat glut as license to waste → market freeze-out until a public re-share.
