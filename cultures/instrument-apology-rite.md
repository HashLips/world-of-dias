# Instrument Apology Rite

## Name

Instrument Apology Rite

## Overview

**Instrument Apology Rite** is speaking aloud to a broken tool before scrap—often in oil-smell dark—so craft does not turn tools into enemies mid-argument. It is manners, not a containment spell.

## Beliefs and Values

A cracked pane remembered kindly is safer than a pane cursed and jammed into the next build. Apology does not guarantee the vault will hold; the Frame night proves that. The midnight oil myth grows around the rite because Ren’s lamps and Orrin’s mutters share hours.

## Practices and Traditions

Orrin uses it on cracked glass while building the Spectrum Frame and again before the vault attempt. Noro pretends not to believe and still waits for the sentence to finish. Tam learns the words and later jokes that the latch did not accept the apology.
