# Unmaking Pattern Rumors

## Overview

**Unmaking Pattern Rumors** seed the idea that something—habit, force, or appetite—keeps trying to unmake or unbind: not a completed war chronicle, a recurring bruise in memory.

## Beliefs and Values

Patterns comfort and frighten. Treating rumor as confirmed general staff plans breaks tone. Treating all unmaking talk as nonsense leaves Null sport unopposed.

### Authoring rule

One rumor per arc as weather—not a named final boss of history unless later canon elevates it.

## Kindness practice

Pair unmaking talk with a care practice (shutters, soft-step, kettle, ribbon).

## Hard edge

Recruiting from unmaking fear into Null Curiosity Cells.
