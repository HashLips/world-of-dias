# Scrape Not Erase Custom

## Name

Scrape Not Erase Custom

## Overview

**Scrape Not Erase Custom** is the record-keeping ethic that treats prior marks as echoes: you may correct them by scrape and marginal note, but pretending the page never held them is a lie the page will eventually sour over.

## Beliefs and Values

Erase seeks convenience. Scrape seeks continuity. Dias holds remainder; paperwork that denies remainder becomes dangerous fiction.

## Practices and Traditions

Copy rooms teach juniors to scrape ink, initial the correction, and stamp Partial when the truth is incomplete. Softfruit borrows the metaphor for apologies. Operators scrape load notes when a tick was misread rather than inventing a clean log.

## Lore

Grew beside House sealing culture and Softfruit honesty. People who sell “total wipe” services are treated like wrong-map sellers: sometimes useful as cautionary theater, never as civic standard.

## Notes

Phenomenon cousin: Sealed Ledger Cold Spot—when sealed memory makes a desk feel colder than the room.
