# F840 Cold Gap Honesty

## Name

F840 Cold Gap Honesty

## Overview

**F840 Cold Gap Honesty** admits recovery quiet—ember quiet after argument or heat—instead of clamping every unfinished state into Forced-Final “done.”

## Beliefs and Values

Recovery is not erasure of error history. Ember quiet after a fight (or a Frame night) is data, not failure. Honesty includes naming the gap where becoming rests.

## Practices and Traditions

Used when Orrin’s graphs showed ember-quiet signatures after bitter tea-cylinder arguments and after cyan drift nights. Ethical cousin to Open Hand clean undo: you may reverse a bad clamp; you may not pretend the scar never happened. Scraped corrections are the paper version of the same manners.

## Lore

Tied to F840’s ember/recovery character in background-band talk. Partial publish sections label recovery claims as theory when instruments only half-show them.
