# F432 Taboo and Soft Words

## Overview

**F432 Taboo and Soft Words** pairs what mouths avoid with what they say instead—social safety, water ethics, and abyss caution.

## Beliefs and Values

Some words wound people; some wound rooms; some wound wells. Soft words are not cowardice when they keep children sleeping and cisterns unadvertised. Taboo without soft substitute becomes cruelty by silence.

## Practices and Traditions

### Soft words that are still strong

- **Assent** (Calareth) — permission with belonging weight.
- **Neighbor** — Shared Table peace offer.
- **Scaffold-honest** — admit unfinished without shame.
- **Kettle peace** — temporary sacred truce.

### Soft words that can be lies

- “Nonactionable” (load discipline) used to dismiss fear.
- “Stabilized” used to seal a witness who needed hearing.
- “Just window talk” used to duck accountability after the window closes.

## Kindness practice

Teach children soft pairs alongside taboos—never only “don’t say that.”

## Hard edge

Social freeze for people who turn taboo-breaking into sport (null-naming games, well-publishing dares, exile-story comedy).

## Notes

Clerks and streets will keep arguing which soft words are honest. That argument is canon.
