# Letter-Keepers of Sallow Strand

## Name

Letter-Keepers of Sallow Strand. The cookhouse called them "the letter-keepers," half as a joke, and the name stuck.

## Overview

The Letter-Keepers of Sallow Strand are a small, informal following that grew around the Letter House and the Kestry Letter in the saga year. They are witnesses, navigators, grieving families and a few converted seekers who help Odile Brask keep the sea's letters and sit with the grieving. After the expedition they keep Maren Kestry's rule. They are not a cult, an order, or a search party. They refuse offerings, worship and maps.

## Beliefs and Values

- **A sea-letter belongs to the person it is written to.** Until then it belongs to the strand.
- **The person it's for comes to it.** Word goes inland, never the letter.
- **It's a letter, not a saint.** Sit with it; don't pray to it.
- **Write what you measured, write what you guessed** (Ansel's rule, taken up by Maren).
- **Every warning is a map to someone** (Odile).
- **Looking for him, not for it** (Maren). No position, bearing or *when* leaves the house.
- **The Keep's courtesy for the Aumeri:** no landing, no measuring, no mark.

## Practices and Traditions

- Sweeping the Letter House, tending the drawers, making tea for the grieving.
- Hour-long sittings with the fair record under glass. The original is never handled by sitters.
- No copying of the margins and no carrying them out.
- Offerings (candles, ribbons, shell crescents) are returned to the people who left them, with tea.
- Arguments over fish stew in the cookhouse, voices kept down.
- Pooling marks for a member's need, as for Maren's race east.
- Knotted-cord counting learned from the Songripe coasters.

## Lore

In Brightpass there were eleven daily keepers. They split in Hushlean. Four seekers left east with the myth of the shore nobody charts. Nine stayed under Maren's rule, the young man from the Basin among them, first at the broom the next morning. Only those nine know why Low Bell Cove matters.

## Notes

Members other than Odile, Maren and Wick stay unnamed: the fisher from Sorel's south shore, two Songripe coasters, a widow of the Outer Rim, the young man from the Basin, and two others.
