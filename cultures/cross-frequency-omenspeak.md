# Cross-Frequency Omenspeak

## Overview

**Cross-Frequency Omenspeak** is rare communication across bands—omen-tech and myth more than telegram. Not a subway of messages.

## Beliefs and Values

Treating omenspeak as reliable mail breaks tone. Ignoring all after-echo talk leaves Bleed-Sky unread. Partial knowledge is the honest middle.

## Practices and Traditions

### How “messages” arrive (disputed)

| Mode | Feel | Reliability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bleed bruise / wrong-color dawn | After-echo of elsewhere quarrel | Low; high fear |
| Window talk / Glassfold patience | Brief true sight | Medium briefly; then gone |
| Dream Passage notes | Delci-scale inward mail | Personal; not civic |
| Ledger fragment waves | Archival gossip | Sticky; provenance thin |
| Distant Companies rumor | Spectrum war weather | Rumor-grade |
| Operator multi-realm thrum | Load language | Sealed; filtered |

### F432 practice

Translate omens into local soft words for children. Do not route trade fleets on Company myths alone.

## Kindness practice

Give shutter advice without selling spectrum-war tickets.

## Hard edge

Forging omenspeak to move markets or recruit Null Cells.
