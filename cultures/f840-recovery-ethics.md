# F840 Recovery Ethics

## Overview

**F840 Recovery Ethics** is care after catastrophe—warmth continuity, cold-gap honesty, and refusal of spectacle—so death and ruin deepen stewardship instead of nihilism.

## Beliefs and Values

Recovery is rotation, not miracle. An ember that stays is a team, not a trophy. Lying about a cold gap kills travelers twice.

## Practices and Traditions

### Ethics kit

| Rule | Practice | Against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Warmth that stayed** | Shared watch; kindling duty | Hero monopolies |
| **Cold-gap honesty** | Name unsafe intervals | Pretty maps over dead roads |
| **No hunt of ash-whales / grief-spectacle** | Marvel without harvest | Trophy recovery tourism |
| **Secondfire cousin manners** | Remake without costume cruelty | Exile theater as entertainment |
| **Legacy of instructions** | Pass relay knowledge | Sealing hearths against need |

### F432 contact

Secondfire and Hearthvale borrow the language. They do not own F840 physics. Using “hearth ethics” to excuse Claimscar cruelty fails the test.

### Kindness practice

Feed the watch before telling the myth.

### Hard edge

Selling “recovery tours” across cold gaps; sealing a hearth to punish a faction.
