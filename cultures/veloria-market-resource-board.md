# Veloria Market Resource Board

## Overview

**Veloria Market Resource Board** is a practical list of what a lived week in the capital buys, borrows, and shares—food first, then ordinary resources that make meals possible.

## Beliefs and Values

Rings eat differently. Outer ring knows ladle fees; core glow does not grocery-shop for you. Resources should be nameable without Operator clearance.
