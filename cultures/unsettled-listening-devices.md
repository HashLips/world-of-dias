# Unsettled Listening Devices

## Overview

**Unsettled Listening Devices** are tools—and tool-adjacent beings/plants—that hear more than consent allows. They keep Dias wonder edged with unease without turning every street into a spy thriller.

## Beliefs and Values

Listening can be care (Storm Choir; Keep water watch). Listening can be predation (null curiosity; unconsented seals). The unsettled zone is when you cannot tell which you are holding.

## Practices and Traditions

### Exemplars (graded)

| Example | Grade | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loft chalk lists | Everyday care | Public listening with known rules |
| Pot Signal arrays | Everyday wonder | Street literacy; collectors hate them |

| Sealed pattern cells | Restricted danger | Keep what should not leak |
| **Listener Ear-Fungus** / Forest Listeners | Unsettled natural | Face speech; do not pick; rumor thick |
| Null-adjacent instruments | Taboo | Thin rooms; erasure sport |
| Dream sealed chambers | Uncanny | Withhold center; not a gadget shop item |

### Authoring rule

Prefer aftermath (“we talked softer after we saw the cups turn”) over explaining the microphone. Soft horror > gadget porn.

### Kindness practice

Warn before bringing a strong listener into a hush place or Softfruit quarrel.

### Hard edge

Smash or seal taboo listeners; social freeze for those who “test” them on friends.
