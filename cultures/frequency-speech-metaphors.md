# Frequency Speech Metaphors

## Overview

**Frequency Speech Metaphors** are how F432 (and travelers) borrow other bands’ behaviors as everyday language—sticky phrases from partial ledgers, not tourist fluency.

## Beliefs and Values

Metaphor is safer than expedition. Saying “window talk” does not mean you have stood on Glassfold Ledge. Abyss names still thin rooms—metaphor does not grant casual rights to Horizon-Null gossip.

## Practices and Traditions

### Band → mouth

| Band | Sticky metaphor in F432 speech | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **F120** | **Again-but** | Something recurring with a difference; “we had an again-but at the gate—same cart, wrong toll.” |
| **F200** | **Filament-bright** / **coherence** | Calm clarity; rare praise for a plan that “holds like meridian light.” |
| **F380** | **Hearttide-loud** | Emotions impossible to hide; “don’t fight hearttide-loud near the horses.” |
| **F500** | **Null pressure** / **absence event** (House) vs street **Nullmouth / Horizon** names | Official cold speech vs tavern hazard—see taboo entry |
| **F610** | **Window talk** | Truth that won’t last; “give me window talk—say it while it’s true.” |
| **F840** | **Hearth-spine** / **cold gap** | Reliable care route vs dangerous interval; “we’re in a cold gap till Secondfire.” |
| **F960** | **Scaffold-honest** / **revision seam** | Admit unfinishedness; “leave the seam visible.” |

### How metaphors travel

Supplemental ledger gossip and story circulation teach bands as **background truths with partial evidence**. Sailors, clerks, and makers pick different favorites:

- Clerks love **window talk** and **again-but** for filing contradictions.
- Makers love **scaffold-honest**.
- Exiles love **cold gap** and **second-slate** braids.
- Rim singers borrow **hearttide-loud** carefully around whale ethics.

### Misuse

Tourists who pepper every sentence with band numbers sound like they are performing scholarship. Locals prefer the metaphor without the catalog number—unless House channel requires cold terms.

## Kindness practice

Translate a band metaphor into local plain speech for children and new arrivals.

## Hard edge

Do not treat F500 street-names as sport; House cold-speech is not moral superiority if it refuses to warn.
