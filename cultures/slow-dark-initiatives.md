# Slow Dark Initiatives

## Overview

**Slow Dark Initiatives** are political possibilities kept as pressures—not declared wars, not completed weapons. They let unease breathe under loveable daily life.

## Beliefs and Values

Naming a pressure is not confirming it succeeded. Dias stays stage-ready for sagas without collapsing into grimdark default.

## Practices and Traditions

### Pressure list (planted, not won)

| Initiative | Who might want it | Method rumor | Counterforces |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Echo exploitation** | Extractive Houses, black markets | Unconsented seals; relic stripping | Witness-share; Open Hand; street freeze |
| **Sealed research overreach** | File-and-stabilize hardliners | Delay release until truth dies | Schism; Calen-scale testimony; Calibration refusal |
| **Null curiosity programs** | Null Curiosity Cells; dare salons | Naming sport; taboo listeners | Soft customs; Ward; House hazard language |
| **Unfinished weaponization of revision** | Forced-final actors misusing F960 logic | Tools that erase undo / lock prophecy | Open Hand clean undo; Draftkin ethics |
| **Bleed timing control** | Containment cults / bad Wards | Hoard bruise intel; steer panic | Good Ward practice; Shared Table calm |
| **Exile pipeline hardening** | Concord hardliners / racket crews | Faster removals; fake aid capture | Exile Mutual Table; Compact fairness |
| **Water chart privatization** | Fruit-money map sellers | Perfect well publishing | Silent Dune Keep; wrong-map mercy |

### Authoring rule

Show one initiative as a rumor, a budget line, a bad tool, or a meeting—not a continent at war. Surface life stays markets, jokes, fruit roads.

## Kindness practice

Teach soft pairs and Mutual Table bowls louder than dark initiative lore to children.

## Hard edge

Treating slow dark as player power fantasy without institutional cost.
