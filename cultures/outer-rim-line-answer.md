# Outer Rim Line Answer

## Name

Outer Rim Line Answer

## Overview

Outer Rim Line Answer is the coastal culture around the First Line Answer and storm check-in songs: joy as coordination, friendship with whales as contested ethic, and public memory of who answered whom.

## Beliefs and Values

No one’s first good catch should be taken alone. A lied hail is worse than a missed fish. The sea is worked with, not owned.

## Practices and Traditions

- Eight-beat first call answered craft by craft
- Storm Choir check-ins as public prayer
- Hush Weeks acoustic discipline
- Apprentice charts fixing Hollowmere, Cinder Nook, Rim-Rest as stars
