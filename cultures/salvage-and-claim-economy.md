# Salvage and Claim Economy

## Overview

**Salvage and Claim Economy** is Driftfall–Claimscar value: hull and tarp as capital, paint as title, kettle as payroll, vent windows as weather markets—and Good Trade as the quiet opposite of hustle.

## Beliefs and Values

Permanence is earned slowly. Shared breakfast ≠ shared claim. Freedom without contribution is tourism. Still-hour fairness on Good Trade mountain is an economy of pause.

## Practices and Traditions

### Value objects

| Object | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Salvage materials | Building, trade, identity texture |
| Claim paint / marks | Temporary title under Vent-Claim Rule |
| Vent-window intel | Priced in risk and timing |
| Kettle contribution | Social payroll (wire/wood/news) |
| Salt Ledger witness | Conflict cooling as service |
| Mask / compact signals | Belonging capital |
| Kiln craft (Hearthvale) | Remaking labor economy |

### Market moods

- **Yard morning:** kettle peace, paint talk later.  
- **Haze week:** thin pots, expensive intel, ugly bargains.  
- **Flagweek:** breakfast-holds vs allegiance flip commerce.  
- **Good Trade still hours:** refuse hustle; sit; fair-deal custom.

### Relation to VEL

VEL works at Westfold and for Nauw-facing deals; inside yards, contribution and paint often outrank coin. Concord boots waving marks as rank earn cold shoulders.

### Kindness practice

News-bowl for new exiles; hold kettle peace; Good Trade pause when tempers are paint-hot.

### Hard edge

Double-paint fraud; kettle theft; selling vent lies; turning Salt Ledger neutrality into crew capture.
