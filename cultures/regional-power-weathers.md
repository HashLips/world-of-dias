# Regional Power Weathers

## Overview

**Regional Power Weathers** contrasts Nauw civic/gate authority, Wabet stewardship/restraint, and Sorel anti-authority/earned belonging—so politics feel like climate, not identical crowns.

## Beliefs and Values

Power that ignores local weather fails. Concord stamps die in hush courts. Keep silence dies in ring-mouth markets. Freeband paint dies under Operator floors—different failures.

## Practices and Traditions

### Nauw — civic / gate

- Deciders: Concord, Ring Protocol, House classification shadow, toll marshals.
- Venues: DEB Gate, Span Toll, Softfruit Hall, clerk desks.
- Legitimacy: markets open; fruit rank comfort; managed panic.
- Exit valve: Long Gate exile—power that can remove you.

### Wabet — stewardship / restraint

- Deciders: Silent Dune Keep, Calareth Watch, Open Hand Way teachers, canopy customs.
- Venues: Quiet Well, Rope Tree, Saltwhisper assent, Elder Shelf.
- Legitimacy: water protected; soft steps; few beds many rules.
- Exit valve: turnback, labor-tax, belonging revoked—power that refuses you entry.

### Sorel — anti-authority / earned

- Deciders: Freebands, Free-Captains, Compact norms, Exile Mutual Table, kiln teachers.
- Venues: Claimscar kettle, Flagweek Strip, Salt Ledger, Secondfire courtyard, Good Trade still hours.
- Legitimacy: contribution; pact-kept; remaking offered.
- Exit valve: exile-within; or walking further south/horizon—power that unmakes your crew seat.

### Cross-pressure

Fruit-money vs hush ethics. Concord boots vs kettle peace. House seals vs Open Hand undo. Bleed Ward vs Null Cell. Distant Companies rumor as diplomacy without embassy.

## Kindness practice

Translate your power weather when you cross regions—don’t Concord-rank-drop in yards or banana-boast at Quiet Well.

## Hard edge

Importing one region’s legitimacy theater as universal law.
