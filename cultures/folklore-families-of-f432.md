# Folklore Families of F432

## Overview

**Folklore Families of F432** groups recurring story-shapes—gate beasts, sky-turtles, ash whales, laughlight, almost-same roads—so tellers can remix without inventing a new cosmogony each night.

## Beliefs and Values

A family is not a bestiary entry. It is a pressure pattern people recognize in different clothes. Official history may call them “unverified.” Children still sleep better when the pattern has a name.

## Practices and Traditions

### Family kit

| Family | Comfort | Unease | Typical teller |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Gate beasts** | Border has a face | The face may be the border | Exile yards; Nauw porch cynics |
| **Sky-turtles** | Sky can be gentle-colossal | Gentle does not mean safe to climb | Glasswater poets; tube heirs |
| **Ash whales** | Warmth returns in bodies | Bodies are not for hunting | Ember Keepers; Hearthvale kids |
| **Laughlight** | Joy leaves a green trace | Forced laughter grows thin grass | Mutual Table; plaza hosts |
| **Almost-same roads** | You survived once | Survival is not a receipt | Returning Span; F120 whisperers |

### Kindness practice

Tell gate-beast stories as warning and bridge, never as proof someone “deserved” exile.

### Hard edge

Using almost-same folklore to gaslight a witness (“you already lived this wrong”).
