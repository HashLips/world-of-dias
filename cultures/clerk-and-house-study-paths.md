# Clerk and House Study Paths

## Overview

**Clerk and House Study Paths** is Veloria’s desk-and-stamp education: ring protocol juniors, House initiates, and Calibration Row craftspeople who may refuse the stamp on principle.

## Beliefs and Values

A wrong tag can exile. A refused stamp can save. Learning power without soft address makes clerks into small tyrants. Calibration pride without ethics makes tools into classification weapons.

## Practices and Traditions

### Clerk apprenticeship

- Softfruit lunch clusters; three scripts + tone-mark manners.  
- Soft address before stamp power.  
- Pot Signal literacy as street parallel.  
- Fear training for stamp-heavy seasons without becoming cruel.

### House study

- Stance clusters (file/stabilize vs witness, etc.).  
- Classification codes; sealed channels; hazard language for Zero/Null.  
- Schism awareness (H-11 vs H-19 type tensions) without recruiting children as soldiers.

### Calibration Row

- Oil, warm metal, argument.  
- Tune loads and small resonant tools.  
- **Dox Khar**-style refusal of House filing stamps on principle.  
- Anonymous Operator work pretended unseen.  
- Cultural sideways glance with Sketchy Gallery.

### Kindness practice

Explain a form once without sighing; teach refuse-stamp courage as care.

### Hard edge

Filing persons as numbers with a smile; selling Row tunes bundled with forced classification.
