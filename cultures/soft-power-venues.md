# Soft Power Venues

## Overview

**Soft Power Venues** maps places where politics happens without needing a throne—galleries, ledgers, watches, tolls, song calendars, pot codes.

## Beliefs and Values

A crown that cannot hold a Softfruit quarrel or a loft hail is theater. Soft power comforts (Shared Table) and frightens (stamp that ends belonging).
