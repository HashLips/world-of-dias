# Holding Cove Tone-Share Custom

## Name

Holding Cove Tone-Share Custom

## Overview

**Holding Cove Tone-Share Custom** is F380’s consent etiquette for sharing mood weather. Cloth down before deep talk. Intent named before the haze is asked to hold it. The custom exists because feelings at the Bloomline do not stay private by default, and a people who leak weather need a way to leak it without drowning the neighbors.

It is care. It is not interrogation with softer lighting.

## Beliefs and Values

Feelings become climate, and climate needs consent the way a house needs a door. Forced cheer is violence with a smile: it spends other people’s reeds on your performance. Grief may pass through. Grief is not a crime and not a guest that must be cheered into leaving before it has crossed the room. What the custom will not host is a person who uses the cove as an instrument, a truth serum, or a stage.

Exit is a value, not a failure. A session that cannot be left is not holding. It is a trap with amber water.

## Practices and Traditions

Lay Hearttide Holding Cloths at the rim before anyone begins the thing they came to say. Announce tone intent in plain words: gathering, mending, argument, quiet. Allow a walk to a quieter current when a feeling gets bigger than the agreement. Cove Tone Guides watch the reeds and end the session when the weather breaks, and ending is applauded more than endurance. Refuse metaphors that turn the cove into a trial. Softfruit guests recognize the kinship with ladle manners: notice, share, do not stamp a mood into law because it was loud.

Otters at the lip are left alone. They are not mascots. They are the cove’s way of saying the Bright Current is arriving too fast.

## Lore

Tidehearts teach the custom; neighbors learn it because they live in the leak. The mythic refusal of a fee attaches here as easily as on the hearthline: warmth that can be bought stops being a commons. Ignore consent and the cove stops holding. People step out. They will not meet your eyes. The consequence is social fatigue, visible as haze gone thin, and it lasts longer than the speech you thought was honesty.

## Notes

Not interrogation culture. Not a weaponized empathy machine. Mood is weather.
