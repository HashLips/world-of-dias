# Fruit Trade Economy

## Overview

**Fruit Trade Economy** is the apex commodity system of F432’s crowded heart: Wabet grows, roads move, Nauw ranks and hungers—and Lucky Fruit can break a convoy’s welcome.

## Beliefs and Values

Fruit is food and status. Banana supremacy is cultural fact in Nauw markets. Dependence on Wabet is openly joked (**Wabet-sweet**) and still politically sharp. Abundance without restoration is raid.

## Practices and Traditions

### Flow

1. Orchard labor & kid culture in Wabet  
2. Eastbound packing / bruise-timing  
3. Open Hand Way reciprocity teaching  
4. Span Toll metering into Basin  
5. Softfruit / stall rank performance  
6. Optional barge spill to Outer Rim (fruit as loft politics)

### Value layers

| Layer | Economic behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Staple fruit | Daily apex pricing; green-crate anxiety |
| Rank theater | Banana-boast; Softfruit manners |
| Lucky Fruit | Share-knife ethics; omen vs commodity fights |
| Reciprocity debt | Waystation restore as mandatory overhead |
| Crisis | Green-crate weeks; farewell fruit on exile roads |

### Who profits / who pressures

- Convoy stewards, stall keepers, ladle halls, toll marshals.
- Pressure on orchard hands when Nauw demand spikes.
- Keepers resist fruit-money rewriting desert water ethics.

### Kindness practice

Share-knife fair; restore waystations; teach kids Lucky restraint.

### Hard edge

Lucky-touch theft; take-without-restore branding; mocking fruit rank as only joke while still extracting.
