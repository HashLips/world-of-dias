# Festival vs Danger Movement

## Overview

**Festival vs Danger Movement** splits how roads feel—joy density versus fear sparsity—so journey chapters can choose weather without confusing Tone.

## Beliefs and Values

Festival movement feeds loveable Dias. Danger movement feeds unsettled pressure. Mixing them carelessly (party on a redmile at dusk) is how characters die stupidly—or live as cautionary slang.

## Practices and Traditions

### Festival-dense movement

| Occasion | Movement texture |
| --- | --- |
| Ring-fair / Banner Moth weeks | Crowded, colorful, pickpocket crush, Softfruit overflow |
| Fruit-barge nights | Loft welcome + inspection |
| Hearth Laughter Week | Kiln-town joy roads; careful with refusal stories |
| Flagweek Strip | Cloth snap, breakfast-holds, comic dread |
| Sun-Sweet Afternoon | Melon carts; low-stakes joy |
| Coral Strand days | Boisterous reef joy (Calareth-allowed) |

### Danger-sparse movement

| Condition | Movement texture |
| --- | --- |
| Redmile after dusk reports | Bell-dense; no solo swagger |
| Saltwhisper without assent | Turnback / labor-tax risk |
| Exile roads | Wolves, underprovision |
| Vent-haze windows | Claim wars; thin pots |
| Bleed-Sky nights | Shut windows; no Null sport travel |
| Listening Heat noon slips | Soft, dense, or don’t |
| Storm Choir failures | Social + sea hazard |

### Authoring rule

A chapter can travel festival-to-danger in one day—but mark the turn (bells, slate, Keep hand, loft silence).

## Kindness practice

Pull strangers into festival-dense columns when dusk danger rises.

## Hard edge

Mockery of bells, hails, or Keep assent as “cowardice.”
