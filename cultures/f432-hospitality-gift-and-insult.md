# F432 Hospitality Gift and Insult

## Overview

**F432 Hospitality, Gift, and Insult** collects ordinary manners that let strangers share a roof—and quarrel without becoming monsters.

## Beliefs and Values

Hospitality is a skill, not a personality. Gifts create memory; insults name wounds. A culture that cannot insult cleanly will invent cruelty instead.

## Practices and Traditions

### Hospitality

| Region habit | Offer | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nauw | Seat + fruit share or Softfruit ladle fee | News, fair trade, not free lodging forever |
| Wabet | Shared trail fruit; hush water | Soft voice; no map demands |
| Sorel | Kettle seat if you brought wire/wood/news | Pact honesty; no Concord name-dropping as rank |
| Calareth | Porch until ribbon; then bed | Assent; no stone moved |
| Outer Rim | Hammock + check-in teaching | Answer the loft when called |

### Gifts

- **Fruit** ranks above most gifts in Nauw; a banana given well can outshine coin.
- **Silence** can be a gift in Lumira—walking with someone without filling air.
- **Salvage useful** beats pretty in Sorel; pretty without use is almost an insult.
- **Revision credit** is a gift among Open Hand makers—naming who held the line.

### Insults (clean, local)

- Nauw: “You eat like someone who never waited for fruit.” (rank blindness)
- Wabet: “You publish wells with your mouth.” (silence-law wound)
- Sorel: “Concord still lives in your boots.” (unreflected exile)
- Rim: “You’d miss your own First Line.” (unreliable at sea)
- Clerks: “You filed a person as a number and smiled.” (House cruelty)

Clean insults target **values**, not bodies. Body insults are considered low and often unite bystanders against the speaker.

## Kindness practice

Offer the regional welcome before asking for story or labor.

## Hard edge

Refuse hospitality that demands assimilation into harm (loud oasis talk, unpaid claim-jumping “guests,” ribbon-skippers).
