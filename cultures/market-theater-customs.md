# Market Theater Customs

## Overview

**Market Theater Customs** are bargains staged as play—callers, mock auctions, bruise-timing jokes—without forgetting that someone’s supper is in the prop list.

## Beliefs and Values

A good market night makes strangers laugh and leave fed. A bad one sells panic as punchline.

## Practices and Traditions

- Basin callers who rhyme price with weather.
- Fruit-road “almost bruise” comedy that still protects soft fruit.
- Salt Ledger yard skits that cool disputes before chalk hardens.
- Softfruit lunch clusters as manners theater—three-script cards performed kindly.

## Kindness practice

Never stage stamp shame as comedy for the stamped.

## Hard edge

Crews planting false theater to crash a Mutual Table tip.
