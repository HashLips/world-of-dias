# Softfruit Certainty Manners

## Name

Softfruit Certainty Manners

## Overview

**Softfruit Certainty Manners** keep cosmic talk from becoming Forced-Final at the table: guests may wonder aloud; they may not promote taboo guesses into lunch law.

## Beliefs and Values

- Wonder is welcome. False certainty spoils fruit.
- Observation may be told as story. Theory may be argued. Law may be trusted. Taboo guess may be whispered—and then the ladle comes before any stamp.
- Making a child afraid of the sky to win a debate is bad hosting.

## Practices and Traditions

Hosts ask, “Is that what you saw, what you model, what we all live by, or what we do not tool?” Guests who answer wrongly get more fruit and a gentler second try. Claim Grade metaphors hang near the Resonance Bowl as cloth tags: Law / Theory / Seen / Careful.

## Lore

Grew beside Softfruit anti-forced-cheer and ladle-before-stamp ethics. Houses sometimes mock the cloth tags—then borrow them for junior drills when pamphlets spike.
