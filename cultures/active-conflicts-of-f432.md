# Active Conflicts of F432

## Overview

**Active Conflicts of F432** are present-tense pressures already biting—without totaling the realm into war.

## Beliefs and Values

Active means people argue this season, lose bowls this week, shut windows tonight. Not every active conflict needs armies.
