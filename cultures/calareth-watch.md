# Calareth Watch

## Name

Calareth Watch

## Overview

Calareth Watch is the island culture of arrival control: gentle welcome, public promises, and strict limits on what a visitor may move—including stone, soil, and story.

## Beliefs and Values

Care is ceremony. Joy is allowed on schedule. Isolation is a resource, not a brand.

## Practices and Traditions

- Ribbon at the Rope Tree on first landing of longer stay
- Day passes for Elder Shelf
- Tide-pool teaching at Coral Strand
- Posted slate windows for safe passage

## Lore

Coris Vale seats the Watch; juniors like Yara Moss carry first contact. The culture can host child-friendly openings that harden into adult threshold drama.
