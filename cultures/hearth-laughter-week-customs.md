# Hearth Laughter Week Customs

## Overview

**Hearth Laughter Week Customs** are Hearthvale’s (and Secondfire-cousin) defiant joy practices: a second life needs room to shed weight aloud—without turning another’s refusal into sport.

## Beliefs and Values

Laughter is dignity repair. Mocking exile pain during the week is a hard fail against the Compact’s restorative lean.

## Practices and Traditions

| Beat | Custom |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Small second-beginning meal after any gentle slate erase |
| **Play** | Games and hearth cricket nights that let weight leave the body |
| **Story** | Refusal stories told only by the one who lived them—or not at all |
| **Guest** | New exile arrivals get bowl before joke duty |
| **Close** | Resume ordinary debt memory without using the week as amnesty for pact-break |

## Lore

Named in Civic Time and Mourning/Celebration sheets. Myth cousin: Hearth Laughter Week.

## Kindness practice

Invite one concrete next meal after the laugh—do not only cheer.

## Hard edge

Do not turn Secondfire refusal stories into entertainment during Laughter Week.
