# Meridian Soft Gathering Manners

## Name

Meridian Soft Gathering Manners

## Overview

**Meridian Soft Gathering Manners** guide how F200 processions include visitors without turning serenity into spectacle or into a beautiful emptiness. Light-community is real. It has elbows. The manners are how brightness shares a hall without one outline eating the next.

Standing is courtesy. Staring is the rudeness people from dimmer bands have to unlearn.

## Beliefs and Values

A procession is a social fact, not a special effect. Quiet is not the absence of persons. A Meridian Quiet Rib is hospitality, a small instrument of not-speaking, not a trophy of dimming and not a defeat. Dimming fear, the visitor’s panic that their hands are going pale at the edges, is allowed as a feeling and refused as a theater. If you perform the fear, you are asking the hall to watch you instead of to include you.

Coherence is not a boarding pass you joke your way onto. Jokes that treat the gate as a puzzle insult the people whose shape the hall is holding.

## Practices and Traditions

Match the breath-pulse lightly, a long bright inhale and a softer exhale, without forcing your lungs to become a demonstration. Accept a quiet rib if it is offered; do not demand one as proof you belong. Leave before your awe hardens into a speech. Step aside when a guide stands in your light. If you will not learn the distance, close enough to share a chord and far enough not to consume, the hall omits you. Omission is the sanction: your color goes slightly wrong at the rim, and the row completes as if your gap had always been margin.

Averra studios and F432 painters are welcomed as failing admirers. They are not welcomed as people who set up a brighter lamp to “help.”

## Lore

Aurel-kind and Soft-Ray watchers share the manners. The Long Peace Frame myth gives them a picture: a frame inside a frame, peace as something you stand inside without owning the outer light. Ignore the watching distance and you are not struck down. You are unfinished by the room, which for a visitor who came to be impressive is consequence enough.

## Notes

Serenity ethics. Not a church, not a purity test, not a war of light against mud.
