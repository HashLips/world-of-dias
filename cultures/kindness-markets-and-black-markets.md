# Kindness Markets and Black Markets

## Overview

**Kindness Markets and Black Markets** twin the economies that feed belonging and the economies that feed harm—without making Dias only one or the other.

## Beliefs and Values

A kindness market is not naive; it is infrastructure (unclaimed breakfast, Softfruit seats, kettle peace, pot sanctuary codes). A black market is not automatically cool; it is pressure with teeth. Some gray lanes (pot favors) are civic underbelly, not villainy—until they are.

### Gray lanes

Pot favors, quiet water paths around Westfold, salvage that might be theft depending on crew—story fuel. Judge by who is fed and who is erased.

### Kindness practice

Defend kindness markets when collectors or Concord try to criminalize care.

### Hard edge

Freeze out black-market sport that sells taboo listeners or well beacons.
