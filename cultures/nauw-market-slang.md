# Nauw Market Slang

## Overview

**Nauw Market Slang** is the quick tongue of crates, ladles, stamps, and banana rank—speech built to move goods and pride at the same speed.

## Beliefs and Values

If you cannot say it in one breath between cart and stamp, it is a speech, not market talk. Fruit words carry moral weight; coin words carry timing.

## Practices and Traditions

### Fruit rank talk

| Phrase | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Banana-boast** | Bragging fruit status you haven’t earned at the table |
| **Green-crate** | Goods (or people) not ready; don’t price them as ripe |
| **Lucky-touch** | Handling a perfect-form fruit without share-rite—almost a crime of manners |
| **Share-knife fair** | Cut and portion that restores reciprocity |
| **Wabet-sweet** | High praise for shipment quality; also teasing Nauw dependence |
| **Rank-blind** | Someone who treats all fruit as equal fuel (insult) |

### Money and desk talk

| Phrase | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **VEL-clean** | Payment settled without favor debt |
| **Pot-favor** | Settled on Pot Signal Lane terms, not ledger |
| **Ring-mouth** | Speaking as if you own core access |
| **Stamp-heavy** | Day when every clerk act feels like exile weather |
| **Softfruit seat** | Temporary truce / shared meal before hard bargain |
| **Gate-small** | Feeling tiny under DEB Gate—humility or fear |

### Road and cart talk

| Phrase | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Bell-dense** | Traveling tight after Redmile reports |
| **Hare-kind** | Softlane omen—go gentle |
| **Eastbound honest** | Convoy that restores waystations (Fruit Road Reciprocity praise) |
| **Split-perfect** | The crisis when a Lucky Fruit nearly breaks a convoy’s welcome |

### Sample dialogue bits

- “Pay ladle or bring fruit—don’t banana-boast on a green-crate day.”
- “Keep it VEL-clean; I don’t want pot-favors in my sleep.”
- “You’re talking ring-mouth to a second-ring bath queue.”

## Kindness practice

Teach a child three fruit phrases before three coin phrases.

## Hard edge

Markets freeze out chronic rank-blind mockery and Lucky-touch thieves.
