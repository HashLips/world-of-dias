# Gallery Going Customs

## Overview

**Gallery Going Customs** teach how to look—until panels move with you—without treating DO NOT OPEN as a sport ticket.

## Beliefs and Values

Taste is work. Uncanny hours are real labor. Forced final viewing is violence dressed as curiosity.

## Practices and Traditions

- Sketchy Gallery afternoons “kept”; small facade, longer inside.
- Averra studio walks; ink-deer quiet; no stamp talk near wet panels.
- Soft applause; soft refusal of the blue door.

## Kindness practice

Offer a leaving path before a dare.

## Hard edge

Tour groups that treat sealed doors as entertainment.
