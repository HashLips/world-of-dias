# F432 Mourning and Celebration

## Overview

**F432 Mourning and Celebration** names how communities mark loss and joy without needing identical rites everywhere.

## Beliefs and Values

Grief should have a shape so it does not become only silence or only spectacle. Celebration should feed neighbors, not only advertise winners.

## Practices and Traditions

### Mourning

| Place | Shape of grief |
| --- | --- |
| Nauw | Fruit left at gates or Softfruit Hall for the absent; bells quiet for a day on a street |
| Outer Rim | Loft songs that name the missing check-in; nets left unmended one tide |
| Wabet / Lumira | Soft processions; no shouting names of the dead across dunes |
| Hearthvale / Secondfire | Kiln slate: name-in-progress erased gently, then a small second-beginning meal |
| Driftfall | Shore hang-about that refuses jokes for one evening—then resumes, fiercely |

### Celebration

- **Ring-fair / Banner Moth weeks** — Veloria color and shared tables.
- **Hearth Laughter Week** — Secondfire’s defiant joy.
- **Flagweek** — Strip chaos with breakfast continuity rules.
- **Fruit-barge nights** — Hollowmere loft welcome.
- **Ribbon first-landing** — Calareth’s strict gladness.
- **Unclaimed breakfast** — Wabet kindness that is also celebration of enough.

## Kindness practice

Invite mourners to one concrete next meal; do not only say “I’m sorry.”

## Hard edge

Do not turn another’s grief into entertainment (especially Secondfire refusal stories during Laughter Week). Do not celebrate conquest of silence or water.
