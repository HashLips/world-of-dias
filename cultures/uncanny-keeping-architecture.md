# Uncanny Keeping Architecture

## Overview

**Uncanny Keeping Architecture** names rare buildings that hold time, attention, or refusal—the sense that a place **keeps you** longer than you meant, or withholds a center on purpose.

## Beliefs and Values

These are not haunted-house tropes. They are **memory with walls**: resonance thick enough that corridors deepen, doors stay shut, or dream-rooms wait for an occasion that has not come.
