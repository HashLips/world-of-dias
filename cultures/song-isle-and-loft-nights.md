# Song Isle and Loft Nights

## Overview

**Song Isle and Loft Nights** are music as weather-meter—tide-song on isles, hail songs in Hollowmere, calendars that refuse to be only entertainment.

## Beliefs and Values

Song is co-authored by wind and load. Applause that ignores a storm warning is bad manners.

## Practices and Traditions

- Hollowmere loft evenings; chalk hail; net-stage jokes that still mean rescue.
- Outer Rim song-calendar speech; patience as beat.
- Ferry-window lullabies that teach assent without sermon.

## Kindness practice

Lower volume when a slate posts quiet hours.

## Hard edge

Using loft song to drown a Bleed-Sky shutter call.
