# Outer Rim Sailor Subculture

## Overview

**Outer Rim Sailor Subculture** is the deck-and-loft life of Singing Fishermen, raft-ring families, and apprentices who learn rope before stone.

## Beliefs and Values

The sea remembers who answered. Whale eyes complicate supper. A missed check-in is a public wound, not a private oops.

## Practices and Traditions

- Hammock hospitality; gangplank gossip faster than roads.
- First Line Answer as civic clock; loft stage as music and roll call.
- Compact arguments after whale sightings—kinship vs tonnage—mended by morning or not.
- Fruit-barge nights as festival and inspection.

## Kindness practice

Teach a new hand one true route stanza before asking for labor.

## Hard edge

Shame silent rafts in storm; refuse crews that treat human-eyed whales as only meat without facing the compact talk.
