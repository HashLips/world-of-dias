# F432 Family and Household

## Overview

**F432 Family and Household** names how belonging is practiced at home scale—blood, crew, kettle, loft, and ribbon—without forcing one kinship model on the whole band.

## Beliefs and Values

A household is who eats and teaches soft words under one roof-rhythm. Houses and Compacts may claim larger loyalty; children learn neighbor first. Second beginnings (exile, remaking) can rewrite household without erasing care debts.

## Practices and Traditions

### Household shapes (common)

| Shape | Where strong | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ring flat household** | Veloria | Kin + lodgers; Softfruit manners taught at breakfast; pot sill as care grammar |
| **Orchard cluster** | Wabet | Multi-age fruit share; unclaimed breakfast honesty; hush toward Silence Law |
| **Kettle circle** | Sorel | Chosen bowls; pact memory; paint sticks kept high from toddlers |
| **Loft kin** | Outer Rim | Hammock rows; check-in teaching as parenting; storm as co-author |
| **Ribbon guest stay** | Calareth | Temporary belonging under Watch assent; few beds, real rest |
| **Revision workshop home** | Open Hand / F960 visits | Apprentices sleep near revision bands; attribution as kinship courtesy |

### Multi-kind households

Shared Table homes treat kinds as neighbors under one kettle. Continuity-names, front-plane courtesy, and pulse-waiting are household manners—not optional “guest mode.” Prejudice at the door is a hard fail against the children watching.

### Care and teaching

| Act | Expectation |
| --- | --- |
| **Morning** | Soft address practice; fruit share or kettle bowl |
| **Day** | Child paths from Learning Paths (soft-step, ladle, rope-before-stone) |
| **Evening** | Games that teach check-in / hush / pot literacy |
| **Grief** | One concrete next meal for mourners (Mourning and Celebration) |
| **Conflict** | Clean value insults only; body insults unite the room against the speaker |

### Kindness practice

Invite one extra bowl before asking which Compact or House a child will “belong to.”

### Hard edge

Do not use household warmth to hide stamp cruelty, well-publishing, or pact-break. Do not steal exile second-names for comedy.

## Lore

Clerk mornings, walker days, exile bowls, Watch shifts, and loft evenings all assume a household waiting at the other end of the day—even when that household is a crew tarp.
