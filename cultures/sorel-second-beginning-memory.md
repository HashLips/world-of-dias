# Sorel Second-Beginning Memory

## Overview

**Sorel Second-Beginning Memory** is southern history as remaking: Long Gate arguments, captainless hours, kiln fires, and Compact belonging earned under stress.

## Beliefs and Values

Displacement is not the end. Refusal of remaking is also history. Masks can mean survival or fear institutionalized—argue in public.
