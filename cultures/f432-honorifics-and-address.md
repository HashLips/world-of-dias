# F432 Honorifics and Address

## Overview

**F432 Honorifics and Address** is how you speak *to* someone without accidentally promoting, demoting, or insulting them.

## Beliefs and Values

Wrong address is louder than wrong facts. Over-honoring can be flattery or mockery; under-honoring can be intimacy or Concord arrogance. Local ears decide.

## Practices and Traditions

### Common civic address

| Form | Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Steward** / **High Steward** | Nauw Concord roles | Using on Driftfall captains as a dig |
| **Regent** / **Ring Regent** | Veloria ring leadership | Applying to Outer Rim loft-singers |
| **Warden** / **Tide Warden** / **Isle Warden** | Rim seas, Calareth | Casual nickname without assent |
| **Keeper** | Lumira / hush authorities | Tourists calling every quiet adult “Keeper” |
| **Captain** / **Free-Captain** | Driftfall / crews | Assuming one captain owns a whole beach |
| **Curator-Lord** | Gallery posts (rare, half-joke, half-real) | Using on Softfruit ladle staff |
| **Operator** | Core Valorian vocation | Calling third-ring techs Operators |

### Soft everyday address

- **Neighbor** — Wabet and Shared Table gold standard.
- **Hand** — Open Hand / fruit-road workers (“good hand”).
- **Line** / **Loft** — Rim shorthand for people you trust at check-in.
- **Bowl** — Sorel kettle intimacy (“sit, bowl”).
- Given name alone — intimacy or clerk coldness, depending on tone.

### Race-aware courtesy (not hierarchy)

- Ask a Palewind’s **continuity-name** before counting motes aloud.
- Face a Lineborn **front-plane** when giving witness-weight words.
- Do not demand a Choirglass “say it in plain” mid-tone; wait a pulse.
- Never open with a Veyrin’s trait-epithet in a market queue.

### Gate and threshold speech

At DEB Gate and lesser arches, protocol favors short compliance phrases: destination ring, business class, companion count. Poetry at the threshold is read as delay—or as oath, if the Gate culture knows you.

At Hallow Bay, address the Watch by role until ribboned; then names open.

### Polite vs rude exchange seeds

| Setting | Polite | Rude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Softfruit Hall | “Neighbor—ladle first?” | “Operator, stamp my favor.” (wrong register) |
| DEB Gate | Destination ring + companion count, short | Poetry delay or House swagger over the oath |
| Hallow Bay | Role-address Watch until ribboned | First-name the Watch before assent |
| Claimscar | “Sit, bowl—wire or news?” | “Steward says…” as Concord rank |
| Choirglass desk | Wait a pulse; use handle | “Say it like a person.” |

## Kindness practice

Default to the local soft address (**neighbor**, **hand**, **loft**) until invited higher or closer.

## Hard edge

Refuse honorifics that launder harm (“Steward” used to silence a witness). Correct public mis-address of swarm or planar persons.
