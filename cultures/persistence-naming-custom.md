# Persistence Naming Custom

## Name

Persistence Naming Custom

## Overview

**Persistence Naming Custom** is how the Unfinished names a place: by how long the form holds, not by a false age, not by who conquered the sketch, not by the first line someone is sentimental about. A door that has stayed a door earns a name. A door that was a corridor by morning keeps the corridor’s honesty and loses the door’s vanity.

Holding is honor. Redraw is care.

## Beliefs and Values

Finality worship erases useful ghosts. The underdrawing is not an embarrassment to be painted out for guests; it is the memory that lets the next revision be kind instead of amnesiac. A ribbon across a scaffold is a lie about weight. Delegations that need a finished city are offered tea and a ledger, not a completed quarter.

Names that outlive their outlines are a kind of litter. They send guests to knock where the hinge moved. The custom would rather sound pedantic than be haunted by its own outdated poetry.

## Practices and Traditions

Persistence ticks beside forms that lasted, small marks, not monuments. Pass language in the Draft Pass Ledger: holding pass, clean pass, the words for “temporary” that are not apologies. Morning redraw after Erasure Verge weather, treated as manners. Children taught the courtyard myth, the place that redrew itself, so they do not grow up thinking a changed stair is a betrayal. Visiting makers learn the ticks before they are allowed to sound wise about becoming.

Open Hand practice and Draftkin practice meet here. Arguments stay about latch height and hands. If the argument becomes about whose ego the building should flatter, the custom has already been left.

## Lore

The Half-Made Atlas borrows the grammar and still argues, which the court allows, because an atlas is a draft of a draft. Ignore the custom and you navigate by a name the stone has resigned from. You sleep in a room that is a corridor by dawn and blame the city. Foundation Revisers will not rescue your pride. They will show you the tick you walked past.

## Notes

Becoming culture. Do not “finish” it into a heritage brand. The unfinished that functions is the point.
