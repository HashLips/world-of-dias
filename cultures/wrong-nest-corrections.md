# Wrong Nest Corrections

## Overview

**Wrong Nest Corrections** are the social scripts used when someone mis-nests the stack—calling a phenomenon a realm, a realm a country, or Dias a single flat province called Veloria.

## Beliefs and Values

- **Correct the rung, keep the wonder.** Do not punish curiosity for noticing bleed.
- **Name the mistake precisely.** “You promoted an event into a place” is clearer than “you’re wrong about cosmology.”
- **Do not invent a solve to win the argument.** Especially not Frequency Zero.

## Practices and Traditions

### Common wrong nests and replies

| Wrong claim | Correction shape |
| --- | --- |
| “F500 is east of Driftfall.” | “F500 is a frequency. East of Driftfall is sea and rumor.” |
| “Bleed-sky is a new district.” | “Bleed-sky is weather-of-meaning. It visits districts.” |
| “Dias means Veloria.” | “Veloria is a city in Nauw in F432 in Dias.” |
| “I studied F120 so I can walk there tomorrow.” | “Study is not a ferry.” |
| “Zero is the ninth band on the tourist map.” | “Zero is an open unknown. It is not a mapped rung.” |

### Where corrections happen

Markets, Softfruit corners, gallery openings (Averra loves a polite public fix), House juniors’ seal practice, and orchard watches when silence law meets panic talk.

### What is not a correction

Ridicule that shuts a child up; Forced-Final lectures that pretend the catalog of frequencies is closed; selling a “corrected” map that still draws F200 as an island.

## Lore

Wrong Nest Corrections travel with wrong-map culture. Some pamphlet sellers print the correction table on the back and the fantasy ferry on the front—proving manners and mischief can share a page.
