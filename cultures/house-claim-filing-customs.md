# House Claim Filing Customs

## Name

House Claim Filing Customs

## Overview

**House Claim Filing Customs** are how Resonant Houses grade incoming cosmic and echo claims before they can steer seals, releases, or public language.

## Beliefs and Values

Unfiled certainty is a governance failure. Over-filed taboo guesses are a different failure. Partial knowledge protocol is not optional decoration.

## Practices and Traditions

Juniors stamp Claim Grade on intake sheets. Seniors may argue promotion from observation to theory in committee—never in a hallway panic. Law-grade statements are rare and cross-checked against Graded Principles of Dias. Softfruit cloth tags are allowed in training rooms when Houses admit they work.

## Lore

Schisms often begin as grade fights: H-leanings that want faster promotion of fear into law; H-leanings that want endless theory. The customs exist to slow both without denying remainder.
