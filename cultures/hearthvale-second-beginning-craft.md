# Hearthvale Second-Beginning Craft

## Name

Hearthvale Second-Beginning Craft

## Overview

Second-Beginning Craft is Hearthvale’s culture of remaking tools and selves after displacement—kilns, slates, laughter weeks, and the hard right to refuse remaking as costume.

## Beliefs and Values

Every person deserves a second beginning. Not every person wants one on the Compact’s schedule. Both truths live in the same courtyard.

## Practices and Traditions

- Teaching fires at towns like Secondfire
- Slate boards of names-in-progress
- Shared workshops over temporary encampment
- Laughter Week as pressure release, not denial

## Lore

Warm reconstruction identity for Sorel’s west—saga-ready without replaying Long Gate crossing as Calen’s chapter.
