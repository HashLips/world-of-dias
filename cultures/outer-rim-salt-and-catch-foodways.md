# Outer Rim Salt and Catch Foodways

## Overview

**Outer Rim Salt and Catch Foodways** is loft-and-deck eating: kelp, catch, shared first lines, and the moral heat of whale eyes at supper.

## Beliefs and Values

Sea knowledge is duty. First good catch is not taken alone. Restraint is survival ethics, not decoration. Fruit-barge nights are festival and inspection—inland guests eat under loft judgment.

## Practices and Traditions

### Staples

- Dried **Whale-Road Kelp**; saltgrass forage; net-catch by season.
- Raft-ring tea and repair snacks in thin weeks.
- Brine barrels and loft-shared pots; hammock breakfasts.
- Net-moss medicine teas after hard hauls.

### Luxury

- **First Line** shared catch portions.
- Fruit-barge gifts from Nauw/Wabet routes (ranked fruit enters loft politics).
- Bright Inshore market swell feasts.

### Crisis / pressure

- **Hush Weeks:** acoustic discipline; thin tea-and-repair eating; less shout-trade.
- Storm Choir aftermath: food for those who answered; social cold for answer-empty until repair.
- Whale compact splits: some tables go quiet mid-fish when eye-back is reported.

### Kindness practice

Teach check-in before boast; share First Line; feed apprentices true route food stories.

### Hard edge

Shame lied hails; enforce catch restraint when greed threatens cycles; refuse crews that treat human-eyed whales as only tonnage without compact talk.

## Lore

Rim food tastes like salt and argument—and still like home if you answered.
