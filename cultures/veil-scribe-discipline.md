# Veil Scribe Discipline

## Overview

**Veil Scribe Discipline** is how F610 recorders learn to capture structural truths in short reveal windows—and how F432 borrows **window talk** without claiming Scribe training.

## Beliefs and Values

Brief clarity is real and not omniscience. Unstamped certainty is professional sin. Overvaluing the window can make you cruel to ordinary opaque days.

## Practices and Traditions

### Training arc

1. Patience drills with ledge fox jokes about apprentices.  
2. Notation protocols with confidence levels.  
3. **Phase stamps** mandatory ([Glassfold Phase Stamp Rule](../rules/glassfold-phase-stamp-rule.md)).  
4. Ethics: do not sell a closed window as still open.  
5. Field work on Glassfold Ledge under timed risk.

### What Scribes produce

Layered records: what appeared, what vanished, how sure we were. Useful to investigators; dangerous if treated as permanent verdicts.

### F432 interface

Clerks and makers say **window talk** for truths that won’t last. Visiting Scribes (rare) expect phase manners; local “window” slang without stamps is metaphor, not credential.

### Kindness practice

Translate a phase record into plain speech for non-Scribes; admit opacity without shame.

### Hard edge

False certainty; using a reveal to humiliate; teaching window-forcing as sport.
