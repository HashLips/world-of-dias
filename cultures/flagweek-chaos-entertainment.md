# Flagweek Chaos Entertainment

## Overview

**Flagweek Chaos Entertainment** is allegiance weather as sport—cloth snap, comic dread, and breakfast tables that refuse to flip with the flags.

## Beliefs and Values

You may cheer a new cloth. You may not starve a neighbor mid-flip. Mir sells the joke; Mutual Table hands sell the hold.

## Practices and Traditions

- Parade goats, garland vines, dye that outlasts allegiance.
- Leaf headlines as evening theater; loft corrections as morning medicine.
- Breakfast-holds: meal continuity over stamp theater.

## Kindness practice

Brush a wrong-citrus moth off a pot before arguing flags.

## Hard edge

Turning breakfast refusal into a loyalty test.
