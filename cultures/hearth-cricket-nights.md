# Hearth Cricket Nights

## Overview

**Hearth Cricket Nights** are soft listening evenings—when the house’s singers are small and the entertainment is not drowning them out.

## Beliefs and Values

A silent cricket week means the house is thinking—or the cat won. Either way, people lower their voices and check on neighbors.

## Practices and Traditions

- Dim lamps; stew shared; stories told between chirps.
- Children learn to count quiet without fear.
- Laughter Week stores banners; cricket nights store patience.

## Kindness practice

Do not force a joke into a thinking house.

## Hard edge

Mocking silence as “exile gloom” for sport.
