## Premise in one breath

Dias is layered by **frequency**. Everything resonates; everything leaves an echo. The Fracture split an older unity into realms (F432 is the broad lived stage). Stories and myths carry belief; rules and records carry institutional present—you need both.

## How to navigate without drowning

1. **Name search** — find the best existing `name:` before creating a twin.
2. **Related graph** — open 3–5 `related:` neighbors; match spelling exactly when you link back.
3. **Dashboard** — [`../dashboard/story-dashboard.html`](../dashboard/story-dashboard.html) for categories, themes, unresolved refs after a rebuild.
4. **Atlas** — [`../dashboard/map-registry.yaml`](../dashboard/map-registry.yaml) for Map-tab pins.
5. **Stage readiness** — [`stage-readiness.md`](stage-readiness.md) before starting a new arc.

## Safe contribution patterns

From [`stage-readiness.md`](stage-readiness.md):

- Local hub arcs (Softfruit, loft hail, Watch slate, Quiet Well, Claimscar breakfast, etc.)
- Evidence genres (toll books, song calendars, forged handbills)
- Mystery clues that thicken without closing Frequency Zero
- Enrichment of hubs already on stage—quality over bulk

## Saga touch discipline

When writing near a saga:

- Prefer thickening side texture and evidence over rewriting chapter files.
- Calibration: Caliburn may remain at large; Zero stays unresolved unless the human plans otherwise.
- Do not add a saga to `SAGAS.md` until a reading-path file exists.
