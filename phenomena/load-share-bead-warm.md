# Load-Share Bead Warm

## Name

Load-Share Bead Warm

## Overview

**Load-Share Bead Warm** is strap-bead heat when a share is honest—and cold refusal when weight is dumped without three thank-yous.

## Description

Oxen ease; neighboring carts feel the lean together. Silent dumps leave beads icy next attempt. Cousin to Brindle stillness, but reciprocity-shaped rather than whole-march hush.

## Conditions

Honest clip-and-unclip with taps; Ashen teams borrowing manners. Absent under Forced-Final haul pride.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Drivers:** the march answering.
- **Dumper:** beads won’t warm for you.
- **Softfruit:** buckle ethics.
- **Hazard:** warm beads ≠ ignore animal injury.

## Notes

Travel/reciprocity phenomenon.
