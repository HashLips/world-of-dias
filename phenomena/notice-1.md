# Notice

## Name

Notice

## Overview

**Notice** is an F500 depth-and-surface event: a dark boat on a yellow field casts a thin line into light-blue water where a headless blue sitter waits among rows of orange profile heads, and the hook hangs before only one of them.

## Description

Realm walkers speak of Notice when the upper weather goes flat yellow and someone in a small sail silhouette drops a line into the crowded blue. Below, orange heads face one way in neat rows, each marked with a light-blue eye-sliver—a chorus of possible attention. The headless blue figure sits cross-legged with arms folded, treated as the depth's own patience: present, protective, not lunging for every profile. When the hook stops in front of a single head, the event is named. Local care forbids dragging a whole row for spectacle. Resonance is the black thread from rod to chosen face; memory is how the unhooked heads stay in their rows; wonder is how quiet the yellow surface remains while the blue holds so many. People use the name when a community must attend to one need without pretending the others vanished. The phenomenon is not a census and not a hunt—it is the manners of noticing one without erasing the many.

## Signs

Yellow surface with dark sailboat and fisher; light-blue depth; headless blue cross-legged sitter; rhythmic orange head rows; hook before one profile.
