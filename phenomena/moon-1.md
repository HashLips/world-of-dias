# Moon

## Name

Moon

## Overview

**Moon** at Mercer Shed names nights when the off-white orb sits half in cerulean under a yellow hood-feeling of shelter, with a black upward crescent reading as bowl beneath and seven pink marks to count before closing work.

## Description

Onset is visual along the red/blue join. Soft Mark neighbors treat the yellow arc as “hood manners”—keep noise soft under it. Pink column may appear as lantern notches, chalk tears, or actual hanging marks on shed walls. Children count aloud. Adults who skip the seven to rush a latch redo the count at dawn.

## Cultural Meaning

Around Mercer Shed, Moon is sheltered celestial care. Resonance favors finished pink counts. Scraping hood paint for jokes empties the phenomenon’s local rule.
