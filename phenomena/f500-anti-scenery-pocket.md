# F500 Anti-Scenery Pocket

## Name

F500 Anti-Scenery Pocket

## Overview

Temporary or falsely stable intervals in F500 where a floor seems to exist inside anti-scenery—then dissolves without polite transition.

## Description

Survivor talk agrees on the temptation: for a stretch that feels like minutes or a held breath, something underfoot behaves like ground. A name wants to stick. A camp wants to form. Then the pocket dissolves without courtesy—no dusk, no road sign, only the return of landmark starvation and continuity thinning. Craft answers with temporary naming bans and retreat honesty. A pocket is not a harbor district east of Driftfall, and a bleed bruise over F432 is not this pocket painted onto a street.

Approach estimates lie. Pride that plants a flag mid-pocket usually returns as rumor holes rather than as a map leaf Houses will stamp Observation without quarrel.

## Conditions

Unpredictable duration. Stronger nearer null concentration. Falsely stable intervals may tease ordinary ash or floor before dissolving.
