# F120 Rhymed Threshold Wear

## Name

F120 Rhymed Threshold Wear

## Overview

**Definition:** In F120, wear on thresholds, rails, and step-stones can rhyme with prior wear—similar grooves and polish with altered timing or consequence. Matter near-returns; it does not rewind.

## Description

Refrain Walkers learn not to trust “this is the same step.” The stone may show a sister groove a finger-width off, or the same polish arriving a breath late. Lab drift logs treat material rhyme as data, not destiny. Treating wear as a ticket into yesterday is soft-limit failure.

Route Rhyme Landscape is the place cousin. Phrase Drift is the memory cousin. Rhymed Threshold Wear is the matter face of the same near-return law.

## Conditions

Common on Returning Span approaches and repeatedly used lab thresholds. Exact loops are denied by craft speech.

## Cultural Interpretations

Softfruit: rhyme, not rewind. Houses: Observation for logged offsets. Tour sellers: seized for “time door” stamps on worn lips.
