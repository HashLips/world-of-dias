# Foundation Ghost Persistence

## Name

Foundation Ghost Persistence

## Overview

**Foundation Ghost Persistence** is the Unfinished habit of letting an incomplete courtyard keep its outline long enough to be useful. The ghost is working memory. It is not a haunt hired to frighten tourists, and it is not a license to declare the quarter ruined. A paler hinge, a line the moss has not yet abandoned, a ledger tick: these are how a living draft remembers without obeying its previous self.

## Description

Walls redraw and the old foundation remains visible, chalk-white under graphite, a thought the court refused to gaslight itself about. Guide-moss follows the line that holds and is shy of the line that failed, which makers read faster than they read arguments. Ledgers tick how long a shape held. After Erasure Verge weather softens a corner overnight, redraw morning does not exorcise the ghost. It restores the working ones and dates the change so memory does not curdle into rumor of vandalism. People feel it as a slight double vision that resolves if they are willing to love two outlines at once. People who want one forever outline feel it as insult.

## Conditions

Ghost Quarter and kin courts during holding passes. Open Hand maintenance actually happening. It thins where finality salespeople have been allowed to paint the underdrawing out for a delegation. A delegation that cannot bear a ghost is in the wrong city.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Draftkin:** the kindness of incomplete form. You can walk on a suggestion.
- **Finality worshippers:** an unfinished insult, proof someone lacks nerve.
- **Atlas disputers:** evidence that cartography here is a draft of a draft, which is useful if you stop crying about it.
- **Hazard:** staring into Erasure Verge weather so long you treat every ghost as decay instead of as a tick you could have read.

## Notes

Alive drafting weather. Do not turn it into ruin tourism or into a horror of trapped builders.
