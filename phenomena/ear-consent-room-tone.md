# Ear-Consent Room Tone

## Name

Ear-Consent Room Tone

## Overview

**Ear-Consent Room Tone** is the clear mid-hum a room makes when a Thin-Room Ear is welcome—and the sour flatness when listening is stealth custody.

## Description

Consented ears hear usable scrap. Stealth ears hear sticky silence or their own late mutter. Softfruit refuses souvenir kits that never find the tone.

## Conditions

Licensed listening with keeper consent; distress exceptions sound urgent, not sour. Planted House ears flatten rooms for everyone.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Licensed listener:** care, not custody.
- **Spy:** broken device (often manners).
- **Softfruit:** consent weather.
- **Hazard:** tone ≠ license to bottle lives.
