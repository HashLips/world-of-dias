# Ritual

## Name

Ritual

## Overview

**Ritual** is an Elder Shelf movement event: white-outlined red, blue, and orange humanoid figures overlap in raised-arm motion on solid black, no ground line—shared rhythm with clear boundaries.

## Description

Shelf walkers speak of Ritual when colored bodies appear bound in thick white outlines against black and the arms go up together. The red center often leads the stride; blue may arrive from the edge; orange fills behind. The white bind is treated as local care—each presence kept distinct even while overlapping. Local care forbids scraping outlines off to force a merge, and forbids turning the hour into a private ownership dance. Resonance is the color chorus; memory is the eye-slits in profile; wonder is motion without floor. The phenomenon is named weather of togetherness, not a hidden doctrine.

## Signs

Black field; thick white outlines; red central dynamic figure; blue partial reacher; orange uplifted figures; no horizon.
