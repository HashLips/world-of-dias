# F840 Warmth-Corridor Sight

## Name

F840 Warmth-Corridor Sight

## Overview

Eyes and attention trained to follow warmth corridors and viable rests in F840—sight as recovery craft rather than monument tourism.

## Description

Ember Keepers teach juniors to look low before they look proud. Warmth-Corridor Sight finds the faint kettle shimmer along an ash path, the matte latchvine that still grips a lean-to post, the rest-stone that holds palm heat after three nights of honest watch. Skyline glitter that hides a cold gap is treated as a lie of the eye. A Forced-Final seller’s bright banner can make the corridor look continuous when the feet still feel a chill seam; trained sight refuses that glitter and names the seam aloud.

The skill smells like ash-sweet and boiled grain more than like perfume. It is not F500 landmark starvation—recovery wants rest points—and not F432 mixed market dazzle. Perception here serves continuity: where can a body still sleep and wake without pretending the scar is gone?

## Conditions

Baseline along Ashen Hearthline practice routes. Weakens when miracle-reset speeches flood a town; strengthens after scraped corrections and kettle rounds resume.

## Cultural Interpretations

Softfruit cousin speech: keep the kettle. Ember Keepers: sacred without temples. Miracle sellers: mock it as “ash stare.” Houses: Observation when logging which rests still glow viable.
