# Market Haggle Aftertone

## Name

Market Haggle Aftertone

## Overview

The mixed residue of fair and unfair bargaining that clings to stall wood and coin-hands until the next day’s first honest trade.

## Description

A fair haggle leaves bright fatigue—tired but clean. A cruel haggle leaves a sticky cleverness in the air that makes the next customer distrust even good fruit. Market aunties air stalls at dusk and tell apprentices: *count coin, then count aftertone.*

## Conditions

Strongest in dense markets after festival pricing fights. Weakens overnight if the stall is aired and the first morning sale is deliberately kind.

## Cultural Interpretations

Merchants treat it as craft hygiene. Houses rarely file it unless fraud is involved. Children learn commerce as resonance manners.
