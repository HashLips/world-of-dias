# Dream

## Name

Dream

## Overview

**Dream** is a named F432 look of night-resonance: a red heat-of-mind profile with a yellow slit of attention appearing when a sleep-thought wants witnessing rather than enforcement.

## Description

Reports cluster in quiet F432 hours: a sense of a red profile watching from blue, eye bright yellow and horizontal, outline faintly glowing. Duration short. Demanding proof usually collapses it into ordinary fatigue. When it shows, look carefully, refuse to stamp it into day law, and do not sell the look as prophecy. Not a calendar guarantee. Culture- and religion-agnostic.
