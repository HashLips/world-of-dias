# Rainbow

## Name

Rainbow

## Overview

**Rainbow** is an F432 sky event: a four-color arc (red, blue, yellow, green) rises from behind dark hills under gray sky, splits into wavy ribbons, and leaves faint matching vertical bands on pale ground beside a mint river.

## Description

Realm walkers speak of Rainbow when the hill line stays charcoal-cold and the sky stays honest gray while color still arrives. The braid begins tight behind a left hill, then the four bands loosen into separate wavy ribbons that feel more like current than like a painted postcard. Down by the pale river, four muted vertical echoes may appear on the ground—same order, quieter—so the event is sky and ground together. Local care forbids racing under the ribbons for ownership, and forbids scrubbing the ground bands away to keep a "clean" path. Resonance is the unbraid; memory is the hill cutout; wonder is how much gray can hold. The phenomenon is not permanent climate. It is a named hour of splitting light you recognize and respect without inventing a chase plot.

## Signs

Gray sky; dark jagged hills; red-blue-yellow-green arc that separates into wavy ribbons; pale mint river; faint vertical ground color bands.
