# Raining Again

## Name

Raining Again

## Overview

**Raining Again** is an F432 void-weather event: a white elongated figure braces an inverted geometric sail on a thin rod while horizontal dashes mark a current returning through solid black.

## Description

Realm walkers use the name when the catcher turns inside-out and the body must arch to keep grip. There may be no ordinary water—only dashes, pull, and black field—but the labor is the same: hold, lean, do not drop the rod. The eye is a single slit of focus. Local care forbids mocking the lean as drama, and forbids abandoning the sail as "only weather." Resonance is rod tension; memory is how often the event earns the word again; wonder is how much white can mean on black. The phenomenon is not a picnic rain. It is a named return that asks for craft and stance.

## Signs

Black void field; white arched figure with slit eye; inverted segmented sail; taut thin rod; horizontal white dashes.
