# Road Trip

## Name

Road Trip

## Overview

**Road Trip** is a Brindle March travel event: a bubblegum-pink figure rides a yellow-frame blue-wheeled minimal cycle on a wide black road with a broken white center line, red scarf streaming, under gray hills and lighter gray sky.

## Description

March walkers name Road Trip when the hills go empty gray and one bright rider owns the black curve without a convoy. The red scarf's two trailing strands are the tell of motion; the blue wheels stay solid and simple; the eye-line stays thin. Local care forbids blocking the vanishing point for jokes, and forbids stripping the scarf as a souvenir. Resonance is bright traveler on bleak road; memory is the broken white dashes; wonder is how quiet the gray can stay. The phenomenon is solitary travel weather, not a tour package.
