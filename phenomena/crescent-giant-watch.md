# Crescent Giant Watch

## Name

Crescent Giant Watch

## Overview

**Crescent Giant Watch** is the name Driftfall's eastern shore people give to a rare sky sighting—a huge blue figure reclining in a white crescent above the sea—and to the quiet vigil kept for it on the Mountain of Majestic Good Trade.

## Description

Reports agree on a few things. The figure is enormous, a single flat blue, lying on its back with knees raised as if resting in a hammock. It lies in a crescent of white light that curves beneath it. Long green strands stream from its head. Under the crescent hangs a row of pale curved arcs, sharp at the tips, which some call cloud ribs and others refuse to name. Sightings last from a few minutes to most of an evening and never come with weather. The figure has never been reported to move toward land, speak, or look down.

## Local Response

When a sighting is called, shore families walk up the mountain rather than out onto the beaches. Watchers stand on the rocks in small groups, sometimes leaning on staffs for a long wait, and keep their voices low. Shouting at the giant is considered both rude and pointless. The custom is to come down before it fades, so nobody is caught on the mountain in the dark wondering what they saw. Children who keep watch are given a notch on a family staff.
