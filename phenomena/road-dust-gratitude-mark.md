# Road Dust Gratitude Mark

## Name

Road Dust Gratitude Mark

## Overview

A faint travel aftertone on fruit roads and rests where prior parties left thanks—water, peel, clean jokes—rather than only taking shade.

## Description

Later boots sometimes find the mile “easier to agree with.” It is not a mapped enchantment. It is remainder of manners, argued about at fires and denied by people who only hurry.

## Conditions

Appears along Eastbound Fruit Road and sibling routes after good camps. Fades under cruel haste, litter, and boastful cruelty at rests.

## Cultural Interpretations

Caravans: practical superstition that works often enough. Clerks: nonactionable. Softfruit: the road’s version of table warmth.

## Notes

Exemplar of ordinary echo under the foundational principle—wonder without omnipotence.
