# Melting Cheer Drip

## Name

Melting Cheer Drip

## Overview

**Melting Cheer Drip** is when Forced-Final cheer physically or socially “melts”—smile pins drip paint, slogans taste like glue, helpful glass warmth turns sticky instead of kind.

## Description

A room trying too hard to be happy starts to sag. Laughter arrives late and wrong. Softfruit treats the drip as diagnosis; Houses call it bad enamel. Row apprentices feel it when they almost stamp a smile quiet.

## Conditions

Festival over-recruitment, helpful-glass mornings, Flagweek lids pulled early for slogans. Rare in honest ladle steam. Can coincide with visitor wrong-tick unease.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Softfruit:** fake cheer confessing.
- **Comedy:** buy a melting pin on purpose.
- **Operator:** morale product failure; reorder stock.
- **Hazard:** do not confuse drip with Bleed Weather.

## Notes

Joy warning phenomenon. Perforated—not every sad party is Caliburn weather.
