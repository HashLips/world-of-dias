# Sorel Vent Haze

## Name

Sorel Vent Haze

## Overview

**Sorel Vent Haze** is a pale, sweet-rot atmospheric haze that rides vent-sprout release and warm scrapyard air—common enough in parts of Sorel to be treated as weather, not a one-off accident.

## Description

Visibility softens; clothes pick up grit; throats itch. The smell is wrong-fruit and warm metal. Spore-mites often travel inside the haze. Forge towns add smoke; coastal Driftfall adds salt; together they make sunsets look bruised and beautiful.

Not every Sorel day is Vent Haze. Clear Flagweek mornings exist. When haze comes, crews wet cloths, delay speeches, and keep children from playing “catch the cloud.”

## Conditions

Clusters around Sprout Vent seasons and busy claim yards; worsens in still air; clears after hard wind or rain.

## Cultural Interpretations

- Yard workers: weather with a chore list.
- Nauw visitors: proof Sorel is “poisoned.” Sorel answers: proof we live anyway.
- Healers: filter first, folklore second.

## Notes

Sorel’s signature climate pressure—keep it textured and survivable, not apocalyptic every chapter.
