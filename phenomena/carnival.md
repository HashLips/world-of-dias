# Carnival

## Name

Carnival

## Overview

**Carnival** is a named Coral Strand look of weather, light, or mood: a carousel-clean hour when blue horse-pace repeats under a bright smiling sun and travelers argue afterward about how long the cheer should last.

## Description

Reports cluster on shore platforms and temporary pole-rings: canopy colors flash red-white-blue, identical blue leaping forms feel nearer than ordinary traffic, and a huge yellow sun-face seems too close to ignore. Duration may be a breath or an evening. Instruments sometimes notice the light; manners notice the pull to join the same pace. When it shows, decide carefully what memory you leave on the next thing you touch—especially poles, seats, and guest cups. Forced cheer that traps a rider is not **Carnival**; it is bad craft wearing a smile. Locals treat the phenomenon as band weather and manners weather at once. Not a calendar guarantee.

## Notes

Plate-led strand phenomenon. Rhythm without toll.
