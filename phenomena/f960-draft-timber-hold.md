# F960 Draft-Timber Hold

## Name

F960 Draft-Timber Hold

## Overview

**Definition:** In F960, timber-like substance holds in passes—outline, volume, adjustment—and stays revisable by default. Incompletion is baseline state for matter, not failure.

## Description

Open Hand makers measure persistence by repeated holds, not first appearance. A draft-timber span that kept three mornings may still accept a fourth pass that thins a strut. Shame speech from F432 bridge yards (“finish it or it is broken”) misreads the law. Undo is craft when the band prefers becoming.

Sketchline Terrain Drift is the place cousin. Draft-Timber Hold is the workshop substance cousin under iterative manifestation.
