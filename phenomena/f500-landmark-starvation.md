# F500 Landmark Starvation

## Name

F500 Landmark Starvation

## Overview

**Definition:** In F500 anti-scenery, the senses fail to find reliable rest points. The eye reaches for a landmark and cannot keep one. This is not ordinary darkness.

## Description

Night in F432 still has streets, names, and edges you can lean on. Landmark Starvation removes that kindness: highlights feel stolen, edges will not sit, and naming a feature often fails to hold. Brief anti-scenery pockets may tease a floor or mark before dissolving. Brave naming without retreat honesty is manners failure, not courage.

Bleed bruises over F432 skies are visiting weather; they are not this starvation redrawn as a district.

## Conditions

Stronger nearer null concentration. Approach increases unreliability. False pockets can briefly fake a landmark.
