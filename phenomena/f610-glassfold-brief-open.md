# F610 Glassfold Brief Open

## Name

F610 Glassfold Brief Open

## Overview

**Definition:** Along Glassfold, hidden structure can become place-visible for a limited interval, then reseal. The open is real geography for that interval—not permanent architecture and not an endless window.

## Description

A brief open changes what a walker can treat as “there”: seams, grain, and structure may show as if a wall or ledge briefly admitted reading. When the interval ends, the place reseals. Veil Scribes phase-stamp what they saw because remembering an open is not the same as the open remaining. Mapping a brief open as always-there country invents a ferry of the eye.

Staring past the seal does not keep the geography open; it raises stare-cost. F432 residual seam light is not a legal endless Glassfold open redrawn as a street.

## Conditions

Interval-bound along Glassfold Ledge and related reveal sites. Duration varies; craft assumes reseal. Porch-light precision invites nests more than it buys truth.

## Cultural Interpretations

Veil Scribes: glance craft and phase stamps. Houses: high-value fragile evidence. Softfruit: stop is part of seeing. Soft limit: no permanent omniscience from window weather.
