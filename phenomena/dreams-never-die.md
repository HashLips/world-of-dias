# Dreams Never Die

## Name

Dreams Never Die

## Overview

**Dreams Never Die** is a named F432 condition of care: a pink living dream-growth that persists when two tenders work back-to-back and fails when one tries to own it alone.

## Description

Witnesses describe a fleshy pink central mass with angular upper branches and hanging root-tendrils through a draped platform, needing paired touch. Solo grabbing usually wilts the look into ordinary wishfulness. Duration lasts as long as the tending manners hold. When it shows, sit balanced, touch only with care, leave a rest pillow honest, and refuse sale of the growth as a private charm. Not a calendar guarantee. Dias name, not an Earth lyric claim.

## Notes

Paired tending phenomenon. Share the hands; refuse the monopoly.
