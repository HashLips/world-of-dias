# F200 Coherence Eye Calm

## Name

F200 Coherence Eye Calm

## Overview

**Definition:** Under F200 coherence, vision and attention are pressed toward aligned calm. The field reduces ordinary F432 fidget in what you look at—and can demand more readiness than a visitor expects.

## Description

Witnesses describe light that clarifies rather than merely blinding, and a quiet that asks the eye to stop arguing with the field. That calm is place-pressure, not ownership of happiness and not Softfruit franchise cheer. Unready visitors may meet alignment fatigue instead of usable calm.

Coherence Eye Calm is perception. Clarity-Without-Flood Recall is the memory cousin. Alignment Pressure Field is the environment cousin. Do not collapse them into one tourist word “lumen vibe.”

## Conditions
