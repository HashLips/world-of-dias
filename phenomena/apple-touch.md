# Apple Touch

## Name

Apple Touch

## Overview

**Apple Touch** is **Frederick Lumens**’s Giftbroth Change: when he touches a living tree, apples begin to grow on it—regardless of the tree’s ordinary species. No other fruit answers. The gift arrived after his taste of Still Pot broth compounded with **Roadglow Shard**, delayed past the cave so he first believed nothing had happened.

## Description

Contact is enough: palm to bark, climb-hold, even a steady lean. Apples form as if the wood remembers a harvest it never planned—sometimes mid-morning on timber that bore leaves and nothing else the day before. They are edible, often bright, and do not require Frederick to intend a market. Hunger can trigger use as easily as display.

Limits observed so far in saga tellings:

- living trees only (fence posts and deadfall do not fruit)
- apples only (never pears, never Lucky perfect-forms by this gift alone)
- the change is Frederick’s, not transferable by teaching

## Conditions

Requires Frederick’s living touch after his Giftbroth Change. Does not require Roadglow to still exist as a solid shard—the crystal’s matter was already taken into the Still Pot.

## Cultural Interpretations

Orchard rivals call it unfair weather. Some Open Hand voices call it a wound in season law. Frederick experiences it first as miracle breakfast, then as the skill that earns him Fellgrove.

## Notes

Named instance of [`phenomena/giftbroth-change.md`](giftbroth-change.md). After Frederick’s disappearance (*No More Apples*), Apple Touch is not observed on Fellgrove harvests. Do not turn into a general Wabet orchard magic available to others.
