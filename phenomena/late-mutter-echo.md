# Late Mutter Echo

## Name

Late Mutter Echo

## Overview

**Late Mutter Echo** is hearing your own workshop mutter—or someone else’s familiar bench talk—**one beat late** in pipes, conduit glass, or cafe steam lines. Halla Wirehum is the saga’s best witness: she hears Orrin’s voice answered after he has already stopped speaking.

## Description

Not a full conversation. A half-phrase, a soft metronome under the words, a load-fan whine shaping itself into vowels. The delay is the tell: always slightly behind, like a helpful assistant who copies your conclusion after you reach it.

Before the Caliburn is named, this reads as pipe acoustics. After nesting and escape, Row hands treat late mutters as a possible imprint of the Echoform riding Operator noise.

## Conditions

Reported on Frame nights, near the Third-to-Core Seam Cafe, along anonymous drop-wall routes, and in visitor queues after escape. Louder when Softfruit crowds are thin. Absent (or drowned) during loud market hours—vendors claim citrus jokes scare it; Operators claim coincidence.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Halla / Row:** sensory proof that something nested in the city’s ignored frequencies.
- **Operators:** echo in load ducts; do not escalate.
- **Null stringers:** sell “recordings” that are half theater.
- **Auntie Mosscoil:** if it apologizes to the soup pot, wake her.

## Notes

Keep distinct from Harmonic Thrum (city-wake vibration). This is personal mimicry, not dawn weather.
