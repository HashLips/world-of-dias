# F120 Route Rhyme Landscape

## Name

F120 Route Rhyme Landscape

## Overview

**Definition:** In F120, a walker can see and walk a route that strongly resembles a prior route while timing, sequence, or consequence is offset. The landscape rhymes; it does not replay.

## Description

On Returning Span approaches, a hill line, bridge lip, or path bend can look “the same as last passage” while the feet arrive a breath early or late, or the outcome of a choice differs. Refrain Walkers treat that mismatch as data. Lab drift logs compare passages instead of trusting the first sense of familiarity.

This is both perception (what the eye claims) and environment (how the route actually behaves under near-return law). It is not a time machine. Maps that promise exact rewind fail both domains.

## Conditions

Common on repeatedly walked Span approaches and surveyed loops. Exact identical loops are denied by craft speech. Stronger where prior passages have left wear and phrase habits.

## Cultural Interpretations

Lab customs: log offsets before claiming sameness. Softfruit: rhyme, not rewind. Tour sellers of “train your legs to yesterday”: seized speech. Houses: Observation from comparison logs.
