# The Shelf Set

## Name

The Shelf Set

## Overview

The Shelf Set is the slow, steady westward coastal current that runs along the Saltjaw shelf and on along Sorel's south coast toward Wabet. It is ordinary sea behaviour. It is why "everything we ever lost off the point went west," why Saltjaw wish-bottles land on the Lumira coast, and how the Kestry Letter's canister reached Low Bell Cove in twelve days.

## Description

Floats dropped on the shelf go west "like a slow hand." By the time the glass has run twice they are specks. It is steady, without groups, and weakens near the Lumira coast, where it goes "slack at the edges" during a Long Slack. It carries nets, floats, caps, planks and bottles to the same few coves.

## Conditions

Year-round. Strongest on the mid-shelf. Fog does not affect it, but fog hides where it is taking you.

## Cultural Interpretations

Saltjaw children send festival notes west on it. Lumira gatherers know which coves it feeds. The Letter House exists partly because of it.

## Notes

Distinct from the Counting Current. Do not confuse the steady set with the counting ring.
