# F960 Draftkin Revision Urge

## Name

F960 Draftkin Revision Urge

## Overview

**Definition:** Draftkin and Open Hand cultures carry a living urge to revise a held form—body and attention lean toward another pass. In F960, that urge is health, not failure, unless it refuses needed shelter.

## Description

Revision urge is to F960 what scrape honesty is to F432 copy rooms. Shame imported from monument cultures hurts makers. The urge sours if it becomes refusal to let anyone rest inside a temporary “enough for tonight” hold—manners still require usable mornings.

Revised Recollection Habit is the memory cousin. Draftkin Revision Urge is the living drive that makes another pass feel right in the hands.

## Conditions

Baseline among Draftkin yards. Contagious to visitors who stay long enough to stop apologizing for drafts.

## Cultural Interpretations

Open Hand Maker Tech: clean undo as care. F432 disputed-line etiquette: name becoming without ferry maps. Houses: Theory on “enough.”
