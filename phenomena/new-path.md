# New Path

## Name

New Path

## Overview

**New Path** is an F200 walking event: a crowned dark silhouette faces one way with nested chest triangles and a purple lens, white cloak-wings open, while a leaning ladder and a red hook curve offer two unfinished invitations under a cut sun and a red arc.

## Description

In F200, New Path is recognized when someone stands as if already mid-decision: blue three-peak crown small and bright, one white eye-line, chest marked with a large blue triangle, a smaller orange triangle inside it, and a purple lens crossing both. The white cloak-wing shape spreads behind like a portable horizon. To one side a light brown and blue ladder leans without being climbed; to the other a thick red hook curves with a teal echo behind it. Low blue-teal ground lobes and a brown sky with olive wisps complete the field. The yellow sun, barred by a dark green stripe, and the red semicircle opposite are treated as weather-marks of the hour, not as gods. People say the path is new when the chest geometry is honest before either ladder or hook is chosen. Local care forbids shoving a walker up the ladder or into the hook for spectacle. Resonance is the pause in the stance; memory is how others later copy the crown and the nested marks without stealing the choice.

## Signs

Blue three-peak crown on a dark silhouette; nested blue/orange triangles with purple lens; white cloak-wings; leaning ladder; red hook curve; yellow sun with green bar; red semicircle on brown sky.
