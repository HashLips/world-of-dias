# Orchard Share Warmth Trace

## Name

Orchard Share Warmth Trace

## Overview

The gentle remainder left on orchard paths and crate edges when fruit is shared under Open Hand or Softfruit-adjacent manners rather than seized.

## Description

Walkers speak of “kind dust” and shade that feels wider than geometry. The trace does not open doors between frequencies; it makes the next sharing easier, as if the trees remember cooperation.

## Conditions

Follows unclaimed breakfast ethics, fair picking, and water shared at row ends. Thins after theft weeks and Forced-Final harvest drills.

## Cultural Interpretations

Wabet: proof reciprocity works. Softfruit: cousin to table warmth. Panic pamphlets: sometimes mislabel it as “another band leaking sweetness.”

## Notes

Exemplar of ordinary echo under the foundational principle—wonder without omnipotence.
