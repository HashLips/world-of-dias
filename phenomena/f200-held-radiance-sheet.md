# F200 Held-Radiance Sheet

## Name

F200 Held-Radiance Sheet

## Overview

**Definition:** In F200, workable substance often behaves as held radiance—luminous presence you align with—rather than as F432 soil, timber grain, or nailable plank.

## Description

Accounts that survive approach speak of floors and walls that are luminous without becoming fire, and of loads that refuse you until your resonance stops arguing. Dirt metaphors fail. Thickness reads as clarity more than as wood grain. Aurel Meridian craft treats the sheet as something you align with, not something you nail.

This is matter under coherence law. It is not ferry lumber. Softfruit uses it as teaching contrast, not building supply.

## Conditions

Reported where coherence is stable enough for temporary hold. Unaligned visitors may attenuate or drift instead of making sheet contact.

## Cultural Interpretations

Softfruit: omen contrast. Houses: Theory-open on matter vs field models. Taboo: selling “lumen timber” as a ticket between bands.

## Notes

Matter face of coherence. Does not grant a ferry ticket.
