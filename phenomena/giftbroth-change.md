# Giftbroth Change

## Name

Giftbroth Change

## Overview

Giftbroth Change is what happens when a being other than Vorrith Kael tastes the living broth of **the Still Pot**. The drinker receives one unnatural capacity unique to them. The capacity can feel like a miracle. It can also feel like a sentence.

## Description

The change is not a menu. It is a **personal resonance answer**: the broth reads the drinker’s frequency history, hunger, fear, and unfinished edges, then installs a single durable alteration. Two seekers never report the same gift.

Examples circulated in rumor (not a complete catalog):

- a person who can hear orchard roots speak
- a person who never misses a thrown blade
- a person who must sleep whenever night thinks of them
- a person whose shadow leaves first and returns late
- a person who can mix small materials by taste alone—and cannot stop tasting

The gift is described as **unnatural** because it does not grow from ordinary F432 training, craft guilds, or Keeper practice. It arrives whole, as if the self were edited.

## Conditions

- Requires an actual taste of Still Pot broth (smell and proximity are not enough in standard tellings).
- Occurs once per drinker in most versions; second tastes are said to deepen the first change rather than grant a new one—or to break the drinker. Accounts conflict.
- Vorrith’s own ongoing consumption does not produce “gifts” in this sense; for him the broth is maintenance, not lottery.

## Cultural Interpretations

Thrill-seekers treat Giftbroth Change as the point of the pilgrimage.  
Families who lost someone to a dire gift treat the mountain as a soft curse under Wabet’s calm.  
Some Resonant House clerks would classify outcomes as unstable personal frequency events if they ever got clean samples—which they have not.

## Notes

Keep examples illustrative. Do not freeze a official power list; uniqueness per individual is the rule that makes later saga visits writable.
