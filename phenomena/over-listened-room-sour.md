# Over-Listened Room Sour

## Name

Over-Listened Room Sour

## Overview

**Definition:** A room that has been echo-read or instrument-listened past craft manners turns harsh—sour air, stubborn tools, unkind aftertone. The feat may have “gotten data”; the cost is the place.

## Description

Softfruit hosts know the difference between a careful listen and a room that has been scraped raw by curiosity. Over-listening looks like diligence and behaves like insult. Gates show a cousin as argument-stain; sealed ledgers show a cousin as cold spots. Over-Listened Room Sour is the kitchen and meeting-room face of Resonance feats have costs.

Craft answer: stop, air the room, scrape corrections, refuse the next complete-reading urge. Wish answer (“make it sweet again by wanting”) fails under Resonance does not grant wishes.

## Conditions

Strongest after prolonged unconsented listening, market “full read” attempts, or sealed-room spill into ordinary halls. Eases with manners, time, and sometimes formal seals that hide access rather than pretend annihilation.

## Cultural Interpretations

Softfruit: stop is care. Echo-readers: craft includes when not to listen. Houses: misfile risk and sour as Observation. Sellers of cost-free reading: social freeze when caught.

## Notes

Lived cost exemplar for I.6 judgment: succeed-at-data can still fail-at-manners.
