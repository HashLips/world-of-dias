# Afterglow Name-Light

## Overview

**Afterglow Name-Light** is a disputed brief glow or warmth said to answer a spoken name of the recently dead—comfort-echo, not proof of afterlife architecture.

## Description

Reports cluster at shared tables, loft wakes, and hearth watches: a soft lift in lamp-color, a chair-back warmth, a one-breath shimmer. Duration is short. Demanding a repeat usually fails—or produces only wishful lighting.

## Conditions

- Recent loss (days to a season, tellings vary)
- Spoken name with consent of kin present
- Quiet enough to notice; not Flagweek noon chaos
- Not reliably reproducible under House instruments
