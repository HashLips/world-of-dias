# Nested Shadow Mismatch

## Name

Nested Shadow Mismatch

## Overview

**Nested Shadow Mismatch** is the reported phenomenon in which a person's cast shadow (or secondary silhouette) takes a shape that does not match their waking body—often read as a frequency imprint nested inside ordinary presence.

## Description

Witnesses describe ordinary lighting behavior with **wrong outline**: animal heads, older postures, doubled limbs, or blank geometric forms. Duration ranges from a breath to a held minute. Some mismatches repeat for the same person; others appear once near echo-thick sites.

## Conditions

More often claimed:

- near thresholds and old Prime Relic roads
- during echo-rise intervals
- when a subject has recently handled a strong Resonant Artifact

## Cultural Interpretations

- **File-and-stabilize** stances treat mismatches as hazard until classified.
- **Witness** stances treat them as honest testimony of layered self.
- Scholars warn against assuming every mismatch is an Echoform birth—most resolve without a lasting entity.
