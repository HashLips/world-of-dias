# Soft-Ray Breath Pulse

## Name

Soft-Ray Breath Pulse

## Overview

**Soft-Ray Breath Pulse** is the slow brightening and dimming along meridian aisles that times Soft-Ray processions. It is the resting-breath rhythm of F200’s social light. A hall without it is brilliance with nowhere to stand beside anyone. A hall with it is a clock you are allowed to feel and not allowed to harvest.

## Description

Ribs of radiance inhale gold-white and exhale toward something visitors call champagne, cold and soft and slightly sweet at the back of the tongue. The cycle is longer than a body wants it to be, which is why visitors’ chests sometimes match without meaning to and then get proud of the matching. Aurel-kind treat that pride as a wobble, not as a crime. Staring to “catch” the pulse, to own the inhale, produces a wrong-tick unease: your outline goes sharp, neighbors complete their row a fraction farther away, a quiet rib that might have been offered stays folded.

When the pulse is acknowledged, coherence gates feel kinder, not because you passed a test, because you stopped trying to be the test. Sound thins. The exhale is where manners live.

## Conditions

Procession hours in the Soft-Ray Procession Hall and along a stable meridian spine. It weakens, in the reports, under dimming folds, when the inner frame of the long peace is afraid. It is not a show you can schedule for a delegation that wants a climax. Climax is the wrong shape.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Aurel-kind:** a community clock, social life made visible as brightness shared.
- **Tourists:** pretty hypnosis, the risk of watching until you think the light is empty.
- **Watchers of the long peace:** a health sign. Pulse steady means the frame has neighbors in it.
- **Hazard:** dimming-leech talk at the edges, and the spectacle of trying to catch a breath that was never game.

## Notes

Serenity with manners. Not a weapon, not a boarding pass, not proof the meridian is vacant.
