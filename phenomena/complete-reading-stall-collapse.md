# Complete-Reading Stall Collapse

## Name

Complete-Reading Stall Collapse

## Overview

**Definition:** When a market stall sells “complete reading” of a person or life as ordinary ware, the claim collapses—socially, and often in the reading itself. Omniscience-as-product is forbidden soft-limit failure, not a valid resonant feat.

## Description

The stall promises total knowledge for a fee. Softfruit and careful Houses treat that promise as a lie about physics. Collapse can look like: seized boards, Partial Echo Slash stamped over the ad, sour crowd, or a reading that delivers noise and pride instead of truth. The buyer may feel briefly “seen”; the cost is misfile, trust debt, and a room that turns harsh.

This is the market face of Resonance behavior soft limits item one: partial echoes only. Reading echoes is craft forbids free total windows.

## Conditions

Appears where tourist crowds and wish-handbills meet Softfruit enforcement. Stronger during ring-fairs. Quiet neighborhoods usually stop it before it becomes a stall.

## Cultural Interpretations

Softfruit: ladle refusal and board scrape. Houses: Theory stamp that completeness is open/false. Restricted and Taboo Tech: cousin to unconsented memory seals. Buyers: teach with chapbook, not only shame.

## Notes

Judgment: **forbidden / taboo** as ordinary practice. Rare mystery exceptions stay mysteries—not product lines.
