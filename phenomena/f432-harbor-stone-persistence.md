# F432 Harbor-Stone Persistence

## Name

F432 Harbor-Stone Persistence

## Overview

**Definition:** In F432, cut stone and pier weight prefer to keep a job across seasons of traffic and argument. Persistence is material-like stability that still resonates—not immortality, and not another band’s specialty matter.

## Description

Harbor crews know a new block from a block that has taken ten years of rope slap and salt. Cracks remember; repairs scrape rather than pretend the first cut never happened. Softfruit kitchen wood is the small cousin; harbor stone is the civic cousin. Visitors from revision-literate talk sometimes call F432 stone “lazy.” Dockhands call it trustworthy.

This is home-band matter under Matter answers its band. It is why roads and harbors can be loved long enough for sagas.

## Conditions

Strongest in continually used piers, gate thresholds, and ring-city foundations. Neglected ruins persist too, but their echo turns sour or quiet rather than helpful.

## Cultural Interpretations

Harbor culture: proof the home band can be loved. Houses: baseline for “material-like” classification. Wrong-map sellers: sometimes paint this persistence onto other bands to sell fake harbors.
