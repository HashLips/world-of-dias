# F840 Scar Continuity Keep

## Name

F840 Scar Continuity Keep

## Overview

**Definition:** In F840 recovery practice, people deliberately keep scar and cold-gap names in memory so care continues. Recollection serves continuity of what still needs work—not shame wallpaper and not fake “all healed” speech.

## Description

Ember Keepers teach: name the quiet; keep error history; do not erase the scar to fake victory. Scar Continuity Keep is why Forced-Final clamps that delete the keep degrade gains in bodies and hearths. Memory here is a tool of recovery geography: if you forget the cold gap, you walk people into it again.

This is not F500 continuity thinning (which loses coherence under null). It is chosen keeping. Softfruit scraped apology is an ethical cousin inside F432.

## Conditions

Baseline in hearthline teaching. Contested by miracle-reset sellers. Strengthens after honest kettle rounds; weakens under Forced-Final speeches.
