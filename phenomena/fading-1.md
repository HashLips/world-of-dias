# Fading

## Name

Fading

## Overview

**Fading** is a named F610 look of quieting: speech thins, a blue mute-courtesy may appear in talk-marks, and orange drip-banners remind keepers that care still falls downward when volume fails.

## Description

Reports cluster at scaffold-like meeting frames and night watches: voices soften, arguments lose heat, and someone hangs or imagines a dripping orange cloth with a down-arrow. Duration varies. Forcing louder speech usually breaks the useful quiet into ordinary noise. When it shows, mark temporary mute courtesy without shame, tend what is dripping, and refuse to sell silence as a weapon. Not a calendar guarantee. Culture- and religion-agnostic.
