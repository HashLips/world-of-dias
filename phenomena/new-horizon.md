# New Horizon

## Name

New Horizon

## Overview

**New Horizon** is an F120 skyline-and-tether event: a pale light-orb holds a crouched figure while a red line rises from blue-water ripples toward them, and a jagged gold city watches under brown sky.

## Description

Walkers along F120 speak of New Horizon when the water-plane throws concentric white rings and a cast line changes color as it climbs—white near the ripple, red where it aims into the orb. The orb is pale yellow and slightly misshapen, large enough to seal a whole crouched silhouette. The figure inside stays bowed, limbs thin, as if the work is listening rather than sightseeing. At the same hour a white leaper may clear the blue with a small splash, and the gold skyline on the far edge shows tall thin towers, one forked at the crown. Mid bands of lavender, pink, and dark red slide without becoming weather reports. People do not treat the event as a free invitation to rush the city. Local care says: stay with the tether's meaning first; the gold edge will still be there when the crouch can stand. The phenomenon is remembered as wonder with manners—resonance between ripple and sealed body—not as a race to plant a flag on the farthest tower.

## Signs

Concentric white rings on blue; a climbing line that turns red; pale-yellow orb with a crouched dark figure; optional white leaper; jagged gold skyline under brown sky.

## Notes

Named for the gold edge and the sealed answer together. Not a tourist sunrise. Pair with the artwork plate in assets.
