# Calareth Stone Refusal

## Name

Calareth Stone Refusal

## Overview

**Calareth Stone Refusal** is the dead weight and wrong angle tools meet when someone tries to rearrange isle stone for convenience—stillness pushing back.

## Description

Crowbars slip; carts refuse to tilt blocks that “should” move. Not magic glue always—sometimes ordinary stubborn masonry—but keepers treat the pattern as law weather.

## Conditions

Alteration attempts; tourist “improvements”; surge rebuild crews ignoring bans. Quiet after leave-stone-alone work.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Isle:** law in the rock.
- **Mainland engineer:** bad geology report.
- **Softfruit visitor:** Forced-Final fails here first.
- **Hazard:** refusal ≠ ignore true collapse risk elsewhere.

## Notes

Stillness atmosphere. Soft limits unchanged.
