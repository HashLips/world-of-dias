# Stars

## Name

Stars

## Overview

**Stars** is an F432 body-flare event: a cobalt kinetic figure outlined in cream moves in an S-curve while about nine cream stalks tip red four-point stars from head, limbs, and torso on charcoal.

## Description

Realm walkers name Stars when red four-points appear on a moving blue body and the charcoal stays empty around the dance. The oversized splayed hand and white almond eye are common tells. Local care forbids harvesting the flares, and forbids forcing a still pose to count them like trophies. Resonance is flare against void; memory is the cream stalks; wonder is kinetic living sky on skin. The phenomenon is named body weather, not a distant map of lights only.

## Signs

Cobalt S-curve figure; cream outline; white almond eye; large splayed hand; cream stalks ending in red four-point stars; charcoal field.
