# Warm Current Convergence Ease

## Name

Warm Current Convergence Ease

## Overview

**Warm Current Convergence Ease** is the body-softening that comes when a holding cove’s amber-rose current steadies. Mood weather invites talk. It does not force confession. Shoulders drop because the climate has agreed to hold a little of what you brought, not because you have been judged innocent.

## Description

Voices round. Reeds lean inward as if listening were a plant’s posture. The haze sweetens without going childish. People who arrived with numbers feel the numbers get less sharp and sometimes panic, thinking they are being robbed of an advantage. They are being offered a room. Fear-narrow weather can stay outside if the guides hold cloth consent; it does not have to be banned to be kept from running the basin. Ease has a smell of warm reed and a sound of small water. It fails in a particular way: brittle cheer, smiles with a hard edge, otters gone from the lip, cloths cooling in fists.

A person who demands happiness on schedule can watch the ease leave in the time it takes to finish the demand. That leaving is the phenomenon’s honesty.

## Conditions

Managed coves on clear-day annotations, after grief-heavy fronts have been given a side channel rather than a gag. It does not appear because a merchant paid for good negotiation air. Payment is how you get a performance of ease, which collapses.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Tidehearts:** a successful hold, infrastructure working.
- **Merchants:** good air for talk, useful and not magical proof.
- **Softfruit guests:** ladle feeling without the fruit, noticing before the stamp.
- **Hazard:** mistaking ease for a truth serum, or faking it until the cove goes brittle.

## Notes

Emotional climate only. Not an F610 reveal and not a lie detector. Consent is what keeps the ease from becoming extraction.
