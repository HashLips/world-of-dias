# Void

## Name

Void

## Overview

**Void** is a Veloria name for a room-condition that settles when a person sets down every role they carry at once. The room goes very still, masks and markers of office slip off their hooks, and birds gather at the window.

## Description

Witnesses describe it as a change in the room's weight rather than its light. Sound flattens. Hanging clothes look emptier than they did a moment ago. Masks, badges, ties, and stamps—anything a person uses to be someone in particular—slide from wherever they were set and come to rest on floors and furniture without breaking. Screens and lamps stay on. Dark birds, usually the ring's ordinary crows, land on the nearest sill and face inward, silent. The condition lasts as long as the person stays roleless, often less than an hour, and ends when they choose one thing to pick up again.

## Local Response

City households treat a void as private weather. Nobody enters to tidy; nobody picks up another person's masks, because handing someone back a role they just dropped is considered a quiet cruelty. Friends wait outside and offer food when the person steps back in, barefoot more often than not. Asking which mask they will wear tomorrow is the classic rudeness.

## Notes

Not a calendar event. Culture- and religion-agnostic.
