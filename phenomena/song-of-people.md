# Song of People

## Name

Song of People

## Overview

**Song of People** is an Ashcalm Rise road event: a bright red featureless walker faces a black dashed road toward purple and yellow mountains while a tilted red octagonal glyph-sign and golden-orange diagonal beams mark the hour.

## Description

Rise walkers name Song of People when the octagon tips and the gold beams cut the gray and the red body still keeps the dashes. White glyphs inside the sign are treated as local rule-marks gone sideways—present, not erased. The dark octagon on the ground is the echo you do not kick away. Local care forbids racing the beams as ownership, and forbids pretending tipped rules mean no care. Resonance is walker and sign both red; memory is cyan wisps over yellow peaks; wonder is how quiet a people's song can look. The phenomenon is road weather of shared travel under unfinished signs.

## Signs

Red away-facing walker; black dashed road; tilted red octagon with white glyphs; gold-orange diagonal beams; purple/yellow mountains; white disc; cyan clouds.
