# Scaffold Plank Refusal

## Name

Scaffold Plank Refusal

## Overview

**Scaffold Plank Refusal** is when living planks lean away from completion ribbons, Forced-Final clamps, or permanent paint—unfinished weather pushing back.

## Description

Boards tip just enough that scissors miss. Pegs stay warm. Walkers feel the mile still drafting underfoot. Cousin to unfinished edge drift, but local and manners-shaped rather than cosmic.

## Conditions

Ribbon ceremonies against persistence pegs; clamp merchants; paint crews. Quiet after honest shore-and-leave work. Can be faked by loose bolts—crews check both.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Open Hand:** becoming defending itself.
- **House:** unsafe structure; condemn and replace.
- **Comedy:** the mile hates speeches.
- **Hazard:** do not romanticize true collapse risk—refusal ≠ ignore rot.
