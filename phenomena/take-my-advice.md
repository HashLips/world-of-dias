# Take My Advice

## Name

Take My Advice

## Overview

**Take My Advice** is a Hearttide room-condition in F380: when counsel is poured at someone who did not ask, the pour itself changes. Tea, water, or broth leaving a spout breaks into hard blue chips in the air and lands anywhere except the waiting cup.

## Description

Witnesses describe it the same way the plate does. The room dims toward green. The pourer feels sure of themselves, tips the kettle further, and the stream stiffens into bright blue fragments that ring faintly when they strike a table, a shoulder, or a bystander's back. The intended cup stays dry. Bystanders who catch the chips report a brief, borrowed certainty that is not theirs and fades within the hour. The condition passes the moment the pourer stops, sets the vessel down, or asks a question instead.

## Local Response

At Warm Current Holding Cove and along the Bloomline channels, hosts sweep the blue chips into a dish by the door rather than throwing them out; they melt back to ordinary water by evening and are poured onto plants, never into cups. The polite phrase when it starts is simply "Ask first," said lightly, without blame. Repeat cases are teased, not punished—the room has already made the point.

## Notes

Not a calendar event. Culture- and religion-agnostic. The blue form in the plate is a bystander shape, not a named being.
