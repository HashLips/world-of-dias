# Shelf-Minute Clarity

## Name

Shelf-Minute Clarity

## Overview

**Shelf-Minute Clarity** is the cool, exact seeing that arrives with a Glassfold window. Under-structure becomes legible. Motives do not stamp themselves honest. The clarity is a gift with a closing note, and the note is part of the phenomenon, not an interruption of it.

## Description

Blind basins show their true sub-layout: braces, channels, corners ordinary glitter had flattered into fog. Reveal-shelf lichen brightens into map-flecks. The chime’s first note tastes like cold water. People hold their breath and think the holding is reverence, when it is only surprise. Soft close returns opacity. For people who packed on time, the afterglow is mild and fades over tea. For people who stared past the second note, opacity nests, headache with opinions, a map that is more confident than the minute was.

The feeling of a good minute is a page turning in a quiet room. The feeling of a stolen minute is eyes drying while you call the theft devotion.

## Conditions

Timed ledge minutes on the window shelves, especially the third. Anti-stare manners kept. Long windows are rare and are not a reward for wanting. They are weather, and the guild does not sell them.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Veil Scribes and Timers:** work weather, the shop being open.
- **Visitors:** miracle temptation, the urge to own a layout.
- **Ask-again clerks:** cousin to other helpful-glass cautions. Asking again is clean. Staring again is not.
- **Hazard:** opacity-serpent rumor after greed, jokes with teeth that should not be promoted into a bestiary.

## Notes

Reveal is not a confession machine. Structure shows. Souls are judged, if at all, by people, later, over tea.
