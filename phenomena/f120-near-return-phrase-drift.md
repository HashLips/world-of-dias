# F120 Near-Return Phrase Drift

## Name

F120 Near-Return Phrase Drift

## Overview

**Definition:** In F120, greetings, report lines, and familiar phrases can return with altered tone, order, or consequence. Memory rhymes; it does not lock into an exact loop.

## Description

A witness says “I’ve heard this” and is only half right. The words feel known; the outcome or timing is offset. Lab drift logs catch the difference. Pride that treats one déjà vu as prophecy misses the law. Softfruit correction is short: rhyme, not rewind.

Phrase Drift is the memory face of near-return. Route Rhyme Landscape is the place/perception cousin. Rhymed Threshold Wear is the matter cousin. None of them are time-travel tickets.

## Conditions

Common in Span talk, lab notebooks, and refrain greetings. Stronger where prior passages left speech habit. Exact identical loops are denied by craft.

## Cultural Interpretations

Houses: Observation from comparison, not from a single déjà vu. Softfruit: rhyme not rewind. Tour sellers of “prophecy tickets”: seized speech. Refrain Walkers: log before claim.
