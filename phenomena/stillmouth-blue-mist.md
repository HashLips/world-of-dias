# Stillmouth Blue Mist

## Name

Stillmouth Blue Mist. Ansel wrote "a blue... like breath in cold." The roads say "the blue shore."

## Overview

Stillmouth Blue Mist is the soft, shadowless blue haze that lies low on the water around Aumere, a few hundred strokes out from the island. It hides the island and defeats sighting. It **is not fog** (it is dry and not cold), **not Vent Haze**, and by the Operators' reading **not bleed weather**. What makes it is unknown.

## Description

- A long low bank on the water, the colour of breath in cold air or of faded sea-glass. It does not move with the wind.
- Inside it the light "goes everywhere at once": no shadows on deck, people or stone, and no sun position.
- It thins "like breath on glass," from the middle outward, on a few clear days a year and during a Long Slack.
- At the withdrawal it **brightens** without warming until everything pales.
- At Low Bell Cove, on the day the water stopped, a thin blue sat on the far horizon at dusk.

## Conditions

Around the Stillmouth year-round. Thinner in rare clear spells. Seen from outside only at dusk or through thinning fog.

## Cultural Interpretations

The Ward calls it bleed. The Operators say "ours, F432, very quiet." The road paints it gold. The Aumeri say the blue is kept.

## Notes

Do not explain it. Do not tie it to any other band.
