# Pot-Signal Dawn Murmur

## Name

Pot-Signal Dawn Murmur

## Overview

**Pot-Signal Dawn Murmur** is the low chord mid-ring arrays make just after mute plugs lift—neighborhood news waking before House bulletins.

## Description

Ledges hum a soft braid of weather jokes, goat whereabouts, and wedding leftover steam. Not words at first; meaning arrives like smell. Shame-pots that tried to run overnight sound thin and get laughed quiet.

## Conditions

Clear mornings after courtesy mute; stronger near Array Court and leaning third-ring streets. Bleed-sky nights can sour the murmur into static. Festival crush may drown it in parade noise.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Street:** the city clearing its throat kindly.
- **Clerk:** load noise; ignore unless clearance-relevant.
- **Softfruit:** proof pots prefer life to sermons.
- **Hazard:** do not treat murmur as Operator orders.

## Notes

Market/civic atmosphere. Pairs with night-mute rule.
