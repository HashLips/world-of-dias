# Gallery Gap-Hold Light

## Name

Gallery Gap-Hold Light

## Overview

**Gallery Gap-Hold Light** is the cool band of brightness that stays in a withheld stroke—beauty living in the gap the painter refused to overwork.

## Description

Forced-Final varnish kills it. One-more-look nights brighten it. Tourists ask who the face is; the light answers “patience.”

## Conditions

Open curation; damp studios; after beauty myths taught. Spoiled by instant masterpiece juice sellers.
