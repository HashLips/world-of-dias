# Road to Gym

## Name

Road to Gym

## Overview

**Road to Gym** is a Brindle March training event: a white lunging figure works black-disc white-bar weights on a light gray diagonal floor under a purple slash and mustard-orange vertical, on dark teal.

## Description

Fruit-road crews and waystation hands name Road to Gym when the discs appear and the purple line cuts the teal like a local rule of effort. The white lunge with one eye-slit is the honest posture; the orange upright is the still measure behind the motion. Local care forbids leaving bars where carts break ankles, and forbids turning the hour into a shout contest. Resonance is lift against slash; memory is the paired black circles; wonder is gray floor holding teal weather. The phenomenon is named practice weather, not an Earth fitness brand.

White lunging profile; black eye-slit; black-disc white-bar weights; light gray diagonal floor; purple diagonal; orange vertical; dark teal field.
