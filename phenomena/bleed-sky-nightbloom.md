# Bleed-Sky Nightbloom

## Name

Bleed-Sky Nightbloom

## Overview

**Bleed-Sky Nightbloom** is a rare blossom that opens only during Bleed-Sky Weather—petals in colors that refuse to match any honest local dye chart.

## Description

Stems are thin and roadside-ordinary by day. When bleed-sky begins, buds crack with a soft sound like wet paper. Petals may read gold-wrong, void-edged, or both in stutter, echoing the sky’s bruise. They close when the weather ends; pressed flowers fade to grey that still makes dyers argue.

Not known as a reliable medicine. Children are told not to make crowns from them. Moths sometimes cluster nearby.

## Conditions

Only during recognized Bleed-Sky nights or short wrong-sky days. Never a scheduled harvest.

## Cultural Interpretations

- Street omen: something in the stack noticed us noticing it.
- Dyers: a color that cannot be sold cleanly.
- Institutions: log if found near instrument spikes; do not plant as policy.

## Notes

Indexed in [`Flora of Dias`](../world/flora-of-dias.md). Keep rare—beauty that unsettles, not a new crop.
