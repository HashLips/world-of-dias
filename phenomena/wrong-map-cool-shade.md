# Wrong-Map Cool Shade

## Name

Wrong-Map Cool Shade

## Overview

**Wrong-Map Cool Shade** is the sudden cool under date-palms when Quiet Well’s kind wrongness is active—paths that look straight bend into shelter weather.

## Description

Skin cools; glare softens; raiders’ “correct” bearings feel sticky. Goldfish-bowl teachers say the shade is how the lesson feels in the body. Peacetime overuse can strand honest travelers—hence confession rules for beacons.

## Conditions

Near Quiet Well when map-wrongness is engaged for shelter; stronger during chase weather. Weak in pure survey seasons when keepers leave maps honest on purpose. Not Bleed cold.

- **Softfruit:** find the goldfish; don’t stamp purity.
- **House:** navigation hazard; correct immediately.
- **Rim:** thank later; confess later.
