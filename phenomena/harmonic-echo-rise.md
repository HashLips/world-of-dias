# Harmonic Echo-Rise

## Overview

**Harmonic echo-rise** is the name archivists give to recent intervals when **harmonic echoes**—emotional residue impressions in stone, metal, water, and crowded air—increase along F432 trade routes, especially near Veloria and old Prime Relic sites.

## Description

Echoes are not full visions. Witnesses report **brief pressure**, ** braided emotion** (grief with triumph, argument with laughter), or **sense of a structure no longer present**. Duration is usually seconds. Repeatability at the same site varies.

Echo-rise does not imply the Fracture is reversing. Scholars propose at least three competing models: **relief settling**, **Bridge fragment alignment**, or **pre-break stress returning**. None is proven.

## Conditions

Reported more often during:

- dawn hours near Veloria's core thrum
- threshold crossings (gates, bridges, reef lines)
- echo-rise seasons marked in [`world/known-eras-and-markers.md`](../world/known-eras-and-markers.md)

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Resonant Houses** prefer classification as hazard until filtered
- **Market cultures** treat mild echoes as omens of good trade days or bad contracts
- **Echo-readers** (rare individuals like Calen Brink) experience echoes **consciously and repeatedly**—a profile institutions do not yet agree how to govern
