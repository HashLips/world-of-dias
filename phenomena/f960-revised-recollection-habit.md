# F960 Revised Recollection Habit

## Name

F960 Revised Recollection Habit

## Overview

**Definition:** In F960, people remember earlier forms as allowed drafts. Changing a held story or atlas leaf is honest when the change is named—not automatically a lie, and not a demand to Force-Final forever.

## Description

Open Hand storykeep says: the atlas leaf that changed is still true if the revision is spoken. Revised Recollection Habit keeps becoming livable. Monument cultures mishear it as lying. Draftkin mishear Forced-Final as care.

When shelter needs “enough for tonight,” manners allow a temporary firm hold without shaming the next morning’s pass. Sketchline Terrain Drift is the place cousin; this habit is the memory cousin.

## Conditions

Common in maker yards and half-made atlases. Strained when guests need a clear temporary hold. Weakens under shame speech imported from monument cultures.

## Cultural Interpretations

Draftkin: workday honesty. F432 disputed-line etiquette: cousin speech without ferry maps. Houses: Theory fights over “enough.” Softfruit: name the change; do not erase-lie.
