# F380 Moodtint Cloth Drape

## Name

F380 Moodtint Cloth Drape

## Overview

**Definition:** In F380, cloth, water-skin, and soft surfaces can take communal mood as slow tint. Substance participates in hearttide climate; it is not a solo dye spell.

## Description

One person’s sour hour may lean a sleeve gray; a crowd’s held joy can warm a drape toward coral without anyone dyeing. Change is usually gradual and distributed. Forcing tint with tools as if it were ferry dye is taboo guess. Tidehearts often notice drape shifts before human eyes admit the weather.

Hearttide Color Shift is the room/place cousin. Moodtint Cloth Drape is the substance cousin under the same coupling rule.

## Conditions

Strongest in communal rooms and estuary edges during shared affect. Private booths tint less unless crowd weather leaks in.
