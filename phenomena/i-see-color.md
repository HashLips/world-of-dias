# I See Color

## Name

I See Color

## Overview

**I See Color** is a Velorian Basin craft phenomenon: a maker’s visible shift into deep focus—body reading as saturated blue in plate-talk—when palette, eye-slit attention, and poised brush agree before the first mark.

## Description

Witnesses describe a crouch that looks uncomfortable on purpose, a white eye-slit that does not flick toward doors, and a palette held tight to the chest as if the color might spill from the ribs. The brush tip stays dark until the stroke begins. Studio grey around the worker goes quiet; shadows lengthen behind the braced legs. The phenomenon is not a spell cast on bystanders. It is a local name for the state when interrupting would smear more than paint. Apprentices learn to recognize it from the doorway and leave water without speaking. Masters sometimes joke that the blue is only metaphor until someone has ruined a morning by asking about lunch mid-crouch.

## Cultural Meaning

Around the Velorian Basin, saying someone is in I See Color is a request for hallway silence and a promise that the first stroke will be honest. Resonance favors the stroke that follows a true crouch. Faking the pose for drama empties the name.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Plate-led: blue crouch, brush, palette, grey room.
