# F500 Contrast-Hungry Ash

## Name

F500 Contrast-Hungry Ash

## Overview

**Definition:** Near F500, substance behaves so that ordinary contrast and landmarks will not hold. Marks do not rest the way F432 soot or paint will. Call it ash, hush-texture, or anti-pigment—the behavior is what matters.

## Description

Survivor talk disagrees on the substance name. Agreement clusters on behavior: highlights feel stolen; edges offer no kindness; painting a name is not cartography—it feeds hunger. Approach increases the sense that substance and identity thin together.

This is not F840 ashwarm workable rest (recovery matter that still holds a kettle). Bleed soot over F432 is not this ash filed as a souvenir.

## Conditions
