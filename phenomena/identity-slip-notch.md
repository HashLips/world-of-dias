# Identity Slip Notch

## Name

Identity Slip Notch

## Overview

**Identity Slip Notch** is rumored approach-weather at F500, a few minutes in which names and self-stories lose their grip. Negation proximity, felt as personal weather. Tools keep working. Narratives do not. The notch is not a solved haunt and not a tour stop, and it does not cash out as an explanation of Frequency Zero.

## Description

Survivors, partial by definition, describe reaching for a name and finding a smooth place, like a word that was in the mouth and then was only the shape of having meant it. Grit will not take a color. A face in memory loses its order of features and keeps its dread. The missing time afterward is not dramatic. There is no soundtrack. There is a cupboard you do not want to open, and a sentence that starts “I saw” and ends in an empty shelf. Watching Absence talk clusters after, people eager to put a silhouette on the smooth place. The courtesy is to refuse the silhouette a permanent name.

It feels, in the least cruel tellings, like a kindness you did not ask for: relief from being someone, for a minute, bought at a price you notice only when the minute is gone.

## Conditions

Null-edge approaches. Prolonged staring at forget-texture ground. Stories also blame taboo devices that “listen too well,” which teachers repeat as a reason tags exist, not as a manual for building the device. Sport approaches make it worse in the social sense even when the physics stays rumor: a crew that went for a view comes back as a cautionary tale other crews want to repeat.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Taboo-tech teachers:** why warning tags exist, and why tours do not.
- **Dock rumor:** an Unraveled brush. Kept unconfirmed. Not a field mark.
- **Seekers of Zero:** a hunger cue, which is the danger. Hunger is not a compass.
- **Softfruit caution:** a story to feed first and title never.
- **Hazard:** sport tourism, and any telling that promotes Horizon-Nulls or Distant Companies from a notch in a name.

## Notes
