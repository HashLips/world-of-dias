# Red Sky

## Name

Red Sky

## Overview

**Red Sky** is an F432 window weather: a cyan room's black-framed four-pane fills with solid coral-red while a white crouched figure watches, a vertical blue stripe down the back, teal floor cut by a green diagonal.

## Description

Realm dwellers speak of Red Sky when the outside pressed to glass is only coral—no familiar blue, no cloud story—and the honest response is to sit white and still with one eye-slit open. The blue stripe on the back is treated as a secondary weather-mark on the body, not as a costume. Green under teal marks the floor's own small difference. Local care forbids panic-breaking the panes, and forbids claiming the red as owned paint. Resonance is coral against cyan; memory is the four-pane grid; wonder is how quiet a crouch can stay. The phenomenon is a named hour, not a permanent climate sentence.

## Signs

Cyan interior; black-framed four-pane filled coral-red; white profile crouch with dark eye-slit; vertical blue back stripe; dark teal floor with grass-green diagonal.
