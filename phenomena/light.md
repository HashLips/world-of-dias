# Light

## Name

Light

## Overview

**Light** is an F200 tended-light condition: a six-petaled primary-blue bloom with bright orange center placed on a pale yellow L-stand so its glow (literal or resonant) leads a room while an olive tablet with a white band stays secondary.

## Description

Onset is the moment the stand is set and the center’s white crescent still shows. Rooms on terracotta floors keep spare blooms cool. When petals droop or the orange dulls, stewards swap the flower rather than crank the tablet. Children learn Light as care, not as a switch. Adults who hide the bloom behind the olive tablet for 'modern' glare are asked to reset the L-stand.

## Cultural Meaning

In F200, Light is bloom-first illumination etiquette. Resonance favors clean orange centers. Mocking the flower as childish empties the phenomenon’s local care.

## Notes on occurrence

Indoor frequency rooms, festival tables, and teaching floors with grey upper fields.
