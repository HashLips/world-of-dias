# Visitor Wrong-Tick Unease

## Name

Visitor Wrong-Tick Unease

## Overview

**Visitor Wrong-Tick Unease** is basin talk for the post-escape feeling that Veloria’s instruments *almost* understand you—then a digit sits wrong. It is how most outsiders meet the Caliburn’s aftermath without ever seeing glass take a person-shape.

## Description

Reported near third-ring slits, core-approach queues (Visitor Queue Ledge), and clerk desks: a dial settles warmly, a visitor relaxes, then the tick jumps half a measure. Softfruit jokes thin. Gate clerks like **Jori Sweepgate** mark more “ask again” stamps without a House brief explaining why. Guides like **Dott Gildhour** invent softer language: *the dial loves you—check twice*.

This is helpful glass agreement moved into public clearance weather. It does not prove realm invasion. It does prove the vault did not end the story.

## Conditions

Stronger after the Caliburn’s escape into city noise; denied in official Operator language; collected as traveler superstition. Louder when post-escape static drift is reported the same week.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Mercy:** the city is tired; clocks misbehave.
- **Hazard:** something is still measuring back.
- **Comic:** you forgot how basin clocks work; buy peel-sweet herb and calm down.
- **Saga aftermath:** *Ask Again at the Ledge* is the procedural face of this unease.

## Notes

Haunt ecology, not proof of Frequency Zero. Keep distinct from Bleed Sky weather and Harmonic Thrum.
