# F200 Alignment Fatigue in Visitors

## Name

F200 Alignment Fatigue in Visitors

## Overview

**Definition:** Living bodies that approach F200 without enough coherence alignment often take bodily and attentional fatigue. Life answers luminosity as pressure, not as postcard calm.

## Description

Accounts speak of thinning, confusion, or a calm so demanding it empties the traveler’s usual fidget. Fatigue is not moral failure; it is band habit meeting an unready body. Successful alignment reports less fatigue and more lucid quiet—still not a sold upgrade. Aurel-kind life remains partial intelligence from F432’s side.

Understanding fatigue does not open the coherence gate. Softfruit uses the tell as omen teaching against spa-weekend posters.

## Conditions

Reported on approach and failed holds. Rare successful alignments describe less fatigue. Edges of alignment pressure fields may feel like soft refusal before full fatigue.

## Cultural Interpretations

Softfruit: omen teaching. Cross-Frequency Travel: exceptional, not commute. Taboo: “spa weekend in lumen.” Houses: Observation for approach notes; no ferry from Theory talk.
