# Skies

## Name

Skies

## Overview

**Skies** is an F432 strata event: roughly a dozen wavy horizontal blue bands grade from near-black to icy pale while a pair of flat saturated red bent legs sits inside them with a semi-transparent grey reflection cast aside.

## Description

Realm walkers name Skies when the bands stack soft but distinct and something hot red belongs in the cool without becoming a full portrait. The grey echo left of the legs is the honesty of presence through medium. Local care forbids flattening the bands into one blue for convenience, and forbids pretending the red legs are a drowning joke. Resonance is hot measure in cool strata; memory is the pointed foot; wonder is how many blues can share one hour. The phenomenon is layered weather, not a single ceiling color.

## Signs

Stacked wavy blue bands dark-to-pale; flat red bent legs; grey translucent reflection; no torso required.
