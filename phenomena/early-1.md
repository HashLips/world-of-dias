# Early

## Name

Early

## Overview

**Early** is a named F380 look of opening attention: a coral-iris watchfulness that arrives before a day’s story hardens, marked in plates by a large eye on gold with jagged black night-edges and a blue permission ribbon.

## Description

Travelers report a sudden clarity in warm-toned hours—surfaces readable, gossip quieter, the urge to declare meaning still resistible. Duration may be a breath or a short watch. Instruments sometimes miss it; manners catch the hush. When it shows, take the look, leave the speech for later, and do not sell the eye as an omen stamp. Not a calendar guarantee.

## Notes

F380 first-look weather. Look early; speak late.
