# F120 Refrain Body Timing

## Name

F120 Refrain Body Timing

## Overview

**Definition:** Refrain Walkers and long Span users carry muscle memory and gait timing that rhyme with prior passages without locking into identical loops. Bodies near-return; they do not rewind.

## Description

Bodies arrive early, late, or sideways to a remembered beat. Confidence grows from comparison logs, not from “I’ve walked this before.” Treating body timing as a rewind ferry breaks soft limits and usually breaks ankles in ordinary ways too.

Phrase Drift is the memory cousin. Route Rhyme Landscape is the place cousin. Refrain Body Timing is the living gait under the same near-return law.

## Conditions

Strongest on Returning Span and repeatedly walked lab routes.

## Cultural Interpretations

Lab drift customs: Observation craft. Softfruit: rhyme not rewind. Tour sellers: banned for “train your legs to yesterday.”
