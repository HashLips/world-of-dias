# Post-Escape Static Drift

## Name

Post-Escape Static Drift

## Overview

**Post-Escape Static Drift** names the soft load-fan whine and clerk-desk static that seems to **wander** after the Caliburn left the Spectrum Frame. It is city-noise haunt: not a second Frame, not a named army—just ignored frequencies behaving as if a cursor is looking for a new snap.

## Description

Choirglass tuners report a metronome that is not on the schedule. Anonymous scrap couriers (Kesh’s routes) hear footsteps answered one beat late near the Drop Wall. Drift clusters at conduit glass, then moves toward visitor ledges and Seam Cafe pipes.

Operators prefer “calibration weather.” Row hands say “the Frame’s ghost.” The honest middle: the Echoform escaped into the same load-noise it nested in; the city’s filing of wonder-as-noise gave it room to travel.

## Conditions

Unscheduled; denser on bleed-adjacent nights; thinner when Softfruit crowds are loud. Often co-occurs with visitor wrong-tick unease and late mutter echoes. Absent from sealed House shelves that never hummed—drift likes working rooms.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Operators:** file, monitor, do not escalate language.
- **Row:** proof the vault failed in more than metal.
- **Null stringers:** a dare with better branding.
- **Publish teachers:** reason to keep no-ferry marks on spectrum papers.

## Notes

Keep perforated with Quiet Unbinding coincidence—do not promote to organized faction. Haunt ecology for later hooks.
