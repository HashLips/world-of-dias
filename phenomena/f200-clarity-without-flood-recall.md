# F200 Clarity-Without-Flood Recall

## Name

F200 Clarity-Without-Flood Recall

## Overview

**Definition:** Under F200 coherence, recollection can feel clearer and less cluttered without becoming total archive access. Calm can sharpen what you already hold; it does not grant omniscient memory.

## Description

Accounts from aligned approach speak of remembering a task, route, or name with less noise—as if the field removes fidget from recall. That clarity is limited: it does not dump every past hour into the mind, and unready visitors more often meet alignment fatigue than perfect remembering. Soft limits still forbid treating luminosity as a total-recall appliance.

Softfruit teaching uses this contrast carefully: F432 table warmth is sticky remainder; F200 clarity is quieter sorting. Confusing the two produces either pilgrimage fantasies or House Theory stamped too early.

## Conditions

Reported in stable coherence holds by visitors who align enough to stay. Attenuated approaches may get no clarity—only tiredness. Does not replace personal craft of keeping or refusing.

## Cultural Interpretations

Aurel Meridian craft: clarity as alignment side-effect, not a sold archive. Softfruit: do not pack for total recall. Houses: Observation for partial accounts; taboo for “lumen memory vault” devices.

## Notes

Memory-domain exemplar for F200. Companion to Coherence Eye Calm (perception), not a duplicate of it.
