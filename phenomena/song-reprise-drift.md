# Song Reprise Drift

## Name

Song Reprise Drift

## Overview

The tendency of well-used songs to return slightly altered—timing, emphasis, or feeling—because prior singings left remainder in throats, rooms, and custom.

## Description

Not an F120 loop. F432 musicians know a tavern chorus that “remembers last winter’s verse” and leans toward it. Drift is echo plus memory culture, a gentle proof that art participates in resonance.

## Conditions

Strongest for songs tied to places and seasons. Weak in one-off performances with no return audience.

## Cultural Interpretations

Entertainers: living repertoire. Echo-readers: safe practice ear. Panic talk: sometimes confuses this with other-band leakage.

## Notes

Exemplar of ordinary echo under the foundational principle—wonder without omnipotence.
