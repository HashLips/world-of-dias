# Soup-Tag Bowl Click

## Name

Soup-Tag Bowl Click

## Overview

**Soup-Tag Bowl Click** is the small resonant click when a honest soup tag meets a second-bowl rim—reciprocity acknowledged before the ladle returns.

## Description

First hunger may eat without click; lifestyle freeloading never earns it. Wishful eternal-soup handbills produce a dull thud instead.

## Conditions

Waystation shared pots after labor/peel/mend credit; Softfruit auditor days. Weak during emergency first-bowl only feeds (by design).
