# Glowfly Unsigned Gather

## Name

Glowfly Unsigned Gather

## Overview

**Glowfly Unsigned Gather** is when tiny yellow-green ticks cluster at a listening pillar because unsigned help is near—care without a signature made visible.

## Description

Flies do not speak House. They brighten, then leave. Graphing them into Distant Companies lists dulls the pillar. Softfruit night jokes still work; panic leaflets do not.

## Conditions

Scrap listening nooks after anonymous tokens; quieter Row edges. Stronger when someone almost filed living scrap as noise. Stare-length watching ends the gather.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Listeners:** unsigned care arriving.
- **Softfruit:** leave it scrap on purpose.
- **House:** pest anomaly; fumigate.
- **Hazard:** taxonomy panic kills the signal.
