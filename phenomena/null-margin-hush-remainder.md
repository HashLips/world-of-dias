# Null-Margin Hush Remainder

## Name

Null-Margin Hush Remainder

## Overview

Even at null margins, encounters leave a hush-remainder: not friendly warmth, but a stubborn quiet that remembers you approached—and that you should be careful about approaching again.

## Description

Forget-Texture edges do not host Softfruit laughter residue. They host absence-pressure that still obeys the double law in a frightening key: participation leaves remainder, including the remainder of nearly not-being-answered. Sailors and scholars argue whether this proves Zero adjacency; soft limits forbid treating the argument as solved.

## Conditions

Reported near Forget-Texture Margin and cairn watches after close approaches. Does not make F500 a tourist island.

## Cultural Interpretations

Caution cultures: respect hush. Null-curious factions: temptation. Softfruit: send no bowls; send people home.

## Notes

Keeps “everything leaves an echo” true without making null cozy.
