# Gate Argument Stain

## Name

Gate Argument Stain

## Overview

The sour aftertone that settles in a gate booth or threshold after bitter farewells, false papers, or cruelty spoken as procedure.

## Description

Officers describe a booth that “sulks”: sound dulls, tempers shorten, and the next traveler catches blame that belongs to the last argument. The stain is not visible dirt. It is remainder—resonance with nowhere polite to go.

Long Gate keeps Nest Phrase Strips and vinegar cloth for this reason. Softfruit sends fruit to booths the way people send water to fires.

## Conditions

Builds after stacked confrontations, exile-road mornings, and days when procedure was used as a weapon. Eases after honest breaks, shared food, and scraped incident notes that admit tone as well as fact.

## Cultural Interpretations

Gate culture: occupational hazard. Travelers: “the booth was against me.” Softfruit: argument-stain is why ladle comes before stamp.

## Notes

Exemplar of ordinary echo under the foundational principle—wonder without omnipotence.
