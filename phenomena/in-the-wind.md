# In the Wind

## Name

In the Wind

## Overview

**In the Wind** is a Claimscar Yard weather tell: when a person’s black hair-stream (or scarf, smoke, ash-flake line) crosses hard from the still peach side of a yard into the grey carry-side, speech and scent will travel farther than courtesy expects.

## Description

The plate shows the tell as a cobalt face with yellow-pupil focus and poppy-red mouth already set careful, hair points sharp in the grey. Yard workers watch laundry lines, cookfire smoke, and hair alike. Onset can be sudden after a secondfire flare or a gate opening. Children learn to point at the stream instead of yelling. Adults who ignore the tell and bargain loudly across the divide find their prices repeated at the next cookfire without consent. The phenomenon ends when the stream falls or the speaker steps fully into shelter.

## Cultural Meaning

Naming In the Wind is yard care: protect what should stay near. Resonance favors short words on stream days. Mocking someone’s windblown hair while ignoring the tell is considered backwards manners.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Plate-led face and hair-stream.
