# Concord

## Name

Concord

## Overview

**Concord** is a named Aelwyn Canopy look of accord: a single sharp line of agreement that seems to bind bird-sign, person-shape, and horned neighbor at once—and always leaves aftercare work on the ground.

## Description

Witnesses argue whether they saw a real white shaft of light, a thrown mark, or only the feeling of three quarrels snapping into one. Plates keep the bird-origin, the red arched body, and the fallen black horned form so the cost stays visible. Duration is short. Demanding a repeat usually fails. When it shows, stop the fight, tend whatever spilled, and refuse to brand the moment as destiny. Not a calendar guarantee. Not an excuse to skip thank-yous afterward.
