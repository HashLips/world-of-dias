# Oilvine Soft Bite

## Name

Oilvine Soft Bite

## Overview

**Oilvine Soft Bite** is when a tool’s next edge softens after an honest oil-dark apology—not guaranteed forgiveness, but a milder bite than pride deserved.

Latch yields a hair; chalk hinge stops screaming; creeper stays pliant. Vault-night tellings remind: soft bite can still fail a wrong cage. Performance apologies at noon do nothing.
