# Careful Partial Reading Hold

## Name

Careful Partial Reading Hold

## Overview

**Definition:** A successful ordinary resonant feat: a trained listener takes a partial echo reading, stops before sour, files the partial as partial, and accepts cost as load rather than as omnipotence.

## Description

Softfruit and careful House juniors practice this hold with First-Listen stones and table aftertone. The reader names what they sensed, stamps or speaks Partial, refuses Forced-Final language, and leaves the room still usable. The feat succeeds because it matches the law: everything leaves an echo; echoes are partial; craft includes stopping.

Contrast Complete-Reading Stall Collapse (forbidden claim) and Over-Listened Room Sour (cost of refusing to stop).

## Conditions

Works where manners and training hold. Fails when pride or payment demands completeness. Stronger after Softfruit orientation and Feat Sorting drills.

## Cultural Interpretations

Softfruit: succeed bowl in the chapbook. Houses: Observation and Partial Echo Slash. Echo interpretation stances: argue what to do after—not whether partiality is real.

## Notes

Primary **succeed** exemplar for I.6 judgment tables.
