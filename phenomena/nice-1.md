# Nice

## Name

Nice

## Overview

**Nice** is a Lumira Sands resting event: a dark figure goes limp on a cobalt daybed while a cyan bird calls from the armrest and a thin yellow beam crosses the body under jagged blue wall marks.

## Description

In the sands, Nice arrives when the room's upper field goes orange-hot and the floor stays lavender, and someone is allowed to sprawl without being accused of quitting the road. The daybed is cobalt with rounded arms. The resting silhouette keeps one white almond eye open—proof that the limp is chosen care, not collapse into forgetting. A cyan bird with dark blue wings throws its beak open on the armrest; locals treat that call as the weather of the hour, not as a scold. Four jagged blue shapes on the wall—five downward teeth each—are the geometric reminder that rest still sits inside a rule. A canary-yellow diagonal may cut the torso like a soft measure of resonance. Purple shadow under the couch and a separate blue floor-shadow near the bird complete the tell. People do not drag the resting body upright for spectacle. Local care says: let Nice finish; answer the bird later with a cleaner step than the one that earned the limp. Wonder is how loud the small bird can be in a quiet room. Memory is the eye that stays open.

## Signs

Cobalt daybed; limp dark silhouette with one white eye; cyan open-beaked bird; four jagged blue wall marks; yellow diagonal beam; orange upper field over lavender floor.
