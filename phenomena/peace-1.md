# Peace

## Name

Peace

## Overview

**Peace** is a Sorel street event: a vivid blue leafless tree stands center on a pale path between receding red block buildings, and its shadow spreads jagged and root-like across the cool ground.

## Description

Sorel walkers speak of Peace when the red buildings look their most rigid—tall blocks, dark window cuts, ranks shrinking to white distance—and the blue tree refuses leaves yet still owns the middle of the pale way. The important tell is the shadow: not a polite gray twin of the crown, but a black network like roots or cracks claiming the foreground. Faint pink-red building shadows may stretch the other way. People slow carts. Children are told not to chip the blue bark for souvenirs. Local care treats the hour as held stillness, not as empty quiet you can fill with shouting. Resonance is blue against red; memory is how the root-shadow looks larger than the branches; wonder is the almost-white ground that makes every color count. The phenomenon is not a permanent climate guarantee. It is a named street weather you recognize and respect while it stands.
