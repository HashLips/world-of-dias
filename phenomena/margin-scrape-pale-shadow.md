# Margin-Scrape Pale Shadow

## Name

Margin-Scrape Pale Shadow

## Overview

**Margin-Scrape Pale Shadow** is the readable ghost under new ink after a proper scrape—error history glowing pale so the next hand cannot pretend certainty was born finished.

## Description

Fiber remembers. Helpful-glass corrections leave stickier shadows. Erase habits leave nothing—and that nothing is the real hazard. Softfruit teaches kids to love the pale.

## Conditions

Inkspan desks, Row ledgers, partial publish packets with ribbons. Absent on margin-eaten stamp grins (no room left to scrape).

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Row:** honesty you can see.
- **House pretty-page:** ugly; ban scrapes.
- **Softfruit:** scars over silence.
- **Hazard:** shadow ≠ full truth restored.
