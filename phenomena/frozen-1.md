# Frozen

## Name

Frozen

## Overview

**Frozen** is a named Elder Shelf look of cold-order: orange stem-leaf fruit-forms appear held on light-blue ice-cubes in countable grids, pausing ripeness until thaw is planned.

## Description

Shelf keepers report sudden neatness in stores and ledges—fruit readable in rows, cubes glittering white-jagged, blue air feeling slower. Duration is until someone names a thaw. Instruments measure cold; manners measure whether the pause still serves. When it shows, count the grid, refuse endless freeze as virtue, and share on thaw. Not a calendar guarantee.
