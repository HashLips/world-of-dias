# Wabet Softfall

## Name

Wabet Softfall

## Overview

**Wabet Softfall** is a fine, quiet rain common to canopy and verdant reaches of Wabet—soft enough to sound like continuous leaf-speech, clean enough that travelers call it washing weather.

## Description

Droplets are small; mist hangs between trunks; colors deepen without turning the day grim. Animals keep grazing. Paths shine. The air smells like wet bark and distant fruit. Softfall rarely drives people indoors for long; it invites slower walking and fewer raised voices.

## Conditions

Most frequent in warm seasons after heat builds under canopy. Less common in Lumira’s open dunes (where Listening Heat rules the lesson instead).

## Cultural Interpretations

- Walkers treat Softfall as the land’s mild yes.
- Traders from Nauw sometimes misread it as “always raining in Wabet”; locals correct them: it is raining *kindly*.
- Songripe cousins say Softfall and tide-song argue politely about who keeps time.

## Notes

Signature Wabet climate beauty—use freely in scenes that should feel safe to breathe.
