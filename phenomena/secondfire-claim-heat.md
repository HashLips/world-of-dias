# Secondfire Claim Heat

## Name

Secondfire Claim Heat

## Overview

**Secondfire Claim Heat** is the sharper social temperature of Claimscar evenings, when stew kindness cools and peg arguments warm. It is rivalry with a fire in it. It is not the default weather of a war, and tellings that promote it into realm-war are doing the yard a disservice. Heat is a tool the ring uses to renegotiate. It can spoil. The spoiling is a choice, not a climate destiny.

## Description

Sparks higher than breakfast’s modest crack. Dogs closer to the ash line, professional about scraps and about tension. Paint pots appear, glowing with the warmth of claims that hoped to dry unseen. News travels meaner and more useful than it did over steam. Double-claim fear tastes like peppersage smoke, green and metallic. Voices rise and the ring notices, and the noticing is already a vote. A flipped peg in this heat is a reveal. Laughter can still win, mean and affectionate, which is Claimscar’s preferred disarmament. Knives remain possible and, on the nights the phenomenon is merely itself, unused.

## Conditions

After the Breakfast Circle has done its work or been skipped, and skipping makes the heat worse. Vent and sprout windows. Freeband rivalry weeks, when marks multiply and breakfast is the only reason the marks still share a kettle. Visitors from beach-calm Driftfall hours are the ones most likely to misread the heat as a battle starting.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Garen Scar crews:** weather for renegotiation, not for a ballad.
- **Visitors:** proof Driftfall is not only hanging-about and flags.
- **Hungry Dogs:** professional interest. They do not pick your side.
- **Hazard:** letting the heat become the only story, until craft and rivalry are flattened into war-as-weather.

## Notes

Frontier phenomenon. Keep breakfast and secondfire as a pair. Neither cancels the vent-claim rule.
