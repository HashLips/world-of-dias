# The Low Bell Answer

## Name

The Low Bell Answer. The letter-keepers say only "the answer."

## Overview

The Low Bell Answer is the signal heard from the sea at Low Bell Cove at dusk on the day of the Long Slack predicted in chapter 29. Maren Kestry knocked two stones together and whistled her cracked rise. Out of a thin blue on the horizon came **two knocks** (stone or knuckle on wood, not metal) and **a clean rising whistle**. Maren, Odile Brask and Wick Hollen heard it. No boat came and no one came ashore. Status **unknown**: it is not established who or what made it.

## Description

- **Conditions:** flat sea since morning; the wreck bell silent; a faint blue bank far out at dusk.
- **Signal:** *tok, tok*, hard, short and dry; then a whistle rising clean all the way up without a break. Maren's whistle always breaks; Ansel's never did.
- **After:** the blue thins and goes. After dark the first wave rolls the wreck bell's clapper.

## Conditions

Observed once. Whether it can recur, or depends on a slack, is unknown.

## Cultural Interpretations

Maren recorded it under MEASURED and left GUESSED blank. Wick: "two knocks and a whistle from the sea at dusk on the day the water stopped... nothing else." Possible readings, none confirmed: Ansel; an islander who learned his signal; an echo off still water; a bird; something else.

## Notes

Do not resolve it. It is not proof of life, of the island's return, or of any crossing. The letter-keepers keep it inside their circle.
