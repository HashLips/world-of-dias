# Road Ahead

## Name

Road Ahead

## Overview

**Road Ahead** is a Brindle March path event: an electric-blue tree grows from a white dashed-center road toward a yellow semi-circular horizon, one long branch hanging a large yellow black-tipped fruit over navy hills and dark green field.

## Description

Fruit-road walkers name Road Ahead when the pavement itself sprouts the blue trunk and the crown goes antler-thin while a yellow fruit hangs in easy reach of the eye. The yellow horizon disc sits where the road points. Salmon shapes and floating dark squares may attend as side weather. Local care forbids felling the blue tree for a straighter cart line, and forbids yanking the hung fruit without thanks talk. Resonance is path and growth locked; memory is the dashed center under grey shadow; wonder is yellow fruit toward yellow light. The phenomenon is a named hour of the way forward, not a promise of easy clearing.

## Signs

White road with black dashes; electric-blue tree from mid-path; yellow hung fruit; yellow horizon semi-circle; navy hill silhouettes; dark green field.
