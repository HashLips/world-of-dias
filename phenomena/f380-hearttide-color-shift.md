# F380 Hearttide Color Shift

## Name

F380 Hearttide Color Shift

## Overview

Gradual shifts in color fields and spatial feel in F380 as communal affect moves—environment answering hearttide.

## Description

On Bloomline shoulders, a morning that begins in pale estuary green can warm toward coral if a crowd holds shared lift through midday, or mute toward slate if grief-weather settles without anyone “casting” it. Rooms feel wider when joy-climate holds; paths feel narrower when anger thickens the air. Tidehearts notice the fringe and the water-skin before human argument admits the weather. Stable change needs communal process—one shout may lean a sleeve, not recolor a bank.

This is environments as frequency weather, not Softfruit franchise paint. Band Confusion Corrections answers the rainbow-cry joke: hearttide couples mood to climate; it is not a postcard of tears.

## Conditions

Strongest in estuary crowds and shared rooms. Slower in carefully held hush. Forcing tint with tools as if it were a ferry dye is taboo guess and usually fails ugly.

## Cultural Interpretations

Hearttide attendants: morning read before dispute. Softfruit interviews: measure “how a place felt” without claiming conversion into F380. Houses: Theory fights over models; Observation stamps for logged shifts.
