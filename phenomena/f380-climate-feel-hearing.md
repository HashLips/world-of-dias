# F380 Climate-Feel Hearing

## Name

F380 Climate-Feel Hearing

## Overview

**Definition:** In F380, hearing and ambient sense track communal affect-climate. Sound texture can mute, brighten, or thicken as shared feeling moves—perception participating in hearttide weather.

## Description

On Bloomline and in shared rooms, a crowd’s lift can make air-sound feel more open; grief-weather can mute edges; anger can thicken noise without anyone playing an instrument. Tidehearts often notice before human argument admits the weather. A solo listener who blames only themselves for a climate-scale tone misfiles the band: private mood and communal climate-hearing are related but not identical.

This is perception under emotional climate coupling—not a joke about rainbow tears, and not mind-control theater.

## Conditions

Strongest in shared estuary and crowd-linked spaces. Quieter in carefully held hush rooms. Forced tools used as “climate ears” are taboo guess.
