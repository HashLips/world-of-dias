# Festival Cloth Afterglow

## Name

Festival Cloth Afterglow

## Overview

Color and mood that linger in festival cloth, flagweek strips, and stored banners after public joy—sometimes bright, sometimes tired.

## Description

Cloth aired in shade keeps a soft glow in memory even when dye is unchanged. Cloth stuffed away damp keeps a muddled aftertaste that next year’s festival must wash out with new honesty, not only soap.

## Conditions

Follows Flagweeks, Hearth Laughter, and ring fairs. Spoils after festivals used as cover for cruelty or Forced-Final attendance.

## Cultural Interpretations

Civic culture: calendars have echoes. Houses: file only if cloth sits near sealed work. Children: proof joy leaves remainder.

## Notes

Exemplar of ordinary echo under the foundational principle—wonder without omnipotence.
