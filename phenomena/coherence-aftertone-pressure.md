# Coherence Aftertone Pressure

## Name

Coherence Aftertone Pressure

## Overview

In F200, strong coherence events leave a bright pressure-aftertone—rooms and halls that keep “holding alignment” after the procession or stair rite has ended.

## Description

Witnesses speak of air that still wants agreement, edges that still gleam one thought too long. Useful for craft; hazardous for people who need privacy of mood. Soft limits still apply: aftertone is not omniscience and not a ticket into F432 cities.

## Conditions

Follows Soft-Ray processions and Lumen Stair completions. Fades as realm opacity returns; forced staring can sour brightness into headache.

## Cultural Interpretations

F200 practitioners: expected cost of coherence work. F432 rumor: second-dawn jokes. Houses: classify as other-band residue when noticed through bleed, not as local district weather.

## Notes

Exemplar that echo law holds across frequencies with different flavors.
