# Table Laughter Residue

## Name

Table Laughter Residue

## Overview

The pleasant aftertone left in wood, cloth, and air when a table has held real shared laughter—not performed cheer.

## Description

Witnesses describe a faint peel-sweet warmth in the grain, a readiness in seats, and a room that “leans toward talk” for hours after. Forced cheer leaves a brittle shine that Softfruit can feel with a hand flat on the board: right color, wrong give. Children notice before adults admit it.

The residue does not speak sentences. It is mood-holding, the kitchen proof that everything leaves an echo.

## Conditions

More likely after honest meals, second helpings, and jokes that include the quietest person at the table. Less likely after rushed stamped meetings that only borrowed Softfruit chairs. Vinegar cloth and a true apology can soften sour; they cannot fake warmth.

## Cultural Interpretations

Softfruit: proof of good hosting. Houses: nonactionable social noise unless it interferes with sealed work. Road guests: homesickness that tastes like fruit.

## Notes

Exemplar of ordinary echo under the foundational principle—wonder without omnipotence.
