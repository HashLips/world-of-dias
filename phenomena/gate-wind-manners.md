# Gate Wind Manners

## Name

Gate Wind Manners

## Overview

**Gate Wind Manners** is the bodily etiquette the Long Gate wind teaches. You stand as if two lands’ scribes were watching, even when the niche holds only you, a rail, and a coat that will not lounge. The wind is not a judge. It is a posture teacher. Dignity stays. Performance of despair does not.

## Description

Coats flatten. Jokes shorten until they are either true or gone. Assent cords get tied with care because a sloppy knot reads as a speech. Softfruit lounge posture feels wrong here the way a shout feels wrong in a kitchen where someone is grieving. In one gust you can smell both weathers: south, the dust of rebuild; north, shame and relief braided. Exiles feel noticed by the Gate itself. Merchants feel tolled, which is a misreading the Watchers do not bother to fine, because the wind is already doing the correction. Myth tellers say the Argument is still speaking in the stone. Skeptics say it is a pocket of air. The manners work either way.

The hazard is hearing “be quieter” as “be smaller.” The niche’s quiet is volume, not worth. A person who confuses the two will try to surrender their whole account to the wind and will be left with a knot that means nothing because they did not say the true thing softly. They said nothing, loudly, in their own head.

## Conditions

Span days with real wind. Exile hours. Concord escort presence, which thickens the sense of being watched without adding a speech. Calm days still have the posture, learned, but the phenomenon proper is when the wind does the teaching for free.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Exiles:** the Gate noticing them without demanding a scene.
- **Merchants:** toll weather, a reading the niche refuses to invoice.
- **Myth tellers:** the Argument blinking, still speaking at a lower volume.
- **Hazard:** mistaking manners for surrender of dignity, or turning the niche into an inn that sells the feeling.

## Notes

Moral infrastructure weather. Mercy without erased cost.
