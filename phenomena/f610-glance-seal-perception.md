# F610 Glance-Seal Perception

## Name

F610 Glance-Seal Perception

## Overview

Perceptual craft of glancing inside a reveal window and sealing attention when the interval ends—seeing without staring into nest hunger.

## Description

Veil Scribes teach stop as part of sight. Inside a brief open along Glassfold, grain and seam may become readable; the craft is to take what the interval offers, phase-stamp it, and seal the eyes when the weather reseals. Glance-Seal Perception keeps truth usable. Endless open-eye fantasy turns into stare-cost strain—headache, false certainty, nest invitation—and soft-limit failure.

Juniors practice on Window-Glasswort beads: sketch while the light articulates, then look at ordinary green without demanding the jewel back. Soft-ray watching distance rule is the F432 residual-light cousin: watching distance is manners, not cowardice.

## Conditions

Required along Glassfold practice. Collapses when porch-light precision invites nests or when someone sells “permanent window eyes” as a life upgrade.

## Cultural Interpretations

Veil Scribes: glance is craft. Houses: fragile evidence over brave eyes. Softfruit: stop is kindness. Tour sellers of forever-sight: seized speech.
