# F840 Warmth Corridor Weather

## Name

F840 Warmth Corridor Weather

## Overview

**Definition:** In F840, place is structured by recoverable warmth corridors and nameable cold gaps. Geography favors sustained restoration routes over sudden “all healed” scenery.

## Description

Along the Ashen Hearthline, viable corridors guide kettle rounds, shelter relays, and latchvine holds. Cold gaps are part of the weather: they must be named so bodies do not pretend continuous summer. Miracle-reset speeches that paint unbroken warmth degrade trust and often cool real gains.

This is not F500 anti-scenery (which starves landmarks). Recovery weather wants usable rests. It is not F432 civic mixed climate either—here the primary place-engine is continuity of care after damage.

## Conditions

Baseline along Ashen Hearthline practice. Maintained by Ember Keeper routines. Weakens under Forced-Final clamps; strengthens after scraped honesty and repeated shelter use.

## Cultural Interpretations

Ember Keepers: shelter first, monument later. Softfruit: kettle before statue. F500 contrast: thinning is not recovery. Houses: Observation for which corridors still hold.

## Notes

Environment face of recovery continuity.
