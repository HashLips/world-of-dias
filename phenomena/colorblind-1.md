# Colorblind

## Name

Colorblind

## Overview

**Colorblind** is a named Wabet look of seeing-weather: color drains to gray value while cold things melt faster in the hand, and manners must keep grip without the usual hue cues.

## Description

Travelers report rooms and approaches going flat—walls, floors, and objects readable only by light and dark. A cone or cup of chill may drip into a dark puddle before you finish naming what it was. Duration is disputed: a breath, an hour, a well-approach evening. Instruments may still measure color while eyes swear otherwise; manners trust the puddle. When it shows, slow down, hold with longer fingers of attention, and do not shame anyone for missing a hue that the hour stole. Not a calendar guarantee. Not an Earth clinical label imported as insult.
