# F610 Interval-Visible Grain

## Name

F610 Interval-Visible Grain

## Overview

**Definition:** In F610, grain, seam, and structure in matter can become visible only inside a reveal window—then reseal. The structure is real; permanent sight of it is not.

## Description

Veil Scribes do not carve “forever true” into a wall they saw once. Interval-Visible Grain is real during the open; a second window may show a sister seam or refuse the first view. Glassfold phase-stamp discipline exists because memory of grain is not the same as grain remaining open.

Glassfold Brief Open is the place-weather cousin. Interval-Visible Grain is the substance cousin under the same reveal rule.

## Conditions

Occurs along Glassfold Ledge and related reveal sites. Porch-light staring invites nest trouble and stare-cost more than it buys lasting grain.
