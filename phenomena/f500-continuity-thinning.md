# F500 Continuity Thinning

## Name

F500 Continuity Thinning

## Overview

**Definition:** Near F500 null concentration, narrative and identity continuity become less reliable. Memory and self-story thin with proximity. This is not a clean erase button and not ordinary forgetfulness.

## Description

Accounts that survive approach are usually reconstructed afterward. Mid-approach notes and later testimony often disagree. Continuity Thinning is why abyss geography stays warning rather than destination. It leaves rumor holes on purpose; Softfruit after bleed must not file a lost market errand as this thinning.

Identity Softening Approach is the life cousin (bodies/self under approach). Landmark Starvation is the perception cousin. Continuity Thinning is the memory face: what you can keep coherent about “who / what / when” fails as null pressure rises.

## Conditions

Worsens with approach. Retreat may restore partial continuity that still conflicts with mid-approach notes. Falsely stable pockets can briefly fake ordinary recall.

## Cultural Interpretations

Softfruit: ordinary forgetfulness ≠ this thinning. Ember Keepers: scar-keeping is not null thinning. Soft limit: Frequency Zero is not “solved” as absolute no-memory. Houses: rumor-perforated Observation only.
