# Blue Litter Underwood

## Name

Blue Litter Underwood

## Overview

**Blue Litter Underwood** is the name for rare Wabet grove floors that run an unnatural saturated blue—treated as pooled resonance litter rather than water or paint.

## Description

Travelers report skeletal or winter-seeming trees even in green months, and a blue underlayer that softens underfoot. Prolonged echo-listening on the litter can drop a person mid-stride. The blue is not proven liquid; boots come away dry, minds do not.

## Conditions

Most often claimed:

- deep interior groves away from harvest roads
- after echo-rise weather farther east
- when a listener insists on finishing a difficult read

## Cultural Interpretations

Open Hand ethics say leave the litter undisturbed. Extraction-minded outsiders have tried to bottle it; stories say the bottles go clear and the headache stays.
