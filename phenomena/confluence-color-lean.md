# Confluence Color Lean

## Name

Confluence Color Lean

## Overview

**Confluence Color Lean** is when Bloomline color fields tip toward a shared mood—hue as weather—asking bodies to sit until the lean ends.

Greens cool or warm; edges blur; heart-deer graze the mist line. Speeches sound thinner than mats. Flexible tick pencils droop if someone tries to graph the lean into Forced-Final numbers.

## Conditions
