# Gray Post Tide Permission

## Name

Gray Post Tide Permission

## Overview

**Gray Post Tide Permission** is the mainland name for weeks when Averra’s pier is present or absent as if the isle had chosen. Reliability, in this telling, behaves like manners. Engineers file it as failure. Sailors who wait well file it as a breath. The Post’s existence is a repeating observed fact. The cause of its absence is not settled, and this entry does not settle it.

## Description

Calm seas, and the gray finger is there: timber, pale stone, a dry boot-sound. The same tide week, fog where the planks were, or planks inside a fog that feels like a turned shoulder. Courtesy tokens come out of pockets and often go back in, unused, which is the correct use when permission has not been offered. Captains who rage do not, in any honest physics, lengthen the absence. They lengthen the story of it, and they spend fuel, and their crews learn a voice they will not trust later. Artists call it the isle inhaling. Old Masters rumor calls it a veil on purpose. Both are interpretations standing on a dock that does not owe them a landing.

Forcing a landing because the absence offended you is the hazard: conquest fantasy with a skiff. The phenomenon does not punish in a mythic bolt. It simply fails to become an arrival, and the person who needed an arrival goes home with a worse contract and a better caution, if they are lucky.

## Conditions

Averra watch seasons, cool months when the Hearthline also shows and people are tempted to connect every sign into a key. Offshore courtesy kept or broken. Crown Ash still catches last light either way, a bearing that does not inhale.

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Harbormasters:** an unreliable infrastructure file, embarrassing and accurate as far as schedules go.
- **Artists:** the isle inhaling. A metaphor that knows it is a metaphor if it is any good.
- **Old Masters rumor:** an intentional veil. Remains rumor.
- **Hazard:** forcing the landing, and any telling that turns absence into an invitation to solve the interior.

## Notes

Cause stays unsettled. The Post as a repeating fact does not require a solved metaphysics. Not a frequency ferry.
