# F610 Stare-Cost Strain

## Name

F610 Stare-Cost Strain

## Overview

**Definition:** When a living being stares past glance discipline in F610, the body and attention pay a cost. Treating reveal windows as permanent eyes produces strain—not upgrade.

Veil Scribes teach stop as craft. Stare-cost can show as headache, false certainty, nest invitation, or hunger to pry the next seal. Soft limits forbid selling permanent omniscience as a life upgrade.

Glance-Seal Perception is the correct craft. Stare-Cost Strain is what happens when that craft is refused. Soft-ray watching distance rule is the F432 residual-light cousin.
