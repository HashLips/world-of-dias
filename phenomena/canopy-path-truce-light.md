# Canopy Path-Truce Light

## Name

Canopy Path-Truce Light

## Overview

**Canopy Path-Truce Light** is the green-gold sift when seven paths share the season without Forced-Final clear-cut—truce made luminous.

## Description

Leaves answer footsteps softly; wrong-season shortcuts dim. Beauty of shared canopy rather than owned corridor.

## Conditions

Seasonal path keeping; after truce renewals; quiet midday. Spoiled by clear-cut crews and band-ferry fantasies through trees.
