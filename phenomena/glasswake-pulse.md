# Glasswake Pulse

## Name

Glasswake Pulse

## Overview

**Glasswake Pulse** is the recurring behavior of the **Glasswake Lantern** at **Glasswake Rock**: a pale blue-white emission of light without visible flame or fuel, sometimes joined by brief shipboard sound fragments. It is a local resonance effect tied to one fused relic—not a weather system and not a summoning rite.

## Description

In ordinary hours the lantern looks dormant: thick glass, corroded ribs, stone through metal, no flame. A pulse is a soft wash or beat of blue-white light inside or through the glass, short enough that travelers argue whether they saw it. During stronger resonance nearby, witnesses report scraps of hull-water, strained timber, rope, footsteps, or a distant bell. The sounds do not form a full deck scene and do not answer questions.

Some field notes claim the brightest pulses lean along a heading associated with the *Glasswake*’s last course. That alignment is **disputed** and must not be treated as a finished navigational answer.

## Conditions

- Most often noticed at dusk, night, or after Listening Heat breaks into cooler coastal air
- More likely when other resonance events or storms press the southern Wabet shore
- Does not require fuel, wick adjustment, or Keep permission to occur
- Removal of the lantern is not available as a test; the object remains fused

## Cultural Interpretations

- **Silent Dune Keep / Silence Law:** listen, do not advertise; a pulse is a reminder, not a show cue
- **Coastal walkers:** “the stone is breathing the ship”
- **Researchers:** residual Fracture imprint and vessel echo; heading-correlation remains a hypothesis
- **Storytellers:** prelude to **The Captain Who Reaches**—pulse first, figure later, or never

## Notes

Does not grant wishes, band travel, or a confirmed wreck site. Leaves the *Glasswake*’s fate open on purpose.
