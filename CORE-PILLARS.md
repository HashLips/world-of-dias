# Core Worldbuilding Pillars — Dias

This page names the **foundational identity** of Dias. It does not replace existing lore entries. It classifies and extends them so the archive can grow without losing what makes the world recognizable.

**Core principle:** Everything resonates. Everything leaves an echo.

Doctrine detail lives in the linked rules and taxonomy entries below. Stories and sagas remain the emotional paths through the world.

---

## Pillars

### 1. Central characters

Recurring characters are entry points into different parts of Dias—not proof that the world is small.

- **Calen Brink** is one primary entry through *The Harmonic Saga*: echo-reading as a way to uncover history, mystery, and hidden pressure.
- Not every major figure needs unique power or destiny. Many should feel like people who already existed before the story arrived.
- Over time, Dias can support multiple protagonists across places, periods, factions, or frequency systems. List narrative journeys in [`SAGAS.md`](SAGAS.md).

### 2. Frequencies as fundamental law

Frequency is a defining principle of Dias—not unexplained magic.

Matter, living beings, environments, technologies, memories, and other phenomena can resonate differently. Frequency can shape abilities, perception, communication, technology, environments, creatures, artifacts, social order, factions, professions, belief, and history.

Someone familiar with Dias should eventually recognize a frequency designation and grasp some of its implications. The system must stay consistent enough for rules, and broad enough for discoveries and exceptions.

→ [`rules/frequency-as-fundamental-law.md`](rules/frequency-as-fundamental-law.md)

### 3. Resonant artifacts

Objects matter because of what they have experienced, contained, carried, or failed to reveal—not because power alone makes them important.

**Resonant Artifacts** is the umbrella classification for objects that retain, carry, amplify, transform, or reveal echoes. Existing classes such as **Prime Relics** sit inside this frame; they are not replaced by it.

→ [`artifacts/resonant-artifacts.md`](artifacts/resonant-artifacts.md)  
→ Flagship subtype: [`artifacts/resonant-shards.md`](artifacts/resonant-shards.md)

### 4. Resonant life and Echoforms

Unusual beings should emerge from Dias’s laws, not arrive as unrelated fantasy creatures.

**Echoforms** are beings created, transformed, or sustained through accumulated resonance—memory, repeated events, environmental frequency, collective emotion, ancient technology, or lasting natural phenomena.

→ [`inhabitants/echoforms.md`](inhabitants/echoforms.md)

### 5. Iconic locations

Places should differ because frequency and history affect them differently—not only because they look different.

A few deeply memorable locations are enough to start. Geography can reveal itself slowly. Existing anchors (Veloria, Driftfall, Wabet, Averra, and the documented frequency realms) already demonstrate this pillar.

### 6. Factions, houses, institutions, and cultures

Organizations and cultures disagree about what echoes are and what should be done with them—preserve, study, silence, exploit, or refuse to listen.

Existing groups such as the **Resonant Houses**, **Valorian Operators**, and **The Open Hand of Making** belong in this structure.

→ [`cultures/echo-interpretation-stances.md`](cultures/echo-interpretation-stances.md)

### 7. A central mystery

Some questions should remain larger than any character and unanswered for a long time—including the origin of frequency itself, the nature of echoes, and whether memory can exist without living beings.

**Frequency Zero** is a named open mystery: the theoretical absence of resonance. Cultures may disagree entirely about what it means. It is not explained here.

→ [`world/frequency-zero.md`](world/frequency-zero.md)

## Resonant Shard (recurring motif)

The **Resonant Shard** is a strong recurring object-type and conceptual symbol: matter that carries or preserves a frequency and its associated echoes. Shards may be common tools or historically priceless; some remain unreadable.

→ [`artifacts/resonant-shards.md`](artifacts/resonant-shards.md) · [`symbols/resonant-shard.md`](symbols/resonant-shard.md)

## How to use this page

1. Read the core principle and rules when expanding lore.
2. Prefer **extending and categorizing** existing entries over rewriting them.
3. When inventing something new, ask which pillar it serves—and which **exemplar** above it should relate to.
4. Do not attempt to define everything that exists in Dias. Define what **can** exist.
