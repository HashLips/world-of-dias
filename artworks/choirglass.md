# Facet Light of a Choirglass

## Overview

*Facet Light of a Choirglass* is a digital image study of a Choirglass figure in F432, read through translucent facets and light rather than flesh features alone.

## Description

The body is built from translucent faceted planes that catch ambient light into color bands. The facial region is smooth and multi-paneled rather than flesh-featured; brightness and refractive shifts carry expression more clearly than a fixed mouth or brow.

The plate presents one ordinary Choirglass presence—prism-like, chordal in bearing—without staging the figure as an oracle or default sage. Surrounding ornament and architecture in the frame remain decorative.
