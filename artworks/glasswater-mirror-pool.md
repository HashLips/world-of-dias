# Birds Above Mirror Calm

## Name

Birds Above Mirror Calm

## Overview

Birds Above Mirror Calm is a digital image study of the Glasswater Mirror Pool’s unnervingly perfect stillness.

## Description

The pool holds its reflection too perfectly while birds pass above it without making a ripple. The quiet water is the subject: a small pocket of beauty whose calm is difficult to trust.

The plate keeps the landmark as still water and does not turn the mirror into a passage or explain what a wrong reflection might mean.

Asset reference: `assets/glasswater-mirror-pool.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A landmark plate for the beauty, harvest calm, and mystery of Glasswater Mirror Pool.

## Lore Connection

The artwork sits with the pool’s open question and leaves the Still Pool Vigil’s warning intact: a perfect surface is not a reason to watch alone.
