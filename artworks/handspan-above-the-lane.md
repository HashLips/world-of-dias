# Handspan Above the Lane

## Name

Handspan Above the Lane

## Overview

*Handspan Above the Lane* is a digital image study of The Stillwood Skiff empty above a Wabet lane: wood hull, pole, unexplained lift.

## Description

A narrow clinker-built wooden skiff hangs level a handspan clear of a cracked stone-and-earth lane. Three thwarts show; the craft is empty of people and cargo. A long rough pole leans from the hull to the ground. Low banks with vines and small white flowers border the path; mist softens the distance.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/the-stillwood-skiff.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of The Stillwood Skiff as rural wood craft that travels a handspan above lanes—not a frequency ferry.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the craft's fixed look without inventing the cause of lift, a hire service, or band-crossing power. Unknown cause stays unknown.
