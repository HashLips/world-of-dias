# Have You Ever

## Name

Have You Ever

## Overview

*Have You Ever* is a still forage on Songripe ground: one blue grazer, head down, chewing black needle-grass while a thin black vertical cut crosses its hindquarters and long grey shadows lean left.

## Description

The creature is solid, saturated blue—long neck, small head, one black almond eye, an undulating back that reads more camel-lean than herd-stock. Two black horizontal marks sit on the rear flank like stamps that never quite become brands. In its mouth, a bunch of black needle-grass; the same spiky black tuft grows from a dark patch on a pink landmass in the lower right. The ground and sky are irregular pale pink and white fields, not mapped orchard rows. A single thin vertical black line drops through the left side of the frame and through the animal’s hindquarters, as if a cut in the plate rather than a fence. Shadows stretch angular and dark toward the bottom-left. No rider, no harness, no market tag.

## Real-World Role

A fauna plate for Songripe wonder: unripe orchards still feed something that knows how to browse without asking the mainland calendar.

## Lore Connection

Songripe Isles keep song over harvest; fruit often will not ripen, and tide-song cousins treat that as mercy rather than failure. This plate does not show a Tide-Bird or a rope bridge. It shows a blue grazer that has found black needle-grass worth lowering a head for. Local care here is small: do not startle a browsing animal into the pink cut-line of weather; do not harvest the black grass for dye until the grazer has finished the mouthful. Resonance in Songripe often arrives as sound. Here it arrives as quiet chewing and a shadow that remembers the light source.

## Notes

One animal only. Black grass in mouth and on ground. Vertical black cut through the hindquarters. No Earth fauna name required.
