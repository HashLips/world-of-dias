# World of Dias Wordmark

## Name

World of Dias Wordmark

## Overview

*World of Dias Wordmark* is the official lettering plate for **WORLD of DIAS**—the words alone, ready for layouts that already carry the emblem separately or need clean type.

## Description

The same hammered-gold fantasy serifs as the full logo lockup, arranged as a horizontal title. Use when the archive needs the name without repeating the full emblem-in-O composition.

Asset reference: `assets/world-of-dias-wordmark.png`.

## Real-World Role

Secondary brand asset for headers, captions, and layouts that pair type with a separate emblem or with plain space.

## Lore Connection

In Dias talk, naming the world is itself a manners act—letters that remember weight. The wordmark holds that naming without adding place or saga scene.

## Notes

Pair with [`Dias Emblem`](dias-emblem.md) when a split lockup is wanted. Prefer [`World of Dias Logo`](world-of-dias-logo.md) for the combined official mark.
