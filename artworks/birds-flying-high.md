# Birds Flying High

## Name

Birds Flying High

## Overview

*Birds Flying High* is an Aelwyn Canopy road-height plate: a large calm blue bird stands through a yellow crescent with a tiny black key in its beak, long legs planted on layered hills—height as permission, not as escape.

## Description

On a stark white field, a rounded mid-blue bird body sits high, one darker blue wing folded, eye a thin horizontal white slit, beak tiny. From the beak hangs a small black skeleton key with a circular bow. Two impossibly long thin blue legs pass through the middle of a bright yellow crescent (black tip at left) and end in simple three-toed feet on undulating hills—deep reddish-brown base, lighter orange-tan, dark chocolate crest—with jagged black mountain silhouettes behind. A jagged dark-blue shape tucks behind the crescent. Tiny black crown-like mark in the top-left. Mood: silent, whimsical, scale-bent, serene.

## Real-World Role

A canopy travel plate: how high you may lift a path-key before you still owe the hills a soft landing.

## Lore Connection

Aelwyn talk treats **Birds Flying High** as a height-manners inhabitant-sign. The key in the beak is path permission—something you carry for a gate, a lean, or a canopy slit—not a trophy. The yellow crescent is a local fruit-road or season-mark the legs must pass through without kicking aside. Long legs mean you can see far and still touch ground. Wonder is disciplined lift: fly high enough to keep the key honest, not so high the hills forget you. The name is a Dias canopy name; it is not an Earth song claim.
