# Wired Blooms

## Name

Wired Blooms

## Overview

*Wired Blooms* shows a red hand lifting a white star-flower with a blue center, threaded by glowing lines to a patterned cradle—harvest proof, not a market toy.

## Description

Three blossom-markers rest on a grey surface beside a white vessel printed with green flower icons. Thin cyan threads link blooms to the cradle like pot-signal strings. The red hand plucks one gently: interaction without extraction greed.
