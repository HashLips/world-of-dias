# Shoes

## Name

Shoes

## Overview

*Shoes* is the Velorian Basin plate for the colored angular swarm. Cyan, magenta, yellow, royal blue. Beak tips, slender legs, three-line crests. All aimed one way on slate.

## Description

Dark slate-gray field packed with dozens of identical sharp-edged forms—bird-in-flight, high form, or energy bolt depending on the teller—overlapping with thin black drop shadows. Each has a pointed beak-like front with a small black dot, a long slender vertical leg ending in a black point, a sharp rear protrusion, and a three-pronged crest marked by three short black lines. Colors cycle cyan, magenta, bright yellow, and deeper royal blue across the swarm. All orient toward bottom-left like a coordinated flock. Energetic, rhythmic, slightly chaotic order.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Velorian Basin. Shoes is the kept craft of the matched angular swarm pattern.

## Lore Connection

Shoes is the basin's name for the hour when the angular colored forms fill slate and all point the same way. Local care says you keep the crest three-lines honest; you do not scramble orientations for noise. Resonance is four bright colors on gray; memory is the black-dot beaks; wonder is how shoe-like and bird-like can share one craft name without needing Earth footwear talk. The plate invents no fashion season. It keeps the swarm as manners for massed matched motion marks.
