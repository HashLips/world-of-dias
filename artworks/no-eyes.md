# No Eyes

## Name

No Eyes

## Overview

*No Eyes* is the F432 plate for care that weeps while it holds. A dark figure kneels. A golden ovoid is cradled to the chest. White eyes spill thin red lines. Pale pink eye-stalks rest nearby.

## Description

The figure kneels in profile toward the right on an orange surface. Head and upper torso are deep black; the lower kneeling half reads charcoal. Two wide white circular eyes carry small red pupils; long thin bright red streaks run down face and neck like weeping that has become a mark. Both arms hold a large smooth golden-yellow ovoid tight to the chest—egg-like, stone-like, clearly the thing being guarded.

Around the knees, pale pink fleshy stalks end in large white eyes with bright blue pupils: one lying on the ground to the left, another against the knee. They are watchers that are not on the face. A pool of pale pink liquid or light spreads under the legs. Background fields are angular orange and darker ochre; a lighter pale orange-yellow glow sits behind head and shoulders like a soft halo. A tiny black geometric stamp marks the top right.

The title's refusal is in the plate: the face weeps, the stalks watch, and still the rule named No Eyes is about not using sight as a weapon while you hold what must be kept.

## Real-World Role

A symbol plate for F432. No Eyes is held care under weeping light, not a blank mask sold as fashion.

## Lore Connection

No Eyes is the realm's mark for the hour when you cradle the golden ovoid and refuse to turn watching into harm. The red lines from the white eyes are resonance made visible; the detached pink eye-stalks are memory that can sit on the ground without stabbing. Local care says: hold what you hold; do not hunt with your gaze. Wonder is the orange field staying warm around sorrow. The plate invents no funeral rite beyond what the kneel already shows. It keeps the ovoid, the weep, and the stalk-eyes as a symbol people can recognize when a story needs a handle for protected grief.
