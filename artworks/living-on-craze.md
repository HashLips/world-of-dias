# Living on Craze

## Name

Living on Craze

## Overview

*Living on Craze* races a huge yellow bird rightward: orange beak gripping a blue fish, grey flat cap, black wing-patch, red-and-white striped lifebuoy midsection, thin blue rope from the trailing ankle up-right, two long-limbed white riders in tall curved black hats—one braced at the buoy, one stretched on the black back—mint-to-deep-blue field dotted with pale circles holding dark red up-triangles.

## Description

Whimsy with tether: speed is real, so is the rope. Grid dots feel like chart weather. The fish stays clenched; the hats mark who may steer. Mood is surreal transit, not calm dock.
