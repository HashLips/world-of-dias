# Reincarnation

## Name

Reincarnation

## Overview

*Reincarnation* is the F840 plate for the red carry over dark echoes. Tall red figure, head bowed. Limp white form in long careful hands. Sage above, charcoal below with fading repeats.

## Description

Diagonal split: sage green upper field, dark charcoal lower plane like still water or reflective ground. A tall slender solid-red humanoid stands vertical, head bowed, one dark slit eye, small downturned mouth. Long thin arms end in large hands with four long slightly pointed fingers cradling a smaller entirely white humanoid lying limp and horizontal—head tilted back, limbs dangling, one spindly arm hanging toward the bottom.

On the dark surface, semi-transparent ghost repeats trail downward: light-gray translucent white figure, faint red extension of the standing body, fading toward the bottom edge—memory layers or water echoes, fingers especially clear in the lower ghosts. Small white mark top-left. Somber care, not spectacle.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for F840. Reincarnation is the kept craft of carrying the white limp form above its echoes.

## Lore Connection

Reincarnation is F840's name for the hour when the red keeper cradles the white limp body and the charcoal plane shows the repeats fading down. Local care says you hold carefully; you do not splash the echoes for drama. Resonance is red against white against sage; memory is the stacked translucent layers; wonder is how many fades the dark can hold. The plate invents no afterlife bureaucracy. It keeps the carry as manners for return-shaped care.
