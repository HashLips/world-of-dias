# Basin Rail in a Hand

## Name

Basin Rail in a Hand

## Overview

*Basin Rail in a Hand* shows a generic hand around a palm-sized curve of worn market rail. The metal looks like scrap that has been leaned on for years. It does not look like a weapon, and it does not contain a face.

## Description

Curved edge, polish from hands and hips, civic metal from a basin lane. The hand is nobody's portrait. Warmth reads as a held quiet, not as a blade and not as a joke with eyes trapped in the scrap. Joy without the joke is the echo the record allows. Bodies without faces is the pressure. The market behind is only distance and color. No sign can be read.

The plate teaches the common end of resonant shards: an object matters by what it went through, not by spectacle. It does not name the hand. It does not turn the fragment into a key, a relic-weapon, or a portrait of any saga figure.

Asset reference: `assets/basin-rail-shard.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A scene plate for ordinary resonance in F432: a lesson you can hold, filed as modest and still true.

## Lore Connection

The Basin Rail Shard is a break of Velorian Basin rail that kept a partial echo of shared laughter. Stewards pass it for teaching new ears. Institutions have treated that class of residue as low-hazard. The picture stays on the object and the hand. It does not finish the market's sentence, because the echo does not either.

## Notes

Generic hand only. No faces in the metal. Not a weapon and not a key.
