# Backstab

## Name

Backstab

## Overview

*Backstab* is an Averra Isle craft-warning plate: from a small open terracotta box rise a blue bird-neck, a white scissors-hand, and a black looming hand-bird with a needle-rod—the moment when helpful cutting turns into a cut from behind.

## Description

A light beige floor holds a simple open reddish-brown box, edged in three-quarter view, casting a sharp gray shadow toward the lower left. From the box three forms climb into a solid golden-yellow field. Left: a cobalt swan-like neck curves up into a simplified bird head with a pointed beak and a thin white eye-slit. Center: a thin white arm ends in an elegant hand holding open white scissors. Right and above: a deep dark silhouette begins as a thick neck and becomes a multi-fingered hand-bird arching over the others, one digit holding a long thin black rod or needle.

The forms intertwine without touching in violence—yet the scissors and needle make the threat readable. Flat color, no gradients, high contrast. A small black-and-white seal sits in the bottom-right corner. Mood: craft, manifestation, and the quiet dread of a tool used from where you cannot see.

## Real-World Role

A studio-manners plate for Averra: when editing help becomes a cut aimed at the maker’s back.

## Lore Connection

On Averra Isle, **Backstab** is the local name for a craft failure, not a tavern brawl. In Averra Damp Studios, scissors and needle are ordinary tools. The plate warns what happens when those tools leave the agreed box and work on a person instead of a piece. The blue bird-neck is the helpful witness that still wants to look forward. The white scissors-hand is the edit that claims it is only trimming. The black hand-bird with the needle-rod is the part that has already decided the cut will land where the maker cannot watch. Studios teach: keep tools in the open box; announce the cut; never sew a judgment into someone’s back while smiling at their face. Wonder here is caution—the box is small on purpose so nothing large should climb out unnoticed.
