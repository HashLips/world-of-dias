# Laugh that Sells Stubs

## Name

Laugh that Sells Stubs

## Overview

*Laugh that Sells Stubs* is a digital image study of Captain Ell Ferrylie at Ferrylie Joke Pier: the laugh that sells stubs, and the soft-limit comedy that never claims a real crossing.

## Description

Ell stands on weathered pier planks, head tipped back in open laughter, eyes shut. Dark-blue captain's coat with heavy gold embroidery, tan waistcoat, ruffled shirt, red sash, tall worn boots, large tricorne with gold trim. Dark curly hair and facial hair match the salt-theater read.

To the right a small square wooden ticket shack with an open window faces the water. Calm sea and cloudy sky behind. No readable ticket text appears on the plate.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/captain-ell-ferrylie.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Captain Ell Ferrylie as pier comedy and ethics warning: sell the longing, return the walker, keep the water honest.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens Ell and Ferrylie Joke Pier without inventing a real band ferry, subway, or readable stub copy. Soft limit stays: same dock, same return walk.
