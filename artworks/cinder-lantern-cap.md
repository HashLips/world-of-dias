# Cinder Nook Lantern Cap

## Name

Cinder Nook Lantern Cap

## Overview

*Cinder Nook Lantern Cap* is a small glowing fungus tucked into a cinder nook under fallen wood. The light is tiny, warm, and local. It is not horror, not a beacon, and not a scene with anyone in it to be frightened or saved.

## Description

One cap stands in the hollow beneath a dark log. The gills and the stem carry a low amber glow that reaches the needles and soil around the foot and then stops. Ferns and moss crowd the edges. The nook is close, cinder-dark, and ordinary: bark, leaf, and a lamp the size of a notice. Nothing looms. Nothing swarms. No face leans in, and no hand picks the cap.

The plate does not settle how long the glow lasts, whether silence laws or yard custom govern picking it, or what else shares the nook when the picture is not looking. It does not grow the fungus into a monster or a miracle large enough to light a road. Scale is the meaning. Tiny light is the whole event.

## Real-World Role

A craft plate for Cinder Nook's small glow culture. Useful light, shown at the size it actually is.

## Lore Connection

Cinder Lantern Cap is flora people notice as tiny light, beetle-adjacent in local talk and indexed as craft rather than as a fright. This plate keeps that scale. It does not illustrate a swarm, a haunting, or a harvest rite. The nook can stay dark past the circle of the cap. That limit is not a threat. It is the size of the tool.

No people. Tiny light, not horror.
