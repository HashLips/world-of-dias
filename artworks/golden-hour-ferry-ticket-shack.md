# Golden-Hour Ferry Ticket Shack

## Name

Golden-Hour Ferry Ticket Shack

## Overview

*Golden-Hour Ferry Ticket Shack* shows Captain Ell Ferrylie’s joke booth on a Nauw pier at sunset—fake band ferry tickets sold under warm lantern light, with a city silhouette across the water that never becomes a boarding schedule.

## Description

Weathered blue-and-white ticket shack on wet planks; gull on a rope-wrapped post; barrel and crates; hanging lanterns. Signs advertise fake band ferry tickets “good for one crossing in your head,” plus a struck boat mark over NAUW. Beyond the pier, a golden-hour harbor city rises—longing scenery, not a subway map.

## Real-World Role

Visual home for Ferrylie Joke Pier: soft-limit comedy you can see—tickets for wanting, not for crossing.
