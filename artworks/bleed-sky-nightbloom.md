# Open Bleed-Sky Nightbloom

## Name

Open Bleed-Sky Nightbloom

## Overview

*Open Bleed-Sky Nightbloom* is one rare flower open under a sky no honest dye matches. Petals carry violet, teal, and ember at once. The plant is an omen companion to that weather, not a crop. No people stand in the plate to harvest it or explain it.

## Description

A single bloom rises on a dark stem, leaves crowded at the base, petals spread wide. The colors do not settle into a garden variety or a vat recipe. They belong to the sky behind them, which is streaked and starry and the wrong hues for ordinary night. Distant spires sit low in that weather. They are not a field plan, not a farm, and not a city the plate names.

Nothing is being cut, dried, or carried. There is no basket, no row, no gardener, and no viewer. The frame makes the flower rare by isolation. It does not make it stock. The plate does not settle which night the sky will do this again, whether the bloom can be moved, or what a dishonest dye would fail at if someone tried. It does not offer a drug, a trade, or a calendar. It shows one opening, and the sky that opening answers.
