# Dining Hall Mark

## Name

Dining Hall Mark

## Overview

*Dining Hall Mark* is a Hollowmere meal plate: a blue runner holds a central fuchsia eye between mirrored candelabras and twin place settings—the hall rule that a shared meal stays under honest watch.

## Description

Pale yellow ground with thin horizontal pink stripes. Centered bright-blue rectangle as table or runner. Exact center: a large stylized eye—pointed almond, fuchsia pupil, white sclera, wider bright-pink surround. Above and below, soft-pink three-armed candelabras with white candles and small orange flames. At each end: orange rectangular placemat, white circular plate holding purple sticks, pale yellow block, peach blob, reddish-brown blob; black knife left, three-tined fork right. Perfect vertical symmetry. Diagram-clean, ritual and bright.

Asset reference: `assets/dinning-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A Hollowmere hall plate: eat as equals under a mark that watches manners, not appetite alone.

## Lore Connection

In Hollowmere, **Dining Hall Mark** is the kept diagram for guest meals. The central eye is the hall’s care-mark—observation as courtesy, not surveillance creed. Mirrored settings mean neither end of the table owns the feast. Wonder is shared looking while you eat. File name keeps an older spelling; display name is Dining Hall Mark.
