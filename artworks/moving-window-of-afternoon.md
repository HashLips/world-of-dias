# Moving Window of Afternoon

## Name

Moving Window of Afternoon

## Overview

*Moving Window of Afternoon* is a digital image study of Meridian Soft-Rays as calm champagne sheets over the Meridian corridor.

## Description

Wide manta-like radiance forms glide across parchment in champagne-gold sheets, edges feathering into glow. They do not read as matter-feeders—only as a moving window of afternoon light. Soft-Ray scale feels vast by F432 measure.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/meridian-soft-rays.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Soft-Rays as good transit omens along Aurel Meridian.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens Meridian Soft-Rays without inventing feeding habits or instrument politics beyond established uncouthness of blocking their path.

## Notes

Distinct from colossal Bandwidth Sky-Mantas. Border framing only.
