# Finding Them

## Name

Finding Them

## Overview

*Finding Them* is a Songripe Isles call plate: a dark brown open palm holds a neat 3x3 of white ovals while teal birds arrive—the island craft of finding neighbors by offering a readable pattern, not by chasing.

## Description

Solid coral-orange field. Deep brown arm and open hand from lower right; nine small white ovals in a precise 3x3 on the palm. Three stylized teal-cyan birds with long thin angular legs and sharp beaks: brightest teal mid-air wings spread, beak to a palm mark; medium teal perched on the forearm facing the hand; darkest teal entering from lower left. Calm, ritual, symbiotic. Flat graphic print mood.

Asset reference: `assets/finding-them.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

An isles artifact plate: set the nine-grid; let them find you.

## Lore Connection

In the Songripe Isles, **Finding Them** is kept call-craft. White ovals may be seeds, salt marks, or song-counts—always a grid you can re-read. Teal birds are non-human responders. Wonder is invitation over pursuit. Chasing empty-handed is emptied craft.

Brown palm, 3x3 white ovals. Three teal birds. Coral-orange field.
