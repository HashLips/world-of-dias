# My Path

## Name

My Path

## Overview

*My Path* is the visual anchor for **Delci Beta**'s inward journey: an elderly figure walking a sparse dream-ground toward distant height—read in-world as the mountainside of **Mountain of Majestic Good Trade**, and as the first stretch of path inside his chosen dreams.

## Description

A flat, high-contrast field of gray ground under a pale sky. A black silhouette walks away from the viewer with a stick, footprints marking the way behind him. A park bench sits alone to one side. Framed pictures lie on the ground like left-behind rooms. In the foreground, another dark figure lies reaching. A small white rectangle hangs in the sky like a cold sun or an open square of light.

Asset reference: `assets/my-path.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A surreal path study in Daniel Eugene Botha's Dias-linked work: journey reduced to silhouette, track, and a few bright rectangles of color against emptiness.

## Lore Connection

Driftfall readers tie the walking figure to **Delci Beta** climbing toward—or dream-walking from—his Good Trade rock. The empty bench echoes his still hours (a seat waiting while the true life happens elsewhere). The fallen frames are sometimes argued as places already visited through the **Passage Round** logbook, set down when the day's signing is done. The reaching shadow is left disputed: past self, guest without handshare, or the sealed center that has not yet opened.
