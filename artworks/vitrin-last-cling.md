# Last Cling in Green-Gold Glass

## Name

Last Cling in Green-Gold Glass

## Overview

*Last Cling in Green-Gold Glass* is a digital image study of the smaller Vitrin fruit still clinging to the young tree in the Mix Orchard.

## Description

The plate holds a smaller glass fruit still on the young Vitrin tree. Its rind is clear and thick, with green-gold depth beneath it; leaf-green cords run through the fruit, and its seeds catch the light like lenses. It is heavier than it looks.

Ordinary share-apple trees and a market banana are nearby, but they are not on the Vitrin tree. The mix remains unknown. The image does not turn the neighboring trees into an answer or add a recipe.
