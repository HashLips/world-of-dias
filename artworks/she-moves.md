# She Moves

## Name

She Moves

## Overview

*She Moves* is the Sorel plate for the low-angle pink volume. White back. Enormous magenta dress. Yellow wavy brim. Black tall crown. Gray field.

## Description

Medium-gray ground. From a low angle behind, a featureless white figure angles slightly right, elevated by perspective. An enormous saturated magenta/hot-pink floor-length dress fills the bottom two-thirds—billowing volume with lighter pink fold strokes, simple close bodice and short sleeves. On the head: a large floppy wide-brimmed yellow hat with irregular wavy edge; perched at a sharp angle on that brim, a tall solid black cylindrical crown. Face hidden. Tiny white gate/W icon upper right. Ceremonial motion implied by scale and stack of yellow-then-black headwear, not by a shown step.

Asset reference: `assets/she-moves.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Sorel. She Moves is the kept craft of the volume dress and stacked brim-crown.

## Lore Connection

She Moves is Sorel's name for the hour when the magenta volume fills the gray and the yellow brim carries the black tall crown without a shown face. Local care says you give the dress room to move; you do not yank the black crown for a joke. Resonance is hot pink against yellow and black; memory is the low-angle white back; wonder is how much skirt can own a field. The plate invents no court plot. It keeps the craft as manners for grand quiet motion.
