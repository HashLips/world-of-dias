# White Lily

## Name

White Lily

## Overview

*White Lily* is a potted plant on a table. A squat orange pot sits on an olive-brown tabletop against a deep green wall. From it rise long white blade leaves and three thin white stems that bend sharply at angles, each ending in a spiky white star-shaped bloom. Two white leaves have fallen and lie curled on the table to the left, casting small dark shadows.

## Description

The plant is drawn entirely in white, like a paper cutout, with no veins or shading. The blooms are wild and starry, petals splayed in every direction like bursts. The stems are thin and kinked, crossing each other on the way up, one bloom reaching far right, two reaching left. The leaves are long, narrow, and curved, some upright, some drooping, one bent nearly horizontal.

The pot is a plain rounded cylinder of bright orange, its rim dark where the soil shows. It casts a broad gray shadow down and to the left on the olive table, and the table meets the green wall in a straight line with a darker strip at the back.

The two fallen leaves are the quiet detail: curved white slivers lying apart from the pot, the kind of thing someone could easily sweep away and has chosen not to. The mood is calm, domestic, slightly wild—a plant that is thriving without being trimmed.

## Real-World Role

A household-flora plate for the white lily in an orange pot, read in Veloria as a signal of rest.

## Lore Connection

Pot-Signal Array Court is the Veloria pocket court where street flower pots are arranged as a readable signal array—beauty first, debt, sanctuary, and warning grammar second. In Pot Signal Customs, a white lily in an orange pot is one of the plainest signs: someone may rest here tonight, no questions, no business. The plate shows such a pot with two fallen leaves on the table beside it, which in the court's grammar means the rest has already been taken by someone. Locals do not sweep fallen lily leaves from a signal table until morning.
