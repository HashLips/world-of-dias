# Recognition

## Name

Recognition

## Overview

*Recognition* is the F380 plate for the raised arm in the mint hall. Black observer in front. Rows of translucent mint figures with red outlines. One solid dark-blue arm up. White floor lines converging.

## Description

Deep charcoal-gray hall. Immediate foreground: solid dark silhouette of the back of a head and shoulders—our lens into the room. Beyond, dozens of identical simplified human forms sit in neat receding rows, most translucent glowing mint-green or light cyan, thin sharp red borders matching red seats. Semi-transparency lets charcoal show through like a collective ghost presence.

White geometric floor lines converge into distance, rhythmic light between chair rows. Breaking the uniform mint: a few rows back, right of center, one solid opaque dark-blue figure raises a right arm high—single interruption, single call. No faces detailed. The plate is the moment the hall admits one different solid among the glowing many, seen by the black watcher.
