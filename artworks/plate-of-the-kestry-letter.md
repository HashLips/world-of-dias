# Plate of the Kestry Letter

## Name

Plate of the Kestry Letter

## Overview

*Plate of the Kestry Letter* is an impressionistic plate of the Kestry Letter: both sides of the waxed leaf, the folded packet and its address, and close views of the seal, the blue chalk, and the signature. It is not a reproduction; see Notes.

## Description

**This plate is an impression of the Kestry Letter, not a reproduction.** It is a dark-ground sheet showing a buff, creased survey leaf from both sides, with five detail panels underneath. It catches the letter's mood and its physical marks: the fold, the seal, the chalk and the signature. Its wording, its map and its layout are not canonical.

**Side A, the letter** (top left). Portrait. Red boxed headings MEASURE and GUESS sit at the top of the two margins. Black handwriting begins "Maren—" and is signed *Ansel* with the two dots and rising stroke. The body is a shortened, reworded letter. It names "the Counting Current" and "the Long Slack," mentions lights in a blue mist to the west, and ends "I love you. Always." Down both margins run **numbered tallies (12–18)** beside stroke bundles.

**Side B, the sketch-map** (top right). A compass rose with N at the upper right. A large blue chalk cloud labelled BLUE MIST, "does not move with wind." Under it, a **fully drawn mountainous island with a waterfall**, ringed by small rocks, some marked with question marks. A red dashed line labelled DRIFTED THIS WAY runs to a red cross marked STRIKE HERE / SW RIB, off a coast labelled SOREL COAST (APPROX) and SALTJAW SHELF. Numbered margin tallies continue under MEASURE and GUESS.

**Details** (bottom row), left to right:

- Folded letter, front: the packet closed under a red wax blot with a hooked crescent.
- Folded letter, back: the address, *Maren Kestry. Bell-keeper. Saltjaw Mine. Driftfall. Sorel. Please.*
- Seal detail: the crescent, unbroken.
- Blue chalk detail: a thumb-smeared blue patch.
- Signature detail: *Ansel* and the two-and-rise mark.

## Notes

- **Impression, not reproduction.** These features of the plate are **non-canonical**: its letter wording; the fully drawn mountainous island with waterfall; the compass rose; the numbered tallies (12–18); the placing of MEASURE and GUESS as margin headings (the original has red boxed GUESS and MEASURE labels, but inside the letter text, not over the margins); the captions BLUE MIST, DRIFTED THIS WAY, STRIKE HERE, SOREL COAST (APPROX) and SALTJAW SHELF; and the overall layout.
- In particular, Ansel **did not know or name "the Long Slack"** when he wrote. He wrote only "I am putting this into the outward water." The Counting Current was also not his term in the letter.
- The original's sketch has no island drawn whole. It shows only a short black cliff line with terrace strokes and dark squares under a thumb-smeared blue bank, captioned *seen once, at dusk. No shore here on any chart.* It has no compass, no scale and no bearings, and its margins carry unnumbered strokes, dots and dashes.
- [The Kestry Letter](../artifacts/the-kestry-letter.md) governs. A faithful reproduction should follow that entry's **reproduction checklist**.
- The fold, seal crescent, address, blue chalk smear and signature detail panels are close to canon.
