# Love Birds

## Name

Love Birds

## Overview

*Love Birds* mirrors two dark charcoal birds on vivid yellow: bodies overlapping mid-frame, heads turned outward, white almond eye-slits, tiny bright red triangular beaks up, thin red legs with three-pronged claws gripping light-blue branches from upper left and right, left bird trailed by a semi-transparent yellowish-green echo.

## Description

Symmetry with a resonance ghost. Tails taper down like banners. Blue branches are canopy-strange, not Earth wood. Mood is paired signalling—together, facing out.

An inhabitant plate: pair-birds that keep a green motion echo when they shift.

Aelwyn Canopy paths know Love Birds as outward-facing pairs on blue branch-lines. Local rule: do not part a pair mid-echo; wait until the green double fades. Resonance prefers shared perches. Stealing one bird’s branch for craft empties the canopy courtesy.
