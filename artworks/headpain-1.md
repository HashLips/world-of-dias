# Headpain

## Name

Headpain

## Overview

*Headpain* is a pale-blue profile on a saturated red field, mouth and skull cut with three dark slots, and three black bodies pouring out—one arched over the crown, one rising from the nape, one spilling from the mouth.

## Description

The head faces left: light blue, smooth nose, a narrow red eye-slit, a tiny red nostril mark. Three crescent openings break the blue—forehead, occipital back, and mouth—each releasing a pitch-dark figure silhouette. The upper figure bends in a fluid backward arc over the skull. The nape figure stands with arms raised in a dance-like curve. The mouth figure extends horizontally, arms wide, legs trailing back toward the left edge. There is no sand dune, no market stall, only red air and the head that will not keep its insides in. A small square monogram sits in the lower right corner beside a faint maker mark.

Asset reference: `assets/headpain-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A symbol plate for pressure that will not stay inside a skull: voices, pains, and remembered errands exiting as bodies.

## Lore Connection

Lumira Sands talk treats Headpain as a handle for days when more than one intention tries to leave a person at once. The local rule is practical: if three exits show—thought, nape-habit, and spoken word—do not force them back through one slit. Sit until the red field cools. Resonance here is not a sermon; it is the admission that a head can host more than one departing shape, and that naming the pain is kinder than pretending the blue profile was ever sealed.
