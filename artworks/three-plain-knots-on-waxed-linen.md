# Three Plain Knots on Waxed Linen

## Name

Three Plain Knots on Waxed Linen

## Overview

*Three Plain Knots on Waxed Linen* is a Market-Loop String laid out as a thin waxed linen cord with three plain knots. The knots are not labeled. There is no map. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

A thin cord of waxed linen crosses the frame with three knots spaced along it. The knots are plain. None of them carries a tag, a letter, a number, or a name. Nothing on the cord says which knot is which. The ends are loose cord, not a drawn street and not a legend.

The plate is the string and the knots. It is not a map of the market loop, not a road out of the ring, and not a line toward anywhere farther than a door already reached. No bakery is drawn on it, no gutter, and no second string is offered as a longer way. The cord does not point onward. It does not name Sorel, the Long Gate, or any band.

No hand holds it, so the plate cannot show the cord going slack. The knots do not argue in the frame. They sit, unlabeled, on waxed linen.

## Real-World Role

A still life against maps to elsewhere. Three knots, and no legend.

## Lore Connection

A Market-Loop String is a knotted cord the length of the walk from Veloria's market loop to Farstock's door, one street and no farther. It helps someone find a shop they have already reached. It does not point onward. Waxed linen is the material. The knots belong to the bakery breath, the gutter, and the hesitation-step. This plate does not label them, and it does not claim to show which knot is which. Held and walked, the cord goes slack in the hand at the right corner when the street is the one. The picture does not show that slack, that corner, or that hand. Used backward, toward the loop, it only proves the way already come. It has no interest in Sorel, the Long Gate, or any band. It is a joke against the wish for a map to elsewhere, and the plate includes no map. The cord gets used as a skipping rope until the knots argue. No one is skipping here. If a longer string is claimed to lead out of F432, that is a different object.
