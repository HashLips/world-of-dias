# 3d Room

## Name

3d Room

## Overview

*3d Room* is an Averra Isle corner interior: a black claw rises from a floor-hole toward a pink sphere on a yellow table while a red urn and a hanging lamp keep the quiet of a studio that measures depth by touch, not by boast.

## Description

Two blue walls meet in a hard corner. A thick semi-transparent white diagonal beam cuts the whole frame from upper left to lower right, the kind of light Averra damp studios treat as a measuring slant rather than decoration. From a dark oval hole in the floor, a heavy dark silhouette undulates upward—limb, trunk, or arm—ending in a sharp three-fingered claw that hangs above a pink sphere. The sphere sits centered on a simple yellow rectangular table with thin, slightly wobbly black legs; a small dark shadow sits under the sphere, and a longer blue shadow stretches from the table.

On the left floor stands a tall curved red vase or urn with a black interior and a simplified silhouette mark on its belly. From the ceiling hangs a small pendant: shallow red conical shade, bright lime-green bulb. The palette is saturated and flat—blue room, yellow table, pink ball, red vessel and shade, black claw—quiet, mysterious, and carefully empty of people.

## Real-World Role

A depth-room plate for Averra studio manners: how far a presence may reach into a room before it owes the room an answer.

## Lore Connection

On Averra Isle, **3d Room** names a practice room where makers learn that depth is a courtesy. The claw from the floor is the part of a memory that wants to grab; the pink sphere is the kept object that must not be seized without asking; the yellow table is the small stage that makes the ask visible. Averra Damp Studios keep rooms like this so apprentices can rehearse reach without rehearsing theft. The red urn holds the room’s own mark—what the place refuses to forget. The green-bulbed lamp is the soft permission light: when it is on, the claw may hover; when it is off, the claw should withdraw. The white diagonal beam is the slant used to check whether a gesture is still in the room’s agreed volume. Wonder here is local and practical: a third dimension is not a license to take.
