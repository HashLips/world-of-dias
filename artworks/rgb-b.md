# Rgb B

## Name

Rgb B

## Overview

*Rgb B* is the Velorian Basin plate for the triple blue stack. Three cobalt elongated figures on cream. White slit eyes. Long tendril fingers. Crawl, recline, kneel.

## Description

Warm cream or pale beige field. Three distinct solid cobalt-blue humanoids stacked top to bottom, paper-cut clean. Top: profile facing right in crouch/crawl; angular head, thin white eye-slit; one hand back in long curved finger-projections like wing or flame; another hand under the head. Middle: reclining/floating on its back facing left, head tilted, white slit eye; wavy body, knees bent; one arm down with long tapered fingers, one up ending in three rounded digits. Bottom: seated/kneeling facing left; long straight horizontal arm reaching toward a cluster of blue wavy tentacle-like extensions; boxy flat-topped head, slit eye; torso and legs one fluid shape.

No red or green companions in this plate—only the B blue set. Small cursive mark top-right. Serene rhythmic reach.
