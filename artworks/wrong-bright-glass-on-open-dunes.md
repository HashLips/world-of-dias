# Wrong-Bright Glass on Open Dunes

## Name

Wrong-Bright Glass on Open Dunes

## Overview

*Wrong-Bright Glass on Open Dunes* is one shard of glass-sand lying on empty dunes, brighter than the sand around it. It does not show a path. It does not show a well. It is a danger object, not a guide.

## Description

The shard is a small faceted lump in the lower middle of the frame, irregular, chipped, and glassy. A hard point of light sits on one face. That glint is a spark on glass. It is not an arrow, not a compass rose, and not a line drawn toward anything off-frame. The lump casts a short shadow and a faint brighter pool in the sand at its base. Nothing is written on it. No map, no well-symbol, no notch that could be read as a bearing.

Around it the dunes are bare. Ridges run left and right and overlap toward a high empty horizon. There is no trail, no stone lip, no palm, no dark water, no second shard making a direction between two points. The brightness does not point. If someone wanted a path, the picture would not give them one. If someone wanted the well, the picture would not give them that either. The object is simply too bright for the hush of the sand, and the sand offers no caption.

No hand holds it, no stall displays it, no keeper raises a hammer. The danger is not performed. It is the thing itself, alone, looking like a signal while showing nowhere to go.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for a water-advertising danger that fails, on purpose, to advertise water. Brightness is not guidance.

## Lore Connection

A Well-Beacon Shard is glass-sand that pulses or marks toward water with an accuracy Silence Law treats as violence. It is a danger object of the chart black market, not a courtesy to the thirsty. Keep schools smash them when they are found. Buyers pay for what they call safety. Wells that get advertised sometimes stop being willing to be found. This plate keeps the shard and deletes the advertisement: no path, no well, no Quiet Well hiding in the next dune. Vesh Wrong-Map is the wound that became a product. The glass does not depict that wound, and it does not inherit a route from it. Kindness markets and black markets can argue over the sale somewhere else. Artifacts of Dias can file the type. The image only proves a wrong-bright lump on open sand, useless as a guide and unfit to publish a well.
