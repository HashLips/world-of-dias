# Realize

## Name

Realize

## Overview

*Realize* is the F200 plate for the downward cyan reveal. Three tan walkers on a brown arch. Orange-red sun semicircle. Long cyan ribbons from their feet. A jagged white colossal face half-hidden below.

## Description

Dark teal field. Upper: three simplified tan humanoids with tiny single blue eye-dots walk left-to-right atop a thick dark brown curved arch; blocky rectangles on the arch's left suggest a small settlement silhouette. Behind them a large vibrant orange-red semicircle sits like rising or setting light.

From each figure's feet, long solid bright-cyan jagged ribbons drop and overlap, stretching toward the bottom. Behind those ribbons a large abstract jagged white shape reads as a fragmented colossal face or skeletal structure partly obscured. A thin dark brown vertical line bisects the lower half. Bottom: flat tan ground band; lower right, three small grey rounded rectangles float. The plate is realization as projection: walking above, truth-shape below, cyan as the revealing fall.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for F200. Realize is the kept craft of the arch-walk that drops cyan onto the hidden white face.

## Lore Connection

Realize is F200's name for the hour when three walkers cross the brown arch under the orange semicircle and cyan ribbons fall until the jagged white form can be admitted. Local care says you do not pretend the lower face is absent once the ribbons show it. Resonance is feet to cyan to white; memory is the tiny blue eye-dots; wonder is teal holding both sun and skeleton-face. The plate invents no doom prophecy. It keeps the craft as manners for noticing what your walk projects downward.
