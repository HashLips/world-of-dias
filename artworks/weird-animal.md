# Weird Animal

## Name

Weird Animal

## Overview

*Weird Animal* is a single dark silhouette on pale sand-pink. A long, thick, tube-like body rises in a high arch from left to right. At the left it stands on two thin, jointed legs ending in bird-like feet with curved claws; at the right, the body simply curves down and rests its rounded end flat on the ground. There is no head anywhere. Either it is hidden in the far end, buried, or the animal does not have one.

## Description

The ground is a soft dusty pink, the color of dune sand at dusk, with nothing else on it. The creature is pure black and very smooth, its outline clean.

The legs are the most detailed part: two long, narrow limbs with a knee joint each, one placed forward and one back, ending in splayed toes with sharp hooked claws. A small bump hangs at the back of one leg like a spur or a dewclaw. They look made for walking on loose ground.

The body is enormous compared with the legs—broad as a log, arching up and over like a bridge or a bent neck, then dropping straight down on the right to a blunt end that sits flat on the sand. It could be a neck with a buried head, a tail used as a third foot, or simply the animal's other end. The pose is still and patient. It looks like it could stay that way all afternoon.

## Real-World Role

A desert-creature plate for an animal that hides whatever end it uses to think.

## Lore Connection

Lumira Sands is Wabet's southern desert of pale dunes, hidden oases, and unusual calm. Its arched black animals—called weird animals even by people who see them every week—stand on two bird-clawed legs and plant their other end in the sand, staying still for hours. The plate shows one in its usual pose. Sands folk say the planted end is listening to the dune, and that a weird animal standing near a path means the sand underfoot is settled. Keepers on the silence routes ask travelers not to step through the arch and not to dig around the planted end, however curious they are.
