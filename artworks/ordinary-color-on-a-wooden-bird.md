# Ordinary Color on a Wooden Bird

## Name

Ordinary Color on a Wooden Bird

## Overview

*Ordinary Color on a Wooden Bird* is a Farstock Click-Bird at rest: a roughly carved wooden bird, palm-sized, with a smear of ordinary paint that does not shift. It sits inert on wood. A basket of pencils is behind it. The plate does not catch a click. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

The bird is small enough for a palm and carved roughly, more toy than instrument. The wood shows tool marks. A thumb-smear of ordinary color sits on it and stays that color. It does not glow, does not match a holder, and does not change across the body. Wings, beak, and belly are a lump of carving, not a mechanism on display. No tongue is shown working. The bird is simply set on a worn wooden surface, and the picture gives it no second event.

Behind it, a basket holds pencils, points up, ordinary stock in a container. They are not arranged as a diagram, and the plate does not say they point anywhere. A small dull mark of color lies on the wood near the bird and does not behave like weather or mood. There is no compass rose, no glow toward a direction, and no pocket.

No hand shakes the bird. No face waits for a second click. The toy is inert in the frame. Whatever a click would be, this plate does not sound it and does not show it.

A still life of a small gift that stops. The bird is a toy on wood. It is not performing a secret.
