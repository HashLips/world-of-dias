# Revelation

## Name

Revelation

## Overview

*Revelation* stages a dark room where a black figure at a red desk is bodily split—torso drinking above, legs kneeling below—while a spindly arm from the wall types on a green typewriter under a huge pink wall-face watching.

## Description

Charcoal interior. Yellow circle in a blue square on the left wall. Teal pools on floor and desk like liquid shadow. The typing arm does the recording; the seated silhouette consumes; the monumental face observes.
