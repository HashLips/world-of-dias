# Olives Over a Quiet Fork

## Name

Olives Over a Quiet Fork

## Overview

*Olives Over a Quiet Fork* is Two-Ways Fork-Olive. Olive trees shade a quiet fork. No one is speaking under them.

## Description

Olive trees stand over a fork in the way, the two-ways the name already carries. They give shade. The fork is quiet. No speakers are under the boughs. Talks under olives stay cooler, which is the etiquette. The plate shows the trees that make that coolness possible and does not show the talk.

The range is Wabet and the Nauw thought borders. Sacred-soft is the kind. Woody presence is shade, boundary, or vow-site more than a single harvest clip. Local use is shade and argument tree. An argument tree with no one arguing is still the tree. The picture does not invent a dispute, a speaker, or a basket of picked fruit to prove the border.

The fork stays a fork. The olives stay shade. The quiet is the point of this frame.
