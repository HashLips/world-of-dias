# Peel Bite over the Basin

## Name

Peel Bite over the Basin

## Overview

*Peel Bite over the Basin* is a digital image study of The Soft Mark loitering over basin water, the sign itself doing the work.

## Description

The long envelope fills the upper frame. Its forward half is painted as a bright, pitted orange-gold peel; the rear half shows the dun doped cloth beneath, stitched in panels, ending in a ragged bite mark at the tail. A dark band runs along the bag's belly where the rigging gathers.

Below hangs a small open gondola of brass and wood on a fan of cables, with ballast bags at its rail. Two modest propeller engines sit out on short struts, one each side, small enough to look sulky against the size of the sign. The craft hovers low over flat water rather than landing.

To the right a timber quay shed on piles holds barrels and crates; across the water a low roofline of basin sheds and docks sits in haze. The sun is low and gold, matching the Velorian Basin gilding hour, and the blimp throws a soft shadow onto the water like a cloud that took a job.

The decorative border is framing only.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of The Soft Mark as a basin advertising blimp: gas, ballast, a gondola, modest engines, and a peel-with-a-bite painted large enough to be seen from every market below.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens The Soft Mark's established image (doped cloth, bright peel with a bite, small gondola, modest engines, loitering rather than landing) and keeps it distinct from Sky Drifters and from any band-crossing craft. It adds no new routes or hirers.

## Notes

Any tiny gondola figures that appear in the plate are plate noise, not canon; the entry fixes the crew as Cress Adel and one ballast hand. The peel's orange tint is a painted sign, not a claim about which fruit the sign depicts. Background shed layout is not a map of Mercer Shed.
