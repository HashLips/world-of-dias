# I'm Back

## Name

I'm Back

## Overview

*I'm Back* is a Sorel return plate: a translucent angular body in pale pink, yellow, and tan, white oval head with a black cross-eye, folded into a white-grey basin chair on a pedestal, a huge red circle behind, dark grey air, and a foreground split yellow left / olive-tan right with blocky shadows.

## Description

Limbs bend at sharp rhythmic angles; one leg lifts high into a jagged silhouette. The torso overlaps the red disc as if the returner and the sun-portal share space. Chair seat is curved like a scoop; high back; thick black sweep underlines the basin. The mood is arrived stillness—not a parade, a sit-down that means the road is done for now. A small white square mark sits bottom-right.

Asset reference: `assets/im-back.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A homecoming furniture plate: being back looks like geometric rest, not a speech.

## Lore Connection

Sorel roads teach leaving and returning without drama. I'm Back is the chair-hour after travel when the red circle (heat, day-end, or resonant disc) sits behind you and the ground under the pedestal still shows two colors of the threshold. Local rule: let the cross-eye settle before you ask for news. Resonance prefers a returned body that sits before it boasts. Neighbors who drag a returner straight into errands are said to tip the basin chair.
