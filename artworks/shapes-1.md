# Shapes

## Name

Shapes

## Overview

*Shapes* is the Bloomline Estuary plate for the floating measure set. Mint-green sphere. Thick red ribbon curve. Yellow open V. Cyan diagonal. Dark reddish-brown field.

## Description

Deep dark chocolate or maroon-brown ground. Upper right: large solid mint-green circle with a subtle dark drop shadow, floating. From the left a thick vibrant red ribbon-like form flows horizontal then curves sharply down toward bottom center; a thinner lighter pinkish-red stroke follows its lower edge. A sharp yellow line makes a large open corner/V—one arm sloping down from left to a vertex near bottom right, the other rising vertically along the right edge. A straight cyan-blue diagonal cuts from top middle toward bottom right with a faint dark red offset shadow. Small white gate logo bottom-left; small white cursive mark bottom-right. Rigid lines and soft ribbon in one estuary grammar.

Asset reference: `assets/shapes-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for the Bloomline Estuary. Shapes is the kept craft of the named geometric-and-ribbon set.

## Lore Connection

Shapes is the estuary's name for the hour when mint sphere, red ribbon, yellow V, and cyan cut share the dark brown field without becoming a map. Local care says you keep each shape distinct; you do not smear them into one muddy claim. Resonance is cool mint and cyan against hot red and yellow on dark; memory is the ribbon's pink edge stroke; wonder is the floating sphere's shadow. The plate invents no school exam. It keeps the craft as manners for holding several measures at once.
