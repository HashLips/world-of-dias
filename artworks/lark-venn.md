# Wool Coat on the Hull

## Name

Wool Coat on the Hull

## Overview

*Wool Coat on the Hull* is a digital image study of Lark Venn doing what crews say she always does: touching a hull before she trusts it.

## Description

Lark kneels on one knee in the left half of the frame, turned toward the right. Her right hand is pressed flat against the curved flank of an aircraft hull, its plates riveted and seamed and decorated with engraved panel work, the curve filling the right side of the picture. Her other hand rests near the ground.

She wears a long dark wool coat with a broad collar and worn cuffs over a quilted, belted flight suit, with sturdy laced boots. Her hair is cut short, close enough that a helmet would not argue. Her expression is level and watchful, looking along the hull rather than at the viewer.

The ground is rough and dark, and the background is plain parchment tone. The decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/lark-venn.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Lark Venn as the freight pilot of The Late Bruise: outer-ring wool over third-ring flight gear, and a ground-crew habit of checking the hull with her hand.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens Lark Venn's fixed description (early thirties, wool coat over a flight suit, hair cut for a helmet, touching a hull before she trusts it) and her tie to The Late Bruise. It adds no new history, skill, or route.

## Notes

Her face is a plate reading of the fixed description (wool coat, flight suit, short hair, left-thumb scar); it is not a law of fixed likeness or Earth identity. The left-thumb scar from a crate nail stays canon even where the plate does not show it. The hull is read as The Late Bruise; its panel ornament is decorative and not an airframe spec. She does not know or sell a way out of F432.
