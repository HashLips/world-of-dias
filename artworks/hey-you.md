# Hey You

## Name

Hey You

## Overview

*Hey You* is an Averra gathering under a huge pink circular light: navy silhouettes standing as perches for long-legged birds, two white figures among them, and a large purple-lavender bird leaning beak-close to a white profile in the foreground.

## Description

Ground is segmented pink, orange, and yellow geometry. Background is deep purple; the pink disc reads as low sun or studio spotlight. Most figures are tall, featureless navy. Small gold and white birds with extreme stilts perch on heads and shoulders. One white figure stands upper left among pink wing-like shapes; another white profile in the lower right meets a tall two-tone purple bird nearly face to face. Sharp white and pink shadows cast forward. The mood is ritual attention, not chaos: dark bodies still, birds mobile, one intimate lean that earns the title.

## Real-World Role

A contact plate for Averra Isle: how a bird calls a person without a crowd turning it into a show.

## Lore Connection

Averra’s damp studios and isle manners know bird-loud mornings and quiet perches. Hey You names the moment a long-legged bird selects one listener. Local rule: if a bird leans to your face, answer soft and let the navy circle keep still—do not yank the bird toward a neighbor for sport. Resonance rides the stilts and the pink light; startling the circle scatters more than feathers. The call is personal. The audience’s job is to remain furniture until dismissed.
