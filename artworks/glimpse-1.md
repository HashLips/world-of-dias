# Glimpse

## Name

Glimpse

## Overview

*Glimpse* is a Sorel meeting plate: two dark figures on tall red chairs touch chin through a red geometric frame under a blue disc—the brief honest contact Sorel allows before talk hardens.

## Description

Muted teal-green walls; darker floor line. Two slender dark silhouettes with thin white almond eyes sit on vibrant red ultra-tall-backed thin-legged chairs facing each other. Between them a bright red thin-lined structure of two inverted triangles meeting at a floor point (X / bowtie frame). Left figure leans forward, long arm cupping the right figure’s chin/jaw through the frame; right leans slightly into the touch. Large solid blue circle at top center partially behind the red frame. Long dual-tone shadows, white light patches at chair feet. Quiet intensity. Tiny white gate icon top-right.

A Sorel phenomenon plate: one chin-touch glimpse through the frame; then speak or leave.
