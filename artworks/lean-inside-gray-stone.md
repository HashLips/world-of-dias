# Lean Inside Gray Stone

## Name

Lean Inside Gray Stone

## Overview

*Lean Inside Gray Stone* is Elder Shelf as a place with no house in it. Trees lean in the wind on a cliff. They stand inside a ring of gray stone. No building is inside the ring. No tree species is named. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

Wind-leaning trees occupy a clifftop, their lean permanent enough to look like the wind's handwriting rather than a gust passing through the frame. They are trees. This record does not name a species. Needles or leaves, if the eye sorts them, are not a botanical claim. The entry does not fix a kind, and the plate does not invent one.

A ring of gray stone holds the trees. The stones are the ring that marks the shelf, not the footings of a structure. No building is inside the ring. There is no roof, no wall, no shelter slipped in among the trunks, and no temple made by implication. The mood the place is given is quiet, not secret. The picture has the quiet of an empty ring. It does not add a hidden door.

No day pass is posted. No naming ceremony is underway. No overnight camp and no tool cache sit inside the stone. A visitor who would treat the silence as emptiness is not here to make that mistake. The picture is the cliff, the leaning trees, and the gray ring, with no building and no species.
