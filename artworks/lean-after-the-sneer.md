# Lean After the Sneer

## Name

Lean After the Sneer

## Overview

*Lean After the Sneer* shows a slender dancer leaning past mockery—black silhouette, yellow slit-eye, blue motion limbs—caught in the instant after a crowd’s insult becomes tempo.

## Description

A geometric stage of yellow walls and pink monolith. The figure leans back on a dark plinth as if gravity agreed to argue; a red crescent and a pale discarded mask-shape rest near the feet. The composition reads as performance after judgment: the sneer has already been spoken; the body answers by continuing.

Asset reference: `assets/lean-after-the-sneer.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A character-energy piece for Sorel expressive survival—pair with *Despise Me* when showing how dance turns insult into courage.

## Lore Connection

Visual companion to [`stories/the-rose-at-second-crossing.md`](../stories/the-rose-at-second-crossing.md) and [`inhabitants/despise-dancer.md`](../inhabitants/despise-dancer.md). The discarded mask-shape is often read as the crowd’s old face, left on the floor when the yard learns it can breathe.
