## Overview

*Night's Breeze* depicts a vast pale face of wind-lines answering a small horn-player—colossal breath meeting ordinary song on a black sea of night.

Monochrome graphic: leaf-void eyes, flowing white strands pouring toward a tiny reclining figure with a trumpet. No House seal. No Frequency Zero caption. Only the suggestion that quiet water may want a song before it wants a door.
