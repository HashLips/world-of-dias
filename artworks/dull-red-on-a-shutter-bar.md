# Dull Red on a Shutter Bar

## Name

Dull Red on a Shutter Bar

## Overview

*Dull Red on a Shutter Bar* is a Night-Latch Thread in its day color: dull red, knotted on a plain wooden shutter bar. It is not near-black. It is not glowing. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

A length of thread is knotted on a plain wooden bar across a shutter. The thread is dull red, easy to take for ordinary mending thread, not a near-black and not a light. It does not glow. The knot is a knot. The bar is plain wood, without a letter, a guild mark, or a second color painted on as a warning.

The plate shows the thread tied and dull red. It does not show a hand trying the latch, a night, or a darkening. It does not sound an alarm, name anyone, or hold the bar shut. The red stays dull. Nothing on the thread drinks the frame down toward black, and nothing on it follows a road.

No wrist wears it. No one stands at the shutter. The thread is household thread on a plain wooden bar, dull red, and still.

## Lore Connection

A Night-Latch Thread is a dark thread tied on a latch. It watches a door. It does not follow a traveler. Spun dull red by day, it is easy to mistake for mending thread. Knotted on a latch or a shutter bar, it drinks color when metal is tested in the night by a hand that does not live there, and morning shows the darkening toward near-black. This plate is the day thread on a plain wooden shutter bar: dull red, not near-black, and not glowing. It does not show the test, the night, or the morning change. It does not identify the hand, sound an alarm, or hold the door shut. A household still has to wake. It is outer-ring practicality, a sign readable without owing a guild, the sort sold beside ordinary thread so no one has to whisper. The plate adds no whisper and no guild mark. On a road it is useless. Worn as jewelry it is a bad design, and it is not on a wrist here. It was not the lock, and the picture does not retcon it into one.
