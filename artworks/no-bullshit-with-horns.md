# No Bullshit with Horns

## Name

No Bullshit with Horns

## Overview

*No Bullshit with Horns* is the Brindle March plate for the horned neighbor. Dark chocolate silhouette. Long straight horns in a wide V. One white almond eye. Foreleg extended. Teal shadows on pale pink ground.

## Description

The upper field is flat salmon-pink. The creature stands on receding pale pink planes, body a solid dark chocolate-brown silhouette in three-quarter view, head turned slightly toward the viewer. The horns are the first fact: exceptionally long, thick, and straight, opening upward and outward in a wide V that owns the top of the frame. One sharp white almond eye is the only interior mark—intense, watchful, not decorative.

The front-left leg extends far to the side on a sharp diagonal, a ritual stance more than a casual step. Shadows fall forward and left in pale teal or turquoise, geometric and wrong-colored for ordinary ground, which is how Brindle plates often mark a presence that the march must take seriously. A tiny black square emblem with a white glyph sits extreme lower left.

No rider. No fence. No market crowd. The plate is the animal and its teal shadow alone.

An inhabitant plate for Brindle March. The horns and the teal shadow are the introduction.

## Lore Connection

No Bullshit with Horns is the march's name for this horned neighbor-shape: wide V, one white eye, extended foreleg, teal shadow that does not match the pink ground. Fruit-road walkers treat the stance as a local care signal—stop inventing soft stories when the horns are already honest. Resonance is the watchful eye; wonder is the turquoise shadow; memory is how children are taught to give the creature room without turning it into a mascot. The plate invents no hunt and no saddle. It keeps the silhouette and the wrong-colored shadow as manners toward a presence that will not shrink to fit a joke.
