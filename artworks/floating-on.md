# Floating On

## Name

Floating On

## Overview

*Floating On* depicts a towering red high-back chair whose spine is pierced with dozens of white almond slits—like eyes or vents—set in an empty blue room.

## Description

Unnaturally tall backrest, blocky legs, sharp geometric shadow. No sitter. The chair itself seems to watch.

Asset reference: `assets/floating-on.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A surreal furniture emblem: power as an empty seat that still observes.

## Lore Connection

Glassfold and House circles treat it as the **witness seat**—what you sit in when you want the wall of eyes to take you seriously. Some say the slits drink attention; others say they release it. Resonant House rumor places similar chairs in sealed chambers without confirming ownership.

## Notes

Companion to *Wall of Eyes* and *Eye in the Sky*.
