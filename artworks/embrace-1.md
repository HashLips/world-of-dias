# Embrace

## Name

Embrace

## Overview

*Embrace* is an Aelwyn Canopy spacing plate: a yellow reclining figure rests among six pink-sphere poles—the hold that measures care by even gaps rather than by crushing closeness.

## Description

Muted teal-grey field. Bright yellow humanoid lies horizontal on its back across the lower third, knees bent up, single small black almond eye on a rounded head, otherwise featureless. Six identical thin white poles rise full height, each capped with a large vibrant pink sphere banded darker magenta with a small white crescent highlight. Poles pass at neck, chest, waist, legs—pinning, framing, or staffing the body without ornate cage drama. Faint grey shadows left from pole bases. Quiet, clinical, enigmatic.

## Real-World Role

A canopy artifact plate: embrace means even contact points, not a smother.

## Lore Connection

In the Aelwyn Canopy, **Embrace** is a kept spacing craft. Pink spheres are soft markers of where touch is allowed; white poles keep the count honest. Yellow reclining form is the guest who accepted measured hold. Wonder is tenderness with meter. Crushing hug that ignores poles is bad craft.
