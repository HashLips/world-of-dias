# Respect Thrown

## Name

Respect Thrown

## Overview

*Respect Thrown* is the F960 plate for bells above the spiked row. Two gold bells on a thin blue thread. Black blade-spikes with white dots. Teal-grey mound. Mint field.

## Description

Light mint-green ground. Across the upper third a single thin vibrant blue line runs horizontal; from it hang two bright orange-yellow flared bells close together, small rounded caps, simple white curved shine-marks on the metal. Bottom edge: a row of sharp black blade-like spikes or thorns, each topped with a small perfectly round white dot—dew, seed, or tiny glow. Behind the spikes a low rounded dark teal-grey mound. No hand ringing. The plate is the hanging pair held careful above a ground that can cut.

Asset reference: `assets/respect-thow.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for F960. Respect Thrown is the kept craft of bells carried above spikes without cheap noise.

## Lore Connection

Respect Thrown is F960's name for the hour when the twin gold bells hang on blue thread and the white-tipped black spikes wait below. Local care says respect is thrown as a soft sound only when the hanging is steady—not as a smash into the blades. Resonance is warm bells over cold spikes; memory is the white dots; wonder is mint air holding both. The plate invents no duel. It keeps the craft as manners for offering sound without disrespecting the ground's teeth.
