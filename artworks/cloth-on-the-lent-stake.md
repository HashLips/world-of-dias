# Cloth on the Lent Stake

## Name

Cloth on the Lent Stake

## Overview

*Cloth on the Lent Stake* is a digital image study of Yarrow Bine marking the Lent Strip in wet weather: lean holder, brimmed hat, stake-mallet hands, cloth that means not today when grass is too wet to forgive.

## Description

Yarrow is shown chest-up in rain mist—lean middle-aged holder. The plate depicts a man with dark wavy hair under a wide-brimmed dark hat glistening with moisture, short salt-and-pepper beard, weary focused eyes. Light long sleeves under a dark wrap-like vest or shawl. Both wet weathered hands grip a thick vertical wooden mallet or stake. To his right the Lent stake is bound with layered wet off-white cloth and bundled green sword-like leaves dripping rain. Background trees blur in haze and falling water.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/yarrow-bine.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Yarrow Bine as holder of the Lent Strip lend: marks, rested field, and the cost when too many feet disagree.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens Yarrow and Lent Strip without making him grower of Vitrin or closing the lend decision. Wet cloth on the stake stays wet-day courtesy, not a signal to another band.
