# Flying with Cats

## Name

Flying with Cats

## Overview

*Flying with Cats* is a Cinder Nook travel plate: a lavender glider moves through mint void with three darker purple ear-pointed companions and white wake ribbons—the nook habit of flying only with neighbors who leave a readable trail.

## Description

Solid vibrant mint-green field, no horizon. Largest figure: light lavender-purple humanoid silhouette diving or gliding diagonally bottom-left to top-right, arms forward, legs trailing. Three smaller darker purple forms share the trajectory, each with two small pointed head protrusions like ears or horns, each trailing a bright white curved ribbon-wake from the lower body. Weightless, graphic, fluid. Tiny black square icon top-right.

Asset reference: `assets/flying-with-cats.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A nook inhabitant plate: share the glide; keep wakes white so others can follow or avoid.

## Lore Connection

In Cinder Nook, **Flying with Cats** names non-human ear-pointed companions of air or ash-soft flight. The lavender lead is any careful glider. White wakes are courtesy trails. Wonder is company without collision. Dias nook name—not an Earth pet meme claim.
