# Bar

## Name

Bar

## Overview

*Bar* is an Averra Isle listening-hall plate: a dark figure sits alone at a white wedge with a green cup while nine smiling blue peg-bodies watch from the red field—company that is cheerful, uniform, and not necessarily kind.

## Description

The upper field is solid vibrant red; the lower is bright yellow. A stark dark figure bust leans at a sharp white diagonal bar or table that wedges in from the lower left. The figure’s head tips slightly up and right, marked by a single small white almond eye. Arms rest on the surface, holding a small rectangular green cup. Behind, nine elongated blue ghost-figures stand in two rows—five medium-blue in front, four lighter cyan behind—each tapering to a point, each with two white slit eyes and a wide white smile. They surround the sitter without touching. Flat color blocks only. A tiny white square mark sits top-right. Mood: quiet contemplation under an eerie cheerful crowd.

## Real-World Role

A guest-hall manners plate for Averra: how to sit with a cup when the room’s smiles are louder than its listening.

## Lore Connection

On Averra Isle, **Bar** names a listening ledge more than a party. The green cup is the guest’s measure—sip slow, speak when ready. The black sitter is anyone who needs one honest seat. The blue smiling row is the hall’s background chorus: useful for warmth, dangerous when it replaces attention. Averra talk says you do not owe the chorus your story just because it is smiling. You owe the cup, the wedge, and whoever actually listens. Wonder here is the courage to stay singular in a red room full of identical good moods. If the smiles start answering for you, leave the cup and walk.
