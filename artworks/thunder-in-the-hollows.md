# Thunder in the Hollows

## Name

Thunder in the Hollows

## Overview

*Thunder in the Hollows* is a digital image study of Ashcalm Rise Thunder-Boars as territorial wallow beasts.

## Description

A shoulder-high bristled ash-dark boar charges or holds a wallow stance—hooked tusks, heavy gait implied. Ember-Deer are absent from the frame; scent-flight is lore, not dramatized chase. Rocky Rise ground behind.

Decorative border is framing only.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Ashcalm Rise territorial fauna travelers respect at wallows.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens Thunder-Boars without inventing taming or hunt rites.
