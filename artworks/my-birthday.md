# My Birthday

## Name

My Birthday

## Overview

*My Birthday* balances a yellow banana on a red stump under a huge cream and black foot, with a cyan line and black hook dangling from the fruit.

## Description

Surreal stack on a light blue floor against deep blue: oversized foot (black sole, cream top) pressing a yellow banana that rests on a jagged red stump. Thin cyan fishing line ends in a black hook. Long jagged black shadows rake left. Small corner mark.
