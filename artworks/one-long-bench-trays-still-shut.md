# One Long Bench, Trays Still Shut

## Name

One Long Bench, Trays Still Shut

## Overview

*One Long Bench, Trays Still Shut* is the Glassfold Phase Bench. One long bench. Empty. Cold glass light. Stamp kits sit in locked trays. The trays stay shut.

## Description

Cold glass light. One long bench worn smooth. Stamp kits in locked trays. The plate keeps the bench empty, which is also what the place does during wall-eye intervals: it empties. After a window it fills with quiet arguments about corroboration. The arguments are not staged. The bench is empty.

The trays are locked. They stay shut. No kit is opened, no wax is displayed, no confidence mark is designed on a stamp face. Fear documented is honorable. Charisma without a stamp is not. The plate does not document a fear and does not show charisma. It shows the furniture of the rule: the long bench, the cold glass light, the shut trays.
