# Princess

## Name

Princess

## Overview

*Princess* is the Coral Strand plate for the dancer on the mask-crown. Dark brown silhouette mid-dance. Seven orange pillars. Magenta ovals with cyan rims. White mask-base with black eye-slits.

## Description

Pale pink field. At bottom, a white semicircle reads like the upper half of a ritual mask or face, two sharp black horizontal marks as narrowed eyes or brows. From that base rises a large vibrant orange structure that splits into seven finger-like columns fanning slightly upward. At each tip sits a bright magenta-pink oval outlined in thin glowing cyan.

Before the pillars, a dark brown humanoid silhouette dances: one leg planted, the other bent sharp at the knee; one arm thrown diagonally up-left, the other extended horizontal right. A subtle orange offset shadow vibrates behind the body. Elongated, graceful, more motion than anatomy. No throne, no court caption. The plate is the dance on the seven-tipped crown of the watching white base.

An artifact plate for Coral Strand. Princess is the kept craft of the named dance on the mask-crown.
