# T1

## Name

T1

## Overview

*T1* is a single red figure on pale pink, head drawn in profile, neck long, eye reduced to a thin white crescent. A loose, hand-drawn white ring loops around the head like a breath that has not closed. The shoulders below are a darker, translucent red that rises into peaks, so the body reads half as person and half as hill.

## Description

Everything warm in the plate is one color family. The head is a saturated, flat red with no shading, an ear marked by a single darker curl, the chin cut sharply back toward a throat that stretches longer than comfort. The eye is not open or shut; it is a white slit lit from inside, like someone listening rather than looking.

The white ring is the plate's only cool element. It is uneven—thicker at the top, thinning to a gap at the lower right where the neck passes through—so it reads as a boundary drawn by hand around a feeling rather than a halo of rank. Behind it the pink ground is almost skin-colored. Below, the darker red shoulders overlap the bright red chest in transparent layers, like two tides of the same color crossing.

The composition is quiet. No background, no tool, no second figure. Just a flushed person inside a ring that does not quite close.

## Real-World Role

A reference image for a first, mild emotional reading—warmth that is visible but still held inside a boundary.

## Lore Connection

In F380 the Hearttide band, feeling and weather couple, and the Bloomline Holding Terrace runs short stabilization holds before people vote, bargain, or mediate. T1 is the plate the terrace's tenders call the first tone card: the body flushed, the ring drawn, the gap in the ring still open. It marks the state where a person is warm enough to change the water around them but not yet carrying anyone else's tide. Tenders show it to new arrivals and ask, without judgment, whether they are past it. The open gap in the ring is the courtesy—at T1 you may still leave the hold without explaining yourself.
