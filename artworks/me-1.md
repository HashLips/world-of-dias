# Me

## Name

Me

## Overview

*Me* stands alone in Cinder Nook scale: black slender body from the waist up out of light blue-grey water, one arm high toward upper left, head tilted up, single white almond eye, elongated dark reflection toward the viewer, vibrant blue sky with soft peach horizontal clouds, jagged dark-grey mountain line, ripple ticks on water.

## Description

Vast quiet. The raised arm is claim and question together—me, here, in this water. No boat, no second figure. Tiny glyph top-left.

Asset reference: `assets/me-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A self plate: naming me without crowding the horizon.

## Lore Connection

Cinder Nook smoke-readers still walk into cold rim water to say Me before winter work. Local rule: raise the arm, keep the eye, do not shout over someone else’s reflection. Resonance prefers solitary first words. Dragging a friend in to “share the me” empties the rite into noise.
