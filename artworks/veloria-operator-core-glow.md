# Glow More than Facade

## Name

Glow More than Facade

## Overview

*Glow More than Facade* is a digital image study of Veloria's central core as most people know it: a colossal glow seen past a rail, not a building you can describe.

## Description

A heavy curved rail sweeps in from the lower left, its surface dark and polished like something leaned on by many elbows, and beyond it a broad plated deck runs toward the center. The space ahead is colossal: walls of dark, finely engraved material curve up and around in layered bands, ribbed and channelled, refusing ordinary names.

At the center those bands pinch into a lens shape, and inside it sits a warm amber glow, nested in concentric rims. The light is the only clear thing in the image. Everything else is texture, scale, and depth, with small lights scattered through the far structure.

No Operators, visitors, doors, or controls are shown. The decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/veloria-operator-core-glow.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Veloria's core as seen from an approach: realm-scale hypertech experienced as glow and silhouette rather than as a tour.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the established image of the central core from Veloria City and Nauw Layered Civic Architecture (materials that refuse ordinary names, sealed approaches, seen as glow more than facade) and the Core Approach Overlook's lesson that you may look but may not assume a door. The work inside belongs to the Valorian Operators and is not shown.

## Notes

The lens-shaped glow is not an eye and not a readable god; do not canonize it as watching anyone. The plate does not reveal Operator chambers, controls, or mechanisms. The rail evokes the overlook's elbow-worn rail; it does not place the viewer inside inner-ring clearance.
