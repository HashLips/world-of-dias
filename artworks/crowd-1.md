# Crowd

## Name

Crowd

## Overview

*Crowd* is an Ashcalm Rise release plate: one figure flings arms wide while hollow white shards radiate outward—the moment a single body feels like many, and the rise must decide whether that is solidarity or shatter.

## Description

Solid black ground. Thick white outlines define a central humanoid: head in right-facing profile with one large wide-open eye, simple ear, prominent nose, slightly downturned open mouth; thin elongated torso with two small white chest dots; lower body a long curved pillar off the bottom edge. Arms stretch wide and slightly up, ending in large three-fingered open hands. From the torso, sharp hollow triangular shards radiate—three large right, two large and one thin left, plus a tiny stray fragment lower right. Stark graphic drama. Small white signature bottom-right.

Asset reference: `assets/crowd-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A rise inhabitant plate: when one voice becomes many pieces, gather carefully or you only collect sharp edges.

## Lore Connection

On Ashcalm Rise, **Crowd** names the non-human (or more-than-one) feeling that breaks a lone body into shard-talk. Arms wide can be welcome or breaking. Rise rule: if shards fly, pick them up with consent, do not pocket them as trophies, and do not force the open-eyed face to smile. Wonder is release that still owes reassembly.

Black field. White-outlined wide-armed figure. Radiating hollow shards. Single shocked profile eye.
