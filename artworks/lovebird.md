# Lovebird

## Name

Lovebird

## Overview

*Lovebird* centers a dive on cobalt: white-headed black-winged bird aimed at a yellow-ochre bell flower, spindly white talons on petal edges, long curved white stem with one leaf rising from a reddish-pink pot rim decorated with darker red eye-circles, deep black pot interior, fluid darker-blue shadow behind stem and bloom.

## Description

Diagonal from pot to bird. Focus is quiet—creature and cultivated flower meeting. Tiny mark top-right. Mood is graceful attention, not frenzy.

Asset reference: `assets/lovebird.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A single-bird plate: lovebird as the dive that respects the pot’s eye-marks.

## Lore Connection

Aelwyn Canopy keeps Lovebird distinct from the paired Love Birds—one diver, one tended bloom. Local rule: do not move the red eye-pot while talons are set; wait for lift. Resonance prefers stems left curved. Snatching the flower mid-hover empties the pot’s care.

## Notes

Black-white bird, yellow flower, red marked pot, cobalt air.
