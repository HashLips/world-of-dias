# Third Chair

## Name

Third Chair

## Overview

*Third Chair* shows a solitary red chair with an impossibly tall back—the empty seat left for the missing stakeholder at a Verdant Reach mediation meal.

## Description

Against a two-tone blue room, the chair’s stretched backrest casts a long bent shadow. No diner sits. The exaggeration is comic and solemn at once: the invitation can feel larger than the room, and still the seat stays open.
