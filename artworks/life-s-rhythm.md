# Life's Rhythm

## Name

Life's Rhythm

## Overview

*Life's Rhythm* is a solitary blue lamp: dome base bottom-right, thin neck arcing wide left into a bell shade half bright blue / half navy, long black shadow stretching left on dark grey desk segments, white upper field with one light-grey horizontal band behind the curve.

## Description

No person, no book—rhythm is the lamp’s ready arc and the shadow that keeps time with it. Tiny black mark bottom-right. Precise, modern, quiet.

## Real-World Role

A work-light plate: life’s rhythm as the lamp you leave arched for the next hour.

F432 desks keep Life's Rhythm lamps for measured work—neck arched, shade split showing which side faces the page. Local rule: do not slam the neck flat when you leave; leave the arc for the next sit.
