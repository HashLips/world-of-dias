# Own Train

## Name

Own Train

## Overview

*Own Train* is the Brindle March plate for shared grip without a center prize. Five white hands reach inward. Each palm holds a light-azure eight-spoke wheel. A jagged black arc cuts the deep blue field.

## Description

Solid deep blue ground. Five stylized solid white hands enter from the edges, each showing four fingers and a thumb in an open reaching gesture aimed at an empty center—no fruit, no coin, no captive object there. In every palm a light azure circular mark: thin outer ring, eight straight spokes from a center point, like a simplified wheel, compass rose, or iris.

Across the upper half a thick irregular jagged black shape arcs—torn-paper rough—from near top-center down over the leftmost hand toward the top-right, a dark weather-line over the otherwise clean hands. Composition is balanced and tense: common reach, empty middle, shared palm-wheels, and one torn black factor the march does not pretend is gone.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Brindle March. Own Train is the kept craft of many hands agreeing to move together.

## Lore Connection

Own Train is the march's name for the hour when five (or more) palms show the azure eight-spoke and reach the same empty center without snatching a private prize. Local care says the train is owned together or not at all; the jagged black arc is the honesty that weather and risk still cut the picture. Resonance is the matched wheels in every palm; memory is the empty center that refuses a trophy; wonder is how white the hands stay on deep blue while the black tear hangs above. The plate invents no locomotive saga. It keeps the shared reach as manners for moving as one without eraseing each hand's mark.
