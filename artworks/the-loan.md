# The Loan

## Name

The Loan

## Overview

*The Loan* is a sequence in one frame. At the left edge, by a teal wall, a figure drawn entirely in shadow-black stands upright and holds out a small red flask on an open palm. Moving right, a pale white figure appears three times: first lunging forward to pluck the flask by its neck, then upright and hunched with the flask held to its face, then stretched flat on the brown floor with the flask rolled away beside its hand. Behind them all hangs a huge salmon-colored circle like a low sun.

## Description

The colors are few and deliberate. Teal wall on the left, chocolate brown wall on the right, rust-brown floor, salmon disc. The lender is solid shadow with one thin white eye and a calm, straight posture. The borrower is pure white with small red eyes that match the flask.

The three borrowers overlap like frames of a run. The first leans in eagerly, fingers pinching the flask by its top rather than receiving it in a palm. The second, further right, stands bent with the flask at nose height, eyes narrowed. The third lies on the floor, arms forward, flask fallen a little out of reach. The red flask appears three times too, always small, always the brightest thing in the plate.

The lender never moves. The plate's motion is entirely the borrower's: grabbing, breathing in, going down.

## Real-World Role

A cautionary plate about how a loan is received, told through the borrower's hurry rather than the lender's intent.

## Lore Connection

Pot Signal Lane is the second-ring Veloria district where flower pots are signals, favors are currency, and debts move quietly between doorways. The Loan is a plate older lane residents show newcomers about how to take what is offered. In the lane, a loan is held out on an open palm and must be received the same way, palm under palm. The borrower in the plate pinches it by the neck and hurries, and the three white figures show where hurry leads. Lane talk does not blame the lender standing in the doorway; it says the red was always meant to be accepted slowly, or not at all.

## Notes

One shadow-black lender at the left, one pale borrower shown three times. Red flask on open palm, sniffed, then dropped. Salmon circle behind. Maker mark top right. The lender's black is doorway shadow, not a description of anyone; keep the lesson on the borrower's manner.
