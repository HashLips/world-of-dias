# Loyal

## Name

Loyal

## Overview

*Loyal* profiles red on cyan: clean forehead-nose-mouth-chin facing right, tan layered edges, upper skull opened to black where white fluid ribbons swirl around a single almond eye looking out at the viewer, thick yellow diagonal band crossing chest and neck.

## Description

Outer face looks aside; inner eye looks at you—loyalty as double facing. Yellow band feels like a vow mark or transversal duty. High-contrast pop flatness. Mood is cerebral and bright.

Asset reference: `assets/loyal-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A loyalty plate: keep an inner eye honest while the profile keeps watch.

## Lore Connection

Songripe Isles use Loyal when tide-song cousins swear a yellow-band duty. Local rule: if the inner eye is shown, do not ask the profile to lie. Resonance prefers cyan-clear air around the vow. Painting over the inner eye to hide doubt empties the band.
