# Tranquility

## Name

Tranquility

## Overview

*Tranquility* is the same person four times, or four people holding the same stillness. dark silhouettes squat in a row across a hot orange-red ground, knees bent wide, feet turned out, hands tucked behind their backs, chins level. Every body is identical. Only the heads differ: the first wears a tall flat-topped hat with a brim, the second a sharp cone, the third has a floating oval hoop hovering above its bare head, and the fourth wears a rounded cap with a long bill.

## Description

The ground is two oranges: a deep vermilion sky and a lighter salmon hill curving up from the lower left. The figures stand on the hill's edge, evenly spaced, the fourth half cut off by the right border as if the row continues.

The pose is low and held, the kind of squat that burns after a minute and calms after five. Shoulders are broad and rounded, necks short, arms hidden. Nothing in the silhouettes moves.

The hats are the only story. Tall hat, cone, hoop, cap. Each is drawn as plainly as the bodies, flat black, and each changes how the same body reads—formal, foolish, lit, casual. The hoop is just a ring on nothing, hovering, its wearer's head bare beneath it. The repetition is so strict that the small differences become loud.

## Real-World Role

A recurrence plate about holding one stillness together while letting the small differences show.

## Lore Connection

F120 is the echo band, where events recur in recognizable patterns but never repeat exactly, and The Returning Span is the region where those recurrences become visible enough to chart. Tranquility is the name of a Span practice for groups of four: everyone holds the same low squat, hands behind the back, for as long as the slowest breath lasts, each wearing a different hat. Then the hats move one place down the row and the hold repeats. The plate shows one round. Span people say the calm does not come from the sameness of the pose but from noticing, round after round, how much a hat changes the same body.
