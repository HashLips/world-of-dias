# Waxed Cork and a Warm Brown Film

## Name

Waxed Cork and a Warm Brown Film

## Overview

*Waxed Cork and a Warm Brown Film* is one small vial on a bare sill. The glass is plain and thick. The cork is waxed. Inside, a faint warm brown film clings and does not gather into a pooled liquid. There is no label. The glass does not show a vision, a street, or a loaf.

## Description

The vial is a thumb of glass, thick enough to look like it was made to be carried and not to be admired as crystal. Its walls are plain. No etching, no house line, no measure marks, no paper band. The cork is waxed shut, so the picture is of something closed. Whatever the object is for happens when it is opened, and it is not open here. On the inner glass a faint warm brown film catches the light. It is a stain of color, a breath of brown, not a level of liquid you could tilt and not a dose you could count. Nothing pools at the bottom as a drink or an ink.

The sill under it is bare. No crumbs, no second vial, no bakery ticket, no cloth, no name written where a gift would explain itself. Light through the glass stays light. It does not resolve into an oven, a ring street, a face, or a morning kept on purpose. A smell cannot be painted as crust and hearth. The plate does not pretend otherwise. Closed, the vial is glass, wax, film, and a sill. That is the whole inventory.

It does not show whose pocket it left, which day's bread the film remembers, or whether the scent would last past a few breaths. It does not show a hard road made into a kitchen. There is no kitchen. There is a sill.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for a memory you cannot see while the cork is in. The limit is visible as absence: no label, no vision, no pooled dose.

## Lore Connection

A Bread-Smoke Vial is a stoppered thumb of glass that, opened, smells of that morning's outer-ring bread in Veloria City: crust and hearth for a few breaths, then gone, and the next day it is that day's bread rather than a preserved perfect morning. The entry is exact about the body of the thing: thick glass, waxed cork, a faint warm brown film that never quite becomes a liquid. This plate is that body, on a bare sill, which is one of the places such a vial is said to sit, beside pockets and gifts for people leaving the ring. The film is not a pooled liquid. The cork stays in. Nothing in the glass is a vision, because the vial does not show visions and does not make a hard road feel like a kitchen. Pim restocks from a bakery he can walk to. The picture has neither the walk nor the baker nor the oven, only the closed glass. It is homesickness with a stopper, and the stopper is still in. It is not an emotional drug, not a Hearttide tool, and not a label you can read instead of remembering.
