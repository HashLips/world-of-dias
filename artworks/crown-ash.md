# Paper-Light on Crown Ash

## Name

Paper-Light on Crown Ash

## Overview

Paper-Light on Crown Ash is a digital image study of the visible bearing at Crown Ash, where pale stone holds the last light above a darker cliff.

## Description

The plate shows pale, wind-scoured blocks on a dark sea cliff. Last light turns the stone like paper held to a lamp, keeping Crown Ash legible as a silhouette and a bearing rather than an invitation to land.

The image stays with cliff bearing, pale stone, and the briefness of the light. It does not establish battlements, a banner, or a stair as part of the landmark.

Asset reference: `assets/crown-ash.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Crown Ash as an offshore bearing and a study in Averra’s last-light weather.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens Crown Ash’s established image without solving its interior or turning its silhouette into a landing story.
