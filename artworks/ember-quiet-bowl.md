# Mended Ember Quiet Bowl

## Name

Mended Ember Quiet Bowl

## Overview

*Mended Ember Quiet Bowl* is one thick clay bowl, cracked and mended where the crack shows, holding plain broth. No hands. No lifted toast. It is a vessel for a last warm mouthful, not a chalice and not a product that looks unused.

## Description

The bowl fills the frame. The clay is thick, ash-tempered in color, and rough at the rim. A crack runs the wall and the mend is left visible, a shiny scar rather than a counterfeit of new clay. Inside, the broth is plain: pale liquid, a scatter of salt, and one small piece of root. Nothing in it asks for a speech. There is no spoon, no hand, no second bowl, and no stack that would show the underside mark Keepers use when they turn a bowl up.

The ground is bare and warm. A border frames the object the way a shelf frames a tool. It does not turn the broth into ceremony. The plate does not settle whose turn this is, whether the heat will last, or what the viability dot would say if the bowl were flipped. It does not show a fee, a toast, or a room. Crack and mend are the honesty. The broth is the use.

## Lore Connection

Kindle Spur rests use these bowls for shared broth without a speech attached. Repairs are supposed to show. A toast poured into one is a misuse, and this plate keeps the bowl down, full of food, with nobody's hand available to lift it for a line.
