# Walking

## Name

Walking

## Overview

*Walking* is only legs and shoes. On the left, a pair of coral-pink legs strides forward, both feet in soft mint-green slip-ons with white soles—one planted flat, one lifting at the heel. On the right, a pale gray leg steps in from the edge wearing a coral-pink slip-on with a mint-green sole. The colors trade places between the two walkers like a rhyme.

## Description

The background is a flat mid-gray wall above a very dark teal floor, and the horizon sits behind the walkers' ankles. There is no road texture, no sand, no sky—only the stride.

The left walker's legs are long and tapered, one in front, one behind, the back foot tipping onto its toes as the heel lifts. The shoes are heel-less and soft, more like slippers than boots, with thin white soles that barely lift them off the floor.

The right walker shows only a calf and a foot, drawn in pale gray. The shoe is the same soft shape but reversed in color: coral upper, mint sole. Its toe points toward the left walker, nearly touching. The plate is about matching without matching: two people in step, each wearing the other's colors.

## Real-World Role

A desert-route plate about walking softly in pairs, each carrying the other's color.

## Lore Connection

Saltwhisper Road is the main restricted dune route across Lumira Sands, less a highway than a negotiated permission; Keepers treat the wind's whisper over salt crust as useful acoustics. Walkers are expected to go soft-footed and in pairs. Walking shows the soft slip-ons sold at the road's start in complementary colors—mint with a white sole, coral with a green sole—so that each partner wears one color of the other's set. The point is practical and courteous at once: Keepers can see at a distance who is walking with whom, and nobody's heel strikes the crust.
