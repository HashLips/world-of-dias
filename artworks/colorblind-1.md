# Colorblind

## Name

Colorblind

## Overview

*Colorblind* is a Wabet seeing-weather plate: a spindly black hand tips a diamond-grid cone of gray melt-spheres while a dark puddle already waits on the floor—the hour when color leaves and value-care must take over.

## Description

Light-gray room corner: vertical wall left, flat floor below. From lower left, a thin black arm with three elongated pointed fingers grips a tall conical vessel marked in dark diamond-grid, tilted down-right. Atop it, three spheres in graded grays—medium speckled top, solid black melting with two thick drips, lighter gray behind. Directly under the tilt, a jagged dark puddle on the floor. Flat grayscale only. Mood: minimalist, melancholy, careful grip on a fleeting chill. Small square mark bottom-right.

Asset reference: `assets/colorblind-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A Wabet phenomenon plate: when color fails, keep holding what still has weight and do not pretend the puddle is nothing.

## Lore Connection

Around Wabet, **Colorblind** names a look when surfaces lose hue and only value remains. Quiet Well Approach talk says the spindly grip is any careful hand—or non-human reach—trying not to drop a melting truth. The cone’s grid is ordinary craft made strange by the weather. Wonder is continuing care without the comfort of color. Not a medical insult; a local weather-of-seeing name.
