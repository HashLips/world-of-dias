# Meet the Post Man

## Name

Meet the Post Man

## Overview

*Meet the Post Man* is a Bloomline greeting face: pale irregular shield face, left eye a large black circle with white dot, right eye a vertical black oval with white crescent, wide rectangular mouth of blocky white teeth in black grid, long olive-green floppy cap trailing left to a bright blue bead, dusky pink neck, red torso dappled with yellow organic spots, background split olive-brown / ochre-tan.

## Description

Fixed grin reads as hold-still countenance, not cheer ad. Mismatched eyes suggest dual notice—route and person. Blue bead tips the cap like a signal weight. Mood is peculiar intensity, useful at a door.

## Real-World Role

A courier-meet plate: know the post face before you take the packet.

## Lore Connection

Bloomline Estuary routes keep Meet the Post Man as the gritted quiet greeting when packets change hands. Local rule: match the blue bead’s bob before you speak your name; do not flinch at the teeth. Resonance prefers mismatched eyes that both stay open. Snatching a packet before the bead settles empties estuary courtesy.
