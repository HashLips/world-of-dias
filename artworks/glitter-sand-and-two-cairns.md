# Glitter Sand and Two Cairns

## Name

Glitter Sand and Two Cairns

## Overview

Glitter Sand and Two Cairns is a digital image study of the Wabet approach where desert manners begin to matter more than a correct map.

## Description

Glitter sand carries the light, and two cairns disagree without becoming a single arrow. The plate stops at the approach: no well is shown, located, or solved. Its quiet is a practice of shortened steps and withheld certainty rather than a clue to coordinates.

A visual record of the approach’s water ethics, where a map can be wrong in order to keep a place from becoming a raid map.
