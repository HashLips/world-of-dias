# Ghost Quarter Court

## Name

Ghost Quarter Court

## Overview

*Ghost Quarter Court* shows the Unfinished's public court still being decided. Scaffold, graphite over chalk, a construction-line figure kneeling at a latch, a fountain that is only a suggested ring of stones, and moss underlining what should not yet be trusted. The court is not finished.

## Description

Walls hold somewhere short of done. Chalk-white underdrawing breathes through graphite. Cobalt guide lines cross copper corrections. A paler hinge scar marks where a door moved a handspan. The figure at the latch is a drawn body of seams and edge-traces, kneeling, occupied with the wood. It is not a finished person and not a horror of melted flesh. A ledger hangs under a scaffold that is still called temporary. Green moss follows the builder's lines like an underline. The fountain is a ring of stones you could walk around, still a suggestion, not a completed basin.

The southwest of the court is softer, as if erasure weather had passed and morning redraw had not panicked. The plate does not complete the quarter, nail a ribbon across the scaffold, or ask anyone to mourn the version that lost.

## Real-World Role

A scene plate for F960 as intentional incompletion that still functions: a place alive because it is being decided.

## Lore Connection

Ghost Quarter Foundation Court is the south-central plaza where buildings stay between forty and ninety percent and naming follows what has persisted. Draftkin revise in public. Their outline is stable enough to recognize and still full of construction lines. The Open Hand of Making's ethic is visible here as revision, not as a finished monument. The Half-Made Atlas may argue in the margins. This picture does not wait for it to agree.

## Notes

Do not finish the court in a later crop. The kneeling figure stays seams, not a portrait and not a monster.
