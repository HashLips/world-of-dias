# Sample

## Name

Sample

## Overview

*Sample* is the Elder Shelf plate for the blue-square lounge. Black body, hands behind neck, ankles crossed. Royal blue square for a head. Bright red spindly chair. Peach wall, tan floor, long shadows.

## Description

Room splits: pale peach upper wall, muted tan floor. A bright saturated red chair—tall flat back, four very thin spindly legs—holds a solid dark figure lounging deep: arms up, hands tucked behind the neck area, legs crossed at the ankles. Where a head should be, a solid vibrant royal-blue square sits centered between the raised elbows. Long sharp diagonal darker-tan shadows from chair legs and crossed feet stretch bottom-left. Tiny black crown/gate icon top-right. Leisure with an abstract measure where identity usually sits.

An artifact plate for Elder Shelf. Sample is the kept craft of the blue-square rest on the red chair.
