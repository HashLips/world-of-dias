# Pisces

## Name

Pisces

## Overview

*Pisces* is the Driftfall plate for the twin-neck walker. Two pale-pink sinuous necks. Identical slit-eyed heads with white jagged crests. Dark red side fins. Black stride legs on geometric ground.

## Description

From one join, two long fleshy pale-pink necks curve opposite ways—one head down-left, one up-right—in a mirrored S. Each head is smooth, eyeless-fish or serpent stylized, with a small dark horizontal slit-eye and a sharp jagged white crest along the top. Dark red flame- or petal-shaped fins cling to the sides and backs of the necks. The pink torso drops abruptly into solid pitch-black featureless legs mid-stride: left knee bent forward, right leg extended back, feet simple pointed shapes.

Background is hard geometric planes: slate-gray upper left, dusty olive right, chocolate-brown lower band with a sharp golden-yellow trapezoid highlight under the forward foot. Soft shading lives only on the pink flesh; legs and planes stay flat. Tiny white gate icon bottom right.

No pier crowd. The plate is the twin heads and the black dance-step alone.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Driftfall. Pisces is the kept craft-figure of the twin-neck stride.

## Lore Connection

Pisces is Driftfall's name for the hour when the two pink necks disagree in direction and still share one dark stride. Local care says you watch both crests before you name a single will; the gold under the forward foot is the honesty of contact. Resonance is the mirrored S; memory is the red side fins; wonder is how soft the pink stays above featureless black legs. The plate invents no zodiac sermon. It keeps the twin-neck walker as a pier craft people recognize when one body holds two attentions.
