# 3 Opinions (The Three Opinions Portrait)

## Overview

*3 Opinions* is the core portrait reference for **The Three Opinions**—a famous traveling trio known across Sorel for never taking a side and always having one.

## Description

Three near-profile figures share a saturated blue plane-body treatment with yellow edge halos and red calligraphic facial lines. Each face turns slightly toward the others, so the composition reads as a closed conversational loop rather than a posed group portrait.

Left to right, the expressions skew skeptical, neutral, and knowing—matching how witnesses describe **Brask Kel**, **Miren Holt**, and **Tav Renn** in daily argument.

Asset reference: `assets/3-opinions.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

Character art anchor for Sorel's most infamous rumor-bearing inhabitants.

## Lore Connection

The image is widely cited when describing how the trio enters a town: they do not fight, steal, or openly command—but they **talk**, and the talking rearranges what people believe.

Market retellings pair the portrait with warnings about **Hearthvale** and **Driftfall** alike: the three refuse both camps, yet still manage to leave both camps more suspicious of each other by nightfall.

## Notes

Often used as the default visual when the trio is named in exile-road stories, bridge gossip, and Driftfall tavern accounts.
