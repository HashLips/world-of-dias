# Pebbles from Impossible Ledges

*Pebbles from Impossible Ledges* is a digital image study of Averra Cliff-Goats browsing where no ladder should hold.

Sure-footed goats stand on narrow cliff ledges above sketched cliffside studios—long horns, calm balance, a few pebbles falling. Impossible footing is the joke; no apprentice faces are shown.
