# Revision Seams of a Draftkin

## Name

Revision Seams of a Draftkin

## Overview

*Revision Seams of a Draftkin* is a digital image study of a Draftkin as finished-in-motion: recognizable silhouette held in translucent line-memory and revision seams.

## Description

Waist-up profile of a strongly non-human process-native being. The body reads as translucent smoky gray-blue sketch-and-vapor rather than fixed flesh. Fine shimmering gold revision seams trace chest, arms, and head; a glowing golden spiral marks the chest. The head is pointed and mask-like without ordinary facial features, with swept flame-like protrusions. Layered translucent wing-like or paper-fold forms rise from the back. Around the figure, drafting overlays—faint arcs, circles, and straight construction lines—hold line-memory like a drawing still revising.

Parchment ground. Decorative border is framing only.
