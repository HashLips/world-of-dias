# Salt-White Sticks, No Board

## Overview

*Salt-White Sticks, No Board* is Loft Hail Chalk still in its pouch. The sticks are salt-white. The pouch is net. There is no board. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

Salt-white chalk sticks sit in a net pouch, the carrying form the tool actually has before anyone marks a loft. The white is the salt-white of the account, not a dyed display color and not a slate already written. The net is the pouch: cord around the sticks so they can go up to a loft. Short sticks, waxy enough to be chalk, are the whole cargo. They have not been used in this frame to make a name.

There is no board. Years of roll call would darken a board. This picture has no board to darken, no list, no erased patch, and no name that refuses to wipe. A lied hail cannot be shown where nothing has been written. The chalk is ready and unused by the picture. Dust is not settled into a lesson. Rope-before-stone is not diagrammed beside the pouch.

No apprentice stands at the net. No hail is being called. The picture is salt-white sticks in a net pouch, and the board they would mark is somewhere else.
