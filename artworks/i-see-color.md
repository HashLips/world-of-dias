# I See Color

## Name

I See Color

## Overview

*I See Color* is a Velorian Basin studio plate: one saturated blue body braced in a wide crouch, white eye-slit locked forward, white-handled brush poised, and a small blue palette held to the chest on a grey floor against a grey wall.

## Description

The figure has no hair, ears, or nose—only the sharp white horizontal eye. Torso twists; one arm reaches with the slender brush (white shaft, dark pointed tip); the other clamps an oval palette the same blue as the skin. Legs angle wide as if precision costs balance. A large charcoal shadow copies the strain behind the body. The room is almost empty: medium-light grey above a darker grey floor line. No canvas is shown; the mark is about to happen off-frame. The mood is solitary focus, not gallery chatter.

Asset reference: `assets/i-see-color.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A craft-phenomenon plate: seeing color as physical work, not as idle looking.

## Lore Connection

In the Velorian Basin, I See Color names the hour when a maker’s body turns the same hue as the palette—when attention and pigment agree. Local rule: do not interrupt a blue crouch for small talk; wait until the brush lifts. Resonance sticks to the first deliberate stroke after that crouch. People who claim they “see color” while standing easy are teased until they show the shadow of real strain.

## Notes

One blue artisan, brush, palette, grey studio. No audience.
