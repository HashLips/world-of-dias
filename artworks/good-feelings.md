# Good Feelings

## Name

Good Feelings

## Overview

*Good Feelings* is a Nauw Outer Rim Seas breath plate: a black reclining silhouette blows through a red looped wand and raises glossy golden orbs—the rim craft of making cheer dense enough to cast a shadow, not thin enough to sting.

## Description

Diagonal split: vibrant medium blue below, pale lavender-blue above. dark figure chest-up in profile, head tilted back, single white eye with black pupil watching upward; slender black arm holds a thin bright red wand ending in a circular loop. Small jagged bright blue shape from mouth toward the loop. Four golden-yellow glossy spheres of varying sizes rise with white crescent highlights and soft translucent blue drop shadows. Solitary ritual quiet. Tiny black gate glyph top-right.

Asset reference: `assets/good-feelings.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A rim inhabitant plate: breathe good feeling into orbs you can still count; do not spray cheer as weaponized mist.

## Lore Connection

On the Nauw Outer Rim Seas, **Good Feelings** names a non-human (or mood-neighbor) breath-craft. Golden orbs are dense wonder, not fragile soap jokes. Red loop is the measure. Wonder is made and held. Dias rim name.
