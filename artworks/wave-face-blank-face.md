# Wave Face, Blank Face

## Name

Wave Face, Blank Face

## Overview

*Wave Face, Blank Face* is one Averra Offshore Courtesy Token. It is a small pale disc, stone or glazed clay. One face has a wave mark. The other face is blank. The picture shows both faces. It is still one token. There is no pier.

## Description

Pale stone or glazed clay, pocket-polished. A wave mark sits on one face. The other face has nothing on it. The blank face is the point. No schedule is engraved, because a schedule would be a lie about Averra and a temptation to the holder.

The plate may show both faces at once. That is a way of looking, not a second disc. It is one token. It does not open a door. It does not summon a pier. It does not impress a harbormaster who wants infrastructure to behave. There is no pier in the frame, and no bollard staged as a ticket window.

Pocket-polished means it has been carried. In a good hour the disc stays in the pocket even while you moor. Showing it at the bollard like a ticket is how you announce you have misunderstood. This plate does not show that mistake. It shows the disc: wave on one face, blank on the other.

## Real-World Role

A manners plate for offshore waiting. One pale disc. Both faces may be visible. No pier.

## Lore Connection

An Averra Offshore Courtesy Token is what captains show, mostly to themselves and to each other, while they wait for the Gray Post to exist again. It is a manners print for an isle that inhales and exhales permission. Captains who rage do not lose the token. They lose the contracts of people who have watched them rage at fog. Harbors that mock the token find fewer Averra-adjacent agreements, not because of a curse, because the captains who wait well talk to each other and stop recommending the mockers.

Artists like the blank face. Old Masters rumor sometimes tries to claim the discs as signs. Wait Captains who let that claim ride have started selling a landing they do not have. Damp-studio gifts, when a landing actually happens, may include a token passed back, still blank. The blankness after the landing is the courtesy surviving success.

The token is not a boarding pass into Averra's interior, not a key to Crown Ash, and not a map of the Hearthline's kilns. Those refusals are already the use. The plate does not draw them. If you need the disc to feel powerful, you are not in the practice. The isle remains obscure on purpose. The token helps you bear that without making it a hunt. Deck pockets and quiet harbor boards are where it lives. The picture keeps the object and withholds the harbor speech.
