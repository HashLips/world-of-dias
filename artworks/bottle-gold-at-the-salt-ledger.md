# Bottle-Gold at the Salt Ledger

## Name

Bottle-Gold at the Salt Ledger

## Overview

Bottle-Gold at the Salt Ledger is a digital image study of the low shore building where Driftfall witnesses overlap.

## Description

The Salt Ledger sits low in driftwood, its bottle-glass light turning the shore-facing room gold. An overpainted board marks the building, but no name can be read from it. No people appear in the windows. The plate stays with the pub’s patient, salvaged exterior and does not make the building more grand than it is.
