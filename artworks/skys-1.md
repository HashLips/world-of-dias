# Skies

## Name

Skies

## Overview

*Skies* is the F432 plate for red legs in blue strata. Solid cadmium-red bent knees. A dozen wavy blue bands from charcoal to ice. Grey translucent reflection left.

## Description

Horizontal slightly wavy bands stack from dark near-black charcoal-blue at top down through denim and slate to very pale icy blue at bottom—water layers, air strata, or sediment without needing a single noun. Through them, a pair of completely flat saturated red human-like legs reclines or wades, knees bent, one foot pointed down in the lower right—no muscle, no toes, symbolic red only. To the left across mid bands, a large semi-transparent grey silhouette mirrors the legs as if through a translucent medium. No torso, no head. The plate is presence as legs inside layered sky-water.
