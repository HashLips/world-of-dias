# Pond of Lust

## Name

Pond of Lust

## Overview

*Pond of Lust* is the Averra Isle plate for the dripping pink shore. A slender pink figure stands on terracotta. Other pink shapes wait half-sunk. Pink melts in long drips into dark teal.

## Description

Upper register: deep umber with four shell-pink rectangles of uneven width like windows, plus a thin pink horizontal line beneath; tiny white house-like icon top-left. Middle: a slender elongated shell-pink standing silhouette holds arms close to the torso, reflective or guarded, planted on a thick vibrant terracotta band. Inside that orange-red band, other pink forms read as bent knees, a rounded head-top, a long angled limb—bodies or parts half-claimed by the band.

Lower: a thick shell-pink layer with a slightly wavy top edge drops long irregular drips and tendrils into a solid dark teal-blue field. Flat graphic, no soft water sparkle. The pond is the melt and the submerged pink, not a tourist mirror.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Averra Isle. Pond of Lust is the kept craft of the named shore's warning.

## Lore Connection

Pond of Lust is Averra's name for the hour when shell-pink stands on terracotta above teal and the boundary drips. Local care says lust here is pull toward the melt—want that can sink other shapes—not a joke about appetite. The standing figure models holding the arms in; the submerged pinks are the memory of what already went under. Resonance is pink against teal; wonder is the umber window-row watching; memory is the drip lengths. The plate invents no scandal saga. It keeps the pond as a craft warning: stand careful, do not shove others into the band for spectacle.
