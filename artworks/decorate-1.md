# Decorate

## Name

Decorate

## Overview

*Decorate* is a Claimscar Yard still-life plate: a golden pitcher painted with cobalt flowers holds one thin black stem that ends in a heavy jagged black bloom—the yard lesson that ornament can still tell the truth about weight.

## Description

Upper wall muted reddish-brown; lower floor vibrant orange, split on a sharp diagonal. A bulbous golden-yellow pitcher patterned with stylized six-petaled cobalt flowers sits on a light-blue rectangular tabletop with four thin white legs; two cast long dark-orange shadows left. From the pitcher’s mouth a single thin curved black stem loops up then sweeps down into a large solid black multi-pointed jagged silhouette—heavy flower or spiky leaf-cluster. Cheerful pitcher, unsettling bloom. Tiny white gate mark top-right.

Asset reference: `assets/decorate-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A yard craft plate: decorate, but do not pretend the heavy bloom is light.

## Lore Connection

In Claimscar Yard, **Decorate** is a kept teaching object-look. Blue flowers on gold are welcome polish. The black drooping bloom is the part of the arrangement that remembers scar and weight. Yard rule: you may pretty a table; you may not hide the jagged truth inside cheerful paint. Wonder is honest ornament.

## Notes

Golden flowered pitcher. Light-blue table, white legs. Heavy black drooping bloom. Clay wall, orange floor.
