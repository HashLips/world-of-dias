# Then

## Name

Then

## Overview

*Then* is a clock held by two hands that do not belong to the same person. From the left reaches a long, pale-blue hand; from the right, a golden-yellow one. Between them, cupped but not quite touched, floats a clock made of overlapping shapes—a rust-red circle, a blue square peeking out behind, and a translucent violet square on top. Two white arrows point from a blue hub: one up and to the right, one straight down.

## Description

The black ground makes every color pop. The two hands are drawn with long, wavy fingers, almost like reeds or flames, and they curve inward to frame the clock without gripping it. Each casts a faint shadow onto the face, so the clock sits just under their fingertips.

The clock itself is assembled rather than whole. The circle is a warm rust; the blue square behind it shows at the upper left; the violet square overlaps both and is the clock face proper. The hands are plain white arrows with triangular heads. The longer one aims at the upper right, roughly toward one; the shorter drops to the bottom, roughly toward six. No numerals, no ticks.

The hands' colors do not match the clock's colors, or each other's. Blue on one side, gold on the other: two people, two temperaments, holding one time between them.

## Real-World Role

A second-beginning plate about two people agreeing on when something ended so something else can start.

## Lore Connection

Hearthvale is the western territory of Sorel, centered on rebuilding stable life after exile, and it receives many who leave Nauw under pressure of shame, law, or custom. Then is the name Hearthvale gives to a two-handed clock used when newcomers and the people receiving them settle a single question: when did the old life stop? The plate shows the clock held between a blue hand and a gold hand, neither owning it. Custom says the hands are set together, by both people, and then left alone. After that, the old time is called then, and nobody in Hearthvale is supposed to drag it into now without asking.
