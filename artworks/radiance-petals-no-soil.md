# Radiance Petals, No Soil

## Name

Radiance Petals, No Soil

## Overview

*Radiance Petals, No Soil* is a Filament-Bloom. The petals are radiance. There is no soil. It is not a person.

## Description

Radiance petals, and no soil under them. That is the F200 habit. The bloom is described in plant words even if it may not match F432 carbon flora. Else-green, beauty, forage-of-light. The plate shows petals of radiance and does not root them in dirt to make them ordinary. It also does not give them a face, a body, or a stance. It is not a person.

Forage-of-light is a use, not a figure eating. No one is foraging in the frame. No gardener is apologizing for the missing soil. The missing soil is the description. The petals are the growth.

A filament here is the bloom's own light, not a path-grass from another country and not a portrait. The subject is the flower that does not need earth.

A flora plate for light that blooms. Radiance petals. No soil. Not a person.

## Lore Connection

Filament-Bloom is reported in F200. No soil. Radiance petals. Local use is beauty and forage-of-light. The kind is else-green. Flora of Dias indexes it. The petals remain the plant.
