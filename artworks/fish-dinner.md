# Fish Dinner

## Name

Fish Dinner

## Overview

*Fish Dinner* is a Nauw Outer Rim Seas meal plate: two finned bright figures lean over a light-blue table toward a white roll and black sticks—the rim custom of dining as neighbors who still have tails on the floor.

## Description

Medium-grey field with a large irregular white cloud or spotlight behind the sitters. Left figure mustard yellow, right reddish-orange; both semi-humanoid with large flat tail fins instead of feet, elongated snouted heads, open mouths, thin arms; seated on dark brown four-legged chairs. Light-blue rectangular thin-legged table between them holds a small white cylinder crossed by two thin black sticks. Faint grey lines radiate from the table base. Formal-energetic, flat and bold.

Asset reference: `assets/fish-dinner.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A rim inhabitant plate: share the center roll; keep both mouths honest about hunger and talk.

## Lore Connection

On the Nauw Outer Rim Seas, **Fish Dinner** names finned non-human neighbors at table. White cylinder and sticks are the shared course—scroll-food or skewer-food by telling. Wonder is loud mouths that still wait their turn. Dias rim meal-name.

Yellow and orange finned sitters. Blue table. White roll, black sticks. White backlight on grey.
