# Looking Into

## Name

Looking Into

## Overview

*Looking Into* is a geometric bust: mustard and cream planes, a teal stripe down the face, a simple brimmed hat—an image of someone mid-attention rather than mid-speech.

## Description

Angular portrait against dark teal. Eyes are dots; a heavy brow line; a vertical teal band bisects the face like a reading mark. The figure does not perform emotion; it holds focus.
