## Overview

*Familiar* is an F960 recognition plate: an orange-red face in mint hat and oversized glasses meets you with lime accents—the frequency portrait for a known neighbor-look that still asks consent before it gets too close.

## Description

Teal, seafoam, and muted turquoise abstract shapes behind. Centered head-and-shoulders: vibrant orange-red face and chest with deep rust shadows under jaw and on neck. Pale minty off-white oversized thick glasses and matching brimmed hat. Bright lime-green for lips, nose bridge-and-tip stripe, eye irises, and a sharp decorative line down the right neck. Half-lidded calm eyes. Watchful, serene, high-resonance color block. Tiny white gate logo top-left.
