# Wind on the High Steward Steps

## Name

Wind on the High Steward Steps

## Overview

Wind on the High Steward Steps is a digital image study of Nauw’s public approach to Concord authority.

## Description

The plate leaves the civic stair empty, with wind moving across the wide stone and plain banners marking the public approach. In the absence of petitioners, the steps still read as a place where corridor priority and continuity can be spoken aloud.

The image does not require a dome, statues, or gold. It keeps authority outdoors and practical, closer to civic theater than to a throne-room display.

Asset reference: `assets/nauw-high-steward-steps.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A public-architecture plate for the civic authority and continuity carried by Nauw’s steps.

## Lore Connection

The artwork supports the High Steward Steps as a place of speech before enforcement while leaving open when speech becomes law.
