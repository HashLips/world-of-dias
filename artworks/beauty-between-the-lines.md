## Overview

*Averra Between-Lines Portrait* shows a cool-toned lower face with cobalt lips—portrait as gap and highlight rather than loud outline.

## Description

Lavender/periwinkle planes; saturated blue mouth; minimal nose marks; soft jaw shadow into neck. The face feels unfinished on purpose—beauty living between strokes.
