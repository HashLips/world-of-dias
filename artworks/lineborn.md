# Flip Plane of a Lineborn

## Name

Flip Plane of a Lineborn

## Overview

*Flip Plane of a Lineborn* is a digital image study of a Lineborn in F432 caught between near-invisible side angle and richly detailed frontal plane.

## Description

The figure reads as a living ink-cut: a thin side silhouette with calligraphic edge that flips into a crosshatched frontal plane. Interior texture suggests sketch shading or charcoal grain more than volumetric flesh. Depth stays minimal; identity holds in outline and plane, not bulk.

The plate presents one fully embodied Lineborn person mid-flip, not an illusion or ghost. Background framing is decorative only.
