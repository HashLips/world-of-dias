# Null-Edge Cairn Rumor

## Name

Null-Edge Cairn Rumor

## Overview

*Null-Edge Cairn Rumor* holds two sketches that do not agree. One is a slate pile shedding grit into nowhere. The other is a paler circle of nothing where a cairn was said to have been. It is a rumor with a shape. It is not a confirmed landmark.

## Description

On one side, dark slate. Pebbles leave a hand and do not arrive at a ground. On the other, drawn lines thin toward a paler absence, as if the pencil resented the assignment. There is no reliable horizon. Where footprints appear, they fill with absence rather than dust. The two versions share a plate so neither can pretend to be the survey.

The picture does not confirm coordinates. It does not open a door. It does not depict Frequency Zero, and it does not give the Unraveled, Horizon-Nulls, or Distant Companies a body. Standing in front of the disagreement is not a crossing and not a tour.
