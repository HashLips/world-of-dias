# Light

## Name

Light

## Overview

*Light* places a vivid blue six-point flower with large orange center and white crescent highlight on a thin pale-yellow L-stand rising from reddish-brown floor into medium-grey air, olive rounded tablet behind holding a black rectangle with one thick white horizontal band.

## Description

Petals slightly asymmetric. Stand casts a dark shadow bottom-left. Tiny black glyph upper-left. The flower is the light; the tablet is the mute companion screen.

Asset reference: `assets/light.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A phenomenon plate: light as a tended bloom on a stand, not only as sky.

## Lore Connection

F200 rooms name Light when the blue bloom is set on the yellow L and the olive tablet stays dim. Local rule: do not cover the orange center to 'save' it—dull centers mean replace the bloom. Resonance prefers the white highlight kept clean. Using the tablet’s white band as the only light while the flower wilts empties the name.

## Notes

Blue petals, orange center, yellow stand, olive tablet, terracotta floor.
