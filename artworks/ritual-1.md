# Ritual

## Name

Ritual

## Overview

*Ritual* is the Elder Shelf plate for the outlined dance in the void. Central red figure mid-stride, arms up. Blue partial figure from the left. Orange figures behind. Thick white borders. Black everywhere else.

## Description

Solid black field. Figures are flat saturated bodies wrapped in uniform thick white outlines that bind them against the void. Center: bright red humanoid in dynamic posture—one leg bent and lifted, arms raised near the face, profile looking left, simple horizontal eye-slit and minimal mouth, white finger-strokes on the hands. Left: sky-blue figure enters from the edge—head, torso, one upward arm—profile toward the red. Behind red: orange and muted yellow-orange figures with uplifted arms and elongated limbs, overlapping into depth. No floor line. Energy is communal rhythm, not a solo stage.

Asset reference: `assets/ritual-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A phenomenon plate for Elder Shelf. Ritual is the named hour of white-bound colored bodies moving together in black.

## Lore Connection

Ritual is how Elder Shelf names the hour when red, blue, and orange rise in white outlines and the void stays honest black. Local care says you keep the outlines intact—presence with a boundary—and you do not shove a lone body out for spectacle. Resonance is color against black through white bind; memory is the raised arms; wonder is how much motion fits without a ground. The plate invents no secret cult plot. It keeps the weather as shared movement manners.

## Notes

Black void. White thick outlines. Central red mid-stride dancer. Blue left reacher. Orange uplifted chorus. No horizon.
