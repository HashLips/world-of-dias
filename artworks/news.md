# News

## Name

News

## Overview

*News* is the Velorian Basin plate for word that arrives as weather. An orange-red figure bows under a blue umbrella. A pale pink double stands half a step behind. Golden-orange rain falls.

## Description

The figure is solid orange-red, shown in profile facing right, head slightly bowed, one thin black horizontal slit for a closed or listening eye. Directly behind, offset left, a pale pinkish-peach silhouette copies the same posture—ghosting, not a second person arguing. The umbrella canopy is bright sky-blue on a thin blue shaft ending in a clear black hook handle. Across the whole frame, elongated triangular drops fall in warm golden-orange, dense enough to count as weather and strange enough not to be ordinary water.

Ground is a dark chocolate-brown band. Above it the field splits along a diagonal into light gray and a white upper-left wedge. No text block, no town crier, no stamped bulletin appears. The plate treats the doubled walker and the colored rain as the news itself: something already walking with you under a small blue shelter while the basin weathers in gold-orange.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Velorian Basin. News here is carried posture and rain-color, not a headline sheet.

## Lore Connection

News is the basin's name for arrival that does not shout. The pink double is memory walking half a step behind; the orange-red body is the living carrier; the blue umbrella is the small local care you are allowed to hold without claiming the whole sky. Golden-orange rain marks the hour when word has weight you can feel on the canopy. Resonance is the match between figure and double. Wonder is that the rain is the wrong color for ordinary water and still belongs. The plate refuses to invent a saga battle; it keeps the bow, the hook handle, and the doubled outline as manners for receiving what travels.
