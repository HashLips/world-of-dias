# Outer-Ring House Cutaway

## Name

Outer-Ring House Cutaway

## Overview

*Outer-Ring House Cutaway* is a digital image study of an ordinary outer-ring home in Veloria, opened up to show how a household uses its rooms.

## Description

On the left, a cobbled street curves past a half-timbered neighbor with lit arched windows and steep slate roofs. The house itself has a lime-plastered stone front, a heavy round-arched wooden door with iron fittings, a few stone steps, and a potted flowering plant on a low stone block beside the door.

The right side of the house is cut away. On the ground floor a stone hearth with a fire and a mantel of jars faces a long plank table set with bowls and cups, a chair, and a rug on flagstones. Timber posts carry a loft above, with a railed sleeping platform, a bed, a lamp, and shelves. At the back a small arched opening leads to another lamp-lit room.

Everything is cobble, timber beam, plaster, and stone, with lamplight and firelight only. The decorative border is framing only.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of outer-ring domestic building in Veloria: cobble underfoot, timber beams, lime plaster, and a household arranged around one hearth.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the outer-ring row of Nauw Layered Civic Architecture (cobble, timber beams, lime plaster, guild-scale doors) and its domestic notes, and sits within Veloria City's medieval-character outer ring. The flower pot by the door echoes outer-ring pot-signal street furniture.

## Notes

Illustrative domestic layout only. The cutaway does not name a household, street, or district and does not create a new region. Room count and arrangement are not a standard plan. Whether the door pot is signalling anything is not stated.
