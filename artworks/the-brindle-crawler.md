# Black Sneeze on the Spur

## Name

Black Sneeze on the Spur

## Overview

*Black Sneeze on the Spur* is a digital image study of the Brindle Crawler on its short spur, drawn as an honest working train rather than a myth.

## Description

A squat riveted engine leads from the left, its tall stack throwing a thick black plume, its brass dome and boiler plate worn by use. Behind it run three low wooden cars with plank sides, built for crates and workers rather than comfort.

The track is plain iron on sleepers, bolted and visible, with weeds pushing up along the ballast. It runs across dry scrub ground toward the right, where a raised timber depot platform stands on posts, with a railing, a stair down to the ground, and a few stacked crates under a plank roof. Low hills sit behind the line.

The engraving-style plate keeps everything mechanical and legible: no glow, no shimmer, no doors opening onto anywhere but the platform. The decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/the-brindle-crawler.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of the Brindle Crawler as a real, slow third-ring-edge train: soot, iron, three cars, and a depot it actually reaches.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the Crawler's established image (stout engine, honest black sneeze, three low cars, visible weedy track, depot platform) and keeps it obviously distinct from The Rail That Sold the Same Platform. It adds no route beyond the short Brindle-side spur.

## Notes

The platform shown is a depot stop, not a claim about which end of the spur it is. Jory Milt is not pictured. Do not promote the Crawler into a band subway.
