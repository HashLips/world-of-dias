# Tricky Mind

## Name

Tricky Mind

## Overview

*Tricky Mind* is a head that is also a vase. A golden-yellow profile with a long, curving neck rises from the bottom of a green plate, its crown flattened like a vase's rim. Out of the top bursts a bouquet of white star-shaped flowers with black centers, petals spiked and twisting, stems branching into jagged leaves. Behind the golden head, a larger brown silhouette of another head and neck leans in like a shadow that does not quite match.

## Description

The green ground is a deep, even leaf green. The golden head is smooth and simple, with one narrow white eye ringed in red and small red lips, slightly parted. Its neck tapers down into a long stem-like base that runs off the bottom edge.

The flowers are white and dramatic: five or six heads, each with eight or more spiked petals that curl like flames, each with a solid black center. The stems are thin white lines with sharp-angled leaves breaking off them, more like lightning than foliage. They erupt from the top of the head as if the thoughts have grown out.

The brown shadow head is the trick. It is bigger than the golden head, offset down and to the right, faceless, with no flowers. It might be a shadow cast by a light the plate does not show, or another self that has nothing in bloom. The golden head does not look at it.

## Real-World Role

A plate about the difference between the bouquet a mind shows and the plain shadow standing behind it.

## Lore Connection

Verdant Reach is Wabet's central region of fertile groves, flowering fields, and clear rivers. Its potters make head-shaped vases with a flat rim at the crown, and hosts give one to a guest along with an armful of white star flowers. The guest arranges the flowers however they like. Tricky Mind is the plate that warns what that means: the bouquet is what you choose to show, and the vase's plain shadow on the wall is what you carry anyway. Reach hosts never comment on a guest's arrangement, but they always glance at the shadow.
