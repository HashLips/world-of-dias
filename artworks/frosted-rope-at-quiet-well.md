# Frosted Rope at Quiet Well

## Name

Frosted Rope at Quiet Well

## Overview

*Frosted Rope at Quiet Well* is a digital image study of Quietwell Serpents at the hush of Quiet Well: pale frosted-glass scale, dry-well eyes, silent motion.

## Description

Two pale off-white serpents in an arid, rocky basin. Scales read like frosted glass over sand—matte, fine, cool—not metallic and not golden. Each has a single triangular head with a dark recessed dry-well eye. One undulates across cracked sand toward a small still pool of deep blue water; the second rests coiled on a rock ledge above it. Each body is roughly the length of a tall person. Sparse thorny shrubs, weathered stones, and low hazy mesas fill the distance.

Muted sepia palette; decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/quietwell-serpents.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of the Quietwell Serpent as Lumira hazard ecology: soft steps and lowered voices near the oasis hush.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the established look (tall-person length, frosted-glass scales, dry-well eyes, near-silent movement) and the 'pale rope' warning without inventing new venom lore or sacred powers.

## Notes

Single-headed—NOT two-headed. Pale frosted glass—NOT golden or metallic. Two serpents shown is composition, not a pairing law. Border ornaments are not oasis law.
