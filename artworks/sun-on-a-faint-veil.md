# Sun on a Faint Veil

## Name

Sun on a Faint Veil

## Overview

*Sun on a Faint Veil* is an Unsold Weather Marble in sun: a clear glass sphere with a faint internal veil, sitting on a lit sill. The veil is not milk. The glass is not a mood and not a person's color. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

The sphere is clear glass, round enough to be a marble and large enough to sit as an object on a sill rather than a toy lost in a pocket. Inside it, a faint veil hangs in the glass, thin enough that the sun still keeps the body bright. The veil is not thickened to milk. It does not swirl, and it does not take on amber, grief, anger, or any skin of someone else's color. Light from the side lays bright bands across the wall and across the wood. The marble answers that light by staying clear.

The sill is plain worn wood. There is no holder, no hand, no storm painted into the glass, and no card explaining a feeling. The plate does not show rain, a basin cloud, or an hour marked on a dial. It shows sun, clear glass, and the faint veil the object has when it is not calling water.

## Real-World Role

A still life of a forecast that only knows rain, caught while the sun still has it. The marble refuses to be an oracle for a heart.

## Lore Connection

An Unsold Weather Marble is a glass sphere that clouds when basin rain is about an hour out. Clear glass with a faint internal veil is its ordinary look, and sun keeps it bright. Before rain the veil thickens to milk. This plate is the bright state: faint veil, not milk, so it is not shown in the act of calling the washing in. It does not swirl with anger, grief, or wanting. It is not a Heartlean Colorstone. If it took a person's color it would be broken, or it would be a different marble. The name stays unsold because the object will not be bought as a mood. The sill holds glass and sunlight, and the picture does not put a heart in either.
