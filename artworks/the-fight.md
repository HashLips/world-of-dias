# The Fight

## Name

The Fight

## Overview

*The Fight* is two figures arranged like a reflection in water. At the top, a white figure lies back with eyes closed, one long arm curving over its head, the other hand's fingers splayed beside its face. At the bottom, a second white figure is turned upside down, face nearly meeting a ghost of its own profile, its fingers raking across its forehead. Between them a broad band of teal and sea-green flows across the plate like a current, and gray-blue echoes of both bodies trail behind in layers.

## Description

The ground is deep navy. The white figures are clean and smooth; behind each one, offset shadows in slate and blue-gray repeat the outline two or three times, so every gesture seems to vibrate. The upper figure's spread fingers become a fringe of pale lines around its head.

The teal band dominates the middle. It bends around the upper figure's body, swells into a bright green-cyan form, and narrows toward the lower right, where it seems to pour off the page. In it, a third shadowy face appears in green, eyes open.

The lower figure is the more troubled. Its face is pressed close to its own echo, nose to nose, and its hand drags fingers across its brow. It is not striking anyone. It is wrestling its own reflection. The fight, as drawn, contains no blows at all.

## Real-World Role

A quarrel plate where conflict is shown as two halves facing their reflections across a cooling current.

## Lore Connection

Lumira Sands is Wabet's southern desert, known for pale dunes, hidden oases, and an unusual calm that travelers learn to protect. The Fight is the sands' name for how a quarrel is held there without breaking that calm. The two parties lie down head to head, one turned opposite the other, hands over their own faces, while a length of teal cloth is laid between them like water. The plate shows the posture: two mirrored bodies, a current between, no blows. Sands custom says each person argues only with their own echo until the cloth has been drawn away, and then they may speak.
