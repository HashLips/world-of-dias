# Is It Time to Grow Up

## Name

Is It Time to Grow Up

## Overview

*Is It Time to Grow Up* shows a dark-green caretaker mid-stride in a pale cyan room with a darker teal panel behind, lime-green rectangular case in one hand, a red cone-cap with white trim and tip-puff in the other, and a dark grey peg wall holding about fifteen identical red-and-white cone-caps in neat rows.

## Description

The figure is featureless but for a narrow white eye-slit, walking right, long shadow cast left. Caps hang like a disciplined collection—not festival clutter. The held cap matches the wall set exactly. No pier boards, no salt spray in frame; the room is storage and transition. The question in the title is practical: when do you take a cap off the wall and carry the case into weather?

Asset reference: `assets/is-it-time-to-grow-up.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A duty plate for Driftfall: growing up means choosing which cone-cap leaves the peg wall today.

## Lore Connection

Driftfall piers keep cone-caps as dampening and role markers for wet work—white trim for soft seal, tip-puff for grab in wind—not as Earth festival costume. Is It Time to Grow Up is the morning ask before a first unsupervised carry. Local rule: take one cap, carry the lime case of tools, leave the peg rows orderly. Resonance favors the caretaker who returns the cap to its hook dry. Grabbing three caps “just in case” is childish in the Driftfall sense: wasteful of the wall’s count.
