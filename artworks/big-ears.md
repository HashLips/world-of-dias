# Big Ears

## Name

Big Ears

## Overview

*Big Ears* is a Brindle March listening-bust plate: a navy silhouette on soft pink, ears huge on purpose, teaching that load-share roads run on hearing more than on loud thanks.

## Description

A muted rose-pink field holds a centered front-facing bust in deep navy. The head is broad-crowned with a sharp triangular chin. Features are spare: left eye (viewer’s left) a closed horizontal line, right eye a small focused dot; nose a straight diagonal; mouth two thick horizontal strokes. The ears dominate—large rounded forms flung wide. The viewer’s-left ear shows inner detail: a crescent mark and a short squiggle. The right ear is a solid dark shape. A long tapering neck widens into a heavy shoulder block. Flat two-tone graphic, serene and watchful. Mood: disciplined observation, sensory readiness.

## Real-World Role

A march manners plate: listen before you grab a strap, a story, or a thank-you count.

## Lore Connection

On Brindle March, **Big Ears** is the roadside reminder that the Three Thank-Yous begin with hearing what help was actually asked for. Oversized ears in the plate are not comedy; they are craft. The asymmetric eyes mean you can rest one look and keep one sharp—Brindle’s way of saying you do not need to stare someone down to serve them. Leans keep a small copy of this bust near chalk boards so travelers remember: smudge the joke, hear the load. Wonder is quiet competence. If your thanks are specific and your ears are open, the clover ditch already knows.
