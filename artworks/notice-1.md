# Notice

## Name

Notice

## Overview

*Notice* is the F500 plate for the cast that picks one face from many. Yellow surface. Dark boat and fisher. Light-blue depth. Headless blue sitter. Rows of orange heads. One hook hanging at a single profile.

## Description

The top third is solid bright yellow. Near center-left a dark gray sailboat silhouette floats; inside it a standing figure holds a thin rod. A delicate black line drops from the rod into the lower field.

Below, light blue fills two-thirds of the plate like a sub-surface. Center sits a large stylized humanoid in two blues—lighter limbs, darker torso—headless, cross-legged, arms crossed over the chest in a compact waiting posture. Sharp dark gray shadows stretch diagonally from beneath it. On both sides, neat rhythmic rows of identical orange head silhouettes face left, each with a small light-blue horizontal eye-sliver. The fishing line ends in a simple hook suspended directly in front of one orange head in the middle-right rows, singling it out from the chorus.

No net hauls a crowd. The plate is the notice: one hook, one face, while the headless sitter keeps the depth's quiet rule.

A phenomenon plate for F500. Notice is selective attention made visible.

## Lore Connection

Notice is how F500 names the hour when a surface fisher casts into the blue depth and the hook stops before one orange profile among many. The headless crossed sitter is local care in the deep—waiting without grabbing every face. Resonance is the thin black line between boat and chosen head; memory is the rows that remain unhooked; wonder is the yellow surface sitting so calmly above a crowded blue. The plate invents no harvest festival. It keeps the singling-out as manners: look, choose carefully, leave the rest of the chorus unclaimed.
