# Pot of Greatfullness

## Name

Pot of Greatfullness

## Overview

*Pot of Greatfullness* isolates a yellow bulbous vessel on a pale blue table with thin red legs against charcoal dark, a looping black line lifting a white jagged flower-object with a red center from the pot’s mouth.

## Description

Flat graphic shapes, no room context—only vessel, table, and the lifted strange ingredient. Fullness here looks less like comfort than like something still becoming.

Asset reference: `assets/pot-of-greatfullness.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

Emblem image for the Still Pot as object.

## Lore Connection

The yellow vessel stands for Vorrith’s compounding craft; the white-red lift suggests a frequency ingredient pulled mid-mix. “Greatfullness” in the title is treated as ironic abundance: the pot gives more than a body may want to keep.
