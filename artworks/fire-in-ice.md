# Fire in Ice

## Name

Fire in Ice

## Overview

*Fire in Ice* shows a golden-yellow figure draped limply over a pale cyan translucent cube that holds a red-orange flame inside.

## Description

Dark charcoal field, long shadow from the cube. The body is back-bent in surrender; the fire burns without consuming the ice-glass.

Asset reference: `assets/fire-in-ice.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A stark emblem of containment costing the container's keeper.

## Lore Connection

Visual for the **Icebound Flame Cell**: a Resonant Artifact pattern where living heat (or living echo) is sealed in cold clarity. The draped figure is read as the last handler emptied by the seal—or an Echoform husk that could not leave the cell.
