# Dull Crust and Steel-Blue Flecks

## Name

Dull Crust and Steel-Blue Flecks

## Overview

*Dull Crust and Steel-Blue Flecks* is Reveal-Shelf Lichen at rest. A dull cool-gray crust lies on glass-gray stone. Steel-blue flecks sit in it like freckles. The plate is the resting state only. It does not call the crust a map, and it does not show an open window. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

The frame is stone and the growth on it, close enough that there is no ledge, no room, and no sky. The stone is glass-gray, cool, with the fine crackle of a hard surface rather than soil. Over it spreads a dull cool-gray crust, low and tight, not a bloom and not a lace pulled up into brightness. Steel-blue flecks are scattered through the crust. In this state they read as freckles in ordinary stone-growth. They are not lifted into an under-pattern. Nothing in the crust is drawn as a tiny map, a brace, a letter, or a diagram a hand could copy.

The account says the lichen brightens only during a ledge window and goes dull on purpose afterward. This plate is the afterward, or the before: resting, dull, flecked, and not opened. It does not show the minute when an under-pattern appears. It does not show a window opening or closing. Calling the resting crust a map would be the plate doing what the lichen, at rest, is not doing. The picture refuses that. There is no scrape, no scar from a theft of timed skin, and no tool left against the stone.

No apprentice is packing. No one is staring the crust back into brightness. The stone stays glass-gray. The crust stays dull. The flecks stay flecks.

## Real-World Role

A flora plate of a timed growth while it is being boring on purpose. Rest is the state. The reveal is not in the frame.

## Lore Connection

Reveal-Shelf Lichen on the Third Window Shelf, kin to the glass of The Glassfold Ledge, brightens only in a ledge window and then returns to dull cool-gray crust with steel-blue flecks. Those flecks are freckles until a window opens. In the minute, an under-pattern can show, small enough to limit an apprentice's hunger to copy everything. This plate does not show that minute. It shows the resting crust, and it does not redraw the flecks into maps or into an open window. After the close, the account says, it is ordinary stone-freckle again. Scraping in the dull phase is rude. Scraping in the bright phase is greed. Neither scrape is here. The lichen is said to smell of cold metal and rain that did not fall. Smell is not in the picture. A claim that the flecks follow a lingerer is treated as myth, not as a propagation event. Nothing is leaving the stone. The pattern, when it comes, is a visitor. This plate is the time when the visitor is not in. You are not being shown a thing you may keep. You are being shown dull crust, glass-gray stone, and steel-blue flecks at rest.

## Notes

Resting state only. Dull cool-gray crust. Glass-gray stone. Steel-blue flecks. Not a map. Not an open window. No person.
