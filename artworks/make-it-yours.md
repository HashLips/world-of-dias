# Make It Yours

## Name

Make It Yours

## Overview

*Make It Yours* reads left to right across mustard over grey: slender dark figure facing left; same figure sprouting a sharp back wing; deep lunge with a large crescent wing; horizontal airborne body with beak beginning and legs trailing; full bird silhouette soaring upper-right with a faint ground shadow far below.

## Description

Each stage casts its own shadow logic—attached, then detached oval, then distant. No sand dunes drawn; the metamorphosis is the landscape. Mood is earned flight, not instant magic.

Asset reference: `assets/make-it-yours.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A becoming plate: make the change yours by passing every middle stage.

## Lore Connection

Lumira Sands teaches Make It Yours as the rule against skipping the lunge. Local care: claim the wing only after the sprout and the push-off. Resonance sticks to each silhouette you actually stood in. Jumping straight to the final bird for show empties the sand’s patience.
