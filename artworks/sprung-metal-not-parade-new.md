## Overview

*Sprung Metal, Not Parade-New* is the Vault Latch That Failed as metal that has already lost. The latch is sprung. The metal is scratched. It is not parade-new. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

A heavy latch lies where a closure failed. It is sprung: the bite that should have held is open, the mechanism past the moment it was asked to pin something shut. The metal is scratched. The scratches are use and refusal, not a finish applied so the object can be admired as new. Nothing about the latch is parade-new. It has been handled, forced, and kept after.

The entry does not record engraving. This plate does not promote any flourish on the metal into engraving-as-canon. Ornament is not the fact that holds. The facts that hold are sprung, scratched, and not fresh from a case. No legend is cut into the latch for this record to read aloud. No motto explains the night.

The Frame is not here to be pinned. The alcove is not rebuilt around the hardware. No hands are on the metal. The picture does not restage a door that closed, because the latch is the evidence that it did not. It is a sprung, scratched latch, and it is not being offered as a second kit.
