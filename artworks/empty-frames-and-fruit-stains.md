# Empty Frames and Fruit Stains

## Name

Empty Frames and Fruit Stains

## Overview

*Empty Frames and Fruit Stains* is a Softfruit Manners Chapbook. A small book. Fruit stains. Geometric jokes in the margins. Picture frames with nothing pictured in them. The pictured subjects are not invented.

## Description

Small bound pages. Fruit stains. Three-script cards simplified into pictures, in the account of the book. The plate does not invent what those pictures show. The frames are empty. No child, no plate of food, no third chair drawn as a scene inside them. Geometry jokes sit in the margins, the Axiomorph jokes the entry names, and they stay geometric. They are not turned into a new alphabet of readable manners-words.

The book teaches share plates, soft address, and third-chair kindness. That teaching is the book's job. It is not a cast of characters supplied by the picture. Adults pretend they do not still check the page on hard days. No adult is checking it here. The stains are fruit. The frames are empty. The margin jokes are geometric.
