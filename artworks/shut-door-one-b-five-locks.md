# Shut Door, One B, Five Locks

## Name

Shut Door, One B, Five Locks

## Overview

*Shut Door, One B, Five Locks* is the door of The Sealed Beta Chamber. The door stays shut. A gold emblem carries a single B. Five different locks hold the way. The benches are empty. The interior is not shown.

## Description

The door stays shut. It is the wooden door of the never-opened inner round room at the center of The Passage Round, and this plate does not open it. A gold emblem is on the door, engraved with a single B. The entry calls that gold solid and of high carat, and takes the B as Beta, Delci's name held at the heart of his own architecture. The plate shows the emblem and the one letter. It does not add a second initial or a sentence around the B.

In front of the door the way is barricaded with five different locks. The count is five. They are different from each other. The plate does not pick them as a puzzle with a solution and does not show what any of them opens, because they do not open. Around the door, the Passage Round's benches continue, empty, as if an audience might one day sit. No audience is sitting.

The interior is not shown and not described. The chamber's contents stay unknown, which is the canon. A wreath is not part of this record. Nothing is required on the door beyond the shut wood, the gold B, the five locks, and the empty benches.

## Lore Connection

The Sealed Beta Chamber is reserved, in Delci Beta's own framing, for a special occasion that has not yet come. It sits in the middle of the tube-like Round, reached only through that door. The door has never been opened in this dreamer's mind. Shore gossip does not explain the purpose. Delci's sparse comments treat it as waiting, not forgotten. Whether the chamber hides a place, a memory, a person, or a vow remains open. Governance in the entry is sealed, unopened, with no leadership seat in force.

This plate is the outside of that wait. Shut door, gold emblem with a single B, five different locks, empty benches. It does not show an interior, because the interior is unknown and the notes forbid inventing contents. Golden Beta Emblem and Delci Passage Logbook are the paper and the mark beside the architecture. They do not become a view through the door. A wreath is not required to recognize the B.
