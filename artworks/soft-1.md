# Soft

## Name

Soft

## Overview

*Soft* is the Wabet plate for the magenta look-up. Head tilted back. White edge lights on eye, nose, lips, ear. Purple under-jaw shadow. Pale blue almost-white field.

## Description

Very pale cool light-blue ground. A single human-like figure from the chest up in vibrant saturated magenta, side profile, head tilted significantly back toward the upper right. Serene awe: thin white crescent for an upward eye, white shapes catching nose bridge, parted lips, and a white hook for the ear. Deeper purple along underside of jaw, neck, and behind the ear gives quiet volume without hard outline. Tiny black three-prong crown/gate icon top-right. No environment clutter—only the soft upward relation.

A phenomenon plate for Wabet. Soft is the named magenta upward-gaze hour.

## Lore Connection

Soft is how Wabet names the hour when the magenta profile tilts back and white edge-light catches eye and lips while purple keeps the jaw honest. Local care says soft means wonder without grabbing what is above; you do not yank the chin down for a joke. Resonance is magenta on pale blue; memory is the white crescent eye; wonder is how quiet a parted mouth can be. The plate invents no sky god. It keeps the weather as manners for looking up gently.
