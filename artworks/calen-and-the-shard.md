# Calen and the Shard

## Name

Calen and the Shard

## Overview

*Calen and the Shard* is a cinematic close portrait of **Calen Brink** holding a jagged luminous blue Resonant Shard, with Veloria’s distant towers soft in the golden-hour haze behind her.

## Description

Calen appears as a young woman, travel-worn, dust on skin and scarf, dark hair pulled back with loose strands. She holds a palm-sized dark fragment whose interior fractures glow electric blue, lighting her hands and face. The land between her and the city is rocky and sparse; the city reads as pale luminous spires on the horizon.

Asset reference: `assets/calen-and-shard.png`.

## Real-World Role

Defining portrait lock for Calen in trailer and README use; anchors the “shard first, city second” opening beat of the World of Dias cinematic.

## Lore Connection

Shows Calen’s early approach to Veloria as echo-sensitivity awakens through a held shard—discovery before civilization. Complements basin shard lessons without claiming this exact stone is the Basin Rail Shard.

## Notes

Treat as Calen’s primary visual reference until a newer portrait supersedes it.
