# Bird Flew

## Name

Bird Flew

## Overview

*Bird Flew* is an Aelwyn Canopy witness plate: angular black birds gather around a fallen companion under a navy orb and a white jagged beam, the moment after flight when the canopy still has to decide how the echo returns to ground.

## Description

A pale yellow field holds a massive deep-navy circular orb in the upper middle. Behind it, a muted green cross-shaped pole stands with two thick black lines cutting across the orb like wires. A bright pink elongated torso-shape overlaps the orb; from its base a sharp jagged white beam points straight down. On the ground, one black bird lies flat on a reddish-orange pool. Another stands on thin angular legs looking down. A third, marked with a red spot on the head, dives mid-flight toward the center. A fourth watches from the far left. Soft blue-grey shadow under the standing bird. Mood: quiet ritual intensity after something has broken or descended.

## Real-World Role

A canopy care plate: what neighbors do when one of their own has finished flying.

## Lore Connection

In the Aelwyn Canopy, **Bird Flew** names the local presence that arrives when a flight ends badly or strangely. The navy orb is the canopy’s heavy sky-memory; the pink residual form and white beam are how tellers mark a hard landing of resonance—not as Earth-faith light, but as a place deciding whether the echo stays. The red-spotted diver is the first to approach, by canopy rule, so the fallen’s touch is returned cleanly to the path. The orange pool is essence that should not be bottled for sale. Wonder is neighbor duty: look, approach in order, do not pretend the quiet is nothing.
