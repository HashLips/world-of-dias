# Antlers as Light Pillars

## Name

Antlers as Light Pillars

## Overview

*Antlers as Light Pillars* is a digital image study of F200 Pillar-Stags as rare luminous majesty.

## Description

Large standing radiance-stag forms hold the frame; antlers climb as vertical light pillars. Approach lore says color temperature warms—here the plate simply keeps warmer gold at the horns and hooves. Herd rare; not for riding.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/f200-pillar-stags.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of large F200 majesty without translating them into F432 elk.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens F200 Pillar-Stags without inventing riding, hunting rules, or elk equivalence.

## Notes

Deep blue body fill on the plate is decorative lighting, not a new race trait beyond radiance. Border framing only.
