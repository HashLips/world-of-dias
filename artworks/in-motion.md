# In Motion

## Name

In Motion

## Overview

*In Motion* is an Outer Rim row of five long-necked mask-heads on a light grey field: center figure a solid dark silhouette with a bright yellow crescent crown tipped in vivid red; flanking figures fade into translucent grey with overlapping yellow crescents and muted hooks.

## Description

Each head shows thin white eye-slits, a sharp white L for brow-and-nose, and a small white mouth line—solemn, identical. Necks are long and straight. Yellow crescents curve over every crown; where translucent bodies overlap, yellow darkens. Only the front dark figure keeps a saturated red hook on the crescent’s right tip. No sea, no boat—motion is implied by layering, like frames of one advance caught in a still. The mood is ceremonial forwardness without a visible stride.

Asset reference: `assets/in-motion.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A procession plate for Rim crews: motion as shared crown-line, not scattered hurry.

## Lore Connection

Nauw Outer Rim Seas work in weather that punishes lone rush. In Motion teaches keeping crescent-line with your row—dark lead with the red hook tip as the readable front, greys as the following echo. Local rule: if your hook goes red while you are not the agreed front, step back into grey until the line resets. Resonance rides the overlapping crescents. Cinder Nook cousins know the same patience in smoke; here it is salt-bright and row-shaped.

## Notes

Five layered figures, yellow crescents, one red hook.
