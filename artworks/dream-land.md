# Dream Land

## Name

Dream Land

## Overview

*Dream Land* depicts a pale profile with closed eyes overlooking layered land and water—serene, inward, already elsewhere.

## Description

A white head and face in calm profile, eyes shut, a dark green band across the brow. Behind: olive sky, brown cloud-shapes, a bright blue band of water or horizon, rolling earth in brown and burnt orange. The mood is not escape as panic; it is chosen quiet.

Asset reference: `assets/dream-land.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A contemplative profile study linked to Dias dream-path imagery alongside *My Path*.

## Lore Connection

Read as **Delci Beta**'s inward country: the still hours made visible. The closed eyes match the Good Trade rock habit; the layered landscape matches destinations opened by the Passage Logbook without naming one place.

## Notes

Does not depict Delci's waking face in fixed detail—only the dream-listening posture.
