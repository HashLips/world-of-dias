# Sheep Just Follow

## Name

Sheep Just Follow

## Overview

*Sheep Just Follow* is the Brindle March plate for the magenta flock. Nine pink cloud-bodies. Black herder with white hat and staff. Huge red-orange disc. Jagged black barrier. Slate sky, gray ground.

## Description

Dark slate-blue sky; dark gray ground. Upper center: massive deep reddish-orange perfect circle, partly cut by horizon. Far right: solid black herder walking right—small rounded white hat, thin black staff over shoulder, white mouth-sliver, white eye-dot. Nine brilliant magenta-pink cloud-edged creatures with four thin black stick legs, stark white triangular faces, small black pointed ears, single black eye-dot—eight clustered left, one nearer the herder right. Center mid: jagged vertical black rock/chasm edge between main flock and leader. Soft dark gray oval shadows under each. Neon flock on somber march weather.

Asset reference: `assets/sheep-just-follow.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

An inhabitant plate for Brindle March. Sheep Just Follow is the magenta cloud-flock and its black guide.

## Lore Connection

Sheep Just Follow is the march's name for the hour when magenta cloud-bodies cross gray ground under the red-orange disc and the black herder keeps the staff horizontal. Local care says the name is a warning about blank following—and also a care rule for the flock: do not scatter them for sport across the jagged barrier. Resonance is magenta against burnt orange and slate; memory is the white triangular faces; wonder is how soft cloud-edges look on desolate ground. The plate invents no farm census. It keeps the presence as road manners toward guided pink resonance-creatures.
