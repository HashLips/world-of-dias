# Citrus on the Bristles, No Crest

## Name

Citrus on the Bristles, No Crest

## Overview

*Citrus on the Bristles, No Crest* is a Banner Soft-Brush. Soft bristles. Short handle. Citrus-stained. It sits beside a pot. There is no crest.

## Description

Soft bristles and a short handle, the tool that lifts Banner Moths from stalls and pots without crushing them. Often citrus-stained. The stain is on the brush. Vendors hang these beside pots. The plate puts the brush beside a pot. It does not pin a moth under the bristles as a specimen, and it does not put a boot in the frame to show the crush the brush prevents.

There is no crest. No house mark, no fair device, no motto on the handle. Joy tech does not need a seal to be the antidote to bad shopkeeping. Crushing a moth with a boot is bad shopkeeping. The brush is that antidote made object. The object is what you get.

## Lore Connection

Market mysticism as gentleness. Children earn trust by brushing first. The children are not shown. Fair care, teaching, and apologizing after a near-crush are the uses. A chapter worry is that brushes vanish before fair week, or that one brush finds a wrong-color moth. Neither event is this frame. The brush is here, citrus-stained, beside a pot, without a crest. Pip Ringroad's serious work and Flagweek are the neighborhood of the object. The neighborhood is not a parade in the picture.
