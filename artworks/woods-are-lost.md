# Woods Are Lost

## Name

Woods Are Lost

## Overview

*Woods Are Lost* centers a yellow spindle figure with black root lines and a black oval head against olive, mustard ground, and thin red stakes.

## Description

Symmetrical yellow body with outspread yellow arms, black spindly overlays like hanging roots, black neck forking under a flat black oval. Olive upper field, grey step left, mustard ground right with five thin red uprights. Long grey shadow. Small mark top right.

Asset reference: `assets/woods-are-lost.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

Aelwyn plate for forest loss as a standing sign, not a map of cut acres.

## Lore Connection

The yellow figure reads as a last tree spirit or path mark. Red stakes stay unexplained pressure on the ground.
