# Blank Board over Westfold

## Name

Blank Board over Westfold

## Overview

Blank Board over Westfold is a digital image study of the supervised checkpoint at Westfold Toll.

## Description

The plate shows a practical timber-and-stone checkpoint, a clerk shed, a chain across the road, and an empty cart. The board over the gate is blank.

Asset reference: `assets/westfold-toll.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Westfold Toll as the supervised crossing between western Hearthvale and southeastern Wabet.

## Lore Connection

The artwork keeps the crossing's practical checkpoint character and barrier in view without adding a price, readable papers, or a person.

Artwork entry for the existing subject **Westfold Toll**. The image adds no new canon beyond the region's fixed checkpoint, shed, chain, cart, and blank board.
