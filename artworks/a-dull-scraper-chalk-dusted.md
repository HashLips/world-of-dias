# A Dull Scraper, Chalk-Dusted

## Name

A Dull Scraper, Chalk-Dusted

## Overview

*A Dull Scraper, Chalk-Dusted* is a Margin Scrape Blade. It is thin, bone or brass, with a dull edge and chalk on it. It lifts ink without erasing the scar. It is not a weapon.

## Description

The blade is thin. Bone or brass. The edge is flat and slightly curved, the clerk's shape, and it is dull. It is not sharpened like a weapon. Chalk dusts it, the dust of margins rather than of a fight. The work is to lift ink and leave the scar of a wrong stamp readable, so a correction stays history instead of becoming silence.

Apprentices learn scrape-before-stamp-over. The plate does not show the lesson, the stamp, or the margin being eaten. It shows the scraper itself, dull and chalk-dusted. The nightmare of clerks who threw their blades away is a myth, The Stamp That Ate Its Margin. The myth is not acted out here. The tool that prevents it is.

No junior is holding it for the portrait. No House room that worships clean pages is forbidding it in the background. The object is small, corrective, and deliberately not a knife for anything but ink.

A clerk-tool plate. Thin, dull, chalk-dusted. The scar of a wrong stamp can remain. It is not a weapon.
