# Split

## Name

Split

## Overview

*Split* is the Averra Isle plate for the striped formal face. Pure black and white. Blank white eye voids. One hanging crown cord. Vertical stripe garment. White center hang.

## Description

Medium close-up, front-facing, chest up. Solid black smooth bald head; one long thin undulating white/black cord from the crown hanging past the viewer's-left shoulder. Face: two blank almond white voids without pupils; single vertical white nose line; thin straight white mouth; stylized white semi-circular ear outlines. Crisp white outline separates jaw/neck from torso. Garment: bold vertical alternating black and white stripes, slightly irregular widths like hand-woven. Center chest: solid white narrow necktie-like shape hanging vertical. No gray. Formal, stoic, enigmatic split of pattern and void eyes.

Asset reference: `assets/split-5.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Averra Isle. Split is the kept craft of the striped formal with blank eyes.

## Lore Connection

Split is Averra's name for the hour when the face holds blank white eyes and the garment splits into vertical black-white rhythm under one crown cord. Local care says split means pattern and void both honest; you do not paint pupils to force friendliness. Resonance is stripe repetition; memory is the hanging cord; wonder is how much form pure black and white can hold. The plate invents no bureaucracy villain. It keeps the craft as damp-studio manners for formal attention.
