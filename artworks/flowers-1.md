# Flowers

## Name

Flowers

## Overview

*Flowers* is an Aelwyn Canopy speaking-bouquet plate: a grey vase holds black stems that end in white star blooms and black profile heads—the flora that hosts conversation without pretending the heads are only decoration.

## Description

Muted olive-grey wall over a black floor strip. Dark brown thin-legged rectangular table. Light-grey rounded vase with a white jagged zigzag band and a straight white band below. Six thick curving black stems fan out: three end in white sharp-petaled star/daisy blooms; three end in large silhouetted human heads in profile with minimalist white eye-nose-mouth lines—one left looking down, one upper right smiling toward center, one lower right mouth open as if answering. Sparse white almond leaves with a black vein-line. Surreal, whimsical, slightly eerie.

Asset reference: `assets/flowers-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A canopy flora plate: arrange stems so living talk and white stars share the vase; do not snip the heads for a prettier still life.

## Lore Connection

In the Aelwyn Canopy, **Flowers** names echo-bloom arrangements that keep guest likenesses and star petals together. Wonder is hosted memory. Cutting only the white stars and discarding head-stems is emptied craft.
