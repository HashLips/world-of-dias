# Hunt Already Survived

## Name

Hunt Already Survived

## Overview

*Hunt Already Survived* is a digital image study of the Almost-Same Wolf as recurrence with teeth: a hunt already survived, returning slightly wrong.

## Description

A lean wolf-shape stalks low from right to left, head down and eyes pale. The dark coat frays upward into smoke-like wisps along the spine and tail, threaded with faint pale vein-lines, as if the animal were partly redrawn. A faint oval of looping lines arcs around it like a track walked twice; jagged crags rise at both edges.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/almost-same-wolf.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of F120's teaching that recurrence is not safety—huge in stories, rare in records.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the 'I already lived this, and it changed' account without settling coat color, attack mechanics, or whether it truly returns. Status stays unknown.
