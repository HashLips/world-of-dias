# Blank Slate at the Peel and Bell

## Name

Blank Slate at the Peel and Bell

## Overview

Blank Slate at the Peel and Bell is a digital image study of the empty outer-ring bar and its practical, layered light.

## Description

The room is empty: stone floor, dark benches, a counter of scarred fruitwood, and a high window. Oil lamps and a few bulbs share the light. A blank slate holds its place without offering a readable board. The peel-bucket and brass bell belong to the bar’s established identity even when the plate lets them remain quiet.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of The Peel and Bell as a road-facing room for drink, gossip, and the careful keeping of what may be said.

## Lore Connection

The artwork preserves the bar as one wet outer-ring room. It does not add a back room, a readable notice, or a passage beyond F432.
