# Ash and Soft Clay at Kiln Laughter Yard

## Name

Ash and Soft Clay at Kiln Laughter Yard

## Overview

Ash and Soft Clay at Kiln Laughter Yard is a digital image study of Secondfire’s open work-court for remaking.

## Description

An open dusty yard holds kilns, ash, broken tools, and soft clay. The scene keeps the work-court’s warmth in the material itself: what has cracked can return to the bench, and what is soft can take a new impression. The image leaves the number of kilns unstated.
