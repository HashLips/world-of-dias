# Spaces

## Name

Spaces

## Overview

*Spaces* is the Driftfall plate for the shared hourglass. Two white profiles face in. Blue glass, yellow-orange caps. Chunky brown terrain pouring. Yellow lines from ears to base. Deep red field.

## Description

Deep red background; angled dark reddish-brown foreground with a small gray patch bottom-left. Left and right: identical white human-like heads in profile facing each other—large solid black almond eyes, simple black curved ear lines. Center: large hourglass with thick solid yellow-orange caps; vibrant saturated blue glass body; inside, chunky brown substance like jagged mountain terrain mid-flow through the neck into a larger bottom mound—not fine sand. Two thin yellow lines run from each ear down to meet at the hourglass base, framing an inverted triangle of listening. Tiny black icon top-right. Quiet shared watch of material time.
