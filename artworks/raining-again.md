# Raining Again

## Name

Raining Again

## Overview

*Raining Again* is the F432 plate for the inverted sail in the void. White elongated figure arched back. Large inside-out geometric catcher. Thin rod. Horizontal white dashes. Black everywhere else.

## Description

Pure black field. Left, a slender featureless white humanoid arches deeply backward—one leg straight down, one bent sharp at the knee—single diagonal slit for an eye. A long thin arm reaches; pointed fingers grip the curved hook of a thin rod. The rod stays straight under tension to a large white object like an umbrella blown inside-out, sharp geometric segments nearly as large as the torso and legs together, shoved toward the right. Short horizontal white dashes streak beside it as wind, rain-strip, or stripped particles.

Tiny white trident-like glyph top-left; faint mark bottom-right. No horizon. The plate is labor against a force you cannot see except as dashes and an inverted sail.

## Real-World Role

A phenomenon plate for F432. Raining Again is the named return of the void weather that turns the catcher inside-out.

## Lore Connection

Raining Again is how F432 names the hour when the white figure must lean into an inverted sail and the dashes prove the current has come back. Local care says you hold the rod; you do not pretend the black void is calm. Resonance is tension in the straight rod; memory is the eye-slit focus; wonder is white labor on total black. The plate invents no rescue saga. It keeps the weather as manners for necessary struggle when rain—or what counts as rain here—returns.
