# Eye in the Sky

## Name

Eye in the Sky

## Overview

*Eye in the Sky* places a swarm of small red-irised eyes opposite one enormous matching eye on a pale pink field.

## Description

Purple outlines, red irises, black pupils, white triangular highlights. The small eyes cluster and drift as if flowing toward the large one.

Asset reference: `assets/eye-in-the-sky.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A graphic of hierarchical watching: fragments and the whole that gathers them.

## Lore Connection

Glassfold and Veloria sky-talk both claim it. Wall-eye events as swarm; Sky Drifter rumor as the large gaze that never lands. The piece refuses to pick a single explanation—only the feeling of being counted by sight.
