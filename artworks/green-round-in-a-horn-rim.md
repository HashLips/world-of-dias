# Green Round in a Horn Rim

## Name

Green Round in a Horn Rim

## Overview

*Green Round in a Horn Rim* is a Sky-Dip Glass lying as an object: a thick round of slightly green glass in a horn rim. There is no handle and no spyglass tube. The glass does not show a face or a Sky Drifter. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

The round is thick glass, slightly green, set in a pale horn rim with a simple repeating edge. It sits flat on worn wood, the size of something a hand used to coins could cover, and it has no handle, no draw tube, and no stand that would turn it into a spyglass. The green is in the glass itself, a tint, not a picture floating inside it. No face appears in the round. No pale cluster, no drifter, and no lantern is resolved there. The surface reflects the room's light and stops.

The wood around it is empty of sky. The plate is not aimed upward. It does not show the rings, a roof hour, or a festival shape. It shows the lens at rest, green and rimmed, with nothing in it that could be mistaken for a person or for a being in the air.

## Real-World Role

A still life of a look that is not a capture. The glass is on the table. The sky is not in the frame, and neither is anyone's face.

## Lore Connection

A Sky-Dip Glass is a palm lens of thick, slightly green glass in a horn rim. Through it, Veloria's Sky Drifters look one shade nearer, and no nearer than that. They do not descend. The glass will not focus on ledgers, faces, or the Long Gate's far end. Pointed at a festival sky-whale lantern, it only shows paper and light. This plate does not point it. It does not show a drifter, a face, or a lantern. It is useless as a spyglass and is shown that way: a round in a rim, no handle, no tube. Farstock sells the look, not the sky. The picture sells neither. It keeps the object on the wood.
