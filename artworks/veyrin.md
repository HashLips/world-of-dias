# Mirrored Gaze of a Veyrin

## Name

Mirrored Gaze of a Veyrin

## Overview

*Mirrored Gaze of a Veyrin* is a digital image study of a Veyrin courier in F432, humanoid at first glance, with one clear signature-trait marker set.

## Description

The figure is broadly humanoid in dress and carriage, read as a courier at focused work. Mirrored irises catch the gaze; vein-light runs along one forearm; a soft halo-like distortion gathers around the hands during use. The markers stay narrow and situational—trait signs, not spectacle armor.

The plate presents workplace competence, not carnival display. Background framing is decorative only.
