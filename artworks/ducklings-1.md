# Ducklings

## Name

Ducklings

## Overview

*Ducklings* is an Aelwyn Canopy flock plate: countless yellow avian figures swim the mint field in alternating tilt while one thick blue stroke cuts a single path—the many that move together, and the one mark that must not herd them into harm.

## Description

Pale mint-green ground. Dense staggered grid of identical warm-yellow birdlets—rounded heads, small pointed orange-red beaks, white eye with black pupil, curved body and small upswept tail—alternating orientation left-up and down-tilt as if swimming. Across them, a deep royal-blue calligraphic ribbon: wide flat start upper left, sharp curve down through center, tapering arc toward the right. Flat textile-clean cheer with a singular path.

Asset reference: `assets/ducklings-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A canopy inhabitant plate: flock rhythm is care; a blue path is guidance only if it leaves gaps.

## Lore Connection

In the Aelwyn Canopy, **Ducklings** names the small non-human many that fill a path after rain. The blue stroke is a keeper’s path-mark, not a net. Wonder is collective motion with exit room. Dias flock-name, not an Earth nursery claim.

Yellow repeating avians. Orange beaks. Mint field. One thick blue stroke.
