# Cracked Upright, Incomplete Aperture

## Name

Cracked Upright, Incomplete Aperture

## Overview

*Cracked Upright, Incomplete Aperture* is a digital image study of Orrin Veld's Spectrum Frame after the Caliburn's escape: cracked, partial, and kept that way on purpose.

## Description

The Frame stands at the center, rendered solid against a pale sketched workshop. It is a tall rectangle with ornate blue-and-brass edging and heavy corner fittings. Down both uprights run thick braided cords, wrapped and knotted, and at the four corners sit cylindrical spools of filament; the lower right and upper right spools are dark, one near-black, against the brass of the others.

The inner plane is a gray-green pane of thin instrument glass. Fine graduated ticks run along its inner edges like rulers, and thin cracks branch diagonally across it. No bands, graphs, or glow appear in the glass: the instrument is at rest and unpowered.

Behind, the workshop is drawn in faint linework: tool walls, a doorway, benches. A leafy climbing vine rises along the right edge. The decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/spectrum-frame.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of the Spectrum Frame as a measurement instrument that became a warning: tuned glass, filament spools, measure-marks, and damage left visible.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the Spectrum Frame's established image (standing rectangular frame in the Veld Workshop Alcove, edges wrapped in waxed cord, inner plane of etched instrument glass, side filament spools, a cracked upright and a blackened corner spool from vault attempts) and its post-escape role as a deliberately incomplete teaching tool on Calibration Row.

## Notes

The plate shows the Frame unpowered; it does not depict a run, the residual calibration seam, or the Caliburn. Which aperture is left incomplete is not legible in the plate and is not fixed here. The vine at the edge recalls the alcove's Oilvine Creeper and is decorative. Shoulder-high scale and heel-cap plates stay as canon even where the plate is ambiguous. It does not open doors.
