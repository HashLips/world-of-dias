# New Path

## Name

New Path

## Overview

*New Path* is the F200 plate for the crowned walker who carries geometry in the chest. dark silhouette. Blue crown. Nested triangles. White cloak-wings. Ladder left. Red hook right.

## Description

Center stands a solid dark figure facing right. One thin white horizontal line marks the eye. A small bright blue crown with three jagged peaks sits on the head. Across chest and belly: a large upward blue triangle, a smaller orange triangle nested inside it, and a purple vertical lens overlapping both centers—inner wayfinding worn as body mark, not as a map held in the hand.

From behind the torso a broad white shape spreads like flattened wings or a heavy ceremonial cloak, pure and wide. On the left, a translucent ladder of light brown and blue lines leans diagonally toward the figure. On the right, a thick dark-red calligraphic hook curves, echoed by a softer teal-green rounded form behind it. Soft blue and teal lobes fill the base like low hills or cloud-water. Above a muted gray horizon band, the sky is deep brown; olive-green wisps float. Top-left: a large yellow sun cut by a dark green horizontal bar. Top-right: a solid red semicircle.

The plate does not draw a road surface. The path is the crowned figure's stance between ladder and hook, sun and red arc, with the nested chest geometry as the decision already being walked.

## Lore Connection

New Path is how F200 names the hour when a walker puts on the blue three-peak and lets the nested triangles and purple lens settle as the inner rule before any ladder is climbed or any red hook is followed. Wonder stays in the white cloak-wing and the cut yellow sun. Local care says the new path is not the loudest curve on the right; it is the geometry you agree to carry. Memory keeps the silhouette facing one way so others can recognize the stance without needing a sermon written in the brown sky.
