# Golden Bath

## Name

Golden Bath

## Overview

*Golden Bath* is a Wabet wash plate: a white figure reclines in a polished golden basin under a cyan orb while glowing footprints approach—the quiet care of Ringwash manners without turning the bath into a stage.

## Description

Dusty rose wall; deep chocolate-brown floor. Bright golden four-legged tub with sharp white metallic highlights. Stark white stylized humanoid reclines—head on right hand at left rim with a small red eye-dot, left arm hanging limp, legs raised high at right. Featureless light cyan circle floats above the midsection. Three glowing cyan footprints lead from bottom center toward the left, stopping short of the tub’s reddish-brown shadow. Large black curved sickle-like shape lies flat before the tub legs. Surreal, medicinal-quiet.

Asset reference: `assets/golden-bath.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A Wabet artifact plate: bathe in gold light; leave cyan tracks of approach; keep the sickle as a floor reminder, not a threat prop.

## Lore Connection

Around Wabet and Ringwash Baths talk, **Golden Bath** is kept wash craft. Cyan orb is the pause-mark above the water. Footprints show who approached with care. Black sickle on the floor is a local tool-shape reminder to cut only what needs cutting—never the guest. Wonder is restorative quiet.
