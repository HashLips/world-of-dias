# Interview

## Name

Interview

## Overview

*Interview* is an Ashcalm Rise consult plate: luminous orange body on a small black one-legged stool, purple floor, long black desk bar, and a solid black writer behind it holding a thin pen-tool beside a small tool pot, dark grey wall, four-pane window outline above.

## Description

The orange figure is lean, long-limbed, dark eye-slit, pointed chin, right leg crossed, one arm hanging, one on the lap—relaxed enough to answer, not collapsed. the dark figure works mid-task, not posing. Contrast does the talking: orange is the spoken presence; black is the record. No stack of papers shown; the desk is a clean bar. Mood is quiet documentation, not interrogation theater.

Ashcalm Rise uses Interview for load talks, craft disputes, and traveler checks.
