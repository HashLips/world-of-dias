# Secret

## Name

Secret

## Overview

*Secret* is the Cinder Nook plate for the sealed orange panel. Four corner grommets. Red wax-like seal with white bird. Orange ribbons from a top slot. Black profile below. Blue diagonal beam.

## Description

Medium-gray field. Center: large orange rectangle like heavy parchment or metal plate, four small dark circular grommets at corners. Exact center: bright red circular seal with a white simplified bird silhouette standing on thin legs facing right. From a narrow black horizontal slot at top center, five thick wavy orange ribbon strands drape over the upper plate. Four thin vertical yellow lines run from the grommets to black slots at top and bottom edges—suspension mounts.

Bottom center: large solid black head-and-neck profile facing left, one thin white curved sliver as closed eye or mouth. A thick vibrant blue diagonal cuts lower-left to mid-right behind the plate. Pale white-blue wavy ribbon weaves near the black face and behind the plate. Tiny black house/H icon top-left. Formal display of a sealed thing above a listening profile.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Cinder Nook. Secret is the kept craft of the bird-sealed orange panel.

## Lore Connection

Secret is the Nook's name for the hour when the orange plate hangs on yellow lines, the red bird seal holds, and the black profile stays below without peeling. Local care says you read what the seal allows; you do not rip ribbons for curiosity. Resonance is orange and red on gray with blue cut; memory is the white bird; wonder is the draped strands. The plate invents no spy novel. It keeps the craft as manners for sealed meaning.
