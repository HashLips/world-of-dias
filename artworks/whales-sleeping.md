# Whales Sleeping

## Name

Whales Sleeping

## Overview

*Whales Sleeping* is a whale held in its own breath. A big coral-red whale with a square head and a curling tail floats tilted across a teal ground, its eye a closed, contented crescent. From its blowhole rise two white arcs of spout; one bends over its head and comes down the other side to become a long white arm with clawed fingers curled beneath its jaw. From above, a black, branchlike arm with its own clawed hand reaches down toward the blowhole.

## Description

The whale is a single flat coral shape, its head blunt, its lower jaw a thin line, its body sweeping up into a broad fluked tail at the upper right. It is clearly asleep: the eye is a gentle white arc, the mouth relaxed.

The white spout is the plate's invention. It leaves the blowhole as two fountains curving outward. The left arc swings wide and down past the whale's head and turns into a long, thin white forearm that runs beneath the chin, ending in five splayed claws at the left edge—as if the breath itself were cradling the sleeper.

The black arm from above is thinner and more knotted, like driftwood or a dark branch, with a clawed hand of its own pointing up. It might be reaching for the spout, steadying the whale, or simply dangling. The teal water is empty otherwise. The mood is tender and strange.

## Real-World Role

A sea-creature plate about whales held afloat in sleep by their own breath.

## Lore Connection

The Nauw Outer Rim Seas hold the realm's densest whale populations and singing fishing communities. Rim crews say whales there sleep at the surface, tilted, and that their slow spouts arc back down and hold them up like arms so they do not sink while dreaming. The plate shows a sleeper cradled that way, with a dark branchlike arm above that crews argue about: some call it drift, some call it a second sleeper's spout gone dark with cold. Rim custom is to cut engines and oars and drift past sleeping whales in silence, and never to pass between a whale and its spout.
