# Three Scratches, No Chair

## Name

Three Scratches, No Chair

## Overview

*Three Scratches, No Chair* is a Third-Chair Token. Palm-sized. Wood or clay. Moss-stained. A crude three scratches. There is no chair. There is no coin.

## Description

Palm-sized wood or clay, sometimes moss-stained, sometimes carved with a crude three. The plate takes the crude three as scratches, not as a finished numeral-logo and not as a picture of furniture. No chair is in the frame. The token is the promise that a chair exists somewhere west. The chair itself is not shown.

Not VEL. Not a Keep pass. Not a coin. Welcome made portable, the small marker Bess-style hosts give travelers who may claim the empty chair. The host and the traveler are not in the picture. The scratches are. Moss stain may be on the wood or clay. That is the whole object.

Slight resonance. A chapter worry is a token that warms when the holder lies about hunger. No holder is lying here. The token is not performing that warmth as a scene.

## Real-World Role

A hospitality-token plate. Palm-sized. Moss-stained wood or clay. Three crude scratches. No chair. No coin.

## Lore Connection

Elder hospitality infrastructure. The third-chair idiom given an object. Travel courtesy, teaching children welcome, and a thing rarely forged because forging welcome is a special shame. The teaching and the forgery are not shown. Verdant Reach is the region. The object stays a marker, not the chair, not a coin, not a pass.
