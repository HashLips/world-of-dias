# 5 Lies

## Name

5 Lies

## Overview

*5 Lies* is an Ashcalm Rise gather-plate: five elongated white figures stand as pillars of speech, banded at the face, teaching that a rise community may name five soft untruths out loud before a harder silence sets in.

## Description

Against a solid dark slate-teal ground, five stark white humanoid pillars rise from the bottom edge. Three tall figures form the back row: a forward-facing center, and two slightly shorter profiles turned inward toward that center. Two smaller, child-height figures stand lower in front, heads tilted up, mouths slightly open as if mid-speech or song. Bodies are limb-light and column-like; the drama is in the heads.

Each white head carries thin black linework—ribbon-like bands crossing the face like ceremonial wire. Most show a single dark almond or teardrop eye caught in the crossings. Profiles keep a sharp nose contour and a parted mouth. The palette is only teal, white, and black. The mood is solemn and ethereal, a formal gather without furniture or landscape. A tiny white cursive mark sits in the top-right corner.

## Real-World Role

A speech-manners plate for Ashcalm Rise: how many soft lies a circle may admit before care requires a stop.

## Lore Connection

On Ashcalm Rise, **5 Lies** is not a celebration of deceit. It is a local rule for naming the soft untruths that keep a hard day walkable—the small softenings people admit in a banded face-circle so they do not harden into secret. Five is the courtesy count. A sixth unspoken softening is usually the one that starts a real wound. The black face bands are the Rise’s listening marks: you speak while marked, and you listen while marked. The two smaller figures looking up are the ones still learning which softenings are care and which are cover. The three tall figures hold the count. Ashcalm talk says the plate should stay nearly empty of setting so the words do not hide behind scenery. Wonder here is quiet honesty: saying the five, then stopping.
