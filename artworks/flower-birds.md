# Flower Birds

## Name

Flower Birds

## Overview

*Flower Birds* is an Aelwyn Canopy sip plate: a blue needle-beaked flyer touches a white star bloom’s pink center while a red crawler keeps petal watch—the flora hour when bird, bloom, and bug share one honest drink.

## Description

Light tan upper field; darker olive-green sloped mound below. Center-lower: large white star bloom with about twelve long tapering petals, soft pale pink center, dark brown curved stem into the green. Upper: vibrant medium-blue sleek avian with forked long tail, swept-back thin wings, needle-thin beak touching the pink center, small white almond eye. Far-left petal tip: small bright red rounded insect with black marks and fine black legs. Quiet coexistence. Tiny black square mark top-right.

Asset reference: `assets/flower-birds.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A canopy flora plate: leave sip-room for flyer and crawler; do not bag the bloom alone.

## Lore Connection

In the Aelwyn Canopy, **Flower Birds** names the white star-bloom ecology that feeds blue needle-flyers and red petal-crawlers together. Wonder is shared nectar manners. Picking the bloom mid-sip is bad craft.
