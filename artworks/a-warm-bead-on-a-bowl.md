# A Warm Bead on a Bowl

A Warm Bead on a Bowl

## Overview

*A Warm Bead on a Bowl* is a Softfruit Keep-Mark Cord. The cord is short, braided peel-fiber, with a warm bead, tied to a bowl. The fruit in the bowl is not named. The cord is not a lock.

## Description

A short cord of braided peel-fiber is tied to a bowl. A warm bead is on the cord. In the account the bead hums faintly when the bowl is greeted aloud. The plate does not show the greeting or prove the hum. It shows the cord, the bead, and the bowl they mark. The fruit in the bowl is not named. No species is given, and none is read off the picture.

The knot means fruit held for sharing rather than sale, for the length of a meal. It is not a lock. It is not a ferry. Vendors knot it before Flagweek breakfast and after restoration picks. Cutting it early is stamp manners dressed as hunger. The plate does not show the cut, the breakfast, or the pick. The cord is still tied.

No coin is winning. No House clerk has been invited yet. The keep-mark is the humble string: peel-fiber, warm bead, bowl.
