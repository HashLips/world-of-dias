## Overview

*Sooty Undersides on Worn Hot Dirt* is a low plant on worn, hot ground. The leaves are fuzzy and gray-green. Their undersides are sooty. It is not in a tended row. There are no flowers, no kettle, and no people. The yard's seasoning is shown as a weed-looking thing that was not asked to be pretty.
