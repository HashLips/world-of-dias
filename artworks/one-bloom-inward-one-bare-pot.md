# One Bloom Inward, One Bare Pot

## Name

One Bloom Inward, One Bare Pot

## Overview

*One Bloom Inward, One Bare Pot* is Pot Signal Lane showing two signals only. One red bloom is turned inward. One bare pot sits on the third step. No other code is given.

## Description

Windowsills and steps in a second-ring Veloria lane, and only two signals. A red bloom is turned inward. A bare pot is on the third step. The entry already treats those as two different kinds of trouble, one and another, without this plate spelling a larger alphabet. No other code is shown. No third pot, no second color promoted into a new rule, no written key beside the clay.

The lane is ordinary housing over an ordinary-looking trade in favors. Lira Pot runs messages for people who cannot afford official channels. Harold's street world is adjacent in spirit. None of those people is required for the two signals to be the two signals. Children learn the code before they learn algebra. The plate does not teach the rest of it. Mistaking a signal can start a week of wrong doors. This frame does not open a wrong door. It leaves the bloom inward and the third step bare.

Guild collectors hate the lane. The hate is not a poster. The pots are the folk infrastructure, and only these two are in the record of the picture.
