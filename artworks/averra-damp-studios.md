# Averra’s Damp Work Rooms

## Name

Averra’s Damp Work Rooms

## Overview

Averra’s Damp Work Rooms is a digital image study of the humid, half-hidden rooms where Averra’s art labor takes place.

## Description

The plate shows cliff work rooms with small windows, blank canvases, black spore stains, and inkcaps in the damp corners. It keeps the rooms cool, wet, and unfinished, with the work held close to the island rather than offered as spectacle.

These are studios and work rooms, not a gallery. The image follows Averra’s obscured art labor without turning the place into a public exhibition.

Asset reference: `assets/averra-damp-studios.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Averra’s craft weather: ink, spores, and unfinished work behind deliberately small windows.

## Lore Connection

The artwork reinforces the studios’ role in Averra’s obscured present and keeps their labor distinct from gallery-going customs.
