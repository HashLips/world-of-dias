# Solution

## Name

Solution

## Overview

*Solution* is the Nauw Outer Rim Seas plate for the bowed hammer. Black claw hammer upright. Bright red floppy bow. Six blue nails on gray. Mint upper field. Off-white disc behind.

## Description

Light mint-green upper half; solid gray lower floor. Behind the tool, a large off-white slightly irregular circle like low sun or spotlight. Center: solid black claw-hammer silhouette standing perfectly vertical on its handle. Just below the head, a vibrant bright red ribbon tied in a large floppy bow, ends draping right onto the gray. Around the base: six bright blue nails—three left angled up with long sharp white light-streaks, one flat horizontal, two right leaning with a long white diagonal shadow. Tiny black bird/letter glyph top-left mint. Ceremonial tool: work-shape dressed for care, nails still present.

Asset reference: `assets/solution-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Nauw Outer Rim Seas. Solution is the kept craft of the ribboned hammer among blue nails.

## Lore Connection

Solution is the Rim Seas' name for the hour when the black hammer stands bowed in red and the blue nails wait with white light-streaks. Local care says solution is not smashing first; the bow marks a pause of respect before the drive. Resonance is red on black on mint and gray; memory is the six blue counts; wonder is the off-white disc. The plate invents no carpentry manual. It keeps the craft as manners for answered work that still shows the nails.
