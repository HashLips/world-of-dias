# Flower Lips

## Name

Flower Lips

## Overview

*Flower Lips* is an Aelwyn Canopy speech-bloom plate: a navy watcher stands behind an orange triangle tied by red lines to a blue blade-flower and a black vase—the flora that teaches mouths to measure words like petals.

## Description

Light tan field. Right: dark navy slender profile, head slightly bowed, one narrow white almond eye, arms behind back. Center: bright orange equilateral triangle before the torso; behind it a cornflower-blue angular blade-petal bloom around a bright yellow core; thin curved red line from yellow core to triangle tip; thin straight red line from triangle base into a small black tapered vase casting a long dark shadow lower left. Medium blue horizontal wash with three jagged downward fingers at left; three small red geometric fragments upper left; three pale peach almond petals lower left. Quiet, symbolic. Small black glyph top-left.

## Real-World Role

A canopy flora plate: speak through the triangle measure; keep the vase honest.

In the Aelwyn Canopy, **Flower Lips** names a blade-bloom kept near talk benches. Orange triangle is the word-measure; red lines are the living connection from core to vessel; navy watcher is the guest practicing silence between petals. Wonder is floral speech craft. Dias canopy name.
