# Held Color Stood Up

## Name

Held Color Stood Up

## Overview

*Held Color Stood Up* is a digital image study of The Lean Echo as held color stood up: a ghostly frequency being, person-tall only by courtesy, whose edges drink light.

## Description

A slender, elongated translucent figure stands at roughly a person's height, made of swirling wisps and fine glowing filaments rather than flesh. Interior color shifts through cerulean, lavender, and soft violet hearttide hues, scattered with sparkling motes. The head is smooth, featureless, and slightly bowed, trailing a wispy crest. Long thin arms taper into flowing trails instead of hands; the lower body dissolves into a base of misting energy. Outer edges and inner eddies sink to deep indigo, so the form seems to drink the surrounding light. A faded circular frequency-chart diagram sits behind it on the parchment.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/the-lean-echo.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of the Farstock Saga's lure-and-captor echoform: wonder and grip in one shape.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens The Lean Echo's established read (person's height by courtesy, shifting hearttide colors, edges that drink light) without depicting a route, a method, or any crossing to F500. Its knowledge of a way stays unknown.

## Notes

Echoform, not a person. Colors shown are one moment of shifting hearttide, not a fixed palette. Background diagram is not a map to F500. Border ornaments are not frequency law.
