# Future Wizard

## Name

Future Wizard

## Overview

*Future Wizard* is a Mercer Shed craft-sequence plate: three panels show hands holding a marked scrap, a lit match, then nothing—the shed rule that tomorrow’s cleverness still passes through mark, flame, and honest empty.

## Description

Three vertical panels. Left: sky-blue ground, golden-yellow hand with burnt-orange nails holds a jagged white scrap bearing a black glyph (stylized A with extra top bar and center stroke); navy shadow left. Middle: lemon-yellow ground, orange-red hand with cobalt nails holds a black matchstick with pale pink teardrop flame; olive-brown shadow. Right: coral-salmon ground, deep purple hand with white nails pinched empty; maroon shadow. Rhythmic, procedural, mysterious. Mid-century flat clarity.

Asset reference: `assets/future-wizard.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A shed artifact plate: mark, burn what must go, open the empty hand—no skipping to “wizard” without the sequence.

## Lore Connection

In Mercer Shed, **Future Wizard** is a kept teaching triptych for craft futures. Glyph scrap is the plan; match is the revision fire; empty pinch is the finished release. Wonder is procedural wonder, not Earth mage costume. Dias shed name.

Triptych hands. Glyph scrap. Pink-flamed match. Empty pinch. Blue / yellow / coral panels.
