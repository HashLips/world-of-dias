# Lost of Reality

## Name

Lost of Reality

## Overview

*Lost of Reality* places a small white house with a red roof on a jagged brown cliff, tethered by a thin blue line from its chimney to a massive floating blue vessel-bowl in a peach sky.

## Description

Isolation, lopsided calm, one house talking to a sky-object larger than itself. The line jitters like smoke that learned to hold shape.

Asset reference: `assets/lost-of-reality.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A surreal habitat emblem: home as antenna.

## Lore Connection

F960 and Open Hand readers see an **unfinished dwelling** still drafting its sky—resonance collected above living space. Fracture-age talk calls the bowl a lost loft of reality that never finished docking. Either way: the house remains, the vessel remains, the tether is the work.

## Notes

Title keeps the artwork's original phrasing; in-world speakers sometimes soften it to "loss of reality."
