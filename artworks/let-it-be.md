# Let It Be

## Name

Let It Be

## Overview

*Let It Be* holds a shore assembly: eleven solid dark figures with large spade-like heads and single pointed peg-bodies arranged in a V pointing toward the viewer on golden-tan sand, long shadows left, lighter then darker blue sea bands, huge circular orange-red sun on white sky.

## Description

Sizes recede; central figure largest. Unified stillness, not chatter. Title is a Dias name for leaving the shore moment unforced—not an Earth song claim.

Asset reference: `assets/let-it-be.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A leave-alone plate: some assemblies are for standing, not fixing.

## Lore Connection

F380 coastal watches use Let It Be when the sun is huge and the V is set—do not rearrange heights, do not shout the sea into answering. Local rule: if you arrived late, take the smallest rear place or wait off the tan. Resonance prefers shared shadows. Dragging a figure out to 'improve' the V empties the quiet.

## Notes

Eleven black shore figures, orange sun, tan sand, blue sea. Dias title only.
