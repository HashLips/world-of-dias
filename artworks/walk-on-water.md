# Walk on Water

## Name

Walk on Water

## Overview

*Walk on Water* is a runner crossing a blue surface at night. On the left, a white figure with a round yellow head—one squinting eye, a wide white grin—leans into a stride, one arm stretched back holding a long orange ribbon that streams up and off the top left edge, the other hand open in front. To the right stands a tall white abstract sculpture on a squared base: a curling, hooked form with an opening through its middle, like a wave or an ear. Both stand on a strip of deep blue at the bottom of a black frame.

## Description

The background is a near-black maroon. The floor is a single band of cobalt that runs across the bottom, slightly tilted, and both figure and sculpture rest on it as if it were solid.

The runner is pure white, long-legged, mid-step with the back foot on its toes. The yellow head is perfectly round, drawn like a smiling sun or a bright mask, its expression gleeful and a little mischievous. The orange ribbon curves from the hand in a long arc.

The sculpture is pale white and soft gray, its curves heavy, its top flattened into a slab that cantilevers out. The opening through its middle catches the darkness. The runner is not looking at it but passing it closely, nearly brushing it, as though using it as a turning post. The joke is in the title: this blue is water, and nobody is sinking.

## Real-World Role

A night-crossing plate about running on still water so lightly that the reflection is not disturbed.

## Lore Connection

The Glasswater Mirror Pool is the still water pocket in the Fields where sky and grain reflect so cleanly that night-watch pairs log mirror calm as its own weather. A white sculpture stands in its shallows as a fixed mark. Walk on Water is the watch pairs' game on the calmest nights: one runs a loop around the sculpture across the shallow surface trailing a ribbon, while the other watches the reflection. If the mirror holds unbroken, the calm is real. If it ripples, the watch logs it and goes looking for what a perfect surface might be hiding.
