# Road Ahead

## Name

Road Ahead

## Overview

*Road Ahead* is the Brindle March plate for the blue tree in the white road. Electric blue trunk from the pavement. Yellow horizon disc. One long branch hangs a large yellow fruit. Dark green field, navy hills.

## Description

Dark forest-green field. Center: a wide white road narrows toward a bright yellow semi-circle on the horizon; black dashed center line. From mid-road rises a saturated electric-blue tree—trunk merging with the white path, crown jagged into thin antler- or neuron-like branches. One right branch curves long and hangs a large yellow fruit with black tip and stem. Soft grey shadow of the tree falls right on the white road.

Left behind the tree: irregular salmon-pink blob. Upper right: dark muted green square floating. Mid: dark navy/black rounded hill silhouettes framing the yellow disc. Tiny white gate/trident icon top-left. Surreal march weather: path and growth one thing, fruit on the way ahead.

Asset reference: `assets/road-ahead.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A phenomenon plate for Brindle March. Road Ahead is the blue growth that owns the white path toward yellow.

## Lore Connection

Road Ahead is the march's name for the hour when the electric-blue tree stands in the dashed white road and a yellow fruit hangs toward the yellow horizon disc. Local care says you do not chop the trunk to clear traffic vanity; the road and tree are one telling. Resonance is blue on white toward yellow; memory is the dashed line under the shadow; wonder is the hanging fruit as path gift, not a joke prop. The plate invents no highway saga. It keeps the weather as manners for what grows in the way you must still walk with care.
