# Hunted

## Name

Hunted

## Overview

*Hunted* is a Brindle path tension: a large white crouching body with one black eye, a smaller red archer drawing a blue arrow on a yellow bow, and a row of glowing teal watcher-heads along a pink-beige diagonal track under olive-dark air.

## Description

The white figure dominates the left foreground—thick simplified limbs, bald head, single long black almond eye turned toward the archer. The red archer stands farther back on the right, beige face-oval, yellow recurve bow, blue arrow aimed up-left toward the white form. A long dark grey shadow stretches from the archer’s feet. Along the path edge, small teal cyan silhouettes of heads and shoulders line up; a few identical shapes float above the row like a hovering gallery. Left of the path is near-black void; above, dark olive. No fruit crates, no lean-to timber—only the watched aim.

## Real-World Role

A caution plate for Brindle March: being hunted is also being observed, and observation has manners.

## Lore Connection

Brindle March roads know thank-you leans and load-share clover, but they also know path contests that must not become blood sport. Hunted teaches: if teal watchers have lined the track, the archer’s blue arrow is under public count. Local rule: do not loose while the white crouch still has a clear eye on you without a spoken yield. Resonance sticks to the path edge; floating teal shapes are treated as the march’s memory of witnesses, not ghosts for scare tales. A thank-you after a stopped aim matters more than a hit.
