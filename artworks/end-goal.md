*End Goal* is an F432 finish plate: a peach path with green dashes runs between white barriers to a red threshold before a massive yellow orb—the frequency reminder that arriving is crossing a care-line, not swallowing the sun.

## Description

Deep cerulean blue field. Wide peach road narrows in perspective with a green dashed center line; thick white rectangular barriers flank it. Mid: thin bright red ribbon stretched on two thin black posts like a finish line. Behind: huge solid yellow circle. Above: large dark crescent with white interior like a wide smile or stylized eye, plus two dark arched lines; faint translucent crescent ghost inside the yellow orb. Forward, surreal, watched.
