# Mask of Mask

## Name

Mask of Mask

## Overview

*Mask of Mask* shows masked bodies in motion-trail—white and red figures with blue mask-faces arching through black space, past selves still visible as grey afterimages.

## Description

A diagonal sequence of athletic bends: one white figure, one red, both wearing simple blue masks with slit eyes; faded grey doubles trail behind them like speed, ritual repetition, or exile memory that will not leave the yard. The piece refuses to show an unmasked face.

Asset reference: `assets/mask-of-mask.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

Visual companion to Sorel mask culture—pair with *Masked* and *Behind the Masked* when teaching anonymity as office, not costume.

## Lore Connection

Reads as Compact motion under **Carrow's Mask** ethic: the role moves; the face stays sealed. Often hung in Freeband talk as “what Long Gate belonging looks like when it runs.”
