# Bonzi

## Name

Bonzi

## Overview

*Bonzi* is a Coral Strand living-tree plate: a blocky white trunk with magenta petal-clusters and roots that seem to float above a teal moon-line, the shore neighbor people greet before they take a cutting.

## Description

Periwinkle blue-violet fills the field. A thick white trunk rises with sharp angular bends into equally angular limbs. Bright magenta-pink petal or leaf clusters sit at joints and tips—overlapping curved segments, not soft botanical fluff. Thin white root-lines spread at the base as if growing into void rather than soil. Lower right: a large solid teal circle crossed by a thin horizontal teal line through its center, horizon or ring. Tree slightly left of center, balanced by the sphere. Tiny black-and-white glyph top-right. Mood: ethereal, serene, geometric rather than wild.

## Real-World Role

A strand neighbor plate: how to meet a living tree-form before you ask it for a piece.

## Lore Connection

On Coral Strand, **Bonzi** is a non-human inhabitant-shape that looks like a kept tree and behaves like a neighbor. Magenta clusters mark seasons of willingness; floating roots mean the form may relocate along shore memory rather than stay planted for your convenience. The teal sphere-and-line is local sky-water talk, not an Earth moon creed. Wonder is consent: greet before cutting, leave petal-clusters intact if the white wood feels closed, and never pot a Bonzi as décor without the strand’s assent. The name is Dias shore-speech, not an Earth software joke.
