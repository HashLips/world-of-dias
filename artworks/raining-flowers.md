# Raining Flowers

## Name

Raining Flowers

## Overview

*Raining Flowers* is the Aelwyn Canopy plate for the blossom fall. Cerulean sky packed with white petals and red centers. Green stems trail. Sage dunes below. One flower already down with a dark shadow.

## Description

Upper two-thirds: solid vibrant cerulean. Dozens of identical flowers float or descend in mixed orientations—long pointed irregular white petals around a circular bright red center, each with a thin bright green stem trailing like a ribbon. Size varies for depth. Lower third: low rolling hills in sage, olive, and deep forest green. Center foreground, one flower lies settled, stem curved behind, dark almost-black shadow under it. Small dark rectangular mark top-right. Horizon low and slightly uneven. Flat graphic, serene, no storm bruise.

## Real-World Role

A flora plate for Aelwyn Canopy. Raining Flowers is the growth weather of drifting blossoms that settle.

## Lore Connection

Raining Flowers is the canopy's name for the hour when white-and-red blossoms fill the blue and one finally takes the sage ground with a clean shadow. Local care says you may gather what has settled; you do not yank the still-drifting ones for greed. Resonance is red centers on cerulean; memory is the trailing green stems; wonder is how quiet the dunes stay under such a sky. The plate invents no market harvest war. It keeps the fall as plant manners.
