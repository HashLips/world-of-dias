# Banana

## Name

Banana

## Overview

*Banana* is a Velorian Basin harvest-silhouette plate: a heavy black stalk-form rises from two open crates, a yellow crescent set into its crest, the local fruit-sign people still call **Banana** even when the shape looks more like a monument than a market snack.

## Description

Pale yellow fills the upper field. A dark gray ground holds two brown rectangular crates with lids open and interiors a clean light blue. From each crate a thick black pillar rises; the pillars join into one forward-leaning crest whose upper edge is scalloped with small rounded notches. Set into the crest’s front-left is a large vibrant yellow crescent tipped with a small dark gray rectangular tab. Long sharp gray shadows rake toward the lower right. The silhouette is monumental, silent, and almost architectural—fruit-sign as standing presence rather than a tidy botanical drawing. A small black-and-white seal sits bottom-right.

## Real-World Role

A basin harvest plate: how a fruit-sign is stored, stood, and shared without stripping a stand bare.

## Lore Connection

In the Velorian Basin, **Banana** is the ordinary name for a crate-risen fruit-form whose yellow crescent marks readiness. Path-keepers and market hands argue season and scent more than ownership. The two crates mean the form is meant to travel in pairs of care—one crate for taking, one for leaving stock behind. The black stalk is the living weight of the stand; the scalloped crest is how locals read age and softness without bruising the flesh. Wonder is ordinary here: a fruit-sign that teaches packing manners. Pulling every crescent for a souvenir is the extractive twin of a false thank-you on Brindle roads, and basin talk treats both as the same bad craft.
