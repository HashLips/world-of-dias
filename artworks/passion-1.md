# Passion

## Name

Passion

## Overview

*Passion* is the Veloria City plate for the hot crown on cool skin. Blue face split cerulean and navy. Red-orange jagged hair. Matching lips. Thick black lines. Black void around.

## Description

Head-and-shoulders crop. Skin is two blues: brighter cerulean on the viewer's left, deeper navy on the right and in the shadow planes, flat but split hard enough to feel directional light. Eyes are narrow almonds in sharp black line with small black iris-circles, unblinking at the viewer. Lips are small, stylized, the same vibrant red-orange as the hair.

Hair is a large solid red-orange shape, jagged and windswept, sharp points dipping over the forehead like a hot crown. A continuous black line builds nose bridge and tip; heavier black builds jaw, thick neck, and the hint of broad shoulders. Background is solid void black. Line weight varies like ink brush. A thin white mark sits bottom right.

No smile performance. The plate is intensity held in color contrast: cool face, hot hair and mouth, black containing both.

An artifact plate for Veloria City. Passion is the kept portrait-craft of heat worn carefully on cool skin.

## Lore Connection

Passion is Veloria's name for the hour when red-orange crown and lips sit on a blue split face without spilling into wild harm. Local care says the unblinking gaze is honesty, not a hunt; the black outline is the container that keeps heat from burning the room. Resonance is the hot/cool clash; memory is the jagged bangs frozen mid-sweep; wonder is how much feeling a flat palette can hold. The plate invents no romance saga. It keeps the portrait as a craft people recognize when intensity needs a named face without a sermon.
