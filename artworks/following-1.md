# Following

## Name

Following

## Overview

*Following* is a Hollowmere crowd-watch plate: many dark figures pack the cyan field with matching white almond eyes—the hall lesson that following is shared gaze, not blind shove.

## Description

Bright cyan-blue ground. Lower two-thirds: dense overlapping dark silhouettes—about sixteen—front row as tall vertical columns, back rows with rounded heads and shoulders. Each shows piercing almond white eyes slanted slightly up. Thin pale peachy-pink vertical seams define gaps between bodies. Silent collective presence. Tiny black square with white crown-like mark top-right.

## Real-World Role

A Hollowmere artifact plate: if you follow, keep your eyes open and your seams respectful.

## Lore Connection

In Hollowmere, **Following** is a kept teaching look for processions and net-loft gathers. White eyes mean attention; peach seams mean you still leave a breath of space. Wonder is collective without crush. Dias hall name.
