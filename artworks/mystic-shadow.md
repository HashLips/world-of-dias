# Mystic Shadow

## Name

Mystic Shadow

## Overview

*Mystic Shadow* shows a pale cyan figure in a long black robe holding a small glowing orb, while a towering clawed black shadow stretches across grey walls—larger and stranger than the body that casts it.

## Description

A hanging green lamp on a red cord lights a sparse corner. The figure’s face is calm; the shadow’s neck curves wrong and its fingers lengthen into claws. The orb carries a red mark like a counted frequency.

Asset reference: `assets/mystic-shadow.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

Primary visual for Vorrith Kael’s quiet body versus dark-presence silhouette.

## Lore Connection

Read as Vorrith in Stillhollow: the exile’s standing form stays polite; the cave keeps the hungrier outline. The held orb rhymes with his craft of isolating and compounding frequency matter for the Still Pot.

## Notes

Sibling mood to Nested Shadow Mismatch without claiming the phenomenon is identical.
