# Footprints to the Open Pane

## Name

Footprints to the Open Pane

## Overview

*Footprints to the Open Pane* is a digital image study of The Window Walker as trace evidence only: footprints, displacement, and an open pane—no body, no verdict.

## Description

A corner of a rough-hewn stone chamber with square floor tiles. A line of faint glowing gold footprints crosses the floor from left-center toward a deep arched window on the right, its multi-paned casement swung open. A small scatter of the same glow rests on the stone ledge, as if displacement ended there. Through the window: misty jagged peaks under an overcast sky. A worn circular diagram is etched faintly into the floor to the left of the path. No figure appears anywhere in the room.

Sepia etched tone; decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/the-window-walker.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Window Walker reports as path evidence—movement ending at a threshold—rather than a sighting.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the established trace-only account without showing a body, confirming phase-dependence, or deciding between real inhabitant and reveal-stress artifact. Status stays unknown.
