# Flowers on Your Table

## Name

Flowers on Your Table

## Overview

*Flowers on Your Table* is an Aelwyn Canopy weight plate: a blue spotted vase on a tall pink stand sends one long green stem into a heavy red flame-bloom while scissors wait—the flora lesson that beauty on your table still has weight and a cut-rule.

## Description

Dark navy field. Tall pedestal: flat square magenta-pink top, four very thin light-pink legs. On it: small bulbous bright blue vase with dark blue heart/teardrop spots; closed black-handled scissors with white/silver blades beside the vase base. Single vibrant green stem arches high then droops to a large scarlet flower with lemon-yellow center and many long pointed flame-like petals hanging heavy. Quiet still-life tension. Small white crown/gate icon top-right.

Asset reference: `assets/flowers-on-your-table.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A canopy flora plate: put flowers on your table; respect the droop; know when scissors are for trim, not for trophy.

## Lore Connection

In the Aelwyn Canopy, **Flowers on Your Table** names the red heavy-bloom that teaches table guests about weight. Scissors present means trim with consent of the stem’s keepers. Wonder is domestic care. Dias canopy name.
