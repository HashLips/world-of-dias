# Body in Motion

## Name

Body in Motion

## Overview

*Body in Motion* is a Sorel motion-memory plate: a long white figure reaches toward a spiked white disc holding a black profile, birds on shoulders, the body teaching that movement is how Sorel keeps a face from freezing into a stamp.

## Description

Terracotta-orange field. A spindly white humanoid reclines and reaches—limbs long, angles sharp, one arm raised, one toward a large white disc ringed with triangular spikes. Inside the disc, a solid black profile faces the figure. A small sharp white bird perches on the main figure’s shoulder; another white bird stands lower right looking in. Behind, darker-orange elongated silhouettes suggest a gathered assembly, one also bird-shouldered. Faint ghost discs repeat in the background. Mood: commemorative, kinetic, clean mythic weight without ornate clutter.

Asset reference: `assets/body-in-motion.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A Sorel practice plate: keep the body moving when a memory-face is being honored, so the honor does not harden into ownership.

## Lore Connection

In Sorel, **Body in Motion** is a kept teaching form for processions and quiet drills. The spiked disc is a memory emblem—face held, not possessed. The white figure’s stretch is the rule: you move while you remember, so the living do not freeze into a single approved pose. Birds mark local witness, not Earth omen systems. Exile-road leftovers sometimes misname this as Exile Road kit; correct talk keeps it Sorel. Wonder is motion-as-care: the body stays answerable by staying able to change angle.
