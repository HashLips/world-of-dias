# Clock of Two Circles

## Name

Clock of Two Circles

## Overview

*Clock of Two Circles* sets overlapping blue and dark-green discs above a crouching red figure with a black bird at the brow. Interval weather as body weather. The circles are not a clock face you can obey into safety. They are durations sharing a sky, and the body underneath them is what it costs to want the overlap to last. Timers read a teaching. Softfruit reads a warning about advice you did not ask to keep.

## Description

Forest-green field. The discs eclipse each other the way a short window and a rarer one can share a chart without becoming the same gift. Red limbs go long, almost clawed, the posture of someone who has decided to hold on. A black bird at the brow points its beak toward a white eye. The eye is the linger. The bird is the unwelcome counsel to keep looking, cousin to every apprentice who talked over a soft close. Nothing chimes. The silence is the point. A real chime would have ended the picture, and the picture is about the minute that would not.

The filename says clock. The image refuses to be one. That refusal is useful. Schedule is not compulsion.

## Real-World Role

A reveal-band print for anti-linger walls. It gives a guild a picture of greed that is mythic rather than administrative, so the rule can be felt before it is enforced. Not a diagram of how to extend a window.

## Lore Connection

Chime ethics. The Minute That Would Not Stay. Shelf-minute clarity with its cold-water taste and its afterglow for people who would not pack. The two circles can sit beside a reveal-timing icon as the icon’s bad dream: bands of duration turned into a body that will not stand down. Ask again is not in the frame. That absence is the lesson.
