# Last Flower

## Name

Last Flower

## Overview

*Last Flower* rises on black: thin red stem arching up-right then hooking left, two small pointed red leaves mid-stem, grey collar, four limp bone-white elongated petals hanging like fingers, and one matching white petal adrift mid-left.

## Description

No soil, no canopy green—only the specimen and void. The droop is the point; vitality is in the red still holding. Mood is somber register, not garden catalog cheer.

Asset reference: `assets/last-flower.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A season-end flora plate: keep the last stem visible even when petals hang.

## Lore Connection

Aelwyn Path of the Season teaches not to clear every spent bloom for neatness. Last Flower is the kept stem with hanging white heads and one fallen petal left beside as witness. Local rule: do not crush the detached petal for tidy; it marks what the stem already gave. Resonance prefers honest droop over painted perk.

## Notes

Red stem, grey collar, white droop petals, one free petal, black field.
