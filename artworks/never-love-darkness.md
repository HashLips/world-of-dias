# Never Love Darkness

## Name

Never Love Darkness

## Overview

*Never Love Darkness* stages a meeting: a smaller black silhouette stands at a round table while a larger horned dark presence sits opposite, shadow-tendrils boiling upward from its shoulders in a pale blue room.

## Description

Orange table and chair, pink flowers in a white vase, ordinary furniture under extraordinary silhouettes. White slit-eyes face each other across courtesy and dread.

Asset reference: `assets/never-love-darkness.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

Visual for the pilgrim-audience at Stillhollow: Wabet’s calm furniture, another realm’s pressure seated politely.

## Lore Connection

The seated figure is read as Vorrith’s dark aspect receiving a seeker. The title warns what Giftbroth Change can cost even when the host remains still.
