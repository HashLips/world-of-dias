# Knowledge

## Name

Knowledge

## Overview

*Knowledge* is a mustard field holding one deep-red body in a spread X—arms out and slightly down, legs wide, hands and feet tapering to points, head smooth, only two horizontal white almond eye-slits awake, soft darker-yellow shadow left behind the form.

## Description

No hair, no mouth, no tool. The pose reads as measure-ready or openly receiving. White eyes are the only spark. Tiny black house-like mark sits top-left. Clinical calm, not triumph. Knowledge here is a stance you can copy, not a library pile.

A stance plate: knowledge begins with an open measurable body and awake slits.
