# Concept

## Name

Concept

## Overview

*Concept* is a Nauw Outer Rim Seas balance plate: a white deer-headed form perches on a periwinkle kneeling stump marked with red drip—the hard idea that a cut has already happened and the next posture still has to hold.

## Description

Upper field warm ochre; lower field deep navy. Centered on the navy ground, a headless armless periwinkle kneeling torso ends cleanly at the waist, bright red drip running down the stump; a dark elongated shadow stretches lower left. Atop the stump, a stark white creature curves in a C—deer- or gazelle-like head turned right with a thin black eye slit, long slender legs lifting up-right in an acrobatic perch. Clinical geometry, quiet and strange. Small black gate-like mark top-left.

Asset reference: `assets/concept-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A rim idea plate: after a severance, the living concept must still balance without pretending the cut did not occur.

## Lore Connection

On the Nauw Outer Rim Seas, **Concept** is a kept teaching form for hard ideas that arrive after loss. The kneeling stump is what remains; the white perched form is the thought that must not deny the red mark. Balance is care, not spectacle. Harbor talk uses it when a plan wants to float free of its cost. Wonder is honesty with posture: hold the idea, keep the scar visible, refuse pretty silence.
