# Skeletal Show

## Name

Skeletal Show

## Overview

*Skeletal Show* is the Calibration Row plate for the bone-marked globe holder. Medium-blue body with black rib and bone lines. Black wide hat. Staff with mapped light-blue globe. Pink ground. Yellow crescent.

## Description

Light gray field. Large dark gray circle behind the figure; bright yellow arc/crescent top-left; small solid white disc upper center. Salmon-pink ground plane. Tall slender medium-blue humanoid on the right: thick black stylized skeletal marks inside—ribs, pelvic shape, long limb lines—single white almond eye, wide flat black hat tapering to side points. Right hand holds a long thin black rod tipped with a small light-blue globe scattered with organic dark-green shapes like map or weather marks. Small dark green oval puddle left of the feet; long sharp black shadow to bottom-left. Tiny black icon top-right. Quiet monumental showing of structure and held world-mark.
