# How We Are

## Name

How We Are

## Overview

*How We Are* is a blue-field pairing: two saturated yellow profiles facing nose-near across a light-grey vertical line that ends in an upward-curving U at throat height, with a small bright orange seed floating beneath the yoke.

## Description

Left figure: thick jagged black hair crest sweeping forward. Right figure: thinner flame-like black hair with three points curving back. Faces are minimal—thin eye lines, slight mouth indents, simple ears. The grey apparatus divides them and cups toward both throats like a shared measuring yoke. Below, the orange semi-ellipse sits alone in blue, seed-small. No estuary water, no reed beds—only the ceremonial symmetry of two people agreeing how they stand. A tiny black glyph marks the top-left corner.

## Real-World Role

A manners plate for Bloomline pairs: state how you are before you share the seed between you.

## Lore Connection

The Bloomline Estuary and its holding terraces keep pair-work common—nets, ladles, watch turns. How We Are is the check spoken across a grey throat-yoke: each person names condition before the orange seed (task token, ration pip, or planting bead) is claimed. Local rule: if either throat is not ready for the U’s touch, the seed stays floating. Resonance between facing profiles is honest only when both hair-crests stay their own shape; copying the other’s crest mid-check is a joke that empties the rite.

## Notes

Two yellow faces, grey U-yoke, one orange seed, blue air.
