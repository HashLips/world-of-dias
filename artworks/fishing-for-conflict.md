# Fishing for Conflict

## Name

Fishing for Conflict

## Overview

*Fishing for Conflict* is a Nauw Outer Rim Seas warning plate: a headless black torso holds two heads under hanging hooks while a white fish-void opens in the chest—the rim diagram for baiting a quarrel you will have to carry.

## Description

Solid white field. dark silhouette torso, headless, long thin arms out and up; each hand cradles a black head—left smirking with white eye-sliver, right somber/downcast with white slit eye. Inside the torso, a large white negative-space fish points down, mouth gaping up. Five thin black lines from the top edge end in sharp curved hooks spaced above the shoulders between the heads. Surreal, symbolic, lure-tense. Flat black-and-white only.

Asset reference: `assets/fishing-for-conflict.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A rim inhabitant plate: if you fish for conflict, name which head you are feeding before the hooks drop.

## Lore Connection

On the Nauw Outer Rim Seas, **Fishing for Conflict** is a non-human (or more-than-mood) warning presence. Two heads are the smirk and the sorrow you will hold. Hollow fish is the empty hunger that wants a fight. Hooks are the lures already hanging. Wonder is refusing the bait. Dias rim name.
