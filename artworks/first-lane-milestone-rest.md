# Two Stones at First Lane

## Name

Two Stones at First Lane

## Overview

Two Stones at First Lane is a digital image study of the wayside rest where near-return travelers compare what differs.

## Description

The image looks across the rest rather than turning the pegboard into a close-up: two slightly different milestones stand near benches, low lanterns, and the small pegboard for pass-count sticks. The stones almost agree, but not quite.

No numbers are readable in the plate. Its quiet scale supports outer-lane discipline and recovery between passes without making the rest a door into yesterday.
