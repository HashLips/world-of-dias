# The Inland Throat of Sprout Vent

## Name

The Inland Throat of Sprout Vent

## Overview

The Inland Throat of Sprout Vent is a digital image study of the modest cluster profile on Driftfall’s southeastern flank.

## Description

The plate keeps Sprout Vent inland and modest: one central throat, smaller sprouting points along its shoulders, steam between events, and a faint warmth held in the dusk. Low frontier ground gives the cluster its scale.

This is the vent’s many-sprout silhouette, not a single cone or a sea scene. The image does not add lava rivers to the established account.

Asset reference: `assets/sprout-vent.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A landmark study of active venting and the multiple-mouth profile that marks southeastern Driftfall.

## Lore Connection

The artwork supports Sprout Vent as both a landmark and a warning clock while leaving eruption timing and vent-sprout spread open.
