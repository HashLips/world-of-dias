# Coat That Takes the Mood

## Name

Coat That Takes the Mood

## Overview

*Coat That Takes the Mood* is a digital image study of a Bloomline Heart-Deer mid-shift: a coat that takes the mood of the estuary around it.

## Description

A soft-eyed deer stands among low flowering grasses at the estuary edge, branching antlers raised. Its coat shades from warm sunlit gold at the head and chest to a muted, speckled blue-gray toward the flank and haunch, as if half in relief and half under cloud. A loose garland of small leaves rings the neck and shoulder. Behind: braided channels, marsh flats, and pale hills.

Decorative border with heart-shaped gems is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/bloomline-heart-deer.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of F380 emotion-as-weather carried by fauna: Heart-Deer drift as informal climate advice.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the established mood-responsive coat without fixing which colors mean which feelings or inventing new reading rites.

## Notes

Gold-to-gray split is one moment of shift, not a fixed pattern. Leaf garland is natural growth, not tack. Heart gems in the border are framing only.
