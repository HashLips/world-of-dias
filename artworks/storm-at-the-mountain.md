# Storm at the Mountain

## Name

Storm at the Mountain

## Overview

*Storm at the Mountain* shows a black mesa-like mountain under a pale blue sky, with seven blue squares releasing pink wavy lines down the face of the stone, and a thin white lenticular shape floating above.

## Description

Flat, symbolic, almost diagrammatic. The mountain is a solid black polygon with a grey base-shadow. Pink streams hang like living measurement. The white aerial lens sits with a small grey shadow of its own.

Asset reference: `assets/storm-at-the-mountain.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A location emblem: landscape as instrument panel.

## Lore Connection

Linked to **Mountain of Majestic Good Trade** as a place where frequency is not only felt but **lined**—echo-rise made visible as hanging streams. Some Driftfall talk compares the white lens to Sky Drifter height; others refuse the comparison. Sprout Vent watchers sometimes borrow the image when arguing that mountains "speak in counts."

## Notes

Not a literal weather chart; "storm" here is resonance weather.
