# Clear Water around Isle of Calareth

## Name

Clear Water around Isle of Calareth

## Overview

Clear Water around Isle of Calareth is a digital image study of Calareth’s natural coast and its restrained landing place at Hallow Bay.

## Description

Cliffs and beach frame clear water around the forested island and smaller islets. Moss-green dories sit near Hallow Bay, where a low watch house and one clapboard guest hall rest above the high-tide line. The view keeps the island’s natural scale and does not add a port.

## Lore Connection
