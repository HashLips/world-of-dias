# Road Trip

## Name

Road Trip

## Overview

*Road Trip* is the Brindle March plate for the pink rider on the black curve. Yellow frame, blue wheels. Red scarf streaming. Gray hills. Lonely road.

## Description

Wide stark black road curves up toward top-right, narrowing, broken white center line. Middle gray undulating hills; lighter gray sky. No trees, no buildings. On the road a bubblegum-pink featureless humanoid sits on a minimal transport: thin bright yellow frame lines, two solid bright blue wheel-circles—cycle without spokes or pedals drawn. Rounded head, one thin black eye-line closed or focused. Long vibrant red scarf wraps the neck and trails left in two fluid ribbon strands as if in strong travel wind. Tiny black square crown/M icon top-right. Bright traveler against bleak gray—solitary purpose on the march road.
