# Figure Mountain

## Name

Figure Mountain

## Overview

*Figure Mountain* shows an elongated yellow-and-white figure in dance-like motion under a red sky and pink mountain line—limbs too long for ordinary anatomy, shadow red like the air.

## Description

A limber humanoid with a yellow oval head and a single slit eye. Body split yellow/white. Blue semicircle with wavy rays in the upper sky. Jagged pale mountains; dark ground; sharp geometric shadows.

Asset reference: `assets/figure-mountain.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A high-energy landscape figure study used to visualize bodies altered by place-frequency.

## Lore Connection

Treated as a visual of **Echoform**-adjacent manifestation—or a person briefly worn thin by mountain resonance—on Driftfall heights such as **Mountain of Majestic Good Trade**. The split coloration is argued as dual imprint: self and site speaking at once.
