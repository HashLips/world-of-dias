# Id of Mine

## Name

Id of Mine

## Overview

*Id of Mine* is a Veloria City registry plate: dozens of cream-framed busts on a black field, each the same blue face and suit, charcoal side-cap, and glowing cyan tie, tiled in even columns until the bottom row crops mid-frame.

## Description

Five columns, six-plus rows. Every frame holds a chest-up figure in flat blues—medium face, lighter features, calm slight tilt of the head to the viewer’s right. Jacket and shirt stay blue; the cyan tie is the only luminous vertical. Caps tilt the other way, charcoal against the cream mat. No nameplates, no numbers, no smile variants. The black gaps between frames feel like filing space. The plate’s joke and seriousness arrive together: “mine” when every tile matches.

Asset reference: `assets/id-of-mine.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A registry craft plate: identity as a kept copy you must still answer for, even when the face repeats.

## Lore Connection

Veloria City clerks know identical stamps and careful differences. Id of Mine teaches that a matching portrait grid is not permission to treat people as interchangeable errands. Local rule: if you pull a tile that looks like all the others, still speak the person’s living name before you move their file. Resonance remembers which hand touched which cream frame. The cyan tie is the shared role-mark; the person behind it is not the tie.
