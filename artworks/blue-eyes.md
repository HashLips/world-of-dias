# Blue Eyes

## Name

Blue Eyes

## Overview

*Blue Eyes* is a Veloria City mark-portrait: a red-hooded figure with a white face and clear blue downward gaze, the look people copy when a promise needs to prove it can still be quiet.

## Description

Close bust on solid black. A voluminous bright red cowl and cloak cover the head and frame a flat brilliant white face. Mustard-yellow outlines define eyelids, nose bridge and tip, and lips. The eyes are piercing clear blue, aimed down and slightly left—contemplative, weary, still. Heavy red planes suggest protective cloth without texture fuss. A small black square with a gate-or-bridge icon and a tiny cursive mark sits bottom-right. Mood: ritual gravity, focused stillness, warm red against cool gaze.

Asset reference: `assets/blue-eyes.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A city meaning-mark plate: the eyes that remember you remembering a vow.

## Lore Connection

In Veloria City, **Blue Eyes** is less a tool than a recognized look. Copied empty, it becomes décor. Copied with care, it still points at the same decision: whether what you touched gets to stay as echo, lesson, or refuse. The red hood is privacy without secrecy-for-harm; the downward blue gaze is attention that refuses spectacle. Yellow face-lines are local mark-craft, not an Earth-faith paint reading. Travelers are told not to carve the eyes into living wood without the place’s assent—resonance prefers consent. Wonder is quiet accountability.
