# Don't Worry

## Name

Don't Worry

## Overview

*Don't Worry* is an orange-faced, high-collared portrait holding a bright white curve. Serious calm offered at a threshold. It does not erase cost, and it does not smile falsely, which is why Niche Watchers tolerate it. Comfort, in this picture, is a straight mouth and an object in the hand that might be a cloth, a scroll, or a cord not yet tied. The title is a risk. The face refuses to cash the title in cheaply.

## Description

Gray field, black wrap, a mustard hand on the white shape. One thick line for the eyes, level, unsurprised. No teeth. No tear as spectacle. The collar is a little like armor and a little like a coat the wind has not been allowed to flatten, so the portrait can stand for the minute before the niche teaches posture. Hung beside a louder exile image, it does the quieter job: you may be afraid, and the fear does not have to become a performance to be respected. People who want a grin of reassurance are the people the picture is not for. Those people are gently directed to a fruit court. This face stays at the Gate.

## Real-World Role

A comfort print for exile paths, the still one. It sits beside more overt distance-pictures and keeps them from becoming only ache or only adventure. Orientation, not therapy-as-brand.

## Lore Connection

Gate wind manners. An assent cord with a plain knot. Second beginning as labor, south light, the cost still in the coat. The white curve can be read as the cord, as a page the wind will take the edge from, as a cloth that signals consent to be quiet. It should not be read as a promise that worry was incorrect. The worry was accurate. The portrait is the Watcher who will not take your speech and will not mock you for having one ready.
