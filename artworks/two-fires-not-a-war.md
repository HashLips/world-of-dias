# Two Fires, Not a War

## Name

Two Fires, Not a War

## Overview

*Two Fires, Not a War* is the Claimscar Secondfire Ring. Two fires. Scorched stones. Tarps, wire, salvage, and pale vent mouths. It is not a war. There is no caption text.

## Description

Two fires, not one. That is the depiction the entry asks for, and the plate keeps both. Scorched stones sit as the ring's ground, outside the morning cookfire's breakfast politics and still the same yard. Tarps are there. Wire is there. Salvage is there. Pale mouths of vents show in the working dark. The entry says nobody should name those mouths casually after dusk. The plate does not name them with a caption. No caption text is on the image. Paint that remembers daylight is not turned into readable words.

It is not a war. Frontier grit, a negotiation that happens to burn. Smoke may carry fat and metal. The plate does not turn smoke into a battle or a slogan. Claim-talk, double-painted sprout nights, and crews with different errands belong to the account of the ring. They are not a caption and not a campaign. The ring does not become a war stage because it has two fires and scorched stone.

Breakfast can be shared when claims cannot. That kindness is the morning's. This frame is the evening's furniture: fires, stones, tarps, wire, salvage, pale vent mouths, and no text telling you who won.
