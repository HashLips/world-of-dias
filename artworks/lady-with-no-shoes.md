# Lady with No Shoes

## Overview

*Lady with No Shoes* sits poised on maroon air: white elongated limbs, red V-neck garment, four thin tan chair-lines without a drawn seat pan, feet tapered to sharp points—no shoes—navy diagonal bands bottom-left, tiny white gate mark upper-right.

## Description

The figure is shown chest-down only; absence of head is part of the plate’s formal cut, not a wound tale. Right arm hangs long; left hand rests on the lap. One foot points forward, one tucks back. The chair’s missing pan makes the sit feel ritual-thin. Mood is stoic elegance without footwear claims.
