# Waiting

## Name

Waiting

## Overview

*Waiting* is a bath with company. A huge white bowl, brimming with bright blue water, sits on a deep red block under an enormous yellow sun with pale triangular rays. Four dark fins break the water's surface in a loose circle. A dark silhouette bather hangs over the bowl's front rim, chin on forearm, arms dangling down the outside, one eye peeking sideways at the fins. On the red block below, a pair of green sandals waits neatly side by side.

## Description

The sky is a soft cyan. The sun fills the top of the frame, only its lower half visible, ringed by long cream triangles. The bowl is wide and shallow-looking, white with a gray inner shadow, and the water inside is a clean cobalt with a lighter ripple at the near edge.

The fins are dark gray and black, different sizes, one tall in the middle, one small at the right, one stubby near the back. A tiny dark sliver floats near the center, perhaps a fifth fin just surfacing. None of them is moving fast.

The bather is relaxed and wary at once: body over the edge, arms limp, but eye on the water. Their shadow falls pale gray down the bowl's outside. The sandals below are bright green, placed carefully, toes pointed out. The mood is comic heat—someone who came for a soak and is now waiting for the fins to lose interest.

## Real-World Role

A bathhouse plate about waiting out circling gossip before getting fully into the water.

## Lore Connection

Ringwash Baths are the second-ring public baths of Veloria City—steam, tiled channels, and the city's softest gossip exchange. Their rooftop sun-bowls are wide white basins left to warm in the open, and regulars joke that the fins circling in them are whatever gossip is going around that day. Waiting shows the bath's oldest courtesy: when the fins are up, you leave your sandals on the step and hang on the rim until they settle, rather than wading into the middle of someone else's story. Nobody has ever been bitten. Plenty have been embarrassed.
