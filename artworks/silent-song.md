# Silent Song

## Name

Silent Song

## Overview

*Silent Song* is a profile portrait of a purple-skinned otherworldly figure with a sharp white nose, slit eye, golden lips, and a raised black hand held near the mouth as if tasting or enjoining silence.

## Description

White hood-shape, salmon bead, grey field. The hand’s long fingers dominate the foreground. The face feels composed rather than kind.

Asset reference: `assets/silent-song.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

Close portrait for Vorrith’s alien calm and tasting craft.

## Lore Connection

Used as a face-memory of the One Who Cooks Quiet: knowledge held behind a gesture that could be appetite or warning.

## Notes

Pairs with *Mystic Shadow* (body/shadow) as face/hand.
