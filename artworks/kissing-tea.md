# Kissing Tea

## Name

Kissing Tea

## Overview

*Kissing Tea* is a quiet Basin sip: deep-brown profile facing right, white eye, light-blue floppy wide hat with an elegant white crescent plume, dark rounded vessel with two small circular handles and a long curved spout pressed to the mouth against solid mustard yellow.

## Description

Hand grips the lower handle; spout kisses the lips—hence the name, not romance theater. Hat brim softens the yellow glare. Plume arcs back, almost another spout of air. Tiny black glyph sits top-left. No tea-house furniture drawn; the whole plate is the sip.

Asset reference: `assets/kissing-tea.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A vessel manners plate: tea meets mouth with spout care, not slosh.

## Lore Connection

Velorian Basin tea houses—including Third Ring talk—know Kissing Tea as the spout-to-lip courtesy that keeps drips off maps and sleeves. Local rule: tip the vessel, do not bite the spout; return it upright before speech. Resonance rides the warm dark vessel. Blowing across a neighbor’s plume while they sip is a small Basin rudeness.
