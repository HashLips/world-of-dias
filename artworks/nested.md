# Nested

## Name

Nested

## Overview

*Nested* shows a black human silhouette whose cast shadow is not human: a purple rabbit head thrown across floor and wall.

## Description

Barefoot figure on white ground-plane, one hand raised near the head. From the feet stretches a purple rabbit-head silhouette that bends up the wall—same light logic, wrong shape.
