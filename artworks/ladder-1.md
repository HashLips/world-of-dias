# Ladder

## Name

Ladder

## Overview

*Ladder* is stark Driftfall geometry: primary red ladder ~14 rungs leaning bottom-right to top-left on deep cobalt, base on a light sky-blue floor band, and a perfect black shadow-ladder offset behind and left, white cursive mark bottom-left.

## Description

No climber. The twin black shadow makes the red read as chosen tool, not decoration. Horizontal split of blues is wall versus floor. Mood is ready climb with a visible cost—the shadow that will follow every rung.

Asset reference: `assets/ladder-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A tool plate: ladders lean honest when their shadows are admitted.

## Lore Connection

Driftfall piers and sheds live by ladders. This plate teaches checking the black twin—meaning the wear, the second person spotting, the drop risk—before you trust the red. Local rule: if the shadow-ladder is ignored, do not climb. Resonance sticks to rungs counted aloud. Painting over a cracked rung to keep the red pretty is pier disgrace.

## Notes

Red ladder, black shadow ladder, cobalt and sky-blue split.
