# Imagine All the People

## Name

Imagine All the People

## Overview

*Imagine All the People* is a Wabet crowd plate: one brilliant white featureless body in the left-center foreground, slightly larger, set against hundreds of overlapping dark reddish-brown and sienna silhouettes packed into the lower two-thirds under an open pale orange sky-band.

## Description

All figures share the same simple humanoid plan—rounded heads, tube arms, no faces, no clothes detail. Only value separates them: white for the near one, deep warm browns for the mass. Rows recede without paths or banners. The upper third stays empty peach, making the density below feel heavier. A tiny white square mark sits top-right. The title is a Dias name for the act of holding every silhouette in mind—not an Earth song claim.

Asset reference: `assets/imagine-all-the-people.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A care plate for Wabet gatherings: imagining the many without erasing the one who stands bright.

## Lore Connection

Wabet crowds know tide-song cousins, markets, and quiet wells. Imagine All the People teaches a local rule: when you picture a crowd, keep at least one figure readable as a person you could greet—here, the white silhouette—so the mass does not become a blur you shove. Resonance prefers speakers who can name the many without pretending they have no fronts. The peach empty air is room for breath before the next push of bodies.
