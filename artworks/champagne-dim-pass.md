# Champagne Dim Pass

## Name

Champagne Dim Pass

## Overview

*Champagne Dim Pass* is a digital image study of Bandwidth Sky-Mantas in passage: colossal manta-forms turning a gold field briefly champagne-dim.

## Description

Two vast manta-forms glide low over a luminous, swirling sea of light, wings broad and tapering, surfaces traced with fine pale-gold filigree, long whip tails trailing. Further off, smaller mantas cross the haze, emphasizing scale. Cloud banks glow at the left; sparks drift upward from the rippling field below. Faint cartographic arcs lie across the sky.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/bandwidth-sky-mantas.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of manta passage as F200 weather-and-animal sublime: awe-disorientation rather than appetite.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the established colossal scale and champagne-dim passage without inventing feeding behavior, riders, or navigation powers.

## Notes

Sky-ocean is F200 light field, not literal water. Distant mantas are scale, not a herd count. Border ornaments are not meridian law.
