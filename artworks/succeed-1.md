# Succeed

## Name

Succeed

## Overview

*Succeed* hangs a black inverted body gripping a yellow chair that balances on one leg over a small red point on teal ground.

## Description

Upside down dark silhouette, legs off the top edge, white crescent at the head, hands on the chair back. Yellow chair tipped hard; contact is a red cone or bowl tip. Soft grey shadow. Two mute teal bands for wall and floor. Tiny mark top left.

Asset reference: `assets/succeed-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

Mercer Shed plate for success as a ridiculous hold: balance that looks like it should fail.

## Lore Connection

Winning here is staying upright on almost nothing. Do not turn the red point into a named relic without more canon.
