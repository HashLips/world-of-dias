# Hard

## Name

Hard

## Overview

*Hard* is a Velorian Basin meeting plate: two blue profiles face each other on a terracotta base while a pink bar frames the right—the basin craft of holding a hard look without turning it into a fight.

## Description

Pale sky-blue field. Two identical-leaning blue heads in profile nearly touching at nose and chin—light sky blue forward planes, deeper teal on backs of heads/necks; high foreheads, long straight noses, thin neutral lips; white almond eyes with blue pupils locked on each other; white-outlined ears. Right head slightly layered/translucent with a second lighter blue profile behind. Large rounded terracotta shape at bottom center as shared base/shoulders. Thin dark brown three-sided frame around the right figure with a solid pale pink vertical bar inside. Quiet, analytical, dual. Balanced negative space.

Asset reference: `assets/hard-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A basin phenomenon plate: the hard look is allowed; the pink bar means register it, then stop short of harm.

## Lore Connection

In the Velorian Basin, **Hard** names the weather of face-to-face difficulty. Terracotta base is shared ground. Pink bar is registration of the hard hour—clerk-cousin manners without city bureaucracy. Wonder is durable attention. Dias basin name.
