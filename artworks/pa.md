# Pa

## Name

Pa

## Overview

*Pa* is the Calibration Row plate for the offered ring. A larger figure in blue and dark tones sits left. A smaller figure in blue and dark tones sits right. Between them, a glowing yellow ring.

## Description

Slate-gray backdrop. Terracotta ground across the bottom third, black bar under that. Left: a larger seated figure facing right—solid black for back and lower body, light blue for torso, arm, and front of head. The head is elongated and simple. One arm lifts, bent, holding a small bright yellow ring between the fingers—the only warm glow in the cool palette.

Right: a smaller, more compact seated figure faces the larger one, same blue-and-dark split, posture curved upward as if watching the ring arrive. No table, no crowd, no written label on the ring. A tiny faint white three-prong icon sits far top-right.

The plate is the transmission moment: ring offered, smaller figure attending, Row quiet around them.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Calibration Row. Pa is the kept craft of handing the yellow ring with care.

## Lore Connection

Pa is the Row's name for the hour when a larger keeper shows a small glowing yellow ring to a smaller watcher without forcing the take. Local care says the ring is resonance you may accept, not a stamp you must wear. Memory is the blue-and-dark split bodies sitting on terracotta; wonder is how loud such a tiny yellow circle can be on slate gray. The plate invents no bloodline sermon. It keeps the offer as manners: hold steady, let the smaller hands decide.
