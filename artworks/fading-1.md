# Fading

## Name

Fading

## Overview

*Fading* is an F610 quieting plate: a yellow figure marked with a blue mouth-X stands at a cerulean scaffold hung with a dripping orange banner—the frequency hour when speech thins and listening thickens.

## Description

Deep navy field. Bright yellow waist-up humanoid turned slightly, head tilted up-side; thin black eye slits; prominent blue X of two diagonals over the mouth. Mustard-grey translucent overlap on one side of body. Center: bright cerulean angular scaffold of flat intersecting planes. Over its left arm hangs a long narrow dripping form—orange core, pale pink border, thin blue line with downward arrow; three drip ends. Somber, still, formal. Small white arch emblem and script upper left.
