# Tree Fairy

## Name

Tree Fairy

## Overview

*Tree Fairy* is a night perch in bare branches. Two black trees with angular, crooked limbs frame a huge blue disc against a pale yellow sky. Three small winged figures share the branches. One sits on a low limb with spiky wings flared upward like a crown of leaves. Another sits facing it, long legs dangling, something thin and curved held at its side. The third hangs upside down near the top, tangled comfortably among twigs, legs kicked up.

## Description

The palette is three flat colors: black for trees and figures, a strong cerulean for the disc, pale butter-yellow for the sky. A dark hill swells across the bottom. Because everything black is the same black, the figures read as part of the trees until you find their heads.

The branches are jagged and bent at sharp angles, like lightning frozen into wood. The low branch that holds two figures crosses the disc from left to right like a bridge. The wings of the left figure are pointed and splayed in many spikes, more leaf than feather. The right figure's limbs are long and folded, its curved object possibly a bow, a stalk, or a tail.

The top figure is the funniest. It hangs head down in the fork of the right tree, wings tucked, limbs at odd angles, as relaxed as someone lying in a hammock. Nobody is flying. The three are simply keeping company.

## Real-World Role

A canopy-creature plate for small wingfolk who gather on bare branches on bright-disc nights.

## Lore Connection

Aelwyn Canopy is the vast jungle-forest across northern Wabet. Tree fairies are its small winged neighbors, seen most often in the few bare, storm-stripped trees that rise above the canopy, where they gather when the big blue disc hangs low. The plate shows three at a perch: two facing each other on a branch, one hanging upside down above. Canopy walkers treat a perch like this as a sign that the grove below is calm and keeps its echoes well. The local rule is quiet hands: you do not shake a perch tree, you do not shout beneath it, and you do not take wood from it.
