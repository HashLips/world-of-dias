# Hoofprints Like Ink Blots

## Name

Hoofprints Like Ink Blots

## Overview

*Hoofprints Like Ink Blots* is a digital image study of Averra Ink-Deer at studio and cliff-path edges.

## Description

A slender charcoal-and-cream deer stands at a cliff-path or studio-edge—eyes reflective like wet ink. Brief dark hoofprints read as ink blots on damp ground before rain or tide takes them. Not aggressive.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/averra-ink-deer.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Averra Isle print myths grounded in coastal soil and gland oils.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens Averra Ink-Deer without inventing speech or studio patronage.

## Notes

Print ink is mineral residue and oils, not literal writing ink spells. Border framing only.
