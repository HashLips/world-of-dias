# Inside My Room

## Name

Inside My Room

## Overview

*Inside My Room* frames a revelation: saturated red outer edges, two stark white doors swung wide, and a light-grey chamber holding a black pedestal with a jagged front crack, a bright cyan T-bar perch, and a teal-breasted charcoal-winged bird looking down-left.

## Description

The bird’s head, chest, and under-tail glow seafoam-teal; wings, back, and upper tail stay charcoal. Thin blue beak; black legs grip the T-bar. One wing lifts slightly—just landed or about to shift. The pedestal’s vertical jagged black line aligns under the stand like a known flaw kept honest. No cage bars. The open doors are the invitation; the red hall is what you leave behind to look.

Asset reference: `assets/inside-my-room.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A studio-care plate for Averra: the inner room is for one living focus, not for hallway noise.

## Lore Connection

Averra damp studios keep inner rooms for work that needs a single perch. Inside My Room teaches: open the white doors only when the bird (or the craft it stands for) is ready to be seen. Local rule: do not slam the doors for effect—startle empties the teal. Resonance stays with the T-bar and the cracked pedestal’s honesty; polishing the crack away is considered lying about the stand.
