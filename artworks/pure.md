# Pure

## Name

Pure

## Overview

*Pure* is the Aelwyn Canopy plate for the one fish above its echoes. Blue spindly pedestal. Glass bowl. Single orange fish swimming left. Five orange fish-shapes on the sandy floor.

## Description

Room splits horizontal: dark chocolate-brown upper wall, sandy-beige floor. Center-right, a tall spindly pedestal stands on four long thin blue legs rising from the bottom edge; the top is a flat square blue surface. On it sits a translucent wide-mouthed glass bowl. Inside, one vibrant orange fish in profile swims left—simple stylized body, sharp dorsal, broad notched tail.

Across the beige floor, five more orange fish shapes lie flat in varied orientations—left, right, diagonal—like shadows or loosened echoes of the bowl fish. Tiny faint white glyph top-left. No hand nets them. The plate is the singular elevated fish and the multiple floor copies that are not inside the glass.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Aelwyn Canopy. Pure is the kept craft of one held measure above its echoes.

## Lore Connection

Pure is how the canopy names the hour when one orange fish stays in the bowl on the blue pedestal while five floor echoes scatter without climbing the glass. Local care says purity here is keeping the one clear, not scrubbing the echoes out of existence. Resonance is bowl fish to floor fish; memory is the blue legs; wonder is how quiet the brown wall stays. The plate invents no aquarium industry. It keeps the craft as manners for a single held living mark.
