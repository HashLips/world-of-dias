# Spoon Hull on Biased Wheels

## Name

Spoon Hull on Biased Wheels

## Overview

*Spoon Hull on Biased Wheels* is a digital image study of Ansel Hip's self-propelled courier craft, shown at rest where it looks most like kitchenware that learned pride.

## Description

The front of the craft is a deep polished scoop, engraved along its flank, opening forward like a serving spoon that lost an argument. Behind it the hull narrows into a riveted cylinder of boiler plate and tin, with pipework and springs underneath where the motor mutters.

High on the rear hip sits a single upholstered seat on a short post, and behind it a small strapped box for sealed cards and small crates. A column of bright round lamps stands at the back, and a long tiller with a leather-wrapped grip angles out to the side. The spoked wheels sit at uneven heights and angles, so the whole body seems about to curtsy and never does.

The craft rests on a circular cobbled terrace with a low parapet; a hazy outer-ring roofline lies beyond. The decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/the-hipspoon.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of the Hipspoon as an oddly shaped, self-propelled ground craft: no animal, no track, a tiller, and a man who can be yelled at.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the Hipspoon's established image (split-spoon scoop, boiler plate and polished tin, single high seat, rear box, too-bright electric lamps, leather-wrapped tiller, biased wheels) without adding speed, range, or new functions.

## Notes

Canon fixes the Hipspoon at three wheels on a bias; read the plate's wheel arrangement through that. The terrace and skyline are setting, not a named place. Ansel Hip is not pictured. The Hipspoon does not leave the ground.
