# Pop Pop

## Name

Pop Pop

## Overview

*Pop Pop* is the Brindle March plate for the double-eyed long neck. Purple column with white stripe and three red drips. Fragmented head in red, purple, cyan. One large almond eye, one small floating white eye. Pink mound base on olive.

## Description

Muted olive-green field. From a smooth curved light-pink mound at the bottom, a long thin neck rises—mostly purple, with a central white vertical stripe containing three small vertical red droplets or streaks. The head is layered, not solid: a large almond eye with black pupil and white iris on a vibrant red field; a purple band outlining top and right; a jagged bright cyan lower face; to the left a smaller crescent white eye with black sliver floating in space; a sharp white angular jaw- or tooth-shape protruding left.

Sharp edges, no soft blend between the head shards. The march plate reads as watchfulness that has popped into more than one eye, still held on one neck.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Brindle March. Pop Pop is the kept craft of split looking on one stem.

## Lore Connection

Pop Pop is the march's name for the hour when one long neck carries a broken-bright head and a second small eye floats beside the large almond. Local care says both eyes count; ignoring the floating crescent is how carts get surprised. The three red drips in the white neck-stripe are resonance marks, not a wound to poke. Wonder is cyan against red on olive; memory is the pink mound that still holds the column. The plate invents no explosion story. It keeps the double watch as a craft reminder: look twice, name both eyes, then move.
