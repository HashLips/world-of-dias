# Animals on the Round Door

## Name

Animals on the Round Door

## Overview

Animals on the Round Door is a digital image study of the dream threshold room known as The Passage Round.

## Description

The plate shows a circular, columned chamber with a red carpet and a great wooden door. The door is carved with wolves, a phoenix, sharks, and dragons. No person appears. This remains a dream threshold room and does not establish travel between frequencies.
