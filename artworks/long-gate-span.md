# Long Gate Span

## Name

Long Gate Span

## Overview

*Long Gate Span* shows the Nauw approach to Hearthvale as plain stone and timber. Wind-bitten rails, empty rest niches, three travelers, and fields opening south. No emblem. No boat.

## Description

The span is long and stubborn. Piers hold it. Rails look chewed by wind. Rest niches are built into the walk and they are empty of inn comfort: no beds, no host, no lamp arranged as welcome. Three travelers carry ordinary bundles. Their faces are not named portraits. South of the mouth the land opens into fields, rebuild weather rather than a city gatehouse. Nobody is selling a ticket.

The plate does not show a ferry, a boat, a subway, or a door out of F432. It does not settle whose yes made the span walkable. The feeling of being watched belongs to the approach. It is not a clerk carved into the stone, and it is not an answer to the old argument.

## Real-World Role

A scene plate for F432's moral threshold between Nauw and northern Sorel: a road people walk, not a band crossing.

## Lore Connection

Long Gate is stone and timber infrastructure for exile, trade, and second-beginning hope. It is distinct from Westfold Toll and from any joke pier that sells imaginary band tickets. The three travelers do not decide the myth. The empty niches do not become inns. The south fields are the opening toward Hearthvale, not a map of who owns them.

No emblems, no boats, no Frequency Zero, no ferry. The open question of consent stays open.
