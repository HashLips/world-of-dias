# Aurel Meridian Gathering

## Name

Aurel Meridian Gathering

## Overview

*Aurel Meridian Gathering* shows F200's bright spine as a choir of light. The beings are filament and corona. They have no flesh and no pupils. The ground is not soil, and there is no treeline.

## Description

Gold-white ribs of radiance run a long hall and pulse like a resting breath. Paths are filament. Volumes are haloed, and they do not throw a familiar shadow. The figures match the Aurel-kind record: a gold-white core, a soft corona, slower honey-colored rings, and a mask-smooth oval of light. If a face is there, it is that oval. Expression is a change in brightness, not a pupil or a mouth.

They stand in a row, the gathering charts call a standing choir. The plate does not invent a government, a rite, or a population count. Serenity here is populated. It is not an empty glow, and it is not a saint's portrait borrowed from F432 flesh.

A scene plate for the luminosity band when someone needs to see habitation rather than a map rectangle.

## Lore Connection

Aurel Meridian is the inner high-clarity band of F200, a luminance step rather than a cliff. Aurel-kind are energy-native persons, serene and non-predatory in the record, socially a pattern of light. The picture stops where the record stops. It does not promote any rumor from the null band into this hall.

## Notes

No soil, no trees, no night, no weapons. Do not add pupils or a celebrity likeness on a later pass.
