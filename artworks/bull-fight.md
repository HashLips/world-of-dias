# Bull Fight

## Name

Bull Fight

## Overview

*Bull Fight* is a Brindle March contest plate: two black horn-headed figures lock white-eyed and cross limbs on magenta ground-paths—the march’s way of settling a load quarrel without ruining the road.

## Description

Periwinkle upper field, mustard-yellow lower. Wide irregular magenta-pink ribbons flow across the ground. Two solid black horn-headed silhouettes dominate: lean elongated limbs, small glowing white almond eyes. Left figure crouches low, one leg arched high over its back like a stinger-curve, one arm grounded, one reaching up. Right figure bends deep at the waist, long spindly arms extended, large four-fingered hands crossing the first figure’s raised arm into a central X. Heads lowered toward each other. Stark contrast, ceremonial tension. Small black gate-like seal bottom-right.

Asset reference: `assets/bull-fight.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A march dispute plate: how two stubborn loads meet without breaking the fruit road.

## Lore Connection

On Brindle March, **Bull Fight** is not Earth bloodsport. It is a named non-human (or horn-marked) contest form used when axle-share and path-right quarrel harder than chalk can fix. Magenta paths mark the agreed strip; stepping off is forfeit. White eyes mean both parties are still seeing, not blind-raging. Crossed limbs are contact with rules. Wonder is contained force: settle, then thank specifically, then move the cart. The name is Dias march-speech.
