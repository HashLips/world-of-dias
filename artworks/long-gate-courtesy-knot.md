# Loosened Courtesy on Gate Wood

## Name

Loosened Courtesy on Gate Wood

## Overview

*Loosened Courtesy on Gate Wood* is a short hemp cord twisted with a dull red ribbon, the knot already slack, lying on a split of weathered timber. It is the object after the one courteous yield. It is not a pass, and it is not a person.

## Description

The plank runs on a diagonal and fills the frame. The wood is grey-brown, checked, and old enough that the grain has opened into cracks a cord could catch in. Against that grain the knot sits small. Hemp makes a pale loop on one side, fibers still twisted and a little frayed at the cut. A ribbon the color of dried clay crosses the same knot and falls in loose tails, one longer than the other, neither end tucked as if the tying were finished for show.

The knot itself is not cinched hard. The loops have room in them. That slack is the point of the plate: the courtesy has already been spent, or is waiting where a hand set it down, and the cord is not pretending to be a lock. There is no cart, no exile pack, no stamp, no steward's fingers, and no face that could be read as permission. The parchment-colored ground behind the plank stays empty. Nothing in the picture sells a shortcut.

The plate does not settle whose line this cord rode in on, whether the yield was accepted, or which speech it was meant to delay. It does not show a false knot being cut, and it does not show a true one being honored. Hemp, ribbon, slack, and worn wood are the whole argument, and they do not win it.

## Real-World Role

A still life for threshold manners. Dignity is a cord you can loosen, not a ticket the picture can cash.

## Lore Connection

At the Long Gate a courtesy knot asks the holder to yield once before stamp arguments get loud. Stewards, drivers, and exile families use the honest version. Sellers of skip-the-gate knots are a known scam, and this plate refuses that reading: there is no queue to jump and no official to bribe, only a slack twist that has already done, or not done, its single polite job. It sits beside the Long Gate Argument as comedy's straight object. Frequency politics stay off the wood. Nauw and Sorel colors can be argued from the hemp and the ribbon; the picture does not label them, and it does not turn the cord into clearance.

## Notes

No person. Not a pass. The knot is loosened on purpose.
