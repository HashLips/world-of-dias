# Knock Soft

## Name

Knock Soft

## Overview

*Knock Soft* is a Mercer Shed interior of almost-entry: deep purple wall, magenta-pink floor, huge cyan circular eye with tan lid-bands and black pupil, white figure with upswept black hair looking up-left, second white figure kneeling at a brown door’s base, small black quadruped mid-room, red profile beyond a thin purple pillar on grey, long purple shadows.

## Description

The eye mounts the wall like a soft-mark watcher—unblinking, not kind, not cruel. The kneeling body listens at the gap rather than pounding. Door handle is a small dark horizontal. Mood is held breath before a soft knock. Filename keeps knok-knok; display name is Knock Soft.

Asset reference: `assets/knok-knok.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A door manners plate: listen, then knock soft under the wall-eye.

## Lore Connection

Mercer Shed sits near Soft Mark care in wider talk—manners about what is sealed and what may be asked. Knock Soft teaches: the cyan eye sees hurry; the brown door rewards a quiet test of the gap before knuckles. Local rule: if someone already kneels listening, wait your turn. Resonance prefers the soft knock that the red outsider also hears without alarm. Hard pounding under the eye is remembered longer than the errand.
