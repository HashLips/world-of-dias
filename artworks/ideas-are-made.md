# Ideas Are Made

## Name

Ideas Are Made

## Overview

*Ideas Are Made* is a Coral Strand growth plate: five thin blue stems rising from olive ground through cream hills into a hard red sky, each flared bell-tip holding a dark silhouette in a different pose—curl, backbend, hunch, arch, reach.

## Description

Stems curve gently, sprout small pointed blue leaves, and emerge from spiky blue basal tufts. Tops open into wide blue funnels that bear weight without breaking in the still image. Figures share one white horizontal eye-slit each and no other face detail. Long thin grey shadows stretch bottom-left. The red sky occupies the upper two-thirds; cream jagged hills split sky from green. Nothing looks accidental: ideas here are grown tall and perched, not whispered in a pocket.

Asset reference: `assets/ideas-are-made.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A making plate: thought given height and posture on living blue stems.

## Lore Connection

Coral Strand talk treats Ideas Are Made as a reminder that a notion needs a stalk before it deserves a crowd. Local rule: do not pluck a blue bell while a figure is still balancing—wait until the pose finishes or the thinker climbs down. Resonance climbs the stem with the perch. People who stamp “done” on an idea still curled fetal on the tip are told to look at the red sky and count to five stems.

Five blue plants, five black perchers, red sky, cream hills.
