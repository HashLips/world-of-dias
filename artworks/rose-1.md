# Rose

## Name

Rose

## Overview

*Rose* is the Sorel plate for the split-bloom five. White straight stems, white leaves, indigo thorns. Bell heads magenta left, deep red right. Cyan vibration fringe. Royal blue field.

## Description

Solid saturated royal blue. Five flowers rise staggered from the bottom edge—no soil drawn. Each has a thin perfectly straight white stem, two small pointed white leaves, and tiny dark purple/indigo thorn marks. Heads are large bell- or trumpet-shaped in side profile, vertically bisected: bright magenta-pink on the left, bold deep red on the right, edges slightly ruffled.

Thin glowing cyan and light-magenta outlines shift slightly off the whites and petal edges—chromatic vibration as if the blooms are not fully settled in the blue. Tiny white crown/gate icon top-right. Artificial, energized, dual-colored path flora.
