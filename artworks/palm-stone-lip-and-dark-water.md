# Palm, Stone Lip, and Dark Water

## Name

Palm, Stone Lip, and Dark Water

## Overview

*Palm, Stone Lip, and Dark Water* is a low green pocket in pale dunes: one date palm, a ring of stones, and dark water. Nobody is in the picture. It is not a map, and it is not a correction of a wrong one.

## Description

The pool sits in a hollow the dunes have not quite closed. Its lip is a single course of rounded stones, uneven, more curb than monument. The water is dark and almost still, small enough to walk around and deep enough in color that the plate refuses to show a bottom. Scrub clumps around the stones, low and ordinary, the kind of green that means damp ground rather than a garden. One date palm stands just behind the lip. Its crown is the only tall thing in the pocket. There is no second tree, no shade-camp, no rope, no jar line.

Dunes repeat in every direction, hatched and empty, meeting a flat horizon under a lightly ruled sky. No trail cuts the sand. No cairn, no arrow, no lettering, no scale, no north. The ridges look alike on purpose: a traveler could not lift a route out of this view and trust it. The picture is a place seen once, not a chart that stays true after you leave. It does not mark a decoy against a true well. It does not show which dune hid the pocket, or from which side the hollow was approached.

No figure stands at the lip, under the palm, or on the slopes. No animal is drawn in as a guide. The hush is the empty sand and the dark water, not a crowd agreeing out loud. Smoke, footprints, and a posted rule are all absent. The plate keeps the pocket at palm, stone, scrub, and water, and declines to become the public drawing Silence Law would treat as theft.

## Real-World Role

A place plate for a concealed oasis that must not function as cartography. The water is visible. The way to it is not.

## Lore Connection

Quiet Well is a concealed pocket in Lumira Sands. Its water is real, and its maps are often intentionally wrong. The Silent Dune Keep treats accurate public cartography here as a kind of theft. *The Map That Must Stay Wrong* and the Quiet Well map-wrongness rule are that ethic in story and in rule. This plate shows the pocket and withholds the path: no bearings, no false mark labeled as false, no true mark labeled as true. Keth Dunewalk maintains false marks and true paths as one craft under Iskar Lüm. Neither of them is standing here to perform it. Night at the well is said to be people agreeing not to speak louder than the water. The picture has the water and none of the people. Visitors who find a well by rumor usually found a decoy first. Nothing in the frame tells you which kind this view is, and the dunes will not help you publish the difference.

## Notes

No people. Not a map. Palm, stone lip, dark water. The dunes do not yield a route.
