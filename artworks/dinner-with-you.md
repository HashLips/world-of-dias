# Dinner With You

## Name

Dinner With You

## Overview

*Dinner With You* shows a white bowl of crimson broth on teal ground, long cyan chopsticks rising like antennae, and a slender blue arm draped over the rim among stylized bites. Softfruit humor with teeth. Hospitality is the subject. Comfort is not guaranteed. The picture is what a court looks like if you admit the guest might also be the garnish, and then you feed them anyway.

## Description

Flat vector clarity: dark teal table, lighter teal field, a heavy black shadow that makes the bowl sit harder than kindness. The broth is too vivid for comfort, a red that wants to be joy and might be a warning. Chopsticks glow cooler than the welcome. The blue arm can be read as guest, as garnish, or as the joke a Host tells with their face when someone tries to win breakfast. Nothing in the frame is a seal. That absence is the argument. You are at the table before you are in the ledger.

People who hang it in the Ladle Court hang it where the queue can see it and not where a speech could use it. Children count the bites. Clerks count the missing stamp and get restless, which is the print working.

Asset reference: `assets/dinner-with-you.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

An orientation print for Softfruit’s uncanny hospitality: joy that notices hunger without pretending meals are only sweet. It teaches the pause by being delicious and slightly wrong. Galleries that want a pure feast will not get one here.

## Lore Connection

Pairs with ladle-before-stamp manners. Share first. Stamp later. Keep the weird visible so fairness does not turn into a sermon. The second-ladle shelf is the real-world cousin of this rim: hooks, tokens, an arm’s worth of reaching. The picture does not illustrate a myth so much as the moment before the myth’s stew spills, when you can still choose the order.

## Notes

Street gallery cousin, not a House seal. Do not tidy the arm out of the bowl to make the joy safer.
