# The Quiet Sea at Distress Listen

## Name

The Quiet Sea at Distress Listen

## Overview

The Quiet Sea at Distress Listen is a digital image study of the Rim-Rest lookout after dark.

## Description

The plate holds the night spit against a too-quiet sea, with a rope rail, a rack for horns, and a blank slate waiting for the last signal. The place is ready to listen even though no boat is present.

There are no boats, rune texts, or pennants in the image. Its stillness belongs to the lookout’s mutual-rescue duty, not to a rescue already underway.

Asset reference: `assets/outer-rim-distress-listen.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A night plate for the listening work that makes Distress Before Debt practical at the Rim-Rest edge.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the place behind the horn patterns and leaves the question of false distress open.
