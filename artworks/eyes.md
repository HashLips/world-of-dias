# Eyes

## Name

Eyes

## Overview

*Eyes* is an F380 keeper plate: a white-masked pale figure holds a giant yellow crescent across the chest—the anonymous look that protects a harvest-form without making a face of ownership.

## Description

Deep black field. Mid-torso-up humanoid with smooth pale-pink skin. Head is a smooth egg-shaped white mask with only two dark downward-slanting oval eye slits—no nose, no mouth. Arms support a massive bright yellow curved form like a stylized fruit crescent spanning nearly the width; grey-brown stem nub at one tip; slight darker yellow underside edge. Quiet, ceremonial, surreal. All attention on masked keeper and yellow hold.

Asset reference: `assets/eyes.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A frequency symbol plate: the eyes that guard a shared crescent without claiming a personal face.

## Lore Connection

In F380, **Eyes** marks anonymous care. Mask slits see; they do not perform. Yellow crescent is a kept resource-form—fruit-sign or measure—held horizontal so it cannot be hidden behind the back. Wonder is faceless responsibility. Dias mark-name.
