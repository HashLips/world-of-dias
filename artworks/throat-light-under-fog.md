# Throat Light Under Fog

## Name

Throat Light Under Fog

## Overview

*Throat Light Under Fog* is a digital image study of Fogbank Siren-Eels hunting where vision fails.

## Description

A long eel longer than a dory oar coils under foggy Outer Rim water—skin like wet slate, pale ventral glow, a pulsing throat-light that reads as lure rather than ornament. Thin singing vibration is implied by ripple lines, not written music. No swimmer is shown.

Decorative border is framing only.
