# Over the Seas

## Name

Over the Seas

## Overview

*Over the Seas* shows two black galley-like ships on a blue sea split by a jagged orange sun-path, under a huge cut-off orange sun and a dark shore line.

## Description

Near ship large and spiked with oars; far ship smaller on the same luminous road. Flat color, sharp edges, monumental journey mood.

Asset reference: `assets/over-the-seas.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A nautical dual-silhouette piece for Dias sea mystery.

## Lore Connection

Read with **Marek Solon** and outer-rim atlas work: two hulls, one path of light—dual presence as voyage rather than portrait. The orange road is argued as a temporary frequency lane across water, visible at certain rises and denied by ordinary charts.
