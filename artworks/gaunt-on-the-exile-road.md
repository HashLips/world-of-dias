# Gaunt on the Exile Road

## Name

Gaunt on the Exile Road

## Overview

*Gaunt on the Exile Road* is a digital image study of Exile Road Wolves as road danger without showing victims.

## Description

Gaunt wolves travel a dust road through barren hills toward distant first shelters—lean hungry forms, no human prey shown. Rejection and shelter are landscape mood, not labeled doctrine.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/exile-road-wolves.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of southern exile-route predators.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens Exile Road Wolves without depicting attacked travelers.

## Notes

Printed labels Rejection / Barren Routes / First Shelters on the plate are decorative captions, not new canon place names beyond the exile-road idea. Border framing only.
