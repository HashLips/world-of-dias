# Drinking Tea with Them

## Name

Drinking Tea with Them

## Overview

*Drinking Tea with Them* is a Velorian Basin guest plate: two lean dark figures face each other at a high red table with one white cup—the basin rule that shared tea is enough without a second cup of performance.

## Description

Vibrant blue center field framed by wide coral-pink vertical bands; charcoal-grey ground. Two featureless dark elongated figures in profile lean slightly forward, small white almond eyes only. They sit on stools with flat red seats and exceptionally long thin light-pink legs; between them a small rectangular red table on matching spindly pink legs. One small white cup on the table casts a long dark shadow toward the left figure. Furniture legs throw long sharp black shadows lower left. Still, careful, slightly tense-delicate.

Asset reference: `assets/drinking-tea-with-them.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A basin hospitality plate: one cup, two watchers, no need to fill the silence with ownership.

## Lore Connection

In the Velorian Basin, **Drinking Tea with Them** is a kept guest craft. The single white cup means the tea is shared, not scored. Spindly height keeps the meeting light and reversible. Wonder is quiet company. “Them” may be humanoid or non-human neighbor; the plate does not force a species file.

## Notes

Two black sitters. One white cup. Red table and seats on long pink legs. Blue field, coral bands.
