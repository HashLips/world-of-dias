# Stillface

## Name

Stillface

## Overview

*Stillface* is a stoic bust on grey: peach planes, blue brow and nose line, thin black mouth that barely moves.

## Description

From the chest up three quarter portrait. Large rounded head, angular jaw, sparse black hair marks. Bright blue strokes for brows, bridge, and eye ticks. Mouth a straight black dash. Two peach tones split by a hard line; deep brown at neck. Tiny mark top right.

Asset reference: `assets/stillface-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

Brindle March face plate for calm that does not perform.

## Lore Connection

The still mouth is the title. Mystery stays in what the blue lines refuse to explain.
