# Alignment Before Speech

## Name

Alignment Before Speech

## Overview

*Alignment Before Speech* is a digital image study of Diva One in luminous composure: blue-violet-yellow light geometry held still before speech.

## Description

Centered head-and-shoulders portrait of a feminine coherent-energy figure facing forward. Viewer's left reads deep blue-violet; right reads golden-yellow; center shimmers where the fields meet. Fine luminous geometric lines map face and upper body, with a bright vertical seam running down the center of the face. Eyes are half-lidded and pale; lips closed. High dark-blue collar with gold filigree catches the light-seams.

Parchment ground; decorative border framing only.
