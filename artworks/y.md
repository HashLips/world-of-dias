# Y

## Name

Y

## Overview

*Y* forks a bright red Y across hills and a pale face in a tan sun disk, with a sharp blue triangle nested in the crotch of the letter.

## Description

Large organic red Y from bottom center. Behind it: pale profile in a tan circle on dark brown, rose and brown rolling hills, jagged dark horizon. Blue triangle at the base of the fork. Flat color, small mark bottom right.

Asset reference: `assets/y.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

Claimscar Yard fork plate: two ways held as one letter.
