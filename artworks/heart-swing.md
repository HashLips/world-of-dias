# Heart Swing

## Name

Heart Swing

## Overview

*Heart Swing* is a pale-yellow sky over olive ground: a long black floating body with a pink-red chest cavity, four orange-red tethers spilling from it, and a bright blue figure mid-leap gripping those lines from a small brown seat.

## Description

The upper form stretches horizontally—thin, black, head to the left with a closed yellow eye-slit. Mid-torso, a pale-pink organic opening shows a red interior; four orange-red cords emerge and run diagonally down-right. The blue figure below is dynamic, arms forward on the cords, body suggesting run or swing, a dark grey shadow underfoot on the olive plane. The brown rectangle under the blue figure reads as seat or harness, not furniture for rest. A tiny white crest sits in the top-right corner. The mood is tethered motion, not romance poster: someone is using a floating weight as anchor.

## Real-World Role

A craft-and-motion plate for Driftfall: swings and tethers that borrow a body’s opening without claiming the whole person.

## Lore Connection

Driftfall piers know borrowed momentum—ropes, seats, and weights that let a runner clear a gap without owning the sky. Heart Swing teaches a local rule: if you grip lines that come from another body’s chest-opening, say what you are borrowing and for how many swings. Resonance sticks to the orange cords. Leaving them knotted after you land is considered unfinished care, like a pier rope left to fray in salt.
