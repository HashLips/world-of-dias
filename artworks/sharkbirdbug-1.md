# Sharkbirdbug

## Name

Sharkbirdbug

## Overview

*Sharkbirdbug* is the Nauw Outer Rim Seas plate for the spotted-mantle watcher. Bright blue spindly body. Large orange-red black-spotted shell. Open beak with small white teeth. Gray platform. Pale yellow horizon disc.

## Description

Beige sky band; large pale yellow-beige semi-circle on the horizon behind the platform; dark navy bottom under the elevation. A flat solid gray rectangular platform in perspective holds a spindly bright blue creature: long thin legs and arms, hands on the platform, elongated pointed head like bird or pterosaur, beak open showing a row of small sharp white teeth, one small white eye with black pupil looking down. On the back a large prominent orange-red mantle/shell with irregular black spots, blue-outlined; two small blue wing-like protrusions behind the shell. Long dark gray shadow of lanky body and spotted mantle falls toward the foreground. Tiny black gate/tower icon top-right. Quiet alien observation at the rim.

## Lore Connection

Sharkbirdbug is the Rim Seas' name for this blue spindly neighbor with the black-spotted orange mantle and toothed open beak. Local care says you give the platform room; you do not poke the mantle spots for luck. Resonance is blue against orange-red spots; memory is the downlooking white eye; wonder is the pale yellow disc behind gray elevation over navy. The plate invents no hunt. It keeps the presence as rim manners toward a mixed-shape watcher.
