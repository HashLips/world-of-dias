# Just Cuz It You

## Name

Just Cuz It You

## Overview

*Just Cuz It You* centers care: a pale blue-and-peach field behind a large disc split ochre-left and warm orange-right, a thick primary-blue band cutting the disc like horizon, and a sweeping black mantle-shape holding a single vertical white capsule, with wispy black arcs above and dashes near the blue.

## Description

The white capsule is the quiet prize—nested, not crushed. Black calligraphy feels protective and slightly grasping at once. The blue band stabilizes the hot disc. Title grammar is yard-plain: because it is you, the capsule gets held. Mood is symbolic containment without a drawn crowd.

Asset reference: `assets/just-cuz-it-you.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A care-symbol plate for Claimscar: some holds exist only because the person is known.

## Lore Connection

Claimscar Yard cookfires teach that not every tool is shared by lottery. Just Cuz It You is the mantle around a white memory-capsule or soft token kept for one named holder. Local rule: if the black form is yours, say so at the circle; do not let others guess until the capsule dents. Resonance prefers named holds. Pretending a common tool is “just cuz it you” to hog it empties the phrase.
