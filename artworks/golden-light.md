# Golden Light

## Name

Golden Light

## Overview

*Golden Light* stages two cyan-legged stands in a dark room: a black lamp casting a hard red triangular beam across a peach mug on the farther table.

## Description

Slate background, glowing cyan legs, opaque red beam cutting the mug's upper half. No people—only method.

Asset reference: `assets/golden-light.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A still life of examination: light as tool, object as question.

## Lore Connection

Named for the **golden** reputation of successful reads, even when the working beam is another color. Operator and House workshops use directed frequency light to test whether a cup, shard, or scrap will answer. The mug stands for any ordinary object that might still carry echo.

## Notes

Does not invent a unique named lamp relic; documents the practice visually.
