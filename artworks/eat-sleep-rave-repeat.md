# Eat Sleep Rave Repeat

## Name

Eat Sleep Rave Repeat

## Overview

*Eat Sleep Rave Repeat* is a Coral Strand cycle plate: four overlapping colored body-states share a pale yellow disc—the shore diagram for eating, resting, dancing, and returning without letting any one state own the day.

## Description

Neutral gray field. Large pale-yellow circle. Over it: magenta-pink silhouette in wide lunge facing right, left arm pointing out, right arm up; behind, deep-orange seated or crouching forward-facing form; bright yellow semi-transparent horizontal floating body across the middle; cobalt blue extremities—hand up with the pink, foot at lower left. Featureless, diagrammatic, instructional. Small black crown-like icon top-right.

Asset reference: `assets/eat-sleep-rave-repeat.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A strand motion plate: keep the four states in honest rotation so cheer does not starve rest.

## Lore Connection

On Coral Strand, **Eat Sleep Rave Repeat** is a Dias cycle-name for shore rhythm—not an Earth meme slogan claimed as song. Pink is motion, orange is centering sit, yellow is horizontal rest, blue is the fourth presence that keeps the loop from closing selfishly. Wonder is repeating without grinding. Forced all-rave is bad craft.
