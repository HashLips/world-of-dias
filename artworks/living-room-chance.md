# Living Room Chance

## Name

Living Room Chance

## Overview

*Living Room Chance* stages an empty Averra room: cream field, five chairs with spindly dark-brown curved legs and bright orange-red cushions topped with twin horn-posts, the fourth from left towering larger, small square teal-framed window with four cyan panes upper-left, long elegant grey shadows sweeping bottom-left.

## Description

No sitters—chance is which chair you take when you enter. The tall seat reads as guest weight or host mark, not throne theology. Quiet expectancy fills the cream air.

Asset reference: `assets/living-room-chance.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A seating manners plate: choose knowing the shadows already lean.

## Lore Connection

Averra damp studios and isle rooms use Living Room Chance when guests arrive to an uneven row. Local rule: do not claim the tall chair unless named; leave it for the one the host indicates. Resonance prefers shadows that match the row you actually sat. Sliding chairs to erase the tall one empties the chance into false equality.
