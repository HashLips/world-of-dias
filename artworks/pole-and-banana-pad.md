# Pole and Banana Pad

## Name

Pole and Banana Pad

## Overview

*Pole and Banana Pad* is a digital image study of The Reedpol as ordinary Wabet river logistics: pole-skiff, crates, banana leaf pad—no ferry myth.

## Description

A weathered wooden skiff sits in calm river water, empty of people. A long pole is lashed along the side, lower end wet. Inside: wooden crates of small round green fruit and a large green banana leaf laid flat as a pad across the ribs. Reeded banks and broad-leaf trees; soft distant hills.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/the-reedpol.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of The Reedpol as real water craft beside the Eastbound Fruit Road—buoyancy only, short hops, ordinary fruit loads.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens The Reedpol without inventing engines, lift, frequency crossings, or a named dog. Water remains a way around a rude bend, not around the world's tolls.

## Notes

River pole-skiff with crates; empty of people. Banana pad is leaf padding, not a cultivar claim.
