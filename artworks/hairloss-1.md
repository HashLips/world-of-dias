# Hairloss

## Name

Hairloss

## Overview

*Hairloss* is an F840 transition plate: a solemn face and tall hat split cream-olive against olive-orange—the frequency portrait of shedding a cover without pretending the two sides never shared one head.

## Description

Vertical split throughout. Face: left cream/light grey, right angular olive-green block on cheek and forehead; large dark almond eyes, heavy black lids; minimal black nose line with reddish-orange at base; small dark reddish-brown lips, neutral/downturned. Tall cylindrical hat: right burnt orange/terracotta, left olive with dark vertical dash pattern like rain or grain. Light grey V-neck garment over dark navy underlayer. Background: left olive with same dash pattern, right flat burnt orange. Contemplative duality. Cubist-flat linework. Small white script top-right.
