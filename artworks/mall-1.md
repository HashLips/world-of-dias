# Mall

## Name

Mall

## Overview

*Mall* is an Elder Shelf grey hall: seven black genderless silhouettes in a horizontal mid-row—looking left, hunched, looking right, arms raised high, reaching right, and a close leaning pair—above a broad pale diagonal reflective band that soft-mirrors them, three tall empty light-grey rectangles on a darker grey wall.

## Description

No goods, no stalls—mall here means the shared open where many moods share one polish. Reflections insist every pose leaves a floor echo. Tiny gate mark top-left. Quiet observational mood.
