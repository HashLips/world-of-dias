# Figs and Strokes That Are Not Letters

## Name

Figs and Strokes That Are Not Letters

## Overview

*Figs and Strokes That Are Not Letters* is Span Toll Shade-Fig. The trees are small. Chalk strokes beside them are tallies. The strokes are not letters.

## Description

Small fig trees stand at Span Toll, ordinary flora for snack and shade. Beside them, chalk strokes tally. The strokes are not letters. They do not spell a toll, a name, or a rule. They are tallies, the chalk marks the entry already places by these figs, and the plate refuses to promote them into writing.

If a post stands among the trees, it is wood in the shade. It is not a cross and not a gallows. Nothing in the entry makes the toll into an execution, and this plate does not add that reading. The figs stay small. The use stays snack and shade. The kind is ordinary.

No one is eating the snack for the portrait. No sentence is chalked for a traveler to obey. Shade, small figs, and strokes that are not letters.
