# Stilt-Horn Stack

## Name

Stilt-Horn Stack

## Overview

*Stilt-Horn Stack* is a Driftfall feast-warning plate: a tall layered stack on red holds a deep-blue horned creature in its middle on stilt legs—the guest who got invited into the meal the wrong way.

## Description

Solid red field with a darker red organic shape behind. Centered: a multi-tier stack—cream seeded dome top, ruffled bright green, thin deep-red band, pale-yellow slab, angled bright-yellow square, dark brown thick disc, more green and red, cream base. Wedged horizontally in the middle is a deep-blue silhouette with pointed ears or horns and a sharp snout; two extremely long spindly stilt legs drop past the lower layers to the bottom edge, ending in small feet. Flat saturated color, surreal and slightly uneasy. Tiny white square seal bottom-right.

Asset reference: `assets/buger-meat.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A pier-feast manners plate: do not build a stack so tall it traps a living guest inside the course.

## Lore Connection

In Driftfall, **Stilt-Horn Stack** names a known bad feast: layers piled for show until a stilt-horn neighbor is compressed into the middle. The blue figure is a non-human guest-shape, not a garnish. Long stilts mean it still tries to stand in the room even while stuck. Pier hosts use the plate to teach: announce every layer, leave a middle gap for breath, never serve a living presence as spectacle. Wonder is hospitality that can still walk away. The file name keeps an older joke; the display name is the honest one.
