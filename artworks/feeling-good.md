# Feeling Good

## Name

Feeling Good

## Overview

*Feeling Good* is a Nauw Outer Rim Seas trial plate: a dark figure with white eyes stands ribbon-bound while a serpentine black presence looms—the rim lesson that a good feeling still needs tethers, a yellow path, and a remembered sword-mark.

## Description

Vibrant teal sky; soft blue mountain band; deep and lighter blue water-expanse with upward light rays. Center: solid dark silhouette from the torso up, two small sharp white eyes, thick flowing white ribbons wrapping chest and arms outward. Above: large dark serpentine arc—left blunt snake-head with one glowing white eye, right spindly three-fingered claw near the head. Lower left: winding golden-yellow path on the blue. Lower right: dark jagged islet with a black sword (or cross-form) thrust in. Mythic tension, clean silhouettes.

Asset reference: `assets/feeling-good.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A rim inhabitant plate: feeling good is allowed; unbound spectacle is not.

## Lore Connection

On the Nauw Outer Rim Seas, **Feeling Good** names a non-human (or more-than-mood) presence that arrives when cheer runs hot. White ribbons are resonance tethers of local care—hold the good feeling so it does not thrash the pier. Serpent-claw watcher is the sea’s attention. Yellow path is the way back to ordinary work. Sworded mound is a finished quarrel left marked, not forgotten. Wonder is warm vigilance. Dias rim name, not an Earth song claim.
