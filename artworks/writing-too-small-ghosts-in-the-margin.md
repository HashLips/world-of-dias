# Writing Too Small, Ghosts in the Margin

## Name

Writing Too Small, Ghosts in the Margin

## Overview

*Writing Too Small, Ghosts in the Margin* is a Span Toll Book. Grease-thumbed. Ruled columns. The writing is too small to read. Chalk sits in the margins. Ghost impressions of fake stamps are in the paper. No stamp is designed.

## Description

Grease-thumbed binding. Columns for route, payer, rest-hours owed, and notes of almost enough. The columns are ruled. The writing in them is the small hand the entry gives the book, and it is too small to read. The plate does not enlarge a line into a name, a route you can follow, or a sentence.

Chalk weather is in the margins. Chalk, not a caption. Fake stamps leave ghosts the paper remembers poorly and marshals remember well. The ghosts are impressions. They are not a stamp you can copy. The plate does not design a stamp, does not give it a device, and does not make the ghost into a readable seal.

Nessa-style hands write small. The hands are not shown. The book is. It is a register, not a novel.
