# Feeding Them

## Name

Feeding Them

## Overview

*Feeding Them* is a Velorian Basin care plate: a pale kneeling figure with orange-red legs pours golden seeds to four red-crested birds—the basin habit of feeding smaller neighbors without turning generosity into a crowd.

## Description

Deep navy two-tone field. Tall slender figure kneels on one knee, leaning forward: ethereal pale-blue torso, vibrant orange-red legs, abstract head with a single thin slanted red eye, long cyan ribbons curling up from the head, small teal rectangle floating at the forehead. Right hand outstretched pouring dozens of glowing golden-yellow seeds. Four small birds—light blue bodies, charcoal wings, bright red crests, twig legs—peck on light-grey oval patches. Quiet symbiotic care. Tiny white crown-like icon top-right.

Asset reference: `assets/feeding-them.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A basin artifact plate: feed the small ones; do not photograph the pour as status.

## Lore Connection

In the Velorian Basin, **Feeding Them** is kept guest craft. Golden seeds are ordinary share-grain in the plate’s telling. Red-crested birds are non-human neighbors. Cyan ribbons mark willingness. Wonder is quiet sustenance. Strip-feeding a place bare for spectacle is bad craft.
