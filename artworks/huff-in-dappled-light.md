# Huff in Dappled Light

## Name

Huff in Dappled Light

## Overview

*Huff in Dappled Light* is a digital image study of Canopy Soft-Bears as quiet walkers' first hello.

## Description

A small moss-brown bear—shoulder-high to a seated child—climbs or rests in a fork-nest of dappled canopy wood. Rounded ears; fur catches leaf-dust like velvet. Soft huff presence, not challenge. No traveler face shown.
