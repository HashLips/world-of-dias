# Tart Fruit, Peels on the Road

## Name

Tart Fruit, Peels on the Road

## Overview

*Tart Fruit, Peels on the Road* is Tollberm Scrub-Apple. The trees are tart scrub-apple. Peels lie on the road as offering. It is not a staged rite.

## Description

Tart scrub-apple trees stand in the Brindle March, ordinary road fruit. Peels are on the road. The entry calls that offering, and peels for foxes. The plate shows the peels on the road and does not stage a rite around them. There is no altar, no speaker, no arranged circle making the offering into theater. The peels are where the road already is.

The fruit is tart. That is the taste the entry fixes, not a sweetness dressed up for a guest. Local use is road fruit and offering. The kind is ordinary. A harvest clip is not the point of the woody presence. The trees are the road's trees. The peels are the offering left in the ordinary way.

Foxes are who the peels are for, in the note. The plate does not pose them. It does not turn their share into a ceremony. Tart trees. Peels on the road. Not a staged rite.

Tollberm Scrub-Apple is flora of the Brindle March, noticed as road fruit and offering. The description is short on purpose. Peels for foxes, tart, ordinary. This plate stays inside that. It does not invent a festival of the peel or a rite with roles. Flora of Dias indexes it as ordinary.
