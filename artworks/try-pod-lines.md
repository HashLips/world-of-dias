# Try Pod Lines

## Name

Try Pod Lines

## Overview

*Try Pod Lines* is a viewer and a very large instrument. On the right, a bright cyan figure crouches, knees bent, one eye pressed to the narrow end of a long gray telescope and both arms reaching to steady it. The telescope stretches across most of the plate, widening to a cream-lit lens at the left. It sits on a tall white tripod. From the tripod's three feet, taut yellow lines run off to the left edge and up out of the frame.

## Description

The wall is a flat burnt orange, the floor a dark chocolate brown, and the two meet in a slightly tilted horizon. The telescope is drawn in two grays—a darker barrel near the lens, a lighter tube toward the eyepiece—and the lens itself is a cream-white oval that seems to glow.

The cyan figure is the only cool, bright color. Its body is lean and bent, one knee forward, weight low, posture eager and careful at once. Its long shadow falls dark teal behind its feet.

The yellow lines are the plate's odd detail. Two run from the floor up toward the upper left corner, crossing each other. Others run along the floor to the tripod's feet. They look like guy-lines or sight cords, staking the tripod to fixed points out of frame so it cannot drift while the viewer looks.

## Real-World Role

A charting plate about tying an instrument to fixed marks so a brief reveal is recorded honestly.

## Lore Connection

Third Window Shelf is the highest of the Glassfold Ledge's three window shelves, where under-structure becomes briefly visible on timed intervals. F610's gift there is a minute of true layout; its discipline is leaving when the minute ends. Try Pod Lines are the yellow cords Veil Scribes tie from a scope's tripod feet to fixed pins in the shelf, so the instrument cannot drift while someone looks through it. The plate shows a viewer crouched at the eyepiece with the lines taut. A sighting taken with slack lines is not stamped. When the window closes, the viewer straightens and walks away, lines still tied for the next.
