# Measure-Slit of the Caliburn

## Name

Measure-Slit of the Caliburn

## Overview

*Measure-Slit of the Caliburn* is a digital image study of Veloria's named Echoform in a present, human-adjacent form, drawn like a figure on a calibration sheet.

## Description

The figure stands centered and frontal, arms hanging slightly out from the body. It is tall and thin, a biped suggestion more than an anatomy, with a semi-translucent body of pale instrument glass that catches the light in silver highlights. A smooth ovoid head has no eyes and no mouth; instead a single vertical slit runs down its face, lined with fine tick marks.

Faint graduation lines and horizontal ticks run down the chest and belly like a ruler set inside the body. The hands end in long, many-jointed fingers that read as slightly too many when you look closely.

To either side, the background is a pale sketch of laboratory glass: spheres, flasks, tubes, coils, and hanging pendulum-like instruments, drawn as linework rather than solid objects. The figure is the only rendered thing in the frame. The decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/the-caliburn.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of the Caliburn as a measurement-drawn Echoform: instrument-glass body, measure-slit head, and a presence that sits between figure and graph.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the Caliburn's established sculpt brief (human-adjacent scale, tall-thin biped suggestion, smooth ovoid head with a vertical measure-slit and no mouth, semi-translucent instrument-glass body with internal tick marks, extra finger-lines). The glassware sketch echoes its material affinity for instrument glass and its first nest near the Spectrum Frame.

## Notes

The plate's neutral silver tone does not fix a mood; canon colors (amber when helpful, cyan when lying, bruise-violet when escaping) still apply. The background apparatus is sketch texture, not a depiction of the Spectrum Frame or the Veld Workshop Alcove. No new abilities, and nothing here answers Frequency Zero.
