# X Plan

## Name

X Plan

## Overview

*X Plan* sets a teal faced bust under a forest green hat with a yellow flourish, red lips and ear accents on peach ground.

## Description

Head and shoulders, gaze down right. Aqua and teal face planes, terracotta eye rims, bright red lips and cheek streak, white highlight patch. Dark green hat and garment, jagged pale collar, yellow curve on the hat. Flat modernist color. Small mark top left.
