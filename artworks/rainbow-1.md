# Rainbow

## Name

Rainbow

## Overview

*Rainbow* is the F432 plate for the splitting arc. Gray sky. Dark hills. Four-color rise—red, blue, yellow, green—breaking into wavy ribbons. Pale river. Faint ground echo bands.

## Description

Uniform medium-gray sky with a small black square glyph upper left. From behind the leftmost of a jagged charcoal and deep purple-gray hill line, a tight four-color band emerges—red over blue over yellow over green—then sweeps up-right as the bands separate into distinct wavy ribbons like pigment banners in a current.

Mid hills stay cold and shadowed. A pale mint-green or icy-blue river winds from midground toward bottom right in one flat water color. At bottom center, four faint semi-transparent vertical bands of muted red, blue, yellow, and green mark the pale ground—sky colors echoing down. No walkers celebrate. The plate is the break of unity into ribbons over a quiet dark land.

A phenomenon plate for F432. Rainbow is the named splitting of the four-color arc.

## Lore Connection

Rainbow is how F432 names the hour when the four-color rise leaves the dark hills and the bands unbraid into wavy ribbons while faint matching marks appear on the pale ground by the mint river. Local care says you do not chase the ribbons to bottle them; you notice the ground echo and keep walking with wonder. Resonance is sky braid to ground bands; memory is the hill silhouette; wonder is gray sky holding such loud color. The plate invents no treasure map. It keeps the weather as manners for shared light that is allowed to split.
