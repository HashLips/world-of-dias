# Kite

## Name

Kite

## Overview

*Kite* is Rise tension on white air: lean black handler on pale grey ground, white eye-slit up, right arm thrust high, five vivid yellow-orange lines fanning to a larger bright-red humanoid floating horizontal—tethers at head, hands, and feet—long grey shadows left.

## Description

The red body reads buoyant or limp-held, not fallen. Lines converge in the black hand like kite strings that happen to hold a person-shape. Strain is visible in the lean. Tiny black glyph top-left. No hills, no wind marks—only the physics of hold.

Asset reference: `assets/kite-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A load plate: flying weight needs five honest lines and one tired arm that still counts them.

## Lore Connection

Ashcalm Rise crews use Kite talk for any buoyant load—cloth, resonant blank, or person-shaped float—that must stay lined. Local rule: five tethers minimum for a body-sized kite; drop one and land. Resonance sticks to equal tension. Showing off with three lines is how Rise stories explain bent wrists.
