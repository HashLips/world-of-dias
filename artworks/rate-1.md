# Rate

## Name

Rate

## Overview

*Rate* is the Mercer Shed plate for the nine-flame count. Dusty pink cylinder. Crimson dripping top. Nine yellow candle-bodies. Lime halo. Teal field.

## Description

Deep teal background; behind the form a large bright lime-green semicircle rises like a secondary sun or backboard. The central craft is a tall two-layer cylinder: dusty pink sides crossed by a thin pale-pink band; saturated crimson top with large rounded icing-like drips spilling down. It sits on a solid white circular base and casts a sharp angular dark-olive shadow lower left.

Stuck into the crimson surface: nine identical yellow tapering humanoid candles without arms or legs—simplified doll bodies—each with two eye-dots, a tiny mouth line, a thin black wick, and a small almond orange-yellow flame. Soft orange-red shadows of the figures fall across the red top. Small black glyph upper left. No party text. The plate is a ceremonial count of nine flames on one shared body.

## Real-World Role

An inhabitant plate for Mercer Shed. Rate is the nine-flame presence counted on one pink craft-body.

## Lore Connection

Rate is the shed's name for the hour when nine yellow candle-figures burn together on the crimson drip-top and the lime halo watches. Local care says you count the flames honestly; you do not snuff one to rearrange rank. Resonance is nine small fires on one cylinder; memory is the eye-dot faces; wonder is teal holding lime and pink. The plate invents no birthday industry. It keeps the rate as a living count you tend with shed manners.
