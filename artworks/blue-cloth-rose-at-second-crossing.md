# Blue Cloth Rose at Second Crossing

## Name

Blue Cloth Rose at Second Crossing

## Overview

Blue Cloth Rose at Second Crossing is a digital image study of the Sorel seam where an empty yard can hold both argument and rhythm.

## Description

Two roads meet between two posts. Chalk marks a surface without forming a readable word, while a plain cloth and one blue cloth rose keep their color against the empty yard. The posts carry no readable letter or rune. The scene is quiet enough to let the rose remain a gesture rather than a solved message.

Asset reference: `assets/second-crossing-yard.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Second Crossing Yard as a place where defiance can breathe into beauty.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the yard’s embodied joy without deciding whether the rose is a vow, a trophy, or the beginning of another argument.
