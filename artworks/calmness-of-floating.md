# Calmness of Floating

## Name

Calmness of Floating

## Overview

*Calmness of Floating* shows a featureless-faced figure in a symmetrical floating pose—palms joined above, ankles crossed below—against a field of horizontal blue streaks like held ripples.

## Description

Peach body, orange-red face covering, red diamond marks at elbows and knees. The background is periwinkle crossed by uneven dark-blue bands that read as frequency weather more than ordinary sky.

Asset reference: `assets/calmness-of-floating.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A body-as-instrument study: stillness as a way of meeting resonance.

## Lore Connection

In Veloria talk, the pose is a **ground answer** to the Sky Drifters—humans trying to match calm float without leaving the basin floor. The face covering is said to reduce sight-noise so the streaks can be felt. Not proof of Drifter nature; a practice born from living under them.

## Notes

Pairs with *Sun-Sweet and Sky* emotionally: wonder above, discipline below.
