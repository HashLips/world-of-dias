# Hello

## Name

Hello

## Overview

*Hello* is a grey room greeting: a featureless dark figure seated on a tan rectangular block, fingertips on a tall black-framed mirror, while the reflection answers with one thin white eye-slit, and a small framed blue-handled brush hangs on the wall.

## Description

Walls are dark grey; floor is light grey. The sittee faces the mirror from a warm tan block that reads as bench or low bed. One arm extends; fingertips meet glass shown by a faint light-grey diagonal streak. The reflection matches the pose but carries a white eye-slit looking back—uncanny difference, not perfect twin. To the right of the mirror, a square black frame holds a paintbrush: blue handle, white bristles. A tiny white gate-like mark sits top-right; a faint signature mark sits bottom-left. No smoke racks, no tide path—only the quiet check before you speak to yourself.

## Real-World Role

A manners plate for Cinder Nook indoor care: greet the reflection before you claim the day’s work.

## Lore Connection

Cinder Nook shelters wind and keeps smoke-reading close. Hello is the smaller indoor cousin of that patience: before you leave the nook for high-tide work, you touch the mirror and wait for the eye-slit that means you are present enough to be spoken to. Local rule: if the reflection’s eye does not meet you, do not force a cheerful greeting into the room—sit longer on the tan block. The framed brush reminds makers that craft watches the greeting; a brush left crooked after a rushed hello is a small unread smoke.
