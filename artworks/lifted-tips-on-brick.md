# Lifted Tips on Brick

## Name

Lifted Tips on Brick

## Overview

*Lifted Tips on Brick* is Static Moss on masonry. The mats are thin. They are on brick. The tips are lifted and stiff. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

Thin mats of moss lie on brick. At rest the account calls the color ordinary and gray-green. This plate is not the resting portrait alone. The tips are lifted and stiff, the bristling Row hands call static weather, as if the wall were listening. The lift is the tell. The mats stay thin. They have not been drawn as a crop, a cushion, or a garden dragged onto the wall. They follow the brick.

The moss is on brick, which is one of its places, along with conduit housings and the cool outside faces of workshop glass. The plate commits to brick. It does not graph a seam, name a band, or draw a pipe's route. The tips do not spell a measurement. Stiff and lifted is as precise as the picture gets, which is as precise as the moss is allowed to be. It is a weather tell for something still humming, not an instrument.

No one is scraping it off glass. No one is filing it as noise or as proof. The picture is thin mats, brick, and tips that have stood up.
