# Dreams Never Die

## Name

Dreams Never Die

## Overview

*Dreams Never Die* is an F432 tending plate: two dark figures sit back-to-back on a gold-legged cream platform and touch a central pink living growth—the frequency rule that a dream kept by two careful hands outlasts a dream owned by one.

## Description

Medium grey upper field; dark navy lower with angular shadows. A pale cream cloth drapes a frame on thin golden-yellow legs. Two identical black curvy silhouettes sit back-to-back, each with a small white eye-dot, white arms reaching inward to a thick fleshy pink mass with jagged salmon branches up and thin pointed roots hanging through the drape. Light-grey rectangular pillow at far left. Dark maroon elongated overhang above the branches. Clinical-sacred quiet. Symmetry as care.

A frequency care plate: tend a living dream-form in pairs so it cannot be privatized to death.
