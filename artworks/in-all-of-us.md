# In All of Us

## Name

In All of Us

## Overview

*In All of Us* shows a giant black silhouette and a small one sharing a mint-green balloon string under a huge yellow-and-blue eye.

## Description

Gray field, dark ground band, long shadows. The large figure lunges with arms up holding the balloon; the small figure steadies the string's end. The eye behind them is blankly blue at center—watchful without a pupil detail.

Asset reference: `assets/in-all-of-us.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

An emblem piece for Dias identity: scale difference without broken connection.

## Lore Connection

Visual of the core principle—**everything resonates; everything leaves an echo**—as relationship. The large form is read as accumulated history or Echo-pressure; the small as a living listener; the string as the thin work of staying linked. The eye insists the world notices both.
