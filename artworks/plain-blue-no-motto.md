# Plain Blue, No Motto

## Name

Plain Blue, No Motto

## Overview

*Plain Blue, No Motto* is an Assent Ribbon Spool still wound. The ribbon is plain blue. No motto is written on it. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

A spool holds ribbon the color of belonging, which in this supply is blue. The ribbon is plain. It is not woven with a sentence, not stamped with a device that can be read as words, and not cut into a message. No motto runs along the cloth. What the eye gets is blue ribbon on a spool, the civic stock before a rite uses it.

The spool is the holder, salt-silvered wood in the account of the object. The plate does not turn the wood into a signed charter or carve a promise into the flange. Any flourish at the edge of the picture is not a motto. The ribbon itself carries none. A loose end, if the wind of the frame suggests one, is still only ribbon. It is not a cut found early, and it is not a story told by a severed length.

No child is being taught the tie. No Watch house is counting spools in the background. No landing is underway. The picture is the supply: plain blue ribbon, spooled, without words.
