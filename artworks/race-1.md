# Notched Pace

## Name

Notched Pace

## Overview

*Notched Pace* is the Hollowmere plate for the measured lean. Orange horse-like head. Purple-framed golden torso. Black forward limb. Five black gauge blocks. White whip tail. Peach field over purple ground.

## Description

Warm peach backdrop; deep purple band as the bottom third ground plane. The creature leans hard left: large bright orange horse-like head with one small black almond eye; thick deep-purple outlines frame a large golden-yellow oval torso; a wavy white whip-like tail streams behind. A thick solid black limb drives diagonally down-forward onto the purple ground; a flesh-toned bent limb shows behind as the other half of the gait.

Far left: five black rectangular blocks stacked vertically in varying lengths—a gauge or notch scale. A long thin horizontal black bar crosses the upper right behind tail and torso. Tiny white gate-like icon top-right. No finish line ribbon. The plate is pace you can notch, not a vanity sprint.

Asset reference: `assets/race-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Hollowmere. Notched Pace is the kept craft of speed under a visible gauge.

## Lore Connection

Notched Pace is Hollowmere's name for the hour when the orange-headed lean runs beside the five black blocks and the horizontal bar, so momentum stays countable. Local care says you do not kick the gauge over to claim you were faster than the notches. Resonance is orange against purple outline; memory is the varying block lengths; wonder is the white whip tail on peach. The plate invents no stadium contest. It keeps the craft as net-loft manners for measured hurry.
