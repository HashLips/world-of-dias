# Kind

## Name

Kind

## Overview

*Kind* seats a dark-brown silhouette mid-mustard ground, white eye-slit down, arms open, with three cornflower-blue lean creatures—sit, stand, watch—while a deep-red trapezoid rises behind and three dark-green abstract floats hang in the grey upper band, cream shadows stretching long.

## Description

Left blue sits gazing at a hand; right blue stands alert; a smaller blue tips behind the right arm. No leashes. The red trapezoid is opaque enough to feel like a soft wall or warm panel, not a threat banner. Green floats read as canopy scraps or memory shapes, not maps. The plate’s kindness is posture: open arms, animals close, shadows shared.

Asset reference: `assets/kind.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A care plate for Hollowmere: kindness as room made with the body for blue companions.

## Lore Connection

Hollowmere nights and net days both need Kind—space where lean blue companions can sit without being shooed from chair-light. Local rule: if your arms close while a blue still looks up, you ended the kindness early. Resonance prefers shared cream shadows. Feeding with one hand and shoving with the other empties the name.
