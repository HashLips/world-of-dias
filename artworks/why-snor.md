# Why Snore

## Name

Why Snore

## Overview

*Why Snore* is a black animal standing in a guest room. Tall, thin-legged, and long-necked, it has two tall pointed ears, a sharp snout, a small pink eye, and a short forked tail like a twig. Behind it hangs a white sheet with one corner lifting. At the right edge runs a thin bright cyan vertical line. Under each of its four narrow hooves lies a small pink patch, like a shadow lit warm.

## Description

The room is two flat planes: a beige wall and a gray-taupe floor that slopes slightly. The sheet hangs from somewhere above the frame, squared at the top and curling at the lower right where it lifts as if in a draft.

The animal is pure black, built from long tapering shapes: a wedge of a head with ears like a split stick, a neck that rises almost straight, a rounded chest and haunch, and four very long, very thin legs. Its forked tail rises at the back like a small branch. Its pink eye is half-closed, sleepy or smug.

The cyan line is a single bright stroke from near the top to the middle of the right side—a cord, a pole, or a sliver of light from a door. The pink shadows under the hooves give the animal a faintly unreal glow, as if it were standing in sunset. It does not look dangerous. It looks like it has been blamed for something and finds that funny.

## Real-World Role

A guest-hall mark that lets sleepers blame the night's snoring on a silhouette instead of each other.

## Lore Connection

Bay-Ribbon Guest Hall is Hallow Bay's clapboard lodging on Calareth: few beds, many rules, and the smell of salt soap on linen. With beds close together, the hall hangs white sheets between sleepers on cyan cords, and on each sheet is painted the snore-buck: a black, long-necked creature with tall ears and a forked tail. Why Snore is the hall's name for the mark and the rule behind it. If anyone snores, nobody accuses anybody; in the morning everyone agrees the snore-buck was in the room. The plate shows the buck before its sheet, pink-footed and unrepentant.
