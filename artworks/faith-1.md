# Faith

## Name

Faith

## Overview

*Faith* is an F840 cost plate: a white kneeling figure reaches for a small white tile while a gray blade passes through—the frequency image for keeping a small true thing even when the keeping hurts.

## Description

Solid black field. Pure white humanoid in left-facing profile, kneeling hunched, elongated limbs and long neck, head down with a single thin black closed-eye line. One long hand touches a small white rectangular tile on the ground ahead. A flat medium-gray sword drives through the back—hilt and guard behind shoulders, point out the chest—simple silhouette. Only black, white, gray. Somber, heraldic, focused on the tile despite the blade. Small white gate-like mark bottom-right.

Asset reference: `assets/faith-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A frequency artifact plate: the small tile is the kept truth; the blade is the cost you do not romanticize.

## Lore Connection

In F840, **Faith** is a Dias local name for stubborn care of a small readable mark—not an Earth religion import. The white tile is the thing still worth touching. The gray through-blade is cost made visible so nobody pretends keeping is free. Wonder is stoic attention. Culture- and religion-agnostic on purpose.

## Notes

White kneeler. Gray through-sword. Small white tile. Black void. No Earth-faith framing.
