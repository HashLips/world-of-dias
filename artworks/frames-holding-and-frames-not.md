# Frames Holding and Frames Not

## Name

Frames Holding and Frames Not

## Overview

*Frames Holding and Frames Not* is the Unfinished Scaffold Mile. Frames and outline walls are along it. Some are holding and some are not. Chalk marks are not readable words.

## Description

Frames stand along a walkable stretch, and outline walls with them. Some of the frames are holding. Some are not. That is the mile's lesson in the entry, frames that hold, then do not, then hold again, shown here as both states at once rather than as a story with a noon drill. The ground is The Unfinished. Becoming is the road. It is not a finished street with a nameplate.

Chalk marks are on the work. They are not readable words. The persistence scale in the account uses tags a teacher could say. This plate does not let you read those tags. The chalk is marks, not a sentence, not a label you could copy into a book from the picture alone. Clean-undo drills and travelers who want ferry geography are not in the frame. Makers who want revision literacy would be home here. None is pictured being at home.

The mile is not a subway and not a ferry. It is unfinished made walkable: frames, outline walls, some holding, some not, chalk you cannot read as words.
