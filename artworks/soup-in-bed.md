# Soup in Bed

## Name

Soup in Bed

## Overview

*Soup in Bed* shows a magenta-purple head resting on a white pillow beside a white bowl of thick yellow broth with pale chunks, a long blue stirring tool laid across the scene.

## Description

Dark brown behind the pillow, grey cover below. Closed or downcast eye-shapes. The bowl is the brightest object—food as device.

Asset reference: `assets/soup-in-bed.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

Intimate image of the Still Pot’s broth as life-maintenance rather than festival feast.

## Lore Connection

Vorrith’s survival rhythm in Stillhollow: rest, stir, taste. For visitors, the same bowl is the threshold of Giftbroth Change.

## Notes

Pairs with *Pot of Greatfullness* as vessel/substance views of the same craft.
