# Overcome Yourself

## Name

Overcome Yourself

## Overview

*Overcome Yourself* is the Velorian Basin plate for the mirror drill. A white figure faces a tall coral-framed mirror. Yellow band on the brow. The reflection is cool gray with slit eyes and a deep-red mitt tied with strings.

## Description

Over-the-shoulder view. The near figure is smooth stark white; a simple solid yellow band wraps the forehead. Upper left, a large angular piece of bright yellow fabric or pennant hangs into frame, twin to the band. The mirror is a tall rectangle in a vibrant orange-coral frame. Inside the glass the figure returns as pale cool gray, face marked by two small dark horizontal eye-slits. One hand—or truncated arm end—wears a rounded deep-red fabric mitt fastened by several thin dangling strings.

Wall outside the mirror is muted blue-gray. Inside the reflection the space is deep black, with a sharp jagged dark-gray silhouette near the top like a distant peak or a torn curtain. A small black square icon with a vertical line sits top-left. No coach yells. The plate is the white back, the gray front, and the red mitt—self as the opponent the basin agrees to meet carefully.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Velorian Basin. Overcome Yourself is the mirror-and-mitt drill craft.

## Lore Connection

Overcome Yourself is the basin's name for the hour when you stand before the coral frame and meet the gray reflection wearing the red stringed mitt. Local care says the yellow headband marks readiness, not vanity; the mitt marks a hand that will not strike bare and wild. Resonance is the gap between white back and gray face; memory is the jagged dark shape inside the black glass; wonder is how the coral frame stays warm around a cold reflection. The plate invents no tournament arc. It keeps the drill as manners toward your own harder outline.
