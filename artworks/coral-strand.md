# Coral Strand Tide Ledge

## Name

Coral Strand Tide Ledge

## Overview

*Coral Strand Tide Ledge* is the southern reef ledge of Coral Strand with nobody in it. Tide pools, shell, shallow water, and rock take the whole plate. Joy is the place, not a portrait. A pale mark on one stone is chalk-like and abstract. It is not readable writing.

## Description

Water runs shallow over a broken reef edge. Pools sit in the rock, clear enough to show the stone under them and loud enough, in the drawing, that the sea beyond is still breaking. Coral stands in the shallows. Shells lie on wet sand. Dune grass holds the upper beach. The light is open and ordinary, a teaching shore rather than a stage.

On a foreground stone there is a pale stroke, the kind of chalk-like mark the Watch is said to leave. The plate does not let that mark become a letter, a word, or a sentence. It stays an abstract trace. Reading it aloud would be inventing a text the image does not contain.

There are no people. No child learning by a scraped knee, no Watcher shortening a play window, no face that could be Coris Vale smiling or not. The plate does not settle Still Months against Tide-Loud Weeks, how long joy is permitted, or whether the island's concession is being kept today. The joy on offer is the ledge itself: shell, pool, and shallow water.

## Real-World Role

A place plate for Calareth's permitted loudness. The strand can be boisterous without becoming a portrait of anyone enjoying it.

## Lore Connection

Coral Strand is where the island teaches reef edges and allows joy that is still watched. This plate keeps the watching out of the frame and keeps the joy in the geography. It does not illustrate a rumor about who smiles here. It does not transcribe a chalk mark into doctrine. Prohibition is elsewhere. This is the concession, shown as water and stone.

## Notes

No people. The mark is abstract, not readable writing. Joy belongs to the place.
