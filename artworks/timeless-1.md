# Timeless

## Name

Timeless

## Overview

*Timeless* is three white figures on a dark slate ground, drawn like a rubbing or a cave mark. On the right, a tall, thin figure stoops forward on long splayed feet, mouth open, and stretches one enormously long arm across the whole top of the plate, its hand hovering over a seated figure at the left. That seated figure holds a round white shape against its lap with long fingers. Between them, floating, a third figure is curled tight on its side, knees drawn up, hands near its face.

## Description

The ground is a single deep green-black. The figures are pure white with no shading, only cut lines for eyes, mouths, and the separations of limbs. Their hands and feet are long and branching, like roots.

The tall figure's arm is the composition's arch. It begins at a narrow shoulder, runs nearly horizontal across two-thirds of the frame, and ends in a hand that hangs down like a roof over the seated figure's head. The tall figure's face is turned up and open, as if calling or singing.

The seated figure looks down, calm, its long fingers wrapped around the round shape—an egg, a belly, a stone, a gourd. The curled figure in the middle has its eyes closed and a small smile. Three positions: arriving, holding, reaching back. The plate reads in a circle with no clear start.

## Real-World Role

A lineage plate showing reaching, holding, and arriving as one continuous loop.

## Lore Connection

Elder Shelf is a clifftop band of wind-scoured trees ringed in gray stone where no building is allowed, and where the Watch issues day passes for meditation and naming ceremonies. Timeless is the plate behind the white-on-slate tablets families carry up for naming days. The image is always the same three figures: one reaching, one holding, one newly curled. Because nothing may be built inside the ring, the tablet is carried in, set against a stone while the name is spoken, and carried out again. Shelf custom says the name is spoken toward the curled figure, so the tablet remembers it.
