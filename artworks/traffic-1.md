# Traffic

## Name

Traffic

## Overview

*Traffic* is a jam in a canyon of unfinished buildings. Tall dark blocks rise at both sides; further back, thinner towers and chimneys stand gray against a pale mint sky, and one heavy slab leans at an angle in the middle distance. Filling the street from the far end to the viewer's feet is a crowd of low, dark, humped shapes, overlapping like scales, each showing a pair of small red slit lights.

## Description

The palette is near-black green, gray-green, mud brown, pale mint sky, and one alarm red. The humped shapes are flat, angular silhouettes, larger at the bottom of the frame and shrinking toward the horizon, so the jam runs back further than the eye can count. Gaps of brown ground show between them like the seams of a broken pavement.

The red lights are short, slanted bars set in pairs on each hump, like tail lamps or narrowed eyes. Up close they are big and bright; far off they become pinpricks.

The buildings are unfinished: blunt tops, one tall stack with nothing attached, the slab tilted as if it stopped halfway through being raised. Nothing moves in the sky. The mood is slow, crowded, and patient—an evening crawl through a place that has never quite been completed.

## Real-World Role

A street plate about moving slowly through half-built space while showing others where you are.

## Lore Connection

Ghost Quarter Foundation Court is the south-central plaza of the Unfinished in F960, where foundations repeat, courtyards refuse to finish, and buildings hold somewhere between forty and ninety percent. The lanes into the court fill at dusk with hand-hauled carts and shelled sledges carrying materials for the next attempt at a wall. Traffic shows that crawl. The quarter's rule is the red slit lamps: every load entering after dark must show two, so carriers can see each other in streets whose edges may not hold. The leaning slab in the plate is a common sight; nobody straightens it, because it persisted that way.
