# Away From Home

## Name

Away From Home

## Overview

*Away From Home* sets a giant black hand above a small white sitter, the two of them sharing coiled red cord-shapes. Folk metaphor for an exile’s call across scale. Longing has infrastructure. The infrastructure is not a ferry, and it is not a shop. Dias readings keep the forms as tether and cord: Gate wind made graphic, a vast attention and a tiny ear, distance that does not collapse just because a line connects them.

## Description

Mint and olive grounds, a pink block like a simplified room, high-contrast red coils linking a vast receiver-shape to a small shape at a solitary ear. The size difference is the feeling. You are not equal to the place you left, and the cord does not fix that. It only proves the leaving still has a tension in it. The white figure is still. Stillness is the niche’s posture, coat flattened, speech not yet performed. Read as an Earth appliance catalog, the picture dies. Read as resonant tether, it belongs on an exile path: assent, not conquest, a knot you could cut and would regret.

## Real-World Role

Orientation art for exile paths. It lets a viewer feel distance without inventing a safe ferry between bands or a device culture Dias does not have. Hang it with the understanding that the cord is cousin to an assent cord, not a product.

## Lore Connection

Niche evenings. A Watcher who will not take your speech. A plain knot. The ache of a second beginning that still faces north in the same gust it walks south. The picture’s mercy is that the small figure is not erased. The cost is that the hand above them is not kind just because it is large.
