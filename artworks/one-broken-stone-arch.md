# One Broken Stone Arch

## Name

One Broken Stone Arch

## Overview

*One Broken Stone Arch* is one remnant of the Prime Bridge. The remnant is stone. It is an arch, broken, and it stands on the ground. There are no crystals. There is no energy frame. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

A single stone arch occupies the frame, broken and still readable as an arch: masonry, curve, and the injury of a structure that did not remain whole. It is grounded. The stone meets earth. It is not hung in the air, not a span departing into another band, and not a gate with a road through it that the picture invites anyone to take. One remnant. Not a set of echoes laid on top of each other until a vanished whole reappears.

No crystalline fragment is suspended in the open air. No partially visible energy framework is drawn around the stone or instead of it. The other ways the Bridge is said to show itself are absent on purpose. This plate is the stone version only, and only one of it. The break is the condition. Completeness is not restored by looking.

The picture does not supply builders, a date, or a use as travel. It does not show an explorer overlaying echoes. It does not show a modern engine, a tunnel, or a ferry. The grounded broken arch is the whole subject.

## Real-World Role

A relic plate limited to one stone manifestation. The arch is a remnant on the ground. It is not a vehicle.

## Lore Connection

The Prime Bridge is a Prime Age structure believed to have served as a reality-stabilizing anchor within the Prime Realm. Despite the name, it is not known to have enabled travel between Frequencies in the modern sense. Most scholarship frames it as a stabilizer or convergence point. When the Prime Realm shattered, the Bridge fractured. In the Present Age it persists as partial echoes, each a different version of the same underlying structure. Observed manifestations include massive broken stone arches, crystalline fragments suspended in open air, and partially visible energy frameworks. This plate chooses the first and refuses the other two: one grounded broken stone arch remnant, no crystals, no energy frame. Explorers propose that if all echoes were overlaid they would reconstruct a single vast structure. The picture does not perform that overlay. It leaves the remnant incomplete. Cultures may take the Bridge as proof that unity is possible again, or as an omen of a wound that never finished closing. The plate depicts neither sermon. Modern civilizations do not use it for inter-frequency travel. Nothing here is a resonance gate, a harmonic engine, or a frequency tunnel. The builders and the consequence of restoration remain unknown. The stone does not answer them. Importance, for this relic, is what it was and what a fragment still is, not usable travel power. The arch is on the ground, broken, and alone.

## Notes

One grounded broken stone arch. No crystals. No energy frame. Not a crossing. No person.
