# Oil and Argument on the Row

## Name

Oil and Argument on the Row

## Overview

*Oil and Argument on the Row* is a digital image study of Calibration Row as a working street, warm light spilling out of open shops into the evening.

## Description

The nearest workshop on the left has its tall plank double doors swung wide, showing a deep interior lit by hanging lamps: benches crowded with instruments, gauges, glass, and tools, a round clock on the back wall, and pipework along the floor and walls. A heavy chain hangs by the door.

Down the street, more workshops of dark timber, sheet metal, and riveted plate stand shoulder to shoulder under pitched roofs and awnings, each with its own lit door or window. Chimneys and vent stacks rise against a dusky sky. The cobbled lane is lit in warm pools where the shops spill light onto it, and the street fades into a hazy line of lamps.

No figures are shown; the shops read as working and occupied without a scene being staged. The decorative border is framing only.
