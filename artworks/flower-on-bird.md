# Flower on Bird

## Name

Flower on Bird

## Overview

*Flower on Bird* is an Aelwyn Canopy domestic flora plate: an orange bird-headed sitter tends red lip-flowers in a yellow vase at a pink table—the quiet hour when bloom rides bird-care and a black cat keeps the floor honest.

## Description

Deep navy field. Tall minimalist light-blue high-back chair. Candy-pink rectangular table. Seated: bright orange humanoid with bird head—sharp downturned blue beak, narrow white eye with blue pupil—one hand on table, one on yellow vase neck. Vase: bright yellow with light-green wavy pattern; thin black spindly branches with small black leaf-shapes and deep red bowl/lip flowers. Foreground: sleek black cat with glowing yellow eyes and curved tail. Small black perch-silhouette at chair top-left. Cast shadow of vase and arm on pink. Domestic, alien, still.

Asset reference: `assets/flower-on-bird.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A canopy flora plate: the flowers live where the bird-sitter tends; cat and perch are witnesses, not décor only.

## Lore Connection

In the Aelwyn Canopy, **Flower on Bird** names red lip-blooms that prefer avian caretakers and pink table light. Wonder is domestic care without Earth pet-brand readings. Neglecting the vase while posing the bird empties the craft.
