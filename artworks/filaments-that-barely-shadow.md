# Filaments That Barely Shadow

## Name

Filaments That Barely Shadow

## Overview

*Filaments That Barely Shadow* is Meridian Filament Grass as path texture. The filaments are gold-white and glassy. They stand in rows. They cast little ordinary shadow. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

Gold-white filaments grow in soft rows, glassy when the light takes them, cool-looking in the way the account describes a brush of the hand. No hand is brushing them. The rows give a direction without a signpost. They read as grass only in the sense that they cover a way underfoot. They are not a soil crop. The ground they occupy is path, not a field turned for harvest.

They cast little ordinary shadow. A plant of leaf and sun would throw a dark copy of itself. These filaments do not. The missing shadow is part of what they are: light-fiber growth, not a bed of ordinary blades. The picture does not invent a hard shadow to make them familiar. It lets the rows sit bright and glassy with almost none of the shade a weed would owe the ground.

No one is kneeling, which the account says visitors should not do. No quiet rib is set among the rows to part them. No speech is being given from on top of them. Spectacle has not burned a dull patch for watchers to read. The picture is rows of gold-white glassy filaments, and little ordinary shadow.

## Real-World Role

A flora plate for path light. The rows are the direction. An ordinary shadow is barely there.

## Lore Connection

Meridian Filament Grass is light-fiber growth that behaves like grass underfoot for Soft-Rays and for visitors who still have feet. It is path texture without soil, a direction you can feel in the procession hall when staring would be rude. Gold-white filaments in soft rows, cool and faintly glassy, and they do not cast an ordinary shadow. This plate holds those facts. Dim patches worry watchers more than weeds, because a weed is a surplus and a dim patch is a thinning of the peace. No dim bruise of the harvesting stare is shown. The sound of distant glass is not available to a picture. The hand-feel of brushed silk is a kneeling report, and kneeling is what visitors should not do. No one kneels. The grass parts for a quiet rib placed with manners and closes when the rib is lifted. No rib is placed. Pressed sheaves in galleries travel under a no-ferry disclaimer. This is not a sheaf and not a cutting for sale. The grass is not fuel, dye, or proof of purity. Walking it lightly is the use. Standing on it to make a speech is how the filaments can decline to hold you in their brightness. There is no speaker. The rows remain, gold-white, glassy, almost without ordinary shadow.
