# Wide Lily on a Grounded Pool

## Name

Wide Lily on a Grounded Pool

## Overview

*Wide Lily on a Grounded Pool* is one lily opened as wide as the day, sitting on a mirror-calm pool with a field horizon behind it. The ground stays under that horizon. This is not a floating island.

## Description

The bloom is centered and fully open, pale petals in tight ranks around a denser heart, the kind of width the lull-lily is said to take when the water has stopped arguing. A few pads lie around it, oval and flat. The pool gives the flower back almost whole: cloud, petal, and pad stacked in reflection, with only the smallest breaks in the glass. Banks of reed and long grass hold both sides. Beyond them the land is continuous: low fields, a scatter of shrubs and trees, and a horizon that meets the sky as farmland meets weather, not as an edge of rock hung in air.

The sky is ordinary cumulus, drawn lightly, bright enough to prove the calm and not staged as a vision. There is no person on the bank, no tube, no watcher, no second flower competing for the omen. Nothing is written on the water. The hush is visual. Reading a sentence out of it would be inventing a text the plate does not contain.

The picture does not settle whether this is omen or only beauty, how long the mirror will last, or which night's lull the fields would claim. It does not show dams, bells, or a road west. It shows one flower at its widest on water that is behaving, in a field that is still on the ground.

## Real-World Role

A flora plate for calm that can be an omen without leaving the earth. Beauty is the open lily; the warning, if any, is the stillness.

## Lore Connection

Glasswater Lull-Lily belongs to Glasswater pools and opens widest on mirror-calm days. Locals take it as beauty and as a calm omen, inside a region whose waters are already known for reflecting the sky like glass. The Mirror Lull is the larger story, about hush stored on still water until work elsewhere softens its pace. This plate keeps that story at the scale of one bloom and one pool. It does not lift the fields into the air, and it does not staff the bank with a night watch. Flora of Dias can index the habit. The image only proves the habit on a day when the surface agreed.
