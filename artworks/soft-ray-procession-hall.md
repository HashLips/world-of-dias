# Filament Aisles in Soft-Ray Procession Hall

## Name

Filament Aisles in Soft-Ray Procession Hall

## Overview

*Filament Aisles in Soft-Ray Procession Hall* is a digital image study of the luminous gathering volume along Aurel Meridian, shown as a frame of brighter quiet inside the meridian's long line.

## Description

The plate looks straight down the hall's long axis. Tall haloed volumes stand in two receding rows, each one a column of pale filament that frays at its base into streaming light, so the columns read as architecture at a glance and as weather when you look longer. Between them the floorplane shines like brushed silk, threaded with fine gold filament lines that act as aisles and converge on a warm white brightness at the far end.

There are no pews, no altar, and no shadows in the ordinary sense. The palette stays in cold silver-blue and soft gold, which keeps the serenity slightly cool at the edges. The empty aisles show the hall between processions: a room arranged for standing distance rather than for crowds of bodies.

The decorative border is a framing device only and is not treated as place lore.

Asset reference: `assets/soft-ray-procession-hall.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Soft-Ray Procession Hall as F200 social architecture made of light: filament rails as aisles, haloed volumes as columns, and a floor that feels like silk without being wet.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the hall's established image (rectangular intensification of meridian light, filament aisles, haloed columns, silk floorplane) without adding new rites. It fits the charted nickname of a "frame inside the long peace" and leaves procession manners, the Quiet Rib, and coherence gating to the region entry.

## Notes

The plate shows the hall empty; that is a framing choice, not a claim that the hall is usually unoccupied. Do not read the bright far end as a door or ferry into another band. No new F200 rules.
