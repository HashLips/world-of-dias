# Inkcaps and a Cheap Black Wash

## Name

Inkcaps and a Cheap Black Wash

## Overview

*Inkcaps and a Cheap Black Wash* is Averra Inkcap Mushroom. The caps are inkcaps. Black spore wash is the cheap ink. No artist is present.

## Description

Inkcaps grow in the damp of Averra studios. Black spore wash is what they give, cheap ink, the use the entry names. The wash is a plant fact, not a painting in progress. No artist stands over the caps to claim the black as a picture. No portrait is being finished with it. The mushrooms are the subject. The ink is their wash.

The range is Averra damp studios. The kind moves from ordinary toward craft because the wash is used, and the local notice is ink and observed. Observed means looked at, not signed. The plate does not supply a hand, a brush speech, or a studio wall of finished work. It supplies inkcaps and the black they make.

Silence laws and gallery custom may apply around Averra. They are not lettered onto the caps. The cheapness is the point. This is not a rare pigment with a provenance speech. It is spore wash.
