# Outlook

## Name

Outlook

## Overview

*Outlook* is the Sorel plate for the path between chorus and lone watch. three dark figures chant with yellow eye-slits. A taller thin figure waits with a white eye. A turquoise path winds toward it under a huge yellow disc.

## Description

The whole field is flat terracotta orange. Top-left, a massive yellow disc is cut by the frame—sun or moon twin to the trio's eyes. Left-foreground, three silhouetted humanoids cluster with elongated heads, mouths wide open as if in shared chant or hard talk; their eyes are bright yellow horizontal slits. Right-back, a fourth dark silhouette stands apart: much taller, needle-thin, tapering, with a single white eye-slit instead of yellow.

A winding light-turquoise path edged in thin yellow runs from lower left behind the trio toward the tall figure's base. From that figure's feet a sharp bright white geometric beam stretches toward the lower left. Near the beam's tip, a small black bird perches on a jagged white object. High contrast, no soft shading—Sorel's outlook as a staged distance, not a casual glance.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Sorel. Outlook is the kept picture of chorus versus lone watch along a turquoise path.

## Lore Connection

Outlook is Sorel's name for the hour when the yellow-eyed three open their mouths and the white-eyed thin one holds the far end of the turquoise path. Local care says the path's yellow edge is the shared rule; rushing the tall figure to make them chant with you is poor manners. Resonance is the match and mismatch of eye colors; memory is the white beam at the lone feet; wonder is the bird on the jagged white scrap. The plate invents no battle. It keeps the outlook as a craft of distance: listen to the chorus, respect the thin watcher's different eye.
