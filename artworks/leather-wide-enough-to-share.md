# Leather Wide Enough to Share

## Name

Leather Wide Enough to Share

## Overview

*Leather Wide Enough to Share* is a Brindle Load-Share Strap off the carts. The strap is wide leather. Beads are on it. Their color is not named. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

A wide leather strap lies where it can be seen as a strap and not as a load already shared. The width is the craft: enough leather to clip weight across a line, not a cord for a parcel. Beads run with the leather. The account calls them resonant beads. This plate shows beads and does not assign them a color. No hue is canon here. Naming a color would be an addition the entry does not make, so the record does not make it either. The beads are beads. That is the whole claim about them.

The strap is not clipped to a cart, an ox, or a mule. No neighboring wagon leans into it. No thank-you tap is being given, because no one has unclipped anything. The leather is the tool at rest. Tooled flourish, if the eye finds pattern in the hide, is not a second text and is not a bead color. The facts are width, leather, and beads.

No driver stands on it. No poorer cart is being dumped on. The picture is the strap that would make a share, unbuckled from every haul.

## Real-World Role

A travel-craft plate for the strap itself. Leather and beads are enough. A color for the beads is not supplied.

## Lore Connection

A Brindle Load-Share Strap lets neighboring carts clip weight across a march line so no single ox or mule takes a Forced-Final haul. It is lived travel craft under the load-sharing rule. The description is wide leather with resonant beads that warm when the share is honest. This plate shows the wide leather and the beads, and it does not name a bead color, because the entry does not. Warmth when the share is honest is a behavior of use. Nothing is shared in the frame, so nothing is shown warming as a verdict. Three thank-you taps are custom after unclipping. There is no unclipping and no tap. Using the strap to dump debt onto a poorer cart voids Brindle goodwill faster than a toll fine. The picture does not stage that abuse. It also does not stage the honest line that leans together. Brindle drivers, Ashen relay teams borrowing march manners, and fruit-road wagons at Second Crossing are the users. They are not here. Reciprocity you can buckle stays in the leather, unbuckled, with beads whose color this record refuses to fix.
