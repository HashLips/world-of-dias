# Red Sky

## Name

Red Sky

## Overview

*Red Sky* is the F432 plate for the coral window hour. White crouched profile. Cyan wall. Black-framed four-pane window full of solid coral-red. Blue stripe down the back. Teal floor, green diagonal.

## Description

Interior of flat color blocks. Primary wall bright cyan. Far left a solid black vertical block like a corner or hard shadow. Right of the figure: a rectangular four-pane window in thick black frame; through it, only vibrant coral-red—no clouds, no blue Earth sky—just red weather pressed to the glass.

The figure is pure flat white, seated or crouching in profile facing right: one dark slit eye, mouth slightly open, one long arm on a raised knee. A vertical vibrant blue stripe runs down the back and into the left arm like a shadow of other weather. Floor is dark teal broken by a sharp grass-green diagonal under the feet. Tiny black gate-like mark top-right. Stillness and waiting toward the red outside.

A phenomenon plate for F432. Red Sky is the named coral fill at the window.

## Lore Connection

Red Sky is how F432 names the hour when the four-pane shows only coral-red and the white crouch watches with a blue back-stripe for contrast. Local care says you do not smash the glass to "fix" the color; you sit with the wonder. Resonance is white against cyan and coral; memory is the black frame grid; wonder is green under teal while the sky refuses ordinary blue. The plate invents no invasion. It keeps the weather as manners for waiting with an open mouth and a quiet eye-slit.
