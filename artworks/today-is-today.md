# Today Is Today

## Name

Today Is Today

## Overview

*Today Is Today* is a golden figure bowing low at the edge of a high path. It crouches along a ledge, one leg stretched forward to the brink, the other knee bent beneath it, head dropped forward with eyes closed. Both arms sweep back and down. From its shoulder a long, pale-yellow pole extends straight back across the top of the frame. In front of its face, a single white slash hangs in the dark gray air.

## Description

The palette is gold, pale butter, rust, and deep gray. The figure is built in two yellows: a richer gold for head, shoulder, and the near limbs, a softer butter for the torso and the far side, as though half of it stands in sunlight. Its shadow spills over the ledge's edge in translucent brown, running down the rust-colored face below.

The ledge is a thin salmon band; beneath it a broad rust wall drops to a row of dark brown rounded arches along the bottom, like the tops of supports or the backs of heads. Seven of them show, with the last cut off at the right.

The pole is long, straight, and slightly tapered, running from the shoulder almost to the right edge. It reads as a staff carried on the back, a marker, or a ray. The white slash in front of the face is sharp and diagonal, like a cut in the air or a breath made visible. The mood is reverent but plain—a bow to a day, not to a person.

## Real-World Role

A canopy-path plate about honoring the route you are allowed today without claiming the next.

## Lore Connection

Aelwyn Path of the Season is whichever elevated canopy route holds priority under the seven-paths rule: the path you may use this season without pretending you own next season. Walkers on it carry a long pale pole on their backs to mark that they are on the season's path. Today Is Today is the plate of the morning bow they make at the path's edge before the first stretch—head down, pole level, eyes shut. The seven arches below are the canopy supports, one for each path, only one of which is in use. Canopy custom says the bow is to the day, not to the path, because the path will change.
