# A Paper Keel and a Careful Flame

## Name

A Paper Keel and a Careful Flame

## Overview

*A Paper Keel and a Careful Flame* is one Festival Sky-Whale Lantern. It is paper and lightwood. The keel is gentle. The flame is careful. It is not a living whale.

## Description

One lantern. Paper and lightwood make the body, a civic light-construct rather than a creature. The shape is a gentle keel of light, the festival form children know is not an animal and still gasp at. A careful flame is the light. It is not a wild burn and not a signal spelling a route. Glow-cell cousins belong to the same family of careful light in the entry. This plate keeps the flame.

It is not a living whale. It is not a Sky Drifter and not an Outer Rim whale. The keel is a made shape, hung or flown in whale-festival seasons so the sky feels briefly companionable. The plate shows the one construct. It does not show a school, a migration, or a body that breathes.

No child is in the gasp. No Softfruit evening is paused around it as a crowd. A runaway lantern toward the core glow, or toward Bleed-Sky, is a chapter hook in the notes, not this frame. Wonder here is the object: paper, lightwood, gentle keel, careful flame.

Children know the difference between the construct and a living whale, and they gasp anyway. The gasp is not pictured. The difference is.
