# Heads

## Name

Heads

## Overview

*Heads* is a red-field chorus: five silhouetted heads and upper torsos packed tight, each mouth open, each eye a white ring with a thin cross-line, and thick white bands weaving between them like casing that will not let the cluster fall apart.

## Description

The figures rise from a shared black mass at the lower left toward the upper right. Profiles and three-quarter faces show hooked noses and rounded skulls. Mouths gape at different angles; red negative space tongues into the open jaws as if sound were the same color as the air. White borders do not merely outline—they snake between skulls, separating and binding at once. Eyes are white circular outlines with a horizontal or cross mark inside, wide and unsettled. No bodies below the merge, no furniture, no shelf wood—only the vocal cluster and the red that holds it.

## Real-World Role

A symbol plate for collective voice on the Elder Shelf: many mouths, one pressure, no quiet vote yet.

## Lore Connection

Elder Shelf manners include knowing when a room has become Heads—when too many open mouths share one black root and white casing. The local rule is not “silence everyone.” It is “count the mouths before you add yours.” Resonance on the Shelf often travels as argument that thinks it is harmony. This plate teaches the difference: white bands can hold a chorus together and still leave every jaw open. If you speak, leave a white gap for the next jaw.
