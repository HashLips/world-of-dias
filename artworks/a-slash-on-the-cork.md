# A Slash on the Cork

## Name

A Slash on the Cork

## Overview

*A Slash on the Cork* is a Blue Litter Warning Cork doing the job of a seal. The cork is blue-stained. A slash marks it. The slash is not a letter. The cork is on a sample jar. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

A cork stained blue sits in the mouth of a sample jar. The blue is the warning color of the stock, not a souvenir dye applied for prettiness. Across the cork is a slash. It is a cut mark, a single refusal, and it is not a letter. No initial, no word, and no alphabet character has been granted to the mark. Reading it as script would be the mistake the plate declines. The slash warns by being a slash.

The jar is a sample jar. It is not a bottle dressed for sale. The cork seals it in the way warning stock seals things that must not become bottles. What is inside the glass is not named by this record and is not turned into a specimen story. The hazard is not illustrated as a treasure. The object on view is the cork, the slash, and the jar it stops.

No warden holds it up. No tourist is corking a jar for a stall. The nose-burn of the stain is a fact of handling, not a line of text on the cork. The picture is blue cork, slash, sample jar.

## Real-World Role

A ban plate for warning stock. The slash keeps the cork from being a label made of letters.
