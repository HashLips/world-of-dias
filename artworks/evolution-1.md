# Evolution

## Name

Evolution

## Overview

*Evolution* is an F500 progression plate: three red figures enlarge into a grounded black fourth while jagged white shadows rake the green—the frequency lesson that change is paced steps under a blue orb, not a sudden erase.

## Description

Salmon-pink upper field; dark forest-green ground. Four humanoids left to right: three vibrant red, increasing size from crouch-emerge to mid-stride lunge, each with a white eye-slit; largest far right a solid dark silhouette in powerful forward lean, also white-slit eyed. Each casts a long jagged brilliant-white shadow forward-left. Upper left: deep blue irregular orb with lighter blue scalloped border. Thick yellow band arcs from right, bends, drops vertically behind the dark figure. Momentum without chaos. Small white mark top-left.

## Real-World Role

A frequency path plate: grow through readable stages; keep the white shadow of what light you already cast.
