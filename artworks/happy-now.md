# Happy Now

## Name

## Overview

*Happy Now* is an F960 care-reversal plate: a large orange fish-form feeds blue flakes to a small black swimmer in a bowl—the frequency joke and rule that happiness now may mean letting the usual caretaker be the one cared for.

Dark stylized bowl shadow on table. Calm, domestic, alien. Flat limited palette.
