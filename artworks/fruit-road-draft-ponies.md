# Rest Berm for Draft-Ponies

## Name

Rest Berm for Draft-Ponies

## Overview

*Rest Berm for Draft-Ponies* is a digital image study of the Eastbound Fruit Road's working ponies hauling orchard carts on a berm stretch.

## Description

Two stocky ponies lead from the left. The front one is a deep chestnut with a pale blaze and shaggy feathered fetlocks; the second is black-and-white piebald with a thick mane. Both have short thick necks, calm eyes, and heavy collar harness studded with brass, hitched in line to two wooden carts piled with round red, green, and yellow fruit.

They walk a dirt road laid along a raised berm edged with fitted stones, curving back into the distance through low orchard trees and scrub. Hills rise behind, with a small roofed pavilion on a far slope.

The scene is steady and unhurried: a pace the ponies have chosen. The fruit-and-tree border is framing only.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Fruit-Road Draft-Ponies as the compact, strong medium haulers of orchard carts on the Eastbound Fruit Road.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the established draft-pony image (compact strong ponies, chestnut and piebald coats, thick necks, calm eyes, orchard-cart hauling, knowing rest berms by smell) without adding new breeding or road rules.
