# I Understand

## Name

I Understand

## Overview

*I Understand* is a quiet Row interior: a faceless white body on a black straight-backed chair, a single red heart-like shape set in the chest, mustard-yellow wall, darker yellow vertical band at left, and pale pink floor with wavy dark shadows under the chair legs.

## Description

The figure’s limbs are simplified, head tilted slightly back as if resting after a long listen. No face features compete with the red mark—roughly triangular or heart-shaped, vivid against white. The chair is solid black, four thin legs, high back. Shadows on the pink floor are soft and wavy, not harsh. The room feels cornered by the darker yellow strip, intimate rather than official. Nothing on the walls; nothing in the hands. Understanding here looks like sitting with a visible center, not like delivering a verdict.

## Lore Connection

Calibration Row keeps careful measurements and careful manners. I Understand is the plate for the moment after a hard reading when someone sits, shows the red center, and stops arguing. Local rule: do not demand more words while the white figure’s chest-mark is still the loudest thing in the room. Resonance prefers a pause that means assent, not a stamp that means dismissal. The black chair is ordinary furniture doing extraordinary patience.
