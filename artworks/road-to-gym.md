# Road to Gym

## Name

Road to Gym

## Overview

*Road to Gym* is the Brindle March plate for the disc-bar lunge. White arched figure. Black eye-slit. Scattered black-disc weights on white bars. Purple diagonal. Orange vertical. Teal field.

## Description

Muted dark teal background. A thick mustard-orange vertical rectangle stands behind the figure. A sharp bright purple diagonal slices top-left to bottom-right across the whole plate. Light gray floor cuts diagonally through the lower half.

Solid white featureless humanoid lunges in profile right: narrow black eye-slit, body low, left leg back, right bent forward, left arm back gripping a black-and-white object like a barbell. Across the gray floor sit several black "plates"—two irregular black circles joined by a tapered white bar—resting or floating slightly. Tiny black gate icon top-right. Discipline without a census gym logo: the road is the lunge among the discs.

Asset reference: `assets/road-to-gym.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A phenomenon plate for Brindle March. Road to Gym is the named training weather of discs and purple rule-line.

## Lore Connection

Road to Gym is the march's name for the hour when the white figure lunges among black-disc bars under the purple slash and the orange upright. Local care says you place and lift with focus; you do not kick discs into the road for show. Resonance is white against teal and purple; memory is the eye-slit; wonder is how orderly scattered weights can look. The plate invents no membership club. It keeps the weather as manners for strength work on the way.
