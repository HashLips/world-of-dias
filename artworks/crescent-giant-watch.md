# Crescent Giant Watch

## Name

Crescent Giant Watch

## Overview

*Crescent Giant Watch* is a sky sighting and its witnesses. At the top of the plate, cradled in a white crescent like a hammock of light, a huge blue figure reclines on its back, knees up, face turned toward the right edge, long green strands of hair streaming out behind. Beneath it, a row of white curved arcs hangs like ribs or claws across a teal sky. Far below, on two rocky outcrops, four small dark silhouettes stand and watch; one crouches forward on a long staff.

## Description

The plate is vertical and layered like distance. The sky is teal at the left and pale aqua at the right, crossed by a dark green ridge-line that runs diagonally from mid-left down to the right edge. Below that, the land is pale mint and seafoam, rising into dark green rocks at the bottom.

The giant is drawn in one flat cyan-blue, relaxed, unbothered, more resting than flying. The crescent behind it is pure white, curving under its whole length. Its hair is a spray of fine green lines, as if blown by wind or floating in water.

The white arcs under it are evenly spaced and sharp at their lower tips, eleven or twelve in a row. They could be cloud ribs, the edge of the crescent's light, or something belonging to the giant. The watchers do not point or run. Three stand straight; the fourth leans on a staff as if settling in for a long look. Their faces are small white slits turned upward.

## Real-World Role

A vigil plate for a rare sky sighting and the people who keep watch from high ground.

## Lore Connection

The Mountain of Majestic Good Trade is a modest inland rise on Driftfall's quieter eastern side, close to the beaches yet far enough from Sprout Vent and the outlaw yards to feel like a different Driftfall. Crescent Giant Watch is what shore people call the vigil kept there when a reclining blue shape is reported in a white crescent over the eastern sky. The plate shows the sighting and the watchers: four figures on rocks, one with a staff. Nobody on the mountain claims to know what the giant is. The custom is only that you watch from the rise, do not shout, and come down before it leaves.
