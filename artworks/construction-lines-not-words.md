## Description

A leaf whose blade is drawn with construction lines, the else-green habit of the F960 Unfinished. The lines are the plant's craft omen. They are not words. They do not spell a plan, a name, or a motto. A builder might recognize the look of layout lines. The plate does not let those lines become a sentence you can read aloud.

The range is the Unfinished. The kind is else-green, growth that leans past ordinary garden behavior and is still described in plant words. Local use is craft omen. An omen is not a caption. The leaf remains a leaf. The lines remain lines.

No hand is inking the blade to finish a drawing the plant already carries. No scaffold is required in the frame to explain it. The subject is the leaf.
