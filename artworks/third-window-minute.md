# Third Window Minute

## Name

Third Window Minute

## Overview

*Third Window Minute* shows the highest Glassfold shelf in the short interval when under-structure is visible. A chime, blank slates, basins that have stopped glittering falsely, and opacity already back at the corners.

## Description

The stone underfoot is cool glass-gray and slick, as if it remembered rain it did not have. Steel-blue foldlines hang in the air like measurements someone almost erased. A Reveal Window Chime is there for the open and the close. The plate is the time between those notes. Below, channels, braces, and shy geometry show in the basins. Slates at the rail are blank. Nobody is holding a weapon. No sentence has been stamped yet.

Opacity creeps at the corners, the ledge putting its coat back on. The minute is a courtesy, not a possession. The plate does not stamp motives onto faces, confirm a serpent in the milk-glass dark, turn the shelf into a puzzle lock, or hold the window open past its spend.

Asset reference: `assets/third-window-shelf.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A scene plate for F610's reveal as a perishable advantage: true layout for a minute, then ordinary opacity.

## Lore Connection

Third Window Shelf is the highest of the three window shelves on the Glassfold Ledge. Structure shows. People do not become honest just because the glass was kind. Asking again is a different act from staring, and this picture is the stare already being ended by the returning dark at the edges. It is not a ferry and not a key to any deeper absence.

## Notes

Blank slates stay blank. Do not extend the minute into a permanent truth machine.
