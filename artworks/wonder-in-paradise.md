# Wonder in Paradise

## Name

Wonder in Paradise

## Overview

*Wonder in Paradise* is a beach watched by the sky. A white sun with jagged, flame-like rays fills the top of the plate, and in its center is a single black almond-shaped eye with a white crescent beneath its pupil, looking down. Below, beyond a low band of purple hills, three small boats with black triangular sails sit on pale cyan water. Their long gray shadows stretch toward the viewer across a cream sand beach.

## Description

The sky is two teals: a darker outer field and a lighter oval halo around the sun. The rays are white, wavy, and uneven, some long and some short, more like flames or petals than beams.

The eye is simple and oddly calm—black almond, a curved white line across its lower half like a smile or a reflection. It is the only face in the plate.

The water is a pale aqua lagoon, flat and bright. The three sailboats are pure dark silhouettes: two-part triangular sails above shallow crescent hulls, one tall at the right, one small in the middle, one medium at the left. Each throws a long diagonal shadow down and to the left across the water and onto the sand, which is drawn in soft cream with a wavy shoreline. The mood is serene and slightly uncanny—a perfect beach day with an eye in the sun.

## Real-World Role

A shore plate for the rare calm days when the sun shows an eye and boats go out under it.

## Lore Connection

Driftfall's quieter eastern side, near the Mountain of Majestic Good Trade and the beaches below The Salt Ledger, feels like a different Driftfall from the vent yards and outlaw camps. On a few still days a year, shore people say, the sun there shows an eye. Wonder in Paradise is their name for those days and for the custom that goes with them: everyone with a boat rigs black sails and goes out into the lagoon, so the eye has something to watch that will not glare back. The plate shows three boats under it. Nobody asks what the eye sees.
