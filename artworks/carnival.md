# Carnival

## Name

Carnival

## Overview

*Carnival* is a Coral Strand weather-of-mood plate: a peaked carousel with five identical blue horse silhouettes turns under a giant smiling yellow sun—the shore hour when rhythm arrives and manners still have to keep the poles honest.

## Description

Light cyan sky; pale lavender ground band. A tent-like canopy peaks to a small orange sphere, panels alternating red-orange, white, royal blue, white, scalloped at the hem. Four thin black poles drop to the platform. Beneath, five identical deep royal-blue horse silhouettes leap left-to-right, legs tucked, heads forward. Upper right: a massive perfect yellow sun with simple black oval eyes and a wide crescent smile. Flat toy-like cheer, dream-clean. Mood: whimsical, rhythmic, watched.

Asset reference: `assets/carnival.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A strand festivity plate: when play-weather lands, keep the ride shared and the sun from becoming a boss.

## Lore Connection

On Coral Strand, **Carnival** names a look of light and mood more than a calendar. The blue horses are the repeating courtesy of the hour—same pace, same direction, room for the next rider. The smiling sun is benevolent only if no one sells its gaze as a decree. Poles must stay thin and visible: no hidden toll inside the canopy. Wonder is communal rhythm without forced cheer. The name is Dias shore-speech.
