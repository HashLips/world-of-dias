# Dream

## Overview

*Dream* is an F432 looking plate: a red profile head with a glowing yellow slit-eye holds watch on a split blue field—the frequency habit of seeing a night-thought without forcing it into day law.

## Description

Upper sky-blue, lower deeper cobalt. A bald simplified head in left-facing profile fills with solid vibrant red—rounded cranium, pointed nose, thin mouth slit, blocky jaw; ear a pinkish curved line. Large almond eye with pale pinkish-white sclera and a bright yellow horizontal slit iris, gaze fixed ahead. Thick uneven pale pinkish-grey outline rings the head. A thin meandering bright yellow line curves through the blue behind. Stoic, watchful, otherworldly. Small white corner mark top-right.
