# The Green Tea

## Name

The Green Tea

## Overview

*The Green Tea* is a table waiting for guests. Dozens of identical white cups, each already filled with pale green tea, stand in loose rows across a long dark-green tabletop. In the middle-left sits one brown wooden bowl holding a single large green fruit, glossy and lumpy, with two yellow bananas draped over its rim. There is no one in the room yet.

## Description

Count the cups and you lose track somewhere past thirty; they run off the right edge. Each casts the same little green shadow. The tea is drawn as a pale olive disc at every rim—poured, not yet sipped.

The room is simple and bright. The back wall is periwinkle blue with a long horizontal strip window divided into cyan panes. A salmon-peach wall angles in on the right, and the floor is the same peach, crossed at the bottom right by a long gray shadow, probably from a door left open. The table stands on thin green legs that look almost too slender for so many cups.

The fruit bowl is the only thing that is not repeated. One fruit, two bananas, one bowl—shared by every cup on the table. The mood is calm abundance and a little comedy: more tea than fruit, and the fruit clearly the reason anyone will come.

## Real-World Role

A hospitality plate about preparing for guests you cannot count and the single shared thing that brings them.

## Lore Connection

Softfruit Table Hall is the long open hall near Veloria's outer-market gates where anyone may sit if they bring fruit to share or pay the ladle fee. The Green Tea shows the hall at its opening hour: cups poured for everyone before anyone arrives, and one bowl waiting for the first fruit. Hall custom says the tea is poured to the number of chairs, not the number expected, so nobody walks in to find themselves uncounted. The first person through the door adds fruit to the bowl or takes a cup and owes the ladle fee. Two bananas over a single green fruit is the hall's private joke about how most days begin.
