# It's Growing

## Name

It's Growing

## Overview

*It's Growing* is an Averra icon portrait: white round face, thin vertical nose-line, heavy-lidded eyes with bright orange irises, pastel-pink and salmon-pink blocky head sections, and a broad upward crescent under the chin layered dark brown, mustard, and bright yellow against cyan air and dark teal shoulder-peaks.

## Description

Near-symmetry holds except the pink split—left pale, right warmer—signaling growth that is not identical on both sides. No mouth on the white disc; the crescent does the speaking as collar, vessel-rim, or ceremonial chin-mark. Jagged teal shapes under the crescent read as shoulders or near hills. A tiny dark square marks the top-right. Stillness is the mood: something enlarging without hurry.

Asset reference: `assets/it-s-growing.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A growth-mark plate: change shown as color split and crescent thickening, not as panic.

## Lore Connection

Averra Isle makers use It's Growing when a craft, apprentice, or damp-studio project is enlarging past last week’s measure. Local rule: note which pink side warmed first, and thicken the yellow edge of the crescent only when the orange eyes still look calm. Resonance prefers growth that keeps the lids heavy—awake, not frantic. Painting a mouth on the white face early is considered forcing speech before the crescent is ready.
