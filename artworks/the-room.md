# The Room

## Name

The Room

## Overview

*The Room* is empty except for three red things. A tall red door stands in the left wall, a small dark knob at its middle. A red cone-shaped lamp hangs on a long red cord from the ceiling. A low red table sits on the right side of the floor, its thin legs throwing long blue shadows. Everything else—walls, ceiling, floor—is blue.

## Description

The blues are layered for depth. The far wall is a deep cobalt, the left wall a slightly brighter blue where light catches it, the floor a clear sky blue. Shadows are darker blue again, long and slanting from the table legs and the door's base, as though light were coming in low from somewhere behind the viewer.

The reds are all the same red. The door is narrow and slightly ajar from the wall, its edge showing a darker strip. The lamp's shade flares like a bell, its bulb a small red sphere. The table is wide and low, more like a bench or a tea table than a desk, with nothing on it.

There are no people, no windows, no chairs. The composition is balanced on a diagonal: door low left, lamp high center, table low right. The room feels prepared rather than abandoned—kept ready for someone who has not come in yet.

## Real-World Role

A room plate that teaches by restraint: three permitted objects, everything else left at the door.

## Lore Connection

Averra Isle is a small island off Nauw's south-west coast whose present life is largely unknown to the mainland. The Room is one of the few interiors that has reached mainland talk, and only as this plate. The account attached to it is a single rule: a meeting room on the isle may hold three red things—a door, a light, and a table—and nothing else that is red. Anything a visitor brings in must be set down on the table or left outside. The plate shows the room ready, nothing on the table, the door already open a crack. It does not say what the room is used for, and mainland copies should not pretend to know.
