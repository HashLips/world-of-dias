# Bottles of Joy

## Name

Bottles of Joy

## Overview

*Bottles of Joy* shows red JOY bottles with person-shaped necks and a yellow pour into a clear glass—Softfruit’s comic image of belonging poured one serving at a time.

## Description

Flat blue field; clustered red bottles labeled JOY; a hand-shaped pour of bright yellow liquid into a outlined glass on pink/gray table planes. Faceless bottle-crowd reads as market line and shared mood at once.
