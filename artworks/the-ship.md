# The Ship

## Name

The Ship

## Overview

*The Ship* comes straight at you out of the night. A towering white bow fills the center, rails drawn as fine lines, two rows of dark portholes running back along each side. A full white moon hangs at the upper left. At the very top right of the bow, a tiny figure stands at the rail and flings a trail of pale fragments up and out into the black, where they scatter off the edge of the frame.

## Description

Everything is white, gray, and black. The hull is a clean white mass that narrows to a sharp keel at the waterline, with one long gray shadow down its right side. Its reflection drops into the water as a darker gray wedge. The sea is near-black, combed with long horizontal white streaks like moonlight on swell.

The tiny figure is easy to miss and the most important thing in the plate. Standing at the starboard rail, it leans out with one arm extended, and from its hand a dotted line of white pieces rises—small at first, then larger, the last fragment a pale chip disappearing at the top edge.

At the lower left, a gray, irregular shape floats on the water: a floe, a raft of weed, a sleeping back. The plate does not say which. The ship is enormous and silent, the moon round and still, the one moving thing a small hand throwing light away.

## Real-World Role

A night-crossing plate about how a huge vessel announces itself gently to whatever shares the water.

## Lore Connection

The Nauw Outer Rim Seas hold the realm's densest whale populations and singing fishing communities, and the Outer Rim keeps distress-listening as a duty. The Ship is the rim's name for the largest hulls that cross those waters at night, and for the habit their crews keep: one hand at the bow rail scatters pale scraps—bread crust, shell chips, salt wafers—so that anything sleeping on the surface hears the patter ahead of the keel. The plate shows the moment exactly: moon, bow, the small scatterer, the gray shape waiting on the water. Rim crews say a big ship that crosses in silence is not being quiet, only careless.
