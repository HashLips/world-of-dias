# The Vamp

## Name

The Vamp

## Overview

*The Vamp* is a grin seen through blinds. A black face fills the frame, crossed by thin horizontal orange lines like slats or strings. A single pale-pink shape forms both eyebrows and the long nose, dropping straight down through the teeth to a rounded tip. The eyes are narrow violet almonds rimmed in pale pink. Below them stretches an enormous smile of square white teeth with two long fangs at the corners, and under that a round lime-green chin sits on a field of red.

## Description

The face is built like a mask, not a portrait. The pink brow-and-nose is one continuous T, as if carved from a single piece. The teeth are evenly spaced, slightly irregular in height, and the two fangs hang lower than the rest, sharp and white.

The green circle is the strangest piece: a bright lime ball beneath the nose tip, reading as chin, fruit, or a mouth-stopper. The red at the bottom swells up from both corners like a cape collar or the edges of a stage curtain.

Over everything, seven or eight thin orange lines run straight across the frame, evenly spaced, cutting through teeth and nose and chin alike. They put the face behind something—blinds, a screen, a slatted fence—so the grin is visible but not reachable. It is more playful than frightening, the kind of scare that wants you to laugh.

## Real-World Role

A festival plate for a playful fright staged at a safe distance behind slats.

## Lore Connection

Flagweek Strip is the middle ground where Hearthvale's restoration ethic and Driftfall's freeband grit face each other, and where some towns change flags weekly without changing breakfast. The Vamp is a Flagweek game mask: black, fanged, violet-eyed, worn behind a slatted orange screen during the noisy week. Children run past the screen to be grinned at. The rule the plate preserves is the slats. The Vamp may grin, growl, and lean close, but only through the screen; if anyone steps around it, the game is over and the mask comes off. Breakfast, as always, stays unchanged.
