# Inverted Scout

## Name

Inverted Scout

## Overview

*Inverted Scout* fragments a bust on royal blue: upper face tipped one way, lower face another, magenta collar and hot pink knot at the base.

## Description

Geometric portrait, flat planes. Top half light blue with a tilted eye, pale yellow and pink accents. Lower half lime and teal with a still dark mouth. Dark band splits the face. Light blue neck column, maroon shirt V, bright pink tie knot. No soft shading.

Asset reference: `assets/inverted-scout.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

Aelwyn plate for the scout ask turned sideways: a face that will not settle into one direction.

## Lore Connection

Named **Inverted Scout**; canopy roads use it when a scout must report two temperatures in one mouth—what they show, and what they withhold while the path is listening. Treat as a mark of split attention, not a census of one beggar or street plea.
