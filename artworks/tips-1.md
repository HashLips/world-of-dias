# Tips

## Name

Tips

## Overview

*Tips* is a talk room after most people have gone. Rows of rounded red-orange chairs on thin blue legs crowd the floor, throwing long gray shadows across a pale sage floor. On one chair near the front lies an enormous yellow banana, curving across the seat and over the edge. At the back left, a single white figure sits half-hidden behind a chair, looking toward the front. On the far wall hangs a framed painting: a light blue field with a yellow wave, a black blot, a dark arc, and three peach dots.

## Description

The chairs dominate. There are nine or ten in view, all identical—tall rounded backs in two reds, a darker seat, four tapering blue legs. They are set in loose rows facing the left, toward a speaker or a wall the plate does not show.

The banana is the absurd center. It is far too big for a snack, nearly as wide as the chair, with a black stem tip and a pale yellow highlight. It rests on the seat exactly where a person would sit.

The room is gray and sage, with one dark wall on the left and an olive wall on the right where the painting hangs. Light enters from the right and lays slanted shadows of chair legs across the floor. The white figure at the back is simple—head, shoulder, one dark eye—and seems patient, or tired, or waiting to see who claims the banana.

## Real-World Role

A gallery-talk plate about the fruit left on a chair as thanks, and the one listener who stayed.

## Lore Connection

The Sketchy Gallery is the known art space in Veloria's third ring, in the technological district just outside the inner core. After artist talks there, it has become the habit to leave a fruit on your chair as a tip for the speaker instead of coins, since coins in that district carry too many questions about where they came from. Tips shows the room after such a talk: rows of empty chairs, one oversized banana left behind, and the last listener still sitting at the back. Gallery custom says the speaker collects the fruit only after the room is empty, so the last listener is a small problem everyone is fond of.
