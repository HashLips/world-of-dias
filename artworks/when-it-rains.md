# When It Rains

## Name

When It Rains

## Overview

*When It Rains* is an umbrella in a flood. A single white umbrella with a small red tip stands upright in the middle of the plate, its scalloped edge echoing the scalloped surface of deep blue water that fills the lower half of the frame. The water has risen almost to the canopy. Only the top of the shaft shows above it. The sky is a soft lavender, and there is no rain falling anywhere.

## Description

The composition is almost entirely two colors. Lavender fills the top half, cobalt blue the bottom. Where they meet, the water's surface is drawn as a row of soft scallops, and the umbrella's lower edge is cut with the same scallops just above it, so canopy and wave rhyme.

The umbrella is pure white, a smooth dome with a little red nub at its peak. Its shaft is a thin white line that disappears into the water a short way below the canopy. No hand holds it. No person stands under it. It seems to have been pushed into the ground and left as the water came up.

That is the oddity: the plate is named for rain and shows none. The sky is calm, pastel, cloudless. The water came from below. The umbrella is not sheltering anyone; it is marking where the ground used to be.

## Real-World Role

A weather plate about water that rises instead of falls, and umbrellas used as gauges rather than shelter.

## Lore Connection

Glasswater Fields is Nauw's eastern heartland of reflective rivers, farms, and windmills, and its watch towns keep listening tubes along waterlines. When It Rains is the Fields' name for a particular weather: the sky goes lavender and calm, no drop falls, and groundwater rises through the fields from below. Farmers plant white umbrellas with red tips at known heights before such evenings, and watchers read the flood by how much canopy still shows. The plate shows the moment the water reaches the scallops. Watch custom says you log the umbrella, not the sky, because the sky will lie.
