# Four Humans at a Work Bench

## Name

Four Humans at a Work Bench

## Overview

*Four Humans at a Work Bench* is a digital image study of ordinary F432 humans at a street kitchen or dock argument, shown as varied neighbors rather than a single uniform type.

## Description

Four humans of varied build and dress stand or lean at a work bench in a street-kitchen or dock setting. Hair, height, body shape, and clothing differ across the group; the plate avoids Earth-ethnic head cloths and other Earth-coded costume shorthand.

The scene stays civic and everyday: tools, shared work space, and the kind of loud or practical talk that marks human presence at F432 docks and kitchens. Background framing is decorative only.

Asset reference: `assets/humans-of-dias.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Humans as a widespread F432 race present in ordinary trade, kitchen, and dock life, with broad variation in appearance and dress.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the established human image from the F432 Major Races Compact and Peoples of Dias. It adds no new institutions, languages, or identity canons—only the visible plurality of ordinary F432 humans at shared work.
