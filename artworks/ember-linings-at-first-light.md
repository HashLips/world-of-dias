# Ember Linings at First Light

## Name

Ember Linings at First Light

## Overview

*Ember Linings at First Light* is a digital image study of Ashcalm Ember-Deer holding morning warmth in coat and ear.

## Description

A soft-brown deer leans to drink from a shallow stone catchment, ember-orange light lining the inside of its ears. The coat holds dawn warmth visually against rocky Rise ground and a hazy gold horizon. Family-scale presence; approachable at distance, not tame.

Decorative border is framing only.
