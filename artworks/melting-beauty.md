# Melting Beauty

## Name

Melting Beauty

## Overview

*Melting Beauty* sets a navy table in a cut landscape: dark chocolate upper field, jagged teal hills, pale mint base; on the table a vessel half structured red, half liquifying teal pouring in thick drops over the edge; three thin yellow stems rise from the mouth into bright many-pointed yellow sparks with white centers.

## Description

Shadow of the vase sits darker blue on the tabletop. Beauty here is unstable—containment failing while sparks still climb. Tiny bowl-in-square mark top-right. Surreal quiet, not panic.

Asset reference: `assets/melting-beauty.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A transformation plate: when form melts, count the sparks still rising.

## Lore Connection

Veloria City makers keep Melting Beauty as the warning that a vessel may leak teal memory-fluid and still throw useful sparks. Local rule: catch drips before they leave the navy table; do not bottle sparks while the red wall is collapsing. Resonance prefers honest melt over painted solid. Ignoring the three sparks to mourn the vase only empties both lessons.
