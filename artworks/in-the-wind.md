# In the Wind

## Name

In the Wind

## Overview

*In the Wind* splits the field peach-left and grey-right: a cobalt face looks down-left with a white-sclera eye and sharp yellow pupil, a thick white nose-line, bright red lips, lilac-pink sleeveless shoulder, and a mass of dark hair streaming horizontally into the grey.

## Description

Hair is a solid dark shape with sharp tapered points crossing the vertical divide—motion without drawn gust lines. The body crops at the upper torso. A tiny black geometric mark sits in the top-right grey. No yard fence, no cookfire—only the face that has stepped into weather. Mood is quiet intensity: still features, moving hair.

Asset reference: `assets/in-the-wind.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A weather-phenomenon plate for Claimscar: when hair and ash-talk both lean the same way, listen before you claim.

## Lore Connection

Claimscar Yard knows secondfire rings and cookfire circles where wind decides who smells the pot first. In the Wind is the face-check before you speak across a yard: if the black hair is already streaming, your words will travel farther than you meant. Local rule: turn your mouth out of the grey side or shorten what you say. Resonance rides the stream. People who shout secrets into a full hair-stream are buying an audience they did not invite.
