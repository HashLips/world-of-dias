# Giving Makes the World Smile

## Name

Giving Makes the World Smile

## Overview

*Giving Makes the World Smile* is a Veloria City gift plate: a smaller figure and a tall slender one share bright blue ribbon strips—the city rule that giving leaves a pale path of light, not a boast-shadow.

## Description

Light grey upper void; mustard-yellow ground with a slight rightward slant. Left: smaller black-silhouette child-scale figure in reddish-orange short tunic and light teal trousers, one leg bent playful, holding a vibrant blue strip. Right: taller pure black graceful silhouette with thin white eye and mouth lines, holding two blue strips dangling toward the smaller. Smaller casts long black shadow lower left; taller casts a pale blue light-path lower right instead of dark shadow. Peaceful transmission mood. Flat graphic.

Asset reference: `assets/giving-makes-the-world-smile.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A city artifact plate: give the blue ribbon; leave a light path; keep both mouths soft.

## Lore Connection

In Veloria City, **Giving Makes the World Smile** is kept gift-craft. Blue strips are share-cloth of courtesy. The pale blue path under the taller giver is the city’s joke and rule: true giving lights rather than darkens. Wonder is soft exchange. Dias city name—not an Earth slogan claim.
