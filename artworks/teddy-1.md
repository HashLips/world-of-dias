# Teddy

## Name

Teddy

## Overview

*Teddy* is a bedside plate. A sleeper lies under an orange blanket striped in brown, shown only as a solid black profile with a closed lavender eye and a pale lavender cap. On the blanket's edge, leaning in toward the sleeper's face, sits a small blue creature with round ears—one lined in green—stubby dangling arms, a dot eye, and a tiny satisfied smile.

## Description

Color does the storytelling. The wall behind is a hot coral red, cut on the left by a dark gray curtain or shadow that curves like a drape. The sleeper's head is pure dark silhouette, the only detail a crescent of lavender where the eyelid closes. The cap is a soft lavender mound. The blanket sweeps up from the bottom of the frame in warm orange with evenly spaced brown stripes, and the creature sits right where the blanket folds over.

The creature is drawn in two blues: a lighter cobalt for the head and ears, a deeper navy for the body and arms, which hang over the blanket edge the way a toy's arms would. But its head is turned with intent, its eye is on the sleeper, and the smile is small and private.

It is impossible to say from the plate whether Teddy is stitched or alive. That ambiguity is the subject.

## Real-World Role

A night-watch plate for a small presence that guards sleep and is only ever half-seen.

## Lore Connection

F610, the Reveal Veil, is the band where concealed structure becomes visible in short windows. Teddy is the name given to a small blue presence that sleepers' families report seeing in those windows: something that sits on the blanket edge, leans toward the sleeper's breath, and in ordinary light looks like nothing more than a soft toy. The plate catches one window. Families do not try to prove what it is. The local rule is to leave a clear spot on the blanket fold and never move the figure while someone sleeps.

## Notes

One sleeper in silhouette with lavender cap; one blue creature on the striped blanket. Red wall, gray drape. Maker mark top right. Silhouette color is graphic shadow, not a description of anyone.
