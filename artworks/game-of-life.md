# Game of Life

## Name

Game of Life

## Overview

*Game of Life* is a Cinder Nook study plate: two black watchers and one bowed figure attend a red tripod bearing a pink circle with a grey leaf—the nook craft of treating a living sample as a game you play carefully, not as a toy you win.

## Description

Pale yellow-beige upper field; white ground lower third. Left: two tall featureless dark silhouettes facing right, long pale yellow shadows left. Center: vibrant red three-legged stool/tripod with triangular top; soft pink circle above holding a detailed grey veined leaf; large irregular ochre/gold mass above; thick dark grey line looping up-right from behind the circle; translucent red-pink vertical bars. Right: third dark silhouette prostrate or kneeling, head bowed, translucent pink bar over lower half. Solemn, clinical-ritual. Small black gate/crown mark top-right.

Asset reference: `assets/game-of-life.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A nook artifact plate: the leaf is the living piece; watchers and bowed care decide whether the game stays kind.

## Lore Connection

In Cinder Nook, **Game of Life** names a kept sample-stand ritual for ash-soft study. Pink circle protects the leaf; red tripod is the altar-of-attention, not of worship creed. Wonder is careful play. Dias nook name—not an Earth cellular-automaton claim, not an Earth board-game claim.

## Notes

Red tripod. Pink circle, grey leaf. Ochre mass, grey loop. Two standing, one bowed. Beige over white.
