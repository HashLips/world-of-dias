# Unlabeled Rings and a Fruit Flourish

## Name

Unlabeled Rings and a Fruit Flourish

## Overview

*Unlabeled Rings and a Fruit Flourish* is a Veloria ring-fair token. It is a small pressed disk. The rings are concentric and unlabeled. A fruit flourish is on it. There is no motto. It is not currency.

## Description

The token is a small pressed disk, palm-sized in the account, the sort issued at outer-ring fairs as a keepsake rather than as pay. Concentric rings recall the city's rings without labeling them. No word sits in the bands to tell you which ring, which year, or which joke. A fruit flourish is the other mark, a fruit-shape with no official meaning required. The flourish is not a motto.

There is no motto. Joke mottos exist in the wider token habit, the ones you had to be there to understand. This disk does not carry one. It is not currency. Ring-fair tokens are not stable money. They are memory coins in the entry's sense: proof a happy hour happened, traded by children, pinned in kitchens. The plate does not show the trade, the kitchen, or a price.

Wheat and loose papers around a disk are not part of this record. The record is the pressed disk, unlabeled concentric rings, and a fruit flourish. No debt. No weapon. Souvenir and belonging, and even the belonging is unlettered.

## Lore Connection

Veloria ring-fair tokens come out at outer-ring fairs, harvest games, and guild-family days in Veloria City. Brass or tin is the usual metal. The obverse might be a simplified map of the concentric city. The reverse might be a fruit, a wheel, a fish, or a smile-shaped flourish. This plate takes the rings unlabeled and the fruit as flourish, and it stops before a readable motto or a year you could spend.

In a city whose inner rings can feel distant and severe, the tokens are a soft boast of a crowd that was only trying to be happy together. Vendors sometimes give them to anyone who tries a game and fails cheerfully. The losers' medal of certain years is a story about designs. It is not lettered onto this disk. Nauw and the Velorian Basin are the wider ground. The disk remains a gift-object, not a coin of account. Nothing around it, paper or grain, is promoted into the token.
