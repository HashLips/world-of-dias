# Gates

## Name

Gates

## Overview

*Gates* shows a small solitary figure before a towering luminous gate-structure—bands of color held between dark door-leaves, a central emblem, and a floor striped like a measured approach.

## Description

The traveler is tiny against the gate: red torso, black lower half, long shadow thrown back by light from the structure. Vertical bars cross horizontal fields of turquoise, white, orange, blue, and magenta. The gate reads as both architecture and instrument—something you enter, and something that measures you.

Asset reference: `assets/gates-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A graphic threshold study in Daniel Eugene Botha's Dias-linked work: arrival reduced to scale, color, and facing forward.

## Lore Connection

Paired with **DEB Gate of Veloria** and Calen's first approach in *The Echo at the Gate*—not as a literal architectural schematic of the DEB silhouette, but as the **feeling** of standing under a frequency-thick threshold that remembers more than the traveler does.

## Notes

Complements *Before I Kneel* (oath strain) with *Gates* (approach awe).
