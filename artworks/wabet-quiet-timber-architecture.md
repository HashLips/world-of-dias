# Fork-Nests in Living Wood

## Name

Fork-Nests in Living Wood

## Overview

*Fork-Nests in Living Wood* is a digital image study of Wabet canopy building, low and quiet, following the tree rather than a grid.

## Description

Three dwellings sit in the forks of great twisting trees. Each is a rounded platform set into the wood itself, roofed with thick thatch of leaf and moss that hangs in long drips from the eaves. None rises above the canopy; the trees remain the tallest thing in the picture.

A narrow railed walkway curves between the nests in an S-shaped loop, bending with the branches instead of cutting straight across. Small round fruit sit in niches along the platforms and branches. Vines and moss soften every edge, and the whole scene is drawn in muted greens and browns.

There are no banners, carvings, or signs of status. The decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/wabet-quiet-timber-architecture.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Wabet Quiet Timber Architecture in its canopy dialect: buildings that do not shout, height earned by trees, and paths that follow the grain.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the canopy practices already set in Wabet Quiet Timber Architecture (fork-nests and walkway loops following branch grain, platforms lashed into living wood, drip-moss and leaf-thatch roofs, fruit-ledge niches along paths). It adds no new building rules.

## Notes

The fruit in the niches is unlabeled and not tied to any species. The scene is canopy dialect only; Verdant Reach stilt houses and Lumira adobe are not shown. Not a map of a specific Aelwyn settlement.
