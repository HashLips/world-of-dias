# Field Guide Ringroad Dog Plate

## Overview

Art brief for a book field-guide plate of Ringroad Dogs—ordinary work life, not fantasy warhound.

## Description

**Subject:** sturdy mixed-breed dog mid-circuit, clay/charcoal coat, white blaze, simple stamped collar tag.  
**Scale cue:** cart wheel or fruit crate beside.  
**Habitat backdrop:** outer-ring stone, stall steam, night lantern—not wilderness.  
**Distinguishing feature:** calm route eyes; nails mid-census click (motion implied).  
**Do not:** spiked collar, glowing eyes, or Strider stilts.

Pairs with Ringroad Dogs entry and Field Guide Pattern of Dias.
