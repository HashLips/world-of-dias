# Glass Mirror

## Name

Glass Mirror

## Overview

*Glass Mirror* shows a line of linked white figures entering a pink translucent vertical barrier; on the far side a cyan figure pushes off, haloed, with yellow rays—one leg still black.

## Description

Dark maroon field. Conformity on the left; bright emergence on the right. The barrier is thin, framed, almost polite—and total.

Asset reference: `assets/glass-mirror.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A transformation diagram in flat color: many become one different.

## Lore Connection

Glassfold readers treat the pink pane as **mirror that keeps you** until you exit changed. The black trailing leg is the unread echo that follows even after attunement. Sometimes compared to Averra veil talk without equating the places.

## Notes

Not a literal map of the Ledge; emotional physics of crossing reflective law.
