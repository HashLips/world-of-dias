# Song of People

## Name

Song of People

## Overview

*Song of People* is the Ashcalm Rise plate for the red walker under gold beams. Black dashed road. Tilted red octagon with white glyphs. Purple and yellow mountains. Cyan clouds. White disc.

## Description

Medium-gray sky; black road with white dashes receding to jagged mountains—deep purple near, vibrant yellow peaks behind. Large white circular disc partly behind yellow mountains upper left. Two elongated cyan-blue wispy clouds. Center road: bright red featureless silhouette facing the horizon. Right of road: red octagonal sign on thin gray pole, tilted horizontal, interior white abstract shapes/glyphs instead of readable text; below on dark gray ground a flat dark octagon echo. Two broad semi-transparent golden-orange bands slash diagonally across scene, overlapping road and figure. Thin jagged gray line zigzags left foreground. Quiet surreal travel under interference light—rules present but tipped.

## Real-World Role

A phenomenon plate for Ashcalm Rise. Song of People is the named road hour of red walker, tipped sign, and gold beams.

## Lore Connection

Song of People is Ashcalm's name for the hour when a red walker takes the black dashed road under golden-orange beams while the octagon tips and its white glyphs refuse ordinary reading. Local care says you still walk with manners; a tipped sign is not permission for chaos. Resonance is red against gray with gold cut; memory is the dark octagon echo on the ground; wonder is yellow peaks and cyan wisps. The plate invents no choir plot. It keeps the weather as a people's road song made of posture and tipped rule-marks.
