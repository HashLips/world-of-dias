# Empty Stones, Leaning Color

## Name

Empty Stones, Leaning Color

## Overview

*Empty Stones, Leaning Color* is the Bloomline Holding Terrace with no one on it. The stones are estuary stones. Confluence mist dampens them. Color leans. No shrine is part of this record.

## Description

Wide stones, the estuary step, empty. Confluence mist damps them, which is how the entry already describes the floor. Color fields sometimes lean. The plate keeps that lean as color, not as a banner with a sentence and not as a shrine. No shrine is part of this record. Feeling weather made into a floor does not require an altar.

Groups sit until a hold ends. Outsiders fidget. Locals treat fidget as data. None of those bodies are on the stones. No Forced-Final speech is being refused in person because no speech is being given. Joy festivals use the terrace too, same craft, warmer stakes. The festival is not underway. The stakes are not staged. The terrace is the damp stone, the mist, and the leaning color.

Who ends a hold when two Tidehearts disagree is an open question in the notes. The plate does not answer it and does not seat the Tidehearts to debate it.
