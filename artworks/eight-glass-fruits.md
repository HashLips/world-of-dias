# Eight Whole Vitrin Fruits

## Name

Eight Whole Vitrin Fruits

## Overview

*Eight Whole Vitrin Fruits* is a digital image study of the Vitrin show in Mercer Shed.

## Description

The plate shows eight whole glass Vitrin fruits resting on a washed cloth in Mercer Shed. Eight chairs stand with the show. Ordinary fruit crates remain at the walls, and lamps light the shed around the cleared middle.

The scene stays with the shed's established show and auction setting. It does not add a glass cloche or dome, readable writing, prices, or a crowd.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of the short Vitrin show inside Dain Mercer's distribution house.

## Lore Connection

The artwork joins Vitrin's eight sold fruits to Mercer Shed's washed cloth, eight chairs, ordinary crates, and lamps without expanding the established show beyond the shed.
