# Black Hole

## Name

Black Hole

## Overview

*Black Hole* splits one scene in two: a hunched figure reaching toward a pale cube from a rigid purple chair, and a sprinting figure racing up a warped chair-back that rises from a dark floor-hole.

## Description

Pink field, purple chairs, black silhouettes with white eye-slits. Left: stillness, downward reach, translucent cube. Right: motion, ascent, chair melting into ramp. A small black void marks the hinge between panels.

Asset reference: `assets/black-hole.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A diptych of stasis and escape used as a Dias emblem of choice under pressure.

## Lore Connection

Read against **Frequency Zero** and stance conflict: one side stays to examine what the cube holds; the other treats the hole as a door and runs the transformed chair. The artwork does not decide which reading of Zero (silence, death, creation, outside) is correct.

## Notes

Cube may be argued as a Resonant Artifact fragment; not named as a specific relic here.
