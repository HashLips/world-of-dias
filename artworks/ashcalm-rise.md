# Grey Shoulder Through the Canopy

## Name

Grey Shoulder Through the Canopy

## Overview

*Grey Shoulder Through the Canopy* is a low grey stone shoulder lifted through jungle, seen from the trees on solid ground. Mist sits in the canopy. The rock has no face. The plate does not open the mountain.

## Description

The ridge is not a spire and not a throne. It is a broad hump of cooled-ash stone, cracked into plates, with trees gripping the ledges and a few crowns breaking the skyline. Below it the forest is dense: broad leaves in the foreground, darker trunks behind them, and a pale dirt path that arrives at the bottom of the frame and then loses itself in undergrowth. That path reads as a game trail, not a road with traffic.

Mist pools in the middle distance, soft between the trunks, so the shoulder seems to rise out of weather rather than out of a cleared stage. The sky is a flat warm grey. Light is even. Nothing on the stone is carved. No profile, no eyes, no mouth worked by shadow into a watcher. No doorway is offered. No stair, no shrine cloth, no marker that would turn the overlook into a queue.

The plate does not settle who walks the path, what the wind does higher up, or why the birds might go quiet. It does not name a hollow, and it does not show one. Fruit is not climbing this rock. The mountain stays a mute break in the green, easy to treat as background, which is how most orchard roads are said to treat it.

## Real-World Role

A place plate for overlooked geography inside the canopy. The shoulder is the subject. What might be under it is not in the picture.

## Lore Connection

Ashcalm Rise breaks the northern canopy of Wabet as stone the color of cooled ash, sparsely used, inside Aelwyn's claim without becoming a seat. Canopy Kin know the ridge. Few make ceremony of it. This plate keeps that neglect visual: jungle, mist, a low shoulder, a path that does not announce itself. It does not illustrate rumors about who might live in the rock, and it does not mark the mountain the way a friendly map would refuse to. The Climb to Ashcalm can still be a walk toward this shape. The image stops at the shape.

## Notes

No face in the stone. No hollow shown. Solid ground, not a revealed interior.
