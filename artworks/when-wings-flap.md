# When Wings Flap

## Name

When Wings Flap

## Overview

*When Wings Flap* is a winged figure holding on to a pole in a wind. At the right edge of a vermilion plate, a white figure grips a tall white pole with one raised arm, body pressed close to it, legs stepping against it. On its head sits a jagged crest of upright points. From its shoulders an immense white wing—or a pair of wings—streams out to the left across the whole plate in long, flame-like feathers, edged here and there with darker red and a thin line of green.

## Description

The ground is a single hot vermilion. Everything else is white, outlined by narrow offsets of deeper red at the top of the wing and green along its lower edge, which gives the feathers a slight sense of depth and motion.

The figure is slender and simple, face in profile with one small dark eye, the crest a ring of sharp triangles. One arm reaches straight up the pole; the body leans out from it; the legs bend and brace. It is not flying. It is hanging on.

The wing is the overwhelming shape: broad at the shoulder, splitting into a fan of long, pointed feathers that taper toward the lower left corner like a flag stretched in a gale or a flame blown sideways. The plate reads equally as a creature in a storm and as a banner snapping on its mast.

## Real-World Role

A market mark for gusty weather, when the wind is strong enough to make a banner fly like a wing.

## Lore Connection

The Velorian Basin is the busy valley and low country that rings Veloria City, one of F432's busiest exchange zones. Basin market masters fly tall white banners painted with this mark: a crested winged figure clinging to its pole. When the wind gusts hard enough that the banner's wing streams out flat, the mark is said to be flapping, and the market's custom takes over—no new deals are struck until the wind drops. The rule exists because Basin traders learned long ago that bargains made in a gale tend to blow apart afterward. The plate shows the mark as it looks at full stretch.

## Notes

One white crested figure clinging to a white pole at right, enormous white wing streaming left with red and green edges, vermilion ground. Maker mark top right. The crest is a market sign, not a mark of rank.
