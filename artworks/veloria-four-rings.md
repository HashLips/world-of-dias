# Four Rings around the Core

## Name

Four Rings around the Core

## Overview

*Four Rings around the Core* is a digital image study of Veloria City's canonical silhouette: four concentric bands around a small, powerful core, seen as one circle.

## Description

The plate looks down at an angle over the whole city. Heavy stone ring walls nest one inside another, each band packed with roofs, towers, lanes, and lit windows. The outermost ground outside the first wall is a scatter of timber-framed sheds and houses on dirt lanes, medieval in scale and lamplight.

Moving inward, the bands grow denser and more vertical, with domes and taller blocks, and the walls step down so the eye feels each ring seam as a grade change. At the center sits a small round precinct flooded with gold light, rays and arcs flaring out of it so it reads as glow more than as buildings.

In the foreground ring wall an arched gate opens onto a lit passage, the traveler's way in. Above the far rim, dark vaguely humanoid shapes drift in the sky, consistent with Veloria's Sky Drifters at plate scale.

The decorative ring-and-dot border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/veloria-four-rings.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Veloria City as the concentric capital of Nauw: medieval rim, denser inner bands, and a hyper-advanced core that powers the realm, held in one circle.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens Veloria City's established layout (four concentric rings around a central core, technology changing ring by ring, the core seen as glow) and the Rings of Veloria Primer's ring glance. It matches Nauw Layered Civic Architecture's habit of streets that step at ring seams and a core read as glow more than facade.

## Notes

The engraved DEB letters on the gate are plate noise and do not fix the DEB Gate's design or position; see the DEB Gate entry for the gate itself. Exact ring widths, wall heights, and building styles per band are illustrative, not an atlas. Sky shapes are not counted or classified. The core glow is not a readable god.
