# Soft Close of the Reveal Chime

## Name

Soft Close of the Reveal Chime

## Overview

*Soft Close of the Reveal Chime* hangs thin glass-metal tubes from a slate bar, alone on a plain ground. One tube is shorter than the others, the built-in softer close. It is not a choir, not a swell, and not a picture of anyone striking it.

## Description

A dark slate bar hangs level. Short lines drop from it to narrow tubes, cool and unornamented, close enough in length to be one instrument and uneven enough not to be a song. One tube is plainly shorter. That shortness is the second note: the close, softer than the open, the ask for the ledge's clarity back. The rest of the set does not blossom into a scale a lingerer could hide inside. A shadow sits under the tubes. No striker, no hand, no window, and no Timer appears.

The plate does not show the reveal itself, a view worth overstaying, or a person packing tools. It does not prove a melody, a choir loft, or a chime someone has sweetened until it could be ignored as music. It does not ring for a liar, because this object is not a lie detector and the picture has no one to test. What it settles is the hardware: slate, thin tubes, and one shorter tube for ending. What it leaves open is which shelf, which minute, and whether the next apprentice hears the close as information or tries to love it as a hymn.

## Real-World Role

A craft plate for anti-stare manners. The chime is a clock with two words, shown without the performance.

## Lore Connection

Veil Scribe hardware sounds once when a ledge window opens and once, softer, when it closes. The second note is the important one. This plate isolates that hardware from talent, from miracle, and from any choir that would turn ending into music. A guild that retunes a pleading chime back into mere information would recognize the unromantic metal. The plate does not depict that retuning. It only refuses the sweeter object.

## Notes

No people. Not a choir. The shorter tube is the softer close, not a soloist.
