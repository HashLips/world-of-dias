# Boat in a Bottle

## Name

Boat in a Bottle

## Overview

*Boat in a Bottle* supports F432 expansion lore around Nauw Outer Rim Seas.

## Description

A black sail silhouette hangs above an empty horizontal glass bottle on terracotta cradles, sage field over coral ground. The voyage is displayed and watched, not released: the ship has air, the bottle has a stage, and nothing in the picture offers a cork that would make the sea obedient. Outer Rim viewers read omen and craft at once. A contained hull can mean a storm rehearsed in safety, or a promise of passage that will not cash. Singing fishermen, in the joke that sticks, say the ship prefers air to glass and will not be talked into the bottle by a schedule. The cradles hold the glass the way a careful harbor holds a rumor of sailing, level, public, and unwilling to be a ticket.

The emptiness inside the bottle matters. There is no miniature crew to rescue and no miniature route to trace. People who want the picture to teach a crossing are left with a silhouette and a joke, which is as far as the image will go.
