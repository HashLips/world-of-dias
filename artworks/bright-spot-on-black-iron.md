# Bright Spot on Black Iron

## Name

Bright Spot on Black Iron

## Overview

*Bright Spot on Black Iron* is one Outer-Ring Door Nail at rest: black iron, a square head, and a small bright spot where the metal has been worried. It is not driven into a hinge. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

One nail lies on wood. The head is square. The shank is black iron and comes to a point. A bright worn spot sits on the dark metal, the little brightness the object keeps where it has been handled. The spot is wear. It is not a rune, not a letter, and not a mark that could be read as a route. Nothing is cut into the head or along the shank.

The nail is spare. It is not the nail holding a hinge, and the plate does not show a hinge at all. It is not set as a lock pick, and it is not glowing like a coal. Warmth, if the metal has any, is the mild warmth of something that lived in a handled door. The picture does not turn that into light, and it does not heat a room. There is no key beside it, and no door opens because of it.

No hand holds it. No thumb is on the bright spot. The nail is one piece of kept hardware, loose, square-headed, and unread.

## Real-World Role

A pocket-piece still life. The nail admits a threshold and refuses to be a key.

## Lore Connection

An Outer-Ring Door Nail is spare iron from an old door, sometimes kept as a pocket piece and sometimes nailed back into a drafty shutter at home. This plate is the spare nail, not that second nailing, and not the nail that holds a hinge. Black iron and a square head are the object. The bright place is where thumbs worry it; the picture has the bright spot and not the thumb. Warmth is mild, the warmth of metal that has lived in a handled door, not a coal. It will not pick a lock, mark a route, or heat a room. A want for a key is disappointed on purpose, and no one is here to be disappointed. It is not a relic of the Fracture and not a gate key. The object can say a street was found. It does not say the street owes anyone a shop's secrets. The plate keeps one nail.
