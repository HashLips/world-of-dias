# Unseen

## Name

Unseen

## Overview

*Unseen* repeats one seated figure and tripod plant across six color-shifted panels—same posture, same stems, different worlds of hue.

## Description

A 2×3 grid. In each panel a featureless seated silhouette faces a three-legged stand holding a pot with three curved stems and star-flowers. Background blocks and figure colors change; composition does not.

Asset reference: `assets/unseen-1.png`.

## Real-World Role

A serial/variation piece: one moment rendered as many frequency reads.

## Lore Connection

Used when explaining how a single event can leave **different-looking echoes** across frequency contexts—same chair, same waiting, different band. The "unseen" is what stays identical underneath the color change.

## Notes

Pairs conceptually with echo-rise disputes: witnesses swear they saw the same thing and bring incompatible palettes.
