# Peel-Yellow at the Quay Winch

## Name

Peel-Yellow at the Quay Winch

## Overview

*Peel-Yellow at the Quay Winch* is a digital image study of the Banana Draft tied up at a basin quay, doing the last wet miles of fruit distribution.

## Description

The barge sits broadside in the foreground: flat-bottomed, beamy, built of heavy planks whose yellow paint has weathered to the color of a peel left on a step. A short stub mast stands near the bow, wrapped and coiled in thick rope, undressed. A long sweep-oar lies over the bow for working the narrows, and a round iron kettle sits on the deck planks.

Aft of the mast, the hold is divided into open, lined compartments for lots that should not take the water's cold all at once. Mooring lines run from the hull to the quay.

On the right, a stone quay steps down into the water, and on it stands a heavy iron winch with a drum of cable: third-ring quay machinery beside an older wooden boat. Behind, a hazy waterfront of roofs, domes, and stone arches spreads across the water.

The decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/the-banana-draft.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of the Banana Draft as ordinary Nauw market distribution on water: slow, wooden, faded yellow, and accountable to the quay it ties up at.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the Banana Draft's established image (flat beamy barge, stub mast rarely dressed, sweep-oar, lined hold, kettle on deck, faded peel-yellow paint, a winch at third-ring quays) and keeps it distinct from The Reedpol. It adds no new route.

## Notes

The hold is shown without readable cargo; do not infer what lot is aboard. The background skyline is decorative and not a map of a specific basin quay. Sarn Vell is not pictured.
