# Red Lips of Passion

## Name

Red Lips of Passion

## Overview

*Red Lips of Passion* is the F500 plate for the cross-marked orange bust. Pale pink field. Orange face and shoulders. Black intersecting lines. Small red lips. Red wavy head-cover, maroon bun.

## Description

Soft pale pink ground. From the shoulders up, a humanoid form in vibrant solid orange faces slightly right—long graceful neck, broad shoulders to the frame edge. No eyes or nose drawn; instead two thin intersecting black lines form a cross: horizontal slightly arched across the eye-line, vertical down the face center. Below, small stylized bright red lips, calm and closed.

Top and back of the head wear a saturated red wavy shape like hair or tight cover; atop it a smaller darker maroon cylinder like a tied bun or flat hat. Tiny black arch/house icon top-left. Flat blocks, tranquil more than loud. The passion is held in the small red mouth and the cross mark, not in a shout.
