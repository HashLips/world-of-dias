# Notched Ladle, Bowl, and Green Crate

## Name

Notched Ladle, Bowl, and Green Crate

## Overview

*Notched Ladle, Bowl, and Green Crate* is a darkened wooden ladle at rest in a bowl, beside a green crate of fruit. Short cuts mark the handle. They are not writing. No hands are in the picture.

## Description

The ladle is one piece of darkened wood, handle scarred by use, bowl of the spoon still holding a coarse mash. It lies across a wide ceramic bowl on a worn board. The handle carries a short row of notch cuts, straight and shallow, spaced like a tally someone meant only for the hand that already knows the spoon. They do not form letters. They do not spell a fee, a name, or a boast. Reading a sentence out of them would be inventing a text the cuts do not contain.

The ceramic bowl is broad, stained at the rim, and mostly empty around the spoon. A few crumbs sit on the board. To the right, a slatted crate painted a worn green holds round citrus, some still green and some gone orange, and a loose hand of bananas. The crate is closed by its own slats. It is not a display staged with a server behind it. The board is the whole room the plate allows: grain, cracks, and the weight of bowl and crate.

No hand grips the handle. No child waits for the arc. No stamp, clerk, or quarrel sits at the edge of the board. The mash is present as work in the spoon, not as a meal assigned to a liar or an honest day. The picture does not show the ladle sticking, pouring thin, or pouring generous. It shows the tool, the bowl, the notches, and the green crate, and leaves the manners unwitnessed.
