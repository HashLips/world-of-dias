# Wrist and Boot at Orrow House

## Name

Wrist and Boot at Orrow House

## Overview

*Wrist and Boot at Orrow House* is a digital image study of Hob Orrow as the extra pair of hands at Orrow House: all wrist and boot, ordinary orchard fruit only.

## Description

Hob stands in orchard light—sixteen, all wrist and boot. The plate depicts an young man with dark textured hair pulled into a small messy bun, contemplative gaze down toward fruit. Coarse cream tunic with sleeves rolled to mid-forearm, loose trousers, knotted fabric sash, sturdy dark work boots. His right hand rests on ordinary red and yellow apples in two stacked wooden crates. Behind him, Mix Orchard trees and dappled gold light. Soft parchment texture.

Decorative border is framing only.
