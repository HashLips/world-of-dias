# Boards Along a Limb, Marks Not Carved

## Name

## Overview

*Boards Along a Limb, Marks Not Carved* is an Aelwyn Path of the Season. Boards along a canopy limb. A rope handline. Leaf-shadow. Plain marks that get repainted. It is not a bridge between trees. The marks are not carved into bark.

## Description

Whichever elevated canopy route currently holds priority. The plate shows that route as boards along a canopy limb, with a rope handline, in leaf-shadow. The entry's rope is the handline. It is not a bridge between trees. The path does not pretend to be a permanent crossing from trunk to trunk.

Markers get repainted when priority rotates. The marks in the picture are plain, and they are repainted marks. They are not carved into bark. Children learn to win a season and not tattoo permanence into bark. The plate follows that lesson. No tattoo, no cut letters in the wood of the tree.

Kin groups meet at junctions to renegotiate. They are not meeting in the frame. Emergency rights can open a rival path for weather, then close again. The rival path is not drawn as a second bridge.
