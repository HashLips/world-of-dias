# Love

## Name

Love

## Overview

*Love* opens a Basin desert: tall black and orange spindly figures mirroring each other, mouths open, single almond eyes, needle fingers reaching center while bodies curve out into a rough heart shape, root-like feet gripping tan sand, pale blue sky with a jagged white central blaze, distant grey mountains, blue hand-like growths rising from the ground, a blue face-mask discarded left, long shadows down-frame.

## Description

Tension between connection and pull-apart is the pose. Blue hands feel like buried memory surfacing. The mask on the sand says someone already shed a face to join the reach. Mood is surreal wonder edged with isolation.

Asset reference: `assets/love-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A pair plate: love as mirrored reach that still keeps two colors.

## Lore Connection

Velorian Basin talk treats Love as the desert dance where black and orange keep distinct hues while fingers meet. Local rule: do not force the mask back on mid-reach; if it lies discarded, finish the song first. Resonance prefers rooted feet. Breaking a blue hand-growth for souvenir empties the plate into theft.
