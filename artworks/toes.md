# Toes

## Name

Toes

## Overview

*Toes* is a foot up close and enormous. A plain white foot fills most of a teal frame, five toes in a row from the large big toe to a small fifth, each nail painted solid black. An orange cord is wound tightly around the big toe in five or six neat coils and then loops up and over the nail like a handle. Behind the toes, at the right, a dark blue shape stands upright—a boot, or a sock, set aside.

## Description

The foot is flat white with a darker teal shadow offset behind it, so it lifts slightly off the background. The toes are rounded and slightly splayed. The nails are irregular black shapes, the big toe's large and square, the smaller ones narrow wedges.

The orange cord is the plate's detail. It wraps the base of the big toe in tight spirals like thread on a spool, and its free end arcs up in a loop that would be easy to hook a finger through—or tie to something else.

The blue shape on the right rises taller than the toes, with a rounded bump near the top like a heel or the curve of a boot's collar, and a long base that slides under the foot's sole like a strap. It feels discarded rather than worn. The plate is funny and blunt: a bare foot, a tied toe, and a boot nobody is using.

Asset reference: `assets/toes.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A claim-yard plate about tying yourself to what you are staking, literally and by the toe.

## Lore Connection

Claimscar Yard is the inland Driftfall crew yard where Freeband crews stake claims on Sprout Vent runoff and salvage rights. Under yard custom a claim is only live while its claimant is present, so crews sleeping on a contested patch tie an orange cord from the big toe to the claim stake, boots off and set aside. Toes shows the cord wound and ready. If someone tugs the line in the night, the sleeper wakes; if the cord is found untied in the morning, the claim lapses. The yard treats it as half joke and half law, which is how most Claimscar rules work.
