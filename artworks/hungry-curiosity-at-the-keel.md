# Hungry Curiosity at the Keel

## Name

Hungry Curiosity at the Keel

## Overview

*Hungry Curiosity at the Keel* is a digital image study of Outer Rim Keel-Sharks as logged marine danger, not myth alone.

## Description

Large sharks longer than a skiff work foggy Outer Rim water—slate backs, pale belly—near an empty wooden craft. One scrapes or noses the keel in hungry curiosity. Day and dusk hunters near shipping lanes.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/outer-rim-keel-sharks.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of sailor-logged Outer Rim sharks that stitch fog lines with fins.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens Outer Rim Keel-Sharks from catch and loss records without inventing new species traits.

## Notes

Gold runes or filigree on shark bodies in the plate are decorative noise; lore is slate backs and pale belly. Empty boat only—no invented crew. Border framing only.
