# Who You Gonna Call

## Name

Who You Gonna Call

## Overview

*Who You Gonna Call* is a call-box in the middle of nowhere. Under a murky green-gray sky streaked with darker clouds, a bright red dial-box with a curled handset sits on a small black square table, held up by a single bent black post. From a hole in the ground at the right, a tall bright blue limb rises, arches high over the box, and comes down on the left side with a long clawed hand reaching toward it. On the ground, a shadow shaped like another clawed hand stretches out from the post's base.

## Description

The landscape is low and dull: a gray-green plain, a darker band of hills, a strip of slate-blue water at the right. The sky is a murky sage with smears of darker green cloud. Nothing else stands for miles.

The dial-box is the only warm color, a glossy orange-red with a white dial face ringed with black holes, its handset resting on top. The black post beneath it bends slightly like a stalk.

The blue limb is enormous and simple, an even tube that rises out of a black hole in the ground, makes a sharp elbow at the top, and descends into a splayed hand with long fingers. It is not grabbing the box; its fingers hang just short. The shadow on the ground beneath the post has fingers too, as if the post itself had a hand. The scene is eerie but not threatening: a call has been placed, and something has come to answer.

## Real-World Role

A roadside mark for a call-post where help answers first and asks about payment later.

## Lore Connection

Sorel occupies the south-eastern lands of F432, where exile roads, freebands, and kiln towns cross a lot of open country. Along its emptier stretches stand call-posts: a red dial-box on a black stake, wired to nobody can quite say where. The mark painted on them and the plate share a name. Sorel folk say that when someone dials in honest distress, something answers—a crew, a neighbor, or, in the old telling the plate keeps, a long blue reach out of the ground. The rule is the same as on the coast: help comes before any talk of debt, and the caller is never asked to pay before they are safe.
