# J in a Box

## Name

J in a Box

## Overview

*J in a Box* is a grey-and-white Rim still of surprise craft: light-grey cube with four flaps open, faint twin-peak crest on the side, thick black spring winding out, and a black profile head leaning right with one white crescent eye-slit, shadows sprawling on the light-grey floor plane.

## Description

The spring is irregular, hand-drawn in feel, not a perfect coil—energy more than precision engineering. The head’s nose is sharp, mouth a small indent, eye focused past the frame. Top half of the air is white; bottom is the floor band. Long shadows from box and spring insist the pop already happened. No crowd laughing in frame; the plate keeps the instant after open.

Asset reference: `assets/j-in-a-box.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A surprise-manners plate for Outer Rim play and drill: open, rise, look—then put the head back without cruelty.

## Lore Connection

Nauw Outer Rim Seas keep spring-boxes for teaching startle without harm—dock drills, child play, fog-signal jokes that must reset clean. J in a Box is the named head. Local rule: flaps open fully before the spring rises; after the look, press the head home and latch. Resonance sticks to honest reset. Leaving the spring out overnight to scare the next watcher is considered a cold echo.

## Notes

Grey open cube, black spring, black head, white eye-slit. Twin-peak side stamp, not Earth costume lore.
