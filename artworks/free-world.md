# Free World

## Name

Free World

## Overview

*Free World* is a Lumira Sands labor plate: a dark figure lunges back pulling white cables through a green disc toward floating orange pods—the sand rule that freedom is still work you do with both hands on the lines.

## Description

Warm mustard-ochre field. Lower right: dark figure in lunging pull, weight back, arms forward-up; long dark shadow toward lower left. Three smooth taut white cables arc from the hands through (or behind) a large dark olive-green circle midframe, ending at three vibrant reddish-orange irregular rounded pods upper left. Industrial-mysterious tension, diagonal effort. Flat high contrast.

Asset reference: `assets/free-world.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A sands phenomenon plate: free does not mean unheld—hold the pods through the dampening disc.

## Lore Connection

In Lumira Sands, **Free World** names the weather-of-work when orange buoyant matter must be pulled and steadied. Green disc is the dampener; white lines are care cables. Wonder is effortful liberty. Dias sands name—not an Earth slogan claim.
