# Hypnotic

## Name

Hypnotic

## Overview

*Hypnotic* centers a bright red pigment stick on a long green pedestal inside irregular black-and-white concentric rings, crossed by thin black lines and marked with a wavy red stroke, a messy red X, and a small red hook.

## Description

The stick stands upright: saturated red diagonal tip, thin tan/gold band, dark rectangular base. The green column rises from the bottom edge to mid-frame like a display post. Rings are hand-drawn in feel—uneven circles alternating black and white—pulling the eye inward. Straight black lines cut horizontally and diagonally through the vortex. Red notations live in the upper half: serpentine wave at left, large X at top right, small comma-hook at mid-right. Outer black curves frame the rings. A tiny square gate-like mark sits top-right. The object is small; the field around it is loud.

## Real-World Role

A focus-symbol plate for Sorel: pigment as attention tool, rings as the field you must not drown in.

## Lore Connection

Sorel talk (road and rest both) treats Hypnotic as a warning against staring past the useful mark. Local rule: the red stick is for placing a precise mouth-mark or craft mark; the black-white rings are the trance risk if you watch the field instead of the tip. Red X and wave are reader notations—stop, redirect—not decorations. Resonance concentrates at the tip; people who pocket the stick and leave the green pedestal bare are said to carry a dizzy echo until they set it down again on something upright.

Red stick, green pedestal, concentric rings, three red notations. Not framed as Earth cosmetics culture.
