# Blank Slots in Emberfile

## Name

Blank Slots in Emberfile

## Overview

Blank Slots in Emberfile is a digital image study of the ash-warm rooms where recovery tools are handled after catastrophe weather.

## Description

The image shows worn, ash-warm rooms, blank drawer slots, and broken tools awaiting the patient work of filing or remaking. The rooms feel used rather than polished; their order is a promise of continuity, not a decorative display.

No hanging banner is made part of the workshops. The plate stays with recovery craft and the visible fact of things that have broken.
