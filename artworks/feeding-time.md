# Feeding Time

## Name

Feeding Time

## Overview

*Feeding Time* is an F500 hour plate: a white four-legged form approaches a small fruit tree behind a dark blue rail—the frequency courtesy of taking fallen yellow crescents before stripping the crown.

## Description

Deep royal-blue upper field. Dark charcoal platform with a brown mottled mound lower left. Dark blue railing of top rail and posts crosses midframe. Left: small palm-like tree, light tan curving trunk, vibrant green spear leaves, four bright yellow crescent fruits hanging, one yellow crescent on the ground. Right: solid white elongated four-legged creature with upright pointed ears, tapered snout, single dark horizontal eye-line; front-left leg stretched toward the tree base, others bent in cautious approach. Quiet, surreal, observational. Small black mark top-right.

Asset reference: `assets/feeding-time.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A frequency feed plate: approach the rail; prefer the fallen fruit; leave the hanging count honest.

## Lore Connection

In F500, **Feeding Time** names the agreed hour when white path-beasts (or their stand-ins) may take from the yellow crescent tree. Rail marks the structured boundary. Wonder is patient hunger. Strip the crown for boast and the hour closes early.
