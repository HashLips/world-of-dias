# Jumper

## Name

Jumper

## Overview

*Jumper* is pure arc on orange: a rich blue body bent in a deep backbend, head and arms left-up, pointed feet right-down, thin black interior lines at nose-brow and limbs, and a cream echo layer offset behind like a lingering trail of the jump.

## Description

No ground plane, no canopy leaf—suspension is the subject. Fingers loosen; knees bend; toes point. The cream shadow-echo is not grey gravity but a pale vibration, as if the leap left a soft copy. A small cursive mark sits top-right. Energy is high, cruelty absent: this is release mid-air.

Asset reference: `assets/jumper-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A motion plate for Aelwyn paths: jumps that keep an echo soft enough to land by.

## Lore Connection

Aelwyn Canopy season-paths include gaps where a jumper must arc and trust the next hold. Jumper teaches keeping a cream-soft echo—meaning leave a readable line for the next body, not a snapped branch. Local rule: if your leap has no soft trail others can read, you jumped for show. Resonance rides the blue arc; orange air is the permitted intensity of the moment.
