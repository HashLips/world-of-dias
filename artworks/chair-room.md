# Chair Room

## Name

Chair Room

## Overview

*Chair Room* is an Averra Isle waiting plate: three crimson chairs with absurdly tall backs stand empty on mint ground in a midnight void—the room that keeps posture honest while nobody sits yet.

## Description

Dark midnight-blue field. Pale mint or celadon floor. Three flat crimson chairs in a row: thin seats, slender legs, backrests as extreme tall rectangles widening slightly at the top. Center chair faces forward; left and right angle inward. Harsh light throws long sharp black shadows toward the lower left. Tiny white gate icon top-right; small cursive mark bottom-left. Austere, formal, expectant silence.

Asset reference: `assets/chair-room.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A studio waiting plate: three seats for ask, answer, and witness—kept empty until the talk is ready.

## Lore Connection

On Averra Isle, **Chair Room** is a kept interior habit. Tall backs discourage slouch-lying and spectacle-sprawl. Three is the courtesy count for a hard conversation: one who asks, one who answers, one who witnesses without stealing the cup. Empty chairs are not neglect; they are readiness. Wonder is formal quiet that still invites sitting when the green lamp of permission (elsewhere in Averra talk) is honest.
