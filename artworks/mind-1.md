# Mind

## Name

Mind

## Overview

*Mind* is a Wabet pause: saturated blue donkey-like quadruped with upright ears, tufted thin tail, white eye-sliver, one foreleg slightly lifted; saturated red humanoid in deep crouch, white horizontal eye-slits, left hand soft on the blue muzzle; long dark grey shadows stretch left on light grey ground under white air.

## Description

Touch is the mind of the plate—attention made contact. No crowd, no rope. Quiet significance.

Wabet roads and quiet wells know Mind as the crouch-check with a blue burden-beast. Local rule: touch muzzle, wait for the lifted hoof to settle, then speak the next load. Resonance prefers unhurried shadows. Yanking ears instead of the soft muzzle empties the name into haste.
