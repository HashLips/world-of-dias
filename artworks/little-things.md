# Little Things

## Name

Little Things

## Overview

*Little Things* scatters a surreal domestic field: tall green pyramid/trapezoid with golden door rectangle and pink triangle handle, dark brown base shadow, thick grey ring around the top with black teardrop pendant and blue oval 'eye' plus blue X, light-blue ribbon across, red vertical pole with six grey pebbles left, dark grey chair with fluid light-blue sitter right, pale yellow oval glow, magenta wavy top on a thin brown frame.

## Description

White ground holds everything like a table of meanings. Mood is quiet enigmatic household—many small parts, one green heart.

## Real-World Role

A care plate: little things arranged so a door and a chair still make a room.

## Lore Connection

F610 benches teach Little Things as the discipline of keeping odd small parts—pebble pole counts, pink handles, blue ribbons—without losing the green door’s purpose. Local rule: if you move a little thing, put it back where the chair-sitter can still see the door. Resonance prefers complete tables.
