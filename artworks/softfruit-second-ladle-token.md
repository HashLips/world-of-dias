# Second-Ladle Chip

## Name

Second-Ladle Chip

## Overview

*Second-Ladle Chip* is a fruit-stained wood chip with a carved ladle silhouette and a hole for a cord, laid with smaller stained chips beside it. It is a fairness token you could clack on a table. It is not a coin, not jewelry, and not a picture of anyone spending it.

## Description

The main chip is irregular, cut from wood and blotched with fruit stain in red and purple. A ladle is carved into the face, bowl and handle, plain enough for a child to recognize and useless as a mint mark. A hole near the edge takes a cord. The cord runs toward a hook at the side of the frame, a hanger, not a chain and not a necklace. Smaller chips, similarly stained and similarly unofficial, lie loose nearby. They read as neighbors in a stack that came apart, not as change and not as a set of denominations.

No hand holds the chip. No face trades it. There is no stamp, no price, and no number. The plate does not settle whether the cord is a keep-mark someone tried to turn into jewelry and was discouraged, or only the way the chip was hung so it could be drawn. It does not show a second helping served, a rush slowed, a pocket, or a debt cleared. Sticky use is implied by the stain. Currency is refused by everything else: no rim like a coin, no setting like a jewel, no portrait like a mint.

## Real-World Role

A manners plate for Softfruit fairness. Hunger noticed, in a shape that spends badly as money and well as a delay.

## Lore Connection

The Second-Ladle Token grants a second helping when the shelf is fair and teaches waiting when it is not. Adults use it to slow a rush. A stack is a tell that someone tried to win breakfast; these loose smaller chips hint at that tell without staging the court. Ladle before stamp stays a rule offstage. If the chip started clearing debts or riding as jewelry, it would have left the culture. The plate keeps it a stained piece of wood with a ladle cut in it.

## Notes

No people. Not a coin. Not jewelry. The cord hole does not make a necklace.
