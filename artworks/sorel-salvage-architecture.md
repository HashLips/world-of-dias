# Hull Ribs and Claim Smoke

## Name

Hull Ribs and Claim Smoke

## Overview

*Hull Ribs and Claim Smoke* is a digital image study of Sorel salvage building: whatever survived the last move, put up again with its history showing.

## Description

On the left, long tarps in faded blue, ochre, and gray are stretched over curved ribs that read as salvaged hull frames, lashed and propped into a low shelter. Beside it rises a patchwork upright of mismatched brick and plank, flying torn banners and a dark claim flag on a pole.

In the open ground at the center a small fire burns inside a ring of stones, the yard's civic hearth. To the right stands a squat kiln-brick structure with an arched mouth, and more tarp-covered heaps and scaffold poles. Smoke drifts across the whole yard, and rubble, salvage, and scrap lie everywhere underfoot.

Behind the yard the plate stacks very tall patchwork towers fading into haze. The decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/sorel-salvage-architecture.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Sorel Salvage Architecture: tarps over hull ribs, kiln brick, painted and flagged uprights, and a cookfire ring as the center of yard life.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the established Driftfall-yard dialect from Sorel Salvage Architecture (tarps over scarred hull ribs, cookfire stone rings, claim marks on uprights, kiln brick, temporary poles and banners). It adds no new claim law.

## Notes

The tall background towers are plate exaggeration. Canon still holds that Sorel's skyline is smoke and flags before towers; do not read the plate as evidence of Sorel high-rises. Flag designs are decorative, not crew marks.
