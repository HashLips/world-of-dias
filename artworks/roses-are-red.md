# Roses Are Red

## Name

Roses Are Red

## Overview

*Roses Are Red* is the Wabet plate for the red vase quiet. Fire-engine red vessel. Dark green stalks. Lime teardrop leaves. Long gray shadow. White room, gray shelf.

## Description

Pure white upper field; light gray surface across the lower third. Center: a solid bright red vase, slightly irregular handmade silhouette—wider rounded middle, tapering to a flat rim. From the rim, several thin dark forest-green vertical stems rise; sparse small teardrop lime-green leaves sit along them like minimal bamboo or still-reeds. A long flat dark charcoal-gray shadow stretches from the vase base toward bottom-left, sharp and elongated. No bloom heads in the frame—the red is the vessel; the green is the living rise. Still, meditative, essential.
