# Seat, Rail, and a Water Skin

## Name

Seat, Rail, and a Water Skin

## Overview

*Seat, Rail, and a Water Skin* is one rest niche on the Long Gate span: a stone seat, a thin rail, and a water skin hanging on a hook. The seat is on the bridge. It is not an inn. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

The seat is a plain stone block in an alcove of the walk, the edge worn where sitting has happened and then stopped. A thin rail runs at the open side, enough to mark the drop and not enough to make a room. On the stone wall above the seat, a hook holds a water skin. The skin is a traveler's flask of the ordinary kind, hung where a hand could take a drink and put it back. There is no bed, no host's counter, no lamp set out as welcome, no fee board, and no bowl for a donation.

The alcove is part of the span. The bridge continues, stone and arches, with the walk going on rather than ending in a door that could be rented. Nothing in the niche has been turned into a hostel: no curtains, no named room, no table laid for a story. Wind has the open side of the frame. The picture does not put a coat, a letter, or a pair of shoulders on the seat.

No one is sitting. No one is standing in escort. The seat, the rail, the skin, and the span are empty of inn comfort on purpose.

## Real-World Role

A place plate for rest that stays infrastructure. You can see where a person could sit and drink. The picture does not make that rest a lodging.

## Lore Connection

Long Gate Rest Niche is one of the wind-bitten alcoves along the span from Nauw toward Hearthvale, a place where exile roads pause without becoming inns. The seat is stone. The rail is thin. The hook is for water skins, and this plate hangs a water skin there and nothing else. Rest here does not pretend the crossing is casual. The niche is small, stone, and shared because both sides once agreed to leave it unowned. It is not a frequency door. It is not an inn, a court, or a shrine. The span around it is Long Gate, stone infrastructure people walk, not a band crossing and not a rented room. The picture keeps the niche at seat, rail, skin, and bridge, and leaves the seat unoccupied.
