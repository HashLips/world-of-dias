# World of Dias Logo

## Name

World of Dias Logo

## Overview

*World of Dias Logo* is the official lockup: hammered-gold lettering for **WORLD of DIAS**, with the Dias Emblem seated in the **O** of WORLD.

## Description

Sharp fantasy serifs carry a beaten-metal surface—bright top edges, deep bronze shadow in the troughs. The small **of** sits between the larger word marks. The circular emblem that replaces the O is a compass-star nest of rings and points, read as the same Dias Emblem used alone elsewhere.

Asset reference: `assets/world-of-dias-logo.png`.

## Real-World Role

Primary public logo for the World of Dias archive—README header, sharing, and brand continuity.

## Lore Connection

The lockup treats Dias as a named world whose “O” is not empty space but a frequency compass: direction, orbit, and a center that does not explain itself. It brands the canon as one archive without depicting a single city or saga beat.
