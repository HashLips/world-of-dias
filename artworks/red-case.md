# Red Case

## Name

Red Case

## Overview

*Red Case* is the F432 plate for the matched carry. Black slender walkers in single file. Each holds a maroon rectangular case. One pale almond eye each. Teal ground, blue sky, darker blue shadows.

## Description

Upper field medium blue; ground light teal. A continuous overlapping line of identical tall dark silhouettes walks left to right in mid-stride—slightly hunched, right leg forward, right arm down on a handle. Every figure carries the same dark red (maroon) rectangular box with a simple top handle—toolkit or specialized transport, identical size. Each head shows one glowing almond eye, white or very pale blue, the only bright mark on the black.

Long diagonal darker-blue shadows stretch bottom-left in even rhythm. Tiny square icon top-right. No leader singled out. The plate is synchronized transport of the red case as realm labor.
