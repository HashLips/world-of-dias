# Pennants That Learned Flight

## Name

Pennants That Learned Flight

## Overview

*Pennants That Learned Flight* is a digital image study of Banner Moths above fair-week plazas.

## Description

Large moths with wingspans near a forearm drift in warm plaza air—citrus, cobalt, and chalk white in blocky banner shapes rather than soft gradients. Shadow-patches slide like moving stalls. No readable pennant text.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/banner-moths.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Veloria fair-week ecology over booths and ring-fair ropes.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens Banner Moths without inventing market guild ownership of the insects.
