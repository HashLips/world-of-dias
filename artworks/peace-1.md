# Peace

## Name

Peace

## Overview

*Peace* is the Sorel plate for the blue tree in the red street. Leafless vivid blue. Red block buildings in rows. Pale path. Black shadow spread like roots.

## Description

A single saturated blue tree stands center, bare of leaves, crooked angular branches spreading up and out in clean silhouette. Flanking the wide pale-blue path are tall rectangular red buildings with darker red window cutouts, diminishing toward a white horizon in hard linear perspective. Ground and sky wash are cool, almost white—quiet enough to feel sterile or snow-lit.

The tree's shadow falls long toward the bottom-left, but it is not a simple copy of the crown: it jags and spreads like a network of roots or cracks across the pale ground. Faint translucent pink-red building shadows stretch right. No figures walk. No birds. The plate is stillness with a hidden root-claim under the blue trunk.

## Real-World Role

A phenomenon plate for Sorel. Peace is the blue tree hour in the red street.

## Lore Connection

Peace is how Sorel names the hour when the vivid blue leafless tree holds the pale path between red blocks and its shadow becomes root-work on the ground. Local care says you do not cut the blue tree to widen traffic, and you do not pretend the black root-shadow is nothing. Resonance is the clash of organic blue and rigid red; memory is the window-cut rows; wonder is the shadow that claims more ground than the branches show. The plate invents no treaty signing. It keeps the quiet street as a weather of held stillness.
