# Masked

## Name

Masked

## Overview

*Masked* presents a row of identical black-cowled figures with white tear-shaped faces and down-curved eye slits—uniform, calm, endlessly repeatable against blue.

## Description

Eight near-identical busts in a line. White mask-faces, minimal features, black hoods merging into torso silhouettes. Empty blue field above. The image insists on **many** rather than **one**.

Asset reference: `assets/masked-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A graphic of anonymity-as-order in Daniel Eugene Botha's Sorel-linked imagery.

## Lore Connection

Anchors **Sorel Exile Compact** mask culture and the wider reading of **Carrow's Mask**: identity as office and pact signal. Not a portrait of Carrow alone—an image of the *practice* of masking.

## Notes

Pairs with *Ball Room* (Carrow-specific) as the collective counterpart.
