# Running Over Nails

## Name

Running Over Nails

## Overview

*Running Over Nails* is the Lumira Sands plate for the spike passage. Pale-pink crouch. Magenta halo. Black spike field on mustard. Dark pierce triangles at contact points.

## Description

Pale yellow field. Lower: dull mustard-ochre horizontal ground planted with a dense regular field of thin vertical black spikes. Across it a pale-pink elongated humanoid crouches/crawls leftward—one small horizontal black eye-slit, pointed chin, magenta-pink oval floating flat above the head. One arm reaches forward touching spikes; the other bends behind; weight on one foot and one knee pressed into the field. Small dark triangular marks show where spikes meet pink flesh at hand, knee, and feet. Calm determination, not panic. The plate is the trial of crossing without denying the pierce.

Asset reference: `assets/running-over-nails.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Lumira Sands. Running Over Nails is the kept craft of the spike-field passage.

## Lore Connection

Running Over Nails is the sands' name for the hour when the pale-pink body must cross the black spike ground under the magenta halo and the dark triangles admit contact. Local care says you do not pretend the field is soft; you cross with the halo steady. Resonance is pink on mustard among black; memory is the pierce marks; wonder is how quiet the eye-slit stays. The plate invents no torture sport. It keeps the craft as manners for a hard local rule of traversal.
