# Don't Sit

## Name

Don't Sit

## Overview

*Don't Sit* is an Averra Isle refusal plate: a black backbent figure smiles over a pink platform while a small yellow chair waits empty—the room that says the chair is not yours yet.

## Description

Vibrant orange-red upper field; light beige floor. A low rectangular bubblegum-pink platform with darker pink vertical stripes. Behind it, a small geometric yellow chair with thin light-blue and pink accents. A pitch-black elongated humanoid arches in a deep bridge over the platform, hands and feet planted, head tucked under the arch facing the viewer with a sharp white crescent smile and a small white eye-dot; claw-like fingers on the surface. Sharp dark-pink shadows. Surreal, mischievous, uncanny. Small black icon top-left.

Asset reference: `assets/don-t-sit.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A studio manners plate: when the empty chair is a test, not an invitation.

## Lore Connection

On Averra Isle, **Don't Sit** is the kept warning for rooms where sitting early steals a talk. The yellow chair is real; the black arch is the room’s grin saying wait. Pink platform is the only place contact is allowed until permission. Wonder is disciplined refusal: stay off the seat until ask, answer, and witness are ready—cousin to Chair Room manners.

Black backbend smile. Pink striped platform. Empty yellow chair. Orange-red hall, beige floor.
