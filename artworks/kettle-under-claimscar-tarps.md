# Kettle Under Claimscar Tarps

## Name

Kettle Under Claimscar Tarps

## Overview

Kettle Under Claimscar Tarps is a digital image study of the shared cookfire ground in Claimscar Yard.

## Description

A scorched stone ring sits beneath tarps that lean toward the kettle’s place. Hooks mark the working edge, and peppers bring a sharp color to the smoke-dark yard. The circle is empty of people and dogs; its invitation is carried by the tools and the ground prepared for a shared meal.

Asset reference: `assets/claimscar-cookfire-circle.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Claimscar Cookfire Circle as frontier hospitality made practical in stone, iron, and heat.

## Lore Connection

The artwork keeps the kettle as the circle’s peace without softening the uneven safety of Claimscar Yard or adding a new settlement.
