# Giants

## Name

Giants

## Overview

*Giants* is a Bloomline Estuary scale plate: a vast brick-red headless seated form holds a tiny golden figure on its thigh—the estuary habit of resting on monumental care without demanding the giant grow a face for you.

## Description

Matte charcoal-gray field. Large solid brick-red seated silhouette facing left, headless with smooth rounded neck-end; left leg bent into a horizontal thigh platform; blocky geometric feet; sparse black lines for breast curve and crease; long thin arm hanging to a rounded point. Tiny solid golden-yellow complete humanoid sits on the thigh, leaning back on thin arms, looking up toward where a head would be. Intimate, protective, quiet. Three-color cut-out clarity.

## Real-World Role

An estuary inhabitant plate: accept scale rest; do not carve a face onto care that prefers to stay monumental.

## Lore Connection

At The Bloomline Estuary, **Giants** names non-human monumental sitters that offer thigh-rest to smaller walkers. Headlessness in the plate is local manners—presence without portrait demand. Wonder is protected smallness. Dias estuary name.
