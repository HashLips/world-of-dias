# Early

## Name

Early

## Overview

*Early* is an F380 first-look plate: a large white eye with coral iris sits in gold ochre under jagged black ink-shapes and a blue ribbon—the frequency habit of opening attention before the day hardens its story.

## Description

Warm gold, ochre, and tan planes. Central almond eye: crisp white sclera, warm coral-pink iris, dark reddish-brown pupil with a sharp white rectangular highlight; medium-brown thick lid curve above. Bold black jagged organic shapes frame like brows or lashes—one descending from top left, one under the eye dripping to a vertical point. Left: vibrant blue vertical ribbon-curve. Sharp black horizontal triangular shards from the left edge. Thin muted-brown horizontal line crosses behind the eye. Watchful, iconic, deliberate. Small white gate logo bottom-right.
