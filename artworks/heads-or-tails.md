# Heads or Tails

## Name

Heads or Tails

## Overview

*Heads or Tails* shows two dark figures sharing a heavy yellow-orange disc: one crouched and pushing from the left, one bracing from the right as the disc tilts, shadows stretched purple-grey across a light-blue ground.

## Description

The field behind them is flat medium grey. The ground plane is light blue. The disc is thick, pill-like, saturated yellow-orange, set on a steep diagonal—bright enough to read as the only warm object in the frame. Left figure: wide stance, arms extended into the lower edge of the disc. Right figure: partially hidden, leaning back to catch weight. Both have simple white eye-slits, focused, not playful. Shadows mimic the labor. A tiny square mark sits in the extreme top right. Nothing spins in midair; nothing flips for luck. The plate is about not dropping what two people already agreed to move.

## Real-World Role

A work-symbol plate for Ashcalm Rise: choice is not a coin toss when the object is this heavy—choice is who braces and who pushes.

## Lore Connection

Ashcalm Rise talk uses Heads or Tails when a load must move and both ends of the name still matter. The yellow disc is treated as a kept weight—memory, tool blank, or resonant plate—never as a gambling token in this telling. Local rule: name which end you hold before you lean. If one person calls “heads” and means the bright face while the other calls “tails” and means the shadowed underside, stop and reset grips. Resonance here is shared strain: the disc remembers both sets of hands.

## Notes

Two figures, one yellow disc, blue ground, grey air. No flip animation; labor only.
