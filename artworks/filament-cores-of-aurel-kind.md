# Filament Cores of Aurel-kind

## Name

Filament Cores of Aurel-kind

## Overview

*Filament Cores of Aurel-kind* is a digital image study of Aurel-kind in Soft-Ray Procession Hall: core filaments, soft corona, and F200 luminosity rather than flesh.

## Description

Eight luminous figures read as energy-native bodies: fine gold-white filament strands forming humanoid silhouettes with soft coronas, trailing as if suspended in a thick luminous medium. A larger central figure is ringed by smaller ones. A radial bright source above pours light down the hall.

The hall behind is cream-tan haze with tall vertical pillar-like volumes—Soft-Ray Procession Hall as luminous gathering space in F200 (frequency realm), not ordinary stone architecture.

Decorative border is framing only.

A visual record of Aurel-kind as energy life of F200: filament cores and calm coherence inside Soft-Ray Procession Hall.
