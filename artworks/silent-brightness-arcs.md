# Silent Brightness Arcs

## Name

Silent Brightness Arcs

## Overview

*Silent Brightness Arcs* is a digital image study of Aurel Field-Gazelles bounding Meridian fields in silent arcs, leaving brightness instead of dust.

## Description

Six gazelle-forms leap in staggered flight from left toward right, bodies of fine golden light-strands with ribbed, back-curving horns. Long streaming ribbons of brightness trail behind each animal; no dust rises beneath them. A faint compass-and-star chart sits behind the herd, sparks scattered through the air.

Decorative border with star, moon, and tree roundels is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/aurel-field-gazelles.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Field-Gazelles as medium luminous beauty—the sight that settles nerves after Dimming Leech talk.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the established silent arcs and brightness wakes without inventing herding use, instruments, or new hazards.

## Notes

Light-forms, not Earth gazelles. Trails are brightness wakes, not motion blur. Border roundels are framing, not Aurel calendar law.
