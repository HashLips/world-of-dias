# Nice

## Name

Nice

## Overview

*Nice* is the Lumira Sands plate for the resting hour that still listens. A dark figure lies limp on a cobalt daybed. A cyan bird sings on the armrest. A thin yellow beam crosses the torso. Jagged blue marks hang on the orange wall.

## Description

The room splits clean: bright orange-terracotta above, light lavender floor below. A thin orange rectangle frames the center, though the bird and the yellow beam refuse to stay politely inside it. On the cobalt daybed a deep charcoal silhouette sprawls—one leg bent up, one hanging, one arm draping toward the floor. A single small white almond eye looks out, awake inside the limpness.

On the right armrest a bright cyan bird with dark blue wings stands on long thin legs, head thrown back, beak wide open mid-call. Four identical jagged blue shapes hang on the orange wall like multi-pointed crowns or inverted peaks with five downward teeth. A thin canary-yellow diagonal cuts from upper right across the resting torso. A large dark purple shadow of figure and couch spreads toward the lower left; a separate dark blue shadow sits lower right as if the bird's call has a floor-weight of its own.

Nobody else is in the room. The plate holds stillness that is not emptiness: repose under a singing bird and a sharp beam.

A phenomenon plate for Lumira Sands. Nice is rest that still answers a call.

The jagged blue wall marks are local care geometry—kept sharp so the rest does not become forgetting. The yellow beam is resonance crossing the torso without demanding a leap to the feet.
