# Wet Paint at the Tarp Edge

## Name

Wet Paint at the Tarp Edge

## Overview

Wet Paint at the Tarp Edge is a digital image study of the Claimscar fringe where camp care meets claim-making.

## Description

The plate shows tarp camps at the edge of a wet painted claim line, with chalk buckets nearby and plain bowls set into the shared camp life. It keeps the boundary provisional: breakfast on one side of the cloth, argument about marks on the other.

The bowls are plain, not a new color code, and the image adds no spiral marks. It stays with Freeband claim manners rather than turning the tarp edge into a settled wall.

Asset reference: `assets/freeband-tarp-edge.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of the frontier camp where care and property are negotiated in the same weather.

## Lore Connection

The artwork supports Freeband identity and the vent-claim rule without deciding when a tarp becomes a wall.
