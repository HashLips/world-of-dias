# Blue Litter Husk

## Name

Blue Litter Husk

## Overview

*Blue Litter Husk* shows the Echoform as witnesses describe it. A featureless dark outline, prone or only as far as a kneel, on soft blue litter among skeletal wood. It is not chasing.

## Description

The grove is deep and off the harvest roads. The air is dry. The ground is blue litter. The trees are leafless grey wood, not a green canopy walk. The outline has no face, no mouth, and no speech. The posture is heaviness: a body that fell in the middle of listening, or the memory of that fall, risen at most to a kneel. It does not reach. It does not pursue.

The plate does not show a bottle, an extraction crew, or a monster assembled from extra limbs. Some canopy stewards still call this fatigue with a story. The picture lets that argument stand. It only fixes the look the witnesses already gave.

Asset reference: `assets/blue-litter-husk.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A scene plate for Echoforms as place plus repeated human event, not as an unrelated beast.

## Lore Connection

The Blue Litter Husk is sustained by pooled listener-overload on Blue Litter Underwood, most often in the deep groves near That Forest. Open Hand custom treats leaving the litter alone as the way the shape dissolves. The plate is the appearance, not the pursuit rumor and not a hunt. Failed listening is the history it carries.

## Notes

Featureless. Still. Not a predator. Do not add a speaking face.
