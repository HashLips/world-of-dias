# First Bowl Before the Story

## Name

First Bowl Before the Story

## Overview

First Bowl Before the Story is a digital image study of the hall where shelter comes before a new arrival’s history.

## Description

The plate shows long tables, kiln warmth, plain bowls, and blank slates waiting for later terms. Its order is deliberate: heat and a place to sit first, the story afterward.

It does not isolate the stew-mark chip or make a mark carry the hall’s meaning. The image stays with the room’s shelter-first practice and its practical welcome to Long Gate arrivals.

Asset reference: `assets/hearthvale-first-bowl-hall.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of mutual aid and second-beginning right made architectural.

## Lore Connection

The artwork reinforces the Hall’s shelter-first ethic without resolving who sleeps outside when the room is full.
