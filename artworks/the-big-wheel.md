# The Big Wheel

## Name

The Big Wheel

## Overview

*The Big Wheel* is pure white on pure black. A great spoked wheel fills the upper left, its hub a white disc, its rim strung with hanging gondolas—little open cabins with pitched roof frames and solid floors. A lattice tower braced with crossed struts rises from a white hill at the lower right, holding the axle. The whole wheel tilts as if seen from below and to the side, so it seems to lean out over the dark.

## Description

There is no gray anywhere. Every line is either light or absence. The spokes are thin white bars radiating from the hub; the gondolas are drawn as frames and floors with the black showing through their walls, which makes them read as lanterns or cages for light rather than cars. Eleven gondolas are visible, and the wheel runs off the top and left edges, so it is larger than the frame.

The tower is the heaviest shape: thick white struts with an X brace, a squared housing where the axle meets it, and a slope of white ground beneath that swells up from the bottom edge like a headland or a snow bank. The ground's upper edge is rough, almost torn.

There are no riders, no stars, no water. Just the wheel, its tower, and the night. It looks paused between turns, waiting for someone to climb up and set it going.

## Real-World Role

A landmark plate for a structure seen mostly at night as a ring of light against the dark.

## Lore Connection

Hollowmere's lantern hooks make its cliff look like it breathes in strings at night, and apprentices learn the town as the first fixed star on the Outer Rim's mental chart. The Big Wheel is the hand-turned lantern wheel on the headland above the net sheds. The plate shows it as boats would see it from the dark sea: a white ring, open gondolas, a braced tower on a pale hill. Local rule says the wheel turns only when someone is still out on the water, one slow revolution per hour, so crews can count time home by watching lit cabins rise and fall.
