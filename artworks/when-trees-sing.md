# When Trees Sing

## Name

When Trees Sing

## Overview

*When Trees Sing* shows a black silhouette on a dark rock, white triangle-eye tilted up, beneath a bright orange willow-like tree whose hanging lines carry pale mint leaves over blue ground and distant water.

## Description

Royal blue sky, white moon-slice, orange light pooling on the ground as if the tree paints the earth. The figure listens more than it acts.

Asset reference: `assets/when-trees-sing.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A Wabet/Verdant contemplative landscape: hearing as harvest ethic.

## Lore Connection

Verdant Reach teachers say some trees **sing**—not with mouths, with hanging frequency lines you can sit under until your hurry softens. Elyra's Open Hand lessons rhyme with this image: take calm, leave the song. Calen's Wabet chapter sits in the same emotional weather.
