# Music House

## Name

Music House

## Overview

*Music House* depicts a lean white figure in a tall black hat, one eye-line closed, playing a long golden flute across a purple cape-shape inside an olive circle.

## Description

Angular limbs, split black/white legs, reddish stops on the golden pipe. Theatrical and precise at once.

Asset reference: `assets/music-house.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A performer emblem: sound as structured worldcraft.

## Lore Connection

Resonant House ceremonies sometimes employ **passage flutes**—instruments said to tune a room before classification votes. The closed eye is intentional: hearing over seeing. Not every House admits the practice; the artwork keeps the rumor beautiful.
