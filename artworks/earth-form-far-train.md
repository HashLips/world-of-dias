# Earth Form Far Train

## Name

Earth Form Far Train

## Overview

*Earth Form Far Train* is a Brindle March far-travel plate: an orange-red figure floats in black void and drips toward a cyan-green curved world—the march image for a load of care sent far enough to seed a place without owning it.

## Description

Upper two-thirds stark black. Center: vibrant orange-red featureless humanoid in right-facing crouch or seated float, elongated, semi-liquid strands hanging from elbows, torso, and lower leg; bent legs point down. Bottom third: curved planet-edge of bright cyan water-shapes and lime-green landmasses under a dark teal atmospheric band. Directly below the figure, an irregular orange-red pool on the surface; small elongated orange droplets hang in the black between. Cosmic, seeding, quiet. Small white block mark top-right.

Asset reference: `assets/earth-form-far-train.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A fruit-road far-send plate: the train of care that arrives as drip and stain, not as conquest flag.

## Lore Connection

On Brindle March, **Earth Form Far Train** names the long linked load that carries form to a far edge. Orange drip is essence shared; the cyan-green curve is the receiving place; black void is the distance that forbids grabbing back too soon. Wonder is seeding without stamp. Dias march name—not an Earth geology claim, not an Earth song claim.
