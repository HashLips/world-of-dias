# Dias Emblem

## Name

Dias Emblem

## Overview

*Dias Emblem* is the official mark alone: a golden compass-star set in concentric rings, with a bright center and orbital spheres along the paths—no lettering.

## Description

An eight-pointed inner star and four long cardinal blades sit inside engraved rings. Small spheres rest on curved orbital lines. The metal reads hammered and beveled, lit from above-left. Transparent ground.

Asset reference: `assets/dias-emblem.png`.

## Real-World Role

Standalone brand emblem for icons, seals, favicons, and layouts that already show the World of Dias wordmark.

## Lore Connection

Keepers and street talk treat the mark as a frequency compass more than a map: center, ring, and directions that refuse to name Frequency Zero. It rhymes with Fracture memory and resonant charts without solving them.
