# Pale Motes of a Palewind

## Name

Pale Motes of a Palewind

## Overview

*Pale Motes of a Palewind* is a digital image study of a Palewind swarm-person in F432, shown as pale motes loosely condensed for social reading.

## Description

The figure appears as an ash-white particle cloud with subtle internal currents, loosely condensed into a rough humanoid outline. Motes thicken and thin across the silhouette; the “body” remains a gathered veil rather than solid flesh, while still reading as one coherent identity.

The plate keeps the subject as a single accountable person easing interaction by condensation, not as anonymous fog. Surrounding ornament and architecture in the frame remain decorative.
