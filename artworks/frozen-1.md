# Frozen

## Name

Frozen

## Overview

*Frozen* is an Elder Shelf storage plate: forty-two orange stem-leaf fruits sit each on a light-blue ice cube in a strict grid—the shelf habit of pausing ripeness without pretending cold is the same as care forever.

## Description

Medium-blue field. Grid of 6 columns and 7 rows: each unit a perfectly spherical bright orange fruit with thin brownish stem and single pointed green leaf up-right, atop a light-blue cube with white jagged icy highlights on the front face. Orderly, massed, clean. Small black square mark top-right.

Asset reference: `assets/frozen-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

An Elder Shelf phenomenon plate: freeze to pause; thaw with a plan; do not worship the grid.

## Lore Connection

On the Elder Shelf, **Frozen** names the cold-order look when fruit-forms are held on ice-cubes of local craft. Wonder is paused generosity. Leaving them frozen past need is emptied care. Dias shelf name.
