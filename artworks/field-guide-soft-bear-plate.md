# Field Guide Soft-Bear Plate

## Overview

Art brief for a book field-guide plate of Canopy Soft-Bears—text-identifiable even before paint.

## Description

**Subject:** adult Soft-Bear in fork-nest, moss-brown coat, rounded ears, one berry-stained paw.  
**Scale cue:** child-height shoulder vs fork trunk; optional Softfall drip.  
**Habitat backdrop:** dappled canopy, drip-moss, distant walkway—not a zoo cage.  
**Distinguishing feature:** velvet leaf-dust on fur; soft huff implied by open mouth, not roar.  
**Do not:** armor, riding saddle, or monster teeth.

Pairs with Canopy Soft-Bears inhabitant entry and Field Guide Pattern of Dias.

## Notes

Production may commission later; brief is canon so artists do not invent combat Soft-Bears.
