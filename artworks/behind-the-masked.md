# Behind the Masked

## Name

Behind the Masked

## Overview

*Behind the Masked* presents a black high-collar bust with a white oval mask, red triangular hat, and seven beaded gold tassels hanging like a veil over the face—a pink circle at the chest.

## Description

Cobalt field. Vertical face-line, arrow mark, tiny eye slits under high arcs. The beads read as measured, almost instrumental.

Asset reference: `assets/behind-the-masked.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A ceremonial identity emblem beyond simple anonymity.

## Lore Connection

Where *Masked* shows the many, *Behind the Masked* shows the **office behind the row**—pact celebrant or House-adjacent envoy wearing silence as jewelry. Sorel compact readers claim the beads keep speech honest; House rumor claims they keep speech classified. Both may be performing the same fear differently.
