# Dad

## Name

Dad

## Overview

*Dad* is a Driftfall guidance plate: a tall dark figure and a smaller one hold hands under an orange orb while a blue bordered circle tethers toward the smaller head—the pier hour when a larger walker shares a vision without stealing the walk.

## Description

Light-pink field. Undulating black ground. Right: tall slender silhouette in profile with a large white almond eye and a slight smile, spindly multi-fingered hands. Left: shorter similar figure looking up, large white eye, open mouth. They clasp hands at center. Three small birds—red bodies, white wings—one on the tall shoulder, one between them, one farther left. Behind: large translucent reddish-orange circle. Upper left: solid bright-blue circle in a thick irregular white border with a long white flowing tether curving down toward the smaller head. Mood: gentle guidance, shared wonder. Small white mark bottom-right.

## Lore Connection

In Driftfall, **Dad** is a kept craft-name for the handhold that teaches. The blue tether-orb is a shared vision or frequency-glimpse offered, not forced. Red-winged birds are pier witnesses. The orange orb is ordinary day-glow. Wonder is pedagogical softness: hold the hand, show the blue look, let the smaller walker keep their own mouth open with questions. The name is Dias family-road speech, not an Earth celebrity claim.
