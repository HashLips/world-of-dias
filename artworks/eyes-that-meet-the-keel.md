# Eyes That Meet the Keel

## Name

Eyes That Meet the Keel

## Overview

*Eyes That Meet the Keel* is a digital image study of Human-Eyed Whales as Nauw maritime survival ecology, not monsters.

## Description

A massive whale breaches or surfaces in Outer Rim fog—body scale larger than a skiff—eyes set forward and unusually attentive, reading almost human without becoming a face transplant. Coastal balance, not attack pose.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/human-eyed-whales.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of whales central to Nauw food continuity and maritime balance.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens Human-Eyed Whales without inventing speech, prophecy, or hunt quotas beyond established ecology.

## Notes

Gold runes or script on the whale body are plate noise unless lore later fixes them. Name is canon; the plate keeps non-human whale morphology with attentive eyes. Border framing only.
