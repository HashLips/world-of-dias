# Pink

## Name

Pink

## Overview

*Pink* is the Averra Isle plate for the salmon head on void. Large rounded pink form. Intersecting black eye arcs. Small black pupil. Beak-like taper. One sweeping neck line.

## Description

Solid dark charcoal-black ground. A single highly abstracted head and neck in flat salmon-pink or muted coral, three-quarter profile looking down and left. The eye is two intersecting black arcs with a small solid black pupil, lines extending slightly past their meetings—gestural, large, calm. A downward-curving nose line ends near a dark teardrop nostril; mouth is two short parallel curved horizontals; the face tapers to a sharp beak-like point. A small solid black teardrop sits where an ear would be. A bold sweeping black line curves up from lower right into the pink mass, splitting neck front from the rest. Small signature mark bottom left.

No body crowd. No landscape. The plate is the pink silhouette and the black eye-work alone.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Averra Isle. Pink is the kept head-craft of quiet looking.

## Lore Connection

Pink is Averra's name for the hour when the salmon head turns down-left on charcoal and the large almond eye holds without hunting. Local care treats the beak taper as a soft point, not a bite threat; the teardrop ear-mark is part of the recognition. Resonance is pink against void; memory is the intersecting arcs; wonder is how little line can still make a face that the isle trusts. The plate invents no dynasty. It keeps the head as a craft mark for attentive quiet.
