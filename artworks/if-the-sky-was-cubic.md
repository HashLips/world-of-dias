# If the Sky Was Cubic

## Name

If the Sky Was Cubic

## Overview

*If the Sky Was Cubic* opens a pale lilac sky filled with sharp cyan rectangular and trapezoid cloud-plates above overlapping green and tan hills, a bright chartreuse path winding into a fold, and one dark bird in a simple V over the valley.

## Description

Lower third: flat layered terrain—forest green, olive, earthy tan—cut by a vivid yellow-green path that narrows as it slips behind a hill. Upper sky: near-white lilac with blocky turquoise “clouds” that refuse soft edges. The bird sits centered in the pale band between hills and highest plates, wings curved, alone. No walker on the path. No village. The surreal calm is the point: geometry in the air, journey on the ground.
