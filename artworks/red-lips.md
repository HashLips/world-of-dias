# Red Lips

## Name

Red Lips

## Overview

*Red Lips* is the F610 plate for the pale profile with ribbon-red. Mint-white face. Thin closed black eye. Small sharp red lips. Red loops on the head. Long black neck. Olive field.

## Description

Muted olive-green ground. Vertical portrait, figure slightly left, facing right. Skin extremely pale greenish-white with light-gray geometric planes at cheek, jaw, temple. Face elongated and angular: one thin slanted black line as closed eye; simple black ear outline; small sharp vibrant red lips; straight prominent nose; pointed chin.

Instead of ordinary hair, thin flat vibrant red loops and ribbons drape the head—some across the forehead, some hanging behind, one long strand curving toward the chest. Neck long and thin merges into broad black shoulders/torso like a high-collared dark garment. Tiny black square glyph top-left. Serene, quiet, otherworldly calm.

Asset reference: `assets/red-lips.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for F610. Red Lips is the kept profile-craft of closed-eye red mouth and ribbon loops.

## Lore Connection

Red Lips is F610's name for the hour when the pale face keeps the eye closed as a thin black line and the small red mouth and ribbon loops carry the only heat. Local care says you do not demand the eye open for your comfort. Resonance is red on pale mint and olive; memory is the long black neck; wonder is the ribbon loops that are not hair and still belong. The plate invents no romance plot. It keeps the craft as quiet presence.
