# 25 Now

## Name

25 Now

## Overview

*25 Now* is a Nauw Outer Rim Seas register plate: two standing figures held between a red board and a green screen while a thin blue arch seats them as a counted pair. The plate is about reading a present moment without inventing a fifth story to fill the count.

## Description

The ground is bright cyan against a dark teal wall. Two featureless humanoids stand side by side. The left figure is split black-and-white; the right is split white-and-warm-orange. A thin luminous blue line arches over both heads like a shared seat of attention, with small geometric shards floating around the curve. Their lower edges fade into the floor as long white tapering rays, as if the moment is projecting forward rather than casting ordinary shadow.

To the left, a three-legged red rectangular board angles inward. A dark wavy serpent-like mark sits on its face, and two pale yellow rays spill across the cyan floor. To the right, a lime-green monitor on a stand angles the other way; its dark geometric eye-or-bird icon throws a wide pale-green pool of light. The composition is clinical and quiet: board on one side, screen on the other, two split bodies in the middle under one arch.

## Real-World Role

A now-count plate for Outer Rim talk—how many readings you may keep of a single moment before the count becomes gossip.

## Lore Connection

Around the Nauw Outer Rim Seas, **25 Now** is the habit of stopping at a counted present: board-mark and screen-mark agree on what is happening, and the twin figures stand for the two ways a witness can split a truth without lying. Locals say you may keep twenty-five honest now-readings of a harbor hour; a twenty-sixth is usually someone polishing the story. The red board holds the older, hand-placed mark. The green screen holds the sharper, later look. Neither is allowed to erase the other. The blue arch is the courtesy that keeps both readings under one count. When the floor-rays go white and clean, the moment has been received; when they smear, someone tried to sell the count as permanence.
