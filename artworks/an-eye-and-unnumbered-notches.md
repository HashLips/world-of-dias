# An Eye and Unnumbered Notches

## Name

An Eye and Unnumbered Notches

## Overview

*An Eye and Unnumbered Notches* is a Fruit-Road Reciprocity Peg as wood. The peg is hardwood. Notch cuts mark it. An eye is cut through it. The notches are not digits. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

A hardwood peg is the subject, cut so it can hang and so it can be notched. The eye is a hole through the wood, the opening a marker or a cord could use. It is an eye in the craft sense, not a gaze and not a face. Notch cuts sit along the peg. They are cuts. They are not digits. No numeral is carved beside them, and the count of cuts is not a number this record will read out. A tally that can still be unfinished does not become a solved sum because someone stares at the wood.

The peg is not shown hanging on a crate with a finished exchange attached. Channel repair, seed return, and labor hour are what notches can mean in the account. The plate does not label any cut with those words. A marker is not threaded through the eye. The eye is empty of that thread. The wood is the tally object before a comedy court snaps it for only ever recording what was taken.

No crew stands at it. No waystation steward is teaching restoration-before-pick. The picture is hardwood, notch cuts, and an eye, and the notches stay unnumbered.
