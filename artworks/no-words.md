# No Words

## Name

No Words

## Overview

*No Words* is the Cinder Nook plate for silence that still has a face. A smooth pink head turns three-quarters. Slot eyes and mouth stay quiet. A dark green echo stands behind. An orange-red disc hangs like a low sun.

## Description

Against deep chocolate-brown, a bald featureless humanoid is rendered in sharp cell-shaded pinks: bright bubblegum on the lit planes, muted magenta-purple in the jaw and cheek shadow. Eyes and mouth are only light-pink horizontal slots—calm, stoic, almost craft-smooth. No ears, no hair, no speech lines.

Directly behind and offset right, a near-black emerald-green silhouette matches the head exactly, a memory twin without a second mouth. A large solid orange-red circle sits mostly left behind the pink head, warm enough to read as halo or low sun without becoming a caption. A tiny white gate-like mark sits extreme top left.

Nobody's lips part. No banner text. The plate is the quiet face, the green echo, and the red disc—Cinder Nook's picture of meaning that refuses to become chatter.

## Real-World Role

A symbol plate for Cinder Nook. No Words is kept silence with a double, not a gag order written out.

## Lore Connection

No Words is the Nook's mark for the hour when the pink smooth face and the dark green echo agree to hold meaning without spilling it into speech. Local care treats slot features as enough expression; forcing talk is considered rough handling of memory. Resonance is the match between pink head and green twin; wonder is the orange-red disc that warms without explaining; memory is how the symbol is shown when a room needs quiet more than a clever line. The plate invents no vow ceremony. It keeps the polished silence as a recognizable handle.
