# Good Things

## Name

Good Things

## Overview

*Good Things* is a Nauw Outer Rim Seas path plate: a white walker with a mint scarf trails an orange shadow along a gray diagonal—the rim reminder that good things still cast a colored afterimage you must not ignore.

## Description

Deep purple-black field. Light gray flat diagonal path from lower left toward middle right. White simplified profile humanoid steps forward, right leg bent; single thin horizontal red eye-line; long light mint-green scarf/sash around the neck trailing far horizontally behind with two thin ribbon ends. Strikingly long vibrant orange shadow behind on the gray path, elongated and slightly amorphous. Solitary journey, quiet mystery. Small white gate logo bottom-right.

Asset reference: `assets/good-things.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A rim artifact plate: carry good things lightly; watch the orange shadow for what they cost the path.

## Lore Connection

On the Nauw Outer Rim Seas, **Good Things** is kept travel craft. Mint scarf is willingness in wind; orange shadow is the aftercare of luck. Wonder is moving gifts without pretending they leave no mark. Dias rim name.
