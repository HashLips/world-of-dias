# Double Echo

## Name

Double Echo

## Overview

*Double Echo* is an F200 measure plate: a tilted charcoal head balances a V-glass of red liquid while cobalt spheres ride a skewer and one falls toward a light-blue eye—the frequency craft of catching a second reading without spilling the first.

## Description

Mustard-yellow field, lighter wavy yellow at right. Lower center: dark charcoal head tilted back ninety degrees, mouth wide open upward, single almond eye light blue. On the open mouth balances a large pale V-shaped stemmed glass half-filled with solid vibrant red; a thin black skewer across the rim holds two bright cobalt spheres; a third cobalt sphere falls mid-air toward the forehead or eye. Precarious, ceremonial, sharp flat color. Small white glyph bottom-right.

## Real-World Role

A frequency artifact plate: hold the first measure in the mouth-glass; let the second blue echo land where the eye can still see.
