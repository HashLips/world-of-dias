# Winter Comes

## Name

Winter Comes

## Overview

*Winter Comes* is a person keeping still enough to be perched on. A dark silhouette figure squats low and wide on a white ground, knees apart, feet planted. Its long arms run straight out along the shoulders like a beam and then fold down so the hands clasp between the knees, outlined in white. A small black bird stands on each arm, one facing in, one facing out with its tail lifted. The figure's narrow head looks forward with two pale, half-closed eyes.

## Description

The palette is black, white, and soft gray. The ground is blank white, as if snow had erased the setting; gray shadows fall long and diagonal from the feet toward the lower left.

The figure's body is built for balance: a narrow torso, broad shoulders made almost entirely of arm, thighs spread wide in a deep squat. The arms are thick and smooth, forming a flat perch from elbow to elbow. The hands are the only place the plate draws detail—long fingers interlaced, picked out in a thin white line so they read against the black.

The two birds are small and ordinary, with pointed beaks and thin legs, one at each end of the arm-beam. They look settled. The figure does not look at them. It seems to understand that looking would make them leave.

## Real-World Role

A seasonal plate about the first cold days, when small birds land on anyone who holds still.

## Lore Connection

Elder Shelf is a clifftop band of wind-scoured trees ringed in gray stone, where no building is allowed and the Watch issues day passes for meditation and naming. When winter comes to the shelf, the small birds that live in the scoured trees lose their shelter in the first hard winds and begin landing on anyone who sits still in the ring long enough. The plate shows a sitter at that moment: arms made into a perch, hands clasped, eyes forward, two birds settled. Watch custom during the first cold week is to come in pairs, hold still for a long hour, and leave the birds wherever they choose to stay.
