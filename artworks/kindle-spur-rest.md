# Kindle Spur Rest

## Name

Kindle Spur Rest

## Overview

*Kindle Spur Rest* shows a secondary stop on the Ashen Hearthline at the hour of recheck. Three niches in a low ridge, a horn peg, bowls mouth-down, plain broth, hounds, tired heather, and people sitting close without much touch. Not a blaze.

## Description

The ridge is the color of a coal remembered, not a fire performed. Ash plain surrounds it. Three shelter niches are scooped into the slope. Bowls are stacked mouth-down so grit stays out. One bowl is turned up for broth that is only salt, root, and heat. A horn hangs on its peg, a call rather than a decoration. Hounds lie on the threshold stones, ribs slow, uninterested in rank. Purple heather tufts grow where the ash is thin enough for a root. Paler lanes nearby read as Cold Gaps, not as invitations.

People sit shoulder to shoulder and do not make a ceremony of contact. The plate does not show a speech, a fee, a laundry line, or a finished hearth. The hounds are still lying down, which is a measurement that can fail by morning. The picture does not promise that it won't.

Continuity here is tending, not a declaration that the hearth is done. The plate keeps that modesty. It does not name a keeper's face.
