# Kandas

## Name

Kandas

## Overview

*Kandas* is a Hollowmere night interior: vibrant blue armchair, black reading silhouette with long fingers on a cream open book, golden-brown lamp with bulbous glass chimney and orange-red flame, concentric warm glow, small seated silhouette on the brown floor, four dark panes of night window, murky brown-green walls.

## Description

Light is local—rings of yellow-brown around the lamp—leaving most of the room in study-dark. The small floor silhouette reads as child, doll, or quiet companion figure; it does not interrupt the page. No net loft gear in frame; the mood is domestic focus after loft work. Peaceful, not lonely.

A household craft plate: lamp, chair, and book as the night’s kept tools.

## Lore Connection

Hollowmere net-loft days end in rooms like this. Kandas names the blue chair-and-lamp set that makes reading honest after rope-hand work. Local rule: trim the wick before the page yellows; do not let the small floor companion become a trip hazard in the dark. Resonance gathers in the cream pages under real flame. Skipping the lamp for a harsh glare is said to thin the night’s memory.
