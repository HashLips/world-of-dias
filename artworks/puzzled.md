# Puzzled

## Name

Puzzled

## Overview

*Puzzled* is the Claimscar Yard plate for the lone red flag. Thin white pole. Unmarked red cloth waving. Teal field split light over dark. Long gray shadow.

## Description

Muted teal splits horizontal: lighter upper two-thirds, darker lower third as flat ground. Center-right, a stark thin white pole stands at the divide, angled slightly right. Atop it a solid warm red flag waves with two crests and a central dip, breeze reading right-to-left, no emblem on the cloth. A long thin dark gray shadow of the pole stretches toward bottom-left. Tiny black three-pillar gate icon sits far top-right.

No crowd arguing the meaning. The plate is the flag that marks without explaining—Yard puzzle held as a clean red shape.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Claimscar Yard. Puzzled is the kept craft of the unmarked red marker.

## Lore Connection

Puzzled is Claimscar's name for the hour when a red flag stands without a painted claim and the yard must sit with not-knowing. Local care says you do not invent a crest to end the discomfort early; the white pole and long shadow are enough honesty. Resonance is red on teal; memory is the empty cloth; wonder is how much a lone wave can ask. The plate invents no lawsuit. It keeps the marker as manners for unfinished meaning.
