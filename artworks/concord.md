# Concord

## Name

Concord

## Overview

*Concord* is an Aelwyn Canopy accord plate: a thin white line from a blue-violet bird-form passes through a red arched figure into a fallen black horned beast—the costly moment when agreement finally lands.

## Description

Two-tone gray field. Center: solid red humanoid in sharp backbend, white line-eye on the tilted head, one arm limp; a darker heart-shaped red blob where a white diagonal pierces the torso. The same thin white line runs from upper left—starting at a stylized blue-violet bird or fletching—down into the back of a black horned quadruped hunched below with dark red fluid at the neck and a white line-eye. High-contrast paper-cut drama. Small black block mark top-left.

## Real-World Role

A canopy phenomenon plate: when three parties finally share one line, name the cost instead of calling it free peace.

## Lore Connection

In the Aelwyn Canopy, **Concord** is a named look of accord-weather—rare, sharp, and never cheap. Bird, person-shape, and horned beast aligned on one white line means the quarrel ended, not that nobody bled meaning. Canopy rule: celebrate the stop, tend the spill, do not sell the line as proof you were always right. Wonder is costly agreement. The name is Dias canopy speech.
