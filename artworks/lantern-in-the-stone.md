# Lantern in the Stone

## Name

Lantern in the Stone

## Overview

*Lantern in the Stone* is the visual plate of the **Glasswake Lantern** as locals name it: a weathered maritime signal lamp physically fused into dark coastal rock, shown dormant—no flame, no staged ghost.

## Description

A paraffin-style ship lantern sits half-claimed by jagged charcoal stone. Brass or copper housing shows verdigris and salt crust. Thick glass, metal ribs, and a top handle remain readable. Stone and mineral growth pass through and around the frame so the lamp cannot be read as merely wedged. The field is plain and quiet, emphasizing corrosion and fusion over spectacle.

Asset reference: `assets/glasswake-lantern.jpg`.

## Role

Outside-fiction plate for the Prime Relic site at Glasswake Rock. Documents material tell and Fracture fusion without illustrating the disputed captain-figure or a confirmed wreck.

## Lore Connection

Anchors **Glasswake Lantern** and the landmark **Glasswake Rock**. Pulse light and shipboard echoes belong to **Glasswake Pulse** and are not shown as continuous glow here. Captain folklore stays in **The Captain Who Reaches**. Vessel fate stays open in **Disappearance of the Glasswake**.

## Notes

If a later plate shows a pulse or figure, keep this dormant view as the baseline object record.
