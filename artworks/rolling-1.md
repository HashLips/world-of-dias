# Rolling

## Name

Rolling

## Overview

*Rolling* is the Songripe Isles plate for the white arch in navy. Extreme backbend. Head between legs. One arm arcs up, four fingers splayed. Hard blue shadows.

## Description

Deep navy blue void. A spindly solid-white entity plants two extremely long thin legs; torso arches forward and down until a small oval head hangs low between the legs facing the ground. From upper back/shoulder a single long thin arm reaches up and back in a graceful arc; the hand opens four elongated tapering fingers. Foreground foot shows three sharp-toothed digits; the other foot is in profile. Long hard-edged muted lighter-blue shadows stretch bottom-right. Tiny white gate/bridge glyph upper left. Impossible stretch held as isle craft, silent and tense.

Asset reference: `assets/rolling-1.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Songripe Isles. Rolling is the kept craft of the named deep arch.

## Lore Connection

Rolling is the Isles' name for the hour when the white body rolls its stretch into the deep arch and the four fingers open on navy. Local care says you practice until the shadows go clean; you do not force a guest into the shape for spectacle. Resonance is white on navy with blue shadow; memory is the head between pillar legs; wonder is how far a roll can go without a floor drawn. The plate invents no circus plot. It keeps the craft as isle manners for extreme honest posture.
