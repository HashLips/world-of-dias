# Shade Cloth over Outer Market

## Name

Shade Cloth over Outer Market

## Overview

*Shade Cloth over Outer Market* is a digital image study of outer-ring market sheds in Veloria, the stone-and-timber handshake with travelers.

## Description

On the left, a long timber shed with a heavy beamed roof runs into the distance on wooden posts. From the beams hang balance scales with brass pans and pots of flowering plants and trailing greens. Below, open crates hold leafy greens and heaps of round fruit and vegetables in reds, oranges, and pale greens.

Across the cobbled lane, faded blue and sand-colored shade cloths are strung on poles in overlapping sails, and a second shed row runs along the right. An empty wooden cart waits on the cobbles. In the haze at the far end, taller pale buildings rise beyond the market, hinting at the inner rings.

The scene is between rushes: goods out, no crowds. The decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/outer-ring-market-sheds.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Veloria's outer-ring market sheds: timber, cobble, scales, shade cloth, and pot-signal flower pots as working street furniture.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the outer-ring row of Nauw Layered Civic Architecture (market sheds on cobble, timber beams, flower-pot signal streets) within Veloria City's medieval outer ring. It adds no new market law or named stalls.

## Notes

Produce is unlabeled; do not identify it as a specific fruit or lot. The hanging pots are shown as street furniture without stating any signal. The far buildings are atmospheric, not a map of the second ring. Not a depiction of Mercer Shed or Softfruit Table Hall.
