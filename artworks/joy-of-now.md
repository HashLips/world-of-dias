# Joy of Now

## Name

Joy of Now

## Overview

*Joy of Now* gathers four warm bodies on tiered pink ground: a bright red-pink figure seated cross-legged at center, a dull brown figure lunging to touch that chest, an orange-red observer leaning in, and another orange-red figure upright with one arm raised high, long geometric shadows, a small dark-brown circle in the upper left.

## Description

Upper rose-pink and lower pale pink split on a soft wavy line. Shadows stretch sharp orange-brown. The seated figure’s single black eye-slit stays calm. The touch at chest reads as check or gift of attention, not violence. The raised arm points the present outward—now, here—without a drawn crowd. Mood is ritual quiet joy.

Asset reference: `assets/joy-of-now.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A present-tense symbol plate: joy as attended stillness, not noise.

## Lore Connection

Coral Strand workshops know when a perched idea becomes a shared now. Joy of Now is the circle that touches the calm chest and signals upward so the moment is marked. Local rule: the seated one may decline the touch by turning the eye-slit aside; force empties the joy. Resonance prefers the raised arm that points to a real task beginning, not to empty sky.

## Notes

Four figures, chest touch, raised arm, pink tiers, brown circle.
