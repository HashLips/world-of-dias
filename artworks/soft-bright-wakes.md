# Soft Bright Wakes

## Name

Soft Bright Wakes

## Overview

*Soft Bright Wakes* is a digital image study of Brightspine Grazers moving slowly along a stable gradient, leaving soft bright wakes.

## Description

A slow line of pale quadruped outlines made of denser white-gold light, heads lowered as if grazing the field itself. Spines rise in flame-like tufts a shade warmer than the bodies. Streaks of brightness trail behind and beneath their legs across the luminous ground. Soft blue-gray haze banks at the lower corners; the farthest animals fade into glow.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/brightspine-grazers.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of F200 pastoral ecology without soil: herd-beings that follow stable luminosity.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the established outline-of-light body and warm spines without settling the failed-but-persistent cattle metaphor or inventing domestication.

## Notes

Lowered heads read as 'grazing' coherence, not eating grass. Not Earth deer or cattle. Border ornaments are framing only.
