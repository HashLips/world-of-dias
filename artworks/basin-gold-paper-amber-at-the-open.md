# Basin-Gold Paper, Amber at the Open

## Name

Basin-Gold Paper, Amber at the Open

## Overview

*Basin-Gold Paper, Amber at the Open* is one Gilding Candy Twist: thumb-sized candy in basin-gold paper, amber where the paper is open. The paper has no printing. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

One twist sits on wood, about the size of a thumb. The paper is basin-gold, closed at the ends, and open along the middle so the candy shows. Where the paper is open, the candy is amber. Nothing is printed on the paper: no word, no street, no hour, and no mark that could be followed. The paper is pretty. It is not a map.

The candy is hard sweet in a wrapper. It is not a light, not a kept memory, and not a mood drawn as weather. The plate does not show it melting, and it does not show a second effect. There is no hearttide lean standing up in it, and no courage written on the gold. Amber is the candy where the paper gives way. It is not a lamp, and it is not a lure drawn as alchemy.

No hand unwraps it. No pocket is in the frame. One twist, basin-gold, amber at the open, unprinted.

A still life of gold that is allowed to be candy. The wrapper does not become a map.
