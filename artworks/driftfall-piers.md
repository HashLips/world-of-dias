# Driftfall Piers at Sunset

## Name

Driftfall Piers at Sunset

## Overview

*Driftfall Piers at Sunset* shows working timber at the water's edge: piers, skiffs, larger hulls, coiled rope, and crates, with the sun low on the water. It is a lived dock, not a tourist boardwalk. No close people. No pier numbers. No captain.

## Description

The planks curve out over the water and meet other lengths of repaired timber. Rope lies in a coil near barrels and crates. Small boats sit against the piles. Larger hulls rise beyond them, patched and rigged, with cloth and flags moving in the same wind. The light is sunset, bright on the water and warm on the wood. The beauty is the machine still being used, not a promenade dressed for visitors.

Nobody is close enough to name. The plate does not offer a face, a crew portrait, or a person in command. It does not paint numbers on the piers, and it does not invent a captain to explain the flags.
