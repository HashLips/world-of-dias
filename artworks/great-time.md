# Great Time

## Name

Great Time

## Overview

*Great Time* is a Veloria City count plate: a sturdy white smiling vessel-form wears a flared cap and floating tally lines—the city icon for cheer that stays countable and does not erase the tally.

## Description

Solid black field. Centered white stencil-like form: broad roughly rectangular body with flat base; interior black cutouts make an asymmetrical face—wide crescent smile arc below, large circular eye cutout with center dot left, smaller vertical teardrop cutout right. Thick curved neck rises to a flared cap/lid. Behind/above neck: four thin white lines—three slanted verticals crossed by one longer diagonal, like a tally. Whimsical, mysterious, manufactured-clean.

Asset reference: `assets/great-time.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A city artifact plate: mark a great time with a tally; keep the smile from wiping the count.

## Lore Connection

In Veloria City, **Great Time** is a kept icon for celebrated hours that still owe a readable mark. Tally lines are the local rule against uncounted hype. Wonder is cheerful accountability. Dias city name.
