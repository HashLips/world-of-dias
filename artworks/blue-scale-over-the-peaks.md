# Blue Scale over the Peaks

## Name

Blue Scale over the Peaks

## Overview

*Blue Scale over the Peaks* is a digital image study of the Great Blue Bird as reported giant avian presence over peaks: blue scale plumage, height, and route-warning scale.

## Description

A massive blue bird grips a sharp mountain tip. Plumage is deep textured blue with fine linework that reads almost scale-like across wings and back; pale hooked beak; sharp golden-brown eye; dark talons locked on stone. Needle peaks, dark evergreens on lower slopes, valley water, swirling parchment-sky linework. A much smaller distant flyer emphasizes scale.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/great-blue-bird.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Great Blue Bird lore as Hearthline-edge fear ecology: stay on marked routes; treat open-zone bravado as avoidable risk.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the established Great Blue Bird image without closing taxonomic certainty. Cultural impact stays; species law stays open.
