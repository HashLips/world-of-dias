# A Crack, Not a Face

## Name

A Crack, Not a Face

## Overview

*A Crack, Not a Face* is a Kiln Laughter Firing Token as clay. The token is a disc. One crack crosses it in the shape of a smile. The crack is not a face. Nobody is in the picture.

## Description

A fired clay disc sits alone, small enough to be a token and not a bowl. The clay is the material: fired, not a coin of metal and not a paper pass. One crack runs in a smile's curve. That is the glaze-crack the yard knows. It is one crack. It is not a face. No eyes are set above it, no portrait is modeled, and no expression has been sculpted into a person. A smile-shaped crack can be read too quickly as a character. The plate keeps it as a crack in glaze that still holds a disc together.

The token is not warm in any way a picture can prove. Successful firings leave warmth in the account. This frame does not show a kiln, a hand testing heat, or a place at a shared firing. It shows the disc and the one crack. Other crazing, if the clay has it, does not become a second symbol. The smile-shaped crack is the mark. It does not grow a mouth that speaks.

No apprentice laughs beside it. No one is selling it as instant mastery. The picture is clay, one smile-shaped crack, and the refusal to call that crack a face.
