# What We Need

## Name

What We Need

## Overview

*What We Need* is a reach between buildings. Two dark green towers tilt at odd angles under an orange sky that gives way to a blue band. Out of a round black hole low in the left tower, a long, thick dark arm stretches all the way across the gap and opens a hand of five long, clawed fingers over the edge of the right-hand roof. On top of the left tower, a small dark figure sits with knees up at the very edge, watching.

## Description

The towers are rendered as slabs with grids of dark windows down one face. The left one leans; the right one is cropped and seen from above. Two thin red rods stick up from the left tower's roof. Two tall red poles rise from the right roof, running off the top of the frame, and from the upper corner a few dark red strands, like cables or hair, sweep diagonally across the orange sky.

The arm is pure dark, smooth, and much too long—it emerges from the hole as wide as a body and tapers across half the plate before splaying into fingers. The fingers are bony and hooked, and their shadows fall across the right roof.

The watcher on the rooftop is tiny by comparison, crouched, elbows on knees, white eye slit turned down. They do not seem frightened. They seem to be checking that whatever is being reached for gets where it is going.

## Real-World Role

A city-care plate about unsigned help passing between buildings, and the neighbor who watches it arrive.

## Lore Connection

The Anonymous Drop Wall is the brick stretch in Veloria where couriers leave chips and papers in left-side drops without signatures—ordinary unsigned care. What We Need is the name ring residents give to one particular round drop, set low in a leaning tower, from which a long dark reach is said to stretch across to the next roof when something urgent is left there. The plate shows that reach and the rooftop watcher who always seems to be present. Drop Wall custom is the same as ever: nothing is signed, nothing is asked, and the watcher never tells who left what.
