# Moving

## Name

Moving

## Overview

*Moving* isolates a Brindle traveler: absolute black angular silhouette facing right, elongated limbs, pointed flat feet, waist bent sharp, long thin pole over the shoulder with a rounded knotted sack hanging aft, hand gripping the forward staff, tiny black comb-like mark upper-right, endless cerulean field.

## Description

No road drawn—blue is the distance. Bundle is modest. Mood is purposeful burden, legendary simple.

Brindle March fruit roads know Moving as the agreed silhouette of a load that still thanks the lean. Local rule: knotted bundle stays aft; do not swing it into oncoming walkers. Resonance prefers blue-open courtesy. Blocking the stride to demand gossip empties the march.
