# Egg Run

## Overview

*Egg Run* is an F120 first-contact plate: a massive white bird strides on while a bright orange hatch-form rises from white spill and grips the bird’s leg—the frequency lesson that a new echo must catch a living pace without stopping the walk.

## Description

Dark brown/maroon upper field; medium-grey ground. Large white bird—lower body, jagged grey-blue tail layers, thin charcoal legs, long webbed three-toed feet with white claws—moves right-to-left. Behind: pale fleshy-pink shell fragments and a stark white liquid pool. From the pool a slender vibrant orange humanoid rises in profile, long arm clamping the bird’s right leg above the foot. Simple dark shadows lower right. Surreal, urgent, sparse.
