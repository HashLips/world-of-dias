# Perform

## Overview

*Perform* is the Nauw Outer Rim Seas plate for the card held above the sunburst. Deep purple neck. Orange-yellow eight-point radial. Small black rectangle in three long fingers. Chartreuse field.

## Description

Solid bright lime-chartreuse background. From a rectangular platform edged in pinkish-red, a tall deep-purple neck rises through a bright orange-yellow eight-pointed radial—rounded spokes like a flat flower or sunburst seated where neck meets base. The purple head tilts sharply back and up; a white curved eye-shape looks toward the upper left; a black notch marks nose/mouth; a small white ear-curve and flat black hair-cap finish the head.

A thin purple arm enters from the left; three long pointed digits hold a small solid black rectangle like a card or chip delicately above the gaze. Below the pink rim, a large solid purple base area anchors the composition. No audience ring. No stage lights drawn. The plate is the held black card, the upward look, and the sunburst collar as the Rim Seas' picture of a performance that is study first.
