# Counted Moth Panes

## Name

Counted Moth Panes

## Overview

*Counted Moth Panes* is a digital image study of Champagne Glass-Moths clustering where F200 striation lines meet: thin glass panes of held light.

## Description

Seven small moth-forms hover across a parchment field ruled with fine gold geometric lines—triangulated seams with bright nodes at intersections. Each moth carries translucent golden wing panes veined like thin glass filled with held light, slender bodies, and fine trailing legs. They gather toward the junctions rather than open space.

Decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/champagne-glass-moths.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Glass-Moths as intimate F200 beauty—the creatures children 'count' to learn focus.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the established pane-wing look and seam-clustering habit without inventing powers, lifecycle, or harvest use.

## Notes

Moth count in the plate is composition, not a counting rule. Gold lattice represents F200 striation seams, not a fixed map. Border ornaments are framing only.
