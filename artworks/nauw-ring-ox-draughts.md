# Doorway Horns on the Ring Haul

## Name

Doorway Horns on the Ring Haul

## Overview

*Doorway Horns on the Ring Haul* is a digital image study of Veloria's ring-ox draughts at work, large everyday power on city stone.

## Description

Two massive oxen fill the left and center of the frame, shoulders swelling high and horns sweeping wide and up in curves as broad as doorway arches. Their heads carry engraved brow bands; heavy chain harness and round brass medallions hang across their chests and run back to a deep plank wagon on spoked wheels.

They stand on worn cobbles beside a towering ashlar wall pierced by a carved arched doorway, which sets the scale: the oxen read as architecture-sized animals working inside a city of monuments. To the left, stacked roofs and spires of Veloria rise in haze.

The plate keeps the animals calm and heavy, with no fireworks or alarm in the scene. The decorative border is framing only.

Asset reference: `assets/nauw-ring-ox-draughts.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A visual record of Nauw Ring-Ox Draughts as the oversized draught oxen used for the heaviest Veloria ring-hauls.

## Lore Connection

The artwork thickens the established ring-ox image (shoulder taller than most adults, horns wide as doorway arches, slow, immensely strong, calm) without adding new uses or ring assignments.

## Notes

Canon says the horns are often capped; this plate shows them bare, which is allowed and not a rule. The wall and doorway are generic Veloria stone, not a named gate. No new traits for the breed.
