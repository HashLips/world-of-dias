# Bee Flowers

## Name

Bee Flowers

## Overview

*Bee Flowers* tiles white star petals, orange centers, and black-and-yellow bee-marks on a peach ground. Pattern joy for leans and ladle courts. It is thank-you weather in cloth form: dense, warm, countable, uninterested in heroism. Children can use it. A march can use it. A House looking for a crest will be disappointed, which is a recommendation.

## Description

Overlap without fuss. Bees drawn as graphic signs, not as specimens, so nobody is tempted to file a new species from a textile. Apricot gaps breathe between the stars. Stripes ask to be counted, and counting is the Brindle game: three, not a lucky fourth, not a contest. The warmth is doing good work. It is not forced cheer. Nothing in the repeat pretends the road was easy. It pretends, accurately, that after a true thanks the ditch can look like a seat.

## Real-World Role

A hospitality print, textile-adjacent, for F432 road stops and Softfruit spaces. It softens a wall without softening the standard. Hang it where packs come off shoulders, not where a stamp is trying to look friendly.

## Lore Connection

Load-Share Clover’s green-apple ease, ladle mint’s ordinary sweetness, joy-bees as a cousin motif that must not be merged into a single taxonomy of “the bee of Dias.” The pattern is manners you can rest your eyes on after the third thank-you, when laughter has come back at a useful volume. Extractive charm has no stripe here. Every bee is working. None of them is a bribe.

Wonder in a floral mood. Common on purpose.
