# Valve Hand on Soft Mark

## Name

Valve Hand on Soft Mark

## Overview

*Valve Hand on Soft Mark* is a digital image study of Cress Adel handling The Soft Mark: practical gas checks, windburn, scarves against gondola draft.

## Description

Cress is shown waist-up, focused left. The plate depicts a windburned handler with short, dark, textured curly hair and a weathered face fully visible. A thick dark scarf wraps the neck only, frayed ends trailing. Dark textured jacket or vest under a harness of leather straps and buckles. Her gloved hand turns a brass valve on pipe fittings in the lower left. At right, The Soft Mark reads as a ribbed envelope above a dense brass tank-and-pipe gondola array.

Parchment ground. Decorative border is framing only.
