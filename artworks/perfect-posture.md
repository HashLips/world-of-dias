# Perfect Posture

## Name

Perfect Posture

## Overview

*Perfect Posture* is the Wabet plate for the chair loop. An orange figure arches into a tall narrow backbend. A pale salmon chair stays straight. Black void around. One white glint on the tucked head.

## Description

Solid black background. The figure is vibrant solid orange, seated on a minimal chair yet radically bent: torso and neck stretch up into a tall narrow loop before curving down, head a simple downward oval tucked low near the knees. A small sharp white glint marks the smooth head. One leg angles sharp with a blocky foot planted; one thin orange line-arm drops to the floor in a simplified touch. Another orange shape folds near the knees.

The chair is light pinkish-beige or pale salmon—thin square seat frame, single tall perfectly straight backrest rising into the open space the arch creates. A faint dark-gray horizontal shadow stretches left from the chair base, the only ground plane. No coach, no mirror, no crowd. The plate is extreme bend held against a straight backrest.

## Real-World Role

An artifact plate for Wabet. Perfect Posture is the kept chair-craft of the named bend.

## Lore Connection

Perfect Posture is Wabet's name for the hour when the orange body loops over the pale straight chair-back and the white head-glint stays calm. Local care says perfect here means honest to the local rule of the bend, not vanity for a street audience. Resonance is the contrast of curved orange and straight salmon line; memory is the tucked head near the knees; wonder is how much height the loop can take in a black void without breaking the chair. The plate invents no contest medal. It keeps the posture as craft you practice until the glint is quiet.
