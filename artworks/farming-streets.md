# Farming Streets

## Name

Farming Streets

## Overview

*Farming Streets* is an Aelwyn Canopy path-crop plate: a cyan-blue tender-form moves over black dashed roads while yellow tethers guide its limbs—the canopy craft of farming the street lines without treating walkers as puppets.

## Description

Pale cream sky-ground. Three black roads with white dashed centers recede; a vibrant cyan-blue featureless geometric humanoid strides or floats over the central road, limbs splayed dancer-like. Thin bright yellow strings attach to wrists, elbows, back, and feet, rising out the top edge. Two jagged dark olive-brown mountain ranges low on the horizon. Surreal, clinical, guided. Small dark square mark top-right.

Asset reference: `assets/farming-streets.jpg`.

## Real-World Role

A canopy flora-manners plate: cultivate the road-edges; never yank the yellow lines as ownership of a body.

## Lore Connection

In the Aelwyn Canopy, **Farming Streets** names the pale cream growing medium and dashed-road plantings that feed path life. Yellow tethers in the plate are guide-lines for seasonal training of growth and gait—consent craft, not marionette cruelty. Blue form is the caretaker silhouette. Wonder is streets that feed without enslaving motion.
