# Salt Light in Quiet Cloister

## Name

Salt Light in Quiet Cloister

## Overview

Salt Light in Quiet Cloister is a digital image study of the Averra Isle archive-and-meeting space where memory is selectively kept.

## Description

Stone arches hold sparse shelves in salt light. The air feels of the sea without becoming a mainland harbor. No person enters the frame, and the room reads as a listening archive-and-meeting space rather than a public gallery.
