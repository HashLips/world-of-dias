# The Late Bruise on Lent Grass

## Name

The Late Bruise on Lent Grass

## Overview

*The Late Bruise on Lent Grass* is a digital image study of the ordinary freight plane landing on the Lent Strip in rural Wabet.

## Description

The plate shows a blunt high-wing freighter with two engines, a peel-green metal hull, and visible rivets, sitting on the grass of the lent strip. Its wheels flatten the grass. A hedge and ditch mark the field, with peeled stakes at the corners and no tower. The cockpit glass is empty, so the plate does not identify a pilot. The craft still has seats for a pilot and one other. The finish here is brighter than the dull nose the entry names. That brightness is the plate, not a change to the craft.

Asset reference: `assets/late-bruise-lent-strip.jpg`

## Real-World Role

A visual record of The Late Bruise using the grass landing field that Lent Strip lends in rural Wabet.

## Lore Connection

The artwork connects the freighter's peel-green hull and two-engine form to the landing field's ordinary grass, hedge, ditch, peeled stakes, and absent tower without turning the strip into a port.

## Notes

The image identifies the machine and the field, not anyone in the windows.
