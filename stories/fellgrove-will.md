# Fellgrove Will

## Premise

Jorin Fell dies of age with no family. His will leaves Fellgrove to Frederick Lumens—who is ecstatic to own the farm that matches his apple gift.

## Story

Jorin’s passing was not a storm. It was a morning without the scrape of his pruning knife.

The will was plain, witnessed the Wabet way that prefers clear care over spectacle: Fellgrove’s house, tools, rows, and market name to **Frederick Lumens**, who had worked the gift into the soil and eaten at the old man’s table when rumor wanted him gone. No children. No cousins with better paper. Only a boy from Driftfall who had arrived with empty hands and left cyan light in a mountain rumor.

Frederick stood in the first row he had ever touched for Jorin and cried in the practical way of someone handed a future. Ecstatic—yes—because the mixture was perfect in a way campfire bands had never described: a farm of his own, and a gift that made apples answer.

Bram Orlen’s lane did not celebrate. Rivalry does not end when a will is read. It only changes who owns the brighter crates.

Frederick slept under his own roof that night—not Vorrith’s hollow, not a stranger’s hedge—and woke already planning which trees to touch at dawn.
