# Poles Before Quiet Bands

## Name

Poles Before Quiet Bands

## Premise

A Softfruit host teaches band difference by starting with F200 and F500 poles before letting guests name quieter ledger bands.

## Story

Guests wanted F610 gossip and F840 comfort stories first. The host laid bright-bar and void-notch glyphs on the cloth.

“Learn the poles. Coherence that weighs. Absence that weighs. Home sits between without being either. Then we speak echo, hearttide, reveal, ember, unfinished—without turning them into districts.”

Fruit followed. The chapbook opened to F432’s home square last, on purpose—so no one mistook the whole of Dias for one city’s noon.
