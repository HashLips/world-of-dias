# A Walker Day in Wabet

## Premise

A canopy-edge walker spends a Softfall day on fruit ledges, unclaimed breakfast honesty, and a choir hush that lasts one beat too long.

## Story

The drip started before dawn—Softfall doing its quiet work. Len left a fruit on the Soft-Bear ledge without announcing generosity. The bear’s huff answered like a neighbor’s hello.

At the kindness table someone had taken the unclaimed breakfast portion. The bowl was empty in the honest way, not the greedy way; crumbs said hunger, not performance. Len refilled it and did not ask who.

Midday walking was bark-tea air and wagon-wave practice toward the eastbound edge. A child raced soft-step on a practice patch of damp earth and lost happily.

Evening brought choir-frogs. The chorus rose, fell, then husked one beat longer than weather usually asks. Len stopped talking. A stranger on the path matched the hush without being told. Storm, or manners. Either way, good.

Len slept under drip-moss sound and wanted another day of nothing that needed a House name.
