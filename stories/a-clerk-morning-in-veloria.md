# A Clerk Morning in Veloria

## Premise

A second-ring clerk begins a stamp-ordinary day with baths, Softfruit manners, and one flea itch that might mean nothing.

## Story

Mira washed at Ringwash before the queue thickened. Soap-citrus, ladle clink, someone humming last fair’s moth song too early for politics. She kept her voice below the whisper-ban—whether the ban was real or bath legend—and left with her hair damp and her soft address ready.

Softfruit Hall took fruit-share, not coin. An Axiomorph reset the plate geometry after the rush; Mira did not mock it. She practiced the three-script manners card in her head the way orchard kids practiced soft-step.

At the desk the first tag was easy: cargo, VEL-clean, no exile weather. The second tag made her palm itch—ledger fleas in the eaves again, or stamp-heavy omen. She explained the form once without sighing. The petitioner almost smiled.

On the walk home a Banner Moth the wrong citrus-shade clung to a pot. Mira lifted it with a soft brush, the way vendors teach. Probably dye dust. Probably.

She wanted tomorrow’s bath already.
