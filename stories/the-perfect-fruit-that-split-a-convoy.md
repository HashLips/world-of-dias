# The Perfect Fruit That Split a Convoy

## Name

The Perfect Fruit That Split a Convoy

## Premise

A near-perfect Lucky Fruit appears in a Wabet-bound crate. Half the convoy wants market glory. Half wants to leave it uncut at Open Hand Way. Tomas Share has to choose what “welcome” is worth.

## Story

They argued under shade that still smelled like canopy. Someone offered double VEL. Someone offered a knife and a prayer. Tomas stranded the fruit in the station’s public bowl and walked the rest east lighter and still employed by future waystations. The buyers who waited for the glory crate found only a story—and a bill for the shade they used while shouting.
