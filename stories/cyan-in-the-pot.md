# Cyan in the Pot

## Premise

Out of options and aching with hunger, Frederick accepts Vorrith’s bargain, hands over Roadglow, and watches the True Blue flicked into the Still Pot—cyan glow, stirring, shared bowls—while Vorrith looks briefly younger and Frederick tastes surprising goodness without feeling changed yet.

## Story

Frederick had no hire left, no marks that mattered, and a stomach that had stopped believing in tomorrow’s berries. The broth was intrigue and meal in the same steam.

"I accept," he said, and hated how small the words sounded in Stillhollow.

He placed Roadglow in Vorrith’s thin hand. The exile turned the shard, inspecting fracture and blue as if reading a recipe only he could taste. Something like a smile touched his mouth—not kind, not cruel. Satisfied.

A flick of one thin finger sent the crystal into the Still Pot.

A silent hum rose as it sank. The broth bubbled without roar. Blue emphasized, then spread, until the whole vessel held a light cyan luminescence—glow like held sky under stone. Vorrith took his stirring rod and worked the mix with the patience of someone maintaining a heart.

Frederick’s legs wanted the cave mouth. He stayed, hesitant, eyes fixed on the light.

Vorrith ladled a scoop into a bowl and set it before him. "Sit. Enjoy." He filled a second bowl for himself.

They drank.

Frederick found it surprisingly tasty—heat, mineral sweet, something that filled the hollow weeks had carved. Across the table, Vorrith’s dark presence did not become human-soft, yet the lines of him read younger, rejuvenated, as if the cyan had paid an exile’s private debt in front of a witness.

"Do you feel different?" Vorrith asked after a while.

"No," Frederick said.

"Give it some time."
