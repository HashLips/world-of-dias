# Gray Post Waiting Week

## Name

Gray Post Waiting Week

## Premise

Averra’s pier absents itself. A reckless crew curses the isle. Gray Post Wait Captains make tea and keep Crown Ash as a bearing. On a later calm the Post returns like a held breath. Contracts resume. Nobody makes a conquest speech, and the damp-studio rumor is allowed to stay rumor.

## Story

Fog sat where the planks had been, not dramatic fog, the kind that looks like a turned shoulder. The reckless crew took it personally. They circled. They spent language on timber. A Wait Captain they had mocked as timid put the kettle on and aimed their talk, when they talked, at Crown Ash, the pale notch that still caught last light and still meant *you know where you are, which is not the same as arrived*. Courtesy tokens stayed in pockets. Pockets were the correct place for a permission you had not been given.

The week was boring on purpose. Boredom was the craft. Someone tried to row “just to see” and was talked down with a story about fuel and about a client who did not need a heroic absence. Crown Ash held. The Hearthline, on the cool days, stitched a low warmth inland that nobody was invited to follow. Damp-studio talk rose and was not fed. The captains had learned that a rumor you chase onto the isle becomes a lie you owe to whoever funded the chase.

On the fifth calm the Gray Post was simply there, gray finger, dry boot-sound, bollard cold enough to believe. It did not apologize. The patient boats moored lightly, as if the pier might still change its mind, because that is how you moor to a thing that inhales. Contracts resumed in ordinary voices. No flag was planted. The reckless crew came in quieter and called it luck. The Wait Captains let them. Luck was a smaller word than manners, and smaller words were all the week had earned.
