# Turned From Every Gate

## Premise

After Bram Orlen, other Wabet hands turn Frederick away for the same reason. With no one willing to hire him, he chooses to go to the presence that will seek the fragment—and try a trade.

## Story

Word moved softer than Driftfall gossip and faster than fairness.

At the next farm the woman did not ask to see the bag. She smelled rumor on him. "Grey shoulder business. Keep walking." A share-row crew offered water and pointed west-north with orchard courtesy that felt like exile. A boy selling knives said the cook quiet did not hunt Wabet if Wabet did not light blue in the open—then stared at Frederick’s shirt as if the cloth were glass.

No hire. No bunk. No wage that could pretend Roadglow was ordinary scrap.

Frederick sat under a tree that had fed him three nights and held the wrapped stone without unwrapping it. The dream of field work had been a bridge to later frequencies. The bridge was closed by the very gift that made him special on a dock.

So he changed the question.

If no one wanted him because of what he carried, he would go to the one who did. Presence in the mountain. Vorrith Kael. Maybe not a job. Maybe a trade—shard for something a boy from Sorel could survive on, or travel on, or become with.

Fear named itself clearly. So did stubborn brightness. He asked three different walkers for the grey shoulder and got three careful answers that agreed on direction if not on wisdom.

He packed his thin bag, checked the cloth around Roadglow, and turned toward **Ashcalm Rise**—not because Wabet had welcomed him, but because refusal had finally drawn a map.
