# Four Bowls on the Fruit Road

## Name

Four Bowls on the Fruit Road

## Premise

Softfruit mentors stop a cart on the Eastbound Fruit Road and teach nesting with four bowls before a wrong pamphlet turns lunch into a ferry fantasy.

## Story

The cart smelled of peel-sweet and road dust. Someone had bought a pamphlet that drew a gold island east of the next rise and labeled it F200. The ladle host set four bowls on the tailboard: clear water, tinted tea, sliced fruit, and an empty that still steamed.

“Point,” she said.

A child pointed the gold island at the fruit. An adult pointed it at the tea. A nervous clerk pointed it at the steam and looked proud.

“Steam visits,” the host said. “Tea is a layer. Fruit is a place. Water is the bowl that holds all of it—Dias, if you need the big word. Your pamphlet drew steam as fruit. Unpack the ferry. Keep the wonder. Eat.”

They ate. The pamphlet was folded so the Nest Mark on its blank back showed. The road remained a road inside Wabet inside F432 inside Dias. The sky did not owe them a ticket for noticing light.
