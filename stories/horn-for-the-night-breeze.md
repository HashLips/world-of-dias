# Horn for the Night Breeze

## Premise

On a windless Outer Rim night, a small fisher answers a vast face in the dark with a horn—and the night breeze returns as song rather than storm.

## Story

The sea had gone flat in a way sailors distrust. No swell opinion. No bird argument. Even the lamps seemed to hold their breath.

A young fisher from Hollowmere’s cousin-boats sat on the stern with a thin horn and dared a note. The dark answered with a shape too large for decks: a pale face of wind-lines and empty leaf-eyes, not quite god, not quite weather—the kind of colossal rumor Outer Rim songs keep perforated on purpose.

The fisher did not ask for fish. Did not ask for Frequency Zero. Did not ask for a ferry. They played the note again, smaller, as if offering the giant a share of breath.

Lines of pale sound poured between face and horn—inspiration, joke, or pressure front, depending on who retells it. By dawn a soft breeze walked the water. Nets moved. Someone named the night **Night's Breeze** and painted it later in black and white so the face would not need a House seal.

Captains still say: if the sea goes too quiet, do not invent a door. Offer a song. Leave room for the breeze to choose you back.
