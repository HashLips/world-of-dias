# Holding Cove Negotiation Day

## Name

Holding Cove Negotiation Day

## Premise

Two parties off the fruit road try to settle a sharing quarrel in the Warm Current Holding Cove. Cloth and consent keep the talk from becoming extraction. A push for forced cheer nearly breaks the weather. The deal that survives is smaller than the one someone wanted to win, and truer.

## Story

They came in with shoulders up and numbers ready, the way people do when they have rehearsed fairness as a tactic. Amber-rose haze over the basin softened the shoulders without asking permission, which frightened the more legal-minded of the two and relieved the other. Cove Tone Guides laid Hearttide Holding Cloths along the rim and waited until both parties could say, in plain words, that they were here to argue and not to be cured. Reeds leaned in. An otter at the lip did not care about either delegation.

The first speaker smiled too brightly and pushed for a tone of gratitude that would have made their terms sound like a gift. Ease went brittle. The haze thinned. A Guide did not scold. They touched the cloth, named the break, and let the Quiet Current sit in everyone’s awareness like an exit no one was mocked for eyeing. The speaker’s apology was short and untheatrical, which was why it worked. They began again without the smile.

What they agreed was smaller than the opening bid: a timing, a portion, a way to leave the arrangement if the weather of either party changed. Nobody extracted a confession. Nobody was proved honest by the cove, because the cove does not do that labor. They walked out with damp hems and a deal they could repeat aloud without wincing. Behind them the cloths were shaken and folded, the basin returned to whoever needed it next, the haze back to a color that was not anyone’s victory.
