# Disappearance of the Glasswake

## Premise

During the Fracture, the Prime Age vessel *Glasswake* vanished at sea near the coast that would later become Lumira Sands in Wabet. Its signal lantern survived as the fused **Glasswake Lantern**. The ship’s resting place and final minutes remain unconfirmed.

## Story

Late in the Prime Age, the *Glasswake* worked coastal and open water under ordinary maritime use of a paraffin-style signal lantern engraved with the ship’s name. When the Fracture separated reality into resonant states, the vessel did not return to any Present-Age harbor ledger anyone trusts. What remains on the southern Wabet shore is the lantern fused through dark rock—stone through metal, mineral growth around the frame—as if part of the ship’s gear resolved into the land while the hull did not.

Post-Fracture local records and later Silence Law teaching treat the site as a scar with manners. Present-Age pulses and shipboard sound scraps keep the disappearance felt without closing it. Claims that pulse strength tracks the ship’s last heading are research talk only. The captain-figure stories are myth pressure around the same wound.
