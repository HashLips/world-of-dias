# Gangplank Before Dawn

## Name

Gangplank Before Dawn

## Premise

A message must cross every Rim-Rest raft ring before Storm Choir check-in, or a boat will be prayed for as missing while still tied. Pell Rim chooses which doors open.

## Story

The gangplanks sweat. Someone pays. Someone lies. Pell moves the true warning through three rings and a false one through two others to flush a thief. By first light the boat answers late and alive. The thief answers never.

## Notes

Outer Rim social engine independent of Hollowmere’s loft mythos—though they can cross.
