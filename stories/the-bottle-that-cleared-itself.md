# The Bottle That Cleared Itself

## Name

The Bottle That Cleared Itself

## Premise

An extraction crew bottles Blue Litter for a second-ring buyer. Overnight the bottle clears—and so does the room’s memory of why anyone wanted it. Only the headache remains honest.

## Story

Riven Salt still denies that particular bottle was theirs. Pot Signal Lane still remembers a windowsill that went bare and a debt that could not be collected because no one could agree what was owed. Keepers cite the episode when they restated the bottling ban.

## Notes

Horror-ethic hook; keep Husk offstage or peripheral.
