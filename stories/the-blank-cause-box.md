# The Blank Cause Box

## Name

The Blank Cause Box

## Premise

After a bleed night, a witness wants to write a villain into the cause box; a clerk keeps it blank and stamps Observation.

## Story

The sky had stuttered. The witness’s hand shook toward explanation—Companies, Zero, punishment. The clerk slid the Observation Intake Slate over and tapped the empty cause square.

“Seen first. Model later. Careful if you must whisper. Not Law.”

Softfruit sent fruit to the copy room. The stamp sounded dull and kind. By morning the observation still mattered, and the cosmos had not been falsely simplified to comfort one fear.
