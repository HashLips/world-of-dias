# Official History and the Other Telling

## Premise

A clerk copies a clean era label, then hears the loft version that refuses the neatness—and files both without choosing a god.

## Story

The House summary called the decade Settled Civic Growth. Mira wrote the phrase because the stamp wanted it. Her hand did not love it.

At Softfruit, a loft cousin visiting from Hollowmere said the same years as Storm Learning and Quiet Hunger, then apologized for talking shop over fruit-share.

Mira did not correct either telling. She added a margin note only she would see: *known / disputed / loft.* Certainty grade, not rebellion.

Walking home she passed a Mir leaf joking that clerks invent calm. She brushed a moth off a pot and kept both calendars in her head—one for stamps, one for songs.
