# The Fellgrove Hand

## Premise

Frederick gifts Fellgrove to Bram Orlen and sets terms: Bram keeps planting; once before each harvest, until Frederick leaves this frequency, Frederick will touch the trees so apples grow—and in return Frederick receives shared apple profits to fund frequency-travel research.

## Story

They met on the lane between the rises, where rivalry used to count crates.

"I’m not buying your farm," Frederick said. Bram’s face hardened toward another refusal. Frederick lifted a hand. "I’m giving you mine."

Silence took a long breath.

Frederick laid it plain, Wabet-practical:

- **Fellgrove**—house, tools, rows, market name—passes to **Bram Orlen**.
- Bram continues to plant and tend.
- **Once before each harvest**, until the day Frederick leaves **F432**, Frederick will walk the trees and give **Apple Touch** so the apples come.
- In return, Bram shares **profits** from those apples with Frederick—coin and credit earmarked for research into how a living person might travel frequencies.

Bram stared as if the grey shoulder had spoken orchard language. "You would hand me the bright ground after I shut my gate on you."

"I read an old letter," Frederick said. "I promised a boy I would try a crossing. I need the road more than I need to own the win."

They shook on it the way Driftfall crews shake when a deal must outlive pride. Paper followed by evening—simple transfer, harvest covenant, profit share—enough for lane witnesses and Open Hand manners.

That night Frederick slept under Fellgrove’s roof as a guest in a place that had been his. The letter stayed against his chest. Research would need marks. Marks would need apples. Apples would need his hands until the frequency finally said yes.
