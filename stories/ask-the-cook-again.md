# Ask the Cook Again

## Premise

Frederick returns to Stillhollow and asks Vorrith Kael how he traveled between frequencies—and whether there is any way to make the dream of leaving F432 real. This is the last place anyone can place him.

## Story

Stillhollow smelled as it had years before: mineral damp, cooked frequency, shadow that disagreed with the lamp.

Vorrith regarded him without surprise. Seekers who survive long enough often come back with thinner pride.

Frederick did not offer a shard. He had none left to flick. He offered the truth: years of research, harvest coin spent, rumored crossings priced beyond an orchard share, a letter to a boy who still waited.

"How did you travel?" Frederick asked. "Is there any way you can help me reach another frequency—make the dream true?"

What Vorrith answered is not held in public Wabet tellings. The hollow keeps its own quiet. Accounts agree only on this: Frederick entered. He asked. He was not seen walking the orchard lanes again.

Whether the cook taught a path, opened a door, refused, or took Frederick somewhere language does not follow—**The Lumen Saga** does not decide.
