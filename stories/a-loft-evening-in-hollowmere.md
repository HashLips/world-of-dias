# A Loft Evening in Hollowmere

## Premise

A loft hand works mending into Storm Choir listening, shares a First Line joke, and ignores a rope that hums until it is time to answer.

## Story

Day was nets-as-curtains and brine on the fingers. Apprentices learned rope-before-stone by falling gently and being laughed back up.

Dusk turned the loft into a clock. Lantern hooks breathed in a line. The First Line eight-beat went up; craft answered craft. Someone almost answered empty, then didn’t—saved by a shove and a grin.

Supper was kelp-bitter and good. Whale talk stayed compact-sized: eye-back last week, no tonnage sermon tonight. A fruit-barge rumor for next Bright Inshore made inland guests into future inspections.

The rope hummed once in the wind. Ordinary. Then hummed like a hail. The loft hand answered aloud, silly on purpose, and the apprentices copied the joke into muscle memory.

Sleep in a hammock over the cove’s breathing. Wanted another evening where the biggest plot was not missing the roll call.
