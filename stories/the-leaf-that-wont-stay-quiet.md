# The Leaf That Won't Stay Quiet

## Premise

Bram Inkspan sells Orrin a Supplemental Leaf that names the quiet background bands (F120, F380, F610, F840, disputed F960)—the catalog Orrin will later try to graph from home.

## Story

Bram Inkspan's copy desk smelled of ink and oranges. Not Fellgrove's Bram—different ledger, different sins, same name drifting through Dias like a joke the world had not finished telling.

"Supplemental leaf," Bram said, sliding a transcription under the blotter the way other people hid love letters. "Four background bands. Echo, heart, reveal, ember. Some copies add unfinished. Some strike it through like the cataloguer lost nerve. I sell the argument, not the verdict. Verdicts are House business and House business gives me hives."

Orrin paid in marks and a promise to cite uncertainty out loud. He read with his loupe crooked on its cord. This was the paper beat he had been hunting: not a ferry map, a naming of quiet bands Softfruit already felt and Houses preferred to leave ungraphed.

F120 near-return: patterns that recur with drift, not clean loops—lab logs that rhyme instead of repeat. F380 hearttide: mood as climate, gatherings that lean a place without converting the realm. F610 reveal: timed truth, windows that close if you have manners. F840 ember quiet: recovery honesty after crisis, the cold gap where Forced-Final tools pretend mercy. And the disputed F960 line, becoming, unfinished, a draft of a week that moved when you looked away.

His wrist itched where three crooked self-done tick tattoos lived—old experiments, affectionate scars. "Background," he murmured. "Policy-light. Story-rich. Exactly what they hope no one graphs."

Archivist Pellun arrived with a smile constructed entirely of stamps. Young, tidy, addicted to the word nonactionable the way Softfruit kids were addicted to peel. "If that leaf leaves your alcove as rumor, I can file it before lunch."

"File lunch," Orrin said pleasantly. "I'm busy making quiet bands loud enough to embarrass your ink."

Pellun's stamp hand twitched. Bram pretended to sand a blotter. Somewhere in the leaf's margins, five ghosts argued in columns—and for the first time Orrin felt less alone in wanting to hear them without turning them into a ferry.

Orrin copied the four background glosses into his scraped ledger by hand, slow enough to feel each one as a lived mood rather than a catalog boast. Near-return. Heart. Reveal. Ember. He left a blank line where F960 wanted to sit and wrote *disputed* only, then scraped the word until the fiber showed white. The leaf would travel with him into the Frame years: target signatures to reproduce from F432, not destinations to board.

"People will say you invented bands," Bram warned.

"I am late to Softfruit," Orrin said. "Vendors invented noticing. I am inventing embarrassment for stamps."
