# House Schism

## Premise

When Resonant Houses split over echo-rise classification, Calen Brink becomes the living argument neither faction can file away cleanly.

## Story

The bulletin war began quietly—two House codes for the same season:

**H-11:** echo-rise as **managed witness event** (basin model tolerated)  
**H-19:** echo-rise as **pre-fracture stress indicator** (core lockdown recommended)

Markets hiccupped. Operators worked double shifts. Tamsin swore at oranges. Calen felt both codes **true enough to hurt**.

A neutral convocation was called—not public, but **leaked** within an hour. Calen received invitation and threat in the same sentence: *Explain your ears or be explained for.*

They came with stamped Glassfold record, atlas charcoal copies, no census leaf—Sael's trust kept. No Sorel maps—Carrow's pact kept.

House speakers argued across a table longer than Brindle trials. Vailor Quentin presided at distance, crown visible, face unreadable. Oriven's channel sat empty chair—message only: *Proceed.*

Calen spoke without performance:

"You classified my first echo as noise because the core could not stop. You are now classifying each other because the **basin did not stop** when I shared B-band truth. I am not your fracture. I am your **mirror**."

Silence—then schism formalized. Two Houses broke quorum. H-11 adherents accepted witness network as **civic hygiene**. H-19 adherents demanded Calen's **voluntary filing silence**—not arrest, **social erasure**.

Calen refused silence. Tamsin refused on their behalf in basin square before convocation ended.

The schism did not resolve. It **named** factions for repository explorers to follow later.

Calen wrote:

*Institutions break like maps revise—by trying to finish too soon. I will stay incomplete with Dias.*
