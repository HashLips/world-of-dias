# Four Tags Beside the Bowl

## Name

Four Tags Beside the Bowl

## Premise

At Softfruit, a guest tries to turn a bleed observation into lunch law; the host uses four cloth tags to demote the claim without humiliating wonder.

## Story

The guest had seen wrong-sky and wanted everyone to swear a new absolute. The host set the Resonance Bowl and laid four tags: Law, Theory, Seen, Careful.

“Point,” she said—same as bowls, different lesson.

The guest hovered over Law. A child moved the tag to Seen. The host nodded. “You saw remainder. We do not yet own a finished model. Careful is for the Zero talk you almost smuggled in. Eat.”

They ate. The sky did not become a district. The observation kept its dignity without stealing the grade of the echo principle itself.
