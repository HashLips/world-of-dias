# The First Time They Heard the Table

## Name

The First Time They Heard the Table

## Premise

A junior Softfruit host learns that table warmth is not superstition—and that five-domain talk starts with a board that has held too many lunches to be “just wood.”

## Story

They arrived proud of Theory words and thin on ladle hours. Midday rush left citrus steam and a sticky ring where a bowl had sat too long. When the hall emptied, the junior wiped the ring and felt the board still warm in a shape that matched no current pot. The senior did not say “echo” first. She said: *Matter kept the heat. Life left the load. Memory is why it still feels like lunch. Perception is why your hand found the ring. Environment is why this hall feels kinder than the sealed room upstairs.* She made the junior knead dough before she allowed any stamp talk. By evening the junior could host without inventing a ferry out of leftover warmth.
