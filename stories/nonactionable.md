# Nonactionable

## Premise

House and Operator officials meet Orrin's residual graph and living Echoform counterexample—then file the whole matter nonactionable to avoid public panic.

## Story

On the way in, Orrin passed a monitoring slit and heard Choirglass status tones that made his teeth understand before his mind did. No one ahead looked heroic. The city was carried by tired hands—and tired hands invented words like *noise* so they could sleep.

They held the argument in a room that pretended it was not the core.

Kesh translated Operator into civilian: load thresholds, civic wonder as noise, keep the city standing. Pellun translated House into stamps. Names like Oriven Tal floated like weather—power without needing a chair. Orrin set the residual graph on the table and watched adults flinch from paper as if fiber could bite. The living problem stayed in the alcove Frame; the paper problem was enough to make the room sweat.

"You filed this shape as nothing," Vela said softly to the air that smelled of clearance. "It grew a roommate."

Harold jogged past the building's outer face, ordinary as a law, multi-day loop unbroken by glass people. Pip Ringroad shouted something about Lap-Cats and actual errands. The city continued being loveable while the room tried to decide if loveable included mischievous instrument tenants.

Pellun's stamp hand twitched. Kesh's voice stayed flat. Someone suggested enhanced monitoring, which is what institutions say when they mean fear with a schedule. Orrin thought of tired heroes in the core—maintenance as devotion—and of how truth and publication were different rings. He was angry and grateful in the same breath, and he hated how familiar that felt.

Verdict, temporary and stamped: nonactionable residual. No ferry maps. No public panic. Wonder kept in the noise file even with a roommate in the glass. The Caliburn, waiting in the Frame, hummed. Orrin walked out into gilding light and felt, for the first time, that winning a filing argument could still mean losing a city by half a digit.

He did not forgive the word *noise*. He also did not pretend he could replace the hands that carried the core. He only refused to call a roommate nothing—and began to understand that if Houses would not act, he would have to publish partial honesty himself.
