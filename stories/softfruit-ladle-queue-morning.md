# Softfruit Ladle Queue Morning

## Name

Softfruit Ladle Queue Morning

## Premise

A proud clerk arrives at the Softfruit court meaning to stamp first and eat as a reward. The Host refuses an almost-orange with opera-grade disgust, a second-ladle token cools the clerk’s hand, and three favors get sealed only after stew, correctly, because they were not sealed hungry.

## Story

Morning in the court was sticky and exact. Tokens clacked on the second-ladle shelf. Peel-boats raced in the damp underneath, serious as any House proceeding. The clerk had a stamp hand that itched the way a tool itches when its owner has confused speed with virtue. He smiled at the Host. The Host was already looking past the smile at a child who had not eaten and at a woman performing not-hungry with too much posture.

An almost-orange was offered, bright and not quite right. The Host refused it with a disgust so theatrical that the queue laughed and the standard landed without a lecture. Softening moved through the court, the small weather of a place that has decided to be fair before it is efficient. The clerk’s stamp hovered. A Second-Ladle Token was put in his other hand, not as a prize, as a delay with weight. It was cool. It made the stamp hand feel suddenly theatrical in the bad way.

He waited. Stew came. Mint from a rinsed ladle cut the sugar in the air. After, the three favors he had meant to seal on an empty stomach were sealed with the people actually in front of him, and two of the three were different favors than the ones he had rehearsed. The Row’s noise waited at the edge of the court and somehow did not win the morning. He left with a clean seal and a story he would tell as if he had always intended the order. The Host, rotating off the apron, let him have the telling. The hooks kept the truth.
