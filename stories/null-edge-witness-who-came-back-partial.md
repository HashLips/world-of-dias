# Null-Edge Witness Who Came Back Partial

## Name

Null-Edge Witness Who Came Back Partial

## Premise

A dockside account, told and retold until the name of the witness is the least reliable part, of someone who approached forget-texture weather as a curiosity and returned missing three hours and any stable word for grit. The story stays rumor. It does not finish Frequency Zero, and it does not identify what, if anything, listened back.

## Story

They carried a null-proximity tag the way Softfruit carries a joke, clipped where a charm would go, and they were still laughing when the ground stopped being describable. Later they would say there was a cairn. Later than that they would say there was the habit of a cairn. The difference did not feel philosophical at the time. It felt like a step that failed to arrive, and then like a kindness, brief and horrible, toward not knowing their own name.

Tools still worked. That was the detail the Houses loved and the detail the witness hated, because working tools made people think the person had returned intact. A blade cut. A lamp lit. The three hours did not come back inside either of them. In Veloria they stood in a room with ordinary shelves and could not finish the sentence that began “I saw,” because the end of the sentence reached for an empty shelf in the mind and found the emptiness more familiar than the room. Softfruit hosts fed them before the questions. The feeding was the courtesy. The questions tried to be a taxonomy.

The witness gave partiality and a request not to sell tours. Some listeners turned the request into a better story, with silhouettes, with companies, with names the polite rooms are not supposed to confirm. Null-Distance Courtesy exists so those improvements can be refused without pretending the fear was imaginary. What remains, when the improvements are stripped, is a person who flinches at bare cupboards and will not be your map.
