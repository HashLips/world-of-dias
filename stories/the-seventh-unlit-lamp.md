# The Seventh Unlit Lamp

## Name

The Seventh Unlit Lamp

## Premise

**The Seventh Unlit Lamp** is an empty hook on Averra’s working dock, beside six lamps that are allowed to burn. A damp filing in the dock drawer claims to describe it and has no owner. The drawer holds both. They cost anyone who signs them the rumor of a council. They are not seven cloaked figures, and they are not a judgment you can take inland.

## Story

Six lamps did their job. They smoked a little in the damp, threw honest light on rope and crate, and had names in the dock book that a clerk could say without lowering the voice. The seventh hook was the same iron and a different manners. No glass. No oil cup. Soot where a flame would have lived if anyone had agreed to be the person who lit it. Cliff birds treated it as architecture. Dock workers treated it as a sentence they did not want to finish.

The filing was a card, not a proclamation. It lived in the damp drawer under spare wick, and it said, in a hand that had tried to be administrative, that a seventh light was unassigned. Below that line were initials, and through every set of initials a later hand had cut a stroke. Nobody would own it. A new clerk, once, put a mark and then scratched the mark out before the end of the watch, which the others counted as wisdom rather than cowardice. Owning the card, they said, is how you inherit a story in which seven silhouettes on a cliff get to be your responsibility.

Market mouths already have that story. Lights where no houses stand. Bells in weather too calm for masts. A hidden council steering what cities cannot see, benevolent or not, or both before supper. The dock did not confirm any of it by having a dark hook. The dock also did not kill it. If you asked the night loader whether the seventh lamp proved the Seven of Averra Isle, she would say the hook proved that Averra likes a gap more than an answer, and that Old Masters get painted into gaps by people who cannot stand an unlit nail. If you asked her whether the council was therefore a lie, she would go back to the six lamps that were actually her work.

Someone leaves a fresh card in the drawer every few seasons. Someone else unowns it by morning. The hook stays empty. Charts of the isle continue to fail at keeping Averra still, which they did before the hook and would do without it. Whatever balance the rumor wants from seven figures, it is not stored in dock oil, and it is not available to a filing that refuses a signature.

## Notes

Dock rumor-object. The council stays unproven. The lamp and the filing explain each other only as manners, not as a revelation.
