# What the Bag Attracts

## Premise

Frederick asks **Bram Orlen** for orchard work. Bram asks what is in the bag, sees the glowing shard, and turns him away—because that crystal will attract the presence in the mountains known as Vorrith Kael.

## Story

Bram’s gate was the kind that looked like yes: rows needing hands, crates waiting, a man wiping sweat like wage.

"Work?" Frederick asked, bright despite the weeks.

Bram looked at his thin bag the way farmers look at weather. "What’s in it?"

Frederick could have lied. Lying had never bought him long in Driftfall. He unwrapped cloth enough for blue to speak.

Bram stepped back as if the light had teeth.

"No," he said. Not angry. Final. "Not here. That is a True Blue. It pulls what lives under the grey shoulder—**Vorrith Kael**. Cook in the mountain. We keep lanes quiet by not lighting beacons. You work my rows with that, and the calm around this farm gets visitors we do not want."

Frederick’s mouth went dry. "I need—"

"I know what you need. Eat from the fallen edge if you must. Do not sleep inside my fence. And if you are wise, walk east before the Rise notices you."

Vorrith’s name stuck like a hook. Campfire bands had given Frederick distant numbers. This was a presence that wanted the exact stone against his ribs.

He wrapped Roadglow again. Bram’s refusal was not Dren’s theft. It was Wabet protecting itself from him—or from what he carried.

Interest woke beside hunger. If the mountain sought the shard, then the mountain was a door with a face.
