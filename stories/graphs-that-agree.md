# Graphs That Agree

## Premise

The Spectrum Frame reproducibly graphs the quiet background bands from F432; the curves agree too well, and Pellun's nonactionable stamp hesitates for the first time.

## Story

The graphs agreed.

Not once—reproducibly. Same protocol, same alcove, same heel plates on floor hum: the Frame drew the Supplemental Leaf's quiet stack as real signatures, not Softfruit rumor. F120 near-return drift in the lab logs, outcomes that rhymed instead of repeated. F380 like a Softfruit mood gradient leaning the alcove without converting Veloria into hearttide. F610 as brief true lines that should have died with a window. F840 as cold recovery quiet after argument. A disputed stutter Orrin refused to baptize F960 in public ink—becoming stayed becoming, etiquette intact.

Sera arrived with peel and refused to look impressed on purpose. "Your glass is doing what vendors do with less arrogance."

Orrin laughed until he cried, which Tam recorded in the margin as scientist weather. Vela Thirdslit checked the curves against load scrap, napkin from the Seam Cafe trembling in her glove, and whispered, "Don't tell the core you made their noise legible." The napkin stutter and the Frame peaks were kissing cousins—civic wonder named at last.

Archivist Pellun arrived with the nonactionable stamp already inked. The glass showed him a helpful amber agreement. His stamp hand hesitated—a historic event on the Row, later mythologized as the stamp that almost blushed. "Rumor," he managed. "File as rumor."

Orrin pulled the first residual leaf anyway: cream stock, oil thumbprints, tea-cylinder ring, five ghost traces and a sixth stutter he would not name. The Veld Residual Graph—paper proof that the stack could be counted from home. He scraped one correction so hard the fiber remembered the wound. Triumph tasted like citrus and fear.

They ran the protocol three more times after Pellun left, because rumor stamps should not get the last word. Each run agreed within polite error—too polite, Tam muttered—then, on the fourth, a half-digit wandered and came home wrong. Orrin scraped it. Nobody had stepped out of the glass yet. Something in the residual seam (the leftover brightness after a run) was already practicing *almost*.

Somewhere in the pipes, a tone arrived one beat late, as if the city were practicing his mutter before he had company to imitate it. Halla would swear about that later. For now Orrin only bowed to the Frame and said thank you to something that had not yet shown a face.

Sera, before she left, pressed a peel bookmark into the residual leaf. "If your paper forgets fruit," she said, "it will start believing it is a ferry."
