# An Exile First Bowl in Sorel

## Premise

A new arrival from the Long Gate gets a news-bowl before paint talk, and learns kettle peace is a kind of politics.

## Story

The tarp edge was for people without wire yet. Renn stood there until someone pointed at the Cookfire Circle with a ladle, not a speech.

“News-bowl,” said a Mutual Table hand. “Paint later.”

The stew tasted like onion, smoke, and not being alone. Renn paid with the only news that mattered: the Gate had been crowded; Concord boots had looked through them; a dog had stolen a crust and been forgiven. Someone laughed mean-affectionate. A Claimscar dog waited with professional patience.

After the bowl, paint sticks stayed on the high shelf. A child glanced up anyway—learning. Double-paint talk drifted from another fire and stopped at the kettle rim like weather hitting a wall.

Renn slept under tarp-smell and wanted, embarrassingly, breakfast to hold tomorrow the way Flagweek promised.
