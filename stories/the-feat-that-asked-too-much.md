# The Feat That Asked Too Much

## Name

The Feat That Asked Too Much

## Premise

A junior echo-reader tries to finish a person’s whole story in one sitting and learns the difference between craft success and soft-limit failure.

## Story

They had talent and a new stone. A market client paid for “everything.” The junior listened past the first aftertone, past the second, into pride. Data arrived in scraps that felt like victory. The room went sour. The client left filed as an object in the junior’s head. Softfruit did not praise the scraps. The senior made them air the room, stamp Partial on every note, return the fee, and copy the Feat Sorting Chapbook page on complete-reading stalls three times. By evening the junior could say the judgment without poetry: the feat asked for omniscience; resonance does not sell that; the cost was the room and the person.
