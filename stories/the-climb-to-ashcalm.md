# The Climb to Ashcalm

## Premise

Frederick leaves orchard lanes for the grey shoulder of Ashcalm Rise—climbing toward Stillhollow with Roadglow hidden, more afraid with every quieter bird.

## Story

Ashcalm did not announce itself with temples. It arrived as stone the color of cooled ash behind Aelwyn’s green wall, wind thinner than forest wind, fruit falling away from the path as if harvest knew better.

Frederick climbed game tracks more than roads. His legs remembered liner ladders; his lungs remembered running from Dren. This fear was different—not hierarchy, not theft—something the land itself seemed to hush for.

Birds went sparse. Canopy talk thinned. He spoke once to a canopy walker who would not take his marks for guiding.

"I don’t lead children to wells," the walker said, and stepped aside.

Near the upper rock the air cooled without night. Frederick saw the mouth of a cave that looked like shade until he was close enough to feel it breathing quieter than trees. **Stillhollow**. Rumor made flesh as an opening.

He stopped outside.

Courage that had stolen from a senior mate in Driftfall felt smaller here. The bag against his ribs warmed. He almost turned back toward berries and soft refusal.

Then, from inside the dark, a voice found him—courteous, brief, certain.

It knew he was there. It knew what he carried.
