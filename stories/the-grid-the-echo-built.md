# The Grid the Echo Built

## Premise

Solven Wetch finishes a week of watching Calen Brink—and hands over a lino grid that maps not a face, but **every place Calen listened**.

## Story

Driftfall welcomed them back through Carrow's pact without incident, as if freedom were a road you could permit.

Calen found Solven Wetch at The Salt Ledger with ink on his fingers and thirty-five small blocks arranged like a calendar of attention. Tamsin ordered tea and did not sit too close—she understood witness needed room.

"You knew," Calen said.

Wetch shook his head. "I **looked**. Same thing in Driftfall."

Seven days: DEB Gate touch, core conduit, basin gilding, Wabet rose rim, Averra cliff, Long Gate rail, Ledger corner. Each panel held a moment Calen had thought private—the echo-face they made when listening, hands on stone, eyes closed, mouth slightly open as if drinking weather.

Not flattering. **Accurate**.

"The week is yours," Wetch said, and slid the grid forward exactly as the stories promised.

Calen's throat closed. They saw Tamsin in the third row—not posed, laughing at an orange peel, love visible because someone patient had been watching **kindness** instead of power.

"I didn't ask—"

"You lived it." Wetch took his knife roll toward the door. "I don't sell memory. I finish weeks."

Tamsin touched the grid edge when Wetch was gone. "Keep it."

"I don't know how."

"Same way you keep me." Simple. Devastating. True.

Calen carried the grid to basin wrapped in wax cloth beside the census leaf Sael had trusted them not to weaponize. Two gifts, two disciplines: **see** and **withhold**.

They wrote:

*Echo is heard time. Wetch is seen time. Dias gives both if you stay long enough to be worth recording.*
