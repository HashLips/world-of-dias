# What the Markets Did with the Knowing

## Premise

The knowing hits the road and the rings. Honest fruit slows. Marks stick to a man the clerks cannot find. Sella is still seen, edged and angry. Whether Dain ever fully returns, and how the mix works, stay open.

## Story

The delay showed up first as chalk.

Span Toll grew a second line on its slate: curious traffic west, not convoy, not raid exactly, not innocent. Nia Toll made them take rest anyway. She made them water animals. She turned two carts around when they talked about night-cutting across a waystation to "get there before the fruit was gone." Take-without-restore did not become sightseeing just because the prize was glass. The honest Softfruit behind them waited, and waiting was bruises, and bruises were money. Sellers in the basin felt it before they heard the story. A stall that had planned on a morning lot got an afternoon. Prices jumped, then sulked, then jumped again when someone said the lent field itself was the show. Ordinary fruit paid for an extraordinary secret. That was the adult part. Children could repeat the wonder. Nia had to count the heat.

Yarrow closed the lend with the cloth on the stakes and a face like a man locking a door after the goats were already in the garden. Feet had come. The strip was rutted where it had been grass. He shouted at a trio from the outer ring until his voice broke, and they still looked past him at the trees. He would be shouting for days. The field did not unbruise because a pilot had been brave in a bar.

Sella met the first of them with the door-bar in her hand and her left ear still missing its rim. She was easy to see. That was the fact the wilder rumors hated. She was not a vacancy in an apron. She was a woman with a faded hem and a full temper who would not serve stew to strangers and would not cut the last fruit down to make them leave. The fruit clung, smaller every hour only in the imagination of people who wanted it smaller. She would not say what she had mixed. A clever man from a third-ring coat guessed share-apple, scrub-apple, market banana, a prayer, and a phase of the moon. She let him finish. She did not correct him. Correction would have been a map. Hob hoed the ordinary rows and would not look at the glass one. Moth was kept at the cousin's. The machine did not come back that day. The absence of The Late Bruise was its own rumor: that Lark had been paid off, that she had crashed, that she had joined the Drifters. She was in Veloria, on a bench behind the public pad, eating bread, not holy.

Sarn Vell's barge came in on time, which offended everyone who wanted the world to reorganize around a tree. The Banana Draft smelled like itself. Market banana still outranked a story. Jory Milt's crawler did its short spur and waved and arrived at the stained depot. No one was carried to another band by wanting it very much. The joke rail remained a joke. The real train remained slow.

Ilen's counting room had a fruit in a locked drawer and a bid in a book and no debtor in any chair they could watch. Messages to Mercer Shed returned with Opal's handwriting: *not on the floor*. A sleeve was reported in a ring-market, then a tipped hat — Hess's, again, though he had not worn it out — then nothing a clerk could serve. Dain's voice was heard once at his own desk, a step left of the chair, asking for the private buyer in a tone that already knew the buyer had heard about a chalkboard. The chair did not hold him long enough to sign. Whether the unseen would ever finish leaving him stayed a blank at the bottom of the slate. He flickered, people said. He worsened, people said. Both reports came from serious mouths. Neither came with a cure.

Hess did not eat. The box stayed in his room like a guest who would not take off a dangerous coat. He went to the bar and read the board and went home again. His fortune sat in glass and would not brown.

Lark walked the outer ring at gilding, when the basin remembered how to be kind without being asked. Sky Drifters held their altitude. She did not hail them. She had no rope they wanted. Wagons passed her, and one driver spat, not all malice, a tax. She paid it by not arguing. The plane would fly honest crates again if anyone trusted the hull. Trust was a waystation she would have to earn in hours, not in chalk. She had spent the chalk. The tree was known. The mix was not. A man was still mislaid inside his own skin. The markets had the bill, and the bill was not a speech. It was fruit arriving late, a field lent too far, a cuff somewhere in the city with stew dried on it, and a farmer at a door who could still be looked in the eye.
