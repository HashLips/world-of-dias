# Jorin’s Rows

## Premise

Frederick seeks work at an elderly apple farmer’s gate—**Jorin Fell** of Fellgrove—shows Apple Touch in the fields, and is accepted. Bram Orlen, rival apple grower on a neighboring lane, remains in the story as competition.

## Story

Jorin Fell’s gate did not look like yes the way Bram’s once had. It looked like age: ladders patched, rows waiting on a season that had not arrived.

"Work?" Frederick asked, brighter than rumor wanted him.

Jorin’s eyes were watery and sharp. "Show me why a boy the hollow talked about should touch my trees."

Frederick walked Fellgrove’s rows and laid his palms on living wood. Apples answered—out of season, out of species stubbornness, out of any calendar Bram would have trusted. Fruit swelled along limbs that had been resting. Jorin stood very still, then laughed once, short, like a man surprised to still be capable of hope.

"You work here," he said. "You eat here. You do not sell the gift on another man’s lane without telling me. Bram Orlen grows apples over the next rise. We have competed since my back was straight. If you stay, his market mornings will notice."

Frederick stayed.

He had wanted Bram’s hire once. He had a different orchard now—and a rival already written into the soil between them.
