# Scrape Not Erase

## Name

Scrape Not Erase

## Premise

A junior clerk tries to erase a wrong tick to keep a clean page and learns why Dias prefers scrape: the page remembers anyway, and lies make cold spots.

## Story

The tick was embarrassing, not criminal. The junior reached for full wipe. The senior put a scraper in their hand instead.

“Admit the mark. Correct it. Stamp Partial if you must. If you erase, the desk will sulk, and the next reader will trust a fiction.”

They scraped. Initialed. Stamped. The drawer that had been edging toward sealed-ledger cold eased by a degree you could almost pretend was imagination—almost. Softfruit would have called it manners. The House called it procedure. Both were obeying the same echo principle.
