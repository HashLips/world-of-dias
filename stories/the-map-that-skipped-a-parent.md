# The Map That Skipped a Parent

## Name

The Map That Skipped a Parent

## Premise

A junior clerk files Veloria City with parent F432, and Softfruit makes them walk the chain until the Basin and Nauw return to the ledger.

## Story

The clerk wanted a clean line: city under home band. The host set the Atlas Hierarchy Ladder Card down and asked for feet, not poetry. *What do you cross before the rings?* Brindle dust and basin market air. *What region owns that basin?* Nauw. The clerk scraped the parent field to Velorian Basin, then wrote the spoken chain in the margin: Dias → F432 → Nauw → Velorian Basin → Veloria City. Softfruit fed them lunch. The wrong map went in the airing cloth pile—not because F432 was false, but because skipped parents breed ferry talk.
