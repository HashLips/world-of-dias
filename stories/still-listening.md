# Still Listening

## Premise

Movement Three closes with Calen Brink still listening—partnered, networked, unfiled—and Dias still **unfinished on purpose**.

## Story

Morning came ordinary on purpose.

Calen and Tamsin ate breakfast above the workshop—bread, oranges, tea—while Harold finished a loop map that now marked **Glassfold** and **Long Gate** and **Gray Post tide** in charcoal constellations only insiders read. Andrew and Russell's ball thumped against wall rhythm. Somewhere Solven Wetch cut a new week. Somewhere Carrow's mask turned toward camp fire. Somewhere Sael wiped golden tear tracks from a shelf that would stay closed until Dias earned it.

The Half-Made Atlas in Driftfall added and erased margins no one forced to final line.

Calen opened the notebook—copy, not archived original—and wrote the closing lines of Movement Three:

*I wanted fairness as procedure. Dias gave me fairness as **practice among people**—tea, stamps, open hands, grids, masks, crowns contested, cores tended, abyss named and not invited in.*

*I love Tamsin in daily ways that do not need myth. I love Harold's loops. I love basin grass that remembers joy.*

*The Fracture is still unknown. Oriven is still static. Sky Drifters still do not land. F200 still bleeds gold. F500 still notches null. F960 still revises. The Bridge still remembers in fragments.*

*I still listen.*

Tamsin read over their shoulder and added: *We still do.*

They held each other while the harmonic thrum rose—steady, familiar, **not the last sound the world would ever make**.

Dias continued.
