# The Flagweek that Kept Breakfast

## Premise

Official Strip talk says the noon flip changed everything. The table remembers the eggs did not.

## Story

By noon the cloth had changed twice. Mir’s leaf called it a clean break—new captains, new jokes, old debts suddenly “historic.”

At the corner bench the same skillet stayed. A Freeband hand and a yard aunt argued flags with their mouths and passed salt with their hands. The breakfast rule did not ask who won the pole.

A child asked which history was true. The aunt tapped the egg. “This one held. The leaf can catch up.”

By evening the leaf had a cartoon of the skillet. Comic dread, soft correction. The flags would flip again. The hold had already been practiced.
