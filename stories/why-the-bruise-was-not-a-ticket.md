# Why the Bruise Was Not a Ticket

## Name

Why the Bruise Was Not a Ticket

## Premise

After a wrong-sky night, a junior surveyor almost redraws a street as F500 until Softfruit forces the nest and ferry tests.

## Story

The sky bruised. The junior’s chalk wanted a district line. Softfruit set two questions: *Does the bruise keep a mayor?* and *Can you buy a return stub?* The bruise had neither. They labeled Bleed-Sky Weather, kept the street as F432 place, and stamped No-Ferry on the pamphlet that had sold “abyss pier at dawn.” By morning the junior could say it without poetry: interaction can visit; travel is not infrastructure; local phenomenon is not a country.
