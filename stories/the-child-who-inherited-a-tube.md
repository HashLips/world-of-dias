# The Child Who Inherited a Tube

## Name

The Child Who Inherited a Tube

## Premise

Nimi Tube inherits a listening tube along Tubehold’s waterline. Inside the reflection, the season is wrong. Elders say fog. The dam still needs watching.

## Story

Nimi walks the night shift with an adult’s checklist and a child’s certainty. When the tube shows blossom-light in a harvest month, Nimi marks the slate anyway. By morning the mark is wiped. By the next night the wrong season returns, polite as water.

## Notes

Saga pilot that can stay child-accessible or darken without leaving Glasswater.
