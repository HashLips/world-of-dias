# Come Into Stillhollow

## Premise

Vorrith Kael senses Roadglow and calls Frederick into Stillhollow. Frightened, Frederick enters; Vorrith reassures him he is not Unraveled F500 horror—only a cook who needs the crystal for life-broth—and offers Giftbroth Change in trade: magnificent or dire, never guaranteed.

## Story

"Come in," the voice said. Not a roar. Not a hymn. A practical invitation, as if Frederick were late for a table.

He went because turning away with the shard still glowing under cloth felt like lying to the mountain.

Stillhollow opened into a low chamber that took shadow too eagerly. A hanging lamp. A table. A vessel that held thick living broth—**the Still Pot**—stirred as if stirring were breathing. And **Vorrith Kael**: dark cloth, courteous posture, a silhouette that sometimes disagreed with the body casting it.

Frederick’s fear showed. He did not try to hide it.

Vorrith did not mock it. "I am not what you fear from **F500**," he said. "I am not Unraveled. I fled ending. I cook to remain. Your crystal is matter my broth can use—frequency dense enough to keep an exile standing in F432 without spilling foreign noise into Wabet’s calm."

He explained without sermon: the pot was life-source, not market potion. He did not sell wages. He did not hire orchard hands. He did not pay in marks.

"If you give me the True Blue," Vorrith said, "I cannot offer you work or money. What I can guarantee is a taste. The broth will answer you with one unnatural capacity—yours alone. It may be magnificent. It may be dire. I do not preview. I do not refund."

Roadglow warmed against Frederick’s ribs as if it recognized the pot. The chamber smelled of mineral damp and cooked frequency. Shadow along the wall seemed longer than politeness required.

Frederick stood at the oldest kind of crossroads for a boy who had owned almost nothing: keep the door-shaped stone, or trade it for a change he could not name.

Outside, Wabet’s calm continued, pretending the hollow was only shade.
