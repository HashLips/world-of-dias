# A First-Pass Walk on the Returning Span

## Name

A First-Pass Walk on the Returning Span

## Premise

A visitor with Softfruit manners walks the Returning Span’s outer lane for the first time, wanting a loop story and receiving a rest, a pegboard, and a hush. They learn to write differences before they claim the road, and they sleep unfinished at knowing, which is the only diploma the lane offers.

## Story

They arrived with a pack and a theory. The theory was that a road which returns must be a trap or a gift, and they wanted to know which before lunch. An Outer Lane Keeper did not take the theory. The Keeper moved nothing for them. Under the lean roof of the First Lane Milestone Rest the twin stones stood half a stride apart, same numbers, different chips, and the visitor was handed a peg the color of a first pass and left alone with it until their hand got bored of being symbolic and actually moved it.

At the wayside the wall was full of other people’s almosts. Reading them aloud felt like gossip until the Keeper said gossip was allowed and certainty was the thing to whisper. The visitor wrote three lines. The hinge on a bench. A moss edge a handspan late. Their own whistling, which they had started and then stopped because the Lane Offset Hush made the whistle sound like a person insisting. Nouns went scarce. They pointed. Pointing, they discovered, was not a failure of language. It was the outer lane’s native grammar.

Sleep tasted of wet rope and cold tea. They did not dream a second pass. They dreamed the embarrassment of the three lines, and woke to find the lines still true, which was more survival than the loop would have been. In the morning the Keeper read the slip, nodded once, and did not say they were ready. Readiness was not on offer. A second pass might rhyme. The visitor left the roof knowing they were not owed the rhyme, and that the not-owing was the kindness.

## Notes

Side journey texture for F120. No time-travel payoff, no ferry, no chosen-savior Keeper.
