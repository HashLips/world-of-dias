# Wall of Eyes Witness

## Premise

Calen Brink joins a Glassfold escort at reveal interval and learns that **recording fear** can be as holy as hearing joy.

## Story

Harold's loop map had a listening stop Calen had avoided until now: **Glassfold Ledge**, where F610's reveal windows turned stone into argument.

Veil Scribes accepted Calen as **nonpolicy witness**—no stamp authority, only presence and notebook. The lead scribe, voice thin as wire, reviewed phase-stamp fields until Calen could recite them in sleep.

"Unstamped insight guides caution," she said. "It does not compel policy. You are allowed to be afraid. You are not allowed to **run**."

The eyes appeared at second hour—gold rows rolling with the team's motion, stopping when they stopped. Calen's echo-read caught **scrutiny** so old it felt institutional, not personal: the wall watching the way archives watch.

Two couriers bolted. Calen did not. They named timestamps aloud for the scribe's recorder, hands shaking, voice steady enough:

"Team hold. Eyes tracking. Confidence high. Corroboration three."

At the final marker the wall emptied—purple stone, no ornament. The scribe stamped the record **complete**. No one cheered. Relief was private and sufficient.

Afterward Calen vomited behind a boulder, ashamed and proud in the same body. The scribe passed water without comment.

"You did not convert fear into rumor," she said. "That is rare."

Calen brought the stamped copy to basin—not for Houses, for **tea**. Witness network learned a new practice: fear **filed with timecodes** beats fear shouted without.

Tamsin read the stamp fields and held Calen longer that night.
