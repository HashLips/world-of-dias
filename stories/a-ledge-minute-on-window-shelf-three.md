# A Ledge Minute on Window Shelf Three

## Name

A Ledge Minute on Window Shelf Three

## Premise

A Window Shelf Timer and an apprentice work a single reveal on the third shelf. The basins show true. The apprentice wants a longer look. The Timer packs the slate at the soft close. What they keep is a named observation and the knowledge of what the glass did not judge.

## Story

The first chime was clean. Reveal-shelf lichen, which had been only a pretty crust, threw map-flecks the apprentice had practiced drawing and never expected to need. Below, the Blind Basins stopped glittering and became structure: a brace, a channel, a shy corner that ordinary sight had been flattering into fog. The minute had a taste like cold metal and mint. The apprentice’s hand hovered, not to touch the view, to keep it. Hovering is how linger starts when you are still polite.

The Timer did not make a speech about property. They closed the slate the way you close a good letter, on purpose, at the softer note. Packing was the kindness. It gave the apprentice’s hands a job that was not staring. Afterglow stayed mild, a milkiness at the corner of the eye that faded before the tea was poured on the lower shelf. Over the cups they named what they had seen, and they named, with equal care, what the glass had not done. It had not told them who was lying in the city below. It had not issued a verdict. It had shown a layout, and the layout was already beginning to be something they remembered rather than something they owned.

The apprentice asked if they could request the window again tomorrow. The Timer said yes, and did not say they could stay in this one. The difference was the whole guild. They walked down while the ledge put its coat back on, and the basins resumed their dishonest shine, beautiful and insufficient.
