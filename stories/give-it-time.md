# Give It Time

## Premise

Feeling unchanged and cheated of his crystal, Frederick leaves Stillhollow upset, refuses to stay the night, sleeps under a tree again—while rumor of the boy who visited Vorrith’s cave spreads through Wabet in uproar.

## Story

Outside the hollow the cyan light did not follow. Only the knowledge that Roadglow was gone—dissolved, unrecoverable, spent on a promise that had not yet touched his hands.

Frederick walked down Ashcalm angry in the quiet way orphans get angry: no shouting, just a jaw set against the next kindness. He would not sleep in Vorrith’s chamber. He would not ask for a second bowl. "Give it some time" sounded like every adult who had ever owed him nothing.

Under lush canopy again, he made a bed of root and leaf and tried not to replay the flick of that thin finger.

By morning the region had already begun its other weather.

Talk moved orchard to orchard: a Driftfall boy had climbed the grey shoulder, lit the cook’s interest, carried a True Blue into the hollow. **The Boy Who Lit the Hollow**—half warning, half spectacle. Doors that might have opened to a hungry worker shut harder. Some voices wanted Keepers. Some wanted the story only. Calm country hated being forced to look at Ashcalm.

Frederick woke under his tree with rumor already thicker than dew, still feeling ordinary, still missing the stone that had made him dangerous and chosen.

He did not know the gift was only late.
