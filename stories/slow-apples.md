# Slow Apples

## Premise

Years after the will, Frederick’s Apple Touch rivalry with Bram Orlen has run long enough that Bram’s apples grow too slow to survive—and Bram comes to Fellgrove offering to sell his farm.

## Story

Time did what rivalry could not: it made both men older inside the same lanes.

Frederick had not stayed only under Wabet shade. Farmer seasons sent him with crates and contracts across **F432**—Nauw markets, Sorel edge runs, basin docks that smelled like the liners of his youth. He had seen more of the lived band than most orchard hands. What he had not done was keep the promise folded in his pocket.

Bram Orlen’s ground stayed honest and seasonal. Honesty lost years of mornings to Fellgrove’s gift-fed brightness. One hard stretch finished what pride had delayed: Bram’s rows would not pay the lane. He stood at Frederick’s gate thinner than the man who had once refused a True Blue.

"Buy my farm," Bram said, without theater. "I cannot survive the slow apples. You already own the weather."

Frederick looked at the rival who had named Vorrith and closed a gate on a hungry boy. Buying would complete the victory chart. It would also plant him deeper in land.

"I’ll think about it," he said.

Bram nodded once, as if thinking were already a kinder answer than yes, and left Frederick with a choice that felt heavier than any crate.
