# The Turtle Day Nobody Logged

## Premise

Surveyors leave the thermal blank. Poets do not. The blank is not absence—it is a kind of seal.

## Story

The ledger line for that afternoon reads *clear sky, no incident.* The tube heir who drifted south remembers a cool shadow wide as a barge and a pause that felt like consideration.

House filing prefers blanks to miracles. Gallery talk prefers miracles to blanks. The fields keep both: children get the shell story; clerks get the empty cell.

At dusk a parent taught the difference between sky-raid claws and turtle hugeness without needing a photograph. The child asked why nobody logged it. The parent said, “Because some truths grow wrong when stamped.”

The cloud—if it was a cloud—had already walked on.
