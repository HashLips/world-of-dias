# The Ribbon Cut at Hallow Bay

## Name

The Ribbon Cut at Hallow Bay

## Premise

A visitor’s blue ribbon is found cut before the week ends. Stone has been moved on Elder Shelf. Yara Moss must decide whether weather, theft, or conscience gets named—and whether welcome survives the naming.

## Story

The bay stays polite while the Watch House slate fills. Children offer a second ribbon “for the sea” as repair. Yara refuses repairs that skip confession. The visitor leaves on the next flat window. The Rope Tree keeps the cut end as teaching fiber.

## Notes

Calareth saga seed; visitor identity left open.
