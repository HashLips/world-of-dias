# Roadglow on the Approach

## Premise

Before the DEB Gate, before echo-reading has a name in her mouth, Calen Brink finds a rare blue Resonant Shard on the dirt road into Veloria—and learns that some stones stop strangers mid-step.

## Story

The approach was dust, carts, and the kind of heat that makes people walk without talking.

Calen had six days of Brindle March still in her legs and a notebook that believed fairness could be written down. Veloria’s towers sat soft on the horizon, pale and unreal, like a promise that might vanish if she blinked too hard.

The shard was simply there—in the wheel rut, dark as old glass until the sun caught the break and the break answered blue.

She stopped. So did two walkers behind her, and a fruit-cart woman who muttered something that sounded like a prayer and a warning in the same breath.

"True Blue," someone said, not to Calen exactly. To the air. To the habit of naming danger when it shines.

Calen picked it up because leaving it felt like lying. The stone warmed her palm. Inside the fracture, light moved like a held breath. She did not yet know how to call what she felt—only that the road had become louder, and the distant city seemed closer without moving.

A boy on a mule stared openly. An older man shook his head once, as if counting what the find would cost later. Nobody tried to take it from her hand. Rarity has manners when witnesses are many.

She wrapped it in cloth and walked on.

Behind her, Veloria brightened. Ahead of her, a gate she had never touched waited with a memory she could not yet hear. In her pocket, Roadglow kept its quiet fire—the first companion of a journey that would teach her listening, and years later teach a Driftfall boy what a gift can demand.
