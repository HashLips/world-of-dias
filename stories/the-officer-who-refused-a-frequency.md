# The Officer Who Refused a Frequency

## Name

The Officer Who Refused a Frequency

## Premise

At Long Gate, an officer uses the Nest Phrase Strip when a traveler names “F500” as if it were a pier down the east road.

## Story

The traveler had good boots and a bad sentence. “East road to F500,” they said, as if null were a harbor with a toll.

The officer touched the indigo strip inside the booth without making a show of it. “You are going to a place. The sky may argue. That is not the same journey.”

They argued for a minute—maps, tavern cold, a bruise that felt imported. The officer nodded at the bruise. “That may be a phenomenon visiting. File it as weather-of-meaning if you must write. Do not ask me for a ferry stamp into a frequency.”

The traveler left toward Driftfall with a corrected form and an uncorrected hunger. The gate stayed a gate in Nauw in F432 in Dias. Somewhere, Softfruit would finish the kindness with fruit.
