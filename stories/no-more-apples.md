# No More Apples

## Premise

After Frederick’s return to Vorrith Kael, no one sees him again. He had kept the harvest promise until that day. Whether he crossed frequencies remains unknown. Wabet is left with empty rows waiting for a touch that does not come—and with his letter.

## Story

Bram waited through one harvest window, then another.

Frederick had always come. Once before harvest, palms to bark, apples on cue. The lane knew his name. The region had watched him grow from refused boy to covenant partner. Word of his second climb to Ashcalm moved faster than fruit ever had.

Then: nothing.

No body on the game track. No confession from Stillhollow. No Apple Touch on Fellgrove’s wood. Trees stood as trees again—seasonal, slow, honest. Bram planted. Markets adjusted. Children asked if the hollow had eaten the orchard man. Elders said only that some seekers do not walk back down.

Did Frederick Lumens travel to another frequency?

**The Lumen Saga** does not say.

What remains is the paper he carried from Driftfall to Fellgrove: **Letter to Myself at the Crossing**—a boy’s vow that outlived the boy’s visible path. Readers who hold it hold the open door he wanted. The rest waits for later discovery in Dias, if Dias ever answers.
