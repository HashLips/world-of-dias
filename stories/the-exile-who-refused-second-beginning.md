# The Exile Who Refused Second Beginning

## Name

The Exile Who Refused Second Beginning

## Premise

A Nauw exile arrives at Secondfire’s teaching fire and refuses the Compact’s remaking schedule—not out of villainy, out of the need to remain continuous with a self that shame tried to erase.

## Story

Hanna Kiln offers clay and a slate line. The exile offers labor without a new name. For a week the courtyard argues in glances. On the eighth morning Hanna sets a blank plate at the exile’s place and says, “Then eat as who you are—and still wash the dish.” The Compact does not collapse. The story becomes a tool for later refusals.

## Notes

Warm Sorel hook; unnamed exile left open for a future saga protagonist.
