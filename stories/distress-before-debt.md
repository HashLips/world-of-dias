# Distress Before Debt

## Premise

Two rival Outer Rim crews owe each other bad coin—but when one hull takes on water, the other answers distress before debt, because the mutual rescue rule is older than their argument.

## Story

The signal was ugly: three short horns, then a break, then three again. Not a song. Not a boast.

On the *Salt-Laugh*, Captain Rill spat over the rail. "That's Coil's cousin. They shorted us last Flagweek."

Marek Solon's apprentice—young, salt-proud—waited for permission to pretend not to hear.

"Capable of safe intervention?" Marek asked the swell, the way Outer Rim captains ask the sea for a second opinion. The swell did not forbid them. That was enough.

They came alongside with nets ready and insults pocketed. Coil's cousin's boat wallowed, one spar cracking, a child lashed to the mast with more hope than skill. No one mentioned ledgers while hands pulled bodies and crates. Salvage talk waited on the dock, as the rule requires: life support cannot be delayed for contract reasons.

Later, at Hollowmere, someone tried to make the rescue into a bill. Marek laughed once—short, like a horn. "Distress before debt," he said, and the quay repeated it until even Coil's cousin nodded, ashamed into manners.

That night the Singing Fishermen sang a soft return song. Joy tasted like dry clothes. Rivalry would return with the next tide. The rule would still be there first.
