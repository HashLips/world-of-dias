# Sky-Whale Lantern Mark

## Name

Sky-Whale Lantern Mark

## Overview

The **Sky-Whale Lantern Mark** is a small whale silhouette with a lantern belly—meaning *festival light that unfinished shadows may still share*.

## Meaning

Joy without Forced-Final completeness. Myth of unfinished whale shadow rides under the lantern as soft wonder, not ferry lore.
