# Load-Share Strap Mark

## Name

Load-Share Strap Mark

## Overview

The **Load-Share Strap Mark** is two linked strap ends—meaning *split the burden before the road splits the body*.

## Meaning

Shared weight is manners, not charity theater. Forced-Final lone-hero wagons fail this mark. Softfruit and fruit-road both borrow the twin ends.

## Usage

- Brindle Load-Share Strap tags and wagon boards.
- Waystation chalk when loaner straps go out.
- Bead-warm lesson cards for children.
- Comedy slash on “I carry alone” pride paints.

## Lore Connection

Brindle March load-sharing rule. Phenomenon Load-Share Bead Warm. Cousin to Lane-Fit Boot Mark for body-first travel.

## Notes

Travel load glyph. Distinct from Softfruit Keep-Mark Bead (table share).
