# Scrape-Scar Tick

## Name

Scrape-Scar Tick

## Overview

The **Scrape-Scar Tick** is a pale underline or ghost-tick meaning *correction scraped, history kept*—the symbol form of the pale shadow under new ink.

## Meaning

Certainty was not born finished. The scar is data. Erase habits leave no tick and earn Softfruit scorn.

## Usage

- Light chalk under revised digits on ledgers.
- Blade-practice cards for Inkspan juniors.
- Row aprons as anti-erase reminder.
- Teaching next to Measure-Mark: check, then scrape if wrong—never wipe clean.

## Lore Connection

Scraped correction preference made iconic. Phenomenon Margin-Scrape Pale Shadow is the felt version; this is the drawable one. Orrin’s ledger culture wears it quietly.

Craft honesty. Distinct from Ask-Again Slit (re-run) and Measure-Mark (checked).
