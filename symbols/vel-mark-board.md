# VEL Mark Board

## Name

VEL Mark Board

## Overview

The **VEL Mark Board** is the posted conversion table at markets and gates where traders settle disputes by converting to **VEL marks** at the day’s published rate—no insult, no delay, trade moves on.

## Meaning

Agreed reality for a day: value made visible so strangers can cooperate. Everyday sorcery—layered economies held together by a number everyone can point to. When the board is trusted, joy stays in the bargain; when it is doubted, markets get loud.

## Usage

Consulted before arguments escalate. Touching the board edge while reading is common but not required. Tampering is a serious civic offense in Nauw trade law. Children learn to read the board before they learn which fruit is sacred.

## Lore Connection

Featured in [`stories/first-day-in-veloria.md`](../stories/first-day-in-veloria.md) and Calen’s arrival path. Visual shorthand that the world works even when it is vast. Artworks of VEL mark denominations sit beside this symbol as money-face cousins.

## Notes

Not a Resonant Artifact of destiny—just civic craft that prevents blood over pennies. Open question: who reprints the board when core load makes rates thrash mid-day.
