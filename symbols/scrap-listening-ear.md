# Scrap-Listening Ear

## Name

Scrap-Listening Ear

## Overview

The **Scrap-Listening Ear** is a simplified ear outline with one inward tick—meaning *this nook hears scrap before it files it*.

## Meaning

Listening is work. Stamp-first rooms fail the ear. Distress and unsigned care both earn a seat under this mark.

## Usage

- Scrap Listening Nook lintels and hour boards.
- Courier sleeve cards for where to leave humming jobs.
- Softfruit chapbooks teaching ear vs mute-plug night.
- Strike versions on House “already heard” doors that never open.

## Lore Connection

Operator Scrap Listening Nook as place. Phenomenon Scrap-Static After Stamp as why the ear stays open after ink.

## Notes

Place manners glyph. Not a spy badge.
