# Reveal Timing Icon

## Name

Reveal Timing Icon

## Overview

The **Reveal Timing Icon** is a thin arc with short, medium, and rare-long ticks, the Glassfold chart mark for window duration. It tells you clarity is scheduled weather. It does not tell you who is good. Apprentices who want the long tick to mean “worthy” are walked through the icon until the worthiness falls off it and only the timing remains.

## Meaning

A window has a length, and the length is not a moral. Short is not a punishment. Rare-long is not a prize for staring. The arc is the open minute; the ticks are the bands the ledge actually spends. People who try to add a fourth tick, an infinite one, are illustrating the myth of the minute that would not stay. The icon’s refusal to include that tick is the teaching. Pack at the soft close. Ask again later. Do not edit the legend until the legend flatters your patience.

## Usage

Dual-overlay maps, graphite for base terrain, steel-blue for the phase, icon in the corner so nobody confuses a reveal with a permanent layer. Chime boxes, where Timers can tap the relevant tick before the first note so the apprentice’s body knows the size of the gift. Guild stamps on slates. Visitor ledges sometimes show only the short tick, not to insult visitors, to keep miracle-hunger from shopping for the long one.

## Lore Connection

Ledge portrayal guidance and the anti-linger rule are the icon’s parents. The chime is the sound; this is the writing. Veil Scribes flinch at images that depict clarity as something injected or pumped. The arc cannot be forced fuller by wanting, which is why it belongs on a teaching wall beside any satire of measure-without-manners.

## Notes

Timing, not a verdict. Not a progress bar toward hidden truth.
