# Joy of Now

## Name

Joy of Now

## Overview

**Joy of Now** is the Coral Strand mark for a consented present: a seated calm figure, a leaner’s hand at the chest, and a raised arm signaling that joy is happening and may be joined without shouting it apart.

## Description

Drawn with four bodies or simplified to seated form plus raised arm. The brown leaner in full copies reminds people that joy often arrives through another’s careful attention. Strand cloths hang the mark near morning mats. Children practice the chest-touch as a tap, not a shove. Adults who raise the arm while ignoring a turned-away eye-slit are corrected: the symbol requires consent in the center.

## Cultural Meaning

Around Coral Strand, Joy of Now is shared attention with a stop rule. Resonance favors quiet centers. Using the mark to demand cheer empties it.

## Usage

Workshop openings, strand festivals, and small healings after hard perch days. Washed when the now has passed.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Plate-led four-figure joy.
