# Rest-Viability Ember Dot

## Name

Rest-Viability Ember Dot

## Overview

A **Rest-Viability Ember Dot** is an orange-gold node on hearthline maps and on the objects of a spur: beds and warmth still agree. It is a quiet signal. It does not mean speeches are welcome, fees may include air, or the glow is a promise until morning. A dot that has gone dark while a fire still burns for show is the symbol doing its most important work.

## Meaning

Open for bodies. Not open for a story about toughness. The gold is dull on purpose; bright gold is how vanity imitates viability. Travelers learn to trust a dim node more than a roar. Keepers mark bowls and horn tags with it so the permission can be checked without a lecture. When the dot is absent, leaving is not shame. Staying to argue the dot back into existence is how companions get spent.

## Usage

Spur charts, beside distance, and outranking distance when the two disagree. Horn tags, so the person holding the horn remembers the horn is a sentence. Undersides of Ember Quiet Bowls, a little sun you see when you turn the bowl up to fill it. A merchant’s rate card decorated with the dot is a counterfeit. The culture wipes those. Myth of the warmth that refused a fee is, in graphic terms, a dot walking away from a price.

## Lore Connection

Rest-viability hospitality and the recheck rule share the mark. Ashen viable warmth is the larger cousin; the dot is the branch-scale honesty. Overnight stories end with the dot either still true at mid-ash or honestly gone. Both endings are compliant. Only the forged dot is a breach.
