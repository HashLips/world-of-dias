# Scraped-Ledger Hash

## Name

Scraped-Ledger Hash

## Overview

The **Scraped-Ledger Hash** is a small crosshatch over a line—meaning *this entry was corrected in place*; history stays under the scratch.

## Meaning

Reprint-as-innocence fails. Hash invites the next reader to see the change path. Misuse: hatching over living scrap to pretend it was always quiet.

## Usage

- Orrin Scraped Ledger page corners and correction stamps.
- Clerk-Operator hybrid desks that still honor scrape.
- Softfruit teaching: hash vs nonactionable grave.
- Strike when a House black-boxes the whole page.

## Lore Connection

Scraped correction preference and Margin-Scrape Pale. Operator craft against Forced-Final clean books.

## Notes

Correction glyph. Pair with Ask-Again Slit when hash tempts “never mind” silence.
