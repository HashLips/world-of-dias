# House-Seal Wax Ring

## Name

House-Seal Wax Ring

## Overview

The **House-Seal Wax Ring** is a simple wax circle with a blank center—meaning *sealed on purpose*; contents wait on ethics, not curiosity alone.

## Meaning

Seal is a vow, not a trophy. Forced-Final seals that hide scrap care fail. Softfruit asks whether the blank center protects or hoards.

## Usage

- House packet corners and Sealed Beta Note wrappers.
- Study-path cards for clerk-and-House students.
- Softfruit satire of wax sold as fashion.
- Paired with Who-Seals Question Mark on mystery primers.

## Lore Connection

Sealed Knowledge Ethics and Who Seals What Mystery. Resonant Houses use wax as institutional breath, not only vanity.

## Notes

Institutional seal glyph. Distinct from Golden Beta Emblem (dream chamber letter).
