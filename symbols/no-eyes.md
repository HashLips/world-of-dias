# No Eyes

## Name

No Eyes

## Overview

**No Eyes** is an F432 mark-and-meaning shape: a kneeling keeper cradles a golden ovoid while white eyes weep thin red lines and pale pink eye-stalks watch from the ground—sight present, hunting sight refused.

## Description

Copied on boards and whispered in realm talk, No Eyes shows the paradox the name carries. The face has eyes—wide white circles, red pupils, red streaks down the cheeks—yet the rule is not to weaponize looking while you hold the golden ovoid. The ovoid is smooth and large, held with both arms against the chest as if dropping it would spill more than liquid. Pale pink stalks ending in blue-pupiled white eyes may lie beside the knees; they are treated as memory-watchers that have left the face so the keeper can care without scanning for prey. Orange and ochre fields, a soft glow behind the shoulders, and a pink pool under the kneel complete the usual plate. People use the mark when a story needs a handle for protected grief or for cargo that must not be stared into possession. Copied wrong, it becomes empty horror decoration. Copied with care, it still points to the same decision: hold, weep if you must, do not hunt with your gaze. Resonance is the match between face-weep and stalk-watch; wonder is how warm the orange stays; memory is the ovoid's weight in the arms of anyone who recognizes the symbol.
