# Ladle-Before-Stamp

## Name

Ladle-Before-Stamp

## Overview

**Ladle-Before-Stamp** is a ladle silhouette over a struck-through stamp box—Softfruit’s core order glyph: *share or greet before invoice*.

## Meaning

Coin may follow the meal; it may not open it. Forced-Final market manners and House clearance-before-soup both fail this mark. The ladle is not decoration above the law. It is the claim that fairness to a mouth outranks speed to a record.

## Usage

- Softfruit Table Hall and ladle-court lintels.
- Court posts at eye height for people in a rush—slightly lower than a proud clerk expects.
- Chapbook covers and keep-bowl cards.
- Softfruit Field Interview kits.
- Second-ladle tokens, carved small and sticky.
- Comedy strike on VIP-first-ladle membership ads.

## Lore Connection

Rule face of Softfruit ladle-before-stamp. Myth: The Ladle That Outranked the Coin. Softfruit Ways and Manners Before Stamps primers both point here. The mark keeps the practice in the room when nobody is telling the story.

## Notes

Joy manners glyph. Distinct from Wabet Open Hand (maker ethic). Comedy is the enforcement mechanism, not a failure of seriousness.
