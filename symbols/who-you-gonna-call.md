# Who You Gonna Call

## Name

Who You Gonna Call

## Overview

**Who You Gonna Call** is a mark painted on call-posts along Sorel's emptier roads: a red dial-box on a black stake, with a long blue reach arching over it. It means that this post connects to someone who will answer honest distress before asking about payment.

## Description

The posts themselves are simple: a black stake, a small square shelf, and a red dial-box with a curled handset, wired underground to a relay house, a freeband camp, or a Hearthvale shelter depending on the stretch. The mark is painted on the stake or on a board beside it—red box, black post, and the blue limb rising from the ground with its clawed hand hanging just short of the handset. Older marks also show a hand-shaped shadow at the post's foot, which painters say represents everyone who has ever stood there waiting.

## Meaning

The mark extends the distress-before-debt rule to land roads: when a call is honest, the answer comes first and any reckoning comes later, as favor rather than interest. The blue reach is not a creature anyone claims to have met; it stands for whatever help arrives. Defacing a call-post mark is treated as seriously as cutting its wire.
