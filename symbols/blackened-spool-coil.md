# Blackened-Spool Coil

## Name

Blackened-Spool Coil

## Overview

The **Blackened-Spool Coil** is a tight spiral darkened on one edge—meaning *worked thread that remembers heat*, not unused purity.

## Meaning

Used tools tell truth. Forced-Final “new spool only” pride fails. Blackening is evidence of listening labor, not shame.

## Usage

- Veld Blackened Spool tags and alcove boards.
- Workshop cards teaching coil before boast.
- Softfruit jokes on shiny-spool merchants.
- Paired with Residual-Graph Tick on graph-and-spool kits.

## Lore Connection

Orrin Veld’s workshop texture. Houses and Operators pressure to look unused vs craft that has heard load.

Craft wear glyph. Not a fire warning alone.
