# Melting-Smile Drip

## Name

Melting-Smile Drip

## Overview

**Melting-Smile Drip** is a yellow smile with a descending drip—Softfruit’s warning that Forced-Final cheer is not joy.

## Meaning

Fake permanent happiness confesses. Helpful-glass grins and loyalty-smile stamps earn this mark as mockery or diagnosis. Real pours happen one glass at a time.

## Usage

- Softfruit Melting Smile Pins and festival volunteer cards.
- Opposite Nonactionable Grave Mark on training posters.
- Comedy tape on “be happy” clearance speeches.
- Bottles-of-joy teaching: pour vs pin forever.

## Lore Connection

Anti-forced-cheer rule’s face. Phenomenon Melting Cheer Drip. Keeps joy literacy sharp without banning honest laughter.

## Notes

Warning/comedy glyph. Not a ban on real Softfruit joy.
