# Harmonic Thrum

## Overview

The **Harmonic Thrum** is the low, directionless vibration many visitors feel just before dawn in Veloria—especially on ridge lanes with sightlines toward the inner core.

## Meaning

To outer-ring inhabitants it is **the city waking**. To Operators it is **work in progress made audible**. To echo-readers it is sometimes described as the Bridge's far heartbeat, filtered through core machinery no public diagram fully explains.

## Usage

Spoken of, not performed. People say "the thrum was early" or "the thrum skipped once" the way others might speak of weather. A skipped thrum is rare enough to unsettle markets for an hour.
