# When Wings Flap

## Name

When Wings Flap

## Overview

**When Wings Flap** is a mark painted on tall white market banners across the Velorian Basin: a crested figure clinging to a pole with a great white wing streaming out beside it. When wind makes the banner stretch out flat, the mark is flapping, and new deals in that market pause until the gust passes.

## Description

The banners are long strips of pale cloth hung from poles at market corners, painted with a slender figure at the pole edge and a wing that runs the banner's full length. Red and green offset lines along the wing make it easy to see whether it is lying slack or standing out. In calm air the wing droops and the figure seems to hang lazily; in a hard gust the whole banner snaps flat and the figure appears to be holding on for dear life. Market masters watch the nearest banner rather than the sky.

## Meaning

The Basin's traders hold that deals struck in a gale blow apart later—tempers are short, goods shift, and nobody hears the terms properly. The mark makes that caution public and automatic. While the wing flaps, existing deals can be completed but no new bargains are opened. Children love to shout "wings!" at the first gust, and traders pretend to be annoyed.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. The crest has no rank meaning.
