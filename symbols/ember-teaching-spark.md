# Ember-Teaching Spark

## Name

Ember-Teaching Spark

## Overview

The **Ember-Teaching Spark** is a small ember diamond—meaning *teach with living heat*, not cold drill alone.

## Meaning

Learning keeps a coal. Forced-Final lecture stacks fail when the spark is stamped out. Softfruit and kiln yards both borrow the diamond.

## Usage

- Ember teaching cards and orchard kid chalk.
- Open Hand Faction kits beside Open-Hand Palm.
- Kiln yard boards when laughter tokens circulate.
- Strike on sold “instant mastery without ember” pamphlets.

## Lore Connection

Open Hand and Ember Teaching. Wabet and Reciprocity primer for warmth as method.

## Notes

Teaching glyph. Spark is care, not arson pride.
