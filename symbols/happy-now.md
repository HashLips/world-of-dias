# Happy Now

## Name

Happy Now

## Overview

**Happy Now** is an F960 mark-and-meaning plate: the orange caretaker feeding a small black bowl-swimmer, used when joy in the present tense requires a consented flip of who gives and who receives.

## Description

The graphic is easy to love and easy to drain into cute cruelty. Careful hands keep the rule inside: the bowl is temporary hospitality, flakes are soft, and the swimmer may leave. Copied empty, it becomes a joke about pets. Copied with care, it still asks whether today’s happiness includes letting the tired giver be fed. No Earth-faith reading required. Frequency doors hang careful copies near guest kitchens and quiet wards.
