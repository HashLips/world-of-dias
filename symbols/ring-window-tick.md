# Ring-Window Tick

## Name

Ring-Window Tick

## Overview

The **Ring-Window Tick** is a short arc-tick like a gate half-open—then a stop bar. It means: *clearance for this window only; power down after*.

## Meaning

Timed belonging, not forever ID. Not a band ferry. Purpose + timing + load compressed into one civic glyph for wafers and sleeve cards.

## Usage

- Etched on Ring-Pass Wafers and day-sleeve leather.
- Posted at mid-ring gates beside clearance boards.
- Festival sleeve borrower cards.
- Softfruit jokes crossing out fake “band subway” ads with this tick.

## Lore Connection

Visual twin of ring clearance rule and wafer window rule. Personal Resonant Tech primer points here so pocket craft stays mythic, not phone-shaped.

## Notes

Civic/tech manners. Soft limits: never a subway glyph.
