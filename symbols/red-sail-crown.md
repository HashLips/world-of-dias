# Red-Sail Crown

## Name

Red-Sail Crown

## Overview

The **Red-Sail Crown** is Vailor Quentin’s crown-standard—the visual mark of High Resonant authority across F432—and one of the most argued-about symbols in realm politics. Grandeur you can see from afar; origin still disputed.

## Meaning

Red sail cloth evokes **reach and command over distance**. Supporters hear unity enforced well. Skeptics hear seizure dressed as destiny. Two incompatible origin stories persist: gift from a Singing Fisherman versus taking by force during Vailor’s rise. The archive does not pick a winner early.

## Usage

Displayed in formal assemblies, reproduced on House seals, referenced in oaths without requiring the physical crown present. Public duplication is restricted; imitation without sanction reads as challenge. Outer-ring children draw crude red triangles and get shushed.

## Lore Connection

Central to [`inhabitants/vailor-quentin.md`](../inhabitants/vailor-quentin.md). Harmonic Saga treats the crown as distant magnificence. Artifact cousins may exist as standards/banners; the symbol is the idea that travels farther than cloth.

## Notes

Do not resolve seizure vs gift in early saga chapters. Open question: what happens to legitimacy if both origin stories are somehow true.
