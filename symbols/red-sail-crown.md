# Red-Sail Crown

## Overview

The **Red-Sail Crown** is Vailor Quentin's crown-standard—the visual mark of High Resonant authority across F432—and one of the most argued-about objects in realm politics.

## Meaning

Red sail cloth evokes **reach and command over distance**. To supporters it means unity enforced well. To skeptics it means seizure dressed as destiny. Two incompatible origin stories persist: gift from a Singing Fisherman versus taking by force during Vailor's rise.

## Usage

Displayed in formal assemblies, reproduced on House seals, and referenced in oaths without requiring the physical crown's presence. Public duplication is restricted; imitation without sanction is read as challenge.

## Lore Connection

Central to [`inhabitants/vailor-quentin.md`](../inhabitants/vailor-quentin.md). The Harmonic Saga treats the crown as **grandeur you can see from afar**—truth about its origin remains open.

## Notes

Do not resolve seizure vs gift in saga early chapters.
