# Nonactionable Grave Mark

## Name

Nonactionable Grave Mark

## Overview

The **Nonactionable Grave Mark** is a small closed smile-slit in a box—the polite grave for wonder stamped quiet. Softfruit teaches it as a warning glyph, not a virtue badge.

## Meaning

*Filed as noise; living question buried.* Legitimate for true non-action items; cursed when used on helpful glass, unsigned scrap, or visitor unease. Melting-cheer culture mocks anyone who wears it proudly.

## Usage

- Training posters: “When not to use this.”
- Softfruit comedy pins opposite melting smiles.
- Red ink on desks that overused quiet stamps.
- Myth retellings of clerks who filed smiles.

## Lore Connection

Story **Nonactionable** and myth **The Clerk Who Filed a Smile** make the mark culturally radioactive after Calibration caution. Ask-Again Slit is the ethical alternative face.

## Notes

Warning symbol. Do not confuse with Measure-Mark checked work.
