# Byte Hard

## Name

Byte Hard

## Overview

**Byte Hard** is a Brindle March mark-and-meaning plate: a biting face used when a load-share, joke, or grab needs a hard stop before care turns into chewing.

## Description

The graphic is loud on purpose—purple ground, red lips, white block teeth on an arm, blue collar, yellow wind-hair, pale double behind. Copied empty, it becomes cruel décor. Copied with care, it still asks the same question: are you chewing your own capacity, or someone else’s margin? March hands post it near leans when chalk alone is too polite. The blue forehead teardrop on the double is read as the softer self still present. No Earth-faith or Earth-tech reading is required. Travelers who laugh it off as a computer pun are corrected once.

People who keep a careful copy say the next promise after seeing it should be smaller and cleaner. Carving it into living wood without assent is still bad resonance craft.
