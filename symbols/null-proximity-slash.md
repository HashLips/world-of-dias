# Null-Proximity Slash

## Name

Null-Proximity Slash

## Overview

The **Null-Proximity Slash** is a dark diagonal cut through an empty circle. It warns. It does not summon, label a species, or close Frequency Zero. The circle is the temptation to draw a place. The slash is the courtesy that will not let the drawing finish. People who find it stark are reading it correctly. Stark is cheaper than a tour.

## Meaning

Do not tour absence. Do not stamp a solution over a margin that eats solutions. The empty circle is not a target and not a face. Filling it in, giving it features, a company, a confirmed kind of being, is the breach the mark exists to make visible. Temporary names sit well beside it. Field guides do not. A slash added to a cairn sketch means the sketcher stopped. That stopping is the content.

## Usage

Warning tags in classrooms and on docks. Boards beside no-ferry jokes, so the joke and the ethic share a wall and neither gets to be the whole culture. Softfruit corners that collect caution comedy redraw it slightly crooked and are corrected if the crookedness starts to look like a character. It is not worn as a charm for luck. Wearing it as a dare is the sport the mark refuses. Teachers tap it when a witness begins a sentence they should not be pushed to finish.

## Lore Connection

Null-Distance Courtesy is the culture; the slash is its handwriting. Cairn myths and identity-slip stories are told under it and not past it. Unraveled, Horizon-Null, and distant-company talk can be heard in the room. The mark is how the room declines to promote them. Zero stays an open pressure, not an emblem you can complete.

## Notes

Ethics mark, not a summoning sign, not a faction badge, not a map symbol you may navigate toward.
