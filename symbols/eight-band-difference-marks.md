# Eight-Band Difference Marks

## Name

Eight-Band Difference Marks

Eight simple glyphs used in teaching chapbooks and cloth tags to stand for established bands without drawing fake coasts: echo-break ring (F120), bright bar (F200), pulse curve (F380), home square (F432), void notch (F500), pane slash (F610), ember dot (F840), draft bracket (F960).
