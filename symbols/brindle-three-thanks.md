# Brindle Three-Thanks

## Name

Brindle Three-Thanks

## Overview

The **Brindle Three-Thanks** is three small bow ticks—meaning *thank three times* before Forced-Final hurry leaves the road cold.

## Meaning

Gratitude is march manners. One muttered thanks fails the three. Softfruit loves the triple as cousin to ladle greetings.
