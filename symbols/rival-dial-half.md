# Rival-Dial Half

## Name

Rival-Dial Half

## Overview

The **Rival-Dial Half** is a dial face cut at the midpoint—meaning *half-measure comparison*, not full ownership of another’s curve.

## Meaning

Rivalry that stops short of theft. Turning past the half is House-bait and Softfruit mockery. Honest use: keep score without swallowing the other workshop.
