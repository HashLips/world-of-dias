# Ask-Again Slit

## Name

Ask-Again Slit

## Overview

The **Ask-Again Slit** is a narrow vertical measure-slit with a second tick beside it. It means: *re-run, re-ask—do not bury this as noise yet*.

## Meaning

Opposite of a smiling nonactionable grave. The first tick is “looked.” The neighbor tick is “look again.” Together they refuse Forced-Final quiet when glass agrees too kindly or a visitor wrong-tick shows.

## Usage

- Ink face of Ask-Again Stamp Pads at Clerk Stamp Gallery and Row alcoves.
- Margin chalk beside helpful-glass readings.
- Softfruit field notes when instrument weather feels sticky.
- Teaching cards next to Measure-Marks so apprentices learn check vs ask-again.

## Lore Connection

Rule twin to ask-again before nonactionable. Story cousin: Ask Again at the Ledge. Keeps Softfruit noticing and clerk craft in one glyph family with Measure-Mark—without becoming a House rank seal.

## Notes

Craft manners, not Operator clearance. Distinct from Caliburn Slit (haunt parody).
