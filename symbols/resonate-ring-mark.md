# Resonate Ring Mark

## Name

Resonate Ring Mark

## Overview

A simple drawn or stitched ring used in Softfruit and teaching spaces to mean **answering**—the moment participation happens—before anyone argues about what echo will remain afterward.

## Meaning

Contact that matters. The ring is not a frequency realm map; it is a reminder that resonance is the verb.

## Usage

Embroidered on Resonance Bowl cloths; chalked beside Nest Marks when children confuse answering with leftover; sketched by makers before resetting a tool’s habit echo.

## Lore Connection

Keeps “everything resonates” visible as action, while Partial Echo Slash and Nest Marks handle remainder and scale.

## Notes

If someone draws the ring around Frequency Zero as a solved diagram, Softfruit wipes it and pours tea.
