# No Puppet

## Name

No Puppet

## Overview

**No Puppet** is an Elder Shelf mark: red-curtained stage, oversized white hands on yellow strings, and a slumped black jointed figure on the gray floor—the picture of control the Shelf teaches people to refuse.

## Description

On the Shelf the symbol is copied as a warning, not as a how-to for operators. The white hands are large and clean against dark brown; yellow dots on the fingers mark where the thin yellow strings begin. The strings drop to a small black geometric humanoid slumped on gray—craft limbs, head down, no fight left in the posture. Red curtains close the sides so the lesson cannot be mistaken for private mischief. Locals say No Puppet when someone is being walked by another person's will and needs a shape to name the trap. The mark does not accuse every teacher or every guide; it names the hour when strings replace consent. Copied as decoration without the refusal, it empties into chic theater. Copied with care, it still asks the same decision: whose yellow line is on your wrist, and will you cut it. Resonance is the tension in the string; memory is the slumped floor figure; wonder is how bright the mechanism stays while the body goes dark. Hands that teach the symbol are expected to show empty palms afterward.
