# No-Ferry Mark

## Name

No-Ferry Mark

## Overview

The **No-Ferry Mark** is a small boat with a strike through it. On spectrum papers it means: *understanding a band is not a ticket to ride there*. It is the visual half of Veloria’s soft limit against subway fantasies between frequencies.

## Meaning

Plain speech under the mark usually reads some form of: **This describes resonance behavior. It does not sell passage. There is no safe ferry between bands.**

It answers Ell Ferrylie’s comedy tickets and Null Curiosity buyers who treat charts as doors. It also answers honest wonder that wants Frederick Lumens’s kind of crossing without paying the cost.

## Usage

- Margins of the **Issa Partial Packet** and school copies of partial spectrum truth.
- Aprons and shelf cards on Calibration Row when apprentices argue “just one widen.”
- Chalk beside F200 / F500 teaching diagrams at the Tea Slit Bench.
- Sometimes stamped on Fake Band Ferry Tickets after the joke, so the ethics land.

## Lore Connection

Required by the **partial spectrum publish rule** and the **no-ferry disclaimer custom**. In **The Calibration Saga**, Issa Partial treats the mark as care, not cowardice. Orrin’s earlier refusal to Frederick is the same mark spoken aloud before it was ink.

## Notes

Keep comic cousins (Ell’s pier) and serious publish use in the same family—both teach the soft limit.
