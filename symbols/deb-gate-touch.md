# DEB Gate Touch

## Overview

The **DEB Gate Touch** is the two-finger greeting travelers give the stone frame of Veloria's DEB Gate before passing through—part courtesy, part self-steadying, part habit so common most forget it is a ritual at all.

## Meaning

Touch before entry says: *I arrive with a body, not only with goods.* For echo-sensitive visitors, the gesture also **grounds** harmonic residue enough to walk without drowning in it—though this effect is not taught in official gate briefings.

## Usage

Performed with the index and middle finger, brief contact at shoulder height on the frame, usually without speech. Omitting the touch is not illegal; it is read as hurry, arrogance, or first-time ignorance.
