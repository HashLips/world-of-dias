# DEB Gate Touch

## Name

DEB Gate Touch

## Overview

The **DEB Gate Touch** is the two-finger greeting travelers give the stone frame of Veloria’s DEB Gate before passing through—part courtesy, part self-steadying, part habit so common most forget it is a ritual.

## Meaning

Touch before entry says: *I arrive with a body, not only with goods.* For echo-sensitive visitors, the gesture also grounds harmonic residue enough to walk without drowning in it—though gate briefings do not advertise that effect. Paired with the dignity rule, the touch is greeting rather than flinch.

## Usage

Index and middle finger, brief contact at shoulder height on the frame, usually without speech. Omitting it is not illegal; it reads as hurry, arrogance, or first-time ignorance. Film and story arrivals love this as the first tactile Veloria beat.
