# Anti-Forced-Cheer Slash

## Name

Anti-Forced-Cheer Slash

## Overview

The **Anti-Forced-Cheer Slash** is a smile cut by a soft diagonal—meaning *no mandatory grin*; cheer must be chosen.

## Meaning

House and festival Forced-Final happiness fails. Softfruit teaches the slash as kindness to tired faces. Kin to Melting-Smile Drip (honest melt).
