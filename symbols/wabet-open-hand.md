# Wabet Open Hand

## Overview

The **Wabet Open Hand** is a harvest gesture and spoken ethic: **take what sustains, leave what continues**— palm open at picking height, fingers relaxed, never closing around more than the meal requires.

## Meaning

Restraint as **moral power**. In saga canon Elyra Mossen teaches the phrase to travelers including Calen Brink; it is Wabet's answer to extraction cultures elsewhere in F432.

## Usage

Used at orchard lines, river draws, and rose-field margins. Closing the fist during harvest speech is considered a small insult unless famine is declared.

## Lore Connection

Central to [`stories/the-open-hand-and-the-rose.md`](../stories/the-open-hand-and-the-rose.md). Not the same as F960's **The Open Hand of Making**—shared language of stewardship, different frequency context.

## Notes

Visual reference: open palm beside uncut branch or unplucked rose.
