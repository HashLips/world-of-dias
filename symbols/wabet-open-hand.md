# Wabet Open Hand

## Name

Wabet Open Hand

## Overview

The **Wabet Open Hand** is a harvest gesture and spoken ethic: **take what sustains, leave what continues**—palm open at picking height, fingers relaxed, never closing around more than the meal requires.

## Meaning

Restraint as moral power. Wabet’s answer to extraction cultures elsewhere in F432. Joy that does not empty the orchard. Distinct from F960’s **Open Hand of Making** (revision craft)—shared stewardship language, different frequency context.

## Usage

Orchard lines, river draws, rose-field margins. Closing the fist during harvest speech is a small insult unless famine is declared. Elyra Mossen teaches the phrase to travelers including Calen Brink. Fruit-road hands echo it when they shout Fruit-Road Ethics at waystations.

## Lore Connection

Central to [`stories/the-open-hand-and-the-rose.md`](../stories/the-open-hand-and-the-rose.md). Cousin ethics: Fruit Road Reciprocity. Visual: open palm beside uncut branch or unplucked rose.

## Notes

Do not merge with Open Hand of Making faction seals. Open question: what the gesture means when the rose field is already dying and leaving more will not save it.
