# Between-Lines Beauty Mark

## Name

Between-Lines Beauty Mark

## Overview

The **Between-Lines Beauty Mark** is two parallel lines with a soft glow in the gap—meaning *beauty lives between repetitions*, not only on the loud stroke.

## Meaning

Forced-Final masterpiece worship fails. Softfruit and Averra teach the glow as patience of looking. Kin to Gallery-Gap Hold.

## Usage

- Between Repetitions culture cards.
