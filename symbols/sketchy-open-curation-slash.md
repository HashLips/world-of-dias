# Sketchy-Open Curation Slash

## Name

Sketchy-Open Curation Slash

## Overview

The **Sketchy-Open Curation Slash** is a frame cut by one soft diagonal—meaning *open curation*; no Forced-Final locked wall of taste.

## Meaning

Sketchy stays permeable. House-only hanging lists fail. Softfruit and Averra use the slash as permission to look again.

## Usage

- Sketchy Gallery door cards and open-curation posters.
- Visitor chapbooks beside Gallery-Gap Hold.
- Comedy slash (literal) on locked-taste membership ads.
- Teaching: slash vs Resonant-House Crest width.

## Lore Connection

Sketchy Gallery open curation rule. Gallery Going Customs. Myth of one more look.
