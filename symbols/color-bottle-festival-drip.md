# Color-Bottle Festival Drip

## Name

Color-Bottle Festival Drip

## Overview

The **Color-Bottle Festival Drip** is a bottle tip with one bright drip—meaning *color for shared looking*; festival paint that waits on damp patience.

## Meaning

Joy color without Forced-Final splash-over wet work. Averra studios and Softfruit festivals share the drip as cousin bottles.
