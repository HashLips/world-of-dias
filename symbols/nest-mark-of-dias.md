# Nest Mark of Dias

## Name

Nest Mark of Dias

## Overview

The **Nest Mark of Dias** is a chalk-and-cloth emblem of concentric rings used to teach the nesting ladder: outer ring for Dias or realm, inward rings for region and place, and a movable leaf-mark for local phenomena that must never harden into a ring.

## Meaning

Concentric containment without confusion. The outer ring is not “more important”; it is *wider*. The leaf is not a failure of geography; it is an event visiting geography.

## Usage

Drawn on Softfruit sidewalks, clerk practice boards, and orchard slate. Children place pebble tokens. Adults use it when correcting wrong maps in public without raising voices.

## Lore Connection

Born from Stack Teaching Customs after panic pamphlets labeled bleed as a district. Pairs with the Scale Ladder Glyph for vertical teaching when rings feel too gentle.

## Notes

Do not tattoo Frequency Zero into the rings as a settled rung.
