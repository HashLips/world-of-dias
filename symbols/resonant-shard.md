# Resonant Shard

## Name

Resonant Shard

## Overview

The **Resonant Shard** is a recurring visual and conceptual emblem of Dias: a fragment that still carries frequency and echo, suggesting that the broken world continues to remember in pieces.

## Meaning

A shard says the whole was never only one object or one story. What remains can still resonate. What resonates can still be misunderstood.

## Usage

Used when artists, storytellers, or in-world makers need a compact sign for echo-bearing matter, fractured history, or the promise that small things hold large pasts.

## Lore Connection

Tied to the artifact class [`artifacts/resonant-shards.md`](../artifacts/resonant-shards.md) and the umbrella [`artifacts/resonant-artifacts.md`](../artifacts/resonant-artifacts.md). Does not claim every broken stone is a Resonant Shard—only that the motif names a recognized pattern.
