# Resonant Shard

## Name

Resonant Shard

## Overview

The **Resonant Shard** is a recurring visual and conceptual emblem of Dias: a fragment that still carries frequency and echo—proof the broken world continues to remember in pieces.

## Meaning

A shard says the whole was never only one object or one story. What remains can still resonate. What resonates can still be misunderstood, gifted, compounded, or feared. Core pillar shorthand: everything resonates; everything leaves an echo—even in fragments.

## Usage

Artists, storytellers, and makers use it as a compact sign for echo-bearing matter, fractured history, or the promise that small things hold large pasts. Not every broken stone is a Resonant Shard; the emblem names a recognized pattern.
