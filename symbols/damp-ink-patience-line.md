# Damp-Ink Patience Line

## Name

Damp-Ink Patience Line

## Overview

The **Damp-Ink Patience Line** is a slow horizontal bleed edge—meaning *ink still drying*; do not stack, stamp, or boast over wet work.

## Meaning

Humidity is law. Forced-Final rush fails damp studios. Softfruit jokes love the line; Houses hate waiting.

## Usage

- Studio lintels and drying racks.
- Inkcap lesson cards for visitors.
- Averra Offshore Ways primer corners.
- Strike on “same-hour publish” tourist presses.

## Lore Connection

Averra damp-ink patience rule. Averra damp studios place manners. Cousin to Offshore-Ink Margin Line for isle ink ethics.
