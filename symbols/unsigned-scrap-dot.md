# Unsigned-Scrap Dot

## Name

Unsigned-Scrap Dot

## Overview

The **Unsigned-Scrap Dot** is a small hollow circle—meaning *help without a House signature*. Care arrives; credit does not.

## Meaning

Not invisibility for crime. Honest use: scrap aid that must not become clearance bait. Cowardly cousins hide under Load-Noise Bars instead of owning this mark.

## Usage

- Anonymous Load Scrap Token faces and courier chips.
- Scrap Listening Nook door cards.
- Softfruit chapbooks teaching unsigned care vs nonactionable graves.
- Red slash when a House stamps a name onto the hollow.

## Lore Connection

Unsigned scrap-care rule and the myth of the scrap that answered without a name. Operator adjacency without Operator clearance.

## Notes

Keep hollow—filled dots mean mute or done, not unsigned.
