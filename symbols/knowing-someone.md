# Knowing Someone

## Name

Knowing Someone

## Overview

**Knowing Someone** is the F120 mark for unfinished recognition: a yellow long-neck portrait with navy asymmetrical hair and a small red mouth, used when you may speak of outlines but must not pretend the person is fully read.

## Description

Drawn with the neck long on purpose—so the eye travels. Hair left-heavy reminds viewers that people are uneven. The red mouth stays small; enlarging it in graffiti is considered claiming speech you were not given. F120 learners hang the mark near introduction mats. Travelers who skip to nicknames after one glance are pointed back at the neck.

## Cultural Meaning

In F120, Knowing Someone is patience with a face. Resonance favors slow climbs. Using the mark to gossip as if complete empties it.

## Usage

Introduction cloths, apprentice name-days, and quiet corrections. Washed when a knowing is truly mutual.
