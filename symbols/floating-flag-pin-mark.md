# Floating-Flag Pin Mark

## Name

Floating-Flag Pin Mark

## Overview

The **Floating-Flag Pin Mark** is a small pin with a soft flag curl—meaning *flag floats; no captain hour still has manners*.

## Meaning

Identity without tyranny. Forced-Final captain pride fails the pin. Softfruit loves no-captain hours under this mark.

## Usage

- No-captain hour chalk and vent-crew cards.
- Harbor satire of crown-flag sellers.
- Paired with Red Sail Crown only as contrast lesson.
