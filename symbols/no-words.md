# No Words

## Name

No Words

## Overview

**No Words** is a Cinder Nook mark-and-meaning shape: a smooth pink humanoid face with slot features, a dark green shadow-echo behind it, and a large orange-red disc—silence kept as care rather than as emptiness.

## Description

In the Nook the symbol is used when a room or a road needs quiet that still has presence. The pink head is bald and polished, eyes and mouth only shallow light-pink slots, expression stoic enough to stop a chatter contest. The green-black echo behind matches the head's outline and carries no second mouth, treated as memory standing with you without speaking over you. The orange-red disc warms the brown field like a low sun that does not demand a hymn. People copy No Words on door cards, kiln walls, and small boards when forcing speech would bruise what is being held. Copied as a fashion blank-face, it loses the care rule. Copied with the echo and the disc intact, it still means: keep the meaning, skip the performance talk. Resonance is the pink/green double; wonder is how much the slot face can hold; memory is the habit of showing the mark before an argument turns into noise. Locals do not treat it as a ban on all language—only as a named hour when words would be the wrong tool.
