# Yes No Yes

## Name

Yes No Yes

## Overview

**Yes No Yes** is a mark-and-meaning plate in **Mercer Shed** talk: less a tool you pocket than a shape people recognize when a story needs a handle. Copied empty, it becomes decoration. Copied with care, it still points at the same decision about memory.

## Description

The graphic is easy to redraw and easy to drain. Around **Mercer Shed**, careful hands keep a question inside the shape—whether what you touched gets to stay as echo, lesson, or refuse. There is no Earth-faith reading required, and none should be imported. The mystery is local: marks that remember you remembering them.
