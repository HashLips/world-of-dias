# Second-Beginning Right Mark

## Name

Second-Beginning Right Mark

## Overview

The **Second-Beginning Right Mark** is a path fork with a soft new sprout—meaning *right to begin again*; refusal is also a story, not a stamp crime alone.

## Meaning

Second beginning is offered, not Forced-Final mandatory rebirth. Softfruit respects both takers and careful refusers under dignity rules.

## Usage

- Sorel compact cards and exile primer covers.
- Story teaching of refusal without contempt.
- Long Gate exit boards.
- Strike on “erase yourself for entry” kits.

## Lore Connection

Sorel second-beginning right. Exile and Second Beginning primer. Story of the exile who refused—kept as dignity texture.

## Notes

Exile rights glyph. Offer ≠ erase.
