# Tea-Tray Cooling Mark

## Name

Tea-Tray Cooling Mark

## Overview

The **Tea-Tray Cooling Mark** is three small cup-circles on a line—meaning *temperature before paper*: offer cooling before clearance stamps harden a quarrel.

## Meaning

No winners speech. Three cups, shared steam, then stamps if still needed. Priority of manners over gossip-as-law.

## Usage

- Tea-house lintels and tray bottoms.
- Mid-ring warden cards during pot-signal fights.
- Softfruit mediation kits.
- Myth posters of the tea that cooled three rings.

## Lore Connection
