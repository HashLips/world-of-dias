# Forced-Final Clamp Slash

## Name

Forced-Final Clamp Slash

## Overview

The **Forced-Final Clamp Slash** is a clamp silhouette cut by a diagonal—meaning *no mercy-clamp in recovery*; finish-forcing fails care.

## Meaning

Houses that call the clamp kindness still earn the slash. Softfruit and Operators both teach the mark when someone tries to seal a living scrape as done.

## Usage

- Recovery ward boards and F840 teaching cards.
- Softfruit strike posters of the mercy-clamp myth.
- Clerk desks beside Ask-Again Slit temptation.
- Red ink over House “closed forever” seals on unfinished files.

## Lore Connection

Forced-Final clamp ban in recovery. Myth of the clamp that called itself mercy. Distinct from honest seals that wait their turn.

## Notes

Ban glyph. Not a House rank emblem.
