# Glassfold Phase-Stamp

## Overview

The **Glassfold Phase-Stamp** is the formal seal Veil Scribes apply to reveal-window records—proof that testimony includes interval class, observer confidence, and corroboration state.

## Meaning

Fear and wonder **count** when timecoded. Unstamped certainty is treated as dangerous charisma; stamped fear is **honorable data**.

## Usage

Applied to wax tablets, ledger copies, and training exemplars. Basin witness network adapted a **folk variant**: timecodes read aloud before tea circles after Calen Brink's Glassfold escort.
