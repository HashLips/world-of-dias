# Glassfold Phase-Stamp

## Name

Glassfold Phase-Stamp

## Overview

The **Glassfold Phase-Stamp** is the formal seal Veil Scribes apply to reveal-window records—proof that testimony includes interval class, observer confidence, and corroboration state.

## Meaning

Fear and wonder **count** when timecoded. Unstamped certainty is dangerous charisma; stamped fear is honorable data. The stamp is how F610’s fragile truth becomes usable without becoming rumor.

## Usage

Wax tablets, ledger copies, training exemplars. Basin witness networks adapted a folk variant: timecodes read aloud before tea circles after famous Glassfold escorts. Forging a stamp is worse than lying—because it weaponizes procedure.
