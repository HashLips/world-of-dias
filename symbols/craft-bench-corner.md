# Craft-Bench Corner

## Name

Craft-Bench Corner

## Overview

The **Craft-Bench Corner** is an L-shaped corner mark—meaning *this bench is for making*; clear tools, leave room, do not stamp over wet work.

## Meaning

Bench manners are world manners. Forced-Final meetings on craft tables fail. Softfruit and Open Hand share the L as portable place law.

## Usage

- Bench lip stamps and studio hour boards.
- Craft Benches primer covers.
- Traveling maker kits on fruit-road.
- Comedy slash on House clearance desks built on borrowed benches.

## Lore Connection

Craft Benches of Dias primer. Open Hand Maker Tech. Averra Damp Studios as humid craft weather.
