# Claim Grade Marks

## Name

Claim Grade Marks

## Overview

Four simple marks used on cloth, chalk, and paper to signal claim grade: a closed bar (law), an open bracket (theory), an eye-dot (observation), and a veiled dash (taboo guess / Careful).

## Meaning

Weight of sentence. Not decoration for its own sake.

## Usage

Softfruit cloth tags; House junior boards; chapbook margins in Echo Metaphor and certainty lessons.

## Lore Connection

Visual language for Claim Grade Discipline, keeping Cosmology of Dias’ table usable in rooms without lecterns.

## Notes

Do not invent extra secret grades for clique power. Four is enough; dispute belongs inside them.
