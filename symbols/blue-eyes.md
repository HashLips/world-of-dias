# Blue Eyes

## Name

Blue Eyes

## Overview

**Blue Eyes** is a Veloria City mark-and-meaning plate: a red-hooded white-faced look with clear blue downward eyes, kept when a story needs a handle that still asks for quiet accountability.

## Description

The graphic is easy to redraw and easy to drain. Careful hands keep a question inside the shape—whether a touch stays as echo, lesson, or refuse. The red hood frames privacy; the blue eyes look down rather than perform; the mustard-yellow lines keep the face readable at market-board distance. Empty copies become decoration. Careful copies still change how the next promise sounds: sharper if you meant it, hollow if you only wanted the look of meaning. City talk forbids carving the mark into living wood without assent. There is no Earth-faith reading required, and none should be imported. The mystery is local: marks that remember you remembering them.

People who keep a careful copy often hang it near guest doors and clerk margins where vows get spoken softly. The gate-bridge seal sometimes printed with the plate is a printer’s corner mark, not a separate creed.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Quiet vow mark of Veloria City.
