# Partial Echo Slash

## Name

Partial Echo Slash

## Overview

The **Partial Echo Slash** is a mark—ink slash, stamp bar, or chalk stroke—used to label a reading, rumor, or residue as **incomplete on purpose**, so no one promotes a fragment into a verdict.

## Meaning

Honesty about remainder. The slash says: *something remains; we do not claim the whole.*

## Usage

Clerk margins, House junior sheets, Softfruit chapbooks when a story is true enough to tell and incomplete enough to harm if closed. Paired with Clerk Partial Echo Stamp in copy rooms.

## Lore Connection

Born where scrape-not-erase met market liars selling “complete echoes.” It is the visual cousin of Resonance behavior soft limits’ ban on casual omniscience.

## Notes

Do not use the slash to hide cowardice. Partial is a truth-grade, not an excuse to stop caring.
