# Eyes

## Name

Eyes

## Overview

**Eyes** is an F380 mark-and-meaning plate: a white mask with dark slits above a held yellow crescent, used when a shared resource needs guarding without a personal face taking credit.

## Description

The graphic is stark—black ground, white mask, pale keeper body, huge yellow curve. Copied empty, it becomes mysterious fashion. Copied with care, it still points at anonymous duty: see the crescent, hold it level, refuse to smile-perform ownership. Frequency stores and guest pantries post careful copies. No Earth-faith mask creed is imported. Travelers who treat the yellow form as an Earth fruit meme are corrected toward Dias measure-talk.
