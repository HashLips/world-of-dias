# Operator-Faction Wedge

## Name

Operator-Faction Wedge

## Overview

The **Operator-Faction Wedge** is a narrow triangle tip—meaning *Operator craft identity*, not House seal and not Softfruit ladle.

## Meaning

Belonging to load-listening work. Does not grant clearance, ferry rights, or stamp authority. Softfruit respects the wedge when it stays scrap-honest.

## Usage

- Valorian Operator Faction Card corners and sleeve pins.
- Workshop alcove boards and Scrap Listening Nook hour signs.
- Teaching cards: wedge vs Golden Beta / House emblems.
- Comedy slash on fake Operator VIP wedges sold at markets.

## Lore Connection

Valorian Operators and Operator Tech Culture. Houses and Operators primer for how wedges sit beside House power without becoming it.

## Notes

Faction identity glyph. Keep thin—wide triangles read as House claims.
