# Breakfast-Lid Seal

## Name

Breakfast-Lid Seal

## Overview

The **Breakfast-Lid Seal** is a closed bowl oval with a steam tick—meaning *lids stay until shared chew*; banners wait on sugar and salt.

## Meaning

Politics-before-porridge fails. Hunger fury is preventable weather. Softfruit volunteers and Flagweek cooks share this glyph family.
