# Who-Seals Question

## Name

Who-Seals Question

## Overview

The **Who-Seals Question** is a small mark like a closed wax ring with a raised curl—meaning *ask who seals before you praise the seal*.

## Meaning

Authority is a person and a vow, not a pretty blot. Softfruit and clerks use the curl to slow worship of blank centers.

## Usage

- Primer covers for Who Seals What Mystery.
- Study-path cards and Softfruit chapbook corners.
- Market satire of anonymous “official wax.”
- Paired with House-Seal Wax Ring as the ethics twin.

## Lore Connection

Who Seals What Mystery primer. Sealed Knowledge Ethics. Publish manners without opening every door.
