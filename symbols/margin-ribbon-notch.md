# Margin-Ribbon Notch

## Name

Margin-Ribbon Notch

## Overview

The **Margin-Ribbon Notch** is a small U-cut mark meaning *leave scrape-space*—ribbon, blank run, or pale edge still alive for corrections and no-ferry ink.

## Meaning

Pretty silence that ate its edge is noncompliant. The notch is honesty architecture: room for ask-again, scrape scars, and disclaimers.
