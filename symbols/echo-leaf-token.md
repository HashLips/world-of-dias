# Echo Leaf Token

## Name

Echo Leaf Token

## Overview

A leaf-shaped token—wood, cloth, or chalk outline—used to mark an **echo or visiting phenomenon** on teaching boards so students do not draw a new district around a remainder.

## Meaning

Remainder that visits. Leaves move; rings (places) stay.

## Usage

Placed on Nest Marks during Stack Teaching and Softfruit lessons; pinned to wrong maps in Averra exhibits; held up at gates when someone tries to destination an omen.

## Lore Connection

Bridges nesting manners and echo manners: scale honesty plus remainder honesty.

## Notes

Not a Resonant Artifact of power. A teaching leaf. If it starts “answering” too strongly, put it down and call a craftsperson—soft limits still apply.
