# Load-Noise Bar

## Name

Load-Noise Bar

## Overview

The **Load-Noise Bar** is a horizontal hatch meaning *treat as load weather first*—a filing caution that can be honest or cowardly depending on the hand.

## Meaning

Honest use: ordinary hum, not crisis. Cowardly use: living scrap, helpful glass, or unsigned care stamped into silence. Softfruit teaches both readings on the same card.

## Usage

- Clerk training sheets with “honest / cowardly” columns.
- Operator-adjacent scrap nook doors (honest sense).
- Red strike when bar was misused as a grave.
- Pairing with Ask-Again Slit when bar temptation appears.

## Lore Connection

Load-Noise Filing Manners culture made iconic. Unsigned scrap-care and nonactionable grave marks police the cowardly reading. Houses and Operators primer points here for institutional texture.

## Notes

Ambiguous on purpose—teach both uses. Not a free license to ignore wonder.
