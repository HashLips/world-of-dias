# Keep-Mark Bead

## Name

Keep-Mark Bead

## Overview

The **Keep-Mark Bead** is a small warm circle on a cord line—meaning *held for sharing this window*, not for surge sale.

## Meaning

The keep outranks coin for the marked meal. Snipping early is a manners crime. Cousin spirit to Wired Bloom tautening without orchard law attached.

## Usage
