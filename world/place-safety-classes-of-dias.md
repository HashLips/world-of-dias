# Place Safety Classes of Dias

## Overview

**Place Safety Classes of Dias** classifies representative places as safe, dangerous, sacred, restricted, or unexplored—using inhabitant speech and reasons, not a combat loot table.

## Core Premise

### Plain classes

| Class | Meaning (inhabitant-facing) | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Safe (crowded)** | Belonging and witnesses protect you if you keep manners | Veloria outer/second-ring markets; Softfruit Hall |
| **Safe (chosen calm)** | Quiet protects if you respect silence and Keepers | Wabet Softfall canopy edges; Open Hand Way day walks |
| **Dangerous** | Teeth, fog, yards, loneliness, or moral weather can harm | Outer Rim fog banks; exile roads after dark; Claimscar yard fights |
| **Sacred** | Approach with offering or leave alone | Quiet Well approaches; some Averra studio edges (invited); Good Trade still hours |
| **Restricted** | Clearance, Keep, Operator, or Freeband say-so required | Veloria core approach; Saltwhisper without assent; Operator scrap nooks |
| **Unexplored / unknown-to-mainland** | Stories thicker than maps | Averra Isle interior now; deep Fellgrove off-path; null-margin rumors (not a district) |

### Who says so

- **Nauw Concord / ring clerks** — clearance, Long Gate moral weather  
- **Keepers / Silence Law** — Lumira and hush roads  
- **Freebands / Exile Mutual Table** — yard claims, salvage shore manners  
- **Softfruit hosts** — table safety vs rumor  
- **Operators** — core glow is work, not tourism

## Global Lore

Settlements and Landmarks indexes carry local tone. Journey Routes use these classes as constraints. Beauty stays primary: danger is weather and manners, not a dungeon brand.

## Notes

Do not turn the atlas into a combat zone list. Sacred does not mean “loot shrine.” Restricted does not mean “solve Frequency Zero.”
