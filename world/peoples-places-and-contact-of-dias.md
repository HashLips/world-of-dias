# Peoples Places and Contact of Dias

## Overview

**Peoples Places and Contact of Dias** documents where major peoples predominantly live and how they meet—homelands, diasporas, and contact-zone manners—not only “also found in.”

## Core Premise

### Plain definitions

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Homeland density** | Where a kind is most ordinary in daily street life |
| **Diaspora** | Settled presence outside densest home without being “exotic visitors” |
| **Contact zone** | Place where meeting manners are taught (market, dock, silence road, yard) |
