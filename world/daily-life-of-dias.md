# Daily Life of Dias

## Overview

**Daily Life of Dias** is the loveable middle—mornings, errands, jokes, small fears—so readers want to stay another day before the plot arrives.

## Core Premise

Institutions and frequencies matter, but so do Lap-Cats, Softfruit ladles, rope before stone, and the itch of a ledger flea. Happiness infrastructure is real. Tiny unsettled details keep the pressure soft and present.
