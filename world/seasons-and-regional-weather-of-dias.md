# Seasons and Regional Weather of Dias

## Overview

**Seasons and Regional Weather of Dias** gives journey writers characteristic season feel, travel constraints, and named environmental phenomena per major F432 region—climate as lived weather, not a meteorology textbook.

## Core Premise

### Plain definition

**Season feel** in F432 is recognizable (wet, dry, harvest, forge-smoke, Softfall)—then each region adds signature anomalies. Other frequencies may wear climate as sensory metaphor; do not paste F380 mood-weather into Nauw orchard rain.

### Year bones (plain)

Inhabitants often speak in four work-seasons, not Earth month names:

| Work-season | Plain feel |
| --- | --- |
| **Wetwake** | Soft rains, swollen cords, Softfall roofs, salvage wet wind |
| **Brightpass** | Clear roads, Listening Heat peaks, mirror-lull days, Flagweek planning |
| **Fruitcarry** | Eastbound freight thickens; orchard humidity; ring-fair cousins; kiln rebuild smoke |
| **Hushlean** | Basin mist winters, quieter dunes, loft storm choir, Vent Haze risk inland |

Exact calendars vary by town; Softfruit and Freebands argue labels cheerfully.
