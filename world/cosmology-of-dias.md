# Cosmology of Dias

## Overview

**Cosmology of Dias** is the orientation frame for how reality is stacked, what counts as law, and what must stay unfinished. It does not replace [`Dias`](dias.md); it helps readers and authors hold the sky steady while the world grows.

## Core Premise

Dias is one cosmos with **layered resonance**. What people call magic is usually **frequency behavior**. What people call destiny is often **echo** meeting choice. What people call the end of meaning is argued under names like **Frequency Zero**—and not answered here.

### Stack (plain speech)

1. **Dias** — the whole world-container of known existence.
2. **Frequency realms** — vibrational layers with different expectations (F432 stability, F200 light, F500 null, and others).
3. **Regions and places** — lived geography inside a realm.
4. **Local phenomena** — weather, stillness, reveal windows, bleed nights, unfinished edges.

Movement between realms is not ordinary travel distance. It is a change in resonance—rare, disputed, and culturally heavy when it happens at all.

### Law / theory / taboo guess

| Kind | Meaning | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Law** | Treated as reliable world behavior across cultures that disagree about almost everything else | Everything resonates; everything leaves an echo. Frequency shapes what is possible. The Fracture separated one Prime Realm into frequencies. |
| **Theory** | Useful, argued, sometimes institutionalized—still revisable | Exact count of frequencies; best model of bleed; whether F500 “hunger” and Zero are related; how echo-reading should be governed. |
| **Taboo guess** | Spoken carefully, sealed, joked away, or prayed around | Forcing Frequency Zero into a tool; treating null as a workplace; claiming to know the Fracture’s culprit as settled fact. |

F432’s own discipline for incomplete cosmic claims lives in the **F432 partial knowledge protocol**: rumor may inform caution; it does not silently become law.

## Global Lore

### Soft limits (keeps wonder from becoming “anything”)

Resonance is vast, not omnipotent on demand. Working soft limits:

- **No casual omniscience** — echoes are partial; reading them is craft, risk, and often wrong.
- **No subway between frequencies** — cross-realm passage stays exceptional, omenized, or institutional-secret—not daily commute.
- **No local repeal of the echo principle** — a place that seems not to resonate is a mystery event, not a default setting you can switch on.
- **No easy rewrite of a realm’s character** — F432 does not become F200 by wishing; tools may lean, bleed, or fail, but realm identity holds.
- **No quick answer to Frequency Zero** — naming is allowed; solving is not ordinary plot currency.

Detail: [`rules/resonance-behavior-soft-limits.md`](../rules/resonance-behavior-soft-limits.md).

### Unsettled implications (planted, not resolved)

These pressures are real enough to feel in weather, rumor, and sealed talk. They are **not** yet a finished war chronicle:

1. **Bleed** — lumen and abyss (and other bands) sometimes leak into F432 as wrong-sky nights, instrument jokes, and cold that refuses forecast. Something in the stack is not perfectly sealed.
2. **Null curiosity** — people and factions are tempted to study absence as power. Even without a named empire of dark, the *initiative* exists: to listen where listening unmakes.
3. **Revision hunger** — unfinished-band logic (and its echoes in craft and rumor) suggests reality can be remade mid-line. Wonderful for makers; terrifying if something large prefers erase-and-redraw over living continuity.

Joy still outnumbers dread in lived F432. The unsettled layer is the knowledge that the music has a wrong string somewhere in the instrument.

### Open questions (saga fuel; do not close early)

- What caused the Fracture—and does “cause” even apply?
- How many frequencies exist beyond the indexed set?
- Is Frequency Zero silence, death, origin, hazard, or a bad name for several different nothings?
- Are bleed events accidents, attacks, weather, or memory?
- Can memory persist without living beings?
- What wants the world smaller, quieter, or unfinished against its will?
