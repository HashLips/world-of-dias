# Measurement and Publish Ethics of Dias

## Overview

**Measurement and Publish Ethics of Dias** indexes how F432 notices, scrapes, and prints without turning every graph into a ferry fantasy or a Forced-Final silence.

## Core Premise

Honest paper keeps margin. Helpful glass is not automatically truth. Indexes alone are not enough.
