# Landforms of F432

## Overview

**Landforms of F432** indexes concrete mountains, rivers, seas, isles, forests, deserts, and plains a journey can route through inside the three major regions—so “west / north / south” is never only a vibe.

## Core Premise

### Plain definition

A **landform** here is named physical geography feet can aim at (plain, sea, canopy, sand, mountain, shore, river body). Civic halls and gates are landmarks of manners; this sheet prioritizes terrain bones.

### Unlike each other (terrain only)

Nauw is basin–march–glasswater–rim sea. Wabet is canopy–meadow–sand–clean coast. Sorel is emberslope–salvage shore–vent–dust reach. Same band; three bodies.

### Journey sample through landforms

Wabet Softfall (Aelwyn Drip Rivers) → Eastbound Fruit Road → Brindle Cord Rivers → Glasswater Feed Channels → Basin mist → (south) Long Gate → Hearthvale Emberslope → Driftfall Salvage Shore.
