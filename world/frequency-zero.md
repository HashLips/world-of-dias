# Frequency Zero

## Overview

**Frequency Zero** is a named open mystery of Dias: the theoretical **absence of resonance**.

## Core Premise

If everything in Dias exists through resonance, Frequency Zero is the idea of a state—or place, or event, or unreachable designation—where resonance does not apply as known.

Its meaning is **not settled**. Different cultures and institutions may treat it as silence, death, creation, a condition before the known world, an unreachable frequency, or something outside the system entirely.

## Global Lore

No Present Age institution has publicly proven what Frequency Zero is. Scholarly talk often begins from negation: not F432 stability, not F200 luminosity, not F500 null hunger as commonly described—something else, or nothing at all.

Resonant Houses are rumored to file Zero-talk under hazard language when it appears in unfiltered echo testimony. Other traditions treat Zero as sacred refusal: a name you do not try to force into a tool.

### Contested readings (unresolved)

| Reading | Who leans this way | What they do with the name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Silence / sacred refusal | Some Open Hand and canopy stewards | Refuse to toolize Zero; speak it rarely |
| Death / erasure | Outer-rim fear talk; F500-adjacent rumor | Treat Zero as the end of echo itself |
| Creation / pre-world | Speculative scholastic circles | Argue Zero as state before frequency catalog |
| Unreachable designation | Index clerks and House filers | Keep a blank row; never assign field teams |
| Outside the system | Hazard channels; Black Hole imagery talk | File as nonactionable cosmic hazard |

None of these readings is confirmed. Contradiction is the point.

## Notes

**Do not resolve Frequency Zero** in ordinary saga chapters. It may be named, feared, prayed to, or argued over. Answers—if any—belong to long-horizon mystery, not quick revelation.
