# Knowledge of Dias

## Overview

**Knowledge of Dias** is how people learn—schools where they exist, apprenticeships where they matter more, hearsay that moves faster than stamps, and sealed study that can comfort or damn.

A young character should have a **believable learning path**.

## Core Premise

Learning is plural: Softfruit manners and Storm Choir check-ins teach as hard as House codes. Joy of learning (galleries, songs, craft mistakes that become style) keeps the world loveable. Dangerous knowledge (Null talk, Zero talk, perfect well maps) has customs of care.
