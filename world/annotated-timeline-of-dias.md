# Annotated Timeline of Dias

## Overview

**Annotated Timeline of Dias** is the Volume VI.10 spine: an annotated timeline a non-saga reader can follow—eras, foundational events, regional beats—without requiring Harmonic/Lumen/Calibration paths.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

Saga-optional. Soft chronology. Grades visible. Mysteries remain bounded.

## How to read this timeline

1. Start at Prime → Fracture → Present.  
2. Add regional beats only as needed for the chapter’s place.  
3. Mark disputed/myth/unknown when teaching children or clerks.  
4. Leave Zero and mystery cards open (Mystery Boundaries).  
5. Saga paths are optional overlays, not required keys.
