# Authorities and Factions of Dias

## Overview

**Authorities and Factions of Dias** is the Volume IV.6 definition sheet: governments, offices, habits, and limits—so power has desks and refusals, not only villain labels.

## Core Premise

### Plain definitions

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Authority** | Who can open/close a gate, stamp, assent, or kettle seat—and be blamed |
| **Office** | Named role with habits (High Steward, Free-Captain, Dune Keeper, Watch junior) |
| **Faction** | Group with purpose / method / public face / private fear / echo lean |
| **Soft power venue** | Gallery, ledger, song calendar, toll, Softfruit—bites without crowns |

### Rule

Every major settlement needs: **who decides**, **where they sit**, **what habit proves legitimacy**, and **what they cannot do** without becoming the hard edge.
