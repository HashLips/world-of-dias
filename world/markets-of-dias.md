# Markets of Dias

## Overview

**Markets of Dias** indexes how goods, gossip, and manners meet—fruit roads, Softfruit tables, pot-signal lanes, and pamphlet weather—without turning trade into Earth mall cosplay.

## Core Premise

A stall is culture. Price, keep-marks, and gossip pots are speech. Full entries below.
