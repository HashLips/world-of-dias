# Unfinished and Becoming of Dias

## Overview

**Unfinished and Becoming of Dias** indexes places and manners where Dias refuses Forced-Final completion—scaffold miles, holds, sky-whale shadows—without solving F960 into a subway of becoming.

## Core Premise

Becoming is weather and craft, not a defect. Clamps that call themselves mercy are danger tools.
