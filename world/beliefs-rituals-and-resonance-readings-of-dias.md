# Beliefs Rituals and Resonance Readings of Dias

## Overview

**Beliefs Rituals and Resonance Readings of Dias** is the Volume IV.8 definition sheet: major beliefs, enactable rituals, and cultural readings of resonance—plural, conflicting, without a church of Zero.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

Do not collapse Dias into one orthodoxy. Resonance is read as gift, warning, resource, illusion, sacred refusal, or market omen—often mixed in one person.

### Soft limit

Frequency Zero stays clue-and-pressure only. Sacred refusal of forced naming is humility; taboo used to silence witnesses is hard-edge abuse.
