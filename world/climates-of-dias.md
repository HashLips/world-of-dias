# Climates of Dias

## Overview

**Climates of Dias** names how places *feel in the air*—baseline weather, signature anomalies, and frequency climates that behave like mood or law. Beauty stays primary. Danger and unease arrive as weather you can film, not as a combat table.

## Core Premise

Inside **F432**, climate is mostly recognizable: rain, heat, mist, wind, salt fog—then a few patterns that remind you the stack is layered. Other frequencies often wear climate as **metaphor made sensory** (light fields, mood weather, ash quiet, anti-scenery). Cross-pressure appears as rare **Bleed-Sky Weather**.

### F432 baselines + signature anomalies

| Region | Baseline feel | Signature anomaly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nauw** | Temperate trade lands: soft rains, orchard humidity, Basin mist, Outer Rim salt air | **Brindle March stillness**; **Velorian Basin gilding hour**; Glasswater mirror-calm (see Mirror Lull lore) |
| **Wabet** | Clear, calm, wild air—forests damp, deserts bright, coasts clean | **Wabet Softfall**; **Lumira Listening Heat**; blue-litter hush in underwood |
| **Sorel** | Harsh, changeable frontier: dust, forge smoke, wet salvage wind | **Sorel Vent Haze**; rebuild-smoke seasons; Flagweek cloth-weather of crowds |

### Frequency climates (sensory, not full almanacs)

| Band | Climate-as-sense |
| --- | --- |
| **F200** | Warm gold stillness; sound soft or absent; “weather” is brightness grain |
| **F500** | Anti-scenery; cold without wind; forgetting as texture |
| **F120** | Air that feels already breathed; timing skew more than temperature |
| **F380** | Mood as weather—relief widens color; fear narrows space |
| **F610** | Brief clarity fronts, then opacity return like fog with homework |
| **F840** | Muted skies, trace heat, hardy air after harm |
| **F960** | Draft-weather; edges soft; copper/cobalt accents when form tries itself out |

## Global Lore

Climate should help a reader **want to stand in a place**. Even Vent Haze and Bleed-Sky exist so the world’s body can show strain—while Softfall and gilding hour keep Dias worth loving.

Season feel, work-season tables (Wetwake / Brightpass / Fruitcarry / Hushlean), and travel constraints: [`Seasons and Regional Weather of Dias`](seasons-and-regional-weather-of-dias.md). Band-as-weather cousin: Environments Are Frequency Weather. Do not paste F380 mood-weather into Nauw orchard rain.

## Notes

Enrich regions with climate pointers; do not replace local culture laws (Silence Law, etc.). Authoring: describe weather in scenes before inventing new anomalies.
