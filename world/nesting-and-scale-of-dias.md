# Nesting and Scale of Dias

## Overview

**Nesting and Scale of Dias** is the plain-speech primer for how big things contain smaller things without lying about frequency. Use it beside [`Structure of Dias`](structure-of-dias.md) (map-of-maps) and [`Cosmology of Dias`](cosmology-of-dias.md) (law vs mystery). The binding rule is [`Dias nesting hierarchy`](../rules/dias-nesting-hierarchy.md).

## Core Premise

Dias is one container. Inside it, **frequency realms** are layers of resonance. Inside a realm, **regions and places** are lived geography. Across places, **local phenomena** recur as patterns of happening. Confusing any two rungs is how wrong maps and ferry fantasies begin.

### Scale ladder (classroom)

| Rung | Question it answers | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dias** | What cosmos are we in? | Dias |
| **Frequency realm** | Which vibrational layer? | F432, F200, F500, F960, F120, F380, F610, F840 |
| **Major region** | Which broad land/sea body inside the realm? | Nauw, Wabet, Sorel; Returning Span; Bloomline Estuary |
| **Settlement / landmark / route** | Where can feet, carts, or boats go? | Veloria City, Quiet Well, Long Gate, Eastbound Fruit Road |
| **Local phenomenon** | What pattern of event can visit here? | Distant lumen-abyss bleed, Quartermark Drift, unfinished edge drift |

### Place-type words inhabitants already use

These match how the archive files places (not a second metaphysics):

- **realm** — frequency layer (parent: Dias)
- **region / territory** — broad geography inside a realm
- **city / town / district** — settlements and their parts
- **island / crossing / landmark** — named geography that is not always a government
- **building / shop / pub / gallery** — rooms and thresholds with civic or craft life

A **phenomenon** is never a place_type. It may be *hosted* by places.

### Worked nest (home band)

**Dias** → **F432 (frequency realm)** → **Nauw** → **Veloria City** → (districts, Softfruit corners, clerk desks) → local events such as **bleed-sky weather** or load-noise that never become a fifth continent.

### How to teach without spoiling mysteries

You may name Frequency Zero as an **open unknown**. You may not promote it to a mapped realm rung on this ladder. You may describe bleed as a local phenomenon. You may not sell tickets into F500 because the sky bruised.

## Global Lore

Street speech often jumps rungs for poetry (“the whole band laughed”). Ledgers must not. Stack Teaching Customs train the difference: Softfruit will correct with a ladle story; Houses will correct with a stamp; children learn with Nest Mark chalk games.

Wrong-map culture and Half-Made Atlas talk belong in map lore; this primer only insists that **scale honesty** is part of resonance manners.

## Notes

Do not invent parallel “nest path” files for every place. Nesting lives in each place or phenomenon entry: follow `parent_region` / `related`, and read the short nesting note in that entry’s body. This primer is the overview; the places themselves are the examples.
