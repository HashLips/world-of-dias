# Mysteries of Dias

## Overview

**Mysteries of Dias** is a menu of named open doors—fuel for imagination and later sagas. Curiosity has addresses; answers stay locked until deliberately opened.

## Core Premise

A **mystery** is a public open question the world itself keeps asking. A **secret** is something someone knows and hides—see [`Secrets of Dias`](secrets-of-dias.md). Mysteries may gain clues. Crown answers stay sealed.

### Handling rules

1. **Frequency Zero** is the crown mystery—name it; do not solve it in ordinary chapters.  
2. Active mysteries may collect clues, witness weather, and cultural habits.  
3. Do not collapse an active mystery into a single villain reveal without an intentional saga decision.  
4. Each card lists **what life it currently affects**—mystery is not only mood.
