# Known Unknowns of Dias

## Overview

**Known Unknowns of Dias** is the orientation list of major open questions the world itself keeps—especially Frequency Zero—so a book can name gaps without inventing answers.

## Core Premise

### Plain definitions

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Known unknown** | A question canon admits is open; clues may exist; closure is forbidden without deliberate plan |
| **Mystery** | Public open question the world keeps asking (Mysteries of Dias) |
| **Secret** | Something someone knows and hides (Secrets of Dias)—not the same as a mystery |
| **Crown mystery** | Frequency Zero—named freely; not solved in ordinary chapters |

### Major known unknowns (working list)

| Unknown | What is allowed | What is forbidden |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Frequency Zero** | Name, fear, refuse, file hazard, argue readings | Solve as tool, wipe, workplace, or settled taxonomy |
| **Fracture cause** | Myths and theories quarrel | Courtroom final culprit as Law |
| **Complete frequency catalog** | Add Observation; keep Theory-open | Claim final complete list |
| **Bleed source** | Weather manners, moths, shutter habits | Single neat villain without saga plan |
| **Horizon-Nulls / Distant Companies / Unraveled nature** | Rumor pressure; soft words | Promote to confirmed taxonomy casually |
| **Who seals what (ultimate authority)** | Ethics fights; House/Operator/Open Hand | One office owns all seals forever |
| **Whether any safe ferry exists** | Exceptional costly stories | Infrastructure / ticket product |
| **Prime Realm full geography** | Relics, aligned ruins, Bridge echoes | Complete Prime atlas |

### How to use this list

1. Pick a mystery for a chapter without needing Zero answered.
2. Add clues and pressure; do not close crown rows.
3. Soft Limits of Dias and Resonance Can and Cannot of Dias catch wishful closure.

## Global Lore

Mysteries of Dias is the menu of open doors. Frequency Zero is the crown card. Prime and Fracture Knowledge of Dias separates known/disputed/unknown for origin history.

## Notes

Clues and pressure only. Do not solve Frequency Zero.
