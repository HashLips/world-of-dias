# Secrets of Dias

## Overview

**Secrets of Dias** are things someone knows and is hiding. They differ from **mysteries** (public open questions). Secrets give the unsettled layer agents and motives—not only mood.

## Core Premise

Do not keep a single readable spoiler bible in canon. Secrets live distributed inside factions, places, and people. This hub names **types** and **where to look**. Author staging notes (saga-key vs texture) stay under `agent/` when needed.

### Mystery vs secret

| | Mystery | Secret |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who asks | The world / many voices | Someone who already has an answer (or thinks they do) |
| Public? | Named open door | Hidden on purpose |
| Example | What is Bleed’s source? | Which House clerk buried a street warning |
| Saga use | Curiosity menu | Motive, leverage, betrayal, kindness |

### Secret types

| Type | Feel | Typical holder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Personal** | Private debt, shame, soft promise | Clerk, sailor, exile, child |
| **Institutional** | Org policy the street may not hear | Houses, Watch, Compact, Keep |
| **Sealed research** | Filtered load / hazard science | Operators, House labs, Calibration |
| **Kinder lie** | Falsehood as care (wrong maps, soft words) | Dune Keep, parents, Mutual Table |
| **Weaponized silence** | Withholding to harm or recruit | Null Cells, bad crews, schism actors |
