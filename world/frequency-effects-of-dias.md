# Frequency Effects of Dias

## Overview

**Frequency Effects of Dias** is the orientation hub that defines how an established frequency changes five concrete domains: **matter**, **life**, **memory**, **perception**, and **environments**. It sits beside Frequency Difference Primer of Dias (how bands differ from each other) and answers: once you are under a given band’s rules, what actually changes.

## Core Premise

### Plain definition

A **frequency effect** is a predictable change in how something behaves because of which frequency layer it is in (or near). It is not decoration, mood-language, or metaphor. If the band changes, the same wood, body, recollection, sense, or place can behave differently in ways people can name, teach, and test.

### What this is not

- Not five separate cosmologies
- Not “vibes” with F-numbers
- Not a travel method between bands
- Not poetry that a book author must interpret into rules

Softfruit kitchen and gate examples come after the definition. Houses may stamp Theory on models; the five domains remain a teaching cut of Frequency as fundamental law meeting Everything resonates.

### The five domains (definitions)

| Domain | Definition (what changes) | Softfruit first tell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Matter** | How physical substance holds shape, heat, contrast, revision, or wear | Dough and table wood keep yesterday’s pace; harbor stone keeps a job |
| **Life** | How living bodies and beings take on strain, timing, contagion, or recovery pace | Visitors tire under coherence pressure; crowds share hearttide load |
| **Memory** | How recollection and world-remainder stick, rhyme, thin, window, or revise | Near-return misfiles “I was here”; null thins continuity |
| **Perception** | What senses can rest on, miss, glimpse briefly, or must stop attending | Anti-scenery removes landmarks; reveal windows punish stare |
| **Environments** | How place behaves as band weather (pressure, tint, corridor, rhyme, sketchline) | Alignment pressure; ashwarm corridors; civic mixed climate |

### Cross-band teaching order

1. Start in **F432**—mixed civic matter, ordinary load, usable maps.
2. Contrast **F200** (coherence / held radiance) and **F500** (negation / continuity risk).
3. Add **F960** (revisable form) and **F380** (mood-as-climate).
4. Finish quieter ledgers: **F120** (rhyme), **F610** (interval truth), **F840** (recovery continuity).
5. Always: understanding a band is not a ferry ticket; Frequency Zero stays open.

### What this hub refuses

- Collapsing all five domains into “magic vibes.”
- Selling one domain as a travel method between realms.
- Claiming complete catalogs of every effect in every band.
- Solving Frequency Zero by calling it “the band with no effects.”

## Global Lore

Domain primers carry depth. Band rules (coherence gate, near-return drift, emotional climate coupling, negation proximity, reveal window, recovery continuity, iterative manifestation) are the hard edges. Phenomena, flora, and craft customs supply lived examples. Five-Domain Effect Teaching and the Five-Domain Effect Chapbook move the hub into markets and junior rooms.

Claim grade for the hub’s spine: **law** that frequencies influence these domains; **observation** for particular tells; **theory** for incomplete cross-band models; **taboo** for ferry fantasies built from effect talk.
