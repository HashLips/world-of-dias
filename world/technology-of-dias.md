# Technology of Dias

## Overview

**Technology of Dias** is echo-aware craft—not generic sci-fi, not medieval cosplay. Tools answer frequency law: they remember, filter, seal, lift, rebuild, or listen. Wonder lives in ordinary devices; dread lives in things that listen too well.

## Core Premise

Tech should answer **how do they live?** without breaking resonance rules. Every major class has entries below—indexes alone are not enough.
