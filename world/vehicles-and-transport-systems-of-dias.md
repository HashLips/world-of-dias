# Vehicles and Transport Systems of Dias

## Overview

**Vehicles and Transport Systems of Dias** is the Volume V.5 sheet: how vehicles and transport systems operate—with constraints. Local ferry ≠ band ferry.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

Document **mode**, **how it runs**, and **what stops it**. No inter-band subway. Journey manners live in Transport of Dias; this sheet is the vehicle/system roster.

---

## Modes (operation + limits)

| Mode | How it operates | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |

| **Cart / crawler** | Fruit-road wagons; Brindle crawlers | Toll; bruise timing; Still Mile hold |

## Adventurer pack (travel choose)

To leave Veloria believably:

1. Pick **mode** (foot + cart common; ferry if isle/rim).  
2. Budget **VEL + local tender** (toll, ladle, wire).  
3. Learn **one manners rule** of the road weather (fruit rank, hush, First Line, kettle).  
4. Do **not** buy a band ferry.
