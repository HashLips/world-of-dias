# Confirmed and Rumor Life of Dias

## Overview

**Confirmed and Rumor Life of Dias** teaches readers to tell field-guide fact from campfire creature—using archive `status` and Life Kinds honesty without erasing useful folklore.

## Core Premise

### Plain grades

| Grade | Meaning | How to write |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Confirmed (canonical)** | Multiple honest witnesses / lived work | Field-guide look/where/behavior; `status: canonical` |
| **Rumor** | Strong culture, thin proof | Trail ethics real; taxonomy thin; `status: rumor` |
| **Legendary** | Story denser than zoology | Keep as myth pressure; do not fake measurements |
| **Unknown / ambiguous** | Presence or reports without kind | Sky Drifters; refuse forced species |
| **Existential partial** | Soft-limit classes (Horizon-Nulls, Unraveled…) | Do not complete into ordinary wildlife catalogs |

### Worked examples

| Being | Grade | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ringroad Dogs | Confirmed | Collar work every ring night |
| Canopy Soft-Bears | Confirmed wonder | Trail etiquette + repeated canopy witness |
| Redmile Striders | Confirmed hazard | Bells and lonely-road custom |
| Forest Listeners | Rumor | Ethics bind; prints disagree |
| Sketchshade Cats | Rumor | Gallery debate unfinished |
| Sky Drifters | Unknown / ambiguous | Always above; never landed |
| Glasswater Sky-Turtles | Rumor | Strong talk; thin proof |
| Long-Gate Beast | Rumor / legend braid | Mystery hub; not a kennel species |
| Horizon-Nulls | Existential partial | Not an F432 park animal |

### Softfruit practice

Children may love rumor cards. Adults must not sell rumor as confirmed meat or confirmed ferry. Legendary beings stay useful without fake zoology.
