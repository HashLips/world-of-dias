# Belief of Dias

## Overview

**Belief of Dias** gathers meaning systems—plural, conflicting, beautiful, sometimes wrong. Belief can comfort and frighten in the same town.

## Core Premise

Echoes may be gift, warning, resource, or illusion depending on who speaks. Local sacred practices (ribbons, silence, fruit thanks, hearth continuity) matter more than a single church. Myth cycles encode Fracture, whales, gates, and ash warmth without confirming cosmology as lab fact. Atheism-of-echoes and mysticism-of-markets coexist. Unsettled faiths (null curiosity, unfinished prophecy, light-purity extremes) are present, not dominant.
