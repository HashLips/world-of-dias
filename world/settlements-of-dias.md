# Settlements of Dias

## Overview

**Settlements of Dias** tiers lived places—jobs, smells, jokes, and uneven safety—so a reader can spend a week in Veloria or a hard week in Sorel in their head.

Regions remain the file of record. This page is the **settlement card** index.

## Tier key

| Tier | Meaning | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Capital / hub** | Dense civic life, layered tech, trade magnet | Veloria City |
| **Port / cove** | Water arrival, nets, ferries, gossip speed | Hollowmere, Rim-Rest, Hallow Bay |
| **Frontier yard** | Claims, salvage, provisional law | Claimscar Yard; Driftfall as territory |
| **Sanctuary** | Few beds, many rules, rest-as-discipline | Isle of Calareth (+ Hallow Bay) |
| **Rebuild town** | Warm craft, second chances | Secondfire |
| **Waystation / strip** | Passing trade, allegiances shift | Flagweek Strip, Westfold Toll |

## Happiness infrastructure (micro-places)

Baths, shared tables, music corners, and guest halls keep Dias loveable between pressures:

- [Ringwash Baths](../regions/ringwash-baths.md) — Veloria steam and gossip  
- [Softfruit Table Hall](../regions/softfruit-table-hall.md) — Shared Table made architectural  
- [Bay-Ribbon Guest Hall](../regions/bay-ribbon-guest-hall.md) — Calareth’s few beds  
- [Claimscar Cookfire Circle](../regions/claimscar-cookfire-circle.md) — frontier shared kettle  
- [Hollowmere Net-Loft Stage](../regions/hollowmere-net-loft-stage.md) — music and check-in call  

Also existing: Pot Signal Lane, Calibration Row, Flagweek Strip, Green Garden Border Lanes.
