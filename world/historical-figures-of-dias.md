# Historical Figures of Dias

## Overview

**Historical Figures of Dias** are names places still argue about—remembered through institutions, songs, and scars. Distinct from living saga casts (Calen, Frederick, Coris, Hanna Kiln, etc.).

**VI.5 verified vs attributed sheet:** [Historical Legacies of Dias](historical-legacies-of-dias.md).

## Archetype key

| Archetype | Role in memory |
| --- | --- |
| **Builder** | Made a lasting civic form |
| **Breaker** | Unmade a rule or opened a dangerous path |
| **Bridge** | Connected regions, kinds, or ages |
| **Warning** | Left a scar that teaches caution |
| **Joy** | Founded glad practice |
| **Unsettled** | Sealed / null-adjacent / unfinished legacy |
