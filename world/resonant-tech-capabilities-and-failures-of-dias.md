# Resonant Tech Capabilities and Failures of Dias

## Overview

**Resonant Tech Capabilities and Failures of Dias** is the Volume V.3 sheet: what resonant devices can do, cannot do, and how they break or mislead—with social consequences, not only sparks.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

Failure modes and social consequences matter as much as powers. Soft limits apply: no wish engines; no band ferry products; Zero stays unsolved.

Doctrine hub: [Resonance Can and Cannot of Dias](resonance-can-and-cannot-of-dias.md).
