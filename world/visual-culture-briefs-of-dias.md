# Visual Culture Briefs of Dias

## Overview

**Visual Culture Briefs of Dias** is the Volume IV.10 definition sheet: recognizable peoples, clothing, cultural symbols, architecture, and relationship diagrams—enough for illustrators to brief without guessing.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

An illustration brief answers: **who** (body tell), **what they wear** (region + labor), **where they stand** (architecture dialect), **what mark is visible**, and **who stands near whom** (relationship weather)—without Earth costume shortcuts.
