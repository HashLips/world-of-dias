# The Vitrin Saga

## Name

The Vitrin Saga

## Overview

The Vitrin Saga is a journey of a known pilot and a successful Veloria distributor who move a secret fruit off a lent field in Wabet, let a bar invent the rest, and learn that a show is not a cure. The fruit looks like glass. The man who eats it does not come back into sight on a schedule.

It sits beside the Harmonic, Lumen, Calibration, and Farstock sagas. It does not replace them and does not finish their open questions.

## Core Premise

**What you hide in a hull still has to meet a room.**

The road keeps hauling ordinary fruit by wagon, pony, and ox. The plane is a hire, not a new spine of the country. When the hidden breed is eaten for applause, the applause is the cheap part. The costly part is a body that will not finish returning, and a farmer who was meant to vanish and does not.

## Global Lore

Frequencies in this saga stay where F432 already keeps them: gossip, toll chalk, and sky. Nothing here is a ferry between bands. Sky Drifters remain high and unlanded; they are not craft. The slow train in the third-ring spur is a real train with soot and a depot. It is not the joke rail that sells the platform you already left.

The breed is **Vitrin**. It was grown in Wabet by mixing certain fruits. Which fruits, in what order, and why the unseen sticks to one eater and only feathers another, are not settled by the auction or the stew.
