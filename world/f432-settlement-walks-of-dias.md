# F432 Settlement Walks of Dias

## Overview

**F432 Settlement Walks of Dias** collects walkable mental maps for major named settlements beyond Veloria’s rings—so “capital” is not the only place a reader can pace in their head.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

A settlement is walkable when a reader can name **approach**, **three or more corners**, **who keeps manners**, and **one pressure / one joy** without inventing a new map.

### Index

| Settlement | Region seat | Walk sketch / file of record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Veloria City | Nauw / Basin | Veloria Walkable Atlas; Veloria Ring Walk Sketch |
| Hollowmere | Outer Rim Seas | Hollowmere Cove Walk Sketch; Hollowmere entry |
| Tubehold | Glasswater Fields | Tubehold Dam Walk Sketch; Tubehold entry |
| Hearthvale (territory approach) | Sorel west | Hearthvale entry walk list |
| Secondfire | Hearthvale | Secondfire Kiln Walk Sketch; Secondfire entry |
| Claimscar Yard | Driftfall | Claimscar Yard entry (yard paths; social safety) |

### Unlike Veloria

These towns are **not** four-ring circles. Cove tiers, dam streets, kiln courtyards, and salvage yards use different bones. Do not paste ring clearance onto loft check-ins or Compact bowls.

## Global Lore

Settlement-level atlas work for Volume II.3 / II.10. Place webs and safety classes still apply.

## Notes

Add a walk sketch when a saga chapter needs feet-on-ground; do not invent subway geometry.
