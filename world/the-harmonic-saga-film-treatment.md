# The Harmonic Saga — Film Treatment

## Purpose

This document adapts **Movement One** of the Harmonic Saga (saga chapters 1–7) into **one feature film** (~110–120 minutes). It is a **production and pitch guide**, not replacement canon. Full prose lives in [`stories/`](../stories/). Deeper lore lives in the repository. The film **closes emotionally** and **opens cosmically**.

**Saga source:** chapters 1–7 only. Movements Two and Three remain sequel / series / repo exploration material.

---

## Working Title

**The Echo at the Gate**  
*(alternates: The Harmonic Threshold, Still Listening)*

---

## Logline

A fair-minded outsider arrives in the layered city of Veloria and discovers they can **hear the world's buried memories**—and when a Prime Bridge echo threatens to become institutional secret, they must choose whether to let power classify the truth as noise, or share it with the people who keep the city alive.

## Three-Act Structure

### Act I — Arrival and Awakening (~30 min)

**Sources:** [`the-echo-at-the-gate.md`](../stories/the-echo-at-the-gate.md), basin beats from [`sun-sweet-and-sky.md`](../stories/sun-sweet-and-sky.md)

Calen walks from Brindle March into Veloria during **echo-rise season**. DEB Gate two-finger touch → first Bridge echo. Ring-by-ring wonder (medieval outer → modern → drone third). Sky Drifters overhead. Tea seller: *"It's one world. That is not the same thing."* Dawn harmonic thrum. Tamsin Vor invites Calen to stay in the basin.

**Act break:** Calen writes *The gate remembers a bridge* — chooses to stay.

### Act II — Load, Change, Light (~45 min)

**Sources:** [`the-load-the-core-carries.md`](../stories/the-load-the-core-carries.md), [`the-open-hand-and-the-rose.md`](../stories/the-open-hand-and-the-rose.md), [`sun-sweet-and-sky.md`](../stories/sun-sweet-and-sky.md), [`luminance-line.md`](../stories/luminance-line.md)

Act II is **tightened** around one interior turn: Calen must be **visibly changed by Wabet** before the F200 midpoint. Wabet is not tourism—it is **recovery that rewires how Calen uses the gift**.

**II-A — Core (~18 min):** Clearance summons. Escort inward. Valorian Operators at work. N-band classification — echoes filed as noise to protect load. Calen leaves **overloaded**: angry, grateful, still compulsively listening—touching every rail, notebook out, trying to **file** fairness the way Brindle taught.

**II-B — Wabet: the change (~8–10 min):** Calen and Tamsin ride out on a fruit cart—not sightseeing, **because Calen's ears will not stop humming until the land changes its tone**. One keeper scene with **Elyra Mossen** (tea and terms, not a tour). One echo beat: the irregular apple from [`Two Ways`](../artworks/two-ways.md)—**contentment**, not bridge, not core. Calen realizes the gift does not always demand action. Moonlit rose rim ([`A Rose for You`](../artworks/a-rose-for-you.md)): Calen **does not touch stone**; hears only **now**. Elyra's line: *Carry it like a cup—level, not full.*

**On-screen proof Calen is changed:** on the return road, Calen closes the notebook without writing; lets a market argument pass un-read; Tamsin notices and says something like *"You came back quieter. Good."* The audience must feel the shift—not be told in voiceover.

**II-C — Basin, with a changed Calen (~12 min):** Sun-sweet and romance **after** Wabet, not before—love lands differently when Calen is not performing usefulness. Harold friendship. Moon-market beat. Sky Drifter dip. Optional ball game **cut first** if runtime bites; do **not** cut Wabet's change beat.

**II-D — Midpoint (~7 min):** F200 **lumen bleed** — gold rectangle in sky. [`Diva 1`](../artworks/diva-1.md) glimpse. Third echo register. A **changed** Calen looks up without needing to capture—then chooses to record because it matters, not because compulsion demands.

**Act break:** Dias is wider than one frequency—and Calen is **present** enough to mean it.

### Act III — Maps, Bridge, Choice (~32 min)

**Sources:** [`the-atlas-that-rewrites.md`](../stories/the-atlas-that-rewrites.md) (compressed), [`what-the-bridge-remembers.md`](../stories/what-the-bridge-remembers.md)

**III-A — Atlas hook (~5 min):** Half-Made Atlas margin line moves toward core — charcoal copy, no Driftfall expedition required on screen. Raises stakes: something is **drawing inward**.

**III-B — Second echo:** Thrum skips. Second ring stone arches. B-band event. Houses call lockdown; Calen runs **outward** to basin tea points — inverted from core summons in Act II.

**III-C — Climax:** Calen facilitates shared witness — two fingers, breathe, *tell me what your body says*. Not everyone hears Bridge; all are heard. Operators confirm load holding. Markets do not freeze.

**III-D — Resolution:** Vailor distant acknowledgment — order holds, panic optional. Calen and Tamsin on workshop roof. Love affirmed quietly. Notebook line: *Same world—not the same place.*

**Tag / sequel tease (~2 min):** Half-Made Atlas adds a line off-screen; Sky Drifters hold; [`F500 Map`](../artworks/f500-map.md) or [`World of Dias Infographic`](../artworks/world-of-dias-info-graphic.md) card — **one world, many frequencies**. No Fracture answer.

### Wabet — director notes (not tourism)

| Before Wabet | After Wabet |
| --- | --- |
| Compulsive echo-touch; notebook always open | Chooses when **not** to read |
| Fairness = capture and prove | Fairness = presence and restraint |
| Overloaded from core N-band | Body quiet; thrum absent in teeth |
| Performs usefulness for Tamsin / basin | Available for love without extracting |

**Required shots:** Elyra tea (one line of terms); apple choice; rose field without touch; return road notebook closed. **Avoid:** orchard tour, keeper exposition dump, travel montage without Calen POV change.
