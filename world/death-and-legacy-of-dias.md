# Death and Legacy of Dias

## Overview

**Death and Legacy of Dias** is how endings are held—grief, inheritance, and echo after bodies—so loss deepens love of life rather than emptying it.

## Core Premise

Bodies end differently by shore, kiln, orchard, and dune. Echoes of the dead can comfort or horrify. Ledgers, ribbons, hearths, and unfinished works carry names forward. Some endings refuse ordinary rites: Unraveled talk, missing time, revision wounds. F840 recovery ethics teach continuity without spectacle.
