# Veloria Walkable Atlas of Dias

## Overview

**Veloria Walkable Atlas of Dias** turns Veloria City into a journey a reader can walk in their head: approaches, rings, famous corners, and surroundings—not a single skyline sentence.

## Core Premise

### Plain layout

Veloria is a **four-ring circle** around a **power core**, set in the **Velorian Basin** (north-west Nauw).

| Zone | What you meet | Famous holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approach | Basin roads, DEB Gate manners | DEB Gate of Veloria |
| Outer ring | Craft-era stone, markets, guild feel | Outer markets, shade cloth |
| Second ring | Ordinary modern living, less tech-saturated | Housing rows, quieter streets |
| Third ring | Resonant tools, pot-signal, operator craft | Pot Signal Lane, tea houses, Softfruit corners |
| Core approach | Opacity, elbow cloth, Operator work | Core Approach Overlook |
| Above | Sky Drifters (never landing) | Sky watch at dusk |

### A sample walkable day

1. Enter by DEB Gate / basin road  
2. Outer market morning  
3. Baths or tea by midday (Ringwash / Third-Ring Tea House)  
4. Softfruit Table Hall or Softfruit Corner by the Row  
5. Calibration Row / pot-signal gossip  
6. Core Approach Overlook from a safe distance (no subway into glow ownership)  
7. Dusk Sky Drifter watch  

### Surroundings

- **Velorian Basin** — caravan and river corridors around the capital  
- Fruit and march traffic toward Brindle / Glasswater  
- Ring-fair weeks thicken joy and pickpocket pressure  

## Global Lore

Rings of Veloria Primer is the glance sheet. Veloria Ring Walk Sketch is the settlement-level plate. Region file of record: Veloria City.

Other major settlements in scope (not ring geometry): see **F432 Settlement Walks of Dias**—Hollowmere cove tiers, Tubehold dam streets, Hearthvale / Secondfire kiln approaches, Claimscar yard paths.
