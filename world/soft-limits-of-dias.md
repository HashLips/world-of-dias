# Soft Limits of Dias

## Overview

**Soft Limits of Dias** is the orientation sheet for what frequency craft and culture refuse to casually do—so wonder stays sharp and Frequency Zero stays a crown mystery.

## Core Premise

Soft limits are manners with teeth. They are not solved cosmology. Indexes alone are not enough; the table points into living rules, myths, and symbols.

## How to use this sheet

- Saga writers: cite these limits; do not invent subway exceptions without a deliberate path.
- Enrichment batches: new tech and travel must fail mythically inside these bounds.
- Readers: wanting a door is allowed; selling a door is comedy or danger.

## Notes

Companion to Stage Readiness. Does not replace cosmology; does not answer Zero.
