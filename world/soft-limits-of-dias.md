# Soft Limits of Dias

## Overview

**Soft Limits of Dias** is the orientation sheet for what frequency craft and culture refuse to casually do—so wonder stays sharp and Frequency Zero stays a crown mystery.

## Core Premise

### Plain definition

A **soft limit** is a working expectation strong enough that stories and institutions rely on it, soft enough that rare exceptions stay mysteries—not routine tools. Soft limits are manners with teeth. They are not solved cosmology.

### How soft limits relate to can/cannot

- Soft limits name what is **refused** as ordinary practice.
- Resonance Can and Cannot of Dias sorts a proposed feat into **succeed / fail / forbidden**.
- Resonance feats have costs explains the bill even when a feat works.
- Resonance does not grant wishes blocks desire-as-physics.

Indexes alone are not enough; the table points into living rules, myths, and symbols.

## How to use this sheet

- Saga writers: cite these limits; do not invent subway exceptions without a deliberate path.
- Enrichment batches: new tech and travel must fail mythically inside these bounds.
- Readers: wanting a door is allowed; selling a door is comedy or danger.
