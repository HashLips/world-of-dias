# Architecture of Dias

## Overview

**Architecture of Dias** is the index for how buildings speak place. Full descriptive dialects live as culture entries—this page points and compares.

A silhouette should tell you where you are before anyone says a place-name.

## Core Premise

Form follows **frequency habit** and **local law**. Indexes are not enough: each major dialect has its own entry with materials, silhouettes, and sacred/domestic/defensive practice.
