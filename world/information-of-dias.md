# Information of Dias

## Overview

**Information of Dias** is how news, lies, songs, and seals move. Information itself can be a plot engine—speed versus truth, care versus censorship.

## Core Premise

Channels already live in roads, tolls, ledgers, watch slates, tide-song, gallery rumor, and instrument harmonics. This hub names them as a system and adds ethics + a few communication artifacts.
