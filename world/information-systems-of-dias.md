# Information Systems of Dias

## Overview

**Information Systems of Dias** is the Volume V.6 sheet: how information is recorded, delivered, preserved, and communicated—echo-aware, not Earth internet.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

Messages move by **paper, slate, song, pot, runner, reed, and seal**. Wishful and forged sheets still move carts. Reliability is a habit, not a protocol stack.

## Communicate (plot logistics)

To stage a message plot:

1. Choose **genre** (toll truth vs handbill wish).  
2. Choose **channel** (runner, reed, pot, loft).  
3. Name **who can read** (clerk, Keep, loft, Softfruit).  
4. Name **failure** (partial, forge, thin-room if taboo).  

Reliable vs unreliable: [Reliable and Unreliable Records](../cultures/reliable-and-unreliable-records.md). Hub cousin: [Information of Dias](information-of-dias.md).
