# Wabet and Reciprocity of Dias

## Overview

**Wabet and Reciprocity of Dias** orients orchard and fruit-road stewardship—restoration before pick, Wired Blooms, Blue Litter bans—without retelling Lumen saga orchard chapters.

## Core Premise

Access waits on mend. Understanding is not a ticket; neither is a bottled hazard.
