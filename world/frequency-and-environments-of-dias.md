# Frequency and Environments of Dias

## Overview

**Frequency and Environments of Dias** defines how place behaves when frequency layer—not only local season—is the climate engine.

## Core Premise

### Plain definition

**Frequency environment** means: how a place presses, colors, opens, reseals, rhymes, recovers, or refuses landmarks because of its band. It is more than rainfall. Ordinary F432 seasons (Bloomline Climate Ways of Dias and kin) still matter; this primer covers band weather on top of or instead of that ordinary calendar.

### What this is not

- Not “every odd sky is another country”
- Not proof that a visiting bleed event is a permanent district (Local phenomenon scope rule)
- Not a ferry schedule disguised as climate talk

Bleed-Sky Weather can visit F432 without turning a street into F500. Band environment is the layer’s default habit; event weather is a guest.

### Event weather vs band weather

Bleed-Sky Weather and Distant lumen-abyss bleed can visit F432 without turning a street into F500. Local phenomenon scope rule keeps that honesty. Band environment is the layer’s default habit; event weather is a guest with manners or without.

## Global Lore

Environments are frequency weather is the rule spine. Phenomena named above are exemplars. Frequency Difference Primer of Dias supplies unlike-tables; this primer supplies place-feel depth.
