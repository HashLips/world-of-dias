# Frequency and Matter of Dias

## Overview

**Frequency and Matter of Dias** defines how physical substance behaves under different established frequency bands.

## Core Premise

### Plain definition

**Band matter** means: wood, stone, cloth, ash, radiance-sheets, draft-timber, and similar substance follow the habits of the frequency layer they are in. Hold, glow, thin, revise, rhyme, tint, window, or recover are band behaviors—not tourist wishes.

### What this is not

- Not “dead stuff plus paint”
- Not a universal material that works the same in every band
- Not ferry lumber (“build a plank and walk to F200”)

A maker who treats F960 draft-timber like Nauw bridge oak fails craft. A traveler who paints a landmark on F500 contrast-hungry ash finds the mark will not sit.

### Band matter habits (working expectations)

| Band | Matter habit | Craft consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F432 | Soil, timber, stone, harbor weight—stable enough to love | Stamps, roads, kitchens; echoes in tools and tables |
| F200 | Held radiance; coherence-as-substance | Ordinary dirt metaphors fail; alignment before load |
| F500 | Contrast-starved / identity-thinning substance | Landmarks refuse rest; approach costs continuity |
| F960 | Revisable form; outline before volume | Persistence measured by repeated holds |
| F120 | Wear and thresholds that rhyme with prior wear | Familiar boards mislead if timing offsets |
| F380 | Substance tinted by communal affect | Cloth and water take mood color slowly |
| F610 | Grain and seams that appear in windows | Phase stamps before “I saw the structure” |
| F840 | Ashwarm workable rests; continuity of use | Shelter and kettle before monument |

### Softfruit kitchen proof

Kneaded Dough Memory is the home-band classroom: yesterday’s pace returns through flour. Softfruit then points outward—same law, stranger matter—without packing boots for another realm.

## Global Lore

Matter answers its band is the rule spine. Phenomena named above are lived exemplars, not tourist samples. Resonant Artifacts sit on this primer: tools that keep band-shaped remainder must be filed with manners.

## Notes

Does not invent ferry materials. Does not claim every mineral in every band is cataloged.
