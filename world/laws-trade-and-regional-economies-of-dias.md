# Laws Trade and Regional Economies of Dias

## Overview

**Laws Trade and Regional Economies of Dias** is the Volume IV.7 definition sheet: concrete regional difference in law, trade, and political economy—tolls, currencies, banned goods, obligations—so two regions can be contrasted with examples.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

“They trade differently” is not enough. Name **what clears**, **what is banned**, **what obligation buys belonging**, and **who enforces**.

### Shared layer

**VEL Marks** clear most supervised markets and tolls across Nauw, Wabet, and Sorel ([VEL Mark Trade Standard](../cultures/vel-mark-trade-standard.md)). Local tenders braid beside them ([F432 Plural Tender Economies](../cultures/f432-plural-tender-economies.md)).
