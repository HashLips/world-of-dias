# Mystery Boundaries of Dias

## Overview

**Mystery Boundaries of Dias** is the Volume VI.9 sheet: known edges of each major unresolved mystery without revealing solutions. Public lore gets clues; no spoiler bible dump. Frequency Zero stays open.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

A mystery card answers: **what we may say**, **what life it touches**, **what is locked**. Secrets (held knowledge) are not mysteries—see Secrets of Dias.

Menu: [Mysteries of Dias](mysteries-of-dias.md). Orientation: [Known Unknowns of Dias](known-unknowns-of-dias.md).
