# Prime and Fracture Knowledge of Dias

## Overview

**Prime and Fracture Knowledge of Dias** separates what is treated as known, disputed, and unknown about the Prime Realm, the Prime Age, and the Fracture—so a reader (and a book) cannot invent false closure.

## Core Premise

### Plain definitions

| Name | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Prime Realm** | The single unified reality before frequencies separated |
| **Prime Age** | The time when the Prime Realm was whole (modern label for a poorly recorded era) |
| **The Fracture** | The ancient event in which the Prime Realm separated into frequency layers |
| **Present Age** | Time after the Fracture; most lived saga events sit here |

### Known (law / historical fact grade)

| Claim | Grade | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reality now exists as multiple frequency layers | Law / established | Frequency as fundamental law |
| The Fracture separated prior unity into frequencies | Law-grade historical fact | Separation happened; see The Fracture |
| No living being has continuous personal memory of the Prime Realm | Observation / consensus | Reconstructed knowledge only |
| Prime Relics and aligned ruins are attributed to pre-Fracture continuity | Observation | Attribution disputed in detail |
| The Prime Bridge persists as partial echoes across bands | Observation | Famous remnant; not a ferry schedule |

### Disputed (theory / myth grade)

| Claim | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fracture cause: natural limit | Myth / theory | The Fracture Was Natural |
| Fracture cause: engineered | Myth / theory | The Fracture Was Engineered |
| Fracture cause: betrayal / war / resonance quarrel | Myth / theory | Competing myth cycle entries |
| Exact count and map of all frequencies | Theory-open | Catalog incomplete |
| Full description of Prime daily life | Unknown / reconstructed guess | Do not invent a complete Prime atlas |

### Unknown (do not close)

| Unknown | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Exact cause of the Fracture | Leave open; myths may quarrel |
| Complete geography of the Prime Realm | No full atlas |
| Whether Frequency Zero equals pre-Prime, post-null, or outside-system | Crown mystery—do not solve |
| Full list of every post-Fracture frequency | Open discovery |

## Global Lore

Read The Prime Realm, The Prime Age, and The Fracture for entry depth. Known Eras and Markers orients soft chronology. Claim Grade Discipline keeps Theory from pretending to be Law.

## Notes

Enrich evidence and remembered accounts; do not fabricate a complete Prime atlas or a courtroom culprit for the Fracture.
