# Languages of Dias

## Overview

**Languages of Dias** gathers texture for dialogue—not a full conlang. The goal is a paragraph that sounds local: Nauw market, Wabet hush, Rim loft, Sorel kettle, House channel, or careful abyss talk.

## Core Premise

People share enough common F432 speech to trade and argue. What changes is **how they name, honor, insult, sing, stamp, and refuse to speak**. Race-speech (Choirglass tone-light, Palewind mote-pulse, Axiomorph facet-turns) sits beside regional habits without replacing them.

No constructed grammar until cultures are stable. Prefer sticky phrases, naming rules, and taboo/soft pairs.

## Sample local paragraphs (exit test)

**Nauw market:** “Don’t banana-boast at Softfruit if your crate’s still green—pay the ladle, share the rank, and keep your ring-mouth shut until the clerk stamps fair.”

**Wabet trail:** A guide answers a shout with three soft steps and a fruit left on the ledge. That *is* the sentence: go quieter, or turn back.

**Hollowmere loft:** “First Line’s up—answer or be named empty. Storm Choir doesn’t care how pretty your excuses sing.”

**Claimscar kettle:** “Stew’s for wire or news. Paint’s for claims. Concord boots stay by the tarp edge if you want a second bowl.”

**Bleed-Sky tavern:** “Null pressure once. Argue once. Third time you’re walking—room’s already thinner.”

## Notes

When a phrase becomes sticky in stories, promote it into the matching culture entry—don’t leave it only in dialogue drafts.
