# Tolls and Spans of Dias

## Overview

**Tolls and Spans of Dias** indexes choke-point paper and lane discipline—toll books, talk tokens, witness chips, returning-span chalk—without making tolls into band doors.

## Core Premise

A toll is speech with a price. Equal numbers can still tip unfair—see fairness cards.
