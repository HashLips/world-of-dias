# Peoples of Dias

## Overview

**Peoples of Dias** clarifies who counts as a neighbor—not a bestiary of people. Creature dread lives in [`Creatures of Dias`](creatures-of-dias.md). This page is for citizens, travelers, and kinds you can share a table with.

## Core Premise

Personhood in Dias is **practiced**, not granted by silhouette. A market stall, a silence law, a song-crew compact, or a swarm ledger can all prove someone is *someone*.

### Personhood spectrum (working map)

| Band | Who | Civic feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **F432 major races** | Humans, Axiomorphs, Veyrin, Lineborn, Kharad, Choirglass, Palewind Swarms | Everyday neighbors across Nauw / Wabet / Sorel — see [Major Races Compact](../cultures/f432-major-races-compact.md) |
| **Energy-kind** | [Aurel-kind](../inhabitants/aurel-kind.md) and Meridian kin | Bright-spine persons of F200; visit F432 as rare guests more often than settlers |
| **Process-kind** | [Draftkin](../inhabitants/draftkin.md) | F960 citizens; revisable form, stable accountability |
| **Echoforms (class)** | [Echoforms](../inhabitants/echoforms.md) | Emergent from accumulated resonance; personhood case-by-case—some kin, some hazard, some rumor |
| **Minority / migrant pockets** | Unindexed races, rotating enclaves | High-contact F432 assumes more kinds than the seven |

Echoforms are **not** “monsters by default.” Classification belongs with origin and behavior, not with fear alone.

## Daily coexistence (F432)

Lived practice is gathered in [`F432 Shared Table`](../cultures/f432-shared-table.md): who cooks, who sails, who keeps silence, how markets translate, where prejudice shows, where friendship wins.

Majority impression should stay: **people you’d love to meet**.

### Texture by kind (compact)

| Kind | Often found… | Texture note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Humans | everywhere civic | Form clubs, argue loudly, rebuild quickly |
| Axiomorphs | docks, archives, systems halls | Precise traders; gesture needs learning |
| Veyrin | Veloria crafts, courier houses | One signature trait each—discipline over spectacle |
| Lineborn | copy houses, map rooms, quiet trails | Planar bodies; not ghosts |
| Kharad | bridges, kilns, long repairs | Dense craft; art ignored by stereotypes |
| Choirglass | mediation rooms, song docks | Sound-light speech; not automatic sages |
| Palewind Swarms | rim winds, canopy air, fleet veils | One person, many motes—law treats continuity |

## Authoring rule

When inventing a new people, ask first whether an existing race or pocket can hold the story. Prefer enriching coexistence over adding a guest-monster species.

## Notes

Exit test: a reader can imagine sharing bread with at least three non-human kinds without defaulting to combat framing.
