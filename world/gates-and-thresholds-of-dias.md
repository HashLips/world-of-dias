# Gates and Thresholds of Dias

## Overview

**Gates and Thresholds of Dias** indexes crossings where bodies meet judgment—DEB Gate, Long Gate, ring clearance, and courtesy knots—so travel chapters can stage dignity before stamps.

## Core Premise

A threshold is architecture as manners. Feeling the line comes before filing the paper.
