# Economy of Dias

## Overview

**Economy of Dias** is why people work, trade, gift, and cheat—without reducing the world to grimdark scarcity. Money and gifts both feel real.

## Core Premise

**VEL** is the shared physical tender across Nauw, Wabet, and Sorel. Beside it run barter, labor-rest, pot favors, kettle contribution, ribbon assent, and Good Trade still-hour fairness. Pressure exists; kindness markets exist too.
