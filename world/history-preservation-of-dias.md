# History Preservation of Dias

## Overview

**History Preservation of Dias** is the Volume VI.6 sheet: how history is kept—monuments, artifacts, songs, documents, and traditions—differing by culture/region.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

Preservation is politics and care. Wrong maps can be mercy. Seals can be care or hoarding. Songs can outlast ledgers.
