# Major Relics and Artifacts of Dias

## Overview

**Major Relics and Artifacts of Dias** is the Volume V.8 dossier sheet: major relics with known / disputed / unknown sorted—capabilities that do not break Volume I soft limits.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

Origins may be disputed—say so. Capabilities must not become wish engines or band ferries. Everyday wonder objects stay on [Artifacts of Dias](artifacts-of-dias.md); this sheet is **saga-weight / realm-weight** class.

Umbrella: [Resonant Artifacts](../artifacts/resonant-artifacts.md).
