# Aelwyn Canopy Ways of Dias

## Overview

**Aelwyn Canopy Ways of Dias** indexes seven-path truce manners—seasonal paths, canopy law—without turning forest routes into band ferries.

## Core Premise

Paths share the canopy by rule and story. Forced-Final clear-cuts fail the truce.
