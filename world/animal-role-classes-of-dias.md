# Animal Role Classes of Dias

## Overview

**Animal Role Classes of Dias** identifies common wildlife, domesticated animals, working animals, and dangerous species—so settlements feel alive with ordinary bodies as well as wonder and hazard.

## Core Premise

### Plain classes

| Class | Meaning | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wildlife (gentle / wild)** | Not owned; encounterable | Softlane Hares; Verdant Choir-Frogs; Ribbon Gulls; Soft-Bears (wild wonder) |
| **Domestic / companion** | Household or trail attachment | Veloria Lap-Cats; Hearthvale Ember-Cats; Wabet Trail-Pups |
| **Working** | Haul, watch, dairy, relay | Ringroad Dogs; Fruit-Road Draft-Ponies; Nauw Basin Oxen; Deb Gate Watch-Geese; Sorel Bridge-Mules |
| **Food / barnyard** | Raised or harvested as sustenance | Market Grin-Pigs; Basin Yard-Chickens; Doorstep-Hens |
| **Pest** | Annoying, sometimes usefully hated | Tubehold Rust-Rats; Ledger Dust-Fleas; Sproutvent Spore-Mites |
| **Dangerous** | Real bodily harm risk (Creatures hazard overlap) | Redmile Striders; Exile Road Wolves; Quietwell Serpents; Keel-Sharks; Dune-Drakes |

### Balance rule

Wonder-creatures are not enough. Every major habitat should have at least one ordinary collar/work/food tell and one wild tell. Dangerous species teach place law—not loot tables.
