# Harbors and Piers of Dias

## Overview

**Harbors and Piers of Dias** indexes water edges—Driftfall flags, rim listen posts, joke piers, offshore courtesy—where rescue and comedy share the same wet wood.

## Core Premise

Piers teach soft limits in public: floating claims, no-ferry jokes, distress before debt.
