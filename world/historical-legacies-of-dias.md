# Historical Legacies of Dias

## Overview

**Historical Legacies of Dias** is the Volume VI.5 sheet: important historical figures with **verified legacy** separated from **legend inflation**. Living saga seats stay on Named Inhabitant Profiles.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

Memory figures are remembered through institutions, songs, and scars—not calendar biographies. Separate what places still practice from what toast invents.
