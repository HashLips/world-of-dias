# Frequency and Life of Dias

## Overview

**Frequency and Life of Dias** defines how living beings take on band habit in body, timing, strain, and recovery.

## Core Premise

### Plain definition

**Band life habit** means: living bodies and beings inherit measurable costs and skills from the frequency layer they inhabit or approach—ordinary load, alignment fatigue, identity softening, affect contagion, revision urge, refrain timing, ember pace, stare-cost.

### What this is not

- Not a ferry ticket (“species X gets you into band Y”)
- Not permanent omniscience as a life upgrade
- Not a settled catalog of every rumor-beast (Unraveled / Distant Companies stay partial)

Home-band F432 peoples carry ordinary resonance as mixed civic load. Visitors toward luminosity or null learn strain that Softfruit and Houses can name without inventing subways.

### Band life habits (working expectations)

| Band | Life habit | Lived tell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F432 | High-contact civic load; mixed peoples and animals | Markets, Softfruit, Houses, road beasts |
| F200 | Coherence pressure on visitors; serene energy life in rumor | Alignment fatigue; Aurel-kind as partial intelligence |
| F500 | Continuity thinning near concentration | Identity softening; survivor accounts perforated |
| F960 | Becoming as baseline urge in Draftkin cultures | Revision hunger without shame |
| F120 | Body timing that rhymes rather than loops | Refrain Walkers; near-return muscle memory |
| F380 | Affect contagion through crowds and Tidehearts | Shared mood as health weather |
| F610 | Stare-cost; glance discipline among Veil Scribes | Eyes that learn to stop |
| F840 | Ember pace; recovery as living craft | Ember Keepers; shelter-first bodies |

### What life teaching refuses

- “Species X is a ticket to band Y.”
- Permanent omniscience as a life adaptation in F610.
- Filing Unraveled or Distant Companies as settled life taxonomy.

## Global Lore

Life inherits band habit is the rule spine. Softlane Hares and kitchen animals teach F432 load; cross-band exemplars stay partial where soft limits require. Flora companions (Band-Answer Moss and kin) show growth answering the same law.
