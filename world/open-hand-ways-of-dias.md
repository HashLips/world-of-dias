# Open Hand Ways of Dias

## Overview

**Open Hand Ways of Dias** indexes maker ethics that prefer visible seams, undo paths, and reciprocity over Forced-Final clamps—Wabet, F960 craft cousins, Wired Bloom proof.

## Core Premise

Making is care you can reopen. Mercy that erases undo is not Open Hand.
