# The Farstock Saga

## Name

The Farstock Saga

## Overview

The Farstock Saga is a journey of three teenagers in Veloria’s outer ring who treat a half-believed shop as a door into a better feeling, steal what the shop would not sell, and walk the real roads toward **Sorel** in the company of a frequency being that wants something worse than a second beginning.

It sits beside the Harmonic, Lumen, and Calibration sagas. It does not replace them and does not finish their open questions.

## Core Premise

**What you take from Farstock does not become yours because it worked.**

The working is the trap. The stone takes color. The echo appears. The south road is real, and for a while it feels like proof. Then the thing they stole will not put itself down.

## Global Lore

Frequencies in this saga stay where F432 already keeps them: rumor, myth, tavern weather, and signs. **F380** leans through the stone as hearttide color, not as a ferry into the Bloomline. **F500** leans through cold corners and stolen contrast, not as a route anyone learns. No one in the saga reaches F500. No one comes home able to teach a crossing.

The country they walk toward is **Sorel**, south of Nauw by the Long Gate into Hearthvale’s weather. They see that weather. They do not arrive as neighbors.
