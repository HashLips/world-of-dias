# Frequency Contact and Discovery Progression of Dias

## Overview

This orientation distinguishes **contact, measurement, controlled crossing, and transport**. It reconciles F432's real visitors with present travel limits and keeps room for later discoveries. Its future stages are conditional storytelling possibilities, not events already known to happen.

## Core Premise

### Existing contact and uneven understanding

F432 supports established diverse communities and exceptional cross-frequency guests. Aurel-kind from F200 and Draftkin from F960 are genuine visitors. Vorrith Kael reached Nauw from another frequency condition and later settled in Wabet; neither his home-band nor the precise explanation is settled here. These examples do not give all F432 peoples foreign origins or make every resident aware of every visitor.

A host may know a guest exists without knowing the physics of their home, how they arrived, or whether they can return. Researchers may possess measurements unavailable to that host. Sealed testimony, direct acquaintance, public rumor, and independently checked evidence remain distinct. A visitor is a person before being a research opportunity.

### Relative progression, not new eras

| Stage | Relative anchor | Established capability or conditional possibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exceptional encounters | Already part of F432's lived record; not created by Orrin's proof | Real visitors and rare arrivals coexist with incomplete explanations. No public cross-frequency transport. |
| Reproducible measurement | Calibration experimentation, agreeing graphs, and partial publish | Orrin measures band signatures from F432. Measurement is reproducible; passenger passage is not thereby demonstrated. |
| Controlled crossing research | A possible later story, explicitly after that experimentation and publish if it builds on them | A new breakthrough could enable deliberate passage under specific conditions. Method, destination, bodily demands, costs, and return capability must be established by that story. No such breakthrough is declared here. |
| Journeys | A possible further development after a story has established workable crossings | Exploration and encounters could expand, eventually to more travelers. Routes, reliability, consent, access, and destination-specific constraints still need evidence; a successful expedition alone is not public transport. |

These stages are not universal ages shared by all realms or every community. Exceptional arrivals can precede reproducible research and continue afterward. Nothing declares a first-ever controlled crossing across all Dias, or gives researchers access to another people's sealed practices.

### What remains law and what can change

Frequency remains fundamental; realm differences and the costs of resonance remain consequential. Current ignorance and absent infrastructure are historically situated. No-Ferry ethics require honest claims: understanding a band is not a ticket, and an outbound success is not a safe return. Later demonstrated capabilities may change what can honestly be offered without repealing resonance itself.

No mechanism is selected here: no universal gate, vehicle, ray, tuning procedure, or automatic use of bleed weather. The Spectrum Frame remains an instrument. A measurement seam is not promoted to a doorway. Future travel must earn its own explanation and limits.

## Global Lore

Preserve the published saga sequence and existing overlaps. Frederick's prior consultation with Orrin remains a remembered event during his research years; nothing here dates his disappearance anew, confirms a crossing, or supplies an outcome. A new story using post-Calibration capabilities should say so in its opening orientation, using relative anchors rather than numbered Fracture years.

The existing `time_era` and `time_span` vocabulary stays intact. This entry concerns present understanding, hence `present` / `ongoing`; its conditional rows do not tag future events as present history or introduce new dashboard eras.
