# Food of Dias

## Overview

**Food of Dias** makes hunger, feasting, and unclaimed breakfast world-real. Meals should be able to carry plot and pleasure. Flora and fauna entries remain the species records; this hub and its culture files are **how people eat**.

## Core Premise

Joy layer: shared tables, fruit rank, loft welcomes, laughter-week kitchens.  
Pressure layer: green-crates, exile roads, vent haze weeks, hush-week thin markets, cold gaps.

Indexes alone are not enough—regional foodways and settlement boards have their own entries.
