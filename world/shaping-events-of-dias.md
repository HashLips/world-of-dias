# Shaping Events of Dias

## Overview

**Shaping Events of Dias** is the Volume VI.4 sheet: migrations, conflicts, discoveries, and disasters that shaped the current world—with aftermath still visible. Conflict ≠ endless war weather.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

Prefer **regional shaping** with present leftovers (a road, a law, a fear, a festival). Legend may encode; grade claims honestly.
