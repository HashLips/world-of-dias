# Place Webs of Dias

## Overview

**Place Webs of Dias** links important locations to inhabitants, civilizations, artifacts, and historical beats so atlas places are never empty stages.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

A major place file should point to: **who lives or works there**, **whose manners apply**, **at least one artifact or craft object**, and **one historical or story beat**—when those exist in canon.

### Filing habit

When enriching a place, add `related:` links to the people/culture/artifact/story rather than rewriting the place as a biography. Many major place files now carry an in-file **Place web** paragraph. History hubs (Nauw Civic Memory, Sorel Second-Beginning Memory, Wabet Restraint Memory) hold deeper chronology.
