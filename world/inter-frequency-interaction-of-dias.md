# Inter-Frequency Interaction of Dias

## Overview

**Inter-Frequency Interaction of Dias** defines how established frequency layers affect each other, how communication across bands works (and fails), and why ordinary travel between realms is exceptional—not infrastructure.

## Core Premise

### Plain definitions

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Inter-frequency interaction** | Any real effect of one frequency layer on another (bleed weather, residual glow, omen pressure, exceptional person/tool contact) |
| **Cross-frequency communication** | Transfer of meaning across bands—usually partial, omenized, or delayed—not reliable mail |
| **Exceptional crossing** | Rare movement of a being or prepared instrument between bands; treated as omen, sealed practice, accident, or legend |
| **Ordinary travel** | Commute, ticket, ferry, subway, grocery tour between bands—**does not exist** as working infrastructure |

### What interaction can look like (without ferry)

1. **Bleed / wrong-sky weather** — another band’s pressure visits a place as event weather (Bleed-Sky Weather; Distant lumen-abyss bleed)
2. **Residual measurement** — instruments and Houses file partial harmonics; catalogs stay incomplete (F432 partial knowledge protocol)
3. **Metaphor and omen speech** — Frequency Speech Metaphors; Cross-Frequency Omenspeak
4. **Icon approaches** — places that teach another band’s law-feel without being tickets (Returning Span, Glassfold, Hearthline, Unfinished, Meridian)
5. **Exceptional persons / sealed tech** — saga-scale, Operator-class, or sealed practice—not public transit

### Why ordinary travel is exceptional (fails as infrastructure)

| Claim | Judgment | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Buy a ticket F432 → F200” | Fail / taboo comedy | No-Ferry; soft limits |
| “Bleed night is a door to F500” | Fail / wrong nest | Local phenomenon ≠ realm |
| “Understanding a band is a ferry” | Fail | Knowledge ≠ ticket (Resonance Can and Cannot) |
| Rare successful approach / saga crossing | Exceptional succeed | Costly, disputed, not schedule |

### Communication limits (plain)

- Messages across bands are **low reliability**
- Bleed bruises carry fear more than clear text
- Window talk is brief then gone
- Ledger fragments are sticky gossip with thin provenance
- Treating omenspeak as telegram is manners failure

## Global Lore

Cross-Frequency Travel is the lived culture sheet. Exceptional crossing is not infrastructure is the binding rule. Soft Limits of Dias and No-Ferry Mark carry the refusal. Bleed Weather Manners of Dias keep visiting weather from becoming country.

## Notes

Does not invent a subway. Does not solve why crossings sometimes happen—only that they are not ordinary logistics.
