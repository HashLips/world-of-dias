# Life Kinds of Dias

## Overview

**Life Kinds of Dias** is the teachable taxonomy for Volume III: how Softfruit, trail hosts, and the archive distinguish **ordinary animals**, **extraordinary creatures**, **peoples**, and **ambiguous life**—without turning neighbors into monsters or mysteries into fake zoology.

## Core Premise

### Plain definitions

| Life kind | Definition | Archive home | Index hub |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ordinary animal** | Living non-person body that settlements treat as companion, work, food, pest, or gentle wild—recognizable without omen first | `inhabitants/` (`nature` often `creature` / `animal`) | Animals of Dias |
| **Extraordinary creature** | Being whose wonder, hazard, rumor, or existential charge exceeds ordinary farm-and-pet life | `inhabitants/` | Creatures of Dias |
| **People / peoples** | Neighbors you can share a table, law, or craft with—persons and person-kinds, not bestiary prey | `inhabitants/` (`nature` often `person` / `human` / race labels) | Peoples of Dias |
| **Ambiguous life** | Confirmed presence or strong witness without settled kind—do not force animal / creature / people | `inhabitants/` (`nature` often `unknown` / contested) | This sheet + status framing |
| **Flora / growth** | Plants, fungi, and growth (separate kingdom for field-guide work) | `flora/` | Flora of Dias |

### Sorting rules (plain)

1. **Table test:** If you can invite them to Softfruit without absurdity, lean **people**—even if their body is strange.  
2. **Collar test:** If a child names them as pet / work / kitchen life first, lean **ordinary animal**.  
3. **Law test:** If the being mainly teaches silence, null, stillness, or social wound as *threat or awe*, lean **extraordinary creature** (Creatures classes: wonder / hazard / rumor / existential).  
4. **Honesty test:** If witnesses disagree and Softfruit refuses a neat label, file **ambiguous**—keep `status` honest (`rumor` / `unknown` / `legendary`).  
5. **Do not casually blur peoples into monsters.** Strange body ≠ prey class.

### Worked examples

| Being | Kind | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ringroad Dogs | Ordinary animal | Work + heart; collar life |
| Softlane Hares | Ordinary animal (omen-adjacent) | Ordinary body; charged reading allowed |
| Canopy Soft-Bears | Extraordinary (wonder) *and* beloved wild | Magnificence; still not a people |
| Mega Butterfly | Extraordinary (wonder / hazard) | Sky-season awe with risk |
| Redmile Striders | Extraordinary (hazard) | Lonely-road predator |
| Human-Eyed Whales | Extraordinary (wonder) + kinship tension | Not “people,” but ethics treat them near-kin |
| Softfruit hosts / Velorians / Keepers | Peoples | Table, craft, law |
| Tidehearts / Draftkin / Ember Keepers | Peoples (band-native kinds) | Persons with band habit—not tickets |
| Sky Drifters | Ambiguous | Always above Veloria; never landed; `nature: unknown` |
| Forest Listeners | Ambiguous / rumor creature | Trail ethics real; taxonomy thin |
| Sketchshade Cats | Ambiguous / rumor | Ordinary stray vs gallery-kept uncanny |
| Echoforms | Ambiguous / special class | Resonance bodies—not farm fauna |

### What this taxonomy refuses

- Calling a people a “creature” because their outline is stylized  
- Calling Sky Drifters a confirmed animal species  
- Using Unraveled / Distant Companies / Horizon-Nulls as a completed life catalog (soft limits)  
- Treating Frequency Zero as a habitat with a species list  

## Global Lore

Animals of Dias and Creatures of Dias remain the deep indexes. Peoples of Dias remains the neighbor index. Frequency and Life of Dias explains band habit in bodies. Inhabitant Nature Vocabulary of Dias maps archive `nature:` words to these kinds. Teaching: Life Kind Teaching; card: Life Kind Sorting Card.

## Notes

Flora is life too, but Volume III plant phases use Flora of Dias. This sheet answers III.1’s four-way distinction for animals / creatures / peoples / ambiguous forms.
