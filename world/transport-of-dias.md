# Transport of Dias

## Overview

**Transport of Dias** is how bodies cross a layered world—fruit roads, spans, ferries, silence routes, exile bridges, and rare cross-frequency passages that are omenized, not subwayed.

A journey chapter needs **logistics and poetry**. Both live in the entries below.

## Core Premise

Movement is culture. Paying a toll, answering a loft hail, walking soft on Saltwhisper, or ringing bells on a redmile are as much speech as wheels.

Indexes alone are not enough—each major movement mode has its own entry.

## F432 movement sketch

```
Wabet orchards ──Eastbound Fruit Road──► Brindle / Span Toll ──► Veloria Basin markets
     │                                         │
     ├── Open Hand Way (makers / reciprocity)   ├── Glasswater east
     ├── Saltwhisper (Keep assent)              └── Outer Rim barges / loft rings
     └── Calareth ferry (infrequent, intentional)
                │
Sorel ◄── Long Gate (Nauw→Hearthvale)     Westfold Toll (Hearthvale↔Wabet)
     ├── Flagweek Strip (allegiance weather)
     ├── Claimscar / Driftfall yards
     └── Good Trade mountain approaches
```

Cross-frequency: rare intervals, sealed talk, Returning Span / Glassfold / Hearthline metaphors—not scheduled service.
