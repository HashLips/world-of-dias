# The Harmonic Saga — Movement Two

## Overview

**Movement Two** follows Calen Brink after the first Bridge witness event: clearance revoked, name flagged on Resonant House channels, love with Tamsin Vor deepening, echo-reading maturing. The stage expands **south and west**—Sorel's exile compact, Averra's sealed memory, F500's horizon—while Veloria's invisible governance closes in.

Movement Two does **not** resolve the Fracture, Oriven Tal's true nature, or the Unraveled.

## Starting Conditions (after Movement One)

- Calen chose **basin-first witness** over channel silence ([`stories/what-the-bridge-remembers.md`](../stories/what-the-bridge-remembers.md))
- Ring clearance **revoked**; notebook **copied** by unknown channel agent
- Tamsin and Calen **stay together** in basin life—pledged, not performed
- Half-Made Atlas margin still **revising** toward unknown destinations

## Movement Question

**When institutions call you noise, where do you belong—and who gets to seal what the world remembers?**
