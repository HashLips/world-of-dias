# Known Eras and Markers

## Overview

Dias does not use a single universal calendar. This entry collects **soft era markers** storytellers and archivists use to orient events without fixing precise dates or resolving cosmic mysteries.

## Eras

### The Prime Age

The unified reality before the Fracture. No living witness. All duration estimates are myth-touched.

### The Present Age

Everything after the Fracture. Most saga events occur here.

## Commonly Cited Markers (F432)

| Marker | Meaning | Certainty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Generations since Fracture** | People count backward in family lines, not years; "four generations of gate-keepers" is a typical phrase | cultural, imprecise |
| **Long Gate era** | Period after the great Nauw–Hearthvale bridge crossing defined Sorel's northern gate | regional, disputed in detail |
| **Ring expansion decades** | Veloria's inner rings rebuilt outward-in several times; outer ring still "medieval" by policy and taste | civic record, approximate |
| **Echo-rise season** | Recent interval when harmonic echo reports increased along Nauw trade routes; Calen Brink's awakening aligns with this period | observational, contested |
| **Echo-rise schism season** | Competing Resonant House codes H-11 and H-19 during Calen Brink witness network expansion | institutional, unresolved |
| **Ledger waves** | Supplemental harmonic ledger fragments circulated in three rumor waves (F200/F500; F120/F380/F610/F840; F960 gloss) | archival gossip |

## Character-Defining Events (Selected)

These are **biographical anchors**, not full histories:

- **Vailor Quentin** — rise to High Resonant tied in competing myths to the red-sail crown; date unknown
- **Carrow Vale** — exile day and mask adoption; remembered in Sorel as "First Exile reckoning," not calendar-dated
- **Oriven Tal** — appears in Ring Council records without verifiable early biography
- **Marek Solon** — dual-form emergence linked in sea cults to "fracture tide return"; unverified
- **Calen Brink** — first conscious echo-read at DEB Gate ([`stories/the-echo-at-the-gate.md`](../stories/the-echo-at-the-gate.md)); basin witness network after Movement Two ([`stories/underground-thrum.md`](../stories/underground-thrum.md))

## Notes

Do not use this entry to pin the Fracture to a numbered year. Filmmakers may treat markers as culturally situated texture; repository explorers may treat them as orienting, not authoritative.
