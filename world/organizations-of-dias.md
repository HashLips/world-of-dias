# Organizations of Dias

## Overview

**Organizations of Dias** are groups with goals—the engines of later sagas. Most are mixed morality. Conflict can be institutional, not only personal.

## Core Premise

For each major org: **purpose, method, public face, private fear, relationship to echoes**. New factions fill empty pressure niches only. Relationship pass: [`Relationships of Dias`](relationships-of-dias.md). This page keeps a light alliance/rivalry sketch.

**IV.6 offices / competing influences sheet:** [Authorities and Factions of Dias](authorities-and-factions-of-dias.md).

Individual cards live as culture/inhabitant entries linked below—indexes alone are not enough.

```
Resonant Houses ←filter→ Valorian Operators (need each other; resent each other)

        ↓ exile roads

        ↔

        ↔

Bleed Weather Ward … petitions Houses; streets call them late or loud
```

Rivalries soft-form: Houses vs witness-share street; Keep vs fruit-money map pressure; Freebands vs Concord boots; Open Hand vs forced-final seals; Ward vs Null Cell (containment vs curiosity).
