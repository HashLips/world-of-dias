# Echo Vocabulary of Dias

## Overview

**Echo Vocabulary of Dias** collects the ordinary and clerical words people use when they talk about resonance without inventing a laboratory dialect. Softfruit, gates, Houses, and road crews share overlapping speech; they fight over precision, not over whether the phenomenon exists.

## Core Premise

If you cannot name the remainder, you will either worship it or deny it. Dias prefers naming with manners.

### Common speech

| Term | Who says it | What they usually mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Warmth / aftertone** | Softfruit, kitchens | Pleasant residue of shared time |
| **Stain / sour** | Gates, booths | Unpleasant residue of conflict |
| **Tick / load-noise** | Operators, clerks | Instrument-adjacent residue and interference |
| **Rise** | Watchers, saga talk | Sudden intensification (see Harmonic Echo-Rise) |
| **Scraped truth** | Copy rooms | Correction that admits prior mark |
| **Sealed** | Houses | Echo filed away from casual listening |
| **Partial** | Everyone honest | Not a full story; never trust completeness |
| **Refusal** | Open Hand, Softfruit, some Houses | Choosing not to listen further |

### Dangerous speech

Calling an echo a **ferry**, a **verdict**, or a **complete person** is how soft limits break in public. Folk Resonance Metaphors allow poetry; ledgers require Partial Echo Slash honesty.

## Global Lore

House juniors learn code bars; Softfruit learns bowls and table warmth; Driftfall learns witness talk; Wabet learns silence as a kind of listening. The vocabulary differs; the law does not.
