# Law Theory Observation of Dias

## Overview

**Law Theory Observation of Dias** teaches how inhabitants and institutions sort cosmic claims into **law**, **theory**, **observation**, and **taboo guess**—so disagreement stays honest and false certainty does not become policy.

## Core Premise

Dias is full of confident talk. Only some of that talk deserves to steer gates, kitchens, and ledgers. Claim Grade Discipline is the working rule; this primer is the plain-speech map.

### Four grades

| Grade | Meaning | How it behaves in life |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Law** | Reliable world behavior across cultures that otherwise fight | Softfruit and Houses both act as if it is true even when they hate each other’s manners |
| **Theory** | Useful, argued, sometimes institutionalized—still revisable | Guides research, filing, and caution; must not silently become law |
| **Observation** | Witnessed or instrumented remainder/event without settled cause-model | Informs caution and story; needs labeling as seen, not as solved |
| **Taboo guess** | Spoken carefully, sealed, joked, or prayed—dangerous if treated as tool | May be filed; must not be sold as commute, wipe, or verdict |

### Worked examples (already in canon)

- **Law:** Everything resonates; everything leaves an echo. Frequency shapes what is possible. The Fracture separated Prime into frequencies (as historical separation fact—not as a solved culprit story).
- **Theory:** Exact count of frequencies; best bleed model; whether null “hunger” relates to Zero; how echo-reading should be governed.
- **Observation:** Distant lumen-abyss bleed nights; gate argument-stain; table laughter residue; unfinished edge drift as reported behavior.
- **Taboo guess:** Forcing Zero into a tool; null as workplace; naming the Fracture’s villain as settled fact; ferry-as-science.

### How Softfruit and Houses differ without breaking the grades

Softfruit resists Forced-Final certainty at lunch. Houses resist unfiled certainty at desks. Both can agree a claim is theory while arguing what to *do*. F432 partial knowledge protocol keeps rumor from becoming law by stamp accident.

## Global Lore

Wrong maps and panic pamphlets thrive by promoting observations and taboo guesses into law-sounding sentences. Stack Teaching and echo craft both spend time demoting claims back to their grade. Graded Principles of Dias keeps a living index.

## Notes

Binding rule: Claim Grade Discipline. Do not invent a fifth metaphysics; grade the one Dias already has.
