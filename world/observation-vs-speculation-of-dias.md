# Observation vs Speculation of Dias

## Overview

**Observation vs Speculation of Dias** is the Volume VI.8 sheet: significant observed phenomena separated from speculation about their causes—keeping scholarly disagreement.

## Core Premise

### Plain definitions

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Observed** | What bodies, instruments, and communities reliably report happening |
| **Speculation** | Competing guesses about why—myth, House theory, street omen |
| **Disagreement** | Honest plurality; not a bug to erase |

Law cousin: [Law Theory Observation of Dias](law-theory-observation-of-dias.md).

## Entry hygiene (phenomena files)

When editing phenomena:

1. Put **what is seen / heard / timed** in Conditions or Description.  
2. Put **why people say it happens** in Cultural Interpretations / Beliefs.  
3. Keep Theory-open where Law Theory Observation requires it.  
4. Never upgrade speculation to documented cause to close a soft limit.
