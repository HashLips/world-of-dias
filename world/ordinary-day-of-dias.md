# Ordinary Day of Dias

## Overview

**Ordinary Day of Dias** is the Volume IV.5 definition sheet: family, education, professions, food, and entertainment woven into narratable hours—so a normal day in Veloria (and cousins) can be told from canon.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

Plot arrives *on top of* baths, ladles, desks, gates, and evening games. If a day cannot be told hour-by-hour without inventing chores, ordinary life is still thin.

### Exit test

Narrate a Veloria second-ring day from dawn wash to lamp-row without new etiquette. Then swap one beat for Wabet soft-step, Sorel kettle, Calareth ribbon, or Rim loft using the day-slice stories.
