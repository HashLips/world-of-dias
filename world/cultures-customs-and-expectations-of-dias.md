# Cultures Customs and Expectations of Dias

## Overview

**Cultures Customs and Expectations of Dias** is the definition-first sheet for Volume IV.3: what a culture *does* in a scene—customs, celebrations, traditions, and social expectations—so daily-life chapters can be staged without inventing manners.

## Core Premise

### Plain definitions

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Culture pack** | Regional or stance framework with kindness + hard edge (Cultures of Dias) |
| **Custom** | Repeatable act others recognize (ladle before stamp; soft steps at a well) |
| **Celebration** | Named joy weather with rules (Flagweek breakfast; First Fruit Arrival) |
| **Tradition** | Custom with memory attached (revision bands; loft First Line) |
| **Social expectation** | What neighbors assume you will do unless you signal otherwise |

### Rule for scenes

A culture is ready for a chapter when you can stage: **welcome**, **shared meal or work**, **mistake**, **repair or refusal**, and **exit**—without inventing new etiquette mid-scene.
