# Frequency Bands Orientation of Dias

## Overview

**Frequency Bands Orientation of Dias** is a feel-map of major bands for stage use—home-sweet F432 and its louder/colder/reveal/recovery/becoming cousins—without retelling saga chapters or inventing ferries.

## Core Premise

Bands are climates of meaning. Travel between them is omenized, costly, or comic—not a subway. Full realm files and rules hold detail; this sheet is the glance.
