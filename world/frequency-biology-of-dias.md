# Frequency Biology of Dias

## Overview

**Frequency Biology of Dias** explains how different frequencies influence biology and lifeforms—with concrete species and habitat examples, thin where soft limits require.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

Bodies and growth **answer their band** (Matter answers its band; Life inherits band habit). Biology difference is lived cost and skill—not a subway ticket grown in a seed.

### Concrete examples

| Band | Biological tell | Example life |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F432 | Mixed civic load; ordinary animals + peoples | Softlane Hares; Ringroad Dogs; Market Banana ecology; Band-Answer Moss teaching |
| F200 | Coherence pressure; luminous life partial | Aurel-kind; Soft-Ray approaches—not a full farm almanac |
| F500 | Continuity thinning; anti-habitat | Horizon-Nulls / survivor perforation—not park wildlife |
| F380 | Affect contagion through crowds and Tidehearts | Mood as health weather in bodies |
| F960 | Revision urge in Draftkin cultures | Form change without shame; still accountability |
| F120 | Refrain body timing | Refrain Walkers; near-return muscle memory |
| F610 | Stare-cost strain | Veil Scribe glance discipline |
| F840 | Ember pace recovery | Ember Keepers; shelter-first bodies |

### What frequency biology refuses

- “Eat this plant to become F200.”  
- Full biospheres invented for bands that should stay thin.  
- Filing Unraveled / Distant Companies as complete wildlife.
