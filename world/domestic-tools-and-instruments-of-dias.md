# Domestic Tools and Instruments of Dias

## Overview

**Domestic Tools and Instruments of Dias** is the Volume V.4 sheet: household objects, tools, agricultural gear, clothing cues, and instruments—so everyday scenes need not invent props.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

Homes teach belonging before Houses do. Props should be place-true: Softfruit bowls in Basin flats, sand trays in Lumira, claim sticks high in Claimscar.
