# Conflict of Dias

## Overview

**Conflict of Dias** stages pressure—border pain, belief fights, bleed weather, null dread—without killing wonder. The world should feel safe enough to love and unsafe enough to need heroes later.

## Core Premise

This section stacks **dry wood**. Sagas may ignite; we do not complete the burn here. Happiness zones remain (Softfruit, Coral Strand, kettle peace, loft songs) even when threatened.
