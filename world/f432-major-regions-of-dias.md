# F432 Major Regions of Dias

## Overview

**F432 Major Regions of Dias** distinguishes the three established major regions of the home band—**Nauw**, **Wabet**, and **Sorel**—by position, terrain feel, culture touch, and travel job so a journey planner cannot mix them up.

## Core Premise

### Plain definitions

| Region | Map seat (F432) | One-line job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nauw** | North-east / trade heart | Crowded heart: markets, rings, gates, fruit hunger |
| **Wabet** | Full west | Chosen calm: wilderness, fruit source, silence manners |
| **Sorel** | South / south-east | Second beginning: exile roads, salvage, Freebands |

### Unlike each other

| Domain | Nauw | Wabet | Sorel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Terrain | Basin, march, glasswater fields, outer rim seas | Canopy, sands, clean coasts, sparse settle | Hearthvale/Driftfall split, dust, salvage shore |
| Culture touch | Concord, Softfruit, Houses, fruit rank | Keepers, restraint, Open Hand Way | Freebands, exile mutual table, kiln remaking |
| Travel feel | Crowded roads, tolls, ring clearance | Softfall walks, silence routes, fruit eastbound | Exile bridges, yard claims, Flagweek chaos |
| Joy / pressure | Ring-fair / Concord judgment | Softfall / conquest temptation | Roof-raising / wolves and vents |
| Architecture | Layered rings and gates | Low listening timber | Salvage and smoke |

### Borders (plain)

- Wabet borders Nauw eastward and Sorel south-eastward  
- Nauw bridges south into Sorel at Long Gate → Hearthvale  
- Fruit moves Wabet → Nauw along Eastbound Fruit Road corridors  
- Do not file any of the three as a different frequency
