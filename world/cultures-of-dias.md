# Cultures of Dias

## Overview

**Cultures of Dias** indexes living disagreement and shared joy—the heart of a loveable world. Characters should be able to conflict about values without a villain monologue.

## Core Premise

Every major culture here has a **kindness practice** and a **hard edge**. Stances about echo are practiced, not only argued.
