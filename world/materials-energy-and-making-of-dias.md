# Materials Energy and Making of Dias

## Overview

**Materials Energy and Making of Dias** is the Volume V.2 sheet: what stuff is made from, what powers work, and how workshops manufacture—place-linked, not generic industry.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

A maker’s chapter needs **material manners**, **energy weather**, and **workshop habit**. Echo-aware craft means traces stay in the object.
