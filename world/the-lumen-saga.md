# The Lumen Saga

## Name

The Lumen Saga

## Overview

The Lumen Saga follows **Frederick Lumens** from Driftfall orphan decks through Roadglow, Stillhollow, Apple Touch, and Fellgrove—to a second climb toward **Vorrith Kael** in search of a frequency crossing. He vanishes from F432 tellings afterward. Whether he crossed remains open. What remains is his letter.

It does not replace **The Harmonic Saga**.

## Core Premise

**If the door is rare enough to steal, who gets to walk through—and what does wanting cost when you start with nothing?**

The saga answers with journey, gift, land, and an unfinished door—not with a confirmed arrival in another band.

## Protagonist

**Frederick Lumens** — Driftfall youth; Apple Touch; Fellgrove heir then giver; missing after asking Vorrith how to travel frequencies.
