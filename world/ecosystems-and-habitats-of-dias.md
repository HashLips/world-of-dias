# Ecosystems and Habitats of Dias

## Overview

**Ecosystems and Habitats of Dias** places living systems on the atlas: named habitats where plants, animals, and peoples meet—frequency-aware where established, dense in F432, thin and honest elsewhere.

## Core Premise

### Plain definitions

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Habitat** | Named living place feet and roots share (canopy Softfall, salt fog cove, salvage tide) |
| **Ecosystem** | Habitat plus its typical interactions (who eats, shelters, pollutes manners) |
| **Band habitat note** | Other-frequency life places that must not be drawn as F432 provinces |

### F432 habitats by major region

| Region | Habitats (files of record) | Signature life |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nauw** | Basin Mist Orchard; Glasswater Ag Channel; Outer Rim Salt Fog | Lap-cats / oxen; mirror-reed / feed channels; Singing Fishermen + salt seals |
| **Wabet** | Aelwyn Softfall Canopy; Verdant Choir Meadow; Lumira Silent Dune; That Forest Blue Underwood | Soft-Bears; choir-frogs; dune silence life; Listeners / blue litter caution |
| **Sorel** | Hearthvale Rebuild Field; Driftfall Salvage Tide; Sprout Vent Haze | Ember-cats / doorstep hens; rope-crabs / salvage shore; spore caution |

### Other-band habitat notes (thin on purpose)

| Band | Habitat feel | Do not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F200 | Luminous field / Soft-Ray approaches | Invent full farm ecology |
| F500 | Anti-habitat; forgetting as texture | Map as a park district of Nauw |
| F380 | Mood-as-weather coasts and crowds | Paste onto Glasswater rain |
| F840 / F960 / F120 / F610 | Ember shelter, draft scaffolds, refrain lanes, stare-cost halls | Treat as F432 biomes with funny names |

### Placeable rule

Every F432 habitat file nests under a real landform or region (`parent_region` honest). Species links go both ways when enriched.

## Global Lore

Landforms of F432 are terrain bones; this sheet is living cover. Seasons change habitat pressure (Listening Heat, Vent Haze, Softfall). Life Kinds still sort who is animal, creature, people, or ambiguous inside a habitat.
