# Folklore Teaching Myths of Dias

## Overview

**Folklore Teaching Myths of Dias** indexes recent and classic teaching tales by lesson—soft limits, craft caution, joy, place jokes—without reprinting myth text or saga beats.

## Core Premise

Myths teach. Use them as manners props; status often stays **rumor** on purpose.
