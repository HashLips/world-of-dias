# Restricted Rare and Taboo Inventions of Dias

## Overview

**Restricted Rare and Taboo Inventions of Dias** is the Volume V.9 sheet: which inventions are dangerous, restricted, rare, expensive, or socially taboo—and who bans or sells anyway.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

Social enforcement matters. Restriction can be care or power-hoarding. Taboo exists because some devices wound rooms, memories, or waters faster than laws catch up.

Detail culture: [Restricted and Taboo Tech](../cultures/restricted-and-taboo-tech.md).

## Adventurer warning card

If packing inventions:

1. Everyday wonder OK (ladle manners, chalk, share-knife).  
2. Personal tech only if licensed holder story.  
3. Restricted = plot hassle, not loot flex.  
4. Taboo = room/well/undo wound—write consequences.  
5. Never treat No-Ferry marks as tickets.
