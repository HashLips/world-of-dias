# Objective and Unreliable Maps of Dias

## Overview

**Objective and Unreliable Maps of Dias** teaches the difference between geography that holds under many witnesses and maps inhabitants make that are partial, motivated, kind-wrong, or simply out of date.

## Core Premise

### Plain definitions

| Kind | Definition | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Objective geography** | What multiple honest travelers can re-find: coasts, basins, named gates, fruit-road spines | Nauw east of Wabet; Long Gate into Hearthvale; Veloria in the Basin |
| **Survey / archive chart** | Best-effort official or House survey; may still miss interiors | Early F432 Survey Map; F432 Map V2 lineage |
| **Regional close-up** | Lived region chart (Map of Nauw / Wabet / Sorel) | Trade and landform emphasis |
| **Settlement sketch** | Walkable local plate | Veloria Ring Walk Sketch |
| **Partial map** | Honest gaps left blank or half-inked | The Half-Made Atlas |
| **Motivated map** | Drawn to sell, claim, or flatter a House | Toll-favor ink; Flagweek allegiance overlays |
| **Kind-wrong / mercy map** | Deliberately wrong to shelter people | Quiet Well map-wrongness |
| **Sailor / rumor chart** | Useful locally; dangerous if treated as law | Outer Rim fog songs as “maps” |

### Teaching rule (plain)

1. **Geography** is not identical to **any single sheet of ink**.  
2. Wrong maps can be manners (Quiet Well)—not proof that coasts float.  
3. Correcting a mercy map is not how you “find” Frequency Zero.  
4. Political borders on cloth are weather; landforms under feet are slower.

### How a clerk grades a map

| Question | If yes… |
| --- | --- |
| Can two Softfruit hosts walk it the same way? | Lean objective |
| Does it hide a well or lie about a road for shelter? | Mercy wrong—keep peacetime honesty later |
| Does it promote a whisper to a harbor? | Motivated—stamp claim-grade down |
| Is the interior Averra or deep Fellgrove off-path blank? | Partial—honest blank beats fake streets |

## Global Lore

Maps and Wrong Maps of Dias indexes mercy cartography and chart lineage. Atlas Map Levels of Dias covers realm / regional / settlement consistency. Soft Limits still apply.

## Notes

Wonder stays: incomplete maps are part of Dias. Unreliable does not mean “nothing is real.”
