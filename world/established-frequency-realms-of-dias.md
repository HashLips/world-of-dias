# Established Frequency Realms of Dias

## Overview

**Established Frequency Realms of Dias** names the indexed bands currently solid enough to teach: not a claim that the catalog is complete, only that these eight can be described with fundamental differences.

## Core Premise

Each established realm is a vibrational layer of Dias with its own expectations for matter, mood, meaning-physics, and life. F432 is home. The others are neighbors in tone—not countries down a road.

### Indexed set (teachable)

| Band | Plain name | Fundamental difference (one line) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F120 | Echo band | Near-return without exact loop |
| F200 | Luminosity pole | Coherence-as-substance; held radiance |
| F380 | Hearttide | Emotion coupled to environmental climate |
| F432 | Home band | Material-like stability that still resonates |
| F500 | Null pole | Absence that weighs; anti-scenery |
| F610 | Reveal veil | Timed disclosure, then opacity |
| F840 | Ember quiet | Aftermath continuity / recovery |
| F960 | Unfinished band | Iterative becoming; revisable form |

Detail lives in each realm entry’s **Unlike the others** section. Comparison craft: Frequency Difference Primer of Dias. Feel-map glance: Frequency Bands Orientation of Dias.

### What “established” does not mean

- Complete exploration
- Safe travel
- Closed count of all frequencies
- Frequency Zero as a ninth mapped realm

Claim grade for “these eight exist as named layers” sits between observation and law in living practice—acted on as reliable designations while the wider catalog stays theory-open.

## Global Lore

F432 institutions treat F200/F500 as older named poles and F120/F380/F610/F840 as quieter ledger arrivals. Folk speech often knew the feelings before the numbers. Softfruit and Houses both warn against drawing any of them as islands on fruit-road charts.

Structure of Dias keeps roles and anomalies. This sheet keeps the roster honest and teachable.
