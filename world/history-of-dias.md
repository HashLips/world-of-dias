# History of Dias

## Overview

**History of Dias** is a past deep enough to cast shadows—without locking every mystery. The present should feel like it inherited unfinished business.

## Core Premise

Cosmic history stays perforated. Regional histories carry more lived certainty. Separate **known**, **disputed**, and **forgotten**. Soft era markers live in [`Known Eras and Markers`](known-eras-and-markers.md).

Full entries below—indexes alone are not enough.

---

## Timeline spine (soft)

```
Prime Age (unified) → Fracture (cause unknown) → Present Age frequencies settle
        → Prime Age echoes / relics persist
        → F432 civic memory thickens (gates, rings, roads, Compacts)
        → Regional recent histories (Long Gate, Keep customs, Freebands…)
        → Ledger waves / echo-rise seasons (near-present observational)
```

Cross-frequency “wars” and Distant Companies remain rumor-grade after-echo, not completed chronicle.
