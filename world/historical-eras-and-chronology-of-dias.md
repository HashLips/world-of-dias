# Historical Eras and Chronology of Dias

## Overview

**Historical Eras and Chronology of Dias** is the Volume VI.1 teachable era sequence—stable names and order without fake precise calendars.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

Dias has **soft chronology**. Eras answer “which age?” Civic time answers “which season of ordinary life?” Do not pin the Fracture to a numbered year.

Detail markers: [Known Eras and Markers](known-eras-and-markers.md). Lived rhythm: [F432 Civic Time](../cultures/f432-civic-time.md).

## Era sequence (teachable)

| Order | Era | `time_era` value | What it means | Certainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Prime Age** | `prime` | Unified reality before Fracture | Known as concept; daily life forgotten |
| 2 | **The Fracture** | `fracture` | Foundational break; cause unknown | Event known; cause disputed |
| 3 | **Settling Present** | `settling` | Early Present: frequencies/bands take shape | Soft; after Fracture, before thick civic memory |

| 6 | **Near-present** | `near` | Echo-rise / ledger-wave observational seasons | Contested / recent |

## How to date a scene (without fake months)

| Need | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Cosmic age | Prime Age / Present Age / “after Fracture” |
| Regional chapter | Long Gate era; ring-expansion talk; Keep customs already old |

| Near-saga present | Echo-rise season; ledger waves—as observation, not lab clock |
