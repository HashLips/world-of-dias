# The Harmonic Saga — Movement Three

## Overview

**Movement Three** follows Calen's witness network into **map revision, institutional schism, and a second Bridge echo**—closing with love and hope while the cosmos stays open.

## Movement Question

**When maps and institutions both revise themselves, who do you trust to tell you what is real?**

Answer across Movement Three: **trust practice among people**—tea, stamps, open hands, timecoded fear—not any single line or crown.
