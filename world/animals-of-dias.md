# Animals of Dias

## Overview

**Animals of Dias** indexes the **ordinary and beloved** living layer—companions, work beasts, food animals, pests, gentle wilds, and seasonal visitors. Strange predators and existential horrors are listed lightly here and deepened under Creatures of Dias; full files live in `inhabitants/`.

**Life kind:** this hub is the **ordinary animal** shelf of Life Kinds of Dias. Peoples are not filed here. Ambiguous sky/gallery life is not forced into pet taxonomy.

## Core Premise

A child in Veloria should have a normal animal life: Lap-Cats on sills, Ringroad Dogs on circuits, Grin-Pigs under stalls, Spring-Swallows when the Fields wake. Wonder can be gentle. Most animals here are loveable before they are dangerous.

### Role key

| Role | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Companion** | Pets, trail friends, hearth company |
| **Work** | Haul, watch, dairy, relay, herd aid |
| **Food** | Raised or harvested as sustenance (ethics vary) |
| **Pest** | Annoying, usefully hated, sometimes necessary |
| **Gentle wild** | Not owned; still kind to encounter |
| **Seasonal** | Arrival marks civic or emotional time |
| **Omen-adjacent** | Ordinary body, charged reading (still animal) |

## Child-in-Veloria test (exit criteria)

A Veloria childhood can include:

1. A **Lap-Cat** on the sill  
2. A **Ringroad Dog** that knows your street  
3. **Grin-Pigs** under the food row  
4. **Banner Moths** in fair week  
5. **Spring-Swallows** news from Glasswater cousins  
6. Respect for **Pocket-Mice** and hatred of **Rust-Rats**  

That is enough animal life to feel at home before any saga begins.

## Notes

Strange, feared, and magnificent beings beyond ordinary farm-and-pet life: [`Creatures of Dias`](creatures-of-dias.md). Prefer linking an indexed animal in scenes before inventing a new ordinary species.
