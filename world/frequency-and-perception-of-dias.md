# Frequency and Perception of Dias

## Overview

**Frequency and Perception of Dias** defines how sense and attention change under different frequency bands.

## Core Premise

### Plain definition

**Band-tuned perception** means: what you can reliably see, hear, rest attention on, or must stop attending depends on frequency layer. Perception is not a neutral camera dropped into every realm.

### What this is not

- Not permanent all-seeing
- Not ordinary market devices that “listen too well”
- Not proof that every glimpse is keepable memory (see Frequency and Memory of Dias)

F432: mixed civic sense. F200: coherence calm. F500: landmark starvation. F610: glance-seal windows. F380: climate-feel hearing. F840: warmth-corridor sight. F120: route rhyme. F960: sketchline terrain.

### Band perception habits (working expectations)

| Band | Perception habit | Manners |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F432 | Mixed-weather sense; ordinary wonder and unease | Softfruit tags before panic |
| F200 | Coherence eye calm; glare as alignment cue | Do not treat calm as ownership |
| F500 | Landmark starvation; anti-scenery | Retreat honesty over brave naming |
| F610 | Glance-seal; window truth | Anti-stare; phase stamps |
| F380 | Climate-feel hearing and color | Communal regulation, not solo spell |
| F840 | Warmth-corridor sight | Shelter first, monument later |
| F120 | Route rhyme in the eye and feet | Log drift; distrust “same as before” |
| F960 | Sketchline terrain; provisional edges | Name becoming without forced final |

### Soft limits on perception lore

No permanent all-seeing. No device that “listens too well” as ordinary market ware. Soft-ray watching distance rule and F610 Anti-Stare Manners are ethical cousins of this primer.

## Global Lore

Perception is band-tuned is the rule spine. Phenomena above are lived tells. Houses may Theory-stamp models; Softfruit keeps the first lesson sensory and kind.
