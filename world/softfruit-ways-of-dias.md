# Softfruit Ways of Dias

## Overview

**Softfruit Ways of Dias** is the primer for Softfruit as civic noticing culture—not only fruit vendors. Interviews, chapbooks, ladles, and keep-marks teach manners before stamps.

## Core Premise

Softfruit owns noticing on purpose. Houses may dislike the anti-classification vibe; streets keep the bowls.
