# Politics of Dias

## Overview

**Politics of Dias** is who decides, who pretends to decide, and who leaves. Power struggles need venues: gates, yards, hearths, ledgers.

## Core Premise

Nauw civic/gate authority, Wabet stewardship/restraint, and Sorel anti-authority/earned belonging are three political weathers in one realm. Soft power (galleries, ledgers, watches, tolls, song calendars) often bites harder than crowns. Cross-frequency politics is mostly rumor diplomacy. **Slow dark initiatives** exist as pressures—not full war canon.
