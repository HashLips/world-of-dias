# Personal Resonant Tech of Dias

## Overview

**Personal Resonant Tech of Dias** indexes pocket-scale echo-aware instruments—wafers, reeds, slates, sleeves—without pasting Earth phones onto F432.

## Core Premise

Personal tech exists near Veloria rings and licensed craft **if** failure modes stay mythic: wrong tick, sealed load, stare risk, null itch. Not app stores. Not band subways.
