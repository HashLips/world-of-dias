# Evidence Grades of History of Dias

## Overview

**Evidence Grades of History of Dias** is the Volume VI.2 sheet: how to classify event claims—documented, disputed, myth, unknown—using canon labels.

## Core Premise

### Grades (book labels)

| Grade | Meaning | Reader use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Documented / Known** | Lived agreement across many communities; records or shared practice | May treat as world furniture |
| **Disputed** | Strong stories, competing frames | Show quarrel; do not pick a silent winner |
| **Myth** | Encoded meaning; comfort/fright; not lab confirmation | Stage as tale with cultural force |
| **Unknown / Forgotten** | Lost, sealed, refused, or never settled | Name the gap; do not invent fill |

Culture detail: [Known Disputed Forgotten](../cultures/known-disputed-forgotten.md).

## Authoring test

Before hardening a past event into Law:

1. Can three regions agree without House coercion? → Documented lean  
2. Do honest sources disagree? → Disputed  
3. Does it teach survival/belonging more than chronology? → Myth lean  
4. Would solving it close Zero / ferry / taxonomy soft limits? → Keep Unknown
