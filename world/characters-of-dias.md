# Characters of Dias

## Overview

**Characters of Dias** indexes living people who open doors into the world—ordinary-majority casting (Harold principle), not destiny factories. Historical-only names live in [`Historical Figures of Dias`](historical-figures-of-dias.md).

## Tag key

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **saga-ready** | Can carry or turn a saga chapter |
| **locale color** | Makes a place feel inhabited |
| **bridge** | Connects regions, kinds, or labs of life |
| **joyful anchor** | Emotional rest without being powerless fluff |
| **antagonist-with-reasons** | Blocks with understandable motive |
| **child / elder** | Age-door entry points |

Do not force every character into destiny.
