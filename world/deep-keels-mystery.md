# Deep-Keels Mystery

## Overview

**Deep-Keels Mystery** asks whether Outer Rim Deep-Keels are beasts, geology asleep, or both—and how they relate to human-eyed whales—without settling the reef.

## What life it currently affects

- Night anchor bans on “song-reefs”  
- Fleet routes and loft hail weather  
- Tide-song custodianship manners  
- Children’s maps that mark false islands
