# Manners Before Stamps of Dias

## Overview

**Manners Before Stamps of Dias** is a cross-cutting primer: feel, share, scrape, ask again—then stamp. It braids Softfruit, gates, publish ethics, and anti-stare habits into one orientation sheet.

## Core Premise

Stamps are useful. Stamps first are often Forced-Final manners wearing ink.
