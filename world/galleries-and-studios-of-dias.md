# Galleries and Studios of Dias

## Overview

**Galleries and Studios of Dias** indexes looking-as-craft—Averra damp studios, open curation, beauty-between-lines manners—without dumping Harmonic gallery plot.

## Core Premise

A frame is manners toward unfinished looking. Forced-Final varnish is bad craft.
