# Resonance Can and Cannot of Dias

## Overview

**Resonance Can and Cannot of Dias** is the judgment hub for proposed resonant feats. It answers: what resonance reliably does, what it costs, what fails, and what is forbidden or taboo—so a book scene does not treat the law as wish-magic.

## Core Premise

### Plain definitions

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Resonance** | How things participate in frequency—how matter, bodies, tools, crowds, habits, and places answer one another |
| **Echo** | Partial remainder left after that answering |
| **Resonant feat** | A deliberate attempt to use resonance or echo for a result (read, seal, carry, amplify, soothe, classify, refuse) |
| **Succeed** | The result happens within craft, cost, and soft limits |
| **Fail** | The attempt does not get the claimed result (wrong tool, unreadiness, partial only, sour room) |
| **Forbidden / taboo** | Cultures and soft limits refuse the attempt as ordinary practice—even if someone tries |

### What resonance can do (ordinary and craft)

Resonance **can**:

1. Leave remainder after use, speech, craft, fear, love, and long habit
2. Let skilled readers take **partial** readings (craft, not omniscience)
3. Let seals **hide access** more often than annihilate remainder
4. Let tools and places keep yesterday’s pace or mood (kitchen, gate, road scale)
5. Amplify or reveal under rare rises and prepared instruments—still partial, still costly
6. Shape band habit in matter, life, memory, perception, and environments (see Frequency Effects of Dias)

### What resonance cannot do (working expectations)

Resonance **cannot**, as ordinary reliable practice:

1. Grant total knowledge of a person, place, or age on demand
2. Provide a ferry / subway between frequency realms
3. Locally repeal “everything resonates” as a convenience switch
4. Casually rewrite one realm into another as a completed conversion
5. Solve Frequency Zero as household inventory
6. Grant wishes without craft, consent, cost, or remainder (see Resonance does not grant wishes)

### Costs (even when a feat “works”)

Every serious feat pays something: load, sour rooms, misfile risk, stare-cost, social debt, scraped correction, or sealed access that later people must live with. See Resonance feats have costs.

### Quick judgment table (use this in scenes)

| Proposed feat | Likely judgment | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Softfruit host notices table warmth and adjusts manners | **Succeed** | Ordinary remainder + craft manners |
| Junior clerk files a Partial Echo Slash and refuses Forced-Final | **Succeed** | Soft limit honored; craft correct |
| Market seller offers “complete reading of your life” | **Fail / taboo** | Soft limit: no casual omniscience |
| Device claims to wipe Frequency Zero into a room | **Taboo / mystery abuse** | Zero unfinished; not a tool |
| Ticket sells passage F432 → F200 as commute | **Fail + taboo comedy/danger** | No-Ferry; exceptional crossing only |
| Ember Keeper names a cold gap and keeps kettle rounds | **Succeed** | Recovery continuity; scar kept |
| Forced-Final clamp declares scar “gone” overnight | **Fail / forbidden in recovery** | Erases care; soft-limit cousin |
| Veil Scribe glances, phase-stamps, stops | **Succeed** | Timed truth; anti-stare |
| Stare to keep a Glassfold window forever open | **Fail + cost** | Windows reseal; stare-cost strain |
| Unconsented memory seal on a person | **Taboo** | Restricted and Taboo Tech |

## Global Lore

Soft Limits of Dias maps the refusal list. Resonance behavior soft limits is the binding rule spine. Resonance Feat Sorting Customs and the Resonance Feat Sorting Chapbook teach Softfruit and junior House judgment. Everyday Resonant Tech shows succeed-scale tools; Restricted and Taboo Tech shows forbidden classes.

## Notes

Does not invent new metaphysics beside Everything resonates. Does not solve Zero. Does not grant ferry tickets.
