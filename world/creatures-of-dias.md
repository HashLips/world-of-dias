# Creatures of Dias

## Overview

**Creatures of Dias** classifies strange, feared, and magnificent beings beyond ordinary farm-and-pet life. It does not replace `inhabitants/` files. It teaches what to **hope to see** and what to **pray not to meet**—without turning Dias into a meat-grinder.

Ordinary beloved animals live primarily in [`Animals of Dias`](animals-of-dias.md).

## Core Premise

Every notable creature should reveal a **law** (silence, stillness, resonance, null) or a **social wound** (exile roads, migration fear, hunt-vs-kinship). Magnificence and dread both belong.

### Classification

| Class | Meaning | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wonder** | Awe, beauty, seasonal spectacle; risk secondary | Mega Butterfly, Soft-Bears, Banner Moths, Choir-Frogs |
| **Hazard** | Real bodily danger in lived F432 (and analogs) | Redmile Striders, Quietwell Serpents, Dune-Drakes, Siren-Eels |
| **Rumor** | Strong culture, thin proof | Long-Gate Beast, Sky-Turtles, Stillhollow Mutes |
| **Existential** | Threatens meaning, memory, horizon—not just flesh | Horizon-Nulls, Nullmouth Shades, Watching Absence, Forget-Texture Worms |

Many beings wear two classes (Mega Butterfly = wonder + hazard).

## Authoring rule

Prefer promoting and deepening an indexed creature before inventing a new monster. If a region still lacks dread, add one that teaches that region’s law—not a random teeth catalog.

## Notes

Exit test: a reader can name three things they want to witness and three they hope never find them.
