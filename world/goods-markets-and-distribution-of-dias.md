# Goods Markets and Distribution of Dias

## Overview

**Goods Markets and Distribution of Dias** is the Volume V.7 sheet: how trade, currency, markets, manufacturing, and distribution work so a merchant plot can price, move, and sell from canon.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

Markets should smell and sound like places. Price in **VEL + local tender**. Move on **fruit road / toll / ferry / salvage**. Sell with **manners** (rank, hush, kettle, ribbon).

Regional law/trade contrast (IV.7): [Laws Trade and Regional Economies of Dias](laws-trade-and-regional-economies-of-dias.md).
