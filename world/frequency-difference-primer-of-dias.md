# Frequency Difference Primer of Dias

## Overview

**Frequency Difference Primer of Dias** teaches how to tell established bands apart by **fundamental behavior**—matter, mood, physics-of-meaning, life—not by paint color alone.

## Core Premise

Aesthetic difference is cheap. Fundamental difference is what Frequency as fundamental law demands: when you hear a designation, some expectations should arrive.

### Quick contrasts (pairs people confuse)

| Confused pair | How they differ |
| --- | --- |
| F200 vs F380 | Light-coherence vs emotion-climate; serene alignment ≠ hearttide weather |
| F500 vs night in F432 | Null anti-scenery vs ordinary darkness that still has streets |
| F840 vs F500 | Recovery warmth/continuity vs hungry hush |
| F120 vs F960 | Near-return rhyme vs wet-paint revision of form |
| F610 vs “always knowing” | Brief windows vs permanent omniscience (forbidden soft-limit fantasy) |
| F432 vs “all of Dias” | Home band vs world-container |

### Teaching order Softfruit likes

1. F432 — here
2. F200 / F500 — bright and null poles
3. F960 — becoming
4. F120 / F380 / F610 / F840 — quieter ledger bands
5. Always: realm ≠ country

## Global Lore

Band Confusion Corrections and the Eight-Band Difference Chapbook carry this primer into markets and junior rooms. Wrong maps fail this primer first by turning difference into tourism.
