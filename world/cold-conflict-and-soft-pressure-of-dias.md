# Cold Conflict and Soft Pressure of Dias

## Overview

**Cold Conflict and Soft Pressure of Dias** indexes how F432 argues without defaulting to war—stamps, silence, rumor, toll pressure, and Distant Companies weather kept unsettled.

## Core Premise

Pressure should thin happiness zones carefully. Wonder outranks war-as-default. Full conflict entries hold detail; this sheet is the soft-pressure glance.
