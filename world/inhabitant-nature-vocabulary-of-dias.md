# Inhabitant Nature Vocabulary of Dias

## Overview

**Inhabitant Nature Vocabulary of Dias** maps the archive `nature:` field on inhabitant files to the teachable Life Kinds—so clerks and Softfruit hosts do not invent a second taxonomy in slang alone.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

`nature:` answers **what kind of living presence is this entry?** It does not answer frequency (that is `region` / nest) and does not replace `status:` (canonical / rumor / myth / unknown / legendary).

### Preferred vocabulary → life kind

| Prefer `nature:` | Maps to life kind | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `animal` | Ordinary animal | Prefer when clearly farm/pet/pest without marvel first |
| `creature` | Ordinary animal *or* extraordinary creature | Use Creatures / Animals hubs to disambiguate in prose |
| `person` / `human` | People | Named individuals and ordinary persons |
| race / people labels (`humanoid race`, `people / hosts`, etc.) | Peoples | Keep specific labels when established; still table-test as people |
| `echoform` | Ambiguous / special | Not farm fauna |
| `unknown` / `contested / unknown` | Ambiguous | Prefer honesty over forced creature |
| `otherworldly being` / `energy being` | Extraordinary or ambiguous | Pair with Creatures class or leave ambiguous |
| `historical figure` | People (past) | Not a species |

### Filing habits

1. Prefer short stable words (`person`, `creature`, `animal`, `unknown`) for new files when a long custom label is not already canon.  
2. Do not change an established custom `nature:` without human request—enrich with Life Kinds language in Overview instead.  
3. `status: rumor` + `nature: creature` is valid (Forest Listeners).  
4. Peoples must not be refiled as `creature` for convenience.

### Cross-check

If unsure, open Life Kinds of Dias sorting rules (table / collar / law / honesty tests).
