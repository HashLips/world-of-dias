# The Calibration Saga

## Name

The Calibration Saga

## Overview

The Calibration Saga follows **Orrin Veld** from Calibration Row craft and Softfruit interviews through the building of the **Spectrum Frame**, the arrival of **the Caliburn**, a partial publish of spectrum truth, a failed vault—and the Echoform’s escape into Veloria’s ignored noise.

It does not replace **The Harmonic Saga** or **The Lumen Saga**. It teaches frequency as **method** beside gift and hunger.

## Core Premise

**If you can finally graph the stack, what were you inviting by looking that carefully?**

The saga answers with instruments, mischief, institutional filing, and an open haunt—not with Frequency Zero solved, and not with a subway between bands.

## Protagonist

**Orrin Veld** — quirky Calibration Row–adjacent scientist; Operator-adjacent; publisher of partial truth; haunted steward after the Caliburn escapes.

## What happens (spoiler summary)

Plain arc for readers who want the plot without the poetry. Soft limits stay soft: no Frequency Zero answer, no Frederick crossing outcome, no subway between bands.

**Movement One — Map the Stack**

- Orrin argues that honest frequency measurement belongs in graphs, not House stamps, and vows a window (graph) not a door (ferry).
- Softfruit vendor interviews become folk field data—the basin already notices quiet band weather.
- He teaches F200 / F500 as loud dramas and F432 as home; quiet background bands come next.
- Bram’s Supplemental Leaf names F120, F380, F610, F840, and disputed F960.
- Vela’s napkin and Kesh’s scrap show Operators file civic wonder as shaped “noise.”
- Flashback: Orrin refuses Frederick a ferry/door; teaches signatures only; files the hunger for later.

**Movement Two — Triumph and Mischief**

- Orrin builds the Spectrum Frame: graphs band signatures from F432 home.
- Graphs agree too well—background bands proven on residual paper.
- A residual calibration seam (leftover reveal brightness) will not close after the run.
- The Caliburn appears helpful in the Frame glass (Echoform, amber slit).
- Half-digit lies and cyan tells show the helpfulness is steering him; Lune offers Null coin.
- House/Operator meeting files the matter nonactionable despite a living roommate.

**Movement Three — Publish, Escape, Haunt**

- The Caliburn probes leaving-band hunger (Frederick residue) and urges widen.
- Widening = extending the residual run / forcing longer reveal—not a subway; Orrin refuses.
- Napkin overlay proves the nest was already in Operator load-noise; measurement was a porch light.
- Issa + Orrin publish a partial spectrum packet (Softfruit → loud dramas → background → soft limits; no Zero answer).
- Vault latch fails; spool blackens; Caliburn escapes alcove → conduit → Drop Wall noise.
- Orrin keeps measuring with an intentionally incomplete Frame; Caliburn at large.
- Optional: ask-again stamps at the Visitor Queue Ledge after escape.
