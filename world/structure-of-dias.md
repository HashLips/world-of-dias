# Structure of Dias

## Overview

**Structure of Dias** is the prose map-of-maps: how the known frequency realms sit in relation to one another, what role each plays, and how F432 people vs institutions know any of it. It complements [`Cosmology of Dias`](cosmology-of-dias.md) (laws and mysteries) without replacing realm entries.

## Core Premise

Dias is one container. Inside it, **frequency realms** are not countries on a flat chart. They are **layers of resonance** that can share cosmic space while remaining normally separate. Geography words (west, road, sea) work best **inside** a realm—especially inside lived F432. Between realms, the honest words are closer to **nearness of tone**, **bleed**, **omen**, and **refusal**.

### Structure diagram (prose)

```text
DIAS (world-container)
│
├── Indexed frequency realms (known enough to name)
│   ├── F432  home band — material-like stability, cities, trade, sagas
│   │     ├── Wabet (west)
│   │     ├── Nauw (north-east)
│   │     └── Sorel (south-east)
│   ├── F200  luminosity — pure light / coherence (contrast pole to F500)
│   ├── F500  null / abyss — absence pressure (contrast pole to F200)
│   ├── F960  unfinished — iterative becoming / revision
│   ├── F120  echo — near-return, recurrence with drift
│   ├── F380  hearttide — emotion coupled to climate
│   ├── F610  reveal — brief windows of hidden structure
│   └── F840  ember quiet — aftermath continuity / recovery
│
├── Structural anomalies (features of the stack, not separate realms)
│   ├── Distant lumen-abyss bleed (F432 notices light/null residue)
│   ├── Unfinished edge drift (F960 margins breathe)
│   ├── Returning Span + Quartermark Drift (F120 route/time texture)
│   └── …other local phenomena inside realms
│
└── Open beyond the index
      ├── Uncharted frequencies (unknown count)
      └── Frequency Zero (named absence — not a mapped realm)
```

This diagram is **orientation**, not a claim that the catalog is complete.

## Global Lore

### Structural roles of known realms

| Realm | Role in the stack | What it feels like from F432 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **F432** | **Home band** — lived baseline for most stories: roads, fruit, gates, exile, laughter | “Here.” Solid enough to love; incomplete enough to wonder. |
| **F200** | **Luminosity pole** — coherence, held radiance, energy-life | Golden rumor, second-dawn jokes, serene envy, instrument glow |
| **F500** | **Null pole** — absence, anti-scenery, Unraveled fear-talk | Cold pockets, careful naming, tavern dread, sealed ledgers |
| **F960** | **Becoming band** — incompletion as law; craft and revision | Wet-paint metaphors, half-made maps, maker pilgrimage talk |
| **F120** | **Echo band** — near-return without perfect loops | “Same road, not the same day”; doubled stories |
| **F380** | **Hearttide band** — mood as weather | Gatherings that change a place; healer and poet interest |
| **F610** | **Reveal band** — timed disclosure, then opacity again | Investigation lore: see truly, not long |
| **F840** | **Ember quiet** — what remains and can be rebuilt | Respectful storytelling; recovery as proof endings aren’t final |

**Emotional cosmic seat of F432:** between light and abyss without being either. F432 is where people **build tables** under a sky that sometimes bruises with someone else’s war of tone.

Internal icons (not separate realms): **Aurel Meridian** (F200 spine), **Returning Span** (F120), **Bloomline Estuary** (F380), **Glassfold Ledge** (F610), **Ashen Hearthline** (F840), **The Unfinished** (F960). F500 resists friendly landmarks; its “geography” is warning shape.

### How people know vs how institutions know

| Channel | What it carries | Trust habit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Street / harbor / road** | Songs, sailor sketches, wrong-sky nights, children’s moth lore, gate arguments | High feeling, low policy weight |
| **Markets & galleries** | Rumors priced as entertainment; maps sold as art | Useful for mood; dangerous as sole evidence |
| **Civic / trade institutions** | VEL logistics, tolls, gate rules, partial indexes tagged by confidence | Act on F432 facts; treat other bands as **context**, not treaty partners |
| **Resonant Houses & sealed channels** | Echo classifications, hazard language, Zero-adjacent filing | Record more than they release |
| **Supplemental ledgers** | Names like F120/F380/F610/F840 arriving after F200/F500 hooks | Circulating knowledge ≠ settled governance |

**F432 partial knowledge protocol** is the civic spine of this split: witnessed fact, copied claim, and symbolic reading must stay labeled. That is why F432 can feel cosmically aware and still refuse to govern F500 as a neighbor state.

### Travel rarity (between realms)

- **Inside F432:** ordinary—carts, ferries, bridges, desert silence routes, exile roads.
- **Between frequencies:** exceptional. Not a subway. When passage is claimed, cultures reach for omen, sealed craft, accident, or lie.
- Soft limit: realm character holds; bleed can bruise a sky without relocating a city into another band.

### Structural anomalies (features, not dump lore)

These are **joints and weather of the stack**—already documented; listed here so structure stays visible:

1. **Distant lumen-abyss bleed** — F432’s intermittent notice of F200/F500-scale residue (wrong light, absence-cold, ear pressure). Proof of imperfect sealing, not a tour brochure.
2. **Unfinished edge drift** — F960 margins that migrate by increments; outsiders panic, locals breathe with it.
3. **Returning Span / Quartermark Drift** — F120’s cartographic and timing near-return made walkable and schedulable-with-error.
4. **Half-Made Atlas / partial indexes** — documents that behave like structure under strain: knowledge that redraws, glosses that arrive before contact.

Anomalies should appear in stories as **texture and pressure**, not as a new cosmology each time.
