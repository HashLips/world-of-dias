# Peoples Physical Traits of Dias

## Overview

**Peoples Physical Traits of Dias** is the teachable comparison of major intelligent peoples: body, signature abilities, and hard limits—so kinds stay distinct without Earth race-tag shortcuts or monster framing.

## Core Premise

### Plain rule

A people entry must answer: **what do they look like**, **what can they do that neighbors cannot casually copy**, and **what they cannot do** (cost, taboo, or body limit). Personhood stays practiced (Peoples of Dias); this sheet is anatomy and capacity.

### F432 compact seven

| People | Body tell | Ability (limited) | Hard limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Humans** | Bipedal variation; culture-dress loud | Civic generalists; rebuild and argue fast | No innate signature power; must train what others are born into |
| **Axiomorphs** | Geometric / lattice / facet assemblies | Pattern continuity; precise systems sense | Hard to read emotionally; some cannot “smile”; hover/reassemble costs attention |
| **Veyrin** | Near-human + one trait marker | One stable signature trait each | Trait is narrow—not unlimited magic; spectacle without discipline is social failure |
| **Lineborn** | Near-2D / sketch-plane bodies | Edge perception; profile-sharp work | Side-on near-invisible; mistaken for illusion; not ghosts, not paper |
| **Kharad** | Dense mineral/metal-grain bodies | Load tolerance; long-horizon craft stamina | Slow in soft-step cultures unless trained; stereotype ignores artists |
| **Choirglass** | Faceted translucent / sound-light | Harmonic + refractive speech | Misread as oracles; single-channel listeners get lost; not automatic sages |
| **Palewind Swarms** | Mote veil / condensed silhouette | Partition and reconverge as one person | Clerks must not count motes as many citizens; anonymity myth is prejudice |

### Other established peoples (thin where needed)

### What this sheet refuses

- Ranking peoples by “power level”  
- Collapsing Lineborn into ghosts or Palewind into vermin  
- Using Earth ethnic tags as Dias kinds  
- Promoting Unraveled / Distant Companies into this roster without plan
