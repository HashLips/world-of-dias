# Vitrin Saga — Reading Path

Fifth long narrative journey of Dias. Follows **Lark Venn**, a known freight pilot, and **Dain Mercer**, a successful Veloria fruit distributor, from a lent landing strip in rural **Wabet** to a bar in **Nauw** and back again. The public load is fruit. The hull does not always give fruit back in a shape the market knows.

**Plot in plain talk (new readers):**

Lark flies **The Late Bruise** for Dain. People are told the crates are Softfruit and market fruit from a field **Yarrow Bine** lent, bound for Nauw and Veloria distribution.
One day fruit goes into the hull and what comes out does not look like fruit, though something is carried.
The whisper fills **The Peel and Bell**. The drinks there do strange small things. None of them is a way between frequencies.
The whisper gets so loud that Lark and Dain come in and call it fruit. Dain hands out tickets to a show.
Next day, under a cloth, he shows **Vitrin**, a glass-looking breed no one in Nauw or F432 has seen, grown by **Sella Orrow**, who mixed certain fruits and does not teach the mix.
He eats a piece and goes unseen, walks the crowd, and lifts hats. People cheer and are afraid. He auctions eight. The tree yielded about ten. He eats one and keeps one. The prices hurt in VEL marks.
The reveal is the glow, not the end. The unseen does not wear off cleanly. He is furious.
He wants to do to the farmer what was done to him. They fly back. He puts the kept fruit into her stew.
The attempt lands on him. His invisibility worsens. Sella fades only at the edges, stays angry, and stays visible. She is not erased.
A last fruit is still on the tree. He wants it flown out as a secret. Lark will not. She stands in the bar and marks where the tree is.
Markets, wagons, and the lent field take the cost. How the mix works, and whether Dain ever fully returns to sight, stay open.

Flight in this saga is a private hire. It does not replace wagons, ponies, or oxen on the **Eastbound Fruit Road**. Take-without-restore and skipped waystations are already raid. **Sky Drifters** do not land and are not vehicles. No band ferry. No Frequency Zero answer. No Frederick Lumens crossing.
