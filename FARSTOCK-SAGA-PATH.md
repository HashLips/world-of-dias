# Farstock Saga — Reading Path

Fourth long narrative journey of Dias. Follows three outer-ring teenagers — **Joss Merrow**, **Harl Fenlow**, and **Rill Ostmark** — from **Farstock**, a myth-treated shop one street off Veloria’s market loop, down the real roads toward **Sorel**. They steal a Hearttide-leaning stone. What leaves the shop with them is not a purchase.

**Plot in plain talk (new readers):**

Pim Farstock does not sell spells or a feeling you can pocket.
The three try to buy one object, the **Heartlean Colorstone**. He refuses. Ease you can purchase is the brittle kind.
They sneak back and take it. Stolen, its color stands up as **the Lean Echo**, a ghostly frequency being.
The echo lures them south toward Sorel and, inside that true road, toward **F500**. It may know a way. It never explains how.
They never reach F500. Nobody in F432 learns a crossing.
They follow until the wonder turns, and the echo will not let them go.
Pim walks after them with a small party, finds them on the Long Gate, and they put the echo back into the stone.
The teens go home with something closer to a toy than a lure. The shop becomes talk. Adults get curious, not a map.

World identity: [`CORE-PILLARS.md`](CORE-PILLARS.md) · Spine: [`world/the-farstock-saga.md`](world/the-farstock-saga.md)

The place they walk toward is **Sorel** (south-eastern F432). Not “Sorrel,” and not a sorrow-country under another spelling.
