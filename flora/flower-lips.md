# Flower Lips

## Overview

**Flower Lips** is Aelwyn Canopy flora: a cornflower blade-bloom with a yellow core kept in a small black vase and talked about beside an orange measure-triangle, teaching mouths to space words like petals.

## Description

The bloom’s petals are sharp and angular rather than soft; the yellow core is the part keepers watch for readiness. Red living lines in plates show how stem-talk connects core to vase and to the orange word-measure used at benches. A navy silhouette in teaching plates is the practicing guest, not a required deity. Pale peach fallen pieces mean spent speech-petals—sweep them, do not hoard. Range is canopy talk-sides and quiet courts. Pulling the bloom to silence someone is the extractive twin of a forced apology.

## Local Use

Talk-bench courtesy flora, apology pacing, and children’s “one petal, one sentence” games. Match etiquette: measure, then speak; keep the vase watered.
