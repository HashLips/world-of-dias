# Farming Streets

## Overview

**Farming Streets** is Aelwyn Canopy path-flora: cream growing ground between black dashed travel lines, trained by thin yellow guide-lines so road-edge crops feed walkers without choking the way.

## Description

Canopy keepers describe the flora less as a single plant and more as a street ecology—pale cream medium that holds moisture after drip, dark road beds that must stay passable, and yellow living or corded guide-lines used to train upright growth along edges. Plates show a blue caretaker silhouette whose limbs touch those lines; the local rule is that yellow guides crops and gait drills, never yanking a person like a toy. Harvest is edge-only: leave the center dashes clear. After rain the cream brightens and children are warned not to dig across a live guide-line. Range talk places it with canopy path etiquette more than with a single garden plot. Stripping a whole street-stand for souvenir “farm art” is extractive craft the canopy refuses.

## Local Use

Seasonal edge greens, path courtesy plantings, and teaching lines for apprentices learning not to block a road with enthusiasm. Match etiquette: farm the margins; keep the dashes walkable.
