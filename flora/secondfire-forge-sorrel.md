# Secondfire Forge-Sorrel

## Name

Secondfire Forge-Sorrel

## Overview

Tart sorrel of Secondfire forge edges—cuts grease of kiln meals.

## Description

**Look:** bright tart leaves; red veins; heat-tolerant  
**Where:** Secondfire courtyard edges, kiln shade  
**Habit:** likes warm grit  
**Tell:** forge sorrel—not Tubehold grease-cress

## Local Use

**Edible.** Tart finish on stew. Share near teaching fires.

Plant Use Classes: edible.
