# Rose

## Name

Rose

## Overview

**Rose** is Sorel flora: white straight stems with indigo thorns and pointed white leaves, bell heads split magenta-pink and deep red, often edged in cyan vibration fringe on blue telling weather.

## Description

Path-keepers notice Rose when the white skeleton stems stand stark and the bells show both magenta and deep red at once. Tiny indigo thorn marks are part of the honesty—handle with care. The cyan/magenta fringe in plates is treated as optical resonance around the plant, not as paint error. Gatherers may take what etiquette allows; stripping a stand bare is poor local care. Resonance is the split petal; memory is the thorn tally; wonder is blue field making white glow. Not filed as an Earth garden rose. Indexed toward Flora of Dias as Sorel path-mark growth.
