# Tubehold Grease-Cress

## Name

Tubehold Grease-Cress

## Overview

Bitter cress of Tubehold pipe seepage—eaten by watchers who trust bitterness more than sweet lies about water.

## Description

**Look:** dark bitter leaves; oil sheen possible; small yellow flowers that look embarrassed  
**Where:** Tubehold dam seeps, warm pipe margins, Glasswater ag-channel habitat edge  
**Habit:** loves warm damp infrastructure  
**Tell:** bitter first—if sweet, wrong plant or wrong joke

## Local Use

**Edible (careful).** Bitter salad for night watch. Wash; do not eat from chalked “do not forage” seeps.

## Notes

Plant Use Classes: edible / observed.
