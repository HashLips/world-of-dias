# Raining Flowers

## Name

Raining Flowers

## Overview

**Raining Flowers** is an Aelwyn Canopy growth: white pointed petals around bright red centers, thin green stems that trail in air, drifting through cerulean until they settle on sage dunes with a dark ground shadow.

## Description

Under the canopy the blossoms are known less as bush flowers and more as a fall. While aloft they turn every way, stems like ribbons, red centers stark on blue. When one lands, the shadow goes almost black and the stem may curve on the olive ground. Locals gather settled blooms for quiet marks and path gratitude; pulling a still-floating flower out of the air is considered rough care. Size differences in a single fall are normal—near and far, not competing kinds. Resonance is the red against cerulean; memory is the first grounded shadow; wonder is how many can fill a sky without bruising it. The plant is not filed as an Earth species. It is canopy weather-growth with a settling rule.
