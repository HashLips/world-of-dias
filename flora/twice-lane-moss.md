# Twice-Lane Moss

## Name

Twice-Lane Moss

## Overview

**Twice-Lane Moss** grows in paired patches along the Returning Span’s lanes. Same green, twice, and never quite the same edge. It is F120’s else-green: a plant that teaches difference by refusing to be a single fact. Walkers read it when they are too proud to read a slip. The moss does not care about their pride. After rain it tells on the road anyway.

## Description

Low velvet cushions, cool under the hand, with a second flush that appears offset by about a handspan once the rain has had its say. Color shifts half a tone between passes, the kind of shift you deny until you have a stylus and then cannot deny. Crush a pinch and the scent is wet stone and tea, the same smell as the milestone rest, which makes newcomers think the plant is domesticated. It is only adjacent to people. In the Lane Offset Hush the pairs look like one organism if you are walking fast and like an argument if you stop. Fast is how you miss it. Stopping is the local agriculture of attention.

## Local Use

Refrain Walkers and Outer Lane Keepers use the pairs as gentle difference teachers for visitors who flinch at pegboards. A mild tea from careful harvest tastes like the crush-scent and does not sharpen or dull the mind into a vision. Overharvest is called certainty greed: taking so much paired green that the next pass has nothing to disagree with. The plant is not a loop drug, not a way to see yesterday, not a medicine for nostalgia. People who brew it hoping for a repeated hour get a cup of tea and a Keeper’s silence, which is the correct dose.

## Notes

Else-green of recurrence, not a pharmacopoeia of time. Leave the second cushion. It is the point.
