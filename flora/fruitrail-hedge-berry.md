# Fruitrail Hedge-Berry

## Name

Fruitrail Hedge-Berry

## Overview

Dark hedge berries along fruit-rail berms—jam for waystations, forage for foxes and children with scratched arms.

## Description

**Look:** small dark berries; thorny hedge; juice that stains like cheap ink  
**Where:** Eastbound Fruit Road hedges, waystation fences  
**Habit:** ripens Fruitcarry; birds argue first rights  
**Tell:** jam-sweet after cook; raw tart; thorns are the toll

## Local Use

**Edible forage / jam.** Share with fox etiquette jokes. Strip-hedge greed is road shame.

## Notes

Plant Use Classes: edible, commercial small.
