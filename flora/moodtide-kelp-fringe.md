# Moodtide Kelp Fringe

## Name

Moodtide Kelp Fringe

## Overview

Estuary fringe growth in F380 whose color and limp-or-lift habit tracks communal affect more than ordinary tide charts alone.

## Description

Moodtide Kelp Fringe lines Bloomline shoulders in soft ribbons that smell of mineral wet and faint sweetness when joy-weather holds. Tidehearts watch fringe before arguing with the sky. Shared lift can coax blades toward coral-green; grief-weather lays them quieter without killing the stand. The change is usually slow enough that a single shout does not dye a bank—crowd process does.

Solo gatherers who blame the plant for a private mood misread climate coupling. Stripping fringe to “fix the weather” is taboo guess and usually fails; the bank sulks dull and the crowd learns who cut first.

## Local Use

Hearttide attendants’ morning read. Shore teaching beside Moodtint Cloth Drape lessons. Harvest only with communal leave for food or cordage.

## Notes

Not a joke rainbow plant. Band Confusion Corrections apply when someone files it as crying scenery.
