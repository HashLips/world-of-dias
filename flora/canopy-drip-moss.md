# Canopy Drip-Moss

## Name

Canopy Drip-Moss

## Overview

Drip-moss that holds spare sky-water in Soft-Bear fork-nests—living cistern of the canopy.

## Description

**Look:** spongy bright moss; cold water squeeze; nest-lining  
**Where:** Aelwyn forks, Soft-Bear nests, drip-river spray  
**Habit:** stores Softfall; wrings dry in long Brightpass  
**Tell:** nest water—not Quiet Well

## Local Use

**Observed / water manners.** Soft-Bears first. Squeeze for emergency drink only with trail ethics.

## Notes

Ordinary; cultural soft.
