# Meridian Filament Grass

## Name

Meridian Filament Grass

## Overview

**Meridian Filament Grass** is a light-fiber growth that behaves like grass underfoot for Soft-Rays and for visitors who still have feet. F200 path texture without soil. It gives the procession hall a direction you can feel when staring would be rude. Dim patches worry watchers more than weeds would, because a weed is a surplus and a dim patch is a thinning of the peace.

## Description

Gold-white filaments in soft rows along the meridian’s ribs, cool and faintly glassy when brushed. They do not cast an ordinary shadow. The sound is distant glass, a chime that forgot to be an event. Visitors who kneel, which they should not, report the hand-feel of brushed silk, kin to the hall’s floorplane, as if the “grass” were the hall deciding to be touchable in strips. It parts for a quiet rib placed with manners and closes, slowly, when the rib is lifted. Spectacle burns it dull. Not fire. Attention of the wrong kind, the harvesting stare, leaves a bruise of dimness that watchers read as a health note about the guests, not about the filaments.

## Local Use

Procession aisles, so a newcomer can find the row without pointing at a Soft-Ray. Markers for where a quiet rib may sit. Rare pressed “sheaves” in F432 galleries, always under a no-ferry disclaimer, a souvenir of light that must not be narrated as a cutting from a realm you can visit by purchase. The grass is not harvested for fuel, dye, or proof of purity. Walking it lightly is the use. Standing on it to make a speech is how you learn the filaments can decline to hold you in their brightness.

## Notes

Energy-life at the border of flora, kept as lived path growth. Not a crop, not a relic trade.
