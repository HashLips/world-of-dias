# Flowers

## Overview

**Flowers** is Aelwyn Canopy flora kept as a speaking bouquet: black stems that may end in white star blooms or profile head-forms, arranged in a zigzag-banded grey vase so a room can host likeness and light together.

## Description

Canopy keepers treat the arrangement as living hospitality craft. White sharp-petaled stars mark open welcome; black profile stems hold remembered faces or guest echoes without claiming Earth ancestor worship. Almond white leaves stay sparse so stems can be read. The grey vase’s zigzag is a local maker band meaning “talk may happen here.” Placing only stars for prettiness, or only heads for spook theater, unbalances the flora’s manners. Range is guest tables and quiet canopy courts. Season talk argues scent; care talk argues stem count.

## Local Use

Guest welcome bouquets, apology tables, and teaching arrangements for children learning not to snip a speaking stem. Match etiquette: keep stars and heads both present when the vase is for talk.
