# Flower Birds

## Overview

**Flower Birds** is Aelwyn Canopy flora: a large white star bloom with a pale pink center that times its open hour for blue needle-flyers and red petal-crawlers together, teaching shared nectar instead of private bagging.

## Description

Keepers know the bloom by its many long tapering white petals and soft pink disc on a dark brown stem rising from olive mound ground. Blue forked-tail flyers arrive with needle beaks; small red crawlers take petal-edge watch. The name binds flora to its partners on purpose—pulling the flower without watching who is mid-sip is extractive. After rain the pink center darkens slightly and children are told to count to three before touching. Range talk places it along canopy path edges more than in boxed gardens. Season and scent arguments are friendly; ownership arguments are not.

## Local Use

Path courtesy mark, nectar teaching for apprentices, and a mild scent cue for rest stops. Match etiquette: wait out a sip; leave crawlers unharmed.
