# Claimscar Cookfire Peppersage

## Name

Claimscar Cookfire Peppersage

## Overview

**Claimscar Cookfire Peppersage** is a harsh fragrant leaf thrown into yard stews. Flavor that fits dirt-and-rope hospitality: pepper bite, sage underneath, ash on every leaf whether you wanted ash or not. It makes breakfast taste like the yard instead of like a feast imported to civilize it. Overuse starts arguments about who owns the pot, which means the plant is also a social instrument, unfortunately.

## Description

Fuzzy leaves, gray-green, the undersides always sooty from living too near rings and circles. Dogs respect the smell and will not steal a pot that has been over-leaved, a fact Hungry Dogs have turned into a professional boundary. In the morning kettle a pinch keeps stew honest. At secondfire a heavier hand makes the smoke taste like a double claim feels: green, metallic, hard to swallow while you lie. The plant grows where heat and foot-traffic meet, not in a tended row. Visitors who want it garden-pretty are offered a raw leaf and a clearer picture. It stings. The sting is educational.

## Local Use

Breakfast Circle stews, where the Caller wants flavor and not a war. Sharper pots at the ring, when renegotiation needs a taste that will not let anyone pretend they are at a banquet. Pinches traded for wire or small salvage, a currency of the mouth, not a luxury good. Not a medicine and not a weapon. If someone is using the smoke to drive a crew off a vent, they have left cooking and the yard will say so at the next kettle, dogs in attendance.

## Notes

Frontier flora. Not garden pretty. Rivalry’s seasoning, distinct from war’s.
