# Tollberm Scrub-Apple

## Name

Tollberm Scrub-Apple

## Overview

Tart scrub-apples of Brindle toll berms—peels for foxes and hares, fruit for tired haulers who know gratitude.

## Description

**Look:** small russet apples; tart enough to wink; trees wind-leaned on berms  
**Where:** Brindle March toll berms, fruit-road edges  
**Habit:** crops uneven; stillness days leave peels longer on the road  
**Tell:** offering peels is manners; stripping a whole tree for market greed is road shame

## Local Use

**Edible + offering.** Snack and peel offerings. Horses and hares get peels first if Softlane courtesy holds.

## Notes

Plant Use Classes: edible, cultural.
