# Draftleaf Sprout

## Name

Draftleaf Sprout

## Overview

A sprout Open Hand yards treat as honest draft growth—leaves that accept another pass of shape without shame, holding usable green while remaining revisable.

## Description

Draftleaf Sprout comes up along sketchline edges and half-made garden beds where soil still admits adjustment. First leaves may be outline-thin, almost translucent at the tip; later passes thicken the blade and deepen the green. Draftkin water it as workday flora and sometimes pinch a leaf mid-morning to teach clean undo: the bed can change without calling yesterday’s layout a lie.

Monument gardeners who demand finished ornamental form in one morning kill more sprouts with pride than drought. When shelter needs “enough for tonight,” a thicker hold is spoken aloud so revision urge does not starve guests. The sprout’s mild pepper smell after rain is a small local joke: drafts that still feed you.

## Local Use

Teaching iterative manifestation to juniors. Temporary edible greens when a hold is enough for the night. Bed-layout undo practice without shame speech.

## Notes

Companion to F960 Draft-Timber Hold in living green. Not broken F432 lettuce.
