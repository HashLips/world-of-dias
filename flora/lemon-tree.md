# Lemon Tree

## Name

Lemon Tree

## Overview

**Lemon Tree** is an Aelwyn Canopy cultivated stem-plant: a bright red central stalk bearing numerous smooth yellow oval pods with white gloss highlights, remembered for bitter-bright pulp used in canopy drinks and for the rule that the stem’s dark jagged silhouette of remaining pods must stay readable after picking.

## Description

Pods attach at tiny darker nubs; a short upper branch often carries a late cluster. Growers stake stems against purple cloth backdrops for teaching plates. Children learn count-pick: two for the basket, one left for the shadow. Adults who strip a stem bald pay in restake labor. The name is Dias flora talk—not an Earth orchard claim about identical fruit.

## Cultural Meaning

Around Aelwyn Canopy, Lemon Tree is abundance with a leave-some rule. Resonance favors glossy unbruised pods. Clear-cutting a stem for market boast empties the teaching.

## Habitat

Cultivated canopy plots, teaching gardens, and season-path edges with purple display cloths.

## Notes

Culture- and religion-agnostic. Flora matching yellow-pod stem.
