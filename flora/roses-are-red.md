## Overview

## Description

Near quiet wells and quiet rooms, the name attaches to the red vessel and the green rise together. Leaves stay few and lime; stems stay thin and dark. The long shadow in plates is part of the telling—low light, still air. Local care says you water and leave space; you do not force bloom heads onto the stalks to satisfy a borrowed rhyme. Resonance is red vessel against white quiet; memory is the shadow length; wonder is how meditative sparse leaves can be. Not an Earth couplet prop.
