# Window-Glasswort

## Name

Window-Glasswort

## Overview

A pale, salt-tolerant wort Veil Scribes note near Glassfold practice sites—growth that seems plainer between windows and briefly more articulate when a reveal opens, then reseals into ordinary green.

## Description

Window-Glasswort hugs damp ledge seams and cool stone lips. Between opens it looks like any humble salt plant: thick leaves, quiet stem, no sermon. Inside a brief open, tiny crystal beads along the leaf edge catch light and make the plant look briefly “written.” Scribes may sketch beads with phase stamps; they do not claim the wort stays jeweled when the seal returns.

Staring at beads past seal invites stare-cost more than botany. Juniors who bottle “forever dew” learn the liquid goes dull and their eyes go hungry. Glance craft treats the wort as a timer you respect, not a trophy you keep lit.

## Local Use

Glance discipline drills. Phase-stamp teaching bowls. Never bottled as endless-window tonic.

## Notes

F432 residual seam light is not this wort’s legal cousin for endless sight.
