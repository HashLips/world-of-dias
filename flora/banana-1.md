# Banana

## Overview

**Banana** is a Velorian Basin fruit-form remembered as a crate-risen silhouette: dark stalk, scalloped crest, and a yellow crescent that marks when the stand is ready to share. Market boards keep the name plain even when the plate looks monumental.

## Description

Basin gatherers describe **Banana** less as a slender hanging cluster and more as a heavy two-rooted presence that prefers open crates to bare dirt when traveling. The living stalk reads almost black in shade. The crest notches soften as the yellow crescent deepens—readiness you can see from a path without touching. Crates used for moving the form are lined pale blue so rot and bruise show early. Locals plant and pack in pairs: one portion for the road, one left so the stand does not go mute. Crush or peel talk varies by village; what does not vary is the rule against stripping a whole rise for a single guest table. Children learn the crescent-tab as a “not yet” mark when it is still gray. After rain the shadows of the stalks run long on gray ground, and path etiquette says you step around rather than through a crate line.

## Local Use

Seasonal cue for basin markets, path courtesy mark at crate rests, and a mild shared fruit when the crescent is fully yellow. Match local etiquette: leave stock; do not treat the stand as a free monument to carve.
