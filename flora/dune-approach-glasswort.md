# Dune-Approach Glasswort

## Name

Dune-Approach Glasswort

## Overview

**Dune-Approach Glasswort** beads clear along the Quiet Well Approach. Succulent glitter that rewards slow feet and punishes anyone who would publish a shortcut. It looks like treasure. It tastes like patience and salt. Cousin to other Lumira glass-succulents, denser in the approach, as if the path wanted a plant that shines without agreeing to be a beacon.

## Description

Translucent joints, a salt kiss on the fingers, cool pulp if you are in the manners and a dry disappointment if you are raiding. It catches the hour of short shadows and makes the sand look mapped. The map is a lie the plant tells to the hurried. Cairns already disagree on purpose. The glasswort disagrees more politely, by being everywhere you might want a unique landmark and therefore being none. Silence Law thickens near it. Chewing loudly is a kind of speech. People who belong to the approach take a bead, slowly, and do not narrate the moisture as a discovery. Published directions that mention “follow the brightest wort” are the raid-notes this plant is constantly being recruited into, and constantly failing to honor, because brightness moves with the sun and the sun is not your guide to the well.

## Local Use

Emergency moisture, taken with the same economy as words. A reminder on the tongue that the land is not a riddle written for your mouth. Never a map beacon, never a surveyed crop, never a reason to “clarify” the approach into a single true line. Keth Dunewalk teaching can point at it as an example of glitter that listens. Pointing is enough. Do not solve the well from the beading. The plant will still be here, shining, after your cleverness has been walked back.

## Notes

Do not turn it into a raid crop or a coordinate. Manners and moisture only.
