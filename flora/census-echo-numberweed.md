# Census Echo Numberweed (folk)

## Name

Census Echo Numberweed (folk)

## Overview

Folk-named weed of census season—leaves that look faintly numeral-shaped if you squint after too much tea; Softfruit treats it as omen humor, not math.

## Description

**Look:** irregular lobed leaves; some claim “3” or “7” silhouettes; green that yellows early  
**Where:** Veloria clerk courtyards, census-week alleys, Softfruit joke pots  
**Habit:** appears where chalk dust and worry gather  
**Tell:** cannot actually count citizens—anyone selling “numberweed prophecy” gets ladle correction

## Local Use

**Observed / omen humor.** Teach children not to fear census with a plant joke. Not pharmacy.

## Notes

Cultural observed. Soft limits: not Prime Mark computation.
