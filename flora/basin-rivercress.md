# Basin Rivercress

## Name

Basin Rivercress

## Overview

Peppery cress of basin ditch edges—pepper for poor tables and Softfruit when bananas feel too proud.

## Description

**Look:** small water-edge leaves; white cross-flowers; pepper bite  
**Where:** Velorian Basin ditches, mist orchard habitat wet seams  
**Habit:** loves clear running edges; sulks in stagnant grease  
**Tell:** hotter than Mist-Herb; Mist-Herb is cool tea, rivercress is bite

## Local Use

**Edible.** Salad and garnish. Wash grit. Do not pick from Tubehold grease seeps by mistake.
