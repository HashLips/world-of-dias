# Flower on Bird

## Overview

**Flower on Bird** is Aelwyn Canopy flora: deep red lip- or bowl-shaped blooms on black spindly stems that thrive in a yellow wavy-marked vase when an avian caretaker keeps a pink-table watch.

## Description

The blooms read like soft mouths at stem-tips—deep red, open upward. Black branches stay thin; light-green waves on the yellow vase are the keepers’ moisture marks. Plates show an orange bird-headed sitter because the flora answers better to that care rhythm than to hurried humanoid fuss—local talk, not Earth species taxonomy. A black yellow-eyed floor-walker often shares the room as a second witness against neglect. Range is canopy domestic shelves and quiet guest tables. Cutting the red mouths for fashion without leaving a stem for regrowth is extractive.

## Local Use

Guest table calm-flora, avian caretaker drills, and night shelf watches. Match etiquette: tend before you display; leave the cat’s path clear.
