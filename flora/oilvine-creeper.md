# Oilvine Creeper

## Name

Oilvine Creeper

## Overview

**Oilvine Creeper** is a thin climbing plant that thrives in workshop oil mist on **Calibration Row**. It marks active craft spaces by growing where tools are used often. In the Calibration Saga it belongs to the same smell-world as Orrin Veld’s alcove—oil, orange peel, and apologetic midnight manners—not to magic doors or ferry plants.

## Description

Oilvine sends thread-fine runners along brick, bench legs, and the underside of work shelves. Leaves are small, dark, and slightly glossy, as if wiped with the same rag as the tools. It does poorly in clean tourist courtyards and well in rented alcoves where filament, chalk dust, and machine oil hang in the air.

Locals treat healthy oilvine as a sign the shop is *working*, not abandoned. When a creeper yellows, someone either stopped building or cleaned too hard. In the **Veld Workshop Alcove**, oilvine fringes the chalk ghosts of moved furniture and the floor marks left by the **Spectrum Frame**. It does not power the Frame, invite the Caliburn, or close the residual calibration seam. It only records that oil was spent there for months.

The folk myth **Oil Apology at Midnight** uses oilvine as backdrop: if you apologize to a tool in oil-smell dark, the vine is supposed to stay soft instead of going brittle. Craftspeople half-believe it. Orrin believes the manners more than the botany. Noro Halfmeasure pretends not to. On the vault night, when apology is spoken and the latch still fails, the creeper does nothing dramatic—it simply keeps growing in unfinished air.

## Local Use

- Left alone as a workshop “still open” indicator; cutting it without cause is rude.
- Snipped sparingly for rag scent or to wipe shutter edges after heavy oil work.
- Avoided as tea (bitter, greasy aftertaste); Softfruit vendors will mock anyone who tries.
- Used in jokes about rivals: “his oilvine died” means he stopped arguing with metal.
- Never presented as a ferry plant, Zero herb, or Echoform lure.
