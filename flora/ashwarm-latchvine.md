# Ashwarm Latchvine

## Name

Ashwarm Latchvine

## Overview

A tough vine Ember Keepers train along warmth corridors—growth that latches shelter frames and keeps mild heat honesty without pretending scars are gone.

## Description

Ashwarm Latchvine likes ash-edge soil and ground that has held a kettle more than once. Leaves stay matte olive-brown; stems grip when mended in layers and loosen when someone tries to force a monument wall overnight. Trace warmth clings under the leaf where a lean-to has been honest for a week. Forced-Final gardeners who want instant green screens find the vine dropping grips until the pace slows to ember time.

The vine will not hide a cold gap. Where recovery has not earned a hold, Latchvine browns at the tip and waits. Ember Keepers read that as speech: name the quiet, keep the corridor, do not paint summer over ash.

## Local Use

Lean-to lashing, corridor markers, junior “warmth first” drills. Not sold as miracle bloom-overnight seed. Cuttings travel between hearthline shelters with a spoken cold-gap note attached.

## Notes

Distinct from F500 contrast-hungry metaphors—this vine wants continuity of care, not erasure of contrast.
