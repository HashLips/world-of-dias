# Freeband Camp-Nettle

## Name

Freeband Camp-Nettle

## Overview

Stinging nettle of Freeband camps and salvage edges—shared pot green and rough fiber when gloves and manners both show up.

## Description

**Look:** serrated leaves, hollow sting hairs, deep green that blacks when overcooked; young tips preferred.  
**Where:** Driftfall Salvage Tide Habitat edges, Claimscar tarp margins, Flagweek Dust Reach ditches after rain.  
**Habit:** returns after scrap fires; loves nitrogen from camp mess.  
**Tell:** sting first, soup second—anyone who grabs barehanded is learning, not foraging.

## Local Use

**Edible + fiber/commercial small.** Soup after blanch; cordage from older stems. Shared pot green—taking a whole stand without breakfast talk is yard rudeness.

## Notes

Indexed in Flora of Dias. Plant Use Classes: edible, commercial. Do not romanticize hunger; nettle is work food.
