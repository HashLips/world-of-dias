# Null-Margin Sootwort

## Name

Null-Margin Sootwort

## Overview

**Null-Margin Sootwort** is a rumored ash-dark growth at approaches to F500, said to shed grit into ground that will not keep a name. It is warning ecology, not forage. Treat every confident leaf-count as a story that wants a field guide. The plant, if it is a plant, does not owe you a specimen. Farming it is the kind of sentence this entry exists to stop.

## Description

Sparse mats, the color of burnt paper, edges that fail to stay in the memory even while you are looking. Sketches disagree on whether there are leaves. Some survivors insist the wort never existed and that the insistence is the only stain it left. Others keep a smear in a notebook that blanks the neighboring page, which teachers display only long enough to frighten a joke-tour and then put away, because display is how a rumor grows legs. The smell, in the accounts that include a smell, is the absence of the smell you expected from ash. That negative is hard to teach and easy to embellish. Embellishment is the hazard. A silhouette, a company, a confirmed eater of notes: none of those are licensed by a mat that might not have been there.

## Local Use

No cuisine. No dye, no medicine, no charm. In F432 the wort is a teaching metaphor: do not harvest what erases the harvester’s notes. Warning tags do the practical work. The plant does not. People who propose a plot “just to see” are answered with the temporary-naming ban and a request to sit down. Zero is not on the other side of a successful germination.

## Notes

Status **rumor**. Do not build a farming plot in the abyss. Do not promote Unraveled ecology from a smear.
