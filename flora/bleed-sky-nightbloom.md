# Bleed-Sky Nightbloom

## Name

Bleed-Sky Nightbloom

## Overview

A rare F432 bloom that opens with Bleed-Sky Weather—omen companion, not dye crop, not pharmacy, not a Frequency Zero clue.

## Description

**Look:** pale petals with bruise-violet veins; opens only under wrong light / absence-cold nights; scent like wet metal and crushed nightshade cousins (do not taste to check).  
**Where:** unpredictable F432 edges after Bleed-Sky; never a reliable garden row.  
**Habit:** hours, not seasons; gone by honest morning.  
**Tell:** no stable dye match—anyone selling “nightbloom ink” is lying or worse.

## Local Use

**Dangerous / observed / cultural omen only.** Do not farm, trade as drug, or “correct” maps toward blooms. Softfruit tags omen weather; Houses may log; neither harvests for profit.
