# Vitrin

## Name

Vitrin

## Overview

Vitrin is a fruit breed grown once, in secret, in a Wabet mix orchard. It looks like glass. Eaten, it can take the eater out of sight. The effect is not a clean trick and does not promise to end.

## Description

A Vitrin fruit is about the size of a large share-apple and heavier than it looks, as if the inside were cooled glass rather than flesh. The rind is clear enough to read a thumb through and thick enough to ring when tapped with a nail. Under the rind the flesh holds a green-gold depth, layered like preserved light, with faint vertical cords the color of new leaves. Seeds sit in a small heart and catch lamp-light the way lenses do. Cut, it does not bleed juice so much as loosen a cold, almost dry sweetness that smells of pear skin, green banana, and the iron of a bell just struck. The cut face stays bright. It does not brown on an ordinary clock. A thin oil on the knife can vanish from the steel and reappear as a pale rim an hour later, which sorters hate, because it makes a clean blade look guilty.

On the tree the fruits hang without looking hung. They take the color of the lane behind them and then refuse it. About ten were sound enough to count. One smaller fruit clung after the count, greener in the glass, not part of the auction.

Vitrin is not a Lucky Fruit form, though road talk tried the name. Lucky Fruit is symmetry and a disputed boon. Vitrin is a mix Sella Orrow made from certain fruits she already knew how to grow. She has not said which, in what cut, or what she asked of the ground. People guess share-apple, scrub-apple, and market banana because those trees stand nearest. Guessing is not the method. The method stays unknown.

## Local Use

There is no settled local use. One fruit was eaten at a show. Eight were sold for VEL marks large enough to frighten a shed. One was kept and later put into a share-pot, where it did not do what the man who put it there wanted. The last cling was not flown out as a secret.

Eaten by Dain Mercer, it took his visibility and did not give it back on a clean curve. He flickered, then worsened. Tasted by Sella from a pot she had not dosed, it faded only her edges: the rim of an ear, the hem-shadow of an apron, the outline of a hand. She remained angry and able to be seen. No one in the saga is erased by it. Whether a later eater would vanish, sicken, or only ring like glass is not known, and the fruit is not a medicine.

It does not ferry a person between frequencies. It does not call Sky Drifters. It does not replace Softfruit in the market. Nauw's fruit reverence has no rank for it yet, only a price and a fear.

## Notes

Indexed in Flora of Dias. Kind: magical-leaning, and still a plant. Canonical that the tree fruited and that the show and the stew happened. Unknown: the true mix; whether the unseen ever fully leaves Dain; whether the tree will set again. Do not write a recipe.
