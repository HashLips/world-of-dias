# Reveal-Shelf Lichen

## Name

Reveal-Shelf Lichen

## Overview

**Reveal-Shelf Lichen** brightens only during a ledge window and goes dull on purpose afterward. Pale lace that teaches anti-stare patience better than a lecture, because the lecture can be ignored and the lichen cannot be argued back into brightness. Veil Scribes watch it alongside the chime. When the two disagree, they trust the soft close and sort the lichen later.

## Description

Cool gray crust on glass-gray stone, steel-blue flecks that are freckles until a window opens. In the minute, under-pattern shows like tiny maps, kin to the basins’ revealed braces, small enough that an apprentice’s hunger to copy everything meets a natural limit. After the close it is ordinary stone-freckle again. Scraping during the dull phase is rude, a refusal to let the shelf be boring. Scraping during the bright phase is greed, a theft of a timed skin. Both leave a scar the next window does not kindly redraw. The lichen smells of cold metal and rain that did not fall. Lingerers with afterglow headaches sometimes claim the flecks followed them. The guild treats that claim as the myth, not as a propagation event.

## Local Use

A second clock for Timers who want a check on the chime’s honesty. Teaching apprentices to pack when the lace dulls, not when they feel finished. Never a pigment, never a souvenir pried up for a gallery. The pattern is a visitor. You may look. You may not keep the looking.

## Notes

Ecology of reveal intervals. Not a lie-detector moss and not a crop of hidden truth.
