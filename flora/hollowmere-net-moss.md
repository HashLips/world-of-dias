# Hollowmere Net-Moss

## Name

Hollowmere Net-Moss

## Overview

Salt-tolerant moss of Hollowmere loft pots and cove stone—used to soften rope and soothe rope-burned hands.

## Description

**Look:** dense olive cushions that hold brine without rotting; cool to the touch; faint sea-metal smell.  
**Where:** Outer Rim Salt Fog Habitat—mending loft pots, cliff seep, brine-barrel shade.  
**Habit:** slow spread; survives salt spray that kills basin herbs.  
**Tell:** if it thrives in pure fresh ditch water only, you have the wrong moss.

## Local Use

**Medicinal / craft.** Softens rope fibers; poultice for rope burn (folk, not House pharmacy). Take with loft assent; stripping a public pot is a check-in offense.

## Notes

Indexed in Flora of Dias. Plant Use Classes: medicinal, commercial (small craft).
