# Near-Return Reed

## Name

Near-Return Reed

## Overview

Tall water reeds along Returning Span approaches whose stands rhyme season to season—similar heights and whistle tones with small offsets that lab crews log as living drift, not prophecy.

## Description

Near-Return Reed prefers shallow margins and damp Span shoulders. Stands look familiar enough that new walkers claim “the same reeds as last passage,” then find a sister gap or a delayed whistle. Refrain Walkers strip one dry stem for a drift tally stick; they do not cut whole banks for souvenirs. The reed’s seed fluff catches light in repeating patterns that refuse exact loops.

## Local Use

- Drift tallies and lab teaching
- Soft warning against time-door tourism
- Occasionally woven into temporary markers that must be remade each hold
