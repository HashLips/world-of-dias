# Averra Cliff-Thyme

## Name

Averra Cliff-Thyme

## Overview

Salt-wind thyme of Averra cliffs—kitchen herb and studio air that smells like old Masters’ gossip.

## Description

**Look:** low woody thyme; purple flower beads; salt-sharpened scent  
**Where:** Averra Isle cliffs, studio window cracks  
**Habit:** survives salt wind; rare mainland transplants sulk  
**Tell:** studio air herb—not mainland market thyme alone

## Local Use

**Edible / studio air.** Cook and clear ink rooms. Mainland “Averra thyme” jars are often ordinary thyme with stories.

## Notes

Plant Use Classes: edible, cultural soft.
