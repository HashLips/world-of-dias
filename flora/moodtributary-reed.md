# Moodtributary Reed

## Name

Moodtributary Reed

## Overview

**Moodtributary Reed** leans with the estuary’s currents. Amber-rose near the Warm Current, smoke-teal near quieter water, other hues where the weather is grief or brightness or the hard work of neither. It is a living color bar. It is not a crop for controlling emotion, and a person who cuts it to force a cove’s mood learns that the reed keeps leaning toward the feeling it last stood in, which is a poor servant and a good teacher.

## Description

Tall hollow stems, soft nodes, leaves that cup humidity the way a hand cups a confidence. In a true hold the reeds lean inward, attentive, not collapsed. Under fake brightness, scheduled happiness, a stem goes brittle and the lean fails in a way children can see and adults try to argue with. Cut reeds remember for about a day. That memory is not magic you can bottle. It is the plant being more honest than the arrangement. Otters ignore the reeds except when the lean goes wrong, and then they leave, which Guides count as a second opinion.

## Local Use

Holding-cove markers, so a Guide can point at a plant instead of accusing a person. Stiffeners slipped into cloth hems, gently, never enough to turn the cloth into armor. Teaching, especially: watch the reed under forced cheer and then apologize to the basin before you apologize to the crowd. Not a bouquet for negotiations. A bouquet is a cut mood, and the estuary has enough of those.

## Notes

Climate flora. Not an emotion-control crop and not a truth serum with leaves.
