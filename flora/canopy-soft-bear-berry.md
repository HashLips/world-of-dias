# Canopy Soft-Bear Berry

## Name

Canopy Soft-Bear Berry

## Overview

Small dark berries of Aelwyn forks—forage for walkers who leave a share for Soft-Bears first.

## Description

**Look:** pea-to-thumbnail dark berries in fork-clusters; juice stains fingers purple-brown; leaf edges soft like felt when wet Softfall.  
**Where:** Aelwyn Softfall Canopy Habitat, especially Soft-Bear fork-nests and walkway edges.  
**Habit:** ripens after Softfall weeks; birds and Soft-Bears compete politely if walkers do.  
**Tell:** etiquette is the tell—empty a cluster without leaving share and trail culture names you poorly.

## Local Use

**Edible forage + cultural.** Eating is allowed; stripping a fork bare is not. Soft-Bear fruit tip is manners, not myth tax.
