# Softfruit Ladle Mint

## Name

Softfruit Ladle Mint

## Overview

**Softfruit Ladle Mint** is the sticky-sweet mint grown in court pots so ladles can be rinsed and fruit steam can be tempered. Small green manners. It is ordinary F432 joy flora, which is a high rank. The court would rather smell of this than of a seal drying on an empty stomach.

## Description

Rounded leaves, stems gone pinkish where fruit has splashed them, scent like citrus and cool water. Bruise a leaf and the air in a rush-hour queue gets a fraction kinder, which is not magic and is not nothing. Children chew it and are only partly discouraged, because a chewed leaf is a child not inventing a hierarchy. Hosts bruise handfuls into wash buckets between servings. The pots sit where peel-boat rivers can be nudged without drowning the plants. Overgrown mint is a sign the shelf was busy. Mint pulled up to make room for a stamp desk is a sign the court has forgotten itself, and the street will say so.

## Local Use

Ladle rinse, the clean smell that tells the next person the tool was used and loved. A little in the damp of peel-boat races. A leaf crushed at the edge of a field interview so questions start after calm, not instead of food. Not a commodity to corner. Not a perfume for Houses that want Softfruit without the waiting. If it becomes a luxury scent detached from buckets, it has left home.

## Notes

Ordinary joy. The importance is the rinse, not the rarity.
