# Band-Answer Moss

## Name

Band-Answer Moss

## Overview

A soft green-gray moss Softfruit and junior surveyors use as a teaching plant: it grows ordinary in F432, yet its folk name reminds students that living growth answers band habit—and that other bands would answer differently.

## Description

Band-Answer Moss prefers damp stone near kitchens, gate steps, and quiet yard walls. It holds morning wet longer than pride admits and takes a faint warmer tint where tables spill broth steam. Softfruit hosts pinch a scrap onto a slate and say the moss answers *here*—do not pack it as a ferry to lumen sheets or null ash. The plant is not travel gear; it is a green reminder of Matter answers its band and Life inherits band habit.

Patches spread like patient handwriting. Leaves stay short. Echo-Lichen is a different craft cousin—more remainder-talk than band-teaching. Gatherers may take what etiquette allows for teaching bowls; stripping a gate step bare for novelty is poor local care. After bleed nights, surveyors sometimes pocket a scrap as proof that home-band still grows, then return it damp to the same wall.

## Local Use

Softfruit orientation bowls and junior chapbook lessons. Surveyor pockets after confusing omen weather. Markets that sell it as “cross-band seed” earn ladle correction and a scraped sign.
