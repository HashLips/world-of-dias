# Flowers on Your Table

## Overview

**Flowers on Your Table** is Aelwyn Canopy flora: a scarlet flame-petaled bloom with a yellow center that prefers a tall pink stand and a blue spotted vase, teaching that table beauty carries visible weight and a scissors rule.

## Description

The stem is thin and bright green; the bloom is large and droops on purpose so guests feel the pull. Dark blue spots on the vase are moisture and maker marks. Closed scissors on the stand mean trim is allowed only after asking who set the vase. Cutting for a pocket trophy while the droop still teaches is extractive. Range is canopy guest tables and night stands. Children learn to stand under the arc without tugging petals.

## Local Use

Guest table centerpiece, apology weight-lesson, and trim drills. Match etiquette: admire the droop; ask before the blades.
