# Anonymous Drop Wall

## Name

Anonymous Drop Wall

## Overview

**Anonymous Drop Wall** is a brick stretch in Veloria where Operator-adjacent scrap appears without signatures. Couriers leave chips and papers in left-side drops so craftspeople like **Orrin Veld** can receive load curves without being cleared—or accused. It is ordinary unsigned care and a Calibration Saga escape trail marker.

## Description

The wall is ordinary city brick with ivy, shallow left-pocket niches, and etiquette stricter than it looks. You do not shout names. You do not wait for thanks. You leave an **anonymous load scrap token**—dull resin or soft metal, sometimes warm from a pocket—with conduit notes or load-fan scratched paper, then walk on.

**Kesh Lineborn**’s route runs here. Tokens mean: this scrap is intentional; do not file the courier; treat the paper as noise unless it walks. The wall does not grant core-slot passage. It is adjacency without admission—the same manners that let Houses call wonder noise while still feeding graphs to people who need them.

Physically it sits on paths between third-ring work and deeper load infrastructure: enough pipe hum to explain why escape stories pass through. After the Spectrum Frame vault fails, tellers say a load-fan whine and bruise-violet thinning trail along this wall into pipes beyond. Whether every whine is the Caliburn is rumor; the wall’s job in geography is clear—unsigned help and a haunt corridor share the same brick.

## Lore

Before the saga, the wall already existed as Operator scrap economy. Row hands knew left-pocket etiquette; Null buyers offered coin for tokens and honest couriers refused. During Movement One, Orrin learns what Operators call noise partly through drops that arrive without a face attached.

After escape, some tokens arrive humming wrong. Kesh does not explain. Post-escape static drift and late mutter talk thicken the ivy jokes. The place stays useful and slightly unclean in reputation: oxygen and insult at once.
