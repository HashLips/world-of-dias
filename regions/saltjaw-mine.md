# Saltjaw Mine

## Name

Saltjaw Mine

## Overview

Saltjaw Mine is a modest ore mine cut into the heat-warped strata of Driftfall's southeastern flank, under the point at Saltjaw. It produces verdigris iron and yellow sulfur crust, which sell to the dye yards and smithies of Claimscar. It is a living, not a treasure. Its backers in Claimscar wanted a new drift driven out under the offshore shelf. That plan is what sent the survey to sea.

## Description

Three working levels open off the main adit under the headframe.

- **Third Drift** runs east under the slope. It has the best ore and the worst stone: plates of cooked rock that cooled in the wrong order. Some are as sound as a kiln floor; some are held up by habit.
- **Second Drift**, older and drier, has two crews and pump hose.
- **First Drift** is mostly worked out and used for storage and the carts.

The **Under-Shelf Drift** was a planned extension seaward under the shelf, toward its edge. It was never begun. Bastien Crale shelved it after the expedition.

At the adit mouth, under a little roof of hull plank, is the tag board. Beside the headframe stands the bell. The stone is read with a short brass-headed sounding hammer: sound plate rings, hollow plate thuds, cracked plate gives "a tired answer." Ore lives in the seams between plates: green-crusted heavy verdigris iron and sulfur crust that burns the nose.

## Lore

Miners say the mountain with many mouths breathes through the strata on bad days, and that you can feel it in your chest before the air changes. Ansel could. The mine's oldest rule, *the bell stays at the bell*, means the bell-keeper never leaves the bell during a shift, whatever happens below.
