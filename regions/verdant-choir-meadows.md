# Verdant Choir Meadows

## Name

Verdant Choir Meadows

## Overview

Open meadow belts inside Verdant Reach where choir-frogs set evening weather and Softfall edges the grass—Wabet’s bright middle, not deep Fellgrove and not Lumira noon.

## Description

Frog evenings, wet grass, paths that join Open Hand Way without becoming conquest roads. Travelers rest here between canopy and dune approaches. That Forest / Fellgrove pressure sits at the darker tree line; meadows are the breath before that hush.

## Lore

Indexed under Landforms of F432. Completes Wabet landform trio with canopy rivers and glass dunes.
