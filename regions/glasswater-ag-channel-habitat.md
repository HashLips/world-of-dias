# Glasswater Ag Channel Habitat

## Name

Glasswater Ag Channel Habitat

## Overview

River-fed plains and irrigation cuts where food-heart farms, mirror-lull water, Tubehold vigilance, and spring swallows share one honest wet ecology.

## Description

Feed channels glitter or mud depending on season. Mirror-reed clicks on lull days; koi hold stillness like a joke; swallows mark Brightpass. Grease-cress and mill damp belong to Tubehold’s edge. Drifters bring sky-raid memory; the habitat’s job is still water honesty.

**Who thrives:** farmers, night watchers, reed-cutters, swallows, koi.  
**Who strains:** those who treat lull beauty as nap permission.
