# Hearthvale Rebuild Field Habitat

## Name

Hearthvale Rebuild Field Habitat

## Overview

Cooperative farms, doorstep hens, ember-cats, and emberslope dust where second-beginning people remake foodways—not wasteland emptiness.

## Description

Fields are patchy and earnest. Hens argue dawn; Ember-Cats choose warm stone; clover and kitchen greens appear where Compact tables hold. Kiln smoke from Secondfire joins slope grit in Fruitcarry. Exile roads feed people into this habitat; belonging makes it safe.

**Who thrives:** Compact neighbors, hens, ember-cats, kiln teachers.  
**Who strains:** underprovisioned lone arrivals; those expecting Nauw fruit-rank comfort unchanged.
