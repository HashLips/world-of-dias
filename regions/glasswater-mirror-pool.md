# Glasswater Mirror Pool

## Name

Glasswater Mirror Pool

## Overview

The **Glasswater Mirror Pool** is a still water pocket in the Fields where sky and grain reflect so cleanly night-watch pairs log “mirror calm” as its own weather—beauty that can hide a dam lie if no one pairs the watch.

## Description

Wind breaks around the pool’s lee. Birds skim without rippling much. Tourists want solitude; locals forbid lone watch here in high-alert seasons. Still Pool Vigil stories teach why pairs matter when the water looks too perfect.

## Lore

Sacred-soft useful landmark: rest after migration, early hazard detection, rumor control. Not a ferry glass to other bands—just F432 water doing uncanny honesty.

## Stories or Depictions

Glasswater night-watch rule; Still Pool Vigil myth/story cousins.

## Notes

Open question: what a wrong reflection means—sky weather, or someone standing where they should not.
