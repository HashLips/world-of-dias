# Clerk Stamp Gallery

## Name

Clerk Stamp Gallery

## Overview

The **Clerk Stamp Gallery** is a long mid-ring room where Veloria clerks practice, display, and argue over stamp faces—partial courtesy shelves beside nonactionable habits.

## Description

Ink smell, drying racks, sample pages under glass. Juniors copy measure-marks and no-ferry boats. Seniors hide preferred stamps in drawers labeled boringly on purpose. Pellun-types hesitate at living agreement; Issa-types draft disclaimers that look like care manuals.

Not a Sketchy Gallery of taste—though Zephan’s patrons sometimes sneer. This is governance craft you can smell.

## Lore

Where ring protocol becomes muscle memory. Partial knowledge tags get taught as stamp discipline: witnessed / copied / symbolic.
