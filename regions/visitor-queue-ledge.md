# Visitor Queue Ledge

## Name

Visitor Queue Ledge

## Overview

**Visitor Queue Ledge** is where visitors wait for core-approach clearances and notice wrong ticks after the Caliburn escapes. **Jori Sweepgate**’s ask-again weather and **Dott Gildhour**’s soft pamphlets live here. Haunt aftermath geography, not a ferry queue.

## Description

A ledge and queue strip on the core-approach side of third-ring manners: dials, clearance stamps, tired feet, guides selling calmer language than Houses write. Visitors relax when a dial settles warmly—then the tick jumps half a measure. Softfruit jokes thin. Peel-sweet herb appears in comic advice more than in actual cures.

Jori marks more “ask again” stamps without a tidy House brief explaining why. Dott invents softer sentences: the dial loves you—check twice. Static lap-cats claim desks. The ledge was always a wait; after escape it becomes a rumor factory for digits that walk into conduits.

## Lore

Before the saga, the ledge was ordinary costly clearance weather—Frederick-class longing and tourist confusion included, without solving anyone’s crossing. After vault-and-break, visitor wrong-tick unease concentrates here. Myth **The Number That Walked Away** starts in mouths that stood on this stone.

Careful Operators still say calibration weather. Careful storytellers refuse to claim every wrong tick is the Caliburn. Saga fact remains: the Echoform is at large in Veloria load-noise; the ledge is where ordinary people feel the haunt without owning a Frame. Frequency Zero stays unresolved. No ferry ticket sold at the clearance window works—Ell’s pier already taught that joke elsewhere.
