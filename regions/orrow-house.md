# Orrow House

## Name

Orrow House

## Overview

Orrow House is the low timber kitchen-house where Sella Orrow cooks, sleeps, and keeps the share-pot. It is a Wabet house: quiet timber, one lamp, a door that latches against weather and not against a myth.

## Description

One main room and a sleeping loft. The hearth is sized for stew, not for audiences. Bunches of ordinary peel dry on a cord. The share-pot sits on the cool end of the board when it is not on the fire, a portion meant for the hands and the field that kept the day. Windows are small. At night the lamp makes a square on the lane that the Stillwood Skiff can pass without explaining itself.

Hob Orrow's boots live by the door. Sella's apron lives on a nail. Nothing in the house is a resonator built to cross bands. The wood smells of onion, apple, and smoke.

## Lore

The house stayed lit the night Dain Mercer came back with the kept fruit. What the pot did, and did not do, is saga. The building remains a farmhouse.

## Stories or Depictions

Movement Three of The Vitrin Saga. The lamp is a practical light, not a signal to any other frequency.

## Notes

Architecture matches Wabet quiet timber: low, listening, no tower.
