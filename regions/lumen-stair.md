# Lumen Stair

## Name

Lumen Stair

## Overview

Lumen Stair is a bright overlook path in F200 where coherent light gathers in steps that feel climbable even when the ground does not agree. F432 travelers only meet it as rumor, bleed-omen, or rare cross-band glimpse.

## Description

The Stair is not a Veloria street and not a Wabet trail. It is a frequency-native landmark: pale terraces, hard shadows that refuse to soften, and a sense that conversation becomes too exact. People who claim to have walked it often return over-literal for days.

## Lore

Kept as a small F200 pin for future non-Harmonic cross-band stories. Do not treat it as an open embassy into F432.
