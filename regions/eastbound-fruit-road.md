# Eastbound Fruit Road

## Name

Eastbound Fruit Road

## Overview

The Eastbound Fruit Road is the living spine of produce moving from Wabet’s orchards and canopy toward Nauw markets. It is not one paved line but a braided set of waystations, wagon rights, and sharing-knife customs that keep abundance from becoming raid.

## Description

Convoy stewards like **Tomas Share** time rest to fruit bruising and heat, not to clocks alone. Lucky Farmers and Verdant stewards argue at junctions about perfect-form fruit: blessing, commodity, or omen. The road brushes Brindle March’s transit discipline before Basin markets take over.

## Lore

Reciprocity harvest ethics travel with the wagons. A convoy that takes without restoring loses more than cargo—it loses future waystation welcome.

## Stories or Depictions

*The Perfect Fruit That Split a Convoy* is the standing cautionary story taught at **Open Hand Way**.
