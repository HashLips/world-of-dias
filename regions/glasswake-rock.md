# Glasswake Rock

## Name

Glasswake Rock

## Overview

**Glasswake Rock** is a dark coastal rock on the seaward edge of Lumira Sands where the **Glasswake Lantern** is fused into stone. Locals often name the whole place by the object: the Lantern in the Stone. It is a landmark for quiet approach, not a salvage pier.

## Description

The rock sits where southern Wabet’s dune country meets clean coastal water—near enough to Lumira’s hush that Silence Law manners apply, near enough to spray that salt and mineral crust never leave the metal. The lantern is not wedged in a crack. Stone runs through housing and frame; mineral growth binds lamp and rock into one scar of the Fracture.

Path talk treats the site as a listening stop on coastal walks related to Saltwhisper Road discipline: soft steps, short visits, no public “perfect map” of how to stage a spectacle. The rock does not hide water the way Quiet Well does; it hides *noise*—the temptation to turn a wound into a show.

## Lore

Keep teaching holds that the lantern arrived with the Fracture’s separation of resonant states, when the *Glasswake* vanished offshore. Present-Age visitors may see a dormant lamp in stone, or, rarely, a pale pulse. Stronger nights bring the shipboard scraps called **Glasswake Pulse**. Stories of a captain-figure that never takes the lantern live as myth, not Keep statute.
