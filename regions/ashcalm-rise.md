# Ashcalm Rise

## Name

Ashcalm Rise

## Overview

Ashcalm Rise is a low stone mountain that breaks the northern canopy of Wabet like a grey shoulder shrugged up through trees. It holds almost no traffic, almost no shrines, and one important hollow: **Stillhollow**.

## Description

From most orchard roads the Rise reads as a darker ridge behind Aelwyn’s green wall—stone the color of cooled ash, sparsely mossed, with wind that sounds thinner than forest wind. Trails that reach it are game paths more than roads. Birds quiet near the upper rock. Fruit does not climb well here; the mountain is not a harvest site.

The Rise sits inside **Aelwyn Canopy**’s claim without becoming a tribal seat. Canopy Kin know it exists. Few make ceremony of it. Fewer still speak of what lives under it.

## Lore

Wabet’s reputation for serenity makes Ashcalm Rise easy to ignore: travelers come west for rivers, orchards, and cleansing air, not for a mute shoulder of rock. That neglect is part of why an exile could vanish into it.

Locals who guide seekers do not mark the mountain on friendly maps. Some call it only “the grey shoulder” or “the quiet one,” as if naming it properly invites a queue.

## Stories or Depictions

Rumors of a dark cook in the mountain’s cave are collected under *The One Who Cooks Quiet*. Visual moods tied to the Rise’s hollow appear in artworks linked from Stillhollow and Vorrith Kael.
