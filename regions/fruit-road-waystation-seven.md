# Fruit-Road Waystation Seven

## Name

Fruit-Road Waystation Seven

## Overview

**Fruit-Road Waystation Seven** is a numbered rest-and-restore stop on the Eastbound Fruit Road—water, shade, sharing-knife etiquette, and a board where wishful handbills get pinned as comedy warnings.

## Description

Dust, peel sweetness, draft-pony noise. Mother Wen–type keepers shout ethics before prices. Convoys log arrival marks; restoration chores (mended troughs, returned seed) earn longer stays. Seven is famous for catching forged “no toll” papers before they rearrange carts.

## Lore

Reciprocity made geographic. Fruit-Road Ethics gets shouted here daily. Not a ferry to other bands—just a waystation that keeps abundance from becoming raid.

## Stories or Depictions

*The Restoration Before the Pick* is Wabet-side; Seven is the road’s teaching porch.

## Notes

Open question: who renumbers waystations when a washout moves the road.
