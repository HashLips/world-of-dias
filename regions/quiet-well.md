# Quiet Well

## Name

Quiet Well

## Overview

Quiet Well is a concealed oasis pocket in Lumira Sands. Its water is real. Its maps are often intentionally wrong. The Silent Dune Keep treats accurate public cartography here as a kind of theft.

## Description

Pale dunes hide a low green node: date-shade, stone lip, insect hush. Travelers who “find” it by rumor usually found a decoy first. **Keth Dunewalk**, apprentice under Dune Keeper Iskar Lüm, maintains false marks and true paths as one craft.

Night at Quiet Well is not mystical theater. It is the sound of people agreeing not to speak louder than the water.

## Lore

The guiding tension: the land provides for those who respect its silence—and silence includes not publishing the well. Lucky Fruit caravans that try to shortcut through Lumira learn expensive lessons.

## Stories or Depictions

*The Map That Must Stay Wrong* is the oral spine. *Take It Easy* is hung in shade-camps as a reminder that hurry kills wells.
