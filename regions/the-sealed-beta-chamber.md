# The Sealed Beta Chamber

## Name

The Sealed Beta Chamber

## Overview

The Sealed Beta Chamber is the never-opened inner round room at the center of **The Passage Round**—reserved, in Delci Beta's own framing, for a special occasion that has not yet come.

## Description

It sits in the middle of the tube-like Round: another round room reached only through a wooden door bearing a solid **24-carat gold** emblem engraved with a **B**. That door has never been opened in this dreamer's mind.

In front of it stands a heavy gate, barricaded and secured with **five** different locks. The chamber's purpose is not explained in shore gossip; Delci's sparse comments treat it as waiting, not forgotten.

Around it, the Passage Round's concrete benches continue as if an audience might one day sit for whatever the sealed occasion requires.

## Lore

The **B** is widely taken as **Beta**—Delci's name held in gold at the heart of his own architecture. Whether the chamber hides a place, a memory, a person, or a vow remains open.
