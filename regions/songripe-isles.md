# Songripe Isles

## Name

Songripe Isles

## Overview

Songripe Isles are the scattered western coastal islet chains of Wabet where fruit often will not ripen but songs do. Tide-song cousins live here who rarely reach Nauw markets and do not need to.

## Description

Small beaches, rope bridges, bird-loud mornings. **Soen Tide** keeps song-calendars that replace harvest calendars. A resonance pocket makes orchard logic fail and musical logic thrive—locals call it mercy; mainland traders call it inconvenience.

*Meet My Bird* and *Blue Banana* are isle humor about companions and unripe jokes.

## Lore

These isles fill Wabet’s blank western ocean edge. They can host gentle or eerie sagas without opening Averra’s sealed mystery or Calareth’s Watch rules.

## Stories or Depictions

*The Isles Where Fruit Will Not Ripen* is the standing explanation-myth.

## Notes

Coastal blank filled; keep distinct from Outer Rim whale culture.
