# Kiln Laughter Yard

## Name

Kiln Laughter Yard

## Overview

**Kiln Laughter Yard** is Secondfire’s open work-court where kilns, slate remakes, and Hearth Laughter Week practice share one dusty square—second beginning as noise and heat.

## Description

Ash on boots, glaze on fingers, laughter that is scheduled and still real. Broken tools get apologized to (Row cousins would recognize the manners). Children stamp soft clay with joke seals. Serious Compact talk happens after the yard cools.

## Lore

Second-beginning craft geography. Remaking selves without costume; laughter as infrastructure. Distinct from Claimscar teeth and Flagweek cloth wars.

## Stories or Depictions

Hearth Laughter Week anchors; slate fragment teaching.
