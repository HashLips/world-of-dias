# Saltwhisper Road

## Name

Saltwhisper Road

## Overview

Saltwhisper Road is the main restricted dune route across Lumira Sands—less a highway than a negotiated permission. Wind over salt crust is said to “whisper” warnings before mirage hours; Keepers treat that folk line as useful acoustics, not prophecy.

## Description

The road appears and vanishes with dune creep. Official markers are sparse on purpose. Caravans without Silent Dune Keep assent are turned, taxed in labor, or left to their own wrong maps.

*Road To* captures the long pale monotony that makes people invent shortcuts.

## Lore

Saltwhisper Road is where Lumira’s calm becomes policy. The conflict is not banditry first—it is whether fruit-money from Nauw may rewrite desert ethics.
