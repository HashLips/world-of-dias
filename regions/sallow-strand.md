# Sallow Strand

## Name

Sallow Strand

## Overview

Sallow Strand is a fishing and glasswort hamlet on the Lumira coast of Wabet. It lies east of Glasswake Rock, where the Lumira sand gives way to shingle and the sea. It is known for one thing: the Letter House, which keeps what the sea brings ashore until the person it is for comes. This is the quiet Wabet shore of the Island Saga. The Kestry Letter came ashore near here, Maren Kestry made her home here, and the letter-keepers formed here.

## Description

Twenty low timber houses with grass on their roofs sit in the lee of the last dune before the sea, with drying racks for fish and glasswort and a cookhouse where arguments happen over fish stew. A dune path climbs from the shingle to the Letter House at the top of the strand. To the west, half a morning's walk, is Glasswake Rock, black against the sky. Low Bell Cove lies between them, a quarter of a morning's walk west of the strand. North, the Keep track runs up through dune country to the Saltwhisper road-head, and salt-carts come down it twice a season.

**Sensory:** salt, peat smoke, drying fish, glasswort green and salt-sweet, wind off the sea, a hush that is a law.

**Climate:** Lumira coast, dry and bright in Brightpass, cold sea fogs in Wetwake.

## Lore

Under the Lumira Silence Law people here walk softly, never shout at the sea, and do not make the sea's things into spectacles within sight of Glasswake. Saltjaw children's wish-bottles have washed up here for generations. After the saga, the Keep sent word to every cove from Glasswake to the Coral side that no boat goes out from Lumira to look for the Stillmouth.
