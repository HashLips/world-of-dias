# No-Captain Hour Pier

## Name

No-Captain Hour Pier

## Overview

**No-Captain Hour Pier** is the Driftfall stretch where, for a declared short window, no one may shout captaincy loud enough to claim the dock—work continues; rank theater pauses.

## Description

A ordinary pier that becomes extraordinary by clock and custom. Lines are shared. Tools pass hand to hand. Insults wait. When the hour ends, flags and obligations return; until then the wood belongs to the work.

Visitors who try to buy the hour with coin are laughed off. Locals who break it earn the floating-flag insult: service abandoned mid-pull.

Salt, tar, and the soft clap of floating flags. Megaphones stay down. A person who was captain at the bell is a person with wet boots at the next minute, entitled to coil rope and not entitled to assign the coiling. Flags on the short staffs lift, stall, and argue with each other in a wind that does not care who owns the hull. Tea appears without a host—that is the tell; if someone hosts, the hour has already failed. Autonomy here is not romance about lawlessness. It is a scheduled refusal to be ordered.

Claimscar runners sometimes watch from the landward end without owning the pier. They are welcome as bodies and unwelcome as delegations. A delegation is a voice with a rank hiding inside it.

## Lore

Comic twin of floating-flag seriousness. Myth cousins about flags that never docked hang in the telling. Keeps salvage days from becoming daily civil war.

Ignore the hour and you do not get a duel. You get a harbor that routes around your name. Boats still need hands. They stop needing yours. Depictions that turn the pier into a lawless carnival miss the discipline: the quiet, the flag, the tea, the bell back into rank that everyone hears and nobody cheers.
