# Averra damp studios

## Name

Averra damp studios

## Overview

**Averra damp studios** are the humid, half-hidden work rooms on Averra Isle where ink, spore-wash, and unfinished canvases live—more workshop weather than tourist gallery.

## Description

Cliff-side and courtyard rooms that stay cool and wet enough for inkcap culture. Floors take black spore wash stains. Windows are small on purpose. Mainland gallery-goers hear of them as rumor cousins to Sketchy Gallery manners: soft looking, soft refusal of doors that are not for them.

Inkcap mushrooms grow in the damp corners; cheap ink for studies that may never leave the isle.

## Lore

Supports Averra’s “obscured present”: art labor happens, but release is gated (Quiet Cloister / Sael). Gallery customs on the mainland point here when they say Averra without meaning the whole cliff.
