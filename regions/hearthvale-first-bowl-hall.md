# Hearthvale First-Bowl Hall

## Name

Hearthvale First-Bowl Hall

## Overview

**Hearthvale First-Bowl Hall** is where new Long Gate arrivals receive heat, a bowl, and a floor before anyone asks their Compact history—shelter-first made into a room.

## Description

Long tables, kiln-warm walls, slate boards for later terms. Volunteers ladle stew while clerks wait at the far end, deliberately not first. Children learn the order: body, then story. Hard-line yards call it naive; rebuild kitchens call it why people survive week one.

## Lore

Practical signature of second-beginning right. Breakfast continuity cousins Flagweek’s anti-weapon table law, but here the politics are exile repair, not weekly flags.

## Stories or Depictions

Long Gate south arrivals; shelter-first rule in action.

## Notes

Parent: Hearthvale. Open question: when the Hall is full, who sleeps outside without becoming “judged first.”
