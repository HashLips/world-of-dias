# Claimscar Cookfire Circle

## Name

Claimscar Cookfire Circle

## Overview

Claimscar Cookfire Circle is the yard’s shared kettle ground—breakfast may be shared even when claims cannot.

## Description

A ring of scorched stones, iron hooks, and tarps that lean toward heat. Morning stew is for anyone who brought wire, wood, or news; noon stew is crew business. Jokes run mean and affectionate. Dogs wait with professional patience.

Smell: smoke, tar, peppers. Sound: ladle arguments that resolve into second bowls.

## Lore

Shared Table culture under frontier law: the kettle is peace; the paint-mark is war. Garen Scar eats here when he wants to be seen as captain, not only scar.
