# Driftfall Piers

## Name

Driftfall Piers

## Overview

**Driftfall Piers** are the working water-edges of Driftfall—rope, salvage crates, brine air, and flags that mean service only while someone still holds the risk.

## Description

Timber that has been repaired more times than named. Skiffs nose between larger hulls. Children learn knots before letters. The Salt Ledger sits a short inland walk; Claimscar’s teeth are farther up the grit. At certain hours a pier goes quiet under No-Captain Hour custom—no one claims the dock loud enough to start a fight.

Smell: tar, fish, wet rope. Sound: hammers, arguments that resolve into shared pulls. Beauty: sunset on water that forgives nothing and still feeds everyone who works it.

## Lore

Authority floats (floating-flag rule). Rescue manners from Outer Rim cousins visit in story. Not a tourist boardwalk—a lived machine for arriving, leaving, and refusing to sink.
