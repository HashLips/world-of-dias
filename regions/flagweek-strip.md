# Flagweek Strip

## Name

Flagweek Strip

## Overview

Flagweek Strip is the underdocumented middle ground where Hearthvale’s restoration ethic and Driftfall’s freeband grit face each other. Some towns change flags weekly without changing breakfast. That is not a joke to residents. It is a survival grammar.

## Description

The strip is farms, lean inns, and painted poles. **Mir Weekly**, a mayor who refuses permanent sides, posts each week’s flag with a public meal that tastes the same regardless of cloth. The Three Opinions sometimes pass through as traveling argument, leaving slogans like weather.

## Lore

Flagweek Breakfast Rule: allegiance may rotate; the morning table must not become a weapon. Break that, and the strip remembers longer than any flag.

## Stories or Depictions

*The Town That Changes Flags* is both myth and municipal minutes.

## Notes

Borderland saga fuel with humor and knives in the same drawer.
