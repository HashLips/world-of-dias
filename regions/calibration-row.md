# Calibration Row

## Name

Calibration Row

## Overview

Calibration Row is a third-ring workshop strip where Kharad and allied craftspeople tune loads, tools, and small resonant devices for Basin clients. Some guilds here refuse Resonant House classification codes on principle.

## Description

The Row smells of oil, warm metal, and argument. **Dox Khar** heads a calibration circle that will fix a cart-axle frequency and still decline to stamp a House form. Operators from the core sometimes send anonymous work; the Row pretends not to notice.

Sketchy Gallery sits culturally nearby—art and craft trading sideways glances.

## Lore

This is craft politics: who owns the right to name a frequency event. Saga fuel without requiring High Resonant schism plots.
