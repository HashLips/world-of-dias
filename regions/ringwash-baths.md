# Ringwash Baths

## Name

Ringwash Baths

## Overview

Ringwash Baths are the second-ring public baths of Veloria City—steam, tiled channels, and the city’s softest gossip exchange.

## Description

Warm mineral water from basin aquifers; cedar benches; chalk notices for quiet hours and fair-week overflow. Outer-ring laborers and third-ring clerks share steam without sharing politics if they choose. Children splash in the shallow court; Choirglass guests modulate brightness low so reflections stay kind.

Smell: soap-citrus and wet stone. Sound: low talk, ladle clink, one stubborn singer every other evening.

## Lore

A Veloria week without Ringwash feels unfinished to locals. Arguments started in Pot Signal Lane are sometimes cooled here on purpose.

## Notes

Happiness infrastructure for capital life—pair with Softfruit Table Hall.
