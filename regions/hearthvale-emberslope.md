# Hearthvale Emberslope

## Name

Hearthvale Emberslope

## Overview

A dry, ash-kissed slope country above and around Hearthvale approach roads—ember-colored dust that reminds newcomers they are not in Nauw’s green basin anymore.

## Description

Boots take the color of the road. Secondfire kiln smoke joins slope dust on rebuild weeks. Clear mornings still happen; Vent Haze cousins can lean inland from Driftfall seasons. Exile roads climb or skirt the slope toward first bowls.

## Lore

Indexed under Landforms of F432. Terrain bone for western Sorel—paired with Driftfall Salvage Shore in the east and Good Trade mountain further Driftfall-side.

### Nesting

**Dias** → **F432** → **Sorel** → **Hearthvale** → **Hearthvale Emberslope**.

## Stories or Depictions

Second-beginning songs call the slope “the first color that is not shame.” Long Gate travelers feel it underfoot before they feel Compact welcome.

## Notes

Dangerous for underprovisioned lone arrivals; safer in Compact company. Not a combat zone brand.
