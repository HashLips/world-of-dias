# Third-to-Core Seam Cafe

## Name

Third-to-Core Seam Cafe

## Overview

**Third-to-Core Seam Cafe** sells bitter tea to techs who work near the core seam. Conduit glass, load-curve napkins, and pipe tones make it a listening post for what Operators call noise. **Vela Thirdslit** first sketches noise-with-shape here.

## Description

A cafe on the approach weather between third-ring work and deeper core slots: bitter tea, sticky tables, napkins that somehow always have load curves doodled on them. Conduit glass shows building hum more honestly than tourist windows. **Halla Wirehum**’s late tones sometimes answer in the pipes—mutter echo without a neat House caption.

Customers are techs, clerks off stamp duty, and people who need caffeine more than comfort. Softfruit jokes thin nearer the core; the cafe’s bitterness matches the air. It is not Orrin’s workshop and not a ferry office. It is where ignored noise gets social.
