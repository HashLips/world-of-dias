# Sprout Vent

## Overview

**Sprout Vent** is a modest but striking active volcano on Driftfall's southeastern flank—small by distant-realm standards, yet dominant against the low frontier ground around it.

## Description

The vent sits inland from Driftfall's shark-heavy eastern beaches, along the southeastern edge of the territory where broken strata and heat-warped stone already read as unstable.

From a distance it does not look like a single cone. Locals describe a **cluster profile**: one central throat with several smaller sprouting points along its shoulders and lower slopes, as if the mountain were trying to grow extra mouths.

Eruptions are infrequent. Between events the vent steams, grumbles, and keeps its reputation alive in dock stories without shutting down daily life.

When it does erupt, overflow and thrown magma can travel farther than the mountain's size would suggest—and that is when **vent-sprout spread** becomes a serious concern.

## Lore

Driftfall crews treat Sprout Vent as both landmark and warning clock.

- A quiet year means routine travel along southeastern routes.
- A visible glow or ash thread means recheck camps, reroute salvage lines, and argue about meaning before the magma even arrives.

Where contagious overflow has landed in past cycles, the landscape now carries **daughter vents**—knee-high to waist-high mounds that still warm at night. Together with the parent cluster, they give the southeastern approach its unsettling "many sprout" silhouette.

## Stories or Depictions

No dedicated artwork entry yet. Southeastern Driftfall maps and oral charts usually mark the vent cluster with multiple tick-marks around one core symbol.

## Notes

Not to be confused with ordinary wildfire or tar seeps common elsewhere in Sorel; Sprout Vent is the only known site in Driftfall where eruption behavior includes **vent-sprout spread**.
