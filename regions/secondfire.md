# Secondfire

## Name

Secondfire

## Overview

Secondfire is a Hearthvale kiln town where displaced people remake tools, plates, and selves. The kilns burn for craft and for the metaphor everyone pretends is only craft.

## Description

Workshop courtyards, slate boards of names-in-progress, laughter week banners stored between seasons. **Hanna Kiln** runs a teaching fire that accepts Nauw shame-cases and Sorel born-tired alike—unless someone refuses the Compact’s second beginning entirely.

**Sensory:** kiln heat on face, slate chalk dust, stew and ash, banner cloth in loft storage, emberslope dust on boots when the dry wind comes, laughter that sounds like defiance becoming home.

**Climate:** Hearthvale frontier air—rebuild-smoke seasons, clear Flagweek-adjacent mornings, dust when Emberslope wind leans in. Not Softfall; not basin gilding hour as a civic gift (though travelers bring the memory).

Alwen Rusk’s stewardship is felt as supply routes and quiet inspections, not sermons.

## Lore

Secondfire is proof-of-concept for “displacement is not the end.” It is also where refusal becomes a story: not every exile wants remaking.

**Place web:** people (Hanna Kiln; Alwen Rusk; Compact neighbors) · manners (second-beginning craft; first bowl) · craft (kiln fires; slate fragment cousins) · story (*The Exile Who Refused Second Beginning*; *The Kiln Fires of Second Beginning*).
