# Outer Rim Distress Listen

## Name

Outer Rim Distress Listen

## Overview

**Outer Rim Distress Listen** is the Rim-Rest lookout spit where horns are watched for three-short distress patterns—rescue obligation before debt, sung about later if everyone lives.

## Description

Wind platform, rope rail, a horn rack, a slate of last signals. Young fishers learn the difference between boast noise and ugly distress. Night’s breeze songs sometimes start here when the sea goes too quiet.

## Lore

Mutual rescue rule made into a place. *Distress Before Debt* could have begun with a listen from this spit. Joy of dry clothes after.
