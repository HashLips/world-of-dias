# Flagweek Dust Reach

## Name

Flagweek Dust Reach

## Overview

The open dust and cloth-weather ground around Flagweek Strip—where allegiance poles meet dry wind and breakfast rules try to outlast rumor.

## Description

Cloth snaps; dust rises; tables appear when rules hold. Between Flagweeks the Reach is just road and grit. During Flagweek it becomes temporary city weather: joy, argument, parade-goats, and the pressure of who claims which pole.

## Lore

Indexed under Landforms of F432. Civic bone on land—landform plus festival weather. Not Nauw ring-fair geometry.

### Nesting

**Dias** → **F432** → **Sorel** → **Flagweek Strip** → **Flagweek Dust Reach**.

## Stories or Depictions

Flagweek songs treat dust as confetti and as warning. Freeband manners decide whether the Reach feels like table or teeth.

## Notes

Safe when breakfast rules hold; Dangerous when allegiance weather turns and you are alone without a Freeband name.
