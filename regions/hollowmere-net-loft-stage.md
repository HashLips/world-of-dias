# Hollowmere Net-Loft Stage

## Name

Hollowmere Net-Loft Stage

## Overview

Hollowmere Net-Loft Stage is the highest public loft where First Line Answers, storm check-ins, and evening songs share one rope-height floor.

## Description

Nets hung as curtains; lantern hooks in a breathing line; a clear circle where singers stand and apprentices listen. Day: mending and gossip. Dusk: songs that are also attendance. Bad weather: roll call that can shame a missing raft.

Joy: fruit-barge nights when the loft becomes a welcome. Fear: a silence where a voice should answer.

## Lore

Jessa Quill’s practical memory lives here. The stage is Hollowmere’s music corner and its civic clock.

## Notes

Port happiness infrastructure that doubles as pressure.
