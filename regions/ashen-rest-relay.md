# Ashen Rest Relay

## Name

Ashen Rest Relay

## Overview

**Ashen Rest Relay** is a declared Hearthline stop where heat, shelter, and Ember Keeper checks must clear before a route push continues—**no rest plan, no route** made into a building.

## Description

Warm corridor, spare mats, chalk board of last heat readings. Unsupported long marches get turned back without shame theater. Recovery continuity is routine care: water, warmth, sleep windows. Miracle speeches get quiet looks.

## Lore

Rest viability rule geography. F840 ethics on the road. Distinct from Hearthvale first-bowl (exile arrival) though both refuse body-last judgment.

## Stories or Depictions

Hearthline travel planning; Ember Keeper maintenance.

Parent: Ashen Hearthline. Open question: who lies on the heat board when a convoy wants to look brave.
