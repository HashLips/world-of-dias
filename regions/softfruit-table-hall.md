# Softfruit Table Hall

## Name

Softfruit Table Hall

## Overview

Softfruit Table Hall is a long open hall near Veloria’s outer-market gates where anyone may sit if they bring fruit to share or pay the ladle fee.

## Description

Long benches, hanging pans of citrus and soft orchard fruit, clay bowls stacked by the door. Axiomorph hosts sometimes reset plate geometry after rush hour; Palewind season the steam with spice-motes. The hall posts the Shared Table manners in three scripts and one tone-mark.

Joke: “If you can’t afford Softfruit, you’re not hungry yet—you’re just proud.” Fear: pickpockets during ring-fair crush, not monsters.

## Lore

Architectural proof of [`F432 Shared Table`](../cultures/f432-shared-table.md). Merchants settle small debts here before they harden into Pot Signal favors.

### Nesting

**Dias** → **F432 (frequency realm)** → **Nauw** → **Veloria City** → **Softfruit Table Hall**. The hall is a local place of shared-table manners inside the capital stack. Table warmth and ladle customs are local resonance practice—not a frequency realm of their own.
