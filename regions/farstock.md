# Farstock

## Name

Farstock

## Overview

Farstock is a narrow shop on Veloria’s outer ring, one street off the market loop, behind an old door and under no guild sign. Adults on the loop treat it as a story children use to make a dull errand feel chosen. The door is real. The street is real. **Pim Farstock** keeps it.

## Description

Stone step worn lower on the left, where people hesitate and then commit. The door is older than the frame, iron-strapped, and sticks in wet weather as if it prefers a conversation to a swing. Inside, the room is one long shelf-run and a counter with a dent where elbows have asked for things. Light comes from a high slit and from whatever small glow the stock allows itself. It smells of peel-sweet, chalk dust, bread-smoke caught in a stopper, and the cold mineral note of a stone that changes color.

The wonder is crowded and ordinary at once. Flexible tick pencils lie in a jar. A festival sky-whale lantern, folded small, hangs where children have to look up. Ring-fair tokens from years nobody is spending fill a clay bowl. A damp-studio color bottle, far from Averra, sits as a guest pigment, not a portal. Peel-sweet herb dries in a bunch by the latch. None of these are the reason three teenagers came. They came for the stone that takes the color of a hand. Most of the other stock does one small true thing and then stops, which is the shop’s manners.

There is no guild mark on the lintel. Pim says a sign would make the street promise a service. Farstock does not promise. It keeps.

## Lore

Outer-ring talk splits. Children say the shop appears when you need a thing you cannot name. Porters say it is where Pim sells leftovers and lets the leftovers grow a legend because legends are free advertising. Both are ruder than the room. The shop is there on ordinary days. What it will not do is sell ease. A card behind the counter, copied in Pim’s hand from holding-cloth talk he will not pretend to own, says that ease you can purchase is the brittle kind. He does not read it aloud as a lesson. He lets it sit where a person asking for a feeling can see the sentence and still choose to hear it as shop policy.

Farstock is not the Sketchy Gallery, not a Softfruit hall, not Pot Signal Lane, and not Calibration Row. Those places have their own work. This one sells, keeps, and sometimes refuses.

## Stories or Depictions

The Farstock Saga is the first long telling. Before it, the shop lived as loop gossip and as a door children dared each other to find.

## Notes

Stock is wonder with a limit. Do not treat the shelves as a catalog of roads, ferries, or purchasable hearttide. The Heartlean Colorstone is kept, not offered. After the saga it is kept again, and the door is no longer only a children’s dare.
