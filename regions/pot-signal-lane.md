# Pot Signal Lane

## Name

Pot Signal Lane

## Overview

Pot Signal Lane is a second-ring Veloria district where ordinary housing hides an ordinary-looking black market: flower pots as signals, favors as currency, and VEL debt that never quite reaches the Operator core.

## Description

Windowsills speak. A red bloom facing inward means one kind of trouble; a bare pot on the third step means another. **Lira Pot** runs messages for people who cannot afford official channels and do not want Resonant House filing.

Harold’s street world is adjacent in spirit—this lane can host its own protagonists without walking them through DEB Gate oaths.

## Lore

The lane’s code is folk infrastructure. Guild collectors hate it. Children learn it before they learn algebra. Mistaking a signal can start a week of wrong doors.

## Stories or Depictions

Street-flower lore from *Veloria Street Flower Pot* and *Twelve Flowers* feeds the lane’s grammar.

## Notes

Urban underbelly saga space that stays off the Harmonic gate/core spine.
