# Outer Rim Keel Banks

## Name

Outer Rim Keel Banks

## Overview

Shallow-to-sudden banks off the Outer Rim where keels learn manners—fog lanes, shark talk, and Singing Fishermen routes that treat depth as speech.

## Description

Not a tourist reef. Banks rise under mist; chart notes disagree after storms. Fishermen name lanes by song and check-in, not by House survey alone. Danger is ordinary: wrong fog, wrong pride, keel-shark weather. Joy is a clean crossing and a loft that answered.

## Lore

Indexed under Landforms of F432. Complements Hollowmere’s cove with the water-body that can kill a story. Local ferry / rim crossing culture applies; not a band ferry.
