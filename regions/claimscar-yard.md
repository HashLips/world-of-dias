# Claimscar Yard

## Name

Claimscar Yard

## Overview

Claimscar Yard is an inland Driftfall crew yard where Freeband crews stake claims on Sprout Vent runoff, salvage rights, and the ugly work that eastern shore hang-about prefers not to advertise.

## Description

Tarps, scarred hull pieces, cookfires, and painted claim marks. **Garen Scar**, Free-Captain of the yard’s dominant crew, treats vent-sprout windows like weather wars. Vexra Coil’s wider Drift Crews politics brush the yard without owning every fire.

This is not Good Trade mountain hospitality and not Delci’s dream path. It is dirt-and-rope Driftfall.

## Lore

Claimscar vent-claim rule governs how long a mark holds and what happens when two crews paint the same sprout night. Breakfast can be shared; claims cannot.
