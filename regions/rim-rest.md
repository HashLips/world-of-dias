# Rim-Rest

## Name

Rim-Rest

## Overview

Rim-Rest is a raft-and-pier cluster in a leeward Outer Rim bay. For part of the year, families sleep over water as much as on land. Boats moor in rings; gossip travels along gangplanks faster than along any road.

## Description

Homes are part pier, part hull, part borrowed shore. Children learn to walk rope before they learn to run stone. Markets swell during Bright Inshore and thin to tea-and-repair circles in Hush Weeks.

**Pell Rim** brokers news between raft rings without claiming captaincy—an informal post that can make or break a season’s trust.

## Lore

Rim-Rest teaches that community can be buoyant and still strict: a missed storm check-in from a raft-ring is as serious as from a loft. Outsiders who treat the place as a holiday pier learn otherwise by the second tide.
