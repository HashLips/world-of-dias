# Driftfall Salvage Shore

## Name

Driftfall Salvage Shore

## Overview

The wreck-and-rib strand of Driftfall where hulls become houses and tide leaves claimable iron—Sorel’s coastal landform, not a Nauw beach holiday.

## Description

Wet rope, crab-pinch, forge breath from yards inland. Ribs of old keels stand like half-temples. Freebands paint claim marks on uprights; salt eats paint; people repaint. Fog can smell sweet-rot when Vent Haze leans coastward. Joy shows as roof-raising from a washed timber; pressure shows as yard teeth and wrong-claim fights.

## Lore

Indexed under Landforms of F432. Piers and Salt Ledger sit on or above this shore’s manners. Not Outer Rim fishing wealth; not Wabet clean coast.

## Stories or Depictions

Drift Hour stories walk this shore when no captain owns the weather. Claimscar cookfires light the inland edge at dusk.

## Notes

Sacred to remakers as work-ground; dangerous to lone new arrivals after dark—inhabitant-facing, not a combat table.
