# Elder Shelf

## Name

Elder Shelf

## Overview

Elder Shelf is a clifftop band of wind-scoured trees ringed in gray stone. No building is allowed inside the ring. The Watch issues day passes for meditation or naming ceremonies. The mood is quiet, not secret.

## Description

Wind writes the trees into permanent lean. Stone markers forbid overnight camps and tool caches. Conflict can happen here without inventing a cult: a day pass denied, a naming disputed, a visitor who treats silence as emptiness.

## Lore

Elder Shelf is Calareth’s pressure valve—space to feel the island’s different resonance without turning it into a temple plot.
