# Cinder Nook

## Name

Cinder Nook

## Overview

Cinder Nook is a wind-sheltered Outer Rim pocket where brine, smoke, and charcoal meet. Children learn not to scorch a boot on the communal cinder path to the high-tide mark—twice is a local joke with a bruise.

## Description

Racks of slow-dried fish line the nook. Tar pots simmer for hull work. In Low Winter, cinder smoke “writes” weather on the bluffs for those who can read it. **Bran Cinder** is the present smoke-reader most apprentices are pointed toward: not a mystic, a technician of wind and ash.

The town is quieter than Hollowmere and denser with work. Romance here looks like shared tongs and correctly timed racks.

## Lore

Cinder Nook supplies preserved protein when Bright Inshore glory fades. Its politics are small: rack rights, charcoal allotments, and who may speak the bluff-reading aloud to outsiders.

## Stories or Depictions

*Meal 1* is sometimes hung in mending rooms as a joke about hunger that still insists on manners.

## Notes

Good setting for craft-and-weather stories; pairs with Hollowmere’s louder ritual life.
