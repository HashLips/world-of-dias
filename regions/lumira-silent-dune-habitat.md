# Lumira Silent Dune Habitat

## Name

Lumira Silent Dune Habitat

## Overview

Pale dunes, hidden water, Listening Heat, and Keep assent paths where silence is ecology—not empty parkland.

## Description

Glass dune flats glare at noon. Lizards and jackals keep hush hours. Serpents and dune-drakes make noise expensive. Quiet Well is a green node the habitat protects with wrong maps. Oasis plants and insect hush are as important as fangs.

**Who thrives:** Keepers, soft-voiced travelers, dune-adapted small life.  
**Who strains:** loud fruit caravans; map-publishers of water.

## Lore

Habitat under Ecosystems and Habitats of Dias. Hazard creatures teach Silence Law; peoples (Keep) steward without paving the desert into Nauw.
