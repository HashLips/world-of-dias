# Driftfall Salvage Tide Habitat

## Name

Driftfall Salvage Tide Habitat

## Overview

Wet rope, hull ribs, tide leave, crabs, and Freeband yards where salvage and shore life braid—Sorel’s coastal living system.

## Description

Tide brings iron and story. Rope-crabs pinch; scav-dogs argue claims; fish of the Salt Ledger keep shore ledgers honest in their own way. People build houses from ribs; paint peels; paint returns. Vent Haze can lean coastward in Hushlean.

**Who thrives:** rope crews, crabs, Freeband breakfast tables.  
**Who strains:** lone new arrivals after dark; wrong-claim painters.
