# Basin Mist Orchard Habitat

## Name

Basin Mist Orchard Habitat

## Overview

The living cover of the Velorian Basin: mist ditches, orchard humidity, market-edge gardens, and gilding-hour light where capital roads and soft green share the same air.

## Description

Morning mist holds herb scent; carts cut silver tracks. Bananas arrive as freight more than local grove kings, but mist-herb, corner mints, and bee-loud hedges make the basin feel edible. Lap-Cats own sills; Ringroad Dogs own circuits; oxen own dawn haul. Softlane Hares flash at berm edges when stillness is not the day’s lesson.

**Who thrives:** market families, garden keepers, bees, cats, draft beasts.  
**Who strains:** dry-country visitors; anyone who expects Wabet Softfall instead of civic humidity.
