# Glassfold Phase Bench

## Name

Glassfold Phase Bench

## Overview

The **Glassfold Phase Bench** is where Veil Scribes sit to stamp reveal-window records—wax, confidence marks, and the refusal to let unstamped certainty become policy.

## Description

Cold glass light, one long bench worn smooth, stamp kits in locked trays. During wall-eye intervals the bench empties; after a window it fills with quiet arguments about corroboration. Fear documented is honorable; charisma without a stamp is not.

## Lore

Phase-stamp rule made furniture. Training exemplars dry here. Basin folk who copy the ritual with tea clocks are soft cousins, not equals.

## Stories or Depictions

Wall of Eyes witness texture; F610 reveal manners.
