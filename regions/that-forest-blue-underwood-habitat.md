# That Forest Blue Underwood Habitat

## Name

That Forest Blue Underwood Habitat

## Overview

Deep shade, blue-litter hush, Listener rumor, and bark-wolf edges—Wabet’s uncanny green, not Softfall picnic canopy.

## Description

Litter dusts boots blue. Paths prefer not to be shouted on. Listeners (rumor) and bark-wolves (teeth) both shape trail ethics. Extraction versus Open Hand pressure lives here as plant and story (Blue Litter Husk). Fellgrove is a named stand inside this pressure.

**Who thrives:** Listeners (if real), careful Forest manners, shadow-lynx rumor.  
**Who strains:** cruel tongues; bottling crews; loud timber dreams.
