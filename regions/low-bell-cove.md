# Low Bell Cove

## Name

Low Bell Cove

## Overview

Low Bell Cove is a small cove on the Lumira coast between Sallow Strand and Glasswake Rock, a quarter of a morning's walk west of the hamlet. Things lost off the Sorel coast and carried west by the Shelf Set, and by the outward run during a Long Slack, tend to come ashore here. The Saltjaw survey canister landed here. It is where Maren Kestry waited, and where the answering signal came from the sea.

## Description

A small bite out of the dune line: a curve of grey shingle and pale sand between two low arms of rock, so ordinary a coast-walker would pass it without a look. A few strokes out in the shallows at its mouth lies an old bronze bell, green with age and half-buried on its side, with weed growing from the lip. It fell from some boat nobody remembers. At the lowest tides, when small waves run over it, the clapper rolls and the bell speaks once, dully, underwater. On the day the water stopped it was silent.

Sorel flotsam collects in the sand: Driftfall nets and floats, a round-turned Saltjaw cap off some older canister, overlapped tarred planking.

## Lore

The hamlet named the cove for its bell. Glasswort gatherers know it as "where the Sorel side comes in." After chapter 30, the nine letter-keepers know it as the place of the answer and tell no one else why it matters.
