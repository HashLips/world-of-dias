# Long Gate

## Name

Long Gate

## Overview

**Long Gate** is the great Nauw-to-northern-Sorel crossing into **Hearthvale**—a long bridge-threshold people walk when exile, trade, or second-beginning hope makes staying north impossible. It is moral infrastructure as much as stone and timber: not a subway between frequencies, and not a casual weekend road.

## Description

From the Nauw south approach the Gate reads as a long, stubborn span: piers, wind-bitten rails, rest niches that never feel like inns. Southward the land opens toward Hearthvale’s rebuild weather. Toll talk, Concord escorts, and yard rumors thicken near the mouth. Travelers feel watched by both lands’ scribes even when no clerk is visible.

Unlike **Westfold Toll** (Sorel–Wabet supervised door), Long Gate is the shame/defiance braid of exile routes—the place *The Long Gate South* treats as a chapter of belonging grammar.

## Lore

Myth calls the building an argument that never fully ended ([`The Long Gate Argument`](../myths/the-long-gate-argument.md)). Civic Nauw treats it as an exit valve. Sorel treats arrivals as people who must learn yard manners. Active conflict lists keep it present-tense: belonging removed or refused often points here.

Open question: whose “yes” finally made the span walkable—and who still pretends they never agreed.

**Place web:** people (exile traffic; Concord shadow; Niche Watchers) · manners (Gate Wind Manners; assent cord; Sorel yard arrival) · craft (Long Gate Assent Cord; rest niche; water skin seat-rail) · story (*The Long Gate Argument*; *The Long Gate South*). South feet meet **Hearthvale Emberslope** before Secondfire bowls.

**Safety class:** Dangerous / heavy at peak argument; walkable with escorts and manners. Not a band ferry.
