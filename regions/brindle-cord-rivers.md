# Brindle Cord Rivers

## Name

Brindle Cord Rivers

## Overview

A braid of glass-bright feeders and barge channels that cord Brindle March to Glasswater and the Velorian Basin—named for how the channels look like ropes laid flat when mist lifts.

## Description

Not one named “great river,” but a working braid: shallow freight cuts, orchard ditches that become serious after rain, and a few deep cords barges trust. Mirror days make the water look like sky; market days make it smell like peel and wet wood. Toll clerks joke that the March has two spines—road and cord—and both want payment manners.

**Sensory:** banana-barge wake, frog-choir under bridges, stillness days when even rope-water holds its breath.

## Lore

Indexed under Landforms of F432. The Banana Draft and other market barges use these cords; they are not Outer Rim sea lanes and not band ferries.

## Stories or Depictions

Road songs sometimes call the cords “the March’s wet twin.” Stillness lore treats river hush and road hush as cousins, not the same event.

## Notes

Journey writers can route freight by water here without inventing a new sea.
