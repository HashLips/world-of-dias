# Freeband Tarp Edge

## Name

Freeband Tarp Edge

## Overview

**Freeband Tarp Edge** is the Claimscar fringe where tarp camps meet painted claim lines—breakfast shared on one side of the cloth, vent marks argued on the other.

## Description

Dust, patched canvas, chalk buckets. Children learn not to spill stew on wet paint. Garen Scar’s manners hold: claims have windows; false paint sinks. Night talk is loud; morning bowls are quieter on purpose.

## Lore

Separates care from property in space. Freeband identity without requiring Veloria stamps.

## Stories or Depictions

Vent-claim rule; Driftfall floating-flag cousins inland.
