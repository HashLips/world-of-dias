# Second Crossing Yard

## Name

Second Crossing Yard

## Overview

**Second Crossing Yard** is the Sorel seam where two roads and too many opinions meet—the place *The Rose at Second Crossing* made famous when dance turned a sneer into courage.

## Description

Compact chalk on one post, Freeband cloth on the other, grit underfoot. Crowds gather for arguments and sometimes stay for rhythm. A blue cloth rose appears after good nights. Not Claimscar’s painted claims; not Flagweek’s weekly cloth—just a yard that can harden or breathe.

## Lore

Embodied joy geography. Despise Dancer lore anchors here. Masks may watch from the edge without becoming the show.
