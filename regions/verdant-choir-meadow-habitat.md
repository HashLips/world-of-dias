# Verdant Choir Meadow Habitat

## Name

Verdant Choir Meadow Habitat

## Overview

Open wet-grass belts where choir-frogs set evening weather and Softfall edges the green—Wabet’s bright middle between canopy and dune.

## Description

Frog evenings rehearse the land. Paths join Open Hand Way without conquest geometry. Travelers rest; That Forest pressure sits at the darker tree line. Insects, wet seeds, and walker manners keep the meadow soft.

**Who thrives:** choir-frogs, Open Hand walkers, Softfall evening guests.  
**Who strains:** those who treat frog-song as noise to silence.
