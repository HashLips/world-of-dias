# The Salt Ledger

## Overview

The Salt Ledger is a known Driftfall pub set a short walk inland from the territory's eastern beaches—a low, salt-stained room where crews, artists, and idle watchers share benches without much ceremony.

## Description

From outside, the Ledger reads like any other salvaged shore building: driftwood fascia, a hand-painted sign whose letters have been repainted so many times the name is almost a texture. Inside, the floor stays slightly gritty no matter how often it is swept, and the light comes in sideways through bottle-glass windows that turn the afternoon gold.

The pub does not rush anyone. Regulars call this the point: you can sit for an hour with one cup, watch the door, watch the lane, watch the same face pass twice, and no one will ask what you are waiting for. Beach people drift in with wind in their hair; market runners collapse at the back; strangers from Hearthvale sometimes arrive looking for "the place where stories get kept without being written down."

A corner table near the rear wall is quietly understood as **Solven Wetch**'s when he is in town—covered in cuttings, small lino blocks, and the faint smell of ink.

## Lore

Driftfall crews treat the Ledger as neutral ground: not a court, not a crew hall, but a room where overlapping witnesses can overlap without picking a side. The name is older than the current building; people say it referred first to a literal salt ledger book kept at the bar, then to the habit of **recording what happened** in talk rather than ink.
