# Tea Slit Bench

## Name

Tea Slit Bench

## Overview

**Tea Slit Bench** is a stone bench under a wall slit on Calibration Row where hands drink cylinder tea and argue stamps. Orrin Veld teaches F200 / F500 loud dramas against loveable home band F432 here. Primary Movement One teaching stage and aftermath wrist-itch geography.

## Description

Stone seat, wall slit breathing a thin ribbon of third-ring air, graduated tea cylinders on the ledge, **cylinder mint** optional. Chalk columns appear and get wiped. Graph-dust finches tick the eaves. Arguments pause for honest refills under **Row Tea-Cylinder Custom**—lines do not flatter; cups do.

The bench is public enough for apprentices and private enough for scrap talk. No-ferry chalk sometimes appears beside teaching diagrams so nobody mistakes a band column for a ticket. Auntie Mosscoil may bring cups and be ignored affectionately. The slit does not open a door to another frequency; it opens air.

## Lore

Long before the Spectrum Frame, Row hands used the bench to cool throats after bitter tool fights. In the Calibration Saga, Orrin sits here with **Tam Brinkoil**, draws two columns, and maps the stack in plain talk: home, loud extremes, background bands later proved from inside F432—not a subway.

After helpful amber, cyan half-digit lies, refused widen temptation, partial publish, failed vault, and Caliburn escape into load-noise, Orrin returns alone some mornings. He teaches imaginary apprentices the same chalk: no Zero answer, no ferry. Sometimes the measure-mark on his wrist itches when a stranger passes—as if something friendly checked him and moved on. Haunt manners, not proof every passerby is the Echoform.
