# Stillhollow

## Name

Stillhollow

## Overview

Stillhollow is the cave inside **Ashcalm Rise** where **Vorrith Kael** resides. It is not a temple, market, or Keeper site. It is a dwelling kept so quiet that Wabet’s calm continues around it almost undisturbed.

## Description

The mouth of Stillhollow sits above the denser understory, a narrow stone slit that looks like shade until you are close enough to feel cooler air. Inside, the cave opens into a low chamber with grey walls that take shadow too eagerly. Light is scarce by design: a single hanging lamp, a table, a resting place, and the permanent presence of **the Still Pot**.

The chamber does not smell of rot. It smells of mineral damp and something cooked that should not have a kitchen smell—metal, fruit-pit bitterness, and a faint sweet heat like over-stirred frequency.

Vorrith does not fortify the entrance. The mountain’s obscurity is the fortification. Those who arrive usually arrive because someone already paid a price for the path.

## Lore

Stillhollow’s rule of survival is simple and unwritten: **do not bring Wabet’s noise into the hollow, and the hollow will not spill its noise into Wabet**. Vorrith keeps that bargain. Seekers sometimes do not.

A few canopy locals know the cave is occupied. They do not lead children there. They do not harvest nearby. They speak of it the way one speaks of a deep well: useful to name, dangerous to dare, best left covered by ordinary days.

## Stories or Depictions

*Never Love Darkness* is read as a seeker-audience at Vorrith’s table. *Soup in Bed* and *Pot of Greatfullness* are tied to the Still Pot’s presence in the hollow.
