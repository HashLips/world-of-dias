# Mercer Shed

## Name

Mercer Shed

## Overview

Mercer Shed is Dain Mercer's distribution house on the inner lip of Veloria's outer ring, where wagon fruit is broken into market lots and where, for one bad morning, a cloth and eight chairs turned the floor into a bidding room.

## Description

High doors for carts. A chalk floor. Ordinary crates of Softfruit and Market Banana stacked where the air can move. Along the inner wall the building borrows the third ring: a loader arm on a rail, bright lamps, a counting desk with resonant tallies that are tools for marks, not a mind that talks. The outer doors still open on medieval stone and on oxen that do not care what the lamps cost.

The shed smells like ripe rooms smell: sweet, a little bruised, vinegar where a crate failed. On the day of the show a table stood in the cleared middle with a cloth that had been washed too carefully.

## Lore

Publicly the shed is how Wabet fruit becomes Nauw distribution. Privately, for a short while, it was also where a breed the road had not met was given a price. After the show the shed went back to crates, except that the clerks could not always find the man who owned the desk.

## Stories or Depictions

The Vitrin Saga's auction floor. Wagons still queued outside while the cloth came off. The shed did not replace the road. It bought from it and, that day, showed off beside it.

## Notes

Layered Veloria on purpose: outer-ring doors, third-ring tools in the same room. Not a core hypertech site. Valorian Operators are not involved.
