# Quiet Cloister

## Name

Quiet Cloister

## Overview

**Quiet Cloister** is the Averra Isle archive-and-meeting space where **Sael Corvin** decides which memories, leaves, and artifacts may leave the isle—and which stay sealed as kindness or power.

## Description

Not a public gallery. Stone arches, salt air that never quite smells like a mainland harbor, shelves that look curated rather than full. Visitors who reach it (rare) report soft light, few footsteps, and the sense that the room is listening harder than they are. Golden-tear legends attach to Sael’s appearances here more than to cliff paths.

Mainland boats usually stop short of the cloister; offshore courtesy keeps ceremonial hours from becoming boarding attempts.

## Lore

In *Veil of Averra*, Calen Brink meets Sael here without procession and receives one harmonic census leaf while bulk archives stay withheld. The cloister’s moral question stays open: is silence care, or control wearing care’s coat?
