# Warm Current Holding Cove

## Name

Warm Current Holding Cove

## Overview

**Warm Current Holding Cove** is a managed confluence pocket on the Bloomline Estuary’s amber-rose tributary, where groups stabilize local tone for gathering, healing, or careful talk. Hearttide is the weather. Consent is the door. Without the second, the first becomes a crowd wearing your feelings back at you.

The cove is infrastructure, the way a kitchen is infrastructure. It is not a clinic you franchise and not a war room with prettier water.

## Description

The cove reads as water and weather at once. Rose-amber haze lies over a shallow basin. Reeds lean toward laughter and away from sharp fear, a tilt you notice only when you have stood still long enough to stop performing calm. Tidehearts set Hearttide Holding Cloths along the rim, each cloth a warmed weight with a color bar at the edge that says, without a speech, what tone is being asked to stay.

Moodtide otters sometimes patrol the lip when the Bright Current tries to spill in too fast, bodies low, eyes bright, uninterested in your importance. Visitors feel their own mood become slightly public: a private grief surfaces as a cooler thread in the haze; a forced joke curdles the amber. Manners demand consent before deep sharing. You may stand in the shallows and keep your weather to yourself. You may not drag the cove into your triumph or your panic and call it honesty.

The sound is small water and cloth. Voices, when they come, come after the cloths are set. Smell is warm reed, salt-sweet, and the mineral of stones that have been held in hands.

## Lore

Holding coves are F380’s answer to the fact that feeling leaks. A group can borrow stability the way travelers borrow a roof, and they are expected to give it back cleaner than they found it. A cove that is forced cheerful collapses into brittle weather: smiles with a hard edge, otters gone, cloths cooling in fists. Grief is not banned. Grief fronts are redirected, given a side channel, a slower cloth, a person who has agreed to hear.

Ignore consent and the cove does not drown you. It stops holding. The haze thins to something like embarrassment made visible. People step out onto the bank and will not meet your eyes, not from cruelty, from the fatigue of having been used as an instrument. Softfruit guests from F432 recognize ladle manners in the cloth ritual: notice first, share first, do not stamp a mood into law because it was loud.

Tidehearts say the cove has refused fees. Warmth that can be bought stops being warmth and starts being leverage. That refusal is told as myth and practiced as Tuesday.

## Stories or Depictions

Maps annotate clear-day and heavy-day timings for the same cove path, because the walk changes when the emotional climate does. Depictions show cloths like low flags and a basin that glows without burning. Negotiation days are remembered by who asked before they spoke.

## Notes

Mood as weather. Not a lie detector, not a weapon, not a ferry. Do not turn hearttide into a machine for extracting truth.
