# Aumere

## Name

Aumere

## Overview

Aumere is a small, inhabited, physical island in F432 waters. It lay south-west of the Saltjaw shelf edge, off Sorel's south coast, inside the ring of the Counting Current and the Aumere Turning Boom, hidden by the Stillmouth Blue Mist. It is home to the Aumeri, about two hundred people. It appears on no chart, and its people wanted it that way. In the present age, after the Houses' expedition, it withdrew. When the blue lifted there was open water and no bottom on the lead. Where it went, and whether it can return, is not known.

Aumere is **not** an Echoform, a door, a crossing, a bleed site, or another band's territory. The Valorian Operators' drum read F432's own register throughout. It is an island whose people learned to keep it unfound.

## Description

The island, as Ansel Kestry saw it and drew it, and as the fleet saw it:

- **Cliffs:** pale, not high (perhaps twice the height of the Saltjaw headframe), on the seaward side.
- **Terraces:** cut level into the stone above the cliffs, stepping up the slope and green with small hard beans and a crisp silver-leaved salt-green. Low stone walls run along the terrace edges.
- **The seaward terrace and count-wall:** a long low wall at the cliff top. Pebbles from each morning's count lie on it in rows, with the oldest worn into the stone.
- **The high terrace:** the island's summit. On a few clear days a year you can see Sprout Vent's smoke from it.
- **The village:** about fifty turf-roofed stone houses in the lee of a long spine of dark rock, grassed over until they read as hillside, with a thread of peat smoke.
- **Cisterns and galleries:** chambers cut into the hillside, fed by rain channels on the terraces and by galleries driven into the rock spine to tap seeps.
- **The harbour:** a cleft in the cliff where stitched-plank narrow boats are drawn up on stone.
- **The cradles:** water-counterweighted lifts that raise people and loads up the cliff without hauling.
- **The south shingle:** where the ring current returns everything that tries to drift out except during a Long Slack.

**Climate:** inside the quiet water, no surf and no wind, except in the Hushlean storms that sometimes part the boom. Soft blue light at the edge of sight.

## Lore

The Aumeri remember **the Clearing**. In a clear year, when the blue was thin, a Long Shore boat found them, measured them and drew them. Then people came with claims and papers, took cut stone off the terrace walls and took the young to work, and called the islanders hazards when they would not go. There had been three or four times as many Aumeri before it. The survivors built the boom, learned the count, learned to keep the blue, and stopped going to the Long Shore. On the mainland the story survives only as the myth *The Shelf Clearing*, and nobody there connects it to a real island.

## Notes

- **Deliberately not pinned** on the map registry. Its approach, The Stillmouth, has an approximate pin in open sea.
- Do not publish a bearing or position. Do not disclose where it went or the mechanism of withdrawal.
- The Kestry Letter's sketch of its cliff is the only drawing from life.
