# Null-Edge Watch Cairn

## Name

Null-Edge Watch Cairn

## Overview

**Null-Edge Watch Cairn** is a contested name for a pile of stones, or for the memory of a pile of stones, said to stand where F500’s null concentration becomes noticeable enough to frighten a watcher without yet unmaking them. It is a rumor with a useful shape. It is not a destination you can put on a tour, and not a coordinate that makes Frequency Zero into a place with a door.

If the cairn is “there,” there is already the wrong word.

## Description

Survivor sketches disagree, and the disagreement is the most reliable thing about them. Some draw a cairn of dark slate that sheds grit into nowhere, pebbles leaving the hand and not arriving at the ground. Others draw an empty footprint, a circle of paler nothing where a cairn “was,” and the artist’s lines get thinner as they approach the circle, as if the pencil resented the assignment. A few refuse to draw and only write *do not finish this line* in the margin, which Open Hand humor borrowed and could not make funny.

Accounts that mention Forget-Texture talk about ground that looks like ground until you try to describe its grit. Color names slip. You say gray and mean the absence of the word gray. Footprints fill with absence rather than dust. People report a pressure behind the eyes, a sudden charity toward not knowing their own name for a minute, and then the shame of having almost been grateful.

No public survey confirms coordinates. Pocket mites, watching absence, shelf-empty fears: these cluster near the name on rumor maps the way moths cluster near a lamp they should not trust. The cairn is a warning story that happens to wear the costume of a landmark.

## Lore

The negation-proximity fear lives in this name. Retreat windows, in the stories, close faster than approach estimates. A person who walks “just to see” comes back missing a piece of afternoon and a willingness to open empty cupboards. F432 taboo-tech culture pins fake warning tags on classroom walls labeled with this cairn, which teaches dread as manners and also teaches how easily dread becomes a sticker.

Dockside rumor ties the Unraveled, Horizon-Nulls, and distant companies to the stones. Those ties stay rumor. They are not a field guide and not a promotion into confirmed kinds of being. Standing at a story about a cairn does not solve Frequency Zero. It does not sell a ferry. It does not make a sport of the edge.

Ignore the temporary-naming ban, the one mercy the stories agree on, and you give the margin a permanent word. Permanent words are handles. Handles are how people convince themselves they can carry null home in a jar. The ban exists so witnesses can say *something like a cairn, for now* and then stop.

## Stories or Depictions

Partial testimony: a clerk who returned without three hours and with a fear of empty shelves, unable to explain which three hours or why the shelves were the part that hurt. The myth says the cairn forgot its stones and the forgetting climbed into the teller. Depictions that try to be precise are the ones teachers cross out.

## Notes

Status **rumor**. Do not promote the Unraveled, Horizon-Nulls, or Distant Companies by way of this place. No ferry, no sport tourism, no solved abyss. Clues and pressure only.
