# Coral Strand

## Name

Coral Strand

## Overview

Coral Strand is Calareth’s southern reef-ledge beach: shallow, loud with water over shell, the island’s best place for tide-pool teaching, first swims, and the one concession to boisterous joy.

## Description

The strand teaches without a classroom. Children learn reef edges by scraped knees and Watch chalk marks. During Still Months the water mirrors; during Tide-Loud Weeks it speaks and the Watch shortens play windows.

Coris Vale is rumored to smile here more than elsewhere. Witnesses disagree on how much is projection.

## Lore

Coral Strand proves Calareth is not only prohibition. Joy is scheduled, watched, and still real.
