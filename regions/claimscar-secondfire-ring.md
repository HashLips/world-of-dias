# Claimscar Secondfire Ring

## Name

Claimscar Secondfire Ring

## Overview

**Claimscar Secondfire Ring** is Claimscar Yard’s evening fire, the hotter talk after morning stew. Vent-claim disputes get smoked soft here, or they get sharpened into night work. Breakfast can be shared when claims cannot. Secondfire is where that kindness frays or renews, and everyone in the yard knows which hour they are in by the way the tarps lean.

It is frontier grit. It is not a war stage. The ring is a negotiation that happens to burn.

## Description

Scorched stones sit outside the Cookfire Circle’s breakfast politics, a second geography for the same bodies. The morning circle faces inward, bowls close, steam doing some of the diplomacy. The ring faces the yard’s working dark: wire, salvage, the pale mouths of vents that nobody should name casually after dusk. Tarps lean differently at night, weighted with tools instead of bread. Crew marks glow low, paint that remembers daylight.

Garen Scar’s people treat secondfire as weather for renegotiation. A claim that sounded cruel at noon can be walked around the stones until it becomes a sequence: who sleeps nearest the vent, who carries water, who does not get to paint a sprout window twice. Hungry Dogs prefer the ash line, the outer grit where you can leave without performing agreement. Their preference is respected and watched.

Smoke smells of fat, metal, and the green bite of cookfire peppersage crushed under a boot that got too close to the pot. Voices stay lower than the fire’s crack. When they rise, the ring notices, and noticing is already a kind of vote.

Double-painted sprout nights sometimes begin with a joke at secondfire, somebody claiming the paint was “just a coat,” and end with painted lies inland. The ring does not always stop the lie. It makes the lie have witnesses, which is a different mercy than innocence.

## Lore

The myth of the breakfast that ended a double claim is a morning story: stew, a caller’s voice, two crews discovering they had been starving on principle. Secondfire is that myth’s sharper twin. It admits that agreement can cool. A peg moved at evening is not the same object as a bowl passed at dawn. Claimscar vent-claim rule still holds; the ring is where people find out whether they meant it.

Driftfall runners sometimes carry stew-news between Circle and Ring and are not thanked prettily. Thanks here look like a cleared seat and a spoon that is not counted afterward. Ignore the ring’s pace, push a noon claim through the smoke as if night owed you speed, and you buy a quiet that lasts until someone moves your kit while you are still talking. The consequence is social and material at once: a colder sleep, a vent whose mouth is no longer yours to narrate, a breakfast you have to attend anyway because the yard will not let a feud skip a meal.

## Stories or Depictions

Side journeys of runners between Circle and Ring. Depictions show two fires, not one, and people whose shoulders have different errands. Freeband rivalry stays in the frame without turning Driftfall into a campaign map.

## Notes

Dirt-and-rope texture. Wonder, manners, and claim-craft over war as default weather.
