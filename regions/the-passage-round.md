# The Passage Round

## Name

The Passage Round

## Overview

The Passage Round is the stable dream threshold inside **Delci Beta**'s mind: a tube-shaped coliseum chamber reached by a fixed descent, from which he signs a destination into a logbook and walks through a grand door into the place he chose.

## Description

Entry never varies in Delci's accounts.

He dream-walks down a corridor laid with a **red carpet**. Ten steps lead downward into the ground. At the bottom he turns **left**, opens a wooden door carved with majestic animals—wolves, phoenix, sharks, dragons, and kindred figures—and steps into the Round.

The Round is circular, like a tube stood on end, ringed with coliseum-like columns. Concrete benches are built into the walls all the way around, though guests are rare. In the center stands a second, smaller round chamber: **The Sealed Beta Chamber**, locked and barricaded, never opened in this dreamer's mind.

Opposite that sealed core is a very large wooden door that can swing open on both sides—curved at the top, fitted with metal shackles and Art Nouveau–leaning decoration. To the right of that door sits the **Delci Passage Logbook**.

Delci writes where he wants to go, signs, closes the book. The grand door opens onto that place. That is how his days inside are spent.

## Lore

Shore rumor calls the whole sequence **passage**. Delci treats it as infrastructure, not metaphor: calm seat on Good Trade above, red carpet and Round below.
