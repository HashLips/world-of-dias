# Hallow Bay

## Name

Hallow Bay

## Overview

Hallow Bay is Calareth’s main sheltered cove and usual arrival point: a low Watch House, a clapboard guest hall with limited beds, and the legendary **Rope Tree** where visitors tie a blue ribbon promising not to move stone, soil, or story without assent.

## Description

Dories painted moss green rest above the high-tide line. The bay looks “hollowed by habit,” not by industry. **Yara Moss**, a junior Watch runner under Isle Warden Coris Vale, posts safe-passage slates and walks new arrivals through ribbon rules without softening them.

Ferries are infrequent. Most traffic is intentional: healers, students of restraint, families seeking a week without a clock.

## Lore

Hallow Bay encodes Calareth’s mean—open, gentle, and strict. The Ribbon rite is public ceremony that functions as law. Breaking it is not a cute anecdote; it is how the island decides you do not belong.
