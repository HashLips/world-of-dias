# Aelwyn Path of the Season

## Name

Aelwyn Path of the Season

## Overview

**Aelwyn Path of the Season** is whichever elevated canopy route currently holds priority under the seven-paths rule—the path you may use this season without pretending you own next season.

## Description

Rope, leaf-shadow, markers that get repainted when priority rotates. Kin groups meet at junctions to renegotiate. Emergency rights can open a rival path for weather, then close again. Children learn: win a season, do not tattoo permanence into bark.

## Lore

Seven-paths rule made walkable and temporary. Truce politics with moss underfoot.
