# Glasswater Feed Channels

## Name

Glasswater Feed Channels

## Overview

The irrigation and mirror-bright cuts that feed Glasswater farms and Tubehold’s waterlines—glass-looking on lull days, muddy work on rain weeks.

## Description

Named cuts braid from Brindle Cord Rivers into fields. Farmers walk banks at dawn; Tubehold watchers listen where channels meet dam approaches. Mirror Lull days make the water a second sky; night-watch treats that beauty as a reason to stay awake, not to nap.

## Lore

Indexed under Landforms of F432. Terrain bone for eastern Nauw food-heart. Not Outer Rim sea; not Wabet Softfall rivers.

## Stories or Depictions

Mirror Lull lore and Tubehold vigilance both walk these banks. Banana Draft barges use deeper cords; feed channels are the farm capillaries.

## Notes

Safe (watchful) on lull days with company; dangerous alone in fog after bad mill nights.
