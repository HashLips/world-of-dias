# Saltjaw

## Name

Saltjaw

## Overview

Saltjaw is a small mining settlement on the southeastern coast of Driftfall, in Sorel, on a rocky point seaward of Sprout Vent. It exists for Saltjaw Mine and its modest ore, and it lives on VEL marks earned a sack at a time. It is home to Ansel and Maren Kestry and to the crew of *The Patient Lead*. It is not a port, a city, or a frontier boomtown. It is a few hundred people, a headframe, a jetty and a bell.

## Description

The settlement climbs a slope above a short stone jetty. The headframe of the main adit stands over the point, and beside it is the bell-stand with its rope. The houses are Sorel salvage architecture: hull-rib rooms made from old keels set on kiln-brick footings, roofed with tarred plank and layers of salvaged sail, windowed with bottle-glass. The cookfire and communal kettle sit under a great set of hull ribs at the centre. A small kiln sends up a grey thread. Inland and above, over a shoulder of broken heat-warped ground, Sprout Vent breathes. Nobody looks at the vent first thing in the morning. Everyone checks it last thing at night, the way you check a sleeping animal.

The jetty faces the Saltjaw shelf. Past the shelf edge the water goes deep and the Shelf Set runs west along the coast. Every float, bottle and lost cap off the point ends up "in Wabet," as the saying goes.

**Sensory:** sulfur crust on the wind, wet salt in Wetwake, kettle steam, the bell's bronze at shift-ends, Vent Haze sweet and choking in its season, tar.

**Climate:** Wetwake fogs off the shelf thick enough to lose the settlement at arm's length; dust in Brightpass; heavy heat in Fruitcarry; Vent Haze risk in Hushlean.

## Lore

Saltjaw children send festival notes off the point in old bottles on the Shelf Set, with wishes in them. Some wash up on the Lumira coast, and the Letter House of Sallow Strand keeps them. The sea-call, a long stroke, a wait and a long stroke repeated, means *boat overdue*. Before the saga it had not been rung for four years.
