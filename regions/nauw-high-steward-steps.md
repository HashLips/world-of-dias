# Nauw High Steward Steps

## Name

Nauw High Steward Steps

## Overview

The **Nauw High Steward Steps** are the public stair approach to Concord authority—where corridor priority speeches, exile pressures, and trade continuity arguments get heard outdoors before they harden indoors.

## Description

Wide stone, wind off the basin, banners that change less often than Flagweek cloth. Petitioners wait on landings. Children count steps. Serik Dovant–era manners still flavor how loud you may shout about Long Gate from the middle flight.

## Lore

Civic theater without becoming a throne room tourist trap. Continuity outranks district gain—spoken here, enforced on roads.

## Stories or Depictions

Concord politics; corridor priority rule.
