# Outer Rim Salt Fog Habitat

## Name

Outer Rim Salt Fog Habitat

## Overview

Salt air, fog banks, cove tiers, and keel-bank water where Singing Fishermen manners and sea life share one wet world.

## Description

Fog makes distance negotiable. Net-moss softens rope; salt-seals haul out on known rocks; whales with human-seeming eyes force ethics into every loft argument. Keel-sharks and siren-eel talk keep pride small. Hollowmere is the habitat’s best-known town face—not the whole sea.

**Who thrives:** loft crews, otters, seals, careful whales-kin talkers.  
**Who strains:** basin tourists who treat fog as scenery only.
