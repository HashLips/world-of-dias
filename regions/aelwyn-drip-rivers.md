# Aelwyn Drip Rivers

## Name

Aelwyn Drip Rivers

## Overview

Canopy rivers and Softfall-fed tributaries under Aelwyn—routes fruit and quiet boats use without turning the forest into a road empire.

## Description

Water moves in filtered light. Softfall makes the rivers sound like a roof. Elevated walkways cross; Reedpol-style skiffs appear where Keepers allow. Lucky Farmer camps survey banks for perfect-form fruit rumor without clear-cutting corridors.

## Lore

Indexed under Landforms of F432. Complements Lumira sand silence and Verdant Reach meadows—Wabet is not one green texture.
