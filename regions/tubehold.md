# Tubehold

## Name

Tubehold

## Overview

Tubehold is a Glasswater dam-and-windmill town where night watchers keep listening tubes along waterlines. Children inherit tubes the way other places inherit knives—and sometimes inherit things the fields prefer to deny.

## Description

Windmills tick the dark. Dam gates are civic religion. Streets follow waterlines more than vanity. **Nimi Tube** inherited a tube that showed a reflection with the wrong season inside it; elders called it fog; Nimi did not.

**Sensory:** mill tick, wet stone, tube breath against the ear, orchard-humidity from Glasswater fields, mirror-lull stillness on calm water days, night-watch footsteps that try not to sound like alarm.

**Climate:** temperate field weather—soft rains, humidity, mirror-calm days; night fog that makes tubes honest or cruel. Not Outer Rim salt; not Softfall canopy.

Drifters pass through with stories of sky-raid memory. Tubehold’s job is to keep water honest even when memory is not.
