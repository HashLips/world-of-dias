# Tubehold

## Name

Tubehold

## Overview

Tubehold is a Glasswater dam-and-windmill town where night watchers keep listening tubes along waterlines. Children inherit tubes the way other places inherit knives—and sometimes inherit things the fields prefer to deny.

## Description

Windmills tick the dark. Dam gates are civic religion. **Nimi Tube** inherited a tube that showed a reflection with the wrong season inside it; elders called it fog; Nimi did not.

Drifters pass through with stories of sky-raid memory. Tubehold’s job is to keep water honest even when memory is not.

## Lore

Child-accessible vigilance epic: a town that survives by listening to infrastructure.

## Stories or Depictions

*The Child Who Inherited a Tube* begins here. *Find the Goldfish* is used as a teaching image about looking twice.
