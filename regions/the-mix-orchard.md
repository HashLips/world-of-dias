# The Mix Orchard

## Name

The Mix Orchard

## Overview

The Mix Orchard is Sella Orrow's small planting past the Lent Strip, where she grew Vitrin by mixing certain fruits and would not say which, or how, or why the tree stopped at about ten fruits and one late cling.

## Description

Not a grand orchard. A dozen older trees of honest kinds — share-apple, a scrub-apple brought up from the road, a market banana stool that dislikes the damp — and among them one younger tree whose fruit set like glass. The ground is kept with a hoe and with quiet. Softfall drips off the leaves in the hours when Wabet is only itself. A path of flat stones runs to Orrow House. Wagons can reach the lane. They were not invited to the tree.

The glass fruit, when it hung, took the color of whatever stood behind it and then disagreed: a green-gold depth inside a rind you could see through, seeds like tiny lenses, a tap that rang.

## Lore

Neighbors who noticed the tree were told it was a failed sweetening and not to eat the failures. That was true enough to protect them and untrue enough to be a wall. After the bar board named the place, the wall became a road problem.

## Stories or Depictions

The Vitrin Saga's hidden source. The yield people counted was about ten. One smaller fruit remained after the show and the stew. The mix is not published here.
