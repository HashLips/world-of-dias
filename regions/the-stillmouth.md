# The Stillmouth

## Name

The Stillmouth

## Overview

The Stillmouth is the sea approach to Aumere: a ring of quiet water south-west of the Saltjaw shelf edge, inside the Counting Current and the Aumere Turning Boom. Driftfall crews coined the name after the expedition. It is where the Houses' fleet met Nessa Orvay, where the shot was fired, and where the island withdrew. Since then it is broken white water around an empty ring of timbers. It is not a strait, a harbour, or a door.

## Description

**Before the withdrawal:** the Counting Current runs around a broad ring in groups of pulls and rests. Inside it lies water so quiet there is no surf, no gull and no wind, held "like water in cupped hands." The Turning Boom lies on that quiet water, roped timbers joint to joint, lifting and knocking on the count. The Stillmouth Blue Mist sits a few hundred strokes out from the island, bright and shadowless. Soundings find no bottom. Sprout Vent's plume is the only outside mark, and it is visible only on a few clear days a year.

**During a Long Slack:** the outer counting stops and the shelf water goes flat, while the ring inside keeps "coasting" for a time. Boats can come in, and narrow boats can go out on the westward run.

**After the withdrawal:** the held quiet has let go. The water inside and around the ring is confused, short and steep, white-topped, and fishers can see it from the point. The boom floats on it, unbroken, with one grey-pennanted ward-bolt standing in a timber. Nobody knows how long the roughness will last. Vexra Coil forbade salvage.

## Lore

Salt Ledger talk tells it as a door, a grave, a mirage, and a sunken island. The Ward calls it a bleed site; the Operators' drum said otherwise. Letter-keepers do not name it at all.
