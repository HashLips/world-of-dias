# Forget-Texture Margin

## Name

Forget-Texture Margin

## Overview

**Forget-Texture Margin** is a fringe description, not a surveyed province, for approaches to F500 where surfaces lose the habit of being memorable. Maps invalidate faster than ink dries. The margin is how people talk about a band edge that will not hold a travelogue still.

Status stays unknown. Description here is pressure, not a brochure.

## Description

Accounts, and there are fewer of them than taverns claim, speak of ground that looks like ground until the sentence starts. Grit becomes a word you cannot cash. You remember that you knelt, and you cannot remember whether the kneeling found stone, ash, or a softness that refused both. Color names slip a half-step and then a whole step: blue leaves, and what remains is the feeling of having meant blue. Footprints fill with absence rather than dust. A walker looks back and sees their own trail as a polite fiction the margin is done hosting.

Wind, if it is wind, does not cool the skin so much as edit it. People report pockets lighter than they should be, not robbed, unrecorded. A Null-Proximity Warning Tag in a careful hand is sometimes tied and later found blank, which may be vandalism, weather, or the margin’s sense of humor. Treat fauna stories, including worms of erasure, as bestiary rumor. The margin does not owe you a creature to point at so your fear can have legs.

Silence here is not the Quiet Well’s chosen silence. It is the silence of a description failing in the mouth.

## Lore

Negation proximity is lived here as a timing problem. The stories say retreat closes faster than approach estimates, which means the brave plan and the sensible plan can share a grave if someone confuses noticing with arriving. Distant lumen-abyss talk sometimes paints the margin in the wrong light, gold where there is only the loss of contrast. Keep companies and the Unraveled partial. A glowing theory is still a theory, and this edge eats the part of a theory that wanted to be finished.

Charts that behave well mark the margin with dashed strokes and a note not to close the shape. Closing the shape is how a rumor becomes a border and a border becomes an invitation. Open Hand makers borrow the dashed stroke as a joke about drafts. On this margin the joke stops being a joke.

Ignore the unknown and write a confident paragraph, and the paragraph will be the thing you lose first. Companions remember that you spoke. They do not remember your conclusion. That is the consequence the stories agree to keep.

## Stories or Depictions

Dashed “do not finish this line” strokes. A cairn that may not be stones. A witness who came back partial and would not let anyone title the thing they saw. Depictions that render the margin as a dramatic canyon are classroom errors.
