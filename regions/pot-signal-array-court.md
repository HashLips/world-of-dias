# Pot-Signal Array Court

## Name

Pot-Signal Array Court

## Overview

**Pot-Signal Array Court** is a Veloria pocket court where street flower pots are arranged as a readable signal array—beauty first, debt/sanctuary/warning grammar second.

## Description

Uneven cobbles, leaning façades, pots in deliberate clusters. Colors and placements speak pot-signal customs: who is owed, who may rest, what to avoid tonight. Children water them; clerks glance without admitting they checked. Not a core Operator floor—outer/mid-ring civic tech that still resonates.

## Lore

Everyday Resonant Tech made spatial. Crooked and leaning ring streets nearby make the court feel handmade. Tourists photograph flowers; locals read the grammar.

Culture: Pot Signal Customs. Artwork cousin: Leaning Ring Streets (city silhouette of mid-ring oddity).
