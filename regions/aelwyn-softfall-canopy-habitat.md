# Aelwyn Softfall Canopy Habitat

## Name

Aelwyn Softfall Canopy Habitat

## Overview

Towering filtered light, Softfall roofs, drip rivers, and fruit routes where Soft-Bears, Lucky Farmer camps, and Keepers’ take-only-needed law share one green body.

## Description

Softfall makes the forest sound like a gentle roof. Soft-Bears huff from fork-nests; berries and bark insects feed them; fruit moves east without the canopy becoming a road empire. Elevated walkways keep feet off some roots. Violence against Soft-Bears is social poison.

**Who thrives:** quiet walkers, Soft-Bears, fruit surveyors with manners.  
**Who strains:** loud conquerors; clear-cut dreams.
