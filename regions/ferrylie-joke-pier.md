# Ferrylie Joke Pier

## Name

Ferrylie Joke Pier

## Overview

**Ferrylie Joke Pier** is a Nauw pier where **Captain Ell Ferrylie** sells fake band-ferry tickets as comedy—and as ethics warning. The bit teaches Dias’s soft limit: there is no subway between frequencies. Orrin Veld cites the pier when apprentices ask for doors.

## Description

Salt air, cheerful ink, a dock that looks ready for a crossing it will never provide. Ell sells **fake band ferry tickets**: cheap card stock, a little boat almost official, then a strike-through **no-ferry mark** or fine print that returns you to the same boards under your feet. Tourists pocket stubs. Crossing-hungry seekers hear the laugh as a threat.

The pier sits far from Calibration Row oil and close to travelers who still hope Veloria hides a band door. It is not inside F432 craft geography; it is coastal comedy that Row science borrows. Rent under the jokes keeps Ell talking. When someone pays serious again, Ell jokes harder—a local tell that longing season has returned.
