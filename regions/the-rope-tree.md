# The Rope Tree

## Name

The Rope Tree

## Overview

The Rope Tree is Hallow Bay’s salt-silvered mooring bollard grown into legend: iron rings in bark, lines worn smooth. On a traveler’s first landing day of longer stay, they tie a blue ribbon—not ownership, but a public promise of restraint.

## Description

Children sometimes add a second ribbon “for the sea.” Coris does not discourage it. Yara Moss keeps a quiet count of ribbons that disappear overnight—weather, theft, or conscience, depending who you ask.

## Lore

The tree is ceremony that behaves like a contract. Saga fuel: a ribbon cut, a stone moved, a week without clocks that suddenly needs clocks again.

**Story staging:** first landing day; ribbon count overnight; a visitor who ties honestly vs one who performs. Pair with Hallow Bay arrival weather and Coral Strand night light.
