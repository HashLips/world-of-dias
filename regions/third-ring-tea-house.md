# Third-Ring Tea House

## Name

Third-Ring Tea House

## Overview

The **Third-Ring Tea House** is Calibration Row’s public cousin to the Tea Slit Bench—graduated cylinders optional, mint common, arguments required to pause for honest refill.

## Description

Steam, oil-adjacent sweetness, benches that remember elbow dents. Auntie brings ordinary cups and is ignored affectionately. Apprentices practice scrape manners on napkins. Not Softfruit Hall scale—smaller, sharper, Row-flavored.

## Lore

Row tea-cylinder custom made hospitable to visitors who are not yet “colleague.” Frame nights sometimes end here when the alcove is too loud with glass.

## Stories or Depictions

Tea Slit Bench sibling; oil-and-argument pauses.

## Notes

Parent: Calibration Row. Open question: whether Operators ever pay in anonymous tokens for tea and call it noise.
