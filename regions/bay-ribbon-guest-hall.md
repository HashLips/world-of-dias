# Bay-Ribbon Guest Hall

## Name

Bay-Ribbon Guest Hall

## Overview

Bay-Ribbon Guest Hall is Hallow Bay’s clapboard lodging—few beds, many rules, and the smell of salt soap on linen.

## Description

One long loft, partitioned by cloth; moss-green shutters; a slate of quiet hours beside the door. Guests who have not yet tied a ribbon at the Rope Tree sleep on the porch bench, not in beds. Yara Moss walks new arrivals through the slate without apology.

Joy: a week without a clock. Pressure: the ferry’s next window may be days away.

## Lore

The hall is Calareth’s kindness made architectural—and its threshold made literal.

## Notes

Child-friendly rest that can harden into belonging drama if ribbon law is broken.
