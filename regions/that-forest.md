# That Forest

## Name

That Forest

## Overview

That Forest is the named deep-grove landmark in Verdant Reach where **Blue Litter Underwood** concentrates and the **Blue Litter Husk** is most often reported. It is a forbidden stress-test under Wabet’s calm—not a tourist canopy walk.

## Description

Approach paths look like ordinary orchard margins until the litter’s blue hush begins. Birds change their timing. Some extractors bottle litter for sale in Nauw underbelly markets; Keepers and Open Hand ethic call that a wound that spreads.

**Riven Salt** leads one such extraction crew when rumor money is high. Elyra Mossen’s people do not romanticize the grove.

## Lore

That Forest can host dark local horror without rewriting Harmonic Wabet chapters. The Husk remains a failed-listening Echoform pressure, not a soup-cooker and not Vorrith.

## Stories or Depictions

*The Bottle That Cleared Itself* is the cautionary tale extractors hate. Artwork *When Trees Sing* is the safer sister mood outside the litter core.
