# Sorel

## Overview

Sorel occupies the south-eastern lands of the F432 frequency realm, directly south of Nauw and east of Wabet.

## Description

Among inhabitants of Nauw and neighboring regions, Sorel has a reputation as a place where society's outcasts often end up. Most who arrive from a single origin are turned out of Nauw (exiled, shunned, or rejected) and move south into Sorel to build life away from northern cities and authorities. Others still come from farther afield, but Nauw supplies the largest share of newcomers.

Because of this history, Sorel developed as a region where traditional rules and authorities hold little power. Many towns and structures are built from salvaged or found materials, producing settlements that appear improvised and constantly changing.

**Sensory:** nail-midge air over scrap, forge breath, wet rope and crab-pinch on Driftfall piers, hearth-orange windows in Hearthvale dusk, Flagweek cloth snapping, Vent Haze sweet-rot grit, dust-bison horizon, laughter that sounds like defiance becoming home.

The landscapes are harsh and unpredictable, and the culture reflects that environment. For some, Sorel is a dangerous frontier shaped by conflict and survival. For others, it is a place of freedom, where individuals can exist outside laws and expectations imposed elsewhere in F432.

**Climate:** changeable frontier air—dust, forge smoke, salvage wind, and **Sorel Vent Haze** in sprout seasons. Clear Flagweek mornings still happen; haze is weather with a chore list, not permanent doom. See [`Climates of Dias`](../world/climates-of-dias.md).

**Who thrives:** remakers, freeband organizers, salvage crews, second-beginning teachers, people who can build a table from three planks and a joke.  
**Who fears it:** those who need Concord approval to feel real; lone new arrivals on exile roads; anyone who mistakes freedom for the absence of consequences.

**Joy:** Hearth Laughter Week, Flagweek chaos, roof-raisings, Ember-Cats on warm stone, the first night you are called neighbor instead of case.  
**Pressure:** wolves and yard teeth; vent seasons; the Long Gate’s shame/defiance braid; Horizon-Walker myths; hunger before the second beginning sticks.

### Architecture

Sorel builds from **salvage and smoke**: tarp-and-hull yards, kiln-brick courtyards, bottle-glass windows, claim-paint uprights, Flagweek poles that outlast allegiances. Permanence is earned slowly; honest many-coat paint beats a lying new facade. Full dialect: [`Sorel Salvage Architecture`](../cultures/sorel-salvage-architecture.md). - **Claimscar Yard** disputes and scav packs.

- Bridge approaches when Watch-Hounds and northern shame arrive together.

## Lore

Sorel forms the southern counterpart to Nauw and the south-eastern counterpart to Wabet, helping define the lived contrasts within F432.

Within Sorel, two major territories divide the land: Hearthvale lies on the west, and Driftfall on the east, with a rough north–south partition through the middle of the region so the two territories face each other across Sorel's interior. A long bridge spans from southern Nauw into northern Sorel (Hearthvale), tying that northern gate to Nauw without requiring travelers to cross Driftfall first.

Sorel is the **country of the second beginning**—wonderful if you need remaking; hard if you expect the west’s quiet or the north’s fruit-rank comfort to follow you unchanged.

## Stories or Depictions

*Map of Sorel* is treated as a canonical close-up representation of Sorel, with clear emphasis on the Hearthvale-Driftfall split. Broad-scale storytelling often invokes **The Long Gate Argument** (the mythic history of the great Nauw crossing into Hearthvale) and **The Drift Hour with No Captain** (Driftfall’s void-hour founding tale).

*3 Opinions* and the traveling trio **The Three Opinions** are recurring social depictions: unaligned gossip-bearers who unsettle towns without openly joining Hearthvale or Driftfall camps.
