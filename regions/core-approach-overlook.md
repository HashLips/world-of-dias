# Core Approach Overlook

## Name

Core Approach Overlook

## Overview

**Core Approach Overlook** is a permitted ridge lane where visitors and outer-ring workers can see toward Veloria’s inner glow without holding inner-ring clearance—the city as colossal silhouette, not tour.

## Description

Wind, thrum, and a rail worn by elbows. Guides like Dott point without promising entry. On early thrum mornings the overlook fills; on high-load nights it empties by clerk advice. Wrong-tick unease sometimes walks here after Calibration escape talk—dials in pockets almost agree, then don’t.

## Lore

Teaches ring clearance emotionally: you may look; you may not assume a door. Leaning ring streets below make the approach feel handmade and slightly wrong in a beloved way.

## Stories or Depictions

*Not Tonight Clearance*; Visitor Queue Ledge downstream toward seams.

Open question: whether Operators watch the overlook as much as visitors watch the core.
