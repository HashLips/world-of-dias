# Veld Workshop Alcove

## Name

Veld Workshop Alcove

## Overview

**Veld Workshop Alcove** is **Orrin Veld**’s rented alcove on Calibration Row where the **Spectrum Frame** is built, nested by the Caliburn, and later left incomplete on purpose. Primary Calibration Saga stage: oil, orange peel, chalk ghosts, cylinder tea rings, and a failed vault.

## Description

A workbench alcove sized for a shoulder-high standing frame. Floor marks show where the Frame stood. Chalk ghosts outline furniture that moved for aperture space. **Oilvine creeper** thrives in the oil mist. Heel-cap contact plates once picked up building load so graphs could be checked against hum. Side spools fed calibration filament; Mio’s folding chalk rule set ticks.

Smell: machine oil, citrus peel, mint if tea was recent. Sound: filament feed, seam-moth soft commas when residual charge thickens, later a blackened corner spool after the latch night. The alcove is rented craft space, not a House lab and not a subway station between bands.

## Lore

Orrin builds the Spectrum Frame here to graph background bands—F120, F380, F610, F840, disputed F960—from inside F432. Graphs agree. A residual calibration seam with porch-light precision will not fully close; looking that carefully invites **the Caliburn** Echoform to nest in the Frame. Helpful amber becomes cyan half-digit mischief. Widen-as-gift temptation is refused. Partial spectrum truth publishes with no-ferry marks.

Vault night: apology manners and a latch meant to hold what measurement invited. The vault latch fails. The Caliburn escapes into Veloria load-noise. Aftermath, Orrin keeps the Frame incomplete on purpose—teaching model, not porch light. Noro may pretend indifference; the floor marks stay honest.
