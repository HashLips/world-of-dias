# Softfruit Ladle Court

## Name

Softfruit Ladle Court

## Overview

**Softfruit Ladle Court** is the open yard beside Softfruit Table Hall where ladles, peel jokes, and keep-time talk spill outdoors—happiness infrastructure with sticky floors.

## Description

Long tables under shade cloth. Ladles hang on hooks like civic tools. Children race peel boats in wash buckets. Sera’s cart sometimes parks at the edge for field interviews when the Hall is too loud. Almost-oranges get refused here with theatrical disgust.
