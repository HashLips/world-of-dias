# Open Hand Way

## Name

Open Hand Way

## Overview

Open Hand Way is the best-known waystation on the Eastbound Fruit Road: crates, shared knives, overnight sheds, and a public table where convoy disputes are heard before they become fights.

## Description

The station sits where canopy shade still reaches and Nauw-bound dust has not yet won. Sharing-knives circulate with meal etiquette. Children learn to cut fruit for strangers before they learn market prices.

Tomas Share treats the Way as a classroom with wheels parked outside.

## Lore

Named for Wabet’s Open Hand ethic (*take what sustains, leave what continues*), not to be confused with F960’s Open Hand of Making.
