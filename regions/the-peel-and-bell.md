# The Peel and Bell

## Name

The Peel and Bell

## Overview

The Peel and Bell is an outer-ring bar in Veloria where wagon crews, sorters, and the occasional third-ring coat share one wet room. It took its name from a peel-bucket by the door and a brass bell Bram Keel rings when a round is on the house or a lie has gotten too large to stand.

## Description

Stone floor, medieval in the way the outer ring still is: benches darkened by wool coats, a counter of scarred fruitwood, a high window that shows Sky Drifters only if you are already looking up. The air smells of peel, warm milk, salt, and the iron of the bell. Lamps are oil and a few third-ring bulbs Bram accepted because the oil smoked the chalk. He keeps both. The bulbs hum. The oil smells like a road kitchen.

The Peel Bell Board hangs where a toll marshal would understand it: a slate for names, debts, and places people are willing to say out loud. Drinks here are local and odd. A voice may ring. A step may go quiet. A sentence may feel overheard. None of that opens a door out of F432.

## Lore

The bar became the room where the hull-gossip about Dain Mercer's freight had to sit down. Outer-ring people already trusted it with smaller truths: who skipped a waystation, whose ox went lame, which VEL mark was shaved. When the whisper outgrew the market, it came here because Bram will serve a stranger and will not broker a ticket.

## Stories or Depictions

The Vitrin Saga uses the bar as the place the secret becomes public, not as a temple of the fruit.

## Notes

Parent is Veloria City. The building stays outer-ring even when third-ring money walks in. Drinks are catalogued as their own artifacts. Sky Drifters visible from the window do not descend and are not hailed by Sky-Salt.
