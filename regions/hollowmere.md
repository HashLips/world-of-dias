# Hollowmere

## Name

Hollowmere

## Overview

Hollowmere is the Outer Rim’s best-known cove town: stacked net sheds, open mending lofts, and lantern hooks that make the cliff look like it breathes in strings at night. Apprentices learn it as the first fixed star on the rim’s mental chart.

## Description

The town climbs a northern cove in tiers. Lower slopes hold drying lines and brine barrels; mid-slopes hold family sheds; the highest net-lofts are public stages for the **First Line Answer** and storm check-ins. Visitors sleep in borrowed hammocks more often than inns. Gossip prefers rope-height to street-height.

Hollowmere is sociable without being soft. Missing a check-in call during Storm Choir is a public matter. Fruit barges that brave the route are greeted like festivals—and judged like guests who might steal a line.

## Lore

Tide Warden Marek Solon’s Compact uses Hollowmere as a coordination hub, not a throne. Loft-singer **Jessa Quill** keeps the practical memory of who answered whom, and when a season’s first spout was honest. Cove politics are about shared lines, inherited routes, and whether friendship with human-eyed whales is reverence or weakness.

## Stories or Depictions

*The Loft* and *Boat in a Bottle* carry Hollowmere’s mood. The rumor-myth *The First Line That Failed* is told quietly after bad winters.

## Notes

Independent coastal saga space: First Line Answer crisis, apprentice politics, whale ethics—without requiring Veloria’s core plot.
