# Reef-Closer Sera

## Overview

**Reef-Closer Sera** is the older scar behind the idiom “closing reefs”—a historical Warden (or Warden-myth) who shut routes before damage became permanent. Later tellings sometimes paste living names onto Sera’s decree.

## Description

Dawn watchers; seized boats; fish runs returning. Statues are rare—Calareth prefers empty rings to heroes—but songs keep Sera.

## Beliefs About Them

Warning toast at Tide-Loud weeks. Smugglers curse Sera. Myth merge with Coris tales is disputed; Keepers of story prefer Sera for deep past, Coris for Present Age seats.

## Narrative Role

Warning—limit before permanence of harm.

## Notes

Pairs with [`The Warden Who Closed the Reefs`](../myths/the-warden-who-closed-the-reefs.md) without overwriting living Coris.
