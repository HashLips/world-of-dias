# Fogbank Siren-Eels

## Overview

Fogbank Siren-Eels are long luminous eels that hunt in Outer Rim fog banks, luring small fish—and sometimes swimmers—with a pulsing throat-light and a thin singing vibration through water.

## Description

Body length beyond a dory oar, skin like wet slate with a pale ventral glow. They coil under fog where vision fails. Bites tear; constriction is rarer but reported. Most dangerous to lone night swimmers and overboard crew.

## Beliefs About Them

Sailors refuse certain fog songs and keep hooks ready. Some claim the eels learned melody from fishermen; Singing crews hate that story.

## Narrative Role

A scary sea encounter that contrasts Outer Rim beauty with real peril.
