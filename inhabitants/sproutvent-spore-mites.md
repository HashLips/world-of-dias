# Sproutvent Spore-Mites

## Overview

Sproutvent Spore-Mites are near-invisible mite clouds that ride vent-sprout release events, irritating lungs and settling as pale grit on clothes.

## Description

Individually tiny; collectively a drifting haze with a sweet-rot smell. They do not hunt, but heavy exposure causes cough, itch, and days of fatigue. Filters and wet cloths help.

## Beliefs About Them

Vent crews treat mite season like weather. Folklore claims mites 'plant thoughts'—usually dismissed, sometimes whispered after bad nights.

## Narrative Role

A non-animal-shaped hazard that still belongs in the creature ecology of Sorel's weird vents.
