# Songripe Nest-Finches

## Overview

Songripe Nest-Finches are tiny bright finches that nest in rope-bridge eaves and answer fragments of tide-song with chips and trills.

## Description

Thumb-sized, fruit-green and banana-yellow flashes. They steal unripe peels for nest lining and sit on shoulders without invitation. Isle children teach them silly refrains; mainland traders call them distractions.

## Beliefs About Them

A finch learning your laugh is luck. Caging them for Nauw markets is an isle insult.

## Narrative Role

Beloved seasonal-feeling isle companion beside Tide-Birds.
