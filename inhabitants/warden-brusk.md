# Warden Brusk

## Name

Warden Brusk

## Overview

**Warden Brusk** is a Bleed Weather Ward shutter-hand who hates instruments that invite sky. Broad, nail-scarred, and protective for reasons that are not wrong. In **The Calibration Saga** Brusk posts warnings on Orrin’s alcove when residual seams and widen temptations make precision look like a porch light.

## Description

Heavy build, scarred knuckles from shutter nails, coat built for wind that arrives with wrong colors. Walks shutter routes like a prayer schedule: latch, check, move on. Speaks in short warnings. Does not debate ontology with Echoforms; debates wood, hinge, and whether a lit instrument is telling the sky where to look.

Not a House clerk and not a Null agent. Brusk’s antagonism is care with bad manners. Outer rings thank Brusk on bleed nights. Row scientists find the nails rude and still sleep better when shutters hold.

Concrete saga beat: in The Seam That Would Not Close, Brusk’s shutter nail appears on Orrin’s alcove post—*instruments invite sky*—without waiting for an invitation to post it. The warning returns harder in Widening as Gift while the Caliburn offers cleaner stacks. Orrin reads the nail twice, bows to the Frame, and chooses not to widen. Brusk never says thank you. The shutters still get walked.

Physical read: broad shoulders, grey-threaded beard kept short for shutter straps, voice like a hinge that needs oil and refuses it. Carries nails in a leather fold stamped with Ward marks so faded they look like scars.

Brusk is wrong about scientists inviting sky on purpose and right that invitations can be accidental. The saga needs both facts in one person.

## Beliefs About Them

Outer rings thank Brusk. Apprentices fear the nails. Houses file Brusk as useful weather. Orrin argues with the posts and obeys the fear underneath them. Visitors think Brusk is scenery until a bleed night proves otherwise.

## Narrative Role

Bleed and shutter pressure across seam and widen chapters. Protective antagonist who keeps measurement ethics tied to sky risk.
