# Wabet Root-Titans

## Overview

Wabet Root-Titans are colossal walking root-masses or root-backed beasts said to shift deep canopy foundations once a generation.

## Description

Accounts describe hills that stand up, antlered with living trees, or beasts so large paths become their footprints. Evidence: sudden new clearings, relocated streams.

## Beliefs About Them

Canopy law forbids hunting what you cannot bury. Most treat Titans as landscape events wearing animal grammar.

## Narrative Role

Largest Wabet awe—scary only if you stand in the wrong valley.
