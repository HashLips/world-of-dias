# Stillhollow Mutes

## Overview

Stillhollow Mutes are reported soft-bodied, deer-shaped creatures of Stillhollow that seem to absorb nearby sound when they stand close.

## Description

Witnesses describe pale coats, no visible mouth movement, and a pressure in the ears like walking into thick cloth. Tracks look ordinary; the silence does not. Sightings are brief and usually at dusk.

## Beliefs About Them

Ashcalm travelers treat Mute stories as 'keep your voice kind' teaching. Scholars want recordings; none hold the effect cleanly.

## Narrative Role

Mysterious, not necessarily evil—good for soft horror and wonder in Wabet's quiet places.
