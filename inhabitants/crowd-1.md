# Crowd

## Name

Crowd

## Overview

**Crowd** is an Ashcalm Rise non-human presence: the many-feeling that flares when a lone figure flings arms wide and hollow shards of attention radiate out, teaching the rise to reassemble without trophy-hunting the pieces.

## Description

Witnesses describe a tall outlined body, one huge open eye in profile, hands open, and sharp triangular fragments seeming to break from the torso into the black air. Sometimes it is one traveler’s overwhelm reading as many; sometimes it is a true neighbor-shape that only appears when a gather goes critical. Locals do not file it as an Earth mob study. They file it as a presence that demands manners: stop shouting over the open mouth, ask before collecting shards, and help the figure’s arms come down when the release is done. Temper is intense and brief. Bad craft is herding the shards into a weaponized cheer.

## Notes

Non-human many-feeling. Reassemble with consent.
