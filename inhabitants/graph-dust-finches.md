# Graph-Dust Finches

## Name

Graph-Dust Finches

## Overview

**Graph-Dust Finches** are small Row birds that bathe in eraser grit and sing in short ticks. Their morning noise is part of Calibration Row’s workday soundtrack. In **The Calibration Saga** they remind readers that measurement leaves dust, and that dust has a life of its own.

## Description

Sparrow-sized, grey-brown backs, pale breasts often stained with graphite. They dust-bathe in the grit that falls from scraped ledgers and chalk rules. Song is not a long melody: three to five hard ticks, a pause, then a softer run that apprentices confuse with dial chatter.

They perch on open shutters, cylinder racks, and the lip of Orrin’s alcove when tea steam rises. They steal Softfruit seeds when Sera’s cart is near and chase each other through filament lines like living commas. Compared with Ink Sparrows, they care less about blotters and more about the dry powder under drafting boards.

They show up clearly in Movement Two mornings while the Spectrum Frame is under construction. Orrin times a graph pause to their ticks without noticing he is doing it. Tam jokes that the finches already publish better than Houses do—short, honest, and willing to bathe in mistakes.

Nests are messy cups of string and ledger scrap in shutter gaps. Eggs are speckled grey. Chicks learn the tick-song before a full warble, which delights apprentices and annoys anyone trying to hear floor hum. Orrin sometimes scatters a pinch of grit on purpose after a long scrape session—an apology to the morning, he claims, not a ritual.

After the Caliburn’s escape, one finch nest near a visitor ledge gathered chalkier grit than usual. Dott noted it; Jori did not file it as omen.

## Beliefs About Them

Craftspeople treat a finch bathing in their grit as a mild compliment: someone has been working hard enough to make a bath. Visitors find the tick-song eerie after the Caliburn’s escape, though the birds themselves do not change. Ward hands ignore them unless nests block a shutter walk.

## Narrative Role

Sound and comedy ecology for Row stages. They keep the science saga grounded in ordinary mornings before residual seams and wrong digits take the air.

## Notes

They remain Row morning sound even when the city learns wrong ticks elsewhere.
