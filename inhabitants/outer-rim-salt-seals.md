# Outer Rim Salt-Seals

## Overview

Outer Rim Salt-Seals are speckled seals of the Nauw Outer Rim Seas that haul out on low rocks and follow fishing songs at a respectful distance.

## Description

Silver-spotted coats that look hammered from wet coin, whiskers stiff with salt crystals, eyes large but animal—not human-eyed like the whales. On rocks they steam gently in sun; in water they corkscrewing after thrown scraps with slapstick dignity. Curious about boats; steal fish when discipline slips. Day-visible and noisy in rookery season—barking like arguments that forgot words. Song crews swear some seals keep time with the easier refrains.

## Beliefs About Them

Singing Fishermen treat Salt-Seals as lesser kin of the whale compact: not sacred equals, still not sport-cruelty targets.

## Narrative Role

Fills the Outer Rim with approachable marine life beside the more uncanny whales.
