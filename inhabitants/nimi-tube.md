# Nimi Tube

## Name

Nimi Tube

## Overview

Nimi Tube inherited a Glasswater listening tube that sometimes shows a reflection with the wrong season inside it. Elders say fog. Nimi keeps watch anyway.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Young, stubborn, polite to dams. Nimi’s saga can begin as child-accessible vigilance and deepen into field-denial horror without leaving Tubehold’s ethic.

## Beliefs About Them

Some Drifters want Nimi’s tube. Night watchers want Nimi asleep on schedule. Nimi wants the water to stay honest.

## Narrative Role

Glasswater epic viewpoint distinct from Veloria plots.
