# Riven Salt

## Name

Riven Salt

## Overview

Riven Salt leads a Blue Litter extraction crew that bottles grove pressure for Nauw underbelly buyers when rumor money runs high.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Practical, not theatrical. Riven does not worship the Husk and does not fear Keepers enough. Bottles are sold through lanes like Pot Signal more often than through honest markets.

## Beliefs About Them

Orchard folk call Riven a wound with boots. Riven calls Keepers naive about what cities pay for.

## Narrative Role

Antagonist or tragic hustler for forbidden-grove sagas—independent of Vorrith and of Calen’s Wabet chapter.

## Notes

Status canonical as a person; success of bottling remains contested and dangerous.
