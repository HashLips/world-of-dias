# Puck Hennet

## Name

Puck Hennet

## Overview

Puck Hennet drives oxen on the fruit road and drinks Ox-Patience as maintenance. He has seen The Late Bruise. He has not agreed to call it an improvement on an animal that knows his voice.

## Description

Large, sun-cut, a gentleness around the shoulders he saves for the near ox. Boots repaired until they are more patch than boot. He smells of hide and sweet feed. Engines make him set his jaw. He is not stupid about machines. He is loyal to a pace that does not panic fruit. At The Peel and Bell he will drink Sky-Salt and look up, then tell the table the things overhead are not for hire.

## Beliefs About Them

Younger porters think he is a relic. His oxen arrive with fruit that can still be sold. That is an argument he does not bother to decorate.

## Narrative Role

Medieval road life in one driver. He is in the bar when the whisper swells and on the berm when the wagons refuse to become obsolete.

## Notes

His team is unnamed. The species of draught he uses sits with Nauw Basin Oxen and the wider fruit-road beasts. He is not himself a beast entry.
