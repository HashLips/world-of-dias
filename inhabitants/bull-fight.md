# Bull Fight

## Name

Bull Fight

## Overview

**Bull Fight** is a Brindle March non-human contest presence: horn-headed paired figures that appear in road talk whenever a load quarrel needs a ruled meeting on a magenta path-strip instead of a ruined cart.

## Description

March tellers describe lean dark silhouettes with horned heads and small bright white eyes, limbs long enough to cross without clumsy trampling. They meet on marked pink or magenta ground ribbons; leaving the ribbon ends the form. One often crouches with a high arched rear limb; the other bends forward with wide hands—poses for contact, not for killing. Locals do not treat this as Earth bullfighting. They treat it as neighbor-shape and manners theater that keeps fruit roads usable. After the meet, the Three Thank-Yous still apply if help was involved. Children watch from off-strip. Selling tickets is bad craft; inviting a quiet witness is not.

## Notes

Non-human contest pair. Ruled strip. Dias name, not an Earth sport import.
