# Sheep Just Follow

## Name

Sheep Just Follow

## Overview

**Sheep Just Follow** is a Brindle March non-human flock presence: magenta cloud-bodied creatures with white triangular faces and stick legs, often seen crossing under a huge reddish-orange disc behind a black staffed herder—named both as guide-care and as a warning against blank following.

## Description

March talk counts them in nines in the full plate: fluffy scalloped magenta edges, four thin black legs, white triangle faces, black ear points, one black eye-dot each. A jagged black mid barrier often splits flock from herder. Soft oval shadows sit under each body. The herder is dark silhouette with white hat and staff—part of the named hour, not an Earth shepherd stereotype to import wholesale. Local care forbids chasing the flock across the chasm edge for laughs, and forbids using the name only to insult neighbors without the care half. Resonance is neon pink on somber slate; memory is the disc behind the line; wonder is cloud-bodies that still cast honest shadows. Depict non-human flock. Soft talk that files them as Earth sheep misses the march rule: they follow, and you are responsible for how you lead.
