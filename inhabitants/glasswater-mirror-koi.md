# Glasswater Mirror-Koi

## Overview

Glasswater Mirror-Koi are pale koi of Glasswater Fields' clean waters whose scales reflect so evenly that still pools can look like they hold a second sky.

## Description

Long-bodied, cream and soft blue, slow-moving. They gather in the clearest freshwater shelves where Mega Butterflies also rest after migration. Touching the water above them rarely startles them; sudden shadow does.

## Beliefs About Them

Locals say Mirror-Koi 'keep the lull honest.' Polluting their pools is a serious social offense in Glasswater communities.

## Narrative Role

Visual calm for Glasswater—mystical beauty that is still an animal.
