# Claimscar Scav-Dogs

## Overview

Claimscar Scav-Dogs are wiry yard dogs of Claimscar that live off scraps, fights, and whatever a claim dispute leaves behind.

## Description

Thin ribs, patchy coats, bright hard eyes. They form loose packs with shifting leaders. Not beloved; sometimes useful as alarm systems. Bites are dirty.

## Beliefs About Them

Yard workers say Scav-Dogs 'file claims with teeth.' Feeding one can buy temporary loyalty; feeding many buys a problem.

## Narrative Role

Sorel texture—survival animals for improvised settlements.
