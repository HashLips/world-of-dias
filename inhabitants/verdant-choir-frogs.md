# Verdant Choir-Frogs

## Overview

Verdant Choir-Frogs are bright green frogs of Verdant Reach whose evening calls braid into layered chords rather than random croaks.

## Description

Thumb-to-palm sized, with gold eyelid rims and sticky toe-pads that shine after Softfall. They gather on Choir-Frog Reed stems and low leaves after rain. The chorus can sound almost rehearsed—low notes first, then bright answers—rising and falling in soft waves across wet meadows until the Reach feels like a held breath learning music. Up close each croak is ordinary; together they invent weather.

## Beliefs About Them

Locals say the frogs 'practice weather.' A sudden silence in the choir is treated as a coming storm or a stranger's wrong step. Walkers who keep talking through a hush are gently corrected.

## Cultural Touchstone

Wabet evening made audible: land that rehearses itself. Travellers who learn to wait for the choir’s restart learn the Reach’s patience in one listen.

## Narrative Role

Happy sonic texture for Verdant Reach scenes and Wabet's living calm. Classed as **wonder** in [Creatures of Dias](../world/creatures-of-dias.md).
