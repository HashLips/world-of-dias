# Flagweek Parade-Goats

## Overview

Flagweek Parade-Goats are decorated goats walked or ridden in Flagweek Strip revels, often wearing scrap-flags and painted horns.

## Description

Whatever goats the Strip can borrow—spotted, horned, stubborn. Handlers braid cloth into beards and teach a few to stop at drum hits. Chaos is part of the charm.

## Beliefs About Them

A Parade-Goat that refuses the route is cheered as having 'better politics.' Serious injury to one ruins the week for many.

## Narrative Role

Pure Sorel joy and improvisation—happy creatures in a hard land.
