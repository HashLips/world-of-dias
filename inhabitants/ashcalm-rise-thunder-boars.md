# Ashcalm Rise Thunder-Boars

## Overview

Ashcalm Rise Thunder-Boars are large boars whose charging gait sounds like distant thunder in the Rise's hollows.

## Description

Shoulder-high to an adult, bristled ash-dark coats, tusks hooked. Territorial at wallows. Ember-Deer flee their scent.

## Beliefs About Them

Travelers clap before entering thickets—better a warned boar than a surprised one.

## Narrative Role

Large sudden danger in an otherwise calming place.
