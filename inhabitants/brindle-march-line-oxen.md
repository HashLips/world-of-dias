# Brindle March Line-Oxen

## Overview

Brindle March Line-Oxen are large oxen trained to hold absolutely still with carts when the March's hush deepens, then walk as one when it lifts.

## Description

Ash-brindle coats, patient faces. Training is famous and expensive. A broken line-ox nerve can ruin a still-march schedule.

## Beliefs About Them

Drivers say the oxen feel stillness before people do.

## Narrative Role

Large working expression of Brindle discipline.
