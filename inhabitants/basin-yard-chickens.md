# Basin Yard-Chickens

## Overview

Basin Yard-Chickens are ordinary orchard-edge chickens of the Velorian Basin, kept for eggs, scrap cleanup, and the comfort of morning noise.

## Description

Speckled and rust-red flocks, dust-bathing in warm lanes after rain. They scatter for Softlane Hares and ignore Gilding Bees. Eggs travel to Veloria in straw; roosters argue with distant toll bells.

## Beliefs About Them

A quiet yard without chickens is said to be 'waiting for bad news.' Stealing a hen is a neighbor crime before it is a Concord crime.

## Narrative Role

Barnyard completeness for Basin life—food and soundtrack.
