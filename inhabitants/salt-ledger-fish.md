# Salt-Ledger Fish

## Overview

Salt-Ledger Fish are small silver fish kept in brine troughs near The Salt Ledger, used for food, oil, and as living markers of water quality.

## Description

Palm-sized, bright-eyed, tolerant of harsh salt mixes that kill softer fish. They flash in dense schools when troughs are stirred. Some troughs are older than the sheds around them.

## Beliefs About Them

Workers say the fish 'read the salt'—if they rise too high, something in the mix has gone wrong.

## Narrative Role

Small, visual, work-adjacent life for Sorel's ledger-and-labor spaces.
