# Saltwhisper Jackals

## Overview

Saltwhisper Jackals are lean coastal-desert jackals whose yips carry oddly far along Saltwhisper Road, often mistaken for human whispers at distance.

## Description

Salt-stained coats, narrow muzzles, eyes reflecting lamp light in pale coins. They scavenge wreckage and hunt small duneside game. Rarely attack groups; lone night walkers are at real risk.

## Beliefs About Them

Road crews answer jackal yips with louder work-songs to reclaim the soundscape. Folklore claims some jackals learn traveler names from dropped papers.

## Narrative Role

Edge-of-Wabet danger with an auditory uncanny that fits the road's name.
