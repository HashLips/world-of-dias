# Bandwidth Sky-Mantas

## Overview

Bandwidth Sky-Mantas are colossal manta-forms that eclipse whole Meridian segments when they pass, turning gold fields briefly champagne-dim.

## Description

Soft-Rays look like children beneath them. Passage is weather and animal at once. Danger is awe-disorientation more than appetite.

## Beliefs About Them

Aurel calendars mark rare manta weeks.
