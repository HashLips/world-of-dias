# Flying with Cats

## Name

Flying with Cats

## Overview

**Flying with Cats** are Cinder Nook non-human companions: darker purple ear- or horn-pointed flyers that travel with a larger lavender glider and leave white ribbon-wakes so ash-soft air stays navigable.

## Description

Nook witnesses describe small pointed-head forms in deep purple, lighter lavender leads, and bright white curved trails that fade slowly in mint-clear air (or ash-haze). They are not Earth cats filed for natural history; the name is local shorthand for ear-pointed air-neighbors. Temper is playful and path-loyal. Bad craft is scrubbing wakes to hide a selfish route. Children learn to read white ribbons before crossing a glide lane.

## Notes

Non-human air companions. Leave a wake; share the lane.
