# Sorel Scrap-Roaches

## Overview

Sorel Scrap-Roaches thrive in salvage walls and warm forge cracks, impossible to fully exile and oddly respected for toughness.

## Description

Thumb-sized, grease-brown, fast. Eat almost anything soft. Startle people; rarely bite.

## Beliefs About Them

Killing one is fine; pretending Sorel has none is lying.

## Narrative Role

Honest small grit fauna.
