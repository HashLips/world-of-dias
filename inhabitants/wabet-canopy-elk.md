# Wabet Canopy Elk

## Overview

Wabet Canopy Elk are large elk that move the lower canopy floors like living furniture of the woods, antlers wide as cartwheels.

## Description

Deep brown coats, quiet steps for their size. Rarely charge without cornering. Browse high shrubs Soft-Bears cannot reach.

## Beliefs About Them

Killing one for trophy alone is a Wabet shame story for years.

## Narrative Role

Large magnificent peaceful forest presence.
