# Hearthvale Hearth-Crickets

## Overview

Hearthvale Hearth-Crickets sing from warm stone seams near door-hearths, marking home more reliably than clocks for some exiles.

## Description

Dark little crickets, song steady and low. Cats hunt them; households often forgive a few survivors.

## Beliefs About Them

A silent hearth cricket week means the house is 'thinking.' Or the cat won.

## Narrative Role

Small sound of belonging in Sorel.
