# Weird Animal

## Name

Weird Animal

## Overview

**Weird animals** are large black arched creatures of Lumira Sands that stand on two thin bird-clawed legs and plant the other end of their long bodies in the sand, holding still for hours. Even locals call them weird; nobody has a better name.

## Description

A grown one stands about as tall as a person at the top of its arch. Its body is thick, smooth, and matte black, warm to the touch even at night. The two legs are slender and jointed, ending in splayed, hooked claws that grip loose sand. The other end—whether head, tail, or something else—curves down and rests flat or slightly buried. Nobody has seen a face. They move rarely and slowly, lifting the planted end, taking a few strides, and planting it again somewhere new. They make no sound anyone has recorded. They are seen alone, never in groups.

## Lore Connection

Sands folk believe the planted end listens to the dune's movement, and that a weird animal standing near a route means the sand there is settled and safe. Silence-route Keepers ask travelers not to walk under the arch, not to dig near the planted end, and not to shout to make it move. Children are told that if you ever see the end lift and turn toward you, you should simply say good evening.

## Notes

Non-human. Head, diet, and senses unknown. Culture- and religion-agnostic.
