# Hearthline Warm-Rats

## Overview

Hearthline Warm-Rats are hardy rats that thrive in trace-heat seams along F840 corridors, neither beloved nor hated—simply persistent.

## Description

Soot-colored, thick-tailed, unafraid of quiet humans. They eat salvage crumbs and keep insect blooms down near stores.

## Beliefs About Them

Killing them wastefully is frowned on; clean trapping near food is normal.

## Narrative Role

Ordinary aftermath life—resilience without romance.
