# Fellgrove Bark-Wolves

## Overview

Fellgrove Bark-Wolves are large wolves with coats that catch bark-dust until they look like moving tree trunks at dusk.

## Description

Big, patient, pack hunters. Camouflage is dirt and patience more than glamour. Threat to lone trail users.

## Beliefs About Them
