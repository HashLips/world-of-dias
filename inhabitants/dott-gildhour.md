# Dott Gildhour

## Name

Dott Gildhour

## Overview

**Dott Gildhour** guides visitors through basin gilding hour and notices when jokes die near certain slits. Bright professional smile, pamphlet stack, and a private flinch when chalk lessons mention null. After the Caliburn’s escape, Dott updates courtesy cards with softer language than Houses use.

## Description

Mass-market neatness: pressed scarf, polished shoes that still collect Row grit, voice trained for pretty views. Carries visitor pamphlets the way Tam carries a rag—always, and never quite clean of the day’s weather. Dott’s job is to keep wonder safe enough for paying guests without lying about the city’s multi-band heart.

Dott is not a scientist and not a Ward hand. Dott is the interface: translates Row talk into basin courtesy, watches faces, counts when a cluster goes quiet. Null talk tightens the smile. Residual seams make Dott steer groups away without naming fear.

Concrete saga beat: in Two Dramas, Dott leads a gilding-hour cluster past Orrin’s bench. When chalk mentions null, the professional smile thins—the fear of scaring people who paid for light. Later, after partial publish and escape, Dott rewrites cards for Jori’s ledge visitors: if a dial loves you, check twice. Houses call that unnecessary. Dott calls it manners.

Physical read: bright scarf colors that change with tourist season, pamphlet corners soft from thumbing, a smile that can be measured in degrees of null-fear. Dott practices jokes that die cleanly—better a quiet cluster than a panicked one.

After escape, Dott and Jori share a private tally mark system for ask-again days. Neither calls it evidence. Both trust it more than House calm.

## Beliefs About Them

Visitors love Dott. Ward hands tolerate Dott. Row apprentices mock the smile and still borrow pamphlets for scrap paper. Jori remembers Dott’s counts when ask-again stamps rise. Orrin nods to Dott as if to a weather station.

## Narrative Role

Visitor interface across teaching, publish, and aftermath. Keeps the saga’s public face human while wrong ticks spread.
