# Long-Gate Beast

## Overview

The Long-Gate Beast is a disputed large creature said to haunt the conceptual and literal thresholds between Nauw judgment and Sorel arrival—sometimes a bridge-shadow, sometimes a road-shaped hunger.

## Description

No agreed body plan: some say wolf-bear, some say a moving knot of coats and eyes, some say only a pressure that makes carts lean. Sightings spike during gate disputes and exile seasons.

## Beliefs About Them

Nauw authorities call it a story to scare compliance. Sorel storytellers keep it alive as the price of borders. Both uses can be true while the beast remains unproven.

## Narrative Role

Big folklore antagonist energy for gate politics—scary, symbolic, optionally literal.
