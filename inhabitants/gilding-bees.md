# Gilding Bees

## Overview

Gilding Bees are small golden-backed bees of the Velorian Basin whose pollen trails catch late-day light like thin threads of leaf-gold.

## Description

Slightly larger than common honeybees, with translucent amber wings and a soft metallic sheen on the thorax that turns true gold in gilding hour. Hives favor orchard edges and clay wall niches. Their hum sits in a narrow warm register—less buzz, more held note—that orchard workers recognize before they see the swarm. Pollen trails hang briefly in still air like scratched sunlight, then dissolve. Honey tastes faintly of Mist-Herb when the Basin has been kind.

## Beliefs About Them

Basin farmers say Gilding Bees 'paint the afternoon.' Disturbing a hive without offering water is considered rude more than dangerous; stings are rare but sharp.

## Narrative Role

Supports the Basin's sun-sweet agricultural mood and gives visual poetry to ordinary harvest days.
