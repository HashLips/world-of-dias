# Secondfire Ash-Crows

## Overview

Secondfire Ash-Crows are soot-gray crows that gather where rebuild fires and forge work leave warm ash beds in Secondfire.

## Description

Slightly larger than common crows, feathers dusted matte ash, calls rough and short. They steal nails, tags, and bright trash. Day-visible and loud about it.

## Beliefs About Them

Some rebuild crews read a sudden Ash-Crow silence as a coming quarrel. Others just throw a crust and keep working.

## Narrative Role

Bird life for Sorel's rebuild-and-scrap aesthetic.
