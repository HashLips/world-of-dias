# Teddy

## Name

Teddy

## Overview

**Teddy** is a small blue presence reported in F610 households during reveal windows: a round-eared, soft-bodied thing that sits on the fold of a sleeper's blanket and leans toward their breathing. In ordinary light it looks exactly like a stitched toy.

## Description

Accounts agree on two blues—bright cobalt head and ears, deeper navy body—and on one ear lined with green, as if it had been patched. Its arms hang over the blanket edge with toy-like slack, but witnesses who catch it during a reveal window insist its head turns to follow the sleeper's face and that it smiles slightly when breathing steadies. It has never been seen walking. It is simply somewhere else in the morning: the windowsill, the foot of the bed, once inside a boot. Children who sleep near one tend to report fewer bad dreams and more dreams about striped fields.

## Lore Connection

F610's gift is showing structure that is usually hidden. Households in the band treat Teddy as a small example of that gift turned domestic: a watcher that is only visible when the veil thins. Families leave a clear spot on the blanket fold, do not wash the figure while someone is ill, and never take it out of the house on a reveal night.

## Notes

Non-human. Whether it is stitched, alive, or both is unresolved. Culture- and religion-agnostic.
