# Jorin Fell

## Name

Jorin Fell

## Overview

Jorin Fell was an elderly Verdant Reach apple farmer, owner of **Fellgrove**, who hired **Frederick Lumens** after seeing Apple Touch fill his off-season rows—and who later willed the farm to Frederick, having no family of his own.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Thin, slow, precise with pruning knives. Jorin had worked the same apple ground longer than most Keepers stay in one posting. He competed for years with neighboring apple grower **Bram Orlen**—not with hatred, with the stubborn math of who reached market first with the brightest fruit.

When Frederick arrived hungry and strange with a gift that made apples answer any living wood, Jorin did not ask for mountain stories first. He asked for a demonstration. He kept the boy. He fed him. He taught season rules even when seasons no longer limited Fellgrove the same way.

## Beliefs About Them

Lane neighbors called him fair and fading. Bram called him a rival who suddenly would not lose. Hands who worked one harvest under Jorin said he spoke little and meant the will when he wrote it.

## Narrative Role

The welcome Bram refused to give; the mortal bridge from Giftbroth wonder to Frederick owning land—and the rivalry thread that keeps Bram Orlen in the saga.

## Notes

Deceased within The Lumen Saga timeline after the will chapter. Do not resurrect without creator instruction.
