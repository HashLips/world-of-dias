# Claimscar Breakfast Caller

## Name

Claimscar Breakfast Caller

## Overview

**Claimscar Breakfast Caller** is the yard voice that opens morning stew. Shared kettle first. Secondfire heat later. The Caller makes breakfast a political hour without making it a trial, and the distinction is the whole craft: people who will not share a vent may still share a bowl, and someone has to say the morning has started before the paint-wars do.

## Description

Loud enough to reach the tarps, careful enough not to name a sprout window before the kettle has gone around. Dogs listen first, Hungry Dogs included, because a voice that feeds is a voice worth placing in the day. Crew marks watch the Caller’s hands around the ladle more than they watch the mouth. Hands tell you whether the stew is a welcome or a claim with steam on it.

They stand in the Cookfire Circle, boots in ash that still remembers night arguments, and they do not pretend the night did not happen. They sequence it. Eat. Then talk about who slept nearest the vent. A Caller who starts with the vent has already lost the morning, and the yard will make them feel it by eating in a silence that is not peace.

Peppersage in the pot. A joke that is allowed to be small. Eye contact that does not become a challenge unless someone brings a challenge in their bowl.

## Beliefs About Them

Some say Callers end more fights than captains, because a fight that has been fed is easier to shrink. Others say Callers delay necessary ugliness until night, and secondfire has to do the ugly work with interest. Both sayings are how the yard admits the role matters. Garen Scar’s people treat a good Caller as weather they can plan around. A bad Caller, one who plays favorite with the ladle, does not stay a Caller past the week the dogs stop coming first.

## Narrative Role

Claimscar breakfast arcs and the myth of the breakfast that ended a double claim. The Caller is a role, not a single named destiny. Stories may put a person in the role for a morning. They should not crown them owner of the yard.

## Notes

Role, not a chosen captain of fate. Frontier hospitality with grit, not a war drum.
