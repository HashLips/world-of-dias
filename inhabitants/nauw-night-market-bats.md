# Nauw Night-Market Bats

## Overview

Nauw Night-Market Bats skim above evening stalls eating moths and midges, a welcome blur over lamp rows.

## Description

Small bats, quick turns, rarely tangle hair despite rumors. Guano under certain eaves is managed weekly.

## Beliefs About Them

Stallkeepers bless the bats and curse the broom work equally.

## Narrative Role

Small helpful night fauna.
