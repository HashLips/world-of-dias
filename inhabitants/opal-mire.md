# Opal Mire

## Name

Opal Mire

## Overview

Opal Mire is ground crew for The Late Bruise. She loads what she is told, including a cloth-wrapped weight that was not Softfruit, and her later silence is louder than Kett's story.

## Description

Practical, rope-burned palms, a knit cap, the habit of counting crates twice and then once more with her hand on the stack. She does not fly. She trusts Lark's landings more than Dain's labels. On the Wabet side she watches rural people cover their ears and does not laugh. On the Veloria side she watches a loader arm take the honest crates and a man carry the other weight into a private corner of the shed.

## Beliefs About Them

Kett says Opal knows. Opal says knowing a weight is not knowing a fruit. After the show she does not contradict anyone. She coils rope.

## Narrative Role

The hands at the hull. Makes the "fruit in, not fruit out" a worked fact rather than a bar invention.

## Notes

Stays employed by the work, not by the legend. When the hire collapses she still knows how to tie a load for a wagon, which matters more than it sounds.
