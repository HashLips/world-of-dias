# Good Feelings

## Name

Good Feelings

## Overview

**Good Feelings** is a Nauw Outer Rim Seas non-human presence: a black breath-worker that raises glossy golden orbs through a red looped wand, teaching that cheer should be dense, countable, and kind.

## Description

Pier witnesses describe a reclining black form with one white eye, a red loop wand, and rising gold spheres that cast soft blue shadows—weight you can track. Temper is quiet and focused. Locals reject Earth bubble-toy readings; these orbs are rim wonder-matter in talk. Bad craft is forcing orbs into someone’s face as fake joy. Children learn to count four before asking for a fifth. When the diagonal blue/lavender field feels sharp, the presence is near.

## Notes

Non-human breath-cheer. Dense orbs; no sting mist.
