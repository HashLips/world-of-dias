# Driftfall Harbor-Seals

## Overview

Driftfall Harbor-Seals haul out on salvage piers, steal fish, and occasionally save overboard drunks by accident of curiosity.

## Description

Speckled coats, scarred flippers from scrap edges. Medium ocean mammal. Loud. Smelly. Beloved anyway.

## Beliefs About Them

Deckhands say a seal at the ladder means someone will fall in—and maybe get help.

## Narrative Role

Medium coastal life for Driftfall's messy humanity.
