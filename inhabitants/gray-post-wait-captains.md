# Gray Post Wait Captains

## Name

Gray Post Wait Captains

## Overview

**Gray Post Wait Captains** are mariners known for waiting well when Averra’s pier inhales. Tokens ready, rage unused, jokes timed for fog. They have made an art of not circling. The isle does not reward the hunt, and these captains have decided, professionally, to stop looking like hunters.

## Description

Salt coats, pale courtesy discs worn smooth in pockets, eyes that check the water for a gray finger of timber without making a speech about it. Their contracts cancel quieter in their hands than in other hands: a delay explained as weather, a client kept from turning absence into an insult that has to be answered with a louder boat. In a good hour they moor lightly, as if the Post might still change its mind, because it might. In a bad hour they brew tea and do not send the skiff to “just see.”

They know Crown Ash as a bearing and the Hearthline as a rumor of heat. They do not sell either as a landing. Crews under them learn that impatience is a kind of noise, and noise is how you spend a week of fuel explaining a gap.

## Beliefs About Them

Harbors respect them in the unsentimental way harbors respect people whose boats come back. Reckless crews call them timid until the Post returns for the patient boat and not for the bold one, and then the word changes to luck, which the captains do not bother to correct. Luck is easier for a reckless crew to say than manners. Damp-studio rumor likes them because they treat bearings as bearings. Old Masters stay rumor. A Wait Captain who claims otherwise has stopped being one.

## Narrative Role

Averra offshore texture without invading the isle. Waiting-week scenes. The person who can carry a story up to the pier and no further, which is as far as the story should go.

## Notes

Manners professionals. Not smugglers-by-default, not chosen interpreters of Averra’s interior.
