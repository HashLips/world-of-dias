# Isle Rope-Tree Beetles

## Overview

Isle Rope-Tree Beetles live in bark seams of the Rope Tree, tiny-to-small beetles that glitter like misplaced ribbon clasps.

## Description

Bead-sized to thumbnail. Harmless. Attracted to blue dye traces on old ribbons.

## Beliefs About Them

Picking beetles off the tree for souvenirs is frowned on harder than the beetles warrant.

## Narrative Role

Tiny sacred-adjacent insect at a key landmark.
