# Vesh Wrong-Map

## Overview

**Vesh Wrong-Map** is the Keep founding mercy: the surveyor-turned-teacher who made intentional wrongness into water ethics after a perfect chart killed a well’s willingness to be found.

## Description

Soft boots; decoy dunes. Some say Vesh *was* the surveyor of the myth; others say Vesh only learned from that wound and built the school.

## Beliefs About Them

Keep apprentices toast Vesh quietly. Map-publishers curse Vesh. Children learn decoys “for Vesh.”

## Narrative Role

Warning/builder—forgetting as care.

## Notes

Living Iskar Lüm seats the Keep now; Vesh is the scar under assent.
