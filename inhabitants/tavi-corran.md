# Tavi Corran

## Name

Tavi Corran

## Overview

Tavi Corran is a young Aumeri engineer of Kharad build, or so near it that nobody on the Long Shore would tell the difference. He rebuilt three cradles of the Aumere Turning Boom after a Hushlean storm and made the stone weights heavier so the timbers would ride lower. That heavier stone broke *The Patient Lead* and drowned Dorrit Vash. Afterwards he is the island's dissenter. He says aloud that the refusal has just cost a life and that silence does not make it free. In the clash he is opened by a Ward boarding hook and sinks, and Ansel holds him up by floating.

## Description

Enormous, iron-grained skin with the dull sheen of worked metal, a face like a rockfall, a voice "like stone being dragged." He moves deliberately. He rows as if the oars were straw. In water he is heavy. He can work hand over hand along the boom timbers, but in open water with no timber to hold he goes down. He loves counterweights, ropes, troughs and anything that does work without hauling.

**Can:** build and mend the boom and the cliff cradles; carry a person up steps; argue with a gathering.
**Cannot:** float. He cannot undo the weight he made.
**Cost:** a wound across the back of the shoulder from the clash. Whether he lived is not known on the Long Shore.

## Beliefs About Them

Some Aumeri think his dissent is grief talking. Others think he is the first honest voice in a generation. Ansel thinks he is the only person on the island who understood why the second letter mattered.

## Narrative Role

He is the cause of the strike and the conscience of the island. He shows that the Aumeri are not of one mind. His rescue by Ansel is the saga's turn of the floating motif: the body that separated Ansel from his crew saves Tavi.

## Notes

Keep him "Kharad-like." The Aumeri are a mixed community of known F432 kinds, not a new race.
