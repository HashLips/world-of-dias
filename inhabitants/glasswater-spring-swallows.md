# Glasswater Spring-Swallows

## Overview

Glasswater Spring-Swallows return each bright inshore-adjacent spring to skim Mirror-Reed water and nest under field eaves—a seasonal joy marker.

## Description

Forked tails, ink-dark backs, white bellies. They arrive in chattering ribbons, hunt midges above lull pools, and leave when heat thickens. Children count first swallow day like a festival without paperwork.

## Beliefs About Them

First swallow means the Fields are 'awake kindly.' Catching one for a cage is socially punished harder than the law requires.

## Narrative Role

Seasonal visitor that makes Glasswater feel cyclical and gentle.
