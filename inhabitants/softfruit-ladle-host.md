# Softfruit Ladle Host

## Name

Softfruit Ladle Host

## Overview

**Softfruit Ladle Host** names a rotating court role, not one destined person. The Host watches shelf fairness, the mint rinse, and the theatrical refusal of almost-oranges. Whoever wears the sticky apron this morning is the Host. Tomorrow the apron is someone else’s, and the court likes it that way, because joy infrastructure rots when it becomes a personality cult.

## Description

Sticky apron, token pockets that clack when they turn too fast, a laugh that ends stamp rushes without a speech long enough to humiliate. They stand where they can see the second-ladle shelf and the faces in the queue. Noticing is the skill: who has not eaten, who is performing not-hungry, who is about to turn a mood into a rule. Sometimes Sera Quillmend parks nearby with notes. The Host role stays plural on purpose even then. Sera can watch. Sera does not become the shelf.

Hands stay busy. A token returned to a hook. A ladle rinsed with mint. A child’s peel-boat nudged back into the race under the shelf. When someone flashes second-helpings like winnings, the Host takes the stack, hangs it up, and is already looking at the next true hunger.

## Beliefs About Them

Children think the Host owns joy, which the Hosts encourage only until the child is old enough to see the rotation. Houses think the Host wastes time that could have been a seal. Streets know the Host prevents worse seals: the stamp that lands before anyone has been fed, the line that becomes a hierarchy with fruit on it. Pride, Softfruit says, is the only poverty worth mocking, and the Host is allowed to mock it lightly and in public.

## Valhop and the Ladle

When a **Valhop** game ends near the court, the loser's custom of pouring the first bowl meets the Host's queue (see [`Valhop rules`](../rules/valhop-rules.md)). The Host keeps the two apart without fuss. The loser pours from their own turn in the line, never by stepping ahead of it. A win at Valhop is not a claim on the second-ladle shelf. Anyone who waves a Valhop win around like a token of extra hunger gets the same light public mockery as someone flashing second helpings. Boards are never unfolded on the shelf itself, because the shelf is for food and fairness, not for a game.

Off duty, Hosts play like anyone else, usually at the back benches of Softfruit Table Hall. They are known for losing with spectacular grace and asking "Bowl?" before anyone else can, a habit they picked up from Sorel visitors. A Host who lands a bear twenty is expected to give the first fruit away anyway. On the court, generosity still outranks the score.

## Narrative Role

The civic face for ladle scenes. Queue mornings. The pause before a stamp. Leave personal canon names empty unless a story already earned a person; the role is the entry.

## Notes

A role, not a chosen hero. Fairness before folklore about a single kind host.
