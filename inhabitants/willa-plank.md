# Willa Plank

## Name

Willa Plank

## Overview

Willa Plank keeps The Stillwood Skiff and will not explain what holds it up. She is kind to children and firm with adults who want a ferry.

## Description

Old, straight-backed, a pole always longer than the conversation. Gray braid under a cap. Clothes mended in the Wabet way, quiet cloth, no emblem. Her face gives away effort and almost nothing else. She nods at stewards. She stops for gates. She has been asked by travelers, clerks, and one drunk poet what frequency she is "on," and she has answered that she is on the lane.

## Beliefs About Them

Some neighbors say she is blessed. Some say the wood is old enough to have forgotten the ground. She says she does not know. The consistency of that sentence is why people still ride past her without trying to steal the skiff. Theft would require understanding which part to steal.

## Narrative Role

Carries the unexplained wooden lift through the rural night of the saga without turning it into the plot's solution. The stew does not need her. The world does, so that lift can exist and stay unclosed.

## Notes

Do not let a later chapter decide the lift. Her unknown is load-bearing.
