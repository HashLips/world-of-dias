# That-Forest Shadow-Lynx

## Overview

That-Forest Shadow-Lynx are medium wild cats that hunt dusk trails, more patient than wolves and harder to hear.

## Description

Smoke-gray coats, tufted ears, eyes like wet leaves. Solo hunters. Attack livestock and careless lone campers; avoid crowds.

## Beliefs About Them

Trail marks warn of lynx weeks. Guides sleep in shifts anyway.

## Narrative Role

Medium feared predator for Wabet forest stories.
