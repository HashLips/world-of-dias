# Sarn Vell

## Name

Sarn Vell

## Overview

Sarn Vell is the working master of The Banana Draft. He takes fruit the last wet miles into the basin and does not reorganize his canal around a hull in the sky.

## Description

Thickset, sun on the nose, a yellow coat that was meant to match the barge and now matches the stains. He knows quays, fines, and the hour when banana crates start to sigh. He pours tea that tastes of the hold. He is polite to third-ring winch crews and rude to anyone who tells him to hurry a ripe load.

## Beliefs About Them

Market stalls trust his timing more than they trust announcements. He finds the Vitrin story interesting the way weather is interesting: something to route around.

## Narrative Role

The other water craft's person. Shows distribution continuing by barge after the show and after the chalk.

## Notes

Nauw fruit reverence is his culture in the practical sense. He ranks bananas above spectacles.
