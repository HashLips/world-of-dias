# Jessa Quill

## Name

Jessa Quill

## Overview

Jessa Quill is Hollowmere’s loft-memory: the singer and tally-keeper who remembers which craft answered which line, and whether a season’s first spout was honestly agreed.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Jessa works the highest net-lofts with chalk, tea, and an unromantic ear. She can bless a First Line Answer and also shame a boat that lied about being within hail. Marek Solon trusts her lists more than speeches.

## Beliefs About Them

Apprentices fear her corrections and want her nod. Traders call her the cliff’s second weather.

## Narrative Role

Coastal saga anchor who can start a crisis by refusing to call a dishonest first spout “first.”

## Notes

Not a Tide Warden; a Compact-critical memory role.
