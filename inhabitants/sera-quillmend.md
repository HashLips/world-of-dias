# Sera Quillmend

## Name

Sera Quillmend

## Overview

**Sera Quillmend** runs a Softfruit cart at the Row corner and treats market talk as field data. Orrin trusts her because she refuses to let Houses own the sky. Joy anchor and folk-data engine for **The Calibration Saga**.

## Description

Middle height, sun-browned forearms, hair tied with peel-twine, a voice that can sell an orange and grade a dawn in the same breath. Keeps a peel-stained field notebook where fruit-keeping and sky moods share columns. Laughs at F-numbers until they prove useful, then charges honest for the proof.

Works Softfruit Corner by the Row: oil on one side, citrus on the other. Children know her for free Peel-Sweet Herb sprigs. Clerks know her for refusing stamps that erase jokes. She will not sell almost-oranges—and she will not let Orrin buy almost-truth without calling it what it is.

Concrete saga beat: in Softfruit as Witness, Orrin interviews Sera and learns the basin already measures with mouths. She returns at triumph, steered-science, publish, and widen temptation—keeping loveable texture in the room when glass turns helpful. In Widening as Gift she stands for market honesty against hunger with better instruments; in Partial Truth for the Basin she backs incomplete truth that people can use.

Physical read: sun-browned forearms, peel-twine hair tie, cart stained with honest citrus, notebook that accepts fruit juice as a citation format. Laugh lines from selling and from refusing bad sky stories.

Sera’s method—Softfruit Field Interview—keeps frequency science from becoming only glass and stamps. Mouths measured the basin first; Orrin learns to write that down.

## Beliefs About Them

Buyers trust her weather talk more than pamphlets. Houses call her unsystematic. Orrin calls her colleague until Auntie Mosscoil scolds him for it. Issa trusts her to tell when a disclaimer sounds like a lie. Tam trades oil rags for fruit and thinks he got the better deal.
