# Rate

## Name

Rate

## Overview

**Rate** is a Mercer Shed non-human presence: a pink craft-cylinder with a crimson dripping crown that holds nine yellow armless candle-figures, each tipped with a small orange flame under a lime halo.

## Description

Shed talk treats Rate as one being made of a shared body and nine flame-faces. The dusty pink sides and pale band are the trunk; the crimson drip-top is the working crown; the nine yellow tapers are not guests—they are Rate's countable selves, eye-dots quiet, mouths tiny lines, wicks thin. When all nine burn, the lime semicircle in the plate is said to brighten. Local care forbids lighting only the "pretty" ones, and forbids using Rate as a joke cake for strangers without the count lesson. Resonance is the matched flames; memory is the olive shadow on teal telling boards; wonder is how ceremonial a cylinder can feel without a sermon. Witnesses disagree on whether the figures can lean; they agree the count matters. Depict non-human. Soft talk that files Rate as Earth party food misses the shed rule: the rate is the nine together.
