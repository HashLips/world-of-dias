# Rae Fruitrail

## Overview

**Rae Fruitrail** is a fruit-road kid who carries notes and bruise-timing messages between Tomas Share–style convoys and Nauw market edges—mule-swap runner, not courier hero.

## Description

Wagon-wave fluent, share-knife respectful, pockets full of hedge-berry stains. Rae has never seen Operator floors and does not want to. Knows which waystations restore and which take.

## Beliefs About Them

Convoys trust Rae with small truths. Rank-blind adults get corrected by a child and hate it usefully.

## Narrative Role

Child + bridge—orchard grammar into market grammar.
