# Redmile Striders

## Overview

Redmile Striders are long-legged crimson-legged predators that haunt lonely stretches between Nauw towns, especially where fruit roads thin into scrub.

## Description

Body like a lean hound stretched upward on stilts, coat dust-tan, lower legs stained a dried-blood red that may be pigment or diet-mark. They chase in silence, preferring lone walkers and separated carts. Packs are rare; pairs are worse. Footfalls on hardpan sound like distant stick-clicks before the rush.

## Beliefs About Them

Caravans ring bells and travel denser after dusk reports. Children are told Redmile Striders smell fear sweeter than fruit. The practice is myth-braided in [The Bells Against Redmile](../myths/the-bells-against-redmile.md).

## Avoidance Customs

After dusk Strider reports: bells, tighter spacing, no single-cart rear. Softlane Hares ahead remain a kindness omen; bells answer when kindness is not enough. Do not romanticize lone midnight walks on redmile scrub.

## Narrative Role

A clear 'do not encounter alone' threat for road stories—dangerous without being supernatural by default. Classed as **hazard** in [Creatures of Dias](../world/creatures-of-dias.md).
