# Foundation Revisers

## Name

Foundation Revisers

## Overview

**Foundation Revisers** publicly adjust the courtyards of the Unfinished: latch heights, guide lines, persistence ticks. They are Open Hand maintenance as a social role, people you can watch revise a city without the revision pretending to be vandalism or destiny. A building that cannot bear them is not precious. It is unfinished in the bad way, the brittle way.

## Description

Chalk on the sleeves, copper pencils behind the ears, a calm that does not panic when someone cries because a door moved. Many are Draftkin, native to iterative making. Some are visiting makers learning pass language, awkward with the ticks at first, then relieved that a place will admit it was wrong in public. They kneel. They talk to wood. They leave the underdrawing visible so the court remembers it had a previous thought and survived it.

In the Ghost Quarter they work where delegations can see, because hidden revision becomes conspiracy in the mouths of people who worship final outlines. A Revisers’ argument is about weight and hands, not about winning history.

## Beliefs About Them

Finality-minded visitors call them vandals, especially if a favorite stair was tragic and is now merely steep. Locals call them caretakers and bring them tea when the southwest corner has softened overnight. Children ask them why the fountain is a suggestion. The answer, patient, is that a suggestion you can walk on is still allowed to change. Atlas disputes want them as witnesses because a ledger without a Revisers’ hand is a rumor about stone.

## Narrative Role

F960 craft faces. Redraw mornings. The person who refuses a ribbon across a scaffold. They let stories of becoming stay about maintenance, which is where becoming actually lives.

## Notes

Iterative stewards. Do not complete their quarter for them, and do not make a single Revisers into the author of the whole Unfinished.
