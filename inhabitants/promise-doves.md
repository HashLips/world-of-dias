# Promise Doves

## Overview

Promise Doves are soft gray doves of Hallow Bay that nest near the Rope Tree and guest hall eaves, often present when newcomers tie their ribbons.

## Description

Round bodies, coral-pink feet, calls like short sighs. They are ordinary doves in body; their timing around ribbon rites makes them feel ceremonial.

## Beliefs About Them

A dove that lands while you tie is read as the bay noticing. Chasing them off during rites is considered clumsy manners.

## Narrative Role

Quiet kindness at Calareth arrivals—happy without cartoon sweetness.
