# Calibration Row Ink-Sparrows

## Overview

Calibration Row Ink-Sparrows nest in eave ledgers and hop desk to desk for crumbs, leaving tiny prints on drying pages.

## Description

Ordinary sparrow size, slightly duskier. Bold around clerks. Droppings are the true enemy of calibration.

## Beliefs About Them

A sparrow on the abacus is either luck or a reason to reclean.

## Narrative Role

Small lively office fauna.
