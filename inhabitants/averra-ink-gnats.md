# Averra Ink-Gnats

## Overview

Averra Ink-Gnats are tiny dark gnats that stain fingertips like dilute ink when slapped, beloved as a studio annoyance.

## Description

Speck clouds near wet pigment and cliff damp. Stain washes off slowly. Bite mild.

## Beliefs About Them

Apprentices claim the gnats edit bad lines. Masters claim apprentices need soap.

## Narrative Role

Comic small pest for Averra craft life.
