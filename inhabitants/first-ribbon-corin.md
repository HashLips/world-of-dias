# First Ribbon Corin

## Overview

**First Ribbon Corin** is remembered as the guest—or Watch cousin—who first tied blue assent at the Rope Tree and made belonging a public promise.

## Description

Salt-silvered hands; moss-green paint. Stories disagree whether Corin was island-born or first intentional ferry. All agree the ribbon outlived the name’s face.

## Beliefs About Them

Watch toasts at ribbon rites. Broken-ribbon shame is “Corin’s cut” in hard speech. Tourists mishear Corin as Coris; locals correct sharply.

## Narrative Role

Bridge—welcome with teeth.

## Notes

Distinct from living Coris Vale; name-similarity is island joke and trap for outsiders.
