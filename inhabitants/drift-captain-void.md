# Drift-Captain Void

## Overview

**Drift-Captain Void** is the paradoxical name for the captainless hour’s first law—breaker of throne-habits, bridge into Freeband motion-as-constitution.

## Description

No agreed face. Some say Void was a person who refused the chair; others say Void is the hour itself wearing a title. Shore pubs toast “Void’s hour” when no one owns the deck and the deck still holds.

## Beliefs About Them

Driftfall sacred joke. Hearthvale sometimes hears Void as chaos demon. Free-Captains claim Void’s blessing carefully—overclaim invites laughter.

## Narrative Role

Breaker/bridge—freedom as earned motion.
