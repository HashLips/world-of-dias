# Tess Softfall

## Overview

**Tess Softfall** is a canopy child who races soft-step on damp practice earth and leaves fruit on Soft-Bear ledges without announcing generosity.

## Description

Moss-stained shins, drip-moss in hair after Softfall, a laugh that stops for choir-frog hush without being told. Tess teaches younger kids the difference between honest empty breakfast bowls and greedy ones.

## Beliefs About Them

Trail walkers use Tess as proof the west still raises neighbors. Loud tourists find Tess quietly correcting their volume.

## Narrative Role

Child + Wabet joy entry—restraint as play.
