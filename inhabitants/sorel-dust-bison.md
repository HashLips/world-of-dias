# Sorel Dust-Bison

## Overview

Sorel Dust-Bison are large shaggy bovines that travel sparse southern grasslands in stubborn herds, hunted carefully and herded when possible.

## Description

Hump-shouldered, dust-caked, dangerous in stampede. Meat, hide, and bone keep frontier winters possible.

## Beliefs About Them

Herd approaches are treated like weather alerts.

## Narrative Role

Large livelihood-and-danger animal for Sorel.
