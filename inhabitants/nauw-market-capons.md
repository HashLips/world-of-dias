# Nauw Market Capons

## Overview

Nauw Market Capons are plump market birds sold and roasted along ring-fair rows—ordinary food animals with festival gravity.

## Description

Heavy, calm, carried in woven crates. Roast scent competes with banana stalls. Not pets; still named by some stall families out of habit. Bones go to Grin-Pigs and Lap-Cats in a peaceful afterlife of scraps.

## Beliefs About Them

Fair weeks without capon smoke feel 'underfunded in joy.'

## Narrative Role

Food-animal completeness for Veloria markets.
