# Averra Ink-Deer

## Overview

Averra Ink-Deer are dark-legged deer of Averra Isle whose hoofprints look briefly like ink blots before rain or tide takes them.

## Description

Slender, charcoal-and-cream, eyes reflective like wet ink. They move at the edges of studios and cliff paths. Not aggressive. Print 'ink' is a dark mineral residue from coastal soil and gland oils, lingering long enough to inspire myths.

## Beliefs About Them

Old Masters lore treats an Ink-Deer crossing a workday as a sign to keep the line honest. Some apprentices chase prints; masters say watch, don't chase.

## Narrative Role

Mystical-feeling but grounded—perfect for Averra's art-holy atmosphere.
