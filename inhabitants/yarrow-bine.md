# Yarrow Bine

## Name

Yarrow Bine

## Overview

Yarrow Bine holds the grass he lends as the Lent Strip. He wanted marks and a rested field. He got a famous one.

## Description

A lean holder in middle age, stake-mallet hands, a hat with a brim he trusts more than gossip. He is not poor and not a merchant of Veloria. His wealth is a field that can be lent without being lost, until too many feet disagree. He marks wet days with a cloth on a stake. He watches Lark work an hour on the grass and counts that hour as part of the price, which is why he tolerated the plane longer than his neighbors did.

## Beliefs About Them

Neighbors say he rented the quiet out from under Wabet. He says he rented a strip and someone else rented the truth. Both can be a little true. After the chalk, he is the adult who must decide whether the lend continues. The saga leaves the grass damaged and the decision sour, not neatly closed.

## Narrative Role

Owner of the courtesy the secret used. The cost of publicity sits on his field.

## Notes

Not the grower of Vitrin. He did not know the mix. He knew the wheels.
