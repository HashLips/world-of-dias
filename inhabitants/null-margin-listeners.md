# Null-Margin Listeners

## Name

Null-Margin Listeners

## Overview

**Null-Margin Listeners** are a contested name for people, or for remnants of people, said to linger at forget-texture fringes and come back with partial hours. The name is a courtesy handle, temporary on purpose. It is not a confirmed kind of being, and it is not a promotion of the Unraveled into a species you can draw in the margin of a bestiary.

## Description

Accounts conflict, which is the only stable description. Some witnesses remember ash-eyed travelers who answer questions a half-second late, as if the answer had to cross a room that was not there. Some remember empty-handed clerks, pockets light, unwilling to open a cupboard. Some remember silhouettes that Watching Absence lore tries to own, and the trying is the tell: a story reaching for a body. F432 courtesy prefers the temporary name and a warning tag over a portrait. Portrait is how rumor puts on a face and starts charging admission.

If one stands near you in a telling, the scene should feel like a missing step, not like a monster reveal. Color names slip. A Listener, so-called, may know they were elsewhere and not know the elsewhere well enough to spend it.

## Beliefs About Them

Dockside talk wants proof of abyss fauna with people’s silhouettes, because a fauna can be hunted or hired. Taboo teachers hold them up as proof of bad tourism: go to the edge for a story and the story comes back missing its middle. Softfruit manners treat the label as a story to refuse inventing further. All three beliefs can sit in one tavern. None of them licenses a field guide.

## Narrative Role

Pressure and rumor. A witness who came back partial. A classroom tag. A reason to keep Frequency Zero unsolved. Never a solved monster-manual entry, never a companion species, never a ferry pilot of the null.

## Notes

Status **rumor**. Do not promote the Unraveled, Horizon-Nulls, or Distant Companies through this label. Temporary naming is the mercy.
