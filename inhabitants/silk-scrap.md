# Silk Scrap

## Name

Silk Scrap

## Overview

**Silk Scrap** is a child errand-runner who steals filament ends for kite tails that fly in measure ticks. Small, fast, pockets full of waste turned into toys. Street joy beside Orrin’s science—and the first kid voice to name the Caliburn a glass person.

## Description

Nine to eleven across the saga’s telling, age left a little soft on purpose. Scrap-tied hair, knees always dusty, a kite stick longer than good manners. Moves like Pip-adjacent street joy without being Pip: local to Row corners, Softfruit carts, and filament piles Tam pretends to guard.

Steals gently. Returns snail shells. Pays for Softfruit with errands when coin fails. Adults call Silk a pest; Silk calls adults slow. Does not understand House stamps and does not need to.

Concrete saga beat: when the Caliburn arrives in Something Helpful in the Glass, Silk peeks into the alcove, whispers “glass person,” and flees with a filament end for the kite. Earlier, during Softfruit as Witness and the Frame build, Silk’s kite already “flew in ticks”—comedy that becomes omen without losing childhood. Tam pretends not to see the thefts both times.

Physical read: patched elbows, one shoe always looser, a laugh that arrives before the joke finishes. Collects filament ends, snail shells, and Softfruit stickers with equal seriousness.

Silk does not follow the Caliburn into city noise. The kite is enough. Adults who want Silk to “testify” get a shrug and a request for string.

## Beliefs About Them

Adults call Silk a pest. Tam protects the pretense. Sera slips free herb sprigs into Silk’s pocket. Auntie feeds Silk without questions. Visitors find the kite charming until they learn what glass person meant.

## Narrative Role

Child joy contrast across Frame construction and Caliburn arrival. Keeps wonder from belonging only to scientists and clerks.

## Notes

Not a Lumen Saga protagonist child. Local Row texture. See Softfruit as Witness, Building the Spectrum Frame, Something Helpful in the Glass.
