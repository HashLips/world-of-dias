# Aumeri

## Name

Aumeri

## Overview

The Aumeri are the people of Aumere, a hidden island in F432 waters south-west of the Saltjaw shelf. They are not a new race. They are a mixed community of about two hundred humans, Kharad-like and Choirglass-like people, descended from the survivors of the *Clearing*, a remembered time when the Long Shore found, charted and took from them. Since then they have kept themselves unfound: by the Turning Boom, by the count, by the blue, and by refusing.

They call the mainland *the Long Shore*. They watch Sprout Vent's smoke from the high terrace on the few clear days a year and call it *the mountain with many mouths*. They did not know the words Sorel, Driftfall, or VEL mark.

## Description

Their houses are thick-walled stone with turf roofs grown so deep they read as hillside from above. Doorways are short tunnels through the wall. Each floor has a central pit of loaf-sized stones over a slow peat fire. They farm cut terraces of small hard beans and a crisp silver-leaved salt-green, prise limpets from the cliff foot, dry fish thin as paper, and drink from cisterns cut into the rock and fed by rain channels and galleries. They build stitched-plank narrow boats with seams sewn in tiny even stitches of dark cord. They lift loads up the cliff in water-counterweighted cradles. They eat together at long tables. Midday is an hour's sleep for everyone. Their singing is mostly arguing set to a tune.

**Can:** engineer with counterweights, ropes and troughs; read the Counting Current from their pebble wall; keep the boom; make gathering decisions; in extremity, withdraw (see the [Aumere withdrawal resonance rule](../rules/aumere-withdrawal-resonance-rule.md)).
**Cannot:** leave freely (the sea only lets boats out on the Long Slack); use the withdrawal lightly, often, or as an attack; know the Long Shore's politics.
**Cost:** isolation, a dwindled population (three or four times as many before the Clearing), and the grief of the drowned skipper and of their own killed rower.

## Beliefs About Them

On the Long Shore, before the saga, there are none; nobody knew they existed. After it, the Ward calls them a bleed event, the backers a population on unclaimed stone, the road a door with people behind it, the seekers "the ones who went through." The letter-keepers call them the people who asked for no landing, no measuring and no mark, and they give them the Keep's courtesy.

## Narrative Role

They are the hidden people with a reasoned refusal. They are not villains (the island's own silence cost a life, and Tavi says so) and not saints. Their offer of words and the survivor is the saga's lost chance.
