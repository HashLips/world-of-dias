# Lumen Filament-Fish

## Overview

Lumen Filament-Fish are ribbonlike light-beings that swim F200's luminous fields as if water were brightness itself.

## Description

Bodies of warm gold filament, no true scales, motion like slow calligraphy. They gather along Meridian current seams. Contact feels like mild sun on skin, not heat burn, for brief touches.

## Beliefs About Them

Aurel-kind treat them as ordinary 'fish' of light; F432 visitors call them angels and get corrected.

## Narrative Role

Defines F200 fauna as light-flow life, not transplanted Earth fish.
