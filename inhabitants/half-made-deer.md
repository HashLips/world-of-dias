# Half-Made Deer

## Overview

Half-Made Deer appear with fully rendered heads and sketched hindquarters—or the reverse—walking stably anyway.

## Description

Seeing them causes depth-perception arguments. They graze on whatever vegetation is currently 'decided' in a zone. Not hostile.

## Beliefs About Them

Linked symbolically to Half-Made Atlas lore. Staring too long is said to leave your own hands feeling drafty.

## Narrative Role

Iconic magnificent weirdness for F960.
