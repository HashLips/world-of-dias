# Revision Wolves

## Overview

Revision Wolves are F960 predators feared for bites that leave wounds looking like erased and redrawn flesh before they settle into ordinary injury.

## Description

Lean, copper-accented, outlines unstable at the shoulders. Packs hunt in revision-heavy weather. Medical outcome is usually normal trauma plus lasting nightmare.

## Beliefs About Them

Draftkin teach: do not let a Revision Wolf decide your silhouette. Practical advice is spears and numbers.

## Narrative Role

Scary unfinished-band encounter—creative cosmology with real threat.
