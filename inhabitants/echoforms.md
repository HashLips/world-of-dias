# Echoforms

## Name

Echoforms

## Overview

An **Echoform** is a being created, transformed, or sustained through accumulated resonance. Echoforms emerge from Dias’s laws rather than arriving as unrelated fantasy creatures.

## Description

Strong memories, repeated events, environmental frequencies, collective emotion, ancient technology, or unusual natural phenomena may eventually produce living or semi-living manifestations.

An Echoform might arise from a battlefield saturated with fear, a forest held at one frequency for centuries, an abandoned city thick with overlapping memories, a joyful place lived in for generations, or a machine that kept resonating after its makers were gone.

Because they form from accumulated imprint, Echoforms **carry history in their existence**. They are not empty monsters; they are consequences.

## Beliefs About Them

Some cultures treat Echoforms as omens or ancestors. Others treat them as hazards to classify and contain. Scholars argue whether every Echoform is “alive” in the ordinary sense, or whether some are persistent phenomena wearing the shape of life.

Existing beings (for example **Sky Drifters**) should not be forcibly relabeled without evidence. Use Echoform as a class when origin-through-resonance is clear or strongly claimed.

## Narrative Role

Echoforms let Dias grow a creature ecology that stays tied to echo, frequency, and place—expandable without breaking world identity.
