# Brindle Rest-Sheep

## Overview

Brindle Rest-Sheep graze March berms near rest markers, providing wool scraps, calm company, and a reason for children to slow down during still miles.

## Description

Dust-tan wool, patient faces, bells too soft to break hush. Line-Oxen ignore them; Softlane Hares borrow their shade. Shearing happens at waystations as small festival.

## Beliefs About Them

Counting sheep on the March is not boredom—it is route discipline disguised as comfort.

## Narrative Role

Barnyard/road soft animal for Brindle texture.
