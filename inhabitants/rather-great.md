# Rather Great

## Name

Rather Great

## Overview

**Rather Great** is an F120 non-human presence: a chest-up watcher whose face splits magenta and royal purple around a cyan nose stripe, framed by an orange bob and a cyan garment with a sharp yellow V.

## Description

Realm walkers recognize Rather Great by the hard color split and the leftward almond eyes under teal brows. The pale lavender smile stays small; greatness is not a loud performance. The yellow triangular collar is part of the recognition, as is the orange blunt bob. People say the cyan nose stripe is the honesty line between the two face weathers. Local care forbids demanding a blessing speech from the presence, and forbids painting over one face-half to force sameness. Resonance is magenta against purple; memory is the yellow V; wonder is how much blue field can hold. Depict non-human; no Earth ethnicity labels. Soft talk that treats the name as empty flattery misses the realm rule: rather great means quietly enough.
