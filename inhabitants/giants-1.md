# Giants

## Name

Giants

## Overview

**Giants** are Bloomline Estuary non-human monumental neighbors: vast seated forms that offer a thigh or ledge rest to tiny walkers without needing a carved face to prove kindness.

## Description

Witnesses describe brick-red or earth-warm bulk, often headless in plates, with a horizontal resting plane at the thigh and a smaller golden or pale sitter looking up. Temper is slow and sheltering. Locals do not file Earth titan myths as creed. They file scale-neighbors useful for teaching children not to demand portraits from every kindness, and for marking estuary rests that should not be climbed as conquest. Bad craft is chiseling a face onto a giant that preferred anonymity. Approach from the side; thank specifically; leave without graffiti.

## Notes

Non-human monumental sitters. Rest; do not claim the face.
