# Ribbon Gulls

## Overview

Ribbon Gulls are pale gulls of Hallow Bay that often carry stray threads and ribbon scraps in their beaks, nesting near the Rope Tree's reach.

## Description

White bodies, soft gray wings, eyes like wet slate. They wheel over moss-green dories and guest halls, stealing nothing vital but everything shiny and stringlike—including dropped ribbon fluff that makes nests look ceremonially messy. Their calls are short and clean, less harsh than deep-sea gulls: almost polite. Close up they smell like salt and sun-warm rope. A gull landing on the Rope Tree during a tying is either comedy or audit, depending on your nerves.

## Beliefs About Them

Arrivals joke that Ribbon Gulls audit promises. A gull that drops a blue thread at your feet is read as a reminder to tie your ribbon honestly.

## Narrative Role

Ties Calareth's ribbon ethic to a visible, charming animal habit.
