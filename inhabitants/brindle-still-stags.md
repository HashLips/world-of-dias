# Brindle Still-Stags

## Overview

Brindle Still-Stags are reported pale stags of Brindle March that stand motionless for hours, as if holding the road's famous stillness in their bodies.

## Description

Accounts describe ash-tan coats with darker brindle striping, antlers like forked measuring rods, and eyes the color of cooled tea. Sightings cluster where the March feels quietest. When they finally move, witnesses say the air 'remembers how to walk.'

## Beliefs About Them

Some treat them as living embodiments of Brindle stillness; others insist they are ordinary deer misremembered after long waits on the road.

## Narrative Role

Extends Brindle March's hush into fauna form—useful for uncanny but not necessarily hostile encounters.

## Notes

Taxonomic certainty left open; cultural force is stronger than survey proof.
