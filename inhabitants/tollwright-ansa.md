# Tollwright Ansa

## Overview

**Tollwright Ansa** is remembered as the first March marshal to accept **documented rest** as payment—builder of Span Toll’s “never a shrug” ethic.

## Description

Chalk on fingers; shade-stall patience. Folktales say Ansa waited through a stillness event mid-tally and finished the chalk when the road breathed again.

## Beliefs About Them

Toll clerks toast Ansa when labor-pay clears. Convoy stewards toast Ansa when bruise-timing beats clock pride. Counterfeiters curse the name.

## Narrative Role

Builder—logistics as fairness.

## Notes

Distinct from living Bren Holloway’s office; Ansa is the scar under the paperwork.
