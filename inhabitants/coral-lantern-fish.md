# Coral Lantern-Fish

## Overview

Coral Lantern-Fish are small reef fish of Coral Strand that glow soft gold-green at night in shallow water, visible from shore paths.

## Description

Palm-sized, translucent fins, light organs along the lateral line. Schools form temporary 'lantern roads' in calm bays. Harmless; easily netted but culturally protected near Hallow Bay.

## Beliefs About Them

Calareth visitors are told not to scoop Lantern-Fish for jars. Watching is the gift; catching is a small betrayal of bay manners.

## Narrative Role

Visual magic for night arrivals at Calareth's coast.
