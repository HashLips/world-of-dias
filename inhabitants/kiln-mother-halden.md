# Kiln-Mother Halden

## Overview

**Kiln-Mother Halden** is remembered as the first teaching fire that treated remaking tools and selves as one craft—builder of second-beginning culture before named Compacts hardened.

## Description

Ash on apron; slate stubs. Halden’s rule: bread before forced slate talk. Living Hanna Kiln is toasted as Halden’s working heir, not Halden reborn.

## Beliefs About Them

Laughter Week toasts. Refusal stories still cite Halden’s hard edge: remaking offered, not cosplayed.

## Narrative Role

Builder/bridge—exile into neighbor.

## Notes

Distinct from living Hanna Kiln and Alwen Rusk.
