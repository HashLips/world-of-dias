# Driftfall Rope-Crabs

## Overview

Driftfall Rope-Crabs are thick-clawed crabs that climb wet ropes, pier legs, and salvage lines, cutting soft cord when ignored.

## Description

Shells mottled salt-green and bruise purple, claws strong enough to fray hemp. They prefer night damp. Painful pinch; not deadly.

## Beliefs About Them

Deckhands swear Rope-Crabs can smell cheap knots. Veterans whip-finish lines and keep a stick handy.

## Narrative Role

Practical nuisance fauna for Driftfall's maritime salvage mood.
