# Dune-Glass Lizards

## Overview

Dune-Glass Lizards are translucent-scaled lizards of Lumira Sands that vanish against bright dune faces until they blink.

## Description

Body length of a forearm, scales catching light like thin bottle glass, soft belly that stays sand-colored. They eat small insects near oasis plant rings. Harmless; startlingly easy to step near.

## Beliefs About Them

Dunewalkers teach guests to watch for 'glass that breathes.' Children try to count blinks for patience practice.

## Narrative Role

Visual wonder of the desert that is real, daytime, and nonthreatening.
