# Opacity Serpents

## Overview

Opacity Serpents hunt in the after-moment when a reveal window closes, striking while eyes and minds are still adjusting to returned opacity.

## Description

Matte scales that drink glare, bodies that look flatter than they are. Venom disorients depth perception. Most dangerous to lone investigators lingering past interval end.

## Beliefs About Them

Training says: when the window ends, step back before you think.

## Narrative Role

F610's predator logic—danger in the comedown from truth.
