# Frederick Lumens

## Name

Frederick Lumens

## Overview

Frederick Lumens is the central figure of **The Lumen Saga**—Driftfall orphan, Roadglow bearer, Giftbroth drinker, Apple Touch orchard hand—who gifts Fellgrove to Bram Orlen, spends years seeking a frequency crossing, returns to **Vorrith Kael** with a final ask, and vanishes from F432 tellings. Whether he crossed remains open.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Frederick does not know his parents. He grew up in Sorel’s salvage grammar and deck labor, received Roadglow from Calen Brink, reclaimed it from Dren Holk, fled to Wabet, accepted Vorrith’s broth bargain, and woke to Apple Touch. He inherited Fellgrove from Jorin Fell, later gifted it to Bram under a harvest covenant, and funded research until rumored crossings priced him out. His last known act is entering Stillhollow to ask how to travel frequencies.

**Status in Present Age tellings:** missing after that visit. Apple Touch no longer appears on Fellgrove’s harvest schedule.

## Beliefs About Them

Some say he crossed. Some say the hollow kept him. Some say he simply left by ordinary roads and chose silence. The letter is the remnant people can still hold.

## Narrative Role

Second long Dias journey: orphan hunger toward other bands, proved costly, planted in land, reopened by a self-letter, closed on an intentional mystery.
