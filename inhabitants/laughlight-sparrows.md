# Laughlight Sparrows

## Overview

Laughlight Sparrows are small sparrows said to appear more often in plazas and tavern yards right after genuine shared laughter among strangers.

## Description

Ordinary sparrow size, with a brief warm glint at the wing edge that witnesses compare to afterglow. Hard to photograph; easy to dismiss. Reports cluster in Nauw towns and rare Wabet markets.

## Beliefs About Them

Tied in folk talk to the disputed 'light after shared laughter' pattern. Scientists want controls; storytellers want another round.

## Narrative Role

A soft, happy rumor-creature that braids fauna to an existing F432 social rule.
