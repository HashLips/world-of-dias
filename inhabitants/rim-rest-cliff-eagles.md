# Rim Rest Cliff-Eagles

## Overview

Rim Rest Cliff-Eagles are large eagles that ride rim thermals and occasionally snatch unattended small pets—or very small packs.

## Description

Wingspans wider than a door, eyes like polished amber. Day hunters. Respect distance.

## Beliefs About Them

Rim Rest posts 'no loose kits' signs that are really eagle treaties.

## Narrative Role

Large sky predator for rim heights.
