# Lira Vesk

## Name

Lira Vesk

## Overview

Lira Vesk is a small-boat runner who works the western Sorel waterline—creeks, reed channels, and night coast—carrying people who cannot or will not pay the **Westfold Toll** into southeastern **Wabet**. She is how Frederick reaches calm country without the proper traveling system.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Weather-darkened, short-spoken, careful with names. Lira’s craft is a low, patched boat that looks like salvage until it moves like intention. She does not advertise in inns. She finds short-coin travelers the way gulls find scraps: by watching who counts marks twice at the toll road and turns away angry.

She asks what you are fleeing and what you are carrying. She does not ask for a full life story. Payment can be coin, labor at the oars, or a promise she decides is worth the risk. She will not take a loud True Blue held in open light—but she will take a boy who keeps cloth tight and rowing steady.

## Beliefs About Them

Toll clerks call her a leak in the system. Hearthvale shore folk call her useful weather. Wabet orchard runners who meet her at quiet landings pretend they arrived by longer roads.

## Narrative Role

Opens Frederick’s unofficial entry to Wabet and proves Dias still has side doors when main bridges price out the hungry.

## Notes

Not a pirate fleet. One boat, one judgment call at a time. Later fate and further fares left open for saga continuation.
