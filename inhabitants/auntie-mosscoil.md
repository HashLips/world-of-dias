# Auntie Mosscoil

## Name

Auntie Mosscoil

## Overview

**Auntie Mosscoil** feeds Orrin soup and refuses to call him scientist when he forgets to sleep. Outer-ring necessity, not destiny. She is the law Orrin obeys when graphs fail to boil—and the Harold-principle anchor that keeps wonder from erasing meals.

## Description

Moss-green shawl, wrists strong from carrying jars, hair pinned with whatever clip is clean. Voice that ends experiments without raising volume. Smells of broth, peel, and the soap used on shared stair rails. Moves through third-ring alleys as if the city owed her a path and usually receives one.

She leaves soup outside alcoves at midnight. She will look at an Echoform and ask about dishes before ontology. She and Harold share the city’s ordinary courage: continue. She is not anti-science; she is anti-starvation dressed as brilliance.

Concrete saga beat: in Something Helpful in the Glass, Auntie brings soup, sees the glass person, and says, “If it eats, it washes dishes.” The Caliburn does not eat. The line still lands as the saga’s domestic verdict on helpful monsters. Earlier, in Softfruit as Witness, she forces a wrapped bundle into Orrin’s hands and bans “colleague” for Sera until he buys fruit. After the vault fails, she still shows up with jars—haunted stewardship includes dinner.

Physical read: soft middle age, practical shoes, jar-callused palms, eyes that inventory sleep before genius. Shawl pinned with a peel-twine spare borrowed from Sera and never returned on principle.

She does not attend publish ceremonies. She attends the night after, when soup matters more than shelves. That is her narrative scheduling genius.

## Beliefs About Them

Neighbors treat her as infrastructure. Orrin treats her as law. Tam fears her silence more than Noro’s verdicts. Visitors never learn her name and still get steered toward warm food by her stare.

## Narrative Role

Harold-principle anchor across Movements One through Three. Keeps the Calibration Saga loveable and bodily when instruments and institutions grow loud.

## Notes

Not a Resonant House figure. Ordinary courage, not destiny plot. See Softfruit as Witness, Something Helpful in the Glass, Vault and Break.
