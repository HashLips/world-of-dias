# Nessa Tolltalk

## Overview

**Nessa Tolltalk** is a Westfold Toll clerk who teaches honest rates loudly—so worse predators don’t own the “almost enough” margin.

## Description

Ink wrists, patience thinner than the chain, a lecture prepared for every short-coin story. Nessa has waved through labor-rest when papers were real, and refused cousins of convenience. Knows Lira Vesk’s quiet paths exist; will not advertise them; will not pretend the toll is kindness.

## Beliefs About Them

Travelers call Nessa cruel until they meet a false guide. Keepers on the Wabet side respect Nessa’s raid-energy nose. Children learning rates from Nessa become harder to cheat.

## Narrative Role

Antagonist-with-reasons + bridge—fee barrier as protection theater that sometimes is protection.
