# Green-Garden Border Ants

## Overview

Green-Garden Border Ants march the border lanes in disciplined black lines, farming sweet drips from garden walls and dropped fruit.

## Description

Ordinary ant size, glossy. Trails are so regular that children play 'don't break the census.' Bite only if crushed underfoot in numbers.

## Beliefs About Them

Gardeners leave a crumb tax at corners. Breaking a trail before noon is considered rude.

## Narrative Role

Small ordered life for garden-lane domesticity.
