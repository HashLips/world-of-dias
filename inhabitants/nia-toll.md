# Nia Toll

## Name

Nia Toll

## Overview

Nia Toll is a wagon steward on the Eastbound Fruit Road. She keeps bruise-time and waystation manners in her pocket even when she is drinking, and she does not believe a plane has retired the ox.

## Description

Weather-brown, cord at her wrist for tally knots, chalk under a nail no matter how often she washes. She walks like a person who has argued with a marshal and been partly right. Her voice can cut a convoy into order without becoming a shout. She hates Redmile Quiet in a cup. She likes a documented rest more than a hero.

## Beliefs About Them

Drivers say she is strict and will share her water. Plane people say she is old-fashioned. The road is not old. It is still how most fruit moves. Nia is current.

## Narrative Role

The saga's reminder, in a body, that skipping stops is raid and that flight is a hire beside the road, not instead of it. When the orchard is chalked, she is one of the adults who will have to deal with the feet.

## Notes

Not a convert to wings. Not a villain of progress. A steward.
