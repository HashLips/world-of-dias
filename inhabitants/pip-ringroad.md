# Pip Ringroad

## Overview

**Pip Ringroad** is a Veloria outer-ring child who names Lap-Cats after Banner Moths and treats fair-week soft-brush care as serious civic work.

## Description

Scuffed knees, citrus-stained fingers, a soft brush hung on a string. Pip knows which pots are “just pretty” and which pots mean sanctuary—without yet knowing debt grammar. Harold sometimes redirects Pip away from Operator rumors toward actual errands.

## Beliefs About Them

Vendors call Pip good luck. Clerks call Pip a future Softfruit problem in the fond way. Houses do not know Pip exists—correctly.

## Narrative Role

Child + joyful locale entry—Dias through moth dust and cat names, not destiny.
