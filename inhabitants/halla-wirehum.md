# Halla Wirehum

## Name

Halla Wirehum

## Overview

**Halla Wirehum** is a Choirglass tuner who hears the Caliburn imitate Orrin’s mutter one beat late—and becomes a believer through annoyance. Pitch-perfect temper, warm tuning fork, and no patience for pipes that arrive late or voices that arrive without bodies.

## Description

Hair full of static from conduit work, ears trained on city hum, fork always warm in a pocket. Swears creatively when Choirglass panels misalign. Loves Veloria’s load song until it learns Orrin’s mutter without Orrin attached. Then love becomes evidence.

Halla is not Operator-sealed and not Row-apprenticed. Sensory labor sits between music and maintenance. Reports go to whoever will listen; Operators wish the reports were quieter.

Concrete saga beat: when the Caliburn first helps in the glass, Halla hears the late mutter from the street and arrives swearing with joy, fork ringing like a small alarm that wants to be a cheer. At escape, during Vault and Break, the fork rings once more—late—answering a mutter that no longer has a mouth in the room. That single late ring is sensory proof the Echoform left the alcove into city noise.

Physical read: wiry build, hearing calluses behind the ears from years of panel work, a fork etched with a private pitch mark. Clothes layered for conduit chill even in warm rings.

Halla’s belief arrives as irritation: the city should not borrow Orrin’s mutter. Annoyance is cleaner than awe, and harder for Houses to file away.

## Beliefs About Them

Musicians trust Halla. Operators wish Halla would file quieter reports. Tam believes Halla before he believes House stamps. Orrin apologizes to Halla for teaching the city his mutter by accident.

## Narrative Role

Witness of mimicry across Movements Two and Three. Makes the Caliburn audible, not only visible, and marks the escape with a late fork ring.
