# Blue-Litter Speck-Mites

## Overview

Blue-Litter Speck-Mites are microscopic mites that live in blue litter dust, giving underwood air its faint chalk-sweet catch in the throat.

## Description

Individually invisible; collectively a soft haze on leaf litter. Not predatory to humans. Heavy exposure causes cough and blue-gray sneeze dust.

## Beliefs About Them

Some link them to Bluefox diet; woodsfolk just wet scarves.

## Narrative Role

Explains blue litter as ecology, not only omen.
