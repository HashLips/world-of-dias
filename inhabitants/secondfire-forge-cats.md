# Secondfire Forge-Cats

## Overview

Secondfire Forge-Cats are heat-tolerant cats that sleep on warm brick and hunt ash-moths in rebuild sheds.

## Description

Larger than Ember-Cats, soot-smudged, half-missing whiskers common. Medium domestic predators. Earn keep.

## Beliefs About Them

A forge without a cat has mice with opinions.

## Narrative Role

Medium Sorel workshop warmth.
