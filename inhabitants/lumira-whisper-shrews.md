# Lumira Whisper-Shrews

## Overview

Lumira Whisper-Shrews are tiny shrews that move under dune crust with almost no sound, hunting sand-fleas near oasis rings.

## Description

Finger-long, pale fur, frantic energy contained in soft motion. Harmless. Rarely seen; often only tracks.

## Beliefs About Them

Silence Law stories use them as proof that life can be loud inside and quiet outside.

## Narrative Role

Tiny desert mammal that fits Lumira ethics.
