# Cinder-Nook Lantern Beetles

## Overview

Cinder-Nook Lantern Beetles glow a soft coal-orange at night, marking safe footpaths between shelters in muted F840 dark.

## Description

Thumbnail beetles with steady, non-blinking glow. Clusters form along marked relay stones. Crushing them is easy and socially scolded.

## Beliefs About Them

Children follow beetle lines home. Adults pretend they do not.

## Narrative Role

Tiny hopeful light against ember-quiet nights.
