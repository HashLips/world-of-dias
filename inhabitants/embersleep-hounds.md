# Embersleep Hounds

## Overview

Embersleep Hounds are low, warm-coated hounds of the Ashen Hearthline that sleep near preserved hearths and wake to gentle work rather than war.

## Description

Ash-brown fur with ember belly tones, heavy lids, steady gait. They guide lost walkers toward relay warmth more than they hunt. Growls are warnings first.

## Beliefs About Them

Ember Keepers treat them as continuity made friendly. A hound choosing your fire is an honor and a responsibility.

## Narrative Role

Happy-hopeful fauna for F840's recovery identity.
