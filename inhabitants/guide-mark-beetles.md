# Guide-Mark Beetles

## Overview

Guide-Mark Beetles crawl along incomplete structures laying tiny dotted trails that resemble artisan guide marks.

## Description

Flat, graphite-sheen beetles. Trails are mineral and harmless. Builders sometimes follow them to find weak joins.

## Beliefs About Them

Joked to be employed by the realm itself. Possibly just clever bugs in a suggestive world.

## Narrative Role

Whimsical craft ecology.
