# The Three Opinions

## Overview

**The Three Opinions** are a distinctive Sorel-known trio who move together, discuss everything, and refuse to align with any major camp—yet are never short of a verdict.

## Description

F432 race record: commonly classified as **Lineborn** (planar bodies, high-contrast outlines, sketch-like interior texture), though some northern accounts insist they are a single recurring social phenomenon wearing three familiar shapes.

They are almost never seen apart. Witnesses describe a constant rhythm: one raises a doubt, one repeats it as fact, one adds a detail no one else said aloud.

### The three

| Member | Usual role | How towns describe them |
|---|---|---|
| **Brask Kel** | Opens with contradiction | "The one who heard it wrong on purpose." |
| **Miren Holt** | Restates and amplifies | "The one who only reports—until everyone believes it." |
| **Tav Renn** | Adds the smile and the extra rumor | "The one who was not there, but remembers best." |

They carry no declared quarrel with **Hearthvale** or **Driftfall**, nor with exile leadership figures such as **Carrow Vale** or **Vexra Coil**—but they also endorse no one. Their loyalty is to the conversation itself.

### Behavior

- Travel as a set through Sorel towns, camps, and crossing markets.
- Hold loud, overlapping discussions in public spaces until listeners gather.
- Spread mixed stories: half-true reports, exaggerated warnings, invented connections, and confident opinions on events they did not witness.
- Leave without direct violence, yet alter mood: neighbors argue, old grudges reopen, and new suspicions appear overnight.

They are not treated as criminals in most places. They are treated as **weather**—unwelcome weather that may still arrive tomorrow.

## Beliefs About Them

Some say they are independent persons. Others claim they are a social curse formed wherever exile shame and frontier boredom meet.

A common Sorel saying: **"Lock your door if you must—but close your ears if they sit down nearby."**

## Narrative Role

The trio functions as a mobile unrest engine: not chaos by force, but instability by speech. They are useful in stories when a settlement needs to fracture **before** any battle, vote, or exile decree.

## Notes

Collective epithets include **the Unsided**, **Kel-Holt-Renn** (spoken as one annoyance), and **Opinion Row** in Driftfall dock slang.
