# Joss Merrow

## Name

Joss Merrow

## Overview

Joss Merrow is an outer-ring teenager who touches the Heartlean Colorstone once in Farstock and decides the amber it takes from him is a life he is allowed to carry out under his coat.

## Description

Sixteen, tall for the stalls, with basin dust permanently in his turn of speed. He works odd mornings for a fruit stall on the market loop and spends the money on small proofs that the day can be kinder than it was. He laughs first and thinks in the laugh. When the stone went the color of gilding hour in his palm, he felt the hard part of the week step back, and he mistook the step for ownership.

He is not cruel. He is unfinished. He suggests the theft because waiting feels like the adult world, and the adult world has already decided Farstock is a story.

## Beliefs About Them

Harl thinks Joss will talk them into weather. Rill thinks Joss hears wonder and then tries to pocket it so it cannot leave. Loop vendors know him as the boy who is good with a crate and bad at closing a hope. After the saga, the loop knows him as one of the three who went south and came back quieter.

## Narrative Role

He is the want that moves the theft. On the road he is the one who believes longest that the echo is a guide. He is also the one who tries, too late, to drop the stone, and learns his hands have opinions the stone encouraged.

## Notes

He does not gain a method, a ferry, or a frequency rank. What he brings home is a click-bird and the knowledge that a color that worked on him was never his.
