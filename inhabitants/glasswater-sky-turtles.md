# Glasswater Sky-Turtles

## Overview

Glasswater Sky-Turtles are rumored colossal turtle-forms that drift above Glasswater Fields on thermal days, shells mistaking for slow clouds.

## Description

Reported shell-widths wider than barges. Legs paddle air as if water. No confirmed landing. Shadows feel cool.

## Beliefs About Them

Some swear Mega Butterfly season and Sky-Turtle days rhyme. Surveyors want triangulation; poets want another look.

## Narrative Role

Colossal soft wonder—optional reality.
