# Window Shelf Timers

## Name

Window Shelf Timers

## Overview

**Window Shelf Timers** keep the chimes, the overlays, and the soft-close discipline on the Glassfold shelves. They are guild hands of brief clarity. A reveal window is a tool they refuse to let anyone pocket, including themselves. Their kindness is ending the miracle on time.

## Description

Cool gray wraps, slate notebooks with dual overlays already ruled in graphite and steel-blue, ears tuned to chime pairs. They overlap the Veil Scribes without replacing that identity; a Timer may also be a Scribe, and a Scribe is not automatically willing to be the person who touches your elbow when you will not blink. You recognize a Timer by the way they face the chime instead of the view. The view is the temptation. The chime is the job.

When the first note opens a window they do not gasp. They count, they mark, they let other people gasp, and they start packing the minute before the second note so the packing does not become a scramble that wastes the close. Their look at a lingerer is not hatred. It is the look of someone who has seen a useful map rot in a greedy hand.

## Beliefs About Them

Apprentices fear that look and later miss it when they are responsible for a shelf alone. Visitors thank them, sometimes tearfully, for ending a clarity they would have ruined. Stamp-minded clerks call them obstructive until the smeared copy in their satchel proves the Timer right. The guild’s own gossip says a Timer who falls in love with a particular window should be walked down and given tea, not a longer shift.

## Narrative Role

F610 craft faces for shelf scenes. They let a story have precision without turning the ledge into a puzzle boss. A minute that would not stay is their teaching myth, and they tell it on themselves first.

## Notes

Precision workers. Not prophets, not jailers of knowledge. Ask-again is welcome. Stare-again is the thing they are paid, in guild terms, to interrupt.
