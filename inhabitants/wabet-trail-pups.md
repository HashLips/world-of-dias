# Wabet Trail-Pups

## Overview

Wabet Trail-Pups are half-wild, half-adopted young dogs that attach to walkers and Open Hand wayfarers—companions of quiet roads rather than city rings.

## Description

Mud-pawed, soft-eared, learning silence by Softfall habit. They carry nothing, guard little, and insist on being present. Keepers tolerate them; loud hunters find them inconvenient witnesses.

## Beliefs About Them

A Trail-Pup that leaves you overnight is a weather warning. One that stays through Listening Heat is family.

## Narrative Role

Beloved Wabet animal for child and walker scenes.
