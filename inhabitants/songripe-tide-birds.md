# Songripe Tide-Birds

## Overview

Songripe Tide-Birds are bright shorebirds of the Songripe Isles that answer human song with patterned calls, often kept as companions rather than caged trophies.

## Description

Slender legs, teal-and-cream plumage, beaks like thin pens. They nest in rope-bridge eaves and beach grass. Morning noise is cheerful and relentless.

## Beliefs About Them

Isle humor in *Meet My Bird* tracks real custom: a Tide-Bird that learns your refrain is family-adjacent. Taking one to Nauw markets is frowned on.

## Narrative Role

Happy face of Songripe's song-over-harvest culture.
