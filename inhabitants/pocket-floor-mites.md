# Pocket Floor Mites

## Overview

Pocket Floor Mites are rumored micro-things that appear only in F500's falsely stable floor intervals, consuming the sense of ground as the pocket fails.

## Description

Never clearly seen; inferred from itching soles, disappearing dust, and the moment a floor becomes concept again. May not be organisms.
