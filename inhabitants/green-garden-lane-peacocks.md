# Green Garden Lane Peacocks

## Overview

Green Garden Lane Peacocks are large ornamental birds walked and half-wild along border gardens, trains like moving stained glass.

## Description

Iridescent, loud, vain. Not native wild so much as civic tradition gone feral at the edges. Spur injuries happen.

## Beliefs About Them

A full display before a proposal is cliché and still works.

## Narrative Role

Large beautiful showy animal for garden lanes.
