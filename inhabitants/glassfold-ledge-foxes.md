# Glassfold Ledge Foxes

## Overview

Glassfold Ledge Foxes pace the Ledge's mirrored paths and freeze when a reveal window opens, as if bracing for truth weather.

## Description

Sleek, ice-tan coats, eyes too exact. They hunt small pane-lizards and avoid sudden mirrors. Rarely threaten people who keep distance.

## Beliefs About Them

Investigators joke foxes are better at patience than apprentices.

## Narrative Role

Living mascot ecology for the Ledge.
