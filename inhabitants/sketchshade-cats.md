# Sketchshade Cats

## Overview

Sketchshade Cats are rumored gallery cats whose outlines look incomplete in certain light, as if drawn and not fully inked.

**Life kind:** **ambiguous / rumor**—may be ordinary strays plus tired eyes, or gallery-kept uncanny. Do not force a collar or a monster stamp.

## Description

Witnesses describe charcoal-gray cats with soft edges that blur when they turn a corner. They favor shadow under hanging canvases and the cool floor near ink tables. Some artists swear a Sketchshade Cat will sit only beside unfinished work.

## Beliefs About Them

Gallery staff debate whether the cats are ordinary strays plus tired eyes, or something the building itself keeps.

## Narrative Role

A light uncanny companion for art-world scenes in Veloria without needing high stakes.
