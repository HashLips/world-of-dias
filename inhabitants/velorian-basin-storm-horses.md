# Velorian Basin Storm-Horses

## Overview

Velorian Basin Storm-Horses are large restless horses that pace fence lines before Basin storms and make excellent fast messengers after.

## Description

Tall, dark manes, weather-smart. Not all Basin horses; this strain is known for storm nerves and speed.

## Beliefs About Them

Saddling one before thunder is either brave logistics or a bad poem.

## Narrative Role

Large kinetic animal for weather-beaten travel beats.
