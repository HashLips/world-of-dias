# Isle Calareth Rope-Elk

## Overview

Isle Calareth Rope-Elk are large elk said to pause near the Rope Tree as if understanding promise-space, though they are simply wary of crowds.

## Description

Sea-salted coats, antlers often draped with shed ribbon scraps they cannot remove alone. Watch sometimes cuts ribbons free as kindness.

## Beliefs About Them

Ribbon lore claims the elk 'inspect' promises. Biologists claim tourists leave trash.

## Narrative Role

Large gentle-mythic island animal.
