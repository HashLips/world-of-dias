# Moth Kear

## Name

Moth Kear

## Overview

Moth Kear is a rural Wabet child who had never seen a machine before The Late Bruise used the lent field. The child asks clean questions and is not a symbol of innocence so much as a person the noise has not trained.

## Description

About eight, bare ankles in softfall season, a shirt that used to belong to a larger cousin. Eyes that track birds more readily than metal. The first time the plane came over the hedge, Moth dropped a stick and did not pick it up until the wheels had stopped screaming. Later, offered the Stillwood Skiff as another wonder, Moth asked what held it and accepted "nobody knows" faster than any adult in Veloria would have.

## Beliefs About Them

Yarrow says Moth will be ruined by watching the sky. Sella says Moth will be ruined by adults who answer questions with shows. Moth has not chosen a ruin yet.

## Narrative Role

Shows the medieval outer life of Wabet meeting a third-ring machine without pretending the meeting is cute. Also the ear that hears Willa refuse to call her skiff a ferry.

## Notes

A child in a market-and-road story. No harm plot. No romance. Questions only.
