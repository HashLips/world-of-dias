# Vorrith Kael

## Name

Vorrith Kael

## Overview

Vorrith Kael is a dark-presence exile who fled another frequency condition and now lives almost unseen inside **Stillhollow**, a cave in **Ashcalm Rise** on the quiet edge of Wabet. Locals who know the rumor call him a cook of matter and frequency. Seekers come for a taste of his life-source brew. Most of Wabet never learns he exists.

## Description

F432 race record: **unknown / other-realm**.

Vorrith does not match ordinary human proportion for long. Witnesses disagree on skin tone—pale cyan, violet, or a darkness that refuses to take light—but they agree on the **shadow problem**: his silhouette often grows larger, longer-necked, and claw-handed than his standing body, as if the cave keeps a second self against the stone. Some travelers swear the shadow arrives before he does.

He wears long, plain dark cloth and rarely raises his voice. When he speaks, it is brief, practical, and oddly courteous—never recruiting, never preaching. He mixes. He tastes. He rests. He does not tour Wabet, trade in Nauw markets, or challenge Keepers.

### Arrival

Canon holds that Vorrith reached **Nauw** first through an unstable cross-band interval—folk speech blames a short **lumen-abyss bleed**, a nested imprint, or a gate that closed behind him—and then walked west into Wabet seeking a place that would not argue with him. The west’s calm is not his morality; it is his insulation. He chose a mountain that almost nobody climbs and a hollow that almost nobody maps.

His home-band is disputed. The darkest rumors point toward **F500** null-adjacent pressure. Vorrith himself does not confirm origin labels. He only confirms that remaining where he came from meant ending.

### Craft

Vorrith’s craft is the compounding of materials, atoms, and frequencies into a thick living broth kept in **the Still Pot**. The brew is his **life-source**: without it, the exile’s body thins toward the shadow that wants him. With it, he persists in F432’s calm without flooding Wabet with foreign noise.

He does not advertise the pot. Seekers find him anyway.

## Beliefs About Them

- A few canopy locals and quiet orchard runners treat him as a real, dangerous neighbor who **does not disturb** Wabet if left alone.
- Wider Wabet treats stories of him as trail superstition.
- Seekers from Nauw, Sorel, and farther bands call him **the One Who Cooks Quiet**, a living kiln of forbidden mixture.
- Keepers who have heard the fuller rumor prefer non-broadcast: Wabet’s calm is not improved by pilgrimage traffic toward a mountain cave.
- Opinions split on whether tasting his broth is courage, greed, research, or a soft form of self-erasure.

## Narrative Role

Vorrith is a **hidden pressure node** under Wabet’s serenity: a dark exile who proves the west can hold something terrible without becoming terrible itself—so long as the something stays still. In **The Lumen Saga** he meets **Frederick Lumens** twice: first for Roadglow and Giftbroth Change; later when Frederick returns asking how to travel frequencies. What is said on that second visit is not public canon. Frederick is not seen again afterward.
