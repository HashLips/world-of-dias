# Wet-Paint Birds

## Overview

Wet-Paint Birds leave faint color accents on perches that fade as if drying, then choose another hue next hour.

## Description

Small, mineral-toned bodies with sudden cobalt or ember flashes at the wing. Song sounds like tapping and scraping mixed with chirp.

## Beliefs About Them

Open Hand tellers love them as mascots of revision. Catching one 'to keep a color' is considered missing the lesson.

## Narrative Role

Creative joy fauna for the unfinished band.
