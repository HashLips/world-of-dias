# Brief-Truth Stags

## Overview

Brief-Truth Stags are rumored stags that appear fully only during long reveal windows, antlers mapping relation-lines in the air before fading with the interval.

## Description

Seen as luminous bone and glass-flesh. Tracks do not persist. Beauty is the main wound they leave.

## Beliefs About Them

Some Veil Scribes want capture; older hands say capture would miss the point.

## Narrative Role

Magnificent, mystical, time-limited—pure F610 wonder.
