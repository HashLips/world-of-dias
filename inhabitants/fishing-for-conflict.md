# Fishing for Conflict

## Name

Fishing for Conflict

## Overview

**Fishing for Conflict** is a Nauw Outer Rim Seas non-human warning presence: a torso that holds smirk and sorrow heads under hanging hooks, teaching pier talk not to bait a quarrel for sport.

## Description

Witnesses describe dark silhouette arms lifting two faces—one mischievous, one downcast—while a fish-shaped emptiness sits in the chest and hooks hang ready. Sometimes only the hooks are noticed in a room before a bad joke lands. Locals treat it as neighbor-diagram more than beast: if you feel both heads in your hands, put the bait down. Temper is unsettling and useful. Bad craft is hanging extra hooks for entertainment. No Earth sport-fishing romance required.

## Notes

Non-human lure warning. Drop the bait.
