# Lumen of Softfruit

## Overview

**Lumen of Softfruit** (no kin to Frederick Lumens) is the joy-founder remembered for turning a market quarrel into the first lasting shared-fruit hall manners.

## Description

Stories say Lumen set a ladle and a bowl where two races nearly stamped each other into exile weather—and made fruit-share outrank coin for one lunch that became a civic habit.

## Beliefs About Them

Softfruit toasts. Children learn “ladle before stamp.” Some Houses dislike the name as anti-classification sentiment; streets love it.

## Narrative Role

Joy archetype—belonging as delicious literacy.

## Notes

Name coincidence with Lumens sagas is in-world joke fuel, not identity.
