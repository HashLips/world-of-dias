# Openhand Draft-Goats

## Overview

Openhand Draft-Goats are sturdy goats used along Open Hand Way to haul light craft loads, tools, and gift-crates between workshops and waystations.

## Description

Thick coats in clay and cream, calm temperaments, horns often wrapped in soft cloth near crowded lanes. They know rest-post smells and will stop without command when a familiar bell rings.

## Beliefs About Them

Makers say a good Draft-Goat 'carries intention.' Mistreatment is a social stain among Open Hand circles.

## Narrative Role

Grounds the craft-road in bodily labor and companionship.
