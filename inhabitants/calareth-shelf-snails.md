# Calareth Shelf-Snails

## Overview

Calareth Shelf-Snails leave glittering mineral trails on Elder Shelf stone, slow enough that Watch runners use them as patience teachers.

## Description

Thumb to palm sized, shells banded sea-glass colors. Harmless. Trails dry into pale calligraphy.

## Beliefs About Them

Crushing one without need breaks ribbon-spirit manners by association.
