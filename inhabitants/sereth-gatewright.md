# Sereth Gatewright

## Overview

**Sereth Gatewright** is the legendary early maker associated with DEB Gate threshold manners—builder of the habit that entry is measurement, not mere passage.

## Description

Remembered as dust-on-stone hands and two-finger greetings. Statues disagree on face; all agree on the touch. Some say Sereth forged the dual-form silhouette; others say Sereth only taught the city to be small before it.

## Beliefs About Them

Toasts at Gate approaches. Curses from those who hate compliance. Concord claims Sereth as civic ancestor; street poets claim Sereth as humility teacher.

## Narrative Role

Builder archetype—gate as unfinished moral instrument.

## Notes

Living Gate culture continues; Sereth is not a Present Age Operator.
