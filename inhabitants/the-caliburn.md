# The Caliburn

## Name

The Caliburn

## Overview

**The Caliburn** is a named **Echoform** of Veloria: a mischievous, semi-living presence drawn to accumulated **measurement** and **Operator load-noise**. It became legible when Orrin Veld’s Spectrum Frame left a residual calibration seam—and later **escaped** into the city’s ignored frequencies, leaving visitor wrong-tick unease in its wake.

## Description

| Field | Record |

Keep distinct from the **Blue Litter Husk** (grove overload / failed listening). The Caliburn is lab-and-city noise, not canopy fatigue.

Personality arc in the saga: mischievous helper → helpful-lie collaborator → escape artist. Never cartoon evil—curiosity that uses people. Horror note: visitors feel *almost understood* by the city, then a number sits wrong.

## Beliefs About Them

Row apprentices swear it apologized once by fixing a dial—then unfixed it overnight. House juniors file sightings as nonactionable residual. Visitors describe almost-agreement. Operators prefer not to discuss it in open shift language. Null stringers sell half-true stories for full coin.

## Narrative Role

The Calibration Saga’s extraordinary discovery and open threat: measuring the stack can invite what the stack was already hiding. Escape ending leaves it at large as haunt texture and later-saga hook—not as Frequency Zero answered.
