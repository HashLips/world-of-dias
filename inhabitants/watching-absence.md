# Watching Absence

## Overview

The Watching Absence is less a species than a folk-named presence: the sense that F500's nothing still attends.

## Description

No body. Reports are of being observed by emptiness, of corners that lean in. Sometimes linked to Frequency Zero talk; scholars separate the ideas carefully and still shiver.

## Beliefs About Them

Moral cosmology uses it as 'opposite of Aurel’s peace.' Proof remains socially impossible and narratively abundant.

## Narrative Role

Mythic-scale fear entity for F500—use sparingly.
