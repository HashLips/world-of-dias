# Tubehold Wire-Moths

## Overview

Tubehold Wire-Moths are small gray moths that rest on warm wiring and leave dusty prints mistaken for ash.

## Description

Finger-winged, heat-seeking. Harmless. Panic crews when dense enough to look like smoke.

## Beliefs About Them

If moths leave, check the heat. If moths arrive, check the heat anyway.

## Narrative Role

Small industrial insect tell.
