# Exile Road Wolves

## Overview

Exile Road Wolves are gaunt southern wolves that hunt the routes between Nauw's rejection and Sorel's first shelters, favoring the newly arrived and the underprovisioned.

## Description

Dust-colored, rib-showing, eyes too awake. They shadow rather than charge until night or injury appears. Packs know water caches and abandoned cart sites.

## Beliefs About Them

Northern shame stories weaponize them: 'the road itself judges.' Sorel veterans call that Nauw poetry and carry spears anyway.

## Narrative Role

The feared animal face of exile—dangerous, social, and politically flavored.
