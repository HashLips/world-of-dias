# Westfold Path-Badgers

## Overview

Westfold Path-Badgers dig under toll berms and night-paths, sturdy medium mustelids with low tolerance for boots near setts.

## Description

Striped faces, strong claws, surprising courage. Bite infected if ignored. Mostly want to be left alone.

## Beliefs About Them

Toll repair budgets include 'badger negotiations.'

## Narrative Role

Medium stubborn roadside animal.
