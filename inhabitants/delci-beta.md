# Delci Beta

## Name

Delci Beta

## Overview

Delci Beta is an elderly Driftfall man known less for what he says than for what he does every day: climb to a fixed rock on **Mountain of Majestic Good Trade**, sit with eyes closed for hours, and spend those hours traveling by dream through **The Passage Round**—signing destinations into a logbook and walking wherever he wrote.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Locals place him on Driftfall's calmer eastern flank—among the hang-about beaches and inland hills inland of **The Salt Ledger**, away from Sprout Vent's omen talk and the sharper crew yards. He keeps a small routine that neighbors can set a clock by: morning errands, the climb, the long sit, the slow walk down before evening wind turns cold.

On the rock he is still. Hands rest. Eyes stay shut. He does not preach, beg, or perform. Driftfall people treat him as part of the mountain's weather: you can point him out to a visitor, but you do not usually interrupt.

Inside, his path is fixed. Red carpet. Ten steps down. Left turn. Animal-carved wooden door into the Passage Round. He ignores—or honors by leaving sealed—the central **Sealed Beta Chamber** with its golden **B** and five locks. He writes in the **Delci Passage Logbook**, signs, and the grand door opens onto the place he chose. That is how he spends his days.

It is said that if he **holds your hand** while dreaming, you see the same dream ([`rules/delci-handshare-dream-rule.md`](../rules/delci-handshare-dream-rule.md)).

## Beliefs About Them

Shore regulars call him harmless and oddly reliable. Some say the mountain keeps better company because he sits there. Others shrug: Driftfall has room for eccentric rituals, and this one costs no one coin.

At **The Salt Ledger**, talk sometimes hardens into a name for the habit—"the still hours"—and into a half-serious warning: if you climb Good Trade at midday, do not expect conversation. You may get only the sound of wind and a man who is elsewhere. A thinner rumor adds the handshare rule, usually dismissed until someone like Calen Brink comes back with too much detail.

## Narrative Role

Delci Beta is a side-path figure in Dias: a living invitation into inward travel for anyone curious enough to find him—most notably **Calen Brink**, who hears the rumor, sits the rock, and later walks the Passage under handshare.
