# Unripe Orchard Slugs

## Overview

Unripe Orchard Slugs are large pale slugs that thrive on Songripe fruit that will not ripen, leaving shimmering trails across forever-green orchards.

## Description

Hand-long, translucent mint bodies, slow but numerous after wet nights. They do not poison; they frustrate. Trails dry into chalky ribbons that squeak under boots.

## Beliefs About Them

Islanders joke the slugs are 'paid in almost-bananas.' Mainland traders find them disgusting; locals find traders' disgust funnier.

## Narrative Role

Comic ecological consequence of Songripe's resonance pocket.
