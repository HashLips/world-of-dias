# Sorel Horizon-Walkers

## Overview

Sorel Horizon-Walkers are disputed colossal bipeds or dune-legged giants seen as moving horizons at the southern edge of maps.

## Description

Silhouettes taller than watchtowers, gait measured in minutes per step. No reliable bones. Some nights the horizon simply 'walks.'

## Beliefs About Them

Exile folklore: the Walkers are freedom too large to govern. Nauw pamphlets: mass hysteria. Both print well.

## Narrative Role

Colossal frontier myth energy.
