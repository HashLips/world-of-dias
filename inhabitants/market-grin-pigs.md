# Market Grin-Pigs

## Overview

Market Grin-Pigs are small spotted pigs kept near Veloria food rows; their permanent mouth-curve looks like a smile even when they are merely rooting.

## Description

Knee-high, round, black-and-cream spotted, with bright eyes and an upturned snout that reads as a grin even in sleep. Their grunt is a contented metronome under stall talk; when a peel hits stone they trot like small mayors. Rope harnesses leave faint freckles of dye on shoulders. Cleaner than their reputation when kept by careful stalls—still somehow always slightly sticky with fruit sugar. Capon bones and banana soft-ends disappear as if the market invented them for this purpose.

## Beliefs About Them

Buyers joke that Grin-Pigs approve fair prices. A pig that refuses a peel is said to have smelled a bad deal—or a bad banana.

## Narrative Role

Happy, visual, and useful for market color without needing magic.
