# Fish Dinner

## Name

Fish Dinner

## Overview

**Fish Dinner** are Nauw Outer Rim Seas non-human finned neighbors: bright yellow and orange table-sitters with tail fins and open snouts who teach that a shared center course beats competing plates.

## Description

Pier witnesses describe elongated snouted heads, thin arms, and large flat fins resting on floor instead of feet. They prefer light-blue low tables and a single white center roll crossed by thin black sticks—whatever the roll is, it stays central. Open mouths mean talk and hunger both; interrupting the other’s reach is bad manners. Locals do not file Earth shark cartoons. They file dinner-neighbors useful for guest drills and for explaining why some rim meals keep chairs despite fins. Temper is energetic and rule-aware. Bad craft is plating two rolls to avoid sharing.

## Notes

Non-human finned diners. Share the center.
