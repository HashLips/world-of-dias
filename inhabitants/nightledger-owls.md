# Nightledger Owls

## Overview

Nightledger Owls are mid-sized gray owls that hunt above Nauw's counting houses, ledgers sheds, and late-lit office lanes.

## Description

Faces pale as paper, eyes ink-dark, wingbeats nearly silent. They perch on cornices where clerks work late and drop pellets that sometimes contain string, wax seals, or tiny bone beads. Harmless to people; terrifying to street rats.

## Beliefs About Them

Bookkeepers say an owl on the sill means the night's sums will 'come clean.' Superstition aside, many leave a high shelf clear for them.

## Narrative Role

A quieter nocturnal counterpart to daytime market fauna—good for mystery-adjacent city nights.
