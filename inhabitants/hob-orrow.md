# Hob Orrow

## Name

Hob Orrow

## Overview

Hob Orrow is Sella's nephew and the extra pair of hands at Orrow House. He brings the lamp. He sees a cuff held in his aunt's faded outline of a hand and understands enough to stay.

## Description

Sixteen, all wrist and boot, a face that has not decided whether to be wary or kind and currently does both. He sleeps in the loft. He knows the ordinary trees and has been told to leave the glass one alone. He obeys that better than he obeys bedtime. He is strong enough to hold a pot and not strong enough to make Sella explain.

## Beliefs About Them

The lane thinks he is her heir. Sella thinks he is a boy who should still be allowed to be bad at hoeing. He thinks the plane is a bird that should have stayed a rumor.

## Narrative Role

Witness in the kitchen so the catch is not a private dream. He does not fight a duel. He brings light, which is what catches are made of in a farmhouse.

## Notes

Survives the night. Does not become invisible.
