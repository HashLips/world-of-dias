# Claimscar Nail-Midges

## Overview

Claimscar Nail-Midges breed in wet scrap piles and metal filings, swarming anyone who stands still over a claim dispute too long.

## Description

Gray specks with a metallic taste if you breathe them. Bites are pinpricks. Prefer sweat and arguments.

## Beliefs About Them

Yard saying: midges settle the claim first.

## Narrative Role

Sorel micro-annoyance with social humor.
