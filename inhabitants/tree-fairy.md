# Tree Fairy

## Name

Tree Fairy

## Overview

**Tree fairies** are small winged inhabitants of the Aelwyn Canopy, seen in groups of two to five on the bare, lightning-stripped trees that stand above the living canopy. They gather on nights when the great blue disc hangs low and bright.

## Description

Seen against the disc, they are dark silhouettes about the size of a large bird but built like long-limbed people, with wings that splay into many sharp points like dry leaves. They sit, hang, and sprawl on branches rather than flying much, often hanging upside down in forks with complete comfort. Some carry thin curved things—stalks, tails, or small bows—no witness agrees which. They do not sing, but canopy walkers report a faint dry rustle, like leaves in no wind, while they are perched. They leave at the first hint of dawn and are never found on the ground.

## Lore Connection

Canopy folk read a tree fairy perch as a sign that the grove beneath keeps its echoes well and has not been rushed through. Walkers on the Path of the Season sometimes adjust their route to pass beneath a perch quietly. The rule is quiet hands: do not shake a perch tree, do not shout beneath it, do not cut wood from it. Children are told that a tree fairy who sees you take a branch will hang upside down above your sleeping place until you return it.

## Notes

Non-human. Culture- and religion-agnostic. Diet and speech unknown.
