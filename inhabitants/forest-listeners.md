# Forest Listeners

## Overview

Forest Listeners are rumored owl-sized beings in That Forest whose ear-tufts turn toward secrets, jokes, and unfinished sentences alike.

**Life kind:** **ambiguous / rumor creature**—trail ethics are real; taxonomy stays thin on purpose (`status: rumor`).

## Description

Described as feathered, almost owl, but with faces too attentive and legs that leave no clear print. They sit where paths fork. Some travelers feel watched after speaking ill of the woods.

## Beliefs About Them

Trail ethics in Wabet include: do not confess cruelty under Listener canopy. Whether literal or cultural, the rule shapes behavior.

## Narrative Role

Folklore pressure that makes That Forest feel aware—mystical without requiring confirmed taxonomy.
