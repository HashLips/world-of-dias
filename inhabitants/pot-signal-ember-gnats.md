# Pot-Signal Ember-Gnats

## Overview

Pot-Signal Ember-Gnats are heat-loving gnats that spiral above warm signal pots and stove mouths along Pot Signal Lane.

## Description

Speck-sized, orange-glinted wings. They die instantly in open flame and still rush it. Smoke drives them; steam invites them.

## Beliefs About Them

Lane cooks say a clean gnat spiral means the pot is 'speaking true heat.'

## Narrative Role

Microfauna for a specific Nauw lane's sensory life.
