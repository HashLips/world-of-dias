# Sprout Vent Cap-Bats

## Overview

Sprout Vent Cap-Bats roost in vent caps and spill out in spore season, eating mites and scaring newcomers with sudden black cloth motion.

## Description

Hand-sized bats, faces wrinkled, useful predators of mite haze. Guano management is a chore.

## Beliefs About Them

Vent crews protect roosts even while cursing guano.

## Narrative Role

Small useful creepy for vent zones.
