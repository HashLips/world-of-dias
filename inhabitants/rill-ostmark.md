# Rill Ostmark

## Name

Rill Ostmark

## Overview

Rill Ostmark is the girl of the three, the one who hears the shop’s refusal clearly and goes back after dark anyway because she cannot stand for wonder to be only an adult’s dismissed story.

## Description

Sixteen, quick-eyed, hair usually tied with whatever the morning offered. She runs messages between outer-ring workshops and the basin slope and has learned the difference between a street’s noise and a street’s attention. The myth-door bell rang once when she first found the lane. She pretended it was a draft.

She argues against the theft in the alley and then climbs the step with them. That is the part she will not decorate later. On the road she is the first to say the echo is not leading them to Sorel’s second beginning. When the drag comes, she is the one who still has a hand that can choose the case.

## Beliefs About Them

Joss thinks she is braver than she is careful. Harl thinks she is careful and calls it mood. Pim, later, thinks she was the one who treated the stone as a thing with a place, not a prize. The market loop, which prefers simple tales, says she was the sensible one. She was not only that.

## Narrative Role

She carries the saga’s turn from the inside: accomplice, witness, and the hand that helps put the echo down. She does not become a guide to F500. She becomes someone who can accept a toy without pretending it is a consolation prize for a world she almost had.

## Notes

No special office, no resonant rank. Her listening is attention, not a gift that opens bands.
