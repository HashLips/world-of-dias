# Bonzi

## Name

Bonzi

## Overview

**Bonzi** is a Coral Strand non-human neighbor: a white angular tree-form with magenta petal-clusters and roots that can ride shore memory, greeted before anyone takes a cutting.

## Description

Strand walkers describe **Bonzi** as plantlike and personlike at once—blocky white wood, sharp limb angles, bright magenta clusters that deepen when the form is willing to share shade or a fallen piece. Roots often look under-anchored, thin white lines over void or shallow water, which is how tellers explain sudden overnight moves along the Coral Strand. The teal horizon-sphere in plates is the water-sky the neighbor prefers to face. Locals do not file Bonzi as an Earth bonsai toy or software mascot. They file it as a shore inhabitant useful for timing a rest, teaching children not to strip living wood, and marking where a path still feels honest after storm. Temper is usually serene. Bad craft is potting one for indoor spectacle or painting over magenta clusters to fake willingness.

## Notes

Non-human tree-neighbor. Greet before cutting. Dias name only.
