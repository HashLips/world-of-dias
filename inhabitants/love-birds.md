# Love Birds

## Name

Love Birds

## Overview

**Love Birds** are Aelwyn Canopy pair-birds: dark charcoal bodies that perch facing outward on bright blue branches, red triangular beaks and three-pronged claws, often showing a yellowish-green motion echo when they shift together.

## Description

They arrive as twos. Overlap mid-body is common at rest; eyes stay white slits, alert sideways. Blue branches of canopy flora suit their grip; yellow open air behind them is the plate’s teaching field. The green echo is not a second bird—it is residual resonance of the last move. Song is short and paired. Children learn not to clap under an echo. Adults who cage one alone find the other refusing the branch until release.

## Cultural Meaning

Around Aelwyn Canopy, Love Birds teach together-facing without clinging face-to-face. Resonance favors waiting out the green double. Separating a pair for market show empties local care.

## Habitat and Behavior

Blue branch edges, yellow clearings, season-path overlooks. Roost in pairs; echo fades before true sleep.
