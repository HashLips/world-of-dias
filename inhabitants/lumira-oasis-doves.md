# Lumira Oasis-Doves

## Overview

Lumira Oasis-Doves gather at true and decoy water in cooler hours—soft grey birds that make silence feel inhabited rather than empty.

## Description

Quieter than Hallow Bay Promise Doves; wingbeats muffled as if trained by Listening Heat. They drink, dust-bathe, and leave before noon glare. Dunewalkers read their arrival as water-near, not map-truth.

## Beliefs About Them

Following doves blindly leads to decoys. Following them with humility sometimes leads to Quiet Well manners.

## Narrative Role

Gentle desert animal for oasis scenes.
