# Wabet Sandstorm Aspics

## Overview

Wabet Sandstorm Aspics are small vipers that ride the leading edge of sandstorms, appearing where visibility dies.

## Description

Arm-length, pale, fast strike. Venom serious; antivenom culture exists among dunewalkers. Storm-associated more than oasis-associated.

## Beliefs About Them

Storm songs include aspic verses. Panic kills as often as fangs.

## Narrative Role

Small but deadly desert threat.
