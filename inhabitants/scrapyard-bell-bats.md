# Scrapyard Bell-Bats

## Overview

Scrapyard Bell-Bats are small bats that roost in hollow metal and ring faintly when they launch, giving Sorel yards an accidental night alarm system.

## Description

Fist-sized, ears oversized, wings smudged with oil. They eat moths and midges drawn to forge light. The 'bell' is bone-and-metal resonance from roost cavities, not true bells.

## Beliefs About Them

Yard kids learn to read bat-ring patterns. A full roost launching at once means something large moved below.

## Narrative Role

Slightly magical-feeling but mechanically grounded—good Sorel night texture.
