# Sorel Bridge-Mules

## Overview

Sorel Bridge-Mules work the long bridge traffic into northern Sorel, sure-footed on planks that scare prettier horses.

## Description

Gray and soot coats, ears like signal flags. Carry packs for new exiles who arrive with nothing wheeled.

## Beliefs About Them

A kind mule handler is remembered longer than a kind speech at the gate.

## Narrative Role

Medium mercy infrastructure.
