# Blackbridge Watch-Hounds

## Overview

Blackbridge Watch-Hounds are dark-coated hounds kept near the long bridge entries into northern Sorel, trained to scent fear, blood, and hidden cargo.

## Description

Tall, quiet, coats near-black with ash paws. Handlers walk them at shift changes. They rarely bark; a hard stare is the first warning.

## Beliefs About Them

Travelers from Nauw tell conflicting stories: protectors, or judges with teeth. Bridge crews insist the hounds answer training, not morality.

## Narrative Role

Embodies the Nauw–Sorel threshold as living enforcement.
