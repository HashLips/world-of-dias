# Road-Between Echo-Hounds

## Overview

Road-Between Echo-Hounds are reported hound-shaped echoforms that appear on long roads between regions, especially where many farewells and exiles have soaked the path.

## Description

Described as smoke-and-dust dogs with too-regular pacing. They do not always attack; sometimes they only follow until a border stone. Physical traces are inconsistent: pawprints that fade mid-stride, breath without fog on cold nights.

## Beliefs About Them

Teamsters leave a spoken goodbye at mile markers to 'satisfy the hound.' Sorel exiles fear them as guilt given legs; Wabet walkers treat them as weather of memory.

## Narrative Role

Mysterious cross-regional presence—scary or sorrowful depending on the traveler.

## Notes

Classed as echoform-adjacent pending better witness agreement.
