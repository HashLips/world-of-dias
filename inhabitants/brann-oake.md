# Brann Oake

## Name

Brann Oake

## Overview

Brann Oake is a human miner and boatman of Saltjaw in his forties. He crewed *The Patient Lead* on the shelf survey and survived the strike on a timber with Tamsy Kell after Dorrit Vash pushed him to it and drowned. He is Maren's steadiest friend on the coast. He rows her out on the failed first voyage and again into the Long Slack to catch the fleet. He keeps the unfinished posts of Ansel and Maren's second room standing on the slope, so that something there is still waiting.

## Description

Big, slope-shouldered, slow-spoken. He lost two fingers of his left hand years ago to a hawser in Claimscar Yard, in some crew fight he does not talk about, and does most things one-handed or with the ruined hand braced. He hums the same five notes under his breath whenever he is working or worried, and does not know he does it. His voice is deep and uneven. On the way home from the Stillmouth he took over the count from Tamsy and beat it out on the rail with all his fingers, the real ones and the missing ones.

**Can:** row on a bearing held in his head; keep a skiff alive in a following sea; carry grief without putting it down on anyone.
**Cannot:** swim well with one good hand. At the strike Dorrit had to save him.
**Cost:** he is alive because Dorrit is not. He knows it every day.

## Beliefs About Them

Saltjaw sees him as reliable furniture: always there, never asked. Maren sees him as the one person who never told her to stop. He sees himself as the man who should have gone into the water instead of the skipper.

## Narrative Role

Strike survivor and witness. Rower of the saga's two small-boat voyages (chapters 17 and 23). Bearer of the ruined-hand count home in chapter 26. Keeper of the posts.

## Notes

The posts on the slope are a quiet open thread. Nobody says whether the room will ever be finished.
