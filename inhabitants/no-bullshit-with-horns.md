# No Bullshit with Horns

## Name

No Bullshit with Horns

## Overview

**No Bullshit with Horns** is a non-human neighbor of Brindle March: a dark four-legger with a wide V of long straight horns, one white almond eye, and a habit of casting sharp teal shadows on pale ground.

## Description

March walkers describe a chocolate-brown body, heavy but clean in silhouette, head slightly turned as if checking who is inventing excuses on the fruit road. The horns rise long and straight into a wide V—impossible to mistake for decoration. The single white almond eye is the detail children remember first. When the creature plants a foreleg far to the side, locals read it as a pause demand: stop the soft talk, look at what is actually in front of you. Shadows fall teal or turquoise on pinkish ground, a color mismatch the march treats as part of the presence rather than as a painting error. People do not file this as an Earth bull or oryx. They file it as a neighbor-shape that thickens road manners. Witnesses disagree on exact mass and temper; they agree you give the horns room and you do not pretend the teal shadow is nothing. Softfruit carts slow. Jokes that shrink the creature into a mascot are considered bad care. Resonance sits in the eye-contact; memory sits in the V against the salmon sky; wonder sits in the wrong-colored shadow that still belongs to the march.
