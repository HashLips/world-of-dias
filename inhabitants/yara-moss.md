# Yara Moss

## Name

Yara Moss

## Overview

Yara Moss is a junior Calareth Watch runner at Hallow Bay who teaches arrivals the Ribbon rite and counts ribbons that vanish overnight.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Young, precise, kind without being soft. Coris Vale uses Yara for first contact because Yara can smile while saying no. Tide-pool teaching at Coral Strand is Yara’s favorite duty; Elder Shelf day-pass refusals are the least.

## Beliefs About Them

Children trust Yara. Rule-breakers underestimate Yara once.

## Narrative Role

Island saga viewpoint that can grow from hospitality into hard threshold judgment.
