# Ledger Dust-Fleas

## Overview

Ledger Dust-Fleas are near-invisible fleas that live in the paper grit of Calibration Row desks, thriving on skin flakes and dried ink.

## Description

Too small to see clearly without a lens; under glass they look like dark commas with twitchy legs. Bites itch in neat rows, as if counted. Harmless beyond annoyance.

## Beliefs About Them

Clerks blame bad sums on flea nights. Sweeping is ritual; crushing is constant.

## Narrative Role

Tiniest urban life—makes Veloria's bureaucracy feel bodily.
