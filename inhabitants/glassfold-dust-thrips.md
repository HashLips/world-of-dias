# Glassfold Dust-Thrips

## Overview

Glassfold Dust-Thrips are tiny thrips that cling to ledge panes and become briefly visible as silver commas during reveal windows.

## Description

Otherwise near-invisible. Do not bite. Their sudden appearance is a soft timer for disclosure intervals.

## Beliefs About Them

Veil apprentices use thrip glitter as a humble clock.

## Narrative Role

Tiniest F610 cue-creature.
