# Veloria Pocket-Mice

## Overview

Veloria Pocket-Mice are soft gray mice that live in stall hems and coat pockets left open too long in markets.

## Description

Palm-sized, bright eyes, rarely diseased in city lore when food is clean. Steal crumbs, not children. Dawn runners.

## Beliefs About Them

A mouse in a till is bad accounting; a mouse in a poem is charming. Both happen.

## Narrative Role

Small cuddly-adjacent urban animal.
