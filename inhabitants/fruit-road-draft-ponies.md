# Fruit-Road Draft-Ponies

## Overview

Fruit-Road Draft-Ponies are compact strong ponies bred for orchard-cart hauling along the Eastbound Fruit Road.

## Description

Chestnut and piebald coats, thick necks, calm eyes. They know rest berms by smell. Kick only when fools deserve it.

## Beliefs About Them

A pony that refuses a mile is trusted over a driver's pride.

## Narrative Role

Medium working backbone of fruit trade.
