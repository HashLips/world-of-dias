# Revision Sky-Whales

## Overview

Revision Sky-Whales are colossal draft-beings that swim unfinished sky, bodies redrawing as they pass—sometimes bone-line, sometimes volume, sometimes erase.

## Description

Watching too long leaves viewers unsure of their own outline. Wolves below reportedly howl. May be realm weather personified.
