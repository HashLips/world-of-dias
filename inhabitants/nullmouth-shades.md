# Nullmouth Shades

## Overview

Nullmouth Shades are whispered predators of F500 that erase contrast around prey—making edges, voices, and last thoughts harder to hold.

## Description

No agreed form: a mouth-shaped dark, a walking occlusion, a cold attention. Survivors (if any) describe losing the ability to picture what attacked them. The wound is not only flesh—it is the memory of the attack thinning until fear has no object.

## Beliefs About Them

Grouped with Unraveled rumors. Naming them too often is considered unwise in F432 taverns. [The Name That Thins the Room](../myths/the-name-that-thins-the-room.md) treats Nullmouth and Horizon-Null talk as the same social hazard with different scales.

## Avoidance Customs

Do not rehearse Nullmouth stories for sport. If a companion’s account of an attack grows blank mid-sentence, stop pressing for detail—pressing is said to thin the room further. Prefer sealed-channel language when official warning is required.

## Narrative Role

Peak scary for the abyss frequency—threat to meaning, not only body. Classed as **existential** in [Creatures of Dias](../world/creatures-of-dias.md).

## Notes

Keep proof thin. The horror is the missing picture.
