# Wick Hollen

## Name

Wick Hollen

## Overview

Wick Hollen is a human glasswort gatherer of Sallow Strand. He found the Saltjaw survey canister on the Lumira shore east of Glasswake Rock twelve days after the strike. As a young man he worked three seasons on Driftfall Salvage Shore, so he recognized it as a Saltjaw-pattern survey tube by its round-turned cap and the Sprout Vent sign scratched on it. He did not open it on the shore; he carried it to Odile Brask. Later he names Low Bell Cove as the place where things from the outward run come ashore, and he is one of the three who hear the answering signal.

## Description

Middle-aged, lean, sun-cracked, slow-voiced. He walks the shingle so softly that people at Sallow Strand say he could pass Glasswake without the Keepers noticing. He carries a flat glasswort basket and a wrapping cloth. He came west from Sorel long ago and stayed, and says little about why. In company he sits in a corner and hands people things.

**Can:** read a shore for what the sea brings; recognize Sorel salvage and survey gear; keep to the Silence Law by habit.
**Cannot:** read, well. He leaves letters to Odile.

## Beliefs About Them

The hamlet thinks him odd and dependable. The seekers wanted him to tell the finding as a marvel. He told it the same way every time, "and no more than that," and Odile was grateful for the *no more* every time.

## Narrative Role

He is the finder, the one who knows where things wash up, and a witness. His testimony on the answering signal is exact and modest: two knocks and a whistle from the sea at dusk on the day the water stopped, "and nothing else, because I don't know anything else."

## Notes

He pooled his glasswort money for Maren's race east in chapter 23 and left before anyone could thank him.
