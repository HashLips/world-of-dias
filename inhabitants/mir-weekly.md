# Mir Weekly

## Name

Mir Weekly

## Overview

Mir Weekly is a Flagweek Strip mayor who posts a new flag each week and the same breakfast every morning—mediation by ritual stubbornness.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Dry humor, ink-stained poles, zero patience for people who weaponize porridge. The Three Opinions find Mir either wise or exhausting depending on the week’s cloth.

## Beliefs About Them

Hearthvale calls Mir slippery. Driftfall calls Mir soft. Strip children call Mir the reason school still happens.

## Narrative Role

Comic-dread border saga lead; allegiance as costume, care as constant.
