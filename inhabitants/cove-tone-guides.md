# Cove Tone Guides

## Name

Cove Tone Guides

## Overview

**Cove Tone Guides** host holding coves on the Bloomline’s warm current. They lay cloths, watch the reeds, and end a session when consent weather breaks. They are climate hosts. The work is facilitation with a spine: you may feel in public only as far as the room agreed, and the Guide is the person who can hear the agreement fail before the haze turns brittle.

## Description

Layered wraps in tributary colors, rose over amber, a darker band near the hem for days when grief is the guest and cheer would be a lie. Voices stay soft and exits stay firm. Some Guides are Tidehearts, at home in the coupling of mood and water. Some are trained neighbors who learned the cloth because their cove needed more hands than mystique. They carry Hearttide Holding Cloths folded, not displayed, and they notice a forced smile the way other people notice smoke.

In a working hour they stand at the rim, not in the center. Center is for the people who asked to be held. A Guide’s hands are busy with edges: a cloth straightened, a reed-lean checked, a person walked to the bank without being made into an example.

## Beliefs About Them

People call them healers without clinics and negotiators without courts, which is flattering and slightly wrong. They do not diagnose you and they do not rule you. They keep a pocket of weather shareable. In fear-narrow hours they go terse; a long speech is a way of avoiding the door. Outsiders who wanted a truth serum resent them. People who have been used as emotional instruments trust them more than they trust charismatic talkers.

## Narrative Role

F380 social infrastructure with a face. A negotiation day, a grief that needs a side channel, a visitor who mistakes serenity for permission: the Guide is how the scene stays kind without going vague. Not an Earth therapist imported under a prettier name.

## Notes

Climate hosts. Consent is the craft. Do not arm them with mood-as-weapon.
