# Noro Halfmeasure

## Name

Noro Halfmeasure

## Overview

**Noro Halfmeasure** is Orrin’s rival calibrator: unfinished instruments are unfinished character—until pride brings latches to a vault attempt. Compact, exact, and furious that Orrin’s graphs work. Arc runs from mockery to uneasy admission to latch help without apology.

## Description

Short-compact build, ceremonial hair crop Orrin refuses, rival dial always at the belt. Clothes finished to a shine that looks like a verdict. Speaks in closed sentences. Over-finishes tools until they lose the maker’s crooked honesty Mio trusts. Respects Dox Khar more than Orrin and hates needing Orrin’s results.

Not a villain. Pride is the engine. Fear can convert pride into help without converting it into warmth.

Concrete saga beat: at the Frame build, Noro leans in the alcove mouth and mocks unfinished glass, then walks away. During half-digit wrongness, Noro admits—uneasily—that something is steering. At the vault, Noro brings latches and holds them with Tam when containment fails: help without apology. Pride converted to fear is still help. Earlier, in Widening as Gift, Noro whispers that if Orrin does not widen, someone worse will—fear speaking through rivalry.

Physical read: compact force, spotless apron edges, a dial polished until it reflects other people’s unfinished work as insult. Jaw set for verdicts; eyes quicker than the jaw when graphs actually work.

Noro will not apologize for helping at the vault. Orrin will not demand it. The dial between them stays unfinished on purpose.

## Beliefs About Them

Apprentices fear Noro’s standards. Orrin calls him colleague until it sticks. Tam calls him a storm with a dial. Houses prefer Noro’s manners and still file Orrin’s graphs when they must.

## Narrative Role

Craft rivalry with reasons across Movements Two and Three. Pressure, admission, and vault aid without a soft reconciliation scene.
