# Mountain Trade Stone-Mammoths

## Overview

Mountain Trade Stone-Mammoths are rumored colossal mammoth-forms whose hide looks like walking cliff-face, said to open or close high trade passes by standing.

## Description

Tusks like buttresses, breath like kiln exhaust in stories. Footprints alleged as new terraces. No live capture ever claimed seriously.

## Beliefs About Them

Merchants toast them when passes clear. Skeptics toast the toast.

## Narrative Role

Colossal trade-mountain legend.
