# Hallow Bay Skiff-Otters

## Overview

Hallow Bay Skiff-Otters play around skiffs and dories, occasionally untie soft knots and return with stolen gloves as gifts.

## Description

Chocolate coats, white muzzle tips, medium river-otter size in salt-sweet bay water. Curious, rarely mean.

## Beliefs About Them

Yara Moss's watch notes treat them as 'negotiators.' Ribbon manners still apply: don't harm them for jokes.

## Narrative Role

Medium happy Calareth water life.
