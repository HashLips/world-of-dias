# Bran Cinder

## Name

Bran Cinder

## Overview

Bran Cinder reads Low Winter smoke on Cinder Nook’s bluffs and teaches apprentices to treat ash as instrument, not oracle.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Scarred forearms, quiet jokes, exact timing with tar pots and drying racks. Bran will refuse to “perform prophecy” for Veloria tourists and still save a boat with a correct bluff reading.

## Beliefs About Them

Locals call Bran necessary. Outsiders call Bran spooky. Bran calls it work.

## Narrative Role

Weather-craft mentor for Outer Rim stories that need competence over mystique.
