# Bess Thirdchair

## Overview

**Bess Thirdchair** is a Verdant Reach elder who keeps a third chair empty for travelers—hospitality as furniture.

## Description

Apple-stained apron, choir-frog patience, stories that stop before Stillhollow tourism. Bess knows Fellgrove gossip and refuses to sell it. The third chair is sometimes literal wood, sometimes a moss-patched stool.

## Beliefs About Them

Walkers leave fruit. Children learn “third chair” as idiom for welcome. People hunting Vorrith rumors find Bess’s door gently shut.

## Narrative Role

Elder + bridge into Wabet calm without Keep office weight.
