# Fellgrove Stitch-Spiders

## Overview

Fellgrove Stitch-Spiders weave webs in repairable geometric grids that trail-menders joke look like needlework patterns.

## Description

Coin-sized bodies, silk strong enough to catch midges and small moths. Harmless to people. Webs glitter after mist.

## Beliefs About Them

Finding an unbroken stitch-web before a hard talk is a quiet good omen.

## Narrative Role

Small beautiful forest detail.
