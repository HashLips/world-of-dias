# Whales Sleeping

## Name

Whales Sleeping

## Overview

**Whales Sleeping** is the Outer Rim name for the coral-red whales that sleep at the surface of the Nauw Outer Rim Seas, tilted on their sides, held up by their own slow spouts.

## Description

Rim crews describe sleeping whales as long coral shapes lying aslant in calm water, eyes closed in what looks like contentment. Every so often the blowhole lets out a slow double spout that does not fall straight back as it would in waking. Instead the white arcs bend outward and down around the whale's head and body, and for a while they look like long pale arms with spread fingers, cradling the sleeper's jaw and keeping it from sinking. Sometimes a darker, knotted arm of spray reaches down from above as well; crews cannot agree whether it belongs to a second whale nearby or is simply cold-weather spray.

## Lore Connection

Rim singing communities have lullabies said to be learned from sleeping whales, sung low across the water at night. The rim's rule is silent passage: cut engines, ship oars, drift past, and never steer between a whale and its spout. Large ships scatter pale scraps ahead of their bows on night crossings partly so sleeping whales hear them coming. Waking a sleeping whale on purpose is considered bad luck for the whole season.
