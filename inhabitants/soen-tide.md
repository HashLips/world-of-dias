# Soen Tide

## Name

Soen Tide

## Overview

Soen Tide keeps Songripe Isles song-calendars in place of harvest calendars—custodian of a resonance pocket where fruit fails and music feeds.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Barefoot on rope bridges, bird-accompanied, allergic to mainland pricing talk. Soen welcomes visitors who can learn a refrain and strands those who plant orchard greed anyway.

## Beliefs About Them

Mainland traders call Soen impractical. Isle children call Soen the clock.

## Narrative Role

West-coast Wabet saga guide for gentle or eerie islet stories.
