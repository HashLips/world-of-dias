# Rainname Butterflies

## Overview

Rainname Butterflies appear in F380 rains that locals describe as having learned names—wings speckled as if with syllables of color.

## Description

Delicate, numerous after warm rains. Patterns differ by storm. Landing on a sleeve can feel oddly personal without being telepathic.

## Beliefs About Them

Tied to rain-name lore; children invent names for new wing patterns each season.

## Narrative Role

Joyful mystical insect life for F380.
