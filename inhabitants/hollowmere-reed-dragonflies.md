# Hollowmere Reed-Dragonflies

## Overview

Hollowmere Reed-Dragonflies skim the mere in blue-green dashes, hunting midges above Latch-Otter wakes.

## Description

Hand-span wings, bodies like enamel pins. Day-active, unafraid of boats at distance. Harmless.

## Beliefs About Them

A dragonfly landing on a net is luck for a soft catch.

## Narrative Role

Bright small beauty for Hollowmere days.
