# Unraveled Remnant-Beasts

## Overview

Unraveled Remnant-Beasts are rumored animal-shaped leftovers of failed coherence in F500—forms that almost remember being beasts.

## Description

Outlines suggest dogs, birds, whales, then fail. Motion is wrong. Some accounts claim they flee light; others claim they flee attention.

## Beliefs About Them

Possibly Unraveled-adjacent rather than separate taxa. Canon keeps doors open and bolts them with caution.

## Narrative Role

Gives The Unraveled a fauna shadow without over-defining the abyss.
