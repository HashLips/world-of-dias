# Underwood Bluefoxes

## Overview

Underwood Bluefoxes are small foxes of Wabet underwood zones whose fur takes a cold blue cast where blue litter gathers thickest.

## Description

Cat-sized, quiet, with brush tails that leave faint blue pollen-like dust. They avoid open trails and watch from root hollows. Not known to attack people; startling one can trigger a wider underwood 'listening' hush.

## Beliefs About Them

Some link them to Blue Litter Husk lore; others say the blue is only diet and mold-light. Either way, hunters leave them alone.

## Narrative Role

Connects living fauna to Wabet's blue-litter uncanny without making every fox an echoform.
