# Glasswater Field-Herons

## Overview

Glasswater Field-Herons stand like pale questions in shallow clean water, hunting fish without breaking the Fields' lull more than needed.

## Description

Tall, ash-white, slow spears. Medium wading birds. Nest in reed stands away from heavy traffic.

## Beliefs About Them

Chasing a heron for sport is a Glasswater social crime.

## Narrative Role

Medium calm bird for mirror-water scenes.
