# Veloria Lap-Cats

## Overview

Veloria Lap-Cats are soft household cats kept as beloved companions in ring homes and stall backs—smaller-tempered cousins to forge and steam cats.

## Description

Round faces, varied coats, a habit of claiming sunny ledger corners and fruit-crate lids. They purr loud enough to interrupt mild arguments. Children name them after colors and market days. They hunt Pocket-Mice with inconsistent success and sleep through Banner Moth weeks as if bored of spectacle.

## Beliefs About Them

A Lap-Cat choosing your lap is treated as a small civic blessing. Losing one starts neighborhood search parties that put some Watch errands to shame.

## Narrative Role

Gives Veloria children a normal animal love life that still feels local.
