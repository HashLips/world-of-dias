# Bram Keel

## Name

Bram Keel

## Overview

Bram Keel keeps The Peel and Bell. He pours drinks that misbehave in small ways and he will not broker a ticket or a secret that belongs on the board if it belongs anywhere.

## Description

A broad man with a bad knee and a good memory for who has not paid. Sleeves rolled, peel-grit in the creases of his fingers, a brass bell within reach that he uses less often than people fear. He listens with his head slightly tilted, not from wisdom but from the knee, which makes standing still a negotiation. He knows which drink is a trick and which one will get a carter killed if they take it to a redmile.

## Beliefs About Them

Regulars say the bar is fair because Bram is slightly disappointed in everyone equally. Third-ring coats say he is slow. He is not slow. He is unwilling to speed a story past its witnesses.

## Narrative Role

Host of the gossip and of the chalk. He does not solve the fruit. He keeps the room that makes Lark's choice public.

## Notes

His drinks are odd and local. None of them is a ferry.
