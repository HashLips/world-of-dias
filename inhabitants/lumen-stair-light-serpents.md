# Lumen Stair Light-Serpents

## Overview

Lumen Stair Light-Serpents are reported colossal serpents of coherent light sometimes seen coiling the Stair during disputed bleed-bright nights.

## Description

No mass, yet they occlude stars in coils. Length beyond counting. May be F200 bleed perceived as animal. Dangerous if climbed toward; mostly distant spectacle.

## Beliefs About Them

Stair keepers clear platforms when coils appear. Prayers differ by culture.

## Narrative Role

Colossal threshold creature between frequencies.
