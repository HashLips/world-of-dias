# Ivy Sketchwalk

## Overview

**Ivy Sketchwalk** is an Averra apprentice who walks unfinished sketches and grid scraps toward Driftfall artists—and sometimes back toward Gallery culture—without claiming mastery.

## Description

Inkcap thumbs, cliff-thyme smell, a satchel of revision seams. Ivy treats “unfinished” as cargo honesty. Will not open doors marked DO NOT OPEN for curiosity; will carry a Solven-style grid gift if asked kindly.

## Beliefs About Them

Studio elders call Ivy useful legs. Driftfall makers call Ivy a bridge that doesn’t lecture. Sael’s sealed habits make Ivy nervous in the correct amount.

## Narrative Role

Bridge—Averra craft quiet into Driftfall finish-culture and Veloria gallery sideways glances.
