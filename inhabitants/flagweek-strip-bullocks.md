# Flagweek Strip Bullocks

## Overview

Flagweek Strip Bullocks are large festival cattle decorated and sometimes raced in chaotic Strip contests.

## Description

Heavy, painted horns, flower ropes. Dangerous when panicked. Handlers drink less than spectators hope.

## Beliefs About Them

A bullock that stops to eat garlands is the people's champion.

## Narrative Role

Large comic-danger festival energy.
