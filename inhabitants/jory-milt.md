# Jory Milt

## Name

Jory Milt

## Overview

Jory Milt drives The Brindle Crawler, a slow real train, and is personally insulted by jokes that confuse his spur with the rail that sells the same platform twice.

## Description

Compact, soot in the ears, a wave he gives whether or not the platform deserves it. He keeps a kettle on the footplate and a timetable in his head that is more honest than the posted one by about four minutes, which he announces. He loves the second platform because it is proof. Different tiles. Different stain. A depot that stays put.

## Beliefs About Them

Riders think he is comically proud of a slow machine. He thinks they have not walked the grade with a brake that works. Children like the wave. He likes children who do not ask if the train goes to another frequency. When they ask, he says no, and points at the ground.

## Narrative Role

Makes the train obviously not the myth. He is the human difference: a driver who arrives where he aimed.

## Notes

Do not enlist him in a ferry plot. He would refuse, loudly, and the refusal would be in character.
