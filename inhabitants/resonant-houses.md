# Resonant Houses

## Overview

The **Resonant Houses** are the structured governing coalition of F432, seated in realm-wide authority and publicly embodied by **Vailor Quentin**, the High Resonant.

## Description

The Houses are not a single building but a **network of oath-bound houses** that ratify realm policy, arbitrate major disputes between regions, and maintain the necessary fiction—many say the **needed** fiction—that F432 moves as one body despite its internal contrasts.

House influence is tiered. Veloria's Ring Council handles city continuity; the Resonant Houses handle **frequency-scale** decisions: trade compacts, harmonic hazard classification, and what counts as canonical danger versus rumor.

## Institutional Wants (Story Drivers)

| Want | Why it creates scenes |
| --- | --- |
| **Stable classification** | Echo events and relic manifestations must be labeled before markets panic |
| **Central narrative control** | Competing Fracture myths are tolerated publicly but managed privately |
| **Core secrecy** | Inner-ring harmonic data from Valorian Operators is filtered before public release |
| **Legitimacy performance** | Vailor's crown-standard must read as uncontested even when it is not |

## Relationship to The Harmonic Saga

Calen Brink's echo-reading threatens House order not because it is loud, but because it produces **unfiltered testimony**—the kind load-balance trials respect but sealed chambers fear.

Oriven Tal is said to operate between Houses and Ring Council with leverage neither side fully documents.

## Beliefs About Them

Supporters call the Houses the only reason Nauw, Wabet, and Sorel still trade during echo-rise seasons. Critics say the Houses treat living resonance as **property to be filed**, not truth to be shared.

## Narrative Role

The Resonant Houses embody **grand, structured power** in the saga: the weight of realm order Calen must navigate without becoming its enemy or its tool.
