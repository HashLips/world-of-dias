# Hearthline Ash-Whales

## Overview

Hearthline Ash-Whales are rumored colossal land-or-sky whale forms that migrate along ancient warmth corridors once in a long while, ash falling like snow from their flanks.

## Description

Scale beyond carts. Sound like distant forge choirs. No stable photo. Great Blue Bird lore sometimes tangles with theirs.

## Beliefs About Them

Seeing one is a lifetime story; hunting one is a cultural obscenity.

## Narrative Role

Colossal F840 wonder against quiet recovery.
