# Bram Inkspan

## Name

Bram Inkspan

## Overview

**Bram Inkspan** sells disputed Supplemental Leaf transcriptions from a desk that smells of ink and oranges. Not Fellgrove’s Bram Orlen—different ledger, different sins, same name drifting through Dias like a joke the world has not finished telling. He gives Orrin the leaf that will not stay quiet.

## Description

Thin, blotter-stained cuffs, ink under one thumbnail that never quite leaves. Allergic to verdicts; sells arguments instead. Keeps F960 strike-through variants in separate drawers like dangerous spices. Speaks softly so buyers lean in and commit.

The copy desk is a Row institution with sticky orange sweetness under the ink. Scholars need Bram. Houses pretend not to. Bram prices transcriptions by how loudly a page might later argue with a stamp.

Concrete saga beat: in The Leaf That Won’t Stay Quiet, Bram sells Orrin a disputed Supplemental Leaf transcription—F120, F380, F610, F840, and a nervous F960—while Archivist Pellun tries to file wonder before it breathes. Bram does not promise truth. He promises a copy that will not sit still in the mind. That is enough to start Movement One’s stack mapping in earnest.

Physical read: ink halo on one cuff, orange oil on the other, spectacles repaired with waxed cord. Speaks like a man allergic to conclusions and paid for possibilities.

Bram will sell you three versions of F960 manners and refuse to pick a favorite. The leaf that will not stay quiet is not his prophecy—it is his inventory moving.

## Beliefs About Them

Scholars need Bram. Houses pretend not to. Apprentices warn each other about name confusion with Bram Orlen. Orrin tips in scraped ledger thanks and orange shares. Pellun finds Bram useful and unbearable in the same hour.

## Narrative Role

Leaf access and archive beat for Movement One. Opens the evidence economy that later fights partial publish and nonactionable filing.
