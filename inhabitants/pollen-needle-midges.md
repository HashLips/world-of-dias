# Pollen Needle-Midges

## Overview

Pollen Needle-Midges are hair-thin midges that ride Basin pollen clouds and leave a sweet itch on exposed wrists.

## Description

Bodies like living pin-points; swarms look like drifting gold dust until they land. Active at gilding hour. Do not carry serious disease in local accounts.

## Beliefs About Them

Orchard workers smear citrus oil on cuffs. Children call the itch 'the afternoon writing.'

## Narrative Role

Tiny seasonal texture for Basin harvest air.
