# Unfinished Eraser-Mites

## Overview

Unfinished Eraser-Mites are microscopic mites that dull fresh graphite edges overnight—living erasers in folk speech.

## Description

Invisible. Artists curse them; Draftkin call them collaborators. Surfaces look softer by morning.

## Beliefs About Them

Cover work or accept revision by realm.
