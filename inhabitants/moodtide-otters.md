# Moodtide Otters

## Overview

Moodtide Otters are playful estuary otters that seem to amplify nearby laughter and dampen nearby sulking simply by joining a bank.

Sleek, warm-brown, bright whiskers. Their play is ordinary otter chaos with unusual social side effects in F380's coupled climate.
