# Almost-Same Wolf

## Overview

The Almost-Same Wolf is a feared F120 predator said to return to a hunt already survived, with small differences that make the second encounter worse.

## Description

Accounts disagree on coat color; they agree on the feeling of 'I already lived this, and it changed.' Attacks are rare in records, huge in stories.

## Beliefs About Them

Used to teach that recurrence is not safety.

## Narrative Role

Scary face of F120—history with teeth.
