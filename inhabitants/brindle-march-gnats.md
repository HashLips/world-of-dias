# Brindle March Gnats

## Overview

Brindle March Gnats are the one thing that refuses the March's famous stillness, whining in tiny orbits even when carts hush.

## Description

Ordinary gnats with unusual persistence in quiet air. Itch mild. Their sound becomes the loudest thing on still days.

## Beliefs About Them

Some say the gnats keep the March from becoming a true dead silence—and that this is mercy.

## Narrative Role

Small ironic counterpoint to Brindle hush.
