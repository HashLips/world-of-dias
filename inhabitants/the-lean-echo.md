# The Lean Echo

## Name

The Lean Echo

## Overview

The Lean Echo is the ghostly frequency being that stands up out of the Heartlean Colorstone after the stone is stolen from Farstock. It is not a shop clerk and not a ferry. It is held color that was never given leave to leave.

## Description

It has a person’s height only by courtesy. Edges drink light the way F500’s rumor says absence drinks contrast, while the interior of it keeps shifting through the hearttide colors of whoever is nearest — Joss’s amber, Harl’s chalk-gray, Rill’s workshop blue — without becoming any of them. It does not cast a useful shadow. It speaks in nearly-words: place-names said as if they were kindnesses, and a pressure in the teeth like a bell under water. It never draws a route. It never names a method. It walks half a step ahead and lets true roads do the convincing.

When it turns, the colors stop offering themselves and close. The echo encapsulates. It drags. The wonder and the grip are the same creature.

## Beliefs About Them

The three, at first, believe it is the stone’s promise made companionable: a guide that knows a wonderful elsewhere, perhaps even a way toward the null band people only dare as a story. After the turn they believe it is a thief of a different order. Pim believes it is what the stone does when keeping is interrupted. Whether it truly knew a way to F500 is not settled by the capture. The cold on the Long Gate was a sign. It was not a door anyone opened, and no one came home with instructions.

Scholars who hear the tale later may class it among Echoforms. They should not class it as proof of a crossing.

## Narrative Role

It is the lure and the captor of the Farstock Saga. It uses the real road toward Sorel because restless outer-ring feet already believe the south is where a life becomes larger. Inside that belief it leans toward F500. It fails to deliver anyone there. Returned to the stone, it is quiet in the way a kept object is quiet, which is not the same as gone from the world’s questions.

## Notes

Distinct from the Caliburn, which nested in measurement and load-noise. The Lean Echo’s origin is a kept hearttide-leaning stone, unbound by theft, not by a spectrum frame.

Its knowledge of a way remains **unknown**. Do not write a later scene in which it explains the how.
