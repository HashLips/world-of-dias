# Bastien Crale

## Name

Bastien Crale

## Overview

Bastien Crale is the human mine master of Saltjaw Mine. He took the mine over from his father and answers to backers in Claimscar Yard who want the Under-Shelf Drift driven. He commissioned the shelf survey that cost *The Patient Lead*, Dorrit Vash, and Ansel. Afterwards he keeps the survey ledger under a promise to Maren, puts his own lighter on the water for the Houses' expedition, and in the end shelves the Under-Shelf Drift himself and tells the backers to find another mine master if they don't like it.

## Description

Fifties, heavy, grey-stubbled, short-tempered with paper and patient with stone. His right palm carries a shiny rope-burn scar from a hoist that ran away when he was young. He keeps a hut on the mine slope with one shelf of books, all ledgers. He does not pray. He says things under his breath that are close to prayers.

**Can:** run a marginal mine through hard years; deal with Claimscar backers without being owned by them, mostly; patch a sail; keep a promise.
**Cannot:** undo the survey he ordered.

## Beliefs About Them

Saltjaw finds him fair and hard. The backers find him stubborn. Maren comes to trust him because he hands the ledger over without being asked.

## Narrative Role

He is the reason for the survey, and its first guilty party. He keeps the ledger, sails his own hull in the fleet, and gives up the drift. "I owe him the drift" is the line he lives by.

## Notes

The Claimscar backers stay unnamed. Their agent appears in chapters 18, 22, and 24–27 without a name or an entry, as a role.
