# Averra Studio-Pigeons

## Overview

Averra Studio-Pigeons loaf on cliff ledges and studio roofs, stealing crusts and posing as if they know they are being drawn.

## Description

Iridescent necks, ink-stained feet from careless palettes, a talent for sitting in good light. Apprentices sketch them when models cancel. They coexist with Cliff-Goats in mutual disregard.

## Beliefs About Them

A pigeon that will not leave your sill is either muse or rent demand—studio jokes disagree.

## Narrative Role

Beloved-ordinary isle bird for art-life texture.
