# Bloomline Heart-Deer

## Overview

Bloomline Heart-Deer are soft-eyed deer of the Bloomline Estuary whose coat warmth visibly shifts with the local mood-weather.

## Description

In relief they look sunlit; in grief, colors mute as if under cloud. They move toward gatherings that soften the estuary and away from panic fronts.

## Beliefs About Them

Tidehearts read Heart-Deer drift as informal climate advice.

## Narrative Role

Embodies F380's emotion-as-weather through fauna.
