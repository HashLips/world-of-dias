# Cress Adel

## Name

Cress Adel

## Overview

Cress Adel handles The Soft Mark, the basin blimp. She sells height as advertisement and is practical about gas, wind, and the vanity of men who hire both.

## Description

Windburned, scarves layered until the gondola drafts give up, gloves thinned at the valve hand. She speaks in short checks: ballast, line, sign. She can hold the blimp over a shed so a painted peel sits in the gilding light, and she can refuse to drop a parcel if the drop would hit a wagon. She has no romance about Sky Drifters. She has shared sky with their altitude only in the sense that numbers can share a column. They do not work her ropes.

## Beliefs About Them

Dain's people call her slow. She is slow the way a gas bag is slow, which is to say on time if you asked the right day. Crews trust her because she will not call a hazard a thrill.

## Narrative Role

Puts a blimp in the auction sky so the saga's air is not only one freight plane. She is hire, not omen.

## Notes

Does not land in the lent field. Does not ferry anyone anywhere but across the basin's own wind.
