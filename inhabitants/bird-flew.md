# Bird Flew

## Name

Bird Flew

## Overview

**Bird Flew** is a non-human canopy presence around Aelwyn: angular black avian neighbors that gather when a flight ends, teaching path-keepers how a fallen echo should be returned to ground without being harvested.

## Description

Witnesses describe them as sharp dark silhouettes rather than soft songbirds—thin legs, flat graphic bodies, eyes that read as attention more than decoration. One may lie still on a reddish-orange pool after a hard descent. Others stand, watch, or dive. The red-spotted head is a known local mark: that bird approaches first, not as a ruler, but as the canopy’s agreed first touch so the fallen’s resonance is not scrambled by a crowd. The navy orb and white beam in plates are not their bodies; they are the sky-event the birds answer. Locals do not file **Bird Flew** as an Earth species. They file it as a neighbor-shape useful for warning children, timing a departure after canopy quiet, and explaining why a yard goes still. Diet and temper vary by telling; manners do not: do not bottle the orange pool, do not shove past the red-spot diver, and do not pretend the echo is nothing.

## Notes

Depict non-human only. Culture- and religion-agnostic. Canopy witness of fallen flight.
