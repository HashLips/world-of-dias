# Summer Birds

## Name

Summer Birds

## Overview

**Summer Birds** is a non-human presence noticed around **Aelwyn Canopy**—not an Earth animal filed for natural history, but a local shape people use to tell whether a road, yard, canopy, or shore still remembers recent traffic.

## Description

In plates and yard talk, **Summer Birds** shows as a clear graphic silhouette rather than a crowded scene: color fields and hard edges that read at a glance on parchment or market board. Around **Aelwyn Canopy**, people treat the image as a reminder that touch leaves a living echo—whether the subject is creature, craft, growth, weather, or mark. The picture does not invent a bureaucracy; it thickens a habit already in the road: slow down, look twice, decide whether the memory gets to stay. Witnesses disagree on exact coat, scale, and temper; they agree the presence is non-human and local enough to matter when packing for the next stretch.

Locals do not file **Summer Birds** as an Earth species. They file it as a neighbor-shape: useful for warning children, timing a departure, or explaining why a yard went quiet. Stories disagree on diet and temper; they agree you should not pretend the echo is nothing.
