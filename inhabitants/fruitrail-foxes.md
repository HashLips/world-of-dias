# Fruitrail Foxes

## Overview

Fruitrail Foxes are slim russet foxes that shadow fruit caravans along the Eastbound Fruit Road, trading caution for spilled crates and discarded peels.

## Description

Lean bodies, white chin marks, and a scent faintly of citrus rind. They move at dusk and dawn, parallel to carts rather than crossing under wheels. Some pairs learn driver schedules and wait at known rest berms.

## Beliefs About Them

Drivers leave a deliberate peel offering to keep foxes from climbing loaded crates. Folklore says a Fruitrail Fox that stares at a cart has smelled rot the crew has not yet found.

## Narrative Role

A lively, slightly mischievous animal beat for fruit-road travel scenes.
