# Trueface Owls

## Overview

Trueface Owls are pale owls of F610 whose stare coincides with uncomfortable honesty in nearby conversation—correlation disputed, culturally respected.

## Description

White-gray, almost geometric facial disks. Day-roosting in fold shadows. Calls sound like a short inhaled question.

## Beliefs About Them

Tied to True Faces lore. Making vows under owl stare is considered binding in some circles, theatrical in others.

## Narrative Role

Supports revelation pacing with an animal witness.
