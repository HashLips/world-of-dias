# Passage Round Echo-Moths

## Overview

Passage Round Echo-Moths are small moths said to fly the Round's loops in patterns that make lost walkers feel accompanied by their own earlier mistakes.

## Description

Dusty wings, soft glow tips in some tellings. May be ordinary moths plus Round psychology.

## Beliefs About Them

Way-Dog handlers ignore moth lore; lost poets do not.

## Narrative Role

Small uncanny for maze-route mood.
