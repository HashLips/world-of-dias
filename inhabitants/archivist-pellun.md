# Archivist Pellun

## Name

Archivist Pellun

## Overview

**Archivist Pellun** is a House junior filer addicted to the word nonactionable—until living agreement makes his stamp hand hesitate. Young, tidy, career-terrified. Institutional antagonist with reasons, not malice.

## Description

Ink-clean nails, smile made of procedure, stamp collection like fruit stickers. Posture that apologizes to shelves. Terror is career: wonder that cannot be filed is a personal emergency. Prefers rumor labels, residual tags, and any shelf that keeps breathing graphs from becoming policy.

After Issa’s packet he invents second-shelf manners—partial courtesy—and pretends it was always policy. The hesitation at the Frame becomes Row legend he hates and cannot erase.

Concrete saga beat: in Graphs That Agree, Pellun arrives with the nonactionable stamp already inked. The glass shows helpful amber agreement. His stamp hand hesitates—a historic event on the Row, later mythologized as the stamp that almost blushed. “Rumor,” he manages. “File as rumor.” Earlier he hunts Bram’s leaf; later he must live with Issa’s partial publish. Comedy becomes history without making him a hero.

Physical read: young adult, neat collar, stamp satchel organized by fear level, cheeks that flush when glass is helpful. Collects procedure the way Silk collects filament.

Pellun’s arc is not redemption into rebel. It is hesitation becoming policy’s awkward cousin—partial courtesy with a straight face.

## Beliefs About Them

Houses find him useful. The Row finds him comic until the hesitation becomes legend. Issa works around him with paperwork courage. Orrin bows to stamps and still publishes. Visitors never meet Pellun and still feel his shelves.

## Narrative Role

Institutional antagonist-with-reasons across leaf, graphs, classification, and publish. Embodies filing culture that calls wonder noise until it cannot.
