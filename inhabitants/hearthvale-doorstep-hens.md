# Hearthvale Doorstep-Hens

## Overview

Hearthvale Doorstep-Hens are tough little hens kept on exile doorsteps—egg gift, scrap patrol, and proof a second beginning includes breakfast.

## Description

Ash-smudged feathers, bold into kitchens, respectful of Ember-Cats only after negotiation. Eggs go into laughter-week pots. They roost on warm stone and wake freebands without apology.

## Beliefs About Them

Receiving a Doorstep-Hen as welcome gift means the town expects you to stay at least one winter.

## Narrative Role

Barnyard belonging for Sorel’s softer face.
