# Tubehold Rust-Rats

## Overview

Tubehold Rust-Rats are copper-tinged rats that thrive in Tubehold's pipe runs, grease seams, and warm machine undercrofts.

## Description

Larger than house rats, with fur that picks up metallic dust until it reads as rust-orange in lamp light. They nest in soft insulation and chew anything faintly sweet or oily. Not venomous; disease worry is the ordinary urban kind.

## Beliefs About Them

Workers curse them as thieves of sleep and wiring. Some tube-kids claim Rust-Rats learn whistle codes and flee before inspections.

## Narrative Role

Gives Tubehold a less romantic ecology—necessary grit beside Nauw's orchard beauty.
