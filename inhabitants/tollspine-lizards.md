# Tollspine Lizards

## Overview

Tollspine Lizards are spiny gray-green lizards that bask on Span Toll stones and startle easily enough to drop grit into open coin trays.

## Description

Hand-length bodies, ridges along the spine that catch sun like dull metal, and quick sideways runs. They eat insects drawn to cart grease and fruit juice. Harmless bites; the real trouble is sudden movement near ledgers and toll pans.

## Beliefs About Them

Toll keepers call them 'the unpaid tax.' Children try to count spines for luck before crossing.

## Narrative Role

Adds texture and small comedy to bridge-crossing logistics.
