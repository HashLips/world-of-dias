# Salt-Ledger Brine-Eels

## Overview

Salt-Ledger Brine-Eels are medium eels kept or invading brine systems, slippery enough to steal tools and bite careless hands.

## Description

Forearm to leg length, silver-olive. Painful bite, not usually deadly. Thrive in harsh salt.

## Beliefs About Them

Workers say eels audit gloves.

## Narrative Role

Medium work-hazard fauna.
