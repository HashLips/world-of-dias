# Toma Reed

## Name

Toma Reed

## Overview

Toma Reed poles The Reedpol on the river bend the fruit road will not straighten. She moves side-loads and has no interest in being a symbol of escape.

## Description

Shoulders from the pole, hair tied with a reed-stained cord, a laugh she uses on the current more than on people. Her hands are permanently slightly wrinkled from water. She sings under her breath, not a spell, a way to keep count of strokes. She knows the landing stumps and the temper of a crate of Softfruit in a tippy hull.

## Beliefs About Them

Wagon drivers are grateful and a little superior. She lets them be superior as long as they load center. She thinks planes are loud gifts people give themselves.

## Narrative Role

Keeps a real boat in the logistics of the saga. Fruit still crosses water while the secret crosses air.

## Notes

Not a ferryman of bands. A poler of a bend.
