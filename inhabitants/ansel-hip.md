# Ansel Hip

## Name

Ansel Hip

## Overview

Ansel Hip built and drives The Hipspoon, the split-spoon ground craft. He delivers sealed cards and enjoys being looked at almost as much as he enjoys arriving.

## Description

Young, grease on one cheek as a kind of signature, goggles he does not need at the speeds he reaches. Long hands. A grin that asks the outer ring to admit the machine is funny and then to get out of the lane. He is a third-ring tinkerer who sleeps above a second-ring bakery and works the ring-edge on purpose. Straight carts bore him. Straight answers about frequencies bore him more. He has none.

## Beliefs About Them

Bram thinks he will eventually hit the bell board. Nia thinks the wheels owe toll patience. Ansel thinks both are correct and still revs the mutter of the motor as he leaves.

## Narrative Role

Brings the oddly shaped self-propelled craft through the night before the show, so the city's machines are not only wings and a train.

## Notes

The craft is his and the shape is a choice. He is not an Echoform and not a Drifter.
