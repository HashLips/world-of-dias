# Gatewarden Rams

## Overview

Gatewarden Rams are heavy-horned rams traditionally kept near Deb Gate as living symbols of threshold firmness and, more practically, as clearers of stubborn cart jams.

## Description

Broad shoulders, chalk-and-soot fleeces, horns curled like gate hinges. They tolerate crowds but dislike sudden shouting. Handlers use them to lean against stuck wheels and to stand as living 'closed' markers when a lane must pause.

## Beliefs About Them

Touching a Gatewarden Ram's horn before a hard negotiation is a Veloria habit of uncertain origin and stubborn persistence.

## Narrative Role

Physical, slightly ceremonial animal energy at one of Nauw's key thresholds.
