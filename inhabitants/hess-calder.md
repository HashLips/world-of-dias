# Hess Calder

## Name

Hess Calder

## Overview

Hess Calder buys one Vitrin at a price he had previously used for talk about a house. He wants the wonder. He is afraid to put it in his mouth.

## Description

A basin trader with good teeth and a restless knee. He dresses one ring finer than his last year's ledgers. He laughs easily until the price is his. At the show his hat leaves his head in an unseen hand and comes back crooked, and the laugh he gives is the brave kind that has a crack in it. He carries his fruit home in a box lined like a relic and then does not open the box for meals.

## Beliefs About Them

Friends say he owns the only honest reaction in the shed: desire and refusal in the same body. Rivals say he wasted a fortune on a lamp he will not light. He sits in The Peel and Bell later and does not order Hullwater, because he does not want his fear arriving early at the next table.

## Narrative Role

Shows the astronomical buy as a human flinch, not only as a number. His uneaten fruit is part of what stays open.

## Notes

Not a member of a newly named house. Calder is his family name, not a faction entry.
