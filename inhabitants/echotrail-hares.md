# Echotrail Hares

## Overview

Echotrail Hares are pale hares of F120 whose paths seem to double: a traveler may see the same hare ahead and beside, half a breath out of sync.

## Description

Soft gray-white coats, eyes like cooled mercury. They pause at path forks as if waiting for a previous self to catch up. Harmless; startling to newcomers.

## Beliefs About Them

Refrain Walkers treat doubled hares as confirmation they are on a recurrence-friendly lane.

## Narrative Role

Teaches F120's near-return law through a small animal image.
