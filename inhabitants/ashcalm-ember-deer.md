# Ashcalm Ember-Deer

## Overview

Ashcalm Ember-Deer are soft-brown deer with ember-orange ear linings that graze Ashcalm Rise at first light and rarely bolt unless chased.

## Description

Graceful, medium size, coats that hold morning warmth visually. They move in small family groups and drink from shallow stone catchments. Approachable at distance; not tame.

## Beliefs About Them

Seeing Ember-Deer at dawn is taken as the Rise 'accepting' the day's walk. Feeding them by hand is discouraged as it spoils their caution toward worse strangers.

## Narrative Role

A beautiful, calming animal image for Wabet's restorative landscapes.
