# Rim-Rest Fireflies

## Overview

Rim-Rest Fireflies give soft green-gold pulses along rim paths at night, enough to mark footing without true lanterns.

## Description

Beetle-fireflies, thumbnail sized. Pulse slow and calm. Children catch and release them in cupped hands.

## Beliefs About Them

Extinguishing a jared swarm for fun is scolded hard at Rim Rest.

## Narrative Role

Happy small light for Wabet nights.
