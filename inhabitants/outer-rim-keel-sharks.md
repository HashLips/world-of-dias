# Outer Rim Keel-Sharks

## Overview

Outer Rim Keel-Sharks are large sharks that scrape boat keels in hungry curiosity and sometimes bite what scrapes back.

## Description

Body longer than a skiff, slate backs, pale belly. Day and dusk hunters near shipping lanes. Not mythical—logged in catch and loss records.

## Beliefs About Them

Singing crews change songs when shark fins stitch the fog line.

## Narrative Role

Large clear ocean danger beside whales and eels.
