# Elder Shelf Cliff-Goats

## Overview

Elder Shelf Cliff-Goats are sure-footed goats that graze the high shelves and lookouts above Calareth's older stone paths.

## Description

Shaggy salt-bleached coats, amber eyes, horns curved like shelf brackets. They pick paths humans should not. Occasional rockfall follows their jumps; mostly they are calm observers of hikers.

## Beliefs About Them

Watch runners say Cliff-Goats 'approve safe lines' by using them first. Folklore only—until the day a goat refuses a ledge that later shears.

## Narrative Role

Vertical fauna for Elder Shelf's height and patience.
