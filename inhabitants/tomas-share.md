# Tomas Share

## Name

Tomas Share

## Overview

Tomas Share is a fruit-road convoy steward who treats Open Hand Way as classroom and courtroom: bruising schedules, sharing-knife etiquette, and disputes cut before they bleed.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Sun-darkened, list-minded, patient until reciprocity breaks. Tomas will strand a perfect fruit rather than let it split a convoy’s future welcome.

## Beliefs About Them

Lucky Farmers argue Tomas is slow. Basin buyers argue Tomas is expensive. Waystation children argue Tomas is fair.

Trade-road saga lead who can carry ethics without sermons.
