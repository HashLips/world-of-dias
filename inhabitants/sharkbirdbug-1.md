# Sharkbirdbug

## Name

Sharkbirdbug

## Overview

**Sharkbirdbug** is a Nauw Outer Rim Seas non-human presence: bright blue and spindly, bearing a large black-spotted orange-red mantle, an elongated toothed beak, and a habit of standing on elevated gray platforms under pale horizon discs.

## Description

Tide talk describes long thin limbs, hands often on the platform edge, small blue wing nubs behind the spotted shell, and one white eye angled down. The open beak shows small sharp white teeth—warning enough without a chase story. The mantle's irregular black spots are the recognition pattern children copy in chalk. Local care forbids climbing the platform to tug the shell, and forbids filing the presence as three Earth animals glued together. Resonance is blue body under orange-red spotted cover; memory is the long platform shadow; wonder is beige sky and navy depth around the elevation. Depict non-human only. Soft talk that turns the name into a joke mashup misses the seas rule: Sharkbirdbug is one neighbor-shape with its own platform manners.
