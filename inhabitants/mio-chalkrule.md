# Mio Chalkrule

## Name

Mio Chalkrule

## Overview

**Mio Chalkrule** makes folding chalk rules with crooked ticks that still measure true. Orrin’s favorite prop for the Spectrum Frame build comes from Mio’s bench. Craft honesty matters more to Mio than House-perfect lines.

## Description

Quiet worker, sawdust in the hair, fingertips permanently pale with chalk. Shoulders slightly uneven from years at a low bench. Speaks little; prices are fair; delivery is on time or apologized for with a spare rule stub. Believes a tool should show its maker’s hand—crooked ticks are not defects if they agree with the world.

Sells to Row alcoves, school kits, and the occasional House junior who wants “character” without admitting it. Keeps Measure-Mites as an accepted cost of wood grooves; rinses rules for buyers who panic, refuses to sand away the maker’s marks.

Concrete saga beat: in Building the Spectrum Frame, Mio delivers the folding rule Orrin treats as law—affectionate imprecision that still measures true. Mio returns once during the build to check etched marks, nods once, and leaves without praise. On the Row, that silence means acceptable. Tam tries to thank Mio anyway and gets a spare stub for the attempt.

Physical read: apron with chalk-pocket geometry, hair cut with the same shears used for cord, a quiet limp on cold mornings from bench years. Workshop smells of wax, wood, and the tea Mio forgets until it is chalk-temperature.

Mio will not attend Frame triumphs. Delivery and a second check are the whole relationship. That distance is respect on the Row.

## Beliefs About Them

Craftspeople trust Mio’s crooked honesty over House-perfect steel. Apprentices collect stub ends like charms. Noro Halfmeasure calls the ticks unfinished character and still borrows a Mio rule when pride allows. Orrin bows to the rule before he bows to the Frame.

## Narrative Role

Artisan bridge in Movement Two’s Frame construction. Makes spectrum science tactile: wood, chalk, and a maker who will not flatter glass with false perfection.
