# Nauw Basin Oxen

## Overview

Nauw Basin Oxen are heavy farm oxen of the Velorian Basin, the unglamorous engines of orchard rows and wagon turns.

## Description

Broad, slow, cream-and-earth coats. Horn tips often capped. They outlast fashion and most owners' knees.

## Beliefs About Them

Blessing an ox before first plow is older than most Veloria coin jokes.

## Narrative Role

Solid ordinary medium life—world densification.
