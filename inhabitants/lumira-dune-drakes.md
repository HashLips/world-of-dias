# Lumira Dune-Drakes

## Overview

Lumira Dune-Drakes are large sand reptiles—drake in folk speech, monitor-like in build—that ambush from dune slip faces.

## Description

Horse-length bodies, glass-dust scales, jaws that crush bone. Prefer silence and stillness in prey. Rare near Quiet Well's busiest hours; deadly on empty noon roads. Slip-face sand often looks ordinary until it is not.

## Beliefs About Them

Linked in fear-talk to Quietwell Serpents as 'the land's longer rope.' Same braid as [The Pale Rope of Lumira](../myths/the-pale-rope-of-lumira.md): serpent as thin end, drake when the rope thickens.

## Avoidance Customs

Avoid solo noon crossings on empty slip faces. Keep caravan noise low and spacing honest. Treat Listening Heat as a travel condition, not a mood.

## Narrative Role

Large desert terror for Wabet south. Classed as **hazard** in [Creatures of Dias](../world/creatures-of-dias.md).
