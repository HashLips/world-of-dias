# Outer Rim Deep-Keels

## Overview

Outer Rim Deep-Keels are colossal undersea beings whose backs are mistaken for moving reefs or false islands until they submerge.

## Description

Length beyond whale scale in sailor talk. Skin like wet basalt. Surfacing rare; wake can turn fleets. Relationship to Human-Eyed Whales unknown and argued.

## Beliefs About Them

Charts mark 'do not anchor on song-reefs after dark.' Some Deep-Keels may be geology; some geology may be sleeping.

## Narrative Role

Largest sea fear/wonder for Outer Rim.
