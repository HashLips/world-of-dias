# Ducklings

## Name

Ducklings

## Overview

**Ducklings** are Aelwyn Canopy non-human small flock-neighbors: yellow rounded avians that fill mint-wet paths in rhythmic alternating tilts, teaching keepers to mark a blue way without herding the many into a trap.

## Description

Witnesses see them as flat graphic yellow bodies with orange pointed beaks and simple white-black eyes, denser after rain or canopy drip. They alternate facing so the flock feels like swimming air or shallow path-water. A single blue calligraphic path-mark in plates is human (or keeper) craft laid over them—allowed if gaps remain. Locals do not file Earth duckling natural history. They file neighbor-many useful for warning children not to stomp, timing a quiet crossing, and testing whether a path still feels honest. Temper is generally mild; panic comes when the blue mark becomes a fence. Bad craft is netting the flock for market spectacle.

## Notes

Non-human small flock. Guide with gaps; do not cage.
