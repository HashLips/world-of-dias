# Ludo Parn

## Name

Ludo Parn

## Overview

Ludo Parn is the auction voice Dain hired for the shed. He can fill a room with a number. He cannot fill the space where a man was standing once the man has eaten glass.

## Description

Outer-ring lungs, a coat chosen to look second-ring, a stick he taps for attention and then forgets. His patter is fruit-market patter: bruise, shine, scarcity, hurry. He has sold bad melons and good ones and knows the difference in his sleep. Vitrin frightens him because it looks like an object from a story and behaves like a meal. When hats lift with no hands shown, his stick keeps tapping because stopping would mean he had left the job.

## Beliefs About Them

Buyers say he inflated the price. He says the room inflated the price and he only repeated it until his mouth hurt. The repetition is his skill and his blame.

## Narrative Role

Runs the auction of eight. Keeps the scene a market scene, not a coronation.

## Notes

He does not eat the fruit. He goes home able to be seen, which he does not boast about in the bar, and then boasts about anyway.
