# Nauw Ring-Ox Draughts

## Overview

Nauw Ring-Ox Draughts are oversized draught oxen used for the heaviest Veloria ring-hauls—stone, timber, festival stages.

## Description

Shoulder taller than most adults, horns wide as doorway arches (often capped). Slow, immensely strong, calm until startled by fireworks.

## Beliefs About Them

Children are lifted for photos; handlers hate fireworks season.

## Narrative Role

Large everyday power in the city.
