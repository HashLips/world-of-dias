# Drift-Phrase Birds

## Overview

Drift-Phrase Birds are rumored songbirds whose calls resemble half-remembered greetings from other days on the same road.

## Description

Small, brown, hard to catch. Witnesses argue whether the likeness is real or homesick pattern-seeking.

## Beliefs About Them

Travelers warn not to answer the birds as if they were people.

## Narrative Role

Gentle uncanny for F120 road folklore.
