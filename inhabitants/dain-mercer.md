# Dain Mercer

## Name

Dain Mercer

## Overview

Dain Mercer is a successful Veloria businessman who distributes fruit into Nauw markets. He hired a known pilot, hid a new breed in the public haul, and ate the proof in front of a crowd. The unseen that followed did not finish leaving him.

## Description

Forties, tall, second-ring house, third-ring contracts. A face ironed by money into something people call handsome when the light is on his side. Clean shoes that insult a shed floor and are meant to. He can be generous in a room: rounds, tickets, a laugh that invites smaller men to feel included. The generosity is real and it is also a tool. He does not like a count he did not set.

After the Vitrin bite he is a gap that still displaces air, then a worse gap: voice arriving a step aside from where the coat is, hands that fail a grip, flour that will not settle on him, a mirror that will not return a face he can use. He remains a person. He does not become a lesson that walks away cured. Whether sight ever fully comes back is unknown.

## Beliefs About Them

Before the show, market people called him a man who could move Wabet into Veloria without bruising the price. After, they call him the man the fruit kept. Clerks call him a debtor they cannot see to invoice. Sella Orrow calls him a taker. She is not interested in his legend.

## Narrative Role

The businessman of The Vitrin Saga. He stages the glow and then tries to put the same fruit into the farmer's stew. The backfire is his: worse invisibility, caught by a cuff, while she stays visible.

## Notes

Not redeemed by a speech. Not erased. The astronomical bids he set in motion do not settle cleanly, because a hand the room cannot find cannot sign.
