# F500 Horizon-Nulls

## Overview

F500 Horizon-Nulls are colossal non-shapes that replace horizon itself—less creature than event wearing appetite.

## Description

When 'seen,' distance dies. Reports collapse into silence. Canon keeps them rumor-adjacent on purpose. Witness accounts disagree on size because size needs a horizon to measure against.

## Beliefs About Them

Taverns that speak of them too often go quiet for other reasons. F432 custom is preserved in [The Name That Thins the Room](../myths/the-name-that-thins-the-room.md): warn once, argue twice, leave before a third casual naming.

## Avoidance Customs

Do not chase horizon anomalies on Bleed-Sky nights. Do not treat Null talk as sport after drink. Channel clerks prefer colder terms—“null pressure,” “absence event”—and call street names undisciplined. Both habits serve the same wound: keep fear from becoming a summoning game.

## Narrative Role

Largest F500 terror—use sparingly. Classed as **existential** in [Creatures of Dias](../world/creatures-of-dias.md).

## Notes

Over-explaining form is a canon error. Prefer aftermath and social discipline over anatomy.
