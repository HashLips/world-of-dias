# The Distant Companies

## Overview

**The Distant Companies** are rumored organizations beyond ordinary F432 sight—named in ledger gossip and myth more than in street addresses. Canon keeps them partial on purpose.

## Description

No agreed roster, charter, or embassy. Mentions cluster around harmonic index fragments, cross-band trade talk, and fear that someone elsewhere already filed what F432 still argues. They may be many companies, one metaphor, or a useful scare.

## Beliefs About Them

Clerks use the name to explain gaps. Sailors use it to name weather that feels intentional. Houses dislike uncontrolled Distant Company talk almost as much as Null sport—different hazard, same dislike of unowned narrative.
