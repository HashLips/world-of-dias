# Slim Camel

## Name

Slim Camel

## Overview

**Slim Camel** is an Averra Isle non-human presence: a vibrant sky-blue ultra-slender four-legger with a long tapering neck, tiny black eye, flame-leaf tail, often standing on a white mound with a mint teardrop shape behind the belly under peach sky.

## Description

Isle talk stresses the deep arc of the thin torso and the needle legs that still hold a walking grace. The black accent on neck and tail-base is part of recognition, not a wound. The mint teardrop behind the belly appears in careful plates as accompanying mark or plant-shadow of the hour. Local care forbids loading packs on the slim arc for jokes, and forbids filing the presence as an Earth camel. Resonance is cyan on peach and white; memory is the tiny eye-dot; wonder is weightless elegance. Depict non-human only. Soft talk that treats the name as a thin insult misses Averra's rule: slim is the body's honesty, and you walk soft near it.
