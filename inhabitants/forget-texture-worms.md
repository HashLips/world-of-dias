# Forget-Texture Worms

## Overview

Forget-Texture Worms are said to crawl through F500's anti-scenery, leaving trails where memory of surface detail thins.

## Description

Described as matte, unreflective, hard to count segments of. Touch allegedly makes a person forget the feel of cloth or stone for a while.

## Beliefs About Them

May be metaphor wearing worm-shape. Kept in the archive as rumor fauna because the metaphor keeps growing teeth in retellings.

## Narrative Role

Psychological dread creature for null-band stories.
