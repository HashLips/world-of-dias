# Rose-Thorn Wolves

## Overview

Rose-Thorn Wolves are deep-forest wolves of Wabet whose fur bristles into thornlike tips when threatened, making close contact dangerous even beyond the bite.

## Description

Larger than ordinary wolves, coats wine-dark with rose-ash tips. Packs hunt dusk edges of Verdant Reach and deeper canopy margins. They avoid dense human trails but punish careless camping and loud night travel.

## Beliefs About Them

Wabet guides mark thorn-scratch trees as warnings. Some myths braid them to rose-gift stories as guardians of sincerity; practical woodsfolk call that poetry after the fact.

## Narrative Role

Wabet's serious danger face—beautiful land still has things you should not meet alone.
