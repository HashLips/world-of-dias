# The Blue Litter Husk

## Name

The Blue Litter Husk

## Overview

The **Blue Litter Husk** is a named **Echoform** of Wabet's deep groves: a semi-living silhouette sustained by pooled blue-litter resonance where listeners have fallen mid-read for generations.

## Description

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Frequency | F432 (Wabet canopy bands) |
| Origin | Accumulated listener overload on Blue Litter Underwood |
| Location | Deep interior groves away from harvest roads |
| Material affinity | Soft blue litter; skeletal wood; dry air |
| Resonance type | Exhausted attention / unfinished reads |
| Recorded echo | Prone posture; urge to finish a sentence that never arrives |
| Behavior | Rarely pursues; appears near those who force a listen too long; dissolves when Open Hand leave-behind custom is kept |
| Rarity | Uncommon; denied by some canopy stewards as "just fatigue with a story" |
| Abilities | Induces heaviness in the limbs; muddies echo-reading toward the grove's own backlog |

Witnesses describe a featureless dark outline on blue ground—sometimes rising to a kneel, never speaking. It carries history as **failed listening**, not predation.

## Beliefs About Them

Open Hand ethics treat the Husk as a warning to leave the litter undisturbed. Extraction crews who bottle blue litter claim the Husk follows the bottle until the glass clears and the headache remains. Echoform scholars accept it as a clean class example: born from place + repeated human event, not from unrelated fantasy.

## Narrative Role

Pillar exemplar for Echoforms: life (or half-life) that exists because resonance accumulated—and because people kept insisting on hearing past their limit.
