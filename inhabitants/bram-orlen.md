# Bram Orlen

## Name

Bram Orlen

## Overview

Bram Orlen is a Verdant Reach apple farmer who turns **Frederick Lumens** away after seeing **Roadglow Shard**, later receives **Fellgrove** under a harvest covenant, and—after Frederick vanishes following a return to **Vorrith Kael**—is left planting without Apple Touch.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Thick-wristed, sun-browned, careful with sharing-knife manners. Years of rivalry, failure, and unexpected mercy define his lane. He kept planting when Frederick kept the harvest promise. He still plants when the promise stops arriving.

## Beliefs About Them

Lanes call him the man who refused the hollow boy and later owned the boy’s bright ground. After the disappearance, some pity him; some envy the years the gift lasted.

## Narrative Role

Refusal; rival; covenant partner; living witness that Frederick’s word held until Stillhollow—and then ended without explanation.
