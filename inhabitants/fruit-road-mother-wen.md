# Fruit-Road Mother Wen

## Overview

**Fruit-Road Mother Wen** is remembered as the steward who braided waystation restore into law—share-knife ethics before Lucky Fruit could split welcome.

## Description

Wagon dust; bruise-timing songs. Wen’s knife is gone; every sharing-knife claims a splinter of the story.

## Beliefs About Them

Convoys toast Wen at Open Hand Way. Rank-blind mockers get Wen’s name as insult (“no Wen in you”).

## Narrative Role

Builder/joy—abundance without raid.

## Notes

Living Tomas Share continues the craft; Wen is the root toast.
