# Verdant Reach Reed-Stags

## Overview

Verdant Reach Reed-Stags are medium stags that wade reed beds at dawn, antlers tangled with living green for a season.

## Description

Shoulder-high to a tall teen, coats moss-brown. Antler gardens are plant hitchhikers, not magic growth. Shy of loud groups.

## Beliefs About Them

Seeing a reed-crowned stag is a marriage-proposal omen in some Wabet jokes and a few sincere hearts.

## Narrative Role

Romantic medium fauna for Verdant Reach.
