# Sella Orrow

## Name

Sella Orrow

## Overview

Sella Orrow is the Wabet farmer who grew Vitrin by mixing certain fruits and would not sell the method. She stays in the story as a visible, angry person. The fruit feathers her edges. It does not erase her.

## Description

Fifties, strong forearms, gray hair in a knot that ignores weather. Apron on a nail when it is not on her. Hands stained with ordinary peel, not with glass. She speaks short. She hoes long. Her face is lined by sun and by the particular attention of someone who has been watching a tree she does not intend to explain. After the stew, the rim of one ear is missing from sight, the hem-shadow of the apron thins as if the cloth ended early, and the outline of her left hand fades when the lamp is low. The rest of her is plainly there: eyes, mouth, stride, temper.

She set the share-pot for restore, not for theater. She throws out a pot she will not serve. She does not beg a pilot. She looks, which is heavier.

## Beliefs About Them

Yarrow Bine thinks she used his lend as cover and is not sure he is wrong. Neighbors thought the glass tree was a failed sweetening. After the board, strangers think she is a witch of fruit. She is a farmer who mixed something and got a result she does not fully own. The gap between those sentences is the open part.

## Narrative Role

The grower. She catches Dain because cloth still smells like her stew and a cuff is still a cuff. She is not the victim who vanishes so the city can feel clever.

## Notes

Do not leave her erased. Do not publish her recipe. Edges may stay faded. That is a mark, not a deletion.
