# Open-Hand Mule-Deer

## Overview

Open-Hand Mule-Deer are mule-sized deer that graze Open Hand Way margins and sometimes accept vegetable peels from makers.

## Description

Long ears, clay-tan coats, not fully wild and not tame. Startle into graceful flight. Antlers modest.

## Beliefs About Them

Feeding them grain meant for goats starts arguments. Peels are the polite offering.

## Narrative Role

Medium in-between animal for craft-road liminality.
