# Banner Moths

## Overview

Banner Moths are large daylight moths whose wing patterns resemble market pennants, often seen above Veloria plazas during fair weeks.

## Description

Wingspans reach a human forearm. Colors run citrus, cobalt, and chalk white in blocky 'banner' shapes rather than soft gradients—as if someone cut market cloth into flight. They fly slowly in warm air, clustering above booths and ring-fair ropes where heat shimmers. Shadow-patches slide over cobbles like moving stalls. At night they settle on laundry lines and shop awnings like folded cloth; morning shakes send soft dust of scale-glitter that children call fair-snow.

## Beliefs About Them

Vendors claim Banner Moths bring buyers. A crushed Banner Moth is considered bad shopkeeping, not bad luck alone. Sweepers are told to lift moths with a soft brush, never a boot.

## Cultural Touchstone

Veloria’s cheerful wonder: proof F432 can be loud with color without needing a monster. Fair-week care of Banner Moths is a small civic test—joy that still asks for gentleness.

## Narrative Role

Useful for scenes of Veloria's public joy and for showing that F432 wonder can be cheerful and ordinary at once. Classed as **wonder** in [Creatures of Dias](../world/creatures-of-dias.md).
