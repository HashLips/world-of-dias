# Sketchline Foxes

## Overview

Sketchline Foxes of F960 show faint construction lines in their fur, as if still deciding their final coat.

## Description

Graphite-and-chalk coloring, edges that soften and resharpen. They trot through The Unfinished like living drafts. Bites are rare; startling them can make nearby edges briefly wobble.

## Beliefs About Them

Draftkin call them neighbors. Visiting artists try to sketch them and end up sketching three versions.

## Narrative Role

Signature F960 animal—becoming made cute and strange.
