# Dimming Leeches

## Overview

Dimming Leeches are rare parasitic light-forms said to cling to bright beings and temporarily dull local luminosity—an F200 horror story with enough sightings to stay uneasy.

## Description

Described as dark-gold ribbons with a hungry matte core. Contact leaves a cold afterimage. Aurel-kind avoid certain fold-planes where dimming is reported.

## Beliefs About Them

Linked in gossip to F500 bleed; unproven. Still, travelers carry 'coherence charms' of disputed value.

## Narrative Role

The dangerous exception that proves F200 is not only serene.
