# Songripe Hum-Ticks

## Overview

Songripe Hum-Ticks are pinhead ticks that hitch on Tide-Birds and isle rope, vibrating faintly when nearby singing is strong.

## Description

Nearly invisible; felt as a soft buzz against skin more than seen. Remove with oil and patience. Rare fever if ignored for days.

## Beliefs About Them

Isle joke: the ticks keep time better than mainland clocks.

## Narrative Role

Micro-companion to Songripe's song culture—slightly creepy, mostly comic.
