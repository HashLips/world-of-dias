# Orr Claimprice

## Overview

**Orr Claimprice** mediates Claimscar↔Secondfire fights by putting prices on pride—so yards don’t burn over paint that could have been coin, wire, or a delayed claim.

## Description

Scarred knuckles that rarely make fists, a slate of “fight costs,” kettle-side seating. Orr will stop a double-paint spiral with an ugly compromise neither Free-Captain loves. Mutual Table bowls are fine; Mutual Table capturing crew loyalty is not—Orr prices that too.

## Beliefs About Them

Garen Scar tolerates Orr as useful grease. Hotheads call Orr Concord-soft. People who still have roofs disagree.

## Narrative Role

Antagonist-with-reasons + bridge—peace as invoice.
