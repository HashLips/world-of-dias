# Keth Dunewalk

## Name

Keth Dunewalk

## Overview

Keth Dunewalk apprentices under Iskar Lüm, maintaining the craft of wrong maps and true paths that keep Quiet Well alive.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human** (training under an Axiomorph keeper).

Keth walks Saltwhisper Road with markers that mean different things to Keepers and to strangers. Speaking loudly near the well is treated as a technical error with moral weight.

## Beliefs About Them

Caravan bosses call Keth obstruction. Oasis families call Keth a lid on greed.

## Narrative Role

Desert saga protagonist or deuteragonist: a map that must stay wrong to keep water.
