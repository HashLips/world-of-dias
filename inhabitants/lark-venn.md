# Lark Venn

## Name

Lark Venn

## Overview

Lark Venn is the freight pilot who flies The Late Bruise for Dain Mercer. She is known on the fruit side of Nauw because she can put a plane onto a lent field without treating the field as conquered, and because she still speaks to wagon people as if they haul the country, which they do.

## Description

Early thirties, outer-ring born, third-ring licensed. A wool coat over a flight suit that looks wrong in both rings and is therefore honest. Scar through the left thumb from a crate nail. Hair cut so a helmet does not argue. She has the ground-crew habit of touching a hull before she trusts it. Her voice is low and spends sentences the way fuel is spent, with a glance at what is left.

She pays Yarrow Bine in marks and in an hour of stake-work when the grass is bad. She will not drink Ox-Patience on a lift day. She knows Sky Drifters well enough to tell a child the difference between a drift and a descent. She does not know a way out of F432 and does not sell one.

## Beliefs About Them

Road crews say she is the rare hire who does not sneer at oxen. Third-ring desks say she is late and careful, which they count as one vice. After the bar board, both rooms say she chose a room over a client. Some add that the choice still rutted a field. That addition is fair.

## Narrative Role

Pilot of The Vitrin Saga. She carries the secret, flies the revenge, and then refuses to fly the last fruit out as a private thing. The refusal is a chalk mark in a bar, not a sermon.

## Notes

Does not solve band passage. Her hire with Mercer ends in the open, with the plane on the ground and the orchard no longer secret.
