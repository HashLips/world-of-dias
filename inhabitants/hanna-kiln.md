# Hanna Kiln

## Name

Hanna Kiln

## Overview

Hanna Kiln runs Secondfire’s teaching fire: plates, tools, and people remade with slate names and heat that is both literal and social.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Ash on sleeves, laugh timed for after the glaze sets. Hanna accepts Nauw shame-cases and still knows how to refuse someone who only wants a costume of remaking.

## Beliefs About Them

Compact stewards trust Hanna’s fires. Refusers fear Hanna’s questions more than Hanna’s heat.

## Narrative Role

Warm Hearthvale saga center; can host the exile who refuses second beginning without becoming a sermon.
