# Hadrin Stell

## Name

Hadrin Stell

## Overview

Hadrin Stell is a human captain of the Bleed Weather Ward. He sat at the Houses' classification table by petition and argued that the blue on the Saltjaw shelf was bleed weather to be sealed and watched. He commands the Ward contingent of forty on the expedition. At the island he takes a bow from a frightened bowman and fires a grey-pennanted ward-bolt into the boom, "Hazard ward, placed," and the brief clash follows. He denies firing on any person and says he gave no order for the second bolt. He is relieved pending an inquiry whose outcome is not given.

He is frightened and ambitious. He is not a monster, and that is the point.

## Description

Broad, with a scar across his chin and a soldier's way of sitting. He walks piers as if he owns them and looks at the sea as if it has insulted him. His voice is steady even when his hands are not. He cut his own hook-lines at the end, when Severin told him to.

**Can:** command a Ward contingent; run warning nets; place hazard wards by procedure.
**Cannot:** tell bleed from quiet. The Operators' drum said F432 only, and he did not listen.
**Cost:** one of his oarsmen drowned and a young islander was killed, and he will carry the bolt nobody owns.

## Beliefs About Them

The Ward is divided: some call him a captain who did his duty, others a man who started a war on a writ. Severin says there are a great many men like him. Saltjaw rang the sea-call for his drowned oarsman, and he stood at the end of the line and did not take off his cap.

## Narrative Role

He is the commander who fires. He shows the security pull curdled by fear and ambition into a shot. War is the exception, and it comes from an ordinary frightened man following a procedure.

## Notes

The second bolt's bowman is unnamed. Hadrin's guilt for the second bolt stays disputed in the four Concord books.
