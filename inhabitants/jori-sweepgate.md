# Jori Sweepgate

## Name

Jori Sweepgate

## Overview

**Jori Sweepgate** is the gate clerk who clocks visitor wrong-tick unease after the Caliburn escapes. Steady hands, soft voice, clearance cards treated like weather instruments. Does not see a glass person. Sees visitors who almost understand, then frown at a digit.

## Description

Calm desk posture, ink that never smears, a habit of counting ask-again stamps the way others count rain. Works the visitor queue ledge where basin tours become entry manners. Files “weather” and knows it is manners for fear. Will not invent a monster to satisfy a form; will notice when forms start lying about calm.

Jori is aftermath texture with a job title. Visitors forget the name. Dott remembers the counts. Operators prefer not to ask why ask-again rates rose on Jori’s shifts.

Concrete saga beat: in Ask Again at the Ledge (optional aftermath), Jori watches visitors almost agree with the city, then stall on a wrong digit. Ask-again stamps rise without a named cause. Jori files the pattern carefully and refuses to stamp it nonactionable just to clear the line. The Caliburn is at large in load-noise; Jori’s ledge is where that fact becomes human faces.

Physical read: steady mid-adult face, soft scarf, clearance cards worn at the corners from honest use. Hands that do not shake when visitors do.

Jori’s job is not to solve the Caliburn. It is to notice when almost-agreement becomes a frown—and to keep the ledge kind while the city ticks wrong.

## Beliefs About Them

Visitors forget Jori’s name. Dott remembers Jori’s counts. Ward hands borrow Jori’s weather language. Row apprentices hear ledge stories secondhand and believe them more than House bulletins.

## Narrative Role

Haunt aftermath witness. Translates escape into visitor manners and wrong-tick unease without needing another lab climax.
