# Hearthvale Ember-Cats

## Overview

Hearthvale Ember-Cats are smoke-orange domestic cats kept near Hearthvale stoves and door-hearths, valued for vermin work and quiet company.

## Description

Medium cats with ember-tip ears and ash-white chests that look dipped in cooled forge light. They sleep in warm stone niches, kneading soot into comfortable circles, and tolerate crowded rooms better than most cats. Their purr sits under laughter-week songs like a second hearth. Mousing is excellent; dignity is optional—especially when Doorstep-Hens negotiate doorway rights. Exiles learn the cat’s preferred sill before they learn the freeband’s meeting hour.

## Beliefs About Them

A home without an Ember-Cat is said to 'leak warmth.' Exiles arriving from Nauw often receive a kitten as a soft form of belonging.

## Narrative Role

Humanizes Sorel's hard reputation with domestic animal love.

## Notes

Valhop: at Kiln Laughter Yard, Ember-Cats claim the warm stones where Valhop boards are laid, and players move the board, not the cat. See [`Kiln Yard Valhop Players`](kiln-yard-valhop-players.md).
