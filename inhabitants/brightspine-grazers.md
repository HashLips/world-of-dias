# Brightspine Grazers

## Overview

Brightspine Grazers are herd-beings that 'graze' coherence gradients—moving where luminosity is stable, leaving soft bright wakes.

## Description

Quadruped outlines made of denser light, spines glowing a shade warmer than the field. Herds travel slowly. Startling them makes the local field flicker.

## Beliefs About Them

Compared by F432 tellers to cattle; the metaphor fails but persists.

## Narrative Role

Gives F200 a pastoral ecology without soil.
