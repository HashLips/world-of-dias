# Orrin Veld

## Name

Orrin Veld

## Overview

**Orrin Veld** is a Calibration Row–adjacent scientist in Veloria who treats frequency as something you can graph, fail at, and try again. He is the protagonist of **The Calibration Saga**: he proves background bands are measurable from inside F432, invites **the Caliburn** by looking too carefully, publishes partial spectrum truth, and survives the Echoform’s escape as a haunted steward of honesty.

### Character

Not a sealed Valorian Operator and not an echo-gift twin of Calen Brink. Operator-adjacent enough to receive anonymous load scrap and to argue stamps with House juniors. Talks to Softfruit vendors and instruments with the same respect. Keeps tea in graduated cylinders “because the lines are honest.”

During Frederick Lumens’s research years he took apple-share marks for a consultation, mapped what he could, and refused any ferry fantasy: there is no safe subway between frequencies. He filed Frederick as *orchard man who wants a door*.

Arc silhouette: curious → triumphant → unknowingly complicit → responsible → haunted steward of partial truth.

## Beliefs About Them

Row apprentices call him brilliant and slightly unemployable. Houses call unpublished graphs a nuisance. Operators who notice him file his wonder as noise until they cannot. Softfruit vendors sell him questions at honest prices. Auntie Mosscoil refuses to call him “scientist” when he has not slept.

## Narrative Role

Carries the science-led third saga: method beside Harmonic gift and Lumen hunger. Proves the background spectrum; meets the Caliburn; chooses partial truth over a widened seam—even as the Echoform escapes into city noise.
