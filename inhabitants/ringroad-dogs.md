# Ringroad Dogs

## Overview

Ringroad Dogs are sturdy mixed-breed working dogs kept along Veloria's outer circuits for cart-guarding, child-finding, and night-watch companionship.

**Life kind:** **ordinary animal** (Animals of Dias—work + heart). Collar test passes; not an extraordinary creature class.

## Description

Short coats in clay, charcoal, and white blaze patterns. Broad chests, calm eyes, ears that tip only when listening for wheel-rhythm. Close up they smell like warm dust and bakery steam. They learn route smells the way clerks learn ledgers: dye vats, fruit crates, rain on stone, the particular oil of a familiar cart axle. Most wear simple collar tags stamped with a house mark or stall number. At night their nails click a soft census along the ring; children fallen asleep on crates are found by nose before by shout.

## Beliefs About Them

A Ringroad Dog that refuses a street is treated as serious advice. Stealing one is a market crime with social consequences beyond the law.

## Ecology

**Feed:** stall scraps, household bowls, cart-guard shares—not wild hunt primary.  
**Shelter:** outer-ring stalls, cart beds, night circuits.  
**Reproduce:** house-marked litters; market fosters common.  
**Interact:** Lap-Cats (sill politics); children on crates; route smells as work map.

**Life kind:** ordinary animal. Role class: working + companion. Habitat: Basin Mist Orchard / Veloria streets.

## Narrative Role

Anchors Veloria as a lived city with animals that work beside people, not only marvels at the edges.
