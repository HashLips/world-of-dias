# Quiet Well Moss-Newts

## Overview

Quiet Well Moss-Newts are small damp-skinned newts living in moss rings at Quiet Well, moving only when unobserved—or so watchers claim.

## Description

Palm-sized, olive and wet-black. Truly shy, not magical. Splash softly.

## Beliefs About Them

Silence Law practice: watch without commenting, and newts appear.

## Narrative Role

Small oasis life teaching patience.
