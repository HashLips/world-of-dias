# Hollowmere Latch-Otters

## Overview

Hollowmere Latch-Otters are playful dark-water otters known for fiddling with dock latches, boat ties, and anything that clicks shut.

## Description

Sleek chocolate coats, pale belly fur, and whiskers always wet. They whistle in short paired notes. Families near the mere leave floating toys to distract them from real gear. Rarely aggressive; boldly curious.

## Beliefs About Them

A Latch-Otter that brings a lost glove is considered a neighborly act. One that opens a storehouse is still forgiven twice.

## Narrative Role

Warm animal presence for Hollowmere's watery domestic life.
