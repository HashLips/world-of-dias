# Span-Shadow Deer

## Overview

Span-Shadow Deer graze the Returning Span with shadows that lag or lead their bodies by a step.

## Description

Ordinary deer silhouettes until the light disagrees. Shadows may drink before muzzles touch water. Not aggressive.

## Beliefs About Them

Cartographers annotate 'shadow lead' zones where deer sightings cluster.

## Narrative Role

Makes the Span's map logic visible in flesh.
