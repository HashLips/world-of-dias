# Passage Round Way-Dogs

## Overview

Passage Round Way-Dogs are trained medium dogs that walk circular route logic with travelers, keeping groups from splitting on confusing turns.

## Description

Brindle coats, bright focus, soft mouths for tugging sleeves not tearing. Work with handlers; not free strays.

## Beliefs About Them

Losing the Way-Dog's attention means you already lost the Round.

## Narrative Role

Medium helper animal for a maze-like place.
