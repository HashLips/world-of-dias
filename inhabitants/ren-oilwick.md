# Ren Oilwick

## Name

Ren Oilwick

## Overview

**Ren Oilwick** sells lamp oil and underwrites late experiments with gossip—which slits leak, which stamps hesitate. Midnight routes and lamp-black fingers keep Calibration Row lit when Houses pretend nothing is happening.

## Description

Lean night-walker, coat smelling of oil and orange peel from desk trades, fingers permanently lamp-black. Voice low; news priced into the pour. Treats gossip as a public utility: not cruelty for sale, but weather for people who work after courtesy hours.

Keeps spare wicks in a tin and knows which alcoves tip honestly. Will not sell oil for Null dare parties. Will sell oil for apology rites and Frame nights without asking for a thesis. Respects Auntie’s soup runs and times routes so the two do not collide in a doorway.

Concrete saga beat: during Building the Spectrum Frame, Ren’s midnight lamp burns slow enough for gossip while Orrin and Tam feed the lattice. Ren reports which slits leaked and which stamps hesitated that week, then leaves before dawn so the oil—not Ren—gets credit. Later Frame nights keep the same lamp manners: light first, story second, never a claim that Ren “knew” the Caliburn.

Physical read: mid-thirties, sharp cheekbones smudged with black, a limp satchel of tins that clink like soft percussion. Eyes always half on shutter gaps—oil sellers learn bleed nights early.

Ren’s gossip has rules: no names for lovers, full names for stamps that hesitated, never a story that would help Lune Static price a method. Oil first; ethics in the pour.

## Beliefs About Them

Apprentices buy oil and news together. Seniors tip for silence as often as for talk. Ward hands use Ren’s routes when shutter walks need a friendly window. Houses pretend not to hear Ren and still somehow know what Ren said.

## Narrative Role

Row night texture for Frame build and apology-adjacent craft. Keeps Movement Two’s triumph from floating free of oil smell and unpaid hours.
