# Westfold Toll Flies

## Overview

Westfold Toll Flies cluster around cart grease, fruit juice, and patient queues at Westfold Toll.

## Description

Common fly size, louder than they deserve. Prefer warm animals and open jars. No special venom.

## Beliefs About Them

Toll keepers swear flies can smell a long argument forming.

## Narrative Role

Petty insect texture for border bureaucracy.
