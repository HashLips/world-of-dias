# Stilt-Horn Stack

## Name

Stilt-Horn Stack

## Overview

**Stilt-Horn Stack** is a Driftfall non-human guest-shape: a deep-blue horned presence on long stilts that appears in feast talk whenever a pile of courses risks trapping a living neighbor in the middle.

## Description

Pier witnesses describe a compact blue body with pointed horn-ears and a sharp snout, legs so long they look like walking poles. In plates the body is often shown wedged between bright layers of a tall stack—the teaching image, not a recipe. Real encounters are usually at the edge of a table or under a pier bench, where the stilts can still find floor. Temper is curious and easily offended by being treated as food theater. Locals do not invent an Earth animal file for it. They invent manners: leave a middle gap in any show-stack, ask before stacking near a guest, and never joke that the blue one is “the meat.” Children learn the long legs as a sign the neighbor wants out more than it wants a seat inside the bun-layers. Driftfall Piers keep the warning plate near feast planning boards.

## Notes

Non-human. Display name Stilt-Horn Stack. Not a garnish.
