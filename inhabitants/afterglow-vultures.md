# Afterglow Vultures

## Overview

Afterglow Vultures are broad-winged scavengers of F840 that clean what recovery cannot keep, flying where trace heat still lifts air.

## Description

Muted plumage, pale collar feathers, patient circling. Not attackers of the living when food protocol holds. Their presence means something ended and something else can begin.

## Beliefs About Them

Ember Quiet cultures read them without melodrama: necessary, not cursed.

## Narrative Role

Honest danger-adjacent ecology—uncomfortable, not evil.
