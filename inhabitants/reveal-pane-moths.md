# Reveal-Pane Moths

## Overview

Reveal-Pane Moths land on glassfold surfaces during disclosure windows, wings becoming briefly transparent enough to show underlying structure behind them.

## Description

Small, silver-dusted. Outside windows they look ordinary. During intervals, wing panes act like tiny lenses.

## Beliefs About Them

Veil Scribes note moth landings as soft timing cues—not instruments, still useful.

## Narrative Role

Tiny clockwork wonder for reveal timing.
