# Coral Strand Tide-Shrimp

## Overview

Coral Strand Tide-Shrimp flash in tide pools by day, a silver glitter that feeds Lantern-Fish by night.

## Description

Finger-length, translucent, swarm densely in safe pools. Edible; overharvest near Hallow Bay is discouraged.

## Beliefs About Them

Children learn counting by catching and releasing shrimp.

## Narrative Role

Small marine abundance for Calareth shores.
