# Veloria Festival Sky-Whales

## Overview

Veloria Festival Sky-Whales are not wild animals but colossal floated constructs—fabric, light, and harnessed lift—paraded as living-seeming sky whales during great fairs.

## Description

Body length of streets, painted eyes, internal lamps. Crews walk gantries inside. From the ground they read as animals; up close they are civic theater.

## Beliefs About Them

Children argue they are real. Adults protect the argument.

## Narrative Role

Colossal joyful 'creature' that blurs animal and artwork—fits Dias festival life.
