# Lune Static

## Name

Lune Static

## Overview

**Lune Static** is a Null Curiosity Cell stringer posing as a research buyer for “harmless band charts.” Smile too ready, coin too clean. Not a final villain—slow-dark texture with a face. Offers markets for methods that should stay unfinished.

## Description

Neat coat, research soap over dare perfume, voice that never rises. Eyes that inventory a room’s instruments before its people. Speaks in collector kindness: private charts, no stamps, no Houses, just curiosity with coin. Kindness is the tool; unfinished methods are the prize.

Lune does not smash the Frame. Lune prices it. After the Caliburn escapes, Lune sells half-true haunt stories for full price and calls that journalism. Quiet Unbinding adjacent without naming an army.

Concrete saga beat: in Wrong by Half a Digit, Lune appears with clean coin and offers to buy Orrin’s method. Orrin refuses. The Caliburn’s slit brightens as if refusal were another kind of agreement—data, either way. In Widening as Gift, Lune’s coin reappears under the door while the Echoform tempts a wider seam. Orrin still refuses the market. He does not refuse feeling seen.

Physical read: ageless-professional smile, coat too clean for Row grit, gloves that come off only to count coin. Leaves no oil smell—only soap trying to smell like libraries.

Lune never touches the Frame without permission and somehow still feels present in its pricing. Refusal feeds Lune almost as well as sale; both are data.

## Beliefs About Them

Collectors love Lune. Ward hands hate Lune. Row apprentices warn each other with a hand sign for “too clean coin.” Orrin refuses Lune and still feels inventoried. The Caliburn treats Lune’s offers as interesting weather.

## Narrative Role

Method-theft pressure across Movements Two and Three. Keeps Null hunger present without turning the saga into a war story.

## Notes

Null Curiosity Cell texture, not a full Quiet Unbinding campaign. See Wrong by Half a Digit and Widening as Gift.
