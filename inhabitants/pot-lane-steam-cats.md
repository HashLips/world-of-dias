# Pot Lane Steam-Cats

## Overview

Pot Lane Steam-Cats are medium cats that haunt steam vents and pot warmth, hunting ember-gnats with theatrical leaps.

## Description

Slick coats from vapor, half-singed whiskers common. Friendly to regulars; rude to boots.

## Beliefs About Them
