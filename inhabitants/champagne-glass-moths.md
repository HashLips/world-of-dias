# Champagne Glass-Moths

## Overview

Champagne Glass-Moths are small moth-forms of translucent golden planes that flicker along F200's finer geometric seams.

## Description

Wing panes like thin glass filled with held light. They cluster where striation lines meet. Crushing one is easy and socially awkward among locals.

## Beliefs About Them

Children of energy-kind 'count moth panes' to learn focus.

## Narrative Role

Tiny beauty for intimate F200 scenes.
