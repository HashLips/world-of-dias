# Ilen Voss

## Name

Ilen Voss

## Overview

Ilen Voss is a third-ring clerk who bids at the Vitrin auction for a counting room that wanted a demonstration. The demonstration outlives the invoice.

## Description

Neat, pale from indoor lamps, a tally slate in a case, shoes that know floors more polished than Mercer's shed. He is not a lord. He is a person trusted with numbers large enough to represent other people's nerve. He writes the bid in a hand that does not shake until afterward. The resonant tally he uses is a tool for marks. It does not advise him. When he wants advice he looks at the fruit and gets none.

## Beliefs About Them

The counting room says he overreached. He says he bought what the room sent him to buy. Both are trying to find Dain to finish the transfer and finding a debtor who does not reliably occupy a chair.

## Narrative Role

One of the adults who feels the cost in VEL marks. Wonder becomes a bookkeeping problem without becoming a joke that cancels the debt.

## Notes

Does not eat his purchase on the floor. What the counting room does with its fruit later stays partly open. The unsettled payment does not.
