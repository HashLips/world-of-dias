# Mountain Trade Llamas

## Overview

Mountain Trade Llamas carry goods on the Mountain of Majestic Good Trade's high paths, spat-and-stubborn and commercially essential.

## Description

Woolly, long-necked, judgmental faces. Medium pack animals. Sure on stone. Spit is diplomatic speech.

## Beliefs About Them

A llama that refuses a load is believed to smell a bad deal—or a bad handler.

## Narrative Role

Medium characterful trade fauna for the mountain market mythos.
