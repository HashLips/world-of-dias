# Saltwhisper Sand-Fleas

## Overview

Saltwhisper Sand-Fleas live in warm dune crust along Saltwhisper Road and leap into boots the moment a traveler stops.

## Description

Pale, hard to see on sand, viciously itchy. Not venomous. Dawn and dusk worst.

## Beliefs About Them

Dunewalkers shake boots as prayer. Folklore claims fleas prefer liars; evidence prefers warm ankles.

## Narrative Role

Tiny desert misery that keeps travel tactile.
