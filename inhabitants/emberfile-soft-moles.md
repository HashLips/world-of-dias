# Emberfile Soft-Moles

## Overview

Emberfile Soft-Moles tunnel under workshop floors in F840, turning ash-soil and occasionally unearthing lost tools into light.

## Description

Velvety charcoal coats, pink noses, nearly blind. They dislike loud hammering directly overhead and will move nests rather than fight.

## Beliefs About Them

Workshop lore credits moles with 'returning what heat kept.' Offerings of peel and quiet are traditional jokes that still happen.

## Narrative Role

Gentle, slightly magical-feeling workshop ecology.
