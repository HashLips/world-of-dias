# Meridian Soft-Rays

## Overview

Meridian Soft-Rays are wide, manta-like radiance forms that glide the Aurel Meridian corridor in calm sheets of champagne light.

## Description

Wingspans vast by F432 measure; edges feather into glow. They do not feed on matter. Passage beneath one feels like walking under a moving window of afternoon.

## Beliefs About Them

Seen as good transit omens along the Meridian. Blocking a Soft-Ray path with crude instruments is considered uncouth.

## Narrative Role

Magnificent, peaceful spectacle for F200.
