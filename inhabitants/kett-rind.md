# Kett Rind

## Name

Kett Rind

## Overview

Kett Rind sorts fruit at the edge of Mercer Shed and talks like a person who has already had a sip of Hullwater. She saw cloth come out of a hull that had taken in fruit, and she did not improve the truth so much as she carried it.

## Description

Small, quick hands, a sorter's squint, nails dyed by market fruit. She wears a scarf even indoors because sheds are drafts with ambition. Her mouth is faster than her caution and slower than her eyes. She can tell a bruised Softfruit from a performed bruise. She could not tell what was under Dain's cloth, and she said so, which is why the bar believed her.

## Beliefs About Them

Some say she invented the hull story for a free cup. Opal Mire's silence later made that harder to enjoy. Kett wanted the room, not a war.

## Narrative Role

Starts the whisper that forces the show. She is a witness with a bar habit, not a spy.

## Notes

Stays in the outer ring. Does not get a fruit at the auction. Does get the afterwards, like everyone else.
