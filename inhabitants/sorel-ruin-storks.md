# Sorel Ruin-Storks

## Overview

Sorel Ruin-Storks are large storks that nest on salvage roofs and pick scraps from claim edges with ceremonial patience.

## Description

Tall, soot-white, red facial skin. Not predators of people. Steal fish and unattended lunches.

## Beliefs About Them

A stork on a new roof is a rebuild blessing in some yards.

## Narrative Role

Large awkward hopeful scavenger bird.
