# Perrin Cale

## Name

Perrin Cale

## Overview

Perrin Cale runs a lean waystation on **Flagweek Strip**—crates, mule swaps, and short hires for travelers moving west toward Hearthvale’s edge and the **Westfold Toll**. Frederick works under Perrin’s rates while trying to buy a proper crossing into Wabet.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Thin, precise, always counting. Perrin is not cruel for sport; he is exact about what a day’s labor is worth in a place where flags change and breakfast must not. He offers Frederick field stacking, fence repair, and wagon watch—honest work at strip prices that never quite reach a toll fee for a boy traveling alone.

He talks while they work: strip gossip, which week’s cloth, who got turned back at Westfold for short marks. He likes Frederick’s energy and still will not invent missing coin.

## Beliefs About Them

Locals treat Perrin as reliable and tight. Travelers call him fair if you already have enough, and a wall if you do not.

## Narrative Role

Humanizes the “almost” of Frederick’s road: conversation, labor, and the math that forces a quieter route by boat.

## Notes

Not a thief. Not a mentor with a secret map. A working contact who tells the truth about tolls.
