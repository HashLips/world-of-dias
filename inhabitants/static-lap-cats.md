# Static Lap-Cats

## Name

Static Lap-Cats

## Overview

**Static Lap-Cats** are lap-cats that prefer desks with residual static; their fur sometimes stands in measure-slit shapes. They are cousins to ordinary Veloria Lap-Cats with a worse taste in furniture. In **The Calibration Saga** they supply omen comedy during the widen temptation and soft unease after the Caliburn escapes.

## Description

Same round faces and soft coats as common lap-cats, but they seek clerk desks, Frame benches, and conduit corners where load-noise leaves a faint charge. Fur lifts in a narrow vertical ridge that visitors joke looks like a Caliburn slit. The ridge falls when you pet them. They purr loud enough to shake a loose dial needle—usually not enough to ruin a reading, always enough to annoy Noro.

They hunt Pocket-Mice with average success and sleep through House stamp arguments. They are not possessed. Static is a preference, like sunny ledgers for ordinary cats.

Concrete saga beat: during Widening as Gift, several Static Lap-Cats arrange themselves on Orrin’s workbench in loose slit shapes while seam-moths halo the glass. Tam laughs once, then stops laughing. After the vault fails and the Caliburn escapes into city noise, visitor queues report cats whose fur “agrees” too neatly—comedy that ages into wrong-tick unease without needing a glass person in the room.

Preferred naps: clerk blotters, Frame heel plates after they cool, and any chair that recently held a person arguing about stamps. Their purr can mask a soft metronome if you are tired enough—which is why Tam banishes them from the alcove during serious ticks and sneaks them back during tea.

They are comedy until visitors start asking whether the fur “agrees” on purpose. Jori’s ledge stories say the asking is the haunt, not the cat.

## Beliefs About Them

Children call a slit-fur cat lucky. Dott’s courtesy cards later warn visitors not to trust dials that love you, and someone always adds a doodle of a cat. Ward hands tolerate them until fur sheds into shutter grease. Orrin lets one sleep on the scraped ledger and moves it when he needs the page.

## Narrative Role

Visitor-omen ecology and aftermath texture. They keep haunt comedy bodily and local while the Caliburn becomes a city-scale problem.

## Notes

Related to, not identical with, Veloria Lap-Cats. Not Echoforms. See Widening as Gift and Ask Again at the Ledge.
