# Severin Ault

## Name

Severin Ault

## Overview

Severin Ault is a Choirglass envoy of a Resonant House classification table in Veloria. He spent eleven years at classification tables as reader, clerk of grades and envoy. The Houses send him west to request the original Kestry Letter under the partial-knowledge manners, carrying the Saltjaw Hazard Notice he did not write. Odile and Maren refuse him, and Maren makes him see what the notice left out. He goes with the expedition as envoy, accepts Nessa Orvay's terms, and orders the hulls back. After the withdrawal his report includes the margins the notice omitted. In the end he carries one more loan request to Sallow Strand and tells Maren she is free to refuse it.

He leans toward legitimacy and truth over security and profit. He is not a rebel. He believes the Houses' stamp should mean something, and that it means nothing if it is used to make a man tidy.

## Description

Tall, faceted and translucent. His thoughts show as faint shifts of colour along the planes of his face to anyone who knows how to look. His voice carries an overtone, like a second speaker in another room, and at full strength it seems to come from two places at once. Envoys are taught to keep their colour still at tables, and he is good at it until the end of the letter. Under great shock, as at the clash, his colour goes white all through. He travels plainly, by fruit wagon, and does not drink the tea he is given.

**Can:** hold four pulls of a file in view at once; speak to a hostile room; parley under writ.
**Cannot:** command the Ward; override a bloc vote; make his colour lie for long.
**Cost:** he carried the notice, and he knows a parley was wasted on his watch.

## Beliefs About Them

The Sealing bloc thinks he went soft in Wabet. The Witness bloc thinks he is the best envoy they have. Hadrin Stell thinks he is a clerk with a writ. Maren thinks he is a man who read the end of the letter.

## Narrative Role

He is the face of the Houses' first reaction, classification rather than conquest. He embodies the "legitimacy" pull, accepts the parley, tries to prevent the shot and fails, and gives honest testimony afterwards.

## Notes

He knocked twice on a jetty post on leaving Saltjaw and did not whistle, because he did not know how.
