# Lovebird

## Name

Lovebird

## Overview

**Lovebird** is an Aelwyn Canopy diver-bird: white head, black wings and back, thin white taloned legs, known for hovering to yellow bell-flowers grown in red pots marked with darker eye-circles.

## Description

Unlike Love Birds pairs, Lovebird often works alone at a bloom. Beak points to the flower heart; talons test petal rims without shredding. Canopy gardeners paint eye-circles on terracotta so birds and people share a look-rule: if the pot’s eyes face you, approach soft. Darker blue shadows under stems warn of wind. Children may watch; they may not tug stems. Adults who swap pots mid-hover apologize with fresh water at the root.

## Cultural Meaning

In Aelwyn Canopy, Lovebird is careful single attention. Resonance favors unmoved pots during the dive. Confusing it with the outward pair for a lazy joke empties both teachings.

## Habitat and Behavior

Cultivated cobalt-bright clearings, pot gardens, and quiet canopy shelves. Hovers, grips, lifts; rarely nests in the same pot it feeds.
