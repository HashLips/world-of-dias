# Twice-Bell Crickets

## Overview

Twice-Bell Crickets chirp in paired phrases that never match exactly, a living miniature of F120's twice-different bell lore.

## Description

Tiny, dark, loud for their size. The second chirp arrives altered in pitch or delay. Fields of them create a textured, almost conversational night.

## Beliefs About Them

Some listeners try to map mood from the mismatch; mostly it is ambient wonder.

## Narrative Role

Sonic fauna for F120's variation theme.
