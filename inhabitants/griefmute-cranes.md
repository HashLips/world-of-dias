# Griefmute Cranes

## Overview

Griefmute Cranes are tall pale cranes that gather where F380's grief-mute textures settle, standing long hours without calling.

## Description

Ash-white plumage, slow steps, eyes kind and tired. They do not cause grief; they punctuate it. When color returns to a zone, cranes often leave first.

## Beliefs About Them

Interrupting a crane vigil is considered emotionally clumsy.

## Narrative Role

Beautiful sorrow-fauna—not evil, still heavy.
