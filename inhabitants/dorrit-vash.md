# Dorrit Vash

## Name

Dorrit Vash

## Overview

Dorrit Vash was the Kharad skipper of *The Patient Lead*, the broad tarred cutter that carried the Saltjaw shelf survey. She drowned at the strike on the fifth survey day after pushing Brann Oake toward a floating timber. Her body was recovered on the shelf on the second day of the search, and Saltjaw buried her on the slope above the mine's outfall with a lump of verdigris iron in her hands. She is the saga's first death. Every later argument about cost comes back to her.

## Description

Broad and basalt-grey, with the dense, mineral-grained build common among Kharad and a skipper's permanent squint against glare. She laughed easily and said she sank in a bath. Years before the saga a storm broke the ore lighter she was crewing. She came ashore on its wreckage with a crate of ore in her arms because she refused to lose a sack, and Bastien Crale's father paid her for the sack and not for the lighter. *The Patient Lead* was built partly from that lighter's keel.

**Can:** handle a heavy boat in a lumpy sea; read a crew; hold a bearing without a vent mark for a while.
**Cannot:** float. Kharad bodies are dense. That is a fact about bodies, not a flaw of character, and it killed her.

## Beliefs About Them

Saltjaw remembers her as the skipper who never lost a sack. The Aumeri, who never met her, remember her as "the heavy one" who died on Tavi's stone. Tavi carries her death as his own.

## Narrative Role

The cost of the strike. Her death is why Tavi breaks with the island's silence, why Bastien listens to Tamsy, and why the Hazard Notice's "one crew member drowned" reads as an insult to the people who buried her.

## Notes

Her grave is on the slope above the outfall, not on the shingle. The verdigris iron in her hands is ordinary Saltjaw ore: a living, not treasure.
