# Outer Lane Keepers

## Name

Outer Lane Keepers

## Overview

**Outer Lane Keepers** maintain the milestone rests and pegboards on F120’s outer arc. They teach comparison. They rarely chase speed, and they treat a traveler’s certainty as a wet coat: something to hang up before anyone sits down. They are ordinary stewards. The Span does not anoint them. The road simply goes worse in the places they stop showing up.

## Description

Travel-worn coats with stylus calluses on the thumb side, the particular shine of people who write more than they announce. Their impatience is calm and specific. They will wait while you boast, and they will not move your peg for you. Many overlap Refrain Walker training without claiming the whole walker identity; a Keeper can be a walker, a retired walker, or a person who only ever loved the rests. You know them by the way they stand between you and the inner lane, not blocking, reminding.

At dusk they smell of wet rope and cold tea. They speak in short corrections: “second pass,” “different chip,” “write it before you sleep.” The lean roof is their real office. Under it, a wrong sentence sounds louder.

## Beliefs About Them

Guides say Keepers sleep best where the hush is honest, which is why night at a milestone rest feels supervised by someone mending rather than by someone hunting. Inner-lane jokes call them slow. Survivors of wrong doors call them kind, and the kindness is procedural: a peg, a slip, a bench that faces the slower arc. Softfruit guests trust them quickly because the work resembles noticing. House clerks trust them slowly because the board will not grow a serial number to make the clerk comfortable.

## Narrative Role

Local mentors for Span scenes. They turn a near-return into a lesson instead of a trap, and they are the faces a first-pass visitor remembers when the nouns run out. They can be tired, funny, or stern. They are not destined saviors, and a story that makes one into a chosen hinge has misunderstood the outer lane.

## Notes

Ordinary stewards. Not time-priests, not loop wardens, not a ferry guild.
