# Quietwell Serpents

## Overview

Quietwell Serpents are pale sand serpents that hunt near Quiet Well and Lumira oasis edges, striking most often when noise breaks the desert's expected hush.

## Description

Body length of a tall person, scales like frosted glass over sand, eyes the color of dry wells. They move with almost no sound. Venom is real and medically serious; deaths are uncommon but remembered loudly. A near-strike often begins as a wrong shout—then sand that seems to listen.

## Beliefs About Them

Silence Law and serpent caution braid together: loud travelers are said to 'call the pale rope.' Guides teach soft steps as survival, not only respect. Teaching-story form: [The Pale Rope of Lumira](../myths/the-pale-rope-of-lumira.md).

## Avoidance Customs

Soft voices near oasis hush. No boasts on open dune. Travel dense when Listening Heat stands hard. If you must shout, shout once for rescue—not for theater.

## Narrative Role

A feared Wabet encounter that reinforces Lumira's silence ethic with teeth. Classed as **hazard** in [Creatures of Dias](../world/creatures-of-dias.md).
