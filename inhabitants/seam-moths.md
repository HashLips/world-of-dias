# Seam-Moths

## Name

Seam-Moths

## Overview

**Seam-Moths** are pale moths drawn to residual calibration seams and bright instrument glass. They are quieter and more glass-loving than bleedweather moths. In **The Calibration Saga** they gather when the Spectrum Frame’s leftover seam stays open—and again when the Caliburn tempts Orrin to widen it.

## Description

Wing-span about two fingers. Wings are chalk-white with faint tick lines that look painted until you touch them and find only dust. They rest on Frame lattice, loupe cords, and the warm edge of residual seams. They do not swarm flame; they swarm agreement—places where glass and measurement have been arguing with the air.

They are not Echoforms. Crushing one leaves ordinary powder, not cyan shimmer. Still, Row hands lower their voices when a cloud of them arrives, the way people quiet for a doctor’s visit.

Concrete saga beat: in Widening as Gift, seam-moths make a soft halo around the Frame while the Caliburn shows Orrin cleaner stacks. The halo looks like blessing. Orrin reads it as pressure. He does not widen. Later, after escape, stray seam-moths still find bright glass in third-ring shops, but the thick halo does not return.

At rest their wings fold into a narrow measure-slit silhouette that static lap-cats seem to mock by coincidence. They taste residual seams the way other moths taste nectar: brief contact, then a dusty lift. Crushing them is considered bad Row manners even when they block a loupe.

Brusk’s shutter walks sometimes end with a pale wing stuck to a nail. He flicks it off without comment. The comment is the nail.

## Beliefs About Them

Apprentices call them seam angels or seam pests depending on sleep. Warden Brusk hates them on principle: anything that loves open invitation near sky-adjacent instruments. Visitors photograph them and caption wrong myths. Operators file clusters as residual fauna when they file them at all.

## Narrative Role

Visual ecology for the residual seam and widen temptation. They make the Frame’s leftover openness feel inhabited before the Caliburn’s escape turns the whole city into quieter wrong ticks.
