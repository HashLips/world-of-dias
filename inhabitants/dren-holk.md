# Dren Holk

## Name

Dren Holk

## Overview

Dren Holk is a senior deck mate on Driftfall’s passenger liners—older than Frederick Lumens, louder in the yards, and the man who took **Roadglow Shard** because rarity looks like wages he was never paid.

## Description

F432 race record: **Human**.

Broad-shouldered, salt-scarred hands, a laugh that arrives after the joke has already hurt someone. Dren keeps junior hands in line with job threats more than fists: one word to a free-captain and a boy like Frederick is off the roster. He drinks at **The Salt Ledger** loud enough to be believed, and he treats True Blues as property that migrates upward.

He told Frederick, plain, that the shard would never come back. He meant it as law.

## Beliefs About Them

Crew below him call him necessary weather. Shore traders call him a hard customer. People who have lost small things to him call him a thief with a title.

## Narrative Role

Pressure node for Frederick’s first act of courage: reclaiming Roadglow from Dren’s bunk forces flight from the only home Frederick knew.

## Notes

Antagonist of early Lumen chapters, not a realm-scale villain. Do not redeem by default; later fate left open.
