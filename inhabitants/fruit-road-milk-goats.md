# Fruit-Road Milk-Goats

## Overview

Fruit-Road Milk-Goats travel with some caravans and waystations, providing milk, company, and weed control along berms.

## Description

Lean travel goats with painted ear-notches for crew marks. They browse peel piles and hedge-berry leaves. Kids learn to milk before they learn VEL counting. Not parade goats—working soft.

## Beliefs About Them

Drivers say a milk-goat’s refusal to walk means the next mile is unkind. Children believe the goats remember kind hands across seasons.

## Narrative Role

Fills dairy/companion gap on the fruit road.
